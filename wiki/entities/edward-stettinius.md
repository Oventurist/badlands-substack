# Edward Stettinius

Edward Stettinius served as U.S. Secretary of State in the closing period of World War II.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The article states that under his leadership the [[state-department|State Department]] explicitly coordinated with American unions to ensure that post-war labor movements aligned with American free-market ideals, preventing Soviet-style worker councils from taking root in Europe.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

This coordination is presented as evidence that the AFL's [[free-trade-union-committee|Free Trade Union Committee]] work in France and Italy was no ad hoc effort but official policy, in which workers' rights were secondary to corralling foreign workforces into an ideological box.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
