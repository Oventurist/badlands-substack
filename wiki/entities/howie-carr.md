# Howie Carr

Howie Carr is a Boston Herald columnist and conservative commentator. In July 2023 his column on Massachusetts migrant housing became the basis for national coverage, reported by [[breitbart]] and aggregated in the [[badlands-news-brief]], asserting that Governor [[maura-healey|Maura Healey]] was sparing "millionaire destinations" from absorbing illegal aliens housed at state expense in motels.[1]

Carr's reporting identified the towns and cities where the state was funding migrant motels and noted that nearly none of the wealthiest Massachusetts communities — Nantucket, Martha's Vineyard, Cambridge or Newburyport — appeared among them.[1] His column drew on data sought from the Healey administration by state Rep. [[peter-durant|Peter Durant]], whose request for a cost accounting the governor's office declined to answer in dollar terms while confirming that nearly 40 motels were in use.[1]
