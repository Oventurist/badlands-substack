# Marc Elias

Marc Elias is a prominent Democratic election lawyer, widely known for litigating voting-rules cases on behalf of Democratic campaigns and committees. In the Badlands corpus he is treated as one of the principal operational figures of what its writers call the election "shadow cabal."

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports, citing Politico, that Elias was among the experts convened by Senator [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] — with former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the heads of Democracy Forward and Protect Democracy — to prepare Senate Democrats for a set of hypothetical election-disruption scenarios ahead of the 2026 midterms and to improve coordination with state and local election administrators.

The Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] comments that "Marc Elias is strategizing election interference instead of preparing for his inevitable criminal trial, which is a bit discouraging" — an assertion of anticipated prosecution presented as the author's opinion rather than as reported fact.
