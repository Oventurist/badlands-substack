# Marc Elias

Marc Elias is a prominent Democratic election lawyer, widely known for litigating voting-rules cases on behalf of Democratic campaigns and committees. In the Badlands corpus he is treated as one of the principal operational figures of what its writers call the election "shadow cabal."

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing Politico, that Elias was among the experts convened by Senator [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] — with former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the heads of Democracy Forward and Protect Democracy — to prepare Senate Democrats for a set of hypothetical election-disruption scenarios ahead of the 2026 midterms and to improve coordination with state and local election administrators.

The Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] comments that "Marc Elias is strategizing election interference instead of preparing for his inevitable criminal trial, which is a bit discouraging" — an assertion of anticipated prosecution presented as the author's opinion rather than as reported fact.

## Trump's attack (May 2026)

In his May 12, 2026 [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement announcing the [[election-integrity-army|Election Integrity Army]] for the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] named Elias alongside Holder as part of the Democrat-led "Election Integrity Group," calling Elias "a terrible lawyer with a horrible track record" and "the same disgusting individual who was responsible for the fake Russia dossier from a foreign nation to meddle in the 2016 Election, which I won in historic fashion." The statement — the subject of the lead item in the May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief — was published in full by the Brief and framed as the administration's opening salvo in the midterm election-integrity fight.
