# Heritage Front

The Heritage Front was a Canadian far-right organisation cited by Badlands Media as a documented case of an intelligence service building and steering an ostensibly grassroots extremist group.[1]

Badlands lists it alongside [[patriot-front|Patriot Front]] and the [[atomwaffen-division|Atomwaffen Division]] as evidence of three-letter agency operations behind right-wing organisations, pointing to the long-established role of a Canadian security-service operative in the group's creation and management.[1] The article invokes these cases to argue that the widening fissures between the "radical left" and "radical right" in 2025 are being actively inflamed rather than simply emerging on their own.[1]
