# Sam Neill

Sam Neill was an actor best known for playing Dr. Alan Grant in the original *Jurassic Park* film.[1] He died at the age of 78, his death reported in the same 24-hour period as those of Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] and former Qatari Emir [[hamad-bin-khalifa-al-thani|Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani]].[1]

Neill's death was cited in the July 13, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as part of an unusually dense cluster of notable deaths — the basis for the observation that "the Grim Reaper was busy yesterday."[1] Unlike the political deaths reported in the same edition, Neill's passing was recounted without accompanying speculation about foul play.[1]
