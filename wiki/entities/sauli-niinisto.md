# Sauli Niinistö

Sauli Niinistö is a Finnish politician who served as President of [[finland|Finland]], holding the office during the country's accession to [[nato|NATO]] in April 2023.[1]

## Balticconnector statement

On Tuesday, 10 October 2023, Niinistö issued a statement on the damage to the [[balticconnector|Balticconnector]] gas pipeline and an adjacent telecommunications cable running between Finland and [[estonia|Estonia]], saying: "It is likely that damage to both the gas pipeline and the communication cable is the result of outside activity. The cause of the damage is not yet clear, the investigation continues in cooperation between Finland and Estonia."[1] The statement was among the first official indications that the incident may have been deliberate rather than accidental.[1] See [[2023-balticconnector-pipeline-damage|2023 Balticconnector pipeline damage]].[1]
