# Banu Bakr

The Banu Bakr were an Arabian clan from which the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]] traces its descent, counted by modern Arab scholars as part of the Hanifa tribe.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Pre-Islamic identity

Prior to the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]], the Banu Bakr was a Christian clan — the Hanifa was not — and Arab genealogists count it among the original Christian tribes of Arabia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) It became one of the more significant clans to resist Muhammad's conquest of the region.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The clan's territory corresponded roughly to al-Yamamah in central Arabia, near where the future capital [[riyadh|Riyadh]] would later stand.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Defeat and the emir's release

The Banu Bakr were defeated by Muhammad in the year 627.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Their Christian emir was tied to a pole and threatened with execution; rather than renounce his devotion to Christ, he calmly recited three times a wisdom demonstrating both his devotion to God and his total lack of fear of death.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Recognizing that he was truly a God-fearing man, Muhammad released him and embraced him as a brother, and the emir later converted to Islam of his own volition.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Both [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]] and, through him, the modern Saudi dynasty descend from this clan and the ancient Hanifa tribe, which predates the Prophet.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
