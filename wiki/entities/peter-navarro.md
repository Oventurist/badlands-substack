# Peter Navarro

Peter Navarro is an American economist who served as a trade adviser in the first Trump administration, where he was a leading advocate of tariffs and a confrontational trade posture toward [[china|China]]. He later became known for his refusal to comply with a congressional subpoena issued by the House select committee investigating January 6, 2021.

Navarro appears in the July 30, 2026 Badlands Brief as a precedent in the discussion of possible legal consequences for Anthony Fauci. After Fauci invoked the [[fifth-amendment|Fifth Amendment]] and declined to answer questions at a [[senate|Senate]] hearing on Covid origins, Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] indicated he would pursue [[contempt-of-congress|contempt of Congress]] charges. The Brief described this as "the charge that put [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and Peter Navarro behind bars," using both men's imprisonment as evidence that the statute has recently been enforced with custodial sentences.
