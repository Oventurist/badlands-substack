# Klaus Schwab

Klaus Schwab is the founder of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and the author of the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] programme. In the Badlands Media corpus he functions as a personification of the technocratic globalist project.

He appears in [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]] as one of the explicit inputs [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] supplied to [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]]: after adding the WEF, the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], [[united-nations|the United Nations]] and the [[esg|ESG]] system to the model's fictional scenario, he prompted it to incorporate "Klaus Schwab's 'Great Reset' as an additional plan for the consortium to utilize." In the resulting synthesis the Great Reset is folded into the multigenerational campaign attributed to the fictional Global Harmony Consortium, alongside ESG scoring and the climate change narrative, as a mechanism for advancing global governance and eroding national sovereignty.
