# Klaus Schwab

Klaus Schwab is the founder of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and the author of the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] programme. In the Badlands Media corpus he functions as a personification of the technocratic globalist project.

He appears in AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO as one of the explicit inputs [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] supplied to [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]]: after adding the WEF, the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], [[united-nations|the United Nations]] and the [[esg|ESG]] system to the model's fictional scenario, he prompted it to incorporate "Klaus Schwab's 'Great Reset' as an additional plan for the consortium to utilize." In the resulting synthesis the Great Reset is folded into the multigenerational campaign attributed to the fictional Global Harmony Consortium, alongside ESG scoring and the climate change narrative, as a mechanism for advancing global governance and eroding national sovereignty.

## The Davos framing (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief quotes Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describing the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] as "an organization founded by Klaus Schwab in 1971 under the guidance of Henry Kissinger, for the purpose of planning and managing the subjugation of the human race" — a framing deployed to celebrate President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s use of the Davos stage to ratify the charter of the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], "an organization that stands in direct opposition to the WEF's core mission."
