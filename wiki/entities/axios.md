# Axios

## 2028 Democratic power rankings

On July 27, 2026 Axios published Democratic presidential "power rankings" placing former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] and Representative [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] atop the party's early 2028 field.[1] The rankings drew on interviews with Democratic strategists, donors, elected officials and party insiders, evaluating political influence, fundraising strength and national profile, and Axios noted the list reflected early positioning rather than declared candidacies in a field that could shift as other governors and senators weighed bids.[1] Axios also reported on venture capital criticism of proposals that could limit [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source AI]] models.[1]
