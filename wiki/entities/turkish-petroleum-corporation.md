# Turkish Petroleum Corporation (TPAO)

The **Turkish Petroleum Corporation** (TPAO) is [[turkey|Turkey]]'s state-owned oil and gas exploration and production company.[1]

## Kirkuk acquisition (2026)

In late July 2026, TPAO acquired a 15% stake in bp's Kirkuk oil project in northern [[iraq|Iraq]], alongside ConocoPhillips, giving Turkey a share in fields holding more than 3 billion barrels of oil equivalent.[1] Turkish Energy Minister [[alparslan-bayraktar|Alparslan Bayraktar]] described the transaction as a major milestone toward TPAO's target of producing one million barrels of oil and gas per day.[1]

The deal was announced in parallel with five bilateral agreements signed in Ankara between President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] and Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali al-Zaidi]].[1]

## Strategic significance

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the associated pipeline arrangements are expected to move one million barrels per day to refineries at Ceyhan in Turkey — an amount equal to the volume that transited the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] before the outbreak of the war in February 2026.[1] He noted that Iraq's [[north-oil-company|North Oil Company]] had announced eleven days earlier that its Kirkuk pipeline was ready to begin pumping, representing roughly one third of the million-barrel target and suggesting the deals had been in preparation far longer than publicly disclosed.[1]
