# Noam Tibon

Noam Tibon is a retired Israeli Defense Forces general who became a prominent critic of the Israeli government's conduct of the Gaza war and of its handling of the hostage file.[1]

## March 2025 Tel Aviv rally

On 27 March 2025, thousands of Israelis demonstrated at Tel Aviv's Habima Square, calling for the release of the hostages held in Gaza and protesting against what demonstrators labelled "the October 7 government." Addressing the Habima protest, Tibon asserted that the IDF's renewed military operations were endangering the hostages.[1]

"But the October 7 government doesn't care about this. They are busy toasting the passage of their budget," Tibon said.[1]

After the rally, hundreds of participants joined Einav Zangauker, mother of hostage Matan Zangauker, in marching to the nearby Begin Gate. Police officers, some on horseback, sought to prevent demonstrators from blocking the road, leading to a clash in which several protesters were thrown to the ground; seven protesters were arrested according to Hebrew media reports.[1]

The protest coincided with the [[knesset|Knesset]]'s passage of a law expanding politicians' control over judicial appointments, a central plank of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s judicial overhaul.[1]
