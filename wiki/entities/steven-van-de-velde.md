# Steven van de Velde

Steven van de Velde is a Dutch beach volleyball player whose selection for the Netherlands team at the 2024 Paris Summer Olympics triggered international outrage because he is a convicted child rapist.[1] He was convicted in 2016 of raping a British 12-year-old girl.[1]

## Conviction and sentence

Van de Velde was sentenced to four years in a British prison for the offence.[1] He served roughly one year of that sentence before being transferred to his home country of the Netherlands, where he was released after approximately one month.[1] The brevity of the custodial term relative to the original sentence became a focal point of criticism when his Olympic participation became public.[1]

## Olympic controversy

On the eve of the opening of the Paris Games, the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]] faced calls for an investigation into how a convicted child rapist had been cleared to compete.[1] Advocacy organisations warned that sporting bodies were sending a dangerous message to sex offenders and inflicting "collateral damage" on victims of sexual abuse.[1] Ciara Bergman, chief executive of Rape Crisis England & Wales, called the inclusion "irresponsible" and said it created an "enormous sense of impunity," noting that all athletes sign a declaration promising to act as role models.[1]

The IOC gave no indication that it would investigate or take action, with the position reported that Dutch experts "had concluded that there was no risk of him reoffending."[1] Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as emblematic of 2024, arguing that his participation removed important deterrents and signalled to sex criminals that consequences would be minimal for those who excel in their craft, and closing with the sardonic instruction to "trust the experts."[1]
