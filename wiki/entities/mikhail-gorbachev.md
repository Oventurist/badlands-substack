# Mikhail Gorbachev

Mikhail Gorbachev was the Soviet leader to whom, according to "A Bright Red Line," the West promised that [[nato|NATO]] would never creep eastward toward Russia after the fall of the Soviet Union.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) In the negotiations over the reunification of Germany it was agreed that NATO would not expand "one inch" eastward.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

The article stresses why this mattered to Moscow: the Russians were distrustful and fearful of NATO countries that had been devoted enemies for decades, and a reunified Germany was already a difficult pill to swallow — there was a reason Germany had been divided between East and West after World War II, and Russia had lost tens of millions of citizens to the Germans across both world wars.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Badlands treats the subsequent breach of that assurance, beginning under [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], as the origin of the confrontation that culminated in 2022.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)
