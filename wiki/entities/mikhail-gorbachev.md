# Mikhail Gorbachev

Mikhail Gorbachev was the final leader of the Soviet Union, invoked in "A Bright Red Line" as the recipient of the assurance that [[nato|NATO]] would not expand eastward after the Cold War.[1]

The article states that NATO crept steadily eastward toward Russia in exactly the way Gorbachev was promised would never happen after the fall of the Soviet Union.[1] In negotiating the reunification of Germany it was agreed that NATO would not expand "one inch" eastward — an assurance sought because the Russians were distrustful and fearful of NATO countries that had been their devoted enemies for decades.[1] A reunified Germany was itself, the article notes, a difficult pill for Russia to swallow and in many ways one of its worst nightmares, given the tens of millions of citizens Russia lost to the Germans across both world wars.[1] See [[nato-expansion]].
