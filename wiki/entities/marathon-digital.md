# Marathon Digital

Marathon Digital is one of the largest publicly traded Bitcoin mining firms in the United States.[1]

The company was represented at the June 2024 [[2024-trump-mar-a-lago-bitcoin-mining-meeting|Mar-a-Lago meeting]] between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[bitcoin]] mining industry by Jayson Browder, a senior vice president, who served as the principal on-the-record source describing the session.[1] Browder said the industry's "Power 30" was present — nine to eleven executives from firms collectively controlling roughly 30% of the Bitcoin network's total computing power — and characterized Trump as prepared and informed, noting that he "understood how bitcoin mining would support these things like AI."[1] Browder also recounted Trump handling an Auradine ASIC chip and remarking approvingly on its American manufacture.[1]
