# Eaton Fire

The Eaton Fire was a wildfire that ignited in the Los Angeles area on January 7, 2025, the same day as the [[palisades-fire|Pacific Palisades fire]].[1] It killed 19 people and razed 9,400 structures, making it one of the deadliest components of the [[january-2025-los-angeles-wildfires|January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires]].[1]

As of October 2025, the cause of the Eaton Fire remained unclear, in contrast to the Palisades fire, for which authorities arrested suspect [[jonathan-rinderknecht|Jonathan Rinderknecht]] that month on suspicion of arson.[1] The unresolved origin of the Eaton Fire has been cited by [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] contributors as part of a wider pattern of official narratives about the Los Angeles fires that they regard as incomplete.[1]

## Initial response

On January 8, 2025, the second day of the fire, [[pasadena-fire-department|Pasadena Fire Department]] Chief [[chad-augustin|Chad Augustin]] said 60 strike teams with 750 firefighters were being mobilized against the Eaton Fire, with reinforcements arriving from across California and as far away as Arizona.[2] At that stage he estimated that 200 to 500 structures had been damaged or lost and said the fire's origins were still undetermined.[2] Augustin described his firefighters as "exhausted" and said the simultaneous outbreak of multiple area fires had strained the mutual-aid resources Pasadena normally draws on through its partnership with Los Angeles County, producing an unprecedented situation.[2] He acknowledged that the water supply had been stretched by demand and power outages, but attributed the fire's expansion to erratic wind conditions rather than to water infrastructure, expressing hope that milder winds that evening would allow crews to gain containment.[2]
