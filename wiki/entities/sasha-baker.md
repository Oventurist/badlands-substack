# Sasha Baker

Sasha Baker is an American defense official who served as the Defense Department's top policy official during the [[lloyd-austin-hospitalization-controversy|January 2024 hospitalization]] of Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]].[1]

Baker was the most senior official physically present at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] during the days in which Austin was secretly hospitalized at Walter Reed Military Medical Center.[1] She attended a meeting at the White House on January 3, 2024 unaware that Austin was hospitalized, an illustration of how narrowly knowledge of the secretary's condition was held within the department.[1]
