# Islamic State of Iraq

The Islamic State of Iraq (ISI) was an umbrella group of Iraqi jihadi organizations formed in 2006 and a major Al-Qaeda formation fought by U.S. forces in Iraq.[1] [[abu-bakr-al-baghdadi|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi]] became its leader in 2010.[1]

## Expansion into Syria

Beginning in April 2013 the group expanded outside Iraq, announcing that the [[al-nusra-front|Al-Nusra Front]] was its Syrian extension and that it had supplied al-Nusra with strategy, planning and monthly funding for the Syrian war.[1] Around the same time it renamed itself the Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant (ISIL), the Levant being the older name for the eastern Mediterranean region now occupied by Lebanon, Syria and Israel.[1] The merged formations later became known as [[isis|ISIS]], the Islamic State of Iraq and Syria, and eventually declared a caliphate.[1]

## Role in the article's argument

The Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution" contends that Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s May 2013 trip to Syria was undertaken to help the Islamic State of Iraq expand into Syria, creating a new enemy for what the author calls the never-ending war on terror after the death of [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] left the old-guard Al-Qaeda diminished.[1] The author also argues that President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s consistent use of the term "ISIL" rather than "ISIS" was deliberate, intended to keep American attention on Al-Qaeda in Iraq while ignoring Al-Qaeda in Syria.[1] Obama additionally characterized the group as a "JV team" relative to Al-Qaeda, an analogy the article treats as a knowing act of concealment.[1]
