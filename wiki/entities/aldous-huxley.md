# Aldous Huxley

Aldous Huxley was the British author of *Brave New World*, named by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language" as one of a group of British writers who moved in the same circles during the first two decades of the 1900s, alongside [[george-orwell|George Orwell]], [[h-g-wells|H.G. Wells]], Huxley's brother [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] and [[margaret-sanger|Margaret Sanger]].[1]

Carlson writes that at least some of this group supported [[eugenics|eugenics]], believing in control over who reproduced and how often, and that Adolf Hitler took many of his ideas from eugenics supporters in early twentieth-century America and England.[1] He adds that Julian Huxley was instrumental in the large psychedelic drug push in America during the 1960s, and argues more generally that the [[cabal|Cabal]]'s plan was shared with these authors, accounting for the predictive accuracy of their fiction.[1]

## Recruitment of Timothy Leary and the drug-based religion

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that Huxley recruited [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] to the cause of retooling humanity under a new drug-based pagan religion, and that the two collaborated for years.[2] Describing their planning of this "final revolution," Leary wrote in 1983 that they had "run up against the Judeo-Christian commitment to one God, one religion, one reality, that has cursed Europe for centuries," that drugs opening the mind to multiple realities "inevitably lead to a polytheistic view of the universe," and that "the time for a new humanist religion based on intelligence, good natured pluralism and scientific paganism had arrived."[2]

Ehret treats [[brave-new-world|*Brave New World*]] as a transparent statement of the oligarchical program rather than mere fiction, with the figure of Mustapha Mond representing the transhuman control system above the test-tube alphas, betas and deltas.[2] The soma, "feelies" and loveless sex of the novel are read as the templates for the LSD and psilocybin cultural matrix Huxley pursued in life, which in conjunction with the new high priests of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] emerging out of [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] would furnish the foundations for a new era of feudalism and willful depopulation.[2]

## Family and the Fabian circle

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] notes that Aldous Huxley, author of *Brave New World*, was the brother of [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]], the first man to use the term "trans-humanism" and a member of the British Eugenics Society.[3] He places Aldous, along with [[george-orwell|George Orwell]] and [[h-g-wells|H.G. Wells]], among the members of the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]], adding that many believe *1984* was an exposé of what Orwell learned through that society.[3] The Huxley family was also one of the four lines — with [[francis-galton|Galton]], [[charles-darwin|Darwin]] and Wedgwood — used in Galton's failed selective-breeding experiment.[3]
