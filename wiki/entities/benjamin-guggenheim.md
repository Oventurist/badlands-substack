# Benjamin Guggenheim

Benjamin Guggenheim was a wealthy American businessman who died in the 1912 sinking of the [[rms-titanic|Titanic]].[1]

Badlands Media commentary recounts a long-circulating theory that the Titanic was sunk because three wealthy businessmen aboard — Guggenheim, [[isidor-straus|Isa Strauss]] and [[john-jacob-astor|John Jacob Astor]] — opposed the creation of a [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]. In that telling, the ship was sunk to eliminate their opposition and to send a message to others who spoke against the plan drafted at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] in November 1910.[1] The article presents this as a theory rather than an established fact.[1]
