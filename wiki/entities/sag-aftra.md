# SAG-AFTRA

The Screen Actors Guild-American Federation of Television and Radio Artists (SAG-AFTRA) is the union representing American film and television performers.[1]

## 2023 strike

In July 2023, tens of thousands of SAG-AFTRA members hit the picket lines, joining 11,000 [[writers-guild-of-america|Writers Guild of America]] screenwriters who had been on strike since May, bringing the film and TV industry to a halt for the second time in three years.[1] The dispute centered on how streaming, which advanced rapidly during the pandemic, had upended the economics of entertainment.[1]

Union president [[fran-drescher|Fran Drescher]] announced the strike in a press conference, arguing that a changed business model necessarily required a changed contract and rejecting "incremental changes on a contract that no longer honors what is happening right now."[1] Striking actors mobilized to picket studio lots and streamer headquarters, an escalation that coincided with the release of "Oppenheimer."[1]

The strike is covered in greater detail at [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]].[1]
