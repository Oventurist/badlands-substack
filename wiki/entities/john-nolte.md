# John Nolte

John Nolte is a columnist for [[breitbart|Breitbart]] who writes primarily on media criticism.[1]

## Coverage of the 2023 CNN town hall

Nolte wrote that [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s 70-minute appearance on [[cnn|CNN]] on May 10, 2023 "might've delivered the final killshot" to a network he described as a propaganda outlet already "taking a ten count" in the ratings, having averaged 587,000 primetime viewers in April.[1] Nolte issued a public "mea culpa" for having predicted earlier that Trump would throw CNN a lifeline by appearing, saying he was happy to be wrong.[1]

He argued the event was a catastrophe for what he called the "Hoax Network," pointing to what he described as tears of frustration in [[jake-tapper|Jake Tapper]]'s voice afterward.[1] Nolte wrote that Trump "put the t-r-u-m-p in 'triumph,'" showed up with a plan and executed it perfectly, exposed moderator [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]] as a "mental lightweight," and charmed viewers who were not "humorless prigs."[1]

## Badlands response

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] credited Nolte with identifying a factor others were underweighting: that regardless of whether centrist and leftist viewers were persuaded on the issues, the decisive cultural fact was that Trump was fun and funny throughout the night, and therefore likable — a quality Burning Bright argued the Democratic Party and all of American politics currently lacks.[1]

## November 2023 column on The Marvels

Nolte's Breitbart column on [[the-marvels|The Marvels]] declared the film "a certified disaster," citing Deadline projections of a $47–55 million opening that would rank as the worst in [[marvel-cinematic-universe|Marvel Cinematic Universe]] history, below the $55.4 million set by 2008's "The Incredible Hulk."[2] He judged the film's prospects poor given its weak "B" CinemaScore despite die-hard turnout, and argued that a franchise which had earned roughly $30 billion worldwide from 30 films had been ruined by ideological content.[2]
