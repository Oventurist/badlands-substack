# J. Edgar Hoover

J. Edgar Hoover was the director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] during the period in which the Bureau seized and reviewed the papers of [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]].[1]

## Role in the Tesla papers episode

In early 1943, two days after Tesla's death, the FBI ordered the Office of Alien Property Custodian to seize Tesla's belongings, and MIT physicist [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] was called in to analyse the artifacts held in government custody.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] emphasised that this meant John G. Trump was working for the FBI while Hoover was its director.[1] Carlson allowed that he did not know whether Trump was working directly with Hoover or bypassing him to work with others inside the Bureau, but insisted he was working with someone there.[1]

## Carlson's characterisation

Carlson argued that the received history of Hoover is unreliable, writing that the more he examines the historical record the more he concludes that taught history "falls somewhere between a manipulation of the truth and a complete lie."[1] He noted that Hoover is conventionally portrayed as "a cross-dressing fascist," said this may or may not be true, and suggested that Hoover was himself being blackmailed — a condition Carlson claims applies to most people in positions of power today.[1] These are the opinions of a Badlands contributor rather than established fact.
