# Deanna Adkins

Dr. Deanna Adkins is a physician and transgender activist who served as director of the Duke Medicine Gender Clinic, launched in 2015 at [[duke-university|Duke]] to provide a comprehensive range of treatments for children as young as two years old presenting with gender dysphoria.[1]

## Public statements

In 2016, Adkins spoke with the Charlotte Observer about her transgender pediatric patients, arguing that very young children do not select their condition: "They are not old enough to consciously just choose to do that. … It is not a choice in any of my patients. It's really an unpleasant thing to have going on in your body to feel that distress about yourself. I can't imagine anyone who would choose to do that."[1]

## Criticism

Adkins's clinic figured in reporting by investigative journalist [[sloan-rachmuth|Sloan Rachmuth]] alleging that North Carolina's leading medical institutions were treating toddlers for gender dysphoria, coverage amplified by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized the field as a "multibillion-dollar industry" and argued that pre-pubertal "social transition" amounts to grooming future customers for later medical intervention.[1]
