# Nelson Rockefeller

Nelson Rockefeller was an American politician and member of the Rockefeller family who was nominated for vice president by President [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]] in 1974.[1]

## Sanders' open letter opposing the nomination

In 1974, while running for the United States Senate on the [[liberty-union-party|Liberty Union]] ticket in Vermont, [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] sent an open letter to President Ford warning of a "virtual Rockefeller family dictatorship over the nation" if Rockefeller were named vice president.[1] In the same letter Sanders called for the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] to be disbanded immediately, in the wake of revelations about the agency's misdeeds.[1] A 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] singled out these two positions as among the most defensible things Sanders had ever said, contrasting the young Sanders' apocalyptic and conspiratorial rhetoric with what the author characterized as his later accommodation to establishment power.[1]
