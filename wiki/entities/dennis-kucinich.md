# Dennis Kucinich

Dennis Kucinich is a former United States congressman from Ohio, long associated with the antiwar wing of American politics.[1]

## RFK Jr. campaign

Kucinich ran the presidential campaign of [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] before stepping down in late October 2023.[1] No official reason was given for the departure, but Badlands Media reported that the split was suspected to have resulted from irreconcilable differences over Israel–Palestine.[1] The publication noted that Kennedy had been a stalwart supporter of Israel, going so far as to oppose renewal of the Iran nuclear deal, a position at odds with Kucinich's noninterventionist record.[1]

The Kucinich departure was presented as evidence that the Israel–Palestine conflict was fracturing not only journalists and pundits but presidential campaigns themselves.[1]
