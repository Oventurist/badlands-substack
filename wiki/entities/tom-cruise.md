# Tom Cruise

Tom Cruise is an American film actor whose work the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]] treats as the connective tissue of the [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]] catalogue. The article observes that Skydance produced *Top Gun: Maverick*, the *Mission Impossible* franchise and *Jack Reacher*, and asks what they have in common — answering: "Tom Cruise." It adds that even before the Skydance–[[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] merger closed, Paramount+ had for a couple of years seemed "like a Tom Cruise streaming app."

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] singles out *Mission Impossible: Dead Reckoning*, released in the summer of 2023 roughly six months after ChatGPT's late-2022 launch, whose plot requires the team to save the world from a powerful [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] system that could be used to take over the world if it fell into the wrong hands. Because production must have begun well before AI became a popular talking point, Carlson reads the film as a warning — an instance of predictive programming — and asks whether Cruise is "one of the good guys" and whether a faction of Hollywood is trying to open the public's eyes to future events.
