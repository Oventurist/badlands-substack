# Tom Cruise

Tom Cruise is the American film star whose work anchors much of the [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]] and [[paramount-global|Paramount]] catalog.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observes that Skydance's productions — *Top Gun: Maverick*, the *Mission Impossible* franchise and *Jack Reacher* — share Cruise as their common theme, and that Paramount+ in recent years "almost seems like a Tom Cruise streaming app."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

Carlson asks directly whether Cruise "is one of the good guys," and whether there is a faction of [[hollywood|Hollywood]] trying to warn the public of possible dangers and open its eyes to future events.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) He points to *Mission Impossible: Dead Reckoning*, released in summer 2023, whose villain is a powerful AI system, as a film that must have been in production well before AI became a popular talking point.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) He also recalls that the original *Top Gun*, released in 1986 under President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]], was the biggest military recruiting tool ever created.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
