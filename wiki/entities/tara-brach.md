# Tara Brach

**Dr. Tara Brach** is described as a psychologist and meditation teacher in the March 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Walking the Path: From Awakening to Integration" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

She is quoted for the claim that "Awakening is not a one-time event, it's a continual opening to presence."[1] The quotation anchors the essay's section "The Unending Path," in which the author tells the reader that [[psychological-integration|integration]] deepens daily rather than concluding — a harsh word will sting tomorrow and be breathed through, a synchronicity will spark next week and be met with a smile.[1] The path, on this account, stretches on "not to a finish line, but to ever-richer terrain."[1]

Brach's line is the last of the essay's expert citations before its concluding call to action, and it establishes the article's closing posture: awakening as an ongoing practice rather than an achieved state, marked by less reactivity, stronger [[discernment|discernment]], shifted priorities and an emerging sense of purpose.[1]
