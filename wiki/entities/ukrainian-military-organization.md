# Ukrainian Military Organization

The Ukrainian Military Organization (UVO) was an interwar Ukrainian nationalist paramilitary group and the direct predecessor of the [[organization-of-ukrainian-nationalists|Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists]] (OUN). As described in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," the UVO was founded in 1920 and operated in secret out of Prague, engaging in terrorism to advance its political interests. Its leadership cadre, including [[stepan-bandera|Stepan Bandera]] and [[andriy-melnyk|Andriy Melnyk]], went on to form the OUN in 1929, which subsequently allied with Nazi Germany.
