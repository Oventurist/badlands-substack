# Lael Brainard

**Lael Brainard** is an American economist and Federal Reserve official who was the preferred candidate of Democrats to chair the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] in 2021–2022, when President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] instead re-appointed the Republican incumbent [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]].[1]

## The 2022 chairmanship decision

In the April 3, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Is Jerome Powell a Patriot?", [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounts that Democrats wanted Biden to elevate Brainard — a fellow Democrat and a woman — to head the Federal Reserve, but that Biden instead re-appointed Powell for a second four-year term in May 2022.[1] Carlson notes the policy contrast between the two: Brainard was "in lockstep with Biden's climate agenda," while Powell had said he would not make climate a top priority.[1]

Carlson treats the decision as anomalous. Biden, he writes, had made it known to those around him that he did not want Powell to head the Fed, and people close to the president said he was pressured by Wall Street to re-appoint him, fearing — with approval ratings at a low after the Afghanistan withdrawal — that Wall Street would crash the stock market if he replaced Powell.[1] Even granting Biden's desire to appear bipartisan, Carlson argues, passing over the opportunity to install a Democratic woman who would push the climate agenda is difficult to explain conventionally; his own conclusion is that Biden "wasn't allowed" to replace Powell.[1]

Brainard thus functions in the essay as the road not taken — the conventional appointment whose rejection Carlson reads as evidence that control of the Federal Reserve had passed out of the Biden administration's hands.[1]
