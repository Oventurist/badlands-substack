# Gitanas Nauseda

Gitanas Nauseda is the President of Lithuania, a NATO member state bordering Belarus, and a frequent voice among the alliance's eastern flank leaders warning about Russian and Russian-aligned military activity near their borders.[1]

## Comments on Wagner in Belarus

In the aftermath of the June 2023 [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] mutiny in Russia, the group's leader was reported to be going into exile in [[belarus|Belarus]], which is governed by [[alexander-lukashenko|Alexander Lukashenko]].[1] Speaking on Wednesday, 28 June 2023 after meeting with [[nato|NATO]] allies, Nauseda said: "If Wagner deploys its serial killers in Belarus, all neighboring countries face even bigger danger of instability."[1]

His remarks were reported alongside a wider Western argument that the mutiny demonstrated Russia was "weakened" and that Washington therefore had to sustain its support for Ukraine — a line [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators described as strange, given that the same period saw Ukrainian forces fail to make any gains against Russian lines.[1] The Nauseda statement was reported by Al Jazeera and recirculated in coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] of the Biden administration's decision to announce a further $500 million in military aid to Ukraine.[1]
