# FCI Tallahassee

FCI Tallahassee is a low-security federal correctional institution in Florida operated by the United States Bureau of Prisons.[1] It gained sustained attention in the Truth Community as the facility at which [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] was held after her conviction.[1]

## Maxwell's incarceration

Maxwell was sentenced in June 2022 to twenty years in prison for, in the words of the Justice Department, "her role in a scheme to sexual [sic] exploit and abuse multiple minor girls with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] over the course of a decade."[1] She served that sentence at FCI Tallahassee, a low-security facility rather than a higher-custody penitentiary — a placement that drew commentary given the severity of the offences.[1] Reporting in February 2023 indicated she was preparing a $10 million appeal from the facility, funded through a divorce settlement with her ex-husband Scott Borgerson.[1]
