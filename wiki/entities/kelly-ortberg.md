# Kelly Ortberg

Kelly Ortberg is the president and chief executive officer of [[boeing|Boeing]], the American aerospace and defense manufacturer. He was brought in to lead the company through its post-2019 crisis era, overseeing efforts to stabilize production and rebuild trust in its commercial jetliners.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Ortberg was among the business executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. The delegation traveled ahead of and alongside the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]], with the administration aiming to secure business deals and purchase agreements — a category in which Boeing's commercial aircraft sales to Chinese carriers have historically featured prominently.
