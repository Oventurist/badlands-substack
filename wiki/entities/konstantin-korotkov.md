# Konstantin Korotkov

**Dr. Konstantin Korotkov** is a researcher in [[biofield-imaging|biofield imaging]] cited in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay reports Korotkov's finding that when individuals enter coherent, heart-centred states, their energy fields extend further and more symmetrically.[1] The author applies this to the [[liminality|liminal]] passage: a person who learns "to rest in your own stillness without seeking external validation" is described as "anchoring a stable signal into a chaotic world."[1]

His citation appears in "The Collective Grid" section alongside [[lynne-mctaggart|Lynne McTaggart]], and underwrites the essay's claim that the liminal pause is "not passive" but "energetic preparation for quantum contribution."[1]
