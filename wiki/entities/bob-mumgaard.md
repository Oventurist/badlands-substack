# Bob Mumgaard

Bob Mumgaard is an American plasma physicist and entrepreneur, co-founder and chief executive of Commonwealth Fusion Systems, a company spun out of MIT research that is developing high-field superconducting magnet technology for compact tokamak-based [[nuclear-fusion|fusion]] power. The company is among the most heavily capitalized private fusion ventures.

Mumgaard was named in March 2026 as an appointee to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], per the March 26 Badlands Brief, placing fusion energy expertise on the council alongside fission advocate [[jacob-dewitte|Jacob DeWitte]].
