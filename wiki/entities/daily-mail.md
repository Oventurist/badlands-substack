# Daily Mail

The Daily Mail is a British newspaper described in Badlands commentary as having been owned by [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]].[1]

## Role in the Vanunu affair

When Maxwell learned in 1985 that Israeli nuclear technician [[mordechai-vanunu|Mordechai Vanunu]] was shopping evidence of the [[israeli-nuclear-program|Israeli nuclear program]] to London news outlets, he had the paper publish several hit pieces designed to discredit and impugn Vanunu's character.[1] The stories were false but damaging enough to lead many to dismiss Vanunu's claims as the vindictive acts of a disgruntled scientist.[1]

## Reporting on the Epstein filings

In late December 2023 the Daily Mail reported that former U.S. president [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] was expected to be named "John Doe 36" when a list of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] associates drawn from court filings was made public, and that some 177 people would be identified across hundreds of files in the [[epstein-court-document-unsealing|court-ordered unsealing]].[2] The report was excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[2]
