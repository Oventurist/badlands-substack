# The Moscow Times

The Moscow Times is an English-language news outlet covering [[russia|Russia]], and one of the sources aggregated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In January 2024 Badlands excerpted a Moscow Times report on President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s vow to "intensify" strikes against [[ukraine|Ukraine]] following the [[belgorod-attack|Belgorod attack]], including his remarks delivered during a visit to a military hospital and his claim that Russia targets military rather than civilian sites.[1]
