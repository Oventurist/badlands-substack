# Mitt Romney

Mitt Romney is an American politician and former management consultant who appears in Badlands coverage chiefly through his early career at [[boston-consulting-group|Boston Consulting Group]].[1]

## BCG years and the Netanyahu connection

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] worked at BCG between 1976 and 1978 as an economic analyst alongside Romney, and that the two men are said to have formed a lifelong bond from that shared experience.[1] The detail was raised in June 2025 while examining BCG's involvement in designing the [[gaza-humanitarian-foundation|Gaza Humanitarian Foundation]], though the commentator cautioned he was offering datapoints rather than asserting a direct connection.[1]

## Transition Integrity Project scenario

The 2020 war games conducted by the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]] predicted that, if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] lost the election, Romney would convince other moderate Republicans in the Senate to recognise [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] as the legitimate president-elect.[2] The group expected [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] to back Romney in this effort on the understanding that it would prove more beneficial for the 2022 elections.[2]

## IRI board membership

"Two Wings, One Ugly Bird" lists the Utah senator and 2012 Republican presidential nominee as a board member of the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]], tying the institute to classic establishment Republicanism.[3] The article argues that prior to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a Republican seeking the presidency effectively had to be of the IRI or at least IRI-approved.[3]
