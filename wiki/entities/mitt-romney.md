# Mitt Romney

Mitt Romney is an American businessman and Republican politician who served as Governor of Massachusetts, was the party's nominee for president in 2012, and later represented Utah in the United States [[senate|Senate]].

## Association with John Bolton

Romney appears in the June 5, 2026 [[badlands-brief-58d|Badlands Brief]] within a career chronology of [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] compiled by Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]. That chronology records that from 2007 to 2018 Bolton was of counsel at the law firm Kirkland & Ellis and served as a foreign policy adviser to Romney during the 2012 presidential campaign.

The list is presented rhetorically, under the question "In which job(s) did Bolton help plan coup d'état?", and situates the Romney campaign among the institutional posts — including [[usaid|USAID]], the [[state-department|State Department]], the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]] and the [[united-nations|United Nations]] ambassadorship — through which Ashe argues Bolton advanced a foreign policy that "sold out the American people so the rest of the world could rise."
