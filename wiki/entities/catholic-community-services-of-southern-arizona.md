# Catholic Community Services of Southern Arizona

Catholic Community Services of Southern Arizona is a non-governmental social services organization named in December 2023 citizen-journalist reporting about the movement of migrants through Arizona airports.[1]

## Role in the Phoenix bussing claims

During the December 2023 [[phoenix-airport-migrant-bussing-story|Phoenix airport migrant bussing story]], X user Tony Ortiz claimed he had noticed a large number of migrants being shipped from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport to different parts of the country, stating that "these Guinea migrants were headed to New York via Catholic Community Services of Southern Arizona."[1] The claim formed part of a wider allegation, amplified by users including [[ashley-st-clair|Ashley St. Clair]], that governmental and non-governmental organizations were coordinating onward domestic travel for migrants arriving at the southern border.[1]

Coverage aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noted the difficulty of verifying the immigration status of travelers at a public airport, while presenting video by [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] of a contracted bus driver confirming he was hauling migrants from the border to the airport as the strongest available evidence.[1]
