# Dawn Addis

**Dawn Addis** is a Democratic member of the California State Assembly representing San Luis Obispo and chair of the Assembly Budget Subcommittee on Health.[1]

## Response to Medi-Cal eligibility changes

Addis publicly criticized the federal environment surrounding California's decision to stop enrolling immigrants with "unsatisfactory immigration status" in [[medi-cal|Medi-Cal]] as of January 1, 2026.[1] "Federal cuts approved by Congress, Trump-era tariffs and ongoing attacks on our undocumented communities have created true fiscal strain for people in states like California," she said, adding that "in response, the legislature acted to protect care by minimizing and postponing reductions in coverage."[1] She characterized the cost of what she called the federal government's erosion of healthcare as extremely high — measured both in tens of millions of dollars to the state and in real health consequences for Californians.[1]
