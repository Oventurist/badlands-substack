# Vyacheslav Volodin

Vyacheslav Volodin is the speaker of the [[state-duma|State Duma]], the lower house of the Russian parliament.[1]

## New START suspension

In February 2023, after President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] announced a freeze on Russian participation in the [[new-start-treaty|New START]] nuclear arms control treaty, Putin submitted a draft law on the suspension to the Duma.[1] Volodin said in a statement that the chamber would consider the bill on Wednesday, February 22, 2023, and take an immediate decision — a timetable that underscored the Duma's role in swiftly formalizing Kremlin foreign-policy decisions.[1] Moscow said at the same time that it would continue to observe the treaty's caps on deployed nuclear warheads notwithstanding the suspension.[1]
