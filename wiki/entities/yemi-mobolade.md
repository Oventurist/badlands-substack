# Yemi Mobolade

Yemi Mobolade is the mayor of Colorado Springs, elected in 2023 in what Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described as one of the reddest cities in [[colorado|Colorado]], following a purported cross burning staged in front of his campaign sign.[1]

## Colorado Springs cross-burning case

Jurors found Derrick Bernard, who is also Black, and his wife Ashley Blackcloud guilty of making a threat or conveying false information about a threat, and of conspiring to do so together. Prosecutors said that setting the cross on fire and writing a racial slur on one of Mobolade's campaign signs — then spreading word of it — harmed Mobolade and his family and affected the city's election.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Ashe in America wrote that when Mobolade was "let off the hook," many observers, herself included, believed the mayor was complicit and skating free.[1] She argued the stunt did not in fact harm Mobolade but instead ushered him into office, noting that he knows the man who carried it out while his own possible complicity was treated as off limits.[1] Writing after the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s superseding indictment of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] over payments tied to cross burnings and Klan regalia, she speculated: "Now I wonder if the SPLC was involved," adding that when "supply for racism outweighed demand, racism had to be manufactured."[1]
