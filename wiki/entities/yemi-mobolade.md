# Yemi Mobolade

Yemi Mobolade is the mayor of [[colorado-springs|Colorado Springs]], Colorado, elected in 2023. He is discussed in the June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who describes him as "the commie mayor of Colorado Springs" and notes that he was elected in "one of the reddest cities in Colorado after a supposed cross burning in front of his campaign sign."

## The cross-burning case

During Mobolade's campaign, a cross was set alight in front of one of his campaign signs and a racial slur was written on the sign. The incident was subsequently prosecuted as a staged hoax. As quoted in the Brief, jurors found Derrick Bernard, who is also Black, and his wife Ashley Blackcloud guilty of making a threat or conveying false information about a threat, and of conspiring to do so together; the prosecutor Rodriguez argued that setting the cross on fire, writing the slur, and then spreading word of it harmed Mobolade and his family and affected the city's election.

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

Ashe in America writes that the story "was always crazy," and that when Mobolade was "let off the hook" many observers, herself included, believed the mayor "was in on it and skating free." She argues that the stunt did not harm him but instead "ushered him into office," that Mobolade knows the man responsible, and that his potential complicity "was off limits" to investigators and press. She raises the possibility that the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] was involved, framing the episode as an instance in which, "when supply for racism outweighed demand, racism had to be manufactured."
