# Jennifer Palmieri

Jennifer Palmieri is a Democratic communications operative who worked in the Obama and Clinton administrations and served on Hillary Clinton's 2016 presidential campaign.[1]

## Commentary on Harris and the 2024 campaign

In February 2024, following the release of special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s report on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of classified documents, Palmieri publicly assessed the intensified scrutiny facing Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] "There was always going to be a lot of scrutiny and pressure on her in the 2024 campaign, and that moment's here now," she said. "I think that the special counsel's report has sort of accelerated that moment."[1] Her remarks were reported by [[wall-street-journal|The Wall Street Journal]] as part of coverage of what had previously been quiet talk of whether Harris could step into the presidency now spilling into the open.[1]

## 2016 Clinton campaign post-mortem

Palmieri, communications director for the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] campaign, appears in public records among the campaign figures who coordinated with Clinton on the plan to publicly link [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] during the 2016 election.[2] That group also included general counsel [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], campaign manager [[robby-mook|Robby Mook]], chairman [[john-podesta|John Podesta]], and policy adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]].[2]
