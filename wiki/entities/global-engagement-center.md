# Global Engagement Center

The Global Engagement Center (GEC) was an office of the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]], established under the Under Secretary for Public Diplomacy and Public Affairs, created pursuant to the [[countering-foreign-propaganda-and-disinformation-act-of-2016|Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016]] signed by President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].

## Mandate

A Swan Song for the MSM quotes the center's official mission: "To direct, lead, synchronize, integrate, and coordinate U.S. Federal Government efforts to recognize, understand, expose, and counter foreign state and non-state propaganda and disinformation efforts aimed at undermining or influencing the policies, security, or stability of the United States, its allies, and partner nations."

## Badlands interpretation

The article treats the GEC's creation as an admission that the establishment's traditional instrument of narrative control, the [[mainstream-media|centralized media]], was already failing. It argues that the apparatus made "Russian disinformation" a durable label for inconvenient evidence of establishment corruption, and situates the center as an institutional forerunner of what the corpus calls the [[censorship-industrial-complex|Censorship Industrial Complex]].
