# Alex Kozinski

Alex Kozinski is a former federal appellate judge appointed by Ronald Reagan to the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals in 1985.[1] [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] clerked for him after graduating from [[yale-law-school|Yale Law School]].[1]

According to Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], Kozinski was a likely Supreme Court pick for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] until multiple women accused him of sexual harassment; Kozinski maintained he had only made off-colour jokes.[1] He retired in 2017 in the wake of the allegations, which Carlson frames as a character assassination timed to Trump's interest in elevating him.[1] One of his accusers was [[katherine-ku|Katherine Ku]], long-time executive director of Stanford University's Office of Technology Licensing.[1]

In July 2022 Kozinski joined Trump's legal team in a suit against Twitter over Trump's removal from the platform two years earlier — four months before [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] completed his purchase of Twitter in late October 2022.[1]
