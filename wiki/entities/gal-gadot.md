# Gal Gadot

Gal Gadot is an Israeli actress, known for the title role in *Wonder Woman*, who in November 2023 hosted a screening in Los Angeles of footage from the October 7 attack by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## The Los Angeles screening

According to a November 9, 2023 *[[new-york-post|New York Post]]* article quoted at length in Badlands commentary, Gadot's screening of "Bearing Witness to the October 7th Massacre" — a compilation drawing on [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] material and bodycam footage taken by Hamas attackers — sparked multiple fights in Los Angeles late on a Wednesday.[1] The event was held at the [[museum-of-tolerance|Museum of Tolerance]], described in the reporting as ironically named given the violence outside.[1]

Police were already deployed in force around the venue.[1] Videos posted online showed people waving Israeli flags brawling in the street with pro-Palestinian protesters, kicking and punching one another.[1] Officers formed a skirmish line and ultimately used pepper spray, according to ABC 7.[1] Several people were detained, though it was unclear whether anyone was arrested or charged.[1] The *Los Angeles Times* reported that about 200 people, including Israeli officials and Hollywood executives, attended the screening while roughly 50 protesters gathered outside.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that the brawl looked staged rather than organic, noting that the event had been announced a week in advance and that Los Angeles police and the [[fbi|FBI]] were present in anticipation of unrest — raising the question of how a fight erupted despite that presence.[1] He compared the spectacle to a staged confrontation on the *Jerry Springer Show*, and offered the opinion that the screening was designed to produce a brawl that would help justify continued Israeli bombardment of Gaza, while acknowledging he did not know whether participants were manipulated or paid.[1] This is the author's speculation.[1]
