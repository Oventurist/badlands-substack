# Ron Perlman

Ron Perlman is an American actor, described by the [[associated-press|Associated Press]] as the "hulking, gravel-voiced" star of "Hellboy."[1]

## Role in the 2023 strikes

Perlman became an emblem of the animosity surrounding the [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]] after responding to a report that studios aimed to prolong the strike long enough for writers to lose their homes.[1] Leaning into the camera in a since-deleted Instagram live video, he said, "Listen to me, mother-(expletive). There's a lot of ways to lose your house."[1] He later clarified the remarks.[1]
