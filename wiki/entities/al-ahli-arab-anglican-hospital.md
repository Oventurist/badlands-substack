# Al-Ahli Arab Anglican Hospital

Al-Ahli Arab Anglican Hospital is a Christian-run medical facility in Gaza City operated under the auspices of the Episcopal Church of Jerusalem and the Middle East.[1] In July 2024 the hospital became the focus of a public dispute between Anglican Church authorities and the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] over whether the Israeli military had ordered it closed.[1]

## July 2024 closure dispute

The Episcopal Church of Jerusalem and the Middle East protested the closure of the hospital, which it attributed to the evacuation of several residential districts of Gaza City ordered by the Israeli military.[1] A statement issued in the name of the Diocese of Jerusalem said the hospital had been "compelled to close by the Israeli army," and reported that a large volume of drone fire had taken place in the immediate vicinity of the hospital on the preceding Sunday evening.[1]

The IDF rejected the accusation, insisting it had made clear there was no need to close hospitals or medical centres in areas where evacuations had been ordered for military operations against terror groups.[1] The medical centre's own director said that patients had fled of their own accord while others were transferred to alternative hospitals.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary observed that the Diocese of Jerusalem dates to 1841, making it substantially older than the modern State of Israel, and argued that this seniority afforded it no protection.[1] The writer noted that this was not the first time Israeli forces had struck Christian targets in Gaza, pointing to the shelling of a Greek Orthodox church in October 2023, and linked the pattern to the influence of the Kahanist current associated with [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]] within [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s security cabinet.[1]
