# Dmitry Rogozin

Dmitry Rogozin is a Russian official and the former head of Roscosmos, Russia's space agency.[1]

## Strategic Defense of Earth

In 2011 Rogozin made headlines by reviving the concept of a joint U.S.-Russia controlled defense system, first announced in President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]]'s 1983 [[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense Initiative]].[1] Rogozin's version, titled the Strategic Defense of Earth, called for turning humanity's arsenal of atomic weapons away from each other and toward the danger of asteroid collisions, for which the world is described as woefully unprepared.[1] The proposal was framed as a response to the two-fold threat of [[nato|NATO]] military encirclement on Earth and asteroid collisions from abroad.[1]

## Reception

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that introducing asteroid defense into emerging joint U.S.-Russia arms control working groups would contribute in powerful non-linear ways that no linear standard of measurement could calculate, and notes that the vision has been echoed by China as well as the European and Japanese space agencies.[1] The proposal is presented as a model case of [[open-systems-vs-closed-systems|open-system]] thinking and [[win-win-cooperation|win-win cooperation]].[1]
