# Turki Alalshikh

Turki Alalshikh is a Saudi official who serves as chairman of the General Entertainment Authority and president of the Saudi Arabian Boxing Federation, making him the central figure in the Kingdom's drive to acquire and host major global entertainment and combat-sports properties.[1]

## WrestleMania 43

In September 2025, Alalshikh announced jointly with [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] Chief Content Officer [[paul-levesque|Paul Levesque]] that [[wrestlemania-43|WrestleMania 43]] would be staged in Riyadh in 2027, the first time the event would be held outside North America.[1] The announcement was made at the Fontainebleau Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas alongside several of professional wrestling's biggest stars.[1] Alalshikh framed the deal as the fulfillment of a promise he had made in 2018 to "change the future by doing big projects together" in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], and described the event as "the biggest date on the wrestling calendar," marking "a significant moment in our partnership with WWE."[1]

## Context and commentary

Badlands commentary treated the Riyadh WrestleMania as one node in a broader pattern of Gulf sovereign capital flowing into American sports and entertainment — alongside LIV Golf's disruption of the PGA, stakes in the [[ufc|UFC]], and emerging media ventures — a trend Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized as a cultural Trojan Horse enabling reconciliation between the West and states long depicted as adversaries.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joked that Saudi Arabia would be hosting "Trump-a-Mania 43."[1]
