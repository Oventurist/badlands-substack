# Charles Falconer

Lord Charles Falconer is a British politician and peer of the governing [[labour-party|Labour Party]], and a former Secretary of State for Justice who served under his close personal friend [[tony-blair|Tony Blair]].[1] He has been one of the leading parliamentary advocates for legalising assisted suicide in the United Kingdom.[1]

## Position on euthanasia and pregnancy

During committee stage debates in the House of Lords on the [[terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill|Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill]], Falconer argued in December 2025 that pregnancy should not automatically prevent a woman from accessing so-called "assisted dying" under Britain's proposed euthanasia legislation, despite the fact that the unborn child would also be killed by the process.[1] Responding to concerns raised by another peer about how assisted suicide laws in other jurisdictions treat pregnancy — Oregon was cited as requiring efforts to keep the mother alive if the unborn child is "viable," while the Netherlands allows for aborting babies before euthanising their mothers — Falconer made clear that the approach he supports would not treat pregnancy as an exclusion.[1] "It is clear from the choice that I am supporting that we take the view that pregnancy should not be a bar to it," he said.[1]
