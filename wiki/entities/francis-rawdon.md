# Francis Rawdon

Lord Francis Rawdon was the British officer left in charge of the British field army in the South when [[charles-cornwallis|Lord Cornwallis]] departed for Yorktown during the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]].[1]

## Pursuit of Marion

Rawdon is described in the Patriot Chronicles as another confident British commander certain he could catch [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]], declaring "I must drive Marion out of [South Carolina]!" — an ambition at which, the article notes, he failed, as had so many of his predecessors that they were "too many of these guys to count."[1] His portrait by Martin Archer Shee illustrates the article.[1]
