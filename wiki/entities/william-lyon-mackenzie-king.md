# William Lyon Mackenzie King

William Lyon Mackenzie King was the Prime Minister of Canada during the Second World War, and the head of government under whom [[technocracy-inc|Technocracy Inc.]] was outlawed in Canada in 1941.[1]

## Ban on Technocracy Inc.

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] records that Technocracy Inc. was illegalized in 1941 as a pro-fascist threat to the nation and that [[joshua-haldeman|Joshua Haldeman]], its first research director in Saskatchewan, was arrested.[1] Although Haldeman denied the charges, the organization had advocated world government by an elite class and the overthrow of nation states, and had been fully opposed to Canada's entry into the war.[1] Mackenzie King stated his belief that the goal of Technocracy Inc. was "to overthrow the government and the constitution of this country by force."[1]

Haldeman's association with the movement ended in 1943, when he resigned in protest after the American headquarters of Technocracy Inc. began supporting the Soviet Union's war against the Nazis, and moved on to the [[social-credit-party-of-canada|Social Credit Party]].[1]
