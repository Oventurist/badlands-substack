# Rachel Maddow

Rachel Maddow is an MSNBC host who, in Badlands Media's coverage, functions as a central conduit for legacy-media narratives about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian collusion.[1] In August 2023 she interviewed [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] about the Trump prosecutions, a segment that Badlands writers used to illustrate what they see as the asymmetry and hypocrisy of establishment media.[1]

## The August 2023 Clinton interview

Clinton sat down with Maddow to discuss the indictments of Trump, characterizing them as a "terrible moment" for America while adding that "The only satisfaction may be that the system is working."[1] The exchange became awkward, according to the ZeroHedge account aggregated by Badlands, when Maddow launched into an extended criticism of "election deniers" and Clinton — herself a longtime critic of the legitimacy of the 2016 result — sat in silence.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for Badlands, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the segment was self-indicting: Maddow "also denied the election results and was one of the main peddlers of the RussiaGate Hoax," making her an implausible interlocutor for questions about election denial.[1] The commentary framed both women as participants in an ongoing cover-up connected to the [[russiagate-hoax|RussiaGate hoax]] and the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]], and treated the interview as evidence that the principals "still think they're going to get away with it."[1]

## Reaction to the 2024 Iowa Caucus

After [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] won the [[2024-iowa-caucus|2024 Iowa Caucus]] in a landslide over his Republican rivals, Maddow complained on air about a "rise of fascism," saying she was "worried about potential rise of fascism in this country."[2] She added that "if we're worried about our democracy falling to an authoritarian and potentially fascist form of government," the leader attempting it "is a much bigger part of that equation."[2] Coverage aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] from Steve Watson at [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] and [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] presented Maddow as leading a broader on-air meltdown at [[msnbc|MSNBC]] and [[cnn|CNN]], alongside colleague [[joy-reid|Joy Reid]].[2] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the reaction as evidence that the establishment media apparatus was "rather panicky" about the early 2024 returns.[2]

## Platform for "the Opposition"

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] named Maddow and Chris Hayes as the primary pundits championing the NGO institutions of [[the-opposition|the Opposition]] and sustaining its morale after the 2024 election.[3] He said he watched their programs to learn what the Opposition was plotting, since they dropped hints and hosted guests deeply involved in that world.[3]

He cited in particular a Maddow interview with [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] executive director [[anthony-romero|Anthony Romero]], in which Romero described the ACLU's study of [[project-2025|Project 2025]], declared that "litigation is going to be key," and outlined the [[firewall-for-freedom|firewall for freedom]] plan to enlist state and local officials against federal immigration enforcement.[3] Maddow raised the absence of large-scale protests comparable to 2016, and both she and Romero indicated that resources were being marshaled for the most opportune moment, the onset of mass deportations.[3]
