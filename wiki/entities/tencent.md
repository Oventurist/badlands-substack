# Tencent

Tencent is a Chinese technology and gaming conglomerate. In March 2026 it drew attention in the United States as a $1 billion equity participant in the financing of the proposed $111 billion merger between Paramount Skydance and [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]].[1]

## Role in the Paramount–Warner merger

Tencent's stake formed part of a foreign-capital package that also included roughly $24 billion from Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds.[1] The investments were structured as non-voting equity, often routed through firms such as Apollo Global Management, in a manner that critics said appeared designed to avoid mandatory review by the Committee on Foreign Investments in the United States.[1]

A group of Democratic senators led by [[cory-booker|Cory Booker]] wrote to the [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] on March 23, 2026, warning that Chinese national-security laws compel technology companies such as Tencent to cooperate with Beijing's intelligence services, and that the stake could translate into leverage over editorial decisions and content licensing at CBS News and CNN.[1] Paramount Skydance downplayed the concerns, asserting that the financing structure poses no governance or control issues.[1]
