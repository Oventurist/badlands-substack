# Sean McCluskie

Sean McCluskie is a former deputy and chief of staff to [[xavier-becerra|Xavier Becerra]] who was charged in the federal campaign-theft and tax-fraud case unsealed in Sacramento in November 2025.[1]

## The indictment

According to a 23-count indictment, McCluskie, [[dana-williamson|Dana Williamson]] — former chief of staff to Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] — and lobbyist Greg Campbell used shell companies to funnel campaign funds through bogus consulting invoices beginning in 2022, while Becerra was serving as Health and Human Services secretary.[1] Federal prosecutors said much of the stolen money went to McCluskie's wife for fabricated work.[1] McCluskie agreed to plead guilty and is cooperating with investigators.[1]

The broader case alleged that Williamson stole $225,000 from Becerra's dormant gubernatorial campaign account and falsely claimed over $1 million in luxury expenses as business deductions; the [[fbi|FBI]] said the matter stemmed from a three-year probe launched during the Biden administration.[1]
