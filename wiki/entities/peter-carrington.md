# Peter Carrington

Lord Peter Carrington (Peter Carington, 6th Baron Carrington) was a British statesman who served as a cabinet minister, secretary-general of NATO, and president of the Royal Institute of International Affairs.[1]

In [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s survey of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]], Carrington is named among the influential individuals who have held the group's chairmanship after [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]] resigned in 1976.[1] DeLarme highlights that the Royal Institute of International Affairs — [[chatham-house|Chatham House]] — is a sister organization to the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], and that Carrington has been linked to the Rothschild banking empire by both business connections and marriage.[1] The chairmanship subsequently passed to French businessman [[henri-de-castries|Henri de Castries]].[1]
