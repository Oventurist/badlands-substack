# Sandra Thompson

Sandra Thompson is identified in Badlands reporting as a Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] (FHFA) who authored the agency's August 2025 criminal referral of Federal Reserve Governor [[lisa-cook|Lisa D. Cook]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1]

## The Cook referral

In a letter dated August 15, 2025, Thompson detailed allegations that Cook committed mortgage fraud by misrepresenting her primary residence status on loan applications for properties in Ann Arbor, Michigan, and Atlanta, Georgia.[1] The referral cited potential violations including mortgage fraud, bank fraud, wire fraud, and falsifying documents.[1]

Mortgage records described in the referral show that Cook obtained a $203,000 loan in June 2021 for the Ann Arbor property, affirming it would serve as her principal residence, and two weeks later purchased a condominium in Atlanta for $540,000 while again claiming it would be her primary residence.[1] Federal housing investigators alleged that these representations were made to secure lower interest rates and more favorable terms.[1]
