# Janet Murguía

Janet Murguía is the chief executive officer of [[unidosus|UnidosUS]], the Latino civil rights and advocacy organization.[1]

## Announcement of Biden's cancelled address

On Wednesday, July 17, 2024, Murguía announced from the stage of the UnidosUS Annual Conference in Las Vegas, Nevada, that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would not appear as scheduled because he had tested positive for COVID-19.[1] "Thank you all so much for your patience and understanding. Regrettably, I was just on the phone with President Biden and he shared his deep disappointment at not being able to join us this afternoon. The president has been at many events as well all know and he just tested positive for COVID," she said.[1]

The announcement drew boos from an audience of more than 1,500 attendees.[1] Murguía added that Biden did not want to put "anybody at risk" and intended to address the conference at a later date, saying, "He's just really sorry he couldn't be with us and obviously we share that disappointment, but we've seen how COVID has still impacted many in our community still."[1]
