# Intesa Sanpaolo

**Intesa Sanpaolo**, referred to informally as Banca Intesa, is the largest bank in Italy, having emerged in that position after a series of mergers over the past several decades.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that the institution ultimately traces its roots back centuries to the days of the Austrian Empire, which in his reading means it probably has "some Old World banking bloodlines somewhere in its history."[1]

## Sovereign Alliance framing

The bank appears in Badlands commentary as one of the entities the author had recorded as belonging to the Sovereign Alliance, based on on-stream conversations with Alex Krainer of Zero Hedge.[1] Krainer appeared on *Breaking History* in 2025 and asserted, with what the commentator described as supporting evidence, that he believed Banca Intesa was part of a white hat operation to save Western Civilization.[1]

## Alleged financing of Lega

Banca Intesa surfaced alongside [[rosneft|Rosneft]] in the Wikipedia account of the development of the political party [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]] founded for his 2018 campaign, a passage GhostofBasedPatrickHenry said nearly made him fall out of his chair.[1] He suggested that this may be what Krainer meant, as it appeared that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] was working with Banca Intesa during President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term to covertly fund Salvini's populist party in Italy.[1]

## Russian holdings after 2022

In the wake of the 2022 invasion of Ukraine by Russia, Banca Intesa made a public pledge to divest all of its Russian holdings, for which it gained the approval of all parties involved, including Putin.[1] As of January 2025 the bank had yet to finalize those sales, meaning it still maintained its Russian holdings in defiance of European norms — a fact the commentator offered as further evidence that Italy is a much bigger player than previously deduced.[1]
