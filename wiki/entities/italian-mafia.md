# Italian Mafia

The Italian Mafia is the organized-crime network popularly identified with American organized crime, a perception [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues was deliberately manufactured.[1]

## Public perception via Hollywood

Carlson recalls watching all the Italian Mafia movies as a child — The Godfather, The Godfather Part II, and later Goodfellas and Casino — and believing the Italian Mafia was in charge of organized crime in America because Hollywood led him to believe it.[1] After [[the-godfather|The Godfather]] was released, he writes, the Italian Mafia became synonymous with organized crime and Italian godfathers were viewed as the top of the pyramid.[1]

## Position in the alleged hierarchy

Carlson contends that the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] was in fact above the Italian Mafia, and the [[cia|CIA]] above even the Jewish Mafia in the pecking order.[1] He is explicit that this is not to say the Italian Mafia is unreal or that its members are not genuine criminals, only that their power and standing in organized crime are greatly exaggerated.[1] The relationship serves in his essay as the model for how a visible group can be elevated as a scapegoat while its superiors operate in the shadows, a pattern he then applies to Israel and the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1]
