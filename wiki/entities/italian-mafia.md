# Italian Mafia

The Italian Mafia is presented in Erik Carlson's essay as a real criminal organization whose standing at the summit of American organized crime was, he argues, a Hollywood-manufactured illusion.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Belief and revision

Carlson describes growing up watching Italian Mafia films — *The Godfather*, *The Godfather Part II*, and later *Goodfellas* and *Casino* — and believing as a result that the Italian Mafia was in charge of organized crime in America. He attributes that belief directly to Hollywood.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

He later concluded that the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] sat above the Italian Mafia, and that the [[cia|CIA]] sat above the Jewish Mafia in the pecking order.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) After *[[the-godfather|The Godfather]]* was released, the Italian Mafia became synonymous with organized crime and the Italian godfathers were viewed as its apex.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

Carlson is explicit that this is not a claim that the Italian Mafia is fictional or non-criminal — only that its power and standing in organized crime are greatly exaggerated.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The pattern serves as his template for the later argument that Jews and Israel function as scapegoats for the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
