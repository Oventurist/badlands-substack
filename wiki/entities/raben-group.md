# Raben Group

The Raben Group is a Washington, D.C. lobbying and public affairs firm. Its client roster became a point of contention at the first hearing of the [[house-weaponization-committee|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] in February 2023.[1]

Under questioning from Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]], the Democrat witness [[elliot-williams|Elliot Williams]] was pressed to acknowledge that he is a partner at the firm and that the firm represents [[google|Google]], [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s [[open-society-foundations|Open Society]], and [[pfizer|Pfizer]].[1] Badlands Media commentary treated the disclosure as illustrating the "unholy union between the DC Swamp and the private institutions targeting sacred rights of Americans."[1]
