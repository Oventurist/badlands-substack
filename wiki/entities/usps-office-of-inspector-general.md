# USPS Office of Inspector General

The Office of Inspector General (OIG) of the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]] is the independent oversight arm of the Postal Service. Badlands Media highlights it as the author of a white paper proposing that the Post Office expand into financial services, and notes that [[qanon|Q]] has mentioned several times the importance of OIG offices being used in the background as part of the plan.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

## The postal financial services white paper

The OIG did not make an official recommendation, but acknowledged that a first logical step for the Postal Service might be to improve and expand the products it can likely provide under current law.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) Those include payroll check cashing, electronic domestic money transfers between post offices, international money transfers, walk-up bill pay, money orders, ATMs and gift cards — though the Postal Regulatory Commission would ultimately decide whether to approve any product expansions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) Badlands Media observes that all of these would create competition for private banks.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

Other products, including small loans, reloadable prepaid cards, and checking or savings accounts, are likely outside the Postal Service's current legal authority; if that authority were expanded, the OIG concluded, the Postal Service could gain significantly more revenue and make a more meaningful impact on financial inclusion.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) The paper also examined the possibility of the Postal Service becoming a licensed bank, which it described as the most time consuming, challenging and risky approach on multiple levels, but also the one with the highest potential for benefiting the financially underserved.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

## Significance in the article

The OIG's work is presented as evidence that a revived [[postal-savings-system|Postal Savings System]] and a [[public-banking-option|public banking option]] have long been quietly prepared inside government, and that the Postal Service could be made a public competitor to the private banking industry in the manner of [[jose-figueres|José "Pepe" Figueres]] in Costa Rica.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)
