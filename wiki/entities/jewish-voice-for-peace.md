# Jewish Voice for Peace

Jewish Voice for Peace (JVP) is a U.S. advocacy organization identified in Badlands Media reporting as one of several non-governmental organizations involved in organizing pro-Palestine campus protests during 2024 and 2025.[1]

## Funding and alleged establishment ties

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that JVP is funded by [[george-soros|George Soros]]' [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the [[tides-foundation|Tides Foundation]] and the [[rockefeller-brothers-fund|Rockefeller Brothers Fund]], and that this funding trail means the protests carry what he called "CIA fingerprints."[1] The article contends that JVP is not an isolated case, and that many similar organizations receive money through tax-exempt "cut-outs" used by the same intelligence and civil society interests that built the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship-industrial complex]].[1]

## Alleged strategic purpose

The article asserts that the aim of this funding is not primarily to halt the conduct of the war in Gaza but is instead a domestic play — the construction of a mobilizable protest infrastructure, described as a "rent-a-riot mob," modelled on the [[gene-sharp|Gene Sharp]] [[color-revolution|color revolution]] playbook associated with events such as the Arab Spring.[1]

## Free speech caveat

Despite this criticism of the organizing layer, the article maintains that ordinary students who object to Israeli government conduct should be free to speak, stating that there is no "Israel exception" to the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and that Americans are permitted to criticize foreign governments.[1]
