# Jonathan Kay

Jonathan Kay is a Canadian journalist and commentator who, with [[barbara-kay|Barbara Kay]], became the target of a defamation suit brought by the [[canadian-anti-hate-network|Canadian Anti-Hate Network]] (CAHN).[1]

## CAHN defamation suit

According to Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], CAHN attempted to sue Jonathan and Barbara Kay for defamation after they exposed how the network had supported the far-left extremist organization [[antifa|ANTIFA]].[1] Deschamps wrote that the effort ended disastrously for the network when an Ontario judge upheld the Kays' characterization, which he said disproved CAHN's self-description as "an independent, nonprofit organization made up of Canada's leading experts and researchers on hate groups and hate crimes."[1]

Deschamps cautioned that the ruling may nonetheless carry little weight with audiences he described as ideologically subverted, because in his analysis [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric warfare]] operations conducted through Canada's electoral system continue to keep [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] in power irrespective of such reversals.[1]
