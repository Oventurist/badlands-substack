# Erika Kirk

Erika Kirk is the widow of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], the founder of [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] assassinated in 2026.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] implicated Erika Kirk in her claims of a conspiracy in the murder, alongside [[israel|Israel]], Egypt, the US military, and TPUSA staff. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that the refrain that doubting the official narrative "hurts Erika" is part of a propaganda campaign intended to influence the jury pool in the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] trial.
