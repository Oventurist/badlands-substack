# David Lloyd George

David Lloyd George served as British Prime Minister during [[world-war-i|World War I]]. In Badlands Media's account of the origins of the [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]], he is the man who struck the bargain.

## The wartime proposal

Writing for Badlands, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] states that while Britain was losing World War I badly to the Germans, "Lord David Lloyd George" made a proposal to Jewish leaders in England: if they could bring the United States into the war, the English elite would promise the Jews their own "homeland."[1]

Carlson describes what he says followed. The Americans were staying out of the war at the time — a sizable percentage of the American population was of German descent — but once the proposal was made, Jewish-American bankers began lending money toward the British war effort.[1] Anti-German propaganda was pushed in what he calls the Jewish-American-run media, including claims that the Germans were "baby killers"; German products were removed from Jewish-American-owned retail stores, damaging Germany's exports; and the [[sinking-of-the-lusitania|Lusitania was sunk]], which Carlson labels a false flag, after which the US entered the war.[1]

## Legacy in the Badlands narrative

The [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] of 1917 is presented as the promise kept.[1] Carlson argues the British elite honored the bargain while turning it to their own advantage, since planting the future [[israel|State of Israel]] amid [[iran|Iran]] and the Arab states created a permanent source of tension at a safe distance from Western Europe.[1] This makes Lloyd George a foundational figure in the wider [[british-cabal|British Cabal]] thesis, alongside [[arthur-balfour|Arthur Balfour]] and [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]].[1]
