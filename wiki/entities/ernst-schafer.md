# Ernst Schäfer

Ernst Schäfer was an SS officer who led [[ahnenerbe|Ahnenerbe]] expeditions dispatched by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] to find the origins of the Aryan root race in Tibet.[1]

Schäfer illegally entered Tibet, befriended and mentored the young Dalai Lama, and rigorously measured the skulls of Tibetans.[1] His team was additionally tasked with mapping out sexual practices, the occult rites of esoteric Tibetan Buddhism, and priestly rituals including the use of human skin for decorative furniture, and with tracking down the mythical Yeti.[1] A 1938 photograph records Schäfer with Dr. Bruno Beger being received by Tibetan dignitaries at Lhasa.[1]
