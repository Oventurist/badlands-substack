# Wuhan

Wuhan is the capital city of Hubei province in the People's Republic of China, and the city whose shutdown in late 2019 marked the earliest widely reported episode of what became the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]

## Role in the Badlands awakening narrative

In "New to the Great Awakening? Making Sense of It All" (November 18, 2022), Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] identified December 2019 coverage of Wuhan as the starting point of her own political awakening.[1] Listening to [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s WarRoom: Impeachment program that month, she heard hosts and guests discuss a virus that had shut down the city, and noted that she had not previously heard of either "Wuhan" or "Hubei."[1] She recalled that the discussion carried no panic but "definitely concern and caution," with participants speculating that the virus would probably spread.[1]

That broadcast prompted her to buy more than $600 of food and staples at Costco on January 24, 2020, well before mass preparedness buying began in the United States.[1] Gillespie's later research into the outbreak encompassed questions of whether the virus could have been man-made, what [[gain-of-function-research|gain of function]] research entailed, how transmission occurred, and whether masks were effective.[1]

The city also figures in the account by way of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 2020 Oval Office remarks, quoted in the article, in which the president described the pandemic as an attack worse than Pearl Harbor or the World Trade Center that "could've been stopped at the source" and "should've been stopped in China."[1] Gillespie treated that framing as the key that led her to conclude the world was in a third world war whose "invisible enemy" might be the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] rather than the virus itself.[1]
