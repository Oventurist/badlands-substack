# Kim Yong-hyun

**Kim Yong-hyun** is a former Defense Minister of [[south-korea|South Korea]] and a political appointee of President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]], who became the central figure in allegations that the South Korean government sought to provoke an armed conflict with [[north-korea|North Korea]] in order to justify the declaration of martial law.[1]

## Allegations of provoking conflict

Mounting suspicions reported in December 2024 held that Kim intended to justify the declaration of martial law — or of a "state of united defense" — by instigating military conflict with North Korea, specifically by ordering strikes on the sites from which the North was launching trash-filled balloons, and by flying unmanned drones over Pyongyang.[1]

Democratic Party lawmaker Park Beom-kye revealed that he had received a tip-off from a military whistleblower indicating that the unmanned drones North Korea claimed Seoul had sent to Pyongyang in October 2024 were in fact dispatched on the orders of the South Korean military — more specifically, on Kim's orders.[1]

## The Defense Counterintelligence Command

Park stated that the [[defense-counterintelligence-command|Defense Counterintelligence Command]], then led by Yeo In-hyeong — described as another high school classmate of Kim's — planned the specifics of the operation, arguing it was "clear that this plan was conceived to offer a pretext for the invocation of martial law."[1] A reference document on the operation of martial law troops and a joint investigation headquarters, issued in November under Yeo's orders and made public by the Democratic Party, showed the Command had reviewed the possibility of simultaneously declaring martial law and united defense to execute military responses and control public order in the event of a crisis such as armed conflict with the North.[1] The document stated that the two could be declared together in the case of "enemy infiltration, provocation and domestic circumstances."[1]

## Assessment

Commentary at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] emphasized that the allegations were purportedly grounded in testimony given by military leaders to the South Korean Parliament, and that this testimony directly contradicted the account given by the now-disgraced president who had attempted to consolidate power through martial law.[1] While more elaborate claims circulating online — including a plan involving the HID special forces unit — remained difficult to verify, the widely reported core was that Kim, as a political appointee, and Yoon were attempting to provoke a war with North Korea by inducing the North to strike first.[1]

## Arrest for alleged treason (December 2024)

Kim was arrested in early December 2024 over his alleged role in President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]]'s decision to declare martial law, according to the Yonhap news agency.[2] Some reports claimed that Kim, who resigned his post on the Wednesday following the declaration, was the official who advised the president to impose martial law.[2] The opposition filed legal complaints accusing Yoon, Kim, and martial law commander Park An-Su of treason.[2]

According to Yonhap, Kim was arrested after voluntarily submitting to questioning by prosecutors on Sunday, December 8, 2024, and was sent to a detention center in eastern Seoul, where his phone was seized.[2] Later the same day, police raided his former office and official residence.[2]
