# IMDEA Networks

IMDEA Networks is a networking research institute whose scientists, together with researchers from [[northeastern-university|Northeastern University]], produced a study documenting security and privacy threats in [[smart-homes|smart homes]].[1]

## Research findings

The joint team demonstrated a variety of threats stemming from local network interactions between Internet of Things devices and mobile applications, analyzing 93 different IoT devices and apps. The resulting paper, "In the Room Where It Happens: Characterizing Local Communication and Threats in Smart Homes," was presented at the ACM Internet Measurement Conference in Montreal.[1]

The study found that IoT devices unintentionally expose sensitive data on local networks through common protocols such as UPnP and mDNS, disclosing UUIDs, unique device names and household locations — data usable by companies engaged in surveillance capitalism — and that local network protocols can act as backdoors around mobile app permission systems.[1]
