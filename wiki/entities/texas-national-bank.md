# Texas National Bank

Texas National Bank (TNB) was a Houston bank created in 1953 through the merger of South Texas National and Union National Bank, and the major predecessor institution of [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]], into which it merged with the National Bank of Commerce in 1964.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The Badlands Media account emphasizes that TNB was created in the very same year that [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and J. Hugh Liedtke formed Zapata Petroleum Corporation in Houston, arguing this was no coincidence but the deliberate creation of a CIA bank to service a CIA front company.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) TNB "quickly established mutually profitable working ties" with [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Off-Shore Company]], an 80 percent-owned subsidiary of Zapata Petroleum, and its loans for drilling rigs and tender vessels made Zapata one of the bank's most significant oil-related accounts in the late 1950s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Three Zapata ships financed by TNB — the *Houston*, the *Barbara J* and the *Zapata* — were loaned to the [[cia|CIA]] for the 1961 [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion, giving the bank an indirect line into the operation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
