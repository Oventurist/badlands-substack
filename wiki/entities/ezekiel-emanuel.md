# Ezekiel Emanuel

Ezekiel Emanuel is a physician and health-policy figure, and a brother of [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] and [[rahm-emanuel|Rahm Emanuel]].[1]

Badlands contributor Chris Paul characterized him as "a doctor who believes people should die at 75 for the good of everyone," a reference to his published argument about the optimal length of a human life.[1] The same commentary noted that the Emanuel brothers' father belonged to the [[irgun|Irgun]], and situated the family within a discussion of [[endeavor|Endeavor]]'s influence over the [[2028-los-angeles-olympics|2028 Los Angeles Olympics]].[1]
