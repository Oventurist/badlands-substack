# James Yoo

**James Yoo**, 56, was the owner of an Arlington, Virginia home that exploded on the evening of Monday, December 4, 2023 as police attempted to execute a search warrant; he is believed to have died in the blast.[1]

## Events

Yoo was identified by Arlington County Police Chief Andy Penn at a news conference as the person whose actions brought police to the home, after he fired a "flare-type gun" from inside the house into the neighborhood more than 30 times.[1] Police responded about 4:45 p.m. Monday after receiving reports of shots fired, and when attempts to communicate with Yoo were largely unsuccessful they obtained a search warrant.[1] As officers breached the door, the suspect fired multiple gunshots from within the house; Penn said it was not clear where in the house the shots originated or what the suspect was firing at.[1] Just before 8:30 p.m. the house exploded, shooting flames and debris into the air in a blast felt for miles.[1]

Details emerged about numerous grievances Yoo had expressed against neighbors and others on social media and in lawsuits.[1] An investigation into the cause of the explosion remained ongoing, and police asked anyone with photos or video of the area to share them with investigators.[1] The incident was reported by [[abc-news|ABC News]] and carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1]
