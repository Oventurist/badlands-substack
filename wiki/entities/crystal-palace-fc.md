# Crystal Palace F.C.

Crystal Palace is an English Premier League football club whose captain, [[marc-guehi|Marc Guehi]], became the focus of a religious-messaging controversy in December 2024.[1]

Guehi wore a Premier League-issued LGBTQ+ campaign rainbow captain's armband on which the message "I love Jesus" had been added during a weekend match.[1] The [[football-association|Football Association]] was understood to be preparing to remind Guehi that religious messaging is banned from equipment, and it was understood that Palace would also be contacted to be reminded of the regulations.[1]
