# Quinnipiac University

Quinnipiac University is a private American university best known publicly for its national opinion polling operation, whose surveys of presidential matchups are widely cited in political coverage.[1]

## 2023 Biden–Trump polling

In June 2023, a Quinnipiac poll on a possible Biden–Trump matchup put [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] at 48 percent and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at 44 percent, a result within the margin of error and therefore a statistical tie.[1] Because the survey was taken largely after the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed its federal indictment against Trump on June 8, 2023, the finding was treated as evidence that the prosecution had not damaged Trump's standing; [[abc-news|ABC News]] anchor [[jonathan-karl|Jonathan Karl]] cited the numbers on air and questioned what they implied about Biden's own political strength.[1]
