# Gerard Encausse (Papus)

Gerard Encausse, better known by the pseudonym Papus, was a French occultist described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as an extremely influential figure in late 19th- and early 20th-century esoteric politics.[1] He was an early member of the Theosophists, a member of the Hermetic Order of Light, the Rite of Memphis-Misraim, and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, and the founder of two orders of his own: the Modern Martinists and the Kabbalistic Order of Rose Croix.[1]

Papus became a trusted consultant to Czar [[nicholas-ii|Nicholas II]] in 1901 and, together with his close colleague the Russian mystic [[yuliana-glinka|Yuliana Glinka]], introduced the superstitious Czar and his wife Alexandra — a granddaughter of Queen Victoria — to [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]], presented as proof of a Jewish conspiracy to take over the world.[1]

Ehret lists Papus alongside [[nesta-webster|Nesta Webster]], [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], [[alice-bailey|Alice Bailey]], and [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] as promoters of narratives about "Jewish bankers' conspiracies undermining the noble honest royal families of Europe."[1]
