# Margaret Brennan

Margaret Brennan is the host of CBS News' Sunday public-affairs program "Face the Nation," a role in which she conducts interviews with senior American political figures and presidential candidates.[1]

## Role in the Biden records coverage

Brennan featured in commentary published by [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] and recirculated in the Badlands News Brief of July 26, 2023, which used one of her interview questions as an illustration of what Turley characterized as a press effort to close down the story surrounding [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and his family's foreign business dealings.[1] Interviewing Republican presidential candidate [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]], Brennan asked, "I wonder after this plea happens if you would advise your party to move on?" — a question posed only days before [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]], a former Hunter Biden business associate, was expected to give testimony to a House committee.[1]

Turley presented the exchange as emblematic of a broader pattern in which mainstream outlets urged the public and Republican officeholders to set the matter aside even as reporting surfaced allegations of millions of dollars in foreign payments, dozens of corporate shell companies, and newly released emails.[1] The Badlands commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the same episode as evidence of a narrative under strain rather than as an isolated interview question.[1]
