# John Krasinski

John Krasinski is an American actor best known to mainstream audiences as Jim Halpert on the sitcom *The Office*, as the lead of the horror film *A Quiet Place*, and as the title character in the streaming series adaptation of Tom Clancy's *Jack Ryan*.[1]

## 2024 "Sexiest Man Alive" designation

On November 12, 2024, People magazine named Krasinski its 2024 Sexiest Man Alive, revealing the selection on an episode of *The Late Show with Stephen Colbert*.[1] The magazine also published its new cover on social media the same evening.[1] During the reveal segment, host [[stephen-colbert|Stephen Colbert]] feigned annoyance at not receiving the title himself, and Krasinski appeared on screen to console him, joking, "It's ok, you're not the Sexiest Man Alive but who cares? Who is? Except for me, apparently."[1] Krasinski subsequently appeared in a comedic video styled after a 1980s infomercial in which he purported to teach Colbert how to be sexy.[1]

## Reception in the Badlands Brief

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief of November 14, 2024 treated the announcement as a symbolically loaded piece of entertainment news rather than a trivial one.[1] Writing for the Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that People had chosen "Jim from The Office" at a political moment defined by a campaign against bureaucratic inefficiency, that Krasinski's *A Quiet Place* character had fought to protect his family from a sound-triggered alien invasion at a time of unidentified anomalous phenomena declassification hearings, and that his final arc as Jack Ryan concluded with a dramatic public act of whistleblowing on corruption and treason inside the Central Intelligence Agency.[1] She endorsed the pick, adding a jab at prior magazine cover choices.[1] The Brief's editor, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], appended an editor's note flagging the item as unusually strange for the newsletter while electing to publish it unchanged.[1]
