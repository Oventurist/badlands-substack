# America Mission

America Mission is a grassroots activist and organizing project run by the married couple [[dustin-stockton|Dustin Stockton]] and [[jennifer-lynn-lawrence|Jennifer Lynn Lawrence]], best known previously for the "We Build the Wall" border wall fundraising effort.[1] The couple describe their work as organizing resistance against the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]

## Use of Twitter Spaces

Following [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s acquisition of [[twitter|Twitter]] and the restoration of previously banned accounts, America Mission adopted [[twitter-spaces|Twitter Spaces]] as its principal organizing venue.[1] A February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] account by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] describes some America Mission Spaces as functioning like formal meetings, with agendas, directives, action items, and follow-ups, while others are conspiracy deep dives or informal late-night conversation.[1]

Participants — both speakers and listeners — are drawn from across the country and from varied backgrounds, and America Mission Spaces frequently spin off into more localized regional discussions.[1] The project also hosted educational sessions such as a "Crypto/NFT for Newbies" Space on decentralized finance, which the essay's author credited with persuading her, a self-described technological luddite in matters of currency, to acquire cryptocurrency and a non-fungible token.[1]

## Assessment

Ashe in America cited America Mission alongside the January 6 community as "proof positive that the American People are innovative, action-oriented, and capable of governing ourselves," and predicted that this capacity would provoke interference from platform insiders and what she termed regime handlers.[1]
