# Robert Redfield

Robert Redfield is an American virologist who served as Director of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] during the initial phase of the COVID-19 pandemic. He became a significant witness in later congressional inquiries into the origins of SARS-CoV-2 because, unlike most senior federal health officials of the period, he publicly favored the possibility that the virus emerged from a laboratory.[1]

## Testimony to the COVID select subcommittee

At the first hearing of the House select subcommittee investigating the origins of COVID-19, held in March 2023, Redfield testified that he had been "sidelined" from internal government debates over the origins of the virus.[1] He stated that [[anthony-fauci]], then the White House chief medical adviser, did not appreciate Redfield's support for the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]].[1]

Redfield's account framed the questioning pursued by [[jim-jordan]], who used the hearing to press on the abrupt reversal of two scientists — Kristian Andersen and Robert Garry — who had initially told Fauci that the virus appeared potentially engineered.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] treated Redfield's sidelining as evidence of a wider pattern in which dissenting researchers were excluded or discredited within federal public-health institutions.[1]
