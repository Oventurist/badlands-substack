# Congressional Cannabis Caucus

The Congressional Cannabis Caucus is a bipartisan group of members of the U.S. House of Representatives organized to advance cannabis policy reform at the federal level.[1]

## June 2025 VA marijuana amendment

Caucus co-chairs Rep. [[brian-mast|Brian Mast]] (R-FL) and Rep. [[dave-joyce|Dave Joyce]] (R-OH) authored an amendment, adopted by the full House in June 2025, that would increase veterans' access to state medical marijuana programs and eliminate the existing [[department-of-veterans-affairs|VA]] directive barring department doctors from issuing cannabis recommendations.[1] The amendment was attached to the Military Construction, Veterans Affairs and Related Agencies (MilConVA) appropriations legislation two days after the House Rules Committee made the cannabis and psychedelics amendments in order for floor consideration.[1] A companion measure supported psychedelics research and access for veterans.[1]
