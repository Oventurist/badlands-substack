# Jordan

Jordan is an Arab kingdom bordering [[israel|Israel]] and a party to longstanding regional diplomacy over Palestine. It appears in the February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief as one of the governments that condemned remarks by US Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]].

## Response to the Huckabee remarks

Huckabee's statement that Israel has a biblical right to land "from the Nile to the Euphrates," and that "it would be fine if they took it all," drew condemnation from Arab and Muslim governments over the weekend of February 21–22, 2026. Jordan is named alongside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[egypt|Egypt]], as well as the [[arab-league|League of Arab States]] and the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]]. The territorial formulation Huckabee invoked encompasses Jordanian territory, and the Brief treats the coordinated regional response — characterized in the Saudi statement as contrary to international law, the UN Charter, and diplomatic norms — as an unusually broad diplomatic rebuke of a sitting US ambassador.
