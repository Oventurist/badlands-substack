# Jordan

Jordan is an Arab monarchy in the Levant whose government took sweeping legal action against the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] in April 2025, formally declaring the organization dissolved and illegal across the Kingdom.[1]

## Ban on the Muslim Brotherhood (April 2025)

Jordan's Interior Ministry announced a set of measures against the Muslim Brotherhood at a press conference on Wednesday, April 23, 2025, with Interior Minister [[mazen-faraya|Mazen Faraya]] outlining steps the government described as aimed at safeguarding national security and public stability.[1] Membership in or affiliation with the group was declared prohibited by law, and all of the group's offices across the Kingdom were permanently closed.[1] A judicial order was issued to facilitate the closure of the organization's headquarters and branches, with security forces deployed to enforce the decision and to confiscate the group's assets.[1]

The Interior Minister characterized the Brotherhood's continued activities as a threat to citizens, a barrier to national development, and a destabilizing force, alleging that members of the dissolved group had planned to target sensitive sites, stored weapons and explosives in residential neighborhoods, and operated covertly to undermine public security.[1] "The presence of hidden agendas and divisive rhetoric from within the group is incompatible with Jordanian unity," Faraya said, adding that "we cannot allow division among members of a single society."[1]

## Relations with Saudi Arabia

On the same Wednesday, Jordan's [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah II]] met Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] during an official visit to [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], arriving in Jeddah accompanied by Crown Prince Hussein and received by the deputy governor of Makkah, Prince Saud bin Mishaal bin Abdulaziz.[1] King Abdullah expressed pride in the historical relations between Riyadh and Amman and acknowledged Saudi Arabia's role in supporting Arab and Islamic causes; the two leaders discussed regional developments, particularly the situation in the Gaza Strip and the occupied West Bank.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted the coincidence of the two events — the Jeddah meeting and the Brotherhood ban on the same day — framing the Brotherhood as a vehicle of Western intelligence and describing the move as evidence that "the scourge that is the [[cia|CIA]]/[[mi6|MI6]] is being kicked out of the Middle East and Africa," while noting that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was due to visit Riyadh in less than three weeks.[1]

## Canceled Biden visit (October 2023)

Jordan was to have hosted a summit between President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah II]], [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] and Egyptian President [[abdel-fattah-el-sisi|Abdel Fattah el-Sisi]] during Biden's October 2023 Middle East trip.[2] The [[white-house|White House]] canceled the entire Jordan leg on October 17, 2023 after the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza, with the announcement coming in the same hour that Arab leaders said they would not meet Biden given United States backing of Israel.[2]
