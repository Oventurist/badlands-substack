# Jodie Ginsberg

**Jodie Ginsberg** is a press-freedom advocate who serves as chief executive officer of the [[committee-to-protect-journalists|Committee to Protect Journalists]] (CPJ), the New York-based non-governmental organisation that documents attacks on the press worldwide.[1]

## Response to the Israeli boycott of Haaretz

After the Israeli Cabinet voted unanimously on 24 November 2024 to sanction [[haaretz|Haaretz]], the country's oldest newspaper, over its critical coverage of the war in [[gaza|Gaza]], Ginsberg issued a statement on behalf of CPJ condemning the measure.[1] "We deplore the Israeli government's attempt to silence a respected Israeli outlet like Haaretz by hurting their advertising and subscription revenue," she said.[1] She added that "Israel's increasing deployment of restrictions on critical media is further disturbing evidence of its efforts to prevent coverage of its actions in Gaza."[1]

The Cabinet measure, proposed by Communications Minister [[shlomo-karhi|Shlomo Karhi]], directed all government staff and staff of state-owned companies to cease communications with Haaretz and to stop advertising in and purchasing subscriptions to the paper.[1] Press-freedom advocates including Ginsberg framed the decision as part of a broader effort by the Israeli government to silence voices critical of its conduct of the war.[1]
