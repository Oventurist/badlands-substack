# Dick Cheney

Dick Cheney is a former U.S. Vice President named by Badlands Media among the figures who populated the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] after its 1997 founding by [[william-kristol|William Kristol]] and [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]].[1]

Cheney is cited as one of the "esteemed individuals" in a network tied to the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]], 14 of whose 25 original PNAC signatories received posts in the George W. Bush administration.[1] The article links that network's [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] doctrine — and its stated ambitions toward Libya, Iraq, Iran, and Syria — to the long arc of U.S. policy that culminated in the December 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Endorsement of Kamala Harris (September 2024)

On Friday, September 6, 2024, Cheney — described as a "lifelong Republican" and then 83 years old — publicly announced that he would vote for Democrat [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] for president rather than Republican candidate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[3] In his statement he said that "in our nation's 248-year history, there has never been an individual who is a greater threat to our republic than Donald Trump," accused Trump of having "tried to steal the last election using lies and violence to keep himself in power after the voters had rejected him," and concluded that Trump "can never be trusted with power again."[3] Framing the choice as a civic obligation, Cheney said that "as citizens, we each have a duty to put country above partisanship to defend our Constitution."[3] The Harris campaign responded that she was "proud" to receive his support and "deeply respects his courage to put country over party."[3]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry treated the endorsement as an unmasking of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]], noting the irony of the warning coming from the architect of "shock and awe" in a post-Global War on Terror environment where George W. Bush socializes with celebrities.[3] The commentary compared Cheney to Winston Churchill, casting both as profiteering warmongers who viewed war as a vehicle for greatness and as a sector of the industrialized economy, and predicted that prominent pro-Trump influencers would follow with similar reversals worth watching closely.[3]

## Death

Cheney died at the age of 84, his family announced on November 4, 2025, from complications of pneumonia and cardiac and vascular disease.[2] CNN described him as America's most powerful modern vice president and the chief architect of the "war on terror," who helped lead the country into the ill-fated Iraq war on faulty assumptions.[2] His family said his wife of 61 years, Lynne, his daughters Liz and Mary, and other family members were with him when he passed, and called him "a great and good man who taught his children and grandchildren to love our country, and to live lives of courage, honor, love, kindness, and fly fishing."[2]
