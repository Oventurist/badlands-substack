# Dick Cheney

Dick Cheney is a former U.S. Vice President named by Badlands Media among the figures who populated the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] after its 1997 founding by [[william-kristol|William Kristol]] and [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]].[1]

Cheney is cited as one of the "esteemed individuals" in a network tied to the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]], 14 of whose 25 original PNAC signatories received posts in the George W. Bush administration.[1] The article links that network's [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] doctrine — and its stated ambitions toward Libya, Iraq, Iran, and Syria — to the long arc of U.S. policy that culminated in the December 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Death

Cheney died at the age of 84, his family announced on November 4, 2025, from complications of pneumonia and cardiac and vascular disease.[2] CNN described him as America's most powerful modern vice president and the chief architect of the "war on terror," who helped lead the country into the ill-fated Iraq war on faulty assumptions.[2] His family said his wife of 61 years, Lynne, his daughters Liz and Mary, and other family members were with him when he passed, and called him "a great and good man who taught his children and grandchildren to love our country, and to live lives of courage, honor, love, kindness, and fly fishing."[2]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the Cheney legacy has been weaponized in subtle and overlooked ways, arguing that from 9/11 to the Forever War "the Machine" has worked overtime to push the idea that Cheney was the puppet master.[2] He characterized that framing as a lie that shields the [[cabal|Cabal]], concluding that Cheney was "just another patsy."[2]
