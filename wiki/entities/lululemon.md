# Lululemon

Lululemon is the athletic-apparel retailer cited in Badlands Media commentary for firing two store employees who called 911 during a theft.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reports reading that the company dismissed the two employees after a group of people entered the store and "started stealing everything in sight."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The store manager, or someone else in charge, fired them on the spot for failing to comply with company policy, which is not to stop people from stealing merchandise.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Carlson's response is the rhetorical question: "what bizzarro world do we now live in?"[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

He argues the incident's publicity compounds the harm — because the firing made the news, it will make thieves "even more comfortable stealing more merchandise from stores."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The episode is presented alongside [[ulta-beauty|Ulta Beauty]]'s profit warnings as part of a broader [[retail-theft-wave|retail theft wave]] that Carlson reads as evidence of a deliberate [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] rather than ordinary corporate mismanagement, since permitting theft plainly reduces profits.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
