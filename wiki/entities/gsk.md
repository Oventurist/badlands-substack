# GSK

GSK is the pharmaceutical manufacturer of Shingrix, a widely used shingles vaccine.[1]

## FDA publication dispute

Shingrix was among the products covered by safety studies whose publication the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] blocked in 2026, alongside COVID-19 vaccines made by [[pfizer|Pfizer]] and [[moderna|Moderna]].[1] The withdrawn research is described in [[fda-blocked-vaccine-safety-studies|FDA Blocked Vaccine Safety Studies]]; [[department-of-health-and-human-services|HHS]] said the authors had drawn conclusions unsupported by the data, while Badlands commentary read the suppression as evidence the underlying data undercut safety claims.[1]
