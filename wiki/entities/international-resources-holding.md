# International Resources Holding

International Resources Holding (IRH) is a United Arab Emirates company that in March 2024 lawfully acquired [[mopani-mining|Mopani Copper Mines Limited]] from [[glencore|Glencore]].[1]

The firm is owned by [[mohamed-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Mohammed bin Zayed]], described in Badlands commentary as a close ally of Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.[1] Its acquisition of Mopani — a Zambian copper operation whose board once included former Glencore executive [[telis-mistakidis|Telis Mistakidis]] — is presented in that coverage as part of a broader realignment of African mining assets away from figures such as Israeli billionaire [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] and the Glencore executives associated with the Congo corruption cases.[1]
