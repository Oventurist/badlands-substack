# National Defense Council (Iran)

The National Defense Council is an Iranian body established in 2025 within the framework of Article 176 of the Iranian Constitution, the same article that governs the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]].

According to the January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief, Iranian media reported in early August 2025 — quoting the Secretariat of the Supreme National Security Council — that the leadership had approved the Council's creation. Related reporting at the time indicated that structural changes to the Supreme National Security Council were under consideration, with a focus on "the separation of defense and security affairs from each other," alongside efforts to rebuild Iran's intelligence and security institutions.

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads these reforms as part of an Iranian [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] architecture closely resembling [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s [[devolution|Devolution]] theory, and speculates that the institutional rebuild followed Tehran's realization that its security services had been penetrated — pointing to the late General [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] and an alleged [[mossad|Mossad]] relationship with [[david-barnea|David Barnea]].

## Related measures

A September 2025 report quoted Iranian Deputy Interior Minister for Security and Law Enforcement and National Security Council Secretary Ali Akbar Pour Jamshidian, who stated on September 27 that the Supreme National Security Council had directed all Iranian military and civilian officials to designate several successors to ensure continuity of governance in the event of leadership disruption. By January 2026, President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] had also delegated expanded powers to provincial governors for rapid decision-making, accelerated imports, and barter trade conducted without foreign currency.
