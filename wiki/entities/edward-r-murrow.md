# Edward R. Murrow

Edward R. Murrow was an American broadcast journalist and long-time [[cbs-news|CBS]] figure whose name remains a byword for the network's mid-century reputation for independent reporting.[1]

## Invocation in the Herridge files controversy

In February 2024, law professor Jonathan Turley opened a widely circulated column on the [[cbs-news|CBS]] seizure of [[catherine-herridge|Catherine Herridge]]'s files by quoting Murrow: "Anyone who isn't confused really doesn't understand the situation."[1] Turley wrote that the line came to mind after he spoke with journalists at the network about the affair, using Murrow as a measure of how far the broadcaster's culture had drifted from the press-freedom standards associated with him.[1] The column was excerpted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024, where [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the seizure ranked among the worst violations of freedom of the press in modern history.[1]
