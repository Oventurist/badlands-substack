# Dominick Reyes

Dominick Reyes is an American mixed martial artist competing in the [[ufc|UFC]]'s light heavyweight division.[1]

## UFC 314 victory over Nikita Krylov

On Saturday 12 April 2025, at UFC 314 inside the UFC Apex in Las Vegas, Nevada, broadcast on ESPN+ pay-per-view, Reyes knocked out Russian contender Nikita Krylov with a counter left hand.[1] Reyes was sharp from the opening bell, landing a stiff jab that wobbled Krylov; Krylov recovered and pressed forward but swung wildly, landing at least one right hand that Reyes felt.[1] Reyes then skipped backwards and planted a left hand down the middle, letting Krylov run into the power shot — a connection MMAmania compared to Conor McGregor's knockout of Jose Aldo — dropping Krylov for the first true KO loss of his long career.[1] Coverage suggested the win restored Reyes's standing among the best light heavyweights in the world and raised the possibility of another run at a UFC title.[1] Reyes saluted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who was seated cageside.[1]

Badlands used the moment to describe Trump's reception at the arena, writing that where he had previously been received as "a gladiator, and the people's champion," on this night he was greeted as "a conquering hero ... the Julius Caesar the establishment, grown fat and weak off their indulgence always feared," concluding: "Rubicon.
