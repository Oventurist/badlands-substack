# The Forward

The Forward is a Jewish-American media outlet described in Badlands coverage as pro-Zionist and historically supportive of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and his political allies.[1]

## Criticism of Netanyahu's 2024 Washington visit

In July 2024, ahead of Netanyahu's address to a joint meeting of Congress, The Forward published an opinion piece arguing that Netanyahu should remain in Washington rather than return home.[1] The piece framed the trip as being primarily about preserving Netanyahu's own political power and evading the corruption charges he had faced since 2020, rather than about serving Israel's interests.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the outlet's turn against Netanyahu — alongside [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]'s decision not to attend the congressional address — as evidence that the prime minister was losing allies across the political spectrum.[1] The article quoted an Israeli college student recently returned from reserve duty in Gaza saying, "Honestly, no one here cares. We all have bigger problems."[1]
