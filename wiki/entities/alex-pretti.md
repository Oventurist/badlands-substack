# Alex Pretti

Alex Pretti was a United States citizen fatally shot on January 24, 2026 during protest clashes connected to [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]], the federal immigration enforcement surge in Minneapolis and St. Paul, Minnesota.

Pretti's death, together with that of [[renee-good|Renee Good]] on January 7, 2026, featured prominently in the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026 described in the [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] of March 4. Democratic senators accused [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] and [[customs-and-border-protection|CBP]] officers of excessive force and criticized Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] for characterizing the two victims as "domestic terrorists"; Noem attributed the violence to protesters.
