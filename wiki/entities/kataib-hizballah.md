# Kata'ib Hizballah

Kata'ib Hizballah is an Iraqi Shia militia and political movement closely aligned with [[iran|Iran]]'s [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]], designated by the United States as a foreign terrorist organisation.

## Role in the Al-Saadi case

In the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," Kata'ib Hizballah is identified as one of the two organisations to which the accused terror suspect [[mohammad-baqer-al-saadi|Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi]] was allegedly connected as a senior operative, the other being the IRGC. Sources cited in the reporting linked Al-Saadi through this network to [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] and subsequently to Quds Force commander [[esmail-qaani|Esmail Qaani]], and alleged his involvement in at least 18 attacks and attempted attacks on US and Jewish sites in Europe and North America.
