# Kata'ib Hizballah

Kata'ib Hizballah is an Iraqi Shia militia organization aligned with [[iran|Iran]]'s [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

## 2026 US prosecution linkage

Sources cited in coverage of the alleged plot against [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka Trump]] identified the accused, [[mohammad-baqer-al-saadi|Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi]], as a senior operative connected to both the IRGC and Kata'ib Hizballah.[1] Analysts and former Iraqi officials said he was closely tied to [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] and later to IRGC Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani, and investigators allege he trained with the IRGC in Tehran before using a travel agency business to coordinate with international terror cells.[1]

## Erbil drone attack and US retaliation (December 2023)

On 25 December 2023, Kataib Hezbollah and affiliated groups operating under an umbrella of Iranian-backed militants claimed credit for a one-way attack drone strike on an air base used by American troops in Erbil, northern Iraq, which injured three US servicemembers, one of them critically.[2] National Security Council spokeswoman Adrienne Watson confirmed the critical injury.[2] President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], spending Christmas at Camp David, was alerted by national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] and ordered the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and his top aides to prepare response options.[2] Sullivan consulted Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]], while deputy national security adviser Jon Finer convened aides at Camp David; within hours Austin and Joint Chiefs chairman Gen. CQ Brown briefed Biden, who opted to strike three locations used by Kataib Hezbollah and affiliated groups.[2]
