# African National Congress

The **African National Congress (ANC)** is South Africa's ruling political party, formerly led by Nelson Mandela.[1]

## Corruption inquiry

In 2025 the party's senior figures featured in a judicial inquiry probing alleged corruption and reported links between senior South African officials and organized crime.[1] Among those implicated was [[nkosinathi-emmanuel-mthethwa|Nkosinathi Emmanuel Mthethwa]], a long-serving ANC member and South Africa's ambassador to France, who was found dead in Paris in late September 2025 after reportedly falling from the 22nd floor of a hotel.[1] Mthethwa had served as Minister of Police from 2009 to 2014 under ANC governments, a period marked by criticism of the 2012 police killing of 34 striking miners at Marikana.[1]
