# Tan Kefei

Tan Kefei is a Chinese military officer holding the rank of colonel who serves as a spokesman for the [[chinese-ministry-of-national-defense|Chinese Defense Ministry]].[1] In that capacity he delivers Beijing's public statements on defense policy, foreign military cooperation and joint exercises.[1]

## Statements on military coordination with Russia

At the end of March 2023, Tan announced that [[china|China]] was prepared to expand military cooperation with [[russia|Russia]], speaking roughly two weeks after [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] met in Moscow.[1] He said that "the Chinese military is willing to work together with the Russian military to fully implement the important consensus reached by the two heads of state, further strengthening strategic communication and coordination."[1]

Tan indicated that the enhanced cooperation could take the form of additional joint exercises and patrols, citing five days of naval training conducted by China, Russia and [[iran|Iran]] in the Gulf of Oman during the week of 15 March 2023, drills that he said would help the participants "jointly carry out diversified maritime military missions."[1]

He also sought to distinguish the arrangement from Cold War–style blocs, insisting that Chinese coordination with Russia rests on "the principles of non-alliance, non-confrontation, and non-targeting of third parties" — a characterisation reporting noted might surprise Ukrainians.[1] Tan's remarks were subsequently folded into commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that such coordination had been developing for years and that only "surface-level conservatives" would read it as the beginning of the end.[1]
