# Patrick Byrne

Patrick Byrne is an American entrepreneur and financier of post-2020 election-integrity investigations. Badlands coverage cites him as a public booster of attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]]'s June 2026 appointment to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].[1]

## Reaction to Olsen's DOJ appointment

Byrne was described as "very excited" about Olsen joining the DOJ, and publicly shared that he and Olsen had agreed as early as December 2020 that Olsen was going to take election fraud "to the firing squad."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted the December 2020 date as significant, because it predates the January 15, 2021 [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] photo op that had generally been treated as the origin point of Olsen's involvement in the election fight.[1]

## Hunter Biden defamation judgment (2026)

In July 2026 a federal judge ruled that Byrne had defamed [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] by repeatedly promoting "false" claims that he participated in an $800 million Iranian bribery scheme, awarding Hunter Biden $1.7 million in punitive damages plus $1 in nominal damages.[2] US District Judge [[stephen-wilson|Stephen Wilson]] found that Byrne acted with "intentional misrepresentation" and "conscious disregard" for Hunter Biden's rights, continuing to amplify the allegations after the suit was filed and encouraging others to spread them on social media.[2] Byrne was also ordered to pay nearly $35,000 in previously imposed court sanctions within two weeks or face an additional $1,000 daily penalty.[2] The underlying claims held that Hunter Biden sought a bribe from [[iran|Iran]] in exchange for influencing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to release $8 billion in frozen Iranian assets and reduce pressure during nuclear negotiations.[2]

## Remaining Dominion litigation (2026)

After the [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] defamation suit against [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] was dismissed with prejudice in June 2026, Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the only apparently remaining Dominion — now [[liberty-vote|Liberty Vote]] — defamation case was the action against Byrne.[3] Byrne is said to hold extensive evidence relating to the 2020 election dispute and has described himself as having ties to the [[cia|CIA]].[3] He had appeared on LindellTV shortly before, lamenting some form of coming "justice," which the commentary read as a hint that the final case might yield a significant disclosure rather than another quiet settlement.[3]

## Dispute over Venezuela commentary (November 2025)

In early November 2025, Byrne published a post disavowing the Venezuela commentary of Badlands host GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, who had been criticizing Nobel laureate [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]]'s call for US military action against [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[4] The host responded publicly that he held no hard feelings and appreciated the mention, but said Byrne had never answered his central question — if [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] is a dictator, why was a prominent public figure like Machado free to film marketing campaigns in Venezuela over the preceding 25 years — and noted that Byrne's post had been heavily ratioed.[4]

## Election night 2024 commentary

During [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s 15-hour live special coverage of the 2024 election, Byrne offered a military metaphor for why the reported results differed from 2020: "There's a military term called 'Defenses in Depth,' and it's where you just create so many different layers of defense that your opponent just can't get through because it just costs them too much; that's really what we've done over the last four years."[5] He contrasted this with 2020, saying the other side "had a walk-on victory four years ago" and "could just cheat shamelessly because they had full information dominance."[5]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] highlighted that Byrne followed the remark not with a call to preserve existing election systems but with a demand that a returning President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] completely reform them, so that elaborate defensive structures would no longer be needed merely to protect the sovereign will of the people.[5]

## Venezuelan election fraud story

In December 2025 Badlands host Ashe in America announced that Byrne would join her and CannCon on Why We Vote to discuss the Venezuelan election fraud story then circulating widely online.[6] She traced the thread from filings in Tina Peters' First Amendment appeal through a Lara Logan interview to reporting by Emerald Robinson, which she said placed Byrne at the center of the matter.[6]

## Capitol Times Magazine disclosures (June 2023)

In late June 2023 Byrne told followers that a major disclosure was imminent, and on Monday, June 26 he briefly posted an article of more than eighty pages published as the inaugural feature of [[capitol-times-magazine|Capitol Times Magazine]], with a foreword by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and a cover presenting Byrne as a "DHS Domestic Extremist."[7]

Much of the piece recapped Byrne's prior disclosures — his affair with [[maria-butina|Maria Butina]], his role in what he describes as a blackmail scheme against [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and his self-description as an eager intelligence asset working in the cause of peace — while adding new color and detail.[7] He recounted, for example, that the United States government signed off on his fictional plan to rape and murder Butina, and asserted for the first time that when the FBI dropped the sting on Clinton, agents also threatened his life.[7]

Two disclosures were entirely new. First, Byrne wrote that "in 2006, 300 million Americans elected a Senate, which choose a Senate Judiciary Committee, which chose a chairman, who told me their power transcended everything else within government, systemic corruption was taking over the US government, and the country needed me to disrupt it," naming a bipartisan group of senators who engaged him that included [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]].[7] Second, he claimed that in the fall of 2021 [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] approached the Iranian government with an offer to unfreeze $8 billion held in a South Korean bank account in exchange for $800 million funneled into a numbered account, telling his interviewer that the characterization of the Bidens seeking an Iranian bribe — known to the U.S. government since December 2021 — was "100% correct."[7]

Byrne insists he is not a spy but a patriot honorably serving his country, and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the piece "reads like the confessions of a spy" and that "his confession should set the establishment on fire."[7] Byrne reportedly testified under oath to all of the information in the piece before the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]], which apparently omitted a substantial portion from its final report.[7] He removed the article shortly after posting it on Telegram, likely because of copy errors throughout that appeared to stem from an issue with tracked changes.[7]
