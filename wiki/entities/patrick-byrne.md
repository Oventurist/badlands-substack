# Patrick Byrne

Patrick Byrne is an American businessman, the former chief executive of Overstock.com, and a prominent private backer of investigations into the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

In the June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reports that Byrne responded enthusiastically to the news that attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] had joined the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]], sharing that he and Olsen had agreed in December 2020 that Olsen was going to take election fraud "to the firing squad." Ashe treats the December 2020 date as significant, since it predates [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]]'s January 15, 2021 White House photo op — in which Olsen's name appeared on documents under Lindell's arm — which she had previously regarded as Olsen's entry point into election litigation. Byrne's account thus reframes, in her telling, the sequence by which Olsen became a central figure in election-integrity work.
