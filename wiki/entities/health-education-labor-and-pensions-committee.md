# Senate Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee

The **Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee** (HELP) is a standing committee of the [[us-senate|United States Senate]] with jurisdiction over labor, health, education and pension policy.[1]

## November 2023 confrontation

During a November 14, 2023 meeting of the committee, Senator [[markwayne-mullin|Markwayne Mullin]] challenged [[teamsters|Teamsters]] president [[sean-obrien|Sean O'Brien]] to a physical fight, reading aloud a June 21 tweet in which O'Brien had called him "a clown & a fraud."[1] Mullin, a former mixed martial arts fighter, told O'Brien "we can be two consenting adults, we can finish it here," and the two men rose from their seats before committee chairman [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] intervened to stop the confrontation.[1]
