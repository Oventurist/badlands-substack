# National Republican Senatorial Committee

The National Republican Senatorial Committee (NRSC) is the campaign committee charged with electing Republicans to the U.S. Senate. It was the lead plaintiff in [[nrsc-v-fec|NRSC v. FEC]], the case argued before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] in December 2025 challenging caps on coordinated party spending imposed by the [[federal-election-campaign-act|Federal Election Campaign Act]] of 1971.[1]

The NRSC was joined in the suit by the National Republican Congressional Committee (NRCC), Vice President [[jd-vance|J.D. Vance]] and former Congressman Steve Chabot (R-OH), all arguing the limits violate the First Amendment and artificially weaken political parties relative to super PACs.[1]
