# JFK Jr.

John F. Kennedy Jr. is described in the article as one of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s closest friends and a genuine threat to the hidden enemy, killed before he could ever get close to the presidency.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The article states that JFK Jr. was loved by the American people, and that the enemy understood his popularity would be difficult to overcome — particularly because he was aware of who the real enemy were, having had his father murdered by them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) It asserts that he was eliminated because he was planning to run for the Senate in New York and harboured higher ambitions beyond that.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The author writes that he has always believed the plane was sabotaged, and that it was no coincidence Hillary Clinton was running for the same New York Senate seat at the time, a contest she had no chance of winning against him.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) With his unexpected death, the article argues, America lost the opportunity for a completely different history and direction.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Trump has not forgotten what was done to his friend and his family.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Crash attribution

A later Badlands feature notes, without endorsing it as the full story, that JFK Jr.'s fatal plane crash was officially attributed to [[spatial-disorientation|spatial disorientation]], as was the crash that killed Kobe Bryant.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) That article uses the phenomenon — in which a pilot's perception of direction, altitude or speed is misled by conflicting sensory information — as an analogy for deception in the [[information-war|info war]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)
