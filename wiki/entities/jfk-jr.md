# JFK Jr.

John F. Kennedy Jr. is described in the article as one of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s closest friends and a genuine threat to the hidden enemy, killed before he could ever get close to the presidency.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The article states that JFK Jr. was loved by the American people, and that the enemy understood his popularity would be difficult to overcome — particularly because he was aware of who the real enemy were, having had his father murdered by them.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) It asserts that he was eliminated because he was planning to run for the Senate in New York and harboured higher ambitions beyond that.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The author writes that he has always believed the plane was sabotaged, and that it was no coincidence Hillary Clinton was running for the same New York Senate seat at the time, a contest she had no chance of winning against him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) With his unexpected death, the article argues, America lost the opportunity for a completely different history and direction.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Trump has not forgotten what was done to his friend and his family.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Crash attribution

A later Badlands feature notes, without endorsing it as the full story, that JFK Jr.'s fatal plane crash was officially attributed to [[spatial-disorientation|spatial disorientation]], as was the crash that killed Kobe Bryant.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) That article uses the phenomenon — in which a pilot's perception of direction, altitude or speed is misled by conflicting sensory information — as an analogy for deception in the [[information-war|info war]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

## Heroes of the Great Awakening

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" names [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] as the figure "poised to carry on the legacy of transparency a generation ago," through his magazine [[george-magazine|*GEORGE*]] and "his projected and even probable win of a Senate seat in New York."[4] That seat, the author writes, was "coveted by the queen of the coven herself, Hilary Clinton," and had Kennedy Jr. won it he "might have risen to the top, eventually, thereby posing a major threat to the establishment."[4]

The article states that Kennedy Jr. "seemingly died under mysterious circumstances when his plane reportedly went down over Martha's Vineyard in 1999, when he was 38 years old," adding that his fate "in purely coincidental fashion—seemed to mirror that of so many political enemies of the Clinton clan."[4]

The essay further addresses the belief, common within the movement, that Kennedy Jr. is still alive. The author writes that "while JFK Jr. being among the living is a myth, that doesn't mean it's not true with the right perspective in mind," and quotes [[charles-eisenstein|Charles Eisenstein]]'s argument that myths are "vehicles of truth" whose validity "does not depend on whether it is objectively verifiable."[4] On this reading the spirit of Kennedy Jr. and his father is "alive and well in the current era," carried by ordinary participants in the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] rather than by any single surviving individual.[4]

## The 1999 crash and the New York Senate seat (Carlson analysis, 2024)

A June 15, 2024 feature by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] set the crash against the timeline of the New York Senate race: [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] formally entered the contest on July 7, 1999, and nine days later JFK Jr., his wife and his sister-in-law died in the small plane crash, which Carlson argued gave Clinton an unimpeded path through the Democratic Party to the election.[3] The Republican front-runner, [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]], later withdrew after a prostate cancer diagnosis, leaving Clinton to win the seat.[3] Carlson also noted that Giuliani had volunteered for RFK's 1968 campaign at a time when [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was a close friend of JFK Jr.[3]
