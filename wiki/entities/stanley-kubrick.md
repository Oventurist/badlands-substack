# Stanley Kubrick

Stanley Kubrick was an American film director cited in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s January 24, 2023 Badlands Media essay "The World is a Vampire" as an exception to the general pattern of [[hollywood|Hollywood]] messaging.[1]

Carlson argues that while entertainment media is most often used to alter how audiences think or to prepare them to accept elite agendas and narratives, in some cases messages have been embedded in entertainment content in order to warn the audience.[1] He places Kubrick in this second category, writing that the director put hidden messaging into his films to warn viewers of how the world works and how they are controlled and used.[1]

## Eyes Wide Shut

Carlson notes that some believe Kubrick was too obvious in his 1999 film *Eyes Wide Shut*, and that after refusing to remove twenty minutes of the film he suddenly died.[1] Carlson adds the parenthetical observation that this occurred well before the COVID-era vaccinations.[1]
