# Lipka Tatars

The Lipka Tartars were an all-Muslim military unit maintained by Poland whose members traced their lineage to the Mongolian Golden Horde.[1]

Exiled from Hungary in the 1500s, they formed a mercenary group in Poland and shared their mastery of horses with the Poles; that lineage eventually produced the [[winged-hussars|Winged Hussars]], the heavy shock cavalry that dominated the plains of Eastern Europe.[1] Part of the cavalry force that relieved Vienna in 1683 consisted of Lipka Tartars, a fact Badlands cites to complicate the popular framing of the [[battle-of-vienna|Battle of Vienna]] as a purely "Christianity vs Islam" conflict.[1]

Badlands notes that Europeans referred to the Mongols as "Tatars," sometimes spelled "Tartars," and to the Mongolian Empire as "Tartaria," and that Crimea — where many Mongols settled — is known throughout Russian history as "Little Tartaria."[1]
