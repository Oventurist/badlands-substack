# GhostofBasedPatrickHenry

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry is a pseudonymous contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and a recurring commentator in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]. He is the author of an ongoing series described in the corpus as "The Book of Trump," a study of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public career including a close reading of the reality program *The Apprentice*.

## Analytical approach

His commentary in the February 17, 2026 edition of the Brief, [[badlands-brief-0a2|"A Virtual Smash & Grab & Another Impeachment Hoax"]], illustrates his characteristic method: treating apparent conflicts within the Trump orbit as deliberate kayfabe, and reading media narratives as operations rather than reportage.

Addressing the reported 2018 text exchange between [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] about the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]], he argued that Bannon was alluding to a known Democratic removal strategy reported by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] in connection with [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]], not endorsing one. He drew on his *Apprentice* research to describe [[omarosa-manigault-newman|Omarosa Manigault Newman]] as a chaos agent Trump deployed to test contestants under pressure, and proposed that the 2017 Trump–Bannon "break-up" and the "Sloppy Steve" epithet may have been the first real instance of what Badlands writers call "Trump-a-Mania." He extended this to speculate that the public spat provided narrative shielding allowing Bannon to approach Epstein, and questioned the standard portrayal of Epstein by pointing to [[adnan-khashoggi|Adnan Khashoggi]]'s recruitment of him out of Bear Stearns.

In the same edition he treated the rumors of [[mohamed-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan]]'s death as a probable psychological operation tied to efforts to divide [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]], noting [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdogan]]'s deleted post and characterizing the Turkish president as aligned with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].

On [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s [[munich-security-conference|Munich Security Conference]] address he wrote that it was "a pretty good speech by Little Marco," approved of sending Rubio "to deliver these terms of surrender to the satanic elite," dismissed any prospect of reconciliation with Europe's Old Guard, and warned that the European elite appear willing to start a third world war rather than cede power, closing with "Choose now, Europe."
