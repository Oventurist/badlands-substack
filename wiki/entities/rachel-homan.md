# Rachel Homan

Rachel Homan is a Canadian curler who skipped the Canadian women's team at the [[2026-winter-olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]].[1]

In a round-robin match against Switzerland, Homan was penalized for what officials ruled was a "double-touch" violation — the same infraction alleged against Canadian men's third [[marc-kennedy|Marc Kennedy]] — resulting in the removal of her stone.[1] Homan protested the call, but officials upheld it without video review, citing the sport's current officiating standards.[1] [[world-curling|World Curling]] later acknowledged that umpires cannot visually monitor every delivery and increased the number of monitoring officials at subsequent matches.[1]
