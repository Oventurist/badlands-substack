# Robert Bigelow

Robert Bigelow is a Nevada real estate investor and funder of space exploration and paranormal research who was the single largest donor to [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]]'s 2024 presidential campaign before publicly signaling in November 2023 that he was considering switching his support to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Political donations

Bigelow gave more than $20 million to the DeSantis campaign in 2023, the largest donation to any 2024 candidate according to federal filings available at the time.[1] His stated reconsideration amounted to a sharp rebuke of the Florida governor's White House aspirations.[1]

## Shift toward Trump

Bigelow criticized DeSantis for running a weak campaign and said that Hamas's attack on Israel the previous month had shown the United States needed a "streetwise" leader such as Trump.[1] "I've got to look at who would probably be the strongest commander, with the most experience . . . And that's only one guy," he told the Financial Times.[1] He added, "Who would you want as a commander? I'd want somebody that would be a hell of an ass kicker if he needed to be. On the face of it, you lean toward Trump."[1]

Bigelow also predicted Trump would defeat DeSantis for the Republican presidential nomination provided he stayed out of jail, saying, "I think Trump is too strong. I think Trump has the momentum, the inertia, to beat him." He characterized Trump as a "bull" and DeSantis as "dinner."[1]
