# Yitzhak Kroizer

Yitzhak Kroizer is a member of the Israeli parliament, the [[knesset|Knesset]], cited in May 2026 Badlands commentary over a video published online of remarks he delivered in the chamber.[1]

In the video, according to the commentary, Kroizer explicitly states that there are no innocent people in Palestine and that killing all the women and children there would be a fair price to pay to ensure [[israel|Israel]]'s security.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry presented the remarks alongside [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s publication of video showing handcuffed [[global-sumud-flotilla|Global Sumud Flotilla]] detainees as evidence of an increasingly common pattern of hubris among Israeli leaders.[1]
