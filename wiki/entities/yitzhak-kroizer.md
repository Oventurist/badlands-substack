# Yitzhak Kroizer

Yitzhak Kroizer is a member of the Israeli [[knesset|Knesset]] whose public remarks about Palestinians were circulated in the May 2026 Badlands Brief as an example of what Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the hubris of Israeli leaders.

## The Knesset remarks

According to a video published online and cited in the Brief, Kroizer explicitly said at the Knesset that there are no innocent people in Palestine, and that killing all the women and children there is a fair price to pay to ensure Israel's security. The comments were invoked in the Brief's coverage of the [[global-sumud-flotilla|Global Sumud Flotilla]] interception and Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]]'s accusation that Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] was acting "like a true Nazi."
