# Robert Kagan

Robert Kagan is an American neoconservative writer and co-founder of the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]].

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus pairs Kagan with [[william-kristol]] as the founders of PNAC in 1997, and pointedly identifies him in a parenthetical as "Victoria Nuland's husband]" — a marital connection the corpus treats as emblematic of the interlocking character of the foreign policy establishment, since Nuland is elsewhere associated with the 2014 events in Ukraine that the article describes as a State Department–orchestrated [[color-revolution]].

Through PNAC, Kagan is linked to the doctrine of [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] and to the strategic program set out in [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]], which the article regards as the blueprint for interventions in Iraq, Libya, Iran and [[syria]].
