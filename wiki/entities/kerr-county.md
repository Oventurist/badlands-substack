# Kerr County

Kerr County is a county in central Texas that was the epicenter of the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]].[1] At least 28 children died in the county in connection with the flash flooding that struck over the Fourth of July weekend in 2025.[1]

## Communities affected

The unincorporated community of Hunt, within Kerr County, was home to [[camp-mystic|Camp Mystic]], the girls' summer camp from which ten campers and one counselor were reported missing.[1] Officials urged Hunt residents to move to higher ground on the afternoon of July 6, 2025, because of the risk of additional flooding.[1]

## Warning infrastructure

Records showed that Kerr County officials had previously considered installing flood-warning sirens, but the plan never came to fruition.[1] Scrutiny also fell on the [[national-weather-service|National Weather Service]], which issued numerous alerts before the storm but faced questions about staffing and about its ability to reach residents in time.[1]
