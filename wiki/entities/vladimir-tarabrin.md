# Vladimir Tarabrin

Vladimir Tarabrin is a Russian envoy to the [[organization-for-the-prohibition-of-chemical-weapons|Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons]].[1]

## OPCW Executive Council statement

At a session of the OPCW Executive Council in The Hague in July 2025, Tarabrin said that "the Russian side continues to document not only instances of Ukrainian nationalists using toxic chemicals and military-grade poison agents, but also evidence of a widespread system for their mass production in Ukraine."[1] He added that Moscow had requested the organization dispatch experts to verify Russia's findings, which included the reported discovery of a makeshift laboratory storing [[chloropicrin|chloropicrin]] and claims that Ukrainian forces had dropped toxic substances from drones.[1]
