# Dustin Burrows

Dustin Burrows is a Texas state representative and a top ally of outgoing Texas House Speaker [[dade-phelan|Dade Phelan]].[1]

In January 2025 Burrows was one of the two principal contenders in the [[2025-texas-house-speaker-battle|Texas House Speaker battle]], running against state Representative [[david-cook|David Cook]], who had been chosen the previous month as the state GOP's Speaker nominee. Burrows represented the remnant of what was once the Texas party's establishment against a rising far-right faction that controlled most of the state's government.[1]
