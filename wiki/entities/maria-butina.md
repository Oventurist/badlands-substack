# Maria Butina

Maria Butina is a Russian national whose relationship with entrepreneur [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]] became a central element of Byrne's public disclosures about his role as an informal intelligence asset for the United States government.[1]

## Role in Patrick Byrne's disclosures

Byrne has repeatedly described an affair with Butina and framed it as part of a broader arrangement in which he cooperated with federal authorities. In the inaugural issue of Capitol Times Magazine, Byrne recapped these prior disclosures and added further detail, including an assertion that the United States government signed off on a fictional plan he presented under which he would rape and murder Butina — a scenario Byrne described as a test of the government's intentions and moral posture.[1]

Butina also features in Byrne's account of what he characterizes as a blackmail scheme directed at [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], in which Byrne says he acted at the direction of federal investigators. Byrne states that when the FBI "dropped the sting" on Clinton, agents also threatened his life.[1]

Byrne's account, as recounted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], reads "like the confessions of a spy," though Byrne insists he was not a spy but a patriot serving his country. Byrne has said he testified to this material under oath before the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]], which he claims omitted substantial portions from its final report.[1]
