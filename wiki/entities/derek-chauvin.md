# Derek Chauvin

Derek Chauvin is the former Minneapolis police officer convicted of murder in the 2020 death of [[george-floyd|George Floyd]].

In the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cites what she calls the "Derek Chauvin op" as a precedent for her claim that media campaigns are used to influence jury pools, in the context of the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] trial coverage following the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]. She argues that influencing the jury pool "is also certainly in play" in the Robinson case, as it was, in her account, during Chauvin's trial.
