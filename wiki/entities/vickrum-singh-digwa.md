# Vickrum Singh Digwa

Vickrum Singh Digwa is a British man convicted of the murder of 18-year-old [[henry-nowak|Henry Nowak]] in Southampton, United Kingdom.[1]

## The killing and trial

On December 3, 2025, Digwa, then 23, stabbed Nowak five times with an eight-inch blade as Nowak walked home after a night out.[1] Digwa carried two blades, including a kirpan worn around his neck, and had trained with weapons since the age of 12.[1] He was arrested at the scene.[1]

At the scene Digwa claimed that Nowak had launched a racist attack that knocked off his turban; police acted on the claim, arresting and handcuffing the wounded Nowak, who died about an hour later.[1] No independent evidence supported the racism allegation.[1]

Digwa was convicted of murder on May 28, 2026, following a trial in which prosecutors stated the case was about murder rather than racism or religion, and in which Digwa was found to have lied about key events.[1] He was sentenced to life imprisonment with a minimum term of 21 years.[1]
