# NetChoice

NetChoice is a coalition of technology companies that litigates against state regulation of online platforms, frequently on [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] grounds.[1]

## Challenge to Colorado's warning label law

In August 2025, NetChoice sued to block enactment of the [[colorado-social-media-warning-label-law|Colorado social media warning label law]] (House Bill 1136 of 2024), arguing that requiring member companies to display warning pop-ups to minors about the health and brain impacts of social media use amounted to unconstitutional compelled speech.[1] The warnings would have had to be shown to minors who had spent more than an hour on social media within a 24-hour period.[1]

U.S. District Court Judge [[william-j-martinez|William J. Martinez]] granted NetChoice a preliminary injunction in November 2025, concluding that the coalition was substantially likely to succeed on the merits of its claim that [[colorado|Colorado]] may not pursue child-safety goals "by compelling social media companies to speak (the state's) expressive messages."[1] The ruling prevented the provision from taking effect on January 1 while the broader lawsuit proceeds through the courts.[1]
