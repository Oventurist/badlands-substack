# Ellen Brown

Ellen Brown is an American author and chair of the [[public-banking-institute|Public Banking Institute]]. She appears in A Seismic Shift via a quotation from In These Times, which cites her book *Banking on the People* for the argument that "a public banking system … can fund the goods, services and infrastructure required to satisfy the needs of the people and the economy without unsustainable debt, taxation or environmental degradation."

The quotation is used by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] to support the wider case made in the article that, rather than having public funds extracted from local communities to fuel Wall Street speculation, [[public-banking|public banks]] can ensure those funds are used to stabilise local economies and support local public priorities. Brown's position is presented as part of the mainstream advocacy literature surrounding the [[bank-of-north-dakota|Bank of North Dakota]] and the movement to establish public banks in California, New York, New Mexico, New Hampshire, Massachusetts and other states.
