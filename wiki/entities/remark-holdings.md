# Remark Holdings

Remark Holdings is a technology company that surfaced in reporting on the corruption investigations surrounding the administration of New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[1]

[[politico|POLITICO]] reported in late September 2024 that Adams adviser [[timothy-pearson|Timothy Pearson]] pushed the New York City fire department to ink a contract with Remark Holdings while Pearson maintained a close personal relationship with a consultant for the firm.[1] The episode was cited in Badlands commentary as an illustration of how contract-approval authority concentrated in a single mayoral aide could be routed toward favored vendors.[1]
