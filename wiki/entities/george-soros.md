# George Soros

George Soros is a financier counted by [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]] among her "close friends" in the West, alongside Larry Summers and Al Gore, and among the figures she was happy to bring into Canada during the re-organization of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party of Canada]] between 2011 and 2014 under the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] banner.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

In Freeland's taxonomy of the new plutocracy, Soros belongs with Bill Gates and Warren Buffett to the category of technocratically friendly plutocrats who are, in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s characterization, ideologically compatible with a New World Order of depopulation — as distinct from the "bad" plutocrats such as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who do not conform to the British Empire's program of global governance under the green agenda.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Polish media acquisition (2023)

In August 2023 a company backed by the Soros Economic Development Fund took control of two major Polish newspapers, raising concerns over media bias attributed to the billionaire's progressive politics.[2] The Dutch company [[pluralis|Pluralis]] completed a deal first struck in 2021, buying additional shares from Polish owner Grzegorz Hajdarowicz to reach 57 percent of [[gremi-media|Gremi Media]] — publisher of the daily Rzeczpospolita and the financial daily Parkiet — and thereby taking control of the company.[2] The transaction was reported by [[remix-news|Remix News]] as evidence that Soros remained active in European affairs despite claims of his withdrawal.[2]

## Texas Spending (2023–2024)

State campaign finance records reviewed by Fox News Digital and reported in January 2024 showed Soros pouring extensive sums into Texas in an attempt to flip the traditionally Republican state to Democrats.[5] Over the preceding year he contributed more than $3 million to at least five left-wing groups in the state to build political infrastructure and help Democrats make gains.[5] Among the recipients was Texas Majority PAC, an under-the-radar group positioning itself to turn the state blue, which Fox News Digital had reported in August 2023 received hundreds of thousands of dollars from Soros' Democracy PAC II; the financier was described as single-handedly propelling that organization.[5]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the story to revisit claims that Soros has been "banned" from multiple countries, noting fact-checkers' distinction that it was the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] rather than Soros personally that ceased operations in Hungary in 2018 after passage of the so-called "Stop Soros" law targeting its work with illegal immigrants.[5] She argued the Hungarian precedent was directly relevant because Soros holds dual U.S. and Hungarian citizenship and because the banned Hungarian activities concerned immigration, much like the Texas spending — with the difference being the degree of influence his money buys over American decision-makers.[5]

On 17 September 2025 the [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] published an exclusive report alleging that Soros's [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], run with his son [[alex-soros|Alexander Soros]], had directed over $80 million since 2016 to organizations tied to terrorism or extremist violence.[6] The report cited grants to the [[center-for-third-world-organizing|Center for Third World Organizing]] and the [[ruckus-society|Ruckus Society]], the [[sunrise-movement|Sunrise Movement]]'s endorsement of the [[stop-cop-city|Stop Cop City]] campaign, $18 million to the [[movement-for-black-lives|Movement for Black Lives]], and more than $2.3 million to the West Bank NGO [[al-haq|Al-Haq]].[6]

## Succession and the OSF retreat from Europe

Soros handed control of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] — which holds most of the assets of his roughly $25 billion family office — to his son [[alexander-soros|Alexander Soros]] in June 2023.[7] Two months later, OSF announced it would terminate most of its European Union operations amid a "radical shift of strategic direction," closing all regional and global programs and cutting headcount by at least 40%.[7] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the handover as bequeathing "a somewhat unceremonious inheritance that may be more of an albatross," and read the retreat as a sign of shifting global narrative terrain.[7]

The same commentary advanced a speculative claim that Soros himself has been dead for years — which the writer argued would explain why his son had been "rolled out as the heir apparent" — while contending that the elder Soros "has come to symbolize the globalist oligarchy," making preservation of his caricature useful both to allies keeping money flowing and to opponents seeking to destroy the symbol.[8]
