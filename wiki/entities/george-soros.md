# George Soros

George Soros is a Hungarian-American financier and philanthropist, founder of the Open Society Foundations. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], he appears as a member of the class of plutocrats that [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]] identified with, and as one of her personal allies.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that Larry Summers, George Soros and [[al-gore|Al Gore]] "were just a few players in the West whom she considered her 'close friends' and whom she was happy to bring into Canada during the period of re-organization of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] (2011-2014)" under the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] banner.

In the article's reading of Freeland's 2011 essay *The Rise of the New Global Elite*, Soros is grouped with [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] and [[warren-buffett|Warren Buffett]] among the "good, technocratic friendly plutocrats who are ideologically compatible with the New World Order of depopulation," in contrast to plutocrats such as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who, in Ehret's framing, do not conform to the program of global governance under a green agenda.
