# WilmerHale

WilmerHale is a large American law firm, formed by the 2004 merger of Wilmer Cutler & Pickering and Hale and Dorr, with major offices in Washington, D.C., and Boston. In the Badlands corpus it appears as the former law firm of [[robert-mueller|Robert S. Mueller III]], and it was the source that announced his death.

On March 20, 2026, WilmerHale confirmed that Mueller had died in Charlottesville, Virginia, at age 81, and stated that he was survived by his wife, Ann, two daughters and five grandchildren. The firm's announcement was the basis for the [[reuters|Reuters]] and [[associated-press|Associated Press]] obituaries reported in the March 23, 2026 Badlands Brief. Mueller had returned to the firm after his service as FBI Director and special counsel.
