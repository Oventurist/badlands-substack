# Jack Ruby

Jack Ruby, born Jack Rubenstein, was the Dallas nightclub operator who shot and killed [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]], the accused assassin of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], while Oswald was in police custody.[1] In the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI," contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presents Ruby not as an outraged bystander who acted spontaneously, but as one link in a chain of individuals whose biographies intersect with United States intelligence activity.[1]

## Alleged MK ULTRA connection

The central claim advanced about Ruby in the article is medical rather than criminal. Carlson writes that Ruby was treated for anxiety by Dr. Louis Jolyon West, a psychiatrist alleged by some researchers to have been a [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] doctor working for the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]].[1] MK ULTRA is described in the article as the mind-control program the CIA developed, using LSD and other drugs together with physical and psychological torture to break down a subject's mind for the purpose of reprogramming that person as an asset.[1]

Carlson treats the overlap as more than incidental, asking rhetorically whether it is coincidence that both Oswald and Ruby were said to be connected to the agency's MK ULTRA program.[1] The pairing forms the article's opening template for its wider thesis: that the men publicly blamed for American political violence tend to have prior documented or alleged contact with the agencies that later identify them as culprits.[1]

## Place in the "patsy" thesis

Ruby appears in the article's summary roster of figures suspected of having taken part in the MK ULTRA program, alongside Oswald, [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]], [[charles-manson|Charlie Manson]], Jim Jones, John Hinckley Jr., Mark Chapman, David Koresh, [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]], and John Salvi.[1] Within Carlson's taxonomy, Ruby belongs to the first category — those used to eliminate, or attempt to eliminate, people in power who opposed the CIA — because his killing of Oswald foreclosed any trial at which Oswald might have described his own intelligence relationships.[1]

The article does not assert that Ruby knowingly served the agency; its broader argument is that most alleged patsies believe they are working with or for the CIA or FBI and are discarded when their usefulness ends.[1]
