# Samantha Power

**Samantha Power** is an American diplomat and official who served as Administrator of the [[usaid|United States Agency for International Development]] (USAID) for the duration of the Biden administration.[1]

## In Badlands commentary

In coverage of the February 2025 shutdown and administrative-leave order at USAID, [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] urged readers not to forget Power's tenure, noting that President Biden had installed her as director of USAID for his entire administration.[1] Sather further asserted that Power was "instrumental in the unmasking of General [[michael-flynn|Flynn]]" during what Badlands writers describe as the RussiaGate debacle — a reference to the practice of requesting the identities of Americans incidentally collected in foreign intelligence intercepts.[1]

Within the Badlands framing, Power's leadership of USAID connects the agency's foreign grant-making to the broader intelligence-community controversies of the first Trump term, making her a recurring figure in critiques of the agency's role abroad.[1]

## Criticism of the Soleimani strike

Following the [[assassination-of-qasem-soleimani|January 2020 killing of Qasem Soleimani]], former U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations Samantha Power condemned the action, pointing to the uncertainty of the outcome and the allegedly precipitous manner in which the decision was made: "Trump is surrounded by sycophants (having fired those who've dissented). He has purged Iran specialists. He has abolished NSC processes to review contingencies. He is seen as a liar around the world."[2] Joe Lange reads this as evidence that [[donald-trump|Trump]] had removed Deep State [[iran|Iran]] specialists who were, in his account, controlled by [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] and left in the dark about the administration's plans.[2]

## Background in the Obama administration

Power joined the Obama State Department transition team in late November 2008 and served as Special Assistant to the President and Senior Director for Multilateral Affairs and Human Rights on the National Security Council from January 2009 to February 2013.[2] She is considered to have been a key figure in the [[obama-administration|Obama administration]] in persuading the president to intervene militarily in Libya.[2]
