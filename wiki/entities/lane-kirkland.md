# Lane Kirkland

Lane Kirkland led the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] from 1979 to 1995, an era the article says deepened the ties between organized labor and U.S. intelligence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) Under his leadership American unions backed Ronald Reagan's counterinsurgency campaign in Central America, with the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]] training Salvadoran death-squad affiliates.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The same period saw the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]]'s role grow, using unions in Asia and Africa for "hearts and minds" work, while [[state-department|State Department]] cables show ambassadors tasking union representatives with gathering intelligence on leftist organizers.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) Kirkland's tenure ended two years before the federation's foreign institutes were consolidated into the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
