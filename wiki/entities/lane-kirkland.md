# Lane Kirkland

Lane Kirkland (1922–1999) was an American labor leader who served as president of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] from 1979 to 1995. In the Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]], his tenure is described as a period that "deepened the ties between labor and intelligence."

## Role in covert operations

The article states that during the Kirkland era, "unions backed Reagan's counterinsurgency in Central America, training Salvadoran death-squad affiliates via AIFLD" — the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]]. This period is presented as part of the AFL-CIO's continued collaboration with U.S. intelligence agencies in covert operations, reinforcing the institution's role as a tool of U.S. foreign policy.
