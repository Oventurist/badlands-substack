# Khmer Rouge

The Khmer Rouge were the Cambodian communist movement that emerged victorious from [[cambodia|Cambodia]]'s civil war in the aftermath of the American bombing campaign of Southeast Asia, and went on to commit a genocide estimated to have killed between 1.5 and 2 million people.[1]

In coverage of the death of [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], Al Jazeera argued that the carpet bombing of Cambodia — which Kissinger personally approved, with declassified Pentagon reports recording 3,875 air raids dropping some 540,000 tons of bombs within the first year of the campaign — did not halt but rather facilitated the Vietnamese and Cambodian communists' seizure of power, with the Khmer Rouge the direct beneficiary in Cambodia.[1]

The 1969 assault on Cambodia was approved in secret and proceeded without Congress being informed, and unexploded American ordnance continues to kill Vietnamese and Cambodian civilians decades after the [[vietnam-war|Vietnam War]].[1] The chef Anthony Bourdain's remark that "once you've been to Cambodia, you'll never stop wanting to beat Henry Kissinger to death with your bare hands" was cited as a summary of the campaign's legacy.[1]
