# Shehbaz Sharif

**Shehbaz Sharif** is the Prime Minister of Pakistan. In Badlands commentary he is counted among the regional leaders whose personal rapport with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] underpins the emerging Middle Eastern security architecture.[1]

## Position during the 2026 Iran crisis

The March 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] reported that Sharif came out in support of [[iran|Iran]] during the 2026 escalation, a stance Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described as notable given historical differences between Islamabad and Tehran and given the "deep love for President Trump that Sharif has tearfully expressed on many occasions."[1]

Sharif was placed alongside [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] of [[turkey|Türkiye]] and Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as strongman leaders declining to be drawn into a regional war, and as participants in what the Brief called a fragile matrix of incentive structures binding Arab monarchies and Turkic democracies to a common peace.[1]

## Role in the June 2026 peace framework

When Trump announced in June 2026 that the deal with Iran was "now complete," Sharif said the pact called for the immediate and permanent termination of military operations on all fronts, including Lebanon.[2] His characterization was reported by Reuters alongside the announcement that a memorandum of understanding halting the U.S. blockade of Iran and reopening the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] was scheduled to be signed in Switzerland.[2]
