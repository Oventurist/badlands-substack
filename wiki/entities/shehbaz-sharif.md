# Shehbaz Sharif

Shehbaz Sharif is the Prime Minister of [[pakistan|Pakistan]]. In the Badlands Media corpus he is presented as one of the regional leaders whose personal rapport with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] underwrites what contributors describe as an emerging Middle Eastern and Muslim-world security architecture.

## Position during the 2026 Iran crisis

The March 10, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, in commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], that Sharif "has just come out in support of [[iran|Iran]]" during the escalating conflict, and characterizes this as notable "despite historical differences and a deep love for President Trump that Sharif has tearfully expressed on many occasions."

Sharif is grouped in that commentary with [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] of [[turkey|Türkiye]] and Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as "strongmen" who, the writer argues, will not be maneuvered into a regional war against their neighbors, and whose loyalty to Trump is framed as loyalty to "the common cause of peace."

## Mediation of the US–Iran agreement (June 2026)

The Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026 reports that Sharif, having served as a mediator in the negotiations, announced that the United States and [[iran|Iran]] had agreed to the immediate and permanent termination of military operations on all fronts, including in Lebanon. [[pakistan|Pakistan]] credited [[qatar|Qatar]], [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[turkey|Turkey]] for their roles in helping facilitate the agreement and said additional meetings would take place that week to prepare for its implementation, with an official signing ceremony scheduled for June 19 in Switzerland.

The announcement accompanied President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s declaration that the deal was complete and included the reopening of the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], the removal of the US naval blockade, and mine-removal operations. Sharif's role gives the accord its reported name, the [[islamabad-agreement|Islamabad Agreement]], and marks the fullest expression to date of the Pakistani mediating position the corpus had anticipated during the March 2026 crisis.
