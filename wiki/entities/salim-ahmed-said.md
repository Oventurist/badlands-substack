# Salim Ahmed Said

Salim Ahmed Said is an Iraqi-British national designated by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] for running a network of companies accused of selling Iranian oil falsely declared as Iraqi oil since at least 2020.[1] The network identified by Treasury included the shipping company [[vs-tankers-fze|VS Tankers FZE]], formerly AISSOT.[1]

## Treasury designation

Sanctions language issued under Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] and quoted in the Badlands Brief stated that Said's companies use ship-to-ship transfers and other obfuscation techniques to hide their activities, blending Iranian oil with Iraqi oil before selling it to Western buyers via [[iraq|Iraq]] or the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] as purely Iraqi oil using forged documentation.[1] The mechanism allowed the oil to reach legitimate markets and helped [[iran|Iran]] evade international sanctions on its oil exports.[1] Said was designated pursuant to Executive Order 13902 for operating in the petroleum sector of the Iranian economy.[1]

The designation further asserted that Said had bribed many members of key Iraqi government bodies, including parliament, reportedly paying millions of dollars in kickbacks in exchange for forged vouchers permitting the sale of Iranian oil as Iraqi in origin.[1]

## Relevance to the 2026 Iraqi purge

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] linked the Said network to the June 2026 anti-corruption sweep in Baghdad's [[green-zone|Green Zone]], in which officials and bankers with ties to Iran were arrested, and to the Iranian strikes on tankers connected to [[evalend-shipping|Evalend Shipping]] in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1] He compared the affair to the 2018 sanctions imposed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Iranian central bankers accused of laundering U.S. cash through Iraqi and UAE banks, noting that those bankers were subsequently arrested by the Iranian government and sentenced to ten years in prison.[1]
