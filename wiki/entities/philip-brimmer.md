# Philip Brimmer

Philip Brimmer is a federal judge of the U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado, described in contemporaneous coverage as chief justice of that district.[1]

In September 2024 Brimmer approved a settlement in litigation brought by the [[public-interest-legal-foundation|Public Interest Legal Foundation]] against Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]], ordering her office to disclose the requested 2021 [[electronic-registration-information-center|ERIC]] reports by November 1 while allowing minimal redactions to the ERIC Report Key.[1] Brimmer had earlier denied Griswold's request to dismiss the case, after which she signed the stipulation; with the agreement in place he dismissed PILF's claim that Griswold violated the National Voter Registration Act of 1993.[1] Badlands commentary noted that Brimmer's ruling came as similar voter-roll "glitches" were being reported in California and Iowa.[1]
