# George Washington University

George Washington University is the Washington, D.C. institution where [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] studied after returning to the United States at age 18 from [[jeddah|Jeddah]], Saudi Arabia, and from which she was, at 19, "assigned" as an intern to the office of First Lady [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] in 1996.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The Badlands account treats the university as more than biographical background. While a student there, Abedin served on the executive board of the campus [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter, heading its Social Committee, at the same time she was interning at the Clinton White House and working as assistant editor of the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) That MSA chapter was later, in 2001, guided spiritually by the Al-Qaeda operative [[anwar-al-awlaki|Anwar al-Awlaki]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Within the article's broader thesis, universities are the recruiting and training grounds of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s "civilization/stealth jihad" — the mechanism by which young Muslims are indoctrinated and then placed in senior positions inside government.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Abedin's path from a George Washington University classroom to the [[state-department|State Department]] is offered as the exemplary case.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
