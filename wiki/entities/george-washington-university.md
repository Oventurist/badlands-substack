# George Washington University

George Washington University (GWU) is a private research university in Washington, D.C.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] cites GWU as the campus from which [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] entered the [[bill-clinton|Clinton]] White House as a 19-year-old intern in 1996. The article treats her presence there as an illustration of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s alleged university-based recruitment strategy rather than as coincidence.

Quoting PJMedia, the article states that in the mid-to-late 1990s, while she was an intern and an assistant editor at the *[[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]]*, Abedin sat on the executive board of the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at GWU, heading its "Social Committee." It further reports that the GWU chapter's spiritual guide in 2001 was Al-Qaeda operative Anwar al Awlaki, who was then ministering to some of the eventual 9/11 hijackers. Within the article's framework, universities such as GWU are "recruiting and training grounds for the infiltration of our government."
