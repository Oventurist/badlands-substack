# Nigel Farage

Nigel Farage is a British politician and broadcaster, the former leader of the Brexit Party, who in February 2024 became one of the most prominent international voices predicting that the Democratic Party would remove [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] from the 2024 presidential ticket.[1]

## Commentary on Biden's fitness

Speaking to GB News, Farage said flatly that Biden suffers from "senile dementia," adding that he had "been saying that for some months."[1] He framed the issue as one of global consequence: "he's not just the leader of America, he's the leader of the free world and under his tenure many of these awful things around the world have happened…he is totally incapable of the job."[1]

Farage's remarks followed the release of the [[hur-report|Hur report]], in which special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]] described Biden's memory as so poor that he could not recall when his vice presidency began or ended, nor recall within several years when his son Beau died.[1]

## Prediction of a Biden replacement

Farage predicted that there would be no Trump–Biden run-off, arguing that Democrats would "ditch him before the Democrat convention takes place in July" because Biden had become "just too embarrassing," and noting the president's plummeting approval ratings.[1]

Badlands Media commentary treated the prediction as plausible but not inevitable. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] disagreed with Farage's timeline, leaning instead toward a contested [[2024-democratic-national-convention|Democratic National Convention]] producing a [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]], or [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] candidacy, while noting that other Badlands writers believed the party would be stuck with Biden — read as [[devolution|devolution]] proof.[1]
