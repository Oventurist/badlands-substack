# Matt Ehret

**Matt Ehret** is a writer and researcher on central banking and geopolitical history whose work has influenced Badlands commentary on Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]].[1]

## Influence on Badlands analysis

Badlands host GhostofBasedPatrickHenry credited Ehret with educating him to understand Carney as "the king of all central bankers," representing interests that can only be described as "globalist" and "one-world government."[1] That framing informed the Badlands reading of Carney's G20 remarks that the world can go on without the United States, which could be interpreted either as typical anti-American rhetoric applauding a waning American influence, or bicamerally as the king of central bankers acknowledging the demise of the central banking system and the shift toward a [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]] with decentralized economic and financial systems.[1]
