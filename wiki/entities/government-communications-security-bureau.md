# Government Communications Security Bureau

The Government Communications Security Bureau (GCSB) is New Zealand's signals intelligence and cybersecurity agency and the country's principal contributor to the [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] intelligence alliance.[1]

## 2024 intelligence leak investigation

New Zealand was implicated in the October 2024 leak of classified documents outlining the United States' assessment of [[israel|Israel]]'s plan to attack [[iran|Iran]].[1] One of the leaked documents stated it had been compiled by the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]], while the other appeared to have originated with the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]].[1] Both were marked "top secret" and had been shared within the Five Eyes alliance of the United States, Britain, Canada, New Zealand and Australia, and carried the acronym FGI, standing for Foreign Government Intelligence.[1]

The documents were published on an Iranian-aligned Telegram account called "Middle East Spectator," though White House National Security Council spokesman [[john-kirby|John Kirby]] said there was no indication further documents would find their way into the public domain and that President Joe Biden was "deeply concerned" about the leak.[1]

## Security posture

The GCSB's exposure came against a backdrop of repeated successful hacks of New Zealand institutions by foreign powers, and the agency had recently absorbed a NZ$7 million (US$4.7 million) budget cut.[1] Because Iran maintains a large and sophisticated cyber-warfare capability, the possibility that the documents were obtained through a hostile hack was under investigation by U.S. intelligence agencies and the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]
