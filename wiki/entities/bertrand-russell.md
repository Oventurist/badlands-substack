# Bertrand Russell

Lord Bertrand Russell was a British philosopher and mathematician identified by Badlands Media as the intellectual fountainhead of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]], the science of control.[1] The article states that the social-engineering programs to rewrite human nature and induce a global reset of civilization were driven by ideological underpinnings developed by acolytes of Russell, led by his disciple [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]].[1]

Through that lineage Russell is connected to [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] and [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]], both described as devotees of cybernetics, and to the postwar repackaging of [[eugenics|eugenics]] that followed 1945 — the same rotten wine, in the article's phrase, made unpalatable by Hitler and poured into new wine skins.[1]
