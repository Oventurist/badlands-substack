# Bertrand Russell

Lord Bertrand Russell was a British philosopher and mathematician identified by Badlands Media as the intellectual fountainhead of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]], the science of control.[1] The article states that the social-engineering programs to rewrite human nature and induce a global reset of civilization were driven by ideological underpinnings developed by acolytes of Russell, led by his disciple [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]].[1]

Through that lineage Russell is connected to [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] and [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]], both described as devotees of cybernetics, and to the postwar repackaging of [[eugenics|eugenics]] that followed 1945 — the same rotten wine, in the article's phrase, made unpalatable by Hitler and poured into new wine skins.[1]

## Guild socialism and the manufactured dialectic

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats Russell as the clearest witness to how the "bottom up" versus "top down" manipulation of the early twentieth century worked, since Russell was both a [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] member and a public advocate of [[guild-socialism|guild socialism]] of the sort promoted by [[alfred-richard-orage|A. R. Orage]].[2] In *Proposed Roads to Freedom: Socialism, Anarchism and Syndicalism*, Russell wrote that "pure Anarchism, though it should be the ultimate ideal… is for the present impossible," and that "the best practicable system, to my mind, is that of Guild Socialism, which concedes what is valid both in the claims of the State Socialists and in the Syndicalist fear of the State by adopting a system of federalism among trades."[2] He also observed that socialism in England "has been inspired in the main by the Fabians," through State Socialism and a doctrine of "permeation" in which civil servants and trade unions were to be brought round to political rather than industrial action.[2]

Ehret argues that Russell's support for anarchism had nothing to do with concern for freedom or happiness, but with control: the ideology would destroy sovereign nation states into chaos, justifying a top-down response in the form of world government, or "Oligarchical Collectivism."[2] He notes that in the same book Russell wrote that if the peace of the world were to become secure, there would have to be "a development of the idea which inspires the project of a League of Nations" — a contradiction Ehret says was always a joke at the reader's expense, and the same joke [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] made when he tweeted "Anarcho-Syndicalism FTW!!" hours after invoking a "Martian Technocracy" in June 2019.[2]

Ehret further identifies Russell as the principal creator of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]], developed by his student [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]] in 1943, which became the intellectual technology used after the Second World War to build Silicon Valley and the technocratic management of world systems under a government of self-professed elites.[2]
