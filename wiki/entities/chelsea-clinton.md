# Chelsea Clinton

Chelsea Clinton is the daughter of former Secretary of State and presidential candidate [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and former President Bill Clinton. She appears in Badlands Media coverage chiefly as a public figure accompanying her mother at high-profile New York events.[1]

## Shubert Theater incident (March 2023)

In March 2023, Chelsea Clinton attended a performance of the musical *Some Like It Hot* at the Shubert Theater in New York City alongside her mother.[1] According to reporting relayed by Breitbart and originally published by *Page Six*, human excrement was discovered in the aisle near where the two were seated when the house lights came up for intermission.[1] Sources quoted in the coverage disagreed over whether the episode was a one-off event or the work of an alleged "serial pooper," with one source attributing it to an elderly patron and describing the situation as sad rather than deliberate.[1] House staff cleaned the aisle quickly and the second act began on schedule, with both Clintons remaining in the theater.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary on the story, written by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], treated it as satirical fodder — calling it the "headline of the week" and joking about whether the intelligence community should declassify its files on "Turd Gate."[1] The item was included in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a comic counterpoint to that edition's heavier coverage of the anticipated indictment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and of banking-sector scandals.[1]

## Inverted cross claim

Chapter 14 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series names Chelsea Clinton as the person the author believes Q was referring to in drop #100's discussion of occult symbolism, on the grounds that she has been photographed openly wearing an upside-down cross.[2] The chapter explains that the inverted cross originally represented the crucifixion of St. Peter but argues that it has since been repurposed as a symbol of Satanism, part of a wider pattern of what the author calls inverted symbolism among the powerful.[2] The passage sits alongside the chapter's treatment of [[spirit-cooking|spirit cooking]] and its assertion that many figures in government worship Satan.[2] The identification is presented explicitly as the author's opinion.[2]
