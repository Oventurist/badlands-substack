# Mark Pocan

Mark Pocan is a Democratic member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|US House of Representatives]] from Wisconsin and a former head of the Congressional Progressive Caucus.[1]

## Reaction to the November 2025 shutdown deal

Pocan emerged as one of the loudest liberal critics of the bipartisan agreement that ended the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|longest government shutdown in US history]], after a group of mostly moderate Democratic senators joined Republicans to advance the funding package without securing an extension of ObamaCare subsidies.[1] Posting on X, he wrote: "What Senate Dems who voted for this horses‑‑‑ deal did was f‑‑‑ over all the hard work people put in to Tuesday's elections. Healthcare matters. Not platitudes."[1] His remarks reflected broader progressive fury that centrists had, in critics' view, given away the store without anything in return, frustrations amplified by Democratic victories in the previous week's off-year elections that liberals read as a mandate to keep fighting for the health care subsidies.[1]
