# Al Jazeera

*Al Jazeera* is a Qatar-based international news network. It appears in the February 12, 2026 [[badlands-brief-405|Badlands Brief]] as the outlet reporting remarks by [[ali-shamkhani|Ali Shamkhani]], senior adviser to Iran's supreme leader, during an event marking the 47th anniversary of the Islamic revolution.

According to the *Al Jazeera* account cited by the Brief, Shamkhani said that "the Islamic Republic's missile capabilities are non-negotiable," a statement issued while [[iran|Iranian]] and American officials were conducting indirect nuclear talks in Oman and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was meeting President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Washington. The Brief pairs the *Al Jazeera* reporting with a [[reuters|Reuters]] item making the same point about Tehran's refusal to negotiate over its ballistic missile program.
