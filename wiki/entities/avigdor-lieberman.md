# Avigdor Lieberman

Avigdor Lieberman is an Israeli opposition [[knesset|Knesset]] member and former defense minister.[1]

## Gaza militia weapons disclosure

In June 2025, Lieberman disclosed in a Thursday morning interview with Kan Bet public radio that Israel was supplying weapons to an ISIS-aligned jihadist gang in Gaza at the direction of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] "The Israeli government is giving weapons to a group of criminals and felons, identified with Islamic State, at the direction of the prime minister," he said, warning that "Ultimately, these weapons will be turned against us."[1]

His remarks were the first public airing of the arrangement, subsequently confirmed by unnamed Israeli defense officials to the Times of Israel; Netanyahu, while declining to validate the characterization of the recipients, confirmed that Israel was "activating clans in Gaza that oppose [[hamas|Hamas]]," and was accused of green-lighting the transfers without securing his cabinet's permission.[1] See [[israeli-arming-of-gaza-anti-hamas-militias|Israeli arming of Gaza anti-Hamas militias]].[1]
