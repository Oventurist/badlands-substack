# Emma-Jo Morris

Emma-Jo Morris is an American journalist, an editor at [[breitbart|Breitbart]] and formerly an editor at the [[new-york-post|New York Post]], where she was involved in the paper's October 2020 reporting on the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]].[1]

## Testimony before the House Weaponization Committee

Morris testified before the House Weaponization Committee about the censorship of the Hunter Biden laptop story, describing the coordinated nature of the social media blackout imposed on the reporting.[1] Her testimony addressed the potential collusion between federal agencies and media outlets — including [[politico|Politico]] — to derail the story by framing it as "Russian disinformation" immediately before the 2020 election.[1]

## Context

Reporting on the testimony by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] placed the episode within the wider pattern of mass censorship in 2020–2022, in which government used Big Tech as, in its phrasing, a "corporate hitman" against free-speech rights.[1] The outlet cited the favorable spin given to Black Lives Matter riots, the attempted deplatforming of Zero Hedge itself over its coverage of COVID-19's potential Wuhan lab origins, and the erasure of the Hunter Biden laptop from public view, arguing that social media had functioned as a tool for controlling public perception rather than as a means of free communication.[1] ZeroHedge noted that congressional investigation had since established that [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] organized the suppression of the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]].[1]

Commenting for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the censorship record to illustrate his framing of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] as a [[system-of-systems|System of Systems]].[1]

## Weaponization subcommittee testimony (2023)

Morris, the Breitbart journalist who in 2020 first revealed the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] story, testified in July 2023 before the House [[house-weaponization-committee|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] alongside [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]].[2]
