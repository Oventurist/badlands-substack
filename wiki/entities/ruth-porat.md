# Ruth Porat

Ruth Porat is the senior vice president and chief financial officer of Alphabet and Google, and is named by Badlands Media as a member of the Board of Directors of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" cites her board seat, together with those of [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]] and [[laurene-powell-jobs|Laurene Powell Jobs]], as illustrating the Council's concentration of corporate and financial power, noting that Google is also among the CFR's corporate members.[1]
