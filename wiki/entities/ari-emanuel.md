# Ari Emanuel

Ari Emanuel is the Hollywood talent executive who heads WME, the talent agency described in Badlands coverage as representing a large share of cable news on-air personalities.[1] He is the brother of [[rahm-emanuel|Rahm Emanuel]], the former Obama White House chief of staff and Biden ambassador to Japan hired by [[cnn|CNN]] as a global affairs commentator in February 2025.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In commenting on the CNN hire, [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] raised the possibility that Rahm Emanuel had become a media client at his brother's agency, arguing that talent-agency representation is the ordinary condition of cable news commentators and suggesting an audit of how many Fox News personalities are Ari Emanuel's clients.[1] The remark reflects a recurring Badlands theme that ostensibly adversarial media brands are staffed through a small number of shared commercial gatekeepers.[1]

## Endeavor and the 2028 Olympics

Badlands contributor Chris Paul identified Emanuel as the head of [[endeavor|Endeavor]] in March 2025 commentary on executive Mark Shapiro's prediction that the [[2028-los-angeles-olympics|2028 Los Angeles Olympics]] would become "Donald Trump's Olympics."[2] The commentary noted that his brother [[rahm-emanuel|Rahm Emanuel]] served as Barack Obama's Chief of Staff and later Ambassador to Japan, that his brother [[ezekiel-emanuel|Ezekiel Emanuel]] is a physician who has argued people should die at 75 for the good of everyone, and that their father belonged to the [[irgun|Irgun]].[2]

## WWE–UFC merger

In April 2023, Emanuel was named chief executive of both [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]] and the new publicly traded company formed by merging [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] with the [[ufc|UFC]].[3] Endeavor took 51 percent of the combined firm, which valued WWE at $9.3 billion and UFC at $12.1 billion.[3] In an interview aired on CNBC's "Squawk on the Street," Emanuel said of the combination, "This is going to be UFC 2.0."[3] He led the company alongside executive chairman [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]], president and chief operating officer [[mark-shapiro|Mark Shapiro]], UFC president [[dana-white|Dana White]], and WWE business president [[nick-khan|Nick Khan]].[3]
