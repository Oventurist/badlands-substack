# Matthew Kacsmaryk

Matthew Kacsmaryk is an American jurist who, before his appointment to the federal bench, served as vice-president of the Fort Worth chapter of the [[federalist-society|Federalist Society]].[1]

## Federalist Society role

In the second year of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first presidency, Kacsmaryk — described as a young lawyer with crisply shorn blond hair — approached the podium at a gathering of Texas Federalist Society members and, as chapter vice-president, had the honor of presenting the first speaker of the evening, Fifth Circuit Judge Edith Jones.[1] Jones went on to introduce Supreme Court Justice [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]] and to credit [[leonard-leo|Leonard Leo]] and the Society for the quality of Trump's judicial appointments to the Fifth Circuit.[1] The episode is cited as an illustration of the tight network binding the conservative legal movement's activists, appellate judges and Supreme Court justices — the same network now shaping litigation that could bankrupt [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[1]

## V-safe disclosure orders

Kacsmaryk, a U.S. District Judge appointed by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], ordered the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] to release data from [[v-safe]], the text-message system the agency created to monitor possible side effects of COVID-19 vaccines.[2] Having earlier compelled release of the topline check-the-box data — which showed nearly 8 percent of the system's 10 million users required medical attention or hospital care after vaccination — he ordered the agency in January 2024 to disclose the free-text entries in which individuals described their experiences in their own words.[2] He dismissed the government's argument that processing the responses and redacting sensitive information would require too much work.[2] The resulting release comprised roughly 780,000 previously hidden reports describing facial paralysis, heart inflammation, miscarriages and seizures following vaccination.[2]
