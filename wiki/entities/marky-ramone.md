# Marky Ramone

Marky Ramone is the drummer of [[the-ramones]] and, as of the Badlands Media account, the band's last surviving member.[1] In an interview with [[newsmax]] he described the political arguments that took place between guitarist [[johnny-ramone|Johnny Ramone]], a conservative, and singer Joey Ramone, a liberal, while the band toured the country in a Ford Econoline.[1]

Marky emphasized that the band members — none of whom were related despite the shared stage surname — prized the ability to argue openly: "That's the country we live in, thank God, and we're able to do that. So I respect people's politics."[1] Badlands Media presents this as the older [[punk-rock]] ethic of free thought and debate, contrasted with what the article calls modern pop-punk's mob conformity and suppression of dissent.[1]
