# Asaad Al Shaibani

Asaad Al Shaibani is the Foreign Minister of [[syria|Syria]].[1]

## August 2025 warning to Israel

Speaking from Ankara on August 13, 2025 alongside his Turkish counterpart, Al Shaibani joined [[turkey|Turkey]] in warning [[israel|Israel]] not to stir up chaos in Syria and demanded an end to all external interventions aimed at destabilising the war-torn country.[1]

"We are also confronting multiple foreign interventions, both direct and indirect... (that) push the country toward sectarian and regional strife," he said, declining to give details but warning against "any reckless attempts to exploit events here."[1]

Al Shaibani also said Syria is committed to holding accountable those responsible for violations in the recent deadly violence that gripped the southwestern [[druze|Druze]]-majority province of Sweida, and reiterated Damascus's assurance that the Druze community is part of Syria and that their protection is the responsibility of the state.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the statements as confirmation of an anticipated Turkey–Israel confrontation in Syria, predicting that [[russia|Russia]] would intervene on Turkey's behalf and speculating about a return of Bashar al-Assad.[1]
