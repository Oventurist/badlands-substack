# Georgia (country)

Georgia is a country in the South Caucasus whose drift away from Western institutions was described in August 2024 as the clearest case of a state NATO and the West were losing to [[russia|Russia]].[1] Across the country the gold-starred blue flags of the [[european-union|European Union]] fly above everything from government ministries to tiny local police stations, appearing almost as ubiquitous as the red and white national flag with its cross of St. George.[1]

## Geopolitical position

Despite enduring pro-Western symbolism and overwhelming popular support for joining the EU and [[nato|NATO]], Newsweek reported that it was President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s Russia that was in the ascendant as the Georgian government's relationship with the United States and other Western powers deteriorated rapidly.[1] While Western countries had trumpeted the way Russia's war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] strengthened solidarity and brought Finland and Sweden into the NATO camp, once-aspiring member Georgia appeared to be heading in the opposite direction.[1]

Georgia's population of 3.7 million is only one-third that of its namesake U.S. state, and the Caucasus is not at the top of U.S. concerns at a time of wars in Ukraine and the Middle East.[1] The region is nevertheless strategically important: it lies on the southern border of Russia and to the north of Iran, and serves as a transit route for energy pipelines.[1] Whether the West is able to keep Georgia within its own zone of influence was said to be closely watched by countries weighing their options in a world in which the U.S. has lost the dominance it held at the end of the Cold War.[1]

Pro-Western opponents of Georgia's government described what they believed was a hybrid war for control by Russia, and feared that an election in October 2024 could be the last chance to reverse the shift.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that CIA obsession with controlling a country of roughly 3.5 million people makes sense only when viewed against the map of the [[north-south-transport-corridor|North-South Transportation Corridor]], the trade route being established through the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]], which he characterised as an eastern counterpart to NATO minus unipolarity, high fashion and Nazi ideology.[1] On that reading Georgia is a strategic chokepoint of the new trade route, threatening the unipolar world order.[1]

He linked this to the reported threat by EU officials against Georgian Prime Minister [[irakli-kobakhidze|Irakli Kobakhidze]], who was said to have been warned he could meet "the same fate" as Slovakian Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] just a week after Fico was shot five times in the chest — a remark that EU commissioner [[oliver-varhelyi|Olivér Várhelyi]] later insisted had been taken out of context.[1]
