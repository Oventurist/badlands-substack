# Khawaja Asif

**Khawaja Asif** is the Defence Minister of [[pakistan|Pakistan]].

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, remarks by Asif are the subject of criticism from U.S. Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]], whose comments about Asif and Pakistanis generally are highlighted by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]. The commentary contrasts Graham's hostility with the warm mutual praise between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Pakistani leadership, and situates the exchange within the wider dispute over the reported U.S.–[[iran|Iran]] negotiations and [[israel|Israeli]] objections to them.
