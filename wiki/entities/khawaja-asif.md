# Khawaja Asif

Khawaja Asif is Pakistan's Defence Minister.[1]

Asif figures in Badlands coverage of the Iran negotiations as the subject of remarks by Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]], who criticized Asif and Pakistanis in general in a post on X.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] contrasted Graham's hostility with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s consistently high praise for the Pakistani leadership and their reciprocal expressions of admiration for Trump.[1]

## "Jihad was created by the West" (April 2025)

Speaking to RT in April 2025, Asif claimed that [[pakistan|Pakistan]] had been a victim of regional terrorism stemming from the policies of Western governments, particularly the United States, dating back decades.[2] He called it a mistake for the country's past rulers to have joined the Soviet-Afghan war in the late 1980s and to have made Pakistan a platform for training and indoctrinating jihadists on behalf of the West, describing his country as a "springboard" for that conflict.[2]

"The introduction of jihad, which was invented by the West, changed the country's ethos and led to its current issues. The whole ethos of the society was changed to support the jihad," Asif said, adding that during the Afghan war Islamabad "supplied all sorts of help" to the United States.[2] He noted that after the September 11 attacks Pakistan again "joined the coalition," with supplies and facilities flowing from Pakistani soil, but concluded that "Both these wars, in my humble opinion, were not our wars."[2]

Asif argued that Pakistan continues to bear the consequences of those policies: "We suffered a lot and the United States abandoned us around '89 or '90. [They] went away and we were left high and dry."[2] He added that the country's security situation deteriorated further after the disastrous U.S. withdrawal from Afghanistan in 2021.[2]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry read the remarks as corroborating the view that the [[state-department|US State Department]] and [[cia|CIA]] function as the world's foremost terrorist financiers, creating a boogeyman to justify their own existence in a Hegelian dialectic.[2]
