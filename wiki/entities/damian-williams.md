# Damian Williams

Damian Williams served as United States Attorney for the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]], the office that brought the criminal fraud case against FTX founder [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]].[1]

## Role in the Bankman-Fried prosecution

Commentary in the February 16, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] reported that Williams had obtained approval of his request to push back both civil trials arising from the [[ftx|FTX]] collapse during the criminal proceedings, without prejudice, allowing the Justice Department to complete its criminal case against Bankman-Fried without overlapping litigation.[1] Prosecutors noted that all three cases would be weighed heavily on the same or similar evidence.[1] Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] argued that substantial corruption remained to be uncovered in what he described as fraudulent slush funding leading to the Democratic National Committee's doorstep.[1]

## Voronchenko sanctions-evasion indictment (February 2023)

Williams announced, alongside Task Force KleptoCapture director Andrew C. Adams and senior agents of [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] and the [[fbi|FBI]], the unsealing of an indictment charging [[vladimir-voronchenko|Vladimir Voronchenko]] with sanctions evasion and money laundering.[2] The indictment alleged that Voronchenko made more than $4 million in U.S. dollar payments to maintain four American properties owned by the sanctioned Russian oligarch [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]] and attempted to sell two of them, and further charged him with contempt of court for fleeing the country after receiving a grand jury subpoena.[2]
