# Ohio Organizing Collaborative

The Ohio Organizing Collaborative is a statewide network of community, labor, and faith organizations engaged in civic engagement and voter registration work in Ohio.

## Center of a widening federal probe

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief identifies the Ohio Organizing Collaborative as the nucleus of a federal investigation into voter mobilization organizations, reporting that a subpoena was issued to the national group [[america-votes|America Votes]] as part of an inquiry "tied to" the Collaborative. A former director and board member is quoted describing the probe's scope as extensive and wide-ranging. The Brief pairs this development with the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] operation on [[skid-row|Skid Row]] to argue that federal scrutiny of registration operations has moved beyond isolated cases.
