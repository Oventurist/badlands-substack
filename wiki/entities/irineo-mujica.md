# Irineo Mujica

Irineo Mujica is a migrant-rights activist and caravan organizer who has helped coordinate large groups of migrants travelling north through Mexico toward the United States.[1]

## October 2023 caravan

Mujica was identified as one of the organizers of a caravan that departed the city of Tapachula, in the southern Mexican state of Chiapas near the Guatemalan border, on Monday, October 30, 2023.[1] Chiapas state officials placed the size of the group at roughly 3,500 people, while Mujica said the caravan numbered around 5,000.[1] According to Mujica, most participants came from Cuba, El Salvador, Guatemala, Haiti, Honduras and Venezuela.[1] The migrants, escorted by civil protection officials and ambulances, walked along a coastal highway and planned to spend their first night in the municipality of Huehuetan, about 16 miles (25 km) north of their starting point.[1]

The departure occurred as the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] faced renewed political pressure over illegal crossings at the southern border ahead of the 2024 election.[1]
