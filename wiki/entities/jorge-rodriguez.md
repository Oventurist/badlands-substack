# Jorge Rodríguez

Jorge Rodríguez is a senior Venezuelan political figure and the brother of acting Venezuelan President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]].[1]

He was the Venezuelan interlocutor in U.S. backchannel diplomacy across both Trump administrations. U.S. envoy [[richard-grenell|Ric Grenell]] met him in Mexico City in late 2020, a contact reported at the time as an attempt to secretly negotiate an exit for [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] — an interpretation Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] says he is unconvinced by.[1] Grenell reunited with Rodríguez in January 2025, after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was sworn in for his second term, when Grenell traveled to Caracas to meet Maduro and bring home six imprisoned Americans.[1]

## Amnesty bill and Stolen Elections (February 2026)

In February 2026 Rodríguez headed the second debate through which the [[national-assembly-of-venezuela|National Assembly]] approved a limited [[venezuela-amnesty-law|amnesty law]], reportedly releasing hundreds of political protestors from Venezuelan prisons while omitting homicide and other violent crimes.[2] He is the brother of President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]], whose partnership with the United States President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] praised during that week's State of the Union address.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] identified Rodríguez as a central figure in [[ralph-pezzullo|Ralph Pezzullo]]'s book *Stolen Elections: The Takedown of Democracies Worldwide*, read as Book 5 of the Badlands Book Club, describing him as "alleged to be waist-deep in stealing all the elections."[2] She questioned the coherence of a narrative in which a supposedly overthrown regime's next-in-line members run the new government and release the political prisoners they themselves had jailed, and asked what those prisoners would have to say.[2]
