# Jorge Rodríguez

Jorge Rodríguez is a senior Venezuelan political figure and the brother of [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]], who became acting President of Venezuela after the January 3, 2026 capture of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. He appears in the January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]] as the Venezuelan interlocutor in a series of American backchannel contacts spanning both Trump administrations.

According to [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s commentary, [[richard-grenell|Ric Grenell]] met Rodríguez in Mexico City in late 2020. The meeting was reported at the time by PJ Media as an attempt by a Trump ambassador to secretly negotiate Maduro's exit, though Ghost writes that he is "unconvinced that was the real intent." Grenell reunited with Rodríguez in January 2025, after [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s second inauguration, when he travelled to Caracas to meet Maduro and bring home six imprisoned Americans. Ghost cites this continuity as evidence of a long-running, deliberate American channel to the Venezuelan government.
