# Rushingbrook Children's Choir

The **Rushingbrook Children's Choir** is a children's choral group based in Greenville, South Carolina, which drew national attention in June 2023 after it was prevented from singing the national anthem inside the United States Capitol.[1]

## Capitol incident

The choir had traveled to Washington, D.C. to perform a capella renditions of American patriotic songs, including the national anthem, at the Capitol Building.[1] [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]], together with a congressional staffer, shut the performance down.[1] According to the choir's director, Rasbach, he was told that the performance of national songs constituted a "demonstration" and was therefore not permitted inside the Capitol.[1]

## Trump invitation and reaction

Former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] invited the choir to sing for him and the attendees at his upcoming South Carolina rally, and called the prohibition "a shame."[1] [[human-events|Human Events]], which reported the story, described the episode as "a stain on our national character and the people's House."[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] under the byline [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] argued that "patriotism was likened to terrorism for young kids who just wanted to show respect for our Country," described the Capitol Police account as absurd and internally contradictory, and contended that the prohibition was contrived on political grounds and had been predetermined.[1] The commentary further argued that songs on themes such as gender equality or transgender rights would have provoked no objection.[1]
