# Torridon Group

The Torridon Group is a Virginia-based law and consulting firm founded by former Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and former Facebook general counsel [[ted-ullyot|Ted Ullyot]].[1]

## Expansion in January 2024

In January 2024 the firm announced that it was adding a team of new lawyers drawn heavily from the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, including former White House counsel [[pat-cipollone|Pat Cipollone]] and former deputy White House counsels [[pat-philbin|Pat Philbin]] and [[kate-todd|Kate Todd]].[1] Tara Helfman and John Coghlan joined as partners, Chase Harrington and Kyle West as associates, and former White House counsel [[fred-fielding|Fred Fielding]] and former acting Drug Enforcement Administration head [[tim-shea|Tim Shea]] as of counsel.[1]

Barr told Axios that the firm would open a Washington office now that it was equipped to handle "every facet of litigation, regulatory and enforcement proceedings, investigations, crisis management, and strategic counseling," while describing Torridon as still a "smaller boutique firm that can give individual attention at reasonable rates."[1] He argued that its lawyers combined public and private experience suited to complicated, high-stakes matters.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the hiring spree as an indication that "Trump Team 2.0 is going to look a lot like Trump Team 1.0," treating the reassembly of former administration lawyers as an "Actual" that cut against prevailing narratives on both sides of the information war.[1]
