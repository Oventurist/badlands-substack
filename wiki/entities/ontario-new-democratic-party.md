# Ontario New Democratic Party

The Ontario New Democratic Party (Ontario NDP) is the provincial social-democratic party in [[ontario|Ontario]], [[canada|Canada]]. It features in Badlands Media coverage of Canadian gender politics as the sponsor of a proposed law restricting protest at drag performances.[1]

## Public safety zones proposal

On Tuesday, April 4, 2023, the Ontario NDP introduced legislation that would make demonstrations during [[drag-queen-story-hour|drag queen story hour]] illegal, presented publicly as a call for public safety zones for drag performances and the LGBTQ2S community.[1] A February 2024 essay by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued the party's announcement language carefully dehumanized every protester while neglecting the principal concern of parents about age-inappropriate content, and continued a pattern of [[lawfare|lawfare]] criminalizing dissent as hate.[1]

## Uniparty critique

Deschamps characterized the measure as political theatre designed to suggest the NDP was pushing back against conservatives, and argued it is absurd to treat [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]], [[parental-rights|parental rights]], and [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative care]] as sites of genuine conflict among Ontario's and Canada's three main parties, since all three contributed to the creation of gender-identity law — a [[uniparty|Uniparty]] problem in his framing.[1]
