# Gerald Connolly

Gerald Connolly is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Northern Virginia who serves on the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[1]

## IRS whistleblower hearing (July 2023)

Connolly was identified as one of several Democrats who used their speaking time at the committee's July 19, 2023 hearing with [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] and [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]] to invoke former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] rather than address the substance of the testimony.[1] Critics of the panel's minority said the pattern — shared with Baltimore's [[kweisi-mfume|Kweisi Mfume]] and Florida's [[maxwell-frost|Maxwell Frost]] — reflected an inability to mount an effective counterargument.[1]
