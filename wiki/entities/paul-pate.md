# Paul Pate

Paul Pate is a Republican politician serving as Secretary of State of Iowa, the state's chief election official.[1]

## 2024 noncitizen voter list

On October 22, 2024, after early voting had already begun, Pate instructed Iowa county auditors to follow a list drawn from Iowa Department of Transportation records identifying 2,176 registered voters who might not be U.S. citizens.[1] Pate said he was working to ensure the integrity of the election.[1]

The [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] and a group of naturalized citizens sued him, arguing that the twelve-year-old list was based on outdated information and wrongly kept "numerous naturalized U.S. citizens" from voting with a regular ballot.[1] In the [[iowa-noncitizen-voter-challenge-ruling|resulting ruling]], U.S. District Judge [[stephen-locher|Stephen Locher]] declined to block the challenges, writing that whatever concerns the plaintiffs had about the nature and timing of "Secretary Pate's letter," it would not be appropriate to grant relief that effectively forced local officials to let ineligible voters vote.[1]
