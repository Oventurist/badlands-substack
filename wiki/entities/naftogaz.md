# Naftogaz

Naftogaz is Ukraine's state-owned oil and gas company. The Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" cites it as the counterparty to $10 billion deals signed by [[chevron]], offered as the precedent for Western energy majors' ambitions in [[iran]].

The article uses the Ukrainian arrangement to explain the interest of Chevron, [[exxonmobil|Exxon]], [[bp]] and [[shell]] in sanctions relief: "they basically wanted to do the same deal, only on a much larger scale, with Iran." As funders of the [[atlantic-council]], the article argues, these firms could lobby the [[state-department]], the [[cia]] and the [[pentagon]] for policies that would open the Iranian market.
