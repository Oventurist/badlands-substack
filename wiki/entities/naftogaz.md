# Naftogaz

Naftogaz is Ukraine's state energy company. Its former chief executive, [[sergii-koretskyi|Sergii Koretskyi]], was confirmed as Prime Minister of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] by a 289–22 parliamentary vote in July 2026.[1] President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] cited Koretskyi's energy-sector experience as the decisive qualification as Ukraine prepared for another winter under continued Russian attacks on its infrastructure.[1]
