# Paul Young

Paul Young is the mayor of Memphis, Tennessee, who in June 2025 was the apparent target of an attempted kidnapping.[1]

## Attempted kidnapping

Memphis police said they believed 25-year-old [[trenton-abston|Trenton Abston]] was targeting Young when he was seen in the mayor's neighborhood carrying a Taser, rope and duct tape.[1] Abston was charged with attempted kidnapping, stalking and aggravated criminal trespassing; officers recovered a taser, gloves, rope and duct tape from his vehicle at the time of arrest.[1] He was held in the Shelby County Jail with no bail or bond listed.[1]
