# Elliott Abrams

**Elliott Abrams** is an American Middle East policy expert listed by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] among the lesser-known members of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] (PNAC).[1]

## Role in PNAC

In DeLarme's November 2025 essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], Abrams is grouped with [[norman-podhoretz|Norman Podhoretz]] as PNAC figures who linked American power to [[israel|Israel]]'s stability.[1] The essay places both within the wider cohort of PNAC members — including [[william-kristol|William Kristol]], [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]], [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[richard-perle|Richard Perle]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] and [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] — whom DeLarme describes as the primary architects of the U.S.-led forever wars of the 1990s and 2000s, responsible for the invasion of Iraq, the war in Afghanistan, the War on Terror doctrine and diplomatic cover for Israeli military operations.[1]

## Significance in the essay's argument

Abrams's inclusion supports DeLarme's contention that [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] and [[zionism|Zionism]] are inseparable in practice, and that the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's apparent outward alignment with these figures is a principal driver of the fracture in the [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]
