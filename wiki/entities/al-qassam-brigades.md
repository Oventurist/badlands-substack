# Al-Qassam Brigades

The Al-Qassam Brigades are the armed wing of [[hamas|Hamas]] in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## October 2023 Khan Younis ambush

In late October 2023, the Al-Qassam Brigades announced that their fighters had destroyed two Israeli military bulldozers and a tank in an ambush, as the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] conducted what it described as "limited" incursions into the strip.[1] The engagement was reported as the first direct ground clash between the two sides inside Gaza during the war.[1]

"The soldiers of the Zionist force that fell into the Khan Younis ambush left their vehicles and fled east of the fence on foot," the Hamas wing said on social media.[1] The IDF did not confirm the claims, stating only that "shots were fired at IDF soldiers operating west of the Gaza Strip security fence, in the area of Kissufim" and that "An IDF tank struck the terrorist cell who fired at the soldiers" — while confirming that its forces were operating inside Gaza at the time.[1] A subsequent IDF statement cited by the *Times of Israel* appeared to corroborate the Hamas account of repelling the tank and bulldozers.[1] Commentators noted that the IDF had begun taking casualties before the major ground offensive had even begun.[1]
