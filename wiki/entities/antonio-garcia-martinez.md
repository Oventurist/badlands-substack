# Antonio Garcia-Martinez

Antonio Garcia-Martinez is a Cuban American author and entrepreneur cited repeatedly as a primary commentator in the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1," which examines [[socialism|socialism]] in [[cuba|Cuba]].[1]

## Account of the Cuban protests

According to Garcia-Martinez, the wave of demonstrations against the Cuban government began when a group of disgruntled citizens started shouting at a Communist Party headquarters, chanting pro-freedom sentiments, criticizing the state, and waving American flags.[1] He disputes the claim, attributed in the essay to the mainstream press, that the unrest was driven mainly by demand for COVID-19 vaccines.[1]

## Rebuttals to socialist praise of Cuba

Responding to the assertion by [[nikole-hannah-jones|Nikole Hannah-Jones]] that "equality in Cuba is because of socialism," Garcia-Martinez quipped, "Sure, if you make everyone poor, then indeed inequality would be lower."[1] He also rejects the frequent citation of Cuba's literacy program as a socialist achievement, noting that Cuba's literacy rate was already higher than its neighbors well before the revolution.[1]

On the question of the [[cuban-embargo|United States embargo]], Garcia-Martinez states that virtually none of the protesters on the ground mentioned or appeared to care about it, pointing out that Cuba trades freely with the rest of the world and that the absence of US trade — apart from a brief opening during the Obama era — has been the standard state of Cuban-American relations for decades.[1]
