# Taiwan Affairs Office

The Taiwan Affairs Office is the body of the Chinese state responsible for articulating and administering Beijing's policy toward [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1] In late December 2023, weeks before Taiwan's January 2024 elections, the office served as the channel for fresh Chinese threats of economic retaliation against the island.[1]

Spokesman Chen Binhua addressed a message to Taiwan's ruling [[democratic-progressive-party|Democratic Progressive Party]], stating that "if the DPP authorities are determined to persevere, continue to stubbornly adhere to their Taiwan independence position, and refuse to repent, we support the relevant departments taking further measures in accordance with the regulations."[1] The remarks accompanied Chinese warnings of further trade sanctions on Taiwan should the ruling party persist in supporting independence.[1]

The statements came amid broader pressure associated with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s vow to use "any means" to prevent Taiwan from seceding, and formed part of the wider pattern of [[china-taiwan-military-pressure|Chinese pressure on Taiwan]] documented through 2023.[1]
