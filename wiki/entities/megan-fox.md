# Megan Fox

**Megan Fox** is an American actress mentioned in Badlands Media commentary on rituals allegedly attached to success in the entertainment industry.[1]

## Badlands claim

Contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that Fox has three transgender children and asked rhetorically, "What are the odds?"[1] The remark appears within his argument that some entertainers are required to sacrifice a loved one to secure success while others, he alleged, transition their children as a substitute.[1] Carlson placed this claim alongside his wider discussion of the [[music-industry-initiation-rituals|music industry's initiation rituals]] and the careers of performers whose success he considered disproportionate to their talent.[1]
