# Rakhman Mustafaev

Rakhman Mustafaev is Azerbaijan's ambassador to Russia. On June 30, 2025, he was summoned by Russia's Foreign Ministry following the arrest of three Russian journalists in Baku.[1]

## 2025 summons

Two senior editors with [[sputnik|Sputnik]] media and one journalist with the Ruptly video news agency were detained by Azerbaijani authorities, prompting Moscow's protest.[1] Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] told reporters that "in light of Baku's hostile actions and the unlawful detention of Russian journalists… the ambassador of the Azerbaijani Republic has been summoned to the Foreign Ministry," and expressed concern that Russian diplomats had been unable to contact the detained journalists, who are Russian citizens.[1]

The summons came amid a broader deterioration in Russian-Azerbaijani relations, following a police raid in the Russian city of Yekaterinburg that targeted the local Azerbaijani diaspora.[1]
