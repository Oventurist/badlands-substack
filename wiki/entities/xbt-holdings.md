# XBT Holdings

XBT Holdings SA is a Cyprus-based web-services company that operated the Webzilla internet hosting business and that is owned by the Russian entrepreneur Aleksej Gubarev.[1]

## Connection to the Steele Dossier

In 2016 [[olga-galkina|Olga Galkina]] — a friend of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] since their schooldays together in Perm — was working for XBT Holdings in Cyprus.[1] Gubarev is named in the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]] as a "hacking expert," and Galkina was the source of the dossier claim that Webzilla played a key role in the hacking of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] in early 2016.[1]

Galkina was also the source for the erroneous allegation that [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]], one of Donald Trump's lawyers, met secretly with Russian intelligence officials in Prague in the summer of 2016 to discuss paying hackers and covering up the operation.[1] All parties involved denied the allegations, and the Mueller Special Counsel found no evidence supporting them.[1] Galkina became known within the dossier's sourcing chain as "Source 3," passing her claims to Danchenko, who relayed them to [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]].[1] [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] travelled to Cyprus to interview Galkina, but was not present when questions about her were put to Danchenko, which limited how far [[john-durham|John Durham]] could pursue the subject with him at trial.[1]
