# Fulton County

Fulton County is the most populous county in Georgia, encompassing most of Atlanta, and the jurisdiction at the centre of long-running disputes over the conduct of the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] in that state.

## Federal investigation

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] reports that, per court documents filed on February 10, 2026 by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], the [[fbi|FBI]] is actively investigating potential improprieties in Fulton County's handling of 2020 ballots. Search-warrant affidavits by Special Agent [[hugh-evans|Hugh Evans]] of the Atlanta Field Office identify missing ballot images, inconsistent totals across recounts, chain-of-custody documentation problems, duplicate ballot images, and absentee ballots that lack the creases expected of ballots folded and mailed in envelopes. Investigators sought access to the physical ballots to determine whether the problems were administrative or intentional.

In January 2026, agents executed court-authorised search warrants at the Fulton County Election Hub and Operation Center to seize relevant materials — the episode documented in the corpus as the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County election raid]]. Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] was present at the president's request.

## Litigation posture

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] enumerates the record categories now in DOJ custody: missing ballot images from the original count and recount, duplicate ballots in both, inconsistent totals reported at different times, missing SHA hash files used to verify ballot-image integrity, potential tabulator-tape manipulation such as inconsistency between machines' reported votes and protective counter data, and ballots with broken security seals and unexplained handling. She observes that the existence of some records is in doubt and that failure to retain records multiple courts ordered preserved would damage the county's defences. In her assessment Fulton County's Fourth Amendment lawsuit against the federal seizure is "dead," reporting that a legal AI model fed the county's complaint, Perdue's testimony and the unsealed warrant rated Fulton's chances of winning at "less than zero."

Fulton County is also the jurisdiction whose district attorney, [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]], brought the Georgia RICO prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and co-defendants.
