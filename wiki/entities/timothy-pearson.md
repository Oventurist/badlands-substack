# Timothy Pearson

Timothy Pearson was a senior adviser for public safety in the administration of New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]], and one of the mayor's most trusted aides before his forced departure amid a widening set of federal corruption investigations.[1]

## Resignation

Pearson resigned from his post on Monday, September 30, 2024, weeks after federal agents seized his cell phone in a mushrooming investigation into the mayor's inner circle — an investigation that also produced federal bribery charges against Adams himself.[1] His resignation, submitted in a letter obtained by [[politico|POLITICO]], took effect on Friday, October 4, 2024.[1] The role had carried a salary of $257,374, among the highest in the administration.[1]

The letter did not acknowledge the investigations surrounding him; Pearson instead wrote that he had "decided to focus on family, self-care, and new endeavors."[1] Adams praised Pearson in a brief statement issued through his press office.[1] The departure was reported as a victory for Adams advisers who had spent weeks pressuring the mayor to cut ties with him.[1]

## Role and portfolio

Pearson's portfolio was unusually broad for a single adviser. He helped Adams set up the upper ranks of the [[nypd|New York City Police Department]], approved contracts with outside firms for migrant shelters, and ran a small office Adams created to improve municipal services.[1] Commentary in the Badlands News Brief argued that the concentration of such disparate functions — and their attendant networks — in one office made Pearson a hub for third-party relationships spanning government, corporate and non-governmental organizations, and characterized the affair as exposing a "festering wound" of public-private partnerships.[1]

Pearson also advised Adams on new technology. POLITICO reported that he pushed the city fire department to sign a contract with the technology company [[remark-holdings|Remark Holdings]] while maintaining a close personal relationship with a consultant for the firm.[1]

## Allegations

Beyond the contracting questions, Pearson faced a set of sexual misconduct allegations, which Badlands commentary compared to the accusations that preceded the resignation of former Governor [[andrew-cuomo|Andrew Cuomo]].[1]
