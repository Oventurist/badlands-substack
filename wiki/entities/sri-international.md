# SRI International

SRI International is an American nonprofit scientific research institute. Badlands Media identifies it as one of the organizations participating in the intelligence community's [[smart-epants|SMART ePANTS]] computerized clothing program.[1]

SRI International, the [[massachusetts-institute-of-technology|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] and the firm Areté received undisclosed amounts to develop the technology, according to reporting in [[the-intercept|The Intercept]], while the larger publicly disclosed contracts — totaling more than $22 million — went to [[nautilus-defense|Nautilus Defense]] and [[leidos|Leidos, Inc.]] under [[iarpa|IARPA]] and the Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific.[1]
