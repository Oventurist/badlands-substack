# Thom Tillis

Thom Tillis is a Republican United States Senator from North Carolina, described in the Badlands corpus as a retiring member whose Senate career is ending and who has consequently broken sharply with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.

## Confrontation with Kristi Noem

At the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026, covered in the Badlands Brief of March 4, Tillis called for the resignation of Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], citing misplaced departmental priorities in the wake of the $220 million advertising campaign featuring Noem and a $143 million no-bid contract. According to the commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], Tillis delivered the most fiery Republican remarks of the hearing, telling the Secretary that he was giving her a performance review during which she would not be permitted to respond. His remarks repeatedly included criticism of Trump allies such as [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] and [[corey-lewandowski|Corey Lewandowski]]. The Brief attributes his combativeness to the fact that his Senate career is over and to unresolved conflict with the President following his earlier confrontation with Trump.
