# Henrico Doctors' Hospital

Henrico Doctors' Hospital is a hospital in Henrico County, Virginia, operated by HCA Healthcare, whose neonatal intensive care unit became the subject of a criminal investigation at the end of 2024.[1]

## NICU fracture investigation

In late November and December 2024 three babies in the hospital's NICU were found to have unexplained fractures, prompting the hospital in late December to announce it was temporarily stopping admissions to the unit.[1] On January 2, 2025 Henrico police arrested NICU nurse [[erin-strotman|Erin Strotman]] on felony counts of malicious wounding and child abuse, with court records dating the alleged offenses to November 10, 2024.[1] A statement from HCA noted that the circumstances of the recent fractures were similar to incidents involving four different babies at the hospital in the summer of 2023, suggesting a longer pattern than the December closure implied.[1]

At least one affected family had Child Protective Services take their baby away on the mistaken assumption the parents were responsible for the injuries, a consequence Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] — whose own son had spent a week in the same NICU — described as unforgivable.[1]
