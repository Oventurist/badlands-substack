# Andrey Yermak

Andrey Yermak is the chief of staff and top aide to Ukrainian leader [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]], described in reporting as Zelensky's "right-hand man" and Kiev's principal negotiator in talks over ending the war with Russia.[1]

## Corruption allegations

In November 2025, journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] claimed in a post on X that the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] had for months withheld a completed story documenting Yermak's "personal corruption."[1] According to Carlson, Yermak "skimmed hundreds of millions in American tax dollars meant for Ukraine aid," and the Journal's editors possessed material capable of proving it but declined to publish.[1]

## Alleged motive for suppression

Carlson argued that the story was being suppressed because Yermak, in his role as Kiev's lead negotiator, was "leading efforts to scuttle" the US-drafted plan to end the Russia–Ukraine conflict.[1] He attributed the decision to the Murdoch family, owners of the Journal, whom he said wanted "to continue the war with Russia."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the episode as a dual exposure — of the Ukrainian government as "a bunch of bloodsucking parasites" and of the American media as complicit in those crimes — concluding with a single word: "Accelerate."[1]
