# Edward H. Harriman

**Edward H. Harriman** was an American railroad magnate and the father of the later world statesman [[averell-harriman|Averell Harriman]], mentioned in Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" as a beneficiary of Wall Street financing tied to the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]] orbit.[1]

## Union Pacific and the Schiff connection

DeLarme writes that it was [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]], head of [[kuhn-loeb-and-co|Kuhn, Loeb & Company]], who financed Harriman's purchase of the Union Pacific.[1] Both Schiff and Harriman, the article states, would go on to play important roles in the rise of communism in Russia.[1]

## The Yale and Skull and Bones line

Harriman's two sons attended Yale University, described in the article as a hotbed for high-society types who flirted with politics, finance and the occult; it was there that Averell Harriman was inducted into the Order of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]].[1] In the 1930s Averell's banking firm, W. A. Harriman and Company, merged with the private international firm Brown Brothers to create [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman and Company]], which employed as partner [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]], a fellow Bonesman and the father of George Bush Sr.[1]
