# Max Miller

Max Miller is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Ohio and a former Trump White House aide. In the Badlands corpus he appears as one of the elected officials pressing for criminal action against [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].

The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] circulated a Mediaite article documenting Miller's call for Carlson to be investigated and imprisoned on the theory that he had acted as an agent of [[iran|Iran]]. The Brief's commentary treats Miller's demand as one component of a broader pattern in which sitting members of [[congress|Congress]] urge prosecution of a domestic broadcaster for speech and contacts, and reads it alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s own threat of treason charges against media outlets as evidence of converging pressure on the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].
