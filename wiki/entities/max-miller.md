# Max Miller

Max Miller is a Republican member of the United States [[us-congress|Congress]] who in March 2026 called for the commentator [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] to be investigated and imprisoned if he had worked with [[iran|Iran]].[1]

Miller's remarks were reported by Mediaite under a headline invoking treason, and were amplified by talk-radio host [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]].[1] Badlands writers cited the episode as evidence that elected Republicans had joined activists and media figures in pressing for criminal exposure of a prominent critic of [[israel|Israel]], part of what they described as a broader campaign against the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]

## Protecting Americans in the IDF Act (2024)

In May 2024, Miller co-introduced the [[protecting-americans-in-the-idf-act|Protecting Americans in the IDF Act]] with Representative [[guy-reschenthaler|Guy Reschenthaler]], legislation that would extend U.S. uniformed-service employment protections to Americans serving in the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Defense Forces]].[2] Miller said that 'as our closest ally in the Middle East continues to defend itself against terror, many brave Americans have decided to lend a hand,' and that he was proud the legislation extended important protections to Americans who chose to risk their lives in the fight against terror.[2]
