# Society for Evidence Based Gender Medicine

The Society for Evidence Based Gender Medicine (SEGM) is an organisation that reviews and publishes analysis of the evidence base for paediatric gender medicine. In Badlands Media coverage it is cited as the source that documented England's abandonment of the [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative model]].[1]

On October 24, 2022, SEGM reported that "the previous presumption that gender dysphoric youth <18 need specialty 'transgender healthcare' has been supplanted by the developmentally-informed position that most need psychoeducation and psychotherapy," that eligibility determinations for medical interventions would be centralised, and that [[puberty-blockers|puberty blockers]] would be delivered only in research protocol settings.[1] SEGM further noted that England's abandonment of the affirmative model had been foreshadowed by the interim report of the [[cass-review|Cass Review]], which defined the "affirmative model" as "a model of gender healthcare that originated in the USA."[1]

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] used SEGM's reporting as the first of three international sources he argued should have decimated the narrative behind medically transitioning children in Canada and the United States, and also cited SEGM's coverage of a NICE systematic review finding poor-quality evidence, which he dated the beginning of the British reversal to 2020.[1]
