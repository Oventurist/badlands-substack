# Right Sector

Right Sector is a Ukrainian far-right nationalist movement and paramilitary formation that emerged during the 2014 upheaval in Ukraine and attracted foreign volunteers to its ranks.[1] The group features in coverage of Western involvement in Ukraine because of allegations that its formation and growth were assisted by American intelligence.[1]

In reporting relayed by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], journalist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] described Right Sector as "a creation of the [[cia|CIA]]" and said that Florida neo-Nazi [[kent-mclellan|Kent "Boneface" McLellan]] fled to Ukraine to join the group in 2014 after an FBI domestic-terrorism arrest.[1] Loomer treated McLellan's ability to travel abroad and fight in a foreign war as an indication of informant status with the [[fbi|FBI]] or CIA.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] placed Right Sector within a broader argument that the bulk of genuine neo-Nazi militancy is concentrated in Ukraine, where he alleged CIA-created proxy armies train such fighters while American tax dollars keep them armed.[1]
