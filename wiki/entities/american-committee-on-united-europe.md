# American Committee on United Europe

The American Committee on a United Europe was an organization formed by [[jozef-retinger|Józef Retinger]] to promote European unification with American backing, described in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 4" as a precursor to the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

According to DeLarme, Retinger formed the committee together with future [[cia|CIA]] Director and [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] member [[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]], then CFR Director George Franklin, CIA official Thomas Braden, and [[william-donovan|William Donovan]], the former chief of the CIA's predecessor, the [[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services]].[1] DeLarme adds that Donovan began his intelligence career as an operative of J. P. Morgan Jr. and was known as an "Anglophile," a supporter of close British-American relations.[1]

The committee's membership is central to DeLarme's argument that American intelligence stood behind the postwar European integration project and, subsequently, behind the Bilderberg conferences themselves.[1]
