# Dimitri Simes

Dimitri Simes (also rendered Dmitri Simes) is a Russian-born American author and foreign policy analyst who advised [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 presidential campaign and who, as of 2024, hosted a current affairs program on Russia's state-run Channel One.[1]

## FBI raid, August 2024

On August 13, 2024, agents of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] raided and searched Simes's home in Rappahannock County, Virginia.[1] The Bureau confirmed the search to the local Rappahannock News, which first reported the story.[1] Simes, whose name appeared more than 100 times in the 2019 Mueller report into Russian interference in the 2016 presidential election, said he was out of the country at the time, had not been notified in advance, and was unaware that he was the focus of any law enforcement investigation.[1] "I'm puzzled and concerned," he told the paper. "I have not seen a warrant. I was not contacted by any law enforcement or anyone else whatsoever."[1]

In an interview with the Russian government-owned outlet Sputnik News, Simes characterized the raid as "clearly ... an attempt to intimidate, not only somebody from Russia, but just anyone who goes against official policies and particularly against the deep state."[1] He added that he suspected the real purpose was not to interrogate or arrest him but "to make sure that I would not come back" to the United States.[1]

## Biography and connections

Born in Moscow, Simes emigrated to the United States in 1973.[1] He served as an informal foreign policy adviser to President [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]] before leading the Center for the National Interest for nearly three decades.[1] After meeting Trump's son-in-law [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] at a March 2016 luncheon held in honor of [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], Simes began providing the Trump campaign with informal counsel on foreign policy, including advice on a speech in which Trump envisioned greater cooperation with Russia.[1] In 2023 he moderated a conversation with Russian president [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] at the St Petersburg International Economic Forum, and in June 2024 he participated in a closed-door meeting with Putin, according to the state-owned news agency TASS.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called Simes "an interesting cat" and dwelt on the Nixon connection, noting that Trump and Nixon were long-time correspondents throughout the 1980s and speculating about what guidance a fellow persecuted Washington outsider might have offered — while suggesting that [[roger-stone|Roger Stone]] must fit into the connection somewhere.[1] He further wondered what Simes had told Kushner about Israel before Kushner's introduction to the Saudi crown prince and the crafting of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]], floating the possibility that Simes advised deliberately excluding Saudi Arabia and Palestine from the initial agreements so they could be offered later as a package deal.[1]

## August 2024 FBI search

The [[fbi|FBI]] searched Simes's home in early August 2024 as part of the Justice Department's investigation into Americans with ties to Russian state media in the run-up to the 2024 presidential election.[2] Simes, who is Russian-born, served as an adviser to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first presidential campaign in 2016 and hosts a weekly current affairs program on Russia's state-run Channel One.[2] Officials cited in reporting on the probe said additional searches were likely and that criminal charges had not been ruled out.[2]
