# Alexus Grynkewich

General Alexus Grynkewich is the commander of [[united-states-european-command|US European Command]].[1]

## Military-to-military dialogue with Russia

Following trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi on February 4–5, 2026 among delegations from the United States, [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and the Russian Federation, the United States and [[russia|Russia]] agreed to establish a military-to-military dialogue to be led by Grynkewich.[1] The channel had been suspended prior to the onset of the conflict in Ukraine and was described in the readout posted by Special Envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] as crucial to achieving and maintaining peace.[1]
