# Mike Turner

Mike Turner is a Republican member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Ohio, known for his focus on defense policy.[1]

## June 2026 procedural revolt

Turner was one of the Republicans who joined the late-June 2026 rebellion against House leadership that sank the chamber's procedural rule by a 224-198 vote and paralyzed floor business in the days before the July 4 recess.[1] The revolt drew in Reps. [[chip-roy|Chip Roy]], Andy Harris, [[anna-paulina-luna|Anna Paulina Luna]], and Victoria Spartz, and grew from frustration over the stalled [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]] as well as demands for amendment votes on defense policy and on pensions for Delphi auto parts retirees.[1]

The resulting impasse forced Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] and Majority Leader Steve Scalise to cancel scheduled votes, including work on the annual [[pentagon|Pentagon]] policy bill and [[state-department|State Department]] appropriations.[1]

## Hunter Biden laptop letters

As chairman of the [[house-intelligence-committee|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]], Turner joined [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] on 1 March 2023 in sending 29 letters to Central Intelligence officials who had publicly discredited the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] laptop story.[2] The letters noted that the officials had not responded to earlier interview requests and asked them to "comply promptly," schedule transcribed interviews, and produce outstanding material.[2] The effort followed acknowledgments of the laptop's authenticity by the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] and the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] in March 2022.[2]

### CIA COVID Discovery Team inquiry (September 2023)

Turner, as chairman of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, co-signed a 12 September 2023 letter with Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] to [[cia|CIA]] Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] seeking all documents, communications and pay records from the agency's COVID Discovery Team by 26 September.[2] The demand responded to whistleblower allegations that the agency had offered payments to analysts who concluded that COVID-19 likely emerged from a Wuhan laboratory, in exchange for changing their assessment.[2]

## Response to the Durham report

As chairman of the [[house-intelligence-committee|House Intelligence Committee]], Turner said shortly after the May 2023 release of the [[durham-report|Durham report]] that it outlined "serious errors and shortcomings" in the [[fbi|FBI]]'s handling of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] investigation.[3] His committee was scheduled to take testimony from Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] on Tuesday, June 20, 2023, with lawmakers expected to probe the report's conclusion that neither U.S. law enforcement nor the Intelligence Community possessed actual evidence of Trump–Russia collusion when the investigation began.[3]

## February 2024 national security threat warning

On Wednesday, February 14, 2024, Turner issued a cryptic public warning of a "serious national security threat" that he said might require help from "our allies" to deal with.[4] He cited unspecified "information" about the threat that he said all members of Congress had already been briefed on, and called on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to declassify it: "I am requesting that President Biden declassify all information relating to this threat so that Congress, the Administration, and our allies can openly discuss the actions necessary to respond to this threat."[4] Capitol Hill sources told ABC News the threat concerned the alleged [[russian-space-nuclear-weapon-program|Russian space nuclear weapon program]] — Russia seeking to place nuclear weapons in space for use against satellites.[4]

Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] responded that there was "no need for public alarm," while National Security Adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] said he had reached out to the Gang of Eight the prior week and appeared baffled that Turner had gone public before their scheduled meeting.[4] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read Turner's warning as timed to pressure the House into passing the Senate's national security supplemental.[4]
