# Ministerial Joint Contact Group

The Ministerial Joint Contact Group is a joint diplomatic body of the [[arab-league|League of Arab States]] and the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]], formed to coordinate a common international position on the war in Gaza and on implementation of the [[two-state-solution|two-State solution]].[1]

## Origins

Badlands dates the group's formation to the November 2023 Riyadh summit at which Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] convened the 57-nation OIC and, in Badlands' reading, told its members to stand down and allow him room to navigate the unfolding situation in Gaza — on the argument that globalist actors were baiting the Muslim world into attacking Israel in a way that would have escalated to world war.[1] Badlands wrote that the Muslim states then "did the one thing that nobody expected them to do: nothing," allowing [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to alienate himself from much of the world.[1]

Following its creation the group conducted a world tour led by Saudi Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] that ran through Beijing, Moscow, Paris and London among other diplomatic capitals; Badlands argued that important groundwork for what it calls the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] was laid at those meetings.[1]

## Madrid meeting (September 2024)

On Friday, September 13, 2024, representatives of the Ministerial Joint Contact Group — including Bahrain, Egypt, Jordan, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye — met in Madrid alongside the foreign ministers and representatives of Ireland, Norway, Slovenia and Spain to address the crisis in the Middle East.[1] In a joint statement the participants highlighted the severe humanitarian crisis unfolding since October 7, 2023, condemned violence and terrorism affecting both Palestinians and Israelis, and stressed that the two-State solution remains the only viable path to lasting peace and security.[1]

The statement called on the parties and all UN members to join a broader meeting on "The situation in Gaza and the implementation of the two-State solution as the path to a just and comprehensive peace," on the margins of the UN General Assembly session on September 26, 2024.[1] It further condemned unilateral measures, settlements, forced displacement and extremism as obstructions to peace, and underscored adherence to UN Security Council resolutions, international law and the Arab Peace Initiative.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented the Madrid meeting as evidence of a widening Sovereign Alliance: first the Arab League, then the OIC, then Africa, then China and Russia, and now Europe, with South America and the Caribbean having joined early.[1] He also credited the concurrent Iran–Saudi reconciliation — following decades of estrangement he traces to the 1979 overthrow of the Shah and the resulting theological arms race — with preventing Iran from delivering a full retaliatory strike on Israel in April 2024 after the Israeli bombing of an Iranian embassy building in Syria.[1]
