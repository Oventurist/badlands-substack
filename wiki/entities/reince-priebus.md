# Reince Priebus

Reince Priebus is a former chairman of the Republican National Committee and former White House chief of staff under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Commentary on the 2026 midterms

In a January 2026 interview on Wisconsin local programming, Priebus predicted that Trump would personally involve himself in the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]]: "My general view is, and I don't push the president on these things, but my general view is the president's going to get involved. You just have to assume that. He likes to have his fingerprints on things."[1]

Priebus also conceded that national politics had become entirely unpredictable, running through recent cycles — 2016, when Trump was not supposed to win; 2018, when Republicans were expected to be "blown out" but were not; 2020, forecast as a blowout but close; the 2022 "red wave" that did not materialize; and 2024, expected to be close, when Trump won the popular vote — before concluding, "nothing makes sense anymore… does anyone really know anything anymore?"[1] His remarks came as analysts noted that Republicans' narrow majority in the US House of Representatives could change before the midterms through special elections, member resignations and vacancies.[1]
