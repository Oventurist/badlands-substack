# European Council on Foreign Relations

The European Council on Foreign Relations (ECFR) is a Western establishment foreign-policy think tank, described by RT in January 2025 as "gilt-edged."[1]

## "Alone in a Trumpian World"

The ECFR published a major study titled "Alone in a Trumpian World," examining the EU and global public opinion after the US elections — that is, after the return of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], characterized by RT as the "bugaboo extraordinaire" of mainstream Euro-centrists and their establishment nomenklatura across bureaucracies, media, academia and think tanks.[1] Among the study's observations, the most straightforward was that much of the world is optimistic about Trump, hoping he will benefit America while also promoting international peace by making the United States a more normal great power.[1] The main outliers to this pattern were the European Union and the "even more splendidly self-isolated" United Kingdom, whose respondents held a pessimistic view — a European isolation that RT argued the report's own authors could not stop illustrating.[1]

## Alleged deep-political lineage

Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme described the ECFR — which he distinguished from the American Council on Foreign Relations, the UK Council on Foreign Relations, and the US Senate Committee on Foreign Relations — as a spiritual successor to the [[institute-for-statecraft|Institute for Statecraft]], on the grounds of considerable overlap between the two bodies.[2] He placed it in what he called a long line of "pro democracy" think tanks steering world events in directions favorable to a supranational amalgam of the military-industrial complex, pharmaceutical interests, international high finance, intelligence services, and organized crime.[2]

[[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]], the EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, is a member of the ECFR, a fact DeLarme cited in explaining her insistence that Europe continue confronting Russia through Ukraine regardless of American policy.[2]
