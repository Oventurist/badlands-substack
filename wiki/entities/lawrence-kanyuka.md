# Lawrence Kanyuka

**Lawrence Kanyuka** is the spokesperson for the rebel [[alliance-fleuve-congo|Congo River Alliance]] (AFC), the coalition that includes the [[m23|M23]] movement operating in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

## December 2025 cross-border dispute

After Burundian Foreign Minister [[edouard-bizimana|Edouard Bizimana]] accused AFC/M23 of bombing Burundi's Cibitoke province, Kanyuka issued counterclaims on the same day, alleging that bombs had been falling "continuously" from [[burundi|Burundi]] territory for more than three days and striking villages in eastern DR Congo.[1] "Burundi should focus on improving the living conditions of its people," Kanyuka stated on X.[1]

The exchange of accusations came despite a peace agreement signed the previous week between the presidents of the DR Congo and [[rwanda|Rwanda]], and underscored the persistence of hostilities in the Great Lakes region.[1]
