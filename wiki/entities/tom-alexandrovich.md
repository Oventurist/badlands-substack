# Tom Alexandrovich

Tom Alexandrovich is the executive director of the Cyber Defense Division at the Israel National Cyber Directorate.[1]

He was arrested in Las Vegas in August 2025 after allegedly attempting to meet a 15-year-old girl, according to a statement from the acting U.S. Attorney's office in Nevada cited by Badlands.[1] Alexandrovich was released on bail and left the country, and is described as residing in Israel, where Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued he is unlikely to be extradited.[1]

The case resurfaced in February 2026 when U.S. Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]], in a televised interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], defended Alexandrovich as "innocent until proven guilty" without offering or promising to seek his extradition — a response Badlands cited as evidence against Huckabee.[1]
