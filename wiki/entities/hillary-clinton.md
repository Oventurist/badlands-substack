# Hillary Clinton

Hillary Clinton served as U.S. Secretary of State from 2009 to 2013 under President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Clinton is portrayed as a central figure in the Libya intervention and as complicit in what the source describes as a scheme to fund and arm terrorist organizations with U.S. taxpayer money.

According to the article, Clinton was one of the primary architects of the U.S. military intervention in Libya in 2011, which the source frames as illegitimate and driven by financial and geopolitical interests rather than humanitarian concerns. It claims that Clinton gave her "full approval and blessing" to the operations to fund and arm rebel groups that were affiliated with [[al-qaeda|Al-Qaeda]] and [[isis|ISIS]], and that she was aware of the true nature of the groups being supported.

The source asserts that Clinton's 2000 Senate run in New York was facilitated by the death of [[jfk-jr|John F. Kennedy Jr.]], who was planning to run for the same seat. The article claims that JFK Jr.'s plane was sabotaged, that the enemy eliminated him because he was aware of who "the real enemy" was, and that his death cleared the path for Clinton. It further claims that Clinton had "no chance of beating him" and that his unexpected death "allowed America to lose an opportunity for a completely different history and direction."

In the article's framework, Clinton is an "insider" who represented the cabal rather than the American people. It claims that she, along with Obama and McCain, was complicit in the theft of taxpayer money and the arming of U.S. enemies, and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has never forgotten what "they did to him and his family" through these operations.

## The 2016 campaign and the press

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] frames the 2016 contest between Clinton and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a confrontation in which "the full weight of establishment power aligned behind Hillary Clinton," making the election a clash between "the once-voiceless masses and a ruling political class" rather than an ordinary partisan race. The article argues that the episode revealed how thoroughly the [[mainstream-media|corporate press]] had become entangled with the Western establishment.

## 2016 aftermath in "Always Fight Back"

In the essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]], Clinton's defeat by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2016 is cited as a modern example of mainstream election skepticism. The article states that in the wake of the election Democrats claimed Trump had colluded with Russia and launched the Mueller investigation, which found no evidence of collusion, and that Clinton "subsequently wrote an election-denying book and went on a speaking tour." It also references the Democratic-aligned documentary *Kill Chain: The Cyber War on America's Elections*, about vulnerabilities in centralized, technology-enabled voting systems, and ongoing litigation such as *Curling v. Raffensperger* in Georgia. The author's point is that questioning election results has been practiced across the political spectrum and is constitutionally protected under the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].
