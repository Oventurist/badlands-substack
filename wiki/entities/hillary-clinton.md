# Hillary Clinton

Hillary Clinton is a Democratic politician whose political operation is central to Badlands Media's account of the origins of [[media-matters|Media Matters]].[1]

[[david-brock|David Brock]] was in his earlier career a tough critic of both Bill and Hillary Clinton, instigating the "Troopergate" story and writing in the vein of his work on Anita Hill.[1] While writing *The Seduction of Hillary Rodham*, expected to be a hit piece, Brock produced a book largely sympathetic to Clinton and thereafter became a steadfast supporter of the Clinton machine.[1] Journalist [[glenn-greenwald|Glenn Greenwald]] observed that Brock went "overnight" from one of the most psychotic Hillary haters to someone who treats Clinton "like a deity."[1]

Media Matters subsequently proved a powerful tool for what the article calls the Clinton cartel, fighting for Clinton in her 2008 primary against Barack Obama and again in 2016, and attacking anyone even slightly critical of her.[1] Brock additionally created the super PAC Correct the Record, which the Los Angeles Times reported in May 2016 was spending roughly $1 million to find and confront social media users posting unflattering messages about the Democratic front-runner.[1]
