# Hillary Clinton

Hillary Clinton served as United States Secretary of State during the 2011 intervention in Libya and the September 11, 2012 Benghazi attack.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Libya and the weapons pipeline

The article asserts that after the overthrow of Muammar Gaddafi, Clinton — together with [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — installed a Libyan government controlled by Al Qaeda elements such as [[abdelhakim-belhadj|Abdelhakim Belhadj]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) As Secretary of State she was responsible for authorizing private U.S. weapons sales worldwide, and is accused of using that authority to funnel weapons to enemies while money flowed into the Clinton Foundation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article links the years of her tenure to the years of [[isis|ISIS]] expansion, citing Q drop 2640.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Stinger missiles that the [[cia|CIA]] turned over to Qatar in early 2011 — weapons her State Department intended for anti-Gaddafi forces — were reportedly passed by Qatar to the Taliban, one of which struck a U.S. Army CH-47 in Kunar province on July 25, 2012.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Benghazi night and the "smoking gun"

The article states that the Diplomatic Command Center at the State Department in Washington learned of the attack immediately, but that notice took roughly an hour to reach the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] — a delay the author attributes to a deliberate choice by Clinton not to have the military respond.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) At a 7:30 p.m. "Deputies Meeting" convened by the [[white-house|White House]], Clinton was the senior official present while both Obama and Secretary of Defense [[leon-panetta|Leon Panetta]] were absent; her stated first concern was that any deployment of U.S. forces into Libya required approval from the Government of Libya.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Citing the [[house-select-committee-on-benghazi|House Select Committee]] report as relayed by The Hill, the article says the committee found a "smoking gun": a direct order of the Commander in Chief was obstructed by Secretary of State Hillary Clinton.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The author asks why she faced no consequences, concluding that Obama defended her and that the two coordinated their public narrative, including a focus on a protest over an online video.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Teneo, the Clinton Foundation and pay-for-play

The America for Sale series casts Clinton's tenure as Secretary of State as the engine of a [[pay-for-play|pay-for-play]] operation.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Her closest aide, [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], was granted "[[special-government-employee|special government employee]]" status that allowed her to draw a $15,000-a-month consulting fee from [[teneo-holdings|Teneo Holdings]] and to work for the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] while advising Clinton at the [[state-department|State Department]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Abedin served as liaison between foundation donors seeking favours and Clinton's State Department, and the article argues Clinton used her power as Secretary to grant those favours while Teneo acted as the go-between.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

While Secretary of State, Clinton approved a paid consulting arrangement between [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[doug-band|Doug Band]], under which Teneo paid the former president $2.5 million per year; Bill Clinton also served for a time as Teneo's "honorary chairman," giving clients rare access to the couple and their network of world leaders.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Clinton personally sought out [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]], her 41-year-old former fundraiser, as her economic envoy to Northern Ireland, giving him special status outside normal diplomatic channels while he continued earning about $2.4 million from Dow Chemical.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article says [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] was among the foundation's biggest donors during her tenure and that she was to be installed for eight years after [[barack-obama|Obama]] as part of a sixteen-year plan — but "Hillary wasn't supposed to lose."[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The Guardian is cited noting that her involvement as Secretary of State contributed to a negotiated settlement in the IRS case against [[ubs|UBS]], after which UBS donations to the foundation grew sharply.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The Russian Reset and Uranium One

"America for Sale — Part 3" places Clinton at the center of the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]].[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) In March 2009, as Secretary of State, she presented Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov with a "Reset" button whose Russian text mistakenly read "overcharged," a gesture meant to symbolize the Obama administration's outreach to Moscow.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article argues the [[russian-reset|Russian Reset]] was planned well in advance and served to recast Russia publicly as a partner rather than an enemy, clearing what a cited Q drop calls the "pathway" for the uranium transaction.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Because uranium is a strategic asset, the sale of Uranium One to Russia's [[rosatom|Rosatom]] required sign-off from a committee of federal agencies including the [[state-department|State Department]], which she headed; the article notes she consistently denied any role in the approval while her office's assent was necessary for it to proceed.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The transaction gave Russia one-fifth of American uranium production capacity along with the company's lucrative [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] mines.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Undisclosed donations

As Secretary of State, Clinton had agreed with the Obama White House to publicly identify all [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] donors in order to avoid conflicts of interest.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The New York Times is cited reporting that Uranium One's chairman [[ian-telfer|Ian Telfer]] used his family charity, the [[fernwood-foundation|Fernwood Foundation]], to make four donations totaling $2.35 million that were never publicly disclosed, alongside between $1.3 million and $5.6 million in reported contributions from people tied to Uranium One or [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy]].[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author calls the non-disclosure tax evasion — the same offense for which [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] was indicted and pardoned — and asks why Clinton would allow Russia to acquire so much uranium and then, six years later, accuse Vladimir Putin of helping [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] steal the 2016 election.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
