# Hillary Clinton

Hillary Clinton served as U.S. Secretary of State from 2009 to 2013 under President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Clinton is portrayed as a central figure in the Libya intervention and as complicit in what the source describes as a scheme to fund and arm terrorist organizations with U.S. taxpayer money.

According to the article, Clinton was one of the primary architects of the U.S. military intervention in Libya in 2011, which the source frames as illegitimate and driven by financial and geopolitical interests rather than humanitarian concerns. It claims that Clinton gave her "full approval and blessing" to the operations to fund and arm rebel groups that were affiliated with [[al-qaeda|Al-Qaeda]] and [[isis|ISIS]], and that she was aware of the true nature of the groups being supported.

The source asserts that Clinton's 2000 Senate run in New York was facilitated by the death of [[jfk-jr|John F. Kennedy Jr.]], who was planning to run for the same seat. The article claims that JFK Jr.'s plane was sabotaged, that the enemy eliminated him because he was aware of who "the real enemy" was, and that his death cleared the path for Clinton. It further claims that Clinton had "no chance of beating him" and that his unexpected death "allowed America to lose an opportunity for a completely different history and direction."

In the article's framework, Clinton is an "insider" who represented the cabal rather than the American people. It claims that she, along with Obama and McCain, was complicit in the theft of taxpayer money and the arming of U.S. enemies, and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has never forgotten what "they did to him and his family" through these operations.

## The 2016 campaign and the press

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] frames the 2016 contest between Clinton and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a confrontation in which "the full weight of establishment power aligned behind Hillary Clinton," making the election a clash between "the once-voiceless masses and a ruling political class" rather than an ordinary partisan race. The article argues that the episode revealed how thoroughly the [[mainstream-media|corporate press]] had become entangled with the Western establishment.

## 2016 aftermath in "Always Fight Back"

In the essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]], Clinton's defeat by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2016 is cited as a modern example of mainstream election skepticism. The article states that in the wake of the election Democrats claimed Trump had colluded with Russia and launched the Mueller investigation, which found no evidence of collusion, and that Clinton "subsequently wrote an election-denying book and went on a speaking tour." It also references the Democratic-aligned documentary *Kill Chain: The Cyber War on America's Elections*, about vulnerabilities in centralized, technology-enabled voting systems, and ongoing litigation such as *Curling v. Raffensperger* in Georgia. The author's point is that questioning election results has been practiced across the political spectrum and is constitutionally protected under the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].

## "America for Sale" and Huma Abedin

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] (December 2024) situates Clinton at the center of two claims. First, quoting [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] research, it asserts "overwhelming evidence that Bill and Hillary Clinton knew of and approved" the sale of seats on [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]]'s [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Department]] trade missions to raise funds for the [[democratic-national-committee|DNC]] and the 1996 Clinton/Gore campaign.

Second, and more extensively, the article traces the twenty-year relationship between Clinton and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], who joined the First Lady's office as a 19-year-old intern in 1996 and rose through the roles of "body woman," traveling chief of staff, senior adviser, deputy chief of staff at the [[state-department|State Department]], and vice chair of the 2016 campaign. Citing *Vanity Fair*, it describes Abedin as Clinton's "confessor, confidante, and constant companion," a "mini Hillary" who controlled access to her. The article notes that Abedin accompanied Clinton to the November 2008 meeting with President-Elect [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and through her eleven-hour congressional testimony on [[benghazi|Benghazi]], and that Abedin was her closest aide during her Senate service on committees overseeing the armed forces, education, and the budget. The author's contention is that Abedin was a [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] placement and, in the reading of Q drop #4750, Clinton's "handler."

## The State Department, Teneo and the Clinton Foundation (America for Sale - Part 2)

[[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that Clinton used her tenure as Secretary of State to route corporate money toward the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and her associates. It reports, via *The Washington Times*, that her closest aide [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] held "special government employee" status permitting simultaneous paid work for the consulting firm [[teneo|Teneo]] and for the Foundation, and, via Breitbart, that Abedin began communicating in 2009 with Foundation donors seeking favors from the department.

The article states that Clinton's State Department approved a paid consulting arrangement between Teneo and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], who received $2.5 million a year from the firm. It also recounts, citing *Politico*, that Clinton personally sought out [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] — a former fundraiser of hers — as her economic envoy to Northern Ireland, granting him status outside normal diplomatic channels while he continued earning about $2.4 million from [[dow-chemical|Dow Chemical]]; the department later stated it had no record of Kelly or his staff being employed by it. *The Guardian* is cited reporting that Clinton's involvement as Secretary of State contributed to the August 2009 [[ubs|UBS]] settlement with the IRS, after which UBS donations to the Foundation rose sharply.

The essay frames these arrangements as a "pay-for-play spider web" and asserts that Clinton was to have been installed for eight years following [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] as the second half of [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]]'s "sixteen year plan." These characterizations are the author's and are contested.
