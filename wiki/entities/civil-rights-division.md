# Civil Rights Division

The Civil Rights Division is the component of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] responsible for enforcing federal civil rights statutes, including the principal federal laws governing voter registration and election administration.

## Voter roll litigation, 2026

The February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the Civil Rights Division, together with Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], announced federal lawsuits filed on Thursday, February 26, 2026 against Utah, Oklahoma, Kentucky, West Virginia and New Jersey for failing to provide complete voter registration lists as required by federal law. Those filings brought the running total of jurisdictions sued to 29 states plus the District of Columbia.

The Division stated that the actions reflect enforcement of the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]] and the [[help-america-vote-act|Help America Vote Act]], both of which impose recordkeeping and public disclosure obligations intended to ensure accurate and transparent voter rolls. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the litigation as part of a broader accountability effort over election integrity, writing that "fake voters are not the full problem, and remedying them is not the full solution, but the machine doesn't work without fake voters."

## Provision of voter roll data to HSI (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the Division would provide voter roll data collected from states to ICE's [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] under a reported [[voter-roll-data-sharing|data-sharing arrangement]] between the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], with the goal of identifying non-citizens who may be registered to vote or who may have participated in past elections unlawfully. The request for access is expected to be submitted by [[todd-lyons|Todd Lyons]], the senior official performing the duties of ICE director.
