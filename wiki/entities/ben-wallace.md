# Ben Wallace

**Ben Wallace** is a British politician who served as UK Defense Secretary.[1]

At the July 2023 [[nato|NATO]] summit in Vilnius, Wallace publicly rebuked Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] for his frustration over the alliance's refusal to give Ukraine a clear timeline for membership.[1] "Whether we like it or not, people want to see gratitude," Wallace told reporters the morning after Zelensky's outburst, adding that "you're persuading countries to give up their stock" of weapons and ammunition.[1]
