# Edward Teller

Edward Teller was a [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]] physicist whose fears about atmospheric ignition are, in Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer*, transferred to the character of [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]].[1]

According to Dr. Steven Biegalski, Chair of Nuclear and Radiological Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, Teller worried that the heat of an atomic explosion would cause the hydrogen in the atmosphere to undergo fusion, setting off a catastrophic chain reaction that would continue around the globe and destroy the Earth.[1] Badlands cites this attribution, reported by ScienceFocus.com, as an example of the film reassigning historical detail for dramatic effect ahead of the [[trinity-test|Trinity test]].[1]
