# Joseph Edlow

Joseph Edlow is the Director of [[united-states-citizenship-and-immigration-services|United States Citizenship and Immigration Services]] (USCIS) in the second Trump administration.[1]

In October 2025, Edlow was the public voice of a policy change ending the Biden-era practice of automatically extending work permits for migrants in the United States. Under the new rule, announced by [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] officials on Wednesday, October 29, 2025, migrants seeking work-permit extensions must undergo "screening and vetting" before an extension is granted.[1]

Edlow said former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s administration had prioritized migrants in the American labor market over national security, a posture he intended to reverse: "USCIS is placing a renewed emphasis on robust alien screening and vetting, eliminating policies the former administration implemented that prioritized aliens' convenience ahead of Americans' safety and security."[1]
