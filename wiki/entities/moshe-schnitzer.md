# Moshe Schnitzer

**Moshe Schnitzer** was an Israeli businessman, a former member of the [[irgun|Irgun]] militia — a self-described terrorist group — and the founder of the Israel Diamond Exchange, the largest such exchange in the world.[1]

## Family and connections

Schnitzer was the grandfather of mining billionaire [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], and the family is described in Badlands commentary as very close to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]. When Schnitzer died, Netanyahu delivered the eulogy at his funeral, a detail cited as illustrating the tight interlock between Israeli political leadership and diamond- and mining-sector wealth — "thick as thieves," in the writer's phrase, and characteristic of how organized crime syndicates operate.[1]
