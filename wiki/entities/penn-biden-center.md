# Penn Biden Center

The Penn Biden Center is a Washington, D.C.-based think tank affiliated with the University of Pennsylvania and associated with [[joe-biden]] during the period between his vice presidency and his presidency. It is the location where the first trove of classified documents connected to Biden was discovered, making it the origin point of the wider classified records investigation.[1]

## Role in the documents investigation

Prior to the discovery of the classified material at the think tank, Biden attorney [[patrick-moore]] had shipped boxes of documents from the Penn Biden Center to his law office in Boston.[1] The [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] contacted Biden's personal counsel on November 3, 2022 to arrange pickup of boxes remaining at the Penn Biden Center, and was informed at that time that other boxes had already been relocated to Boston.[1]

Acting Archivist [[debra-steidel-wall]] later disclosed to Senators [[ron-johnson]] and [[chuck-grassley]] that nine boxes were retrieved from the Boston office and secured at the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] characterized the Penn Biden Center discovery as the first stage in a scandal that subsequently expanded to a garage, a beach home and the University of Delaware, and that came to involve U.S. Attorney David Weiss, Special Counsel [[robert-hur]] and the [[department-of-justice]] Inspector General.[1]

## FBI search (November 2022)

The [[fbi|FBI]] searched the Penn Biden Center offices in mid-November 2022, according to sources familiar with the investigation, after lawyers for President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] found about ten documents marked classified at the site on November 2.[2] The material originated from Biden's tenure as vice president, and some of the documents recovered from the center were labeled top secret.[2] The search was carried out with the cooperation of Biden's representatives, and no search warrant was sought; it was not clear whether the sweep turned up additional classified or presidential material.[2] Across the center and Biden's Wilmington home, federal authorities recovered between 25 and 30 documents marked classified, including records from his vice presidential and Senate years.[2]
