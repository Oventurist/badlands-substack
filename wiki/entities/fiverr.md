# Fiverr

Fiverr is an Israeli online freelance services marketplace, publicly traded in New York, that employed roughly 770 people in Israel and around the world as of mid-2024 at a market value of about $1 billion.[1] Its co-founder and chief executive, [[micha-kaufman|Micha Kaufman]], became a prominent voice in warnings about the deteriorating condition of Israel's technology sector during the Gaza War.[1]

## Role in the 2024 high-tech warnings

Kaufman told the Israeli business outlet [[calcalist|Calcalist]] that while large companies had always distributed personnel across different countries, by 2024 "there is a tendency to be more strict about this to ensure continuity in situations of massive reserve recruitment or regional instability."[1] The remark reflected a broader shift in which Israeli firms — including early-stage startups — increasingly recruited workers abroad to spread risk and avoid shutting down operations amid repeated reservist call-ups.[1]

Kaufman was among the signatories of a high-tech industry letter sent to the Prime Minister, the Minister of Finance and the Minister of the Economy warning against the flight of high-tech jobs from Israel.[1] The letter was dispatched against the background of escalation on Israel's northern front and ahead of High Court deliberations on the ultra-Orthodox exemption from military service.[1] Kaufman also warned that Israeli companies were finding it harder to raise money because of pressure on investment funds to reduce their exposure to Israel.[1]
