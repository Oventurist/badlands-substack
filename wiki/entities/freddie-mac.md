# Freddie Mac

Freddie Mac is the US government-sponsored enterprise whose weekly survey of mortgage rates is the standard benchmark cited in American housing coverage. It appears in the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] as the source of data behind a reported milestone in mortgage pricing.

## The sub-6% milestone

Citing Barron's reporting on Freddie Mac data, the Brief states that the average US 30-year fixed mortgage rate fell to 5.99%, the first time it had dipped below 6% since 2022. The drop was described as prompting renewed refinancing interest, with the sub-6% environment expected to expand refinancing eligibility for borrowers who secured loans at higher rates over the preceding two years. The item is paired with news that the lender [[ratefi|RateFi]] had launched a mortgage product allowing cryptocurrency holdings to count toward borrower qualification.
