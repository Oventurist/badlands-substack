# Freddie Mac

Freddie Mac, the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation, is the government-sponsored enterprise whose weekly survey of mortgage rates serves as the standard benchmark for US home-loan pricing. Badlands coverage cites its data as the reference point for movements in the 30-year fixed rate.[1]

## February 2026 sub-6% milestone

According to Freddie Mac data reported by *Barron's*, the average US 30-year fixed mortgage rate fell to 5.99% in February 2026 — the first time it had dipped below 6% since 2022.[1] The drop prompted renewed refinancing activity and was expected to increase refinancing eligibility for borrowers who had taken out loans at higher rates over the previous two years.[1]

The same period saw lender [[ratefi|RateFi]] announce a mortgage product permitting cryptocurrency holdings to count toward borrower qualification without liquidation.[1]

## Leadership in 2026

By June 2026, [[william-pulte|William J. Pulte]] served as chairman of Freddie Mac and [[fannie-mae|Fannie Mae]] while also directing the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]].[2] When President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] appointed Pulte Acting Director of National Intelligence, the announcement stated that he would retain his chairmanships of both enterprises alongside his FHFA role.[2]

## Regulatory oversight and the 2025 Powell dispute

Freddie Mac is one of the mortgage organizations whose financial stability the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] oversees, a role cited in July 2025 coverage of FHFA Director [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]]'s call for [[congress|Congress]] to investigate [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]].[3] Reporting emphasized that Pulte's remit includes working with fellow regulators such as Powell to protect Americans from major financial shocks, making his campaign against another independent regulator unusual.[3]
