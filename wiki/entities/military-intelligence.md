# Military Intelligence

**Military intelligence** is treated in the [[q-operation|Q]] drops, and in commentary on them, as the institutional counterweight to the civilian intelligence agencies.[1]

In Chapter 1 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]], [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] notes that military intelligence enters the drops as early as Drop #2 of October 28, 2017, and recurs in Drop #4 alongside references to military generals.[1] He writes that military intelligence is "of vital importance" because it possesses the capability to combat the three-letter agencies, a capacity he presents as central to the reading that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was never outmatched by figures such as [[george-soros|George Soros]], [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] or the Rothschilds.[1]

Fleuret reads Q's statement that POTUS is "insulated" to mean that Trump was protected from harm, and takes the drops' references to generals as an indication that those at the very top of the military supported him.[1] Writing in December 2023, he ties these early references to then-current expectations that Trump's forthcoming trial would produce tangible evidence of a stolen 2020 election.[1]

The same drop set raises the [[posse-comitatus-act|Posse Comitatus Act]], which limits the domestic use of the military but which Fleuret says is subject to laws and provisions that would permit such deployment.[1]

## Q drops #8 and #11 in "Q for Dummies" Chapter 2

Badlands' "Q for Dummies" Chapter 2 presents Military Intelligence as the workaround for a compromised [[fbi|FBI]]: because the Bureau would neither investigate nor preserve evidence, the operation was routed through military channels.[2] Drop #11 is read as making the structural point that Military Intelligence requires neither Congressional approval nor Congressional oversight, which the author frames as essential given "how many rats there are in Congress" — bypassing them keeps information away from the enemy.[2] The chapter stresses for posterity that the President of the United States commands the military, and notes that a president has several statutory instruments, chief among them the [[insurrection-act|Insurrection Act]], for taking over investigations from the three-letter agencies.[2]

## Drop #36 and the "Military is the only way" formula

Chapter 5 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] treats drop #36 of November 2, 2017 as the operation's clearest statement of method, opening with the triad "Military Intelligence," "No media," "No leaks."[3] The commentary reads this as Q confirming that the effort is run through military intelligence specifically to bypass three-letter agencies that would leak to the press and compromise the operation.[3] Q's instruction to count how many military generals had passed through the [[white-house|White House]] in the preceding thirty days is taken as corroboration of an ongoing military operation, and is linked to imagery from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration in which hat bands are said to denote Military Intelligence and the [[judge-advocate-generals-corps|Judge Advocate General's Corps]].[3]

The chapter repeatedly interprets the Q formula "Military is the only way" as referring to military intelligence rather than to troops in American streets, while allowing that the military might eventually have to make a public appearance; the author states his own position that large military operations on U.S. soil "are not the play," and that the military's physical presence is not yet required.[3] Drop #36 also asserts that had [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] won in 2016, the U.S. military was prepared to do what was needed to stop it.[3]
