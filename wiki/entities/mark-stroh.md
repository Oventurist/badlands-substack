# Mark Stroh

Mark Stroh is the United States Chargé d'Affaires in Copenhagen who was summoned by Danish Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen in August 2025 over an alleged American influence operation in [[greenland|Greenland]].[1]

## 2025 summons

After Danish media reported that at least three Americans with ties to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had allegedly worked to weaken ties between Greenland and [[denmark|Denmark]] and to recruit people into a secession movement, Rasmussen ordered Stroh to appear at the Danish foreign ministry.[1] A [[state-department|State Department]] spokesperson subsequently confirmed the meeting had taken place and described it as a "productive conversation" that "reaffirmed the strong ties" among Greenland, Denmark and the United States.[1]

The same spokesperson said he could not comment on the "actions of private US citizens in Greenland," while stressing that Washington respects the right of the island's residents to "determine their own future."[1] The [[white-house|White House]] refused to confirm whether an influence campaign existed at all, with one official telling CBS News that "we think the Danes need to calm down."[1]
