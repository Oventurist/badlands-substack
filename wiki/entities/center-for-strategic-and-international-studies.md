# Center for Strategic and International Studies

The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) is a Washington-based foreign policy think tank whose analysts are frequently cited in mainstream coverage of Middle East affairs.

## Assessment of the Iran operation

In the April 21, 2026 Badlands Brief, CSIS is quoted through Mona Yacoubian, director of its Middle East program, in [[cnn|CNN]] reporting that disputed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s claim of regime change in [[iran|Iran]]. Yacoubian said, "This regime is more hardline, less prone to compromise and, frankly, more nakedly tied to the IRGC (Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps)," adding that "We've seen the decapitation of the reigning leader in Iran at the time, but that has not translated into dramatic change in terms of who holds the power, or their position vis-à-vis the United States." The assessment served as the mainstream counterpoint to Trump's assertion that the Iranian outcome would mirror the "results in Venezuela," and, in the Badlands reading offered by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], as evidence of media dread that continuity beneath the optics is precisely the point of the operation.
