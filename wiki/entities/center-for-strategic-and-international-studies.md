# Center for Strategic and International Studies

The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) is a Washington, D.C., think tank whose analysts were cited in November 2024 reporting on the prospects for a U.S.–Venezuela migration agreement under the incoming second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Future of Venezuela Initiative

Ryan Berg, who leads the think tank's Future of Venezuela Initiative, told [[axios|Axios]] that although Venezuela does not currently accept deportees from the United States, Trump's mass deportation plans could push him to strike a deal with President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1] Berg said that "we may end up seeing some kind of arrangement with (Maduro) if it means the ability to deport more people, for Venezuela to accept deportee flights."[1] He added that while there could initially be talk of pressure and sanctions of the kind seen in Trump's first term, that might amount to an "opening gambit to leverage an eventual negotiation."[1] Other Latin American analysts similarly anticipated a "transactional approach" from the second Trump administration toward Maduro.[1]
