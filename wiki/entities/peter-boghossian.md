# Peter Boghossian

Peter Boghossian is a philosopher and one of the three authors of the [[grievance-studies-affair|Grievance Studies affair]], the academic hoax also known as "Sokal Squared."[1]

Working with the writer [[helen-pluckrose|Helen Pluckrose]] and the mathematician [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]], Boghossian wrote and submitted deliberately absurd papers to peer-reviewed journals in identity-oriented humanities fields, several of which were accepted and published.[1] The submissions included an argument for men self-penetrating with dildos to reduce transphobia, a study of the patrons of "breastaurants," and a paper on rape culture among dogs in Portland dog parks.[1]

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] cited Boghossian's work in February 2024 as a precedent for the exposure of the peer-review system after a scientific journal published an AI-generated paper containing nonsense imagery, describing the hoax as "a phenomenal experiment successfully exposing woke academia as moronic and low grade nonsense upheld by no meaningful standards at all."[1]
