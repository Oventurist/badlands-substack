# Congressional Budget Office

The Congressional Budget Office (CBO) is the nonpartisan agency that produces budgetary and economic projections for the United States [[congress|Congress]].

## 2026 projections

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief cites CBO projections in its assessment of the interest-rate outlook. According to those projections, the federal funds rate is expected to decline to approximately 3.4% by the end of 2026. Inflation is projected to remain above the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s 2% target in the near term before gradually moving closer to that level in subsequent years. Longer-term Treasury yields are projected to remain elevated or rise modestly, which could keep long-term borrowing costs — including mortgage rates — relatively high even as short-term rates fall.

These projections framed the Brief's report that the Federal Reserve is expected to begin cutting rates in 2026 amid slowing labor-market conditions and easing inflation.
