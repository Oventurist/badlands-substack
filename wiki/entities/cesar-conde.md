# Cesar Conde

Cesar Conde is the chairman of NBCUniversal News Group. In March 2024 he took "full responsibility" for the decision to hire former Republican National Committee chair [[ronna-mcdaniel|Ronna McDaniel]] as a paid contributor at [[nbc-news|NBC News]] — and to fire her less than a week later — in a memo to furious staffers sent on the Tuesday evening following the announcement.[1]

The reversal came after a revolt among the network's on-air talent, including MSNBC prime-time headliner [[rachel-maddow|Rachel Maddow]] and the co-hosts of "Morning Joe," who objected publicly to bringing the [[donald-trump|Trump]]-backing politico on board.[1]
