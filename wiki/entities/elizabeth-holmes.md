# Elizabeth Holmes

Elizabeth Holmes is the founder of the blood-testing company [[theranos|Theranos]] and a former Silicon Valley star whose company was once heralded as a breakthrough in laboratory science before its core technology proved faulty.[1] She is serving an 11-year federal prison sentence for misleading investors about Theranos, and is incarcerated at a federal facility in Bryan, Texas.[1]

## Continued involvement in biotech ventures

Reporting in May 2025 indicated that, from prison, Holmes has been advising her partner [[billy-evans|Billy Evans]] on a new artificial-intelligence and medical-testing startup called [[haemanthus|Haemanthus]], whose name is Greek for "blood flower."[1] Two sources with direct knowledge of the venture, who could not speak publicly because the company had not officially launched, said Evans had raised millions of dollars for the effort; the precise nature of Holmes' role in supporting the venture was unclear.[1]
