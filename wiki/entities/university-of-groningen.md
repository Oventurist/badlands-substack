# University of Groningen

The University of Groningen is a Dutch research university whose researchers conducted a landmark fifteen-year longitudinal study of gender identity in children, the results of which were published in 2024.[1]

## The gender non-contentedness study

The study, carried out in the Netherlands, involved more than 2,700 children tracked from age 11 into their mid-twenties, with participants asked every three years how they felt about their gender.[1] At the outset roughly 11 percent — about one in ten — expressed "gender non-contentedness."[1] By age 25 only 4 percent, or one in 25, said they "often" or "sometimes" felt discontent with their gender, leading researchers to conclude that most children who experience confusion regarding their gender identity grow out of it and go on to feel content with their lives as men and women.[1]

The findings were published as controversy continued over administering [[puberty-blockers|puberty blocking hormones]] or gender reassignment surgery to minors.[1] The results were reported by Modernity News and the Daily Mail and circulated in the [[truth-community]] via [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s news brief.[1]
