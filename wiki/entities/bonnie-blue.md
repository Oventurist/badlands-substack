# Bonnie Blue

Bonnie Blue is an English [[onlyfans|OnlyFans]] performer who, along with [[lily-phillips|Lily Phillips]], became famous for filming sexual encounters with between a hundred and a thousand men at a time.[1] She is discussed at length in a March 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article by the pseudonymous contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], which links her prominence to the exposure of the [[grooming-gangs|UK grooming gangs]].[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

The article observed that Blue, like Phillips, is publicly said to be "supported by her family," and rejected that framing as a cover story concealing something the author regarded as sinister.[1] It reported that Blue's own mother "books" men for sex sessions with her daughter, and asked rhetorically what kind of mother would receive emails from strange men requesting sex with her daughter and check a calendar app for availability, answering that only "the kind of mother who would pimp out her own daughter" would do so.[1]

American Hypnotist argued that Blue was "raised by pimps," strongly suspected that she is herself an abuse and grooming victim, and maintained that she is not psychologically capable of making adult decisions — noting that an outward appearance of happiness is no evidence to the contrary.[1] He connected the case to the broader pattern of the [[loverboy-method|Loverboy Method]] and to the testimony of a former trafficking victim running a YouTube channel called "Seizure Girl," who stated that her customers never knew a pimp was managing the girls they were purchasing, and concluded that the entire sex industry warrants criminal investigation.[1]

## Alleged narrative function

The article held that Blue and Phillips were made famous at the very moment the UK grooming gangs were gaining international attention, in order to reinforce a stereotype of British girls as sexually loose and thereby sustain the claim that grooming victims consented to their abuse.[1] The author speculated that their families might be connected to the [[cabal|cabal]].[1]
