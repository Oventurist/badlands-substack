# Imran Ahmed

Imran Ahmed is a British political operative and the chief executive of the [[center-for-countering-digital-hate|Center for Countering Digital Hate]] (CCDH), a UK-based nonprofit that campaigns against what it designates online hate and misinformation.[1]

## Profile

Reporting carried by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] in July 2023 described Ahmed as a far-left British Labour Party operative running what it characterized as a UK-based dark money nonprofit.[1]

## Dispute with X Corp

In late July 2023 [[x-corp|X Corp]], the parent company of [[twitter|Twitter]], was reported to be exploring a lawsuit against CCDH and Ahmed.[1] A July 20 letter from X's attorneys addressed to CCDH and Ahmed accused the organization of targeting the company with multiple unfounded accusations in an attempt to hurt it financially, citing a June CCDH report titled "Twitter Fails To Act On 99% Of Twitter Blue Accounts Tweeting Hate" that the company said was "false, misleading or both."[1] The dispute followed a Twitter Files exposé by journalist Paul Thacker showing that pre-[[elon-musk|Musk]] Twitter employees had taken action against several conservative accounts after CCDH published a report alleging that just twelve accounts produced the majority of anti-vaccine disinformation on social media — a claim [[facebook|Facebook]] rejected, saying "There isn't any evidence to support this claim."[1]
