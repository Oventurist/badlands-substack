# José Raúl Mulino

José Raúl Mulino is the President of Panama, a position that placed him at the center of United States–China competition over the [[panama-canal|Panama Canal]] during 2025.[1]

## 2025 meeting with Pete Hegseth

On April 8, 2025, Mulino met US Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] for talks amid rising tension over global trade and alleged Chinese influence in the strategic canal waterway.[1] Hegseth used social media after the meeting to hail "increased security cooperation" between the United States and Panama.[1]

The visit came only hours after Panama announced it would call for an investigation into local executives of a Hong Kong–listed firm that owns two ports at either end of the canal — [[ck-hutchison|CK Hutchison]].[1] Panama's move to file a criminal complaint against the ports' owner ran parallel to the Trump administration's pressure campaign over the waterway.[1]

## Context

The ports dispute unfolded after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] vowed to take control of the canal, prompting CK Hutchison to seek a sale of its Panama ports to a US-led consortium; Beijing blocked the transaction.[1] Reporting cited by the article indicated Beijing hoped to use the Panama port issue as a bargaining chip in negotiations with Washington, with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s refusal to clarify his position read as an attempt to build leverage on tariffs and Taiwan.[1]
