# Richard Sauber

Richard Sauber is an American attorney who served as special counsel to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], handling the [[white-house|White House]]'s legal response to congressional investigations of the president and his family.[1]

## Role in the Biden impeachment inquiry

In April 2024 Sauber authored the letter by which the White House formally declined an invitation from House Oversight Committee Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] for President Biden to testify in connection with the business dealings of his son [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1] In the letter, dated Monday, April 15, 2024, Sauber told Comer that "as our Office has demonstrated, and you acknowledged in a recent fundraising email, your impeachment investigation is over," and urged the House to focus on "the issues that matter to the American people rather than continuing to waste time and taxpayer resources on this partisan charade."[1]

Sauber further argued that the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]]'s [[biden-impeachment-inquiry|impeachment inquiry]] "has succeeded only in turning up abundant evidence that, in fact, the President has done nothing wrong," and that Comer's March 28, 2024 letter recycled "the same litany of false allegations that have been repeatedly debunked and refuted by the very witnesses you have called before your Committee and the many documents you have obtained."[1] He closed by asserting that Comer's persistence made "one thing about your investigation abundantly clear: The facts do not matter to you."[1]
