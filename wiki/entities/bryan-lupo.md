# Bryan Lupo

**Bryan Lupo** is a contributor and on-air commentator associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Coverage of the Putin interview

On the evening of February 8, 2024, Lupo joined [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] and [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] in providing live Badlands Media commentary on the [[tucker-carlson-putin-interview|Tucker Carlson–Putin interview]], a broadcast carried on Rumble alongside the interview itself.[1] The Badlands brief for that day pointed readers to the trio's post-interview coverage for those who missed the original release.[1]
