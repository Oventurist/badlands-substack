# Nabatieh

Nabatieh is a city in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], north of the [[litani-river|Litani River]], and the focal point of the June 2026 Israeli ground operation described in the Badlands Media corpus.

## June 2026 operation

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that hours after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posted to [[truth-social|Truth Social]] about Israeli conduct, social media filled with reports of a major [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] operation concentrated around Nabatieh. Accounts described vicious close-quarters combat and armored columns including armored bulldozers moving through the area, with the IDF said to have sustained heavy losses including at least four destroyed Merkava tanks. Israeli media, cited via [[haaretz|Haaretz]], confirmed at least four IDF soldiers killed, among them a senior officer, and [[times-of-israel|The Times of Israel]] reported the IDF confirming an overnight operation against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] targets in south Lebanon with sixteen reported killed.

## Security zone

The IDF published a map of the security zone in which its soldiers were operating. The Brief notes that while Israel had announced such an objective before, this appeared to be the first published map placing the security zone north of the Litani River — and that because the operation was being waged against Nabatieh and its surroundings, the map likely understated the full extent of the territory Israel intended to claim. Journalist [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]] is cited affirming reports that Israel had effectively invaded Lebanon.
