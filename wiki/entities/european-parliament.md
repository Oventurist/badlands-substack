# European Parliament

The European Parliament is the directly elected legislative body of the [[european-union|European Union]], with committees that hold approval authority over the bloc's international trade agreements.[1]

## Freeze of the US trade deal (January 2026)

In response to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 2026 Davos remarks on [[greenland|Greenland]] — which closed with the warning "you can say no, and we will remember" — the European Parliament's trade committee froze approval of a United States trade deal indefinitely, citing "coercion" and "territorial integrity."[1] The freeze formed part of a broader European reaction in which Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] said the bloc was "prepared to act" and French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] floated the [[anti-coercion-instrument|Anti-Coercion Instrument]] against the United States for the first time.[1]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized these moves as posture rather than leverage, noting that markets which had panicked earlier in the week "shrugged in response to the response," and citing Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]]'s judgment that the EU is "not taken completely seriously" by world leaders.[1]
