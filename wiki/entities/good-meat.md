# GOOD Meat

GOOD Meat is a California-based cultivated meat company, with an additional office in Singapore, whose lab-grown chicken product was one of two cleared by the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] for human consumption.[1]

## Regulatory clearance

The FDA green-lit not one but two different lab-grown "chicken meat" products for human consumption: one produced by [[upside-foods|Upside Foods]] and the other by GOOD Meat.[1] Both firms are based out of California, with GOOD Meat maintaining a Singapore office as well.[1]

## Criticism

The approvals were reported by [[natural-news|Natural News]] under the framing that artificial chicken grown in stainless-steel bioreactors — and, in its account, derived from cancer cells — was about to hit the American market with federal blessing.[1] Badlands commentary treated the clearances as further evidence of a compromised food supply, urging readers to source real food directly from farmers where possible.[1]
