# Ty Cobb

**Ty Cobb** is an American attorney who served as a White House lawyer under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In July 2023 Cobb predicted on CNN that a federal indictment of Trump was imminent in the investigation into the January 6, 2021 events and the former president's role in them.[1] "I believe it's about to happen," he told CNN's Erin Burnett.[1] Cobb added that "what is called the Jan. 6 case, which really encompasses all the attempts to cling to power and prevent the peaceful transfer of government," had been a very important matter to the special prosecutor, who he said had gone about it deliberately while moving "heaven and earth to do it in a timely way."[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] responded that only a year earlier it had been unthinkable to most in the MAGA and America First communities that Trump could be indicted, let alone convicted in federal court, whereas it had become almost surprising for a full week to pass without new rumors leaking from jurisdictions seeking "their pound of Trumpian flesh."[1] Noting Trump had already been indicted twice in 2023 and awaited trial later that summer, he described himself as an Anon who grows more confident the stormier things become, and urged readers to see through the narrative haze to the legal and psychological precedents being set.[1]
