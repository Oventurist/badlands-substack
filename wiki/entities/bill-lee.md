# Bill Lee

Bill Lee is the Republican governor of Tennessee, who in September 2025 publicly backed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s decision to deploy federal law enforcement and [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops to [[memphis|Memphis]].[1]

## Support for the Memphis deployment

Lee joined Trump at the White House on Monday, 15 September 2025 for the announcement of an executive order creating the [[memphis-state-task-force|Memphis Safe Task Force]] and sending Guard troops into the city.[1] At the signing ceremony he said, "I have been in office for seven years. I'm tired of crime holding the great city of Memphis back."[1]

Lee's endorsement placed him in direct conflict with the mayor of Memphis, who said publicly that he had learned of the decision only the previous morning, acknowledged that the governor and president had the authority to act, and stated that he had not requested the Guard and did not believe it would reduce crime.[1]

## Tennessee gold and silver reserves law (2023)

On March 23, 2023, Lee signed a bill into law creating a process for the state of Tennessee to buy, sell and hold gold and silver.[2] The [[tenth-amendment-center|Tenth Amendment Center]] reported that the law sets a foundation for greater financial independence through state precious-metals reserves and could help undermine the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s monopoly on money, allowing Tennessee to shield its assets and hedge against rapidly depreciating Federal Reserve notes.[2] The measure accompanied a separate bill filed in the Tennessee House to establish a state precious-metals bullion depository along the lines of the [[texas-bullion-depository|Texas Bullion Depository]].[2] Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents Tennessee's actions as part of a coordinated multi-state push toward a [[gold-standard|gold standard]].[2]
