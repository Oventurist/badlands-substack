# US Southern Command

US Southern Command (SOUTHCOM) is the unified combatant command responsible for United States military operations in Central America, South America and the Caribbean.[1] Together with [[united-states-northern-command|US Northern Command]], it currently holds responsibility for military operations across the Western Hemisphere.[1]

## Proposed consolidation

Under the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] overhaul drafted in December 2025 and driven by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], Southern Command and Northern Command would be placed under a newly created [[us-americas-command|US Americas Command]], reported as "Americom."[1] The wider plan would also downgrade [[us-central-command|Central Command]], [[united-states-european-command|European Command]] and [[us-africa-command|Africa Command]] beneath a new [[us-international-command|US International Command]], reducing the number of four-star generals and reshaping the balance of influence among senior officers.[1]

Badlands commentary read the emergent two-pole structure — one international, one domestic — as evidence that the US military will increasingly be directed at homeland defence and at cartels operating in the hemisphere.[1]
