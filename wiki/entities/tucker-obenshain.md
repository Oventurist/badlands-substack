# Tucker Obenshain

Tucker Obenshain led external affairs for Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]]' 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Move to an outside group

In July 2023, Obenshain and campaign communications adviser [[dave-abrams|Dave Abrams]] departed to support a pro-DeSantis outside political nonprofit group, with Obenshain slated to lead the organization, which was expected to host events as part of the campaign's "DeSantis is everywhere" tour across early nominating states.[1] The departures came amid broader questions about the campaign's strength relative to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s lead among the Republican base.[1]
