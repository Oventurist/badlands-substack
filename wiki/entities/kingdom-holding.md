# Kingdom Holding Company

Kingdom Holding Company is the Saudi investment vehicle founded and chaired by Prince [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]], holding stakes in major international companies including Twitter (now X), McDonald's and the Walt Disney Company.[1]

## Technology investments

Kingdom Holding, together with Alwaleed personally, was named in 2024 among the top investors in [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s artificial intelligence startup [[xai|xAI]], which raised a $6 billion Series B to launch its first products, build infrastructure and accelerate research.[1] Other participants in the round included Valor Equity Partners, Vy Capital, [[andreessen-horowitz|Andreessen Horowitz]], Sequoia Capital and Fidelity Management & Research Company.[1] Kingdom Holding had previously maintained a $1.89 billion stake in X after Musk's 2022 acquisition, making it jointly the second-largest investor in the platform.[1]

## Control after the Saudi Royal Purge

Badlands commentary argued that reporting on the xAI round misleadingly presented Kingdom Holding as Alwaleed's instrument.[1] Following the November 4, 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi Royal Purge]] carried out by Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], the crown prince assumed effective control over the detained royals' companies; Alwaleed's release in January 2018 came with the condition that Kingdom Holding's $12.5 billion in global investment decisions be subject to approval by the Crown Prince's court.[1]
