# Joshua Reid

Joshua Reid is a commentator and host within the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective, part of the network of independent broadcasters and citizen journalists whose analysis is aggregated in the Badlands Brief.

## Mention in the May 2026 Comey debate

Reid appears in the May 1, 2026 edition of the Brief in the context of an internal audience dispute. Discussing the "8647" indictment of former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] on the previous day's Badlands Daily, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]] concluded that the charge was "weak sauce" and that Comey's social media post was likely protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]]. The position angered part of the audience, and according to Ashe in America one viewer went so far as to ask Reid to reprimand the two hosts — an appeal she cited as an illustration of how readily audiences will accept erosion of speech protections when the target is unsympathetic.
