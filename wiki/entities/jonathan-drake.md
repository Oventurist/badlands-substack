# Jonathan Drake

Jonathan Drake is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who appears in the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief alongside [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].

According to the Brief, Drake and Ashe debuted the [[badlands-jury|Badlands Jury]] experience at the recent Deadwood [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] "to smash reviews," and he joined Ashe on [[culture-of-change|Culture of Change]] Episode 154 to discuss the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] case, trials and juries, illegitimate international courts, and Colombian elections.
