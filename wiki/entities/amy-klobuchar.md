# Amy Klobuchar

Amy Klobuchar is a Democratic United States Senator from Minnesota.[1]

In January 2026, following Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]]'s announcement that he would not seek a third term, Klobuchar was reported to be considering a run for Governor of Minnesota.[1] The prospective race unfolded against the backdrop of a large welfare and child care fraud scandal in the state involving hundreds of millions — and by some estimates billions — of dollars in misused federal funds.[1]

## Ties to the Center for Countering Digital Hate
Leaked internal documents from the UK's Center for Countering Digital Hate, reported in October 2024 by Paul D. Thacker and Matt Taibbi, showed the organization planning to strengthen its relationships with the Biden/Harris administration and with Democrats including Klobuchar, while simultaneously planning in writing to "kill Musk's Twitter."[2] The reporting described Klobuchar as having introduced multiple bills to regulate online "misinformation," including legislation on AI-generated content in political ads and a Section 230 measure targeting vaccine misinformation.[2] Klobuchar's office did not respond to a request for comment on the leak.[2]

## Bondi Hearing Questioning (January 2025)

At the January 15, 2025 confirmation hearing for attorney general nominee [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], Klobuchar directed her concern toward FBI director nominee [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], warning about his past comments regarding targeting the media.[3]
