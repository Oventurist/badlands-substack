# Douglass Mackey

Douglass Mackey is an American social media personality who posted under the handle @Ricky_Vaughn99 and who was prosecuted over a 2016 meme.[1]

## Prosecution and acquittal

Mackey was convicted in 2023 of interfering in an election, though the court stayed his sentence pending appeal.[1] The case centered on his @Ricky_Vaughn99 account, through which he posted a meme jokingly encouraging supporters of then-presidential candidate [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] to cast their votes by text.[1]

On Wednesday, July 16, 2025, the Second Circuit overturned the conviction, holding "that the evidence was insufficient to prove that he knowingly agreed to join the charged conspiracy. We agree. Accordingly, we REVERSE Mackey's conviction and REMAND the case to the district court with instructions to enter a judgment of acquittal."[1]

## Account reinstatement

Following the acquittal, the social media platform [[x|X]] reinstated Mackey's meme account; as of reporting, the @Ricky_Vaughn99 account was active and no longer subject to platform restrictions, though it had not posted since 2016, when the platform was still Twitter.[1]
