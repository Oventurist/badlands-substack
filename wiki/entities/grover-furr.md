# Grover Furr

Grover Furr is a historian cited in Badlands Media coverage as having documented, using recently declassified material, testimonies and archival evidence from both the USA and Russia, the post-expulsion conspiracies of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]].[1]

According to the article, Furr's work shows that Trotsky made several attempts to return to power in Russia after his 1927 ouster, and that he did so with the help of fascist forces in Britain, Japan, Ukraine and Germany until his death in 1940.[1] The author announced that Furr's research would be the subject of a future review.[1] Among Furr's most recent writings on the topic is *New Evidence of Trotsky's Conspiracy* (Erythos Press, 2020), and his academic website is described as an invaluable resource.[1]
