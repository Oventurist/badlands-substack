# Jason Murray

**Jason Murray** is the attorney who represented the petitioners seeking to disqualify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the Colorado ballot in [[anderson-v-griswold|Anderson v. Griswold]], arguing their case before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] on February 8, 2024.[1]

## Oral argument before the Supreme Court

Murray's central contention was that Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]] was unambiguous in its application to Trump.[1] Justice [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] challenged that assertion of unambiguity directly, and Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that even the justice "most likely to pontificate about insurrection" had real problems with Murray's Fourteenth Amendment theory.[1]

Badlands coverage characterized Murray's talking points as "hysterical nonsense about an insurrection that hasn't been litigated," arguing that he asked the Court to usurp the powers of the state and federal legislatures.[1] During his questioning, Justices [[samuel-alito|Samuel Alito]], [[neil-gorsuch|Neil Gorsuch]], and [[brett-kavanaugh|Brett Kavanaugh]] pressed him hardest; Alito dissected the inclusion of hearsay and the testimony of Professor Simi, and criticized the underlying findings of insurrection.[1] Justice [[amy-coney-barrett|Amy Coney Barrett]] focused on constitutional nuance, and even Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] and the Court's liberal justices appeared conflicted by Murray's arguments, which Badlands described as novel legal theory constructed to persecute Trump.[1]

Opposing counsel Jonathan Mitchell argued first on Trump's behalf and was assessed by Badlands as having "by far, the winning argument."[1]
