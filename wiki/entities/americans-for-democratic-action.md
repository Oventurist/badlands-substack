# Americans for Democratic Action

Americans for Democratic Action is an American liberal political organization co-founded by the economist [[john-kenneth-galbraith|John Kenneth Galbraith]] alongside figures including Eleanor Roosevelt, Arthur Schlesinger Jr. and Ronald Reagan.[1] Badlands Media cites Galbraith's role in the group as evidence of how deeply he became involved in the machinations of the American political elite before he went on to help [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] establish the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
