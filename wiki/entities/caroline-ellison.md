# Caroline Ellison

Caroline Ellison is a former co-chief executive of [[alameda-research|Alameda Research]], the trading firm affiliated with the cryptocurrency exchange [[ftx|FTX]], and a central figure in the criminal proceedings arising from the exchange's collapse.[1]

## Role in the FTX case

Ellison reportedly cut a cooperation deal with federal prosecutors in 2022, in the wake of FTX's failure and the arrest of founder [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]].[1] Her cooperation positioned her as a witness against Bankman-Fried rather than a co-defendant facing the same exposure, even as commentators noted that the reported $8 billion fraud could not plausibly have been carried out by one person acting alone.[1]

## Media portrayal and Badlands commentary

By May 2023, coverage of Ellison had begun to frame her sympathetically. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing in the *[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]*, objected to what she described as "recent narrative attempts" to paint Ellison as a victim, including arguments invoking wage-gap theory and suggesting she had been underpaid because she is a woman.[1] Ashe argued that "the narrative that SBF is the lone big bad cannot stand," and that Ellison and "the shadowy network that enabled this massive fraud" — including the officials, regulators and celebrities Bankman-Fried had cultivated at the height of his wealth — should be "dragged into the light alongside him."[1]
