# General Atlantic

General Atlantic is a global growth-equity investment firm whose co-president, [[martin-escobari|Martín Escobari]], sits on the board of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

"The Harvard Control Grid" cites the firm as one of the private-capital vehicles whose leadership is interwoven with Harvard's endowment governance, part of what the article calls "the operating layer of a globalist architecture."[1] Through Escobari, General Atlantic is connected in the article's account to Latin American business and political networks and to the [[david-rockefeller-center-for-latin-american-studies|David Rockefeller Center for Latin American Studies]] at Harvard.[1]
