# Frederick T. Gates

Frederick T. Gates was an adviser to John D. Rockefeller and, as described in the Badlands Media feature "Hijacked Consciousness - The Hijacking," the architect of the [[general-education-board|General Education Board]] founded in 1902.[1]

## The "molding hands" quotation

Gates appears in the article chiefly through a quotation the author presents as the clearest statement of the intent behind mass schooling: "In our dreams, people yield themselves with perfect docility to our molding hands... We shall organize children and teach them to do in a perfect way the things their fathers and mothers are doing in an imperfect way."[1] The essay comments that this was "not education" but "software installation," and treats the passage as evidence that the standardization of American schooling was a deliberate program of behavioral conditioning rather than an accident of industrial-era administration.[1]

## Role in the article's thesis

Gates is placed at the origin point of a lineage the author traces forward through the Ivy League model, behavioral psychology, and the twentieth-century think-tank network.[1] In this account the funding decisions Gates helped direct made curriculum, not weapons, the primary instrument of multi-generational population control.[1] He is discussed alongside other figures the piece names as psychological-warfare architects, including [[edward-bernays|Edward Bernays]] and [[b-f-skinner|B. F. Skinner]].[1]
