# Sultan al Jaber

Sultan al Jaber is an Emirati energy executive who served as president of the United Nations [[cop28|COP28]] climate summit held in Dubai.[1]

## COP28 presidency

In December 2023, al Jaber drew international attention by insisting that there is "no science" behind calls to phase out fossil fuels.[1] Challenging critics at the conference, he said: "You're asking for a phase-out of fossil fuel. Please, help me, show me for a phase-out of fossil fuel that will allow for sustainable socio-economic development, unless you want to take the world back into caves."[1] The exchange drew a rebuttal from U.N. Environment Program Executive Director [[inger-andersen|Inger Andersen]], who noted she lives in Kenya with solar power and clean utility electricity and was "not living in a cave."[1]

His remarks were criticized by scientists and contrasted with the position of U.N. Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|Antonio Guterres]], who called for a full phase-out with a clear timeframe.[1] Days later, a draft summit agreement removed all references to a fossil fuel phaseout following opposition from oil and gas producing states led by [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1]
