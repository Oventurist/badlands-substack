# Kenneth Lay

Kenneth Lay founded Enron, described in the Badlands Media account as a CIA-connected company.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) In the 1980s he became a board member of [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] in Houston, joining a board that had previously included Lady Bird Johnson and former President Gerald Ford.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article offers his presence as further evidence of the bank's "right pedigree" for close work with the [[cia|CIA]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
