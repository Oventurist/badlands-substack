# Kenneth Lay

Kenneth Lee Lay (1942–2006) was an American businessman and the founder, chairman, and chief executive officer of Enron Corporation. In the Badlands Media article a family affair, Lay is identified as a board member of [[texas-commerce-bank]] in the 1980s and as the founder of a "CIA-connected" energy company.

## Enron and Texas Commerce Bank

According to the [[wayne-madsen-report]], Kenneth Lay founded Enron and became a board member of Texas Commerce Bank in the 1980s. The article frames this as evidence of the bank's continued CIA connections through successive generations of corporate leadership. Texas Commerce Bank is described as having "the right pedigree to enable it to work closely with the CIA," and Lay's board membership is cited as confirmation of that pedigree.

## Houston financial network

The article places Lay within the broader Houston financial network centered on the [[bush-family]], [[james-baker]], and Texas Commerce Bank. Enron's 2001 bankruptcy, which resulted from massive accounting fraud, is mentioned in the source as part of the same corrupt financial culture that allowed Texas Commerce Bank to operate with impunity.

## Legacy

Lay was convicted of multiple counts of fraud and conspiracy in 2006, but his convictions were vacated after his death in July 2006. In the Badlands corpus, he is framed as a representative of the CIA-linked Houston business elite who operated with political protection for decades before being brought down by the Enron scandal.
