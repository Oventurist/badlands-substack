# Kenneth Lay

Kenneth Lay founded [[enron|Enron]], which the Badlands article describes as CIA-connected. During the 1980s he became a board member of [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] in Houston, joining a board that had previously included Lady Bird Johnson and former President Gerald Ford.[1]

The article presents his presence on the board as unsurprising confirmation that the bank had "the right pedigree" to work closely with the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1]
