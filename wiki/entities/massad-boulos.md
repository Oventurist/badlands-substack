# Massad Boulos

Massad Boulos is a businessman and senior adviser in the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] who served as the U.S. point man on Central African diplomacy in 2025, and is the father-in-law of Tiffany Trump.[1] He was recently appointed as ambassador to the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] at the time of the April 2025 peace effort.[1]

## DRC–Rwanda Declaration of Principles

Boulos attended the April 25, 2025 signing ceremony in Washington at which the DRC and [[rwanda|Rwanda]] concluded a [[drc-rwanda-declaration-of-principles|Declaration of Principles]], hosted by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[1] His attendance, alongside Deputy Secretary Christopher Landau, was cited as underscoring the depth of U.S. involvement in the negotiation.[1]

## Badlands assessment

Badlands commentary credited Boulos with "bringing this story full circle," tying the 2025 peace declaration back to Executive Order 13818 of December 20, 2017, which froze the assets of Israeli mining oligarch [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] over his exploitation of the DRC.[1] Contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that Boulos's role demonstrated "the superiority of entrepreneurs from the private sector in conducting diplomacy over the bureaucrats in the [[state-department|State Department]]."[1]

## Appointment as senior adviser on Arab and Middle Eastern affairs (December 2024)

On Sunday, December 1, 2024, President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he would appoint Boulos — described as a billionaire from Lebanon whose son Michael is married to [[tiffany-trump|Tiffany Trump]] — as his senior adviser on Arab and Middle Eastern affairs.[2] In a Truth Social message Trump called Boulos "an accomplished lawyer and a highly respected leader in the business world, with extensive experience on the International scene," a longtime proponent of Republican and conservative values, and "a dealmaker, and an unwavering supporter of PEACE in the Middle East," crediting him with building new coalitions with the Arab American community.[2] Tiffany Trump, the president-elect's youngest daughter, married Michael Boulos in 2022.[2]

## Business background

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Boulos's company, Boulos Enterprises, is a Nigerian distribution, assembly and trading company for motorcycles, power bikes, tricycles and outboard motors, supplying several notable brands across West Africa and acting as the sole importer for Suzuki.[2] He wrote that public information on the firm's operations was difficult to find and said he was attempting to determine whether Boulos supplies vehicles to Burkina Faso's Captain Ibrahim Traoré and the Sovereign Alliance, observing that Nigeria had signed a security pact with Mali and Burkina Faso against ISIS and what he characterized as NATO proxy forces.[2]

## Lebanese and Palestinian ties

Badlands highlighted Boulos's service as personal liaison between Trump and Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas during Trump's first term, linking it to a promise the contributor said Trump made to Abbas in May 2017 regarding the Palestinians and [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[2] As a Lebanese Christian, Boulos has connections to Christian politicians in Lebanon, and is described as a political ally of Suleiman Frangieh and Michel Aoun, figures publicly aligned with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and Syrian President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[2] Analyst Aron Lund of the Century Foundation said Boulos's career "does not exactly indicate a firm commitment to either side in Lebanese or regional politics," and argued that Trump's Middle East policy "will often be more readily understood in light of the personalities orbiting Mar-a-Lago … than through an ideological prism or in terms of U.S. national interest."[2]

## Badlands framing of the appointment

Badlands predicted mainstream coverage would dismiss the appointment as nepotism, and argued instead that placing the Boulos announcement between the [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] FBI nomination and the [[charles-kushner|Charles Kushner]] ambassadorship amounted to [[narrative-shielding|narrative shielding]], ensuring Boulos would be largely overlooked.[2]

## DR Congo minerals negotiations (April 2025)

As President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s newly appointed Africa adviser, Boulos confirmed in April 2025 that a "path forward" had been agreed with Congolese President [[felix-tshisekedi|Félix Tshisekedi]] on a prospective [[us-dr-congo-critical-minerals-deal|US–DR Congo critical minerals deal]].[3] In a video statement released by the Congolese government, Boulos emphasized U.S. intent to drive mining-sector investment in lithium, copper and cobalt, positioning the United States as a key player in a region long dominated by Chinese mining interests.[3]
