# National Reconnaissance Office

The National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) is a United States intelligence agency responsible for the nation's overhead reconnaissance satellite systems.[1]

## Role in UAP matters

The NRO supplied a representative to the [[unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force|Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force]]; from 2019 to 2021 that representative was [[david-grusch|David Grusch]], who later became a whistleblower alleging U.S. possession of craft of non-human origin.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] noted that the NRO "has the eyes in the sky to capture UAP data, even if it doesn't release or confirm sightings," framing the agency as a key holder of unreleased sensor evidence.[1] Grusch subsequently moved to the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]], where he co-led UAP analysis until July 2022.[1]
