# Amin Nasser

**Amin H. Nasser** is the president and chief executive officer of [[saudi-aramco|Saudi Aramco]], the world's largest oil firm.[1]

## Remarks on ESG and energy security (February 2023)

Speaking at the Saudi Capital Markets Forum 2023, Nasser warned that [[esg|environmental, social and governance]] investment, if outright biased against the oil and gas industry, poses a threat to energy affordability and energy security.[1] He acknowledged the trend as broadly positive in principle, saying: "As far as the future of capital markets is concerned, ESG is clearly a rising trend. And in my view, an increased emphasis on ESG is a move in the right direction."[1] He then qualified the point: "However, if ESG-driven policies are implemented with an automatic bias against any and all conventional energy projects, the resulting underinvestment will have serious implications. For the global economy. For energy affordability. And for energy security."[1] Nasser added that the cost of capital for the oil and gas sector had increased because of higher perceived risks, themselves driven by ESG policies.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] contributor [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] framed Nasser's intervention as significant because it came from the head of the world's largest oil firm, and noted that with Saudi Arabia likely to join [[brics|BRICS]] in 2023 — a development said to further threaten the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]]'s global reserve status — the statements were all the more intriguing.[1]
