# Marvel Cinematic Universe

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is the interconnected film and television franchise produced by [[marvel-studios|Marvel Studios]] for [[walt-disney-company|The Walt Disney Company]]. Once described as the most untouchable brand in Hollywood, the franchise absorbed a series of setbacks through 2023.[1]

## The Jonathan Majors verdict

In December 2023 the MCU took another blow when actor [[jonathan-majors|Jonathan Majors]] — cast as the villain Kang the Conqueror and intended as the cornerstone of the franchise's next phase — was found guilty of assault and harassment, while being acquitted of other charges including assault with intent to cause injury.[1] Majors had already appeared as the villain in February's "Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania" and in the second season of the Disney+ series "Loki," and was to reprise the role in Marvel's 2026 film "Avengers: The Kang Dynasty."[1]

Within an hour of the guilty verdict — arising from a March incident involving his ex-girlfriend — Disney cut ties with Majors, a move the industry broadly regarded as a no-brainer.[1] One producer, speaking anonymously for fear of retaliation, told Business Insider: "I wouldn't hire him. He was on his way to being big, but I can't have him front a movie now."[1] The firing followed Majors being dropped by his management and PR teams and Disney specialty arm Searchlight Pictures pulling his star vehicle "Magazine Dreams," a Sundance acquisition, from its release schedule.[1]

## Consequences

The verdict forced Disney to hit the reset button on Marvel, requiring the studio to pivot in the midst of a swath of interconnected billion-dollar story arcs in which Majors served as a plot fulcrum.[1] The jolt arrived at a moment when the studio, its parent company and CEO [[bob-iger|Bob Iger]] could have used good news, amid what Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described as a "Crisis Cascade" of lawsuits, box office failures, cultural exposure and a board-seat proxy war — and which he linked speculatively to the Q-adjacent claim that "Disney is a distraction" and to the resurfacing Epstein thread.[1]
