# iShares Global Clean Energy ETF

The iShares Global Clean Energy ETF is an exchange-traded fund holding renewable energy equities, heavily weighted toward solar, wind and hydrogen companies including SolarEdge, First Solar, Sunrun, Orsted and Plug Power.[1]

## 2023 drawdown and investor sentiment

By late November 2023 the fund was down more than 30% on the year and on track for its worst annual performance since 2011, as its underlying holdings suffered demand warnings, project cancellations and going-concern disclosures.[1]

A [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]] MLIV Pulse survey conducted November 13–24, 2023 among more than 600 professional and retail investors using the Bloomberg Terminal asked what would happen next for the fund. A majority, 57% (353 respondents), expected the selloff to continue into the following year, while 43% (267) expected green stocks to find a near-term bottom and viewed the drawdown as a buying opportunity.[1]
