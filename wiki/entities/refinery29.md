# Refinery29

Refinery29 is a women's lifestyle digital media property that became emblematic of the contraction of the digital media sector in the mid-2020s.[1]

## Acquisition and decline

[[vice-media|Vice Media]] purchased Refinery29 for $400 million in 2019.[1] After Vice entered bankruptcy, [[fortress-investment-group|Fortress Investment Group]] took over the company and, by January 2024, was seeking to sell Refinery29 as a standalone asset following a failed attempt to find a buyer for Vice in its entirety — a package that had included Vice's namesake news brand, its production studio, and its creative agency.[1]

The Wall Street Journal reported that Fortress was in discussions with prospective bidders for Refinery29, whose revenue had fallen to roughly $30 million from around $50 million the previous year.[1] The attempted sale was reported alongside [[buzzfeed|BuzzFeed]]'s efforts to sell its Tasty and WeFeast food properties, and both were treated as evidence that the digital media business built on advertising and scale had collapsed.[1]
