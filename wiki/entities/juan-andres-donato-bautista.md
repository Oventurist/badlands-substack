# Juan Andres Donato Bautista

**Juan Andres Donato Bautista** is the former Chairman of the Philippines' Commission on Elections ([[comelec|COMELEC]]) identified in United States federal court filings as the recipient of more than $1 million in alleged bribes from executives of the voting technology firm [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]].[1]

## Alleged bribery scheme

A superseding indictment filed in Miami federal court in October 2025 alleges that Smartmatic co-founder [[roger-pinate|Roger Alejandro Piñate Martinez]] and executive [[jorge-miguel-vasquez|Jorge Miguel Vasquez]] orchestrated the payments to Bautista between 2015 and 2018.[1] The bribes were allegedly intended to secure and maintain government contracts for SGO Corporation Limited — including a deal to assist in administering the Philippines' 2016 presidential election — and to ensure favorable VAT reimbursements and prompt payment for services rendered.[1]

Prosecutors allege the scheme was financed by inflating the cost of the voting machines used in the 2016 elections, with the payments concealed through coded language, fake contracts and loan agreements, and funds routed through bank accounts in Asia, Europe, and the Southern District of Florida.[1]
