# Yuliya Mendel

**Yuliya Mendel** is a Ukrainian journalist who served as press secretary and spokeswoman for President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] before resigning in 2021 and later becoming a critic of his administration.[1]

## Allegations against Andrey Yermak

In December 2025 Mendel said publicly that she feared for her life because of [[andriy-yermak|Andrey Yermak]], Zelensky's powerful long-time aide and right-hand man, who had recently been forced to resign amid a major corruption scandal.[1] Yermak, head of the Office of the President from 2020, stepped down after Western-backed anti-corruption agencies raided his residence as part of the sweeping probe known as [[operation-midas|Operation Midas]], which centered on an alleged $100 million money-laundering scheme; he denied any wrongdoing.[1]

Speaking to Ukrainian media, Mendel suggested Yermak might still retain influence through his network of loyal officials and would "do absolutely everything" to keep shaping policy behind the scenes or to return to power.[1] She described him as "a very dangerous person," said that even criticizing the former chief of staff frightened her, and stated that she prays daily and thanks God "for being alive."[1] She further alleged that Yermak used smear campaigns, political attacks, and his influence over law enforcement to target opponents, branding critics "pro-Russian" or "traitors," and claimed that "criminal cases are created out of thin air" against those he viewed as threats.[1]
