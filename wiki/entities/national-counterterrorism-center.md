# National Counterterrorism Center

The National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC) is the United States government's primary organization for integrating and analyzing terrorism intelligence, operating under the Office of the Director of National Intelligence.

In the March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], the NCTC appears as the agency headed by [[joe-kent|Joe Kent]], who also served as a top aide to Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]. Kent's resignation as NCTC director made him the first senior official of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to leave over the U.S. war in [[iran|Iran]], and the Brief treats the departure of a sitting counterterrorism chief as a significant rupture inside the administration's national security apparatus. Kent's resignation letter argued that Iran "posed no imminent threat to our nation," a judgment carrying particular weight coming from the official responsible for terrorism threat integration.
