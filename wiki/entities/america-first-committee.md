# America First Committee

The America First Committee was a mass-membership pressure group formed in 1940 to keep the United States out of the Second World War.[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson describes the organization as having grown to roughly 800,000 members and as having counted the aviator [[charles-lindbergh|Charles Lindbergh]] among its most prominent figures.[1]

## Legacy and contested framing

Carlson argues that the Committee's members, despite campaigning on a straightforward anti-war platform, were publicly branded as Nazi sympathizers — a smear he treats as the direct historical antecedent of the way contemporary self-described "America First" supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] are labeled racists and Nazis.[1] In his framing, "there's nothing new under the sun": the same rhetorical mechanism used to discredit non-interventionists in 1940 is redeployed against populist nationalists in the 2020s.[1]

## Relationship to the "America First" slogan

The Committee did not originate the phrase. Carlson attributes the first use of "America First" to President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]], whom he characterizes as a Deep State instrument, during Wilson's re-election campaign on a promise to keep the country out of the First World War — a promise Carlson says Wilson abandoned immediately after his victory by bringing the United States into the war.[1] The slogan was later revived by the Committee in 1940 and, decades afterward, by the Trump movement and the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] bloc that extracted concessions from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] in January 2023.[1]
