# Seth Dillon

Seth Dillon is the chief executive of the satirical news outlet [[babylon-bee|The Babylon Bee]].[1]

In May 2025 Dillon became a target of criticism from Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] after publicly aligning himself with the argument that "conspiracy theorists" belong to the [[woke-right|woke right]].[1] Paul characterized Dillon in caustic terms as a former Ron DeSantis supporter who had "joined the chorus of his fellow establishment neocons," and cast the episode as vindication of warnings Paul said he had made in 2022 — warnings that were then dismissed as "kayfabe" by people now advancing the same framing.[1]
