# Truong Hue Van

Truong Hue Van is a Vietnamese executive who served as chief executive of the real estate company [[van-thinh-phat|Van Thinh Phat]].[1] She is the niece of company chair [[truong-my-lan|Truong My Lan]].[1]

In April 2024, in the same Ho Chi Minh City proceeding that sentenced her aunt to death over a $12.5 billion fraud tied to [[saigon-joint-stock-commercial-bank|Saigon Joint Stock Commercial Bank]], Truong Hue Van was sentenced to 17 years in prison for aiding her aunt.[1]
