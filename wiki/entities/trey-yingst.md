# Trey Yingst

**Trey Yingst** is a correspondent for [[fox-news|Fox News]] covering the Middle East.[1]

## Interview with Trump on Iran (June 2026)

In June 2026 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told Yingst that the United States would resume major military strikes against [[iran|Iran]] if ongoing peace negotiations failed, saying he would "bomb the shit out of them" if Tehran did not immediately agree to a deal.[1] The exchange followed a fresh round of U.S. strikes on Iranian military targets and renewed fighting despite months of ceasefire negotiations, and became the most widely quoted element of that news cycle.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read Trump's remarks to Yingst as part of a deliberate narrative performance rather than a straightforward policy statement, arguing that Trump was testing how far he could push a wrestling-style promo while reporters treated it with complete seriousness.[1] He highlighted Trump's claim in the same period of speaking directly with Iranian leaders who were begging him to stop the bombing as a further test of the credibility of media-constructed reality.[1]
