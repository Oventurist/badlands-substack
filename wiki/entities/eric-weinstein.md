# Eric Weinstein

Eric Weinstein is an American mathematician, former Managing Director of Thiel Capital, and host of the podcast *The Portal*, where he addresses the failures of academia, the rise of artificial intelligence, the limits of physics, and what he describes as urgently needed scientific breakthroughs.[1] He is frequently characterized as one of the more provocative public intellectuals of the era.[1]

## Diary of a CEO appearance

In July 2025 Weinstein appeared on *The Diary of a CEO with Steven Bartlett* in an episode framed around the question "Are we already living through societal collapse?"[1] He raised alarms about AI threats, corrupt science, drone warfare, nuclear war, and deep-state secrets including [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], asserting that Epstein "was a front" and asking whether [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the laws of physics constitute the only available escape route.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] found Weinstein's Epstein commentary "interesting and insightful" but pointedly noted the network context: Weinstein had been [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]]'s Managing Director at Thiel Capital, and Thiel was a defendant in the $8 billion Facebook trial then set to begin.[1] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], responding in the same segment, said he was "still waiting for somebody to show me one example of Trump deliberately pushing the Epstein narrative since he became President in 2017."[1]
