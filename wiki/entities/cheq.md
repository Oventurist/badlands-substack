# CHEQ

CHEQ is an Israeli cybersecurity company cited in Badlands Media commentary as part of what contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described as a matrix of Israeli software and technology firms underpinning the modern surveillance environment.[1]

## Meeting with Elon Musk

CHEQ was reported to have met secretly with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in 2023, promising to help X (formerly Twitter) rid the platform of bots.[1] Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was reported to have attended that meeting as well.[1] Badlands commentary raised the episode alongside the later controversy over the identity-verification vendor [[au10tix|Au10tix]] as evidence of close commercial ties between X and Israeli technology firms, asking pointedly how the bot-removal effort had worked out.[1]
