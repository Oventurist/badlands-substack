# Kathryn Kaminski

Kathryn Kaminski is chief strategist and portfolio manager at [[alphasimplex-group|AlphaSimplex Group]], a quantitative investment firm.[1]

## April 2025 commentary on Treasuries and tariffs

Amid the bond rally that followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s April 2025 tariff announcements, Kaminski warned that investors piling into U.S. Treasuries on recession fears were discounting the inflationary side of the tariff shock.[1] She argued that market participants fixated on slowing growth were ignoring [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]]'s comments about the inflationary impact of tariffs at their own peril.[1]

"The market is reacting strongly to the potential for weaker growth and demand destruction," Kaminski told Bloomberg.[1] She added that this should put the Fed in a difficult position, likely pushing it to cut rates on weak growth even as inflation complicated that process.[1]
