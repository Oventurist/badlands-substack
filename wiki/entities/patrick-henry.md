# Patrick Henry

Patrick Henry was an American founder and leading Anti-Federalist, invoked in the September 2, 2024 Badlands News Brief as the intellectual ancestor of its constitutional critique.[1] The Badlands contributor writing under the pen name GhostofBasedPatrickHenry commented on a New York Times essay questioning whether the [[united-states-constitution|United States Constitution]] is dangerous.[1]

## Warning about executive power

The brief credited Henry with being "1,000% right" in his warning that [[alexander-hamilton|Alexander Hamilton]]'s [[federalist-papers|Federalist Paper]] No. 67, "The Executive Department," was an abomination that would lead to a new form of tyranny.[1] Hamilton had argued that the federal government would not become a monarchy or dictatorship because power inside the Executive Branch would be decentralized across an unlimited number of appointed bureaucrats, each holding only a portion of authority, so that no single person or agency wielded absolute control.[1] The brief presented the modern administrative state as the refutation of that claim.[1]

## Anti-Federalist legacy

Henry's camp, the Anti-Federalists, demanded that the rights of the people be explicitly protected, a demand answered by [[james-madison|James Madison]] with the [[bill-of-rights|Bill of Rights]] in 1791.[1] The brief called for a resurrection of the Federalist/Anti-Federalist debate, a theme also discussed at the GART gathering in Deadwood during a "Federalism/Anti-Federalism" panel.[1]
