# Dragos, Inc.

Dragos, Inc. is a private company that describes itself as an "industrial cybersecurity technology and services provider," specializing in the defense of [[operational-technology|operational technology]] environments in sectors such as energy, manufacturing, and water.[1] Its chief executive officer and co-founder is [[robert-m-lee|Robert M. Lee]], a veteran of the United States Air Force and the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 water sector hearing

Dragos was one of two commercial cybersecurity vendors represented at the February 6, 2024 hearing of the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]]'s Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Protection Subcommittee, "Securing Operational Technology: A Deep Dive into the Water Sector," the other being [[tenable|Tenable]].[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the witness panel — a vendor CEO, a federally funded research nonprofit executive from [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]], a water industry association official, and a second vendor executive — as an illustration of the anatomy of a [[public-private-partnership|public-private partnership]], arguing that the participants' motivations and incentives are necessary context for interpreting their testimony.[1]

Through Lee, Dragos took the position at the hearing that the digitization and network connectivity of American infrastructure cannot be reversed, that hardening should be concentrated on selected strategic sites, and that broad manual or intranet-only fallback across more than 50,000 water systems is not resourceable.[1]
