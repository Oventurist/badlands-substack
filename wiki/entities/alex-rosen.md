# Alex Rosen

**Alex Rosen** is the founder of [[predator-poachers|Predator Poachers]], a Houston-based vigilante organization that conducts sting operations across the United States against adults seeking sexual activities with minors.[1]

## Knaapen sting

In June 2024 Rosen publicized a sting involving [[michael-knaapen|Michael Knaapen]], head of the [[maryland-democratic-party|Maryland Democratic Party]] LGBTQ+ Diversity Leadership Council, announcing: "We just busted the head of the LGBTQ Dems of Maryland going after a 14-year-old. Groomers are about to be mad."[1] Rosen posted the accompanying video on X.[1]
