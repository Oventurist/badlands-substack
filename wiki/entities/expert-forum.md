# Expert Forum

Expert Forum is a Bucharest-based think tank whose analysis of the first round of Romania's 2024 presidential election became the evidentiary basis for official claims of foreign interference.[1]

## Report on the 2024 election

Expert Forum reported that the [[tiktok|TikTok]] account of sovereigntist candidate [[calin-georgescu|Călin Georgescu]] saw an explosion of engagement in the run-up to the vote, which it described as appearing "sudden and artificial, similar to his polling results."[1] Romanian officials seized on the report to argue that Georgescu had "benefited from massive exposure due to preferential treatment" granted by TikTok, and attributed his victory to Russian interference.[1]

## Institutional ties and criticism

Coverage reproduced in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] described Expert Forum as a leftist organization working in collaboration with the European Commission, the Council of Europe, the World Bank and the United Nations Development Programme, alongside numerous NGOs.[1] The Brief used those affiliations to argue that the interference narrative amounted to an establishment tactic — comparable to Russiagate and the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]] in the United States — deployed to nullify an electoral outcome adverse to the progressive establishment.[1] It further questioned the causal logic that an artificial TikTok following could translate into a mass shift in votes.[1]
