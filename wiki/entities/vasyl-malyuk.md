# Vasyl Malyuk

**Vasyl Malyuk** is the head of the [[security-service-of-ukraine|SBU]], Ukraine's State Security Service.[1]

In May 2024, Malyuk announced that the SBU had foiled an alleged Russian plot to kidnap and assassinate President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] and other senior Ukrainian officials, saying the operation had been intended as a "gift" to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] ahead of his inauguration.[1] Under his leadership the service reported arresting two suspected "moles" within the Ukrainian state guard whom it believed Russia had recruited before the invasion, and claimed a Russian agent had transmitted the coordinates of a presidential safe house in preparation for a rocket attack.[1] See the [[2024-zelensky-assassination-plot|2024 Zelensky assassination plot]].[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary treated the SBU's public presentation of the case skeptically, noting that the agency provided no evidence for its central claim.[1]
