# Rodney Joffe

Rodney Joffe is an internet and cybersecurity executive who became a central figure in conservative critiques of the Russiagate affair. In commentary published by Badlands Media, he is described as having used internet data to fabricate a link between Donald Trump and Russia in order to bolster the Russia collusion narrative.[1]

## Alleged motive

Badlands hosts placed Joffe alongside [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] in what they characterized as a pattern of officials and contractors who advanced the collusion story while anticipating senior appointments in a Hillary Clinton administration: Joffe was said to have expected the top United States cybersecurity job had Clinton won the 2016 election, just as Schiff was said to have expected to become director of the CIA.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands narrative

For Badlands commentators, the pairing of Joffe and Schiff illustrates the argument that the Russiagate story was not an intelligence failure but a career-motivated fabrication, and that the release of underlying documents in 2025 was steadily exposing the incentives behind it.[1]
