# Jean Tatlock

Jean Tatlock was an American physician and physicist, an active member of the Bay Area Communist Party, and the woman described as [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]]'s "truest love."[1] She is played by Florence Pugh in Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer*.[1]

## Portrayal versus record

In the film she is introduced at a [[communism|communist]] meeting, where she meets Oppenheimer as a self-described skeptic of communism and answers that skepticism only means he does not understand it.[1] The film portrays her chiefly as Oppenheimer's troubled mistress, but the historical record describes a promising physicist trained at Stanford.[1]

## Death

Her relationship with Oppenheimer, alongside his ongoing flirtation with communism and union politics, threatened his career and contributed to the [[atomic-energy-commission|Atomic Energy Commission]] stripping his security clearance.[1] Both the film and the historical record present that controversy as the reason Tatlock took her own life at the age of 29.[1]
