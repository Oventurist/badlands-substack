# Federal Security Service

The Federal Security Service (FSB) is Russia's principal internal security and counterintelligence agency. In March 2026 the FSB announced the discovery and seizure of hundreds of booby-trapped heated boot insoles rigged with small explosives and intended for Russian soldiers fighting in Ukraine.[1]

## The insole sabotage plot

According to the FSB, the devices were smuggled from Poland through Belarus under the guise of humanitarian aid.[1] Each pair contained explosives equivalent to 1.5 grams of TNT and was designed to detonate when connected to a power source — such as when the built-in heating function was activated — potentially blowing off a soldier's foot or lower leg.[1] The insoles were specifically engineered for cold-weather use, when troops would be most likely to activate the heating element.[1] Officials estimated the consignment could have maimed as many as 504 troops.[1]

Russian officials accused Ukrainian intelligence services of organizing the smuggling operation, and a Tajik national born in 1994 was detained in Moscow as the alleged courier.[1] No soldiers were reported injured by these specific devices.[1] The FSB described the plot as an attempt to use "unconventional tactics" in the ongoing conflict, and Russian media and security services presented the find as evidence of continued efforts by Kyiv to target front-line forces through covert supply-chain infiltration.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry compared the scheme to Israel's earlier weaponization of consumer electronics against Hezbollah, arguing that the parallel raises the question of Israeli involvement in the insole campaign, and framing the wars in Eastern Europe and the Middle East as two expressions of a single conflict.[1] The commentary closed by asking what [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] is prepared to do to protect the Middle East from what it called the Greater Israel Project.[1]
