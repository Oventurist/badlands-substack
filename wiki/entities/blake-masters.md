# Blake Masters

Blake Masters was the America First, Trump-endorsed Republican candidate for the United States Senate from Arizona in the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]].[1]

Masters is notable in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] coverage of the post-election litigation chiefly for his absence from it. Writing on December 12, 2022, [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] reported that she could find no indication that Masters had filed any lawsuit contesting his race before the deadline that fell five days after the December 5 certification — the same deadline met by [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]], [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]], [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] and [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]].[1] She noted that she could not find that Masters had "made a peep about anything this past week, even when all the lawsuits dropped," and speculated as to whether someone had told him not to file or whether he had simply been worn out by the vitriol of a year of campaigning.[1]
