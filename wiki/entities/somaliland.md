# Somaliland

Somaliland is a self-declared state in the Horn of Africa that seeks international recognition as independent from [[somalia|Somalia]], which regards it as part of its sovereign territory.[1]

## 2026 recognition controversy

In early January 2026, Somalia's president said that Somaliland had agreed to resettle Palestinians from Gaza and to host an [[israel|Israeli]] military base in exchange for international recognition, according to reports carried by the BBC.[1] Somaliland authorities publicly denied the claim, stating that no agreement existed to host Israeli military bases or to resettle Palestinians.[1]

Several governments in the Middle East and North Africa — including Jordan, Qatar, and Algeria — denounced any move by Israel to recognize Somaliland, arguing it would violate Somalia's territorial integrity.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situated the Somaliland dispute within what he calls "Conflict C: The War for Logistics" in the Horn of Africa, arguing that the underlying contest is over control of the Red Sea and Suez Canal, through which roughly 30% of global shipping container traffic passes.[1] In his framing, Israeli interest in bases in the Gulf of Aden is tied to operations against the Houthis in Yemen, while civil conflict in Sudan is linked to Russian plans for a naval base on the Sudanese coast.[1]
