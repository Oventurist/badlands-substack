# Military.com

Military.com is a defense and veterans news outlet whose daily news coverage of U.S. military operations is cited in Badlands Media news briefs.[1]

## Taiwan Strait transit coverage

In April 2023, Military.com reported that the U.S. Navy had sailed a warship, the [[uss-milius|USS Milius]], through the [[taiwan-strait|Taiwan Strait]] in the first known transit since China carried out an encirclement exercise around Taiwan.[1] The report carried the [[us-7th-fleet|U.S. 7th Fleet]]'s characterization of the passage as routine and beyond the territorial sea of any coastal state, China's protest through the [[eastern-theater-command|Eastern Theater Command]], and background on the drills that followed [[tsai-ing-wen|Tsai Ing-wen]]'s meeting with [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1]
