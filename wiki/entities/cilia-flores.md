# Cilia Flores

Cilia Flores is a Venezuelan politician and lawyer, the wife of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and a longtime figure in the Chavista political establishment, having previously served as president of the National Assembly.

## Capture and transfer to the United States

According to the January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-577|Badlands Brief]], Flores was captured together with her husband on January 3, 2026 during a U.S. military operation in Venezuela, and both were subsequently transferred to the United States to face charges. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said in a January 25 interview that American forces used a classified device he called the "[[discombobulator|Discombobulator]]" to disable Venezuelan defense equipment during the raid and prevent the launch of Russian and Chinese missiles.

A leaked recording later circulated in which Acting President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] described the immediate aftermath, saying U.S. forces gave members of Maduro's cabinet "15 minutes to comply or they would kill us." Rodríguez also claimed in that account that the remaining leadership was initially told Maduro and Flores had been killed rather than captured — a detail that Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues is likely embellishment or fabrication intended to burnish Rodríguez's credibility as an opponent of American hegemony.
