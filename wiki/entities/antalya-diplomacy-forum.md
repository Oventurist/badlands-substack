# Antalya Diplomacy Forum

**Antalya Diplomacy Forum** is an annual diplomatic conference hosted in Antalya, [[turkey|Turkey]]. In the Badlands corpus it appears in April 2026 as the venue where the emerging post-war security architecture of the Middle East was reportedly assembled.

## The April 2026 meeting

The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 notes that Turkey "coincidentally" hosted [[egypt|Egypt]], [[pakistan|Pakistan]], and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] at the Antalya Diplomacy Forum to discuss the Iran situation — a meeting that the Brief's contributor, GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, identifies as the concrete emergence of the new "security architecture" he had previously forecast for the Middle East. The four countries, he argues, represent the pillars of the emerging order: Egypt as the traditional military power of the Arab world, Pakistan as the sole Muslim nation with nuclear weapons, Turkey as a NATO member with the largest army in the Middle East, and Saudi Arabia as the lynchpin that brought the grouping together along with the United States. He expects [[iran|Iran]] to eventually join the arrangement as well.

## Significance

The Brief presents the Antalya meeting as the diplomatic counterweight to the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s campaign of territorial conquest, arguing that the five-country bloc — potentially backed by Russia, China, and a Europe souring on Israel — is the only force capable of stopping the establishment of Greater Israel. The forum is thus cast as the crucible that could forge the Middle East into a stable region with common interests.
