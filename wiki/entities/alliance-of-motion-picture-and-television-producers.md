# Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers

The Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers (AMPTP) is the trade association that negotiates industry-wide labor agreements on behalf of Hollywood studios and producers. It was the management-side counterparty to the [[writers-guild-of-america|Writers Guild of America]] during the [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]].[1]

## 2023 tentative agreement with the WGA

On Sunday, September 24, 2023, the WGA and the AMPTP reached a tentative agreement ending the writers' strike after nearly five months, and picketing was suspended.[1] The parties finalized the framework of the deal after untangling a stalemate over artificial intelligence and writing-room staffing levels — the two issues that had blocked settlement longest.[1]

In a joint statement the two organizations announced simply that "The WGA and the AMPTP have reached a tentative agreement."[1] The guild told its members it had "reached a tentative agreement on a new 2023 MBA, which is to say an agreement in principle on all deal points, subject to drafting final contract language."[1] The announcement came just after sunset and the start of the Yom Kippur holiday, which many had treated as the effective deadline after five days of long negotiations.[1] Details of the agreement were not released immediately, but the guild indicated they would be revealed in advance of membership ratification votes.[1] The settlement was reported by Deadline.[1]
