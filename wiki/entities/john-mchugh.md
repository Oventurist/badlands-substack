# John McHugh

**John McHugh** is a Colorado attorney who represented transgender attorney [[autumn-scardina|Autumn Scardina]] in her long-running discrimination suit against Christian baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] and his Lakewood bakery, [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]].[1]

## Colorado Supreme Court dismissal (October 2024)

On October 8, 2024, the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] voted 4–3 to dismiss the case on procedural grounds rather than decide whether Phillips could refuse, on religious grounds, to make a pink-and-blue cake celebrating Scardina's gender transition.[1] McHugh expressed disappointment with the outcome and said he was evaluating whether any remaining legal options were available to his client.[1] He accused the court of avoiding the substance of the dispute, saying the justices "decided to avoid the merits of this issue by inventing an argument no party raised."[1]

The majority opinion, written by Justice [[melissa-hart|Melissa Hart]], stated that the court could not answer the constitutional question of how governments should balance anti-discrimination protections for transgender individuals against the rights of religious business owners in the public market.[1]
