# Kate Todd

Kate Todd is an American attorney who served as a deputy White House counsel during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

She was among the Trump-administration lawyers announced in January 2024 as joining the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]], the firm founded by former Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and [[ted-ullyot|Ted Ullyot]], together with [[pat-cipollone|Pat Cipollone]] and [[pat-philbin|Pat Philbin]].[1]
