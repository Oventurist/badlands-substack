# Hugh Crichton-Miller

**Hugh Crichton-Miller** was the psychiatrist who headed the [[tavistock-clinic|Tavistock Clinic]] on its founding in 1920 in London, England.[1] He had made his name developing psychological treatments for soldiers suffering from shell shock during and after the First World War, the condition the clinic was ostensibly created to research and treat.[1]

Crichton-Miller was heavily influenced by [[carl-jung|Carl Gustav Jung]], the founder of analytical psychology.[1] Despite the clinic's stated focus on traumatised soldiers, the first patient treated there was a child — a fact highlighted by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as evidence that the institution's interest in trauma extended beyond veteran care into the study of how trauma shapes and controls the developing mind.[1]
