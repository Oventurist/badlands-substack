# Nicole Shanahan

Nicole Shanahan is a California-based attorney and entrepreneur, once married to Google co-founder Sergey Brin, who in March 2024 was reported to be [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s expected running mate for his independent presidential bid.[1]

## Selection as running mate

Mediaite reported that Kennedy was planning to tap Shanahan for the ticket, and that NFL quarterback [[aaron-rodgers|Aaron Rodgers]], previously under consideration, was out of the running after the prospect of his selection prompted concerns among campaign donors.[1] Shanahan had earlier been behind Kennedy's Super Bowl commercial, giving her prior visibility within the campaign's orbit.[1]

A source close to the campaign said Kennedy and Shanahan "align on numerous issues," and that the campaign was also "looking for a candidate who can help finance the ballot access initiative" that would be crucial if Kennedy were to compete seriously in the election.[1] The same source was blunter about the calculation: "She might be infusing millions of dollars into the campaign to help fund the ballot initiative, which makes her attractive financially; however, she lacks the qualifications to actually do the job."[1]
