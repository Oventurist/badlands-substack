# Dov Lando

Rabbi Dov Lando is the spiritual leader of the ultra-Orthodox Degel HaTorah party in Israel and one of the most prominent rabbinic authorities of the so-called "Lithuanian" stream of ultra-Orthodoxy.[1] In August 2025 he became the public face of Haredi resistance to Israeli military conscription after the arrest of two Haredi draft evaders.[1]

## Confrontation over the IDF draft

Through a spokesman, Lando accused the Israeli government of waging war on the Haredi community, declaring that "The State of Israel has declared war on yeshiva students. Haredi Judaism will embark on a global struggle like never before."[1] The statement was issued on Wednesday, August 6, 2025, and was reported by The Times of Israel.[1]

Lando's declaration followed an "emergency consultation" he convened with Haredi activists at his home in Bnei Brak, intended to produce "a consensus for a response" among the various Haredi sub-groups.[1] The threat of a coordinated worldwide campaign marked one of the sharpest escalations in the long-running dispute over whether yeshiva students should be exempted from service in the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the episode as a looming civil conflict within the Jewish diaspora, arguing that the ultra-Orthodox tend to support continued annexation of neighboring lands while declining to supply their own children as soldiers to achieve it.[1]
