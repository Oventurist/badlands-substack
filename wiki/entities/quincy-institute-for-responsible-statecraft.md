# Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft

The Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft is a Washington-based foreign policy research organization that advocates military restraint and diplomacy-first statecraft.[1]

## 2026 brief on US–Israel defense integration

A Quincy Institute brief by [[steven-simon|Steven Simon]], cited in Badlands coverage of the Fiscal Year 2027 [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]], argued that deeper defense-industrial integration between the United States and [[israel|Israel]] could shift elements of the bilateral relationship away from annual military aid votes in [[congress|Congress]] and into defense acquisition processes, which the brief characterized as less publicly visible.[1] The analysis was invoked in connection with a draft provision of the [[house-armed-services-committee|House Armed Services Committee]] bill creating a proposed "United States-Israel Defense Technology Cooperation Initiative."[1]
