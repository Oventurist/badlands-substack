# Colin Kaepernick

**Colin Kaepernick** is a former National Football League quarterback who began the practice of taking a knee during the National Anthem to protest perceived racial injustice toward the black community in America.[1] Badlands Media coverage characterized the resulting wave of anthem kneeling across professional sports as a "craze."[1]

## Influence on sports protest

The kneeling protest Kaepernick initiated became a reference point in later disputes over political activism in professional sport. Flyers head coach [[john-tortorella|John Tortorella]] had been outspoken against players taking a knee, later softening his hard-line position without endorsing the practice and instead leaving participation to player choice — a stance he echoed when defending defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]]'s decision to sit out a Pride Night warmup in January 2023.[1]

Commentary in the Badlands article placed anthem kneeling alongside BLM advocacy, mask wearing, vaccination campaigns and LGBTQ Pride promotions as elements of a broader activist agenda pushed through sports institutions, arguing that declining attendance and television ratings indicated American audiences were tiring of it.[1]
