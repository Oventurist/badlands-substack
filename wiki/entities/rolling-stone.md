# Rolling Stone

Rolling Stone is an American music and politics magazine, described by Badlands Media commentary as one of the "key culture trend-setters of the old guard" within what the outlet terms the Media Industrial Complex.[1]

## May 2024 report on Trump and Mexican cartels

In May 2024, Rolling Stone published a report claiming that if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] won a second term in November, he intended to covertly deploy American assassination squads into Mexico soon after being sworn in, according to three people who had discussed the matter with the former president.[1] The report noted that both during and after his presidency Trump had floated ideas for bombing or invading Mexico in response to the American [[fentanyl-crisis|fentanyl crisis]] and to "wage WAR" on [[mexican-drug-cartels|drug cartels]].[1] It cited former defense secretary [[mark-esper|Mark Esper]] as saying that as president Trump believed it possible to bomb the cartels' drug labs and then potentially attribute the strikes to another country.[1]

Rolling Stone characterized what had once been a fringe notion — one senior Trump administration officials moved quickly to shut down — as having become a mainstream Republican policy proposal embraced by influential lawmakers on Capitol Hill and by conservative think tanks.[1] The piece quoted Trump's public campaign vow to "make appropriate use of Special Forces, cyber warfare, and other overt and covert actions to inflict maximum damage on cartel leadership, infrastructure, and operations."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the article functioned as inadvertent campaign material for Trump, since it warned readers about plans to rid the United States of cartel influence, organized crime, and a fentanyl crisis killing tens of thousands of Americans annually.[1] He linked the piece to TIME Magazine's earlier coverage of Agenda 47, describing both as evidence of "Narrative Convergence" and of the establishment retreating along previously held psychological ground.[1]
