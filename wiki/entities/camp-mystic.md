# Camp Mystic

Camp Mystic is a summer camp for girls located in Hunt, a community in [[kerr-county|Kerr County]], Texas.[1] The camp was devastated by the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]] over the Fourth of July weekend in 2025.[1]

## 2025 flood

Following the flash flooding, ten girls and one counselor from Camp Mystic remained unaccounted for as of July 6, 2025, according to authorities.[1] The camp's losses formed part of a death toll that included at least 28 children in Kerr County.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the episode as the most emotional and disturbing story of the weekend and pointed readers to video of Camp Mystic girls singing praises as they were being rescued.[1]

## Cultural controversy

The camp became the subject of a viral controversy when [[sade-perkins|Sade Perkins]], a former appointee to a Houston city board, posted a TikTok video describing the flood-ravaged camp as "White-only, conservative [and] Christian."[1] The remarks drew widespread criticism and prompted the City of Houston to announce it would no longer collaborate with Perkins.[1]
