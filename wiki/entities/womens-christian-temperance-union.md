# Women's Christian Temperance Union

The Women's Christian Temperance Union (WCTU) was the principal organization of the American temperance movement and, with its [[anti-saloon-league|Anti-Saloon League]] offshoots, the vehicle through which [[prohibition|Prohibition]] was enacted.[1]

## Backing and leadership

[[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]] describes the WCTU and its offshoots as "a small, well-financed and highly organized circle" enjoying the financial backing of the Astors, the Vanderbilts, the Warburgs, and the Rockefellers — families with direct connections to the American financial system, the [[city-of-london|City of London]], and real estate.[1] The movement was run by [[jane-addams|Jane Addams]], who had studied the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]]'s London settlement house experiment at [[toynbee-hall|Toynbee Hall]].[1]

## Origins and character

The temperance movement was founded at [[oberlin-college|Oberlin College]] following the Civil War.[1] In its early years it was characterized as a violent cult known as "Organized Motherlove," and during the peak Prohibition demonstrations of the 1910s its members were said to have made headlines through assaults on saloons throughout the Ohio Valley.[1] Carlson argues these features only make sense if the elite financiers were supporting a cult opposed to worldly pleasures because Prohibition was a means of creating an organization and system for trafficking bootleg liquor, drugs, and possibly humans.[1]
