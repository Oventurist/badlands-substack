# Bill Harris

Bill Harris is a technology executive described in reporting on the 2024 Democratic donor revolt as a former Intuit and PayPal chief executive.[1] He contributed $620,000 to the Biden Victory Fund during the 2020 campaign cycle.[1]

In July 2024, after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s debate performance and his follow-up interview with [[george-stephanopoulos|George Stephanopoulos]], Harris told ABC News that most observers would regard Biden's departure from the race as "inevitable."[1] Rather than simply withdrawing support, Harris was identified among mega-donors engaged in what ZeroHedge called "creative political-financial engineering" — structuring political action committees capable of funding a "mini-primary" and an alternative Democratic nominee.[1] He was cited alongside [[reed-hastings|Reed Hastings]] and [[whitney-tilson|Whitney Tilson]] as part of that group.[1]
