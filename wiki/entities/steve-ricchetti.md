# Steve Ricchetti

**Steve Ricchetti** is a senior counselor in the [[white-house|White House]] under President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 shift on crime and the border

In May 2024, following the defeat of a liberal Portland prosecutor by a tough-on-crime challenger in Oregon, reporting described Ricchetti as among the leading voices inside the West Wing arguing that Democrats needed to distance themselves further from the party's left flank on law-and-order issues, while also advocating greater toughness on the southern border.[1] The account was attributed to a Biden official and one other, both granted anonymity to discuss private conversations.[1]

Biden aides argued that the Oregon results validated long-running concerns that crime and an immigration crisis at the border risked overwhelming the president's case for reelection, particularly if the broader party were seen as soft on both fronts.[1] One official said: "Particularly right now, Americans don't want to feel like things are out of control. Well-meaning ideas have gone too far, and we need a sensible approach."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] denounced the [[politico|Politico]] piece reporting this repositioning as "one hell of a gaslight" and "dangerous government propaganda," disputing claims of falling murder rates and dismissing late-term border rhetoric as an insult to voters.[1]
