# ICR, INC.

**ICR, INC.** is a privately held, employee-owned engineering firm based in [[colorado|Colorado]] that performs work for the United States [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] and the intelligence community. It is the vendor awarded the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order examined in an October 2023 investigation by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

Two companies share the ICR name; the correct entity was identified by matching the address on the company website to the address on the DOD delivery order.[1]

## Mission and structure

The company describes its mission as being "the first to identify and solve the Intelligence and Defense Communities' toughest engineering and operational problems" through partnership with its customers, emphasising advanced degrees, heavy R&D focus and hands-on field experience, and framing itself as an employee-owned company where the best minds in their field want to work.[1] In federal contracting data it is classified as a Small Business that is US Owned and Veteran Owned.[1]

Five executives are listed with their compensation on the Red Dawn III award; the executives named on the parent [[cognition-cognos-support|Cognition Cognos Support]] contract differ, with the exception of Robert T. Dunn, whose reported compensation is not the same across the two records.[1] Only one executive maintains an active LinkedIn presence, apparently because the company is actively recruiting for many roles; the article observed that the remainder appear off the grid, which it noted is not unusual for defense contractors doing highly classified national security work.[1]

## Contracting history

ICR, INC. has been a government contractor since 2016, when it received about $964,000 in awards.[1] The article reports that the firm was largely inactive during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, with no awards in 2017–2018 and modest awards of $1.9 million in 2019 and $3.2 million in 2020.[1] Obligations then rose sharply after [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] assumed the presidency: $36 million in 2021, $69 million in 2022 and $41 million year-to-date in 2023.[1]

The article found this pattern notable given that Trump was rebuilding military infrastructure during those quiet years, and suggested the timeline could indicate a program paused pending a friendly administration — noting that activity resumed in 2019, after the 2018 midterms that restored [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] to the speakership.[1]

## Red Dawn III

Red Dawn III was awarded to ICR, INC. in July 2021 on a [[sole-source-contracting|sole source]] basis for roughly $2.6 million, with the justification that no other provider was capable of the work — a justification the company's records show dating back to 2016.[1] The scope is custom computer programming (NAICS 541511) supporting advanced development research on shipboard defense systems (PSC AC33), delivered to the [[united-states-air-force|United States Air Force]] via the [[air-force-life-cycle-management-center|Air Force Life Cycle Management Center]].[1] As of October 2023 the end date had been extended to April 2024 and [[usaspending-gov|USAspending.gov]] showed no payment recorded against the order.[1]
