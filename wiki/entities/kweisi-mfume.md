# Kweisi Mfume

Kweisi Mfume is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives representing Baltimore, Maryland, and a member of the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[1]

## IRS whistleblower hearing (July 2023)

Mfume was among several Democrats who invoked former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during their speaking time at the committee's July 19, 2023 hearing with [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] and [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]].[1] He quipped that he wanted to thank Republicans for gathering lawmakers and "almost distracting us from the biggest investigation that is going on right now… involving the former president."[1] Critics argued the remark exemplified the majority of Democratic responses at the hearing, which they said substituted deflection for a substantive rebuttal of the whistleblowers' testimony.[1]
