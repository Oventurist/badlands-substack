# Isser Harel

Isser Harel was the founding director of both [[mossad]] and [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] and is known as the "Father of Israeli Intelligence."[1]

Badlands Media highlights a 1980 interview in which Harel is said to have prophesied that "Islamic terrorists" would one day attack the "tallest building in NYC."[1] The account pairs this apparent prescience with Harel's role behind the failed [[lavon-affair|Lavon Affair]] of 1954, a Mossad false flag operation in which Egyptian-Israeli agents placed bombs inside British, American and Egyptian targets in the hope that Muslim groups would be blamed; Israel finally admitted to the operation 51 years later, in 2005.[1]

The article notes that Harel was not the only Israeli official to voice such a scenario in advance: [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] warned of the same in 1995.[1]
