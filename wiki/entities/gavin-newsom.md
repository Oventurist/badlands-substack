# Gavin Newsom

## 2028 Democratic power rankings

California Governor Gavin Newsom was placed atop [[axios|Axios]]'s July 2026 Democratic presidential "power rankings" alongside former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and Representative [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]], based on interviews with strategists, donors, elected officials and party insiders assessing influence, fundraising and national profile.[1] Axios cautioned the field remained fluid and could shift as additional Democratic governors and senators weighed bids.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the trio as evidence that Democrats "aren't interested in putting up competent contenders."[1]
