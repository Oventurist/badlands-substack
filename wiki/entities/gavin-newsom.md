# Gavin Newsom

Gavin Newsom is the Governor of California. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears principally as an antagonist of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's election-integrity and settlement policies.

## Senate Bill 73

As reported in the [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]], Newsom signed [[california-senate-bill-73|Senate Bill 73]], a measure sharply restricting law enforcement access to ballots and election records ahead of the California primaries. Newsom said the measure was intended to "clarify the rules of engagement," noting that violations can carry criminal penalties, fines, and up to three years in jail. Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that, given the weakness of existing ballot chain-of-custody practice, the law risked criminalizing status quo election administration.

## Tax on anti-weaponization fund payouts

Newsom also vowed to impose a 100% tax on any payouts California residents receive from the $1.776 billion "anti-weaponization" fund established through Trump's settlement with the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. He described the fund as a "slush fund" and tied it to Trump's pardons of January 6 defendants, stating that recipients were being "rewarded" after being pardoned for "beating up cops."

The Brief's commentary characterized the proposal as self-refuting: if a Californian receives compensation for having the federal government weaponized against them, the state government would in turn be weaponized to confiscate the payout through taxation. Ashe in America called the measure "petty and punitive and intended to preserve a false narrative," questioned how "this taxation come through representation," predicted it would not survive legal challenge, and urged Californians to sue.
