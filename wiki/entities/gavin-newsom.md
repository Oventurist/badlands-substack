# Gavin Newsom

Gavin Newsom is the Governor of California. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears principally as an antagonist of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's election-integrity and settlement policies.

## Senate Bill 73

As reported in the Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026, Newsom signed [[california-senate-bill-73|Senate Bill 73]], a measure sharply restricting law enforcement access to ballots and election records ahead of the California primaries. Newsom said the measure was intended to "clarify the rules of engagement," noting that violations can carry criminal penalties, fines, and up to three years in jail. Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that, given the weakness of existing ballot chain-of-custody practice, the law risked criminalizing status quo election administration.

## Tax on anti-weaponization fund payouts

Newsom also vowed to impose a 100% tax on any payouts California residents receive from the $1.776 billion "anti-weaponization" fund established through Trump's settlement with the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. He described the fund as a "slush fund" and tied it to Trump's pardons of January 6 defendants, stating that recipients were being "rewarded" after being pardoned for "beating up cops."

The Brief's commentary characterized the proposal as self-refuting: if a Californian receives compensation for having the federal government weaponized against them, the state government would in turn be weaponized to confiscate the payout through taxation. Ashe in America called the measure "petty and punitive and intended to preserve a false narrative," questioned how "this taxation come through representation," predicted it would not survive legal challenge, and urged Californians to sue.

## The DOJ investigation (June 2026)

The Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026 reports that Newsom announced California is facing a [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] investigation, which he characterized as politically motivated. Newsom said the state intends to challenge the federal action while seeking additional details about the scope of the inquiry. The [[white-house|White House]] responded that there are multiple investigations into Newsom and his wife, including with regard to their taxes.

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the episode as an inversion of the governor's own framing, writing that Newsom "is claiming that the government is being weaponized against him in the DOJ's investigation of, apparently, Newsom and his wife's taxes — is there anything more 'weaponized government' that a governor cheating on his taxes?" She appends the qualification that the allegation is unproven and that "Gavin Newsom is innocent until proven guilty." The item appears alongside the [[james-comey|Comey]] amicus ruling and the [[carter-page|Carter Page]] certiorari denial in a set of stories the Brief reads as a single pattern of [[weaponization-of-government|weaponized government]].
