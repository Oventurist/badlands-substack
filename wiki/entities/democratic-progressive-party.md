# Democratic Progressive Party

The Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) is the ruling party of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] and the principal target of Beijing's rhetorical and economic pressure in the run-up to Taiwan's January 2024 elections.[1]

In late December 2023, with the election just over two weeks away, China warned of further trade sanctions against Taiwan should the DPP "stubbornly" continue to support independence.[1] Chen Binhua, spokesman for the [[taiwan-affairs-office|Taiwan Affairs Office]], said that if the DPP authorities "refuse to repent," Beijing would support "the relevant departments taking further measures in accordance with the regulations."[1] The warnings coincided with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s pledge to use "any means" to stop Taiwan seceding from China.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the escalating cross-strait rhetoric as an instance of a recurring narrative pattern — seeds planted in advance of a possible conflict, as had been the case before the Russia-Ukraine war "went hot" in 2022 — and argued that global instability during the Biden years would strengthen the public mandate for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return.[1]
