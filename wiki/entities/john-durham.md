# John Durham

John Durham is a former United States Attorney who was appointed Special Counsel to investigate the origins and conduct of the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2016 counterintelligence investigation into the Trump campaign, known as [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]].

## The Durham report

As summarized in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, Durham's final report, released in 2023, criticized aspects of the FBI's handling of intelligence but did not establish a criminal conspiracy involving senior officials. The Brief treats the disclosure of a new congressional witness, [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]], as part of the continuing "Durham-era fallout" — that is, the ongoing scrutiny of how assessments of Russian interference in the 2016 and 2020 elections were developed and communicated, including the [[2017-intelligence-community-assessment|2017 Intelligence Community Assessment]].

Within the Badlands framing, the limits of Durham's findings are contrasted with what contributors describe as a subsequent "justice phase" in which cooperating witnesses and rapid disclosures may reach conclusions the special counsel did not.
