# R. Kelly

R. Kelly is an American singer who became one of the most prominent figures convicted in the wave of celebrity sex-crime prosecutions of the early 2020s.[1] In February 2023, at the age of 56, he was sentenced by a federal judge in Chicago — his hometown — to 20 years in prison following convictions for producing child pornography and for the enticement of minors for sex.[1]

## Chicago federal sentencing

U.S. District Judge Harry Leinenweber imposed the 20-year term on Thursday, February 23, 2023, but ordered that nearly all of it be served simultaneously with a separate 30-year sentence handed down the previous year in New York on racketeering charges.[1] Leinenweber additionally ordered Kelly to serve one year in prison following the completion of the New York sentence, meaning the Chicago case added a single year of incarceration in practice.[1]

The central question going into the Chicago sentencing was whether the judge would run the new sentence concurrently with, or consecutively to, the New York term arising from Kelly's 2021 racketeering and sex-trafficking convictions.[1] A fully consecutive sentence would have been tantamount to a life sentence for the singer.[1]
