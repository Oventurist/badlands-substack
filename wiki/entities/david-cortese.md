# David Cortese

**David Cortese** is a Democratic member of the California State Senate representing San Jose. He is the author of [[california-senate-bill-553|Senate Bill 553]], a workplace-violence measure that passed the state Senate on May 31, 2023 by a vote of 29 to 8.[1]

The bill would require employers to keep violent-incident logs, provide active-shooter and shoplifter training, and abandon policies requiring workers to confront suspected shoplifters.[1] Cortese said the legislation is intended to "help employers keep employees safe at work."[1] Critics, including the California Realtors Association, argued the measure went far beyond retail and would encourage theft.[1]
