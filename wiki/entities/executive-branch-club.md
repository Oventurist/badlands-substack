# Executive Branch (club)

Executive Branch is an ultra-exclusive private club in Georgetown, Washington, D.C., created by and for the wealthiest figures in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s orbit.[1]

## Inaugural dinner (September 2025)

The club's much-anticipated inaugural dinner was held on a Wednesday evening in early September 2025 and doubled as a birthday party for MAGA-friendly podcaster [[chamath-palihapitiya|Chamath Palihapitiya]].[1] A long table for the roughly thirty guests was set with top-of-the-line crystal and china.[1]

The guest list included Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Interior Secretary Doug Burgum, Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins, Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], SBA Administrator Kelly Loeffler, Medicare and Medicaid chief Mehmet Oz, and venture capitalist [[david-sacks|David Sacks]], Palihapitiya's partner on the "All In" podcast.[1]

The evening was marred when Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] confronted [[federal-housing-finance-agency|FHFA]] director [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] in an expletive-laden altercation.[1] Club co-owner and financier [[omeed-malik|Omeed Malik]] intervened, with Bessent demanding that Malik choose between the two men.[1]
