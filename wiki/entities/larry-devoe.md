# Larry Devoe

Larry Devoe is a Venezuelan official who was named acting Attorney General of [[venezuela|Venezuela]] in February 2026, succeeding [[tarek-william-saab|Tarek William Saab]].[1]

## Background and appointment

Devoe previously served as head of Venezuela's National Council of Human Rights.[1] He assumed the acting attorney general role after Saab resigned following nearly nine years in office and was immediately appointed acting Ombudsman by the [[national-assembly-of-venezuela|National Assembly]].[1] The legislature simultaneously created a 13-member parliamentary commission with a 30-day deadline to evaluate candidates and define the permanent heads of both the Attorney General's Office and the Ombudsman's Office, leaving Devoe's tenure explicitly provisional.[1]

Human rights organizations criticized the reshuffle, questioning the independence of Venezuela's prosecutorial and human rights institutions even as other reforms — including a limited [[venezuela-amnesty-law|amnesty law]] — advanced through the legislature.[1]
