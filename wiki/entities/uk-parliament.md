# U.K. Parliament

The **U.K. Parliament** is the legislature that passed the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] in September 2023, the measure Badlands Media identified as the most recent effort in the ongoing wave of pre-election censorship attacks.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Claims made for the legislation

Parliament claimed the bill would make the United Kingdom "the safest place" in the world to be online.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The article reads this claim inversely, arguing that the practical result will be a far more censored, locked-down internet for British users and a legislative precedent exportable to other nations aligned with the Western hegemonic establishment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Anticipated enforcement posture

In the months following passage, the article predicted Parliament would issue threats to various platforms demanding the removal of effective critics of the state on the grounds that they are "peddling harmful conspiracy theories."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The statute's asymmetric structure — criminalising false messages under clause 156 while immunising newspapers, television channels and streaming services under clause 157 — is cited as evidence that the legislature intended to regulate independent voices rather than incumbent media.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Enforcement authority is vested in [[ofcom]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)
