# UK Parliament

The **UK Parliament** is the legislature of the United Kingdom and, in Badlands Media's account, a central actor in the 2023–2024 wave of Western censorship activity.[1]

## Passage of the Online Safety Bill

In September 2023 Parliament passed the long-awaited [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]], legislation the [[british-government|British Government]] had, in the author's words, "been drooling over for a while."[1] Parliament claimed the bill would make the UK "the safest place" in the world to be online; Badlands read this as meaning a much more censored, locked-down internet for British users and a precedent for other nations aligned with the Western hegemonic establishment.[1]

## Anticipated enforcement posture

The article predicts that in the months following passage, Parliament will issue threats to various platforms demanding the removal of effective critics of the state on the grounds that they are "peddling harmful conspiracy theories."[1] The targeting of [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]] is presented as an early instance of this dynamic, and the removal of [[rumble|Rumble]] from the UK as its logical endpoint.[1]
