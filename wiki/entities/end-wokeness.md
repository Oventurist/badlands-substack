# End Wokeness

End Wokeness is a prominent conservative account on [[x-corp|X]] with over 1.8 million followers that in November 2023 publicized [[target|Target]]'s Christmas merchandise as an example of corporate "woke" excess.[1]

The account shared several photographs of Christmas ornaments then offered for sale at Target, writing "Target outdid itself this year for Christmas," with one image showing a black Santa Claus seated in a wheelchair and another a figurine wearing a multi-colored "pride" hat while holding a rainbow flag.[1] The post drew a wave of critical replies, several of which hinted at or explicitly called for a renewed boycott — "Go woke, go broke!" wrote one user, while another said "Everyone getting ready to boycott Target again until they go bankrupt."[1] Other commenters recalled the earlier boycott calls that followed Target's summer "pride" clothing line, which had included LGBT-themed items marketed for children.[1]
