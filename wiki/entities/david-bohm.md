# David Bohm

**David Bohm** is the physicist whose concept of the *implicate order* is used in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series to describe what the essay calls the sixth dimension of consciousness.[1]

## Implicate order in the article

The essay presents Bohm's implicate order as positing that phenomena which appear independent are in fact unfoldings of a deeper, hidden whole, with everything enfolded into everything else.[1] This is offered not as philosophy but as physics, supporting the claim that in the sixth dimension consciousness is no longer an isolated signal but a node in a vast field, so that "what happens in one, resonates in all."[1]

## Pairing with Sheldrake

Bohm is paired with [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]], whose [[morphic-field|morphic resonance]] theory is described as complementing the implicate order by holding that memory is stored in fields rather than brains.[1] The article distinguishes the two: Bohm focused on quantum unity and the fluidity of matter, while Sheldrake introduced the idea that behavior and pattern are transmitted across time and space vibrationally rather than genetically.[1] Together, the essay argues, they produce a model in which coherence becomes communicable and contagious — the basis for its claim that whistleblowers, citizen journalists, healers, and artists function as "energetic field influencers."[1]

## Cited in "The Shift" (March 2025)

Bohm is invoked again in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], which cites his suggestion that reality is an "unbroken wholeness" beneath its fragmented surface.[2] The essay uses the phrase to argue that awakening attunes a person to that wholeness even while the external world clings to division, and pairs it with [[ervin-laszlo|Ervin Laszlo]]'s description of the universe as a symphony of vibrations in support of its account of [[discernment|discernment]] as a compass.[2]
