# Uganda

**Uganda** is a landlocked country in East Africa bordering the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]], with its capital at Kampala.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 Uganda appears as a country into which the 2026 [[ebola|Ebola]] outbreak spread from Ituri province in the northeastern DRC. Confirmed cases of the [[bundibugyo-virus|Bundibugyo]] strain were reported in major population centers including Kampala. The Ugandan government participated with the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], the Congolese government and partner organizations in scaled-up response operations involving intensified surveillance, patient isolation and contact monitoring, supported by roughly $500 million pledged for response and prevention. The Bundibugyo strain itself is named for a Ugandan district.
