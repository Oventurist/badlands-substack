# Ashleigh Merchant

Ashleigh Merchant is a criminal defense attorney in Georgia who represented [[michael-roman|Michael Roman]], a co-defendant in the [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|Georgia election interference (RICO) case]] brought by Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani T. Willis]].[1] Merchant authored the motion that first publicly alleged an improper romantic relationship between Willis and special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], the filing that triggered the effort to disqualify Willis from the prosecution.[1]

## Role in the disqualification fight

Defense attorneys sought to disqualify Willis on the theory that her romantic relationship with the lawyer she hired to run the case had created an untenable conflict of interest.[1] Willis and Wade maintained that the relationship began only after she hired him in November 2021; attorneys for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] accused them of lying.[1]

Text messages between Merchant and [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]] — Wade's former law partner and divorce lawyer — became central evidence. In exchanges entered into the record, Merchant asked Bradley, "Do you think it happened before she hired him?" and Bradley replied, "Absolutely," indicating the romance predated Wade's November 1, 2021 hiring.[1]

At the February 27, 2024 hearing, the movants established that Bradley had reviewed Merchant's motion, made edits, and pronounced it "looks good," and that he raised no objection to any defect in the filing beyond his own edits — testimony the defense used to argue Bradley had effectively approved the motion's account of the relationship.[1] Reporting from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] noted that Trump attorney Steve Sadow pressed Bradley on why he would speculate to Merchant about allegations against a man he considered a friend, knowing she intended to file them.[1]
