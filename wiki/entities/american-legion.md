# American Legion

The American Legion is a United States veterans' organization that figures in accounts of the 1930s plot against President [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]].[1] In the scheme exposed by General [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]], Butler was to serve as a "puppet dictator" leading thousands of American legionnaires in a storming of the White House to displace Roosevelt.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that in the early 1920s and 1930s the Legion was modeled on [[benito-mussolini|Mussolini]]'s fascist squadristi.[1] Legion leader Alvin Owsley made the parallel explicit in 1921: "If need be the American Legion is ready to protect the institutions of this country and its ideals, in the same way as the Fascists have treated the destructive forces threatening Italy. Don't forget that the Fascists are for today's Italy what the American Legion is for the United States."[1]

Butler's public revelations before Congress amplified Roosevelt's popular support and inoculated much of the population against Wall Street propaganda, and the [[business-plot|coup attempt]] came undone before it could succeed.[1]
