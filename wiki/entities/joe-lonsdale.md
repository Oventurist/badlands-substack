# Joe Lonsdale

Joe Lonsdale is an American venture capitalist, the founder of the venture capital firm [[8vc|8VC]], and a co-founder of [[palantir|Palantir]], the defence-focused data analytics company associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1]

In July 2025, Lonsdale was reported to be among the high-profile technology investors backing [[erebor|Erebor]], a proposed United States bank led by [[palmer-luckey|Palmer Luckey]] and intended to serve start-ups and cryptocurrency firms after the collapse of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]].[1]

## Comments on Zelensky's Pennsylvania trip (September 2024)

In September 2024, Lonsdale — Palantir co-founder and [[8vc|8VC]] founding partner — said that Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s taxpayer-funded campaign-season visit to Pennsylvania was "a big, big deal."[2] Writing on X, he said: "Team Kamala is so concerned about Pennsylvania, they're using military assets to fly in Zelensky to campaign!"[2] He added that "they're implying their war creates jobs, and are forcing Z to attack Trump & Vance," describing the arrangement as "literally funding a foreign leader and using the military to bring him to campaign with them in a battleground state."[2]
