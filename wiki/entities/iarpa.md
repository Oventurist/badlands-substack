# IARPA

The Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity (IARPA) is the advanced research and development arm of the United States intelligence community, operating under the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]].[1]

## SMART ePANTS

On August 22, 2023, ODNI announced that IARPA would develop [[smart-epants|SMART ePANTS]] — Smart Electrically Powered and Networked Textile Systems — a computerized clothing program, over the following three and a half years. It was described as a first-of-its-kind effort by the intelligence community to fashion smart clothing.[1]

IARPA, in partnership with the Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific, awarded research contracts totaling more than $22 million to [[nautilus-defense|Nautilus Defense]] and [[leidos|Leidos, Inc.]], according to a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announcement of August 9. [[sri-international|SRI International]], the [[massachusetts-institute-of-technology|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] and Areté received undisclosed amounts to develop the technology, according to [[the-intercept|The Intercept]].[1]

Badlands Media presents IARPA's role as evidence that the intelligence community has begun turning civilian clothing into another vector for surveillance, part of a wider [[technocracy|technocratic]] push toward pervasive biometric data collection.[1]
