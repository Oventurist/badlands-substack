# Committee of 300

The **Committee of 300** is a body alleged in conspiracist literature to be a supreme council of aristocratic, financial, and intelligence figures directing world affairs.[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* names the Committee of 300 alongside the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]] and the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] as bodies that convened "not merely to discuss policy, but to script the spiritual and technological scaffolding of control."[1] The essay places these groups at the junction where, in its telling, monarchic authority gave way to financier and technocratic authority — "as kings bowed to financiers, another layer fused: secret societies."[1]

Figures the essay associates with this intellectual scaffolding include [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]], Henry Kissinger, and [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]], with rising technocrats such as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] described as having been initiated into their legacy networks.[1]

No verified documentation establishes the Committee of 300 as an existing organization; the claim originates largely with the writings of John Coleman.[1]
