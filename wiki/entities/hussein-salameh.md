# Hussein Salameh

Hussein Salameh is the intelligence chief of [[syria|Syria]] under the post-Assad government.[1]

Salameh took part in the US-hosted Paris talks that produced the Israel–Syria joint "fusion mechanism" for intelligence sharing and de-escalation, attending alongside Syrian Foreign Minister [[assad-al-shaibani|Assad al-Shaibani]] and US envoys [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]].[1] The channel the parties agreed to establish is designed to manage sensitive exchanges, coordinate security messaging and resolve disputes promptly under US supervision.[1]
