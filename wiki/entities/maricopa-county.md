# Maricopa County

Maricopa County is the most populous county in Arizona and encompasses the Phoenix metropolitan area, the "Valley of the Sun." Because it contains a majority of the state's voters, its election administration has an outsized effect on statewide outcomes, and in the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as the central battleground of American election-integrity disputes — the "ground zero" of [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]]'s November 22, 2022 article [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]].

## Election administration in 2022

The county is governed by the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]], chaired in 2022 by [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], and its ballots are tabulated at the [[maricopa-county-tabulation-and-election-center|Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center]]. Legal representation for the county's election functions is handled by the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]], whose Civil Division Chief in 2022 was [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]]. The county contracts with [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]] for ballot printing and processing, and transport trucks running between the county recorder's office and that vendor were the subject of one of Gates's explanatory videos.

## The November 8, 2022 general election

On election day 2022 the county experienced widespread equipment failures. According to the county's own admission, cited in a November 19 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]], "at least 60 voting locations had issues related to some ballot-on-demand (BOD) printers having printer configuration settings that were non-uniform," producing ballots that on-site tabulators could not read. Although machines had been tested the previous day, malfunctions began within thirty minutes of polls opening.

Voters at affected sites were directed to deposit ballots in an auxiliary container known as [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]] (labeled "Box 3" at some locations) for later central-count tabulation. Wright's letter asserts that the county "appears to have failed to adhere to the statutory guidelines in segregating, counting, tabulating, tallying, and transporting the 'Door 3' ballots," that the county admitted non-tabulated Door 3 ballots were commingled with tabulated ballots at some locations, and that a sworn observer complaint alleged more than 1,700 non-tabulated Door 3 ballots from a single location were placed in black duffle bags reserved for tabulated ballots. A further set of complaints concerned "check-out" procedures: voters redirected from a failing location were shown in the system as already checked in at the first site, leaving them without a means to vote, apparently because poll workers were inadequately trained in the provisional ballot process.

Wright requested a response before the county submitted its official canvass to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]], which under A.R.S. § 16-642(A) had to occur on or before November 28, 2022. The article notes that under subsection C of the same statute, missing returns require the canvass to be postponed "from day to day" for up to six postponements.

## Treatment in the corpus

The corpus presents Maricopa County officials — most of them nominally Republican — as either incompetent or complicit, and treats the county's 2022 failures as continuous with disputes over the 2020 election and the subsequent Arizona audit. Gillespie describes the county's public communications effort as a "comedy show" and reports that a volunteer for [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] received a dismissive reply from Tom Liddy when seeking answers.
