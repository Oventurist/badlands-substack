# Ben Fulford

**Ben Fulford** is a writer in the alternative-media space who, together with the publication [[veterans-today|Veterans Today]], is credited in Badlands Media's "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Agents from Russia" with popularizing the term "[[khazarian-mafia|Khazarian mafia]]" from at least 2015 onward.[1]

## Treatment in the source

The essay's author states plainly that he does not personally trust either Fulford or Veterans Today as sources, while arguing that their role in introducing the phrase into common conspiracy parlance is nonetheless notable.[1] He recalls having learned the term from those outlets long ago, and frames their output as part of a broader body of conspiracy literature that may have been disseminated to steer public attention toward the historical significance of the [[khazaria|Khazarian]] region — adding the caveat that "all professional-grade disinfo contains some truth."[1]
