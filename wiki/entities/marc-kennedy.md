# Marc Kennedy

Marc Kennedy is a Canadian curler who competed as third for the Canadian men's team at the [[2026-winter-olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]].[1]

## Double-touch accusation

During Canada's 8–6 men's round-robin victory over Sweden, Swedish curler [[oskar-eriksson|Oskar Eriksson]] alleged that Kennedy had touched his stone after release, a "double-touch" violation that under [[world-curling|World Curling]] rules requires removal of the stone.[1] Kennedy vehemently denied any wrongdoing and responded on the ice with profanity, telling Eriksson, "I haven't done it once. You can fuck off," to which Eriksson replied that he would show video after the game in which the touch occurred "two meters over the hog line."[1]

Kennedy later suggested his team may have been deliberately targeted in what he characterized as a planned effort to catch violations.[1] World Curling subsequently issued a verbal warning to Canadian officials regarding the language used by a Canadian men's player, noting that further inappropriate behavior under rule R.19 could bring additional sanctions.[1]
