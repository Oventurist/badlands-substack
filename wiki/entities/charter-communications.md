# Charter Communications

Charter Communications is a leading American pay-television and broadband provider. It appeared in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief of July 2, 2025 after concluding that a sabotage attack on its network infrastructure in Van Nuys, California amounted to domestic terrorism.[1]

## Van Nuys fiber attack

On June 15, 2025, Charter's network in Van Nuys was attacked in the early morning hours. An internal company investigation subsequently determined that the incident constituted "an act of domestic terrorism," a conclusion the company said was based on "the nature of the fiber cuts, extent of damage and makeup of impacted customers."[1]

The investigation found that thirteen cables had been severed, spanning more than 2,600 individual optical fibers.[1]

## Impact

The cuts affected a broad cross-section of critical services. Emergency services were disrupted, including communications serving a US military base, emergency dispatch and 911 systems, and local fire and police departments. Financial institutions, court buildings, healthcare facilities and hospitals, educational institutions, and cell towers providing mobile service were also affected.[1]

## Policy response

Citing the pattern of targeting, Charter called for stronger federal and state legislation to protect first responders, military bases and other institutions that depend on commercial communications infrastructure.[1]
