# Antonio Gramsci

**Antonio Gramsci** was a political theorist whose concept of [[cultural-hegemony|cultural hegemony]] is invoked in Badlands Media commentary on psychological control.[1] A February 11, 2025 essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] quotes him as saying that "every system of control begins with fragmentation," and describes his theory as highlighting how dominant groups manipulate societal values to normalize their own authority.[1]

The essay argues that this principle is employed under [[fourth-generation-warfare|Fourth-Generation Warfare]] to fracture communities, erode trust in local institutions, and amplify division, creating conditions in which isolated individuals seek security in centralized governance.[1] It further applies Gramsci's notion of a "culture of consent" to explain the passivity it attributes to populations raised under GDS: compliance is not enforced through overt coercion but normalized through subtle, pervasive cultural cues embedded in daily life, from media consumption to workplace hierarchies, until obedience appears to be a rational and even moral imperative.[1]
