# Crown Prosecution Service

The Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) is the principal public prosecution agency of England and Wales, responsible for deciding whether to charge criminal cases investigated by the police and for conducting prosecutions in the courts.

## Definition of misconduct in public office (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief cites the CPS for the definition of the offence at the centre of the former Prince Andrew's arrest: [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]] applies to abuse or neglect of power by individuals in government and public service roles and carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment, according to the Crown Prosecution Service. The citation appears in the Brief's coverage of the February 19, 2026 arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor by [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]] on suspicion of that offence.
