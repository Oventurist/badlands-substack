# Crown Prosecution Service

The Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) is the principal public prosecuting authority for England and Wales, responsible for charging decisions and for prosecuting criminal cases investigated by police forces such as [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]].[1]

## Role in the Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor case

Following the February 2026 arrest of [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]] on suspicion of [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]], the CPS definition of the offence was widely cited: it applies to abuse or neglect of power by individuals in government and public service roles, and carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment.[1] Andrew was released under investigation on the evening of his arrest, leaving any charging decision open.[1]

Commentators in the [[truth-community|Truth Community]], including [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], argued that the reliance on a reputation-and-process offence rather than charges touching the substance of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein operation]] was itself evidence of a managed outcome.[1]
