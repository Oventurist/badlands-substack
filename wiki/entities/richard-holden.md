# Richard Holden

Richard Holden is a Conservative Member of the United Kingdom Parliament and a leading critic of first-cousin marriage in Britain.[1]

## Criticism of NHS cousin-marriage guidance

Holden was among the critics who condemned the [[nhs|National Health Service]] in September 2025 after [[nhs-england|NHS England]]'s Genomics Education Programme issued guidance highlighting supposed benefits of first-cousin marriage, including "stronger extended family support systems and economic advantages."[1] Holden responded that "Our NHS should stop taking the knee to damaging and oppressive cultural practices."[1]

He further criticised the governing Labour Party for ignoring calls to end [[cousin-marriage|cousin marriage]] as a means of deterring chain migration, though reporting noted that his own Conservative Party did not end the practice during its fourteen years in office from 2010 to mid-2024 either.[1]
