# Tatneft

Tatneft is a Russian oil company whose Taneco refinery, located in the city of Nizhnekamsk in the industrialized republic of Tatarstan southeast of Moscow, is Russia's third-largest refinery.[1] The plant has capacity to process 340,000 barrels per day of crude oil.[1]

## April 2024 drone strike

On Tuesday, April 2, 2024, Ukrainian drones struck the Taneco refinery's primary refining unit — a unit with capacity of roughly 155,000 barrels per day — in an attack carried out more than 800 miles from the front line, the latest in a series of Ukrainian strikes on Russian refining infrastructure.[1] The unit caught fire, though Russian media reported that the blaze was swiftly extinguished.[1]

Ramil Mullin, the mayor of Nizhnekamsk, wrote on Telegram that "there are no injuries or serious damage" and added that "the technological process of the enterprise has not been disrupted."[1] A source with Ukrainian intelligence in Kyiv told Reuters that Ukraine had struck the major Russian oil facility in Tatarstan in order to reduce Russian oil revenues.[1]
