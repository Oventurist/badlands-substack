# Rodney Hinton Jr.

Rodney Hinton Jr. is an Ohio man charged with aggravated murder in the death of a Hamilton County sheriff's deputy who was struck by a vehicle in Cincinnati in May 2025.[1]

## The incident

Authorities said Hinton "purposely" struck and killed the deputy, who was directing traffic near the University of Cincinnati at the time.[1] The killing occurred the day after Hinton's 18-year-old son, Ryan Hinton, was fatally shot by a Cincinnati police officer.[1] According to news reports cited in Badlands commentary, Ryan Hinton was shot after pointing a gun at police during an active pursuit.[1]

Badlands commentary noted that the deputy killed was unrelated to the shooting of Hinton's son, describing the act as indiscriminate.[1]

## Prosecution

Prosecuting attorney Ryan Nelson stated that Hinton's movements in his car were "calculated and premeditated," adding: "He lined up his car, deliberately accelerated his car and purposely caused the death of an on-duty deputy sheriff."[1] Commentary argued that if that account is proven at trial, Hinton should never be released from prison.[1]
