# Healthline

Healthline is a health-information media outlet singled out by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as an example of what he called a "well-financed propaganda mouthpiece."[1]

In an author's note appended to his survey of food additives, DeLarme predicted that readers researching additives online would inevitably encounter Healthline offering assurances that claims of harm are false, despite what he described as scientific evidence to the contrary in his sources section.[1] He directed readers to examine the outlet's primary funders — characterized as "a who's who of big-money investment firms containing many dozens of corporations each" — and noted that lawsuits against those funders include everything from securities fraud to violations of the Clean Water Act.[1] The recommendation was that readers weigh that ownership structure before extending blind trust to the outlet's conclusions.[1]

DeLarme's broader advice in the same passage concerned search engines: he argued that Google would supply a surplus of damage-control articles and testimonials, that DuckDuckGo would be marginally better, and that Qwant or Freespoke would surface material otherwise unavailable.[1]
