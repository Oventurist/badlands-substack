# Albert Pike

Albert Pike is identified in the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin" as a Scottish Rite Grandmaster, Confederate general and leading occultist.[1]

The article cites Pike as having taken note of what it calls the Templar–Jesuit connection when he wrote in his *Morals and Dogma* that "Templars were unintelligent and therefore unsuccessful Jesuits."[1] The remark is invoked in the course of the essay's argument that the order of St. Francis of Assisi served as a bridge between the Cistercian elite who created the Knights Templar and the later Jesuits — a lineage that [[francis-dashwood|Sir Francis Dashwood]] is said to have referenced in naming his [[hellfire-club|Hell-Fire Club]] chapter "The Order of Knights of St. Francis."[1]
