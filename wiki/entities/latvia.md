# Latvia

Latvia is a Baltic state and member of [[nato|NATO]].[1] In 2025 it emerged as one of the alliance members moving to expand its armed forces in response to what member governments describe as a Russian threat — a characterization Moscow denies.[1]

## Conscription policy

Latvia reintroduced mandatory military service in 2023, having scrapped the draft in 2006; under the reinstated system only men are conscripted.[1] In September 2025, Defense Minister Andris Spruds told Delfi TV that his party, the Progressives, would submit a proposal the following year requiring both men and women to serve in the military from 2028, and noted that female-specific equipment was already being introduced into the National Armed Forces in preparation.[1]

Latvia reportedly plans to more than double the size of its military to 31,000 troops by 2028, up from around 15,000 in 2021.[1] If the proposal is adopted, Latvia would follow [[denmark|Denmark]], which announced in March 2025 that it would begin drafting women from 2026, with Copenhagen framing the move as ensuring "gender equality" in its armed forces and helping meet NATO commitments.[1]
