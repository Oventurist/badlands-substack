# Yossi Cohen

Yossi Cohen is the former head of [[mossad|Mossad]], the Israeli foreign intelligence service.[1]

## DR Congo ban and resignation

Cohen was banned for life from the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] after he was caught traveling covertly around the country with sanctioned mining magnate [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] in 2019, allegedly attempting to launch a coup against the new government that had ousted [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]] in an election.[1] When journalists reported these revelations, the disclosures led to Cohen's forced resignation from the leadership of Mossad in 2022.[1]
