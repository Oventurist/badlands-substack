# Orbis Business Intelligence

Orbis Business Intelligence is the private intelligence firm founded by former MI6 officer [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]], through which the research underlying the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]] was produced.[1]

## Employment of Danchenko

Beginning in March 2016, Steele hired [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] to work for Orbis and to investigate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[paul-manafort|Paul Manafort]], [[carter-page|Carter Page]], [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] and others in or associated with the Trump campaign, the goal being to establish some basis for the accusations of collusion between Russia and the campaign.[1] Information Danchenko supplied was placed into what were variously called the Steele Reports or Company Reports, and he became Steele's sub-source.[1] Some of that material was later used in [[carter-page-fisa-warrants|FISA warrant applications]] targeting Carter Page and the Trump campaign, and the reporting reached the [[fbi|FBI]] on 19 September 2016.[1]

Individual Orbis products were identified at the 2022 Danchenko trial by number — most prominently [[dossier-report-2016-95|report 2016/95]], entered as Government Exhibit 109, whose "Source E" was identified as [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]].[1] Testimony from FBI Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] established that the Bureau was never able to corroborate the allegations in the firm's reporting, and that Steele himself could not substantiate it even when offered as much as $1,000,000 to do so.[1]
