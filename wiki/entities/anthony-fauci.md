# Anthony Fauci

Anthony Fauci appears in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s *A Virus, or a Metaphor?* as one of the two officials Carlson holds responsible for the bioweapon program he believes preceded COVID-19.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Allegation in the article

Carlson writes that while he believes some sort of bioweapon was released from a lab in [[wuhan-lab|Wuhan]], China, he does not think it originated there; he places its origin in a lab in North Carolina.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He alleges that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and Fauci funded and ran this program, and that with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] coming into office they had to move it overseas to maintain secrecy and control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Carlson further believes other bioweapons were under development in biolabs around the world, specifically in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and possibly Taiwan.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

No documentary evidence for these claims is presented in the article; they are stated as the author's belief.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## "Soldier for the Communist Cause"

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] published a December 20, 2022 opinion column titled "Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause," the first written submission to the outlet by contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]], who writes under the handle RattlerGator.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) White framed the piece as a companion to the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] disclosures, which he expected would eventually turn to Fauci, and cited [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s tweet declaring that his pronouns were "Prosecute/Fauci" as a sign that accountability for the man he called "Public Enemy Number One" was near.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

White noted that Fauci was educated in the Jesuit tradition of "men for others," and argued that, like many Catholics and other Christians, he had evolved into a "humanist" — a transition White compared to the rise of liberation theology in South America and characterised as leaving the discipline of "men for God."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) In White's view humanists are "most often indistinguishable from Communists," and the label functions as word warfare.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Role in the COVID-19 narrative

The column's central complaint concerns the "cases, cases, cases" hysteria of 2020, which White treats as a manufactured metric: claims of cases rather than incidents confirmed by any reliable testing method.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He insisted the only meaningful measure was deaths, not cases, and said he refused to credit the panic amplified by what he described as a remarkably coordinated range of organizations, corporations, and governments until credible evidence of extraordinary deaths was produced.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He cited a September 16, 2020 tweet by [[daniel-levitt|Daniel Levitt]] summarising eight claims about COVID — that lockdowns increase deaths, that Sweden was right, that Neil Ferguson was wrong, that masks are ineffective, that 95% are asymptomatic, that up to 80% may be immune, that transmission stops at roughly 20% antibodies, and that more cases do not equal more deaths — noting it had drawn 136 replies, 2,053 retweets and 4,085 likes before both the tweet and Levitt's account were erased from [[x-twitter|Twitter]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Characterisation as a Deep State instrument

White described Fauci as "the face of anti-American governmental overlords and the face of the willing executor of the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s bidding," a man who in his account overstepped his legal authority decades ago.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) Fauci is not portrayed as acting alone: White argued that he and others like him across governments, corporations and groups deploy subordinates — described using the "Bottom Bitches" coinage from *American Greatness* — who disseminate preferred narratives and insist on partisan censorship, offering Arizona's [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] as an example.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) The essay closes by promising a future column on a Florida example of Fauci's alleged direct hand in the reporting of COVID case counts.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Framing of censorship

White situated Fauci within a broader theory of subversion drawn from commentator [[j-e-dyer|J. E. Dyer]], who argued that censorship exists to prevent competition for preferred speech and that its purpose is gaslighting.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) On this reading the "cases, cases, cases" messaging and the narrative surrounding the January 6th event are instances of the same technique — lies of omission advanced through media purporting to speak for government agencies.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) White dated his own engagement with these themes to a 70-post Twitter thread on "the new kind of war," read aloud by [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] on the *Reading Epic Threads* series.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Senate Covid origins hearing (July 2026)

At the [[fauci-senate-covid-origins-hearing-2026|Senate hearing on Covid origins]] held in July 2026, Fauci refused to answer questions about the pandemic's origins and the pandemic response, invoking the Fifth Amendment and saying he feared Republicans would use his testimony to prosecute him for perjury.[3] In his opening statement he accused Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] of an "obvious obsession" with putting him "behind bars," citing the senator's calls for prosecution, "slanderous comments," and release of Fauci's personal journal, and said that under advice of counsel he would not answer questions despite decades of cooperating with Congress.[3]

Paul responded that the chairman had denied Fauci's assertion of privilege and directed him to answer, warned that obstructing a congressional investigation is against the law, and said the committee would consider what action to take.[3] Paul subsequently indicated he would pursue Fauci for contempt of Congress, the charge that imprisoned Steve Bannon and Peter Navarro.[3]

## Biotech ties and pardon question

Reporting by [[natalie-winters|Natalie Winters]] published around the hearing stated that Fauci quietly joined a stealth mRNA vaccine startup in 2023, that the Biden administration then awarded it more than $28 million to develop vaccines for viruses with pandemic potential, and that [[eli-lilly|Eli Lilly]] was buying the company for up to $1.55 billion.[3] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the accumulated allegations by writing that "the portfolio of crime is thick."[3]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] remarked that presidential pardons are very powerful and that Fauci could not be touched except through the Autopen angle.[3] Ashe in America argued that Fauci is the ideal test case for challenging a Biden-era pardon because support for prosecuting him is broad, and that the resulting litigation could reach the Supreme Court and force Justice [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] to recuse.[3]

## Senate subpoena and the 2026 accountability wave

On July 7, 2026, Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] announced a subpoena compelling Fauci to testify before the Senate.[4] The subpoena capped a series of legal and administrative actions that had accumulated since April 2026 against scientists affiliated with the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]], the institute Fauci ran for nearly 40 years.[4] Those actions included the debarment referral of virologist [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]], the indictment and armed arrest of former Fauci adviser [[david-morens|David Morens]], the removal of [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]] as acting NIAID head, and the indictment of [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]] over alleged mpox sample smuggling.[4]

Proponents of the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]], including figures in the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], framed these cases as accountability for scientists they accuse of hiding evidence of a possible laboratory origin in Wuhan, with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[4] [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] noted that all five cases centered on the small NIAID-affiliated scientific community Fauci had led.[4]

## The Fauci Files (July 2026)

In late July 2026, Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] released more than 1,100 pages of Fauci's private diary entries spanning late 2019 to 2022 as part of his investigation into COVID-19's origins and related research, a corpus known as the [[fauci-files|Fauci Files]].[5] The journals allegedly show Fauci privately noting as early as January 2020 that the Wuhan wet market was an "amplifier" rather than the source of the virus, and recording early scientific discussions in which multiple experts viewed features such as the furin cleavage site as potentially non-natural or "constructed" — private reflections that contrast with his public emphasis on natural animal-to-human spillover.[5] The diaries also capture Fauci's awareness of his rising media profile, which he described as flattering.[5]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the release as showing Fauci enriching himself and employing lawyers to find loopholes for further enrichment, lying about case and mortality rates, attempting to hide potential vaccine harms to pregnant women while mandating their vaccination, and concealing his own vaccine injury.[5] The entry regarding the vaccine injury was subsequently redacted by Paul, a change tracked by the writer Kanekoa, who also surfaced material indicating Fauci stopped the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] from reporting that the vaccines were not effective.[5]

HHS Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] accused Fauci of concealing a pulmonary infarction suffered five months after his January 2021 vaccination while continuing to promote the shots and dismiss risks as he received elite medical care, and charged that Fauci's policies produced the highest COVID mortality rate among major countries.[5] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to the release by saying he "grew tired of" Fauci, who "wanted to close everything down," while emphasizing that he left lockdown decisions to governors.[5]

Fauci was scheduled to testify before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee chaired by Paul, who has long alleged that Fauci misled [[congress|Congress]] on gain-of-function research and has referred the matter for potential prosecution; Paul indicated that a false statement in that testimony would strip Fauci of the protection of his [[autopen-scandal|autopen]] pardon.[5]

## CIA whistleblower allegations (2026)

In May 2026, CIA whistleblower and current agency employee [[james-erdman-iii|James Erdman III]] told the [[senate-homeland-security-and-governmental-affairs-committee|Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee]] that Fauci "injected" himself into the intelligence community's review of COVID-19's origins and influenced how the [[cia|CIA]] handled its assessment of a potential lab leak.[6] Erdman alleged Fauci played an "intentional" role in the agency's shift away from a 2021 plan to characterize the virus as lab-originated following a 90-day review, testifying that the CIA was "considering calling this a lab leak" on August 12, 2021, before its position changed on August 17 without documented explanation.[6]

"Dr. Fauci's role in the cover-up was intentional," Erdman testified, alleging that Fauci steered the analytical process toward "curated" lists of subject matter experts and scientists including contacts in the United States, United Kingdom, and Australia.[6] Erdman further alleged Fauci had contact with intelligence officials in 2020 and 2021, and that concerns about potential conflicts of interest tied to Wuhan-linked research were never raised internally.[6]

## ODNI declassification (June 2026)

In June 2026 the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] published previously undisclosed communications and documents concerning government assessments of COVID-19 origins and Fauci's role as former director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases.[7] Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] said the release followed a yearlong declassification review that incorporated testimony from multiple Intelligence Community whistleblowers.[7]

ODNI alleged that Fauci influenced Intelligence Community assessments regarding COVID-19 origins and asserted that the newly released correspondence contradicts testimony he gave to Congress in 2024.[7] The agency also said it had gathered whistleblower accounts alleging retaliation against analysts who challenged prevailing conclusions about the virus's origins, including claims that analysts supporting the lab-leak hypothesis faced professional repercussions and that dissenting views were suppressed; examples were referred to the [[intelligence-community-inspector-general|Intelligence Community Inspector General]].[7] The episode is covered in detail at [[odni-covid-origins-declassification-2026|the 2026 ODNI COVID-19 origins declassification]].[7]

## House COVID select subcommittee (March 2023)

At the first hearing of the House select subcommittee investigating the origins of COVID-19, held on March 8, 2023, former [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] Director [[robert-redfield]] testified that he had been sidelined from internal debates over the origins of the virus and that Fauci, then the White House chief medical adviser, did not appreciate Redfield's support for the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]].[8]

[[jim-jordan]] used the hearing to press on two of Fauci's top scientific advisers, [[kristian-andersen]] and [[robert-garry]], who had emailed Fauci on January 31, 2020 reporting that anomalies in the virus pointed toward a non-natural origin — Andersen writing that it had "unusual features" that "(potentially) look engineered" and that other scientists found the genome "inconsistent with expectations from evolutionary theory."[8] Jordan argued that the pair reversed their position three days later and that the only intervening event was a conference call with Fauci and [[francis-collins]], pointing to roughly $9 million in subsequent grant funding as the motive.[8]

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] described Fauci as a longtime intermediary for the biotech industry who knowingly advanced a lab-leak cover-up while presiding over pay-for-play research funding, and lamented the researchers, physicians and citizens whose reputations were destroyed for questioning "the science."[8]

## Third COVID-19 infection (August 2024)

In August 2024, Fauci revealed that he had been infected with COVID-19 for a third time despite having been "vaccinated and boosted a total of six times," saying "I got infected about two weeks ago, it was my third infection."[9] Reporting by [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] contrasted the admission with his 2021 assurances that "if you get vaccinated, you are protected" and that vaccinated people "can feel safe that they are not gonna get infected."[9] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the episode showed "the narrative is falling apart across the board," and called for two responses: public mockery as the appropriate sanction for a lack of virtue, and accountability for a COVID-19 response that, in her framing, coerced millions of Americans into a mass medical experiment without informed consent.[9]

## Congressional records request (February 2023)

Fauci was among the officials whose records were sought when the Republican-led [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]] opened its review of the federal pandemic response in February 2023.[10] Chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] requested phone records, calendars and internal documents covering Fauci in his capacity as former White House COVID adviser, alongside [[national-institutes-of-health|NIH]] Director [[francis-collins|Francis Collins]] and Secretary [[xavier-becerra|Xavier Becerra]], and the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] released some 900 pages to the panel.[10] Wenstrup said he would consider subpoenas against Fauci and others if the committee did not receive complete answers about COVID-19's origins.[10]

## Alleged CIA headquarters visit (September 2023)

In September 2023 the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]] alleged that Fauci had been "smuggled" into [[cia|CIA]] headquarters "without a record of entry," where he participated in the analysis in order to "influence" the agency's investigation into the origins of COVID-19.[11] The allegation was disclosed in a Tuesday-night letter from Subcommittee Chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] to the Inspector General of the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]], which demanded documents, communications and other evidence of contacts between Fauci and the CIA.[11]

The claim followed a report two weeks earlier, based on a self-described senior-level CIA whistleblower, that the agency had offered six analysts a significant monetary incentive to abandon their finding that SARS-CoV-2 likely originated in a laboratory in Wuhan, China.[11] According to Wenstrup's letter, six of the seven members of the review team believed the intelligence and science supported a low-confidence assessment of a laboratory origin, while the seventh — the most senior officer — was alone in favoring zoonosis; the six were described as experienced officers with significant scientific expertise.[11]

Badlands contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] wrote that the episode fit a widening pattern of revelations about Fauci's "true involvement with Covid," from his role as the voice behind lockdowns, masks and vaccines to his possible role in creating the virus, and asked whether Fauci was "a doctor, or a CIA operative working against his own people."[11]

## "Decidedly suboptimal" vaccine paper (2023)

In February 2023, Just the News reported that Fauci had waited until after stepping down as director of the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]] to publicly explain why [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]] had been so unreliable at halting infection and transmission beyond a few months.[12] Elsevier's medical journal Cell Host and Microbe published a "perspective" led by Fauci's office the previous month indicating that NIAID had good reason to believe COVID vaccines would fail even before they were authorized, based on research spanning Fauci's 38-year tenure leading the institute — a performance the paper characterized as "decidedly suboptimal."[12]

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] called Fauci a "liar and snake oil pusher" now in the hot seat as media and medical authorities began acknowledging the harms of mRNA vaccines.[12] Deschamps restated his theory that Fauci, [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] and others are not at the top of the pyramid but are fall guys meant to absorb public backlash, and argued the Cabal would use prosecution as an option only if forced.[12] He contended that the population had been so dumbed down that even a full confession would not produce demands for justice, and concluded that there would be no justice "until WE THE PEOPLE take back our civilization."[12]


## Prediction of another pandemic (April 2023)

On April 3, 2023, the by-then retired Fauci appeared at a town hall discussion with CNN's Jim Acosta at James Madison University, where he stated that "there will absolutely be an outbreak of another pandemic."[13] He suggested the next outbreak could come as soon as "next year," or might not arrive until within "grandchildren's lifetimes."[13] Writing at [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], [[canncon|CannCon]] argued that given Fauci's track record on predicting outbreaks the "absolutism" of the statement should not be taken lightly, and quipped that he could "already hear the mail-in ballot printers firing up."[13]

Commenting for the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situated the remark within what he called the [[pandemic-industrial-complex|pandemic industrial complex]], writing that "they didn't spend years building up this Pandemic Industrial Complex to blow it all on one measly global pandemic."[13] He linked the prediction to a claim by Dutch virologist [[marion-koopmans|Marion Koopmans]] that the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] was preparing for "10 years of viruses" with 2020 as year one, and argued that officials who war-game pandemic scenarios and hold financial stakes in the sector are conspicuously able to forecast when nature will supposedly unleash a pandemic without ever explaining how they reach those conclusions.[13]

## Rand Paul's call to test the Biden pardon (July 2025)

In July 2025, Kentucky Republican Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] urged the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] to bring charges against Fauci for allegedly lying to Congress about gain-of-function research, explicitly framing the prosecution as a way to test whether former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s blanket pardons would hold up in court.[14] Paul, chairman of the Senate Homeland Security Committee, told Turning Point USA founder [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] that he believed Fauci "committed a felony by lying to Congress," adding: "You have to charge him with a felony; take him to court, and then the court will decide whether or not the pardon is upheld."[14]

Badlands commentary endorsed the approach, arguing that pardons will likely stand until challenged and that challenging even one could serve as the catalyst for accountability on the [[autopen-scandal|autopen scandal]], described as an overt act in what the outlet calls the "Grand Conspiracy."[14] Commentators held that Fauci was the appropriate test case because his pardon was "the most offensive."[14]

Badlands also republished a summary timeline of controversies attached to Fauci's career: criticism of a slow and bureaucratic response to the AIDS crisis in the 1980s; the 1992 AZT drug pricing and safety controversy; Project BioShield oversight and biodefense spending in 2003–2004; [[gain-of-function-research|gain-of-function]] and global biosecurity concerns from 2011 to 2014; the 2020 reversal on mask guidance during the early COVID-19 response; NIH and Wuhan lab gain-of-function research in 2020–2021; the 2021 release of his early pandemic emails; NIAID-funded beagle experiments in 2022; and his appearance in 2024 House investigations and congressional testimony.[14] The outlet noted that the list omitted Remdesivir, mRNA gene therapies, and disputes over the foundations of virology itself.[14]

## 2023 Slack message disclosures on COVID origins

In July 2023, newly released Slack messages reported by ZeroHedge — sourcing the outlets Racket and Public — were presented as evidence of media deception by Fauci and allied scientists over the origins of COVID-19, showing that authors of the [[proximal-origin-paper|Proximal Origin]] paper privately regarded a Wuhan lab leak as "so friggin' likely" while publicly discounting it.[16] Fauci, along with Jeremy Farrar and [[francis-collins|Francis Collins]], featured in the reporting on how the natural-origin consensus was assembled and communicated.[16]

## Considered for a preemptive pardon (December 2024)

In December 2024, Politico reported that Fauci, the former head of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, was among the names mentioned by aides to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for a [[preemptive-pardons|preemptive pardon]] ahead of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return to office, having become a lightning rod for criticism from the right during the Covid-19 pandemic.[17] Badlands contributor Burning Bright argued that pairing Fauci with figures such as [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] in pardon discussions codified years of dismissed "conspiracy" narrative, and stressed that a pardon must be accepted — which he framed as an implicit admission of guilt before any charge.[17]

## Former DNI acknowledgment of gain-of-function deception (2023)

In April 2023, [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] reported that a former Director of National Intelligence had publicly acknowledged that Fauci misled Congress and the American public about [[gain-of-function-research|gain-of-function research]].[18] The report noted that only two years earlier, alternative media outlets suggesting that the Level 4 virology laboratory in Wuhan, China — located across town from the initial outbreak — might be the source of COVID-19 had faced outright censorship on social media.[18]

The account argued that the intensity of that censorship became explicable once the connections between Fauci, the [[national-institutes-of-health|NIH]] and the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan laboratory]] were exposed: elements of the US government including Fauci had bankrolled coronavirus gain-of-function work at Wuhan and shielded it from government oversight.[18] By that reasoning, accepting a laboratory origin for the pandemic entails accepting that Fauci and his associates helped create it.[18] ZeroHedge asserted that Fauci had lied about these connections incessantly under oath, while questioning whether scrutiny had arrived too late for him ever to face consequences.[18]

Commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] placed the admission within a broader list of contemporaneous disclosures, alongside the [[cia|CIA]]'s implication in the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]], sworn testimony from a former FBI agent regarding CIA involvement in recruiting Saudis alleged to have participated in the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], the presidential candidacy of [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], a forthcoming June batch of declassified material on CIA communications surrounding the [[john-f-kennedy|JFK]] plot, and the enduring popularity of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[18]


## NIH royalty payments disclosure

In August 2023 the transparency watchdog [[openthebooks|OpenTheBooks]] published more than 1,500 pages of unredacted records, obtained through a mostly successful Freedom of Information Act battle with the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]], showing that Fauci and NIH director [[francis-collins|Francis Collins]] had personally received 58 royalty payments from companies licensing inventions developed with taxpayer money.[19] The wider database covered 56,000 transactions totaling more than $325 million, though individual payment amounts were not listed.[19] Fauci received 37 payments from three companies between 2010 and 2021: 15 from Santa Cruz Biotechnology, 14 from Ancell Corp., and eight from Chiron Corp., which was acquired by Novartis in 2006.[19]
