# Anthony Fauci

Anthony Fauci is an American physician and immunologist who served as director of the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]] (NIAID) for nearly 40 years, becoming the public face of the United States' COVID-19 response before his 2022 departure. In the Badlands Media corpus he is a recurring antagonist, cast as a central figure in the pandemic narrative and — in the July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief — as the final target of the 2026 COVID origins reckoning.

## The 2026 Senate subpoena

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that on July 7, 2026, Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] announced a subpoena for Fauci to testify before the [[senate|Senate]]. The subpoena is presented as the culmination of a wave of legal and administrative actions against NIAID-affiliated scientists — the debarment referral of [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]], the indictment of [[david-morens|David Morens]], the removal of [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]] as acting head of NIAID, and the indictment of [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]] — pursued by proponents of the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]], including figures in the Trump administration, with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].

The Brief quotes *The Atlantic* — which Badlands commentary identifies as one of the pandemic era's complicit "central narrative deployers," noting its October 2022 "desperate plea for amnesty" — lamenting that "all five are centered on a small community of scientists affiliated with the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, which Fauci ran for nearly 40 years."

## Position in the broader corpus

Beyond the 2026 reckoning, the corpus treats Fauci as a principal architect of the COVID narrative. The [[covid-19-pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]] page records the claim, from Erik Carlson's A Virus, or a Metaphor?, that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and Fauci funded and ran a bioweapon program allegedly relocated from North Carolina to the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan]] facility. J.B. White's December 2022 Badlands column "Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause" frames him as the face of [[deep-state|Deep State]] censorship and of the "cases, cases, cases" hysteria, coining the "Deaths, not cases" standard. The [[eli-lilly|Eli Lilly]] page separately records reports that Fauci joined a stealth mRNA startup later reported to be acquired by the pharmaceutical company.
