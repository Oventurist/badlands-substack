# Anthony Fauci

Anthony Fauci is the American physician and longtime director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases who served as the most visible federal medical authority during the [[covid-19-pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]]. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears as a central figure in claims about the engineered origin of the virus.

## Alleged role in bioweapon research

In [[a-virus-or-a-metaphor|A Virus, or a Metaphor?]], [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that while he believes a bioweapon was released from a laboratory in Wuhan, China, he does not believe it originated there. The article claims the program began in a laboratory in North Carolina and was moved to the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan]] facility "to maintain secrecy and control." It states directly: "I believe Obama and Fauci funded and ran this program, and with Trump coming into office, they had to move it overseas."

The article pairs Fauci with [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] as the two officials responsible for the program, and extends the claim to other biolabs said to be developing bioweapons around the world, specifically in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and possibly Taiwan. The source offers no documentary evidence for these assertions, presenting them as the author's stated beliefs. By implication, the article positions Fauci's program as something [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s arrival in office disrupted rather than enabled.

## Portrayal as the face of the Deep State (2022)

In the December 20, 2022 Badlands Media column [[anthony-fauci|"Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause"]], [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] describes Fauci as "the face of anti-American governmental overlords and the face of the willing executor of the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s bidding," asserting that this "man for others" overstepped his legal authority decades ago. The column notes that Fauci was educated in the Jesuit tradition of "men for others" and argues that he subsequently evolved into a "humanist," a label White regards as "just word warfare" and as functionally indistinguishable from [[communism|Communism]].

White holds Fauci responsible for the "cases, cases, cases" framing of the pandemic, contending that case claims were never "confirmed incidents verified by any known reliable testing method" and that deaths were the only meaningful measure. He argues Fauci did not act alone but worked with others in governments, corporations and groups who employ what an American Greatness essay called "Bottom Bitches" to disseminate preferred narratives and enforce partisan censorship, naming [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] in Arizona as an example. The piece was written to accompany any future [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] release concerning Fauci, and cites [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s tweet that his pronouns were "Prosecute/Fauci" as a sign that accountability for "Public Enemy Number One" was near. White promised a follow-up column offering a Florida example of Fauci's alleged direct hand in case reporting.
