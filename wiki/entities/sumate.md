# Súmate

**Súmate** is a Venezuelan opposition NGO named by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the recipients of United States democracy-promotion funding during the confrontation with [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]].[1]

After Chávez survived the [[2002-venezuelan-coup-attempt|2002 coup attempt]] and the ensuing owners' strike, the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] quadrupled its Venezuela budget, funneling millions to opposition NGOs with what DeLarme calls "bland names" — among them Súmate and [[primero-justicia|Primero Justicia]].[1] The funding formed part of what he characterizes as Washington's swift and multipronged response to Chávez's nationalization of [[pdvsa|PDVSA]] revenues.[1]
