# Jack Sellers

Jack Sellers is a member of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]], serving as its chairman during the 2022 election cycle. He is named in his official capacity as a defendant in the election contest [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] filed on December 9, 2022 after the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], together with [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], [[clint-hickman|Clint Hickman]], [[thomas-galvin|Thomas Galvin]], and [[steve-gallardo|Steve Gallardo]], county recorder [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]], elections director [[scott-jarrett|Scott Jarrett]], and Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]].

The complaint, as summarized in [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]], faults the Board for representing that Election Day tabulator failures had been resolved by mid-afternoon and for suppressing information about the extent of long lines at [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] vote centers.
