# International Rescue Committee

The **International Rescue Committee (IRC)** is an international humanitarian aid organization that responds to conflicts and emergencies, including disease outbreaks.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the IRC is quoted warning that "the warning signs are flashing red" in the [[ebola|Ebola]] outbreak in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]], and stating that the epidemic is outpacing responders. The organization called for relaxing import restrictions on personal protective equipment and for rapid funding to support health workers and safe burial teams. The article's headline characterizes the IRC and the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] as "fearmongering" over the outbreak, contrasting the aid groups' urgency with Congolese officials' description of the affected provinces as "well contained."
