# International Rescue Committee (IRC)

The International Rescue Committee (IRC) is an international humanitarian aid organization active in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

During the [[2026-ebola-outbreak|2026 Ebola outbreak]] of the Bundibugyo strain, the IRC warned that "the warning signs are flashing red," stating that the epidemic was outpacing responders.[1] The organization called for relaxing import restrictions on personal protective equipment and for rapid funding to support health workers and safe burial teams.[1]

Badlands commentary, in particular from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], treated the IRC's warnings alongside those of the [[world-health-organization|WHO]] as part of a pattern of public-health fearmongering.[1]
