# Anwar Ibrahim

Anwar Ibrahim is the Prime Minister of Malaysia. In October 2025 he received widespread praise for mediating a peace settlement between Cambodia and Thailand, which culminated in the signing of the [[kuala-lumpur-peace-accord|Kuala Lumpur Peace Accord]] on October 26, 2025, on the sidelines of the 47th ASEAN Summit.[1]

Praise for Anwar's role came directly from U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who told the APEC CEO Summit in Korea that Malaysia had been "fantastic" in assisting with the signing of the peace deal.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright placed Anwar's mediation within a broader reading of Trump's Asia tour, arguing that the Thailand–Cambodia peace deal, along with investment agreements with Japan and South Korea and the meeting with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], had all been settled well in advance: "NONE were decided this week. The deals are done. This is about the show."[1]
