# Hilton Cocoa Beach Oceanfront

The Hilton Cocoa Beach Oceanfront is a beachfront hotel in Cocoa Beach, Florida that served as the conference venue for [[gart-2-cocoa-beach|GART 2]], the second stop on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]].[1]

## Role in GART 2

Badlands Media announced in August 2023 that the September 7–10, 2023 event would be held in Cocoa Beach, with the conference itself taking place at the Hilton Cocoa Beach Oceanfront.[1] The hotel hosted the weekend's programming, which included live in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, panel Q&A sessions, a Friday night welcome reception for General Admission ticket holders, and breakfast and lunch service across the weekend.[1] Meet-and-greets between show hosts and attendees ran throughout the stay, and the venue doubled as the social hub where attendees mingled between panels.[1]

The property is operated under the Hilton brand; see [[hilton-worldwide|Hilton Worldwide]].[1]

## Selection as venue

The hotel was named as the GART 2 conference site in the tour's initial ticket announcement of June 29, 2023, which linked directly to the property's Hilton listing while noting that speakers and the itinerary were still to be published.[2] The same announcement said tickets were moving quickly on the strength of the first GART in Arizona, and that in-person packages at the venue would cover a Friday night Welcome Reception plus breakfast and lunch all weekend, with a VIP package adding an opening dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands hosts.[2]
