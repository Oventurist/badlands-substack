# J. Christian Adams

J. Christian Adams is president of the [[public-interest-legal-foundation|Public Interest Legal Foundation]], an election integrity watchdog organization.[1]

Following PILF's September 2024 court victory compelling Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] to disclose [[electronic-registration-information-center|ERIC]] reports on suspected dead registrants, Adams said in a press release that "PILF has knocked down ERIC's wall of secrecy in the voter list maintenance process," adding that "states cannot use third parties to hide election records that the public has a right to see."[1]
