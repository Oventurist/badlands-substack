# Marc Randolph

**Marc Randolph** is a co-founder and the first chief executive of [[netflix|Netflix]].[1] The Badlands account emphasises his family lineage: Randolph is a great-nephew of [[edward-bernays|Edward Bernays]], the pioneer of public relations and modern advertising, who was himself the nephew of [[sigmund-freud|Sigmund Freud]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats this lineage as more than coincidence, noting that Netflix went on to produce what he characterises as "woke" material with the Obamas as board members, including what he describes as the normalization of pedophilia.[1] In his framing, the descendant of the family that invented psychological persuasion ended up running one of the most influential mass-media distribution platforms in the world.[1]
