# Global Crossing

Global Crossing was a telecommunications company that aimed to lay high-speed internet cable worldwide and became one of the most spectacular failures of the [[dot-com-bubble|dotcom bubble]].[1]

Its chief executive made a fortune selling stock before the crash, as did [[terry-mcauliffe|Terry McAuliffe]], a close friend of President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], who used the profits to help the Clintons buy their mansion in Chappaqua, New York after they left the White House.[1] Ordinary shareholders who believed the promises about the company's potential lost everything.[1]
