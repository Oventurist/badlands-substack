# Phill Kline

Phill Kline is an American attorney who directs the [[amistad-project|Amistad Project]] of the [[thomas-more-society|Thomas More Society]].[1]

Kline was the principal public voice for the project's post-2020 election claims regarding the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]].[1] He stated that "130,000 to 280,000 completed ballots for the 2020 general election were shipped from Bethpage, NY, to Lancaster, PA, where those ballots and the trailer in which they were shipped disappeared."[1] He presented sworn declarations alleging that over 300,000 ballots were at issue in Arizona, 548,000 in Michigan, 204,000 in Georgia and over 121,000 in Pennsylvania, and appeared alongside contract driver [[jesse-morgan|Jesse Morgan]] at the December 2020 press conference where the allegations were made public.[1]
