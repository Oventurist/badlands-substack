# Jesús Huerta de Soto

**Jesús Huerta de Soto** is a Spanish economist of the [[austrian-school|Austrian School]] and a prominent critic of [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]].[1] Together with [[murray-rothbard|Murray Rothbard]], he called for the practice to be outlawed and criminalized.[1]

The pair argued that bank money creation not only causes macroeconomic instability, as described by the Austrian Business Cycle Theory, but constitutes a form of embezzlement or financial fraud that has been legalized only through the influence of powerful, wealthy bankers over corrupt governments.[1]
