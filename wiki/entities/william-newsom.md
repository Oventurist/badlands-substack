# William Newsom, Jr.

William Newsom, Jr. was a California jurist and the father of Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], and is described in Badlands Media coverage as the pivotal figure connecting the Newsom family to the wealth and influence of the [[getty-family|Getty family]] of San Francisco.[1]

## Getty consigliere

Newsom, Jr. was intimately involved with San Francisco's political elite and served as the financial consigliere to the heirs of the Getty Oil fortune.[1] In one of what Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] called "many acts of ingratiation," he personally delivered a ransom to the Italian mafia in exchange for the release of one of [[j-paul-getty|J. Paul Getty]]'s grandchildren, who had been abducted.[1] He subsequently advocated successfully for a modification in California state legislation that expanded the beneficiaries' entitlements to the Getty trust fund.[1]

## Influence on Gavin Newsom's ascent

The Newsom family is a fourth-generation Irish Catholic household with extensive connections to old San Francisco patronage machine politics.[1] As a result of his father's allegiance to the Gettys, Gavin Newsom enjoyed an enviable upbringing and was treated as an almost adopted member of the family, accompanying the Gettys on luxurious vacations and African excursions, and later founding a hospitality enterprise of resorts, wineries and nightclubs with childhood companion Billy Getty.[1] DeLarme noted that the fullest account of these details, the SF Weekly article "Bringing Up Baby Gavin," had been removed from that outlet's website but remained accessible through the Wayback Machine.[1]
