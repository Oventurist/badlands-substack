# Dmitri Mehlhorn

Dmitri Mehlhorn is a Democratic political operative and adviser to LinkedIn founder Reid Hoffman, described in July 2024 reporting as a Democratic "powerbroker" who had visited the [[joe-biden|Biden]] [[white-house|White House]] at least ten times.[1]

## Role in the staged-shooting claim

Within hours of the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|July 13, 2024 attempt on Donald Trump's life]], Mehlhorn sent a memo to reporters urging them to portray the shooting as a false-flag operation drawn from [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s playbook and staged to hand [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] an iconic photo opportunity.[1] "This is a classic Russian tactic, such as when Putin killed 300 civilians in 1999 and blamed it on terrorists to ride the backlash to winning power," he wrote.[1] Reporting by the Washington Free Beacon, summarized by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], identified this memo as the origin of the claim that Trump faked the attempt — a theory a [[morning-consult|Morning Consult]] poll later found was believed by 34 percent of Democrats, and which critics grouped under the label [[blueanon|BlueAnon]].[1]

Commentators noted that Mehlhorn's framing ignored photographic evidence of a bullet passing near Trump's head, the wound across Trump's right ear, and the death of rally attendee Corey Comperatore.[1] Hoffman, whom Mehlhorn advised, had joked about assassinating Trump the week before the attempt.[1]
