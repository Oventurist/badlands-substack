# Eli Feldstein

Eli Feldstein is an aide to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] who was arrested in connection with the Israeli security leaks scandal over the removal of a highly classified document from the Prime Minister's office.[1]

## The leak affair

Prosecutors allege that Feldstein sought to sway public opinion surrounding negotiations for the release of hostages held by [[hamas|Hamas]] in Gaza in a direction more favourable to Netanyahu, in the days after six hostages were murdered by the group in August 2024.[1]

According to a newly released transcript of a December 5 Supreme Court hearing — first obtained and published by the Ynet news website on Wednesday, January 8, 2025 — Feldstein's lawyer Oded Savoray told Supreme Court justice Alex Stein that Netanyahu knew his aide intended to leak the highly classified document to the foreign press.[1] Savoray, who was arguing for his client's release from detention to house arrest, further alleged that Feldstein had been acting under Netanyahu's instructions.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the intelligence Feldstein allegedly removed concerned the hostage negotiations and was to be released to the press in hopes of thwarting a deal, and asked why Netanyahu would seek to scuttle his own negotiation — suggesting either that Bibi wanted to prolong the hostage crisis, or that, as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has hinted, there may be no remaining hostages.[1]
