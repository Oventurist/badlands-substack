# Sam Bregman

Sam Bregman is the District Attorney for Bernalillo County, New Mexico, which includes Albuquerque.[1]

In September 2023 Bregman publicly refused to enforce the emergency public health order issued by Governor [[michelle-lujan-grisham|Michelle Lujan Grisham]] that suspended the carrying of firearms in Albuquerque and Bernalillo County.[1] "As an officer of the court, I cannot and will not enforce something that is clearly unconstitutional," he said, adding that his office "will continue to focus on criminals of any age that use guns in the commission of a crime."[1] Albuquerque Mayor Tim Keller and Police Chief Harold Medina took the same position.[1] His stance was highlighted approvingly by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as an example of a local official defending the Bill of Rights against claimed emergency powers.[1] See [[new-mexico-public-health-order-suspending-concealed-carry]].[1]
