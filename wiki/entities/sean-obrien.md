# Sean O'Brien

Sean O'Brien is the president of the International Brotherhood of [[teamsters|Teamsters]], the American labor union that in September 2024 declined to endorse a candidate in the presidential race for the first time in nearly three decades.[1]

## 2024 non-endorsement

Announcing the decision on Wednesday, September 18, 2024, O'Brien said in a statement that "neither major candidate was able to make serious commitments to our union to ensure the interests of working people are always put before Big Business."[1] He explained that the union "sought commitments from both Trump and Harris not to interfere in critical union campaigns or core Teamsters industries – and to honor our members' right to strike – but were unable to secure those pledges."[1]

The statement accompanied the release of internal Teamsters polling showing that roughly 60% of members believed the union should endorse [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], against 34% for [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and about 6% for another candidate; the electronic member poll had been initiated after Joe Biden withdrew from the race.[1] Badlands commentary characterized the abstention as effectively an endorsement of Trump and a devastating blow to a Democratic campaign that had been positioning itself as a labor movement.[1]

## Opposition to the H-1B visa program (2025)

In early January 2025, O'Brien said the [[h-1b-visa-program|H-1B visa program]] "displaces" Americans from their jobs while allowing billionaires at the top of corporations to exploit foreign workers.[2] Responding to Senator [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]]'s characterization of the program as nothing more than an outsourcing scheme, O'Brien posted on X that he "100%" agreed.[2]

## Senate hearing confrontation (November 2023)

At a November 14, 2023 meeting of the Senate [[health-education-labor-and-pensions-committee|Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee]], Senator [[markwayne-mullin|Markwayne Mullin]] challenged O'Brien to a physical fight, reading aloud a June 21 tweet in which O'Brien had called Mullin "a clown & a fraud" and written, "Quit the tough guy act in these senate hearings. You know where to find me. Anyplace, Anytime cowboy."[3]

When Mullin, a former mixed martial arts fighter, said "we can finish it here," O'Brien answered "OK that's fine, perfect," and then said he would "love to do it right now."[3] After Mullin told him to "stand your butt up," O'Brien retorted "You stand your butt up!" and both men rose from their seats before committee chairman [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] halted the confrontation.[3] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted the exchange as an illustration of hypocrisy among officials who accuse speech-based critics of being prone to violence.[3]
