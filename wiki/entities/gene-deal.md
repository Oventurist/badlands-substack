# Gene Deal

Gene Deal is a former bodyguard for [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] who was
present on the night in 1997 when the rapper Notorious B.I.G. was fatally
shot.[1]

## Claims about blackmail tapes

In an interview with the YouTube channel "The Art of Dialogue" in early April
2024, Deal alleged that Combs maintained blackmail recordings of politicians,
princes and other prominent figures who took part in his sex parties.[1] "I
don't think it's only celebrities gonna be shook. He had politicians in there,
he had princes in there. He also had a couple of preachers in there," Deal
said, adding that Combs "had every room bugged."[1] Deal further suggested that
the silence of Combs' media staff on the abuse allegations indicated they
either participated in the conduct or feared brand damage.[1]

Deal's remarks were reported by Modernity News and circulated in the
alternative-media ecosystem shortly after the
[[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] raids on
Combs' Miami and Los Angeles homes.[1]
