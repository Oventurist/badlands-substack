# Andrés Manuel López Obrador

Andrés Manuel López Obrador, frequently abbreviated AMLO and referred to in the corpus simply as "President Obrador," is the former president of [[mexico|Mexico]] and the predecessor of [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]].

## Relationship with Trump

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief characterizes Obrador as "a hardcore commie" who nevertheless "seemed to respect Trump and certainly got along with him." The commentary by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recalls that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "always talked about how Obrador deployed those 29,000 Mexican troops to the border to protect it from the Mexican side, among other things."

## Contrast with his successor

The Brief uses Obrador as the benchmark against which it judges Sheinbaum, whom it describes as singing a different tune despite being his purported protégée. Its interpretive framing holds that "Obrador gave Trump everything he asked for" and that Sheinbaum was subsequently dispatched to conduct damage control on behalf of what the author calls the Latin American Deep State hub.
