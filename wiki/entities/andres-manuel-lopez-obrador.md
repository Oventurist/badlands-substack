# Andrés Manuel López Obrador

Andrés Manuel López Obrador is the President of [[mexico|Mexico]] who cut his
country's diplomatic ties with [[ecuador|Ecuador]] in April 2024 after
Ecuadorian police stormed the Mexican embassy in Quito to arrest former
Ecuadorian Vice-President [[jorge-glas|Jorge Glas]].[1]

## The Quito embassy incident

López Obrador said Ecuadorian forces had "forcibly entered" the embassy in a
"flagrant violation of international law," and announced the end of Mexico's
diplomatic relationship with Ecuador on Friday evening, 5 April 2024.[1] Mexico
had granted Glas political asylum earlier that day "after a thorough analysis"
of his situation, an action Ecuador regarded as illegal.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized Mexico
as "big mad" over the raid, and noted that the reaction of the
[[white-house|White House]] to López Obrador — given US security assistance to
Ecuador's new president [[daniel-noboa|Daniel Noboa]] — would be "very
interesting."[1]
