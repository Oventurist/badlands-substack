# Mohammad Yaqub

Mohammad Yaqub is the Defense Minister of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] under the [[taliban|Taliban]] government.[1]

## Moscow security forum, May 2026

Yaqub travelled to the Moscow region in late May 2026 for an international security forum, where he met Russian Security Council Secretary [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]].[1] The meeting was followed on May 27, 2026 by the signing of a military-technical cooperation agreement between [[russia|Russia]] and Afghanistan, according to [[interfax|Interfax]].[1] Yaqub said that ties with Russia were important and that the two countries had expanded their bilateral relations.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the visit as part of a longer arc of Taliban international rehabilitation, alongside the movement's 2024 appearance at the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum and Russia's public demand that the West lift sanctions on Afghanistan.[1]
