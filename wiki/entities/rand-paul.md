# Rand Paul

Rand Paul is a Republican United States Senator from Kentucky and chairman of the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee.

According to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, Paul is one of the leading congressional investigators of the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]], working in parallel with House Oversight Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] and House Majority Whip [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]]. The Brief reports that the investigators characterize the alleged fraud — estimated at up to half of $18–20 billion in federally funded welfare spending — as a "coordinated effort" to build voting blocs and manipulate elections, congressional apportionment and federal funding in blue states, and that the inquiries were widening to other Democratic-run states.

## The Fauci subpoena (July 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that on July 7, 2026, Paul announced a subpoena for [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], the former director of the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]], to testify before the [[senate|Senate]]. The subpoena is presented as the culmination of the wave of legal and administrative actions against NIAID-affiliated scientists over COVID-19 research — including the debarment referral of [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]], the indictment of [[david-morens|David Morens]] and the removal of [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]] — with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. Badlands commentary frames the reckoning as the "Justice Phase" for what it calls a "portfolio of crimes against humanity."
