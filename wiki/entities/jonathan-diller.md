# Jonathan Diller

**Jonathan Diller** was a [[nypd|New York City Police Department]] officer who was murdered by a lifelong criminal during a routine traffic stop in Queens in March 2024.[1]

Diller's wake became a nationally covered political moment when former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attended it, hours before President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] appeared at a $500,000-per-ticket fundraiser in New York alongside [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], the singer [[lizzo|Lizzo]] and other celebrities.[1] Commentators noted the juxtaposition between the fundraiser, which raised $25 million for Biden, and Trump's presence at the slain officer's wake; critics also argued that Trump had effectively turned the wake into a campaign stop.[1] Earlier the same day, Biden's social media team had posted an attack advertisement suggesting Biden was hard at work while Trump was idle on the golf course.[1]
