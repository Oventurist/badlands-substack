# Hossein Amirabdollahian

Hossein Amirabdollahian served as Foreign Minister of [[iran|Iran]] and was a central figure in Tehran's diplomacy during the Gaza war.[1]

## Role in Iran–US backchannel via Saudi Arabia

In November 2023, Amirabdollahian attended an emergency summit in Riyadh on the Gaza war, convened with leaders from the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation and [[arab-league|Arab League]] countries. According to an Iranian insider cited by [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]], he carried with him a message intended for the United States, to be handed to Saudi officials; the message was a response to one Tehran had recently received from Washington. Saudi officials then conveyed the message to senior figures in Washington.[1]

His participation marked the beginning of a process in which [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] — nine months after restoring ties with Tehran — assumed the role of intermediary between Iran and the United States, relaying messages and working to reduce tensions over [[israel|Israel]]'s war on Gaza.[1]

Badlands commentary read this arrangement as evidence for the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] thesis and for the "Regions of Responsibility" framing, in which regional powers such as Saudi Arabia under [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] act as fulcrums of a multipolar order rather than as clients of a single hegemon.[1]

## Claim of advance warning, April 2024

On the Sunday after the [[april-2024-iranian-attack-on-israel|April 2024 Iranian drone and missile attack on Israel]], Amirabdollahian, as Iranian Foreign Minister, said that [[iran|Iran]] had given neighbouring countries and Israel's ally the United States 72 hours' notice that it would launch the strikes.[2] Turkish, Jordanian and Iraqi officials corroborated that wide notice had been given days in advance, and [[turkey|Turkey]]'s Foreign Ministry said it had relayed messages between Washington and Tehran as an intermediary to keep reactions proportionate.[2]

A senior official in the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] denied Amirabdollahian's account, saying Washington had contact with Iran through Swiss intermediaries but did not receive 72 hours' advance notice, and that Tehran had aimed to cause significant damage.[2]
