# Ari Ben-Menashe

Ari Ben-Menashe is a former Israeli intelligence figure who handled [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]]-era arms deals.[1]

Ben-Menashe has described encounters with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] in the offices of [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] during the mid-1980s, an account cited as evidence of Epstein's integration into networks blending finance, arms and intelligence.[1] Maxwell, the British media mogul and father of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], is widely regarded as an asset of Israeli intelligence and collaborated on arms transfers to Iran in the same period.[1]
