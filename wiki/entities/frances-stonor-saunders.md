# Frances Stonor Saunders

Frances Stonor Saunders is a writer and historian known for researching covert [[cia|CIA]] funding of cultural organizations during the Cold War.[1]

She characterized the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] as "the umbilical cord of gold that leads directly back to Washington," adding that she referred not only to official U.S. government programmes but to "the vast network of clandestine players that plan and enact its information warfare operations," and that "the cultural Cold War has never gone away, it's just shifted from target to target."[1] Badlands coverage uses her framing to argue that the same information-warfare machinery once aimed at the Soviet bloc has been redirected against national populism.[1]
