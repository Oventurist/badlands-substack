# Zachary Charles Newell

Zachary Charles Newell is a 25-year-old North Carolina man taken into federal custody in early September 2025 and charged with interstate threat to kidnap or injure.[1]

## Alleged preschool threat

[[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] revealed that the bureau had disrupted a potential mass shooting after Newell allegedly threatened to massacre a group of twenty Black preschoolers.[1] The threat was allegedly posted on YouTube on August 27, 2025, under the username "CommentatorsHateMe," in which the poster stated an intention to "shoot up" a preschool, injuring or killing twenty Black children; a separate racist threat was allegedly made online the previous day.[1] No specific school was named in the posts.[1]

Patel said that "threats of violence against children are beyond unacceptable acts of cowardice," and credited the FBI's work with partners in Carteret County, North Carolina, with ensuring the individual was taken into custody before he could act.[1] He added that parents "should know we will never hesitate to respond with urgency to protect their kids, their schools, and their communities."[1]
