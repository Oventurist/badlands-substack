# Zehava Galon

**Zehava Galon** is an Israeli left-wing political figure and commentator whose writing appears in the Israeli newspaper [[haaretz|Haaretz]].[1]

## "If Israelis Don't Rise Up Now" column

In a column published on Sunday, August 4, 2024, headlined "If Israelis Don't Rise Up Now, We'll Have Nothing Left to Fight For," Galon argued that Israel was "steadily becoming a pariah state," with what had once been a trickle of international sanctions turning into a daily occurrence — "a few settlers here, an organization there; one day it's from Spain, the next day it's from Japan."[1] She wrote that Israelis had grown used to the trend rather than noticing it, and that the sanctions should be understood as a privilege and a warning signal, the equivalent of an intervention by friends explaining that the days of paying Israel's bills were over.[1] Because "there is no tantrum like the tantrum of the privileged," she wrote, the Israeli government responds by crying "antisemitism!"[1]

Galon singled out [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]], writing that he "isn't some strange creature dragged by its beard out of a cave in Hebron" but a man who comes and goes freely at the [[knesset|Knesset]] — the same Knesset that bars entry to relatives of the hostages.[1] She also described [[tzav-9|Tzav 9]] as more than a fringe outfit of "pimply youths from the hilltops," noting that its members are protected by the [[israeli-police|police]] and by the minister responsible for the police, and asking how many times Israelis needed to see a truck driver beaten while officers stood aside before the point sank in.[1]

Badlands Media quoted the column at length alongside remarks by Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] on starving Gazans, presenting both as evidence of the direction of Israeli politics.[1]
