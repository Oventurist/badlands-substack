# Badlands Book Club

Badlands Book Club is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program in which hosts read and discuss books chapter by chapter on stream, distributed via Rumble.[1]

## Episode 5

Episode 5, aired Tuesday, April 15, 2025, covered chapters concluding Part II of [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s *[[government-gangsters|Government Gangsters]]*, hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]].[1] The discussion focused on Patel's proposed reforms, particularly his call to purge officials disloyal to the [[us-constitution|Constitution]] from positions of power, and was subsequently cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] commentary on the [[2025-pentagon-leak-investigation|Pentagon leak suspensions]].[1]
