# Louis Uridel

Louis Uridel is the owner of Metroflex Gym and an Instagram personality with roughly 134,000 followers.[1]

## Viral post and Badlands critique

Uridel posted a widely circulated video depicting a visibly frightened young child in front of an aggressively sexual performance, captioning it sarcastically, "Kids need to be exposed to new and educational experiences."[1] He presented the clip as evidence of what liberals were purportedly advocating as a unified political movement.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] agreed that such incidents should be publicized but rejected the framing.[1] He disputed that a political wing is the source of the manipulation, rejected the idea that the majority of the liberal left-wing population is driving these cultural shifts, and rejected the notion that the change is occurring organically from the grassroots up.[1] In his account, the manipulation originates with a small number of powerful elites pursuing a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] through a political [[uniparty|Uniparty]], drawing on a far deeper understanding of human behavior than the average person possesses.[1]

Deschamps questioned whether pejoratives such as "lib" or "libtard" further cement pre-engineered, carefully polarized political identities, arguing that the adversary is significantly more complex than a binary political conflict and that [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] plugged into [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric operations]] depends on that binary being maintained.[1]
