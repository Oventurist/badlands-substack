# Absolute 1776

Absolute 1776 is a pseudonymous host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], appearing across three distinct programs in the network's December 2022 broadcast schedule and making him one of the most heavily featured personalities on the roster.[1]

## Programs

Absolute 1776 co-hosts **Sports Talk** with [[jb-white|JB White]] on Mondays and Fridays at 12pm ET, a program framed around removing politics from sports commentary and "regaining America's Pastime."[1]

He co-hosts **Eye of the Storm** with Stormy Patriot Joe on Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET. Badlands describes the pair as two of the most knowledgeable analysts of the Q operation, which the network characterizes as "the biggest Military Intelligence Information Dissemination Program ever launched."[1]

He also co-hosts the **NCSWIC Podcast** with Tru Reporting on Sundays at 7:30pm ET, a weekly deep dive into [[information-war|Info War]] topics analyzed through the framework of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]], with the stated advantage of hindsight and accumulated context.[1]

The breadth of his assignments — sports, Q analysis, and fifth-generation warfare — illustrates the crossover between cultural and geopolitical programming that characterizes the Badlands lineup.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## Commentary on NHL Pride Night (2023)

In the March 9, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief]], AbsoluteTruth1776 commented on the refusal by [[minnesota-wild]] players to wear rainbow-themed warm-up jerseys for the team's Pride Night, describing it as a growing trend in the NHL as more players and teams declined to participate.[3] He suggested that pushing the agenda on a sport composed mostly of Caucasian Christians had been a poor idea.[3]

He predicted that such refusals would become more common, and posed a hypothetical about a mandatory "Christian Pride Night" in which athletes would wear crosses and say a prayer, arguing that this would not be tolerated and that America was nearing a "fed up" point with the LGBTQ agenda.[3]

## Commentary, January 2023

In the January 20, 2023 Badlands News Brief, Absolute1776 wrote that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s potential return to [[twitter|Twitter]] came as the Biden administration faced mounting challenges, cautioned that anonymous "GOP sources" were not to be trusted, and suggested such a return could signal a shift in public perception and narrative as Trump geared up for a return to the [[white-house|White House]].[4] With [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] he also commented on the backlash against [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] as an instance of [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]].[4]
