# Hussein Kanani

Hussein Kanani is a former commander of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] who, in an exclusive interview with RT aired on Monday, February 9, 2026, claimed that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] currently possesses a nuclear bomb and related capabilities.

As reported in the February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief, Kanani asserted that the United States is fully aware of this development and that both [[israel|Israel]] and the U.S. have been informed. He further warned that [[iran|Iran]] could respond to American aggression by striking Israel with missiles or by closing the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], potentially disrupting global oil flows including to China and Russia, and characterised Washington's approach as "playing a game of chicken."

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats Kanani's claim sceptically, describing the story as feeling "like a trap — a narrative pincer set by the Muslims against Israel." He argues there is no real evidence that Saudi Arabia possesses nuclear weapons and no reason for Iran to attack the one regional party that had argued against kinetic action toward it, and hypothesises that the claim is bait intended to provoke [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet into publicly attacking Saudi Arabia and demanding verification — a move he suggests could boomerang, given that Saudi Arabia has signed the relevant international treaties on nuclear materials while Israel has not, despite allegedly possessing hundreds of warheads.
