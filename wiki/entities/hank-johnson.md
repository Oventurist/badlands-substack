# Hank Johnson

Hank Johnson is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Georgia, widely known in political commentary for remarks during a congressional hearing suggesting that the island of Guam might capsize.[1]

## Badlands reference

In November 2024 commentary on Representative [[wiley-nickel|Wiley Nickel]]'s [[shadow-cabinet-proposal-2024|shadow cabinet proposal]], Badlands Media wrote that it never expected to see the day Johnson would lose his informal title as the least intelligent member of [[congress|Congress]], adding sardonically that Johnson's misunderstanding of marine topography at least made a kind of sense to very young children.[1] The reference is used as a rhetorical yardstick rather than a substantive claim about Johnson's record.[1]
