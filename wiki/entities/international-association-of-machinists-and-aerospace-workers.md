# International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers

The International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers (IAM) is a North American labor union representing skilled manufacturing and aerospace workers, including a substantial portion of the production workforce at [[boeing|Boeing]].[1]

## 2025 Boeing defense strike

IAM District 837, the union's St. Louis chapter, voted on 28 July 2025 to reject a contract agreement with Boeing, setting the stage for a strike if no deal was reached by the end of the following Sunday.[1] Members rejected Boeing's latest offer on Sunday, 3 August, and roughly 3,200 machinists at Boeing's St. Louis-based defense unit walked off the job at midnight on Monday, 4 August 2025 — the first such action for that local chapter since 1996.[1]

Boeing had put forward a revised offer the preceding week that included some improvements to retirement benefits and rescinded an alternative workweek proposal opposed by workers, but did not increase wages beyond the initial proposal.[1]

Tom Boelling, directing business representative for IAM District 837, said in a statement: "IAM District 837 members have spoken loud and clear, they deserve a contract that reflects their skill, dedication, and the critical role they play in our nation's defense. We stand shoulder to shoulder with these working families as they fight for fairness and respect on the job."[1]

The walkout affected production of flagship defense products including the F/A-18 Super Hornet and F-15EX fighters, the Joint Direct Attack Munition, the MQ-25 tanker drone, and the T-7 trainer; the same workforce is slated to build the sixth-generation F-47 fighter, for which Boeing won a contract in March 2025.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary was critical of the strike's timing, arguing that bargaining over an alternative workweek and retirement improvements is misjudged in an industry facing rapid automation, artificial intelligence, and robotics adoption, and linking the discussion to McKinsey's "new operating model for a new world" framework examined on the program *Culture of Change*.[1]
