# Paul Engelmayer

**Paul Engelmayer** is a United States District Judge who in July 2025 took over the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s request to unseal grand jury records from the criminal case of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], an associate of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Epstein–Maxwell grand jury request

In a four-page order filed Tuesday, July 22, 2025, Engelmayer said he planned to rule "expeditiously" on the motion but required additional information first.[1] "The Court intends to resolve this motion expeditiously," he wrote, adding that "the Court cannot rule on the motion without additional submissions."[1] The New York proceeding remained pending even after a parallel request covering older Florida grand juries was rejected by Judge Robin Rosenberg.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the matter as an opportunity to compare what the grand jury was actually shown with the material the Southern District of New York provided to Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] and FBI Director Kash Patel, while cautioning that the release effort might itself be a diversion.[1]


## Epstein records ruling

In December 2025, Engelmayer ruled that the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] could publicly release investigative materials from the sex trafficking case against [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], acting on the department's November request to unseal grand jury transcripts, exhibits and related records from the Maxwell and [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]] matters.[2] His order followed passage of the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]], which requires publication of Epstein-related records in searchable format by December 19th.[2] He became the second judge to authorize disclosure of previously sealed Epstein records, after a Florida judge approved release of transcripts from an abandoned 2000s-era grand jury investigation; a separate request tied to Epstein's 2019 case remained pending.[2]
