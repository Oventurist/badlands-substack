# Kyrylo Budanov

Kyrylo Budanov is the chief of the Main Directorate of Intelligence (HUR) of Ukraine's Ministry of Defense.[1]

## Statement on Navalny's death

Speaking to journalists on the sidelines of the "Ukraine. Year of 2024" forum on Sunday, February 25, 2024, Budanov said that Russian opposition leader [[alexei-navalny|Alexei Navalny]] had died of a blood clot.[1] "I may disappoint you, but as far as we know, he indeed died as a result of a blood clot. And this has been more or less confirmed," he told reporters, adding: "This wasn't sourced from the internet, but, unfortunately, natural [causes]."[1] Navalny, the most prominent critic of Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], had died on February 16 in one of Russia's toughest prisons in northern Siberia while serving a 19-year sentence that Putin's critics denounced as political retribution.[1] Navalny's team reported that his body had been handed to his mother, Lyudmila Navalnaya, more than a week after his death.[1]
