# John Phelan

John Phelan is the United States Secretary of the Navy in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Role in the battleship announcement

On Monday, December 22, 2025, Phelan appeared alongside President Donald Trump at Mar-a-Lago for what the [[white-house|White House]] billed as a "major announcement," in which Trump unveiled plans for a new class of battleships for the [[us-navy|US Navy]]'s so-called "Golden Fleet."[1] Phelan was joined at the event by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[1] Trump said he had approved two ships to start, with plans for up to 25, describing the vessels — larger than the WWII-era Iowa class and reported by the Wall Street Journal as being procured beginning in 2030 — as carrying hypersonic missiles, rail guns, and lasers.[1] The announcement formed part of a wider naval expansion of both manned and unmanned ships, driven in part by official warnings that the United States trails China in shipbuilding capacity and output.[1]
