# GART

**GART** is an event attended by the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] team in the week of 7 November 2025, significant enough to the outlet's operations that the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] suspended its normal news coverage for that week and ran a retrospective of recent feature articles instead.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

Virtual tickets to GART were sold through Badlands Media's own site at badlandsmedia.tv, and the Brief reminded readers that it was "not too late" to obtain them, indicating that the event was still ongoing or imminent at the time of publication.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Normal News Brief publication was scheduled to resume the following week.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Great American Restoration Tour

GART stands for the Great American Restoration Tour, a travelling series of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] events.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) The fifth stop, GART 5, was held in Deadwood, South Dakota in mid-August 2024.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) The outlet describes GART as unlike any other "conference," being a relatively small and accessible gathering at which Badlands hosts and audience members spend a few days getting to know each other, laughing and learning, and experiencing the local area; the author says she would prefer to call it a festival.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

For regular attendees the off-book events are a special part of the weekend, and once someone has been through GART they understand its casual, unscripted vibe and begin planning for the next one.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) Many participants arrived in Deadwood early for unscheduled activities including trips to Mount Rushmore, a hyper-competitive golf "tournament," and, for a small group, the [[cosmos-mystery-area|Cosmos Mystery Area]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) The Mount Rushmore party — which included retired general [[steve-kwast|Kwast]] and his wife Joni — attended the evening programme at the monument, where the stories of the presidents are told as the sun lowers, the monument is illuminated at dusk, the national anthem is sung and veterans descend to the stage to retire the US flag.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

Digital Replay tickets for GART 5 gave access to all panel replays from the weekend along with the premiere of the Badlands Media documentary The Crucible of Donald Trump.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) GART 6 was scheduled for Myrtle Beach just after the 2024 presidential election, with VIP packages sold out and general admission tickets still available at the time of writing; Badlands also planned to return to Deadwood the following summer.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

## GART 11 (April 2026)

GART 11 was held on 9–12 April 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee, and was promoted in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] of 9 April 2026 under a "Last Call!" heading as starting that week.[3] The Brief described the format in general terms: [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] "hits the road four times a year for a three-day patriot-packed event" with its hosts and the wider Badlands community, inviting readers to "be part of something real" either in person or from home.[3]

For those attending remotely, Badlands sold a GART Virtual Pass, which was presented as the way to "get your Badlands fix" and to socialise with other attendees in the official Badlands Telegram group.[3] The virtual pass was promoted with particular emphasis in that edition because [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]] was off the air for the weekend, leaving GART as the outlet's principal live offering in the interim.[3]

## GART 9 in Deadwood

In July 2025 Badlands Media announced that the event was exactly one month away from GART 9, to be held in Deadwood, South Dakota, and directed readers to badlandsmedia.tv/events for in-person and virtual tickets.[4] The program included the premiere of [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s film [[codex-911|Codex 911]], scheduled a few weeks before the 24th anniversary of the September 11 attacks; [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the premiere alone was justification enough for a virtual ticket for those unable to attend in person.[4]

## GART 4: Dallas (April 2024)

GART 4 took place from April 19 to 21, 2024 in Dallas, Texas, with Badlands Media issuing a "last call" for attendance in its April 19 news brief.[5] For those unable to attend in person, virtual tickets to stream the entirety of the event were made available through the organization's ticketing page.[5] Virtual purchases included access to the full weekend live stream, entry to an exclusive Telegram chat shared with other Dallas virtual and in-person attendees, and the ability to access recorded replays of all GART 4 panels both during and after the event.[5]

## GART 9 (Deadwood, South Dakota)

In mid-July 2025, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] announced that GART 9 in Deadwood, South Dakota was exactly one month away, promoting both in-person and virtual tickets through badlandsmedia.tv/events.[6] During the event the network planned to premiere [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s new film [[codex-911|CODEX 911]], available with either an in-person or a virtual ticket.[6]
