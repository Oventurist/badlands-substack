# Abu Bakr

Abu Bakr — a kunya meaning "the father of camels" — was the most trusted companion of the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]] and the first caliph of the [[rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun Caliphate]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

When Muhammad died leaving no dictated line of succession, most of his followers supported Abu Bakr as leader.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) For thirty years Abu Bakr and three other trusted companions of Muhammad were sequentially elected by their peers to rule the Rashidun ("Rightly Guided") Caliphate, expanding the empire from modern-day Libya to India and making it the most powerful military, cultural, and economic force in West Asia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The Quran was written under Abu Bakr.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

The alternative view, that [[ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] was Muhammad's designated heir, produced the [[shia-islam|Shia]] branch; the majority who accepted the elective principle became the [[sunni-islam|Sunni]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
