# Mike Davis

Mike Davis is an attorney whose analysis of court filings is cited in [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] Director [[william-pulte|William Pulte]]'s March 2026 referral of New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].

## Role in the James referral

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Pulte's referral letters to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois cite analysis from Davis, who pointed to court filings he says show discrepancies in James' insurance representations regarding her Norfolk, Virginia property — including claims that the home would remain unoccupied for five months each year and would be occupied by a single adult with no children, assertions contradicted, per the referral, by testimony and public reporting that her niece Nakia Thompson and three children lived there.

The referral requests that the claims be "authenticated and investigated" for potential criminal prosecution.
