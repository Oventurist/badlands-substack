# Jon Favreau

Jon Favreau is an American political commentator, podcast host and former speechwriter for President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1] He is a host of the political podcast [[pod-save-america|Pod Save America]], a program produced by former Obama administration staffers that occupies a prominent place in Democratic-aligned new media.[1]

## Reaction to the Valley Forge speech

In January 2024, Favreau was cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as an example of the media reaction to [[biden-valley-forge-speech|President Biden's Valley Forge campaign address]], with Favreau publicly gushing over [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s performance.[1] DeLarme treated Favreau's praise as illustrative of a broader pattern in which Democratic-aligned commentators portray Biden as an altruistic defender of democracy rather than a career politician acting in his own interest, and argued that the same media ecosystem had minimized federal court findings against the Biden administration over social media censorship.[1]
