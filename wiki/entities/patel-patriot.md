Patel Patriot is the host of the Rumble channel on which all [[badlands-media]] shows air live.[1] The network's live broadcasts are streamed on Patel Patriot's Live Channel (rumble.com/c/PatelPatriot), while replays are hosted separately on the Badlands Media Library Channel (rumble.com/c/BadlandsMedia).[1] Patel Patriot is thus the live-distribution anchor for the entire Badlands Media show slate.[1]

## Commentary on the January 6 footage release

Writing under his own name, Jon Herold, as well as the Patel Patriot handle, he was among the most prominent Badlands voices analyzing the March 2023 [[january-6-capitol-security-footage-release|release of Capitol security footage]] by [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].[2] He argued that the [[january-6-committee|January 6 committee]]'s 845-page final report could be discarded in its entirety after the first tranche of footage aired, focusing on a single paragraph describing [[jacob-chansley|Jacob Chansley]] entering the Capitol and praying in the Senate chamber, from which two important details were missing.[2] On X he asked committee member [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] why the report contained no mention of [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] escorting Chansley around the Senate chamber and only a portion of his prayer.[2]

He also framed the bipartisan objections of [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] and [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] to the broadcast as evidence of selective commitment to transparency, writing that January 6 had been "the primary weapon of the establishment in their attempts to destroy [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s political future" and that the establishment "came out in full force" against Carlson and [[fox-news|Fox News]] once the footage undercut its narrative.[2]
