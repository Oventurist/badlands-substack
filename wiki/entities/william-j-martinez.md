# William J. Martinez

William J. Martinez is a United States District Court judge in Denver, [[colorado|Colorado]].[1]

## Ruling on Colorado's social media warning law

In November 2025, Martinez blocked the warning label provision of the [[colorado-social-media-warning-label-law|Colorado social media warning label law]], granting a preliminary injunction to the technology coalition [[netchoice|NetChoice]] and ruling that requiring companies to display warnings to young users likely violates the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] as compelled speech.[1]

In his order, Martinez wrote that the court "fully appreciates Colorado's legitimate effort to protect the children and adolescents of our state from the impacts of social media use on their health and wellbeing," but concluded that the state "may not pursue this laudable goal by compelling social media companies to speak (the state's) expressive messages."[1] The order prevented the provision from taking effect on January 1, its scheduled start date, while the underlying lawsuit continues.[1]
