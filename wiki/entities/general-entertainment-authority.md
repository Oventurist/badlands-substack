# General Entertainment Authority

The General Entertainment Authority is the Saudi government body responsible for developing and regulating the Kingdom's entertainment sector. It has been the principal vehicle through which [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] has acquired and hosted major international entertainment, music, and combat-sports events as part of its broader economic and social diversification drive.[1] Its chairman is [[turki-alalshikh|Turki Alalshikh]], who simultaneously serves as president of the Saudi Arabian Boxing Federation.[1]

## WrestleMania 43

In September 2025, the Authority's chairman announced jointly with [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] Chief Content Officer [[paul-levesque|Paul Levesque]] that [[wrestlemania-43|WrestleMania 43]] would be held in Riyadh in 2027, the first time the event would be staged outside North America.[1] Alalshikh cast the agreement as the delivery of a 2018 promise to "change the future by doing big projects together" in the Kingdom and as "a significant moment in our partnership with WWE."[1]

Badlands commentary situated the Authority's activity within a larger stream of Gulf sovereign capital entering American sports and entertainment — including LIV Golf, stakes in the [[ufc|UFC]], and emerging media ventures — which contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] interpreted as a cultural bridge-building effort rather than simple investment.[1]
