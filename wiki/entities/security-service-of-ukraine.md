# Security Service of Ukraine

The Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) is [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s principal internal security and intelligence agency, tasked in mid-2026 with executing a 40-day "influence operation" aimed at pressuring [[russia|Russia]] to end the war.[1]

President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] said he had approved the operation, which would include long-range and medium-range sanctions plans as well as an assessment of results achieved by the service.[1] Zelenskyy also said Ukraine's security services had demonstrated strong performance in supporting frontline operations through the use of drones over recent months, highlighting the work of the Security Service's special operations units in actions against Russian forces.[1]

The announcement came as Ukraine continued drone strikes against targets inside Russia, including energy and refinery infrastructure; Ukrainian official Mykhailo Fedorov said in June that Russian logistics were being disrupted and that Crimea was being increasingly isolated.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the phrase "influence operation" may be a tacit disclosure that the war in the Donbas is itself an influence operation designed to draw [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] onto the board and allow him and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to codify a peace neither could achieve alone.[1]

## Claimed Zelensky assassination plot, August 2023

On Monday, August 7, 2023, Ukraine's security services announced that they had thwarted a major assassination plot targeting President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[2] A woman living in Ochakov in southern Ukraine, who worked as a clerk in a military store on a military base and was not publicly identified, was arrested and accused of planning to inform Russian intelligence of Zelensky's precise whereabouts during his visit to the Mykolaiv region, where the southeastern front with Russian forces is located.[2] Officials alleged she was "gathering intelligence" so that a large-scale airstrike could be executed during the president's late-July trip, which included a medical facility in Ochakiv, and said she was "caught red-handed" after they received a tip.[2] Ukrainian media reported that "the suspected Russian agent tried to find out the schedule of the presidential route in the region," that "the SBU managed to stay ahead of her actions and caught her in the act," and that she had also travelled to photograph electronic warfare systems and ammunition warehouses near Ochakiv on the Black Sea coast, mapping key military locations and filming sensitive facilities.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued the claim could be read three ways, none favourable to its promoters: that Russia could have killed Zelensky at any point in the preceding eighteen months had it wished; that if other parties were involved the only logical culprit would be someone within the NATO orbit, strengthening proxy-war theories; or that the claim was simply false and represented "the latest in a long line of desperate media plays by a failing Proxy War."[2]

## Announced coup plot foiling (July 2024)

On Monday, July 1, 2024, the SBU announced it had stopped an attempted coup that would have "played into Russia's hands."[3] According to the service, a group of organizers had planned to stage a rally in Kyiv's city center on June 30, spark a riot, then seize the parliament and replace the country's military and civilian leadership.[3] "In this way, they hoped to undermine the socio-political situation inside Ukraine, which would have played in Russia's hands," the SBU said.[3] It was not clear whether the organizers were connected to [[russia|Russia]], and the Ukrainian claims could not be independently verified.[3] Reporting noted dozens of Russian-organized attempts to assassinate President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] since the start of the war.[3]

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry dismissed the announcement as a low-effort intelligence product, arguing the [[cia|CIA]] had recycled the January 6 template and merely reframed it as a "foiled plot" because mounting a real staged demonstration in Ukraine would be impractical — with many Ukrainians hiding from conscription, fleeing the country, or deserting, and the government announcing the conscription of thousands of prisoners.[3] The commentary also charged inconsistency in casting the plotters as Russians working for "NGO's known for anti-Ukrainian actions," given Western criticism of Georgian prime minister [[irakli-kobakhidze|Irakli Kobakhidze]] for passing foreign-agent legislation that would expose NGO funding.[3]

## Foiled plot against Zelensky (May 2024)

In May 2024 the SBU announced that it had foiled a Russian plot to kidnap and assassinate President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] and other senior officials, saying it had caught Russian agents operating within the Ukrainian state guard.[4] Agency head [[vasyl-malyuk|Vasyl Malyuk]] said the operation had been intended as a "gift" to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] ahead of his inauguration, and the service reported arresting two suspected "moles" it believed Russia had recruited before the invasion.[4] On Telegram the SBU claimed a Russian secret agent had passed on the coordinates of a safe house ahead of a planned rocket attack and that Russia intended to destroy all traces of the drone used against the president with a second missile; the agency provided no evidence for its assertion that "the enemy was actively developing plans to eliminate President Volodymyr Zelensky."[4] See the [[2024-zelensky-assassination-plot|2024 Zelensky assassination plot]].[4]
