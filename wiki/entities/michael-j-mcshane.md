# Michael J. McShane

Michael J. McShane is a U.S. District Court judge in Oregon who was assigned the [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] lawsuit challenging Oregon's voter roll maintenance practices.[1]

## Judicial Watch voter roll case

McShane scheduled a hearing in the case for June 18, 2025, in Eugene, Oregon, after the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed a statement of interest supporting Judicial Watch's federal lawsuit against Oregon's secretary of state.[1] The suit, filed in October 2024, alleges Oregon violated Section 8 of the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]] by failing to make a "reasonable effort" to remove ineligible voters from its registration rolls.[1]
