# Lisa Monaco

Lisa Monaco is a former United States Deputy Attorney General under President Joe Biden and a former national security aide in the Obama administration who, in 2025, joined [[microsoft|Microsoft]] as President of Global Affairs.[1] In May 2026 she became the subject of a criminal referral to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] filed by the [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]], which accused her of shielding Microsoft from cybersecurity enforcement actions before taking the company position.[1]

## Microsoft referral

The referral alleges that the Biden-era Justice Department failed to pursue major enforcement action against Microsoft despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 attributed to Russian and Chinese hackers, intrusions that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.[1] The complaint emphasizes that Monaco oversaw the department's [[civil-cyber-fraud-initiative|Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative]], which pursued other federal contractors over allegedly less serious misconduct, while no comparable case was brought against Microsoft.[1]

The foundation asked Justice Department inspectors general to examine whether Monaco and other officials violated ethics rules, recusal obligations, or revolving-door restrictions, and whether Microsoft itself could face liability under the [[false-claims-act|False Claims Act]] for representations made to federal agencies about the security of its systems.[1] The complaint also names other former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who subsequently took positions with Microsoft or firms connected to it, including former DOJ official [[john-p-carlin|John P. Carlin]] and former FBI cyber official [[bryan-vorndran|Bryan A. Vorndran]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America characterized Monaco as, in her opinion, one of the central architects of the coordinated campaign against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during his first administration, speculating that Monaco was a central player in [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]], [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]], and other RussiaGate plotlines allegedly directed by Barack Obama and his intelligence community.[1] She noted, however, that the present referral concerns a different matter — the alleged concealment of politically damaging cybersecurity failures in an official capacity followed by a senior position at the same company, framed as a seemingly unlawful quid pro quo — and stressed that Monaco was referred rather than charged and remains innocent until proven guilty.[1]

## Ties cited in 2025 prosecutor removals

In October 2025, [[michael-benary|Michael Ben'Ary]], head of the national security section at the [[eastern-district-of-virginia-us-attorneys-office|U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia]], was reportedly fired hours after journalist [[julie-kelly|Julie Kelly]] publicized his prior service as senior counsel to Monaco during her tenure as Deputy Attorney General in the Biden administration.[2] Kelly argued that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could not de-weaponize the Justice Department "if you have in powerful positions people who were top advisers to the very architects of the lawfare against the president."[2]
