# Lisa Monaco

Lisa Monaco is a former Biden administration Deputy Attorney General and former Obama administration national security aide who joined [[microsoft|Microsoft]] in 2025 as President of Global Affairs. In the Badlands corpus she appears as the subject of a May 2026 referral to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] accusing her of shielding Microsoft from cybersecurity enforcement actions before taking her senior position at the company.

## The DOJ referral

The [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]] referred Monaco to the Justice Department after accusing her of protecting Microsoft from cybersecurity enforcement while she oversaw the DOJ's [[civil-cyber-fraud-initiative|Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative]]. The referral alleges the Biden DOJ failed to pursue major action against Microsoft despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 tied to Russian and Chinese hackers that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.

The foundation noted that the initiative Monaco oversaw targeted other contractors for less serious alleged misconduct while no comparable case was brought against Microsoft. The complaint also points to several former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who later took jobs with Microsoft or firms connected to the company, including former DOJ official [[john-p-carlin|John P. Carlin]] and former FBI cyber official [[bryan-vorndran|Bryan A. Vorndran]]. The group asked DOJ inspectors general to investigate whether Monaco and others violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions, and whether Microsoft could face liability under the [[false-claims-act|False Claims Act]] for representations made to federal agencies about the security of its systems.

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Monaco as, in her opinion, "one of the central architects of the coordinated campaign against President Trump during his first administration," speculated to be a central player in [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]], [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]], and other key RussiaGate plotlines. Ashe noted that Monaco was referred — not charged — and stressed the presumption of innocence, while arguing that the case exemplifies how corruption in global business "only does so with the help of current and former government officials shepherding it and covering it up."
