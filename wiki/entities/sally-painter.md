# Sally Painter

Sally Painter is a co-founder of the Washington consulting firm [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]] and a board member of the [[truman-national-security-project|Truman National Security Project]].[1] She appears repeatedly in the email archive recovered from [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop]], both as a professional contact of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and as the point of contact for Blue Star's 2015 engagement by [[burisma|Burisma]].[1]

## Truman connection

Searches of the laptop archive show Painter in contact with Hunter Biden as far back as 4 March 2010.[1] On 16 February 2011 she emailed him to ask whether he would consider a seat on the board of the Truman National Security Project, and the following month an exchange titled "Truman Board" recorded Hunter saying he was "excited to get involved" and Painter replying that he had been a "big hit" and would likely receive a formal offer within three weeks.[1] [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] joined the same board around 2017, after leaving the [[state-department|State Department]], placing Painter, Hunter Biden and Sullivan on one board.[1]

## Burisma engagement

Painter appears in an October 2015 scheduling email showing a meeting on hold with Hunter Biden, [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] and [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]], and a week later Blue Star sent Burisma a "revised proposal, contract and initial invoice."[1] Pozharskyi's response pressed for deliverables that would bring "widely recognized and influential current and/or former US policy-makers" to Ukraine with "the ultimate purpose to close down for any cases/pursuits against Nikolay" — [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]] — in Ukraine.[1] Hunter Biden told Pozharskyi, "I trust Sally and Karen implicitly so I believe we are all aligned," referring to Painter and her co-founder [[karen-tramontano|Karen Tramontano]].[1]

## Contact with Antony Blinken

In his December 2020 transcribed interview before the [[senate-homeland-security-and-governmental-affairs-committee|Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs]] and Finance committees, [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] described contacts with Painter, including a 27 June 2016 email in which she referenced speaking with him at a Truman event and asked to meet about "troubling events we are seeing in Ukraine."[1] Roughly a month later a Blue Star message to [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]] remarked, "No help from our friend(s) however."[1]
