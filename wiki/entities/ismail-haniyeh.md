# Ismail Haniyeh

Ismail Haniyeh was the head of the political bureau of [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] On July 31, 2024, Hamas reported that Haniyeh had been killed in an Israeli attack on his residence in Tehran, [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Killing and aftermath

Haniyeh's death came one day after Israeli airstrikes on the [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] stronghold in southern Beirut killed Hezbollah military chief [[fouad-shokr|Fouad Shokr]].[1] Hamas Deputy Chairman Mousa Abu Marzook warned that Haniyeh's assassination would provoke a response.[1]

The killings triggered a sharp regional escalation. The U.S. Defense Department deployed 12 warships to the Middle East, including the Persian Gulf and the Mediterranean Sea, with the USS Theodore Roosevelt and six destroyers stationed in the Gulf and a group of three landing ships and two destroyers carrying 4,000 marines and sailors sent to the eastern Mediterranean.[1] The deployment was described not as an official announcement of increased forces but as a response to the deaths of key Hamas and Hezbollah figures.[1]

## Aftermath of the assassination

Haniyeh was killed in Tehran in late July 2024, hours after Israeli forces killed [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] military chief [[fuad-shukr|Fuad Shukr]] in a strike in Beirut.[2] [[iran|Iran]] blamed [[israel|Israel]] for the killing and vowed that Jerusalem must be made to pay a price, while Israel declined to comment publicly.[2] The Middle East braced for a wave of retaliatory attacks by Iran and its allies.[2]

[[white-house|White House]] officials believed that intensive diplomatic efforts to temper Iran's retaliation were having an effect, according to Washington Post reporting, which also said the Biden administration was enraged by the timing of the assassination.[2] Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] said Washington had communicated directly to both Israel and Iran that the conflict must not escalate.[2] Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] separately asked supreme leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] for a restrained response, delivering the message through [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] and advising against attacks on Israeli civilians.[2]

## Aftermath of the Tehran killing

The killing of Haniyeh, the Hamas chief, in Tehran in late July 2024 left Israel bracing for a large Iranian retaliation in the days that followed.[3] Limited exchanges of fire with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] continued along Israel's northern border, many international airlines suspended flights to Tel Aviv and Beirut, and Israeli officials warned that citizens travelling or working abroad could be targeted in a reprisal or terrorist attack.[3] Officials predicted an [[iran|Iranian]] strike as soon as the following Monday, with Hezbollah expected to play a larger role than in the ballistic missile and drone attack of April 13.[3] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] told his cabinet that Israel was in "a multi-front war against Iran's evil axis."[3]

## Named in the 2024 ICC warrant applications

Haniyeh was among the [[hamas|Hamas]] leaders named in [[international-criminal-court|ICC]] prosecutor [[karim-khan|Karim Khan]]'s May 2024 arrest warrant applications, alongside [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]] and Muhammad Deif, a pairing with Israeli officials that Britain publicly criticized.[4]
