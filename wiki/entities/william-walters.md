# William Walters

William Walters was a Denver real-estate developer and one of the two close friends who became [[neil-bush|Neil Bush]]'s partners in Colorado.[1]

In 1983 Bush formed an energy company, JNB, with just $100 in cash, and Walters invested what Bush described as "a small amount" — $150,000, equal to Bush's salary from the company for the next two years.[1] After becoming a director of [[silverado-savings-and-loan-association|Silverado]] in 1985, Bush voted to approve $96 million in loans to Walters without telling the bank that Walters was his benefactor or that he owed Walters money.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] calls this a plain conflict of interest: a small gift to Neil Bush bought a $96 million loan.[1]

Walters and fellow Bush associate [[kenneth-good|Kenneth Good]] defaulted on Silverado loans totalling $106 million, contributing to a collapse whose bailout was expected to cost $1 billion.[1]
