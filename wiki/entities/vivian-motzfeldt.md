# Vivian Motzfeldt

Vivian Motzfeldt is the Foreign Minister of [[greenland|Greenland]].[1]

In January 2026 Motzfeldt announced via social media a joint Greenlandic–Danish request for a high-level ministerial meeting with US Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] to address escalating American interest in acquiring the Arctic territory.[1] The joint request followed repeated unsuccessful attempts throughout 2025 to engage Rubio, and aimed to establish direct diplomatic channels in response to strong statements from the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]
