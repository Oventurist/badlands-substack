# Progressive Corporation

Progressive is an American insurance company, widely known for its quirky advertising campaigns, that became a defendant in a 2023 civil rights lawsuit over a race-restricted small business grant program.[1]

## 2023 grant program lawsuit

Progressive offered $25,000 grants to help black-owned businesses purchase vehicles, excluding white, Asian, Latino, and other entrepreneurs from applying. The company said the grants were intended to help black-owned firms that had historically struggled to access capital.[1]

The conservative legal group [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] and others sued Progressive, calling the program "patently unlawful" racism. The suit was brought on behalf of Nathan Roberts, the owner of the Ohio-based firm Freedom Truck Dispatch, who was ineligible to apply because he is white.[1] AFL lawyer [[gene-hamilton|Gene Hamilton]] framed the case as part of a broader campaign against large corporations that inject "racial considerations into every aspect of their business operations, employment practices, and so much more" — a challenge to corporate [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|diversity, equity and inclusion]] programs generally.[1] Progressive did not respond to press requests for comment on the suit.[1]
