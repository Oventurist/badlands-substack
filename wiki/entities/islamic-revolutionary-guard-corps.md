# Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), referred to in reporting as Iran's Revolutionary Guards, is the branch of [[iran|Iran]]'s armed forces that issued Tehran's principal responses during the March 2026 escalation with the United States.[1]

## Response to the Trump ultimatum

After President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened on March 22, 2026 to strike Iran's power plants unless the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] was fully reopened within 48 hours, Reuters reported that the Revolutionary Guards said the strait would be "completely" closed if the United States followed through on the threat.[1] Iranian officials warned in parallel that energy infrastructure in countries hosting U.S. military bases would become legitimate targets if Iranian power plants were attacked.[1]

Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described this counter-threat against water and energy infrastructure as the scenario he had warned about for months, since it makes the most strategic sense for Iran if the goal is to cripple an enemy society without directly attacking civilians.[1]

## Information operations

Over the weekend of March 21–22, 2026 the IRGC released a video showing its spokesman [[ebrahim-zolfaghari|Ebrahim Zolfaghari]] mocking President Trump during a press briefing over his two signature phrases, "You're fired" and "Thank you for your attention to this matter."[1]
