# Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) is the branch of [[iran|Iran]]'s armed forces charged with defending the Islamic Republic's political system, operating independently of the regular army and controlling Iran's ballistic missile and naval asymmetric-warfare capabilities.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports the claims of former IRGC commander [[hussein-kanani|Hussein Kanani]], who told RT in an interview aired that Monday that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] possesses a nuclear bomb and related capabilities and that the United States, along with [[israel|Israel]], is fully aware of it. Kanani warned that Iran could respond to U.S. aggression by targeting Israel with missiles or by closing the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] as a pressure tactic, potentially disrupting global oil flows including to China and Russia, and described Washington's strategy as "playing a game of chicken."

The Brief situates these statements amid U.S. airstrikes on Iranian nuclear facilities in June, anti-government protests inside Iran during December and January that Tehran attributed to U.S. and Israeli instigation, U.S. deployments led by the USS Abraham Lincoln carrier group, and Iran's announcement of missile upgrades and a shift toward an offensive military doctrine in response to [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s demands on enrichment and ballistic missiles.
