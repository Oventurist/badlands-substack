# Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) is the branch of [[iran|Iran]]'s armed forces that functions as the regime's ideological military and security apparatus, and whose current and former commanders frequently serve as public voices of Iranian strategic messaging.[1]

## Role in the 2026 US–Iran confrontation

In February 2026, former IRGC commander [[hussein-kanani|Hussein Kanani]] used an RT interview to claim that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] possesses a nuclear bomb, and to warn that Iran could retaliate against U.S. aggression by striking Israel with missiles or closing the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1] The remarks landed amid escalating U.S.–Iran tensions, which included U.S. airstrikes on Iranian nuclear facilities in June, anti-government protests inside Iran during December and January that Tehran blamed on U.S. and Israeli instigation, and new U.S. military deployments led by the USS Abraham Lincoln carrier group.[1]

Iranian officials responded to pressure from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — who demanded restrictions on uranium enrichment and the ballistic missile program — by announcing missile upgrades and a shift toward an offensive military doctrine, a posture in which the IRGC is the principal instrument.[1]

## Alleged plot against Ivanka Trump (2026)

In May 2026 an Iraqi national with alleged ties to the IRGC, [[mohammad-baqer-al-saadi|Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi]], was accused of plotting to assassinate [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka Trump]] in retaliation for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2020 order of the drone strike that killed [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] in Baghdad.[2] Sources identified Al-Saadi as a senior operative connected to both the IRGC and [[kataib-hizballah|Kata'ib Hizballah]], closely tied to Soleimani and later to IRGC Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani, and investigators allege he trained with the IRGC in Tehran before using a travel agency business to coordinate with terror cells internationally.[2] Federal prosecutors allege he was involved in at least 18 attacks and attempted attacks targeting US and Jewish sites across Europe and North America.[2]

## Oil-price warnings (March 2026)

An IRGC spokesman warned in March 2026 that continued US and Israeli actions against [[iran|Iran]] could trigger retaliation affecting oil production and economic infrastructure across the Middle East, part of Iranian warnings that escalation could drive crude to $200 per barrel.[3] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described IRGC strikes on Gulf assets, alongside Israeli attacks on Iranian oil facilities, as the deliberate targeting of energy infrastructure underlying the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] shipping collapse and the accelerating [[nuclear-renaissance|Nuclear Renaissance]].[3]

## March 2026 Hormuz threats and information operations

On March 22, 2026, after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened to strike [[iran|Iran]]'s power plants unless Tehran fully reopened the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] within 48 hours, the Revolutionary Guards responded that the strait would be "completely" closed if the United States followed through.[4] Iranian officials additionally warned that energy infrastructure in countries hosting U.S. military bases would become legitimate targets if Iranian power plants were attacked.[4] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described this as the scenario he had warned about for months, arguing it made strategic sense as a way to cripple an adversary's society without directly attacking civilians, and that Iran — advised by [[russia|Russia]] and [[china|China]] — sought to retain the moral high ground rather than mimic Israel's conduct in Gaza.[4]

The IRGC also engaged in overt information operations: a video released over the weekend before March 23 showed spokesman [[ebrahim-zolfaghari|Ebrahim Zolfaghari]] mocking Trump at a press briefing by using his signature phrases "You're fired" and "Thank you for your attention to this matter."[4]

## Warnings during the 2026 strike crisis

Amid reports that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration was weighing precision strikes on senior Iranian officials and a full blockade of Iranian oil exports, the Revolutionary Guards warned that attacks could disrupt global oil supplies through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[5] They further warned that neighboring countries permitting the use of their territory for operations against [[iran|Iran]] would be treated as hostile.[5] See [[us-precision-strike-planning-against-iran-2026|US precision strike planning against Iran (2026)]].[5]

## Warning over the Strait of Hormuz (July 2026)

In July 2026 the IRGC warned that continued U.S. aggression would only delay the reopening of the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], a statement issued as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] dropped his [[hormuz-transit-fee-proposal|20% transit fee proposal]] in favor of Gulf trade deals.[6] The warning accompanied [[iran|Iran]]'s deputy foreign minister's rejection of the premise that military pressure could force Tehran to negotiate, amid a collapse in shipping traffic through the waterway.[6]

## March 2026 Hormuz safe-passage offer

On March 9, 2026, the IRGC announced that it would grant safe and unrestricted passage through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], beginning the following Tuesday, to any Arab or European country that expelled the ambassadors of both the United States and [[israel|Israel]] from its territory, presenting the offer as a direct response to the ongoing regional conflict.[7] Badlands commentary judged that few states would expel American diplomats but that Israel's diminished standing made downgrades of Israeli ties plausible, and treated the maneuver as a demonstration that unipolar hegemony had given way to a [[multipolar-world|Multipolar World]].[7]

Reporting in the same period recounted that the entire IRGC had been restructured in August 2025 under Iranian "Devolution" — implemented by [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] — because of concerns the organization had been infiltrated and compromised, with military and intelligence structures reorganized and the Ayatollah removed from the chain of command to guard against a decapitation strike.[7] A message attributed to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard was also reportedly texted en masse to Israeli phones, claiming the last US radar systems in the region had been destroyed and telling recipients that "no shelter can provide safety."[7]

## Alleged planning of the October 7 attacks

According to [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] reporting cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], IRGC officers worked with [[hamas|Hamas]] from August 2023 to devise the air, land and sea incursions that became the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]] — the most significant breach of Israel’s borders since the 1973 [[yom-kippur-war|Yom Kippur War]].[8] Details were refined during several meetings in [[beirut|Beirut]] attended by IRGC officers and representatives of four Iran-backed militant groups, including Hamas and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], with senior members of those groups saying the green light was given at a meeting the Monday before the assault.[8] U.S. officials, including Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], said they had seen no evidence corroborating the account.[8]

## Losses in the Damascus embassy strike (April 2024)

The IRGC said in a statement that seven Iranian military advisers died in the April 1, 2024 bombing of [[iran|Iran]]'s embassy compound in Damascus, [[syria|Syria]], including [[mohammad-reza-zahedi|Mohammad Reza Zahedi]], a senior commander in its [[quds-force|Quds Force]], the elite foreign espionage and paramilitary arm.[9] Three of the seven were senior commanders, and the strike marked the first time [[israel|Israel]] had hit the embassy compound itself.[9] See [[2024-israeli-strike-on-iranian-embassy-in-damascus]].[9]

## Losses in the June 2025 Israeli strikes

In the Israeli strikes on Tehran launched in the early hours of June 13, 2025, the IRGC's commander in chief, General [[hossein-salami|Hossein Salami]], was killed, according to Iranian state media and Israeli military statements.[10] Reporting indicated that other "top Revolutionary Guard generals" and prominent nuclear scientists were also killed as simultaneous raids struck nuclear and military sites and the homes of high-value targets.[10] See [[june-2025-israeli-strikes-on-iran|the June 2025 Israeli strikes on Iran]].[10]

## 2024 election phishing operation

In August 2024 [[google-threat-analysis-group|Google's Threat Analysis Group]] reported that it had disrupted a "small but steady" phishing operation run by a hacking unit tied to the IRGC.[11] In May and June the operation targeted the personal email accounts of roughly a dozen individuals affiliated with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], including current and former U.S. government officials and people associated with the respective campaigns.[11] Google blocked numerous login attempts and said the operation accessed the Gmail account of at least one high-profile political consultant in July before the account was secured and the matter referred to law enforcement.[11]

## Targeting in the October 2023 U.S. strikes in Syria

In late October 2023, U.S. Air Force F-16s struck a weapons storage facility and an ammunition storage facility in eastern Syria used by the IRGC and its proxies, in retaliation for rocket and drone attacks on American forces in Iraq and Syria.[12] U.S. officials characterized the targeting as an escalation because it hit facilities used by Iran's own forces rather than only the militias Tehran arms, trains, and equips.[12] Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd J. Austin III]] described the operation as "precision self-defense strikes" against "Iranian-backed militia groups."[12] See [[2023-us-strikes-on-iranian-linked-targets-in-syria]].

## Damascus consulate strike, April 2024

Seven members of the Revolutionary Guards, including two generals, were killed when [[israel|Israel]] destroyed an Iranian consulate building in Damascus on April 1, 2024.[13] Reporting also described the toll as a senior general and six other Iranian officers killed in an airstrike on the Iranian embassy compound.[13] The deaths triggered threats of reprisal from Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] and a period of acute regional escalation in which the United States pledged "ironclad" support for Israel.[13]
