# Aimee Huber

**Aimee Huber** is the executive director of [[first-choice-womens-resource-centers|First Choice Women's Resource Centers]], the New Jersey crisis pregnancy center network that won a 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruling allowing it to challenge a state investigation in federal court.

Responding to the decision, Huber said: "Since pregnancy centers like ours do not perform or refer for abortion, we are targets for a government that disagrees with our views. If our attorney general can bully us, it can happen in other states that promote abortion." The remark frames the subpoena issued by [[matthew-platkin|Matthew Platkin]] as viewpoint-driven enforcement rather than routine regulation, and is quoted approvingly in Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].
