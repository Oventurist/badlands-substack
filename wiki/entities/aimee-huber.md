# Aimee Huber

**Aimee Huber** is the executive director of [[first-choice-womens-resource-centers|First Choice Women's Resource Centers]], a [[new-jersey|New Jersey]] [[crisis-pregnancy-centers|crisis pregnancy center]] network.[1]

Following the 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruling allowing First Choice to pursue its federal challenge to a state subpoena, Huber said: "Since pregnancy centers like ours do not perform or refer for abortion, we are targets for a government that disagrees with our views. If our attorney general can bully us, it can happen in other states that promote abortion."[1]
