# Michael Sussmann

Michael Sussmann is an American attorney who was one of only two defendants brought to trial by Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] in the course of his investigation into the origins of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] inquiry.[1]

## Role in the Durham investigation

Sussmann's prosecution, alongside that of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]], became the principal yardstick by which critics measured the Durham inquiry's output.[1] Because neither case produced a conviction, commentators across the political spectrum used the results to argue either that Durham had been obstructed by a corrupt system or that his mandate had never been serious.[1] [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] pointed to the Sussmann and Danchenko outcomes when asking how well the strategy of pursuing individual charges had actually worked, noting that "people wanted to see charges" and were left dissatisfied.[1]

During Durham's June 21, 2023 testimony before the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]], Democratic members cited the small number of trials — two — and the absence of major convictions as evidence that the [[durham-report|Durham Report]] exonerated the officials [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had accused of running the [[russiagate-hoax|Russia collusion hoax]].[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued the opposite: that the thinness of the trial record suggested the more serious offenses remained unexposed.[1]
