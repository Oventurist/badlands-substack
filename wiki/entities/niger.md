# Niger

Niger is a Sahelian state identified in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] as part of the regional grouping with which [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] has deepened security cooperation, alongside Russia and [[mali]]. That realignment, following the expulsion of French troops from Burkina Faso, forms the background against which Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] made his July 2026 allegations that Western intelligence operatives offered his government chemical agents for use against populations in areas held by armed groups. The Brief frames Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso as states pursuing sovereignty from [[france|France]] and the [[european-union|European Union]] while confronting [[isis|ISIS]]-aligned insurgencies.
