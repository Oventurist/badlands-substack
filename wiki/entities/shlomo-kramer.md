# Shlomo Kramer

Shlomo Kramer is an Israeli-American technology executive and the chief executive officer of [[cato-networks|Cato Networks]].[1]

## Call to limit the First Amendment

On December 29, 2025, Kramer appeared on CNBC and argued for limiting the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] in order to "protect it" against AI-generated deepfakes and misinformation that he said could polarize society.[1] He proposed AI-driven "authenticity scores" for social media content and greater government-platform control to verify information, framing these measures as essential for national security amid rising cyber warfare risks.[1] Commentators circulating the interview characterized his position as an open call by an Israeli billionaire for the First Amendment to be eliminated, with artificial intelligence playing a major role in restricting free speech.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] linked Kramer's remarks to what he described as a broader pattern of Israeli billionaires, politicians, and influencers suddenly challenging free speech and the First Amendment, alongside a flood of revisionist claims that America was founded on "Judeo-Christian values" — a term he said had not appeared in print media before the 1960s and was not adopted into the cultural lexicon until the 1980s.[1] He argued that [[israel|Israel]] was founded by Eastern European socialists, many openly Bolshevik, who created a nation championing the idea that the security of the state outranks any individual, and that this ethos underlies Kramer's proposal to limit the First Amendment "so that it can be saved."[1]
