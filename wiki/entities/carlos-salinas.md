# Carlos Salinas

Carlos Salinas de Gortari was president of Mexico and the brother of [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]], and a member of the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] described in the Badlands series as intimate friends of the [[bush-family|Bush family]].[1]

Late in his term Salinas appointed [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] — brother of the murder victim [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]] — as special prosecutor to investigate the assassination. The article argues the appointment was designed to bury the case against his own brother Raúl, because nobody would question findings delivered by the victim's brother; Mario was paid substantially, with the money coming from the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]].[1] Mario resigned a few months later, saying ruling-party power brokers were blocking the investigation, and Mexican investigators subsequently found he had altered witness statements to delete references to Raúl's role.[1]

Salinas is described as having started out popular, like Venezuela's [[carlos-andres-perez|Carlos Andrés Pérez]], and as having ended in self-exile — a pattern the article calls a CIA template, noting that [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] was connected to both presidents.[1]
