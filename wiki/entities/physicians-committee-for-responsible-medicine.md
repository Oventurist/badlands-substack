# Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine

The Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine (PCRM) is an animal-welfare advocacy organization that opposes the use of animals in medical research.[1] It triggered the February 2023 federal inquiry into [[neuralink|Neuralink]]'s handling of biological materials.[1]

## Complaint against Neuralink

On February 9, 2023, PCRM wrote to Secretary of Transportation [[pete-buttigieg|Pete Buttigieg]] to alert the [[us-department-of-transportation|U.S. Department of Transportation]] to records it had obtained concerning Neuralink.[1] PCRM said the emails and documents suggested unsafe packaging and movement of implants removed from the brains of monkeys, and that these implants may have carried infectious diseases in violation of federal law.[1]

PCRM did not identify any harm resulting from the incidents, but said Neuralink's actions "may pose a serious and ongoing public health risk."[1] Badlands Media commentary characterized the resulting probe as resting on conjecture, and noted PCRM's categorical opposition to animal research as context for the complaint.[1]
