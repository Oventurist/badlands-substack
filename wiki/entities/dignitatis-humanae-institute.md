# Dignitatis Humanae Institute

The Dignitatis Humanae Institute is a Catholic organization created by [[otto-von-habsburg|Otto von Habsburg]] as a sister body to the [[pan-european-union|Pan-European Union]], with the stated aim of uniting "the right of the world" under what Matthew Ehret calls a gnostic Catholic veneer and a Clash of Civilizations rebranding for the alt-right.[1]

## Trisulti headquarters

The institute is based in an 800-year-old monastery at Trisulti, Italy.[1] The abbey was constructed on the orders of [[pope-innocent-iii|Pope Innocent III]] in 1204 to celebrate the [[fourth-crusade|Fourth Crusade]], which saw the strategically important sacking of Constantinople by hordes of Christian crusading mercenaries killing fellow Christians.[1] Today the abbey is being set up as the headquarters of a new "Gladiator School" run by Bannon's right-hand man at the institute, Benjamin Harnwell, dubbed "the Academy of the Judeo-Christian West."[1]

## Patrons and personnel

The Jesuit-trained [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] joined the institute alongside a plethora of highly connected figures exposed in a study by researcher Stan Ezrol.[1] In his 2019 study, Ezrol described Bannon's four co-patrons as Archduke Otto von Habsburg; His Royal Highness Charles of Bourbon Two Sicilies and Duke of Castro, "a leading figure in the anti-Renaissance wing of European nobility"; Field Marshal the Lord Guthrie GCB, LVO, OBE; and Father Matthew Festing.[1]

A former chairman and current member of the institute is Lord Nicholas Windsor of the British royal family who, like the former Anglican operative Lord Guthrie and former British prime minister [[tony-blair|Tony Blair]], converted to Catholicism — in Ehret's framing, to become an aspiring handler of the global alt-right.[1]
