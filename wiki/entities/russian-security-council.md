# Russian Security Council

The Russian Security Council is the senior consultative body advising the Russian president on national security policy, headed by a secretary appointed by the president.[1]

## Shoigu's appointment, May 2024

In the cabinet reshuffle announced on 12 May 2024, President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] proposed removing [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]] as defense minister — replacing him with acting First Deputy Prime Minister [[andrey-belousov|Andrey Belousov]] — and appointed Shoigu secretary of the Security Council.[1] The [[federation-council|Federation Council]] scheduled consultations on the nominations for 13 and 14 May 2024.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the move less as a demotion than as a signal about Russian strategy, noting that Belousov is an economist with long experience in the Russian banking sector rather than a defense professional, and arguing that Putin was treating the Russian economy as a matter of national defense ahead of a further phase of asymmetric warfare.[1]
