# Dan "Razin" Caine

Dan "Razin'" Caine is identified in the Badlands Media corpus as Chairman of the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs of Staff]] under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. He appears in the April 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-07c|Badlands Brief]] in connection with the announced Israel–Lebanon ceasefire.

According to Trump's public statement, the president directed Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], and Caine as chairman to work with [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]] "to achieve a Lasting PEACE" following the ten-day ceasefire scheduled to begin at 5 p.m. EST Tuesday. Caine's inclusion places the senior uniformed officer of the United States military inside a diplomatic negotiating team alongside the vice president and the secretary of state, a pairing the source presents without further elaboration.
