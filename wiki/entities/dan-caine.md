# Dan "Razin" Caine

Dan "Razin" Caine is the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, the senior uniformed officer of the United States armed forces, serving under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In April 2026 Trump publicly directed Caine, alongside Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], to work with [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]] to convert a newly announced ten-day ceasefire into a lasting peace agreement.[1] The assignment placed the Chairman in a diplomatic-military coordinating role alongside civilian principals, reflecting the military dimension of monitoring a truce involving [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]

Caine's role sits within a broader period of institutional strain and repositioning at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], which was simultaneously reaching into the civilian industrial base to expand weapons production.[1]

## Munitions acceleration roundtable

General Caine, as Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, co-led with Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] the June 2025 [[pentagon|Pentagon]] roundtable that inaugurated the missile production drive later known as the [[munitions-acceleration-council|Munitions Acceleration Council]].[2] The meeting drew major arms contractors, startups such as [[anduril|Anduril Industries]], and crucial component suppliers, and the resulting effort focuses on twelve weapons the Pentagon wants stockpiled for a potential conflict with [[china|China]].[2]
