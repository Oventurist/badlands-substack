# Stack Data Strategy

**Stack Data Strategy** is a political data and polling firm whose November 2023 analysis of the 2024 presidential race was widely reported.[1]

## 2023 electoral college analysis

The firm surveyed 15,205 registered voters nationwide between October 13 and November 3, 2023, and released the results through [[politico|Politico]] on Monday, November 13.[1] The analysis found that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would win 292 electoral votes against [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 246 in a hypothetical general election matchup, returning Trump to the White House, even though Biden would narrowly win the popular vote 49 percent to 48 percent.[1]

The same analysis found that Biden would defeat Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] decisively, while Trump also beat Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] in hypothetical matchups.[1] The findings were reported by [[breitbart-news|Breitbart]] and carried in the Badlands News Brief.[1]
