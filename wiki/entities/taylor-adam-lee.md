# Taylor Adam Lee

Taylor Adam Lee is a US Army soldier from El Paso, Texas, who was arrested in August 2025 at the age of 22 on federal charges of attempted transmission of national defense information to a foreign adversary and attempted export of controlled technical data without a license.[1]

## Charges

According to the criminal complaint announced by the FBI and the [[justice-department|US Department of Justice]], Lee sought to transmit sensitive national defense information to Russia regarding the operation of the M1A2 Abrams, described by prosecutors as the United States' main battle tank.[1] Assistant Attorney General for National Security John A. Eisenberg said the National Security Division "will continue to work with our law enforcement and military partners to ensure that such serious transgressions are met with serious consequences."[1]

US Attorney Justin R. Simmons for the Western District of Texas said national security had long been among the Justice Department's highest priorities and that his district remained "alert for those who wish to help our adversaries and harm the United States."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that in an earlier era the arrest of an accused spy would have been the biggest story in the country, invoking Richard Nixon's remark about Alger Hiss, but that in the current news cycle the case barely registered.[1] She noted the timing — arriving amid [[russiagate-hoax|RussiaGate]] revelations and the prospect of peace in what she characterized as a fabricated war — and speculated that Russia itself might be outing traitors.[1]
