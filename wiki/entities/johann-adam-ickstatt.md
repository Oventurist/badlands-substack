# Johann Adam Ickstatt

Baron Johann Adam Ickstatt (1702–1776) was a Bavarian jurist, curator of the [[university-of-ingolstadt|University of Ingolstadt]] and member of the Privy Council who became the guardian of [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]].[1]

After the death of Weishaupt's father George in 1755, the boy was turned over to Ickstatt, described as his liberal godfather.[1] Though Weishaupt was educated by the [[jesuits|Jesuits]], he was "accorded free range in the private library of his godfather," where "the boy's questioning spirit was deeply impressed by the brilliant though pretentious works of the French 'philosophers' with which the shelves were plentifully stocked."[1] The Badlands account attributes Weishaupt's fascination with the [[eleusinian-mysteries|Eleusinian]] and Pythagorean Mysteries — later reflected in the symbolism of the [[bavarian-illuminati|Illuminati]] — to early access to Ickstatt's considerable library.[1]

Weishaupt married in 1773 against Ickstatt's wishes.[1]
