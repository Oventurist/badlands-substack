# LaMonica McIver

LaMonica McIver is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from New Jersey.[1] She became a subject of national controversy in May 2025 when federal criminal charges were filed against her over her conduct during a protest outside an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] detention facility in Newark, New Jersey.[1]

## 2025 charges

The charges were announced by [[alina-habba|Alina Habba]], previously a personal defense lawyer for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and at the time serving as acting U.S. attorney for the District of New Jersey.[1] The announcement was reported by NBC News and carried in the May 21, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where it appeared alongside coverage of the renewed legal exposure of former FBI director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1]

The Brief's editors juxtaposed the two developments deliberately: the McIver prosecution was presented as evidence that the Trump-era Justice Department was willing to bring charges against sitting members of Congress at the same moment that Comey — a private citizen and author on a book tour — was publicly dismissing the likelihood that he would face criminal exposure of his own.[1]

## Framing in Badlands coverage

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the pairing to advance the outlet's recurring "no one is above the law" theme, contrasting the treatment of figures aligned with the current administration against that of figures associated with the prior one.[1] The McIver matter is treated in that framing less as an immigration-enforcement story than as a data point in the outlet's argument about asymmetric application of federal criminal law.[1]
