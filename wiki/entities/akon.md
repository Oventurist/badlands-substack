# Akon

Akon is the recording artist behind the failed plan to build [[akon-city|Akon City]], a $6 billion futuristic metropolis in [[senegal|Senegal]].[1]

## Akon City

The Senegalese government granted the "Locked Up" artist 136 acres of land just outside the capital, Dakar, for a Wakanda-inspired "city of tomorrow" that was to include state-of-the-art condominiums, amusement parks, a seaside resort, hospitals and a university, all powered by solar energy and transacted in his Akoin cryptocurrency.[1]

After several missed payments to the government and a dramatic devaluation of the Akoin cryptocurrency, Akon received an ultimatum in 2024: Sapco-Senegal, the state-owned company charged with developing the country and driving tourism, gave him two weeks to begin making the idea a reality.[1] Bloomberg subsequently reported that the head of Sapco-Senegal told the Senegalese press the project was officially dead, with the government reclaiming the land and Sapco moving forward with a $1.2 billion plan to convert the area into a tourist destination featuring hotels, apartments, a marina and a promenade.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]], drawing on his own construction background, argued that the pictured structure would have been built in six months at most using New York City concrete methods, with the top structure taking three weeks, and that the entire city could have been completed in five years.[1] He cited a project called "Ridge Hill," built from scratch between 2008 and 2011 after crews flattened a small mountain, as his benchmark, and concluded that for six billion dollars American builders "would've built two."[1]
