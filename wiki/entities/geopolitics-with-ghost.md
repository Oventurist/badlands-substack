# Geopolitics with Ghost

Geopolitics with Ghost is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program hosted by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], streamed on the Badlands Media Rumble channel.[1] The show covers international affairs from the network's sovereignty-versus-globalism analytical frame.[1]

In September 2025, the host announced a 2:00 PM EST episode devoted to the [[greta-van-susteren|Greta Van Susteren]] interview in which [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was asked whether Israel had any involvement in the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]].[1] The program has also been the venue for his commentary on Middle East realignment, including Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]]'s condemnation of Israeli strikes on [[qatar|Qatar]] and the diplomatic position of Gulf leaders such as [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Emir Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]], [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], and [[mohammed-bin-zayed|Mohammed bin Zayed]].[1]
