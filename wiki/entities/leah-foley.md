# Leah B. Foley

Leah B. Foley is the United States Attorney for the District of Massachusetts, whose office announced charges in the 2026 global insider trading case.[1]

## 2026 insider trading prosecution

Announcing the unsealing of charges against thirty defendants in a decade-long insider trading scheme, Foley said: "Our country's financial markets and professional firms should be free from the rampant fraud and breaches of duty that these charges allege."[1] The case, brought by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], alleges that corporate attorneys and other financial professionals stole confidential information on nearly 30 merger and acquisition deals from premier U.S. law firms, including a firm headquartered in Massachusetts.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Foley's statement in arguing that the role of professional services firms in global corruption had finally entered public view, and that liability should extend to the firms themselves rather than being written off as rogue actors.[1]
