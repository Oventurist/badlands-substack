# H. Lawrence Culp Jr.

H. Lawrence Culp Jr. is the chief executive officer of GE Aerospace, the American jet engine manufacturer that became an independent publicly traded company after the 2024 split of General Electric. He previously served as CEO of General Electric itself, leading its restructuring.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Culp was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. His presence tied GE Aerospace's jet engine business to a delegation of American industrial leadership — alongside [[kelly-ortberg|Kelly Ortberg]] of [[boeing|Boeing]] — traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]], with the administration aiming to secure business deals and purchase agreements.
