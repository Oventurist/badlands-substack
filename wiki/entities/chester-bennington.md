# Chester Bennington

Chester Bennington was the lead singer of the American rock band Linkin Park. His 2017 death is treated in Badlands Media commentary as one of a cluster of suspicious musician deaths tied to an alleged exposé of [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]].[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] stated that Bennington, his friend [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]], and the Swedish DJ [[avicii|Avicii]] "were said to be producing a documentary exposing child trafficking at the time," and that all three were subsequently reported to have killed themselves.[1] The essay treats the sequence as evidence of retaliation by the [[cabal|Cabal]] against insiders who attempt to expose it.[1]

## Context

Carlson argued that others associated with the same effort — naming [[brad-pitt|Brad Pitt]] and producer [[rick-rubin|Rick Rubin]] — reacted to the deaths by stepping back in line, which he described as understandable.[1] The account forms part of a wider claim that entertainers who break with the industry's bargain die untimely deaths, while politicians are removed through character assassination instead.[1] Badlands Media notes that its articles represent the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
