# Angela Merkel

Angela Merkel is the former German chancellor whose government deepened Germany's reliance on [[russia|Russian]] energy imports.[1]

## Trump's 2018 warning

Badlands commentary recalls that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] warned Merkel and other European leaders on June 9, 2018, at the Group of Seven summit in Canada about the predicament of depending on Russia for energy while expecting American funding for European defense — a confrontation remembered largely through a widely circulated meme image.[1] The warning was cited in 2026 as prescient amid the [[european-fuel-supply-crisis-2026|European fuel supply crisis]].[1]

## Alleged cover-up of the BND Covid origins report

In March 2025 a joint investigation by the German newspapers Süddeutsche Zeitung and Die Zeit alleged that Merkel, as chancellor, and her successor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]] both turned a blind eye to an explosive intelligence report by Germany's foreign intelligence agency, the [[bundesnachrichtendienst|BND]], which found a strong probability that the Covid pandemic was caused by a laboratory accident in China.[2] The BND had concluded in 2020 — drawing on public records, data from the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]], and an intelligence operation codenamed "Saaremaa" — that there was an "80 to 95 per cent" probability that Covid-19 originated in Wuhan.[2] According to Süddeutsche Zeitung, the BND presented the findings to Merkel, who chose to keep the information "under lock and key."[2]
