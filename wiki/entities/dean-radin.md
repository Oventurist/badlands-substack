# Dean Radin

**Dr. Dean Radin** is cited in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for research on intention.[1]

The essay states that Radin's research "shows that intention, when focused and emotionally coherent, alters outcomes at a distance," and concludes from it that the reader is "not just part of the field" but "influencing it."[1]

The claim appears in the essay's section on the awakening collective field, following [[rollin-mccraty|Rollin McCraty]] of the [[heartmath-institute|HeartMath Institute]] and [[clare-graves|Clare Graves]], and leads directly into the article's definition of leadership "in the age of resonance," in which one leads "with signal" rather than strategy.[1]
