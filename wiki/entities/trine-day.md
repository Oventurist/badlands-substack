# Trine Day

Trine Day Press is an American independent publisher specialising in suppressed and dissident history. It published [[valediction-three-nights-of-desmond|Valediction: Three Nights of Desmond]], the account of Afghanistan and the Cold War intelligence wars written by the husband-and-wife team of [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]] and [[elizabeth-gould|Elizabeth Gould]].[1]
