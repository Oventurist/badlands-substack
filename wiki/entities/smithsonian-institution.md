# Smithsonian Institution

The Smithsonian Institution is the United States' national complex of museums, education and research centers, and the National Zoo, based principally in Washington, D.C. In March 2025 it became the target of a presidential executive order aimed at removing what the administration called "improper ideology" from its programming.[1]

## March 2025 executive order

On 27 March 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed an executive order targeting funding to Smithsonian programs featuring "divisive narratives" and "improper ideology." Trump said there had been a "concerted and widespread" effort over the previous decade to rewrite American history by replacing "objective facts" with a "distorted narrative driven by ideology rather than truth."[1]

The order placed Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] in charge of the effort to remove improper ideology from the Institution, encompassing its museums, education and research centers, and the National Zoo. It specifically named the National Museum of African American History and Culture and the Women's History Museum, then still in development.[1]

The text of the order stated that museums in the nation's capital "should be places where individuals go to learn — not to be subjected to ideological indoctrination or divisive narratives that distort our shared history."[1]
