# Charles Littlejohn

Charles Littlejohn is the former consulting-firm contractor who stole and leaked the tax records of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and thousands of other Americans, and who was sentenced in January 2024 to five years in prison.[1] In October 2023, Littlejohn, then 38, pleaded guilty to one count of unauthorized disclosure of income tax returns.[1]

## The theft

According to his plea agreement, Littlejohn stole Trump's tax returns along with the tax data of "thousands of the nation's wealthiest people" while working for a consulting firm that held contracts with the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].[1] He leaked the information to two news outlets, deleted the documents from his IRS-assigned laptop before returning it, and covered the rest of his digital tracks by deleting the locations where he had initially stored the information.[1]

## Sentencing

Judge [[ana-reyes|Ana Reyes]] emphasized the gravity of the offense, stating multiple times that it amounted to an attack on the United States and its legal foundation. "What you did in attacking the sitting president of the United States was an attack on our constitutional democracy," Reyes said, adding that Littlejohn "pulled off the biggest heist in IRS history."[1] The judge compared his actions to the January 6, 2021 Capitol attack, noting that "your actions were also a threat to our democracy."[1]
