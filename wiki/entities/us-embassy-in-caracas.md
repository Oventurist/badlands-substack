# US Embassy in Caracas

The United States Embassy in Caracas is the American diplomatic mission to [[venezuela|Venezuela]]. It formally resumed operations on a Monday in late March 2026, a step presented by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration as a major milestone in advancing Venezuela's economic recovery and eventual political transition.[1]

## Closure and reopening

The embassy had been closed since 2019, with diplomatic functions handled in the interim through the Venezuela Affairs Unit based in Bogotá, Colombia.[1] A [[state-department|State Department]] memo seen by Bloomberg News said the reopening would "strengthen our ability to engage directly with Venezuela's interim government, civil society, and the private sector."[1] [[laura-dogu|Laura Dogu]], a former US ambassador to Honduras and Nicaragua, has led efforts on the ground in Caracas as chargé d'affaires, overseeing restoration work at the chancery building ahead of a broader return of personnel and the eventual resumption of consular services.[1]

## Policy framing

State Department spokesman [[tommy-pigott|Tommy Pigott]] described the reopening as a key milestone in implementing the administration's three-phase plan for Venezuela, which outlines stabilization, economic recovery, and political reconciliation leading toward democratic elections.[1] Officials noted that Dogu had met with US investors the previous week, saying the private sector would play a central role in rebuilding the country's economy.[1]

## Commentary

The commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the reopening to recap the sequence of events in Venezuela: [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] departed the country and is ostensibly jailed in New York and appearing in court, while the rest of his government remained largely intact, with the defense minister replaced only days earlier.[1] He noted that Trump had dropped all economic sanctions against Venezuela, partnered with it to extract Venezuelan oil, and restored relations including the Caracas embassy — while current President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] maintains Maduro is still the rightful leader — and argued this pattern suggests Trump had been working with Maduro and Rodríguez all along.[1]
