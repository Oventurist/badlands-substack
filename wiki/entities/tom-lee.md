# Tom Lee

Tom Lee is a Wall Street strategist associated with the research firm Fundstrat, known for publicly bullish forecasts on [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]].[1]

## $200,000 Bitcoin target (September 2025)

In early September 2025 Lee pushed a headline-grabbing $200,000 year-end price target for Bitcoin, attributing the market's recent weakness to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s reluctance to cut interest rates.[1] He argued that Bitcoin is sensitive to Fed rate cuts and that the central bank's two-month pause explained why the asset had not rallied over that period.[1]

Lee has further argued that the influx of institutional investors gives Bitcoin new "counter-cyclical characteristics," and that larger players could push prices substantially higher over time.[1]

## Criticism from Peter Schiff

Gold advocate [[peter-schiff|Peter Schiff]] challenged Lee's thesis, noting on X that gold had risen roughly 10% over the preceding two months to a record $3,620 while Bitcoin had failed to follow, a divergence Schiff said undercut the rate-cut explanation for Bitcoin's stagnation.[1]
