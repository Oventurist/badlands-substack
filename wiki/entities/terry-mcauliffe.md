# Terry McAuliffe

Terry McAuliffe is an American Democratic political figure and a close friend of President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]].[1] He sold his stock in [[global-crossing|Global Crossing]] before the company's collapse in the [[dot-com-bubble|dotcom crash]], and used the resulting profits to help the Clintons buy their mansion in Chappaqua, New York after they left the White House.[1] McAuliffe later became governor of Virginia.[1]
