# John F. Kennedy

John Fitzgerald Kennedy was the 35th President of the United States, serving from January 1961 until his assassination in November 1963.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names Kennedy — and, "to a lesser but relevant extent," [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — as a president who attempted to revive the noble origins of the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods institutions]] and of the United States government after they had, in the author's account, been hijacked by deep state operatives in the years following the Second World War. Kennedy thus functions in the article as evidence that the tradition of [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]]'s [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]] and development-oriented finance retained American adherents into the post-war period, even as institutional power moved against it.

Elsewhere in the Badlands corpus, Kennedy features in connection with the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion|Bay of Pigs invasion]] and the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]].
