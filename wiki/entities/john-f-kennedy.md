# John F. Kennedy

John F. Kennedy was a United States president cited in Badlands Media commentary as one of the figures who attempted to revive the noble original purposes of the postwar institutions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Role in the argument

The article states that although the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods institutions]] such as the IMF and World Bank — and the US government itself — were hijacked by deep state operatives representing the worst oligarchical inclinations of humanity in the post-World War II years, the truth of the noble origins of these institutions should not be ignored, and that Kennedy attempted to revive them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is credited with a lesser but, in the author's view, relevant attempt at the same.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) The institutional design Kennedy sought to restore is traced to [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] and the [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## The War of the Letters (1963)

In 1963 Kennedy engaged Israeli Prime Minister David Ben-Gurion in what became known as the War of the Letters, pressing him about a suspected covert Israeli nuclear program that President Eisenhower had warned Kennedy about during the customary presidential transition meeting.[2] Ben-Gurion blocked and restricted the American inspectors Kennedy dispatched to Israel, barring them from certain buildings, and Kennedy assured him he would learn the truth.[2] Badlands commentary counts his assassination among the events it links to the [[israeli-nuclear-program|Israeli nuclear program]], noting that the optics afterward were bad enough that Ben-Gurion resigned.[2]


## Parkland doctors dispute the official account

The [[warren-commission|Warren Commission]] concluded that two shots fired by lone gunman [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]] from the sixth floor of the Texas School Book Depository struck Kennedy from behind during the Dallas motorcade, one bullet entering his upper back and exiting near the larynx and a second entering the right side of his head and exiting through his forehead.[3] Kennedy was still breathing when he arrived at Parkland Hospital and was pronounced dead roughly thirty minutes later.[3]

In November 2023, footage of seven Parkland emergency-room physicians interviewed in 2013 was released in the documentary [[jfk-what-the-doctors-saw|"JFK: What the Doctors Saw"]], in which the doctors disputed the commission's account.[3] Executive producer Jacquelynn Lueth reported that the physicians reacted strongly to the autopsy photographs and that the president's appearance at Parkland did not match the images taken at Bethesda, that those who saw the neck wound believed it to be an entrance wound, and that several described a gaping hole in the back of his head.[3]
