# 4chan

4chan is an anonymous English-language imageboard frequently cited in discussions of online radicalization. It was named as a defendant in the civil litigation arising from the 2022 Buffalo, New York, supermarket shooting.[1]

## Buffalo radicalization lawsuit

Plaintiffs alleged that 4chan, together with [[meta-platforms|Meta]], [[google|Alphabet]] and [[reddit|Reddit]], profited from racist, antisemitic and violent content in order to maximize user engagement, and that this content radicalized the then-18-year-old gunman [[payton-gendron|Payton Gendron]].[1] Erie County Supreme Court Justice [[paula-feroleto|Paula Feroleto]] denied a motion to dismiss the suit in March 2024, holding that the complaint stated viable causes of action at that stage of the litigation.[1] The platforms' attorneys had characterized the sites as message boards containing third-party content.[1]
