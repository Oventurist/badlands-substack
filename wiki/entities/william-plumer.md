# William Plumer

William Plumer was a United States Senator and, per [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], a leader of the [[essex-junto|Essex Junto]] whose journal supplies documentary proof of [[aaron-burr|Aaron Burr]]'s leading role in the New England secession plot.[1]

Plumer recorded a conspiratorial meeting at his own house in the winter of 1803–04 attended by Senator James Hillhouse, Burr, and Senator Uriah Tracy.[1] He wrote that Hillhouse "unequivocally declared that it was his opinion that the United States would soon form two distinct governments," and that Burr "conversed very freely on the subject," leaving Plumer with the impression "that Burr not only thought a separation would not only take place but that it was necessary."[1] Ehret cites this entry against popular historians who deny Burr's treachery, some of whom have gone so far as to celebrate him as a hero.[1]
