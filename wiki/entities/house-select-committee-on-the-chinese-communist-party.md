# House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party

The **House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party** is a select panel of the United States House of Representatives that examines the strategic, economic, and security challenges posed by the Chinese Communist Party.[1]

On Wednesday, January 31, 2024, the committee served as the venue for [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]]'s announcement that the Bureau had shut down the China-backed hacking group code-named [[volt-typhoon|Volt Typhoon]], which had compromised hundreds of outdated office and home-office routers in a campaign aimed at United States water treatment plants, the power grid, oil and natural gas pipelines, and transportation systems.[1] Testifying before the panel, Wray warned that Chinese actors were "actively attacking our economic security, engaging in wholesale theft of our innovation, and our personal and corporate data" on a daily basis.[1]

The hearing's disclosures were covered by [[cnbc|CNBC]] and drew commentary from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who argued that Wray's testimony amounted to an official admission of deep vulnerabilities in centralized American infrastructure — vulnerabilities she said extend logically to election systems despite government assurances to the contrary.[1]
