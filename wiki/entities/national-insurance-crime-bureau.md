# National Insurance Crime Bureau

The National Insurance Crime Bureau is a U.S. non-profit organisation focused on insurance fraud and crime, noted in Badlands Media commentary as the employer [[olivia-troye|Olivia Troye]] joined immediately after leaving the [[donald-trump|Trump]] White House.[1]

## Troye's appointment

In August 2020 — the same month her departure from the Office of the Vice President became public — Troye joined the bureau as vice president of strategy, policy and plans, mirroring the title she had held as an intelligence official in the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] Office of Intelligence and Analysis.[1] Erik Carlson's December 27, 2023 article "The Sabotage of Trump's Administration" lists the position alongside her advisory role at [[defending-democracy-together|Defending Democracy Together]] as part of the soft landing she received after what he argues was a firing for sabotage rather than a resignation.[1]
