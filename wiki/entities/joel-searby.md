# Joel Searby

Joel Bruce Searby is an American political consultant known for his work in NeverTrump and anti-Trump political organizing, and the leader of a national anti-Trump operation.[1] He was reported by the Badlands News Brief of June 27, 2024 to have been arrested in Florida on charges related to the alleged solicitation of a 15-year-old boy.[1]

## Arrest and charges

According to WCJB-TV in Gainesville, Florida, cited by the Western Journal, Searby was arrested on a Thursday in June 2024 on various charges related to allegedly attempting to meet up with and molest a 15-year-old boy.[1] The Alachua Chronicle reported that "Joel Bruce Searby, 43, was arrested this morning and charged with lewd/lascivious conduct, two counts of obscene communication related to luring a minor to meet him for sex, and using a two-way communications device to facilitate a felony."[1] Searby had also been associated with a charter school initiative in Newberry, Florida, and served on an education board there.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the arrest as part of a recurring pattern in which figures who most loudly condemn [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over allegations of sexual misconduct are themselves accused of sexual crimes, comparing the "Christians Against Trumpism" branding to the [[lincoln-project|Lincoln Project]] and its own scandals.[1] The commentary argued that MAGA opposes child predators "full stop," that the demonization of the movement makes more sense in light of the "secret sins" of Trump's fiercest opponents, and that grooming conditions children to protect their abusers.[1] It further contended that young boys, and especially young gay boys, are among the most vulnerable members of society, and that a system protecting pedophiles at the expense of children requires revolution.[1]
