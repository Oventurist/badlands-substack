# Kelly Magsamen

**Kelly Magsamen** served as chief of staff to Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] from the beginning of his tenure in 2021.[1] Described as Austin's "right-hand woman," she kept a low public profile throughout her tenure and worked largely behind the scenes.[1]

## Austin hospitalization secrecy

Magsamen took the blame for the Defense Department's failure to inform the [[white-house|White House]] in January 2024 that Austin — characterized in press accounts as an intensely private former Army general — had been hospitalized.[1] The episode produced a public secrecy scandal and calls for Austin's firing, which President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] declined to act on.[1]

## Departure (June 2024)

The Pentagon announced in June 2024 that Magsamen would step down from her position that month.[1] In a statement, Austin called her "the chief architect of every initiative I have launched to defend our nation," and said she had "proved instrumental in navigating difficult international challenges — from Russia's unprovoked invasion of Ukraine to the devastating Israel-Hamas war after the October 7th [[hamas|Hamas]] atrocities," as well as embassy evacuations under extraordinary pressure and "standing up to bullying and coercion by the People's Republic of China in the South China Sea."[1]
