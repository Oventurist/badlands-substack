# Ariel Smith

Ariel Smith is the Attorney General of the [[us-virgin-islands|United States Virgin Islands]], the US territory that sued [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase & Co.]] over the bank's ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Epstein litigation

In July 2023 the USVI, under Smith, stated in a Manhattan federal court filing that it was seeking at least $190 million from JPMorgan in its lawsuit accusing the bank of enabling Epstein's sex trafficking, and that it also wanted the bank to commit to reforms protecting against human trafficking.[1]

"I am gratified that the victims have received some measure of compensation from the bank, but more needs to be done to hold JPMorgan Chase accountable and to ensure this does not happen to another generation of women and girls," Smith said in a statement.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] highlighted the significance of a sitting US territorial attorney general directly accusing JPMorgan of facilitating sex-trafficking operations, summarizing the trajectory of the disclosures as "Drip. Drip. Flood."[1]
