# Brannon Howse

Brannon Howse is a broadcaster and former employee of [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] who was called to testify in the [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]] defamation trial in the U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado.[1] His testimony was scheduled for the Monday opening the trial's second week, alongside Lindell's own appearance and the video-deposition testimony of Dennis Montgomery.[1] Howse's association with Lindell's FrankSpeech broadcasting venture places him within the network of media figures who amplified [[joe-oltmann|Joe Oltmann]]'s allegations about [[eric-coomer|Eric Coomer]].[1]
