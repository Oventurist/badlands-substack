# MI5

MI5 is the British domestic security and counterintelligence service, created as one of two branches of the official secret service in the 1909 restructuring of British intelligence carried out by King Edward VII and Viscount Richard Burdon Haldane, the other branch being the foreign service later called [[mi6|MI6]].[1]

[[claude-dansey|Claude Dansey]] became a founding deputy director of the new agency in 1909 alongside Haldane and William Melville, and later rose to be its deputy director while simultaneously constructing American intelligence organs from New York's [[sleepy-hollow-club|Sleepy Hollow Club]].[1] The historian [[anton-chaitkin|Anton Chaitkin]] records that beginning in 1910 the new Home Secretary, [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], arranged sweeping new powers of surveillance for the secret service, including a "registry" of subversives and the clandestine interception of private mail, pushed a drastic new Official Secrets Act through Parliament, and recommended a eugenical sterilization law to spare the war-destined Empire the expense of caring for the unfit.[1]
