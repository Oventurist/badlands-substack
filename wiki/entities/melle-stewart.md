# Melle Stewart

**Melle Stewart** is an Australian theatre star and former vaccination advocate who sued the pharmaceutical company [[astrazeneca|AstraZeneca]] after suffering a life-threatening stroke that she attributes to the company's COVID-19 vaccine.[1]

## Career

Stewart, 42 at the time of the lawsuit, is known for acting work in both the United Kingdom and Australia, including playing the lead in the musical *Mamma Mia!* and appearing on Channel 7's *Home and Away*.[1]

## Vaccine injury

Stewart received her first dose of the AstraZeneca COVID-19 vaccine on May 24, 2021.[1] Two weeks later she began having seizures, lost her ability to speak, and lost all movement on the right-hand side of her body.[1] Brain surgeons diagnosed her with Vaccine-Induced Thrombocytopenic Thrombosis (VITT), a blood-clotting condition related to [[thrombosis-with-thrombocytopenia-syndrome|thrombosis with thrombocytopenia syndrome]] that the manufacturer and regulators now acknowledge as a "very rare side effect" of that particular vaccine.[1]

She underwent multiple procedures and rigorous therapy, including a craniectomy — a three-hour operation to remove a portion of her skull in order to reduce pressure in her brain and keep her alive.[1] Surgeons later replaced that part of her skull with a titanium plate the size of her hand.[1]

## Lawsuit

Stewart's suit against AstraZeneca claims that the stroke left her unable to work.[1] The case was reported alongside a rash of newly developing class action lawsuits against vaccine manufacturers.[1]
