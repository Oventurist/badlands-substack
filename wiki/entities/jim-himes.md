# Jim Himes

Jim Himes is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Connecticut and the ranking Democrat on the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence.[1]

## June 2025 deportation vote

On June 26, 2025, Himes was among 37 House Democrats who joined Republicans in passing legislation introduced by [[barry-moore|Barry Moore]] to deport illegal immigrants convicted of driving under the influence.[1] The bill passed 246 to 160; Himes's support was notable given his senior position on the intelligence committee, and he was grouped in reporting alongside moderate Democrats such as Tom Suozzi, Jared Golden, Laura Gillen, Don Davis, and Marie Gluesenkamp Perez.[1]

## Call for Biden to step aside (July 2024)

Himes, the Connecticut congressman serving as the top-ranking Democrat on the House intelligence committee, called on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to step down from his campaign on July 11, 2024, shortly after Biden finished his solo press conference at the close of the [[2024-nato-washington-summit|NATO Washington Summit]].[2] The press conference had been billed by the administration as proof of the president's fitness but was dominated by questions about his mental acuity and marred by his reference to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] as "vice-president Trump."[2]
