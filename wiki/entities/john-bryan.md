# John Bryan

John Bryan is a British royal family advisor who became the subject of an undercover video released by [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]] in May 2025, in which he is recorded claiming that [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] "was f*cking underage girls" and had close ties to the financier and convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## O'Keefe Media Group video

[[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] explained that two weeks after Prince Andrew's interview with the BBC — in which the prince denied knowing Epstein victim [[virginia-roberts-giuffre|Virginia Giuffre]] — Bryan said he was brought to Andrew's private residence to provide "crisis" management.[1] Bryan described the prince at that time as "distressed" and "struggling to focus," and reportedly constructed a five-page public relations strategy advising Andrew to show empathy for Epstein's victims publicly.[1] In a 2022 interview, by contrast, Bryan said he believed Prince Andrew was innocent.[1]

At the end of the video O'Keefe referenced the Project Veritas logo on the wall behind him alongside his O'Keefe Media Group logo and said there would be more information to come the following week, prompting speculation on social media.[1] O'Keefe founded Project Veritas and led it until February 2023, and in August 2024 launched a countersuit against the group he founded for defamation and breach of contract.[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentator Ashe in America characterised the release as a "rug," arguing that a week of hype preceding a non-revelation is a recurring monetisation pattern among influencers, and contrasting the video with the substantive Epstein news she awaited — indictments of those who abused children.[1]
