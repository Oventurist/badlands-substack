# Douglas Macgregor

Colonel Douglas Macgregor is a retired United States Army officer, armored-warfare theorist, and commentator known for his opposition to open-ended American military interventions. He served as a senior military advisor during the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.

## Account of John Bolton's appointment

Macgregor is cited in the [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]] as the source for a claim about how [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] entered the Trump cabinet. According to [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s summary of Macgregor, President Trump wanted nothing to do with Bolton until the Israeli-American casino magnate [[sheldon-adelson|Sheldon Adelson]] asked that Bolton be brought aboard, after which Trump abruptly assented.

The Brief's author builds on this account to argue that Bolton functioned as an agent of foreign influence inside the administration, and that U.S. policy toward [[venezuela|Venezuela]] between April 2018 and September 2019 — including the sanctioning of [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] and other figures in the [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] circle — reflected neoconservative objectives that Trump was pursuing, in the author's phrasing, possibly unwittingly. Macgregor's testimony thus serves in the corpus as a load-bearing element in a wider interpretive timeline that continues through Bolton's dismissal, [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]]'s secret 2019 mission to Caracas, and the January 2020 strike on [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]].
