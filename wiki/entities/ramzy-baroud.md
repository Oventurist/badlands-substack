# Ramzy Baroud

Ramzy Baroud is a U.S.-educated Palestinian-American journalist and the founder of the [[palestine-chronicle|Palestine Chronicle]], a United States-based 501(c)(3) organization.[1] He previously served as managing editor of both the London-based [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]] and the Qatar-based [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]].[1]

## Badlands analysis

In a June 2024 Badlands News Brief item, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used Baroud and Palestine Chronicle contributor [[iqbal-jassat|Iqbal Jassat]] as a worked demonstration of open-source intelligence methodology for identifying suspected [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] media assets.[1] The stated checklist included affiliation with "Middle East" outlets headquartered in Qatar, London, or the United States; vocal support for the 2011 Arab Spring uprisings, characterized in the analysis as Brotherhood-coordinated color revolutions; and insistence that the public accept the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s findings regarding the death of journalist Jamal Khashoggi.[1] The author concluded that Baroud checked all of these boxes.[1]

The analysis noted Baroud's vocal support for Mohamed Morsi, described as a Muslim Brotherhood operative who led the 2011 overthrow of the Egyptian government, and recalled that a counter-coup led by General Abdel Fattah el-Sisi in 2013 resulted in Morsi's imprisonment and his death in a courtroom during trial in 2019.[1] Baroud was further characterized as demanding that Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] be deposed over the Khashoggi killing despite, in the author's view, an absence of presented evidence.[1] On this basis the commentary suggested that Baroud's platform was "carrying the water for western intelligence agencies" in a controlled-opposition role.[1] These characterizations represent Badlands Media opinion analysis rather than established fact.
