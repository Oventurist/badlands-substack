# Jordan Schachtel

Jordan Schachtel is a journalist who writes The Dossier on Substack.[1]

## Criticism of FedNow

Schachtel raised surveillance concerns about the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s [[fednow|FedNow]] instant payments system, writing that "FedNow appears to be a prototype CBDC" and that "while instant, 24/7 payments seem good, there's implications to leaning into a credit-based system. FedNow can quickly transform to a surveillance system."[1] He also asked whether FedNow has "AI or human circuit breaker managing it," concluding that "FedNow is a giant red flag."[1]

Badlands Media notes that these are the exact same fears associated with [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]], placing Schachtel among the skeptics in a debate that also included Lawrence Lepard, who called FedNow a CBDC launch, and Scott Santens, who argued it was nothing of the kind.[1]
