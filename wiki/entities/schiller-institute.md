# Schiller Institute

The Schiller Institute is the organization alongside which the American economist [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]] promoted the New Silk Road as early as 1996.[1]

## Role in Ehret's account

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites the Institute's New Silk Road advocacy as one of the very few western efforts to resist the decades-long destruction of bank separation and productive credit.[1] He connects that advocacy to the later China-led [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], which has grown to embrace over 130 countries and which he compares to an Asian-led version of the New Deal of the 1930s.[1] Ehret argues that discussion of reforming the dead trans-Atlantic system is increasingly shaped not by calls for a "[[new-world-order|New World Order]]" but by calls for a New Silk Road and a true New Deal.[1]
