# Sauce and Effect

Sauce and Effect is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Mondays at 6pm ET in the January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[woke-societies|Woke Societies]] and [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]].[1] The show analyzes the news of the week with an emphasis on what its hosts call bringing the receipts — the documentary sourcing that they argue the mainstream media tends to omit.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule placed Sauce and Effect on Mondays at 5pm ET, with hosts Woke Societies and Johnny Q analyzing the week's news with a heavy emphasis on bringing receipts and sourcing omitted by the mainstream media.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Sauce and Effect in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
