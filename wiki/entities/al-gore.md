# Al Gore

Al Gore is an American politician who served as Vice President of the United States from 1993 to 2001 and became a leading advocate of climate policy. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], Gore appears twice: as a participant in the Western management of post-Soviet liberalization, and as a personal ally of [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]].

## Perestroika

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that "from the western side, the Perestroika (aka: Operation 'Hammer')… involved the IMF's [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]], Vice President Al Gore, Rhodes Scholar [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbot]] and Oxford trained investment banker at Goldman Sachs… [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]." The article frames this group as the Western counterpart to the monetarist "reformers" [[anatoly-chubais|Anatoly Chubais]] and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] operating under [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]].

## Association with Freeland

The article lists Gore, alongside Larry Summers and [[george-soros|George Soros]], among the Western figures Freeland "considered her 'close friends'" and brought into Canada during the reorganization of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] between 2011 and 2014 under the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] banner.
