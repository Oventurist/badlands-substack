# Elizabeth MacDonough

Elizabeth MacDonough is the Parliamentarian of the United States Senate, the nonpartisan official who advises the chamber on the application of its rules and precedents.[1]

## Role in the 2025 reconciliation fight

In June 2025, MacDonough rejected several controversial provisions of the Republican "big beautiful bill," ruling that parts of the tax and spending legislation violated Senate rules and had to be removed.[1] The review process, known informally as the "Byrd bath," determines whether provisions are eligible to travel in a reconciliation package that can pass with a simple 51-vote majority rather than overcoming a filibuster.[1] Senate Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] had aimed to place the bill on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s desk by July 4, making the parliamentarian's rulings a significant obstacle to the legislative timetable.[1] The measure is generally known as the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill Act]].[1]
