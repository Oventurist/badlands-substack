# National Treasury Employees Union

The National Treasury Employees Union (NTEU) is a labor union representing employees of the US Treasury Department and other federal agencies.[1]

## Challenge to Trump workforce order

Late on Monday, January 20, 2025 — the first day of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term — the NTEU launched a legal challenge to Trump's executive order making it easier to fire career employees in the federal government, according to reporting by Bloomberg cited by Al Jazeera.[1] The union argued that the order would operate in violation of civil service law.[1] The suit was part of a broader legal backlash to Trump's day-one executive actions, alongside challenges to his birthright citizenship order brought by the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] and the [[legal-defense-fund|Legal Defense Fund]].[1] The NTEU litigation was of particular interest to Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who framed it as a test of the administration's ability to execute the workforce reductions implicit in the [[department-of-government-efficiency|DOGE]] program.[1]
