# Jake Tapper

Jake Tapper is an American television anchor at [[cnn|CNN]].[1]

## 2023 Trump town hall reaction

Following [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 70-minute town hall on CNN on May 10, 2023, [[breitbart|Breitbart]] columnist [[john-nolte|John Nolte]] wrote that "tears of frustration" could be heard in Tapper's voice immediately after the broadcast, and characterized him as an "election meddler."[1] Tapper's on-air response included the assertion that Trump's "lies keep coming fast and furious at the town hall."[1] The segment formed part of Nolte's broader argument that the town hall had been a catastrophe for CNN rather than a win.[1]

## Ratings collapse amid the "Original Sin" book tour (May 2025)

In May 2025, Tapper recorded his lowest-rated month at [[cnn|CNN]] in nearly a decade, despite a whirlwind publicity tour that generated an onslaught of attention.[2] The book he wrote with Axios reporter Alex Thompson — "Original Sin: President Biden's Decline, Its Cover-Up, and His Disastrous Choice to Run Again" — was released that month to considerable fanfare and helped set the news cycle with fresh accusations that the Biden White House had hidden the truth about the former president's fitness for office.[2] Tapper promoted the project across podcasts, talk shows, and cable news, and the book was pushed relentlessly on CNN, but the spotlight failed to translate into viewership.[2]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Tapper's ratings were failing because he was still lying to the American people, continuing to sell the idea that the Biden White House had fooled everyone and that the press bore no responsibility for its own reporting.[2] She summarized his implicit defense as "Don't blame us! We're too stupid to have gotten the story right," and contended that the press corps was not stupid but rather attempting to avoid criminal exposure for complicity.[2] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] called the "Jake Tapper retribution tour" pathetic, observing that alternative media was refusing to let him off the hook while regime media was doing so, and that it had never been easier to tell the two apart.[2]

## 2024 presidential debate moderation and the Kasie Hunt incident

In June 2024 Tapper was scheduled to co-moderate the [[cnn|CNN]]-hosted presidential debate between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], a role that gave him control of a mute button for the candidates' microphones.[3] Days before the debate, Trump 2024 national press secretary [[karoline-leavitt|Karoline Leavitt]] began discussing CNN's historical bias against her candidate — specifically Tapper's record of anti-Trump claims — during a live CNN interview, whereupon anchor [[kasie-hunt|Kasie Hunt]] "ma'am'd" her, cut her microphone, and ended the segment.[3]

Reporting aggregated by Badlands noted that JustTheNews had separately reported Tapper was at the center of a defamation suit brought by a consulting company alleging that CNN and one of its reporters lied about the company's work, with the network defending its language as "opinion or ambiguous."[3] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] commented that Tapper's anti-Trump bias was squarely relevant to the story because he was moderating the debate, and that Hunt's inability to have that conversation — muting and dismissing her guest instead — showed she was bad at her job, while noting the limited reach of the exposure given CNN's small audience.[3]

## "Trump Was Right" admission (August 2023)

In August 2023, Tapper acknowledged on air that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had lied about his son [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] "making a fortune in Ukraine, China, Moscow," and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been right on the point — a clip circulated widely under the headline "Trump Was Right."[4] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] highlighted the segment as an instance of a mainstream anchor conceding ground previously dismissed as conspiracy theory.[4]
