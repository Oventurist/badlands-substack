# Alnur Mussayev

**Alnur Mussayev** is a former Kazakh intelligence chief who served in the KGB's 6th Directorate in Moscow.[1] In February 2025 he became the source of a widely circulated but uncorroborated claim that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been recruited as a Soviet asset.[1]

## The "Krasnov" allegation

Mussayev alleged that Trump was recruited as a Russian spy in 1987 and assigned the codename "Krasnov."[1] He asserted that the recruitment of Western businessmen was a top priority for Soviet intelligence at the time and that Trump was high on the KGB's list of targets.[1]

According to Mussayev, the recruitment took place during Trump's 1987 visit to the Soviet Union — a trip ostensibly concerned with real estate, during which the New York developer was exploring a hotel project in Moscow facilitated by Soviet officials.[1] Mussayev contended that these meetings may have amounted to a covert intelligence operation rather than a purely commercial exercise.[1]

## Reception

The claim was reported in outlets including The Economic Times, which framed it as reading "like the plot of a Cold War thriller."[1] Badlands commentator Brad Cologero Getz dismissed the allegation outright, responding with mock laughter and calling the continued promotion of such claims "pathetic at this point."[1] The Badlands brief in which it appeared was subtitled in part "Uncorroborated KGB Claims," reflecting the editorial judgement that the story lacked substantiation.[1]
