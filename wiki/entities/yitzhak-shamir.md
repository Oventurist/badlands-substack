# Yitzhak Shamir

Yitzhak Shamir was a Prime Minister of Israel and, earlier in his life, a member of the [[irgun|Irgun]] militia in pre-state Palestine.[1]

## Role in Netanyahu's early career

[[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] served as Deputy Foreign Minister under Shamir. It was during that period, in 1990, that Netanyahu had a notable meeting with Rabbi [[menachem-mendel-schneerson|Menachem Mendel Schneerson]], an encounter Netanyahu later publicised.[1] Badlands commentary treats Shamir's Irgun background as part of a continuous ideological lineage connecting pre-1948 Zionist paramilitary organisations to the modern Israeli political establishment.[1]
