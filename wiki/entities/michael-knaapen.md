# Michael Knaapen

**Michael Knaapen** was the head of the [[maryland-democratic-party|Maryland Democratic Party]] LGBTQ+ Diversity Leadership Council (DLC).[1]

## Predator sting allegation

In June 2024 Knaapen was recorded on video in what was described as a disturbing predator sting conducted by [[predator-poachers|Predator Poachers]], the Houston-based vigilante organization founded by [[alex-rosen|Alex Rosen]].[1] Rosen announced, "We just busted the head of the LGBTQ Dems of Maryland going after a 14-year-old. Groomers are about to be mad," and posted video on X featuring Knaapen discussing his role as head of the MDP LGBTQ+ DLC.[1] Coverage noted that Knaapen was not a fan of conservative talk show hosts, nor of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
