# Debra Houry

Debra Houry is a physician and public health official who served as chief medical officer of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]].[1]

Houry resigned in late August 2025 as part of a wave of departures from CDC leadership that followed the [[white-house|White House]]'s firing of CDC Director [[susan-monarez|Susan Monarez]].[1] Her resignation came alongside those of other senior officials, including [[daniel-jernigan|Daniel Jernigan]], director of the National Center for Emerging and Zoonotic Infectious Diseases, and [[demetre-daskalakis|Demetre Daskalakis]], director of the National Center for Immunization and Respiratory Diseases.[1]

The shakeup coincided with tightened restrictions on eligibility for the COVID-19 vaccine and drew criticism from Republican senators including [[bill-cassidy|Bill Cassidy]] and [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]], the latter describing herself as "alarmed" by Monarez's firing and noting that the departure had "triggered the immediate resignation of four long-time experts at the agency, who will not be easily replaced, and who are respected worldwide."[1]
