# Catherine the Great

Catherine the Great was Empress of Russia, whose 34-year reign is described in Badlands commentary as a golden age for the country and who is counted among Europe's "enlightened despots."[1]

## Reign and legacy

Catherine came to power by overthrowing her husband, Peter III, who had been born and raised in Germany — a background that made him an outsider in the eyes of Russians, who did not trust Prussia. Like [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], Catherine greatly admired Peter the Great, who was the grandfather of Peter III.[1]

Badlands commentary credits Catherine with being a great friend of the American Founding Fathers and with establishing all of the major cities on the Ukrainian coastline, including Odessa and Mariupol, a territory then called "New Russia." Under her rule Russia also became the first European power to colonize Alaska; a century later Russia sold Alaska to the United States to avoid it falling into British hands.[1]

Both Peter the Great and Catherine the Great are characterized as modernizers who imbued Russia with Western culture — a contrast drawn by commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] with Vladimir Putin, who has sought to modernize Russia by rejecting modernity and restoring traditional values.[1]

## Osterman monument

In December 2025 a monument was unveiled in Moscow to [[ivan-osterman|Ivan Osterman]], the 18th-century statesman who served as the empire's minister of foreign affairs during Catherine's reign.[1]
