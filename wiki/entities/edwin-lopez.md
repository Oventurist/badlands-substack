# Edwin Lopez

Edwin Lopez is a former United States Homeland Security agent identified in reporting by the Associated Press, summarized in the Badlands News Brief of October 29, 2025, as the federal agent who secretly attempted to recruit Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s personal pilot in a plot to capture the leader and deliver him into American custody on drug trafficking charges.[1]

## Recruitment attempt

Citing three current and former US officials and one of Maduro's opponents, the account states that Lopez met Maduro's pilot, General Bitner Villegas, in the Dominican Republic in 2024.[1] Lopez allegedly offered the pilot money and protection in exchange for diverting Maduro's plane to a location where US authorities could arrest him.[1] Villegas remained noncommittal but continued exchanging messages with Lopez for more than a year, even after Lopez retired in July 2025.[1] Lopez reportedly cited a US Justice Department announcement doubling the bounty for Maduro's capture to $50 million and urged Villegas to "be Venezuela's hero"; the pilot ultimately refused, called Lopez a "coward," and cut off contact.[1]

## Context

The revelations emerged as Washington escalated military and intelligence pressure on Caracas, with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] authorizing the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] to conduct covert operations inside Venezuela and deploying warships, aircraft and thousands of troops to the Caribbean for what the administration described as an anti-drug campaign.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry observed that the pilot appeared to have been in contact with US officials since at least May 2024, indicating a substantial investment of time and money in a single recruitment, and speculated that a "faux arrest" of Maduro might be used as cover for extracting him from the country.[1]
