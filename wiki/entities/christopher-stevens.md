# Christopher Stevens

Christopher "Chris" Stevens was the United States Ambassador to Libya who was killed during the September 11, 2012 assault on American facilities in Benghazi.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Role in the Libya weapons network

According to the article, Stevens served as the **liaison** between weapons dealers and Al Qaeda elements in post-Gaddafi Libya, and helped coordinate the transfer of weapons from Libya to Syria to arm [[isis|ISIS]] fighters seeking to overthrow Bashar al-Assad.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) This is described as a secret operation run by the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[cia|CIA]], with the full approval of President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Stevens is said to have worked directly with the new Libyan government associated with [[abdelhakim-belhadj|Abdelhakim Belhadj]] and Libyan leader [[mustafa-abdul-jalil|Mustafa Abdul Jalil]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Benghazi and his death

Stevens was based at the embassy in Tripoli, which had greater security, yet was present at the lightly protected Benghazi compound on the night of the attack — a fact the article treats as central.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) His last known public meeting was with Turkish Consul General Ali Sait Akin, whom he escorted out the consulate front gate roughly an hour before the assault began at about 9:35 p.m. local time; the Libyan-flagged vessel Al Entisar, reportedly carrying weapons for Syrian rebels, had docked at the Turkish port of Iskenderun on September 6, five days earlier.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article argues Stevens was **sacrificed**: an insider who "thought he was untouchable" but became expendable once congressional and media scrutiny of the weapons pipeline intensified during the spring and summer of 2012.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Stevens died alongside information management officer Sean Smith and former Navy SEALs [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]] and [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]] during an extended assault by more than 100 Islamist militants.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) It was the first time in 33 years that an American ambassador died in the line of duty.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
