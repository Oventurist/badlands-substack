# Christopher Stevens

**J. Christopher Stevens** was the United States Ambassador to Libya who was killed during the [[benghazi-attack|attack on the U.S. compound in Benghazi]] on September 11, 2012, the first American ambassador to die in the line of duty in thirty-three years.[1]

## Role in the Libya weapons pipeline

Badlands Media's account describes Stevens as the **liaison** between weapons dealers and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] in Libya, and as a coordinator of weapons transfers from Libya to Syria intended to arm [[isis|ISIS]] in its campaign against [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1] He is said to have worked directly with the post-Gaddafi Libyan leadership around [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhadj]] and [[mustafa-abdul-jalil|Mustafa Abdul Jalil]], the same leadership that had struck an agreement to send Libyan weapons to Syria.[1] In this telling Stevens operated inside a covert program run by the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] under [[david-petraeus|David Petraeus]], with President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s approval.[1]

## Death

Stevens was in Benghazi rather than at the better-protected embassy in Tripoli on the night of the attack.[1] Fox News reporting cited in the article notes that the Libyan-flagged vessel *Al Entisar*, reportedly carrying weapons bound for Syrian rebels, had arrived at the Turkish port of Iskenderun on September 6, five days before Stevens, Sean Smith, [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]], and [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]] were killed by more than a hundred Islamist militants.[1] Stevens's last known public meeting was with Turkish Consul General Ali Sait Akin, whom he escorted out of the consulate gate roughly an hour before the assault began at about 9:35 p.m. local time.[1]

The article argues Stevens was **sacrificed**: an insider who "thought he was part of the protected class" but became expendable once shipments of Libyan missiles, exposure of [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] smuggling through [[turkey|Turkey]], and American [[stinger-missile|Stinger missiles]] appearing in enemy hands were drawing [[congress|congressional]] attention.[1] It contends Clinton delayed notifying the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and conditioned any rescue on Libyan government approval precisely so that no help would reach him.[1]
