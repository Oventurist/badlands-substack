# ICTS International

ICTS International is an aviation- and transportation-security company identified in Badlands Media commentary as the parent company of the Israeli identity-verification firm [[au10tix|Au10tix]].[1] According to that commentary, ICTS was founded by former members of [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]], Israel's internal security service.[1]

## Screening technology

In the late 1990s ICTS developed the Advanced Passenger Screening (APS) software, an algorithm used to cross-check passenger data in real time in order to identify security threats.[1] The company also developed a suite of other airport-related security products, including TravelDoc, IP@SS (Integrated Passenger Processing Solutions), and APIS Solution — described as the scanning software used by US Customs to extract passenger data in real time.[1]

## Subsidiaries

Two subsidiaries drew particular attention in the Badlands account: I-SEC International Security Group and Huntleigh USA.[1] I-SEC, founded in 2005, was described as providing nearly all of the technology and equipment used by the Transportation Security Administration at American airports, having capitalized on the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]].[1] Huntleigh USA is an airport-security service provider.[1]

## Security failures cited

The commentary catalogued a series of screening failures attributed to the ICTS group. Huntleigh was described as responsible for allowing hijackers at Boston Logan International Airport to board two of the four aircraft used in the attacks of September 11, 2001.[1] ICTS itself was said to have been responsible for allowing Richard Reid, the "Shoe Bomber," to board a flight at Charles de Gaulle Airport in Paris in December 2001, and for allowing the "Underwear Bomber" to board a flight in the Netherlands in 2009 — a bomber later reported to have been working for the CIA.[1]

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry presented this record as an illustration of the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian dialectic]]: ICTS was, in his framing, directly responsible for creating the conditions that "made necessary" the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]], and then became the principal beneficiary of that legislation.[1]
