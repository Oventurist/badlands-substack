# Brandon Gill

Brandon Gill is a member of the United States [[congress|Congress]] who appears in the Badlands corpus as a questioner at the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali fraud]] hearings.

## Role in the fraud hearings

In the January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] describes watching Gill "rush through his questions" during the hearings. She uses his performance to make a structural criticism rather than a personal one, writing that "five minutes isn't enough. Five minutes can only be for show. Campaign clips. Five minutes is an unserious amount of time for the People's representatives' pursuit of truth." The observation is offered in support of her broader argument that congressional oversight of alleged multibillion-dollar fraud in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] has been structurally inadequate.
