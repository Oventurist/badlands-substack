# Ray Kurzweil

Ray Kurzweil is identified in Badlands Media reporting as the intellectual heir of the Jesuit transhumanist [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]].[1] He appears in a lineage that also includes Marshall McLuhan, [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]] and cybernetics founder [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]], whose theories are said to have been adopted by "these new priests of the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]]" as they preached a new gospel to humanity.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats this school as advancing a neo-Darwinian synthetic religion resting on the assumption that random stochastic processes at small scale drive an inexorable growth of "complexity", and that humans are destined to suffer an irreversible loss of mental powers as artificial intelligence replaces obsolete organic life forms the way mammals replaced dinosaurs.[1] This is the same framework that underwrites [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]]'s claims about hackable humans and the coming useless class.[1]
