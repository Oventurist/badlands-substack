# David Perdue

David Perdue is a former United States Senator from Georgia whose September 6, 2022 special grand jury testimony, unsealed in early 2026, became a central document in renewed scrutiny of Georgia's handling of 2020 presidential election fraud allegations.[1]

## Grand jury testimony

Perdue sat for a transcribed interview before the Fulton County special grand jury on September 6, 2022, and was examined by prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], among others.[1] Wade asked Perdue whether he agreed that investigations by the Secretary of State's Office, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation, and "numerous investigations" had shown that nothing illegal occurred at State Farm Arena on election night in 2020. Perdue answered only, "I agree that an investigation happened, yes."[1]

More significantly, Perdue claimed under oath that Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]] told GBI Chief Vic Reynolds to relay to Perdue that the state would not investigate the election, invoking the notion of being a "team player."[1] Badlands commentary characterized the exchange as evidence that officials who publicly assured Americans the GBI had investigated the election were lying.[1]

The transcript became available after Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] lifted the protective order over the special grand jury record in January 2026, following the November 25, 2025 dismissal of State of Georgia v. Trump, et al.[1] Badlands writer [[canncon|CannCon]] published the exclusive reporting on the transcript.[1]

## Related events

Perdue was among three Georgia figures — alongside Kemp and Senator Kelly Loeffler — who called for a signature audit shortly before the death of aide [[harrison-deal|Harrison Deal]], a sequence highlighted by [[sidney-powell|Sidney Powell]] in February 2026.[1]
