# Bill Ackman

Bill Ackman is an American hedge fund investor and shareholder in [[hilton-worldwide|Hilton]].[1]

Following Hilton's January 2026 termination of the Lakeville, Minnesota Hampton Inn franchise over allegations that the hotel denied rooms to federal immigration agents, Ackman publicly praised Hilton chief executive Chris Nassetta for his swift response in addressing what Ackman characterized as a "rogue franchisee."[1]

## Deleted post on the Loureiro killing, December 2025

Following the fatal shooting of MIT fusion scientist [[nuno-loureiro|Nuno Loureiro]] in Brookline, Massachusetts, Ackman posted and then deleted a message asking, "Did the Iranians kill one of our nuclear scientists?"[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the deletion in arguing that the Iranian-involvement theory was unsupported internet speculation propagated by "a certain lobby," with no evidence or confirmation from investigators.[2]

## Advocacy for a temporary deposit guarantee (March 2023)

During the March 2023 banking turmoil Ackman repeatedly urged a blanket federal backstop, writing that "I continue to believe that the best course of action is a temporary @FDICgov deposit guarantee until an updated insurance regime is introduced."[3] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noted that in the same period he reversed his 2022 position that the [[federal-reserve|Fed]] "must hike with shock and awe," instead urging a pause in rate increases.[3] His lobbying preceded reports that officials were studying an expansion of [[federal-deposit-insurance-corporation|FDIC]] coverage to all deposits, discussed at [[2023-proposal-to-guarantee-all-us-bank-deposits|the 2023 proposal to guarantee all US bank deposits]].[3]
