# Andrew Jackson

**Andrew Jackson** was the seventh President of the United States and, in the Badlands Media narrative, the archetypal presidential opponent of central banking.[1]

## Opposition to the Second Bank

When the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] was established, Jackson concluded that the bankers were overreaching and effectively acting as a fourth branch of government run by the elite. He believed that if the bankers succeeded in their ultimate plan they would enslave every citizen in the country, and he therefore sought to root them out. Jackson also held that bankers would extend loans preferentially to prominent people rather than common citizens, and would use that power to enable further corruption.[1]

## Hard money and the national debt

Jackson favored "hard money" over the bankers' paper money and was the only US President in history to completely pay off the national debt.[1] The article observes the irony that the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]] later placed Jackson's likeness on the twenty dollar bill.[1]

## Legacy in the Trump era

Jackson was described as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s favorite US President because he took on the cabal bankers and won, a posture the article says Trump has tried to emulate since taking office in 2016.[1]
