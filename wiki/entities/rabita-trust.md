# Rabita Trust

The Rabita Trust is a Pakistan-based charitable organization formally designated as a terrorist organization under U.S. law because of its funding of [[al-qaeda|al-Qaeda]].

## In the Badlands corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]], quoting PJMedia, reports that the Rabita Trust was established by [[wael-jalaidan|Wael Jalaidan]] — a former leader of the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at the University of Arizona who went on to help [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found al-Qaeda — in partnership with "the Abedins' patron," [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]].

The article underscores that the Rabita Trust was a Pakistani organization, linking it rhetorically to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s Pakistani family background: "That's not a coincidence." Within the essay's argument the trust illustrates the dual character it attributes to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] network — publicly disavowing violence while using global charities to fund groups such as Hamas, Hezbollah, and al-Qaeda.
