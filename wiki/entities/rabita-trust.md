# Rabita Trust

The Rabita Trust is a Pakistani charitable organization formally designated as a terrorist organization under United States law due to its funding of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) It was established by Wael Jalaidan — who had run the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] at the University of Arizona in the 1980s and went on to help [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found Al Qaeda — in partnership with [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], the patron of the Abedin family.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The Badlands account attaches significance to the Trust's Pakistani character, noting that [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s parents were Pakistani and Indian Muslim academics and treating the overlap as something other than coincidence.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Trust is presented as an illustration of how the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s global charity network funded groups such as Hamas, Hezbollah and Al Qaeda even while the Brotherhood publicly denounced violence to avoid terrorist designation in the United States.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
