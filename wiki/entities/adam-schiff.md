# Adam Schiff

Adam Schiff is a Democratic United States Senator from California and formerly a member of the House of Representatives, where he chaired the House Intelligence Committee and led the first impeachment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. He is a recurring antagonist in Badlands commentary, where he is mocked with the nickname "Pencilneck."

Schiff appears in the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] as one of the questioners at the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026, at which Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] testified; the commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] notes having listened to "Pencilneck Schiff" while skipping the questioning of Cory Booker and Mazie Hirono.
