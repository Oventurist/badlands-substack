# Larry Johnson

Larry Johnson is a retired CIA intelligence officer and former State Department official who has become a frequent commentator on U.S. foreign policy.[1]

## Criticism of the second Trump foreign policy team

In November 2024, Johnson told Sputnik that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s foreign policy appointments for his incoming administration showed that "he didn't learn a thing during his first term," observing that Trump had campaigned on ending needless wars yet was "staffing himself with really a lot of young people who are locked up with old ideas."[1]

Johnson singled out the selection of [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] as ambassador to the United Nations for her "very strident pro-Zionist point of view," and predicted the team would "double down on stupid" on [[ukraine|Ukraine]], the Middle East and China.[1]

He argued that the incoming team still believed it could split [[russia|Russia]] from China despite what he described as a natural alliance between them, and that it underestimated regional realignment in the Middle East — noting that Saudi Arabia and [[iran|Iran]] had reestablished diplomatic relations, were exchanging defense-minister visits and planning joint military exercises, and that the Organization of Islamic Countries had ruled out normalization with [[israel|Israel]] absent a Palestinian state.[1]

## Commentary on the Putin residence drone attack (December 2025)

In an interview with RT published Tuesday, December 30, 2025, Johnson suggested that the Ukrainian drone attack on [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s residence in Novgorod Region earlier that week may have been staged by elements within the government in Kiev seeking to undermine [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]].[2] He called the timing suspicious because the strike coincided with Zelensky's US visit to negotiate with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], adding, "I don't think he is that stupid to launch that kind of attack while meeting with Trump."[2] Johnson said he would not be surprised if Ukrainian intelligence personnel, possibly acting on orders from [[kyrylo-budanov|Kirill Budanov]], head of the military espionage agency HUR, were involved.[2]
