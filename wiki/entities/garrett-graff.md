# Garrett Graff

Garrett Graff is a journalist and the Director of Cyber Initiatives at [[aspen-digital|Aspen Digital]], the technology arm of the [[aspen-institute|Aspen Institute]].[1]

One week before [[miranda-devine|Miranda Devine]]'s heavily censored [[new-york-post|New York Post]] story on the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]], Graff published an article in [[wired|Wired]] arguing for how the media should cover hacks and leaks in the closing weeks of the 2020 campaign.[1] Badlands Media characterized the piece as "a playbook for how the media should respond to an 'October Surprise'" and read its byline — "The media knows it screwed up in 2016 with John Podesta. Here's how it should do better in the final weeks of the 2020 race." — as closer to marching orders than analysis, given the participants in Aspen's contemporaneous [[aspen-institute-hack-and-dump-exercise|hack-and-dump exercise]].[1]
