# Jack Dorsey

Jack Dorsey is the co-founder of Twitter and was the company's chief executive at the time President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was deplatformed following the January 6, 2021 riot at the U.S. Capitol.[1]

## Trump deplatforming lawsuit

Trump, along with other plaintiffs, filed suit in 2021 against the company — then called Twitter — and Dorsey, its chief executive at the time, over his removal from the platform.[1] [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] reinstated Trump on the platform in November 2022 after acquiring the company.[1]

In February 2025, Musk's [[x-corp|X]] agreed to pay about $10 million to settle the lawsuit, making X the second social-media platform to settle litigation Trump filed over his deplatforming.[1] Trump's team had considered letting the suit fizzle out, citing Musk's proximity to the president and the $250 million Musk spent to help elect him, but ultimately moved forward with the settlement despite the two men's close relationship.[1] A spokesman for X did not respond to requests for comment.[1]
