# Kevork Almassian

Kevork Almassian is a Syrian-born Armenian Christian commentator and podcaster who, in Badlands Media's coverage, functions as a primary sourcing node for Syria-related reporting. He was born in Syria and was forced to flee with his family in 2012 after ISIS captured his brother; he now lives in Germany.[1]

## Syriana Analysis

In 2017 Almassian founded the podcast *Syriana Analysis*, distributed on Rumble, through which he covers Syrian politics, the Syrian Civil War, and broader Middle East geopolitics.[1] He has hosted Badlands writers several times to discuss President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and geopolitics from an American/MAGA populist perspective.[1] Almassian also made his first appearance on [[andrew-napolitano|Judge Andrew Napolitano]]'s program *Judging Freedom*, a show Badlands has frequently clipped for its News Brief.[1]

## Verification of the Assad statement

Following the fall of Damascus, Almassian used his X account to assert that he had verified, through contacts inside Syria, the authenticity of a statement attributed to [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] that was published on the official "Presidency of the Syrian Arab Republic" Telegram channel — a statement whose legitimacy some observers had questioned.[1]

## Origins of the Syrian Civil War

On *Syriana Analysis*, Almassian aired an interview broadcast in the Middle East with a former defense minister of Qatar who went public with details of the plot to overthrow Assad, reportedly out of concern that the whole affair would be pinned on him and the Qatari government.[1] According to that official, the plot was a group effort involving Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Jordan, Turkey and the West (the United States, NATO, and presumably Israel), with a budget of roughly $2 trillion largely funded by Gulf oil states and an initial schedule of about three months.[1]
