# Jonathan Rinderknecht

Jonathan Rinderknecht is a 29-year-old man arrested in October 2025 on suspicion of starting the [[palisades-fire|Pacific Palisades fire]] in Los Angeles, a January 2025 blaze that killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,000 homes.[1]

## Arrest and evidence

Justice Department officials said evidence collected from Rinderknecht's digital devices included an image he had generated using [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] depicting a burning city.[1] The arrest came roughly nine months after the fire, which erupted on January 7, 2025 near a hiking trail overlooking the wealthy coastal neighborhood.[1]

The Palisades fire scorched more than 23,000 acres (9,308 hectares) and caused about $150 billion in damage, raging for more than three weeks and also ravaging parts of Topanga and Malibu.[1] It was one of the most destructive blazes in Los Angeles history.[1] The separate Eaton Fire, ignited the same day in the Los Angeles area, killed another 19 people and razed 9,400 structures; its cause remained unclear.[1] Among the structures destroyed were homes belonging to celebrities including Mel Gibson, Paris Hilton and Jeff Bridges.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated Rinderknecht's arrest with open derision, characterizing him as a designated "patsy" rather than a genuine suspect.[1] The Brief satirized investigators as saying, "It took us nine months to find this patsy. We really broke the case when we saw the picture ChatGPT hallucinated for him. We knew right then: this is our guy!"[1] Contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote, "You know he's guilty and evil because of the picture ChatGPT made for him... We know they got the right patsy because the Machine made him a scary drawing."[1] The commentary reflects a broader Badlands skepticism toward AI-derived evidence and toward official accounts of the [[january-2025-los-angeles-wildfires|January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires]].[1]
