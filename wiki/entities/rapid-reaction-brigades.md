# Rapid Reaction Brigades

The Rapid Reaction Brigades are formations released by the Communist Party of [[cuba|Cuba]] to police and squash dissent during the [[2021-cuban-protests|2021 Cuban protests]], according to the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1."[1]

The essay describes the brigades as carrying out "actual government-sanctioned police brutality" against pro-freedom demonstrators, and criticizes [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] for not objecting because Cuba is held up as a successful socialist experiment.[1] Their deployment accompanied [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]]'s public call that "the order to combat has been given. Revolutionaries need to be on the streets."[1] Protesters who escaped a beating could not, the essay argues, expect fair treatment, competent media exposure, or a trial by jury after arrest.[1]
