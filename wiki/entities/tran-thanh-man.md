# Tran Thanh Man

Tran Thanh Man is a Vietnamese politician who serves as Chairman, or speaker, of the National Assembly of [[vietnam|Vietnam]].[1] He took office on May 20, 2024, shortly before Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s June 2024 state visit to the country.[1]

## Meeting with Vladimir Putin, June 2024

During Putin's visit to Hanoi in June 2024, the Russian president met with Tran Thanh Man and stated that cooperation between the parliaments of Russia and Vietnam helps strengthen the two countries' relations in security, economy and humanitarian development.[1] "The parliaments play a significant role in the development and strengthening of our relations," Putin said, adding that "on a practical level, the adoption of relevant decisions at the legislative level creates a solid and reliable basis for our interaction."[1]

Putin described the Vietnamese direction in Russian foreign policy as "one of the unconditional priorities," and reported having held productive talks with Vietnam's president, prime minister, and the secretary general of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee during the same trip.[1] He congratulated Tran Thanh Man on his recent election as speaker and expressed hope that the parliamentary dimension of Russian–Vietnamese interaction would intensify under his leadership.[1]

## Badlands framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary placed the Hanoi meetings within a broader interpretation of Putin's June 2024 itinerary as a deliberate mirror of the 2017 foreign tour undertaken by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — a sequence of visits to nations long stigmatized in Western media, marked by public spectacle and historic bilateral agreements.[1] In this reading, Vietnam and [[north-korea|North Korea]] were selected precisely because of their status as former proxy theaters of the [[cia|CIA]] and [[state-department|State Department]] in the post-World War II period.[1]
