# Ralph Pezzullo

Ralph Pezzullo is the author of *Stolen Elections: The Takedown of Democracies Worldwide*, a book read and critiqued as Book 5 of the Badlands Book Club.[1]

## Stolen Elections

The book centers on self-proclaimed "whistleblowers" whose account, in the view of Badlands hosts, rewrites the history of the [[cia|CIA]]'s involvement in "everything."[1] [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]] — brother of Venezuelan President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] — is described as a central figure in the narrative.[1]

## Badlands criticism

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing with [[canncon|CannCon]] and ColonelTowner, said the book appeared to have been "rushed to market without a fact check, an edit, or even a proofread," with typos on nearly every page and factual errors in most chapters read so far.[1] She argued that the participants in the "Venezuela Election Fraud" rewrite seemed to be trying to get ahead of something — "like maybe they're trying to stay out of prison" — a suspicion predating both the book club reading and the operation that removed Maduro.[1] Her open question was whether the rewrite was intended to undermine the restoration of [[venezuela|Venezuela]] then under way.[1]
