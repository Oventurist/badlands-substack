# 3rd Assault Brigade

The 3rd Assault Brigade is a unit of the Ukrainian armed forces closely linked to the neo-Nazi Azov movement, from which it draws personnel and ideological lineage.[1] It is associated with the wider Azov network that grew out of the [[azov-battalion|Azov Battalion]].[1]

## 2024 European public-relations tour

In July 2024 the brigade announced that its members would travel to nine cities across Poland, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, the Czech Republic and Lithuania between 21 July and 2 August.[1] The stated goal of the trip was to tell attendees "only the truth about service in the brigade," relate front-line stories, and drum up European support for Ukraine in its conflict with Russia.[1]

The brigade was forced to curb the tour after public backlash.[1] It held a gathering in Warsaw, but audiences in several other cities proved less hospitable.[1] In Rotterdam, the welfare organisation Wmo Radar said it had decided "to no longer host the controversial event" because of "negative media attention," with local politicians calling the event "undesirable" and not politically neutral.[1] In Berlin, left-wing groups announced plans to protest outside the hotel hosting the brigade; the politician Ferat Kocak said the German capital "does not need additional meeting points for the extreme right… no militaristic propaganda, not even under the guise of solidarity with the Ukrainian people."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the cancelled tour within a broader argument that nationalist militias, Kyiv mayor [[vitali-klitschko|Vitali Klitschko]] and globalist warmongers were converging in opposition not only to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] but to [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] himself, as Zelensky drifted toward negotiations.[1]
