# Ben Barnes

Ben Barnes is a Texas political figure who, more than four decades after the fact, publicly described a 1980 mission through the Middle East whose real purpose, he said he only later realized, was to sabotage the re-election campaign of the president of the United States.[1]

## The 1980 account

Barnes said his longtime political mentor, [[john-connally|John Connally]], invited him on a trip to the Middle East in the summer of 1980, at a time when [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] was in the White House and bedeviled by a hostage crisis in Iran that had paralyzed his presidency; Carter's best chance for victory was to free the 52 Americans held captive before Election Day.[1] Barnes largely kept the story secret for nearly 43 years.[1] He said Connally took him from one Middle Eastern capital to the next, meeting regional leaders to deliver a message to be passed to Iran: don't release the hostages before the election, Mr. Reagan will win and give you a better deal.[1] On returning, Connally briefed [[william-casey|William J. Casey]], [[ronald-reagan|Reagan's]] campaign chairman and later CIA director, in an airport lounge.[1]

## Corroboration

Records at the Lyndon Baines Johnson Library and Museum confirm part of Barnes's story: an itinerary in Connally's files shows a July 18–August 11, 1980 trip from Houston through Jordan, Syria, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Israel, with Barnes listed as accompanying him.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats Barnes's testimony, alongside [[gary-sick|Gary Sick's]] research, as corroborating evidence for the [[october-surprise|October Surprise]] deal attributed to Casey and [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]].[1]
