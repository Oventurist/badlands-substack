# Tony Bobulinski

Tony Bobulinski is a former business associate of the Biden family who became a central witness in congressional inquiries into the family's overseas commercial activity.[1] A onetime partner in ventures involving [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[james-biden|James Biden]], Bobulinski has publicly maintained that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was not a bystander to those ventures but their organizing principle.[1]

## Testimony before Congress

On Tuesday, February 13, 2024, Bobulinski testified behind closed doors before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] and the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] as part of the Republican-led investigation into the Biden family's foreign dealings.[1] In his opening statement he argued that the president had been aware of the influence-peddling operation conducted by his son Hunter and his brother James during Biden's vice presidency, and that he had actively enabled it.[1]

"It is clear to me that Joe Biden was 'the Brand' being sold by the Biden family," Bobulinski said in the statement, a copy of which was obtained by [[national-review|National Review]].[1] He further alleged that the family's "foreign influence peddling operation — from China to Ukraine and elsewhere — sold out to foreign actors who were seeking to gain influence and access to Joe Biden and the United States government," describing the elder Biden as "more than a participant in and beneficiary of his family's business," but rather "an enabler, despite being buffered by a complex scheme to maintain plausible deniability."[1]

## Relationship to other testimony

Bobulinski's account echoed the July 2023 testimony of another former Hunter Biden associate, [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]].[1] Archer and Hunter Biden had both served on the board of the Ukrainian energy company [[burisma|Burisma Holdings]] and were once closely associated.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] framed Bobulinski's depiction as a portrait of Biden at a moment when he was still, in Esler's phrasing, "a coherent, functioning human" and therefore capable of directing complex organized criminal arrangements — a contrast Esler drew deliberately against the president's later cognitive decline as described in the [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]] report.[1] In Esler's reading, the self-serving character of the networks Biden operated within meant that his diminished usefulness made him expendable, and his public unravelling amounted to a sacrifice by the same interests he had served.[1]

## Public testimony (March 2024)

On Wednesday, March 20, 2024, Bobulinski testified publicly before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] as congressional Republicans advanced their impeachment inquiry against President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], in a hearing that produced several tense and revealing moments.[2] He appeared in person on Capitol Hill alongside [[jason-galanis|Jason Galanis]], another former [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] associate who testified virtually from a federal prison camp in Montgomery, Alabama.[2]

Bobulinski accused Hunter Biden of telling "blatant lies" and of lying under oath to lawmakers earlier that year about his father's involvement in his business dealings.[2] He also targeted Rep. [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]], saying he had "begged" Khanna and his staff to sit down with him to review the text message evidence stored on his BlackBerry regarding alleged wrongdoing by members of the Biden family.[2]

Badlands Media commentary described the nearly six-hour hearing as one in which Republicans recapped extensive evidence of alleged Biden family corruption while Democrats redirected the proceeding toward former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], concluding that the forum had lost credibility with the public.[2]
