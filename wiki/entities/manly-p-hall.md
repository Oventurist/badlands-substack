# Manly P. Hall

Manly P. Hall is described in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as an author, scholar and lecturer on the esoteric tradition.[1]

Hall is cited as the authority for the judgment that the [[divine-pymander|Divine Pymander]] was the greatest work of [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]].[1] That text, written in a manner similar to the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]], takes the form of a discourse between Hermes — or the archetypical truth seeker — and a being known as Pymander, understood as a source of higher wisdom.[1]
