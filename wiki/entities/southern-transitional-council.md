# Southern Transitional Council

The Southern Transitional Council (STC) is a separatist movement in southern [[yemen|Yemen]] that initially fought within the Saudi-led coalition against the [[houthis|Houthi]] rebels before pivoting toward seeking self-rule in the south.[1]

In late December 2025 the STC became the focus of an open rift within the coalition when a Saudi-led airstrike hit the southern Yemeni port of Mukalla, targeting what Riyadh said were weapons and combat vehicles unloaded from ships arriving from the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] and allegedly bound for the STC.[1] The UAE rejected the claim that the shipment contained weapons, and announced it would withdraw its remaining counterterrorism units from Yemen, citing concerns for personnel safety.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the underlying dynamic as the Saudis and Emiratis each backing individual Yemeni tribes that had fought together against the Houthis, with the UAE-backed faction now splintering off to pursue its own claim to govern — a pattern he attributed to the tribal and clan basis of Middle Eastern political identity, which forms at the local rather than national level.[1]
