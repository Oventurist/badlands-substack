# Marko Elez

Marko Elez was a staffer on the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] (DOGE) team led by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] under the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Elez was one of only two DOGE employees — along with [[tom-krause|Tom Krause]] — granted read-only access to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] payment records system under the temporary order issued by U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] on Wednesday, February 5, 2025.[1]

Elez resigned on Thursday, February 6, 2025, one day after the order, following reporting that linked him to offensive social media posts.[1] His departure meant that one of the two DOGE employees named in the judge's access limitation was no longer in government when the restriction took effect.[1]
