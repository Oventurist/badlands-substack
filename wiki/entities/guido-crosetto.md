# Guido Crosetto

Guido Crosetto is the Defence Minister of [[italy|Italy]] in the government of Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]].[1]

Crosetto was one of the senior ministers consulted before the April 13, 2026 decision to suspend the automatic renewal of Italy's 2003 defence cooperation memorandum of understanding with [[israel|Israel]], alongside Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]].[1] The suspended memorandum covered military procurement, training, the exchange of defence equipment and technology, and arms transit, and had renewed every five years since entering into force in 2006 unless one side opted out.[1] The decision followed Italian criticism of Israeli operations in [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and an incident in which Israeli forces fired warning shots at Italian troops serving with [[unifil|UNIFIL]].[1]

## Consultations on drone threat claims, September 2025

Italian Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]] said on 28 September 2025 that he had discussed claims of a possible Russian drone threat to [[italy|Italy]] with Defense Minister Crossetto that morning before publicly reassuring Italians that he did not believe President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] wanted to start World War III or to send drones to swarm the country.[2]
