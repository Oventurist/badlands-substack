# Sandia National Laboratories

Sandia National Laboratory is a United States national laboratory in the Los Alamos complex that, according to Badlands Media, was and is at the core of the U.S. nuclear weapons system.[1] The article also relays the allegation that Sandia contains "a base within a base" where classified technology of the highest order is housed.[1]

## Target of the PROMIS operation

[[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]], then head of [[lekem|Lekem]], tasked [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] with selling the bugged [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to the U.S. laboratories in the Los Alamos complex, Sandia foremost among them.[1] The objective was not merely the general flow of classified information the software already produced but specific nuclear information Israel was seeking.[1]

This effort occurred in 1984, concurrent with Eitan's most notable controversy — his handling of [[jonathan-pollard|Jonathan Pollard]], the American Navy intelligence analyst who was divulging U.S. nuclear secrets to Israel.[1]
