# Ali Mohamed

Ali Abdul Saoud Mohamed was an Egyptian double agent whom, according to Badlands Media, the United States sent to recruit American Muslims out of the [[al-kifah-refugee-center|Al Kifah Refugee Center]] in Brooklyn to go fight in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1]

Mohamed preached a fiery message of holy war to impressionable Muslim youths in order to bolster the ranks of the [[mujahideen]].[1] He eventually made his way to Pakistan, where he used his U.S. military training to instruct the newly recruited fighters who would go on to form the very terrorist cells the United States later engaged in Afghanistan after [[september-11-attacks|9/11]].[1] FBI special agent Jack Cloonan called Mohamed "bin Laden's first trainer," noting that he trained both [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] and Ayman al-Zawahiri, both of whom went on to lead [[al-qaeda]].[1]
