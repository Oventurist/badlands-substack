# Collin Allred

Collin Allred is a former Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas and a former statewide Senate candidate.

According to the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], Allred sought a return to Congress by challenging incumbent Representative [[julie-johnson|Julie Johnson]] in the Texas primary of March 3, 2026. The Brief lists the race alongside the challenges to [[al-green|Al Green]] and [[valerie-foushee|Valerie Foushee]] as early skirmishes in the Democratic Party's intra-party contest before the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].
