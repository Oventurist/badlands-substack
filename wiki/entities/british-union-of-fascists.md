# British Union of Fascists

The British Union of Fascists was the fascist movement led by Sir Oswald Mosley, which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as rising faster than ever in the interwar years while the British governing class, led by the royal family, fully backed Nazism.[1]

Mosley's lieutenant and liaison with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] was Major General [[j-f-c-fuller|J.F.C. Fuller]], the innovator of tank warfare and an initiate of [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]]'s Argentum Astrum Thelemic order.[1] The Union is grouped in the article with the American [[silver-shirts|Silver Shirts]], the Canadian blue shirts of [[adrien-arcand|Adrien Arcand]] and the Rhodes Scholar-founded League for Social Reconstruction as movements that came in different flavors but were united by a cold utilitarian philosophy of government, a devout love for [[eugenics|eugenics]] and an addiction to City of London and Wall Street money.[1]
