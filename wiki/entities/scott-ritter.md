# Scott Ritter

Scott Ritter is a former United States Marine Corps intelligence officer and United Nations weapons inspector who has become a prominent commentator on the Russia–Ukraine war, frequently interviewed by Judge [[andrew-napolitano|Andrew Napolitano]].[1]

## September 2024 long-range missile commentary

In a Napolitano interview conducted on the same day as one with Colonel [[lawrence-wilkerson|Lawrence Wilkerson]], Ritter explained technical details of the long-range missiles under consideration for Ukraine — including their user interface — in order to emphasize the direct role American military personnel would necessarily play in employing them against Russia, a point President Vladimir Putin had himself made publicly.[1] Ritter framed the stakes bluntly, saying "We were all going to DIE on Saturday!" — a reference to September 14, 2024.[1]

Ritter said that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] had overruled the military's initial decision to deny the missile request because the matter had become political, but that "phone calls were made" and [[pentagon]] officials intervened at the September 13 meeting with [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] to tell both leaders the deal was off.[1] Ritter, Wilkerson, and Napolitano each separately remarked on Biden's visibly angry mood following the meeting.[1] Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]] corroborated the same account in a third interview that day.[1]

## August 2024 FBI search

In early August 2024 the [[fbi|FBI]] searched Ritter's home as part of a Justice Department investigation into Americans with ties to Russian state television networks, an effort framed by prosecutors as countering potential Kremlin interference in the 2024 presidential election.[2] Newsweek described Ritter as a former [[united-nations|United Nations]] Special Commission weapons inspector, U.S. Marine Corps intelligence officer, and convicted sex offender who regularly contributes to Russian state media and often mirrors Kremlin talking points on the war in Ukraine.[2] In January 2024 Ritter visited the Russian republic of Chechnya, offering its leader [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]] and his fighters "friendship" with the United States during an address in the capital, Grozny.[2] U.S. officials said further searches were likely and did not rule out criminal charges.[2]
