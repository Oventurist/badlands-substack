# Michael Maloof

Michael Maloof is a former [[pentagon|Pentagon]] official who works as a defense and security commentator, frequently quoted in international media on U.S. foreign policy and the war in Ukraine.[1]

## Assessment of Western support for Ukraine

At the end of December 2023, Maloof told Sputnik that there is "no stomach" any longer in the West to fund Ukraine or the ongoing proxy conflict against Russia, arguing that the Kiev regime would have to seriously consider some form of negotiation and confront the realities on the ground.[1] He tied the funding crisis to the U.S. budget fight, noting that appropriations were hung up and that the government could shut down by January 17 if the administration and [[congress|Congress]] could not reach an arrangement covering funding for Ukraine and Israel while also enforcing the border.[1] Maloof said Republicans had held firm to that point and credited the public with recognizing that "the war is over," adding that Ukraine's counteroffensive had failed, that Kiev had gone into "total defensive mode," and that "the so-called counteroffensive just does not exist."[1]

The remarks were published against a backdrop of Ukraine's failed counteroffensive, political infighting, and corruption allegations that had been eroding donor support in both the United States and the European Union.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary treated the shift as evidence that the media and military-industrial complexes were abandoning one of the key dramas they had sustained for two years, producing a mix of awakening and apathy among general audiences.[1]
