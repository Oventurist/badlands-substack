# Věra Jourová

**Věra Jourová** is a Czech politician who served as Vice President for Values and Transparency of the [[european-commission|European Commission]], the executive arm of the European Union.[1] In that role she became one of the most visible European officials advocating for the regulation of online speech, and she was a recurring participant in the annual [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meetings at Davos.[1]

## Davos 2023 hate speech remarks

Speaking at the World Economic Forum's January 2023 annual meeting in Davos, Jourová predicted that the United States would soon follow Europe's lead in criminalising so-called hate speech.[1] "Illegal hate speech, which you will have soon also in the U.S. I think that we have a strong reason why we have this in the criminal law," she said on Tuesday, January 17, 2023.[1] The prediction was reported by Breitbart and circulated widely in alternative media because it appeared to disregard longstanding U.S. Supreme Court case law holding that there is no hate-speech exception to the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterised Jourová's statement as an "ominous" signal of the Davos agenda, arguing that in practice "hate speech" designations are applied to any language expressing frustration or distrust toward the ideologies grouped under "wokeness."[1] DeLarme tied the approach to the playbook described by [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] in his manual on [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]]: curate subgroups within the general population, set neighbour against neighbour, and then label the rhetoric of any group threatening the social order as hate speech.[1] He concluded that American constitutional protections remained a barrier the Davos class had underestimated.[1]
