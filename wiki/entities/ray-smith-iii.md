# Ray Smith III

Ray Smith III is an attorney and one of the nineteen defendants charged by Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] in the Georgia election interference case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his associates.[1]

On August 21, 2023, Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] set Smith's bond at $50,000, part of a slate of bond decisions that included $200,000 for Trump, $100,000 each for [[john-eastman|John Eastman]] and [[kenneth-chesebro|Kenneth Chesebro]], and $10,000 for [[scott-hall|Scott Hall]].[1] Like the other defendants, Smith was required to appear at the [[fulton-county-jail|Fulton County Jail]] for processing by noon that Friday, and his bond agreement barred him from performing any act to intimidate a codefendant or witness or otherwise obstruct the administration of justice.[1]
