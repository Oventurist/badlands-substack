# Kevin O'Connor

Kevin O'Connor is the physician to the president who served as [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s doctor during his term in the [[white-house|White House]].[1]

## Position on cognitive testing

In February 2024, after special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s report described the president as unable to recall basic details of his own life and career, attention turned to whether Biden's forthcoming annual physical would include a formal cognitive examination.[1] On Monday, February 12, the White House confirmed it would not.[1] Press Secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] relayed O'Connor's view that Biden demonstrates his cognitive ability "every day [in] how he operates and how he thinks."[1]

That position drew criticism from outside physicians. Dr. Stuart Fischer, a primary care doctor at a Bronx nursing home, told the New York Post that "something isn't right, and even if it's minor, it must be explained to the public," adding that "the horse is out of the barn" and that the president had "delayed producing objective evidence."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the refusal as emblematic of a wider pattern in which the [[white-house|White House]] press operation and its rotating cast of administration spokesmen gaslight the press corps about the president's condition, which she characterized as debilitating dementia.[1]
