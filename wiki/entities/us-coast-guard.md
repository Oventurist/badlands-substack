# United States Coast Guard

The United States Coast Guard is the federal maritime service responsible for law enforcement, search and rescue, and border security in US waters and approaches. It operates under the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

## Role in the Cayo Falcones incident

Following the February 2026 shooting of a Florida-registered speedboat in Cuban territorial waters, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] said that agencies including the Coast Guard and the Department of Homeland Security were investigating whether any of the occupants were US citizens or lawful permanent residents.[1] Because the vessel was unregistered for the voyage in question and departed from Florida, the Coast Guard's jurisdiction over maritime departures and vessel documentation placed it at the center of the US inquiry into the [[cayo-falcones-boat-incident|Cayo Falcones boat incident]].[1] As of the initial reporting, US authorities had not independently confirmed the basic facts asserted by [[cuba|Cuba]], including who fired first or the identities of the dead.[1]

## Key Bridge collapse response

After the container ship strike toppled Baltimore's Francis Scott Key Bridge on March 26, 2024, the Coast Guard participated in the search for six construction workers who had been repairing potholes on the Interstate 695 span, and announced by Tuesday evening that it was ending rescue efforts as the workers were presumed dead.[2]
