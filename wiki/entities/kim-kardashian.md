# Kim Kardashian

Kim Kardashian is an American media personality and businesswoman who appears in Badlands Media commentary as an illustration of manufactured celebrity and of alleged blackmail operations within the entertainment industry.[1]

## Allegations in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reported "rumors coming out that Kim Kardashian was used in blackmail honeypot operations."[1] The essay characterized her as a person of fame and wealth without conventional performing talent — noting that she does not sing, dance, or act, and does not claim to — and argued that her family's fortune originated with a "purposely leaked sex-tape."[1]

## Role in the article's argument

Carlson used Kardashian, alongside [[jennifer-lopez|Jennifer Lopez]] and [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]], as examples supporting his thesis that success in entertainment is allocated by a [[cabal|Cabal]] rather than by merit, and that compromising material is the currency of that system.[1] The claims are presented as rumor and opinion; Badlands Media states that its articles represent the views of contributing authors rather than the outlet.[1]
