# Paragraph 88

Paragraph 88 is the name of a neo-Nazi group whose members were reported arrested by Russia's Federal Security Service ([[fsb|FSB]]) in July 2023 in connection with an alleged Ukraine-linked plot to assassinate Russian journalists.[1]

## The alleged plot

Russian media reported widely that RT editor-in-chief [[margarita-simonyan|Margarita Simonyan]] had been targeted in an assassination plot uncovered and thwarted by the FSB.[1] According to RIA Novosti, the FSB announced on a Saturday in mid-July 2023 that members of Paragraph 88 had been arrested before any attempt was carried out.[1] Authorities said the group was also preparing to target [[ksenia-sobchak|Ksenia Sobchak]], and alleged that its members had "carried out intelligence at the addresses of work and residence of Simonyan and Sobchak."[1] TASS reported that six people had been detained and were expected to appear in a Moscow court.[1]

## Context

Commentary from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that neo-Nazi formations operating on behalf of the Ukrainian cause are well documented, citing the [[azov-battalion|Azov Battalion]] and pointing to a March 2019 [[new-york-times|New York Times]] article that described Azov plainly as "a Ukrainian neo-Nazi paramilitary organization," in contrast to later wartime coverage recasting such claims as Russian propaganda.[1]
