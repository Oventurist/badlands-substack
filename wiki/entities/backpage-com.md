# Backpage.com

Backpage.com was a classified-advertising website widely accused of hosting listings used for sex trafficking, including the trafficking of minors.[1]

## Takedown

Badlands Media contributor AbsoluteTruth1776 reported that legislation signed by [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] in April 2018 removed liability protections for certain online platforms that enabled [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]], and that this change subsequently led to the takedown of Backpage.com.[1] The site's seizure is presented in Badlands commentary as a concrete outcome of the Trump administration's anti-trafficking legislative push rather than a symbolic gesture.[1]

## Context

The article situates the Backpage takedown within a broader claim that more human trafficking arrests and prosecutions occurred under Trump than under the three preceding presidents combined, and that figures such as [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] had been allowed to operate freely until Trump took office.[1]
