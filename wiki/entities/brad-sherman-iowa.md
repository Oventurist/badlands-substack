# Brad Sherman (Iowa State Representative)

Brad Sherman is an Iowa state representative who in December 2023 led public opposition to a Satanic holiday display installed inside the Iowa State Capitol.[1] He is distinct from the California congressman of the same name.

## Opposition to the Iowa Capitol display

After [[satanic-temple|The Satanic Temple]] placed a display featuring a Baphomet goat skull and a blood-red pentagram in the Iowa State Capitol, Sherman labelled the installation "evil" and "disgusting" and pressed Governor [[kim-reynolds|Kim Reynolds]] to have it removed.[1]

In a written statement he said: "I am calling for our governor to have this blasphemous display removed immediately based on the grounds that it is unconstitutional and offends God upon whom the State of Iowa depends for blessings."[1] He further called for "clarifying legislation to be adopted in accordance with our State Constitution that prohibits Satanic displays in our Capitol building and on all state owned property."[1]

## Coverage and commentary

The episode was reported by [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] and reprinted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of December 13, 2023.[1] Badlands writer [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] argued that Reynolds should be ashamed for permitting the display and that its defenders had inverted their own separation-of-church-and-state arguments.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] offered a contrasting view, noting that the Temple held a permit and warning that objecting to detestable speech risked surrendering First Amendment protections; she framed the real problem as cultural degradation rather than the permit itself.[1]
