# Pierre Teilhard de Chardin

Pierre Teilhard de Chardin is described in Badlands Media reporting as a "Jesuit transhumanist" whose theories, along with those of Marshall McLuhan, [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]] and cybernetics founder [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]], were followed by the priests of the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]] in preaching a new gospel to humanity.[1]

His intellectual heir is identified as [[ray-kurzweil|Ray Kurzweil]], and the lineage is treated as the doctrinal foundation of the [[transhumanism|transhumanist religion]] promoted by figures such as [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]] within the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] orbit.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] has treated Chardin's thought elsewhere under the heading of the Darwinian perversion of Christianity.[1]
