# Credit Suisse

Credit Suisse was a major Swiss bank that collapsed into insolvency in 2023 and was acquired by fellow Swiss institution [[ubs|UBS]] in a transaction widely described as engineered by Swiss authorities.

The January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]] presents an alternative account of that collapse. [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that testimony from the private banker [[matthias-krull|Matthias Krull]], arrested in 2018 for laundering funds embezzled from Venezuela's state oil company [[pdvsa|PDVSA]], "led to massive asset forfeitures of a number of banks and financial institutions, namely Credit Suisse, which faced insolvency in 2023 and had to purchased in an acquisition by fellow Swiss bank UBS that was ostensibly forced by the Swiss government." In his telling, the forfeited money was owed to the government of Venezuela, making the bank's failure a downstream consequence of American anti-money-laundering enforcement rather than of ordinary market distress.

Ghost frames Credit Suisse as one node in what he suspects is a broader structure — asking whether litigation reopened by the lifting of Venezuelan sanctions will reveal "that the entire global banking system is the same cartel that traffics drugs, weapons, and children."
