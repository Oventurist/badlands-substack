# Petro Poroshenko

Petro Poroshenko is a former President of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and the predecessor of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]], who currently heads one of the leading opposition parties in the country.[1]

## Claim of 2025 Ukrainian elections

In an interview published by the Ukrainian news site Censor.net on February 16, 2025, Poroshenko claimed that Ukrainian authorities were planning to hold presidential elections by the end of that year, saying, "Write it down – October 26 this year."[1] He claimed the country's state printing service was already calculating the number of ballots needed, information he attributed to anonymous insiders in Zelenskyy's office who were also quoted by local media outlets.[1]

"According to our sources, the [Ukrainian] Election Commission is starting to make changes in the voter register. [...] This is solely for the purpose of preparing for elections," Poroshenko said.[1]

## Context

The claim surfaced as the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration pressed for a rapid ceasefire in the Russia-Ukraine war and as U.S.-Russia talks convened in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], developments Badlands writers read as the transition from narrative "seeding" to "germination" and "harvest."[1]

## Oligarch wealth ranking (2024–2025)

As of September 2024, the outlet Ukrainian Focus placed Poroshenko third on the list of Ukraine's wealthiest people, behind [[rinat-akhmetov|Rinat Akhmetov]] and [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] and ahead of [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]] and [[konstantin-zhevago|Konstantin Zhevago]], with most named figures having contributed to Kiev's war effort.[2] The ranking was cited in March 2025 coverage of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s proposal to sanction Ukraine's top ten oligarchs as a means of ending the war, made in reply to Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]]'s call to halt US financial aid to Kiev.[2]
