# Petro Poroshenko

Petro Poroshenko is a Ukrainian businessman and politician who served as President of Ukraine from 2014 to 2019. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, he is listed among the oligarchs created by the privatization and liberalization reforms applied to Ukraine and other Warsaw Pact countries in the 1990s.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names Poroshenko alongside Rinat Akhmetov, [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]] and [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] on the Ukrainian side, and [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]], Boris Berezovsky, Mikhail Fridman and [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]] on the Russian side, as products of the same process directed by [[anatoly-chubais|Anatoly Chubais]] and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] under [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]]. The article treats this class as the social base of the post-2014 Ukrainian order that followed the removal of [[viktor-yanukovych|Viktor Yanukovych]].
