# Imelda Cuellar

Imelda Cuellar is the wife of longtime Texas Democratic congressman [[henry-cuellar|Henry Cuellar]]. In May 2024 she was indicted alongside her husband by the United States Justice Department on bribery and money laundering charges.[1]

## 2024 indictment

The Justice Department released the indictment on a Friday in early May 2024, charging the couple with bribery and money laundering connected to their ties with a bank in Mexico and an oil and gas company controlled by Azerbaijan. Each was released on a $100,000 bond after a first appearance in federal court in Houston, according to a department spokesperson.[1]

According to the indictment, from 2014 to 2021 the Cuellars allegedly accepted roughly $600,000 in bribes from the two foreign entities in exchange for the congressman performing official acts. The Justice Department stated that "the bribe payments were allegedly laundered, pursuant to sham consulting contracts, through a series of front companies and middlemen into shell companies owned by Imelda Cuellar, who performed little to no legitimate work under the contracts."[1]
