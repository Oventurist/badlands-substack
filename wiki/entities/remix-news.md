# Remix News

Remix News (rmx.news) is a Central European news outlet covering politics in Poland, [[hungary|Hungary]], and the wider region from a conservative perspective.[1]

In August 2023 Remix News reported that a company backed by the Soros Economic Development Fund had taken control of two major Polish newspapers — the daily Rzeczpospolita and the financial daily Parkiet — raising concerns about media bias given [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s progressive politics.[1] The outlet reported that Polish owner Grzegorz Hajdarowicz sold a large share of his stake in [[gremi-media|Gremi Media]] to the Dutch company [[pluralis|Pluralis]], completing a 2021 deal and leaving Pluralis with 57 percent of the shares and control of the company.[1]
