# Mike Rothmiller

Mike Rothmiller is an American author, former [[lapd|LAPD]] intelligence officer and television producer who has written more than 26 books and has served since the early 1980s as the pre-eminent global authority promoting the narrative that the Kennedy brothers were involved in the death of [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]].[1] He is the sole conduit for the anonymous sources said to have read Monroe's otherwise unseen secret diary.[1]

## Biography and intelligence claims

Rothmiller's official biography boasts of work for the [[fbi|FBI]], [[cia|CIA]], the US Secret Service, Army Intelligence, Naval Intelligence, Scotland Yard, [[mossad|Mossad]], [[interpol|Interpol]] and the Canadian RCMP, and states that he "conceived and directed" global intelligence operations focused on anti-terrorism.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] contrasted this résumé with his own argument that no case of naturally emerging terrorism, domestic or foreign, can be found over the past half century without a coordinating hand of Western intelligence agencies behind the scenes.[1]

## Role in the RFK grand jury

Rothmiller organized and testified at the "secret grand jury" proceeding concerning the murder of Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]], which made no mention of [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]], of the close-range bullet holes in the back of Kennedy's neck, or of any conspiracy beyond the "[[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]] lone gunman" line.[1] Kennedy's son [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] has stated that "the people that were closest to [Sirhan], the people that disarmed him all said he never got near my father," and described Sirhan's trial as "really a penalty hearing… not a real trial."[1]

## Media career

Rothmiller has been marketed as "the Serpico" of the LAPD, whose exposés of corruption resulted in the shutdown of entire sections of LAPD intelligence branches in the early 1990s.[1] Ehret questioned whether that outcome was beneficial, noting that it presumes all LAPD intelligence officers were corrupt and ignores figures such as former LAPD intelligence officer Mike Ruppert, who exposed CIA control of narcotics operations in America.[1]

Ehret also noted that Rothmiller was rewarded with executive positions at three [[sony|Sony]] departments and roles as a producer and host for [[pbs|PBS]] and ESPN, overseeing dozens of documentaries and television programs and making him a significant influencer of the modern zeitgeist.[1] Rothmiller played a key role in [[netflix|Netflix]] films on Marilyn Monroe and other historical topics.[1]
