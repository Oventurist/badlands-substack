# Ryan McInerney

Ryan McInerney is the chief executive officer of Visa, the American global payments technology company that operates one of the world's largest card networks.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that McInerney was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. He was listed alongside [[michael-miebach|Michael Miebach]] of Mastercard in a delegation of American payments and financial leadership traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
