# HUMA (Harvard University Muslim Alumni)

HUMA — the Harvard University Muslim Alumni association — is the organization the Badlands account identifies as the referent of the capitalized "HUMA" in [[qanon|Q]] drop #111, posted November 5, 2017.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) That drop asked what organization HUMA is, which U.S. president is affiliated with it, who funded that president on his behalf, and whether the Muslim Brotherhood was affiliated with any of these organizations or people.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The article answers that the president in question is [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], whose Harvard education it says was paid for by [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] in his pre-political days — a claim it connects to Obama's school records remaining sealed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) It notes that the same drop asked what had just happened in Saudi Arabia, who had been arrested and whose funds were frozen, and observes that November 5, 2017 was the day Alwaleed was arrested and his assets frozen.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Groups such as HUMA are presented as a central part of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization/stealth jihad]] strategy, with universities serving as recruiting and training grounds for the infiltration of government by students indoctrinated to seek official posts and to replace the constitution with sharia law.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The deliberate resonance between the acronym HUMA and the name of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] runs through the article's reading of the drop.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
