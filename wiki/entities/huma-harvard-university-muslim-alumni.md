# HUMA (Harvard University Muslim Alumni)

HUMA — the Harvard University Muslim Alumni association — is the organization the Badlands account identifies as the referent of the capitalized "HUMA" in [[qanon|Q]] drop #111, posted November 5, 2017.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) That drop asked what organization HUMA is, which U.S. president is affiliated with it, who funded that president on his behalf, and whether the Muslim Brotherhood was affiliated with any of these organizations or people.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The article answers that the president in question is [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], whose Harvard education it says was paid for by [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] in his pre-political days — a claim it connects to Obama's school records remaining sealed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) It notes that the same drop asked what had just happened in Saudi Arabia, who had been arrested and whose funds were frozen, and observes that November 5, 2017 was the day Alwaleed was arrested and his assets frozen.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Groups such as HUMA are presented as a central part of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization/stealth jihad]] strategy, with universities serving as recruiting and training grounds for the infiltration of government by students indoctrinated to seek official posts and to replace the constitution with sharia law.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The deliberate resonance between the acronym HUMA and the name of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] runs through the article's reading of the drop.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Treatment in Q for Dummies Chapter 16

Chapter 16 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series, which is devoted entirely to [[q-drop-111|drop #111]], reiterates that HUMA refers to the Harvard University Muslim Alumni and not to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], while noting that Abedin was back in the news at the time of writing.[2] The chapter states that [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] shares connections with many prominent and "not necessarily benevolent" Muslims, including figures tied to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]], and that the Brotherhood is itself affiliated both with HUMA and with [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid Abdullah Tariq al-Mansour]].[2]

Answering Q's question about which president is affiliated with HUMA, the chapter identifies [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], recounting that al-Mansour wrote to former Malcolm X attorney [[percy-sutton|Percy Sutton]] asking him to send a letter supporting Obama's admission to Harvard Law School, and that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] financed Obama's education and rise.[2] Where a later section of the drop asks which faith HUMA, the MB and Huma Abedin represent, the chapter answers that the accurate label is not simply "Muslim" but [[wahhabism|Wahhabi extremism]].[2]

## Drop #117 and the Manchurian Candidate framing

Chapter 17 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series returns to HUMA while unpacking drop #117, stating that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] and [[barack-obama|Obama]] are tied to HUMA by way of Obama's past membership and Alwaleed's financing of his schooling at Harvard.[3] The chapter argues Alwaleed extended that financing because Obama appeared to be a potential Saudi Manchurian Candidate.[3] It again cautions that when all the letters are capitalized Q is referring to the Harvard University Muslim Alumni and not to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]].[3]

## Earlier appearance in drops #68 and #71

Chapter 9 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series traces the HUMA thread back to its first appearances, in drops #68 and #71 of November 3–4, 2017, where Q instructed readers to "Follow HUMA."[4] The chapter argues that the instruction carries a double meaning — a deliberate example of the series' recurring maxim that "double meanings exist" — pointing simultaneously at [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and at the Harvard University Muslim Alumni.[4] Asked who HUMA is connected to, the chapter answers "[[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Al Waleed Bin Talal]], to start," and flags that this connection would become central over the following two to three chapters of the series.[4]

In the reading of drop #71, the "Follow HUMA" instruction is paired with Q's question about who links the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] to [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]; the chapter answers that the connector is Abedin, and that the significance lies in her family's ties to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] and the Brotherhood's stated goal of destroying Western culture and America.[4]
