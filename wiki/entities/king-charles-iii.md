# King Charles III

Charles III is the King of the United Kingdom and, in his capacity as Canadian head of state, the sovereign of [[canada|Canada]]. He appears in the Badlands corpus as the figure through whom the Crown's residual authority over Canada is invoked.

## Alberta independence commentary

In the January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] frames the [[alberta-prosperity-project|Alberta Prosperity Project]]'s campaign as an effort to win independence "from Britain" rather than merely from Ottawa, and concludes that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "should call King Charles and make a deal." The remark reflects the corpus's treatment of Canada as a Crown-administered "globalist proxy state" rather than a fully independent polity.

## Statement on Prince Andrew's arrest (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that King Charles issued a statement following the arrest of his brother, [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten]] (formerly Prince Andrew), on suspicion of [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]]: "the law must take its course." The Brief treats the statement as a deliberate distancing of the monarchy from the arrested former prince, whose Epstein-linked conduct had shadowed the institution for years.
