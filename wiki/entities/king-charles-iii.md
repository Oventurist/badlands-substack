# King Charles III

Charles III is the reigning monarch of the United Kingdom and the elder brother of [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]], the former Prince Andrew.[1]

## Response to Andrew's arrest

When Andrew was arrested by [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]] in February 2026 on suspicion of [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]], Charles issued a statement declaring that "the law must take its course."[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the statement as framing the episode "as a routine application of institutional process while the cameras capture every angle of the fall from grace for one of the most visibly compromised figures in the Epsteinian web."[1]

In that analysis, the palace's posture of detachment is part of the staging: the monarchy publicly concedes a single compromised figure in order to preserve the institution, an example of the [[deep-state-burn-cards|burn card]] pattern applied to royal reputation management.[1]

## Reported Commonwealth offer to Trump (2025)

In March 2025, British press reported that Charles was expected to make a "secret offer" to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during a planned state visit, following a letter from the King hand-delivered by Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]]: an invitation for the United States to become an "associate member" of the [[commonwealth-of-nations|Commonwealth of Nations]].[2] The idea had first been raised during Trump's first term by the Royal Commonwealth Society with Queen Elizabeth II's blessing and was revived in his second, with a source saying it was "being discussed at the highest levels."[2] Reporting connected the proposal to the tariff conflict with [[canada|Canada]], a Commonwealth realm of which Charles is head of state.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary was dismissive, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writing that associate membership was "not a thing we're doing" and separately describing the monarch on Canadian currency in derisive terms while arguing Canada should declare independence from the Crown.[2]

## Ottawa visit and throne speech (May 2025)

King Charles III and Queen Camilla were greeted by loud cheers from a large crowd while touring an Ottawa farmer's market on the first leg of a historic visit to the Canadian capital, where the King was to open Parliament with a speech from the throne on Tuesday.[3] Cries of "God Save the King" drowned out a handful of protesters holding anti-monarchist placards, and some onlookers had flown across Canada and waited hours in the sun to meet the royal couple during the two-day visit.[3] The pair were received at Ottawa's airport by an honour guard from the Royal Canadian Dragoons and music from the Canadian Armed Forces Central Band, beginning two days of pageantry widely seen as a symbolic show of support for Canada's sovereignty.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the framing, noting the incongruity of a British monarch travelling to deliver a "throne speech" to reassure "his colony" of its sovereignty, and questioning the use of the title "Queen Camilla."[3]

## Diana's letter

Chapter 14 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series raises Charles in connection with the death of [[diana-princess-of-wales|Diana, Princess of Wales]], noting that Diana wrote a letter to her butler expressing concern that Charles — then Prince of Wales — would use a car accident to remove her.[4] The chapter links to Telegraph coverage of the letter and treats it as supporting its theory that Diana was killed to stop her exposing the royal family.[4] The claim is the author's interpretation and no finding of responsibility is established in the source.[4]
