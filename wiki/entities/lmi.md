# LMI

LMI is a consulting organization that describes itself as "dedicated to improving the management of government," and which Badlands identified in September 2024 as one of the institutional affiliations listed in the public biography of former CIA analyst [[soo-kim|Soo Kim]].[1]

## Badlands characterization

Badlands noted that LMI's pinned social media post advertised a training program run across the Department of Defense, and mocked the post's minimal engagement.[1] The publication characterized LMI as "another NGO based out of Tyson, Virginia that simultaneously extorts our tax dollars while implementing its will onto our government in the most nefarious ways, while wrapping itself in a blanket of self-indulgent altruism."[1]

The reference to LMI formed part of a broader argument in the brief about the network of federally funded consultancies and strategic-studies institutions — including the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] and the Philip Merrill Center for Strategic Studies at the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies — that Badlands described as "vehicles of modern tyranny."[1]
