# The Shawn Ryan Show

The Shawn Ryan Show is a long-form interview podcast hosted by [[shawn-ryan|Shawn Ryan]] and distributed on YouTube, where its episodes with national-security, military and intelligence figures regularly draw large audiences.[1] In alternative-media coverage the program functions as one of the larger independent venues capable of carrying figures from the defense and intelligence world to audiences outside legacy broadcast media.[1]

## Kwast interview (May 23, 2025)

On May 23, 2025 the show released its interview with retired U.S. Air Force Lieutenant General [[steve-kwast|Steve Kwast]], published to YouTube.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted the release the same day, telling readers the episode was "worth the investment of your time" and characterizing it as a sweeping conversation about unleashing innovation, triangulating truth, and "the art of the possible for humanity in the Golden Age."[1]

Badlands framed the material as familiar to its own audience, noting that "General Kwast is a Badlander" who often appears on Badlands shows and attends its events, and that the network had been discussing the art of the possible in the Golden Age with him for years.[1] It described the wider release of that conversation on Ryan's platform as an exciting development, concluding, "The future is amazing."[1]

The notice asked readers to watch the interview, like the episode, share it, and note in the show's comment section that Badlands Media had sent them — an explicit cross-promotional appeal between two independent-media outlets.[1] The same post directed readers toward the network's next in-person event, a return to [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]] in August.[1]
