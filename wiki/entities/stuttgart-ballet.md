# Stuttgart Ballet

The Stuttgart Ballet is a German ballet company that returned to the [[john-f-kennedy-center-for-the-performing-arts|John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts]] in Washington, D.C. after more than 30 years, having last appeared at the venue in 1992.[1]

The company's return production was John Cranko's *Onegin*, a balletic interpretation of Alexander Pushkin's 1825 novel in verse *Eugene Onegin*, with 2025 marking the work's diamond jubilee.[1] Tchaikovsky's score was scheduled to fill the Opera House between October 8 and 12, 2025, opening the Kennedy Center's 2025–2026 ballet season.[1]

Jane Raleigh, Director of Dance Programming for the Kennedy Center, said the Stuttgart Ballet and Cranko's *Onegin* "were a staple of Kennedy Center ballet programming in the 1970's and 80's," adding that "the timeless relevance of Cranko's choreography and Pushkin's storytelling made this ballet an instant classic, and to be able to welcome this glorious company back to the Center decades later, especially as the work celebrates its 60th anniversary, is such a special moment for Washington audiences."[1]
