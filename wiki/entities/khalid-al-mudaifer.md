# Khalid bin Saleh Al-Mudaifer

Khalid bin Saleh Al-Mudaifer is the Saudi Arabian deputy minister of mining affairs.[1] He announced plans to scale up the kingdom's lithium program by launching a commercial pilot focused on direct lithium mining, following [[saudi-aramco|Saudi Aramco]]'s successful extraction of lithium during an earlier pilot project in oil fields near the sea.[1] The commercial pilot is to be led by [[lihytech|Lithium Infinity, also known as LiHyTech]], a startup out of [[king-abdullah-university-of-science-and-technology|King Abdullah University of Science and Technology]].[1]

The announcement was presented as a milestone in Saudi Arabia's economic diversification effort under Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s [[saudi-vision-2030|Saudi Vision 2030]], which seeks to shift the oil-dependent kingdom toward new sectors including mining, technology, and tourism.[1]
