# Mohsen Karimi

Mohsen Karimi is an Iranian financial official who served as Deputy Governor of the [[central-bank-of-iran|Central Bank of Iran]].[1]

In late January 2023, Karimi announced that [[iran|Iran]] and [[russia|Russia]] had signed an agreement integrating their interbank communication and transfer systems, connecting 52 Iranian banks and 106 Russian banks through the Russian Financial Message Transfer System.[1] He stated that the arrangement would facilitate economic relations between the two countries, and it was widely understood as a mechanism for bypassing sanctions imposed on both states' financial infrastructure and reducing reliance on [[swift|SWIFT]].[1] The announcement, reported by [[the-cradle|The Cradle]], formed part of the broader [[iran-russia-banking-integration|Iran-Russia banking system integration]].[1]
