# Klaus Iohannis

Klaus Iohannis was the incumbent President of Romania at the time of the annulment of the November 2024 Romanian presidential election.[1]

## Election annulment and resignation

After populist candidate [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]] won 23% of the vote in what was considered a major upset, the incumbent Romanian government annulled the results of the first round and then cancelled the election altogether, ostensibly in defense of democracy.[1] The official justification took the form of accusations that Georgescu was a "fascist," and Romanian authorities cited supposed "irregularities" in his campaign while claiming without proof that Russia had covertly supported him.[1]

Cancelling the election caused a major uproar among the population, and an impeachment proceeding was launched against Iohannis, who resigned in disgrace in February 2025.[1]


## Declassification Preceding the Annulled 2024 Election

As incumbent Romanian president in late 2024, Iohannis released secret files alleging that the presidential vote won in the first round by [[calin-georgescu|Călin Georgescu]] had been compromised by a likely Russia-backed "hybrid" attack and a vast TikTok influence operation.[2] Romania's Constitutional Court subsequently canceled the entire election.[2]
