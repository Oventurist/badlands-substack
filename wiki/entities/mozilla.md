# Mozilla

Mozilla is an open-source software company best known for developing the [[firefox|Firefox]] web browser, which has long been promoted as a compelling alternative to Google's Chrome and Apple's Safari.[1]

## AI pivot and user backlash (December 2025)

After being appointed chief executive of Mozilla, [[anthony-enzor-demeo|Anthony Enzor-DeMeo]] announced that the company would be "tripling down" on artificial intelligence. In a blog post dated December 16, 2025, Enzor-DeMeo said Firefox would become a "modern AI browser and support a portfolio of new and trusted software additions."[1]

The announcement triggered an immediate and forceful backlash from Mozilla's user base, arriving amid a broader revolt against AI features being inserted into nearly every aspect of the computing experience — a trend critics associate with "AI slop" subsuming news feeds and turning the technology into a distraction.[1] One user wrote that they had "never seen a company so astoundingly out of touch with the people who want to use its software," while another said they had switched back to Firefox specifically "BECAUSE it was the last AI-free browser."[1]

The outcry was strong enough to push Mozilla into damage control. In an update posted to Mastodon, the company clarified that "Firefox will have an option to completely disable all AI features" — effectively a kill switch for the entire AI feature set.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized Firefox as "a cult classic among open source maxis" and argued the company should have anticipated the reaction. She framed the episode as encouraging evidence that, absent the ability to audit code personally, users must place their trust in honorable developers and the "watchdog nerds" who hold them accountable — describing the open-source world as a Wild West with its own code of honor among outlaws.[1]
