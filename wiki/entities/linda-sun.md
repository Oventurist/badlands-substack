# Linda Sun

Linda Sun, 41, is a former New York state government official who was charged in September 2024 with acting as an illegal agent of the Chinese government.[1] She and her husband, Chris Hu, 40, were arrested on Tuesday, September 3, 2024, and both pleaded not guilty during an initial court appearance in Brooklyn that afternoon.[1]

Sun held numerous posts in New York state government, including deputy chief of staff to Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]].[1] Federal prosecutors said she used those positions to further Beijing's agenda in exchange for compensation and gifts worth millions of dollars.[1]

In an interview on WNYC, Hochul said she was outraged and shocked by Sun's alleged behavior, stating that her office had fired Sun the previous year after discovering evidence of misconduct and had reported her actions to authorities immediately: "I'm furious, and I'm outraged and absolutely shocked at how brazen her behavior was… It was a betrayal of trust—the trust of government, the trust of the people."[1] Former Governor [[andrew-cuomo|Andrew Cuomo]], in whose administration Sun had also served, likewise responded to the charges.[1]
