# Harry Dexter White

Harry Dexter White was a close ally of [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] who led the fight to shut down the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] at the July 1944 [[bretton-woods-conference|Bretton Woods conference]].[1]

## Bretton Woods and the BIS

White's resolutions to dissolve the BIS and audit its books were passed, but never put into action.[1] He went on to become the first head of the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]], where he defended FDR's program to create a new anti-imperial system of finance against [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] leader and eugenicist [[john-maynard-keynes|John Maynard Keynes]], who defended the Bank and pushed instead to redefine the post-war system around a one-world currency called the Bancor, controlled by the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] and the BIS.[1]

## Postwar targeting and death

After 1945, an anti-communist witch hunt turned America into what Ehret calls a fascist police state under FBI surveillance, and FDR's close allies were the first targets.[1] White's death in 1948, while campaigning for [[henry-wallace|Henry Wallace]]'s presidential bid, put an end to anti-colonialists running the IMF.[1]
