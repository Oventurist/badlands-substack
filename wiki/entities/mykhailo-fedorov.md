# Mykhailo Fedorov

Mykhailo Fedorov is a Ukrainian government official cited in June 2026 reporting on [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s intensifying campaign against [[russia|Russia]].[1]

Fedorov said in June that Russian logistics were being disrupted by Ukrainian strikes and that Crimea was being increasingly isolated.[1] His remarks accompanied continued Ukrainian drone strikes on targets inside Russia, including energy and refinery infrastructure, and the announcement by President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] of a 40-day "influence operation" to be run by the [[security-service-of-ukraine|Security Service of Ukraine]].[1]
