# David Anthony

**David Anthony** is an academic cited in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Creation Myth Clues" as the author of a book on the Proto-Indo-European language (PIE), *The Horse, the Wheel and Language*.[1]

The article reports that Anthony explained that the many mysteries surrounding the PIE language remain impenetrable because linguists and archaeologists are unable to share their findings with one another.[1] The author of the essay treats this observation as evidence of what he calls "academic malpractice": the compartmentalization and over-specialization of academic disciplines, combined with needlessly obtuse jargon, which prevents researchers in adjacent fields from collating data that would otherwise support obvious connections.[1]

The essay describes this fragmentation as a form of man-made, artificial scarcity that leaves everyone poorer, remarks that "it's almost as if the Tower of Babel is STILL in the process of collapsing," and concludes that independent researchers are necessary because they alone are not hamstrung by the academic system.[1]
