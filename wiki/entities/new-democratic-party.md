# New Democratic Party

The New Democratic Party (NDP) is a Canadian social-democratic political party. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, the NDP appears both as an institutional descendant of a Depression-era Fabian project and as the party of [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]].

## Origins as described in the source

A footnote in the article states that the [[league-of-social-reconstruction|League for Social Reconstruction]], founded in 1932 by five Rhodes Scholars and described as the "[[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] of Canada," created a political party called the Cooperative Commonwealth Federation, which later changed its name to the New Democratic Party in 1961. [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] adds that while "good people have found themselves members of the NDP and Liberals over the years," the party's founding core was tied to "the highest echelons of the British oligarchy."

## Bob Rae

The article notes that Bob Rae, the Rhodes Scholar who later managed [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]]'s entry into federal politics, served as the NDP Premier of Ontario from 1990 to 1995.
