# Evelyn Baring

Evelyn Baring was a British Lord and Comptroller General of Egypt during the period of British control over Egyptian finances and administration in the late nineteenth century.[1]

## Role in the origins of Salafi institutions

In commentary published by Badlands, Baring is identified as the official under whose direction the British government established a Salafi (radical) Islamic school in Cairo in the 1880s.[1] That school is described as the institution from which [[hassan-al-banna|Hassan al-Banna]] — founder of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] — graduated, and whose head imam, [[rashid-rida|Rida Rashid]], was said to have been employed by British Intelligence.[1] The claim underpins the Badlands position that the Muslim Brotherhood was a creation of British Intelligence rather than a purely indigenous Egyptian movement.[1]
