# Moshe Gafni

Moshe Gafni is a Haredi (Ultra-Orthodox) member of the Israeli [[knesset|Knesset]] whose role in the political survival of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government drew attention in Badlands commentary during July 2025.[1]

## Iran strike and the coalition crisis

According to a New York Times report discussed by Badlands, three days before Israel's first attack on Iran on June 13, Haredi lawmakers had scheduled a Knesset vote to dissolve Netanyahu's government over his failure to codify into law the exemption that spares Ultra-Orthodox students from the two years of service otherwise required in the IDF.[1] Netanyahu is said to have reached out to Gafni and informed him of the planned strike against Iran, asking that he refrain from dissolving the government so that the prime minister could prosecute the war.[1]

## Background and legislative record

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Gafni and Netanyahu likely first met in 1990, when both were appointed deputy ministers in Yitzhak Shamir's cabinet — Netanyahu as Deputy Foreign Minister and Gafni as Deputy Minister of Religious Affairs.[1] In March 2023 Gafni, together with another Knesset lawmaker, submitted a bill that would have outlawed the proselytizing of Christianity in Israel, with violators facing one to two years in prison.[1]

## Commentary

The proposed anti-missionary legislation was cited in Badlands commentary as evidence that Israeli religious-party figures do not share American Christian assumptions of alliance, and as part of an argument that the United States has no moral duty to underwrite what the author described as their warmongering.[1]
