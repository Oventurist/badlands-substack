# Karl Rove

Karl Rove is an American Republican political operative and strategist, best known as the senior adviser and deputy chief of staff to President [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]. In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary he is treated as an emblem of the pre-2016 Republican establishment that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] displaced.[1]

Rove was named directly by Trump in the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]], in which the former president told the [[conservative-political-action-conference|CPAC]] audience that "we are never going back to the party of Paul Ryan, Karl Rove, and Jeb Bush," having first described the old party as one "ruled by Freaks, Neocons, Globalists, Open Border Zealots, and Fools."[1] Badlands contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] cited the line as the single most important passage of the address, arguing that the naming of Rove alongside [[paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]] and [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] signalled that the "infiltrated version of the Republican party is dead" and that its remaining officeholders' "days are numbered."[1] Getz situated Rove's cohort within a broader account in which [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] blurred party lines and produced the [[rino|RINO]] faction responsible for the [[forever-wars|forever wars]].[1]
