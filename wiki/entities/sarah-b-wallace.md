# Sarah B. Wallace

Judge Wallace is the Denver District Court judge who presided over the 2023 trial on whether former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could appear on Colorado's 2024 presidential ballot under the disqualification clause of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

## The Colorado ballot trial

The trial began in Denver District Court, brought by a group of Republican and unaffiliated voters working with a liberal organization; Trump's campaign countered that his role in the January 6 riot was limited to constitutionally protected political speech.[1] The hearing was expected to run a full week, with a written ruling to follow, and neither side expected the litigation to end there — an appeal was considered likely, potentially reaching the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]].[1]

During the proceedings, Wallace denied a motion for a directed verdict brought by Trump's lead attorney, Scott Gessler, after the testimony of Deputy Elections Director Hillary Rudy, allowing the case to continue.[1] Wallace also handled an interruption raised by petitioners' attorney Eric Olson over an unauthorized recording of the hearing livestream, asking whether the streamer was part of the court's expanded media coverage arrangement.[1] Day four of the trial was scheduled to begin at 8:00 a.m. Mountain Time on Thursday.[1]
