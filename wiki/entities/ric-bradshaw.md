# Ric Bradshaw

Ric Bradshaw is the sheriff of Palm Beach County, Florida, who served as the principal law enforcement spokesman following the [[2024-trump-golf-course-assassination-attempt|September 2024 apparent assassination attempt]] against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Trump's West Palm Beach golf course.[1]

## Account of the September 2024 incident

Bradshaw described the standard protective arrangement in place that day: a [[us-secret-service|Secret Service]] agent moved one or two holes ahead of the former president, roughly 300 to 500 yards away.[1] He said that around 1:30 p.m., between the fifth and seventh holes, the agent noticed a rifle muzzle poking through the tree-lined chain-link fence surrounding the course, opened fire, and that Trump's detail rushed the former president to a holding room.[1] According to Bradshaw, the gunman fled in a black Nissan, leaving behind an AK-47-style rifle, two bags and a camera that had been mounted to the fence to record.[1]

Bradshaw emphasized the lethality of the setup, telling reporters: "With a rifle and a scope like that, that's not a long distance."[1]
