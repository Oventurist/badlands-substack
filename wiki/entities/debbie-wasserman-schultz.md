# Debbie Wasserman Schultz

Debbie Wasserman Schultz is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives and a former chair of the Democratic National Committee. She appears in Badlands coverage of the declassified [[clinton-annex|Clinton annex]] as a figure named in intercepted Russian intelligence relating to the 2016 investigation of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server.[1]

## Intercepted communications claim

According to material discussed in the July 2025 disclosures, the U.S. intelligence community intercepted Russian intelligence describing "purported communications between Congresswoman Debbie Wasserman Schultz... and two different individuals who worked for the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]]."[1] In those purported communications, Wasserman Schultz is said to have stated that the [[fbi|FBI]] had no hard evidence against Clinton because "data was removed from the mail servers just in time."[1]

Badlands commentator Jon Herold argued the statement implies Wasserman Schultz believed hard evidence against Clinton had existed, that the bureau lacked it only because it had been removed from the servers, and therefore that she was aware of a cover-up.[1] The same body of intelligence reportedly indicated that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] used Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] to pressure FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] into burying the email scandal.[1]

## Gabbard "Russian asset" remarks (2024)

On a November 2024 broadcast of MSNBC's "José Díaz-Balart Reports," Wasserman Schultz said DNI nominee [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] is "someone who is likely a Russian asset" and "considered to be, essentially, by most assessments, a Russian asset."[2] She argued that Gabbard "has met with war criminals, violated the Department of State's guidance, and secretly, clandestinely, went to Syria and met with [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]]," and said that as DNI Gabbard would hold the nation's most significant secrets and "would be a direct line to our enemies."[2]

Badlands Media described the [[gabbard-russian-asset-allegation|allegation]] as a revival of a narrative birthed by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], calling it a misogynistic and defamatory attack timed to the confirmation process.[2]

## The Awan brothers in Q commentary

Chapter 9 of the Badlands series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] names Wasserman Schultz, in her capacity as [[democratic-national-committee|DNC]] chair, as one of the members of Congress served by the [[awan-brothers|Awan brothers]], the Pakistani IT administrators it says worked for well over two dozen legislators.[3] The chapter's complaint is that neither brother held a security clearance and neither was properly vetted before being hired, and it links the episode to the later claim that Pakistani IT administrators were caught sending top-secret information to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]].[3]
