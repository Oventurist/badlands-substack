# Debbie Wasserman Schultz

Debbie Wasserman Schultz is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives and a former chair of the Democratic National Committee. She appears in Badlands coverage of the declassified [[clinton-annex|Clinton annex]] as a figure named in intercepted Russian intelligence relating to the 2016 investigation of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server.[1]

## Intercepted communications claim

According to material discussed in the July 2025 disclosures, the U.S. intelligence community intercepted Russian intelligence describing "purported communications between Congresswoman Debbie Wasserman Schultz... and two different individuals who worked for the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]]."[1] In those purported communications, Wasserman Schultz is said to have stated that the [[fbi|FBI]] had no hard evidence against Clinton because "data was removed from the mail servers just in time."[1]

Badlands commentator Jon Herold argued the statement implies Wasserman Schultz believed hard evidence against Clinton had existed, that the bureau lacked it only because it had been removed from the servers, and therefore that she was aware of a cover-up.[1] The same body of intelligence reportedly indicated that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] used Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] to pressure FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] into burying the email scandal.[1]
