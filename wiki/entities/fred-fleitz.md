# Fred Fleitz

Fred Fleitz is an American national security figure who served as chief of staff of the National Security Council during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term and who later became vice chair of American security at the [[america-first-policy-institute|American First Policy Institute]].[1]

Following the sudden death of Sen. [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] in July 2026, Fleitz was quoted describing the close advisory relationship between Trump and Graham, saying that Trump "would call him for advice, and Graham would call him constantly to try to give him his counsel to help him push responsible national security policy."[1] Fleitz added that Trump "learned from what Graham told him, from Graham's trips abroad, from Graham's vast number of international contacts."[1]

The Badlands Brief treated Fleitz's characterization skeptically, with contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reading the description of Graham's constant counsel and foreign contacts as suggesting Graham functioned less as a friendly adviser than as an "asset" positioned close to the president.[1]
