# Konstantin Zhevago

Konstantin Zhevago is a Ukrainian businessman and owner of the iron ore producer Ferrexpo.[1]

## Ranking among Ukrainian oligarchs

As of September 2024, Ukrainian Focus listed Zhevago as the fifth-wealthiest person in Ukraine, behind SCM Group owner [[rinat-akhmetov|Rinat Akhmetov]], Interpipe Group owner [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]], former president [[petro-poroshenko|Petro Poroshenko]] and Dneprazot owner [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]].[1] Most of the individuals on that list have contributed to Kiev's war effort.[1]

## Musk sanctions proposal (March 2025)

Zhevago was among the oligarchs implicated in [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s March 2025 proposal on X that sanctioning Ukraine's top ten oligarchs would immediately end the war with Russia, a suggestion made in response to Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]]'s call to end US financial aid to Kiev.[1]
