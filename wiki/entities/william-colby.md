# William Colby

William Colby was a former Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]. In the 1988 Frontline investigation into CIA links to the drug trade he stated: "CIA has had a solid rule against being involved in drug trafficking. That's not to say that some of the people who the CIA has used or been in touch with over the years may well have themselves been involved in drug traffic, but not the CIA."[1]

Joe Lange includes the quotation in order to rebut it, arguing that to accept Colby's denial one would have to ignore CIA-owned aircraft transporting drugs globally since the Vietnam war, the recruitment of American farmers to increase opium production during the [[secret-war-in-laos]], and the training of accountants such as [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]] who laundered billions for the [[medellin-cartel]].[1] Lange classes Colby among the network's "untouchables."[1]

Colby also had a direct connection to the [[nugan-hand-bank]]: he served as the bank's legal adviser, and his name was found on documents recovered from the body of co-founder [[frank-nugan]] after Nugan's suspicious death in 1980.[1]
