# Chris Murphy

Chris Murphy is a Democratic United States Senator from Connecticut who has been among the most vocal Senate Democrats warning of authoritarian danger from the second Trump administration.[1]

## January 2025 "locked up" remarks

Appearing on MSNBC's "All In" on Friday, January 3, 2025, Murphy claimed that once in office, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] would lock up Democrats and shut down media companies sometime in the spring.[1] He said it was necessary "to raise alarms on what is a really dangerous assault on democracy that has been underway since the election and will continue after the inauguration," adding that "it is just not hyperbole to suggest that we might be looking at Democrats being locked up, media companies being shut down by this new administration sometime in the spring."[1] In the same segment he pivoted to party rebuilding, saying he was "all for rebuilding the Democratic Party" and had "a lot of thoughts on how we can become much more of an economic populist party, reaching out to folks who have been duped by Donald Trump."[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] placed Murphy's remarks alongside Representative [[debbie-dingell|Debbie Dingell]]'s offer of cooperation on trade and the stalled Senate confirmation calendar as evidence of a fractured "uniparty left," summarizing the split as "dare to dream vs. danger to democracy."[1]
