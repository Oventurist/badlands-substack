# Margaret Thatcher

Margaret Thatcher was the British prime minister whose 1986 "Big Bang" deregulation of the [[city-of-london|City of London]] is described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the announcement of a new era of economic irrationalism.[1]

## The Big Bang

In 1986 the City of London launched the [[big-bang-deregulation|Big Bang]], sweeping aside the separation of commercial, deposit and investment banking that had been the post-war cornerstone ensuring that private finance would never again hold more sway than sovereign nation-states.[1] After decades of chipping away at the regulatory structure built by [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]]'s intervention, the Big Bang set a precedent for similar deregulation into the "universal banking" model across the western world.[1]

## Thatcherism and Reaganomics

Ehret pairs Thatcherism with the Reaganomics of [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]], arguing that under both, western nations saw a total destruction of national regulation, protectionism and oversight over private banking and corporate interests, which accelerated the stripping of nations into consumer-society husks while transferring wealth to the wealthiest 0.1%.[1]
