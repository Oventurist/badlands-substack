# Debrina Kawam

Debrina Kawam was a New Jersey woman who died after being set on fire in a stopped New York City subway car on December 22, 2024.[1] Her identity was released by the New York Police Department on Tuesday, December 31, 2024, which the reporting noted served to further debunk false information and political narratives that had spread online about the victim.[1]

## The attack

Police officials said that a man set fire to Kawam while she was sleeping in a stopped subway car, leading to her death.[1] Undocumented immigrant Sebastian Zapeta-Calil was arrested and charged with murder and arson in connection with the killing.[1]

Little was shared publicly about the victim — though multiple reports referred to her as a homeless woman — until the NYPD's December 31 release.[1] New York Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] called Kawam's death a "horrific incident" at a news conference on Tuesday, saying she had spent a "brief stint in our homeless shelter system" and that nothing else was known about her beyond those few details.[1]
