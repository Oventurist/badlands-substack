# Julie Inman Grant

Julie Inman Grant is Australia's eSafety Commissioner, the official who heads the country's online harm regulator.[1]

## Powers

Under Australia's Online Safety Act (2021), the eSafety Commissioner has authority to order the removal of "class 1 material" within Australia, backed by the threat of substantial fines against non-compliant platforms.[1]

## The X takedown case

In April 2024 Inman Grant's office obtained a temporary injunction requiring [[x-corp|X]], the platform owned by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], to remove footage of the Wakeley church stabbing, an alleged religiously motivated terror attack in Sydney. eSafety argued that X had not gone far enough because a geo-block confined to Australia could be circumvented with a VPN; X countered that the regulator was effectively demanding a global takedown, straying outside Australian jurisdiction.[1]

eSafety applied to the Federal Court of Australia to extend the injunction, with a hearing held on Friday 10 May 2024. On 13 May 2024, [[geoffrey-kennett|Justice Geoffrey Kennett]] refused the extension, ruling that "the orders of the court will be that the application to extend … is refused," which left the injunction no longer effective. eSafety said the matter would return to court for a case management hearing on Wednesday, 15 May.[1]

The outcome was reported by the Dystopian Down Under blog and circulated in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s news brief as a win for Musk in an ongoing global censorship fight.[1]
