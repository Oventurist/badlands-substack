# USAspending.gov

**USAspending.gov** is the official spending website of the United States government, publishing federal award records including contract values, awarding agencies, classifications, periods of performance, executive compensation and outlays.[1]

The site served as the primary documentary basis for an October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] investigation into the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order awarded to [[icr-inc|ICR, INC.]].[1] Records there established the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] as awarding agency, the Department of the Air Force as sub-agency and the [[air-force-life-cycle-management-center|Air Force Life Cycle Management Center]] as awarding office, along with the NAICS and Product or Service Code classifications and the parent contract [[cognition-cognos-support|Cognition Cognos Support]].[1]

The same records showed that the project's end date had been extended from October 2023 to April 2024, and that no payment appeared to have been made to the vendor — leading the author to infer that the project may not yet have been delivered, while acknowledging the possibility of reporting variance or delays tied to government shutdown turmoil.[1] The article also relied on a screenshot of the original listing, supplied by a researcher in December 2022, to document the pre-extension delivery date.[1]
