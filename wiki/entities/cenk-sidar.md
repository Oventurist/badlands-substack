# Cenk Sidar

Cenk Sidar is the principal of the consulting firm Sidar Global Advisors and a correspondent of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] whose email exchange with Danchenko was entered into evidence at Danchenko's 2022 trial.[1]

## Trial evidence

On redirect examination of FBI Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]], [[john-durham|John Durham]] introduced an email from Danchenko to Sidar dated July 18, 2016 — three days before Danchenko's July 21, 2016 email to [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]].[1] The exhibit formed part of Durham's effort to establish the documentary record of Danchenko's communications in the summer of 2016 and to rebut the defense theory that contact with Millian could have occurred through channels invisible to phone records.[1]
