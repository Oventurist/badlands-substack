# Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)

The IPCC is the United Nations climate science body which, in Badlands Media's account by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], functions as the technocratic authority to which sovereign states have been required to defer under the [[gaia-cult|Gaia cult]] paradigm.[1]

## Role in the depopulation thesis

Ehret writes that nation states were increasingly expected to act only in conformity with un-elected and often anonymous boards of IPCC "experts" demanding drastic cuts to living standards in order to stop climate change.[1] This deference is presented as the operational mechanism by which the [[united-nations|United Nations]] sustainable development framework — the Millennial Development Goals, [[agenda-21|Agenda 21]] and the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]] — was converted from doctrine into binding pressure on national policy.[1]

## Scientific criticism advanced

The article disputes the panel's core premise, arguing that CO2 is plant food and that the carbon molecule is the driving force behind life on earth.[1] In a footnote Ehret cites [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s repeated position that it has never been proven that human-made CO2 drives climate variations, and points to the post-1998 "pause" in the warming trend dominant since 1977, to the 1938–1977 period in which rising CO2 output accompanied cooling severe enough for scientists in 1977 to warn of an approaching ice age, and to the argument that CO2 tends to follow climate variations rather than precede them.[1] He also cites NASA observations that global biomass has increased by roughly 10% in recent years, attributed in large measure to the industrial growth policies of China and India.[1]
