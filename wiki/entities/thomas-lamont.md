# Thomas Lamont

Thomas Lamont was a Democratic Party kingmaker and [[jp-morgan|JP Morgan]] figure who, in Matthew Ehret's account, played the role in Wall Street's Italian operation that [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] played in its German one.[1]

Two years before the [[young-plan|Young Plan]] began, JP Morgan had already given a $100 million loan to [[benito-mussolini|Mussolini]]'s newly established fascist regime in Italy, with Lamont brokering the relationship.[1]
