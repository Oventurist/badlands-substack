# Maureen Comey

Maureen Comey is a federal prosecutor and the daughter of former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1] She was part of the government team that prosecuted the music mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] on racketeering and sex-trafficking charges.[1]

## Combs verdict and commentary

On July 2, 2025, Combs was acquitted of racketeering and sex trafficking while being convicted on two lesser counts of transportation to engage in prostitution.[1] Much of the press framed the split verdict as a defeat for Comey, but Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued the opposite, writing that many observers had speculated she was "landing the plane" for Combs and his network and that, on that reading, "she did her job perfectly."[1]

That commentary pointed to prosecutors' decision late in the trial to downgrade or abandon certain claims — specifically those relating to attempted kidnapping and attempted arson within the racketeering count — and to drop the theory of aiding and abetting sex trafficking as a predicate act.[1] According to reports cited in the brief, the move was made to "streamline" jury instructions ahead of deliberations, leaving the jury to focus on other aspects of the racketeering conspiracy charge such as sex trafficking and forced labor, of which Combs was acquitted.[1]
