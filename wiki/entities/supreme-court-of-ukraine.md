# Supreme Court of Ukraine

The Supreme Court of Ukraine is the highest judicial body in [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s court system. In May 2023 its chief judge was arrested on allegations of "large-scale corruption," an event reported in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as part of a cluster of same-day developments bearing on Western support for Kyiv.[1]

## May 2023 arrest of the court's head

The arrest was listed alongside reporting that Ukrainian aid funding was expected to run out by mid-summer as the U.S. approached its [[debt-ceiling|debt ceiling]], that leaked plans described a Ukrainian scheme to blow up an oil pipeline vital to [[hungary|Hungary]], and that Ukraine had formally joined the [[nato|NATO]] Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence, inching toward full membership.[1] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] presented the arrest as one of several signals that the war's political and financial underpinnings in the West were eroding even as fighting intensified around [[battle-of-bakhmut|Bakhmut]].[1]
