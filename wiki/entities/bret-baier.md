# Bret Baier

Bret Baier is an American television news anchor at [[fox-news|Fox News]], known for conducting high-profile interviews with domestic and foreign political figures.[1]

## Araghchi interview (January 2026)

In January 2026, Baier interviewed Iranian Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] during a period of sharply escalating rhetoric about a possible US military strike on [[iran|Iran]].[1] In the exchange, Araghchi asserted that operatives masquerading as protesters were shooting people inside Iran in order to provoke American intervention.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the interview while arguing that no war with Iran was forthcoming, noting that Araghchi and President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] are generally regarded as moderates representing a shift away from the zealotry associated with the Ayatollah, and that Pezeshkian had made a similar assertion on video.[1] He treated the interview as evidence that the impending-strike narrative — accompanied by the withdrawal of US personnel from [[al-udeid-air-base|Al Udeid Air Base]] and Iran's brief airspace closure — functioned as a distraction from the finalization of Phase Two of the Gaza plan.[1]

## October 2024 Harris interview

On Wednesday, October 16, 2024, Baier interviewed Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] on [[fox-news|Fox News]] in a widely discussed exchange.[2] ZeroHedge described Harris as alternating between deflection and blame before melting down when shown a clip of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2] When Baier asked whether she would take responsibility for the flood of illegal immigration after she and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] rescinded Trump's immigration executive orders on day one, Harris offered the standard argument that Trump had killed a border bill; Baier pushed back, earning credit even from critics who considered him a NeverTrumper.[2] Fox correspondent Bill Melugin subsequently noted that the Democratic bill would have created a pathway to amnesty for millions of illegal immigrants and had failed despite Democratic control of the White House and Congress.[2]

## Named as a 2024 debate moderator

In his August 2, 2024 announcement of a September 4 debate with [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] named Baier and [[martha-maccallum|Martha MacCallum]] as the moderators for the [[fox-news|Fox News]]-hosted event in Pennsylvania, to be held before a full arena audience under rules similar to his earlier debate with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[3]
