# Bret Baier

Bret Baier is an American news anchor associated with [[fox-news|Fox News]]. He is referenced in the Badlands Brief as the interviewer to whom Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] said, during the Biden administration, that he wanted not only nuclear power but nuclear weapons for [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]. Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited that interview as part of a chronology arguing that the tentative U.S.–Saudi civilian nuclear cooperation agreement permitting uranium enrichment was long foreseeable and unsurprising.
