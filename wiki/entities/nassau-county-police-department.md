# Nassau County Police Department

The Nassau County Police Department is the law enforcement agency serving Nassau County on Long Island, New York.[1]

## 2024 Trump rally bomb report

On September 18, 2024, a department spokesman denied social media claims that explosives had been discovered near the site of a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign rally at the Nassau Veterans Memorial Coliseum in Uniondale, hours before the event was scheduled to begin.[1] Unconfirmed reports had wrongly claimed that an explosive device was found in a vehicle during security checks at the rally site.[1] "Reports of explosives being found at the site are unfounded," the spokesman said, adding that police were questioning a person "who may have been training a bomb detection dog near the site."[1] The story was reported by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]].[1]
