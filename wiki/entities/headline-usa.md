# Headline USA

Headline USA is an American conservative news website that aggregates and reports on national politics, economics and monetary policy.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

Headline USA was cited in an April 2023 Badlands News Brief for its reporting on the introduction of the [[gold-standard-restoration-act|Gold Standard Restoration Act]], H.R. 2435, by Representatives [[alex-mooney|Alex Mooney]], [[andy-biggs|Andy Biggs]] and [[paul-gosar|Paul Gosar]].[1] The outlet framed the bill as a sound-money response to the twin threats of inflation and bank failures, describing it as enabling the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] note "dollar" to regain stable footing for the first time in more than half a century.[1]
