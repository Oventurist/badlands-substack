# Best Buy

Best Buy is a United States consumer electronics retailer whose quarterly results have been treated in Badlands Media coverage as a bellwether for the health of American discretionary spending.[1]

## November 2023 earnings warning

Days before Black Friday 2023, Best Buy reported weaker-than-expected third-quarter revenue and lowered its annual guidance, warning of a deteriorating macro environment in which "consumer demand has been even more uneven and difficult to predict."[1] For the quarter ending October 28, 2023, sales came in at $9.76 billion — a decline of almost 8% year over year and short of analyst expectations of $9.9 billion — while comparable sales, which adjust for store openings and closings, fell a steeper-than-expected 6.9%.[1]

Chief Executive [[corie-barry|Corie Barry]] framed the quarter as "better-than-expected profitability on slightly softer-than-expected revenue," attributing it to "strong operational execution as we navigate through the near-term sales pressure our industry has been experiencing for the past several quarters."[1] She said the company judged it "prudent to lower our annual revenue outlook" based on Q3 trends and early November sales, while noting the midpoint of annual non-GAAP diluted EPS guidance remained slightly above the original midpoint.[1] Barry added that the retailer was "prepared for a customer who is very deal-focused" over the holiday season — a posture [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] tied to the "choiceful" consumer language proliferating on corporate earnings calls.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the report to argue for a "#RedFriday" counter-campaign, noting that Black Friday historically marked the day corporate books turned "from 'in the red' to 'in the black'" and urging readers to spend "ever-evaporating, discretionary dollars" at local Main Street businesses and the Badlands Marketplace rather than large retailers.[1]
