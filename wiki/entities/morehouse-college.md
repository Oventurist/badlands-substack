# Morehouse College

Morehouse College is a historically Black men's college in Atlanta, Georgia, which drew national attention in May 2024 when President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] delivered its commencement address to a conspicuously cool reception.[1]

## 2024 commencement address

Biden spoke to the graduating class on Sunday, May 19, 2024, using the occasion to renew his call for a Gaza cease-fire and telling the audience, "I support peaceful, nonviolent protest. Your voices should be heard. I promise you I hear them."[1] The Wall Street Journal reported that a small number of graduates turned their seats away from the stage during the speech and that many listened with their heads down.[1] Faculty members seated on the stage behind the president displayed a large flag of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] for the duration of the address, a gesture connected to antiwar activists' calls in preceding weeks for international aid to support Congolese citizens amid worsening violence.[1]

Fewer than two dozen of the more than 400 graduating students stood to applaud the speech, though a bloc of alumni rose to whistle and cheer.[1] ZeroHedge, reviewing the numbers, described the applause as dismal and argued that Biden's support among Black voters might be weaker than contemporaneous polling indicated.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Biden "has a real problem with Black voters in November, despite his ongoing gaslighting about their actual suffering under his policies," and called the Morehouse students' rejection of the president "a strong visual" warranted by what she characterized as an overtly racist speech.[1] She pointed to reaction on Black Twitter as evidence of the depth of the problem and noted that the episode was driving renewed circulation of Biden's past remarks about "Corn Pop," "You Ain't Black" and "roaches."[1]
