# Chaim Weizmann

Chaim Weizmann (rendered "Chaim Weizman" in the source) was the Zionist leader who later became Israel's first president.[1]

## The 1914 statement

In 1914, Weizmann stated: "Should Palestine fall within the British sphere of influence, and should Britain encourage a Jewish settlement there, as a British dependency, we could have in 20 to 30 years a million Jews there — perhaps more; they would ... form a very effective guard for the Suez Canal."[1]

The article treats this as evidence of a "deeply underappreciated value that leading Jewish Zionists had for the British empire's plans for global control over a century ago," arguing that these Zionists believed the empire could further their own plans for a Jewish state while the empire in turn valued a garrison population astride the Suez route to India.[1] The passage opens the article's broader thesis that [[zionism]] and British imperial strategy developed as intertwined projects.[1]
