# Georgia Senate Ethics Committee

The Georgia Senate Ethics Committee is a standing committee of the Georgia State Senate. In the Badlands corpus it appears in connection with the state-level effort to compel Georgia's compliance with federal requests for voter registration data following the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

## Resolution 563

On Thursday, January 22, 2026, the committee passed Resolution 563 urging the Georgia Secretary of State, [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]], to fully comply with the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s request for Georgia voter registration information, in order to meet obligations under the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]] (NVRA), the [[help-america-vote-act|Help America Vote Act]] (HAVA), and the [[civil-rights-act-of-1960|Civil Rights Act of 1960]].

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Badlands Media carried the committee's meeting live; [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]] broadcast the session on the same day they recapped the preceding day's eleven-hour [[georgia-state-election-board|State Election Board]] hearing, in which investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] presented evidence of alleged intentional human intervention in the 2020 election. The Resolution 563 vote thus followed directly from the pressure generated at the State Election Board hearing and reinforced the administration's demand for access to Georgia's voter rolls.
