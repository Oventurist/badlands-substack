# Donald Barr

Donald Barr was an American educator and a former officer of the [[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services]] (OSS), the wartime precursor to the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]].[1] He was the father of former Attorney General [[william-barr|Bill Barr]].[1]

Barr served as dean of the [[dalton-school|Dalton School]] in New York City during the period when [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] was hired to teach there without a degree or conventional credentials.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the overlap as one of many instances of Epstein operating in the orbit of intelligence, while declining to speculate on what the connection meant.[1]
