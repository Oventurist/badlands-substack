# Clinton Foundation

The Clinton Foundation, officially the Bill, Hillary & Chelsea Clinton Foundation, is a nonprofit organization founded by former U.S. President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]. In the Badlands Media corpus, it is cited as a vehicle for money laundering connected to covert U.S. weapons sales and the funding of terrorist organizations during the Obama administration.

## Allegations in the Source

The article "A Dereliction of Duty" claims that as Secretary of State, [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was in charge of authorizing all private U.S. weapons sales around the world, and that she took advantage of that power by funneling weapons to U.S. enemies along with money into the Clinton Foundation. The source asserts that the foundation received illicit proceeds from the covert arms-smuggling operation that supplied [[isis|ISIS]] and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] in Libya and Syria.

According to the article, the weapons deals were designed to enrich government officials while using taxpayer money to fund terrorist organizations. The source claims that the Obama administration's "dereliction of duty" in failing to rescue Americans under attack in Benghazi was motivated in part by a desire to protect this lucrative operation from exposure.
