# Carlos de Oliveira

Carlos de Oliveira is the property manager of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Mar-a-Lago club in Florida who was added as a co-conspirator and co-defendant in the superseding indictment filed by the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] in the classified records case on July 27, 2023.[1]

## Role in the superseding indictment

The Justice Department accused Trump of acting together with de Oliveira and Trump's other co-defendant [[walt-nauta|Walt Nauta]] in an attempt to delete surveillance footage at the Mar-a-Lago property.[1] The allegation of evidence destruction formed the centerpiece of the new charges in the [[trump-classified-documents-case|classified documents case]], which the superseding indictment expanded to a total of 40 counts against the former president, including an additional charge under the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]].[1]

De Oliveira's inclusion marked the first time a Mar-a-Lago facilities employee, rather than a personal aide, was named as a defendant in the prosecution.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary on the superseding indictment, published in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 28, 2023, focused less on the individual defendants than on the framing of the prosecution as another installment of "walls are closing in" media rhetoric.[1] Writing for Badlands, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the escalating indictments were being absorbed by the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] as evidence of a broader disclosure campaign rather than as a legal threat.[1]
