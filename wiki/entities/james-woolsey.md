# James Woolsey

James Woolsey is a former Director of Central Intelligence. "The Harvard Control Grid" lists him among the intelligence figures who circulated through [[harvard-university|Harvard]]'s orbit, stating that Woolsey "participated in Harvard-linked symposiums and sat on advisory panels with Ivy League affiliates."[1]

He appears in the article's catalogue of Harvard's historic use of intelligence channels, alongside [[john-mccone|John McCone]]'s reported 1970s advice on Arab investments, [[robert-gates|Robert Gates]]'s seat on the Board of Overseers, and [[ash-carter|Ash Carter]]'s directorship of the [[belfer-center|Belfer Center]].[1]
