# Marc Caputo

**Marc Caputo** is an American political reporter for Axios whose reporting figures in two threads of the March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief.[1]

## Reporting on the Carlson story

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] updated its March 2026 article on [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s claim of an impending [[cia|CIA]] criminal referral to report that "top admin officials" had told Caputo there was "no CIA investigation of Carlson."[1] That denial ran alongside the account that activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] had said she would take credit if Carlson were charged and had been "relentless" in contacting GOP representatives, law enforcement and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] about him.[1]

## Reporting on Cuba negotiations

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Cuban President [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]]'s announcement of open negotiations with the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] — made alongside [[raulito-castro|Raulito Castro]] — all but confirmed Caputo's Axios reporting that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and Castro had been in secret negotiations over Castro potentially taking over as leader of [[cuba|Cuba]].[1]

## Access to Trump

In June 2026 Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] grouped Caputo with [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]] as Axios journalists who maintain a unique relationship with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], routinely conducting direct phone interviews in which Trump reveals exclusive information.[2] That access was cited as a reason to take seriously Axios reporting that Trump had berated Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] over the Lebanon escalation, rather than dismissing it as fake news.[2]

## Commentary on UFC 309

Speaking on CNN in November 2024, Caputo compared [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appearance at [[ufc-309|UFC 309]] to ancient Rome, describing "a conquering, Republican Caesar, who is going into the Colosseum, and everyone's cheering" with "his political gladiators with him."[3] He argued the appearance was not merely about applause but a message to the [[us-senate|Senate]] regarding Trump's nominees: "these are my people, are you willing to fight? Here's who I am."[3]
