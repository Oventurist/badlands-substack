# Marc Caputo

Marc Caputo is an American political reporter for [[axios|Axios]], noted for sourcing inside the Trump administration and for coverage of Florida and Cuban-American politics. The Badlands corpus treats his reporting as an early indicator of administration intentions that later surface publicly.

The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief cites Caputo in two contexts. [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] reported that "top admin officials" told Caputo there was "no CIA investigation of Carlson," contradicting [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s account of an imminent criminal referral. Separately, Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the Cuban government's public confirmation of negotiations with Washington "all but confirms all of Mark Caputo's reporting in Axios" that [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and [[raulito-castro|Raulito Castro]] had been in secret negotiations over Castro's potential accession to the leadership of [[cuba|Cuba]].
