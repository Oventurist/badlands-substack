# Nelson W. Aldrich

**Nelson W. Aldrich** was a United States Senator and, at the time, leader of the Republican Party, who occupies a central place in the Badlands Media account of the origins of the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]].[1]

After the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]], Aldrich set up the [[national-monetary-commission|National Monetary Commission]] to investigate a better economic system for the United States; the Commission concluded that the European model, including the Central Bank of England, was preferable, and its reports became a principal basis for the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act of 1913]].[1]

In 1910, according to the article, leaders of the [[populist-party|Populist Party]] convinced Aldrich to transform the entire monetary system of the United States, and the resulting master plan was hatched at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]].[1] Among the attendees mapped by the article, Aldrich is noted for having a daughter who married a Rockefeller.[1]
