# David Deutsch

**David Deutsch** is cited in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series, together with [[amit-goswami|Amit Goswami]], as a quantum theorist whose interpretation supports the essay's claim that timelines are not fixed.[1]

## Role in the argument

The article states that quantum theory as interpreted by Goswami and Deutsch shows that timelines are possibilities collapsed by awareness.[1] On this basis it argues that a single act of remembrance — a whistleblower's testimony, a declassified file, or a collective realization — has the power to reroute the collective trajectory.[1]

The claim serves as the turn in the essay's fourth-dimensional section, which had described history-writing, [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]], and the standardization of public memory as instruments of generational entrainment.[1] The article predicts that when the JFK, MLK, RFK, and Hunter Biden files are fully exposed, when mRNA injections are exposed, and when [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud]] is proven, the energetic coherence required to maintain a false timeline begins to unravel.[1] It concludes that in the fourth dimension memory is a weapon, while in higher dimensions memory becomes a tool of liberation.[1]
