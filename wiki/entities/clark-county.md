# Clark County, Nevada

Clark County is the most populous county in [[nevada|Nevada]] and the home of Las Vegas.[1] Because of its size and Democratic lean, it has long supplied the vote margin that decides statewide races in Nevada, a cushion known in state political shorthand as the "Clark firewall."[1]

## 2024 early voting

Reported early-voting turnout in 2024 showed Republicans out-polling Democrats in in-person early voting in Clark County — an outcome without recent precedent in a presidential year — as well as in Washoe County.[1] [[jon-ralston|Jon Ralston]] wrote that "the Clark firewall is only 6,500, about a seventh of what it was in 2020."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary held that, on the strength of these figures, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could conceivably carry Clark County itself, an outcome the outlet described as the real headline of the Nevada data, while stressing that turnout by party registration is not the same as vote totals.[1]
