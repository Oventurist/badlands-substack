# Harvard University Muslim Alumni

The Harvard University Muslim Alumni association (HUMA) is an alumni organization at Harvard University. In the Badlands corpus it is treated as the referent of the term "HUMA" in Q drop #111.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] decodes Q drop #111 of November 5, 2017 — which asks "What organization is HUMA? Which US President is affiliated w/ HUMA?" — by identifying HUMA as the Harvard University Muslim Alumni association rather than solely as a reference to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]. The article notes that the drop appeared on the day [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] was arrested in Saudi Arabia and his funds frozen, and links this to the author's earlier claim, made in his article "Bad Actors," that Alwaleed paid for [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s Harvard education in his "pre-political days" — offered as the reason Obama's school records remain sealed.

The article asserts that groups such as HUMA "are a central part of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s 'civilization/stealth jihad' strategy," in which universities serve as recruiting and training grounds for the placement of ideologically committed graduates inside government.
