# Golan Heights

**Golan Heights** is a plateau in southwestern Syria, occupied and unilaterally annexed by [[israel|Israel]] since the 1967 Six-Day War. In the Badlands corpus it functions as the eastern anchor of the 2026 Israeli campaign in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]].

## The 2026 campaign

The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 reports that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] has moved northeast toward [[mount-hermon|Mount Hermon]], capturing the Lebanese lands bordering the Golan Heights and [[syria|Syria]]. The Brief's contributor, GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, notes that this advance follows the pattern he predicted: the occupation of Lebanese territory adjacent to the Golan enlarges the buffer Israel already holds on the Syrian side of the border, and once the IDF fortifies Mount Hermon it will be positioned to capture and annex both southern Lebanon and southern Syria.

## Significance

The Golan is thus presented not as a settled annexation but as a staging ground for further expansion under the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]] — the plateau's existing military infrastructure and its observation lines over Damascus making it the natural launch point for the push toward the Syrian capital. The corpus reads the 2026 ground movements as the realization of a long-held map, with the Golan as proof that Israel's territorial claims are never final.
