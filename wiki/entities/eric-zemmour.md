# Éric Zemmour

Éric Zemmour is a French writer, broadcaster and politician who founded and leads the hard-right party [[reconquete|Reconquête]].[1] A former presidential candidate, he built his political identity on immigration and national-identity themes that overlap substantially with those of the larger [[national-rally|National Rally]].[1]

## 2024 European Parliament elections

For the June 2024 European Parliament contest Zemmour's party fielded [[marion-marechal|Marion Maréchal]] as its lead candidate.[1] Ipsos polling published in March 2024 showed Reconquête attracting just 3 percent of French voters aged 18 to 24, down from 8 percent three months earlier, while [[jordan-bardella|Jordan Bardella]]'s National Rally rose to 31 percent in the same group.[1]
