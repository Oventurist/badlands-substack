# Erik Prince

Erik Prince is the American founder of the private military company Blackwater and a recurring figure in United States covert and quasi-official operations abroad. He features in the January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief in connection with Venezuela.

## Venezuela advocacy, 2026

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reports that Prince appeared on Steve Bannon's programme in mid-January 2026 to argue that the United States must not repeat the mistakes it made in Iraq, and should allow acting Venezuelan President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] to contract directly with any private military contractor needed to shore up security. Ghost links this to the contemporaneous American warnings to Interior Minister [[diosdado-cabello|Diosdado Cabello]] against deploying security forces or militias against the opposition.

## The 2019 Caracas trip

Ghost also recalls that Prince secretly travelled to Caracas in November 2019 to meet Delcy Rodríguez, as reported by The Guardian. Mainstream media speculated at the time that the visit represented an attempt by [[donald-trump|President Trump]] to establish a backchannel with the [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] regime, despite the administration's public support for opposition leader Juan Guaidó.

## Iraq comparison

Within an hour or two of Prince's 2026 television appearance, Trump publicly invoked the same Iraq analogy, saying: "If you ever remember a place called Iraq where every single person was fired—the police, the generals, everybody was fired and they ended up being ISIS… I remember that." Ghost interprets this as Trump disclosing that the [[state-department|State Department]] created ISIS through its use of private military contractors in Iraq, while warning that installing [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] in Venezuela would produce a similar outcome.
