# Scott Pressler

Scott Pressler is a conservative grassroots organizer known for large-scale voter registration work on behalf of the American right.[1]

## Role in the 2024 Trump coalition

In "The Exploitation of Charlie Kirk," Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] placed Pressler among the handful of figures whose efforts were decisive in returning [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to office, writing that Pressler "registered countless voters."[1] DeLarme situated him alongside [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], who spent heavily to free Twitter from establishment control, but argued that neither matched [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], who "reshaped a generation" and whose work with the youth vote would have lasting consequences on elections well into the future.[1] The comparison appeared in DeLarme's argument that Kirk had become a prime target for the enemies of populism.[1]
