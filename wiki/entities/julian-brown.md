# Julian Brown

**Julian Brown** is a 21-year-old American inventor who developed "Plastoline," a process he says converts waste plastic into fuels such as gasoline and diesel.[1] He has approximately 1.8 million followers on Instagram, where he documented his work.[1]

## Disappearance and "under attack" claims

In early July 2025, Brown posted a video stating that he could not "go into too much detail, but there is some very, very odd stuff going on," adding, "I'm certainly under attack right now."[1] In the caption he wrote that "something is happening" and asked followers to "keep me in your prayers please."[1] His most recent post at the time of reporting was dated July 9, after which he went dark online.[1]

Brown's mother, Nia Brown, subsequently told DailyMail.com that her son was safe, saying: "I can confirm Julian is safe but in the best interest of his security I'm not able to provide any more information."[1] The item was reported by *Newsweek* and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, with commentary from [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], who summarized the situation as Brown being okay but in hiding.[1]
