# Peter Comey

Peter Comey is the brother of former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] and, in the account given by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] in the Badlands Media article "The Biggest Boomerang In History," a central figure in the alleged financial entanglement between the Comey family and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].[1]

## Position at DLA Piper

Citing Big League Politics, the article reports that Peter Comey works at the Washington law firm [[dla-piper|DLA Piper]] as "Senior Director of Real Estate Operations for the Americas," and that shortly before the 2016 election a source telephoned the firm's Chinatown offices and confirmed that callers were patched directly through to his line.[1] DLA Piper is described as one of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s top ten all-time career campaign donors and as the firm that prepares the Clinton Foundation's taxes, including the 2015 audit performed after the Clinton Foundation scandal broke — an audit presented publicly as independent.[1]

Lange writes that Peter Comey oversaw that audit of the Clinton Foundation following allegations of corruption and tax evasion and publicly signalled that everything was legal and proper, a conclusion the article says nobody believed.[1] In Lange's framing, while James Comey was investigating Clinton's private email server, his brother had been the person who helped the Clinton Foundation conceal tax evasion and money laundering.[1]

## The mortgage claim and alleged conflict of interest

The article further cites Big League Politics for the assertion that property records show James Comey held the mortgage on Peter Comey's house in Virginia, meaning the FBI director had a direct financial relationship with a DLA Piper executive at the time he was investigating Clinton.[1] Lange presents this as one strand of the [[pay-for-play|pay to play]] web that, in his telling, made the Clinton email investigation a foregone conclusion and reinforced the [[two-tier-justice-system|two tier justice system]].[1]

## Q drop 4782

Lange applies Q drop 4782 of September 28, 2020 — which describes a "simple process" in which the family of a "supporter" is hired by a Clinton Foundation–engaged law firm, the Foundation donates funds to that firm, [[dla-piper|DLA Piper]] pays the family member in salary and bonuses, and the family member transfers value back to the supporter — to the Comey brothers, identifying James Comey as the "supporter."[1] He characterises the arrangement as a convoluted web of money laundering designed to hide pay-to-play schemes.[1] These claims represent Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
