# Bryan Freedman

Bryan Freedman is an American entertainment litigator who served as counsel to broadcaster [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] during Carlson's contract dispute with [[fox-news|Fox News]] in 2023.[1]

## Fox News dispute

After Carlson launched a program on Twitter in June 2023 that drew a combined 169 million views across its first two episodes, Fox sent him a cease-and-desist letter asserting that the show violated his contract, which gave the network exclusive rights to his content through December 31, 2024.[1] Freedman responded publicly on his client's behalf, telling Axios: "Fox defends its very existence on freedom of speech grounds. Now they want to take Tucker Carlson's right to speak freely away from him because he took to social media to share his thoughts on current events."[1]

Freedman and Carlson's wider legal team advanced two additional lines of argument: that Fox had itself committed material breaches of the contract, and that the network had broken a promise not to settle the [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] defamation case in a manner that would imply wrongdoing on Carlson's part.[1] Carlson, whose departure from Fox followed the network's $787 million Dominion settlement, accused senior Fox executives of fraud and of reneging on commitments made to him "intentionally and with reckless disregard for the truth."[1]
