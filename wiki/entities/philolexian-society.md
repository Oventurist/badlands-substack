# Philolexian Society

The **Philolexian Society** is a literary and debating society at [[columbia-university|Columbia University]] named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as one of Columbia's instruments of media control.[1]

The article states that Columbia "embeds media control through the Philolexian Society and its journalism school," which together are said to produce "the gatekeepers of acceptable narrative," tracing their lineage to [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]]-era influence.[1] Within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]], the society sits inside Columbia's designated role as the "syndicate of narrative," a node whose graduates the article claims dominate newsrooms including the [[new-york-times|New York Times]].[1]
