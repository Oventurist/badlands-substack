# Matthew B. Colangelo

**Matthew B. Colangelo** is an American attorney and former senior official at the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] whose December 2022 hiring by Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] became central to allegations of federal coordination in the prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Hiring and controversy

According to Badlands Media coverage, Colangelo was brought into the Manhattan District Attorney's office to advance its investigation of Trump, a role attributed to his prior history with Trump-related cases at both the Justice Department and the New York Attorney General's Office.[1] The reporting further noted that Colangelo had contributed financially to Bragg's campaign.[1]

## FEC complaint

[[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] filed a formal complaint with the [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] against Bragg and Biden's principal campaign committee, Biden for President, alleging violations of the Federal Election Campaign Act, with Colangelo's hiring cited as evidence that the prosecution was coordinated with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in order to influence the 2024 presidential election.[1] Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]], testifying at a congressional hearing, did not deny the existence of communications between the Biden Justice Department and Bragg's office but declined to disclose them.[1]

## Allegations regarding 2020 election investigations

In June 2024 [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] reported that, before transferring from a senior post at [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] to a lower-level position in [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]]'s office, Colangelo had shut down all federal investigations into the 2020 election.[2] The outlet characterised the move as designed to resurrect and ensure that Bragg pressed forward with what it called a garbage case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2] It further asserted that when Trump publicly discussed Colangelo's connection to the Biden Justice Department, presiding judge [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]] imposed what the outlet described as an unconstitutional gag order, and that Trump was subsequently indicted and convicted in a case in which, in the outlet's framing, no crimes were committed.[2] The item ran as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief of June 20, 2024.[2]
