# Environmental Defense Fund

The Environmental Defense Fund is an environmental advocacy organization that condemned the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s 2026 move to rescind the [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]]'s [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]].[1]

## Opposition to the 2026 rescission

Together with [[earthjustice|Earthjustice]], the group described the rescission as a major assault on federal climate policy, warning of increased pollution, thousands of premature deaths, higher health and fuel costs, and accelerated climate impacts, and pledged immediate litigation grounded in settled science and Supreme Court precedent upholding the finding.[1]
