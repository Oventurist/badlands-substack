# Environmental Defense Fund

The Environmental Defense Fund is a United States environmental advocacy organisation active in climate and air-quality policy.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief identifies the Environmental Defense Fund, together with [[earthjustice|Earthjustice]], as among the groups condemning the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's rescission of the [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]]'s 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]]. The organisations described the rescission as a major assault on federal climate policy, warned that it would produce increased pollution, thousands of premature deaths, and higher health and fuel costs alongside accelerated climate impacts, and pledged immediate litigation resting on settled science and on Supreme Court precedent, notably [[massachusetts-v-epa|Massachusetts v. EPA]].
