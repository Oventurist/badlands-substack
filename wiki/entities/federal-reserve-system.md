# Federal Reserve System

The **Federal Reserve System** is the central banking system of the United States, created by the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] signed into law on December 23, 1913.[1]

## Badlands Media framing

Badlands Media characterizes the Federal Reserve as the culmination of a long campaign by a financial "cabal" to replace the Founding Fathers' hard-money system of tradable coins with a paper and, later, fiat currency regime.[1] In this account the Fed is described as a privately owned bank that became the most powerful organization in the United States, enabling permanent devaluation of the dollar and removing the citizen's ability to demand redemption in gold.[1]

The article notes that the "cabal" added insult to injury by placing [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]] — the president who destroyed the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] and the only president to fully pay off the national debt — on the twenty dollar bill issued by the system he opposed.[1]

## Relationship to the gold standard

The article situates the Fed's creation as the endpoint of banker hostility to the [[gold-standard|gold standard]], which ran from 1873 to 1933 and which bankers sought to abandon because redeemable gold constrained [[fractional-reserve-lending|fractional reserve lending]].[1] It states that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nominated [[judy-shelton|Judy Shelton]] to the Fed because she advocated restoring the gold standard.[1]
