# Trey Reed

Trey Reed was a 21-year-old Black student at [[delta-state-university|Delta State University]] in Cleveland, Mississippi, whose death in September 2025 drew national attention.[1]

## Death and autopsy

Reed's body was discovered hanging from a tree on the Delta State campus early on Monday, 15 September 2025.[1] The death immediately ignited strong emotions in a state with a history of racist violence.[1] On Thursday, 18 September 2025, the Cleveland police department announced that an autopsy by Mississippi's state medical examiner had concluded he died by suicide, a finding consistent with the Bolivar County coroner's preliminary examination, which had identified no evidence of foul play.[1]

Before the autopsy findings were released, Reed's family and their lawyers had called for an independent autopsy and implored police to show them security camera recordings and other video evidence.[1] Civil rights attorney [[ben-crump|Ben Crump]] said he would lead an independent investigation.[1]

## Media narrative

Badlands contributor Ashe in America observed that Reed and Cory Zukatis, a white homeless man, were found hanging in different Mississippi locations on the same day, and that the internet initially reported the deaths as the lynching of two Black people — an illustration of how early details of a story are often wrong.[1] The commentary noted that Black Lives Matter protesters marched in New York City "demanding justice for the hangings," while arguing that video of the protest suggested other motivations.[1]
