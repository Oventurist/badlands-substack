# Jeffrey Sachs

Jeffrey Sachs is an American economist known for his role in advising post-communist governments on rapid market transition. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, he is identified with the [[imf|International Monetary Fund]] and named as a Western participant in the liberalization of the former Soviet bloc.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the Western side of Perestroika — which he identifies with "Operation 'Hammer'," named for Occidental Petroleum's Armand Hammer — "involved the IMF's Jeffrey Sachs, Vice President [[al-gore|Al Gore]], Rhodes Scholar [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbot]] and Oxford trained investment banker at Goldman Sachs… [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]."

The article situates this work alongside that of the monetarist "reformers" [[anatoly-chubais|Anatoly Chubais]] and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] under [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]], and argues that the same privatization and liberalization reforms were "applied viciously to Ukraine and other Warsaw pact countries during the same period," producing the oligarchic class the essay goes on to catalogue.
