# Jeffrey Sachs

**Jeffrey Sachs** is an American economist and academic who has become a prominent critic of United States foreign policy in the Middle East.[1]

## Cambridge Union remarks on Netanyahu

In January 2025, President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] shared to Truth Social a video clip of Sachs speaking at a Cambridge Union event in which Sachs called Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] a "deep, dark son of a bitch."[1] In the clip, Sachs accused Netanyahu of manipulating US foreign policy and orchestrating "endless wars" in the Middle East, claiming that Netanyahu had pursued a systematic strategy since 1995 to eliminate Hamas and Hezbollah by targeting their supporting governments in Iraq, Iran and Syria.[1]

Sachs argued that "[Netanyahu's] gotten us into endless wars and because of the power of all of this in US politics, he's gotten his way," a reference to the influence of pro-Israel lobbying groups.[1] Trump's aim in promoting the video was not immediately clear, and the post came just weeks after Netanyahu had described a "very friendly, warm" discussion with Trump about hostage negotiations and Syria policy.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that almost everything Sachs said would sound familiar to the Badlands audience, and connected the episode to reports that Netanyahu would not attend Trump's second inauguration despite having been invited and having attended the 2017 ceremony.[1]

## Academic and institutional positions

Sachs is a professor at [[columbia-university|Columbia University]] and has long been embedded in the elite policy milieu.[2] He directs Columbia's Center for Sustainable Development and serves as president of the UN's Sustainable Development Solutions Network, and is an advocate of the [[sustainable-development-goals|Sustainable Development Goals]], which Badlands characterizes as the UN's trojan horse for global control.[2]

Despite that background, Sachs is described as one of the most articulate voices speaking out about Ukraine, able to address the U.S.-sponsored regime change operation Euromaidan because he watched it unfold from a privileged position.[2] His testimony is presented as among the strongest modern evidence of the State Department/university interplay in the deep state's brand of "democracy promotion" abroad, and as an example of an insider now challenging the system from within.[2]
