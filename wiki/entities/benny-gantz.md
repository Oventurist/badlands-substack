# Benny Gantz

Benny Gantz is a retired general of the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] and an Israeli politician. A former IDF chief of staff, he later entered electoral politics and has served in senior government roles, at times as a rival to and at times as a partner of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].

## Commentary on a ground invasion of Iran

The March 3, 2026 Badlands Brief cites Gantz as confirming the possibility that a ground invasion of [[iran|Iran]] could be undertaken by either [[israel|Israel]] or the United States during the ongoing air campaign. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the remark as supporting his theory that Netanyahu's earlier interest in controlling Syria was aimed at establishing "[[davids-corridor|David's Corridor]]," a land bridge from Israel to the Euphrates River that would place Israeli forces adjacent to Kurdistan and enable the movement of soldiers and equipment toward Iran with the assistance of the [[kurds|Kurds]].

The Brief pairs this with the observation that a ground invasion is "the one thing that [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]] said we should never do," and with reporting that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had begun asking the Kurds to assist in the war effort.
