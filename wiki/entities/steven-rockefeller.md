# Steven Rockefeller

Steven Rockefeller is a member of the Rockefeller family described in Badlands Media commentary as a "transhumanist billionaire" who chaired the International Earth Charter drafting committee.[1]

## The Earth Charter

The [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]] was co-authored between 1996 and 2000 by former Soviet Premier [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] and Canadian [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], formalizing the doctrine set out at the 1992 [[rio-earth-summit|Rio Earth Summit]] and in [[agenda-2030|Agenda 21, later renamed Agenda 2030]].[1] Rockefeller's chairmanship of the drafting committee places the Rockefeller network at the institutional center of the document, which in this account represents the consolidation of NGOs and corporations under a "green" agenda directed at limiting industrial development.[1]

## Interpretation

Within the broader argument of the source article, Rockefeller's role is offered as evidence that the western "sustainability" paradigm is an oligarchical project rather than a grassroots environmental one — an inheritance of the same [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] scarcity logic articulated by [[james-schlesinger|James Schlesinger]] and enforced through the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
