# Yitzhak Goldknopf

Yitzhak Goldknopf is an Israeli Hasidic politician and chairman of [[united-torah-judaism|United Torah Judaism]], the ultra-Orthodox political party representing Ashkenazi Haredi voters in the [[knesset|Knesset]]. He has been among the most prominent political defenders of the Haredi community's exemption from mandatory Israeli military service.[1]

## Conscription crisis remarks

On Thursday, July 24, 2025, Goldknopf declared that Israel's ultra-Orthodox Jews may be forced to leave the country en masse if authorities begin arresting yeshiva students who refuse to report for mandatory military service. The statement followed the detention of several draft dodgers earlier that week.[1]

In an interview with the national religious newspaper Makor Rishon, Goldknopf insisted that "if there is no choice and the ultra-Orthodox have to leave the country, then they will leave the country."[1]

He continued: "There will be a state without ultra-Orthodox. The ultra-Orthodox will not change their way of life because there is an army and a war. There is an army, and there are also those who will go to the army. If you say you are short 50,000 soldiers, take these draft dodgers… There are 100,000 draft dodgers in Tel Aviv and Gush Dan," referring to Israel's densely populated coastal region.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary framed Goldknopf's threat as a significant structural danger to the Israeli state. In that reading, the Hasidic community has long served as the demographic and ideological foundation of the Zionist project and as the connection between Israel and Ukraine, from where both ultra-Orthodox Judaism and Zionism are said to originate. A mass Haredi emigration, the commentary argued, would diminish the culture of Jewish supremacy, worsen an already failing economy, and demoralize a country that had never previously fought a war lasting more than a few weeks — producing dissolution by attrition rather than by military destruction.[1] See [[haredi-conscription-exemption-law]].
