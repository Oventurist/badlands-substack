# Beau Biden

Beau Biden was the son of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. He died of brain cancer in 2015, on May 30.[1] His death became a focal point of the controversy surrounding the special counsel report by [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]] into the president's handling of classified documents.[1]

## Role in the Hur report controversy

The [[hur-report|Hur report]] stated that Biden "did not remember, even within several years, when his son Beau died."[1] At an impromptu White House press conference, Biden lashed out at Hur over the line, saying, "How in the hell dare he raise that?" and adding, "Frankly, when I was asked the question, I thought to myself, it wasn't any of their damn business."[1]

NBC News subsequently reported, citing two people familiar with Hur's five-hour interview with the president conducted over two days in October 2023, that Hur never asked when Beau died; it was Biden himself who first introduced his son's death.[1] According to those sources, Biden raised the subject while being asked about his workflow at a Virginia rental home he used between 2016 and 2018, a period when a ghost writer was helping him write a memoir about losing Beau to brain cancer.[1] Investigators had a 2017 recording in which Biden told the ghost writer he had found "classified stuff" in that home.[1] The sources said Biden appeared confused about the year of his son's death while trying to reconstruct that period, though he correctly recalled the date of May 30.[1]
