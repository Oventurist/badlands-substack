# Rich McCormick

Rich McCormick is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Georgia who authored the successful November 2023 resolution censuring Michigan Democrat [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] over her comments about Israel and the Palestinians.[1]

## Censure resolution against Rashida Tlaib

McCormick introduced the GOP-led censure measure that the House adopted on November 7, 2023, by a vote of 234 to 188, with four Republicans opposing and 22 Democrats joining in support.[1] The resolution advanced earlier the same day after a Democratic-led effort to block it failed.[1] The censure was a rare and significant rebuke of Tlaib, the first Palestinian-American woman to serve in Congress, and it came amid Israel's war against [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

Tlaib defended herself by arguing that the censure attempts were designed to silence her and that colleagues had "resorted to distorting my positions in resolutions filled with obvious lies."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America described the McCormick resolution as "another day, another meaningless virtue signal that doesn't move the needle," observing that Tlaib retained her committee assignments and would likely see her support grow among sympathizers.[1] The commentary held that spotlighting and contextualizing Tlaib's rhetoric would have been more effective than censuring her, and judged the episode "a fail for House Republicans."[1]
