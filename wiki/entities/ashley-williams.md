# Ashley Williams

Ashley Williams was a senior adviser in the [[white-house|White House]] during the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] In July 2024 she was named in a set of subpoenas issued by the Republican-led [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] seeking depositions from senior aides about the president's mental state.[1]

## July 2024 subpoena

Alongside Williams, the committee subpoenaed first lady Jill Biden's top aide Anthony Bernal and deputy chief of staff Annie Tomasini.[1] Committee chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] said the White House had shielded the trio from testifying about Biden's mishandling of classified documents and that the same aides were reportedly "seeking to cover up President Biden's declining cognitive state inside the White House."[1] Comer concluded that Biden was "clearly unfit for office."[1]

The demands came two weeks into the political fallout from Biden's June 27, 2024 debate against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], which set off the first broad wave of calls for the president to abandon his re-election campaign.[1]
