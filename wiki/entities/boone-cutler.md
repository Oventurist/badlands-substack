# Boone Cutler

Boone Cutler was an American author, combat veteran, and advocate who died in September 2025 at the age of 45.[1]

## Death and remembrance

News of Cutler's death was published on 18 September 2025 in a statement issued by "Team BC" and circulated through his social media account.[1] The statement was framed in Norse warrior imagery, declaring that "the Valkyrje carried a great Viking Warrior from the battlefield to his place in Valhalla," and describing him as a "proud, loyal, honest and humble warrior who fulfilled his destiny with reverence and honor."[1] It closed with an invocation of the Valhalla prayer, ending on the injunction "nor shall we mourn but rejoice for those that have died the glorious death."[1]

Badlands host Zak Paine offered a personal remembrance, describing Cutler as "a true fighter and one of the good ones" who was "smart, cunning, incredibly gracious and not only a wealth of knowledge but also decency."[1]

## The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare

Cutler co-authored *The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare* with [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]], a text used within Badlands Media as the standard reference for analyzing information operations.[2] The book defines hybrid warfare as "the combination of cyberwarfare in social media being influenced by bots sharing contrived news stories that focus on reporting selective information," which creates emotionality that polarizes an audience while the opposition is censored and a smaller supportive audience is promoted.[2] It also describes shadowy governments that use "an unseen hand to manipulate political, societal, and economic change directly through the masses" by suppressing unwanted messengers and amplifying favorable ones, a point illustrated by its Diagram 2-11, "It's a Big Club and You Ain't In It."[2]

In March 2024, contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] applied these definitions to the suppression of [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]], arguing that the censoring and fact-checking of posts about the story by outlets such as [[politifact|PolitiFact]] matched the book's account of hybrid warfare exactly.[2]
