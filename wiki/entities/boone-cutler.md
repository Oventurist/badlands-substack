# Boone Cutler

Boone Cutler was an American author, combat veteran, and advocate who died in September 2025 at the age of 45.[1]

## Death and remembrance

News of Cutler's death was published on 18 September 2025 in a statement issued by "Team BC" and circulated through his social media account.[1] The statement was framed in Norse warrior imagery, declaring that "the Valkyrje carried a great Viking Warrior from the battlefield to his place in Valhalla," and describing him as a "proud, loyal, honest and humble warrior who fulfilled his destiny with reverence and honor."[1] It closed with an invocation of the Valhalla prayer, ending on the injunction "nor shall we mourn but rejoice for those that have died the glorious death."[1]

Badlands host Zak Paine offered a personal remembrance, describing Cutler as "a true fighter and one of the good ones" who was "smart, cunning, incredibly gracious and not only a wealth of knowledge but also decency."[1]
