# Metropolitan Transportation Authority

The Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA) is the public transit agency serving New York City and its surrounding region, and the operator of New York City's congestion pricing program.[1]

## Congestion pricing standoff (2025)

In February 2025 the MTA became the center of a confrontation between New York State and the federal government over congestion pricing.[1] Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]] told a laughing MTA Board on Wednesday, February 26, 2025 that she had used "big words" and "nice colors" in a presentation delivered to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during a White House visit the previous week in an effort to persuade him to drop his opposition to the plan.[1] The appeal did not work.[1]

The federal [[us-department-of-transportation|Department of Transportation]] added pressure with a letter sent that Wednesday to top New York transportation officials informing them that termination of the plan would be effective on March 21.[1] The MTA signalled it was unlikely to comply: "We're not done. We are in court. We have said that you may have asked for 'orderly cessation' — that was the phrase that came in the letter to us, 'orderly cessation.' I will propose something in the alternative: 'orderly resistance.' We will keep standing up for New Yorkers," Hochul said.[1]
