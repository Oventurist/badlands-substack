# The Citadel

**The Citadel** is a South Carolina military college, described in the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series as a naval military school, which drew attention in December 2024 when [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posed for a photo at the Citadel booth after ringing the opening bell at the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]].[1]

## The NYSE photo op

According to the article, Trump rang the NYSE opening bell on December 12 and then went to the Citadel booth for a photo opportunity.[1] The author treats this stop as deliberate, noting that The Citadel is a naval military school and linking it to the broader military-signalling theme running through the chapter.[1] A badge worn by one of the figures in the photograph is highlighted because its digits sum to seventeen — "2+5+8+2 = 17" — the number associated with Q.[1]

## The red castle symbolism

The article connects The Citadel to Q Drop #2582, which the author reproduces and describes as depicting a "Red Castle."[1] The claim advanced is that this red castle image closely resembles The Citadel's own logo, a find and graphic the author credits to NewsTreason Dave.[1] In Q research the "red castle" motif is commonly associated with the Army Corps of Engineers, but the Badlands treatment adds The Citadel as a further candidate reading, consistent with the series' acceptance that "double meanings" exist.[1]

## Placement in the chapter's argument

The Citadel reference sits inside a longer sequence in which the author reads the Army/Navy game weekend, [[palantir|Palantir]]'s advertising, [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s cemetery visit, and the Trump NYSE appearance as a coordinated set of military "comms."[1] The institution's naval character matters to the argument because the chapter repeatedly foregrounds the Navy — including the Navy's skull-and-crossbones uniforms and its 31–13 victory in the game, which the author sums to 44 and links to the forty-fourth president.[1]
