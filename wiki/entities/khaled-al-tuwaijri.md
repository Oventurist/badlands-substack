# Khaled Al Tuwaijri

**Khaled Al Tuwaijri** was the head of the Saudi Royal Court, and was among the figures detained in the November 2017 anti-corruption sweep in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] that also swept up [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], National Guard head Muteib Bin Abdullah, Riyadh mayor Turki Bin Abdullah, MBC head Waleed Al Ibrahim, former finance minister Ibrahim Al Assaf and a member of the Bin Laden family.[1]

Badlands links Tuwaijri directly to the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]]: a March 2016 filing under the Foreign Agents Registration Act showed [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]] registered as an active foreign agent of the Saudi government working with the "Center for Studies and Media Affairs at the Saudi Royal Court," with the firm reportedly receiving $140,000 per month from the Saudi government.[1] Because Tuwaijri ran the Royal Court, the article identifies him as the official behind those payments.[1] His arrest on the same day as Alwaleed is presented by the author as part of the chain of events that preceded the Podesta Group's abrupt closure.[1]
