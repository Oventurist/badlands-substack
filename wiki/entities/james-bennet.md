# James Bennet

James Bennet is a former editorial page editor of [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] who departed the paper in the aftermath of the 2020 controversy over the publication of Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]]'s op-ed calling for the possible use of the National Guard to quell violent rioting around the White House.[1]

## Criticism of the Times

Bennet subsequently joined a line of former Times editors and writers — including [[bari-weiss|Bari Weiss]] and his former colleague [[adam-rubenstein|Adam Rubenstein]] — who publicly lashed out against the paper over its handling of the Cotton episode and its broader treatment of conservative viewpoints.[1] Legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] grouped these accounts together as describing a common work environment in which staff rejected conservative arguments outright and imposed added scrutiny on those who published them.[1]
