# Christine Maxwell

Christine Maxwell is a daughter of the publishing magnate [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] and, with her twin [[isabel-maxwell|Isabel]], one of the seven surviving Maxwell siblings.[1] Badlands Media argues that despite receiving minimal media attention compared with their sister [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], the twins occupied senior positions at the Israeli intelligence front company [[inslaw|Inslaw]] and are the true inheritors of their father's espionage legacy.[1]

## Inslaw and PROMIS

Christine and Isabel's roles at Inslaw are described as having facilitated their father's most heinous act of espionage on behalf of Israel — the sale of the compromised [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to the U.S. national laboratories that house the core of the country's nuclear weapons system.[1]

## Post-PROMIS ventures

After the multimillion-dollar sale of the venture to a competitor, the twins became associated with successors to the PROMIS software scandal.[1] Ghislaine Maxwell became involved in these affairs as well, as did [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] following his first arrest; the group courted some of the biggest names in the U.S. technology scene, a period that dovetailed with Epstein's investments in Israeli intelligence-linked tech firms such as [[carbyne|Carbyne]] and his claims of holding troves of blackmail on prominent tech CEOs.[1] The article concludes that Christine and Isabel, rather than their more notorious siblings, were the ones who genuinely carried their father's work forward by cashing in on the revolutionary new technology of the Internet.[1]
