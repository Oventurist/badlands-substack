# Stephen Feinberg

Stephen Feinberg is the U.S. Deputy Secretary of Defense in the second Trump administration.[1]

## 2025 Pentagon leak investigation

Feinberg's office was drawn into a wide-ranging [[pentagon|Pentagon]] leak investigation in April 2025 when his chief of staff, [[colin-carroll|Colin Carroll]], was placed on administrative leave on Wednesday, April 16, 2025 — the third top official suspended in the probe and the first to come a day after two other political appointees were sidelined.[1] The leaks under investigation reportedly involved military operational plans for the Panama Canal, a second carrier headed to the Red Sea, Elon Musk's visit to the Pentagon to discuss China, and a pause in intelligence collection for Ukraine.[1]
