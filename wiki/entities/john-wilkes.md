# John Wilkes

John Wilkes was a member of the [[hellfire-club|Hell-Fire Club]] and of the Royal Society, described in the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin" as the grandfather of Abraham Lincoln's assassin.[1]

Wilkes left one of the few first-hand descriptions of the rites conducted at [[medmenham-abbey|Medmenham Abbey]], stating that "No profane eye dared to penetrate the English Eleusinian Mysteries of the Chapter Room where the Monks assembled on solemn occasions […] secret rites performed and libations to the Bona Dea."[1] The article glosses Bona Dea as a name for the earth mother Magna Mater, known variously as Demeter, Aphrodite, Venus and Cybele.[1]
