# Alex Krainer

Alex Krainer is a market analyst and commentator associated with Zero Hedge who writes on finance, banking dynasties and geopolitics.[1]

## Appearances on Breaking History

Krainer has appeared as a guest on Breaking History, the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program co-hosted by [[matt-ehret|Matt Ehret]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[1] In discussing whether Italy plays a central role in the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited Krainer among guests who had offered what he called compelling evidence that certain banking dynasties — naming Banca Intesa — were working to defeat what he described as a satanic scourge seeking to destroy Christianity and enslave humanity.[1] The remark was made in the context of Italian Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]]'s opposition to a European troop deployment to Ukraine.[1]
