# David Clements

David Clements is a professor and election-integrity activist whose public remarks became a factor in the defamation case brought by [[eric-coomer]] against [[mike-lindell]] and [[frankspeech]].[1]

## Role in the Coomer defamation verdict

Of the three statements the Denver jury found defamatory in June 2025, two were made directly by Lindell and the third was made by someone other than Lindell at an event broadcast on FrankSpeech.[1] Badlands host [[ashe-in-america]], who covered the trial, identified that third statement as remarks made by Professor David Clements at the Cyber Symposium.[1] Because the statement was carried on Lindell's platform rather than uttered by Lindell himself, it contributed to the $1.9 million share of damages assessed against FrankSpeech, as distinct from the $440,500 assessed directly against Lindell.[1]

Ashe in America had predicted before the verdict that the platform distinction might not have been made strongly enough to the jury, and that the most damning statements had been made by other people — naming Clements-type third-party speakers such as Oltmann and Peters — on FrankSpeech.[1]
