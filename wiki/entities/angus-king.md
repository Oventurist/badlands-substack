# Angus King

Angus King is an independent United States Senator from Maine who caucuses with Democrats and who, during the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]], was among a small group of senators repeatedly breaking with his caucus to vote with Republicans on reopening the government.[1]

## Role in the 2025 shutdown votes

In the [[united-states-senate|Senate]] on Thursday, October 23, 2025, Democrats blocked the Shutdown Fairness Act of 2025, a Republican-sponsored bill from Sen. [[ron-johnson|Ron Johnson]] of Wisconsin that would have paid active-duty military members and other essential federal employees required to work during the shutdown; the mostly party-line vote was 54-45, short of the 60 needed to advance.[1]

King, along with Sens. [[john-fetterman|John Fetterman]] of Pennsylvania and [[catherine-cortez-masto|Catherine Cortez Masto]] of Nevada, had been voting with Republicans to reopen the government.[1] Separately, Sens. [[jon-ossoff|Jon Ossoff]] and [[raphael-warnock|Raphael Warnock]] of Georgia crossed party lines to support the Shutdown Fairness Act itself while continuing to vote against the stopgap funding bill.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded to the dueling blocked bills by proposing a popular vote on the shutdown, favoring funding the military, eliminating most federal government jobs, and requiring [[congress|Congress]] to work for minimum wage.[1]
