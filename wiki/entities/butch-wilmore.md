# Butch Wilmore

Butch Wilmore is a [[nasa|NASA]] astronaut who, together with [[sunita-williams|Sunita Williams]], saw an eight-day stopover aboard the International Space Station extend into a nine-and-a-half-month mission.[1]

## Return to Earth

Wilmore returned to Earth on Tuesday, March 18, 2025, aboard the [[spacex|SpaceX]] Dragon capsule *Freedom*, which splashed down off the coast of Tallahassee, Florida, at 5:57 pm EDT.[1] Also aboard were NASA astronaut Nick Hague and Roscosmos cosmonaut Aleksandr Gorbunov.[1] The crew splashed down into 78 degree Fahrenheit weather, with dolphins circling the capsule while recovery teams worked, before the capsule was brought onto a recovery ship and opened.[1]

The prolonged mission and its conclusion drew heavy public attention, which Badlands commentators contrasted with the comparatively muted public interest in election integrity questions.[1]

## Arrival of replacement crew (March 2025)

A [[spacex|SpaceX]] crew capsule carrying four replacement astronauts from the United States, Japan and Russia reached the [[international-space-station|International Space Station]] on Sunday, March 16, 2025, ending the wait for Wilmore and [[sunita-williams|Suni Williams]] after a mission that began the previous June and passed the nine-month mark earlier that month.[2] The incoming crew was to be briefed on station operations by the two before they departed later in the week aboard a SpaceX capsule that had been docked since the prior year.[2] Wilmore and Williams had expected a roughly week-long flight when they launched on [[boeing|Boeing]]'s first crewed Starliner test, but the capsule's problems led [[nasa|NASA]] to bring it home empty.[2]
