# Young Turks

The **Young Turks** are described in Badlands Media commentary as a freemasonic agency tied to Giuseppe Mazzini's P1 lodge, used to put the Balkan Wars into motion in what the article calls Russia's soft underbelly.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] lists this operation alongside the destruction of the Russian army by the Imperial Japanese army in 1904 and the 1905 revolutionary attempt led by [[leon-trotsky|Trotsky]] and [[vladimir-lenin|Lenin]] as the preparatory moves that allowed the pieces to fall into place for a final war of global annihilation, formalized through the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] and the [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]].[1] The [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]], then allied to Germany, was subsequently dissolved and its territories divided under the [[sykes-picot-agreement|Sykes-Picot]] arrangement.[1]
