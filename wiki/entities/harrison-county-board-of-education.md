# Harrison County Board of Education

The Harrison County Board of Education is the school governing body in Harrison County, West Virginia, which oversees Lincoln Middle School.[1] In April 2024 the board became the defendant in a lawsuit brought by West Virginia Attorney General [[patrick-morrisey|Patrick Morrisey]] after five Lincoln Middle School girls were barred from future track meets.[1]

The five students had protested at an April 18, 2024 shot put competition by stepping up to the circle and then refusing to throw, objecting to the participation of transgender athlete [[becky-pepper-jackson|Becky Pepper-Jackson]].[1] Following the protest the board blocked them from upcoming meets, prompting Morrisey to sue on the students' behalf and to ask the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] to weigh in on transgender student-athlete bans for a second time.[1]

Badlands commentary criticized the board's action, arguing that it was treated as unacceptable to exclude biological males from female sports but acceptable to exclude female athletes for peacefully protesting, and characterizing the decision as the district's embrace of a political fad at the expense of the girls involved.[1]
