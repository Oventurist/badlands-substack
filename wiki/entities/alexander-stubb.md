# Alexander Stubb

Alexander Stubb is the President of Finland, a European head of state who intervened publicly in the diplomatic rupture between the United States and Ukraine in early March 2025.[1]

## Sauna diplomacy proposal

Following the collapse of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s February 28, 2025 White House visit — during which the Ukrainian leader was asked to leave after a heated exchange with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Vice President [[jd-vance|J.D. Vance]] — Stubb suggested that the two leaders repair their relationship by going to a sauna together, likening the dispute to a quarrel between spouses.[1] Speaking to Bloomberg on Monday, March 3, 2025, Stubb said "it always takes two to tango" and observed that "sometimes in marriage or in other types of relationships, you go into your corner and sulk," adding that eventually both sides have to talk, shake hands and move on.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry treated the suggestion as emblematic of European irrelevance, arguing that with the risk of a wider war at stake, the Finnish president's remedy of two men sitting naked in an overheated room to work on their "marriage" illustrated why Europe is not taken seriously and could not confront Russia without American backing.[1]
