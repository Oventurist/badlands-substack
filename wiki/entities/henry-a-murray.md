# Henry A. Murray

Henry A. Murray was a Harvard psychologist and chairman of the Department of Social Relations at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] who led the 1959–1962 experiments on twenty-two undergraduates that included the young [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]].[1]

## The Harvard experiments

Writing in a June 2000 issue of *[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]]*, Alston Chase reported that "from the fall of 1959 through the spring of 1962, Harvard psychologists, led by Henry A. Murray, conducted a disturbing and what would now be seen as ethically indefensible experiment on twenty-two undergraduates."[1] Subjects were referred to by code name only; the student dubbed "Lawful" was Kaczynski, who would later mail or deliver sixteen package bombs over seventeen years, killing three people and injuring twenty-three.[1] The subjects were selected for their high intelligence.[1]

## World government and human reprogramming

Chase also noted that Murray was a fanatical devotee of world government and used "science" to attempt to transform the nature of humankind, working for the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], and the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]].[1] Writing to his fellow globalist Lewis Mumford, Murray stated that "the kind of behavior that is required by the present threat involves transformations of personality such as never occurred quickly in human history; one transformation being that of National Man into World Man."[1]

Badlands Media argues that the *Atlantic* review omitted a crucial fact: Murray was a close collaborator of [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] contractors [[margaret-mead|Margaret Mead]] and [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]], who drove their human-reprogramming project through the use of LSD and psilocybin.[1]

## Sponsorship of Leary

By the late 1950s Murray had become interested in hallucinogens, and in 1960 [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] returned to Harvard and, with Murray's blessing, began his psilocybin experiments — the [[harvard-psilocybin-project|Harvard Psilocybin Project]].[1] Leary later described Murray as "the wizard of personality assessment" who, as OSS chief psychologist, had monitored military experiments on brainwashing and sodium amytal interrogation, and who offered support to Leary's drug research.[1]
