# Mohammed bin Zayed

Mohammed bin Zayed is the President of the United Arab Emirates.[1]

## Role in the Qatar embargo

Badlands Media identifies bin Zayed, alongside Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and with the backing of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], as a principal architect of the 2017 embargo against [[qatar|Qatar]], joined by Jordan, Egypt, Yemen, Bahrain, and a number of African nations.[1] Under the embargo, ports in Saudi Arabia and the UAE were ordered to refuse Qatari ships, and Qatar's land border was closed, "effectively making it an island."[1] The blockade remained in place for several years until Trump negotiated a reconciliation on January 4, 2021, two weeks before leaving office.[1]
