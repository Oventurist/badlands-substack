# Ivanna Klympush-Tsintsadze

Ivanna Klympush-Tsintsadze is a senior Ukrainian member of parliament who chairs the committee on Ukraine's integration with the European Union.[1]

## Reaction to the McCarthy ouster (October 2023)

In early October 2023, as the removal of U.S. House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] threw American support for Kyiv into doubt, Klympush-Tsintsadze became one of the most widely quoted Ukrainian voices on the crisis.[1] "We are freaking out. For us it is a disaster," she told Politico, adding that Ukrainians were "interested in getting things sorted out so American democracy can function, and so we can restore the bipartisan consensus on supporting their own national interest by supporting Ukraine."[1]

Her comments came days after U.S. lawmakers shelved a plan to send billions of dollars in aid to Kyiv, with aid to Ukraine cited by [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] as one of the reasons for his [[motion-to-vacate|motion to vacate]] against McCarthy.[1] Ukrainian officials were reported to be at a loss as to what might happen next, despite assurances from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] that the United States would remain steadfast.[1] The episode formed part of the broader [[ukraine-aid-funding-controversy|Ukraine aid funding controversy]] and coincided with President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s public admission that "fatigue" was setting in around the war effort.[1]
