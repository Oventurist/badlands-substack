# Hannah Gutierrez-Reed

Hannah Gutierrez-Reed is the film armorer who was responsible for weapons handling on the set of the Western movie *Rust*, and who was convicted of involuntary manslaughter in connection with the on-set death of cinematographer [[halyna-hutchins|Halyna Hutchins]].[1]

## Conviction and sentence

Gutierrez-Reed was sentenced in April 2024 to 18 months behind bars for bringing onto the set the bullets that killed Hutchins.[1] She was serving that sentence at the Western New Mexico Correctional Facility.[1]

## Aftermath of the Baldwin dismissal

On July 12, 2024, a New Mexico judge dismissed the involuntary manslaughter case against actor [[alec-baldwin|Alec Baldwin]] mid-trial after finding that prosecutors had withheld evidence from the defense.[1] Her father, the veteran Hollywood armorer [[thell-reed|Thell Reed]], told the *Daily Mail* that he thought the outcome was "good" and that "Hannah's case should also be dismissed," arguing that prosecutors "suppressed all the important evidence" and that Baldwin's attorneys had brought it out.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the contrast between Gutierrez-Reed's imprisonment and Baldwin's dismissal as a question of whether the rules governing a "free and fair process" apply equally to defendants with unequal legal resources, and said the unanswered question was who brought the live rounds to the set.[1]
