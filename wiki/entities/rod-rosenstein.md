# Rod Rosenstein

Rod Rosenstein is an American lawyer who served as United States Deputy Attorney General from April 2017 until May 2019, spanning the period of the Russiagate investigations.[1]

## Representation of NSO Group

Rosenstein later appeared as one of the lawyers defending the Israeli cybersecurity firm [[nso-group|NSO Group]] in the long-running U.S. lawsuit brought against it by [[whatsapp|WhatsApp]] over the [[pegasus|Pegasus]] spyware.[1] According to leaked files reported by The Guardian, Rosenstein at one stage appears to have asked one of Israel's U.S. lawyers whether the Israeli government would "come to the rescue" in the legal battle with WhatsApp.[1] The Israeli government subsequently seized documents about Pegasus from NSO Group in order to prevent the company from complying with disclosure demands in the U.S. court, a manoeuvre officials believed necessary to avoid "serious diplomatic and security damage."[1]

Badlands commentary treated Rosenstein's involvement as the most striking element of an under-covered story, arguing that the episode highlights the tight and "precarious" relationship between the Israeli state and its corporate partners, and speculating about what interest [[israel|Israel]] had in shielding NSO's internal documents.[1]
