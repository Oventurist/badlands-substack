# Rod Rosenstein

Rod Rosenstein served as Deputy Attorney General of the United States during the first administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a period in which he oversaw the special counsel investigation at the center of the [[russiagate|Russiagate]] affair.

## The 25th Amendment reporting of September 2018

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief recalls that in September 2018 [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] published an article reporting that Rosenstein had discussed invoking the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]] to remove President Trump from office. The Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites this reporting as the essential context for interpreting a December 31, 2018 text exchange between [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] in which Bannon wrote "I think it's beyond borderline — 25 amendment."

On his reading, Bannon was referring to a known Democratic and internal-DOJ strategy against Trump rather than advocating removal himself. He adds that [[q|Q]] posted about the 25th Amendment for the first time on September 21, 2018 — the day the Rosenstein story was first reported — linking the *New York Times* article and, hours later, reposting the same link together with an article about [[omarosa-manigault-newman|Omarosa Manigault Newman]] conspiring against Trump. In this framing Rosenstein functions as an emblem of internal [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] resistance to the first Trump presidency.
