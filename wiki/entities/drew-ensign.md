# Drew Ensign

**Drew Ensign** is the [[justice-department|Justice Department]]'s deputy assistant attorney general, called to testify in the revived criminal contempt inquiry led by U.S. District Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]].[1]

## Contempt inquiry testimony

In December 2025, Boasberg scheduled testimony from Ensign and from former Justice Department lawyer [[erez-reuveni|Erez Reuveni]] as part of an investigation into whether senior Trump officials willfully defied Boasberg's March order attempting to temporarily block the administration's use of the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] to deport hundreds of Venezuelan immigrants to El Salvador.[1]

Administration lawyers moved that week to cancel the two witness appearances or to allow the administration to block the testimony citing executive privilege, arguing the court was overstepping its authority because the investigation and prosecution of crimes is a core executive power.[1]
