# New Civil Liberties Alliance

The New Civil Liberties Alliance is a public-interest litigation organization that brings constitutional challenges against federal administrative power. In December 2023 it appeared in Badlands Media coverage as counsel in a major [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] case against the federal government's domestic speech-policing apparatus.[1]

## Daily Wire v. State Department (2023)

On the night of Tuesday, December 5, 2023, the New Civil Liberties Alliance filed suit in federal court in the Eastern District of Texas on behalf of [[daily-wire|The Daily Wire]], [[the-federalist|The Federalist]] and the state of Texas against the [[state-department|State Department]], alleging that the agency had funded censorship technology designed to bankrupt domestic media outlets holding disfavored political opinions.[1] The complaint argued that the State Department, which is tasked with foreign relations and has no authority over domestic affairs, took the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] — an office created to counter foreign terrorist propaganda — and "unleashed it against Americans" accused of spreading what it labeled disinformation.[1]

The suit described the program as "one of the most audacious, manipulative, secretive, and gravest abuses of power and infringements of First Amendment rights by the federal government in American history," and named Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] along with five other officials as defendants.[1] It asked a judge to declare the department's attempt to interfere with domestic speech illegal and to permanently bar it from developing, promoting or encouraging others to use technology to de-amplify, shadow ban or otherwise restrict "the lawful speech of the American press and Americans."[1]

## Reception in the truth community

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the filing as a reminder that "the law takes its time," noting that independent researchers had been exposing the Global Engagement Center as "a centralized control mechanism within the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship industrial complex]]" for over a year before the litigation arrived.[1] She argued that the case should be litigated around intent rather than mechanism, contending that government collusion with corporations and nongovernmental entities to censor Americans is itself a crime, but that the underlying purpose — in her view the managed decline of America and the removal of the last barrier to the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] — amounted to treason and ought to be the heart of the argument.[1]
