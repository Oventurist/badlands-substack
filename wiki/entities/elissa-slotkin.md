# Elissa Slotkin

**Elissa Slotkin** is a U.S. Representative from Michigan.[1]

## Lake Huron shootdown

On Sunday, February 12, 2023, Slotkin and fellow Michigan Representative [[jack-bergman|Jack Bergman]] publicly disclosed that an unknown object had been shot down over Lake Huron, part of the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|February 2023 series of aerial object shootdowns]].[1] "The object has been downed by pilots from the US Air Force and National Guard," Slotkin wrote, adding: "Great work by all who carried out this mission both in the air and back at headquarters. We're all interested in exactly what this object was and it's purpose."[1] Her disclosure, like that of other lawmakers, preceded detailed official comment from the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Forecast of National Guard violence

Less than a month before the [[2025-washington-dc-national-guard-shooting|November 26, 2025 shooting of two National Guard members in Washington, D.C.]], Slotkin publicly forecast violence involving the [[national-guard|National Guard]].[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited that forecast — alongside the suspect's [[cia|CIA]] ties and Representative [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]]'s advocacy for the 2021 Afghan resettlement program — as part of a pattern she found difficult to dismiss as coincidence.[2]

## "Seditious Six" video (November 2025)

Slotkin was one of six Democratic lawmakers — all military veterans or former intelligence officers — who appeared in the November 18, 2025 [[seditious-six-video|"Seditious Six" video]] urging U.S. service members and intelligence personnel to defy illegal orders from the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[3] Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] called the video a "politically-motivated influence operation," and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] labelled the participants "traitors" guilty of "SEDITIOUS BEHAVIOR"; the lawmakers said they subsequently received a variety of threats.[3]
