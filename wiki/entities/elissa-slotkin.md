# Elissa Slotkin

Elissa Slotkin is an American politician who served as a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Michigan and was elected to the U.S. Senate in 2024. She previously worked as a Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) analyst. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," she is named among the supporters of the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] whom the source associates with the "deep state."

## Role in the article

The article describes Slotkin as "a CIA analyst turned Democrat politician" and one of the bill's "biggest supporters" who "were born and bred from the interagency blob, or 'deep state,' political spooks." It presents her support for the Antisemitism Awareness Act as evidence of the author's claim that the interagency blob "does not care how it achieves its goals" — advancing censorship frameworks under either a Democratic or Republican administration.
