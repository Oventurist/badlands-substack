# Kilmar Abrego Garcia

Kilmar Abrego Garcia is the defendant at the center of a Tennessee human-smuggling prosecution whose dismissal produced an ethics complaint against Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], as reported in the June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief.

## Indictment

A May 21, 2025 indictment in the Middle District of Tennessee alleged that Abrego Garcia and others conspired from about 2016 to about 2025 to bring undocumented immigrants into the United States. The indictment also alleged that Abrego Garcia was an El Salvador citizen rather than a U.S. citizen, and that he was a member or associate of the transnational gang [[ms-13|MS-13]].

## Dismissal for vindictive prosecution

U.S. District Judge [[waverly-crenshaw|Waverly Crenshaw]] dismissed the human-smuggling case on May 22, 2026 after granting Abrego Garcia's motion alleging vindictive prosecution. Per [[abc|ABC News]], Crenshaw found that the government had not rebutted a presumption of vindictiveness.

## Ethics complaint against Todd Blanche

According to a May 31, 2026 commentary by [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]], a public interest group filed an ethics complaint against Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] after the dismissal. The Brief notes that the underlying complaint PDF could not be accessed in-browser and was not used to establish independent facts. The episode is invoked in Badlands commentary on bar associations "ending themselves," with [[ed-martin|Ed Martin]] quoted declaring that "the Bar Associations are ending themselves."
