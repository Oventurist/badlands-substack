# Archduke Franz Ferdinand

**Archduke Franz Ferdinand** of Austria was assassinated on June 28, 1914 by the [[black-hand|Black Hand]], an anarchist terror cell from Serbia.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents the killing as the trigger rather than the cause of the First World War: the surrounding alliance architecture — Russian intervention guaranteed on Serbia's side and German support guaranteed to Austria — ensured that a single act would cascade into general war.[1] Kaiser [[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]] wrote in August 1914 that England, Russia and France had agreed to "take the Austro-Serbian conflict for an excuse for waging a war of extermination against us."[1] The article traces the underlying design to [[edward-vii|Edward VII]] and the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]].[1]
