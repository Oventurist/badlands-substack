# Badlands Media Library Channel

The Badlands Media Library Channel is the [[rumble|Rumble]] channel on which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] archives replays of its programming in library format, distinct from the live broadcast feed.[1] Under the distribution arrangement described in the network's December 2022 show schedule, all Badlands Media shows aired live on the [[patel-patriot-rumble-channel|Patel Patriot channel]], while the Badlands Media channel served as the on-demand repository where viewers could watch those broadcasts after the fact.[1]

## Role in the network

The two-channel structure allowed the network to concentrate live viewership — and the associated chat and audience interaction — on a single feed while preserving each episode of its roughly two-dozen weekly programs for later viewing.[1] The library channel therefore carried the full slate described in the schedule, including [[badlands-daily|Badlands Daily]], the [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]], [[baseless-conspiracies|Baseless Conspiracies]], [[trash-talk|Trash Talk]], [[rugpull-radio|Rugpull Radio]] and [[defected|Defected]], among others.[1]

Badlands Media stated that its content would always be released free of charge, with the network supported instead by voluntary paid subscriptions to its newsletter — a model it framed as enabling a collective of citizen journalists to "take back the narrative" from the mainstream press.[1]
