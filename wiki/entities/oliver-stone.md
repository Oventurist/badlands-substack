# Oliver Stone

Oliver Stone is the American filmmaker whose 2004 film Alexander dramatized the life of [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]].[1]

## The Alexander film and the Greek dispute

During the February 2024 controversy over the [[netflix|Netflix]] series "The Making of a God," an opinion piece in the Greek daily Eleftheros Typos blamed Stone's 2004 film for "launching a propaganda campaign about Alexander the Great's homosexuality," describing the resulting portrayal as a "distortion of the truth."[1] The claim was reported by [[remix-news|ReMix News]] and reproduced in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024.[1]
