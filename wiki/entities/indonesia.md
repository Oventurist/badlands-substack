# Indonesia

Indonesia is the largest country in Southeast Asia, with a population described in March 2023 reporting as 278 million people.[1]

## Move away from Visa and Mastercard

In March 2023 Indonesia became a prominent example of a state deliberately reducing its exposure to the American-centred financial system. [[bank-of-indonesia|Bank Indonesia]] prepared to transition away from [[visa|Visa]] and [[mastercard|Mastercard]] while developing an indigenous national payment platform, as reported by the Antara news agency.[1] President [[joko-widodo|Joko Widodo]] urged local agencies and authorities to abandon international payment systems in favour of cards authorised by domestic banks, calling the foreign networks a "problem."[1]

Widodo justified the shift by citing the financial-sector sanctions that the United States, the European Union and their allies imposed on [[russia|Russia]] over the war in Ukraine, arguing that Indonesia had to protect itself against comparable geopolitical shocks.[1]

## Place in the de-dollarization narrative

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] presented the Indonesian decision alongside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia's]] partial membership in the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] and the first yuan-settled LNG trade between [[cnooc|CNOOC]] and [[totalenergies|TotalEnergies]] as evidence of accelerating [[de-dollarization]].[1] Financial analyst [[kirk-elliott|Dr. Kirk Elliott]] described Indonesia's exit as a falling domino set in motion by the American decision to expel Russia from the system, and recommended precious metals as the asset class central banks were accumulating in response.[1]
