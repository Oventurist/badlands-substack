# Sigal Chattah

Sigal Chattah is a federal prosecutor serving as First Assistant U.S. Attorney and Special Attorney to the U.S. Attorney General, a posting Badlands described as giving her a national profile.[1]

Chattah's office dismissed the federal firearms complaint against [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]], the property manager tied to the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas illegal biolab]], in May 2026, without prejudice and citing the interests of justice after reviewing additional information.[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Chattah is Jewish and was born in [[israel|Israel]], making her a dual citizen, and treated that fact — alongside the Jewish identity of releasing magistrate [[elayna-youchah|Elayna J. Youchah]] and Solomon's Israeli nationality — as material to the outcome, arguing it suggested "the control of the US justice system for the benefit of a foreign country."[1] The commentary further invoked Israel's reputation for blocking U.S. extradition requests as context.[1]
