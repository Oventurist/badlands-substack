# Sigal Chattah

**Sigal Chattah** is a federal prosecutor in [[nevada|Nevada]] identified in the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 as the First Assistant U.S. Attorney and Special Attorney to the U.S. Attorney General — a role the source notes gives her a national profile.

## Role in the Las Vegas biolab case

Chattah is named as the prosecutor who in May 2026 dropped the federal firearms charges against [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]], the property manager tied to the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab]]. The complaint for illegal firearm possession was dismissed without prejudice, with prosecutors citing the interests of justice after reviewing additional information. Solomon remained subject to a pending state charge for improper hazardous waste disposal.

## Contested commentary

The Badlands commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] states that Chattah is Jewish and was born in [[israel|Israel]], making her a dual citizen, and treats that as "a pretty important detail" given Solomon's Israeli nationality. Combined with similar remarks about judge [[elayna-youchah|Elayna J. Youchah]], the piece advances the contested claim that the disposition of the case reflects foreign influence over the American justice system. These are the author's assertions and inferences, not documented findings of misconduct.
