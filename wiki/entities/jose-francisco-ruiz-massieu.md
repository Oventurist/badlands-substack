# José Francisco Ruiz Massieu

José Francisco Ruiz Massieu was secretary general and second-ranking official of Mexico's ruling [[institutional-revolutionary-party|Institutional Revolutionary Party]] (PRI). He had once been married to Adriana Salinas, sister of [[raul-salinas|Raúl]] and [[carlos-salinas|Carlos Salinas]].[1]

He was killed in September 1994. Federal Judge Ricardo Ojeda Bohórquez found that interwoven evidence proved Raúl Salinas was the "intellectual author" of the killing.[1] His brother [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] was appointed special prosecutor to investigate the assassination and was later found to have altered witness statements to remove references to Raúl Salinas — conduct the article attributes to payments from the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]].[1]
