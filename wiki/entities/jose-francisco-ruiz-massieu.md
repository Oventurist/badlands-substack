# José Francisco Ruiz Massieu

José Francisco Ruiz Massieu was the secretary general and second-ranking official of Mexico's ruling [[pri|Institutional Revolutionary Party]], assassinated in September 1994.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) He had once been married to Adriana Salinas, sister of [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]] and President Carlos Salinas de Gortari.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

A Mexican federal judge, Ricardo Ojeda Bohórquez, found that interwoven evidence proved Raúl Salinas was "the intellectual author" of the killing.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The subsequent investigation was assigned by President Carlos Salinas to the victim's own brother, [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]], who resigned alleging obstruction and was later found to have altered witness statements to remove references to Raúl Salinas — conduct the article attributes to [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] payments.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
