# National Response Center

The National Response Center (NRC) is the federal point of contact for reporting hazardous material and chemical release incidents.[1]

## 2023 ammonium nitrate incident report

On May 10, 2023, [[dyno-nobel|Dyno Nobel]] filed a report with the National Response Center concerning the disappearance of approximately 61,000 pounds of ammonium nitrate from a sealed railcar traveling from Cheyenne, Wyoming, to Saltdale, California.[1] The filing helped trigger parallel inquiries by the [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]], the [[california-public-utilities-commission|California Public Utilities Commission]] and [[union-pacific|Union Pacific]].[1]
