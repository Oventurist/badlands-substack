# Bill Clinton

Bill Clinton, President of the United States from 1993 to 2001, appears in the article as the author of the legal restriction that shielded [[osama-bin-laden|Osama Bin Laden]] before the September 11, 2001 attacks.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Citing the Daily Mail's coverage of the documentary The Longest War, the article reports that former CIA agents said they were prevented from killing Bin Laden in the build-up to 9/11 because of a bill signed by Clinton: a Memorandum of Notification enacted in August 1998 under which deadly action was forbidden.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Then-CIA station chief in Islamabad Bob Grenier described a "lethal finding" signed by Clinton that permitted "lethal activity" against Bin Laden while forbidding attacks whose purpose was to kill him.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article characterizes this as a [[stand-down-orders|stand down order]] issued despite Bin Laden's responsibility for the bombing of an American embassy in East Africa and his declaration of holy war on the United States.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article argues Clinton feared that the [[nine-eleven-commission|9/11 Commission]] would expose the memorandum and assign him blame for the attacks, which it offers as the motive for his National Security Adviser [[sandy-berger|Sandy Berger]] removing and destroying classified documents from the National Archives.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Scandals of the 1990s

A separate Badlands series argues that Clinton's administration was inundated with scandal from the moment he took office, and treats his survival as proof of a two-tier justice system, describing the Clintons as insiders and part of a protected class — "the modern day Ahab and Jezebel."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The affair with White House intern [[monica-lewinsky|Monica Lewinsky]], begun in 1996, dominated the media narrative for years and was widely thought capable of toppling the presidency; the article contends this was never a real risk, since infidelity had been well documented before he took office, and that the story functioned instead to sweep the administration's graver scandals out of the headlines.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) What would genuinely have ended the presidency, in this telling, was treason.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Technology transfers to China

Congress opened several investigations into whether the administration's policy of exporting space satellite technology to [[communist-china|China]] had helped China and other countries develop and use nuclear missiles, following *New York Times* reporting on a Pentagon finding that the reliability of China's nuclear missiles was significantly advanced in 1996 after scientists working for American satellite companies supplied Beijing with guidance-system expertise.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Senator Thad Cochran of Mississippi remarked, "I'm not blaming China. We're talking about our own Government policy-makers responsible for the transfer of this technology."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The transfers were enabled by waivers signed by Clinton in 1996 — the same year the Lewinsky affair began.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Clinton is also said to have allowed the sale of supercomputers to China, advancing its nuclear missile program by roughly twenty years.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## "Everything was for sale"

The article's central charge is that under Clinton nothing was not for sale.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Seats aboard [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Department]] trade missions run by Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] were sold to raise funds for the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] and the 1996 Clinton/Gore campaign, a scheme [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] documented and which the article says Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] knew of and approved.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Lincoln Bedroom was likewise "rented out" to large donors.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Brown, under criminal investigation by an [[independent-counsel|independent counsel]] and reportedly threatening that "if I go down, I'll take everyone else down with me," died in a suspicious plane crash in Croatia in April 1996; the investigation passed to [[janet-reno|Janet Reno]]'s Justice Department and Clinton was re-elected that November.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## The post-presidency, Doug Band and Teneo

Part 2 of the series turns to the machinery of Clinton's post-presidency, built by his personal aide [[doug-band|Doug Band]], a former [[white-house|White House]] intern described as the "key architect" of that post-presidency and the creator of the [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Band helped launch the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], brokered the paid speeches that enriched Clinton, and then co-founded [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]] with [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]] in June 2011.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) With approval from [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[state-department|State Department]], Teneo paid Clinton $2.5 million per year as a client and paid adviser, and he served for a time as the firm's "honorary chairman," giving clients rare access to the couple and their network of world leaders.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Clinton appeared alongside [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and former British Prime Minister Tony Blair at a September 20, 2012 Teneo reception at the Essex House staged with [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s help days after [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]], an unpublicised private event designed to impress prospective Teneo clients.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He also lobbied [[fifa|FIFA]] in 2010 on behalf of the U.S. World Cup bid whose committee had hired Keary and on whose board Band sat.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Teneo arranged the "Revitalizing America" events beginning in May 2011 at which Clinton and Bush appeared with [[ubs|UBS]] executive Bob McCann; McClatchy reported Clinton received $1.5 million for his participation, and the Wall Street Journal reported UBS paid him more than $1.5 million in speaking fees between 2001 and 2014.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The 2009 North Korea mission

In 2009, with [[barack-obama|Obama]]'s blessing, Clinton flew on a secret mission to [[north-korea|North Korea]] that secured the release of two American journalists sentenced to twelve years of hard labour for spying — a trip made at the request of leader [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Q drop 227 of November 24, 2017 consisted only of a photograph from that trip, in which [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] stands behind Clinton and Band stands behind Kim.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Ahead of the trip Band emailed Abedin requesting diplomatic passports for himself and aides Justin Cooper and Jon Davidson, none of whom worked for the State Department.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article asks what the hostage story was really "cover" for, placing it alongside the Iran hostages of the Iran-Contra era and the hostages involved in Obama's Iran nuclear deal.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Sanctions, oil and the North Korea nuclear deal

"America for Sale — Part 3" argues that Clinton's dealings with [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]] began long before 2009, with the 1994 [[agreed-framework|Agreed Framework]] announced on October 18 of that year and touted as a landmark nuclear agreement that would halt [[north-korea|North Korea]]'s program.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Under the four-page accord, North Korea was to shut its Yongbyon reactor, abandon two others and seal weapons-usable fuel, while the United States supplied replacement oil and built two "light fuel" plants.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author observes that North Korea resumed weapons work almost immediately and asks why an administration seeking to stop proliferation would help build nuclear plants at all.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

In the article's reading the key to the deal was oil: lifting [[sanctions|sanctions]] and supplying fuel freed North Korean cash for weapons development, accelerating rather than restraining it.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Five months later, in March 1995, Clinton imposed some of the toughest sanctions ever placed on [[iran|Iran]] through Executive Order 12957, barring American trade in Iran's oil industry, followed in May 1995 by Executive Order 12959 ending trade with Iran altogether.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author treats the juxtaposition — sanctions lifted on one nuclear-armed state and clamped on another — as evidence that the point was to obtain cheap Iranian oil for delivery to North Korea.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## The Marc Rich pardon

On his last day in office Clinton issued 140 pardons and 36 commutations, a volume the article calls a deliberate distraction meant to obscure one act: the pardon of fugitive commodities trader [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]], indicted in 1983 for tax evasion, racketeering and wire fraud and then the largest tax evader in American history.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The resulting [[pardongate|Pardongate]] scandal produced congressional and criminal investigations and outraged even Democrats.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author argues the pardon was about money, speculating that Rich — an expert at buying sanctioned Iranian oil and reselling it to other pariah states, and the pioneer of uranium-for-oil swaps — had helped the Clintons profit from the North Korea oil arrangement.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Rich's associates [[gilbert-chagoury|Gilbert Chagoury]] and [[sergei-kurzin|Sergei Kurzin]] later gave millions to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and the [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]].[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Kazakhstan and the origins of Uranium One

The article dates the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] to September 2005, when Clinton flew aboard the private jet of Canadian mining financier [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]] to Almaty, [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], and dined with authoritarian president [[nursultan-nazarbayev|Nursultan Nazarbayev]].[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Clinton publicly endorsed Nazarbayev's bid to lead an international elections-monitoring body, undercutting American foreign policy and his own wife's criticism of Kazakhstan's human rights record; within days Giustra's fledgling [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy]] signed a preliminary deal for stakes in three uranium mines controlled by the state agency [[kazatomprom|Kazatomprom]].[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Months later Giustra donated $31.3 million to Clinton's foundation, which the article characterizes as quid pro quo.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Shortly after the Russians announced their intention to take a majority stake in [[uranium-one|Uranium One]], Clinton was paid $500,000 for a Moscow speech by a Russian investment bank with Kremlin links that was promoting Uranium One stock.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) A cited Q drop describes the whole scheme as "organized/planned by BC/HRC" and carried out by the Obama administration.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author concludes that Clinton, having secured some of the world's best uranium mines in 2005, was in Pyongyang four years later with Band to cut a deal that may well have included uranium.[[4]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Epstein testimony agreement (2026)

In February 2026 Clinton and former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] reached a final agreement with House Republicans to testify under oath in the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Oversight Committee]]'s investigation into [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and his associate [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]].[5] The closed-door depositions were scheduled for February 27 for Bill Clinton and February 26 for Hillary Clinton, with transcription and video recording and the potential later release of transcripts; the arrangement marked the first instance of a former president being compelled to testify before Congress.[5]

The deal followed months of resistance to subpoenas issued in August, during which the Clintons argued the subpoenas were invalid and initially offered only sworn declarations of limited knowledge of Epstein's crimes.[5] Disputes over format and scope led House Republicans to advance criminal contempt of Congress resolutions carrying potential fines or imprisonment, which drew some bipartisan support including nine Democratic votes against Bill Clinton.[5] Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] announced on February 3 that contempt efforts were "on pause" after the deal was finalized.[5]

Clinton had a documented association with Epstein in the late 1990s and early 2000s, including travel on Epstein's plane, though he has not been accused of wrongdoing related to Epstein's abuse of minors and has stated he was unaware of the crimes before public charges.[5] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called the depositions "a shame" and expressed personal liking for Bill Clinton.[5] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that she most wants the Clintons questioned not about the sexual allegations but about their role in subverting the U.S. Constitution for foreign interests, citing Epstein's claim in the [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]–Epstein footage that Bill Clinton set up the 2008 financial collapse.[5]

## February 2026 Epstein deposition

Clinton was scheduled to testify on Friday, 27 February 2026, in the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]]'s ongoing congressional inquiry into [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s network, one day after [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] gave closed-door testimony in which she said she had no knowledge of Epstein's criminal activities.[6] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that the Clintons had been unable to quash their subpoenas and were therefore testifying before Congress about Epstein.[6]

## March 2026 Epstein testimony

Testimony from Bill Clinton was released in March 2026 as part of ongoing congressional inquiries tied to the late financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[7] Clinton said that former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had once told him he had "some great times" with Epstein.[7] He also claimed to have severed ties with Epstein and denied knowledge of Epstein's criminal conduct at the time of their association.[7]

His testimony came a day after that of his wife [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], who stormed out of her own hearing.[7] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] discussed supercuts of both testimonies on the program *Culture of Change*, and argued that the "humiliation ritual" of the hearings should not be mistaken for justice: "The Golden Age depends on the Justice Phase. A Justice Phase, by definition, must include justice — not merely its pursuit."[7]

## Refusal of Oversight Committee subpoenas (January 2026)

In January 2026 Bill Clinton and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] declined subpoenas from the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Oversight Committee]], led by Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]], to provide depositions in a congressional investigation related to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[8] Their attorneys stated that the subpoenas lack a valid legislative purpose, are legally unenforceable, and serve a politically motivated effort to harass and embarrass them, adding that the Clintons had already submitted sworn written statements and possess no additional relevant information.[8] In response to threats of contempt proceedings, the Clintons indicated they anticipate and are prepared to contest any such action.[8]

Badlands contributor Ashe in America allowed that Clinton "may have a partial excuse on some of this — he is a former President, after all," but argued that his protections end at official acts under the precedent set in [[trump-v-united-states|Trump v. United States]], and that the Epstein-related conduct occurred long after he left office.[8] She paired that precedent with the contempt-of-Congress imprisonments of Steve Bannon and Peter Navarro, writing that those men "went to prison for exactly what the Clintons are doing now" and that equal justice would demand the Clintons serve time.[8]

## June 2000 Kremlin summit with Putin

Declassified minutes of Clinton's first summit with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], held at the Kremlin on June 4, 2000, show Clinton proposing a joint US–Russian effort against international terrorism.[9] The transcript was released in August 2025 following a Freedom of Information lawsuit filed by the National Security Archive, an independent research institute at George Washington University.[9] According to the minutes, Clinton asked Putin, "What about anti-terrorism cooperation between our countries, particularly against Usama bin Laden?" and proposed a "coordinated strategy" between Washington and Moscow.[9]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that the record's suppression during the early days of the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration was convenient, since it would have defused public gaslighting that painted Putin as an evil dictator.[9] He added that the disclosure reinforces [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s repeated claim that Putin was fighting [[al-qaeda|al-Qaeda]] and ISIS long before the United States was, noting that Washington had unleashed the radicalized mujahideen through [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] in the 1970s and 1980s as [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] documented in [[codex-911|CODEX 9/11]].[9] The goal, he wrote, is not to get such figures to start telling the truth but to expose their corruption so their influence over the public is lost.[9]

## Named as John Doe 36 in the Epstein filings

At the end of December 2023, reporting by the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]] stated that Clinton, then 77, was expected to be named as "John Doe 36" when a list of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] associates drawn from court filings was made public in the first days of 2024.[10] He was said to be among dozens of Epstein's high-profile associates in documents set for release as part of a vast unsealing ordered by a judge just before the New Year, with some 177 people identified across hundreds of files expected to shed new light on Epstein's sex trafficking operation and network of influence.[10] The nominal release date of January 1 fell on a holiday, making publication the following day likely.[10]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] urged caution about the unsealing, framing it as a possible [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]] in which genuine disclosures are used to disguise a larger truth, and warning that victims and targets of the Epstein influence operation could be smeared alongside the guilty.[10]

## Crypto Bahamas speaking fee

In January 2023 the New York Post reported that disgraced crypto mogul [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]] had allegedly paid Clinton roughly $250,000 to speak at the Crypto Bahamas Conference in April 2022, an event that also featured other world leaders and top crypto executives.[11] Not long afterwards, Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] invited Bankman-Fried to speak at the 2022 [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]] in September.[11]

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] suggested the sequence illustrated how the Clinton machine operates, speculating that Clinton may have vetted Bankman-Fried in the Bahamas — a five-hour flight from [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s island — as a future money-laundering conduit before the CGI invitation followed.[11] He argued that Bankman-Fried subsequently acted as a bagman for globalist interests in Ukraine, the Biden administration and the Democratic National Committee via the [[ftx|FTX]] scandal.[11]

## Trump's May 2025 "body count" posts

Among a series of social media posts in May 2025, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] revived a 2016 video implying that Clinton and former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] were tied to several deaths, an allegation known as the "Clinton body count" conspiracy theory.[12] The Washington Post reported the claims as false and long-standing.[12] Badlands writer Burning Bright framed the sitting president's amplification of the material as evidence of "the timeline war we're engaged in."[12]

## House Oversight deposition order (November 2025)

The [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] ordered Clinton and former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] to appear for closed-door depositions in December 2025, rejecting an attempt by their attorney [[david-kendall|David Kendall]] to block subpoenas tied to the panel's [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-[[ghislaine-maxwell|Maxwell]] investigation.[13] Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] wrote that the Committee "disagrees" that limited written responses would be "the most efficient and equitable way to proceed," and that Kendall's request relied on assertions already "disproved by public information."[13]

## Vatican audience and Albania visit (July 2023)

Pope [[pope-francis|Francis]] received Clinton in a private audience at the Casa Santa Marta papal residence on Wednesday, July 5, 2023, with a delegation of prominent Americans that included [[alex-soros|Alex Soros]] of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[14] Clinton, whose post-presidential work centres on philanthropy and public affairs, had visited Albania on July 3–4, where the Albanian prime minister awarded him a public gratitude medal for his support of the country and for NATO's intervention in the Kosovo War.[14] Soros accompanied Clinton both in Albania and at the Vatican.[14] Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] dismissed the meeting as an unsurprising convening of "godless self-philanthropists."[14]

In commentary on the Biden family investigation in the same brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted Clinton's impeachment — described as being for lying to the American people — with the impeachments of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the conduct alleged against [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[14]
