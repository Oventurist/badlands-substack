# Bill Clinton

Bill Clinton served as President of the United States during the 1990s and appears in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as a central figure in the privatization of the American prison system.[1]

## Prison privatization and the Crime Bill

Carlson writes that under President Clinton the prison system was becoming privatized, meaning private companies would begin running America's prisons for profit.[1] He argues the obstacle was that crime was starting to drop, so those who benefited from full prisons needed the trend reversed.[1] Clinton, together with Senator [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], is credited by Carlson with producing the [[crime-bill-1994|Crime Bill of 1994]], which increased sentences for non-violent offenders, principally drug dealers.[1] Carlson states that kickbacks came to the politicians who helped pass the new crime bills.[1]

Carlson describes the early 1990s as an important time to push racial division precisely because of these incentives, linking the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] beating and the [[la-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]] to the same period.[1]
