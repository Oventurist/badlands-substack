# Norma Torres

Norma Torres is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from California.[1]

## September 2023 Capitol security hearing

Torres participated in the September 18, 2023 House subcommittee hearing "Oversight of United States Capitol Security: Assessing Security Failures on January 6, 2021," at which former Capitol Police Chief [[steven-sund|Steven Sund]] testified.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described her as "the head of gaslighting for the hearing," citing her assertions that the January 6 crowd was armed, that it marched on the Capitol with the intent to "hang Mike Pence," and that "if they didn't have a weapon, they found one."[1] The exchange was cited by Badlands writers as an argument for requiring legislators to testify under oath in public hearings, on the grounds that members currently face no penalty for making false statements from the dais.[1]
