# Lianys Torres Rivera

Lianys Torres Rivera is [[cuba|Cuba]]'s senior diplomat to the United States.[1] In late March 2026 she publicly proposed expanded economic cooperation with the Trump administration, offering "mutually beneficial discussions" at a moment when a senior US official characterized the outreach as sounding "like the regime is getting desperate."[1]

## Economic Outreach

Torres Rivera said there is "a huge range of issues that we can discuss with the US that are, we're pretty sure, for the benefit of the national interests of the US and the national interest of Cuba," adding: "So, the US wants to be engaged in the economic transformation in Cuba? Let's do it."[1] She stated that Havana is open to American involvement in the island's economic transformation without compromising its sovereignty.[1]

Her remarks offered an early glimpse into Cuba's posture in ongoing talks led on the American side by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], following a US oil blockade imposed roughly two months earlier.[1] Cuba is seeking sanctions relief including eased embargo restrictions, a waiver of Title III of the [[helms-burton-act|Helms-Burton Act]], and removal from the state sponsors of terrorism list; the largely undisclosed talks were confirmed in mid-March by Cuban President [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]].[1]

Torres Rivera also pointed to possible cooperation in Alzheimer's research and drug-trafficking enforcement.[1] US officials have said any deal would require major political and economic changes in Cuba, including privatization and greater financial independence for citizens.[1]
