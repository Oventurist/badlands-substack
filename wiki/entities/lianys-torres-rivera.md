# Lianys Torres Rivera

Lianys Torres Rivera is [[cuba|Cuba]]'s senior diplomat to the United States, identified in the Badlands Brief of April 2, 2026 as the public face of Havana's 2026 outreach to the Trump administration proposing expanded economic cooperation.

## Economic cooperation proposal (April 2026)

Torres Rivera proposed "mutually beneficial discussions" with the administration, saying: "There's a huge range of issues that we can discuss with the US that are, we're pretty sure, for the benefit of the national interests of the US and the national interest of Cuba." She added, "So, the US wants to be engaged in the economic transformation in Cuba? Let's do it," and said Havana is open to U.S. involvement in its economic transformation without compromising sovereignty.

The proposal came as largely undisclosed talks continued between the two governments — led on the American side by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] following a U.S. oil blockade imposed roughly two months earlier and confirmed in mid-March by Cuban President [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]]. A senior U.S. official said the outreach "sounded like the regime is getting desperate."

## Demands and cooperation areas

Torres Rivera identified the relief Cuba is seeking: easing embargo restrictions, waiving Title III of the [[helms-burton-act|Helms-Burton Act]], and removal from the U.S. state sponsors of terrorism list. She also pointed to potential cooperation in areas such as Alzheimer's research and drug trafficking enforcement. U.S. officials said any deal would require major political and economic changes in Cuba, including privatization and greater financial independence for citizens.
