# House Ways and Means Committee

The House Ways and Means Committee is the tax-writing committee of the U.S. House of Representatives and, in 2023, the vehicle through which IRS whistleblower material relating to the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] criminal investigation was made public.[1]

## Role in the Hunter Biden disclosures

Once-secret IRS memos documenting the tax investigation of Hunter Biden were released publicly by [[congress|Congress]] through the committee's process, and were subsequently reviewed by outlets including Just the News.[1] The memos revealed that Edward White & Company accountant [[jeffrey-gelfound|Jeffrey Gelfound]] had been a cooperating witness in the IRS criminal probe.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described "the most recent disclosures from the Ways & Means committee based on documents and testimony from whistleblowers by Joseph Ziegler and Gary Shapley" as painting a detailed picture of the influence-peddling schemes alleged against the Biden family, and criticized House Republicans for not prioritizing an impeachment inquiry despite the volume of material the committee had surfaced.[1]

## Probe into nonprofits and election money (2023)

In August 2023 the committee opened an investigation into whether liberal activists were misusing tax-exempt nonprofits to mobilise Democratic voters in battleground states, injecting foreign money into elections, or evading campaign finance laws.[2] Rep. [[claudia-tenney|Claudia Tenney]] of New York, a member of the tax-writing committee and its Oversight Subcommittee as well as a founder of the House Election Integrity Caucus, announced the probe on the Just the News, No Noise television programme.[2]

The inquiry turned on the [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] rule permitting 501(c)(3) organisations to conduct voter registration and get-out-the-vote drives only where those activities are neutral and non-partisan, without reference to any candidate or party.[2] Tenney said she was concerned that activists were instead engaged in "vote trafficking," using the system to turn out only Democrat-supporting voters while accepting large sums from liberal donors or foreign interests.[2] The story was reported by [[just-the-news|Just the News]].[2]

## IRS whistleblower interviews (December 2023)

Badlands writer [[just-human|Just Human]] reported that during the first week of December 2023 the Ways and Means Committee again interviewed IRS whistleblowers in connection with the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] tax investigation, part of a week of Biden-family developments that also included [[james-biden|James Biden]]'s failure to appear before the [[biden-impeachment-inquiry|House impeachment inquiry]] panel.[3]
