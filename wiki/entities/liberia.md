# Liberia

Liberia is a West African country on the Atlantic coast, known for its tropical forests, beaches, and warm, humid climate.[1] Founded in the early 19th century by freed African Americans, it is Africa's oldest republic and retains deep historical ties to the United States.[1] Its population speaks English alongside many indigenous languages, and its economy relies on rubber, timber, iron ore, and agriculture, though many areas continue to face poverty and infrastructure challenges.[1]

## Role in the Abrego Garcia deportation dispute

In October 2025, senior Trump administration officials told U.S. District Judge [[paula-xinis|Paula Xinis]] that they had planned to deport Salvadoran national [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]] to Liberia as early as Friday, October 31, despite an ongoing criminal case in Tennessee.[1] Department of Justice lawyer [[drew-ensign|Drew Ensign]] told the court, "If there were no prohibition, we would remove him on Friday."[1] Badlands commentator Ashe in America drew on descriptions of Liberia from the CIA World Factbook, the World Bank, and the U.S. State Department to argue sardonically that the country "sounds like a great place" for Abrego Garcia.[1]
