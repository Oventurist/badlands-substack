# Ron DeSantis

Ron DeSantis is the Florida Republican whose 2023 presidential campaign is examined in the Badlands corpus as another case in which surface conduct is held to be insufficient evidence of underlying loyalty.[1]

## Competing readings of the campaign

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] posed DeSantis's candidacy as a set of open questions rather than a settled judgment: whether he was running because he is a Neocon who had sold out to the [[deep-state|Deep State]], whether he had always been a Deep State player commissioned to appear conservative in the manner of John McCain, Mitt Romney and the Bushes before him, or whether he was working with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] willingly or unwillingly.[1] Carlson argued that regardless of which answer is correct, DeSantis was in fact exposing the RINOs, Neocons, the Deep State and the [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]].[1]

## The cooperation hypothesis

If Trump and DeSantis were working together, Carlson held, the purpose would be to expose people who claim to be conservatives and to support Trump and America.[1] He offered a testable prediction: the attacks between the two men would remain "pretty playful" rather than personal, and when DeSantis eventually dropped out he would throw his support and endorsement to Trump — along with the Neocon campaign money he had collected, funds those donors would never have sent to Trump directly.[1]

Carlson also noted that in the days before Sean Hannity's town hall, Trump had spent his public commentary "playfully making fun of DeSantis" rather than addressing the [[2023-debt-ceiling-agreement|debt ceiling agreement]], which he found conspicuous given that the deal was the largest story in the news cycle.[1]
