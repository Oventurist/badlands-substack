# Ron DeSantis

Ron DeSantis is the Governor of Florida and was a candidate for the 2024 Republican presidential nomination at the time of this article. In the Badlands corpus, DeSantis is analyzed primarily as a case study in the broader theory of a Deep State-orchestrated political strategy. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Role in the "Communist Plot" Narrative

According to a June 2023 Badlands Media article by Erik Carlson, DeSantis's presidential campaign is interpreted through a conspiratorial framework. The author poses a series of questions about DeSantis's true allegiance: whether he is a Neocon who has sold out to the deep state, whether he was always a Deep State player commissioned to appear conservative in the manner of past figures such as John McCain, Mitt Romney, and the Bush family, or whether he is working with [[Donald Trump]] "willingly or unwillingly." Regardless of the answer, Carlson argues that DeSantis is "in fact exposing the RINOs, Neocons, the Deep State and the Communist plot." ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

If Trump and DeSantis are working together, Carlson speculates that their shared purpose is to expose people who claim conservatism and profess support for Trump and America while acting otherwise. He predicts that when DeSantis drops out of the race, he will endorse Trump and bring Neocon campaign funding with him—money that Neocons would never have sent to Trump directly. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]
