# Grace Ocean

**Grace Ocean** is the Singapore-based owner of the container ship [[dali|Dali]], the 984-foot vessel that struck and destroyed the Francis Scott Key Bridge at the Port of Baltimore in March 2024.[1]

In the immediate aftermath of the [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|bridge collapse]], Grace Ocean contended that no pollution had resulted from the marine disaster, a claim [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted with reports of sheening observed on the water near the crash site.[1] Subsequent reporting that the Dali had suffered hull damage severe enough to complicate moving the ship — and that the vessel might be resting atop a high-pressure underwater gas line — raised further questions about the 1.8 million gallons of fuel aboard and the possibility that salvage operations could create spill conditions.[1]
