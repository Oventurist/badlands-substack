# Gulf Clan

The Gulf Clan is described as the largest cocaine cartel operating in [[colombia|Colombia]].[1]

## Qatar-hosted surrender negotiations

According to Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], [[qatar|Qatar]] announced in September 2025 that it had hosted Colombia and the Gulf Clan for negotiations over the surrender and dismantling of the organization.[1] He cited this alongside reported airstrikes and other military operations against cartels in Colombia, arrests and extraditions of high-profile cartel bosses, and mass arrests of foot soldiers as evidence that the cartels may already have been neutered.[1]

On that reading, although mainstream outlets portray [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] as cartel bosses, the two leaders in fact worked together to dismantle the cartels operating in the Bolivarian region of the Americas.[1]


## Doha Surrender Negotiations (2025)

Badlands commentary in October 2025 described the Gulf Clan as the largest cartel currently operating in Colombia and the top target of [[gustavo-petro|President Gustavo Petro]]'s military campaign, with many members killed and leaders arrested.[2] In September 2025 — shortly before Petro used a UN speech to accuse much of Washington of being on the cartel payroll — his delegates traveled to [[qatar|Qatar]], where they met Gulf Clan representatives to begin negotiations for the organization's surrender and disbandment.[2] The commentary noted the significance of members being brought to Doha and offered the opportunity to peacefully cease operations, likely in exchange for amnesty, and questioned why mainstream outlets were not covering the development.[2]
