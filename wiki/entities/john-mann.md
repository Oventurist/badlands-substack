# John Mann

Lord John Mann is a British peer associated with the United Kingdom government's work on antisemitism.[1]

## NHS antisemitism review

In October 2025 the Prime Minister ordered an urgent review of antisemitism and all forms of racism in the [[nhs|National Health Service]], and Mann was named to lead it.[1] The review was announced following recent incidents of antisemitism from doctors that drew stark attention to problems of culture and regulation in the health system, and was tasked with looking at how to protect patients and staff from racism and hold perpetrators to account.[1]

The review was accompanied by the immediate rollout of strengthened mandatory antisemitism and antiracism training across the health service and a directive for [[nhs-england|NHS England]] to review its uniform guidance.[1]

## Context

The Badlands News Brief presented the Mann review alongside commentary arguing that [[antisemitism|antisemitism]] in the UK is frequently downplayed, denied, or justified by reference to the war in Gaza, and that many British Jews are now afraid to be visibly Jewish in public.[1]
