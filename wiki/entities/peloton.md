# Peloton

Peloton is a publicly traded American connected-fitness company selling exercise equipment and subscription workout content.[1]

## Lockdown-era growth

In a February 2023 Badlands Media commentary, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] named Peloton, together with [[zoom-video-communications|Zoom]], as an example of a company that thrived during the [[covid-19-lockdowns|COVID lockdowns]], when people could not meet in person and gyms were closed.[1] Carlson presented such firms as evidence that the publicly traded segment of the economy was insulated from — and in some cases enriched by — restrictions that devastated privately owned small businesses.[1]
