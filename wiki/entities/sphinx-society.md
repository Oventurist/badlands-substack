# Sphinx Society

The **Sphinx Society** is a senior society at [[dartmouth-college|Dartmouth College]] characterized in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the quietest and, in the author's assessment, most dangerous of the Ivy grooming institutions.[1]

The article claims Dartmouth "masks its influence through the Sphinx Society," whose alumni populate behavioral surveillance projects including [[palantir|Palantir]] and [[google|Google]] AI, "driving quiet technocratic control."[1] From the society's "cloistered inner circles," the piece says, alumni migrate to Palantir, [[in-q-tel|In-Q-Tel]] and Google AI — behavioral governance platforms that allegedly do not merely surveil populations but condition them.[1] With [[darpa|DARPA]]-linked contracts and behavioral prediction capabilities, the article concludes, this pipeline is "building the digital cage" of the [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
