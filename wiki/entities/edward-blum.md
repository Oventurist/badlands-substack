# Edward Blum

Edward Blum is an American legal activist and former stockbroker who has organized and financed a long series of lawsuits challenging laws and policies that make distinctions based on race or ethnicity in areas such as voting and education.[1]

## Role in the affirmative action cases

Blum organized the lawsuits against [[harvard-university|Harvard College]] and the [[university-of-north-carolina|University of North Carolina]] that challenged the use of race in university admissions. Lower courts initially rejected those suits, siding with the universities' argument that decades of [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] precedent permitted race to operate as an unenumerated plus factor in admissions.[1] The cases nonetheless reached the Supreme Court, which on June 29, 2023 ruled 6–3 that considering race in university admissions is unconstitutional, ending [[affirmative-action|affirmative action]] in college admissions and forcing a reworking of admissions criteria across American higher education.[1]
