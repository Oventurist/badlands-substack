# S. Jaishankar

Subrahmanyam Jaishankar is India's External Affairs Minister and the country's principal voice on foreign policy and strategic partnerships.[1]

## Remarks on predictability and Germany

On September 3, 2025, following a meeting with German Foreign Minister [[johann-wadephul|Johann Wadephul]], Jaishankar underlined that "predictability today has a huge premium in global politics," framing Germany as a reliable partner for India.[1] With the world facing "economic volatility," he argued there was a "very powerful case" for India and the [[european-union|European Union]], and India and Germany, to work "much more closely with each other."[1]

The Indian Express reported that his remarks amounted to a thinly veiled reference to the unpredictability of the partnership displayed by the US administration under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], with whom New Delhi was in dispute over tariffs and Indian purchases of Russian oil.[1] Wadephul, on a two-day visit to India, echoed the theme without criticizing the United States, saying that "reliability," "friendship" and "predictability" in times of uncertainty are "invaluable."[1]

## BRICS virtual meeting on Gaza (November 2023)

Jaishankar represented India at the extraordinary virtual [[brics|BRICS]] meeting on the Gaza crisis held on November 21, 2023, attending in place of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.[2] He condemned the killing of civilians in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict but stopped short of echoing Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza, a divergence noted in contemporaneous coverage of the summit.[2]
