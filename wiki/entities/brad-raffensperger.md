# Brad Raffensperger

Brad Raffensperger is the Secretary of State of Georgia and the official who ordered the statewide manual recount of Georgia's ballots following the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief cites Raffensperger's 2020 recount as part of the procedural history that federal investigators are now revisiting. Many of the specific allegations set out in [[fbi|FBI]] Special Agent [[hugh-evans|Hugh Evans]]'s search-warrant affidavits concerning [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] — including discrepancies between ballot images and reported vote totals raised by a redacted witness described as a chemical engineer — had, according to the source, previously been addressed through Raffensperger's manual recount, reviewed by the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]], and litigated in post-election challenges brought by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his legal team, all of which courts dismissed for lack of standing or merit.

## The December 2020 count and the January 2026 hearings

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports new allegations aired before the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] on January 21, 2026, in which investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] presented evidence that Fulton County knew as early as November 19, 2020 that its counts did not match expectations, and that a report run at 12:06 p.m. and emailed at 12:13 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020 to consultant [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] marked the moment the counts were "made to match." Rossi also cited [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]]'s certification-week testimony — "We knew we were short" — and said the county "called the state," which Rossi assumed meant the Secretary of State's office.

The Brief connects these allegations to Raffensperger directly: contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], in his January 22, 2026 testimony before the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]], repeatedly relied on Raffensperger's authority and mentioned him as his only witness — a coincidence Ashe highlighted given that Raffensperger had been implicated the previous day for allegedly lying to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 2, 2021, when, per the Brief, officials already knew the vote was "under what they thought they needed."

The same edition reports that the [[georgia-senate-ethics-committee|Georgia Senate Ethics Committee]] passed Resolution 563 on January 22, 2026 urging Raffensperger to fully comply with the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s request for Georgia voter registration information under the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]], the [[help-america-vote-act|Help America Vote Act]], and the [[civil-rights-act-of-1960|Civil Rights Act of 1960]].
