# Brad Raffensperger

Brad Raffensperger is the Secretary of State of Georgia and the official who ordered the statewide manual recount of Georgia's ballots following the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] cites Raffensperger's 2020 recount as part of the procedural history that federal investigators are now revisiting. Many of the specific allegations set out in [[fbi|FBI]] Special Agent [[hugh-evans|Hugh Evans]]'s search-warrant affidavits concerning [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] — including discrepancies between ballot images and reported vote totals raised by a redacted witness described as a chemical engineer — had, according to the source, previously been addressed through Raffensperger's manual recount, reviewed by the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]], and litigated in post-election challenges brought by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his legal team, all of which courts dismissed for lack of standing or merit.
