# Huntleigh USA

Huntleigh USA is an American airport-security service provider and a subsidiary of [[icts-international|ICTS International]].[1]

## Role in the September 11 attacks

Huntleigh is best known, in the Badlands Media account, for having been responsible for allowing the hijackers at Boston Logan International Airport to board two of the four aircraft used in the attacks of September 11, 2001.[1] The last wrongful-death claim arising from the attacks was settled in New York by United Airlines and Huntleigh.[1]

## Placement within the ICTS group

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] grouped Huntleigh with I-SEC International Security Group as the two most significant subsidiaries of ICTS, an Israeli-founded firm whose screening technology and services proliferated after the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]].[1] He argued the pattern amounted to a [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian dialectic]] in which the group helped create the conditions that made the Patriot Act "necessary" and then became its principal beneficiary.[1]
