# Air America

**Air America** was the airline operated by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] in Southeast Asia during the Vietnam War era.[1]

Historians such as [[alfred-mccoy|Alfred McCoy]] have argued that the carrier became a conduit for moving opium and heroin precursors out of the region, with the profits helping to sustain anti-communist operations.[1] The airline's role is inseparable from the CIA's alliance with the Laotian warlord [[vang-pao|Vang Pao]], whose Hmong forces cultivated opium poppies as a long-standing tradition.[1] McCoy documents that [[usaid|USAID]] funded Vang Pao's airline in the apparent knowledge that it would carry opium.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situates Air America within a longer pattern in which American agencies tolerated or facilitated narcotics trafficking in the service of geopolitical aims — a pattern he traces from [[operation-underworld|Operation Underworld]] through the [[french-connection|French Connection]] and the [[golden-triangle|Golden Triangle]] to Laos and beyond.[1] Former [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] agents including Hector Berrellez have claimed that CIA contract pilots knowingly transported drugs into the United States, landing on military bases with impunity.[1]
