# Nikolai Bukharin

Nikolai Bukharin was a Bolshevik leader identified in Badlands Media coverage as a close ally of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] during the disputes over Russia's participation in the First World War.[1]

Where [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] sought to bring Russia out of the conflict in the first moments of the 1917 coup, Trotsky and Bukharin demanded that Russia remain in the war with the aim of converting it into a total pan-European and ultimately global revolution.[1] This position aligned Bukharin with Trotsky's doctrine of [[permanent-revolution|permanent revolution]] against Lenin's more pragmatic line.[1]
