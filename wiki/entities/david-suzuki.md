# David Suzuki

**David Suzuki** is a Canadian broadcaster and environmentalist described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a "climate guru" who publicly echoed [[andreas-malm|Andreas Malm]]'s thesis on sabotaging energy infrastructure.[1]

Speaking at an [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]] event in November 2021, Suzuki said: "We're in deep, deep doo-doo. This is what [we've] come to, the next stage after this, there are going to be pipelines blown up if our leaders don't pay attention to what's going on."[1]

Ehret cites the remark as evidence that the argument for destroying fossil fuel infrastructure has moved from the academic fringe into mainstream environmental advocacy, alongside Malm's writing and the endorsement of [[naomi-klein|Naomi Klein]].[1]
