# Southern Poverty Law Center

The Southern Poverty Law Center (SPLC) is an American advocacy organization grouped in Badlands Media coverage with the network of watchdog and gatekeeping institutions that includes [[media-matters|Media Matters]], the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]], the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]] and the [[center-for-countering-digital-hate|Center for Countering Digital Hate]].[1]

The article treats these bodies as interchangeable participants in a censorship regime that enforces obedience through advertiser pressure and reputational labeling, and expresses the hope that the SPLC will receive its own day of legal judgment alongside the others.[1]
