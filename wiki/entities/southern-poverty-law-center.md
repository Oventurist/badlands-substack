# Southern Poverty Law Center

The **Southern Poverty Law Center** (SPLC) is an American nonprofit legal advocacy organization, based in Montgomery, Alabama, best known publicly for its litigation against white-supremacist groups and for its "hate map" and hate-group designations, which are widely cited by journalists, technology platforms, and government agencies.

## Treatment in Badlands coverage

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treats the SPLC as an example of a nominally independent civil-society body operating as an enforcement arm of a broader narrative and [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship apparatus]], with its designations serving to place disfavored organizations and individuals outside the bounds of acceptable discourse.

[[badlands-blitz-episode-14|Badlands Blitz Episode 14]], published April 25, 2026, opens with what the announcement calls "the massive indictment against the SPLC," presenting it as the lead item in a week of what hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] describe as uniformly encouraging news and as the opening chapter of a "dawning age of American accountability." The episode announcement gives no detail on the charges, the jurisdiction, or the defendants, and treats the indictment principally as a signal that institutional consequences are beginning to reach organizations previously regarded as untouchable. In the episode the SPLC discussion is said to dovetail directly into the collapse of the [[russiagate|Russiagate]] narrative, reflecting the program's view that the two involve the same set of institutional actors and methods.
