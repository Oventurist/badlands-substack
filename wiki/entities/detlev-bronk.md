# Detlev Bronk

**Dr. Detlev Wulf Bronk** was an American biophysicist who served as president of the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] while [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] chaired its board of trustees from 1948.[1]

## Scientific career

A specialist in the human nervous system, Bronk was credited with establishing biophysics as a recognized discipline.[1] He served as president of Johns Hopkins University from 1949 to 1953, president of [[rockefeller-university|Rockefeller University]] from 1953 to 1968, and president of the National Academy of Sciences between 1950 and 1962.[1]

## MJ-12 claim

Badlands Media quotes researcher and author Jim Marrs — rendered in the article as "Bill Marrs" — as asserting that Bronk was not only a member of MJ-12, the legendary supersecret group supposedly in charge of the UFO issue at the time, but the leader of the team that autopsied "extraterrestrial biological entities recovered from a crashed disk near Roswell, New Mexico, in July 1947."[1]
