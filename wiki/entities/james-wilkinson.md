# James Wilkinson

Colonel James Wilkinson was an American military officer described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as an arch-traitor and confidante of [[aaron-burr|Aaron Burr]], and counted among the aristocratically minded figures within the eastern establishment — with [[timothy-pickering|Timothy Pickering]], [[george-cabot|George Cabot]] and Albert Gallatin — who worked to break the republic into two confederacies aligned with the British Empire.[1]

## Role in the Burr conspiracy

Three weeks after Burr killed [[alexander-hamilton|Alexander Hamilton]] in their July 1804 duel, the British ambassador [[anthony-merry|Anthony Merry]] met with Wilkinson before writing giddily to the British Foreign Secretary that he had recruited Burr to the Empire's cause of creating a new western confederacy through a joint U.S.–British war on Mexico, with Burr at its head.[1]

By 1807, Burr had persuaded President [[thomas-jefferson|Thomas Jefferson]] to place his co-conspirators in powerful positions in the newly acquired [[louisiana-territory|Louisiana Territory]], with Wilkinson appointed Governor.[1] The plan involved British soldiers working alongside American mercenaries under Burr's employ, who would take full control of Louisiana and New Orleans and expand the territory by declaring war on Spain, after which Burr would depose the sitting president and install himself as monarch of a new British American confederation.[1]
