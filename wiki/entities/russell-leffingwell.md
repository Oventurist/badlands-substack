# Russell Leffingwell

Russell Leffingwell was an American banker who served as the first chairman of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" describes Leffingwell as one of [[jp-morgan-jr|J.P. Morgan Jr.]]'s partners and a trustee of the Carnegie Corporation.[1] His chairmanship, together with the presidency of Morgan attorney [[john-w-davis|John W. Davis]] and the vice presidency of [[paul-cravath|Paul Cravath]], is cited by the article as evidence that the young Council was dominated by men connected to Morgan, leading to the claim that it was heavily influenced by Morgan's interests.[1]
