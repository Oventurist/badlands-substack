# Al Qaeda

**Al Qaeda** is the jihadist network founded by [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]]. In "A Dereliction of Duty" it is presented less as an enemy the United States fought than as an instrument the United States armed, protected and ultimately governed through in post-Gaddafi Libya.[1]

## Libya after Gaddafi

[[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]] had been fighting Al Qaeda inside Libya; when his government was toppled the group gained free rein.[1] Benghazi became a safe haven for training camps and a hub for funding and supplying jihadists whom American troops were fighting in Iraq and Afghanistan, and the newly formed Libyan government was controlled by Al Qaeda elements such as [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhadj]].[1] Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] served as the liaison between weapons dealers and this Al Qaeda-linked leadership, moving arms onward to [[isis|ISIS]] in Syria.[1]

## Benghazi

Investor's Business Daily, quoted in the article, reported that al-Qaida had retrenched in Libya and the Sinai Peninsula and that al-Qaida in the Islamic Maghreb (AQIM), using heavy arms seized from the Libyan army, struck the American consulate in [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] and assassinated the U.S. ambassador and three of his aides — the group's worst attack on U.S. soil since 9/11.[1] Former [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] officers had tried for months to warn the [[white-house|White House]] that thousands of shoulder-fired missiles were falling into Al Qaeda's hands, but their warnings went unheeded because, the article argues, the administration itself was selling those weapons.[1]

## Protection

The article contends that Obama-era restrictions requiring [[white-house|White House]] approval for every battlefield action amounted to a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] protecting both ISIS and Al Qaeda, echoing earlier protection of bin Laden as a CIA asset.[1] It concludes that both the Libyan government and the terrorists who killed four Americans in Benghazi were Al Qaeda-affiliated, and that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[john-mccain|John McCain]] knew it.[1]
