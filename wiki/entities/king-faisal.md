# King Faisal

Faisal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud was King of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] from 1964 until his assassination in 1975, a reign associated with the oil embargo of 1973 and with an assertive Saudi role in Arab and Islamic affairs.

In the Badlands corpus his death functions as a turning point. Writing in the [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]], [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that "Saudi started serving the [[deep-state|Deep State]] in 1975 when King Faisal was assassinated (if not sooner)," after which many Saudi royals were, in his telling, "in bed with the [[cia|CIA]], funding their operations and otherwise collaborating on projects" — a relationship he says reached its apex under the George W. Bush administration before shifting during the Obama years with the rise of [[salman-bin-abdulaziz|Salman bin Abdulaziz]] and later [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]. This account is interpretive commentary rather than documented history.
