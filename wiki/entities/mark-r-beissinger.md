# Mark R. Beissinger

Mark R. Beissinger is a Princeton scholar cited by Badlands Media for his Dissent Magazine article on democracy promotion and revolution export.[1]

Beissinger observed that overthrowing governments "has now become an international business," involving millions of dollars of aid and numerous consulting operations, many led by former revolutionaries themselves.[1] Since the Serbian revolution, he wrote, [[otpor|Otpor]] youth activists trained by Robert Helvey had become — in one Serbian analyst's phrase — "a modern type of mercenary," traveling the world in the pay of the U.S. government or NGOs to train local groups in how to organize a democratic revolution.[1]

He documented that a number of leaders of the Ukrainian youth movement [[pora|Pora]] were trained in Serbia at the Center for Nonviolent Resistance, and that after the Rose and Orange Revolutions Georgian and Ukrainian youth movements began challenging Otpor's consulting monopoly, with Pora activists joking about creating a new Comintern for democratic revolution.[1]
