# Abdul Manan Omari

**Abdul Manan Omari** is the [[taliban|Taliban]] Minister of Labor and Social Affairs in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1]

## Delegation to Russia (June 2024)

In June 2024 Omari led a Taliban delegation to [[russia|Russia]] to take part in the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, an Afghan embassy official confirmed to [[tass|TASS]].[1] The visit occurred while the Taliban movement was still formally outlawed in Russia, though Russian officials were then moving toward delisting it.[1] Zamir Kabulov, Russia's special presidential envoy for Afghanistan, said the delegation would also include the head of the Afghan Chamber of Commerce and Industry.[1]

Taliban representatives stated that they had traveled to Russia seeking economic investment in their country, a framing Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated as a further sign of the movement's normalization outside the Western orbit.[1]
