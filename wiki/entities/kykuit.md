# Kykuit

Kykuit is the 3,500-acre Rockefeller family estate at Pocantico Hills, a subdivision of Tarrytown, New York.[1]

Badlands Media identifies Kykuit as the site where the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] began organizing on July 23–24, 1972, after the concept had been presented to the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg group]] at Knokke-Heist, Belgium, that April.[1] The private meeting was hosted by [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] and attended by [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]], [[mcgeorge-bundy|McGeorge Bundy]], Henry Owen, Robert Bowie, [[c-fred-bergsten|C. Fred Bergsten]], Bayless Manning, Karl Carstens, Guido Colonna di Paliano, François Duchêne, René Foch, Max Kohnstamm, Kiichi Miyazawa, Saburo Okita and Tadashi Yamamoto — founders hand-selected by Rockefeller and Brzezinski.[1] The Commission was formally founded roughly a year later, on July 1, 1973.[1]
