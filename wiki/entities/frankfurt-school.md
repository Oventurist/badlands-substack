# Frankfurt School

The Frankfurt School was a German intellectual institution associated with critical theory. In Badlands Media commentary it is presented as the birthplace of the doctrines that became [[political-correctness|political correctness]] and [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] in the United States.[1]

## Alleged purpose

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that political correctness and critical race theory "were not unleashed on America 30+ years ago to nurture respect for one another," but were created in pre-Nazi Germany at the Frankfurt School.[1] Their stated purpose, in his account, was to infiltrate and destroy an enemy state from within — "to weaken and defeat an enemy without a shot being fired."[1]

This origin story anchors the article's argument that contemporary woke policy is a form of unconventional warfare rather than social reform, in which the [[deep-state|Deep State Cabal]] "doesn't care about your feelings or your rights."[1]
