# International Foundation for Electoral Systems

The International Foundation for Electoral Systems (IFES) is a nonprofit organization linked to the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] network that works on election administration and political-process programming abroad.[1]

In 1995 IFES joined with the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] and the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] to form the [[consortium-for-elections-and-political-process-strengthening|Consortium for Elections and Political Process Strengthening]] (CEPPS), a joint venture aimed at "advancing democratic practices and institutions globally" and funded primarily by [[usaid|USAID]].[1] Through that consortium, IFES is implicated by critics in the wider counter-disinformation and censorship programming that CEPPS has carried out in more than 140 countries.[1]
