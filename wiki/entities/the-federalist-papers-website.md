# The Federalist Papers (website)

The Federalist Papers is a conservative opinion website whose reporting is aggregated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In January 2024, Badlands excerpted a Federalist Papers opinion piece reporting that [[ohio|Ohio]] Governor [[mike-dewine|Mike DeWine]] had vetoed [[ohio-house-bill-68|House Bill 68]] and had received over $40,000 in donations from children's hospitals supportive of transgender medical treatments between 2018 and 2023.[1] The piece detailed the Ohio Children's Hospital Association's two $10,000 contributions to the DeWine–Husted transition fund and explained the permitted uses of such funds under Ohio's campaign finance handbook.[1]
