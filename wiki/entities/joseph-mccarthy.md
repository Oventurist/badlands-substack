# Joseph McCarthy

Joseph McCarthy is cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as one of the early "Communist-fighting-Patriots" whose work he links to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a theme he had developed in an earlier essay on Trump, JFK, Patton and McCarthy.[1]

## Timing argument

Carlson's chief use of McCarthy is chronological. He notes that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] was formed in 1947 following World War II, though it already existed in substance as the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], and that by 1953 the agency was covertly overthrowing the Iranian government in the [[1953-iranian-coup|coup against Mohammad Mosaddegh]].[1] McCarthy was going after Communists in the United States at that same moment.[1] To Carlson, the coincidence of dates is evidence that the CIA had been strategically planning to overthrow America since at least the early 1950s, with the intent of replacing the US government with a Communist-like, totalitarian one — raising for him the question of whether that was the real reason the agency was created.[1]
