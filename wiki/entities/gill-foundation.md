# Gill Foundation

The **Gill Foundation** is an LGBT-focused philanthropic organization chaired by technology entrepreneur [[tim-gill|Tim Gill]]. According to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, the foundation made possible the June 2023 Pride Month celebration held at the [[white-house|White House]] by the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

Gill's husband, [[scott-miller|Scott Miller]], is a co-chair emeritus of the foundation and serves as the Biden administration's ambassador to Switzerland — a country that Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized as "the epicenter of globalism that houses Davos and Geneva."[1] Neither man attended the celebration; Biden remarked at the event, "I also want to thank two people who couldn't be here but who made this possible: My dear friend Tim Gill and his husband Scott Miller, who is doing a terrific job as my Ambassador to Switzerland."[1]

The event became the focus of a dispute over the [[us-flag-code|United States Flag Code]] after a rainbow Pride flag was displayed at the center of the White House South Lawn arrangement, flanked by two American flags.[1]
