# Morgan Geyser

Morgan Geyser is an American woman convicted in connection with the 2014 "[[slender-man-stabbing|Slender Man]]" stabbing in Wisconsin, in which she and another 12-year-old girl stabbed a classmate 19 times, reportedly because the fictional internet horror character "Slender Man" had told them to.[1] The victim, Payton Leutner, also 12 at the time, survived the attack.[1]

## Group home escape and recapture

By November 2025 Geyser was 23 years old and living in a group home under the authority of the Wisconsin Department of Corrections, wearing an ankle monitor as a condition of her limited release.[1] On Saturday, November 22, 2025 she cut off the ankle monitor and escaped the group home, triggering a nationwide manhunt.[1]

The Madison Police Department confirmed on Sunday night that Geyser had been taken into custody in Posen, Illinois.[1] The Posen Police Department reported encountering her while responding to a report of loitering at a nearby truck stop, where she was found sleeping on a sidewalk alongside a male suspect.[1] That second individual, also taken into custody, was a 43-year-old with the legal name Chad Mecca who identified as a female named "Charly"; the Posen mugshot showed the suspect in a pink tank top with short blue hair.[1] Mecca said Geyser had fled the group home because the two had been banned from seeing each other.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer Ashe in America described the episode as "a tale of forbidden love," characterizing Mecca as the predatory party and Geyser as the younger and confused one, and argued that permissive handling of such cases amounts to "enabling the mental health crisis" rather than compassion — asking how far the trend would go "before we reopen the state sanitariums."[1]
