# Robert McDonnell

Robert McDonnell is a former Republican governor of Virginia who was indicted and convicted in 2014 on public corruption charges. His conviction was reversed unanimously by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] in 2016, with Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]], writing for the Court, criticizing the prosecution's "boundless" interpretation of the bribery statute and calling the case "distasteful."[1]

## Connection to Jack Smith

The McDonnell prosecution was brought while [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] led the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s public integrity section, and Badlands Media's coverage treats it — alongside the collapsed [[thomas-drake|Thomas A. Drake]] case — as part of a pattern of aggressive statutory theories advanced by Smith before he was appointed special counsel to prosecute [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The Federalist observed that although McDonnell ultimately prevailed at the Supreme Court, he did not escape the personal and financial cost of the prosecution: he "certainly has not" beaten the ride.[1]
