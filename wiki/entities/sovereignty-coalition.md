# Sovereignty Coalition

The Sovereignty Coalition is an advocacy organization co-founded by attorney [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]] that opposes the transfer of public-health decision-making authority from nation-states to the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]

## Focus

The coalition's central campaign concerns the proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and the package of amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] taken up by the World Health Assembly in Geneva in May 2023.[1] Analysts associated with the coalition and allied groups have warned that ratification of either instrument would diminish or strip away sovereignty from nation-states and vest emergency powers in the WHO and its director-general.[1]

Critics of the instruments, including international law professor [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]], have gone further, describing them as a vehicle for a takeover of the United States backed by the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]], Chinese authorities, and the pharmaceutical and biowarfare industries.[1]

## Role in "Behold, a Trojan Horse" (May 2023)

A May 2023 Badlands Media article, "Behold, a Trojan Horse," cited the coalition's co-founder, attorney [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]], warning that the proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and [[international-health-regulations|IHR amendments]] "would basically be the end of our national sovereignty."[2] The coalition's work — alongside Littlejohn's leadership of the Stop Vaccine Passports Task Force and [[womens-rights-without-frontiers|Women's Rights Without Frontiers]] — was presented by the article as part of a broader resistance to the subordination of U.S. constitutional and state authority to the [[world-health-organization|WHO]].[2]
