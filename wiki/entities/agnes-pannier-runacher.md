# Agnes Pannier-Runacher

Agnes Pannier-Runacher is a French politician who served as Ecology Minister in the government of President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]].[1]

## COP29 withdrawal

In November 2024 Pannier-Runacher pulled out of the COP29 United Nations climate talks in Baku after Azerbaijani President [[ilham-aliyev|Ilham Aliyev]], the host of the summit, accused France of colonialist "crimes" in New Caledonia during a combative opening speech.[1] Aliyev told delegates that "the crimes of France in its so-called overseas territories would not be complete without mentioning the recent human rights violations," and charged that "the regime of President Macron killed 13 people and wounded 169… during legitimate protests by the Kanak people in New Caledonia."[1] The remarks drew loud applause from delegates of several Pacific island nations.[1]

Pannier-Runacher responded by telling lawmakers in Paris that she was cancelling her trip in protest at Aliyev's "deplorable" speech, calling the attack "unacceptable… and beneath the dignity of the presidency of the COP" and a "flagrant violation of the code of conduct" governing the running of United Nations climate talks.[1]

## Context in the Badlands Brief

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief paired the COP29 spat with [[turkey|Turkey]]'s severing of relations with [[israel|Israel]], arguing that Azerbaijan's attack on French colonialism followed Macron's call one month earlier for an arms embargo against Israel and evidenced escalating fracture among globalist leaders.[1]
