# As-Safir

**As-Safir** is a Beirut daily newspaper variously described as "pro-Assad" and as supporting "the pro-Assad Lebanese group [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]."[1] The New York Times has described it both as a "pro-Assad Lebanese newspaper" and as "a left-leaning publication that often supports the pro-Assad Lebanese group Hezbollah."[1]

In August 2011, Syrian-born businessman [[jamal-daniel|Jamal Daniel]] — founder and owner of the news site [[al-monitor|Al-Monitor]] — acquired a large stake in the paper.[1] Badlands Media commentary presents this acquisition as part of a broader effort to give Hezbollah-aligned journalism mainstream credibility, and as a link in a chain connecting Lebanese media influence to [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], whose mother was Lebanese and whose grandfather was the first Prime Minister of Lebanon.[1]

## Assessment

The ownership and editorial-orientation details derive from quoted reporting; the network inference is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
