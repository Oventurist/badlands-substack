# Sky News

Sky News is a British television news broadcaster whose reporting is regularly picked up by American outlets covering United States politics.[1] Because it sits outside the domestic U.S. press ecosystem, alternative-media commentators often treat its coverage of American subjects as a bellwether for narratives that domestic legacy outlets are reluctant to advance.[1]

## February 2024 coverage of Biden's cognitive health

In February 2024 a Sky News journalist highlighted an episode of garbled speech by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], stating that it illustrated why many Americans were concerned about the state of his cognitive health.[1] The clip in question came from a monologue on the Israel–Gaza situation, during which Biden appeared confused and struggled to complete his sentences, saying "There is some movement, and I don't wanna, I don't wanna," pausing at length, then stuttering "choose my words…" and trailing off with "There's some movement, there's been a response from a uh…".[1]

The episode was reported by Modernity News and circulated widely on X after being posted by a Sky News correspondent.[1] Badlands Media commentary framed the incident as a "double edged sword," arguing that Biden's visible decline both supplied evidence of a shadow presidency operating behind him and served, on the global stage, as a symbol of American weakness inviting a reordering of world power toward a [[multipolar-world|multipolar]] arrangement.[1]
