# Harvey McLean Jr.

Harvey McLean Jr. was a former Dallas real estate developer who served as southern finance chairman for [[george-hw-bush|George Bush]]'s 1980 presidential campaign.[1]

Together with [[stefan-halper|Stefan Halper]], the campaign's policy director, McLean set up the Washington-based [[palmer-national-bank|Palmer National Bank]] in 1983, as the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan scandal]] was beginning to heat up.[1] The seed money — $2.8 million — came from [[herman-k-beebe|Herman K. Beebe]], the Louisiana figure with alleged Mafia connections responsible for roughly $10 billion in thrift fraud.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] describes both founders as Texas real estate developers and good friends of Bush, and notes that Palmer went on to channel money to [[oliver-north|Oliver North]]'s [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] network through the [[national-endowment-for-the-preservation-of-liberty|NEPL]] account.[1]
