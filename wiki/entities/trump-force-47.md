# Trump Force 47

Trump Force 47 is a get-out-the-vote joint venture between the Republican National Committee and the campaign of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], created for the 2024 general election.[1] The effort is dedicated to recruiting local Republicans and deploying them to turn out voters in their own communities.[1]

## Operations in Nebraska's 2nd District

In [[nebraskas-second-congressional-district|Nebraska's 2nd Congressional District]], centered on Omaha, local Republicans lacking large outside spending relied on grassroots organizing through Trump Force 47 to defend the GOP's traditional advantage in the area.[1] Volunteer Maggie Bellino-Bunde of Douglas County told *The Epoch Times* that the initiative focused on identifying low-propensity Republican voters — people who do not vote in every election — and canvassing their homes to secure commitments to vote either in person or by mail.[1]
