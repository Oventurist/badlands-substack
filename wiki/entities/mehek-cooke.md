# Mehek Cooke

Mehek Cooke is an Ohio attorney who in late December 2025 publicly alleged that members of the Somali community in Ohio had defrauded millions of dollars from the state's [[medicaid|Medicaid]] program.[1] Her allegations were published in an interview with Breitbart News on Sunday, December 28, 2025, in which she said that authorities "at the highest levels" were investigating "what is happening in Ohio."[1]

Cooke's claims that federal investigators were examining the matter immediately preceded the arrival of [[muckraker|Muckraker]] founder [[anthony-rubin|Anthony Rubin]] in Columbus, who began visiting Somali-affiliated daycare facilities there less than a day later.[1]
