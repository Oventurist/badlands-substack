# Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud

**Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud** is a Saudi prince linked to the international [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB scandal]] through his role as a co-founder of the energy company [[petrosaudi|PetroSaudi]].[1]

He was one of the eleven princes detained by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in the 2017 Saudi anti-corruption sweep, a connection Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] highlighted when describing how far the 1MDB network extended across governments and financial institutions.[1] In April 2023 Swiss prosecutors indicted two PetroSaudi executives as further 1MDB conspirators, deepening the company's exposure in the affair.[1]
