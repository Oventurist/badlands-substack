# Qassem Soleimani

Qassem Soleimani was the Iranian military commander of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] Quds Force, killed in a US strike.

## Anniversary and the 2026 protests

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that protests and labor strikes spreading across [[iran|Iran]] escalated in the wake of the anniversary of Soleimani's killing, which Iranian officials cited as a reason for heightened security measures across the country. Authorities imposed widespread internet restrictions and deployed riot police and plainclothes officers to protest areas; non-governmental organizations reported dozens killed nationwide, while Iranian authorities released no independent casualty figures and state media blamed foreign actors for inciting violence. Iran also addressed a letter to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] on the assassination anniversary.
