# Nikki Lu

Nikki Lu was the Pennsylvania campaign manager for Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] during the 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Pennsylvania campaign complaints

In October 2024, top Pennsylvania Democrats told reporters they were worried that the Harris operation was being poorly run in the largest battleground state.[1] They said some Harris aides lacked relationships with key party figures, particularly in Philadelphia and its suburbs, complained of being left out of events and of surrogates being deployed ineffectively, and urged Harris staff in private meetings to do more to turn out voters of color.[1]

Several pointed specifically at Lu, saying she lacked deep knowledge of Philadelphia, where Harris needed to drive turnout in order to win.[1] Ryan Boyer — the first Black head of the city's influential building trades council and one of the state's most powerful labor leaders — said, "I have concerns about Nikki Lu. I don't think she understands Philadelphia."[1]

Twenty Pennsylvania Democratic elected officials, party leaders and allies described anxiety that the in-state operation had set them back.[1] Latino and Black Democratic leaders met with Harris officials behind closed doors in separate Philadelphia meetings in late September 2024, in conversations that included Lu and Harris deputy chief of staff Sergio Gonzales, and pressed for a greater presence at local events, an improved surrogate operation, and a more sophisticated understanding of how to engage diverse voting blocs.[1]
