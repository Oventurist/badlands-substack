# Ihor Klymenko

Ihor Klymenko is Ukraine's Interior Minister.[1]

## June 2025 Kyiv strike

Klymenko joined President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] and presidential office head [[andrii-yermak|Andrii Yermak]] on June 20, 2025 in visiting the site of a nine-story apartment building in Kyiv's Solomianskyi district destroyed by a direct Russian missile hit, where they laid flowers and paid tribute to the 23 people killed.[1] The overall drone and missile assault on Kyiv killed 28 people and wounded 142 across the city, the deadliest attack on the capital that year.[1]
