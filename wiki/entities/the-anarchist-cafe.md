# The Anarchist (Toronto cafe)

The Anarchist was a Toronto coffee shop that described itself as an "anti-capitalist, anti-colonial cafe, shop and radical community space on stolen land." It announced in May 2023 that it would close permanently by the end of that month, after just over a year in business.[1]

## Business model and closure

In an attempt to counter the perception that the shop was itself a capitalist enterprise, the cafe offered a "pay what you can" coffee, which its owner acknowledged cost the business money and was supposedly subsidised by more expensive drinks elsewhere on the menu.[1] Owner [[gabriel-sims-fewer|Gabriel Sims-Fewer]] attributed the closure to circumstances beyond the model, writing that "the lack of generational wealth/seed capital from ethically bankrupt sources left me unable to weather the quiet winter season, or to grow in the ways needed to be sustainable longer-term."[1] The story was reported by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] under a headline framing the outcome as unsurprising.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that the headline read like satire from the Babylon Bee but was genuine, calling the venture "the perfect microrepresentation of what communism or socialism would actually look like."[1] He observed that offsetting the losses of the "pay what you can" gimmick with higher-priced drinks amounted to introducing "more capitalism," and compared the episode to the collapse of the CHAZ autonomous zone in Seattle as a failure its adherents refuse to learn from.[1] He closed by quoting the line, attributed to a House of Commons speech of October 22, 1945, that "the inherent vice of capitalism is the unequal sharing of blessings; the inherent virtue of socialism is the equal sharing of miseries."[1]
