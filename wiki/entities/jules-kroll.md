# Jules Kroll

Jules Kroll is an American businessman associated with the corporate-investigations industry and with [[newsguard|NewsGuard]], the for-profit "fact-checking" and news-rating firm that became a target of Republican scrutiny during the 2024 presidential transition.[1]

## NewsGuard and the FCC Inquiry

Kroll was invoked by Badlands Media commentary in November 2024 after incoming [[federal-communications-commission|Federal Communications Commission]] chairman [[brendan-carr|Brendan Carr]] sent letters to Google, Microsoft, Meta and Apple demanding information about their dealings with NewsGuard as part of an inquiry into what Carr called a "censorship cartel."[1] Writing that NewsGuard was "the same organization that has threatened countless independent media outlets, including Badlands Media," the commentary closed by addressing Kroll directly — "How do you like them apples, Jules Kroll?" — treating him as a principal figure behind the ratings operation.[1]

The firm had previously drawn criticism from congressional Republicans for allegedly labeling conservative outlets as more "risky" than liberal outlets, and [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] had spent months publicizing its activities on X before the FCC letters became public.[1]
