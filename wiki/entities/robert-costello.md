# Robert Costello

**Robert Costello** served as chief information officer of the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] (CISA). On Tuesday, March 3, 2026, he announced that he was leaving the agency, ending an 18-year career at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

## Tenure and departure

As CISA's CIO, Costello helped address internal vulnerabilities, promoted new tools intended to enhance agency services, and advocated for expanded use of artificial intelligence within the agency.[1]

His tenure had recently been turbulent. Conflicting reports circulated over whether former acting CISA director [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]] had attempted to push him out of the role.[1] In the week before the announcement, Costello received transfer orders for possible reassignment.[1] House Homeland Security Chairman [[andrew-garbarino|Andrew Garbarino]] had said the previous month that it was "good" that an earlier reported attempt to move Costello out of the CIO role had failed.[1]

## Context

Costello's exit came amid broader leadership churn at CISA. [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]] was named acting director to replace Gottumukkala, acting chief human resources officer Kevin Diana also received transfer orders, and the nomination of [[sean-plankey|Sean Plankey]] to lead the agency remained stalled.[1]
