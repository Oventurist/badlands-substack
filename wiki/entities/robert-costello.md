# Robert Costello

Robert Costello is a US government technology executive who served as chief information officer of the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]].[1] He announced on Tuesday, March 3, 2026, that he was leaving the agency, ending an 18-year career at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

As CISA's CIO, Costello helped address internal vulnerabilities, promoted new tools to enhance agency services, and advocated for increased use of artificial intelligence within the agency.[1]

His final months were marked by turbulence. Conflicting reports circulated over whether former acting CISA director [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]] had attempted to push him out of his role, and in the week before his announcement Costello received transfer orders for possible reassignment.[1] House Homeland Security Chairman [[andrew-garbarino|Andrew Garbarino]] had said the previous month that it was "good" that an earlier reported attempt to move Costello out of the CIO position had failed.[1] His exit coincided with wider leadership changes at CISA, including the naming of [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]] as acting director and transfer orders issued to acting chief human resources officer [[kevin-diana|Kevin Diana]].[1]
