# Micron Technology

Micron Technology is a United States memory-chip manufacturer that featured prominently in the [[white-house|White House]]'s promotion of the [[chips-and-science-act|CHIPS and Science Act]] and in later criticism of that law's results.[1]

## CHIPS Act commitments

A White House fact sheet dated August 9, 2022 announced that Micron would invest $40 billion in memory chip manufacturing critical for computers and electronic devices, creating up to 40,000 new jobs in construction and manufacturing, as part of nearly $50 billion in semiconductor investments spurred by the CHIPS and Science Act.[1]

## Criticism of implementation

Micron subsequently announced a $16 billion project in Syracuse, New York, but the build was reported to be behind schedule in 2023, and by early 2024 the company said it was waiting on additional government funding before making good on its 2022 commitment.[1] Weeks before March 2024, Micron also announced an expansion of its investment in [[china|China]].[1] Badlands commentary cited this record, alongside [[globalfoundries|GlobalFoundries]]' Singapore expansion, in arguing that the roughly $280 billion CHIPS and Science Act had effectively financed corporate expansion and job creation outside the United States.[1]
