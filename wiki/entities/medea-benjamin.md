# Medea Benjamin

Medea Benjamin is the founder of [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]] and, according to reporting cited in the 2026 grand jury coverage, the wife and ally of [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]].[1]

## Role in the Singham network

CodePink, founded by Benjamin, was listed among the six principal nonprofit recipients in the alleged Singham funding structure, receiving $1,330,000.[1] Separately, Singham's wife was described as sitting on the board of People's Support Foundation Ltd., the Chicago 501(c)(3) that received the largest single allocation in the network at $167,540,000.[1]

## Cuba convoy subpoena (2026)

Benjamin, 73, was subpoenaed by federal investigators as part of a [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] probe into her participation in the March 2026 [[nuestra-america-convoy|Nuestra America Convoy]], a humanitarian aid convoy organized with help from CodePink that delivered roughly 20 tons of goods to Havana, [[cuba|Cuba]].[2] The subpoena sought financial, logistical and communications records related to the trip, and more than 40 Americans are reportedly under scrutiny by the Treasury's [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]] for potential sanctions violations.[2]

A longtime prominent critic of U.S. Cuba policy who has faced prior scrutiny over her activism, Benjamin was subpoenaed alongside streamer [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]].[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that CodePink "has long operated in the same ecosystem of activist NGOs that parallel the [[southern-poverty-law-center|SPLC]] model — leveraging humanitarian framing and narrative positioning to advance anti-American rhetoric while maintaining operational ties that are now facing scrutiny."[2]
