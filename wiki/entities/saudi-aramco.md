# Saudi Aramco

Saudi Aramco is the state-controlled petroleum and natural gas company of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], and among the largest oil producers and most valuable companies in the world. Its refining and export infrastructure along the Persian Gulf coast is central to global crude supply and therefore a recurring target in Middle Eastern conflicts.

## Attacks during the 2026 Iran conflict

According to the March 3, 2026 [[badlands-brief-52f|Badlands Brief]], Saudi oil facilities were "pulled into" the widening regional conflict following conventional and drone attacks on Saudi oil infrastructure. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] writes that Saudi Aramco refineries were struck, "including the largest oil refinery in the world," while Iran's deputy foreign minister appeared on [[cnn|CNN]] to deny Iranian responsibility and to state that Saudi Arabia was aware Iran had not conducted the attacks.

The Brief connects the attacks to broader disruption of shipping through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], where roughly 20 percent of global oil supply transits, and to rising oil prices and tanker costs. The commentary further reports [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s claim that [[qatar|Qatar]] and Saudi Arabia had arrested [[mossad|Mossad]] agents allegedly planning false-flag bombings to be attributed to [[iran|Iran]] — presented in the source as an unverified claim — and argues that the cumulative effect is the deliberate destruction of the regional security architecture built by Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].
