# Financial Times

The *Financial Times* is a London-based international business and financial newspaper widely cited in coverage of diplomacy, markets and energy. In the Badlands corpus it appears as an originating outlet for reporting that the Brief then summarizes and reinterprets.

The [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]] credits *Financial Times* reporting for the disclosure that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] had floated a Middle East non-aggression pact with [[iran]] as part of postwar planning with allies, with diplomats pointing to the 1970s [[helsinki-accords|Helsinki Accords]] as a possible framework. The same reporting described Riyadh strengthening coordination with [[pakistan]], [[turkey]] and [[egypt]], and Pakistan's desire to add [[qatar]] and Turkey to the Saudi–Pakistan defense pact.
