# Financial Times

The *Financial Times* is a London-based international business and financial newspaper widely cited in coverage of diplomacy, markets and energy. In the Badlands corpus it appears as an originating outlet for reporting that the Brief then summarizes and reinterprets.

The Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026 credits *Financial Times* reporting for the disclosure that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] had floated a Middle East non-aggression pact with [[iran]] as part of postwar planning with allies, with diplomats pointing to the 1970s [[helsinki-accords|Helsinki Accords]] as a possible framework. The same reporting described Riyadh strengthening coordination with [[pakistan]], [[turkey]] and [[egypt]], and Pakistan's desire to add [[qatar]] and Turkey to the Saudi–Pakistan defense pact.

## Trump interview on Iran (June 2026)

In a phone interview published around June 8, 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told the Financial Times that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] "won't have any choice" but to accept any agreement the United States negotiates with [[iran|Iran]]: "I call the shots. I call all the shots. He doesn't call the shots." Trump said Iran's missile strikes were "not going to have any impact on the deal," describing them as "attacks that did not kick at all" in a conflict that has "been going for 3,000 years, or 47 years, depending on how you count.
