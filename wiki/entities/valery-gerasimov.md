# Valery Gerasimov

Valery Gerasimov is Chief of the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces.[1]

## Kursk campaign and North Korean deployment

In April 2025, Gerasimov delivered a report to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in which he praised the contribution made by North Korean servicemen to the liberation of the Kursk region from Ukrainian forces.[1] The report was among the first official Russian acknowledgements of the [[north-korea|North Korean]] presence on the front line, which Moscow had previously neither confirmed nor denied.[1] Pyongyang formally confirmed the deployment days later, on Monday, with the Korean Central News Agency publishing a statement from the country's Central Military Commission declaring the Kursk operations "victoriously concluded."[1]

The troops had been deployed under the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement between Moscow and Pyongyang, which entered into force in December 2024.[1]
