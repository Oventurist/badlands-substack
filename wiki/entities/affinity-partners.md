# Affinity Partners

Affinity Partners is an investment firm whose founder and chief executive is [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], the son-in-law of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Electronic Arts acquisition

In September 2025 Affinity Partners joined [[silver-lake-partners|Silver Lake Partners]] and Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]] in an agreement to purchase the video game publisher [[electronic-arts|Electronic Arts]] for $55 billion, the largest private equity-funded buyout on record.[1] Stockholders were to receive $210 per share in cash, a 25 percent premium to the pre-rumour price, and the transaction required regulatory approval because of Saudi participation.[1] Commentary on the deal highlighted the tightness of the relationship between the Saudis and the Trump family in recent years, attributed in no small part to the Saudi-backed LIV Golf tour hosting events at Trump's clubs.[1]
