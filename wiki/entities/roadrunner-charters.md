# Roadrunner Charters

Roadrunner Charters is a Texas bus company paid by the Texas state government to transport illegal immigrants from the southern border to New York City.[1]

## New York City lawsuit

In March 2024 the company agreed to stop transporting migrants to New York City while the city's lawsuit against it proceeds.[1] Roadrunner was one of 17 bus companies sued in January 2024 by the administration of New York Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] over their collective transport of roughly 33,600 individuals from Texas to the self-declared "sanctuary city."[1] The Adams administration argues that the carriers should be held liable for the $708 million the city has spent caring for their passengers, and the complaint alleges that the operators earned "millions of dollars" while acting in "bad faith" to advance Texas Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]]'s agenda.[1]

Abbott's office did not respond to a request for comment on the case, though the governor has publicly condemned the lawsuit as "baseless and deserves to be sanctioned."[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that suing the carriers was improper because the company was executing a lawful contract with another government entity, making the transport arguably a state action; on that reasoning Adams would need to sue Texas directly, which would put New York's [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary city]] status itself into litigation and might "spell the end of sanctuary cities more broadly."[1] She also criticized Texas for not intervening in the suit on the companies' behalf, asking whether the bus operators should be grateful for Abbott's "strongly worded letter."[1]
