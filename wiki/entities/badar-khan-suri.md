# Badar Khan Suri

**Badar Khan Suri** is an Indian-born researcher at Georgetown University who was placed in immigration detention by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration in March 2025 as part of a crackdown on pro-Palestinian academics.[1]

## Release order

On Wednesday, May 14, 2025, US District Judge Patricia Giles ordered Khan Suri released, finding that the government had offered no evidence that he posed a danger to the community.[1] Giles said his arrest likely violated his free speech rights as well as his rights to freedom of association with his Palestinian-American wife.[1] Speaking from her Alexandria, Virginia courtroom, the judge stated that "the First Amendment extends to noncitizens and doesn't distinguish between citizens and non-citizens."[1] She emphasized that the release ruling was "totally separate" from the ongoing deportation proceedings Khan Suri faces in immigration court.[1]
