# Albert Wohlstetter

Albert Wohlstetter was, in Matthew Ehret's account, a devout Trotskyist who became a leading neoconservative and the controlling hand behind the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]].[1] He is listed among the devotees of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] — with [[james-burnham|James Burnham]], Sidney Hook, Max Schachtman and Irving Kristol — who abandoned Trotskyite socialism after 1940 for the right-wing paradigm that became [[neoconservatives|neo-conservatism]].[1]

Under Wohlstetter's influence, RAND became the principal conduit for the intellectual takeover of all branches of US policy at the military, economic and cultural levels, a process accomplished through [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] and its operational language of "systems analysis."[1] The result, Ehret writes, was that the United States was driven into full-scale insanity by a military outlook built on computer models that presumed nuclear war was a winnable endeavor, bringing the world closer to nuclear holocaust.[1]
