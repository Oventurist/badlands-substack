# BlackRock

BlackRock Inc. is the world's largest asset manager. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as one of the institutional investors whose cross-holdings are said to unify the nominally competing media conglomerates.

## Media ownership argument

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] argues that while the "Big Six" media groups — [[time-warner|Time Warner]], [[comcast|Comcast]], [[walt-disney-company|Disney]], [[sony|Sony]], [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] and [[news-corp|NewsCorp]] — appeared to compete, they "were mutually owned by the world's largest institutional investors—asset managers like Blackrock Inc., [[vanguard-group|the Vanguard Group]], [[state-street|State Street]] and [[berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]." The article supports this with links to publicly listed institutional holder data for each conglomerate.

It further asserts that representatives of these asset managers "remain regular fixtures at elitist policy think tanks such as the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]," a connection the author uses to move from a claim about share ownership to a claim about coordinated narrative control by "the big 1—the Western establishment, the system of systems."
