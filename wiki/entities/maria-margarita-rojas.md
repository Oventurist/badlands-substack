# Maria Margarita Rojas

Maria Margarita Rojas is a midwife, aged 49 at the time of reporting, who was indicted in Texas on 15 felony counts including the unlawful killing of unborn babies and practicing medicine without a license.[1] Prosecutors allege she operated unlicensed clinics in northwest Houston where she and others performed illegal medical procedures, including abortions.[1]

## 2025 Houston arrests

On Wednesday, October 8, 2025, Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] announced the arrests of eight people accused of operating illegal abortion clinics in the Houston area, with the arrests connected to Rojas's alleged network.[1] The suspects were said to have been working under Rojas, providing unauthorized medical services without proper licensing; half were reported to be foreign nationals.[1]

Those named by Paxton's office were Yaimara Hernandez Alvarez, Alina Valeron Leon, Dalia Coromoto Yanez, Yhonder Lebrun Acosta, Liunet Grandales Estrada, Gerardo Otero Aguero, Sabiel Bosch Gongora, and Jose Manuel Cendan Ley.[1] Paxton said the individuals "endangered the very people they pretended to help" by operating outside the law.[1]

The arrests followed the recent closure of a major [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]] facility in Houston, described as once one of the largest abortion clinics in the country.[1]
