# Alparslan Bayraktar

**Alparslan Bayraktar** is [[turkey|Turkey]]'s Minister of Energy and Natural Resources.[1]

## Kirkuk stake announcement

Following the [[turkish-petroleum-corporation|Turkish Petroleum Corporation]]'s acquisition of a 15% stake in bp's Kirkuk oil project in [[iraq|Iraq]] — alongside ConocoPhillips — Bayraktar called the transaction a major milestone toward TPAO's stated target of producing one million barrels of oil and gas per day.[1] The fields involved hold more than 3 billion barrels of oil equivalent.[1]

The announcement accompanied five bilateral agreements signed in Ankara on 29 July 2026 during talks between President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] and Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali al-Zaidi]].[1]

## Qatar–Turkey pipeline remarks (December 2024)

Speaking after a cabinet meeting in Ankara on Monday, December 16, 2024, Bayraktar said the long-dormant [[qatar-turkey-pipeline|Qatar–Türkiye natural gas pipeline]] could be revived following the fall of the decades-long Assad regime.[2] The project would connect Qatar to Türkiye and European markets via Saudi Arabia, Jordan and Syria.[2] Bayraktar said realization could be possible "if Syria achieves its (territorial) integrity and stability," adding, "If so, that line must be safe. We hope it will be so. If so, there are many projects to produce."[2] He described electricity as an essential need and noted that "with no existing infrastructure, everything is lacking" in Syria, concluding: "We need to look at how we can turn energy into a tool to meet these needs."[2]
