# OpenAI

## Open-source AI dispute (2026)

OpenAI, led by [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], was grouped with [[anthropic|Anthropic]] in July 2026 as a company that has generally supported stronger safeguards for frontier AI systems, in contrast to advocates of [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source AI]] who argue that excessive regulation would entrench incumbent firms and reduce America's ability to compete globally.[1] Venture capitalist [[chamath-palihapitiya|Chamath Palihapitiya]] warned that a government ban on open-source AI would tank the market and crater both OpenAI and Anthropic.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the push by Altman and [[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] for government regulation was an attempt to slow and control the industry's acceleration rather than supercharge an American Age of Abundance, and cited former White House AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] as openly stating that the two companies seek regulation in order to stave off competitors.[1]
