# OpenAI

OpenAI is the American artificial intelligence laboratory best known for ChatGPT. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]], OpenAI is named as one of the three corporate partners — with [[oracle|Oracle]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]] — in [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]], the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's announced $500 billion U.S. investment in [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] infrastructure.

The article also dates the beginning of "the big push for AI" to the release of ChatGPT in late 2022, using that timeline to argue that *Mission Impossible: Dead Reckoning*, released roughly six months later in the summer of 2023, must have been conceived before AI became a mainstream talking point.
