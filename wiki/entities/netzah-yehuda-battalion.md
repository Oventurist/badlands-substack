# Netzah Yehuda Battalion

The Netzah Yehuda Battalion is a unit of the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] composed of over 900 soldiers that became the subject of unprecedented United States sanctions in April 2024 over alleged human rights abuses in the occupied West Bank.[1]

## US sanctions

Reporting by Axios, relayed by Business Insider and summarized in the Badlands News Brief of April 22, 2024, indicated that Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] was expected to announce sanctions against the battalion for alleged human rights violations in the West Bank.[1] It would mark the first time the United States had imposed sanctions on an Israeli military unit, and the measures were described as prohibiting the battalion and its members from receiving US military assistance or training.[1]

The timing was notable: news of the prospective sanctioning emerged as the [[us-house-of-representatives|US House of Representatives]] passed a foreign aid bill including more than $14 billion in military aid to Israel along with $9 billion in humanitarian assistance, much of it destined for Gaza.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry framed the battalion as a "Hebrew Azov Battalion," drawing an explicit parallel to sanctions imposed on Ukraine's [[azov-battalion|Azov Battalion]] during the Trump administration.[1] The commentary argued that the sanctions being issued under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] supported "Heroldian" devolution theories holding that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had stepped away deliberately while the [[deep-state|Deep State]] appeared to run things, and pointed to what the writer described as deeper connections between Ukraine and Israel beyond intelligence-community activity and foreign aid money laundering.[1] Reports of abuses in the West Bank attributed to the unit were said to have circulated for roughly twenty years.[1]
