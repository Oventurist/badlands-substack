# Amber Heard

Amber Heard is an American actress and the defendant in the 2022 defamation suit brought by her former husband [[johnny-depp|Johnny Depp]], a case cited by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as a template for how public opinion can reverse once evidence is presented in open court.[1]

## The 2022 trial

Depp alleged that Heard had accused him of psychological and physical abuse and that she had in fact been the abuser.[1] The televised Virginia trial captivated a large portion of the American public, and many who had supported Heard before proceedings began turned against her as testimony unfolded.[1] Carlson describes the widespread view that Heard was a liar and possibly unstable, and the argument that she had harmed the "Me Too" movement by making genuine abuse victims less likely to be believed.[1] He characterises her exaggerated claims as being about obtaining attention as a victim, referencing the widely publicised allegation that she defecated on Depp's bed.[1]

During the trial Heard referred to Depp pushing a former longtime girlfriend down the stairs — a rumour subsequently refuted by Kate Moss.[1] Because Heard raised the incident, Moss could be called as a character witness after the judge had previously excluded her, delivering what Carlson describes as a real blow to Heard's case that she herself had inadvertently allowed in.[1]
