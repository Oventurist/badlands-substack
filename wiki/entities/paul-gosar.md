# Paul Gosar

Paul Gosar is a Republican U.S. Representative from Arizona.[1]

In April 2023 Gosar joined Rep. [[alex-mooney|Alex Mooney]] (R-WV) and Rep. [[andy-biggs|Andy Biggs]] (R-AZ) in introducing H.R. 2435, the [[gold-standard-restoration-act|Gold Standard Restoration Act]].[1] The bill would give the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] and the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] 24 months to disclose all gold holdings and transactions, after which Federal Reserve notes would be repegged to and redeemable for a fixed weight of gold, with Treasury gold reserves backstopping the Federal Reserve Banks as guarantor.[1]

Coverage of the introduction described Gosar, Biggs and Mooney as acting amid inflation and bank failures, with the bill giving the Treasury and the Federal Reserve 24 months to publicly disclose all gold holdings and gold transactions before the repegging took effect at the then-market price of gold.[2]


## House Oversight Committee appointment (2023)

In January 2023 the [[white-house|White House]] criticized the appointment of Gosar and Rep. [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] to the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], saying through spokesperson Ian Sams that the Biden administration stood ready to accommodate legitimate oversight needs but that the assignments suggested political theatrics instead.[3] Badlands commentary argued the committee would pursue substantive investigations into border fentanyl, the botched Afghanistan withdrawal, COVID-19 origins and Biden family influence peddling.[3]
