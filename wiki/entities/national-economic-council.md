# National Economic Council

The National Economic Council (NEC) is the White House body responsible for coordinating domestic and international economic policy advice to the President.[1]

## Role in the 2025 tariff debate

In April 2025 the NEC served as a principal public defender of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s sweeping global tariffs after markets sold off sharply.[1] NEC Director [[kevin-hassett|Kevin Hassett]] appeared on ABC's "This Week" on 6 April to argue that he did not expect a "big effect on the consumer in the U.S.," noting that more than 50 countries were "coming to the table" to negotiate.[1] His defense came after the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 2,200 points on the Friday and the S&P 500 lost 10 percent over two days, with vulnerable House Republicans on the defensive over the economic fallout.[1]
