# National Economic Council

The National Economic Council (NEC) is the [[white-house|White House]] body responsible for coordinating domestic and international economic policy advice to the President of the United States. Its director is a senior presidential adviser without Senate confirmation, and in practice serves as the administration's chief economic spokesperson.

In the Badlands corpus the NEC appears through its director, [[kevin-hassett|Kevin Hassett]], who in March 2026 gave the administration's public accounting of the economic effects of the war with [[iran|Iran]] — placing the cost at approximately $12 billion, asserting that existing appropriations were sufficient for the moment, and signalling that further requests might be sent to [[congress|Congress]]. The council's messaging in that period centered on the claim that expanded domestic oil production leaves the United States far less exposed to a Gulf supply shock than it was during the energy crises of the 1970s, and far less exposed than trading partners dependent on imports through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].
