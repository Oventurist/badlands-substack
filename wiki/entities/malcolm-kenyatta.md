# Malcolm Kenyatta

Malcolm Kenyatta is a Pennsylvania state representative and Democratic Party official who served as a vice chair of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]].[1]

## 2025 DNC vice chair re-vote

In June 2025, DNC members voted 294–99 to redo the vice chair election that had installed Kenyatta and [[david-hogg|David Hogg]], setting up two separate votes for the positions the two men held.[1] The challenge originated with Oklahoma DNC member [[kalyn-free|Kalyn Free]], who had run for a vice chair position and lost, and who filed a complaint in late February arguing that the way the election was conducted unfairly advantaged the male candidates over the female vice chair candidates.[1]

The DNC Credentials Committee determined the previous month that it would move the challenge forward and put the matter to the full committee. The re-vote was structured as two virtual ballots: one running June 12–14 for a male vice chair, and a second running June 15–17 open to candidates of any gender.[1] Hogg announced through his Leaders We Deserve group that he would forgo a run for his seat, leaving Kenyatta to contest the redo.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributors treated the episode as ironic given Democratic rhetoric about election denial. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called the participants "election deniers" and described claiming a loss on grounds of "internalized misogyny" — and then holding a new election to appease the loser — as "just plain sad."[1] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] quipped that "the DNC is attempting to abort David Hogg."[1]
