# Ayman al-Zawahiri

Ayman al-Zawahiri (1951–2022) was an Egyptian physician and the second general emir of [[al-qaeda|Al-Qaeda]], succeeding [[osama-bin-laden|Osama Bin Laden]] after his death in 2011. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," al-Zawahiri is cited as the figure who announced that the [[libyan-islamic-fighting-group|Libyan Islamic Fighting Group]] (LIFG) had become an official affiliate of Al-Qaeda.

According to the article, al-Zawahiri made this announcement in early November 2007. The source cites this as evidence that LIFG was not merely a former Al-Qaeda affiliate but an active and integrated part of the global terrorist network at a time when [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and the U.S. government were advocating for military support for the group. The article notes that LIFG had been named a Specially Designated Global Terrorist and added to the UN 1267 Committee Consolidated List of individuals and entities associated with Al-Qaeda or the Taliban in 2001.

The article uses al-Zawahiri's announcement to argue that McCain and other U.S. officials knowingly supported an Al-Qaeda-affiliated organization, and that the U.S. government's own designations proved they were aware of the group's terrorist ties. The source presents al-Zawahiri as a key node in the network connecting Libyan rebels, Al-Qaeda, and the covert U.S. foreign policy apparatus it describes as corrupt and treasonous.
