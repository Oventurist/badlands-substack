# National Democratic Institute

The National Democratic Institute (NDI) is one of the four core surrogate organizations created beneath the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1] Badlands Media describes its function as exercising CIA/NED influence over Democratic Party politics both in the United States and in developing countries.[1]

## Position in the network

NDI is the partisan counterpart to the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]], which performs the same role on the Republican side; the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], which covers unions and the workforce in affiliation with the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]; and the Center for International Private Enterprise, which covers business and chambers of commerce.[1] NED sits at the head of this structure as the grantmaking body funding cut-outs specializing in regime-change operations.[1]

## Origins

The institute belongs to the wave of institutions created in the 1980s after [[ronald-reagan|Reagan]]'s CIA director [[william-casey|William Casey]] secured bipartisan support for moving Cold War influence work from the agency level down to the NGO level, multiplying the deniability layers separating operations from the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the Department of Defense, and the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]
