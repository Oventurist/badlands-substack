# National Democratic Institute

The National Democratic Institute (NDI) is a U.S. non-profit organization founded in 1983 that promotes democracy abroad, closely aligned with the Democratic Party and heavily funded by the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]]. In the Badlands Media article A More Perfect (Labor) Union, NDI is listed among the "CIA-adjacent cut-outs" funded by NED.

## Role in the NED network

The article groups NDI with the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute (IRI)]] and the [[center-for-international-private-enterprise|Center for International Private Enterprise (CIPE)]] as part of the NED-funded network of institutions that the article characterizes as serving the "democracy promotion" agenda. Alongside the AFL-CIO's [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], these groups form what the article describes as NED's funding of CIA-adjacent influence operations abroad.
