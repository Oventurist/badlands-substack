# Lehava

**Lehava** is an Israeli movement led by [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]], known for militant opposition to non-Jewish presence and influence in Israel.[1] Gopstein was arrested in 2015 for inciting violence after calling for all Christians to be expelled from Israel and all churches to be burned; his defense attorney was [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]].[1]

## Role in Badlands commentary

Lehava features in Badlands analysis of the religious dimensions of the Israel–Iran conflict. Contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the movement — and the Ben Gvir–Gopstein legal relationship — while arguing that [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionists]] do not fully understand the character of the Israeli religious-political factions with whom they have allied, particularly regarding plans surrounding the [[third-temple|Third Temple]].[1] A widely shared 2026 video attributed to Ben Gvir was, in his reading, actually Gopstein speaking with Ben Gvir standing behind him.[1]
