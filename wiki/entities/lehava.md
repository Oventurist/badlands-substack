# Lehava

Lehava is an Israeli movement led by [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]].[1] Gopstein was arrested in 2015 for inciting violence after calling for the expulsion of all Christians from Israel and the burning of all churches, and was defended by attorney [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], later Israel's National Security Minister.[1]

The movement drew renewed attention in March 2026 when a viral video circulating as footage of Ben Gvir was identified by Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as showing Gopstein speaking instead, with Ben Gvir standing behind him.[1]

## Cited in Badlands commentary on Netanyahu's cabinet

Badlands commentary describes the ministers [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] and [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] as protégés of Lehava, characterized as a Christian-hating movement, and notes that together they control three of the six seats in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s security cabinet.[2] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] links this bloc to a broader claim that the [[sanhedrin|Sanhedrin]] has returned to the Holy Land and that modern Pharisees hold Netanyahu's political leash.[2]
