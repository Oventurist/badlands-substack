# Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg

**Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg** is [[tesla|Tesla]]'s vehicle manufacturing plant located near Grünheide, Germany.[1]

In May 2024 the site was stormed by hundreds of demonstrators described in coverage as "woke Marxist extremists," with videos circulating on X showing crowds running toward the factory.[1] One X user argued that most of the demonstrators did not come from Grünheide "if any at all," and that they were "not just against Tesla but against any form of capitalism," describing the crowd as "a wild mixture of people mainly believing in the lies of the media and activated from environmental organisations who amplify an ill designed protest."[1]

Observers questioned why self-described anticapitalist protesters targeted Tesla while ignoring other automakers serving wealthy buyers, with X user Warren Redlich asking, "Why not protest factories making BMW 7 series and Mercedes S Class or Maybach?" and answering, "Because they're full of crap."[1] [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] replied to the exchange: "Something super weird is going on, as Tesla was the *only* car company attacked!"[1]
