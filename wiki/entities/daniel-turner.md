# Daniel Turner

Daniel Turner is the founder and executive director of [[power-the-future|Power the Future]], a nonprofit organization advocating for American energy jobs.[1]

In May 2025 Turner pressed for an investigation into eight climate and energy executive orders issued under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], contending that the president had never publicly discussed any of them and that they may have been signed by autopen without his knowledge.[1] Speaking to Fox News Digital, Turner said: "These are not obscure bureaucratic memos; these were foundational shifts in American energy policy, yet not once did Joe Biden speak about them publicly."[1] His remarks placed Power the Future among the organizations pressing the [[autopen-scandal]] into the energy-policy arena, arguing that orders such as the 2023 Arctic drilling ban and the 2021 net-zero-by-2050 directive should be treated as void.[1]

## Breitbart interview on the autopen findings (June 2025)

Turner appeared on Breitbart News Saturday on May 31, 2025, to discuss the report [[power-the-future|Power the Future]] had released the previous week, saying his investigation into several of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s climate-related executive actions had uncovered "criminal" and "evil" evidence that members of the [[white-house|White House]] acted without presidential authorization while Biden was in mental decline.[2] The review found that at least eight major Biden actions were allegedly signed via autopen, among them the January 6 offshore drilling ban and the March 2023 arctic drilling ban.[2] Turner's findings tracked a March 2025 report by the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]]'s Oversight Project, which found that every document it could locate bearing Biden's signature during his presidency — except the one announcing the end of his reelection campaign — carried the same autopen signature.[2]
