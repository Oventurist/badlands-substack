# ZAKA

ZAKA is an Israeli volunteer rescue and recovery organization whose personnel handle the identification and dignified recovery of human remains after mass-casualty events.[1] The group played a central role in the search, rescue and recovery operations conducted in Israeli border communities after the [[october-7-attacks|Hamas assault of October 7, 2023]].[1]

## Role at Kibbutz Be'eri

On the night of Monday, October 9, 2023, ZAKA said it had discovered more than 100 bodies in [[kibbutz-beeri|Kibbutz Be'eri]] near the Gaza border.[1] "Large teams cleared bodies all day from kibbutz Be'eri," the organization said. "There were weapons everywhere, grenades, and RPGs. We cleared 108 bodies and we are not done yet."[1]

The organization's figures were among the earliest concrete casualty accountings from the border kibbutzim and were widely cited as Israeli authorities revised the national death toll upward past 900 killed and nearly 2,400 wounded, with an estimated 150 hostages held by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]
