# Salem Idris

General Salem Idris was the head of the Supreme Military Council of the [[free-syrian-army|Free Syrian Army]] and the figure presented publicly as the moderate face of the Syrian opposition during 2013.[1]

## The May 2013 McCain meeting

Idris is the man pictured beside Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — wearing a white shirt and glasses — in the widely circulated photographs of McCain's May 28, 2013 trip into Syria.[1] According to the Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution," McCain deliberately drew attention to Idris in order to cast the gathering as a meeting with moderate "freedom fighters" while seeking American military intervention and funding to topple [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

The article argues that the presence of Idris in the same photographs as men it identifies as [[isis|ISIS]] figures — including [[abu-bakr-al-baghdadi|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi]] in the background and the ISIS press officer Abu Mosa — disproves the claim advanced by McCain, [[elizabeth-obagy|Elizabeth O'Bagy]] and [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] that moderate rebels and terrorist extremists did not commingle.[1] Also present was Muahmmad Noor, a wanted Syrian terrorist described as specializing in the kidnapping of American journalists and affiliated with the Northern Storm Brigade.[1]
