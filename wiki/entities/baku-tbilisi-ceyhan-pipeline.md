# Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan Pipeline

The Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan pipeline, commonly abbreviated BTC, is a major crude oil export pipeline running from the Caspian basin to the Mediterranean coast of [[turkey|Turkey]]. It provides a route to European markets that bypasses maritime chokepoints such as the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

## Role in the April 2026 Iraqi oil realignment

Badlands Media's April 3, 2026 Brief treats the BTC pipeline as a decisive clue to the geometry of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]]. Reporting on Iraq's revival of a land corridor through [[syria|Syria]] for oil exports to Europe carried a footnote stating that [[iraq|Iraq]] is also sending oil to Turkey via the BTC pipeline.[1]

For the Brief's contributors, that detail indicates that Turkey under President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] and the Iraqi government associated with [[al-malika|Al-Malika]] are working together, which in turn supports the reading that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public hostility toward Iraq is [[kayfabe]].[1] Combined with the Syrian corridor, the BTC route is presented as part of a deliberate diversification of export paths that undercuts [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s March 19 proposal to run all Gulf pipelines to [[israel|Israel]].[1]
