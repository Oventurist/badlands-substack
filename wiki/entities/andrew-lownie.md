# Andrew Lownie

Andrew Lownie is a British historian whose then-forthcoming book formed the basis of the Channel 4 documentary *Edward VIII: Britain's Traitor King*.[1]

The film, drawing on recently declassified reports from the Royal Archives, tells the story of [[edward-viii|Edward VIII]] as a monarch who not only desired a Nazi victory in the Second World War but actively worked toward that goal from his 1936 abdication onward.[1] Matthew Ehret described the resulting revelations as having "sent shockwaves through the western zeitgeist."[1]
