# Brennan Center for Justice

The Brennan Center for Justice is a nonpartisan law and policy institute at NYU School of Law, focused on democracy, voting rights, and criminal justice reform. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a frequently cited source for opposition estimates in the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]] debate.

In the March 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, opponents of the SAVE America Act cited the Brennan Center in arguing the bill's documentary proof-of-citizenship requirement would disenfranchise voters: "The Brennan Center For Justice reports over 21 million Americans lack ready access to the required documents, such as birth certificates or passports, and would face significant challenges and hurdles in getting it, especially people of color, low-income individuals and married and divorced women." Commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] disputed the figure's force by pointing out that all American citizens were already required to hold [[real-id|REAL ID]]-compliant identification under the previous year's federal deadline, asking why the same documents would pose "new barriers" for voter registration. The exchange illustrates the Center's role in the corpus as the leading institutional source for the disenfranchisement argument against citizenship-verification legislation.
