# Brennan Center for Justice

The Brennan Center for Justice is a law and policy institute frequently cited in American voting-rights debates.[1]

## Role in the SAVE America Act debate

During the March 2026 Senate debate over the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]], press coverage cited the Brennan Center's finding that more than 21 million Americans lack ready access to documents such as birth certificates or passports and would face significant challenges and hurdles in obtaining them, with particular impact on people of color, low-income individuals, and married and divorced women.[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] disputed the relevance of that claim, arguing that the same objections had been raised against REAL ID the previous year and asking why Americans who already obtained REAL ID compliance before that deadline would face new barriers under the proof-of-citizenship requirement.[1]

## Opposition to military immigration judges

In a September 2025 analysis, the Brennan Center argued that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s reported authorization of 600 military lawyers to serve as temporary immigration judges "would deprive immigrants of a fair hearing and further erode the line between the military and civilian government," adding that this was true "regardless of the professionalism of the military lawyers."[2] It noted that immigration judges are specially trained administrative judges in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] who oversee deportation hearings.[2] Badlands host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the statement critically while discussing the [[military-lawyers-as-immigration-judges-program|military lawyers as immigration judges program]], questioning why critics held administrative judges in such high regard.[2]
