# Baruch Goldstein

**Baruch Goldstein** (died 1994) was an American-born Israeli physician and one of the most devout followers of [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]], the founder of the [[kahanism|Kahanist]] Jewish supremacy movement.[1]

## Cave of the Patriarchs massacre

Goldstein was outraged by the signing of the Oslo Accords in 1993, which held out the promise of a two-state solution.[1] On February 25, 1994, he walked into the Cave of the Patriarchs — a mosque in Hebron, in the Palestinian territories — carrying an automatic weapon and opened fire on the worshippers, killing 29 people.[1] Badlands Media notes that the death toll was limited only because survivors inside the mosque mobbed and beat him to death.[1]

## Posthumous veneration

Goldstein's gravesite has become a destination for Jewish extremists who adhere to Kahanism.[1] Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] is widely and openly reported in Israel to display a portrait of Goldstein in his living room; Ben-Gvir also served as defence attorney for [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]] in January 2024, when Gopstein faced incitement charges arising from anti-Christian remarks made in 2015.[1]
