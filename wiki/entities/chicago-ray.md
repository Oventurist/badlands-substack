# Chicago Ray

Chicago Ray is the online handle of a conservative social media influencer and commercial truck driver who, in February 2024, publicized an informal boycott of freight deliveries to New York City by truckers protesting the civil fraud judgment against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## The New York City freight boycott

After a Manhattan Supreme Court judge issued a ruling on Friday, February 16, 2024 imposing a $355 million penalty on Trump in his New York civil fraud case, Chicago Ray posted a video recorded from inside his truck describing conversations he had held over the CB radio with other drivers.[1] "I've been on the radio talking to drivers for about the past hour and I've talked to about ten drivers … and they're going to start refusing loads to New York City starting on Monday," he said.[1] He added that he had already spoken with drivers working alongside him who had told their bosses they would not go to "The Big Apple."[1]

Chicago Ray acknowledged he did not know the scope of the action, saying, "I don't know how far across the country this is or how many truckers are going to start denying loads going to New York City, but I'll tell you what — you f–k around and find out."[1] The episode was reported by [[new-york-post|The New York Post]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] placed the trucker action in the lineage of the Canadian [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]], arguing that blue-collar pushback against what he termed globalist elites was being systematically misread by Marxist commentators who attribute worker anger solely to employers and wages.[1] Esler argued that the truckers defending Trump demonstrated a "nuanced understanding of [[lawfare|lawfare]] being used as a cover for a larger operation strategically tied to election interference," and that working-class people had become "narrative-breakers."[1]
