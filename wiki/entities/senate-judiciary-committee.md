# Senate Judiciary Committee

The Senate Judiciary Committee is the standing committee of the United States Senate with jurisdiction over the federal judiciary, the [[justice-department|Justice Department]], and federal law enforcement, and is the principal venue for oversight testimony by the Attorney General and the Director of the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]

## September 2025 Patel hearing

In September 2025 the committee hosted testimony by FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], during which a yearslong feud between Patel and Senator [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] of California erupted into a shouting match that briefly derailed the hearing.[1] The confrontation occurred when Schiff questioned Patel about the Justice Department's decision to move [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] — an associate of the convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein — to a lower-security prison facility.[1] Schiff asked whether Patel believed the American people were "stupid" enough to accept his answer that the Bureau of Prisons had independently made the decision rather than it being a politically motivated move by the White House.[1]

Patel interrupted to say, "What I am doing is protecting this country … and combating the weaponization of intelligence by the likes of you," adding, "We have countlessly proven you to be a liar in Russiangate, in January 6. You are the biggest fraud to ever sit in the United States Senate," and going on to call Schiff "a political buffoon at best."[1] Committee chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] appeared at one point to cut Schiff's microphone, leaving Patel to continue unopposed.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the hearing as annoying on the grounds that committee members were so tone deaf that they were still producing political advertising for the following year's elections rather than attempting to demonstrate any value to the public, concluding that there is no value exchange between the governing class and the citizenry — "only pain."[1] She singled out the Patel–Schiff clash as the most entertaining moment of the proceedings and asked whether anyone intended to prosecute Schiff or merely yell at him.[1]

## Scott Green's tenure

A January 2024 Badlands feature by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], citing a 2020 [[politico|Politico]] report, noted that lobbyist [[scott-green|Scott Green]] accepted an offer from [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in the early 1980s for a job on the Senate Judiciary Committee, where he worked for a decade in roles including staff assistant and senior advisor before moving to the private sector and co-founding the [[lafayette-group|Lafayette Group]].[2]
