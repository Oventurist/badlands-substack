# Senate Judiciary Committee

The United States Senate Committee on the Judiciary is the standing Senate committee with jurisdiction over the federal judiciary, federal law enforcement and a broad range of legislation. The Badlands Media essay [[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] devotes a section to the committee's powers because of the role it assigns to chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] in a coming prosecution and declassification phase.

The article enumerates the committee's oversight powers as: overseeing the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]; considering executive and judicial nominations; reviewing pending legislation; holding hearings and investigations; and exercising legislative jurisdiction. It singles out DOJ oversight as "a huge oversight power, especially considering how much [[barack-obama|Obama]] corrupted and weaponized the DOJ."

On legislative jurisdiction, the essay explains that the committee's remit is broad, encompassing proposed constitutional amendments and legislation related to federal criminal law, human rights law, immigration, intellectual property, antitrust law and internet privacy. [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] maps these categories onto [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated objectives — ending the two-tier justice system, an executive order on crimes against humanity, ending illegal immigration, protecting American intellectual property, breaking up monopolies, and an internet bill of rights — and concludes that Grassley's committee will be central to Trump's second-term agenda.

The article further claims that [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] handed evidence from their [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary]] investigations to Grassley, making the Senate committee the repository of what the author calls evidence of treason connected to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], [[doug-band|Doug Band]] and [[teneo|Teneo]].
