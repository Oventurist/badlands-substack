# William Donovan

William “Wild Bill” Donovan was the head of the [[office-of-strategic-services]] during World War II.[1] He directed Colonel [[paul-helliwell]] to covertly assist [[chiang-kai-shek]]'s [[kuomintang]] army with weapons, a mission to which Helliwell added opiate trafficking.[1] Donovan created the [[world-commerce-corporation]] specifically as the vehicle through which money would be funnelled in the covert financing network designed with [[allen-dulles]], [[james-jesus-angleton]] and [[william-stephenson]].[1]
