# Latham & Watkins

Latham & Watkins is a large international law firm. It appears in the Badlands corpus in connection with the congressional inquiry into [[kathryn-ruemmler|Kathryn Ruemmler]]: Justice Department records indicate that Ruemmler's relationship with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] began in 2014 while she was a partner at the firm, and continued through 2019.[1] Ruemmler later moved to [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] as its top lawyer, resigning in 2026 amid scrutiny of those ties.[1]

## Withdrawal from the Hunter Biden defense

In August 2023, three Latham & Watkins attorneys representing [[hunter-biden|Robert Hunter Biden]] — Brian C. McManus, Timothy H. McCarten and Matthew S. Salerno — filed a motion to withdraw as counsel, bringing the total number of departures from his defense team to five.[2] The motion came the day after Delaware U.S. District Judge [[maryellen-noreika|Maryellen Noreika]] granted a [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] request to dismiss Biden's two tax misdemeanors without prejudice, opening the possibility of charges being refiled in Washington, D.C. or the Central District of California.[2]

The filing stated that Biden "has been advised of, and consents to, our withdrawal," that it would cause him no material adverse effect or prejudice, and that he remained "completely satisfied" with the continued representation of [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]] and co-counsel Jones.[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] wrote that the exit made sense on its face given the client's toxicity and looming exposure, and that "those jumping ship are probably wiser than the rest."[2]
