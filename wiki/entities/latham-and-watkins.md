# Latham & Watkins

Latham & Watkins is a large international law firm with a substantial Washington, D.C. practice representing corporate and government-facing clients.

The firm enters the Badlands corpus through the July 17, 2026 Badlands Brief account of the [[house-oversight-committee]] interview of [[kathryn-ruemmler]]. Justice Department records cited in that reporting indicate that Ruemmler's documented relationship with [[jeffrey-epstein]] began in 2014, while she was a partner at Latham & Watkins following her tenure as White House Counsel under [[barack-obama]], and continued through 2019. Ruemmler subsequently left the firm to become general counsel at [[goldman-sachs]], a post she resigned amid scrutiny of the Epstein association.

In the wider Badlands framing, the revolving door between senior executive-branch legal posts, elite private firms such as Latham & Watkins, and Wall Street general counsel roles is treated as a structural feature of the [[deep-state]] rather than an incidental career pattern.
