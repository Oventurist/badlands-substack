# National Hockey League

The **National Hockey League (NHL)** is the professional ice hockey league of North America.[1] In January 2023 the league was drawn into the culture-war debate over corporate and sporting participation in LGBT advocacy after one of its players declined to wear a themed jersey.[1]

## 2023 Pride Night controversy

Philadelphia Flyers defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] refused to wear an LGBT "pride" jersey during warmups at the Flyers' Pride Night promotion at the Wells Fargo Arena on Tuesday, January 17, 2023, citing his Russian Orthodox Christian beliefs.[1] The league issued a statement on the incident; because the NHL declined to completely cave to activist demands and did not discipline Provorov for standing up for his convictions, LGBT activists reacted with fury.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentators [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute1776]] argued the episode placed the NHL on the same slippery slope previously walked by the NFL and MLB, in which fans who want sport as an escape are instead confronted with political virtue signalling.[1] They framed the resulting activist campaign against Provorov as an example of [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] targeting religious dissent.[1]

## Pride Night reconsidered (2023)

In late March 2023 commissioner [[gary-bettman|Gary Bettman]] suggested that the league's "Pride Night" promotions might not survive, telling a CTV Ottawa reporter that the practice was "something that we're going to have to evaluate in the offseason."[2] He acknowledged that it was one issue where players "for a variety of reasons may not feel comfortable wearing the uniform as a form of endorsement."[2]

[[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], described the episode as a sustained headache for the NHL and argued that the league had resisted the agenda more openly than most institutions, citing Ivan Provorov, James Reimer, the Staal brothers, the Chicago Blackhawks and the Minnesota Wild.[2]
