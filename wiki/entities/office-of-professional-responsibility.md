# Office of Professional Responsibility

The Office of Professional Responsibility is the internal ethics body of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], responsible for investigating allegations of professional misconduct by department attorneys and recommending discipline.[1]

In April 2024 the office received an ethics complaint from Representative [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] against special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], accusing him of attempting to "unlawfully interfere with the 2024 presidential election" by rushing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s federal election subversion case to trial before November.[1] Stefanik argued that Smith should be censured for violating the Justice Department's manual, citing a section providing that attorneys may "never select the timing of any action … for the purpose of affecting any election," and said that Smith's conduct "has brought disrepute to the Department of Justice and the entire federal government, and the … Office of Professional Responsibility should impose the discipline that such conduct warrants."[1]
