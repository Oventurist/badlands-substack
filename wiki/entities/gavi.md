# GAVI

GAVI is a global vaccine alliance named in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* among the funders whose contracts allegedly compromised the neutrality of Harvard-affiliated public health researchers.[1]

## Role in the article

The article states that researchers framing the recommendations delivered to the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] through the [[harvard-global-health-institute|Harvard Global Health Institute]] and the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|T.H. Chan School of Public Health]] "were not neutral scientists," with many under contract to [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] initiatives, GAVI, or consulting arms such as the McKinsey Global Institute.[1] The recommendations in question included behavioral nudging frameworks to increase vaccine uptake, justification models for quarantine extension, and algorithmic impact assessments for misinformation response.[1]
