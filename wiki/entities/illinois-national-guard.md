# Illinois National Guard

The **Illinois National Guard** is the state militia force of Illinois, whose activity in October 2025 drew attention amid the federal protection deployment to the state.[1]

On October 7, 2025 — the same day that 200 [[texas-national-guard|Texas National Guard]] troops arrived in Illinois to protect federal personnel and property amid anti-immigration protests — the Illinois National Guard was reported to have been ordered to conduct a "training" evolution.[1] Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] treated the coincidence as significant, juxtaposing the Texas Guard's movement into the state with the Illinois Guard's sudden training order and concluding that "shits about to get spicy."[1] The episode unfolded against rhetoric from Chicago mayor [[brandon-johnson|Brandon Johnson]] invoking "Civil War."[1]
