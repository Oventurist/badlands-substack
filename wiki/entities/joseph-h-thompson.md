# Joseph H. Thompson

Joseph H. Thompson is the Acting United States Attorney for the District of Minnesota.[1]

## Minnesota pandemic fraud prosecutions

Thompson's office announced the August 2025 guilty plea of [[guhaad-hashi-said|Guhaad Hashi Said]], a former campaign "enforcer" for Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]], on charges of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering tied to a pandemic-era child nutrition program scheme.[1]

Commenting on the case, Thompson said, "The scale of the fraud in Minnesota is staggering, and every rock we turn over reveals more. We must be honest and clear-eyed about the scope of this problem, because ending it will take an unyielding, all-hands-on-deck effort from all of us."[1]
