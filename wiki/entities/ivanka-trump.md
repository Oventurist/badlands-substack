# Ivanka Trump

Ivanka Trump is the daughter of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the wife of [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]]. In May 2026 she was named as the intended target of an alleged assassination plot with claimed links to Iran's [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

## 2026 assassination plot allegation

Federal authorities accused Iraqi national [[mohammad-baqer-al-saadi|Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi]] of plotting to kill her in retaliation for the 2020 drone strike, ordered by her father, that killed Iranian commander [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]].[1] The suspect allegedly obtained a blueprint of her Florida home and posted a map of the area surrounding the residence she shares with Kushner, along with a threat that neither "palaces nor the Secret Service" would protect Americans.[1] Sources told the New York Post the plot was intended to "burn down the house of Trump."[1]

## January 6 grand jury subpoena

In February 2023, Ivanka Trump and her husband [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] were subpoenaed by special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] to testify before a federal grand jury about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s efforts to stay in power after losing the 2020 election and his role in the pro-Trump mob's attack on the Capitol on January 6, 2021.[2] The subpoenas were reported as evidence of how deeply into Trump's inner circle Smith's investigation was reaching, and that no potential high-level witness was off limits.[2]

The disclosure followed by two weeks the revelation that Smith had subpoenaed former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]], who intended to resist on "speech or debate" clause grounds tied to his role as president of the Senate.[2] Because both Ivanka Trump and Kushner had served as White House officials, it was unclear whether Trump would attempt to block their testimony by asserting executive privilege, as he had with other witnesses; he had not tried to stop them from appearing before the [[january-6-committee|House select committee]] investigating the attack.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] framed the subpoenas as favorable news, predicting that Smith would clear Trump's team while surfacing evidence of 2020 election fraud.[2]
