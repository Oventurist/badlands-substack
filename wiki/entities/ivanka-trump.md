# Ivanka Trump

Ivanka Trump is the daughter of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the wife of [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], with whom she maintains a residence in Florida.

## Alleged assassination plot, 2026

According to the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," an Iraqi national with alleged ties to Iran's [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]], [[mohammad-baqer-al-saadi|Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi]], was accused of plotting to assassinate her in retaliation for President Trump's ordering of the 2020 drone strike that killed Iranian commander [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]].

Al-Saadi allegedly pledged to kill Ivanka Trump and obtained a blueprint of her Florida home. Sources told the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] that the plot was intended to "burn down the house of Trump" in revenge for Soleimani's death in Baghdad. He reportedly posted a map of the area surrounding the Florida residence she shares with Kushner alongside a threat claiming that neither "palaces nor the Secret Service" would protect Americans, and that operatives were in a "stage of surveillance and analysis." Al-Saadi was arrested in [[turkey|Turkey]] on May 15, 2026 and extradited to the United States.
