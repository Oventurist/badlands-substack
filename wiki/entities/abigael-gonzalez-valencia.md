# Abigael González Valencia

Abigael González Valencia is a Mexican cartel figure identified as a leader of [[los-cuinis|Los Cuinis]], a criminal group closely aligned with the [[jalisco-new-generation-cartel|Jalisco New Generation Cartel]].[1]

## Transfer to the United States (2025)

González Valencia was among 26 high-ranking cartel members whom Mexico agreed on August 12, 2025 to send to the United States as part of a major deal with the Trump administration amid the U.S. crackdown on drug smuggling.[1] The Trump administration promised not to seek the death penalty for any of the cartel members transferred under the arrangement.[1] Another individual included in the same group was wanted in connection with the 2008 killing of a Los Angeles County sheriff's deputy.[1] The transfer came after Mexico had sent 30 other highly sought drug cartel members to the United States in February 2025.[1]
