# Abdullah Omar Naseef

Abdullah Omar Naseef is a Saudi academic and official described in the Badlands account as a wealthy Saudi financier of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and as the patron of the Abedin family.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He has held numerous senior positions in Saudi Arabia, including Vice-President of the Kingdom's Shura Council, President of King Abdul Aziz University, and — most significantly — Secretary-General of the [[muslim-world-league|Muslim World League]] from 1983 to 1993.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Naseef has also served as an officer of the International Islamic Council for Dawa and Relief (IICDR), an umbrella body of more than one hundred Islamic organizations headquartered in Cairo, Egypt, whose affiliates include groups associated with the global [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]], Hamas fundraising, or support for Al Qaeda.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He is additionally President of the Muslim World Congress.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) His long ties to the Brotherhood run through two channels in particular: media and charities.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## The Abedin connection

Naseef established the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]] and recruited the parents of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] to run it, bringing the family to [[jeddah|Jeddah]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Institute's main purpose since its founding in the late 1970s has been to publish the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]], and Naseef remained closely involved with the journal for decades, sitting on its advisory board.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He was removed from the masthead in 2003, around the time he was named as a defendant in the civil suit brought by victims of the 9/11 attacks.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Huma Abedin served as assistant editor of the journal for twelve years, overlapping with Naseef for seven of them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Terror financing and education

Beyond the terror-funding dimension of the Muslim World League's Saudi-funded effort to spread Islamic supremacist ideology globally, Naseef is described as having lavishly underwritten efforts to indoctrinate young Muslims at educational institutions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He also partnered with Wael Jalaidan — who ran the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] at the University of Arizona in the 1980s and went on to help [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found Al Qaeda — to establish the [[rabita-trust|Rabita Trust]], formally designated a terrorist organization under U.S. law for funding Al Qaeda.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
