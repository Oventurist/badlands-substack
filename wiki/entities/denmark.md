# Denmark

Denmark is the Northern European kingdom whose realm includes [[greenland|Greenland]] as a semi-autonomous territory, a status that placed Copenhagen at the center of the January 2026 confrontation with the United States over Arctic control.[1]

## Greenland dispute with the United States

Denmark was among the eight European states hit by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s announced 10% tariff on imports, a measure explicitly tied to U.S. ambitions regarding Greenland and scheduled to begin February 1, 2026.[1] European leaders, including Danish officials, emphasized support for the sovereignty of Greenland and Denmark and condemned the use of tariffs as coercive leverage.[1] The Danish Prime Minister issued a grave public warning that any American move to seize the island would "destroy NATO."[1]

## Joint Arctic Command

Denmark's Joint Arctic Command, the military headquarters responsible for the Arctic portion of the Danish realm, stated in mid-January 2026 that there were no immediate Chinese or Russian naval threats near Greenland waters, and reaffirmed continued [[nato|NATO]] collaboration despite the diplomatic rupture with Washington.[1]

## Greenland standoff (January 2026)

Following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 2026 Truth Social post declaring US control of [[greenland|Greenland]] necessary for American national security, Danish officials met Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] in Washington and reiterated that Greenland is not for sale, noting that Greenlanders had recently said they wish to remain with Denmark.[2] Greenland's international status is formally determined by Denmark, which defers to the territory's self-governing authorities.[2] The Danish foreign minister told a post-meeting press conference that "it's clear the president has a wish to conquer Greenland."[2]

Denmark subsequently began sending military resources into Greenland as tensions escalated, joined by other European [[nato|NATO]] members, with contributions catalogued by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as thirteen German reconnaissance troops, two Norwegians, one Briton, an unknown number of Swedes and French units under [[operation-arctic-endurance|Operation Arctic Endurance]].[2]

## "Greenland is not for sale" (January 2026)

Danish officials reiterated in January 2026 that [[greenland|Greenland]] is not for sale, responding to renewed public statements by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] expressing interest in acquiring the territory.[3] Denmark said Greenland's status is determined by international law and by the self-governing rights of Greenland's people.[3] The Danish prime minister publicly warned that a US military attack on Greenland would undermine [[nato|NATO]] — a statement Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] paired with the simultaneous, quieter European effort to assemble a multinational NATO Arctic force as an appeasement package.[3]

## Nord Stream investigation dropped (2024)

On Monday, February 26, 2024, Denmark dropped its investigation into what it had described as the "deliberate sabotage" of the Nord Stream gas pipelines in 2022, citing insufficient grounds to pursue a criminal case.[4] The Copenhagen police said in a statement that "The joint investigation conducted by the Copenhagen Police and the Danish Security and Intelligence Services (PET) into the Nord Stream explosions has been concluded."[4] Gas leaks in both Nord Stream 1 and 2 had been discovered at the end of September 2022 from infrastructure just outside Swedish and Danish territorial waters in the Baltic Sea.[4]

Denmark's decision followed [[sweden|Sweden]]'s move in early February 2024 to close its own preliminary investigation for lack of jurisdiction, leaving [[germany|Germany]] as the only country still investigating the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]].[4] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the Danish rationale as a slight spin on Sweden's excuse and argued the closures were consistent with suspicions of a CIA-planned operation against German energy infrastructure.[4]

## Sass Larsen child pornography case (2025)

On September 1, 2025, former Danish trade minister [[henrik-sass-larsen|Henrik Sass Larsen]], once a senior member of the ruling Social Democrats, was sentenced by the Copenhagen City Court to four months in prison for possession of child pornography after admitting to holding more than 6,000 photographs and 2,000 videos of child sexual abuse.[5] Police uncovered the material during searches of his devices in 2023 and 2024, and the case, which became public in March 2024, cost him his party membership.[5] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the sentence as evidence that "something is rotten in Denmark" and used it to argue that the United States should take [[greenland|Greenland]] from Danish control.[5]

## Conscription of women

In March 2025 Denmark announced that it would begin drafting women from 2026, with Copenhagen saying the move would ensure "gender equality" in its armed forces and help meet [[nato|NATO]] commitments.[6] The decision was cited as a precedent when [[latvia|Latvia]]'s defense minister proposed in September 2025 that Latvia extend compulsory service to women from 2028.[6]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] tied the policy to earlier Danish friction with the United States over Greenland and to scandals involving Danish politicians, arguing such developments explain why Badlands commentators favored diplomatic engagement with leaders such as Vladimir Putin, Xi Jinping, Kim Jong Un, Mohammed bin Salman and Nicolás Maduro in 2025.[6]
