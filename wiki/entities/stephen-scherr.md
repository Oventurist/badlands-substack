# Stephen Scherr

Stephen Scherr is an American business executive who served as chief executive officer of [[hertz|Hertz Global Holdings]] during the company's 2023–2024 retreat from electric vehicles.[1]

On a third-quarter 2023 earnings call in late October, Scherr told investors that Hertz would begin reducing its EV fleet because of high repair costs relative to the rest of the fleet, which had hurt the company's bottom line, stating that "EV's will be slower than our prior expectations."[1] In the same period he told CNBC's Jim Cramer that electric vehicles were seeing more damage than combustion-engine cars and that the cost to repair them was much higher.[1] The decisions he announced culminated in Hertz dumping roughly 20,000 EVs onto the used car market and reinvesting part of the proceeds in internal combustion engine vehicles.[1]
