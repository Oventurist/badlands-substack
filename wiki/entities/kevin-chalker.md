# Kevin Chalker

Kevin Chalker is a former [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] officer and the principal behind the private intelligence firm Global Risk Advisors, a company that has performed contract work for Qatari clients.[1]

## Doha presentation and media-asset claims

Badlands Media commentary cites a 2017 presentation that Chalker and Global Risk Advisors are said to have delivered to Qatari figures in Doha, in which the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] was specifically named as a Muslim Brotherhood media asset.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked that presentation to argue that the Times is "a known CIA asset," using it to discredit the paper's January 2025 reporting on the economic risks of designating Mexican cartels as foreign terrorist organizations.[1]

That reporting argued that isolating American companies from cartel activity would be nearly impossible because criminal groups are embedded across the legal economy — from avocado farming to Mexico's billion-dollar tourism industry — and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order could force U.S. firms to abandon Mexican business rather than risk sanctions.[1] The Badlands commentary mocked the avocado framing as too on-the-nose for the paper and characterized the argument as shameless, noting congressional investigations from the 1970s that documented CIA entanglement with drug cartels in Central and South America.[1]
