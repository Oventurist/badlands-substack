# Martin Geddes

Martin Geddes is a writer and commentator on information warfare frequently cited within the [[qanon|QAnon]]-adjacent [[truth-community|truth community]]. He is quoted in the Badlands Media article "Countering an Ocean of Deceit" for a formulation the author describes as brilliant: that the main goal of "our side" of the [[fifth-generation-warfare|5G war]] is to help the masses understand that they are in a 5G war in the first place.[1]

The article builds on this framing with its own metaphor, treating the public's inability to perceive that a conflict is underway as the cognitive equivalent of the Death Star's outer shell — a nearly impenetrable outer layer of defence that requires a precision strike to bypass.[1] The author adds the caveat that most people will refuse to see the conflict until the eleventh hour, and that it is enough to do what one reasonably can.[1]
