# Senate Select Committee on Intelligence

The **Senate Select Committee on Intelligence** is the U.S. Senate panel responsible for oversight of the American intelligence community.[1]

## Role in the Russiagate dispute

The committee's bipartisan 2020 report on Russian interference in the 2016 election became a central point of contention in July 2025, when [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s office cited it in rebutting the Trump administration's claims of a "treasonous conspiracy," stating that the intelligence findings "were affirmed in a 2020 report by the bipartisan Senate Intelligence Committee."[1]

Badlands writer [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] countered that the committee had released a heavily redacted report whose conclusions relied on testimony from the same officials — including [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]] — whom Trump accuses of committing treason, rendering the affirmation circular.[1] The dispute formed part of the broader controversy over the [[2025-gabbard-treasonous-conspiracy-disclosure|2025 Gabbard treasonous conspiracy disclosure]].[1]
