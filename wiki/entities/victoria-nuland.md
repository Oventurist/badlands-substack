# Victoria Nuland

Victoria Nuland is the American diplomat described in "A Bright Red Line" as "perpetually bloodthirsty" and identified as U.S. ambassador to [[nato|NATO]] during the Bush administration.[1]

The article places her, together with Condoleezza Rice, among the Bush-era officials who were affirmatively discussing the expansion of NATO to include Ukraine specifically — the period in which [[william-burns|William Burns]], then working in the intelligence community, wrote his warning cable from Moscow.[1] Nuland is also referenced through the late Gonzalo Lira, one of the independent voices the article credits with surfacing an alternative account of the conflict.[1]
