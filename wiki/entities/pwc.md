# PwC

PwC (PricewaterhouseCoopers) is a global professional-services and consulting firm cited by Badlands Media as one of the institutional actors advancing [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] adoption during 2023 despite contemporaneous calls for a development pause.[1]

In a June 2023 statement quoted in the article, PwC wrote: "AI isn't just a new set of tools. It's the new world. From automation to augmentation, generative AI and beyond, AI is changing everything. $15.7 trillion—that's the global economic growth that AI will provide by 2030, according to PwC research. Who will get the biggest share of this prize? Those who take the lead now." The firm described itself as already a veteran at helping clients navigate AI safely and strategically, institutionalizing and deploying AI across organizations and applications in a way that is explainable, secure, and robust.[1]

Badlands groups PwC with the [[united-nations|United Nations]], the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], and [[mckinsey-and-company|McKinsey & Company]] as evidence that there is no pause in what it calls the globalist machine — that all these entities are proceeding full steam ahead on AI research while public access to consumer tools such as [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] is framed as the threat to the future of humanity.[1]
