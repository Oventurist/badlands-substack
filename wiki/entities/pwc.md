# PwC

PwC (PricewaterhouseCoopers) is a multinational professional-services and consulting firm. It appears in the July 2023 Badlands Media article "[[artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason]]" as one of four institutions cited to demonstrate that, despite public calls for a development pause, the global corporate and governance establishment was proceeding at full speed on artificial intelligence.

The article quotes PwC's June 2023 AI practice materials:

> "AI isn't just a new set of tools. It's the new world. From automation to augmentation, generative AI and beyond, AI is changing everything. $15.7 trillion—that's the global economic growth that AI will provide by 2030, according to PwC research. Who will get the biggest share of this prize? Those who take the lead now. With AI pilots and projects live all over the globe, and new use cases added daily, at PwC we're already veterans at helping clients navigate the new world of AI safely and strategically."

Placed beside statements from the [[united-nations|United Nations]], the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], and [[mckinsey-and-company|McKinsey & Company]], the PwC material is used to support the article's contention that institutional AI development continues unabated while restrictions are urged only on public access to consumer tools such as [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]].
