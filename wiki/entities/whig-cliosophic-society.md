# Whig-Cliosophic Society

The **Whig-Cliosophic Society** is a Princeton debating and political society named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as one of two grooming portals at [[princeton-university|Princeton University]].[1]

Where the [[ivy-club|Ivy Club]] is said to handle the financial aristocracy, the Whig-Cliosophic Society is described as training "rhetorical masters and global NGO liaisons," supplying the persuasive and diplomatic layer of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]] alongside Princeton's central-banking pipeline.[1] The article groups it with the broader class of Ivy societies it characterizes as instruments of "loyalty, leverage, and legacy" rather than mere social networks.[1]
