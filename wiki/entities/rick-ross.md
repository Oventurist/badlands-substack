# Rick Ross

Rick Ross is described in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as an aspiring young tennis player who could not even read and who became fabulously rich selling drugs in Los Angeles.[1]

## Alleged connection to CIA trafficking

Carlson presents Ross as the retail end of an operation in which the [[cia|CIA]] smuggled narcotics in from South and Central America to be sold on the South Central streets of Los Angeles.[1] He characterizes the arrangement as a racket in which the agency traded guns in Central America for drugs and then transported those drugs to American cities, with the resulting drug trade filling US private prisons.[1] In the essay's framing, Ross belongs to the class of visible participants who are scapegoats for a structure above them — comparable to the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] standing in front of the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] — rather than principals with genuine control.[1]
