# Rick Ross

Rick Ross ("Freeway" Rick Ross) is described by Erik Carlson as an aspiring young tennis player who could not even read and who became fabulously rich selling drugs in Los Angeles.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Role in the article

Ross serves in the essay as the visible retail face of an operation Carlson attributes to the [[cia|CIA]]: the agency is said to have smuggled drugs in from South and Central America for sale on the South Central streets of Los Angeles, with Ross distributing them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The resulting drug trade filled the [[prison-privatization|privatized US prisons]] created in the same period, in tandem with the [[crime-bill-1994|1994 Crime Bill]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

He belongs to the class of figures Carlson later generalizes about — groups used as scapegoats whose control and power are not what the public has been led to believe, with puppet masters pulling their strings.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
