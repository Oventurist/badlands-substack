# Public Banking Institute

The Public Banking Institute is an American advocacy organisation promoting the establishment of publicly owned banks. It appears in A Seismic Shift through its chair, [[ellen-brown|Ellen Brown]], whose book *Banking on the People* is quoted by In These Times in support of the claim that a public banking system can fund the goods, services and infrastructure required by the people and the economy without unsustainable debt, taxation or environmental degradation.

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] situates the institute within the century-long argument for [[public-banking|public banking]] that begins with the [[bank-of-north-dakota|Bank of North Dakota]] and continues in the contemporary state-level campaigns in California and nearly two dozen other states. Its case — that public funds should stabilise local economies rather than be extracted to fuel Wall Street speculation — parallels the arguments the article makes for reviving the [[postal-savings-system|Postal Savings System]] and creating [[fedaccounts|FedAccounts]].
