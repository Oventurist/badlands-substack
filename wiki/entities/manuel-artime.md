# Manuel Artime

Manuel Artime was a Cuban exile leader described by Frontline as "the agency's favorite Cuban," handpicked by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] to command both the [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion and the covert operations that followed.[1] He led [[brigade-2506]], the CIA-created and CIA-trained exile force based in Miami.[1]

Artime's most consequential act in the Badlands Media account is his 1972 recruitment of the young accountant [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]], for whom he arranged CIA training in accounting.[1] Milian Rodriguez went on to launder billions of dollars for the [[medellin-cartel]] — a chain Joe Lange presents as direct proof that the agency did not merely tolerate the drug trade but trained the men who cleaned its proceeds.[1]

Milian Rodriguez told Frontline that Artime "ran covert operations out of Miami for the CIA," that the operation was "very large" and "very active, all over Central and South America," and that Artime "was in charge of, among other things, the [[watergate|Watergate]] burglars."[1] Milian Rodriguez said he personally made payments to the Watergate burglars, and that after Watergate the Miami group Artime was running was disbanded.[1] [[felix-rodriguez]], later a key Contra-era operative, was a member of the same brigade Artime commanded.[1]
