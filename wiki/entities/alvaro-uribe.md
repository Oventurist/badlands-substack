# Álvaro Uribe

**Álvaro Uribe Vélez** is a former President of Colombia and one of the dominant figures of the Colombian right, long associated with close security cooperation with the United States and with a hard line against left-wing guerrilla movements and the government of neighboring [[venezuela|Venezuela]].

## Role in the Tren de Aragua dispute

Uribe appears in the Badlands corpus principally through Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s January 2025 address at the inauguration of the "Bolivarian Shield 2025" military and police exercises. In that speech Maduro described [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] and Tren del Llano as constructed instruments designed to attack the Venezuelan people, destabilize the country, and create pretexts for foreign intervention. He named Uribe and fellow former Colombian president [[ivan-duque|Iván Duque]] as "public enemies number one" of Venezuela's Bolivarian National Armed Forces and of the Venezuelan people, tying both men to paramilitarism and drug trafficking originating in Colombia.

Badlands commentary places these accusations alongside later statements by Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] alleging that Duque had collaborated with the American [[deep-state|Deep State]] to use Colombia as a staging ground for an invasion of Venezuela — reading the Uribe–Duque political lineage as the regional instrument of a longer-running U.S. destabilization project.
