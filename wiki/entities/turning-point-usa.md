# Turning Point USA

Turning Point USA (TPUSA) is the conservative youth organization founded by [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1]

## Role in the Owens–Robinson dispute

In July 2026, TPUSA became a focal point of a public rupture among prominent conservatives over the preliminary hearing of [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], the accused in Charlie Kirk's murder.[1] [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] argued that Robinson did not act alone, implicating [[israel|Israel]], Egypt, the US military, TPUSA staff, and Kirk's widow [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]].[1]

Politico reported that TPUSA staff received death threats and increased security after Owens' broadcasts named them.[1] Owens responded on [[x-platform|X]] by alleging a coordinated "Zionist PR" campaign against her, and her spokesperson accused TPUSA figures of targeting her for months.[1]

## Politico investigation and security response (July 2026)

A [[politico|Politico]] Magazine report published July 11, 2026 examined the feud in detail, noting that [[candace-owens|Owens]] worked for Turning Point from 2017 to 2019 before turning her podcast toward accusations that people associated with the organization were withholding information about [[charlie-kirk|Kirk]]'s killing.[2] Politico reported that Turning Point increased security at its Phoenix headquarters and at several employees' homes after staff received threats following Owens' broadcasts, citing three anonymous people with direct knowledge of the arrangements.[2] Contracted camera operator [[terryl-farnsworth|Terryl Farnsworth]] and his family went into hiding for over a week after multiple death threats.[2]

The organization served Owens with a cease-and-desist letter in January 2026 demanding that she stop suggesting it knew about the assassination beforehand, participated in it, or concealed the truth afterward.[2] Kirk's former bodyguard filed a defamation suit against Owens in May, and in the same month a man was arrested for allegedly threatening to kill [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]] before a Turning Point event in Texas.[2]

## Criticism of TPUSA-aligned influencers

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Turning Point-aligned influencers — among them [[benny-johnson|Benny Johnson]], [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]] and [[brandon-tatum|Officer Tatum]] — employed tactics reminiscent of the [[derek-chauvin|Derek Chauvin]] trial in pressing for a rapid conviction of [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], and objected to their inversion of the Constitution in claiming that Erika Kirk and the organization possess a right to a speedy trial.[2]

## December 2025 convention and the Owens-Kirk detente

Ahead of TPUSA's December 2025 convention, CEO [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]] met with podcaster [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] for a four-and-a-half-hour session after weeks of tension, which Owens said left "tensions thawed."[3] Badlands commentators derided the reconciliation as scripted "kumbaya" ahead of what they called the "TPUSA Soap Opera Convention," with [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] arguing that the ease of predicting the participants' moves in advance was itself evidence the sequence was staged.[3]

## Proposed role in military recruiting (September 2025)

In September 2025, [[pentagon|Pentagon]] leaders considering a recruiting campaign built around the legacy of assassinated founder [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] were reported to be weighing the use of Turning Point USA chapters at schools across the United States as military recruitment centers.[4] The effort was led by [[anthony-tata|Anthony Tata]], undersecretary of defense for personnel and readiness, and discussed slogans included "Charlie has awakened a generation of warriors."[4]

## Student Action Summit and the Epstein controversy (July 2025)

At Turning Point USA's Student Action Summit on Friday, July 11, 2025, [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] suggested that [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] had been working for Israeli intelligence throughout the period in which he was allegedly trafficking underage girls.[5] Badlands commentary reported that "hundreds" of attendees walked out during the remarks while others cheered, and that [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], during a later onstage discussion with [[megyn-kelly|Megyn Kelly]], lamented that the [[epstein-files|Epstein files]] storyline had taken over his conference.[5] The Badlands writer disagreed with that assessment, arguing that the Epstein story was the main reason many viewers tuned in and that it energized the messaging of speakers including [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and Carlson.[5]

## 2023 DeSantis invitation

In July 2023, [[turning-point-action|Turning Point Action]] — the organization's political affiliate, led at the operational level by [[tyler-bowyer|Tyler Bowyer]] — invited every Republican presidential candidate to a West Palm Beach event.[6] Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] declined, prompting founder [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] to share [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]]'s report with the comment "Too bad!"[6]

## AmericaFest 2025 and the antisemitism feud

The December 2025 Turning Point USA convention became the focal point of a MAGA feud over whether antisemitic figures such as [[nick-fuentes|Nick Fuentes]] would be permitted in the Republican coalition after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] leaves the stage.[7] Observers watched to see whether Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] would draw a red line against bigotry; in his closing speech he declined, saying "I didn't bring a list of conservatives to denounce or to de-platform" and that MAGA Republicans have "far more important work to do than canceling each other."[7] That followed an address by [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] criticizing Fuentes, [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] and [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] as "grifters" who "do not deserve your time."[7]

Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote that TPUSA had signaled that "full messaging compliance will be necessary going forward" for members of what he called its "creepy club," and argued that the [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] stadium funeral telethon and the "AmFest" convention could plausibly be viewed as soft disclosure of "the total corruption of the institutional mainstream of American Judeo (inverted) Christianity and the megachurch style of personal advancement through pseudo-worship."[7]

## Florida partnership on Club America chapters (2025)

On Monday, October 27, 2025, Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] announced that the state was partnering with Turning Point USA to ensure that all "Club America" chapters on high school campuses could launch without impediments or holdups, warning that districts or schools that opposed them would get a warning before the state would "drop the hammer."[8]

The announcement, delivered at the College of Florida, accompanied a new state debate and civics initiative featuring a Ronald Reagan Presidential Debate with a $50,000 championship prize and a trophy named for the late Turning Point USA president [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[8]

## After the assassination of its founder (2025)

Turning Point, the youth organization founded by [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], was based in Arizona, where Kirk lived at the time of his [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination]] at [[utah-valley-university|Utah Valley University]] in September 2025.[9] Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] traveled with Kirk's casket to Phoenix aboard Air Force Two after meeting the family in Salt Lake City.[9] Kirk, 31, was described in coverage of his death as one of the most influential figures on the right and as central to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 outreach to young voters.[9]


## Criticism from within the online right (2025)

TPUSA's election-focused activism drew criticism from parts of the dissident online right. In April 2025, writing in the Badlands News Brief after Democratic officials in 19 states sued over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[2025-trump-elections-executive-order|elections executive order]], contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] asked why "all the popular Attention Farmers downstream from TPUSA" had spent the week convincing what he called "Standard-Issue Villagers on the Uniparty Right" that elections are real, when in his view the obvious conclusion was that elections are fake.[10] He characterized the resulting ask — greater power and funding for TPUSA — as the point of the exercise, and contrasted that posture with [[liz-harrington|Liz Harrington]]'s more combative approach to the litigation.[10]

## AmericaFest 2025

Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] spoke at Turning Point USA's AmericaFest 2025 in Phoenix, Arizona in December 2025, where he declared that gutting federal [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|diversity, equity, and inclusion]] laws was one of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s big wins since returning to office.[11] His line that DEI had been relegated "to the dustbin of history, which is exactly where it belongs" drew a big roar from the crowd.[11]

## Targeting in the Arctic Frost probe

On October 29, 2025, Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] released FBI files showing that Turning Point USA, founded by the late [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], was among the organizations that received records requests in the [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]] probe conducted by special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]].[12] Grassley made public 197 subpoenas issued to 34 individuals and 163 businesses, seeking records and communications related to more than 430 individuals and organizations, all of which appeared to target Republicans; alongside Turning Point USA, the [[republican-attorneys-general-association|Republican Attorneys General Association]] was named.[12] Grassley described the probe as a "fishing expedition" that became "the vehicle by which FBI agents and DOJ prosecutors could improperly investigate the entire Republican political apparatus."[12]

## Locus of the right's Israel debate

In "The Exploitation of Charlie Kirk," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described Turning Point as having increasingly become the primary venue where the fracture on the American right over U.S.-Israel policy was playing out, between Israel loyalists and Israel scrutinizers.[14] Founder [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] loved [[israel|Israel]] but, believing in the importance of debate, actively platformed respectful dissent.[14] At the organization's July 2025 Student Action Summit in Tampa, [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] suggested a link between [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and Israeli intelligence and raised concerns about American citizens serving in the IDF, drawing backlash from [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] and [[stopantisemitism|StopAntisemitism]]; Kirk defended him.[14] The same summit hosted a debate between [[dave-smith|Dave Smith]] and [[josh-hammer|Josh Hammer]], for which TPUSA also took criticism.[14] DeLarme reported, via Carlson, that some TPUSA donors expressed intense disapproval of Kirk's moderate stance on Israel in the months before his murder.[14]
