# Turning Point USA

Turning Point USA (TPUSA) is an American nonprofit organization founded by [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] that advocates for conservative politics among young people, particularly on college campuses.

## In the Robinson case (July 2026)

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] implicated TPUSA staff in her claims of a conspiracy in Kirk's murder, arguing the accused killer [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] did not act alone. Politico reported that TPUSA staff received death threats and increased security after Owens's broadcasts named them, and Owens's spokesperson accused TPUSA figures of targeting her for months. The Brief notes that Owens's claims drew condemnation from prominent conservative figures including [[dinesh-dsouza|Dinesh D'Souza]], [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], and [[lauren-southern|Lauren Southern]].
