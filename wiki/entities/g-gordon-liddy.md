# G. Gordon Liddy

G. Gordon Liddy was an American political operative best known for his role in the Watergate scandal, and later a radio broadcaster. He is the father of [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]], the Civil Division Chief of the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]] during the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]] dispute.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] flagged the lineage in a sidebar to her November 2022 article, confirming that Tom Liddy is the son of "THAT G. Gordon Liddy from Watergate," and noting that she had worked with Tom Liddy years earlier when he was a radio talk show host — adding only, "No comment."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
