# Kenneth O'Donnell

Kenneth O'Donnell was a close aide to President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and one of the Boston-area childhood friends who formed the inner circle around the Kennedy brothers.[1]

## The "Irish Mafia"

O'Donnell is named among the members of the informal advisory group associated with [[executive-committee-of-the-national-security-council|ExComm]], the unofficial council [[john-f-kennedy|John]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert Kennedy]] assembled to keep peacemaking efforts siloed from the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs]] and the intelligence agencies.[1] Officials shut out of those deliberations derisively called the group the "Irish Mafia."[1] Badlands Media presents this circle as the model for an emergent "new American Mafia" around [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the 2024 transition.[1]
