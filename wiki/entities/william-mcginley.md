# William McGinley

**William McGinley** was named White House Counsel in the incoming second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], announced during the November 2024 presidential transition.[1]

## Context

The counsel appointment came as Badlands Media catalogued roughly twenty transition picks, from [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] at the Justice Department and [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] at the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] to [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] at [[department-of-homeland-security|Homeland Security]] and [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]] at the Environmental Protection Agency.[1]

The legal function of the incoming White House was expected to matter considerably given contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s prediction that [[lawfare|lawfare]] driven by progressive NGOs and their lawyers — rather than street protest — would be the principal mode of opposition to the second Trump term.[1]
