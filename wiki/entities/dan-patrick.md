# Dan Patrick

Dan Patrick is the Lieutenant Governor of Texas and the presiding officer of the [[texas-senate|Texas Senate]].[1]

## Role in the Paxton impeachment trial

Patrick presided over the September 2023 impeachment trial of Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]], held in the Senate chamber at the state Capitol in Austin after the chamber was converted into a courtroom.[1] Following the swearing-in of the 30 senators who would decide whether to remove Paxton from office, Patrick worked through the pretrial motions — including motions to dismiss the impeachment articles, which Republicans rejected — before opening statements were heard.[1]
