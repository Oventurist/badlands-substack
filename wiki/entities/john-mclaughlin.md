# John McLaughlin (intelligence official)

John McLaughlin is a former acting director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] who served in that capacity under President George W. Bush, and who remains a frequently quoted commentator on American intelligence and Middle East policy.[1]

## Commentary on the Israel–Iran war

In October 2024, amid speculation that a decisive Israeli battlefield victory over its adversaries might create conditions for the next U.S. president to launch new regional peace talks, McLaughlin offered a cautious assessment of that premise. He said the argument was "interesting," but noted that it "assumes all of this is going to give Israel greater strength," adding that while the possibility was "plausible," observers did not yet know where the conflict was heading.[1]

His remarks were made in the context of a year-long war in which [[israel|Israel]] had severely degraded [[hamas|Hamas]] in Gaza and inflicted heavy losses on [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in Lebanon, while the broader confrontation with [[iran|Iran]] threatened to widen.[1]
