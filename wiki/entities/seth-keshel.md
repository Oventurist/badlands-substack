# Seth Keshel

Seth Keshel is an election-integrity analyst and author associated with the American election-fraud research community.[1]

## The American War on Election Corruption

Keshel appeared on episode 144 of [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]'s program Culture of Change to discuss his book *The American War on Election Corruption*, with the conversation ranging across fake voters, fake ballots, and fake counts, but concentrating most heavily on voter registration.[1] Ashe summarized the thesis that fake voters are the foundation of the corrupt election machine and that none of its other components function without them.[1] During the discussion Keshel also spoke about his recent meetings in Washington, DC.[1] The segment appeared alongside coverage of the [[north-carolina-voter-id-law|North Carolina voter ID law]] ruling in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1]
