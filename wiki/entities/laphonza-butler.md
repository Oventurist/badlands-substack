# Laphonza Butler

Laphonza Butler is an American political operative and lobbyist who was appointed to the United States Senate by California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] on October 1, 2023 to fill the vacancy created by the death of Senator [[dianne-feinstein|Dianne Feinstein]].[1]

## Appointment and criticism

Newsom's press release announcing the appointment lauded Butler as "the first openly Black lesbian to serve in Congress."[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the announcement's emphasis on identity omitted material facts about her background — namely her prior experience as a consultant for Newsom's inner circle of operatives and her work as a lobbyist for Uber and AirBnB.[1]

DeLarme presented the appointment as a characteristic example of Newsom's use of [[identity-politics|identity politics]]: a selection framed in the language of historic representation that simultaneously advanced an insider with corporate lobbying ties, allowing the governor to channel donor-class resources while inflaming voters' passions with emotionally charged rhetoric about identity.[1]
