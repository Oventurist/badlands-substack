# PetroSaudi

**PetroSaudi** is an oil and energy company at the centre of the international [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB scandal]]. It was co-founded by [[turki-bin-abdullah-al-saud|Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud]], a Saudi prince who was among the eleven princes detained by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 2017.[1]

On 26 April 2023 — the same day a federal jury in the United States convicted the entertainer [[pras-michel|Pras Michel]] over his role in the affair — Swiss authorities announced the indictment of two additional 1MDB conspirators, described as executives at PetroSaudi.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] cited the parallel Swiss action as evidence that the "massive kleptocratic scandal ain't over yet."[1]
