# Walter Reed National Military Medical Center

Walter Reed National Military Medical Center is the military hospital in Bethesda, Maryland that treats senior United States government and defense officials. It became the focus of a transparency controversy in January 2024 over the undisclosed hospitalization of Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]].[1]

## Austin hospitalization disclosure controversy

It emerged only weeks after the fact that Austin had been admitted to Walter Reed on December 22, 2023 to be treated for prostate cancer.[1] According to the hospital, Austin underwent a "minimally invasive surgical procedure called a prostatectomy to treat and cure prostate cancer."[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] revealed what had happened only after days of speculation and criticism.[1]

The delayed disclosure prompted Fox News reporter [[peter-doocy|Peter Doocy]] to question whether a broader pattern of concealment surrounded presidential health, and National Security Council coordinator [[john-kirby|John Kirby]] acknowledged that the Pentagon had faced a "challenge to credibility" by failing to quickly reveal the details of Austin's treatment.[1]

## February 2024 Austin admission

On Sunday, February 11, 2024, Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] was taken to Walter Reed at around 2:20 p.m. and admitted to the critical care unit for treatment of symptoms "suggesting an emergent bladder issue."[2] Hospital officials said in a statement released Sunday night that it was "not clear how long Secretary Austin will remain hospitalized."[2] Austin travelled to the facility with the unclassified and classified communications systems required for his duties, and transferred the functions of his office to Deputy Secretary [[kathleen-hicks|Kathleen Hicks]] shortly before 5 p.m.[2] The episode followed Austin's earlier, secretively handled stay at the hospital for cancer treatment in December 2023.[2]

## Biden's aneurysm surgery

Speaking at the International Association of Fire Fighters Legislative Conference in March 2023, [[joe-biden|President Joe Biden]] recounted that he had suffered a cranial aneurysm requiring immediate surgery, and said the operation took place "down at Walter Reed" because the surgeon there "had operated on so many folks."[3] Biden described being diagnosed after returning from a trip with severe headaches during a snowstorm, and joked that doctors "had to take the top of my head off a couple times, see if I had a brain."[3]

## Cold War deaths and the Kennedy autopsy

A Badlands Media essay on anti-communist figures treats the Bethesda hospital as the common setting for a cluster of suspicious deaths.[4] Senator [[joseph-mccarthy|Joseph McCarthy]] died there mysteriously on 2 May 1957.[4] Eight years earlier, on 22 May 1949, another anti-communist — Secretary of Defense [[james-forrestal|James Forrestal]], mentor to the future president [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] — allegedly jumped from the sixteenth floor of the same military hospital.[4] The article further notes that Kennedy's body was examined at the same Maryland facility after his assassination, and that his corpse was thought by some to have been altered and tampered with while there.[4] These coincidences of place are offered as evidence for the article's thesis that Americans who fought [[communism|communism]] were systematically eliminated.[4]
