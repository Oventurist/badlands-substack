# Polymarket

Polymarket is a prediction market platform on which users trade contracts tied to political, electoral, and current-events outcomes. It is referenced in the April 24, 2026 Badlands Brief alongside [[kalshi|Kalshi]] as one of the two major venues drawing regulatory and legislative attention in 2026.

## Regulatory scrutiny

According to the source, enforcement actions taken by Kalshi against political candidates who bet on their own primary races came amid growing scrutiny of prediction markets generally, including Polymarket. Lawmakers cited in the reporting warned that large stakes placed on elections could create incentives for interference or otherwise undermine the electoral process. The Brief's commentary, by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], treats such markets as imperfect but comparatively auditable relative to United States election systems, while cautioning that reported market odds can themselves influence voter behavior.
