# David Frum

David Frum is an American political writer named by Badlands Media, alongside Bill Kristol, as a neoconservative architect of the public messaging that sold the [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]].[1]

## Manufacturing the rationale for war

According to Badlands Media, the explanations circulated after the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] — that the attackers were motivated by hatred of American freedoms or of domestic social policy — were invented by figures such as Frum and Kristol to build support for the coming wars.[1] The publication contends that [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]]'s own arguments had historically concerned America's long record of instigating and carrying out violence in the Middle East, not Western liberal social norms.[1]

Badlands Media presents this episode as a case study in how [[atrocity-propaganda|atrocity propaganda]] and elite narrative management convert public trauma into war support, to the benefit of the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial-congressional complex]].[1]
