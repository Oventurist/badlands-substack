# Roscoe Conkling

Roscoe Conkling was a United States Senator from New York, leader of the Stalwart Republicans and the dispenser of patronage in New York.[1] The [[new-york-custom-house|New York Custom House]] was his stronghold, and he had appointed [[chester-arthur|Chester Arthur]] to run it, a post in which Arthur became the highest-paid federal employee before becoming Vice President.[1]

President [[james-garfield|James Garfield]] moved against Conkling's control by nominating his bitter rival to head the customs house; Conkling contested the nomination, tried to persuade the Senate to block it, and appealed to the Republican caucus to force its withdrawal, without success.[1] Joe Lange places Conkling's patronage machine within a wider system in which politicians controlled by [[citibank|Citibank]] handpicked the officials running the customs house, where estimates of lost revenue ran as high as $25 million a year.[1]
