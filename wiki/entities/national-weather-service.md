# National Weather Service

The **National Weather Service** (NWS) is the United States federal agency responsible for issuing weather forecasts and hazard warnings, including the fire-weather advisories at issue in litigation over the January 2025 Los Angeles firestorm.[1]

## Role in the Palisades fire litigation

In their lawsuit against the [[los-angeles-department-of-water-and-power|Los Angeles Department of Water and Power]], federal judges [[dean-pregerson|Dean Pregerson]] and [[vijay-gandhi|Vijay Gandhi]] cited the National Weather Service's warnings as evidence that the utility should have been better prepared.[1] The complaint states that "despite dire warnings by the National Weather Service of a 'Particularly Dangerous Condition-Red Flag Warning,' of 'critical fire weather' which had the potential for rapid fire spread and extreme fire behavior, the LADWP was unprepared for the [[palisades-fire|Palisades fire]]."[1] The agency's Red Flag Warning framework thus functioned in the litigation as the benchmark against which the utility's readiness was measured.[1]

## 2025 Central Texas floods

The agency again came under scrutiny after the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]], which killed at least 82 people over the Fourth of July weekend in 2025.[2] The National Weather Service issued a slew of alerts before the storm, but questions surrounded the agency's staffing levels and its ability to reach residents at the time of the disaster.[2] Reporting on the disaster paired those questions with records showing that [[kerr-county|Kerr County]] officials had considered but never installed flood-warning sirens.[2]
