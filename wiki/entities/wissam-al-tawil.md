# Wissam al-Tawil

Wissam al-Tawil, known by the nom de guerre "Jawad," was a senior [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] commander killed in an Israeli air strike in southern Lebanon on Monday, January 8, 2024.[1]

## Role in Hezbollah

Tawil served as a deputy head of a unit within Hezbollah's elite Radwan force and played a leading role in managing the group's operations in southern Lebanon, according to an unnamed official cited by AFP.[1] He was described as having played an important part in Hezbollah's confrontations with the Israeli army during the low-intensity conflict that had run along the Israel-Lebanon frontier since 7 October 2023.[1]

## Killing and reaction

The Israeli strike targeted a car in the village of Majdal Selm, killing Tawil along with another fighter from the Lebanese armed group.[1] One security source called it "a very painful strike," while another warned that "things will flare up now," and the killing was widely expected to drive further escalation between [[israel|Israel]] and Hezbollah alongside the ongoing war in Gaza.[1]
