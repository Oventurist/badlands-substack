# Tonya Ugoretz

Tonya Ugoretz is a career FBI intelligence analyst who, according to an Office of the Director of National Intelligence biography quoted by Badlands Media, was assigned to ODNI as Director of the Cyber Threat Intelligence Integration Center (CTIIC).[1] She joined the government in 2001 as a Presidential Management Fellow and as an all-source analyst with the FBI's Counterterrorism program, and in 2003 became the first analyst to serve as the FBI Director's daily intelligence briefer.[1]

## Career

From 2008 to 2012, as a Unit Chief and then Section Chief in the Senior Executive Service, Ugoretz oversaw intelligence products and briefings for the FBI Director and the Attorney General, as well as FBI analysts' contributions to the [[presidents-daily-brief|President's Daily Brief]].[1] As Chief Intelligence Officer from 2010 to 2012 she led the Bureau's cadre of Senior National Intelligence Officers and created a 24/7 Intelligence Watch.[1]

## Badlands Media claims

Writing for Badlands Media in August 2023, [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argued that Ugoretz "played a key role in this war and nobody knows her name," calling her a hero who "entrapped Obama and his whole administration in treason."[1] The claim, drawn from QAnon drop 1991, is that Ugoretz inserted evidence into President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s President's Daily Brief indicating that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server had given China access to American secrets, thereby creating a "legal paper trail" preserved in the official archives for future investigations.[1] Lange framed this as an answer to the recurring [[qanon|Q]] question, "How do you introduce evidence legally?"[1]

In this account the insertion was a deliberate trap: because Obama and other senior officials were themselves using the same private server under pseudonyms, acknowledging the briefing would have required implicating themselves, while ignoring it created a documented record of willful inaction.[1] Lange asserted that Ugoretz already knew Obama was using the server when she included the material in his brief.[1] Badlands attributed the design of the trap to General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]], describing him as the man who "knows where all the bodies are buried."[1]
