# Jared Dowell

Jared Dowell, also known as "Riley" Dowell, is the child of Democratic House Minority Whip [[katherine-clark|Katherine M. Clark]], who represents Massachusetts's fifth congressional district.[1] Dowell was arrested in Boston in January 2023 in connection with [[antifa|Antifa]]-related violence.[1]

## Charges

According to a press release from the Boston Police Department, Dowell was charged with assault by means of a dangerous weapon, destruction or injury of personal property, and damage of property by graffiti or tagging.[1] Dowell was allegedly participating in rioting intended to show solidarity with the Antifa rioting in Atlanta that followed the killing of [[tortuguita|Tortuguita]].[1]

[[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argued that the family members of what he termed subversive bureaucrats are frequently subversive operatives themselves, citing the 2020 arrest of New York Mayor [[bill-de-blasio|Bill de Blasio]]'s daughter Chiara at a Manhattan protest and the employment of [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]]'s daughter at Twitter during the period in which, Morgan contended, dissenting experts and facts about COVID-19 and the vaccines were suppressed.[1]
