# Ray West

Ray West is an American activist and former member of the Black Panther Party, and the father of the rapper and designer [[kanye-west|Ye (Kanye West)]].[1]

## Role in the "White Lives Matter" episode

Ray West features in Badlands Media's account of the October 2022 controversy that followed his son's appearance at the Yeezy Season 9 fashion show in Paris, where Ye and [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] posed for photographers in T-shirts reading "White Lives Matter."[1] According to the account, when Ye asked his father what he thought of the shirt, Ray West replied that he found it funny; asked what was funny about it, he answered that it was "just a Black man stating the obvious."[1]

The exchange is cited in the Badlands narrative as evidence that the slogan was intended by those involved as a joke or a provocation rather than as a white-supremacist statement, and that the outrage that followed was manufactured by what the author calls liberal propaganda media.[1]
