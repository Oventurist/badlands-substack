# All-In Podcast

The **All-In Podcast** is a technology and politics program hosted by Craft Ventures co-founder [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] together with entrepreneurs Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, and David Friedberg.[1]

## 2023 RFK Jr. interview

In May 2023 the podcast hosted Democratic presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], in an episode that drew wide attention in the [[truth-community|Truth Community]].[2] Host [[jason-calacanis|Jason Calacanis]] asked Kennedy directly whether he believed the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] had been involved in the murder of his uncle, President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], and Kennedy answered without hesitation that the agency was "definitely involved in the murder and the 60-year cover-up," adding that it was still withholding records it is legally obligated to release.[2]

## 2024 Trump interview

In June 2024, two weeks after Sacks hosted a high-profile fundraiser for former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at his Silicon Valley mansion attended by venture capitalists and tech elites, Trump appeared on the podcast for roughly 50 minutes.[1] Coverage described the hosts as asking intelligent and clear questions without the "gotchas" typical of corporate media.[1] The conversation ranged across taxes, tariffs, energy, artificial intelligence, foreign policy, Ukraine, China, Covid and immigration.[1]

Sacks opened by relaying business leaders' complaints about the economy under the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration — crypto firms unable to obtain a regulatory framework, an absence of tech M&A, and real estate operators unable to get loans amid high interest rates and a credit crunch — then asked what three things Trump would do to get things moving again.[1] Trump answered "Regulation Regulation and Taxes," citing his corporate rate cut from combined levels of 40–45% down to 21% and expressing a desire to go lower.[1]

On AI, Trump argued that leadership would require double or triple current US electricity output and that "a windmill turning with its blade knocking out the birds and everything else is not going to be able to make us competitive."[1] He added that "Nuclear is okay with me ... and you have to do it in a way that makes sense," indicating that a nuclear revival would likely be based on small, advanced reactors rather than massive plants — a reference to [[small-modular-reactor|small modular reactors]].[1] Badlands commentary read the appearance as continuing a pattern of Trump overtures to libertarian, Bitcoin-maximalist and techno-optimist constituencies.[1]
