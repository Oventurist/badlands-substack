# Federalist Society

The Federalist Society is a conservative legal group that wields immense power in the United States judicial system, operating through local chapters and a national network that supplies and vets judicial nominees.[1]

## Leadership and influence

Guardian reporting recounted a gathering of Texas members of the Federalist Society during the second year of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first presidency, at which [[matthew-kacsmaryk|Matthew Kacsmaryk]] — then vice-president of the group's Fort Worth chapter — introduced Judge Edith Jones of the U.S. Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals.[1] Jones in turn introduced her friend, Supreme Court Justice [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]], and praised the four new conservative judges Trump had appointed to the appeals court, saying "They've raised the bar for the fifth circuit since I got on. And that's thanks to the Federalist Society, to Leonard."[1]

The "Leonard" referenced needed no last name to that audience: [[leonard-leo|Leonard Leo]], the conservative activist who built the Federalist Society into a political powerhouse and helped Trump construct the Supreme Court majority that erased federal protections for abortion in 2022.[1]

## Role in the Planned Parenthood litigation

The Society's network features centrally in a case described as one of the most consequential moving through the American legal system — a lawsuit seeking to strike another blow to abortion rights and potentially bankrupt [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[1] The suit was filed by an anti-abortion activist tied to Leo and has been heard by judges, from the lower courts through the Fifth Circuit, who are likewise linked to Leo.[1]
