# Edward Bulwer-Lytton

Sir Edward Bulwer-Lytton was a British occultist and novelist described as a Rosicrucian high priest, and the author of the fictional novel *The Coming Race*, from which the [[thule-society|Thule Society]] drew its belief in the mystical fluid "Vril."[1]

Bulwer-Lytton's novel imagined an ancient race of superbeings living within the hollow earth, telepathically commanding human-like robots while preparing to invade the less evolved surface race of man; the etheric fluid permeating this master race was called "Vril-ya."[1] According to Nazi occultists and the earlier followers of [[helena-blavatsky|Blavatsky]], this fantasy was treated as a living gospel, and the [[vril-society|Vril Society]] of the early 1920s took its name directly from the novel.[1]

Thule Society members strove to practice the magical workings developed by Bulwer-Lytton's Rosicrucian followers.[1] [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] was likewise obsessed with Bulwer-Lytton's Rosicrucian writings, assigning teams to seek the entrance to the hollow earth in Antarctica and supposed Atlantean colonies in the South American Andes.[1]
