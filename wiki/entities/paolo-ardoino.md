# Paolo Ardoino

Paolo Ardoino is the chief executive officer of [[tether|Tether]], the digital assets company that issues the dollar-pegged USDt stablecoin.[1]

## Rumble investment

In December 2024, Tether disclosed a $775 million strategic investment in the video platform [[rumble|Rumble]].[1] Ardoino publicly framed the partnership as a means of encouraging decentralization and free speech, and indicated that Tether also wanted to investigate prospects with Rumble in cloud services, crypto payment solutions and advertising.[1] Under the terms of the deal, Tether agreed to buy 103.3 million Rumble Class A common shares at $7.50 each, taking a minority position without board participation, with closing expected in the first quarter of 2025 subject to customary conditions and regulatory clearances.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the transaction to explain Tether's role to readers unfamiliar with the crypto landscape, describing it as not a speculative crypto asset in the traditional sense but a digital token pegged to the value of the U.S. dollar that allows near-frictionless peer-to-peer transacting outside traditional banking institutions.[1]
