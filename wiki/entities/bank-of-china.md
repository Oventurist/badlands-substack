# Bank of China

The Bank of China is one of the People's Republic of China's largest state-owned commercial banks and a central institution in the country's financial sector.[1] In Badlands Media coverage it features primarily as the institution formerly headed by [[liu-liange|Liu Liange]], who served as its chairman for four years until resigning in March 2023 — weeks before Chinese authorities disclosed that he was facing corruption charges.[1]

## Corruption case

In November 2024, Xinhua News Agency reported that Liu had received a suspended death sentence for accepting bribes worth nearly $17 million and for illegally issuing loans while at the bank.[1] The case was presented as part of a broader anti-corruption crackdown ordered by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] across China's approximately $60 trillion financial sector, a campaign that had already produced a suspended death sentence for former deputy central bank governor [[fan-yifei|Fan Yifei]] in October 2024.[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], used the Bank of China case to argue that Beijing's willingness to execute corrupt bankers stood in contrast to Western practice, suggesting half-seriously that America "send all of our corrupt bankers to Beijing," and reading the crackdown as consistent with the [[sovereign-alliance|sovereign alliance]] thesis.[1]
