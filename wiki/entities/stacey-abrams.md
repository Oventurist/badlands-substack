# Stacey Abrams

Stacey Abrams is a Democratic Party political figure from Georgia whose associated nonprofit network drew scrutiny in 2026 in connection with Biden-era green energy grant awards.[1]

## Power Forward Communities award

Criminal referrals made by [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]], administrator of the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]], highlighted a $2 billion award to [[power-forward-communities|Power Forward Communities]], a nonprofit tied to Abrams that reported only $100 in revenue in the year prior to receiving the grant.[1] The award, drawn from funding authorized under the [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]], was intended for residential decarbonization projects.[1]

Zeldin characterized the broader pattern of grantmaking as routing funds to "pass-through entities" connected to former Obama–Biden officials, Democratic donors, and politically connected individuals, describing the rushed 2024 distribution as "blatant waste and abuse" that "feels criminal" and centered on "self-dealing."[1]

### 2025 EPA funding controversy and Georgia ethics fine
In February 2025 the Environmental Protection Agency under Administrator Lee Zeldin disclosed that the Biden administration had awarded $2 billion to Power Forward Communities, a nonprofit partnered with multiple left-wing groups founded by Abrams and which the Georgia Democrat said she was "thrilled" to be part of.[2] Abrams, described in press accounts as a fierce supporter of former President Joe Biden, had run unsuccessfully for governor of Georgia; the award drew scrutiny because the recipient organization reported just $100 in revenue in 2023.[2]

Badlands commentary emphasized that Abrams "is all about elections" and is connected to a wide ecosystem of NGOs, making the size of the award significant.[2] It also noted that in January 2025 the Georgia Ethics Commission voted unanimously to fine two nonprofit organizations founded by Abrams after they admitted violating state law by campaigning for her 2018 gubernatorial campaign — a fact treated as directly relevant to the decision to route another $2 billion toward her network.[2] Commentators further pointed to her attendance at the Bilderberg meeting in 2019.[2]

## Arrest of Jimmie Gardner (2023)

In November 2023 Abrams's brother-in-law [[jimmie-gardner|Jimmie Gardner]] was arrested in Tampa, Florida, on human trafficking allegations, with the [[tampa-police-department|Tampa Police Department]] saying the 57-year-old had allegedly attempted to engage in sexual acts with a 16-year-old girl at a hotel at International Plaza.[3] Abrams was identified in coverage of the arrest as a former Georgia gubernatorial candidate; the item was carried by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as part of a sequence on politically and media-connected figures charged in child exploitation cases.[3]
