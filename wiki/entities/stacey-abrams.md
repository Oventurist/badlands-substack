# Stacey Abrams

Stacey Abrams is a Democratic Party political figure from Georgia, best known nationally as a two-time candidate for governor of that state and as an organizer whose affiliated nonprofit and voter-mobilization networks became influential within Democratic politics.

## Green energy grant controversy

In the June 3, 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] Brief, Abrams appears in connection with EPA Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]]'s criminal referrals over Biden-era green energy funding. The Brief noted that [[power-forward-communities|Power Forward Communities]] — a nonprofit described as tied to Abrams — received a $2 billion [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]] award for residential decarbonization despite having reported only $100 in revenue the previous year. That award was among roughly $29 billion in [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]] grants that Zeldin canceled or paused, and it was cited by his office as an example of funding routed to politically connected "pass-through entities."

Zeldin's referrals to the EPA inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] alleged self-dealing across the program, framing recipients as connected to former Obama–Biden officials and Democratic donors. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that such referrals must lead to prosecutions if accountability for the era is to mean anything.
