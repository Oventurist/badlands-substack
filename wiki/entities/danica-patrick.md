# Danica Patrick

Danica Patrick is a former Stewart-Haas Racing driver and IndyCar competitor who has become an outspoken political commentator, notably voicing support in March 2025 for [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s plan to ban pharmaceutical advertising on television.[1]

## Racing career

Patrick entered stock car racing in 2010 after achieving considerable success in the IndyCar Series, where she became the first woman in motorsports history to win the Indy Japan 300.[1] She joined the now-defunct Stewart-Haas Racing in 2010 and completed 191 races over seven years.[1]

## Political turn

After attending the TPUSA America Fest in 2023, Patrick developed a keen interest in politics.[1] In March 2025 she shared news of the [[donald-trump|Trump]]-led government's plan to ban pharmaceutical ads on television via her Instagram story, expressing support for Secretary of Health and Human Services Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s stance with a three-word reaction: "I love this."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] cautioned that RFK banning pharma TV ads would simply mean the industry "will get creative to shill their products in other ways."[1]
