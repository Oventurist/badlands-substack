# Molly Jong-Fast

Molly Jong-Fast is an American columnist and political commentator described in conservative coverage as a far-left writer.[1]

## Connection to the Carroll suit

Jong-Fast hosted the party at which [[george-conway|George Conway]] met [[e-jean-carroll|E. Jean Carroll]] and, according to Carroll's own later testimony, convinced her to bring suit against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Because of that gathering, Jong-Fast's name appears in accounts framing the Carroll litigation as the product of a networked political circle opposed to Trump rather than an independently initiated claim.[1]
