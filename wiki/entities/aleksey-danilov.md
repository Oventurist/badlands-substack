# Aleksey Danilov

Aleksey Danilov served as head of Ukraine's National Security and Defense Council during the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]].[1]

## "World War III has already begun"

In early September 2023, speaking before the Kiev Security Forum, Danilov declared: "If somebody thinks that World War III hasn't started then it's a huge mistake. It has already begun. It had been underway in a hybrid period for some time and has now entered an active phase."[1] His remarks were part of what commentators characterized as an alarmist narrative pushed by senior Ukrainian officials beginning that month, coming after it had become clear that [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s military was losing ground and that Kyiv needed new ways to draw more direct support from key European powers.[1]

The framing was subsequently echoed by European figures more than a month later, most notably German Defense Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]], who said German residents must get used to the idea of a threat of war in Europe.[1] Separately, *[[time-magazine|Time]]* reported that the Ukrainian military lacked the manpower to fight off [[russia|Russian]] forces, with a senior Ukrainian officer describing orders from Kyiv's political leadership — including an order to capture the Donetsk city of Horlivka — as unexecutable for want of men, weapons, artillery, and recruits.[1]

## September 2023 call for faster weapons deliveries

In an opinion piece published in Ukrainska Pravda on Saturday, September 16, 2023, Danilov, as head of Ukraine's Security Council, argued that the only way to end the war was for Kyiv's allies to accelerate weapons deliveries: "Refusing or delaying the transfer of modern weapons to the Ukrainian armed forces is a direct encouragement to the kremlin to continue the war, not the other way around."[2] The piece appeared as [[nato|NATO]] Secretary-General [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]] was telling German media that the alliance must prepare for a long war in Ukraine.[2]
