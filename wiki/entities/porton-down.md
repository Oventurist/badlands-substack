# Porton Down

Porton Down is the British military science complex identified as the facility that manufactured the Novichok agent used in the Skripal Affair.[1]

The laboratory is invoked in the context of [[richard-dearlove|Sir Richard Dearlove's]] biography: as a former head of [[mi6|MI6]], Dearlove is described as knowing "a thing or two" about Porton Down, just as he knew of the [[pentagon|Pentagon's]] internationally extended bioweapons laboratories.[1] That expertise is treated as grounds for suspicion when Dearlove became a loud proponent of the [[lab-leak-theory|Wuhan lab leak hypothesis]] in June 2020, since it placed a career practitioner of misdirection at the head of a campaign directing attention exclusively at a Chinese facility.[1]

Porton Down thus appears in the essay as one element of a pattern of British-linked chemical and biological incidents — alongside the "yellowcake" dossier that launched the Iraq war and reports of nerve gas use attributed to Libya and Syria — cited to argue that anglophone intelligence services have an established record of weaponizing such claims.[1]
