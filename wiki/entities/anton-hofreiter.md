# Anton Hofreiter

Anton Hofreiter is a German member of parliament from the Green Party and the chairman of the [[bundestag|Bundestag]]'s European policy committee.[1] In September 2024 he attracted attention in alternative-media circles after calling for tighter state control over social media platforms, including the outright blocking of major platforms if deemed necessary.[1]

## Calls to block social media platforms

Speaking to reporters from the Funke Media Group on a Saturday in early September 2024, Hofreiter said Germany must act to stop the dissemination of "extremist content" online and should be prepared to block major social media platforms.[1] "One of the biggest problems of extremism is online radicalization," he stated, adding that the dissemination of "anti-constitutional content on the Internet" must be stopped.[1] He also said, "We need to tackle the root of the problem and push back radicalization in digital space as well as in society."[1]

The remarks were reported as implicit threats against [[x-corp|X]] and [[telegram|Telegram]] specifically.[1]

## Commentary

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], whose report was reproduced in the September 10, 2024 Badlands News Brief, described Hofreiter's party as "a mutant combination of socialists, frothing environmentalists and rabid progressives," and argued that "online radicalization" is one of those "intentionally vague terms which dictators and authoritarians leave purposefully ambiguous so they can have a green light to censor and throw in jail anyone they want."[1] The outlet framed Hofreiter's comments as confirmation of its earlier suggestion that the arrest of Telegram CEO [[pavel-durov|Pavel Durov]] in France was "a trial balloon for how Europe will go after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]."[1]
