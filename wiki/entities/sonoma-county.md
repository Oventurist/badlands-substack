# Sonoma County

Sonoma County is a county in northern California whose public health department drew national attention in October 2025 when it reinstated a mask requirement in certain healthcare settings.[1]

## 2025 healthcare mask order

Interim county health officer Dr. Karen Smith issued an order on Monday, October 6, 2025, requiring face masks to be worn inside certain healthcare facilities because of the "risk of COVID, flu and other respiratory virus-related illnesses" during the fall and winter months.[1] The mandate was set to take effect on November 1.[1]

A spokesperson for the Sonoma County Department of Health said the order was designed to protect the county's "most vulnerable populations," including the elderly and cancer patients in health care facilities, and also to prevent medical staff from becoming ill.[1]

## Context

Coverage of the order noted that influenza was believed to be emerging in that season as even more deadly than COVID-19 in the United States; according to estimates from the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]], flu made between 47 and 82 million people sick in the U.S. the previous winter, with between 27,000 and 130,000 deaths attributed to the illness.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the return of a localized mask mandate as an instance of persistent public health overreach, arguing sardonically that such measures should be allowed to run their course so that the public draws its own conclusions.[1]
