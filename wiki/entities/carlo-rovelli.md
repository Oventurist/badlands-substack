# Carlo Rovelli

**Dr. Carlo Rovelli** is cited as a theoretical physicist in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay quotes his line, "We are not things, we are events," and applies it to personal identity: the reader is "not a static identity" but "a dynamic unfolding," described as "the becoming that never stops."[1] The quotation appears in the "Crossing into Embodied Truth" section, where it follows the neuroscience of [[andrew-newberg|Andrew Newberg]] and precedes the claim that the reader is "not performing groundedness" but "emitting it."[1]

Rovelli's process framing supports the article's broader argument that [[coherence|coherence]] is an ongoing physiological event rather than a fixed achievement.[1]
