# David Laufman

David Laufman was an official of the Justice Department's National Security Division who attended the first of the three days of [[fbi|FBI]] interviews of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] in January 2017 and signed the immunity letter extended to him.[1]

## Role in the January 2017 interviews

Testimony from FBI Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] at the 2022 trial established that Danchenko was interviewed over three days by Auten and Agent [[steve-somma|Steve Somma]], with someone from the National Security Division present each day — Laufman on the first day and Richard Scott on the second — and with Danchenko's counsel Mark Schamel also present.[1]

The [[danchenko-immunity-agreement|immunity letter]], entered at trial as Government Exhibit 118 and dated 24 January 2017, was signed by Laufman, Danchenko and Schamel.[1] Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] had Auten read it aloud to the jury and walked him through its requirements, the government's position being that Danchenko violated the agreement by failing to make full and complete disclosure of the information the agents sought.[1]
