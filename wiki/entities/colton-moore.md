# Colton Moore

Colton Moore is a Republican state senator from Trenton in northwest Georgia and a former member of the Georgia House of Representatives. He was arrested on January 16, 2025, after attempting to enter the Georgia House chamber in defiance of a ban imposed on him by the House speaker the previous year.[1]

## 2025 arrest

Moore, described in press coverage as a "far-right" senator, scuffled with legislative staff and Georgia State Patrol officers before being handcuffed and escorted to a police car bound for the Fulton County Jail.[1] He was charged with "willful obstruction of law enforcement officers," a misdemeanor.[1] House Speaker [[jon-burns|Jon Burns]] had warned Moore two days earlier that the ban remained intact following Moore's "vile, disparaging and false comments" about the late House Speaker David Ralston, and would remain in place until Moore delivered a "sincere apology" to the family and friends of the chamber's longtime former leader, who died in 2022.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor Ashe in America characterized the incident in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as excessive.[1] She noted that in video of the encounter Moore appeared to be moving as though intoxicated before the tackle, but that people close to the senator insisted he is not a drinker; both Moore and associates she spoke with maintained he was simply attempting to do his job when he was tackled, arrested, and apparently taken to a hospital.[1] The commentary observed that the confrontation appeared to trace back to criticism of a painting, and argued that state legislatures "can be shockingly despotic" — citing Colorado legislators being removed from debate for using the word "groomer" while debating legislation about groomers — concluding that important political work must happen at the state level.[1]
