# Joaquin Oliver

Joaquin Oliver was a 17-year-old student killed in the 2018 mass shooting at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in Parkland, Florida, one of seventeen people who died in the attack.[1]

## AI avatar and the Acosta interview

Oliver's parents created an interactive AI-generated avatar of their son and granted [[jim-acosta|Jim Acosta]] — the former CNN chief White House correspondent, who now hosts an independent program on YouTube — the first "interview" with the recreation, published on what would have been Oliver's 25th birthday.[1]

Acosta asked the avatar for its solution to gun violence. The AI responded that it believed in "a mix of stronger gun control laws, mental health support and community engagement," adding that society needed to "create safe spaces for conversations and connections" and build "a culture of kindness and understanding."[1] Acosta described the project as "just a beautiful thing."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America said she had "so many problems with this, and none of them are about guns," and discussed the segment on the program Culture of Change, focusing on how the synthetic resurrection of the dead may reshape the way living people relate to death and to the stages of grief.[1] The commentary summarized the practice as "morally repugnant — but super profitable."[1]
