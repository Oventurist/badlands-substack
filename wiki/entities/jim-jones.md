# Jim Jones

Jim Jones was the leader of the Peoples Temple, named in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI" among the individuals suspected by many of having taken part in the [[cia|CIA]]'s [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] mind-control program.[1]

## Function ascribed by the article

The essay sorts its roster of alleged patsies by purpose. Jones is grouped with [[charles-manson|Charlie Manson]] as figures used to create fear and race wars — distinct from those used to eliminate people in power who opposed the CIA, and from those such as [[david-koresh|David Koresh]] and [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]], who in the author's reading were used to make anti-government groups appear violent and crazy.[1]

MK ULTRA is described in the article as the mind-control system the CIA developed, using LSD and other drugs along with physical and psychological torture to break people's minds for the purpose of reprogramming them as assets.[1] The article's summary answer to its own title question is that most alleged CIA and FBI patsies not only believed they were working with those agencies but also had alleged ties to CIA mind control.[1]

## Context

The article offers no specific documentary claim about Jones beyond his inclusion in this list, in contrast with the more detailed allegations it makes about [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]], [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]] and [[james-earl-ray|James Earl Ray]].[1] Author [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] concludes more broadly that it would be a shorter list to name what the CIA has not been involved in than what it has, spanning political assassinations, government overthrows, wars and mass-casualty events.[1]
