# Jim O'Neill

Jim O'Neill is the Deputy Secretary of the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] (HHS) and Acting Director of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]].[1] He came to broad public attention on December 30, 2025, when he announced from his official account that HHS had frozen all child care payments to the state of [[minnesota|Minnesota]], citing allegations that the state had funneled millions of taxpayer dollars to fraudulent daycares over the preceding decade.[1] The post stated that HHS had taken three actions that day.[1]

## Career

O'Neill received a bachelor's degree from Yale in 1990 and a master's degree from the University of Chicago in 1997.[1] Commentary in the Badlands brief noted apparent gaps in his public professional record between 1990–1996 and 1997–2001.[1] His documented professional career began in 2001 as a speechwriter for the U.S. Department of Education, after which he moved to HHS in 2002, rising in just under six years from Director of Speech and Editorial to Principal Associate Deputy Secretary.[1]

In 2008 O'Neill shifted into the intersection of the health industries, emerging technology, and high finance.[1] From 2008 to 2012 he served as Managing Director of [[clarium-capital|Clarium Capital]], the hedge fund of [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], and concurrently as CEO of the Thiel Foundation and co-founder of the [[thiel-fellowship|Thiel Fellowship]].[1] From 2012 to 2019 he was a Managing Director of [[mithril-capital-management|Mithril Capital Management]], another Thiel-linked vehicle.[1] He subsequently headed an anti-aging nonprofit and served as a board member and/or paid advisor to multiple health-tech and biotech companies before returning to government.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that O'Neill's unassuming, auditor-like appearance had initially been encouraging, on the theory that "nerdy"-looking investigators are terrifying to powerful people engaged in financial crime — before concluding that he is "not an auditor" but "a speech writer turned bureaucrat turned hedge fund manager turned venture capitalist turned bureaucrat again."[1] The commentary raised the irony that O'Neill would now be tasked with stopping corruption and money laundering flowing through a department he had helped build before becoming wealthy, and speculated about his Yale ties to Thiel and JD Vance.[1]

## Call for monovalent replacements for MMR (October 2025)

In October 2025, as Acting Director of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]], O'Neill called on vaccine manufacturers to develop "safe monovalent vaccines to replace the combined MMR."[2] He made the appeal citing comments by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who during a Cabinet meeting had called for shots to be given "in smaller doses" to young children while describing himself as "a vaccine believer."[2] The statement coincided with the announcement by the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]] that a work group would conduct a multi-year review of the childhood vaccine schedule, including the timing of shots and possible risks from common vaccine ingredients.[2]
