# Yemen

Yemen is the Arabian Peninsula state whose territory hosts the [[houthis|Houthi]] movement, and which became the focal point of the December 2023 debate over American naval deterrence in the [[red-sea-shipping-crisis|Red Sea]].[1]

## Role in the Red Sea shipping crisis

By late December 2023 the Houthis were conducting what commentary described as a full-blown war on commercial shipping in the Red Sea, with container ships coming under drone and missile attack on a daily basis and major liners avoiding Red Sea transit altogether, producing delays and rising goods prices.[1] Weeks earlier, US defense officials had voiced frustration that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] was being held back from responding forcefully against Houthi positions inside Yemen by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] White House.[1]

[[politico|Politico]] reporting cited by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] recorded that some current and former military officials were frustrated by the administration's initial response to the Houthis' attacks on shipping, while top military brass pushed for a more forceful posture lest the Iranian proxies grow more emboldened.[1] American warships in waters off Yemen came under direct attack without a military response being ordered, a state of affairs the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] summarized in the headline "U.S. Naval Deterrence Is Going, Going, Maybe Even Gone."[1] Analysts quoted in the coverage linked the restraint to Biden's desire to make nice with [[iran|Iran]] to keep global energy prices down ahead of the 2024 election, and worried it was already too late to reestablish deterrence.[1]

## Red Sea naval clash (December 2023)

In the final days of 2023, forces of the Yemeni [[houthis|Houthi]] movement attacked a Maersk container ship in the Red Sea and lost ten fighters when U.S. attack helicopters destroyed three boats in response.[2] Houthi military spokesman [[yahya-sarea|Yahya Sarea]] described the dead as members of the "Yemeni Naval Forces" and said on Al-Masirah television that they were deterring Israel-related commercial shipping "in solidarity and support for the Palestinian people."[2] The [[maersk-container-ship-attack|clash]] raised fears of wider escalation as [[iran|Iran]] sent a warship to the Red Sea.[2]
