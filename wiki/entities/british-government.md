# British Government

The **British Government** is presented by Badlands Media as a principal driver of the censorship offensive preceding the 2024 election cycle.[1]

## Online Safety Bill

The government long sought passage of the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]], which Badlands describes as an "atrocity of a bill" that empowers [[ofcom|Ofcom]] to fine or ultimately ban non-compliant platforms.[1]

## Involvement in the Russell Brand affair

The same government that passed the bill took an active interest in the sexual-abuse allegations against [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]].[1] Conservative legislator [[caroline-nokes|Caroline Nokes]] urged police in both Britain and the United States to investigate the "incredibly shocking" allegations, and Prime Minister [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]] issued a statement through his spokesman [[max-blain|Max Blain]] calling the claims "very serious and concerning."[1] The government subsequently asked big-tech platforms to censor Brand's online content, and some platforms complied quickly — a sequence the article ties directly to the Online Safety Bill and the [[trusted-news-initiative|Trusted News Initiative]].[1]
