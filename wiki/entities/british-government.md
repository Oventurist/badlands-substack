# British Government

The **British Government** is treated in Badlands Media's coverage as an active participant in the 2023–2024 censorship offensive, having long "drooled over" the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] before its passage through the [[uk-parliament|U.K. Parliament]] in September 2023.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Interest in the Brand allegations

The same government that passed the bill took an active interest in the sexual-abuse allegations against [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Conservative legislator Caroline Nokes urged police in both Britain and the United States to investigate the "incredibly shocking" allegations, and Prime Minister [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]] addressed the matter through his spokesman Max Blain, who called the claims "very serious and concerning."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Platform pressure

The British Government subsequently asked big-tech platforms to censor Brand's online content, and some complied quickly.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Badlands Media links this request directly to the Online Safety Bill and to the [[trusted-news-initiative|Trusted News Initiative]], and forecasts that the government will issue further threats to platforms demanding removal of effective state critics on the grounds that they peddle "harmful conspiracy theories."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The projected endpoint of this pressure is the complete removal of [[rumble]] from the UK.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)
