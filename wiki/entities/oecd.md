# Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

The OECD appears in Badlands Media's account of the Malthusian revival as an institutional home for several of the figures who founded and staffed the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]].[1]

## Personnel overlap with the Club of Rome

[[alexander-king|Sir Alexander King]], co-founder of the Club of Rome, was the OECD's Director General for Scientific Affairs and head of its Science Program, and was also a consultant to [[nato|NATO]].[1] OECD Vice Chair Hugo Thiemann was likewise a Club of Rome member, and in 1972 told *Europhysics News* that past research had been aimed at "understanding" in the belief it would help mankind, that experience had not borne this out, that a serious conflict was developing between planetary dimensions and population, and that "science policy should be guided by preservation of the biosphere."[1]

## Significance

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] uses these overlaps to argue that the [[world-problematique|World Problematique]] doctrine and the zero-growth paradigm behind [[limits-to-growth|*Limits to Growth*]] were not fringe positions but were carried into policy through the science bureaucracies of the trans-Atlantic system.[1]
