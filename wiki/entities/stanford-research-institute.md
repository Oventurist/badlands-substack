# Stanford Research Institute

Stanford Research Institute is invoked in Badlands Media commentary as a principal channel for intelligence-community funding of technology development and mind-control research.[1] Contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that "Stanford Research Institute" should be three words that mean something to informed readers, describing the institute as a place where much CIA funding goes to develop technology as well as to conduct research of the [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] type.[1]

In Carlson's account, technology developed under this funding is subsequently handed to private citizens who become the public face of the resulting companies, with Google, Facebook, Apple and Amazon offered as examples.[1] He connects this to Stanford University's Office of Technology Licensing, run for 27 years by [[katherine-ku|Katherine Ku]], whom he characterises as having overseen the licensing of CIA- and DARPA-created technology to private-sector figureheads.[1] The claim is presented as the author's interpretation rather than documented fact.[1]

## Role in the Tavistock network

The Stanford Research Institute was established in 1946, the year after the Second World War ended, and is described as one of the many institutes that spawned from or are connected to the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute]].[2] According to [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], it has taken on the bulk of culture manipulation in America today, including manipulation of the American school system.[2] Former MI6 officer [[john-coleman|John Coleman]] claims that, through Tavistock, the Stanford Research Institute controls America’s [[national-education-association|National Education Association]], with the goal of the American education system being to produce easily controlled people trained not to be independent thinkers.[2]
