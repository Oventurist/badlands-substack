# Lisa Blatt

**Lisa Blatt** is an American appellate attorney and a partner at the Washington law firm of Williams and Connolly who appears frequently before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]].[1]

## Heritage Foundation panel on the administrative state (2023)

Blatt took part in a September 20, 2023 event hosted by [[heritage-foundation|The Heritage Foundation]], where lawyers who regularly practice before the Court said the justices might move in the upcoming term to curtail the [[administrative-state|administrative state]].[1] Also participating was [[paul-clement|Paul Clement]] of Clement and Murphy, a former U.S. solicitor general.[1] The panel's discussion centered on [[sec-v-jarkesy|SEC v. Jarkesy]], accepted for review on June 30, 2023, which raises questions about the constitutionality of the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]]'s internal adjudication.[1]
