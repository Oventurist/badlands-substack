# Peterson Institute for International Economics

The Peterson Institute for International Economics is a Washington-based economic policy think tank. "The Harvard Control Grid" names it, along with the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]] and the [[group-of-thirty|Group of Thirty]], as a body where Harvard-linked elites including [[glenn-hutchins|Glenn Hutchins]] and [[mohamed-el-erian|Mohamed El-Erian]] hold seats or fellowships.[1]

The article characterizes these institutions collectively as shaping "narratives around central banking, international development, digital currency rollouts, and the global debt system," part of what it calls the shadow scaffolding through which Harvard's stewards manufacture rather than merely reflect elite consensus.[1]
