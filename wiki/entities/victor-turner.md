# Victor Turner

**Victor Turner** is the anthropologist cited in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] as the source of the essay's central definition of [[liminality|liminality]].[1]

The article credits Turner with describing the liminal as "a sacred disorientation, a moment outside time and structure where transformation becomes not only possible, but inevitable."[1] His formulation is introduced immediately after the essay's systems-theory framing of liminality as "a threshold state, the boundary between two modes of being," and it sets up the piece's subsequent neurological argument drawn from [[andrew-newberg|Andrew Newberg]] and [[shamini-jain|Shamini Jain]].[1]

Turner's authority functions in the essay to establish that the disorientation its readers report is a recognised anthropological category rather than a personal failure — the basis for the article's repeated insistence that "transformation demands disorientation."[1]
