# Human Rights Activists News Agency (HRANA)

The Human Rights Activists News Agency, known as HRANA, is an Iranian human-rights monitoring outlet whose casualty and arrest figures became the principal statistical source used by Western legacy media covering the [[2026-iran-protests|2026 Iran protests]].[1]

## Protest reporting

HRANA reported that Iranian security forces responded to the protests with force, including live ammunition in some locations, and documented demonstrations across 222 locations nationwide by the eighth day.[1] The agency claimed at least 20 people had lost their lives, including at least one member of the security forces, along with at least 51 citizen injuries and 990 arrests.[1] Badlands cautioned readers that although HRANA metrics were being reported across legacy media as the authoritative source for impact on the ground, those figures differ from official Iranian accounts.[1]
