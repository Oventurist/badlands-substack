# Kim Yo Jong

Kim Yo Jong is the sister of North Korean leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] and one of the most powerful figures in the North Korean state apparatus, frequently serving as the public voice of the leadership on matters of foreign policy and relations with Russia, South Korea and the United States.[1]

## Role in North Korea–Russia relations

Kim Yo Jong featured in Badlands Media's coverage of the deepening relationship between [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and her brother. In February 2024, after Kim Jong Un expressed admiration for Putin's personal Aurus limousine, the Russian president had a custom-built Aurus manufactured and shipped to Pyongyang for Kim Jong Un's personal use — a transfer that Badlands noted was in flagrant violation of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] sanctions Putin himself had endorsed in December 2017.[1] Kim Yo Jong publicly characterised the gift as a "clear demonstration of the special relationship" between the two leaders, a formulation Badlands read as an intentional signal of the alignment between Pyongyang and Moscow.[1]

Badlands described Kim Yo Jong as her brother's powerful sister, treating her statements as authoritative expressions of the North Korean leadership's posture rather than as ceremonial commentary.[1] Her remarks came in the run-up to Putin's announced two-day state visit to [[north-korea|North Korea]] in June 2024, his first trip to the country in 24 years.[1]
