# Thornton Township

Thornton Township is a township in the south suburbs of Chicago, Illinois, whose board governs local services and whose meetings became the site of repeated disorder during the tenure of Supervisor [[tiffany-henyard|Tiffany Henyard]].[1]

## 2025 board meeting brawl

A Thornton Township Board meeting on Tuesday, January 28, 2025 — the board's first in months — ended abruptly when a brawl erupted, with punches thrown and expletives flying, forcing leaders to clear the hall and stop working again.[1] Henyard, described as embattled, made a rare appearance and was drawn into the fray.[1] The violence began after the public comments portion of the meeting, when speaker Jedidiah Brown finished a lengthy criticism of Henyard with a profane insult; Henyard's allies, including her boyfriend Kamal Woods, confronted Brown before he could return to his seat, and a fistfight followed.[1]
