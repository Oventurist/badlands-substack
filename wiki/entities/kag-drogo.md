# KAG Drogo

**KAG Drogo** is a painter and anon active in the online Q community, described by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] as "a very talented painter and anon."[1]

## Warp Speed video

KAG Drogo published a video, circulated on X, arguing that [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] stopped the federal government from placing American citizens into [[fema-camps|FEMA camps]].[1] American Hypnotist cites the video approvingly in Part 3 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], stating agreement with KAG Drogo's assessment of Warp Speed and arguing that the event demonstrates a change of timelines.[1] The essay notes that watching the video is not necessary to follow its own argument, but embeds it for interested readers.[1]
