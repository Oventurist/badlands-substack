# John Oliver

**John Oliver** is a television host referenced in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series for having publicized the fact that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s ancestral family name was *Drumpf*.[1]

The series argues that although Oliver intended the revelation to mock Trump over his name, the irony is that he inadvertently disclosed something of profound importance: Drumpf is a German name, a fact the author says will help decode one of [[qanon|Q]]'s posts in the following installment.[1] Combined with [[erhard-landmann|Erhard Landmann]]'s claim that ancient German was once spoken worldwide, Oliver's mockery is recast by the series as evidence that Trump's very name comes from a globally influential language and culture.[1]
