# Abdel Fattah El-Sisi

Abdel Fattah El-Sisi is the President of [[egypt|Egypt]] and a central participant in regional diplomacy concerning [[israel|Israel]], Gaza and [[iran|Iran]].

## 2026 Abraham Accords initiative

According to the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," El-Sisi was among the leaders with whom [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held talks on a comprehensive settlement with Iran. In the [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement summarising those discussions, Trump named Egypt among the states that should simultaneously sign onto the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]], alongside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], [[qatar|Qatar]], [[pakistan|Pakistan]], [[turkey|Türkiye]], [[jordan|Jordan]] and [[bahrain|Bahrain]]. Trump acknowledged that one or two of these countries might have reason not to sign, which he said would be accepted.
