# Abdel Fattah el-Sisi

Abdel Fattah el-Sisi is the President of Egypt and a former general of the Egyptian armed forces.[1]

## Relationship with Donald Trump

At the 2026 G7 summit, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] recounted the story of how he first met el-Sisi, remarking that "we fell in love, deeply in love."[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted the exchange, along with Trump's declaration of "I'm the boss!" upon entering the official G7 meeting, as emblematic of the president's rhetorical style during a summit dominated by the fallout from the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding|US–Iran memorandum of understanding]].[1]

## Canceled Amman summit with Biden (October 2023)

Sisi was to have joined President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah II]] and [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] at a summit in [[jordan|Jordan]] during Biden's October 2023 Middle East trip.[2] The [[white-house|White House]] canceled the meeting on October 17, 2023 following the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza, as Arab leaders announced they were unwilling to meet Biden given United States support for Israel.[2]
