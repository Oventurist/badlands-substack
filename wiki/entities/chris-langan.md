# Chris Langan

Chris Langan is cited in Badlands Media commentary as the originator of the term "ziobanksters," which the author of "On Infiltration and Israel" says he borrows from Langan for expediency's sake when describing the financier class he holds responsible for the creation of the modern state of [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Use of the coinage

Within the article the term denotes figures such as [[max-warburg|Max Warburg]] and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], and is deliberately distinguished from Jewish people in general.[1] Beyond attributing the word to him, the article provides no further biographical detail about Langan.[1] For the concept itself see [[ziobanksters|ziobanksters]].[1]
