# Chris Langan

Chris Langan is cited in Badlands Media commentary as the originator of the term "ziobanksters," which the author of "On Infiltration and Israel" says he borrows from Langan for expediency's sake when describing the financier class he holds responsible for the creation of the modern state of [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Use of the coinage

Within the article the term denotes figures such as [[max-warburg|Max Warburg]] and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], and is deliberately distinguished from Jewish people in general.[1] Beyond attributing the word to him, the article provides no further biographical detail about Langan.[1] For the concept itself see [[ziobanksters|ziobanksters]].[1]

## Reality theorist and the CTMU

Badlands Media commentary also identifies Langan as a reality theorist and the author of the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] (CTMU).[2] "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) states that Langan "managed to demonstrate that reality quite literally is a *living language*," and directs readers to the CTMU paper and to the earlier article "The Metaphysics of 5G War" for the supporting proofs, adding that the topic is too large to treat fully in that essay.[2]

The author builds directly on Langan's framework, drawing from it the conclusions that human beings are pieces of the same living language as the rest of reality, that particular tongues are sub-languages of the "One Language" or Uni-Verse, and that the CTMU's distinction between physical and descriptive proximity supplies a plausible mechanism by which symbolic ritual could affect real events.[2] These claims underpin the essay's reconstructed [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] and its argument about [[authority-as-authorship|authority as authorship]].[2]

## The 2002 CTMU paper

"The Metaphysics of 5G War" (April 14, 2023) describes Langan as "a relatively obscure reality-theorist" who published a paper in 2002 entitled *The Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe*, and credits that paper with finally enabling metaphysics to be defined properly in relation to physics.[3] The essay attributes to Langan the proposition that reality is a self-contained, self-processing language, and the logical demonstration that cognition or awareness gives the universe a means to observe itself and process the feedback needed to generate new events.[3] It notes that the full paper is extremely dense and technical and points readers to a simplified treatment hosted at Hology.org.[3] Badlands commentary uses these results to argue that God uses human experience and cognition to structure reality, a claim central to the essay's account of the [[5g-war|5G war]] and to the thesis that [[truth-is-a-force-of-nature|truth is a force of nature]].[3]

## Citation in the Werewolf series

Part 8 of [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] series, "Modern Day Cyber-Necromancy" (August 20, 2024), again invokes Langan, asserting that it has "already been proven beyond any shadow of a doubt" that the universe is a living language which creates itself by talking to itself, and crediting the demonstration to his [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]].[4] Readers are directed both to the original CTMU paper and to the earlier Badlands article "The Metaphysics of 5G War" for fuller treatment.[4] The essay uses Langan's framework as the metaphysical basis for its account of [[voodoo|voodoo]] and dark occultism, and specifically for the CTMU's reconciliation of Descartes' extended matter and non-extended mind as a single monic substance.[4]

## IQ and cancellation

Part 1 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (22 October 2024) supplies the fullest biographical characterisation of Langan in the Badlands corpus, describing him as "one of the most canceled figures of the 20th and 21st centuries" and noting in a footnote that his IQ has been measured around 200, "making him one of the smartest human beings on the planet, if not THE smartest one."[5] The author speculates that "the controllers of this world simply don't appreciate it when a super-genius logically proves the existence of God, and the resultant, intrinsic value of the human spirit."[5] He adds that no professor, researcher or scientist has yet proposed a formal refutation of the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|CTMU]], preferring in his view to cancel the theory and its author in the hope that the public will be none the wiser.[5]

The same essay carries an explicit disclaimer that the author's comprehension of the CTMU comes from reading the paper himself, that Langan is not responsible for any errors or misjudgments on the author's part, and that where Langan contradicts him readers should go with Langan.[5] The author also notes he has expressed the theory in poetic rather than painstakingly technical terms to make it digestible for newcomers, and that he knows of nobody besides himself using the CTMU to interpret the [[q-operation|Q operation]].[5] Readers are directed to the original CTMU paper hosted at knowledgebase.ctmu.net.[5]

## Part 4 characterisation

Part 4 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (15 February 2025) calls Langan "one of the world's most well-hidden reality theorists" and gives his IQ as 195 points.[6] It states that he has published several papers on the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|CTMU]] and that widespread recognition of his work would completely upend the atheistic bias of mainstream academia, which the author offers as the reason Langan "has been subject to *severe* cancellation, going back several decades before the term 'cancelled' was well known."[6] The essay summarises his result as a proof that the universe had to be created by its own "global mind" doubling as a living language woven through everything, notes that in the CTMU each person is a "secondary image" of that language, and observes that Langan's name resembles the phrase "Christ Language."[6] Readers are again pointed to the foundational CTMU paper at knowledgebase.ctmu.net.[6]

## Part 3 characterisation and the "Professor Dave" dispute

Part 3 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (24 December 2024) describes Langan as "one of the smartest men in recorded history" and the author of the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|CTMU]], a theory of everything whose proof was published in a highly technical paper more than two decades earlier.[7] The essay states that mainstream academia has steadfastly refused to debate Langan on the merits of his work.[7]

It further alleges that "an insidious troll by the name of 'Professor Dave'" produced a take-down video purportedly debunking the CTMU in which he lied dozens of times about Langan and his work and failed to make even one factually correct statement about the theory.[7] [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] adds a caveat that he has simplified CTMU terminology for lay readers and that Langan, not himself, is the ultimate authority on these matters.[7]
