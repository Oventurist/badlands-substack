# Chris Langan

Chris Langan is cited in Badlands Media commentary as the originator of the term "ziobanksters," which the author of "On Infiltration and Israel" says he borrows from Langan for expediency's sake when describing the financier class he holds responsible for the creation of the modern state of [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Use of the coinage

Within the article the term denotes figures such as [[max-warburg|Max Warburg]] and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], and is deliberately distinguished from Jewish people in general.[1] Beyond attributing the word to him, the article provides no further biographical detail about Langan.[1] For the concept itself see [[ziobanksters|ziobanksters]].[1]

## Reality theorist and the CTMU

Badlands Media commentary also identifies Langan as a reality theorist and the author of the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] (CTMU).[2] "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) states that Langan "managed to demonstrate that reality quite literally is a *living language*," and directs readers to the CTMU paper and to the earlier article "The Metaphysics of 5G War" for the supporting proofs, adding that the topic is too large to treat fully in that essay.[2]

The author builds directly on Langan's framework, drawing from it the conclusions that human beings are pieces of the same living language as the rest of reality, that particular tongues are sub-languages of the "One Language" or Uni-Verse, and that the CTMU's distinction between physical and descriptive proximity supplies a plausible mechanism by which symbolic ritual could affect real events.[2] These claims underpin the essay's reconstructed [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] and its argument about [[authority-as-authorship|authority as authorship]].[2]

## The 2002 CTMU paper

"The Metaphysics of 5G War" (April 14, 2023) describes Langan as "a relatively obscure reality-theorist" who published a paper in 2002 entitled *The Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe*, and credits that paper with finally enabling metaphysics to be defined properly in relation to physics.[3] The essay attributes to Langan the proposition that reality is a self-contained, self-processing language, and the logical demonstration that cognition or awareness gives the universe a means to observe itself and process the feedback needed to generate new events.[3] It notes that the full paper is extremely dense and technical and points readers to a simplified treatment hosted at Hology.org.[3] Badlands commentary uses these results to argue that God uses human experience and cognition to structure reality, a claim central to the essay's account of the [[5g-war|5G war]] and to the thesis that [[truth-is-a-force-of-nature|truth is a force of nature]].[3]
