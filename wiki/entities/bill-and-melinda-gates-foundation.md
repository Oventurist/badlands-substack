# Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation

The Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation is the private charitable foundation established by Microsoft co-founder [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] in the year 2000. In "America Has a CIA-Run Economy," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats the foundation less as a philanthropic institution than as a reputation-management vehicle.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Reputational reversal

The article recalls that by the late 1990s Bill Gates was "one of the most hated men in the world," a status it attributes to his conduct at [[microsoft|Microsoft]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy) It then observes that in the year 2000 he started the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation, and "in no time, he was considered one of the world's greatest philanthropists."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

The article's pointed counter-observation is financial: while the world believed Gates was giving away all his money, his net worth doubled within a year.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy) The implication drawn is that the foundation functioned as an image-laundering mechanism rather than a genuine transfer of wealth away from its founder.

## Context within the article's thesis

The foundation appears in a passage questioning the authenticity of big-tech founder mythology more broadly. In the same paragraph, the article passes on the rumour that Gates's mother hired engineers from IBM to produce the early Microsoft software, undercutting his reputation as a self-made technical genius, before extending similar suspicion to [[apple|Apple]] by suggesting that [[darpa|DARPA]] probably created Siri.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy) These claims support the article's larger argument that America's dominant technology firms were manufactured assets of the defense and intelligence establishment rather than organic garage startups.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

More recently, the article notes, Gates is planning to fund nuclear power plants to power Microsoft's artificial intelligence operations — cited as one of several gestures by big-tech figures aligning themselves with the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's economic program.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Infant autopsy and tissue-harvesting study (2025)

In August 2025 Modernity News reported on a Gates Foundation–funded autopsy project, published in *Forensic Science, Medicine and Pathology*, describing newborn corpses chemically embalmed for weeks so their organs could be harvested, catalogued, and standardized into databases tied to artificial intelligence, forensic investigations, and global mortality surveillance programs.[2] The report placed the study after the Gates-financed "Baby Open Brains" project, in which researchers at the Masonic Institute for the Developing Brain released MRI scans of infants as young as one month old into global AI databases to build the backbone for a nationwide government brain-tracking program.[2] It also followed a Gates-funded tuberculosis vaccine trial in South Africa said to have left 260 previously healthy children infected with TB after injection with live *Mycobacterium bovis* bacteria, and Gates money backing U.S. gain-of-function experiments that engineered tuberculosis to grow unchecked.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded by invoking the "Baby Hitler Dilemma" and asking why the ethical debate concerns Hitler in hindsight rather than [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] in the present.[2]
