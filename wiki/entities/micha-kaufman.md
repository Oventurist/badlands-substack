# Micha Kaufman

Micha Kaufman is an Israeli technology executive, co-founder and chief executive officer of [[fiverr|Fiverr]], the freelance services marketplace that employs approximately 770 people in Israel and abroad and trades in New York at a valuation of roughly $1 billion.[1]

## Warnings about the Israeli high-tech sector

In 2024, Kaufman emerged as one of the industry figures publicly documenting the strain that prolonged conflict was placing on Israel's technology industry.[1] He observed that although multinational firms had long distributed staff across countries, Israeli companies had become notably stricter about geographic dispersion in order to ensure continuity during periods of massive reserve recruitment or regional instability.[1]

Kaufman was a signatory to a letter from high-tech industry leaders addressed to the Prime Minister, the Minister of Finance and the Minister of the Economy, warning against the abandonment of high-tech jobs to locations abroad.[1] The letter was sent amid escalation on Israel's northern border and ahead of High Court proceedings concerning the ultra-Orthodox exemption from military service.[1] He further warned that Israeli firms faced growing difficulty raising capital because investment funds were under pressure to reduce their exposure to Israel.[1]

These warnings formed part of the reporting that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited when arguing that Saudi capital routed through [[affinity-partners|Affinity Partners]] into the Israeli technology sector amounted to an indirect stabilization of an economy under severe wartime stress.[1]
