# C. S. Lewis

**C. S. Lewis** was a British writer and Christian apologist cited in the Badlands Media essay "On Nations and Natural Law" (5 October 2025) for his book *Mere Christianity*.[1] The essay notes that Lewis posits as evidence for the existence of God the fact that mankind, throughout all cultures and all history, has always sought something to worship.[1]

The author identifies this as retroductive reasoning — working backwards from an observed phenomenon to the simplest explanation for it — and observes that it is exactly the same method [[lysander-spooner|Lysander Spooner]] had employed sixty-five years earlier in arguing for the reality of justice and [[natural-law|Natural Law]].[1] By the same logic, the essay holds, we can know that justice exists because all cultures throughout all time have possessed some rudimentary understanding of the basic expectations for living peacefully with one another.[1]
