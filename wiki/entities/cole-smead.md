# Cole Smead

**Cole Smead** is an American investor and the chief executive officer of Smead Capital Management.[1]

## Commentary on the Federal Reserve

During the [[august-2024-global-market-selloff|August 2024 global market sell-off]], Smead told CNBC's "Squawk Box Europe" that the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] had little room for manoeuvre as recession fears mounted, because it remained occupied with the after-effects of very large fiscal stimulus that made the true condition of the economy hard to gauge.[1] He characterised the central bank's position with the phrase that gave the segment its title: "The Fed is trying to fight a ghost in some respects. The ghost is a massive amount of federal spending on deficit, 7% of U.S. GDP, and it's very hard to counteract a problem like that, that you didn't create."[1]

Smead added that he believed Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome "Jay" Powell]] was doing all he could to understand and counteract the problem with monetary policy, but insisted the underlying difficulty was fiscal in nature and showed no sign of ending.[1] His remarks were made on Monday, August 5, 2024, as United States futures tracked losses in Europe and Asia following a weaker-than-anticipated July jobs report and a higher unemployment rate.[1]
