# MITRE Corporation

The MITRE Corporation is a federally funded research and development organization named in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* as one of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s principal partners in the development of surveillance and public health control infrastructure.[1]

## Harvard collaboration

The article states that Harvard's pivot from thought leadership to applied surveillance predated COVID-19 and proceeded through pre-pandemic preparedness simulations, strategic research contracts, and high-level partnerships with organizations including MITRE, [[palantir|Palantir]], and the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]

A 2021 MITRE–Harvard collaborative paper is cited as evidence that the resulting tools — digital identity models, predictive policing algorithms, and Bluetooth and geolocation contact tracing protocols — were explicitly designed for rapid scalability during national crises.[1] Badlands Media interprets this design intent as proof that the systems were never conceived as temporary interventions but as permanent infrastructure.[1]
