# Bilderberg Group

The Bilderberg Group is a transatlantic private forum founded in 1955. [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes it as having been created by leading SS operatives such as [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]] of the Netherlands, and as having managed the takeover of nation states since its founding.[1]

Ehret raises the group in support of his broader argument that Nazism was not defeated in 1945 but absorbed: the majority of Nazis, including leading SS members, were sent to South America after the war and, instead of being punished, received new jobs working for [[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]], MI6 and NATO as part of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1] He points to the same continuity in the Trans-Atlantic banking system, the revival of eugenics, proxy wars in Ukraine, and the technocracy of the European Union.[1]
