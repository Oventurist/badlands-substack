# Edward Hackerman

Edward Fredrick Hackerman is a retired California firefighter and political donor who was arrested in June 2023 and accused of starting the 2022 [[oak-fire|Oak Fire]] in Mariposa County, California.[1] He was 71 years old at the time of his arrest, which took place on a Friday in mid-June 2023, and he reportedly had twelve years of experience as a firefighter.[1]

## Allegations

Hackerman was accused of igniting a blaze that charred more than 19,000 acres, destroyed 127 homes and damaged more than 60 other buildings.[1] He faced a number of charges following his arrest, including aggravated arson.[1] Reporting on the case noted that Hackerman had made donations to Democratic campaigns and to the [[lincoln-project|Lincoln Project]], the anti-Trump political action committee, although the number of such donations was described as low.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] characterized Hackerman as a "mostly peaceful Democrat donor" and argued that a Republican accused of the same conduct would almost certainly have been labeled a domestic terrorism case.[1] Johnny Q questioned whether Hackerman would be sentenced severely or "let off the hook," suggesting that justice "seems to swing in favor of being politically correct."[1] He also emphasized that the deeper tragedy was the destruction of the land itself, and noted the irony that the fire had initially been blamed on climate change before an arson arrest was made.[1]
