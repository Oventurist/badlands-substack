# SWAPO

SWAPO is the ruling left-wing political party of Namibia.[1]

The party dominates Namibian national and regional politics, holding office at the constituency level across the country's regions.[1] Among its longer-serving local officeholders is [[adolf-hitler-uunona|Adolf Hitler Uunona]], a SWAPO councilor in the Ompundja constituency of the Oshana region since 2004, who won about 85 percent of the vote there in 2020 and stood for re-election in the regional elections held on November 26, 2025.[1] Uunona's candidacy attracted international press coverage on account of his name, which his father gave him in reference to the Nazi leader and which Uunona has repeatedly disavowed in ideological terms.[1]
