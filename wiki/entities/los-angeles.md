# Los Angeles

Los Angeles is the largest city in California and, in the Badlands corpus, a focal point for arguments about the integrity of American election administration.

## The 2026 mayoral primary

[[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]] reports that in the city's 2026 nonpartisan mayoral primary, voters chose among fourteen candidates. City Council member [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] advanced to the November runoff against incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]], finishing ahead of former reality television personality [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]]. The contest drew national attention because of Pratt's celebrity profile and his criticism of city leadership following the [[palisades-fire|Palisades Fire]]; issues in the race included housing affordability, homelessness, crime, and the quality of city services.

## Election technology

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], citing [[canncon|CannCon]], writes that Los Angeles is the only jurisdiction in the United States that still uses [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] voting technology directly. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] describes the city's primary as an exhibition of familiar mechanics — a mail-in system with extended windows, results that "evolve" over days and weeks, and "the same counting choreography that has defined contested cycles since 2020."
