# I-SEC

I-SEC is an Israeli security company described in the Badlands News Brief as the parent company of the identity-verification firm [[au10tix|AU10TIX]] and as an Israeli cybersecurity firm founded by [[mossad|Mossad]] agents.[1]

## Claims made in Badlands coverage

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that I-SEC and its subsidiaries specialize exclusively in all aspects of airport security, and alleged that the company was both responsible for the security failures of the September 11, 2001 attacks — which created the public mandate to legislate the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]] — and a direct financial beneficiary of that Act's passage.[1] He described AU10TIX's very reason for existing as providing the compliance required by Section 326 of the Patriot Act, which "requires financial institutions to obtain, verify, and record identifying information for customers who open accounts or make changes to existing accounts."[1]

In June 2024, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] appeared to end the relationship between [[x-corp|X]] and I-SEC/AU10TIX, replacing the verification vendor with [[stripe|Stripe]].[1] The Badlands commentary speculated about "what Elon discovered during his investigation."[1]
