# Georges Bergès

Georges Bergès is the New York art dealer who represented [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s paintings, and who in January 2023 became a subject of congressional interest over the sale of that artwork.[1]

## House Oversight Committee request

Bergès, who has described Hunter Biden as "an American story" and who is reported to have Chinese business connections, confirmed on Thursday, January 26, 2023 that he had received a letter from the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] dated the previous Wednesday demanding his appearance before the investigative panel in February.[1] As of the end of January 2023 he had not agreed to testify, citing the need to consult legal counsel.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situated the request within the newly launched House Republican investigations into the Biden family's political profiteering, arguing that the committee's interest in the art sales suggested it viewed the shows as a potential channel for the family to receive funds in exchange for political favors.[1] DeLarme compared the arrangement to lucrative book deals, writing that "these people have long since mastered all the ways to launder money and peddle influence."[1]
