# Ray Dalio

Ray Dalio is a billionaire investor who, in June 2025, joined a list of prominent financiers publicly warning about the trajectory of the United States economy.[1]

## "How Countries Go Broke"

In a book titled *How Countries Go Broke: The Big Cycle*, published in early June 2025, Dalio argued that the US government debt situation is "nearing the point of no return" and approaching a "death spiral" that could threaten the stability of the world's largest economy.[1] He simultaneously assessed the risk of an imminent debt crisis as low, while rating the long-term risk as high.[1]

CNN's coverage placed Dalio's warning in the context of a bond market — normally described as the quiet bedrock of the US and global economies — that had grown volatile during 2025 amid [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s tariff program and tax bill agenda.[1] The outlet noted that some economists and investors had been sounding the alarm about the federal deficit for years, but that Wall Street had only recently begun to heed the caution.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] dismissed the framing of the story as novel, observing that "Ray Dalio has been doing this the entire time" and that CNN was presenting a long-standing position as newly urgent.[1] Paul tied the timing to the publication of Dalio's book and argued that the coverage served a political purpose: to build pressure against the Trump administration's "Big, Beautiful Bill."[1]
