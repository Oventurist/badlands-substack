# Kathy Chung

Kathy Chung is a longtime aide associated with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] whose role in coordinating Biden family travel has drawn attention from congressional investigators.[1]

Analysis by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] published in the Badlands News Brief identifies Chung as the person who arranged the September 2017 flight itineraries for Joe Biden and, separately, for [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[jim-biden|Jim Biden]] on the same dates, 11–17 September 2017, for travel to Ireland.[1] Herold flags the overlap as relevant to the oversight activity of Representative [[james-comer|James Comer]], who has pursued Chung's testimony and records in connection with the handling of Biden documents and travel.[1]

The itineraries feature in Herold's larger argument that the Ireland trip provided the occasion for a "sensitive" meeting between the younger Bidens and representatives of [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC China Energy]].[1]
