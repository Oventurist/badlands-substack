# William Knox D'Arcy

William Knox D'Arcy was the British speculator who obtained the first major petroleum concession in Persia and founded the company that became BP.[1]

In 1901 D'Arcy negotiated a petroleum concession with Mozzafar Al-Din, the Shah of Persia. The D'Arcy Concession gave him the exclusive right to prospect for oil, which was finally discovered at the Masjed Soleiman oil field in 1908 — the first oil discovery in the Middle East. D'Arcy formed the [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo-Persian Oil Company]] to begin extraction, marking the first enterprise of its kind in the region; in 1914 the British government purchased 51% of the company, effectively nationalizing it.[1]
