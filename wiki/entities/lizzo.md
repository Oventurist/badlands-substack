# Lizzo

**Lizzo** is an American singer and entertainer who appeared at a high-dollar Democratic fundraiser in late March 2024 alongside President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], former presidents [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], and other celebrities.[1]

The event, priced at up to $500,000 per ticket, raised $25 million for Biden's re-election campaign and was held hours after former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attended the wake of slain [[nypd|NYPD]] officer [[jonathan-diller|Jonathan Diller]].[1] Her presence at the fundraiser was cited by critics as emblematic of the contrast between "partying Dems" and Trump's mourning appearance.[1]
