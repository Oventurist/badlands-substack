# Ben Cohen

**Ben Cohen** is a co-founder of the ice cream company Ben & Jerry's and a left-wing political activist.[1]

## 2025 Senate hearing removal

On Wednesday, May 14, 2025, Capitol Police appeared to remove Cohen from a hearing of the Senate Committee on Health, Education, Labor and Pensions featuring Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]].[1] Anti-Israel protesters disrupted Kennedy's opening statement, and footage showed Cohen standing up before being removed by police.[1]

Earlier that day Cohen had spoken at a pro-Palestinian press conference outside the US Capitol alongside Democratic Michigan Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]].[1] During the disruption Cohen said, "You're killing poor kids in Gaza and paying for it by cutting Medicaid for kids here," according to a post on X that he later reposted himself.[1]
