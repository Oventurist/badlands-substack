# Briahna Joy Gray

Briahna Joy Gray, often abbreviated BJG, is an American commentator and journalist who served as National Press Secretary for [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] before moving into independent and web-based media.[1] In Badlands Media's coverage she is presented as a leading example of a rare species: an anti-establishment journalist operating on the political left.[1]

## Media roles

Gray is a contributing editor for [[current-affairs|Current Affairs]] and co-hosted [[the-hill|The Hill]]'s web series [[rising|Rising]].[1] Her on-air criticism of Bernie Sanders over his attacks on [[cornel-west|Cornel West]] was cited by Badlands Media as an illustration of her willingness to break with establishment Democrats.[1]

## Israel–Palestine and the Rising clash

In the weeks following the October 2023 outbreak of the Israel–Palestine conflict, Gray had a heated on-air exchange with her Rising co-host Robby Soave over her criticism of the Israeli government.[1] Badlands Media characterised Soave's response as a dramatic performance in the mould of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], and argued that neither man was displaying the sober mindset appropriate to decisions with extreme consequences.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands narrative

Badlands Media's argument is that figures like Gray — people who were "given hope and then duped" by Sanders and [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] — have been shaped by those betrayals into effective critics of the Democratic establishment, and that the populist left will actually listen to them.[1] The publication concedes its own audience will disagree with Gray on many topics, while framing her as evidence that common ground on war and empire is emerging across the left–right divide.[1]
