# Hamilton Fish III

Hamilton Fish III was a United States Congressman from New York identified by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the legislators successfully recruited to the Nazi cause by [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]].[1]

At one Madison Square Garden Nazi rally organized by Viereck, Fish was a featured speaker and advocated fervently for a pro-Nazi foreign policy centered on a German-American special relationship.[1] According to Albert H. Kahn's 1942 book Sabotage: The Secret War Against America, the headquarters of Viereck's Washington propaganda machine was Room 1424 in the House Office Building — the office of Congressman Hamilton Fish of New York — from which pro-Axis material was placed in the Congressional Record, reprinted in the tens of thousands and mailed nationwide postage-free using the franking privileges of various congressmen, many of whom were doubtless unaware of the use being made of their franked envelopes.[1]
