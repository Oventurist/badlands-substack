# Reconquête

Reconquête is a French hard-right political party founded by the polemicist and former presidential candidate [[eric-zemmour|Éric Zemmour]].[1] For the June 2024 European Parliament elections the party's lead candidate was [[marion-marechal|Marion Maréchal]], the niece of [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]].[1]

## Decline in 2024 polling

An Ipsos survey published in March 2024 found only 3 percent of French voters aged 18 to 24 backing Reconquête, down from 8 percent in December 2023.[1] The fall coincided with a nine-point rise for [[jordan-bardella|Jordan Bardella]]'s [[national-rally|National Rally]] among the same cohort, indicating that Reconquête was losing its share of the youth right-wing vote to the larger party rather than expanding the bloc.[1]
