# Fire Point

Fire Point is a Ukrainian drone manufacturer that, according to accounts cited in Badlands commentary, originally produced unmanned aerial platforms for scouting film locations before pivoting to military drone production after the outbreak of full-scale war with [[russia]].

The company became notorious in Ukrainian political reporting because of its rapid growth and large state contracts. In the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] noted that businessman [[timur-mindich]] — a co-owner of [[kvartal-95-studio]] and a figure described by critics as close to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] — is allegedly connected to Fire Point. The trajectory from film-industry equipment to wartime procurement is offered in that commentary as an illustration of how entertainment-world networks around Zelenskyy were converted into defense-contracting positions once Western military aid began flowing through Kyiv.

These connections are allegations reported in political commentary and have not been established by official Ukrainian findings.
