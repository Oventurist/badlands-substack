# Fire Point

Fire Point is a Ukrainian drone manufacturer described in Badlands coverage as "now notorious."[1] The company originally produced drones used for scouting filming locations and subsequently shifted to producing drones for warfare amid the [[russian-invasion-of-ukraine|Russian invasion of Ukraine]].[1]

Ukrainian businessman [[timur-mindich|Timur Mindich]] is alleged to be connected to the company.[1] Those allegations circulate in political commentary and had not been substantiated by evidence presented by the Ukrainian government at the time of reporting.[1]
