# ZIM Integrated Shipping Services

ZIM is an Israeli state-owned shipping corporation that maintained a 10,000-square-foot office in the North Tower of the World Trade Center.[1]

On September 4, 2001, one week before the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], ZIM abruptly vacated its premises, forfeiting a $50,000 security deposit and terminating its tenancy.[1] Badlands Media notes that no one subsequently inquired about the abrupt move and that the episode has been discreetly disregarded and forgotten.[1] The departure is cited alongside the [[odigo]] warning messages and the [[dancing-israelis|"Dancing Israelis"]] episode as circumstantial evidence in the article's discussion of possible Israeli foreknowledge.[1]
