# GART 2: Cocoa Beach

GART 2 was the second stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live conference series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1] Announced on June 29, 2023, the event was scheduled for September 7–10, 2023 in Cocoa Beach, Florida, with the conference held at the [[hilton-cocoa-beach-oceanfront|Hilton Cocoa Beach Oceanfront]].[1]

## Announcement and framing

The ticket announcement opened with the slogan "We are the news, now," which Badlands described as a saying that had come to define a growing movement within the Truth and America First communities and which the network said it had been doing its best to make a reality.[1] It credited reader support with Badlands' rapid growth into what it called one of the most-watched independent networks online, carrying dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities across its shows, and presented GART 2 as taking the network "to the next level" while inviting readers along for the ride.[1]

## Programme and ticketing

The weekend was billed as offering live, in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, live Q&A sessions with Badlands panels, and meet-and-greets with show hosts throughout the event.[1] In-person tickets included a Friday night Welcome Reception with Badlands Media, breakfast and lunch across the weekend, and open time to socialise with other "Badlanders" and with hosts as attendees sought to "level up in the [[information-war|Information War]] together."[1] An optional VIP package added a dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands show hosts and personalities to open the weekend "in style."[1]

Ticket sales were handled through the network's TicketSpice page.[1] Badlands reported that, following the success of the first GART in Arizona, tickets for the Cocoa Beach event were already moving fast, and said speakers and the itinerary were still to be published.[1] For readers unable to travel, virtual tickets to stream the entirety of GART 2 were announced as going on sale Monday, July 3, 2023.[1]
