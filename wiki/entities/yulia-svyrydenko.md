# Yulia Svyrydenko

Yulia Svyrydenko is a Ukrainian economist and politician who served as Prime Minister of [[ukraine]] before being replaced in July 2026.

She rose through the economic ministries and was closely associated with Kyiv's negotiations with Washington over minerals, reconstruction financing, and wartime budget support. Her removal came as part of the government reshuffle described in the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026: the [[verkhovna-rada]] voted 289–22 to confirm former [[naftogaz]] chief executive [[sergii-koretskyi]] in her place, with President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] arguing that energy-sector experience was the decisive qualification heading into another winter of Russian strikes on Ukrainian infrastructure.

Zelenskyy characterized the change as a leadership "renewal." Opposition lawmakers questioned the purpose of rotating officials without accompanying structural reform, and critics highlighted unverified reports linking businessman [[timur-mindich]] to members of the new cabinet.
