# Yulia Svyrydenko

Yulia Svyrydenko is a Ukrainian politician who served as Prime Minister of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] until July 2026.[1] She was replaced when parliament voted 289–22 to confirm former [[naftogaz|Naftogaz]] chief executive [[sergii-koretskyi|Sergii Koretskyi]] as prime minister, part of a broader wartime government reshuffle by President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] that also included planned changes at the Defense Ministry and other senior posts.[1]
