# Colorado Department of Elections

The Colorado Department of Elections is the division of the Colorado Secretary of State's office responsible for administering elections in the state, including the granting or denial of ballot access.[1] At the time of the 2023 [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|ballot disqualification trial]], the office was headed by Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]], who was the named respondent in the lawsuit seeking to keep [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] off the state's 2024 presidential ballot.[1]

## Testimony in the ballot trial

Deputy Elections Director Hillary Rudy, an employee of the department since 2006, testified in [[denver-district-court|Denver District Court]] about the processes and the teams responsible for granting or denying ballot access and other departmental procedures.[1] She was cross-examined by Scott Gessler, lead attorney for Trump and himself a former Colorado Secretary of State — and Rudy's former superior.[1]

Griswold's dual position drew commentary from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who noted that Griswold was simultaneously a witness for the plaintiffs in the NAACP-led voter intimidation suit filed against her and two co-defendants and the respondent in the Trump ballot case.[1]
