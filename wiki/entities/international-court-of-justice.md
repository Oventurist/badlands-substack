# International Court of Justice

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations, whose advisory opinions carry significant diplomatic weight even where they are not binding.

The ICJ features in the June 9, 2026 *Badlands Brief* through its 2019 advisory opinion on the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]], which found that the United Kingdom's separation of the islands from [[mauritius|Mauritius]] in 1965 was unlawful.[1] That opinion is described as having influenced the sovereignty dispute and the eventual British decision to transfer the territory to Mauritius while retaining a 99-year lease over the base at [[diego-garcia|Diego Garcia]].[1]

## South Africa's Gaza petition in Badlands commentary (May 2025)

Badlands coverage of the May 2025 White House meeting between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[cyril-ramaphosa|Cyril Ramaphosa]] emphasized that it was Ramaphosa's [[south-africa|South African]] government that filed the original petition at the ICJ seeking war crimes charges against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and the Israeli government over the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] offensive in Gaza that followed the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack.[2] The commentary suggested that publicizing allegations of a "white genocide" in South Africa could bring pressure to bear on the pending ICJ case, and noted the dynamic of a petitioner state simultaneously standing accused of atrocities of its own.[2]

## May 2024: South Africa's Gaza genocide arguments

In mid-May 2024, the ICJ convened to hear South Africa's new arguments alleging Israeli genocide in Gaza.[3] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted that the hearing fell on the same day as the [[arab-summit-2024-bahrain|33rd Arab Summit]] in Bahrain, arguing the convergence was no coincidence but evidence of coordinated pressure on Israel by a Saudi-built network of bilateral partners.[3]
