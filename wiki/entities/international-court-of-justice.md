# International Court of Justice

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations, empowered to settle disputes between states and to issue advisory opinions on questions of international law.

In [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]], the ICJ appears as the source of a 2019 advisory opinion finding that the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]]'s separation of the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Islands]] from [[mauritius|Mauritius]] in 1965 was unlawful. The Brief states that this opinion has shaped the ongoing sovereignty dispute and the British decision to transfer the archipelago to Mauritius while retaining lease access to the base at [[diego-garcia|Diego Garcia]].
