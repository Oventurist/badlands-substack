# Khalid bin Salman

Khalid bin Salman is the Defense Minister of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the younger brother of Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[1] The Badlands Brief identified him as the figure appearing in a widely circulated photograph of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s box at the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final, after readers asked the author to identify him.[1]
