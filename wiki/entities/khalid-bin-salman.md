# Khalid bin Salman

Khalid bin Salman is the Defense Minister of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the younger brother of Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]. He appears in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] through a photograph taken in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s box at the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final at [[metlife-stadium|MetLife Stadium]]. Readers who circulated the image asked contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] to identify one of the guests; he answered that he believed it to be Khalid bin Salman. The identification is presented in the source as the author's belief rather than confirmed reporting, and it appears in the same section as the Brief's discussion of the draft U.S.–Saudi civilian nuclear cooperation agreement.
