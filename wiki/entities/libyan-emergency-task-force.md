# Libyan Emergency Task Force

The Libyan Emergency Task Force (LETF) was a Washington lobbying group for which [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] served as Political Director during the 2011 Libyan Revolution, alongside his role as Executive Director of the Libyan Council of North America and his close work with top officials of the [[transitional-national-council|Transitional National Council]].[1]

The Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest" treats the LETF as the direct forerunner of the [[syrian-emergency-task-force|Syrian Emergency Task Force]] — "a similar name, and the same playbook" — arguing that both were vehicles by which enemies of the United States extracted taxpayer money through the lobbying system.[1] Before becoming a lobbyist for ISIS in Washington and escorting [[john-mccain|John McCain]] into Syria, the article says, Moustafa was a lobbyist for the Libyan insurgents seeking to overthrow [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]] under the LETF banner.[1]
