# Steven Crowder

Steven Crowder is a conservative political commentator known for publishing material outside of official channels, including leaked documents connected to high-profile criminal investigations.[1]

## Publication of the Covenant School manifesto pages

In November 2023, Crowder posted photographs of three pages that he alleged were part of a manifesto written by [[audrey-hale|Audrey Hale]], the shooter who killed six people at the Christian Covenant School in Nashville, Tennessee in March 2023.[1] The release renewed public pressure over material that authorities had withheld from publication.

The Metropolitan Nashville Police Department responded on Monday, November 6, 2023, saying the three images were not "crime scene images" and that it was in communication with the Metropolitan Department of Law as a newly opened investigation examined the dissemination of the photographs during an online discussion about the school.[1] The department told the Daily Caller earlier the same day that it did not know whether the three pages were authentic.[1] Authorities had previously confirmed the existence of a manifesto in which Hale allegedly planned the attack over several weeks, material under ongoing review by the FBI's Behavioral Analysis Unit.[1]
