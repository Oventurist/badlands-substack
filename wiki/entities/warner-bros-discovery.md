# Warner Bros. Discovery

Warner Bros. Discovery is a major American media and entertainment conglomerate. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears at the center of the February 2026 consolidation scramble among legacy Hollywood studios.[1]

## The February 2026 talks

Warner Bros. Discovery disclosed in a regulatory filing that it had reopened talks with [[paramount-global|Paramount]] about potential business arrangements, after receiving a waiver from [[netflix|Netflix]].[1] AP News reported that Warner was reopening takeover talks with Skydance-owned Paramount while still backing its existing merger agreement with Netflix.[1] Any resulting deal would require regulatory approval because it involves broadcast assets owned by Paramount and the licenses attached to them — placing the [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] in the path of the transaction.[1]

## Interpretation in the corpus

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the Paramount–Warner talks had been dead as of December 2025, and read their revival as a defensive reflex: "a 'spider' centralizes when it perceives a new threat."[1] She argued the studios were repeating the music industry's Napster-era error — attempting to enforce traditional governing rights and preserve monopoly position rather than transforming their business model — and that merging their "spiders" so they have only one head makes them more vulnerable, not less, to decentralized "starfish" competitors.[1] In her framing, the studios' market share is not the natural result of market forces, and the loss of their ability to maintain that ruse is existential.[1]
