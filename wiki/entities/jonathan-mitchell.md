# Jonathan Mitchell

**Jonathan Mitchell** is the attorney who represented former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] in [[anderson-v-griswold|Anderson v. Griswold]], the Colorado ballot-disqualification case argued on February 8, 2024.[1]

## Supreme Court argument

As the petitioning party seeking review, Trump's attorneys went first, and Mitchell presented multiple interrelated and distinct arguments for overturning the Colorado Supreme Court's decision to remove Trump from the state's 2024 ballot.[1] The justices questioned him about the evidentiary record below, particularly the lower court's decision to admit the January 6 Report and the testimony of Professor Simi, which Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized as "minority-reporting pseudoscience."[1] Although the justices challenged many of Mitchell's arguments, Badlands coverage judged him solid and credited him with "by far, the winning argument" of the day.[1]

Observers including Fox News legal correspondent Shannon Bream reported that the justices appeared to be leaning in favor of Trump's position, making it unlikely that the high court would uphold the Colorado ruling.[1]
