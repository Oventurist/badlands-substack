# Notre Dame Cathedral

Notre Dame is the iconic Gothic cathedral in Paris, badly damaged in a 2019 fire and reopened in December 2024 following a restoration financed by donors from 150 countries at a cost of nearly 700 million euros.[1]

## 2024 reopening

President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he would attend the reopening of Notre Dame in Paris on Saturday, December 7, 2024 — his first major outing since winning the election.[1] "President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] has done a wonderful job ensuring that Notre Dame has been restored to its full level of glory, and even more so. It will be a very special day for all!" Trump said in a post on Truth Social.[1]

The reopening of the World Heritage site was expected to be a major global affair, with heads of state and government from about 50 nations attending.[1] Macron had been the first foreign leader to congratulate Trump after his win, and the two worked together closely during Trump's first term, though at times with a strained relationship.[1] Entertaining world leaders was not Macron's only concern that week: the French government led by Prime Minister [[michel-barnier|Michel Barnier]] faced a no-confidence vote it was expected to lose.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing as an architecture professional, said he liked the story because it advocates for the restoration of thoughtful architecture and craftsmanship in construction, and because it brought resolution to an episode "that began with [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]."[1] He referenced the notorious photograph of [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]] drinking champagne with a smug glare as Notre Dame is seen burning in the reflection on the glass, which he read as "Michelle toasting to the death of western civilization," manifested physically in Notre Dame as an iconic Christian cathedral and monument of high culture.[1] Citing Budapest's restoration of Baroque buildings lost during World War II, he expressed hope that a revival of high culture would spread across Europe.[1]
