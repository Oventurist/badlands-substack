# Jewish Insider

Jewish Insider is a Washington-focused news outlet covering the intersection of American politics, Congress, and Israel-related policy.[1] Its reporting is frequently cited as a barometer of sentiment among pro-Israel lawmakers and their staff.[1]

## Reporting on Republican unease with the January 2025 ceasefire

In January 2025 Jewish Insider reported that, despite [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s push to secure the release of remaining hostages before his inauguration, some Republicans worried that the terms of the emerging [[gaza-ceasefire|Gaza ceasefire]] could hurt Israel's ability to defend itself and to eliminate future terrorist threats.[1] The outlet reported that congressional Republicans had begun "fretting that Trump could force them to back a deal that involves terms they've opposed for over a year," with lawmakers and senior staffers discussing the matter privately and raising concerns with Trump's transition team.[1]

Among the few willing to speak publicly was Senator [[thom-tillis|Thom Tillis]], who told the outlet he was "really in the skeptical column for anything related to [[hamas|Hamas]]" and objected to the group being included in negotiations at all.[1] Badlands Media used the Jewish Insider reporting to illustrate the bind Republicans faced: they could not criticize Trump's deal without effectively endorsing the near-identical framework they had denounced under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]
