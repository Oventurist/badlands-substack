# Smedley Butler

Smedley Butler was a decorated United States general whose 1930s writing is cited in the Badlands corpus as an early and authoritative exposure of the economics behind war.[1]

## "War Is a Racket"

Butler told the public in the 1930s that war is a racket, and that the puppeteers play both sides against each other — a formulation quoted by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] as the truth that the general public will rediscover once [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] conditioning breaks down.[1] In that essay Butler's conclusion is presented as something Badlands readers and comparable groups have understood for many years, and which the wider public will encounter as narratives around the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Palestine conflict]] and a stalled [[world-war-iii|WWIII]] collapse.[1]
