# Smedley Butler

**Smedley Butler** was a decorated United States general remembered in alternative-media commentary for his 1930s declaration that [[war-is-a-racket|war is a racket]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Legacy in Badlands commentary

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay invokes Butler as an early witness to the truth that readers are expected to rediscover when mainstream war narratives collapse: that war is a racket and that "the puppeteers play both sides against each other."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The essay presents Butler's thesis as continuous with its own claim that the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]] is being used to enflame and direct public emotion.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Butler's argument is preserved in his book *War Is a Racket*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Thwarting the 1934 bankers' coup

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] credits Butler with defeating the pro-fascist Wall Street establishment's 1934 coup d'état plans against President [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]], describing him as a patriotic general.[2] The [[business-plot|coup plot]] came a year after a failed February 1933 attempt to assassinate Roosevelt, and formed part of a campaign against the New Deal that had begun the day the anti-fascist president was elected in 1932.[2]

## Blowing the whistle on the coup

Butler played along with Wall Street's plans for some months before deciding to publicly blow the whistle in Congress, exposing the intention to use him as a "puppet dictator" who would lead thousands of [[american-legion|American Legion]] legionnaires in a storming of the White House to displace [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]].[3] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] credits the truth-telling atmosphere created by the [[pecora-commission|Pecora Commission]] with causing the [[business-plot|coup plot]] to come undone before it could succeed, and writes that Butler's startling revelations amplified Roosevelt's popular support and inoculated much of the population from the fake news pouring out of Wall Street propaganda agencies.[3]
