# American Academy of Pediatrics

The American Academy of Pediatrics is a professional medical organization for pediatricians that served as lead challenger to the federal vaccine policy changes advanced by Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]].[1]

## Challenge to federal vaccine policy

The Academy, joined by other medical organizations, sued over the administration's revision of federal vaccine guidance, arguing that federal health officials acted outside legal procedures while advancing a broader restructuring of national immunization policy.[1] The suit targeted both the reduction of recommended childhood immunizations implemented by the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] on January 5 — which cut the routine list to eleven vaccines and downgraded recommendation status for rotavirus, influenza, and hepatitis A — and Kennedy's removal of all seventeen members of the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]].[1]

U.S. District Judge Brian Murphy, sitting in Boston, sided with the Academy and its co-plaintiffs in a ruling issued in March 2026, halting parts of the policy and staying the replacement advisers' appointments and further policy votes.[1]
