# Zacarias Moussaoui

Zacarias Moussaoui (rendered "Zaccarias Moussaoui" in the source) is described in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as a hijacker associated with the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]

## Relevance to the Q corpus

Moussaoui appears in Chapter 14 of the series only through his legal representation: the article states that [[sadiq-khan|Sadiq Khan]], later Mayor of London, acted as Moussaoui's lawyer.[1] That connection is offered as part of the answer to Q's drop #100 question about the Mayor of London, with the implication that Khan's client list is significant to understanding his later political role.[1]
