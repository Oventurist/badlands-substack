# Just the News

**Just the News** is an American news outlet cited in the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 as the publication that obtained the August 10, 2022 internal [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] email in which [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] questioned whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had declassified the documents seized in the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]].

The outlet is treated in the corpus as one of the independent and alternative-media sources through which documents relevant to the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]] investigation reach the public, in contrast to legacy outlets.


## April 2026: the Brennan records report

The Brief of April 20, 2026 cites a Just the News report that the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] requested transcripts and records from two [[senate|Senate]] committees involving former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], setting a February 23 deadline that the Senate did not meet, delaying an investigation based in Fort Pierce, Florida; it also reported that [[joseph-digenova|Joseph diGenova]] was scheduled to begin work as a lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters.
