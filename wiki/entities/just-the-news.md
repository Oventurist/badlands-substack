# Just the News

Just the News is an American news outlet whose records request prompted the 2026 declassification of [[intelligence-community-inspector-general|Intelligence Community Inspector General]] memos relating to the whistleblower complaint behind the [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|first impeachment]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] declassified the August 2019 documents at the outlet's request, producing what Badlands described as "a starkly different portrait" of the alleged whistleblower — a figure whose name and face were never shown to the public and whose written complaint accusing Trump of hijacking [[ukraine|Ukraine]] policy for political gain was used by Democrats to launch impeachment proceedings.[1]

## Reporting on the Trump classified-documents case (2023)

In June 2023, Just the News reported that federal prosecutors had notified [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] he was a criminal target likely to face imminent indictment in the classified-documents probe, and that the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] had rebuffed a request to delay charges pending investigation of prosecutorial misconduct claims.[2] Sources told the outlet that the alleged misconduct — a senior prosecutor discussing a federal judgeship with a key witness's lawyer — was pending in a secret case before Chief Judge [[james-boasberg|James E. Boasberg]], and that an indictment crafted by Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] could be handed up within the week.[2]
