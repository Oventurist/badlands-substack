# Mahmoud Abbas

Mahmoud Abbas is the president of the Palestinian Authority. In the Badlands Media corpus he is listed among the regional leaders held to be participants in the emerging Middle Eastern security architecture described by contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].

## Role in the regional framework

The March 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-509|Badlands Brief]] names Abbas as "Mahmoud Abbas of Palestine" in a roster of leaders — alongside [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah]] of [[jordan|Jordan]], President [[joseph-aoun|Aoun]] of [[lebanon|Lebanon]], President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Al Sharaa]] of [[syria|Syria]], [[ilham-aliyev|Ilham Aliyev]] of [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]], [[hamad-bin-isa-al-khalifa|King Hamad]] of [[bahrain|Bahrain]], General [[abdel-fattah-el-sisi|Al-Sisi]] of [[egypt|Egypt]], [[mohammed-bin-zayed|Mohammed bin Zayed]] of the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]], Sultan Haitham bin Tariq of Oman and Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim Al Thani]] of [[qatar|Qatar]] — who the commentary asserts have at some point pledged loyalty to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and, in doing so, to "the common cause of peace."

The same edition reports Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]]'s accusation that [[israel|Israel]] was exploiting shifting global attention to sabotage efforts toward a two-state solution in the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israeli–Palestinian conflict]], the diplomatic track in which Abbas is the principal Palestinian interlocutor.
