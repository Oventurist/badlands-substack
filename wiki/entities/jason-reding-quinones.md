# Jason Reding Quinones

Jason Reding Quinones is the United States Attorney for the Southern District of Florida and a prosecutor involved in developing the broad [[grand-conspiracy-probe|grand conspiracy inquiry]] examining whether past investigations and prosecutions involving President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] amounted to criminal conspiracy.[1]

## Office and personnel

The inquiry is being run within Reding Quinones's district, where prosecutors are reviewing prior federal probes into Trump, including the [[russiagate-hoax|Russian Collusion investigation]] tied to his 2016 campaign, cases brought by former Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], and the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2022 search of Mar-a-Lago for classified documents.[1] In June 2026 the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] confirmed that [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]], a White House official who had assisted Trump's efforts to examine the 2020 election results, joined the office as a senior attorney reporting to Reding Quinones.[1] The overall effort is supervised by [[joe-digenova|Joe diGenova]], counselor to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].[1]

## Brennan grand jury (December 2025)

As U.S. Attorney, Reding Quinones informed lawyers for former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] in December 2025 that a Miami grand jury had identified Brennan as a target of a criminal investigation examining his role in the [[2017-intelligence-assessment-on-russian-interference|2017 Intelligence Community Assessment]] on alleged Russian interference in the 2016 election.[2] Brennan's attorneys responded with a letter to chief U.S. District Judge [[cecilia-altonaga|Cecilia M. Altonaga]] accusing the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] of judge shopping, alleging that prosecutors were manipulating grand jury and case assignment procedures to place the matter before U.S. District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]], and citing alleged leaks that violated grand jury secrecy rules.[2]
