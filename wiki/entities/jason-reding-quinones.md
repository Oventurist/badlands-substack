# Jason Reding Quinones

Jason Reding Quinones is the United States Attorney for the Southern District of Florida. The June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief describes him as a prosecutor involved in developing a broad "grand conspiracy" inquiry examining whether past investigations and prosecutions involving President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] amounted to criminal conspiracy.

Newly hired [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] senior attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] reports to Reding Quinones in that office. The inquiry under his district's supervision is reviewing prior federal probes into Trump, including the Russian Collusion investigation tied to the 2016 campaign, cases brought by former Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], and the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2022 search of [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] for classified documents. The broader effort is supervised by [[joe-digenova|Joe diGenova]], counselor to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].
