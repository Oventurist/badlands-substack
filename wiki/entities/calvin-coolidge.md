# Calvin Coolidge

Calvin Coolidge was President of the United States during much of the 1920s, assuming office after the early death of President [[warren-harding|William Harding]] in 1923.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] characterizes him as "JP Morgan's President Coolidge," describing his administration — with Treasury Secretary [[andrew-mellon|Andrew Mellon]] — as having deregulated the banks, imposed austerity on the country, and devised the broker-loan scheme that let speculators borrow 90 percent against their stock.[1]

In this account the resulting bubble was not an accident of the market but a deliberately inflated structure: investment in the real economy was halted during the 1920s while speculation reached "insanity," setting up the controlled blowout of the [[stock-market-crash-of-1929|1929 crash]].[1]
