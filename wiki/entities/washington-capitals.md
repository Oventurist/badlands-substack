# Washington Capitals

The Washington Capitals are a National Hockey League franchise based in Washington, D.C., best known in recent decades as the club of Russian forward [[alexander-ovechkin|Alexander Ovechkin]].[1]

## Ovechkin era

Ovechkin has spent his entire twenty-year professional career with the Capitals, and Washington is described as his second home — the city where he became an NHL legend after Moscow gave him his start in the sport.[1] On April 6, 2025, playing for the Capitals against the New York Islanders at UBS Arena, he scored his 895th career goal against goaltender [[ilya-sorokin|Ilya Sorokin]] to set the all-time NHL scoring record previously held by [[wayne-gretzky|Wayne Gretzky]].[1]

## Sports diplomacy

Because the franchise's marquee player is a Russian national closely followed by the Kremlin, the Capitals became an incidental element of United States–Russia cultural diplomacy in 2025, after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] discussed hockey and possible exhibition games between Russian and American players during a March 18 phone call.[1]
