# Jon Harrison

Jon Harrison is a former chief of staff to the Secretary of the Navy who was dismissed by Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] in October 2025.[1]

## Dismissal

Hegseth reportedly fired Harrison on Friday, October 3, 2025; Harrison had helped carry out a reshuffle of the Navy's bureaucracy.[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] did not state a reason for the firing, telling Politico only that "Jon Harrison will no longer serve as Chief of Staff to the Secretary of the Navy" and that "we are grateful for his service to the Department."[1] Harrison did not immediately comment on his departure.[1]

## Context

The removal came after the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] warned of major trimming of the federal government during the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]], and in the same week that the [[us-senate|Senate]] confirmed Navy Undersecretary Hung Cao.[1]
