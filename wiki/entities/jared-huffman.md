# Jared Huffman

**Jared Huffman** is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from California who emerged in June 2024 as a leading congressional critic of [[project-2025|Project 2025]].[1]

## Project 2025 working group

Huffman spoke to *The Guardian* ahead of the launch of a congressional working group designed to sound the alarm about the conservative roadmap and to ensure that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] never had the chance to implement what Huffman called its extreme agenda.[1] He warned that the blueprint for a potential second Trump presidency would take "a wrecking ball" to America's democratic norms and institutions.[1]

Huffman argued that the plan would lay waste to democratic institutions, checks on executive power, individual rights and freedoms, and church-state separation in order to impose a far-right agenda: "You can look at what they're going to do to our democracy: weaponising the Department of Justice, clearing away any norms that limit executive power."[1]

## Badlands response

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the framing, writing that Democrats claimed Project 2025 would take a wrecking ball to America's institutions "after taking a wrecking ball to America's democratic norms and institutions."[1]
