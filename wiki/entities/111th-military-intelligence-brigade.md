# 111th Military Intelligence Brigade

The 111th Military Intelligence Brigade is a United States Army intelligence formation based at [[fort-huachuca|Fort Huachuca]], Arizona. Badlands Media's "Q For Dummies" series identifies command of the brigade as one of the most prominent postings in the career of [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]].[1]

## Role in Q For Dummies commentary

Chapter 13 of the series addressed Q's question in Drop #97 about Flynn's background, answering that Flynn's career lay within the intelligence sector of the Army, that he retired as a lieutenant general — a three-star officer — and that his command of the 111th at Fort Huachuca was among his most notable assignments.[1] The author highlighted that the brigade's Arizona location placed Flynn in charge of an intelligence unit just south of [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], the only county named directly in the Q drops.[1]

The chapter treated the assignment as evidence of the clearance level and institutional access Flynn would necessarily have held, which underpins the drop's sequence of rhetorical questions — who knows where the bodies are buried, who has access, what is MI, who was part of MI during the Obama term, and who was fired during the Obama term — each answered by the author with Flynn's name.[1]
