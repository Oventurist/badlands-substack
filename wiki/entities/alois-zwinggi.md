# Alois Zwinggi

Alois Zwinggi is a Swiss executive of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] who was named the organization's interim chief executive officer in late February 2026.

## Appointment as interim CEO

Zwinggi's elevation followed the resignation of [[borge-brende|Børge Brende]], the Forum's president and chief executive, who stepped down on February 26, 2026 amid scrutiny of his documented dinners and 2018–2019 communications with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]. As reported in the [[badlands-brief-40d|February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief]], Zwinggi was designated to serve in the role on an interim basis while the Forum searches for a permanent successor to Brende. The Brief does not detail Zwinggi's prior career at the organization beyond his assumption of the interim post.
