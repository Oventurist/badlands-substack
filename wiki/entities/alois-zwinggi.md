# Alois Zwinggi

Alois Zwinggi is a [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] executive who was appointed interim chief executive officer of the Forum in February 2026.[1]

Zwinggi assumed the interim role following the resignation of president and CEO [[borge-brende|Børge Brende]] on 26 February 2026, which came after scrutiny of Brende's past interactions with convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] The Forum indicated that Zwinggi would serve while a permanent successor was sought.[1]
