# Wintermute

Wintermute is a crypto-trading firm that acts as a market maker and liquidity provider. Its annual review and outlook, cited in Badlands coverage, forecast a deeper integration of cryptocurrency into traditional finance.[1]

## Predictions

Wintermute predicted that crypto would become more deeply integrated into traditional finance through exchange-traded funds and corporate holdings, and that a large corporate event such as an acquisition or merger would be settled in stablecoins.[1] Among its other forecasts: that the United States would begin consultations to create a [[us-bitcoin-strategic-reserve|strategic bitcoin reserve]], with China, the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]] and Europe following suit; that a publicly listed company would sell debt or shares to buy ether, mimicking MicroStrategy's bitcoin acquisition policy; and that a systemically important bank would offer spot cryptocurrency trading to clients.[1]

## Market context

The predictions followed substantial demand growth, with over-the-counter institutional trading volumes more than tripling after the approval of bitcoin ETFs and the later arrival of ether ETFs.[1] Wintermute attributed the interest to improved regulatory clarity and demand for capital-efficient trading, reporting that the average OTC trade size increased 17% and total volume rose 313%.[1]

Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] highlighted the report alongside [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s commentary on Middle East sovereign alliance dynamics.[1]
