# Paul Ryan

Paul Ryan is a former Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Wisconsin who served as Speaker of the House.[1]

## Speakership contest with Kevin McCarthy

According to March 2024 reporting summarized in the Badlands News Brief, [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] turned down the role of White House Chief of Staff during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first administration, opting instead to compete for the Speakership — a leadership battle he ultimately lost to Ryan.[1] The detail resurfaced in coverage of a Bloomberg report that McCarthy was under consideration for Chief of Staff should Trump retake the White House in the November 2024 election.[1]

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] named Paul Ryan in the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]], telling the audience that the party would "never go back to the party of Paul Ryan, Karl Rove, and Jeb Bush" after describing the pre-2016 Republican leadership as "Freaks, Neocons, Globalists, Open Border Zealots, and Fools."[2] Badlands contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] cited the naming of Ryan as evidence that the "infiltrated version of the Republican party is dead."[2]
