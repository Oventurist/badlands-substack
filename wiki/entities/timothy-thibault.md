# Timothy Thibault

**Timothy Thibault** is a former key official of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] who became a focus of Republican scrutiny following the declassification of records related to the bureau's [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]] investigation.[1]

Republicans argue that Thibault showed extreme anti-Trump bias, demonstrated a willingness to target [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] early in his first term, and attempted to slow-walk or block the FBI's investigation into [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1] In April 2022 he is said to have helped spark the investigation dubbed Arctic Frost, which was later carried on by then-Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and which led to criminal charges against Trump related to the Capitol riot.[1]

The scrutiny of Thibault accompanied broader criticism of then-FBI Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]], under whose tenure the effort occurred.[1]
