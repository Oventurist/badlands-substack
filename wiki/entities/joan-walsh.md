# Joan Walsh

Joan Walsh is an American progressive commentator and the former editor-in-chief of the online publication Salon.[1]

## Response to the 2024 Washington Post non-endorsement

Walsh was among the liberal journalists who reacted angrily to [[washington-post|The Washington Post]]'s October 2024 decision not to endorse a presidential candidate.[1] Going beyond condemnation, she publicly pushed for a boycott of the paper.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited her call as an example of media figures treating an abstention from partisan endorsement as grounds for punishing the outlet, part of the wider reaction to the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
