# Royal Academy of Arts

The Royal Academy of the Arts was organised in London in 1772 by the Philadelphian republican painter [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]], according to the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin."[1]

West, a close friend of [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]], had been sent to Italy and then Britain, where he created the Academy in the heart of the Empire and remained its president until his death in 1830.[1] The essay argues that West's access through the Academy to the courts of Europe gave him entry to conversations within the inner court of George III, which undoubtedly provided invaluable intelligence for his colleagues in France and the young republic before, during and after the revolutionary war.[1] West also painted the famous image of Franklin plucking electricity from the heavens.[1]
