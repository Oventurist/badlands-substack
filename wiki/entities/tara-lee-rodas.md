# Tara Lee Rodas

Tara Lee Rodas is a U.S. Department of Health and Human Services employee who became a whistleblower on the federal government's handling of unaccompanied migrant children, testifying before Congress in April 2023 that the government was effectively delivering children into the hands of human traffickers.[1]

## Detail at the Pomona intake site

Rodas was detailed with [[department-of-health-and-human-services|HHS]] at an Emergency Intake Site in Pomona, California, where she observed the sponsor-placement process first-hand.[1] She told lawmakers, "I thought I was going to help place children in loving homes. Instead, I discovered that children are being trafficked through a sophisticated network that begins with recruiting in their home country, smuggled to the U.S. border, and ends when [Office of Refugee Resettlement] delivers a child to a sponsor — some sponsors are criminals and traffickers and members of Transnational Criminal Organizations."[1] She added that some sponsors view children as commodities and assets to be used for earning income, which she said explained an explosion of labor trafficking.[1]

## Congressional testimony

Her testimony came at a hearing titled "The Biden Border Crisis: Exploitation of Unaccompanied Alien Children," held by the House Judiciary Subcommittee on Immigration Integrity, Security and Enforcement, a panel of the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]].[1] Rodas called on [[congress|Congress]] to act to crack down on the U.S. government's role in the network.[1] Related coverage described her as testifying that the government acted as a middleman in a multi-billion-dollar migrant child trafficking operation.[1]

## Reception

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] summarized the hearing's tenor sardonically — that Congress conceded trafficking of children exists while simultaneously declining to verify it or dwell on it — but argued that forcing the issue into the American limelight makes it harder to suppress over the long haul.[1] The Gateway Pundit likewise folded Rodas's testimony into a broader indictment of the [[barack-obama|Obama]]-era and Biden-era federal apparatus.[1]
