# El Mencho

Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes, known as "El Mencho," was the alleged leader of the [[jalisco-new-generation-cartel|Jalisco New Generation Cartel]] (CJNG). According to the February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, he was killed by Mexican military forces during an operation in Jalisco state over the preceding weekend.

## The Jalisco operation

A reported 25 members of the Mexican national guard were killed in the operation. In its aftermath, violence erupted across parts of western [[mexico|Mexico]], including vehicle burnings and road blockades attributed to cartel retaliation, and security forces reportedly remained deployed in Jalisco and surrounding areas. The Mexican government had not released a full after-action report at the time of publication.

El Mencho had long been one of Mexico's most wanted drug traffickers, with authorities previously offering multimillion-dollar rewards for information leading to his capture.

## US involvement and Badlands interpretation

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to news of the operation by posting, "Mexico must step up their effort on Cartels and Drugs!" Separate reporting by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] said the Trump administration had quietly secured the transfer of roughly 100 cartel leaders — including El Mencho's brother — from Mexico into US custody before the raid, with no additional detail on the scope of US coordination.

[[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the sequence as potentially staged coordination, writing that "it could be kayfabe to keep the cartels guessing," while stating his own view that the campaign against the cartels is genuine.
