# Saudi Arabian Military Industries (SAMI)

**Saudi Arabian Military Industries (SAMI)** is a state-owned defense-industrial company of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], created to localize military manufacturing within the kingdom.[1]

## World Defense Show agreements, 2024

At the World Defense Show held in Riyadh in early February 2024, SAMI signed eleven agreements, according to the Saudi state news agency.[1] The event coincided with the announcement that [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] had signed sub-contracts allowing Saudi companies to manufacture components of the Terminal High Altitude Area Defense ([[thaad|THAAD]]) missile defense system, an arrangement Lockheed described as enhancing Saudi manufacturing capabilities and transferring expertise to strengthen the national defense industry.[1] Reporting indicated that [[qatar|Qatar]] would also produce parts for the system.[1]

## Significance

The localization of advanced missile-defense manufacturing in the Gulf was read by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a marker of the end of unipolar hegemony: if Middle Eastern states can build their own weapons systems, those systems cease to function as leverage held over them by outside powers.[1] The deals were signed against a backdrop of intensifying regional conflict involving American and British strikes on Houthi targets in Yemen and US strikes on Iran-backed groups in Iraq and Syria.[1]
