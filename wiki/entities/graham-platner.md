# Graham Platner

Graham Platner is a Democratic candidate for the United States Senate in Maine whose campaign was thrown into turmoil in July 2026 by public allegations of sexual assault made by a former romantic partner.[1] His campaign had been mounted with the stated aim of unseating Republican Senator [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]].[1]

## Assault allegations

Jenny Racicot publicly accused Platner of sexually assaulting her in late 2021, saying he arrived at her home uninvited and heavily intoxicated.[1] She described him ignoring her protests, grabbing her forcefully, and proceeding with sex against her will, and said he claimed the following morning not to remember the incident.[1] Racicot did not file a police report at the time, but said she later shared details with a therapist and others, and that she sent Platner a message weeks afterward stating the encounter had been non-consensual.[1]

## Response and political fallout

Platner released a video statement denying the allegations as "categorically false," framing them as a coordinated political hit timed just before Maine's candidate filing deadline.[1] He announced he was stepping back temporarily to reflect on the best path forward for his campaign, his supporters, and the goal of defeating Collins.[1]

Several prominent Democrats withdrew their endorsements in short order, including Senator [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]]; Khanna described the allegations as serious and credible and urged Platner to drop out of the race.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] expressed general skepticism toward sexual assault allegations that surface during political campaigns without contemporaneous police reports, arguing such claims carry built-in incentives to lie, while questioning why the allegations were treated as disqualifying by the Democratic Party at all.[1] The commentary also referenced a tattoo controversy attached to Platner, and concluded with the assertion that "elections are fake," rendering the campaign's collapse of limited consequence.[1]
