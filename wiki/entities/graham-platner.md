# Graham Platner

Graham Platner is a Democratic candidate for the United States Senate from [[maine|Maine]] whose 2026 campaign was thrown into turmoil by public sexual assault allegations from a former romantic partner.

## The allegations and response

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[jenny-racicot|Jenny Racicot]] accused Platner of sexually assaulting her in late 2021 after he showed up at her home uninvited while heavily intoxicated, ignoring her protests, grabbing her forcefully and proceeding with sex against her will, and that he said he did not remember the incident the next morning. Racicot did not file a police report at the time but later shared details with a therapist and others, and said she sent Platner a message weeks afterward stating the encounter was non-consensual.

In response, Platner released a video statement denying the allegations as "categorically false" and framing them as a coordinated political hit timed just before Maine's candidate filing deadline. He announced he was stepping back temporarily to reflect on the best path forward for his campaign, his supporters, and the goal of defeating Republican Senator [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]]. Several prominent Democrats, including Senator [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]], quickly withdrew their endorsements, with Khanna calling the allegations serious and credible and urging Platner to drop out of the race.
