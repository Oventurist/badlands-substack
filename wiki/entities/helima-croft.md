# Helima Croft

Helima Croft is head of commodity strategy at RBC Capital Markets and a frequently cited analyst of oil markets and Middle Eastern energy politics.[1]

## Commentary on the 2023 OPEC+ production cut

Following the surprise decision by OPEC+ to cut crude production by roughly one million barrels per day in April 2023, Croft argued that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] was staking out an economic strategy independent of the United States, pointing to the deterioration in relations between Riyadh and Washington under the [[joe-biden|Biden administration]].[1]

She characterised the move as "a Saudi-first policy," adding that the Kingdom was "making new friends, as we saw with China," a reference to the Beijing-brokered diplomatic normalisation between Saudi Arabia and [[iran|Iran]].[1] In her reading, the cut was a deliberate message to Washington that "it's no longer a unipolar world."[1]

Her analysis was quoted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated it as corroboration of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] thesis that the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system is being progressively hemmed in.[1]
