# Politburo Standing Committee

The Politburo Standing Committee is the apex decision-making body of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[1] The Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" treats its restructuring under [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] as one of the central data points in his consolidation of power.[1]

## Restructuring under Xi

According to the article, Xi created a Politburo Standing Committee in which formerly independent heads of state now report to him personally on economic issues.[1] This body is described as one of eight "small groups" that Xi chairs himself — a set that also includes the [[national-security-commission-china|National Security Commission]] — an arrangement the article says reverses decades of institutionalised rule and gives the appearance of autocratic government.[1]

The piece further reports that Xi has continued appointing military and strategy loyalists to the committee as he consolidates power and, more importantly in the author's reading, loyalty, ahead of making good on long-standing promises to join [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in ushering in a multipolar international order to replace Western hegemony.[1]

## Purged members

Former security chief [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]] is identified as a Politburo Standing Committee member removed early in Xi's rule over alleged coup attempts, alongside [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]], [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]] and the generals [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]], [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]].[1]
