# Rumble

**Rumble** is a video-sharing platform positioned as the premier free-speech alternative to YouTube, and described by Badlands Media as probably the censorship regime's primary target in the run-up to the 2024 election.[1]

## Pressure campaign

Rumble is being pressured by various governments, media outlets, and "watchdog" — in the author's framing, attack-dog — organizations.[1] The [[associated-press|Associated Press]] was described as already probing the team for weaknesses and establishing narratives.[1] Journalists demanded that Rumble deplatform [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]], one of its largest channels, over an alleged crime for which he had not been convicted, and have publicly maligned the company — including calling advertisers directly to sabotage existing deals and render Rumble toxic to prospective sponsors.[1]

## The France precedent

In 2022 the French government, upset that Rumble continued to allow Russian news sources such as Sputnik and RT on the platform, demanded the accounts be removed.[1] Rumble rejected the demand and instead switched off France's access to the site entirely.[1] Badlands cites this as evidence that the endgame of the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] and the Brand affair is the complete removal of Rumble from the United Kingdom.[1]

## Strategic significance

Rumble and [[x-twitter|X]] are described as the frontlines of the information war; Truth Social exists but is dismissed as an echo chamber.[1] CEO [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]] lacks even a fraction of the resources available to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] yet challenges the regime more intensely, leaving Rumble in a more vulnerable and possibly higher-priority position.[1] Badlands Media states that it is able to operate because of Rumble's hard stance against the censorship regime, and warns that if Rumble falls it will set an example deterring any other platform hoping to fill the void.[1]
