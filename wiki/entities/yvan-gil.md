# Yvan Gil

Yvan Gil is the Foreign Minister of [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## December 2025 call with Lavrov

On Monday, December 22, 2025, Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] held a phone conversation with Gil, during which Moscow reaffirmed its full support and solidarity with Venezuela as the country faced a US military blockade in the Caribbean.[1] According to a Russian Foreign Ministry statement, Lavrov expressed "serious concern over Washington's increasingly escalatory actions in the Caribbean Sea" and warned that the US military buildup could "lead to far-reaching consequences for the region and create a threat to international maritime navigation."[1]

The call came against the backdrop of a US naval deployment to the region begun in September, in which Washington attacked boats it claimed were involved in drug trafficking and blockaded oil tankers from entering or leaving Venezuela.[1] The United States alleged that narcotics traffickers operate out of Venezuela with government support; Caracas consistently denied the claims and insisted Washington was plotting regime change in order to gain access to the country's natural resources.[1]
