# MyPillow

**MyPillow**, formally My Pillow, Inc., is the American bedding manufacturer founded and led by chief executive [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]].[1] The company became a defendant in high-profile election defamation litigation because of Lindell's public claims about the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## Dominion litigation

[[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] sued Lindell and My Pillow, Inc. in February 2021 for $1.3 billion, accusing him of repeatedly and falsely claiming that the company had rigged the 2020 presidential election.[1] The litigation ran for years and was described in Badlands commentary as a crucible for the once-profitable bedding company.[1] Lindell countersued Dominion and added [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] as a third-party defendant, but those counterclaims were largely unsuccessful, and Smartmatic obtained sanctions against Lindell for frivolous filings that exceeded $92,000 in fines by early June 2026 after he failed to pay.[1]

The case ended in June 2026 when [[liberty-vote|Liberty Vote Holdings]], My Pillow, a Dominion subsidiary, and Lindell filed a joint stipulation in federal court in Washington, D.C., dismissing all claims with prejudice, with each party bearing its own attorneys' fees and costs.[1]

## Coomer defamation trial

MyPillow was also a named defendant, alongside [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] personally and [[frankspeech|FrankSpeech]], in the defamation trial brought by former Dominion executive [[eric-coomer|Eric Coomer]] in the U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado, which entered its second week in June 2025.[2] See [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]].[2]
