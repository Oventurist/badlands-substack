# Walgreens

Walgreens is a major American pharmacy chain and one of the principal retail outlets through which COVID-19 vaccines are administered in the United States.[1]

In late August 2025, Walgreens and rival chain [[cvs-health|CVS]] moved to restrict COVID-19 vaccinations after federal authorities tightened eligibility rules for the vaccine.[1] CVS said the vaccine was available in only 13 states plus Washington, D.C., to people with a prescription, citing "the current regulatory environment."[1] The restrictions followed a leadership shakeup at the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] in which Director [[susan-monarez|Susan Monarez]] was fired and several senior officials resigned, and coincided with reported COVID-19 case rates rising 11.2% for the week ending August 23.[1]
