# Barbara Comstock

Barbara Comstock is a former Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Virginia.[1]

## 2024 endorsement of Kamala Harris

Comstock was among the former Republican lawmakers who joined more than 100 former national security officials in signing the September 2024 letter endorsing Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and opposing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Other ex-GOP lawmakers signing alongside her included [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] of Illinois.[1] See [[2024-republican-national-security-endorsement-of-harris|2024 Republican National Security Endorsement of Kamala Harris]].
