# Doug Ward

Doug Ward is a United States defense official who serves as deputy director of the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]]'s Commander's Action Group and as Senior Nuclear Deterrence Advisor at [[united-states-strategic-command|United States Strategic Command]].[1]

## April 2024 case involving his wife

Ward, 53, became the subject of national coverage in April 2024 when it was revealed that his wife, [[erin-ward|Erin Ward]], an Omaha, Nebraska substitute teacher, had been charged with felony sexual abuse by a school employee after admitting to having sex with a 17-year-old student in the family vehicle.[1] The couple have three children together, including a daughter around the same age as the victim, and had posted numerous photographs together on social media.[1]

Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the case as significant chiefly because of Ward's position in the nuclear-deterrence apparatus, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noting that the story concerned "the wife of Biden's senior nuclear deterrence advisor" and arguing that its appearance in the press — rather than being buried to protect a senior Defense Department official — was itself encouraging evidence that protective mechanisms were weakening.[1]
