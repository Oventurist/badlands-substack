# Leslie Kean

Leslie Kean is an American investigative journalist known for her reporting on unidentified aerial phenomena.[1] She is a cousin of Republican Senator Tom Kean Jr. of New Jersey.[1]

## Reporting

In 2017 Kean co-authored a piece in [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] revealing that the Department of Defense had spent $22.5 million on a secret program to investigate UFOs.[1] In June 2023 she and veteran New York Times reporter [[ralph-blumenthal|Ralph Blumenthal]] published the account of whistleblower [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] in [[the-debrief|The Debrief]], reporting his claim that the U.S. government possesses retrieved craft of non-human origin.[1] Coverage aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] characterized the report as "stunning insight into the US Government's history with UFOs."[1]
