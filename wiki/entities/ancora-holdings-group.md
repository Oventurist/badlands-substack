# Ancora Holdings Group

**Ancora Holdings Group LLC** is an activist investment firm and a significant stakeholder in [[us-steel|U.S. Steel]].[1] In February 2025 the firm publicly demanded a leadership overhaul at the steelmaker following the collapse of its proposed $14.9 billion sale to Japan's [[nippon-steel|Nippon Steel Corp.]][1]

## February 2025 letter to the U.S. Steel board

In a letter dated February 10, 2025 and addressed to the U.S. Steel Board of Directors, Ancora executives Fredrick DiSanto and James Chadwick reiterated their position that the Nippon transaction was dead, citing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s recent public comments as confirmation that regulatory approval would not be forthcoming.[1] "President Trump's remarks on Friday should confirm — once and for all — that the sale has no chance of being resurrected," the Ancora leaders wrote, referring to a February 7 press conference Trump held after his briefing with Japanese Prime Minister [[shigeru-ishiba|Shigeru Ishiba]] in Washington, D.C.[1]

Ancora praised Trump's stance on protecting American manufacturing and accused U.S. Steel chief executive [[david-burritt|David Burritt]] of leading the company "off a cliff" in pursuit of a $72 million transaction-related payout tied to the sale.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Ancora letter as vindication of long-standing suspicions about the deal's motives, framing Burritt's payout as evidence that the company's leadership was "selling out America for a pay day."[1]
