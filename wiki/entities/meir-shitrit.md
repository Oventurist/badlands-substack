# Meir Shitrit

Meir Shitrit is identified in the March 13, 2026 Badlands Brief as one of four [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers arrested in connection with the alleged sexual assault of a Palestinian detainee at the [[sde-teiman|Sde Teiman]] detention facility.

According to commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Shitrit was the only one of the four willing to appear on camera without a mask, giving an interview on Israel Channel 14. The other three appeared on primetime Israeli television with their faces and identities concealed while defending their conduct. Israeli media, the commentary argues, turned the group into celebrities and folk heroes. Military prosecutors later dropped the charges, citing evidentiary problems.
