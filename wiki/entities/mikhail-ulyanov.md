# Mikhail Ulyanov

**Mikhail Ulyanov** is a senior Russian diplomat who heads Moscow's delegation to the international organizations headquartered in Vienna.[1]

## Confirmation of Assad's asylum

Early on the Monday following the December 2024 [[fall-of-the-assad-regime|fall of the Assad regime]], Ulyanov stated that [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] and his family were in Moscow, seemingly confirming earlier media reports that the former Syrian president had been granted asylum after the government in [[damascus|Damascus]] fell to militants.[1] Ulyanov presented the Assads' presence in Russia as a demonstration of principle, saying it showed that "Russia does not betray its friends in difficult situations… unlike the US."[1] Russian news agencies separately cited diplomatic sources reporting that the family had been granted asylum "on humanitarian grounds," while the [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]] said Assad had agreed to step down after back-channel talks and had instructed officials to conduct a peaceful transfer of power.[1]
