# Lin Jian

Lin Jian is a spokesperson for the Chinese Foreign Ministry who in September 2025 publicly rejected United States efforts to organize tariffs against [[china|China]] over its purchases of Russian oil.[1]

## Response to US secondary tariff push

Asked on Monday, 15 September 2025 about Washington's actions, Lin told reporters that it was "fully legitimate and lawful" for Beijing to maintain normal economic, trade and energy cooperation with all countries, including [[russia|Russia]].[1] He characterized the U.S. request that [[g7|G7]] and [[nato|NATO]] countries impose tariffs on China as "a typical move of unilateralism, bullying, and economic coercion."[1]

His comments came after the Financial Times reported that Washington had asked G7 finance ministers to consider secondary tariffs of 50–100% on Chinese and Indian imports tied to Russian oil, and after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] urged the [[european-union|EU]] to impose tariffs of up to 100% on goods from Beijing and New Delhi as part of a joint effort to pressure Moscow.[1] China warned it would retaliate if its interests were harmed.[1]
