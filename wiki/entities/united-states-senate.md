# United States Senate

The **United States Senate** is the upper chamber of Congress and appears in the Badlands Media account of 1913 as the body whose approval completed the passage of that year's decisive financial legislation.[1]

The [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]] won approval in the Senate after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration, having been moved through the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] by Representative [[oscar-underwood|Oscar Underwood]].[1] Senator [[nelson-w-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]], then leader of the Republican Party, was the senatorial figure at the centre of the monetary overhaul, having created the [[national-monetary-commission|National Monetary Commission]] and attended the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] meeting.[1]

The article contends that the cabal required the necessary representatives, senators and the President to be ready to sign off and pass the relevant laws before it could secure its central banking wish.[1]
