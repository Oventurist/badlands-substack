# Marko Djuric

Marko Djuric is a Serbian diplomat and politician who served as [[serbia|Serbia]]'s Acting Foreign Minister in 2025.[1]

## EU accession statements

In April 2025, Djuric met with [[european-union|European Union]] foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]] and declared that membership in the European Union remained Serbia's strategic choice.[1] According to a Serbian government press statement, Djuric stressed that his country was firmly committed to its European path and wished to contribute to peace, stability, and prosperity in Europe as a reliable partner.[1] He expressed hope that the European Union would acknowledge and positively assess Serbia's efforts in advancing its European integration process, and reiterated that Serbia would continue constructive efforts to ease tensions and contribute to the broader stability of the Balkans.[1]

At the time, Serbia was an official candidate for EU membership alongside Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Türkiye, and Ukraine.[1]
