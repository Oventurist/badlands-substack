# Arizona Association of County Recorders

The Arizona Association of County Recorders (AACR) is the professional association of Arizona's county recorders. It came under scrutiny in March 2026 through an obstruction-of-justice investigation into [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]], the former Maricopa County Recorder who served as the association's president in 2023.[1]

## 2023 public-records directive

During a state House committee hearing, Arizona lawmakers disclosed that while serving as AACR president, Richer allegedly advised fellow county recorders not to comply with a legislative public records request concerning the 2022 election.[1] The committee described the directive as intentional and is examining it as part of a broader inquiry into voter registrations that has produced criminal referrals to federal agencies including the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the Department of Homeland Security.[1]

## Significance

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] paired the AACR matter with the resignation of [[steve-schleiker|Steve Schleiker]] from the [[colorado-county-clerks-association|Colorado County Clerks Association]], noting that both stories involve the heads of professional associations that serve as subject-matter experts, escalation points, and PR wings for election officials while operating without meaningful oversight or lawful authority.[1]
