# Dollar General

Dollar General is a multibillion-dollar American discount retail chain whose leadership publicly described a deteriorating financial picture for its core shoppers in early 2025.[1]

## 2024 fourth-quarter results and consumer distress

According to the company's 2024 fourth-quarter earnings report, year-over-year customer traffic dropped by one percent, while operating profits plummeted by roughly 49 percent — a decline attributed in part to shuttered locations.[1] Chief executive [[todd-vasos|Todd Vasos]] said on the earnings call that "our customers continue to report that their financial situation has worsened over the last year, as they have been negatively impacted by ongoing inflation," and that many report having only enough money for basic essentials, with some sacrificing even on necessities.[1]

Vasos added that entering 2025 the company was "not anticipating improvement in the macroenvironment, particularly for our core customer," who therefore expected value and convenience more than ever, and noted that core customers were already "always strained" and could not absorb additional financial burdens.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] framed the results as an indictment of the preceding administration's economic record, remarking on the absurdity of a presidency whose lasting effect was to torpedo the economy badly enough to force Dollar General stores to close.[1]
