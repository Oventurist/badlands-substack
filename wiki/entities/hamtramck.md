# Hamtramck

Hamtramck is a city in Wayne County, Michigan, with a large Arab-American and Muslim population. It is governed by Mayor [[amer-ghalib|Amer Ghalib]], a Democrat who helped lead the "uncommitted" movement in the 2024 Democratic presidential primary.[1]

## Political significance in 2024

Hamtramck, along with Dearborn and other Wayne County cities, has sizable Muslim and Arab-American communities that turned out in force during the Democratic primary to protest the Biden–Harris administration's handling of the Israel–Hamas war.[1] Michigan was where the [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]] — composed of Arab-Americans, Muslims, young voters, and progressives — originated, with more than 100,000 uncommitted primary votes cast against the administration.[1]

Ghalib said support for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] among many residents of Hamtramck and neighboring communities was the basis for his communication with Republican leadership, which culminated in a meeting with Trump in Flint in September 2024.[1]
