# United States Navy

The United States Navy is the maritime service branch of the US armed forces. In the Badlands Media corpus it recurs chiefly as the instrument of American pressure campaigns in the Caribbean and in Middle Eastern chokepoints such as the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].

## Interdiction of fuel shipments to Cuba

The March 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, Alternative Annexations & New(ish) Regime Change, reports that the Navy has increased efforts to restrict fuel shipments to [[cuba|Cuba]] as a Russian oil tanker, the *Anatoly Kolodkin*, approached the Caribbean carrying crude oil to the island. The naval effort is presented as one component of a wider American pressure campaign against the Cuban government, which is facing a deepening economic crisis marked by widespread blackouts, shortages, and emigration — roughly 10 percent of the population has left since COVID-19. According to [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]], officials familiar with internal planning say the United States is considering legal and strategic measures to escalate pressure on Cuban leadership, including possible indictments and broader contingency plans, with anonymous officials stating that regime change in Cuba is "lined up." In that framing, naval interdiction of the island's energy lifeline functions as the physical enforcement arm of a strategy otherwise conducted through sanctions and legal instruments.


## April 2026: the M/V Touska interception

The Brief of April 20, 2026 reports that [[u-s-central-command|CENTCOM]] said the [[uss-spruance|USS Spruance]] enforced naval blockade measures against the Iranian-flagged cargo vessel M/V Touska in the north Arabian Sea on April 19, issuing warnings, firing into the vessel's engine room, and taking it into custody after U.S. Marines boarded — an action [[iran|Iran]] called "armed piracy."
