# Vatican

The **Vatican** is the governing body of the Roman Catholic Church, headquartered in Vatican City.[1]

## 2025 seminary guidelines on gay candidates

In January 2025 the Vatican gave the green light for gay men to become priests provided they remain celibate, described as an unexpected adjustment to practice within the Catholic Church.[1] New guidelines approved by Italian bishops allow gay men to enter seminaries, whereas a 2016 instruction had barred men with "deep-seated homosexual tendencies," though the Vatican had never explicitly prohibited gay men from the priesthood.[1]

The guidelines direct seminary directors to consider a candidate's sexual preferences as only one aspect of their personality, stating that discernment should not be reduced to that aspect alone but understood "within the whole framework of the young person's personality."[1] It was not clear whether the new guidelines would be reflected in other countries.[1]


## Interregnum After the Death of Pope Francis (2025)

The Vatican announced on Monday, April 21, 2025 that [[pope-francis|Pope Francis]] had died at 88 after a stroke, a day after his Easter appearance at St. Peter's Square.[2] His death began the leadership transition known as the interregnum, during which there is no pope in power — a period referred to as *sede vacante*, the "vacant seat."[2] Traditional Vatican practice calls for nine days of mourning, the *novendiales*, after which the election of a successor begins between 15 and 20 days from the death, organized by the camerlengo in the process known as the [[papal-conclave|conclave]].[2]

## Proposed venue for Russia–Ukraine talks

Following the May 19, 2025 telephone call between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], Badlands commentary noted that the Vatican, as represented by the Pope, had stated it would be very interested in hosting negotiations between Moscow and Kiev.[3] Contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry wrote that Trump had chosen the Vatican as the stage for his meeting with Putin.[3]
