# Brad Edwards

Brad Edwards is an American attorney known for representing women who say they were sexually abused by the financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] In late March 2023 he was identified as a lawyer involved in litigation against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]], the largest bank in the United States, over the institution's long relationship with Epstein as a client.[1]

## Role in the JPMorgan litigation

Edwards was the source cited by Reuters for the report that JPMorgan Chase chief executive [[jamie-dimon|Jamie Dimon]] would be deposed under oath in connection with the bank's dealings with Epstein, with the interview expected to take place in early May 2023.[1] The plaintiffs he represents allege that the bank enabled Epstein's sex trafficking by continuing to provide him with financial services.[1]

## Commentary in the Truth Community

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief that covered the deposition report framed the JPMorgan and [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]] lawsuits as significant less for their legal particulars than for the imagery of major banks appearing in headlines next to the phrase "Epstein victims" during a period of financial stress.[1] Commentary attributed to [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the Epstein case as "the biggest and most effective redpill of all time."[1]
