# Tom Homan

Tom Homan is the border czar of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, responsible for directing field operations to remove illegal entrants and criminal aliens, and a former acting director of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]].

## Contrast with Kristi Noem

In the March 4, 2026 Badlands Brief, the commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] presents Homan as the counterpart to Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] during the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026. While Noem absorbed bipartisan criticism over [[department-of-homeland-security|DHS]] contracting and [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]], Homan was "in the field, doing the work," and numerous senators invoked his example favorably in rebuking the Secretary. The Brief observes that Homan enjoys "bipartisan" cover while Noem receives "bipartisan" evisceration, both officials reporting directly to Trump, and that immigration enforcement continues unimpeded throughout — a pattern the commentary suggests may be deliberate.

## Conclusion of Operation Metro Surge (February 2026)

As Border Czar, Homan announced in February 2026 the end of [[operation-metro-surge]], the federal immigration crackdown in [[minnesota]]. In a statement posted to X, he said that after time on the ground assessing progress and conducting federal, state, local, and community stakeholder outreach, he had proposed concluding the surge and that "the President concurred."

He listed four claimed outcomes: [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]]'s target list had been significantly reduced with many public-safety threats removed; counties had reached an "unprecedented level of coordination" in notifying ICE before releasing criminal aliens from jail so custody could be assumed in a jail setting; unlawful agitator activity had notably decreased; and state and local law enforcement had stepped up against unlawful actions by agitators. Homan added that "the far left and extreme, organized agitator groups can try to spin this however they want, but these are the facts."

Officers and agents deployed to Minnesota, he said, would continue enforcement in other locations to deliver on mass-deportation goals. He closed by thanking [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] and [[customs-and-border-protection|CBP]] personnel and crediting [[donald-trump]], repeating his standing claim that no president has delivered more on border security.
