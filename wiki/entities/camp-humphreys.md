# Camp Humphreys

Camp Humphreys is the largest United States military facility in South Korea.[1] It was completed in 2018 after a decade-long relocation project funded with billions of dollars from both the American and South Korean governments.[1]

## Role in the 2025 land-ownership dispute

In August 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], seated alongside South Korea's new president [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]], said he would like the United States to obtain outright ownership of the land beneath its military bases in South Korea rather than continue leasing it.[1] Trump noted that Washington had invested heavily in infrastructure on the Korean Peninsula and maintained "over 40,000 troops" there while the underlying land remained leased from Seoul.[1] "Maybe one of the things I'd like to do is ask them to give us ownership of the land where we have the big fort," he said, adding that he would "like to see if we could get rid of the lease and get ownership of the land where we have a massive military base."[1] Trump did not specify which "fort" he meant, though Camp Humphreys is the largest candidate.[1]

The United States operates its overseas bases under long-term lease agreements and Status of Forces treaties, which grant Washington operational control while preserving de jure sovereignty for the host country.[1] Badlands commentary speculated that outright ownership might be intended to let the United States withdraw at will, since breaking a lease would likely carry severe penalties.[1]
