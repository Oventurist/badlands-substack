# Scott Bennett

Scott Bennett is a former U.S. Army psychological warfare officer and counter-terrorism analyst who comments publicly on media and intelligence matters.[1]

## Commentary on the Carlson firing

In April 2023 Bennett argued that [[fox-news|Fox News]] removed [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] as part of a broader regime agenda to maintain an "uninformed semi lobotomized quasi retarded population."[1] He said Carlson posed too great a threat to institutional power because he turned Americans into proper "researchers and thinkers," and credited the host with an "intellectualism, truthfulness, and an analytical depth that no other news personality has ever done in the history of the United States as far back as I can remember."[1] In Bennett's framing, Carlson needed to be "silenced" because he threatened the "powers and principalities, institutions and agendas that seek an unenlightened uninformed semi lobotomized quasi retarded population that do not question, do not research, do not analyze but simply digest and follow instructions."[1]

The remarks were carried by Summit News and aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], whose contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the item to argue that the vocabulary of psychological operations is migrating from Anon and alternative media into the general news cycle.[1]
