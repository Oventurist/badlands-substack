# Carlo Maria Viganò

Carlo Maria Viganò is an Italian archbishop and the former papal nuncio to the United States who became one of the most prominent traditionalist critics of the contemporary Catholic hierarchy.[1] He publicly questioned the legitimacy of Pope Francis and the authority of the Second Vatican Council, and was excommunicated by the Vatican for "his refusal to recognize and submit to the Supreme Pontiff" — a decision reported roughly three weeks before his July 2024 statement on the Paris Olympics.[1]

## Rebuke of the 2024 Olympics opening ceremony

Viganò issued a scathing denunciation of the [[2024-paris-summer-olympics|Paris Olympics]] opening ceremony, which had drawn condemnation from French bishops for a segment in which transgender performers and a child were widely understood to have reenacted Leonardo da Vinci's "The Last Supper," alongside other sexualized scenes.[1] The display prompted worldwide outrage and the withdrawal of several corporate sponsors.[1]

Viganò called the ceremony "the latest in a long series of vile attacks on God, the Catholic Religion and natural Morality by the antichristic elite" that he said holds Western countries hostage.[1] His letter described "a dystopian dance macabre," holograms of the horsemen of the Apocalypse, "a plump blue Dionysius," and "the parody of the LGBTQ+ Last Supper."[1] He linked the ceremony to what he characterized as a pattern of occult symbolism in mass public spectacles, citing the 2012 London Olympics, the 2016 inauguration of the Gotthard Tunnel, and the 2022 Commonwealth Games, "featuring infernal figures, goats, and terrifying animals."[1]

In his theological framing, Viganò argued that the organizing elite demand "not only the right to blasphemy and the obscene display of the foulest vices, but even their mute acceptance by Catholics and decent people."[1] He wrote that "Satan makes nothing: only he ruins everything. He does not invent: he tampers," and accused the ceremony's authors of humiliating "woman's femininity in order to erase the motherhood that recalls the Virgin Mother" and castrating "man's manhood in order to tear from the image of God's fatherhood."[1]

## Claims about Macron and Obama

Viganò said it was "no coincidence" that the ceremonies were presided over by French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]], whom he described as an emissary of the World Economic Forum who "passes off a transvestite as his own wife with impunity, just as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] is accompanied by a muscular man in a wig."[1] The remarks, reported by ZeroHedge and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, restated long-circulating unverified claims about [[brigitte-macron|Brigitte Macron]] and [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]].[1]
