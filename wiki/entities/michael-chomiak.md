# Michael Chomiak

Michael Chomiak was a Ukrainian nationalist and, according to research cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] and the analyst [[john-helmer|John Helmer]], a Nazi propagandist and spy from 1939 onward.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He is the grandfather of [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]], who in Ehret's account was "bred from a zealous family of Ukrainian nationalists under the patriarchy of her Nazi grandfather Michael Chomiak."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Helmer's reporting holds that Chomiak volunteered for Hitler before Ukraine was invaded and was hunted by the Polish police until the 1980s.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret writes that the network to which Chomiak belonged was brought to Canada after the Second World War by Anglo-American intelligence and cultivated ever since as a force with ties to pro-Nazi Ukrainian counterparts.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
