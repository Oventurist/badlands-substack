# Brian Fitzpatrick

Brian Fitzpatrick is a Republican U.S. Representative from Pennsylvania, generally counted among the House GOP moderates representing competitive districts.[1]

## Obamacare subsidies and the Johnson health plan

In December 2025 Fitzpatrick was among a band of vulnerable House Republicans who had long warned about the expiration of key Obamacare subsidies. Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] moved toward a Wednesday vote on a health care bill that he and other Republican leaders presented as an alternative to the tax credits set to expire at the end of the month, with no plans to allow a vote before then on extending the subsidies.[1]

Early signs indicated the moderates would fall in line. "I haven't seen anything objectionable yet," Fitzpatrick said on Monday, December 15. "For me to vote against it, I'd have to find something objectionable. I wouldn't vote against it in protest." He said it would be "a huge mistake" not to include an extension, but that he votes "for or against legislation based on the merits of the bill."[1]

## Opposition to an imposed Ukraine peace deal (2025)

In February 2025, Fitzpatrick, a moderate Pennsylvania Republican, said Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] should not be forced to accept any peace agreement with Russia that falls short of his country's demands.[2] Writing on X, Fitzpatrick said Zelensky "need not, and must not, have any deal forced upon him by any outside nation that does not guarantee the security and the sovereignty of the Ukrainian people."[2] He added that "we all want an immediate end to this brutal Russian invasion and killing," but that for a peace agreement to be fair and lasting it "must be done in a way that holds the Russian invaders accountable, protects the dignity of the Ukrainian victims, and provides for fair and equitable cost-sharing amongst all European nations."[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the pushback as part of a pattern in which the system calls for war, secrecy and scarcity whenever [[donald-trump|Trump]] pushes for peace, transparency and abundance, glossing the establishment position as "No PEACE without representation."[2] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] noted that Zelensky was upset that the United States and Russia were negotiating without him and had said he would not sign anything without a seat at the table, and reported that Zelensky had apparently shut down Truth Social in Ukraine.[2]
