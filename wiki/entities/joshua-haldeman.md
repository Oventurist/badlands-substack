# Joshua Haldeman

Joshua Haldeman was a leader of [[technocracy-inc|Technocracy Inc.]] and the grandfather of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

## Significance

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites Haldeman's leadership of Technocracy Inc. when warning of a possible dystopian revival of technocratic governance inside the MAGA movement, and specifically of a perversion of [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s [[agenda-47|Agenda 47]] along technocratic lines.[1] The lineage from Haldeman to Musk is used to argue that the transhumanist Silicon Valley current now influential in American politics has deep ideological roots rather than being a novel phenomenon.[1]
