# Joshua Haldeman

Joshua Haldeman was a leader of [[technocracy-inc|Technocracy Inc.]] and the grandfather of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

## Significance

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites Haldeman's leadership of Technocracy Inc. when warning of a possible dystopian revival of technocratic governance inside the MAGA movement, and specifically of a perversion of [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s [[agenda-47|Agenda 47]] along technocratic lines.[1] The lineage from Haldeman to Musk is used to argue that the transhumanist Silicon Valley current now influential in American politics has deep ideological roots rather than being a novel phenomenon.[1]

## Early life and the Canadian Fabian movement

Haldeman was an aviation enthusiast, chiropractor and devout social reformer who found himself leading the Saskatchewan branch of the newly created Canadian Fabian Society in 1933 as a response to the Great Depression.[2] His daughter [[maye-musk|Maye Musk]] — the mother of Elon Musk — was born into the family's wealth in Regina, Saskatchewan in 1947, one of twin sisters.[2] When the [[league-for-social-reconstruction|League for Social Reconstruction]], the formal name of the Fabian Society of Canada, established the [[cooperative-commonwealth-federation|Cooperative Commonwealth Federation]] in 1933, Haldeman was assigned to build the Saskatchewan branch in the Assiniboia federal constituency, where he would have interfaced with some or all of the five Oxford Rhodes Scholars who founded the organization.[2] The party published the [[regina-manifesto|Regina Manifesto]] in his hometown of Regina that same year while he ran the Saskatchewan CCF.[2]

## Technocracy Inc.

Disappointed that Canadians were not embracing "scientifically managed governance" under the CCF, Haldeman shifted gears in 1936 and joined [[howard-scott|Howard Scott]]'s Technocracy Inc., becoming its first research director in Saskatchewan.[2] He adopted the movement's uniform of grey suits and navy blue ties and even painted his car "Technocracy Gray," a hue created for members by General Motors; by this time the organization claimed over a million members, with more than 500,000 registered in California alone.[2] Like all technocrats he was known by a number rather than a name: his was "10450-1."[2] Badlands contributor Matthew Ehret speculates that Elon Musk's choice of names for his children with Claire Boucher — "X Æ A-Xii," Exa Dark Sideræl and Techno Mechanicus — may be an homage to his grandfather's movement.[2]

In 1941 Technocracy Inc. was outlawed in Canada as a potential pro-fascist threat and Haldeman was arrested.[2] He denied the charges, though the organization did advocate world government by an elite class, the overthrow of nation states, and opposition to Canada's entry into the Second World War; Prime Minister [[william-lyon-mackenzie-king|William Lyon Mackenzie King]] said its goal was "to overthrow the government and the constitution of this country by force."[2] When the American headquarters began supporting the Soviet Union's war against the Nazis, Haldeman resigned in protest in 1943.[2]

## Social Credit

That same year Haldeman joined the [[social-credit-party-of-canada|Social Credit Party]] founded on the theories of [[c-h-douglas|Major C. H. Douglas]], becoming Provincial Co-ordinator of the Saskatchewan Social Credit League in 1943 and, by May 1945, the party's Vice-President and Provincial Secretary.[2] He ran for federal parliament in 1948 but failed to win a seat.[2] Ehret notes that the Social Credit Party of Joshua Haldeman was the first and only Canadian political party to publish and widely distribute the [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion]], and that in Quebec its leadership published the text in French and collaborated for a time with the Nazi leader [[adrien-arcand|Adrien Arcand]].[2]

## South Africa

When it became clear the Saskatchewan party would not attain power, Haldeman moved his family in 1949 to [[apartheid|apartheid]] South Africa, which he embraced as the most freedom-loving country on earth.[2] In Pretoria he worked as a chiropractor, flew his private plane across Africa searching for the lost city of the Kalahari, and wrote in promotion of apartheid and Jewish conspiracy theories, declaring that "South Africa will become the leader of white civilization in the world."[2] Asked about the anti-black legislation of Hendrik Verwoerd's Nationalist Party, he replied that "the natives are very primitive and must not be taken seriously... Some are quite clever in a routine job, but the best of them cannot assume responsibility and will abuse authority. The present government of South Africa knows how to handle the native question."[2] Ehret observes that neither Haldeman nor Maye Musk, [[errol-musk|Errol Musk]] or Elon Musk ever offered a critical word about the apartheid regime.[2]
