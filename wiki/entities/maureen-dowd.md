# Maureen Dowd

Maureen Dowd is a columnist for the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] whose August 2024 commentary on the replacement of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] atop the Democratic ticket was cited approvingly in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## "A coup is still a coup"

As the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] opened in Chicago, Dowd slammed Democrats for executing what she called a coup to remove Biden from the 2024 ticket, writing that "a coup is still a coup."[1] Her column depicted what was supposed to be a triumphant gathering as a hotbed of tension and betrayal inside the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]], with the party's power players having orchestrated a behind-the-scenes effort to push Biden out of the race and hand the reins to Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] The political backstabbing was reportedly orchestrated by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] and [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]], all of whom nudged Biden out the door and left the president feeling betrayed by his allies.[1]

Badlands cited Dowd's column alongside a [[washington-post|Washington Post]] editorial criticizing Harris's economic program as "populist gimmicks," treating the pair as evidence that establishment outlets were breaking ranks on the eve of the convention.[1]
