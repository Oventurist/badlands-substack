# Janet Mills

Janet Mills is the Democratic governor of Maine, who in February 2025 became the target of a formal federal warning from the U.S. Department of Justice over her state's policy of allowing male athletes to compete in girls' sports.[1]

## Title IX dispute with the Justice Department

In a letter dated February 25, 2025, U.S. Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] warned Maine officials that the Justice Department was prepared to take legal action if the state continued to require girls to compete against boys in athletics, a policy the department said violates federal antidiscrimination law.[1] Bondi grounded the warning in [[title-ix|Title IX]] of the Education Amendments Act of 1972, which prohibits sex-based discrimination in federally funded education programs, including athletics, and said the department would hold states accountable if they defied federal law.[1] Reporting on the letter carried the framing that "Maine should be on notice."[1]

Mills had confirmed the previous week that she supported the resistance of the Maine Department of Education and the Maine Principals' Association to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive orders on the subject, in what was described as a high-profile public spat with the commander in chief.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul dismissed Mills as "the illegitimate governor of Maine" and proposed the nickname "Manet Mills," instructing readers to "update your records."[1]
