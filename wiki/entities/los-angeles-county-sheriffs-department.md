# Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department

The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department is the California law enforcement agency with which former model [[lonna-drewes|Lonna Drewes]] filed a rape claim against Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] in April 2026.[1] Drewes alleged that in July 2018 Swalwell drugged her with a spiked glass of wine in a room at the Montrose Hotel in West Hollywood, then raped and choked her unconscious.[1] The Sheriff's Department confirmed the location of the alleged incident, a detail that aligned with FEC records showing "Swalwell for Congress" spent $361 at that hotel on July 18, 2018.[1] The filing formed part of the wider [[eric-swalwell-sexual-misconduct-scandal|Swalwell sexual misconduct scandal]] that preceded his resignation from [[congress|Congress]].[1]
