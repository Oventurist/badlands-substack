# William Hartman

Maj. Gen. **William Hartman** is a United States Army officer who served as chief of the Cyber National Mission Force (CNMF), the element of [[us-cyber-command|Cyber Command]] tasked with defending the nation against significant cyber threats.[1]

In April 2023, at the RSA Conference, Hartman joined [[eric-goldstein|Eric Goldstein]] of the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] on a panel that publicly detailed two previously unknown incidents in which the partnership between the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]-based Cyber Command and CISA had stymied the impact of state-linked attacks.[1] [[axios|Axios]] characterised the disclosures as unusual clarity about which office is responsible for what during an active cyber incident.[1]
