# Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi

Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al-Saadi is an Iraqi national, aged 32 at the time of his arrest, accused by US federal prosecutors of plotting to assassinate [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka Trump]] in retaliation for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2020 order of the drone strike that killed Iranian military commander [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] in Baghdad.[1]

## Alleged plot

According to reports, Al-Saadi pledged to kill Ivanka Trump and allegedly obtained a blueprint of her Florida home; sources told the New York Post the plot was intended to "burn down the house of Trump" in revenge for Soleimani's death.[1] He reportedly posted a map of the area surrounding the Florida residence she shares with [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], alongside a threat claiming that neither "palaces nor the Secret Service" would protect Americans and that operatives were in a "stage of surveillance and analysis."[1]

## Alleged network and charges

Federal prosecutors allege Al-Saadi was involved in at least 18 attacks and attempted attacks targeting US and Jewish sites across Europe and North America.[1] Sources identified him as a senior operative connected to both the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] and Kata'ib Hizballah; analysts and former Iraqi officials described him as closely tied to Soleimani and later to IRGC Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani.[1] Investigators further allege he trained with the IRGC in Tehran before using a travel agency business to coordinate with terror cells internationally.[1]

Al-Saadi was arrested in Turkey on May 15, 2026 and extradited to the United States, where federal charges accuse him of coordinating attacks tied to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.[1]
