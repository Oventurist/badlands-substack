# Marc Siegel

Dr. Marc Siegel is a physician who serves as senior medical analyst for [[fox-news|Fox News]].

## Analysis of Biden's cancer (2025)

Speaking on *Fox Report* in May 2025, Siegel assessed [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s [[biden-prostate-cancer-diagnosis|prostate cancer diagnosis]], which was characterized by a Gleason score of 9 and Grade Group 5 with bone metastasis, as indicating disease that is "pretty far advanced."[1] "It's the most aggressive type," he said. "That means it has the highest risk of spread — which obviously he has had."[1] He added that Biden's urinary symptoms were another sign of advanced disease, noting that prostate cancer is often asymptomatic in its early stages.[1] The form of cancer described carries a five-year survival rate of between 30% and 40%.[1]
