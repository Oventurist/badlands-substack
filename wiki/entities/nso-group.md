# NSO Group

NSO Group is the Israeli firm that produces the [[pegasus|Pegasus]] surveillance software.[1]

In April 2026, reporting cited by Badlands stated that the [[cia|CIA]] had used NSO Group's Pegasus software to run a deception operation against [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] officials as part of the rescue of a U.S. pilot reported to have been shot down in [[iran|Iran]].[1] The report, attributed to the Times of Israel, was highlighted by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as evidence that the U.S. military was openly deploying deception psychological operations within the [[iran-war|Iran War]] narrative.[1]

## Israeli intervention in the WhatsApp lawsuit

Leaked files reported in July 2024 suggested that the Israeli government took extraordinary measures to frustrate the U.S. lawsuit brought against NSO Group by [[whatsapp|WhatsApp]], seizing documents about [[pegasus|Pegasus]] from the company so that it could not comply with disclosure demands in a U.S. court.[2] The seizures were described as part of an unusual legal manoeuvre created by [[israel|Israel]] to block disclosure of information the government believed would cause "serious diplomatic and security damage" to the country.[2] NSO has been battling the WhatsApp suit since late 2019 over allegations that it exploited a vulnerability in the messaging service to target more than 1,400 users in 20 countries over a two-week period; NSO denies the allegations.[2] One of the company's lawyers in the case was former U.S. Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]], who according to the leak asked one of Israel's U.S. lawyers whether the Israeli government would "come to the rescue."[2]

Badlands commentary noted that the firm was founded in 2010 by a group that included Niv Carmi, a former [[mossad|Mossad]] intelligence operative, and that Pegasus itself only launched in 2016.[2]
