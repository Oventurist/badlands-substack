# NSO Group

NSO Group is the Israeli firm that produces the [[pegasus|Pegasus]] surveillance software.[1]

In April 2026, reporting cited by Badlands stated that the [[cia|CIA]] had used NSO Group's Pegasus software to run a deception operation against [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] officials as part of the rescue of a U.S. pilot reported to have been shot down in [[iran|Iran]].[1] The report, attributed to the Times of Israel, was highlighted by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as evidence that the U.S. military was openly deploying deception psychological operations within the [[iran-war|Iran War]] narrative.[1]
