# Le Duc Tho

Le Duc Tho was the North Vietnamese diplomat who served as counterpart to U.S. Secretary of State [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] in the negotiations that produced the accord ending American involvement in the Vietnam War.[1]

In 1973, Kissinger shared the Nobel Peace Prize with Le Duc Tho for hammering out that agreement, which was signed on January 27, 1973.[1] The Nobel committee said the accord had "brought a wave of joy and hope for peace over the entire world."[1] Coverage of Kissinger's death at age 100 in November 2023 noted that the prize was awarded prematurely, for ending a war that in fact kept going.[1]
