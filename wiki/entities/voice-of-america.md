# Voice of America

Voice of America (VOA) is the state-funded international news broadcaster of the United States government, overseen by the [[us-agency-for-global-media|U.S. Agency for Global Media]].[1]

## History

VOA was launched in 1942 to counter Nazi propaganda during the Second World War, and subsequently served as a principal vehicle for pro-American messaging throughout the Cold War.[1]

## Trump administration campaign to dismantle VOA

In June 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly called on Republicans to support his campaign to dismantle the broadcaster, arguing that in recent decades VOA has drifted toward partisanship and that shutting it down fits within his broader effort to eliminate wasteful government spending.[1] On the same day, senior Trump adviser [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] told the House Foreign Affairs Committee that USAGM, VOA's parent agency, is "rotten to the core" and should be reshaped to align with the administration's 'America First' agenda.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributors welcomed the move but framed it as insufficient on its own, arguing that nominally private corporate outlets also function as state-aligned media, and calling for an end to what they describe as illegal psychological warfare operations conducted by the intelligence community against American citizens.[1] The same commentary referenced [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s accusation that Fox News is controlled by Mossad and his call for a FARA investigation into the network.[1]
