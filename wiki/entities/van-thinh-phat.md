# Van Thinh Phat

Van Thinh Phat is a Vietnamese real estate company at the center of the country's largest financial fraud case.[1] Its chair, [[truong-my-lan|Truong My Lan]], was sentenced to death by a Ho Chi Minh City court in April 2024 over fraud amounting to $12.5 billion, close to 3 percent of Vietnam's 2022 GDP.[1]

Lan was found to have illegally controlled [[saigon-joint-stock-commercial-bank|Saigon Joint Stock Commercial Bank]] from 2012 to 2022, approving 2,500 loans that cost the bank $27 billion.[1] The company's chief executive, [[truong-hue-van|Truong Hue Van]] — Lan's niece — received a 17-year prison sentence for assisting her.[1] The court described the operation as an orchestrated and sophisticated criminal enterprise.[1]
