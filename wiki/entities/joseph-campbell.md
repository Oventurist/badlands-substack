# Joseph Campbell

**Joseph Campbell** is the mythologist invoked in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for his model of the [[heros-journey|Hero's Journey]].[1]

The essay identifies the [[liminality|liminal]] state with the *Belly of the Whale* stage of Campbell's monomyth, placing it alongside Inanna's descent into the underworld, the Buddha's night beneath the Bodhi tree with Mara whispering temptations, Isis reassembling the body of Osiris, and Odin hanging upside down on the World Tree.[1]

Campbell's schema serves the article's argument that the reader's disorientation is ancient rather than novel: "These stories remind us: transformation demands disorientation. The pause before clarity is not failure. It is passage."[1]

## Cited in White Squall commentary

Campbell is also the authority relied on in the April 25, 2023 essay "The Significance of White Squall" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], which describes [[the-hero-with-a-thousand-faces|The Hero with a Thousand Faces]] as the book many consider the primary text on the Hero's Journey and quotes its formula that a hero ventures forth from the world of common day into a region of supernatural wonder, encounters fabulous forces, wins a decisive victory, and returns with the power to bestow boons on his fellow man.[2] The essay uses that schema to read the film [[white-squall|White Squall]] as a collective hero's journey applying to [[donald-trump|Trump]], [[qanon|Q]] and the [[anons|anons]].[2]
