# Alvin H. Rosenfeld

Alvin H. Rosenfeld is an English professor at Indiana University and the author of the 2007 [[american-jewish-committee|American Jewish Committee]] paper [[progressive-jewish-thought-and-the-new-antisemitism|*Progressive Jewish Thought and the New Anti-Semitism*]].[1]

## The 2007 paper

In the paper, Rosenfeld characterized a cohort of progressive American Jews who had expressed dissent toward Israel — including the historian Tony Judt, the playwright Tony Kushner, and the Washington Post columnist Richard Cohen — as standing "alongside" a new antisemitism that denies the legitimacy of Israel's existence.[1] The Badlands Media essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'" treats the publication as the moment at which the inclination to label critics of Israel and dissenting Jews as antisemitic "peaked (or reached a new nadir)."[1]

The individuals Rosenfeld named vehemently refuted his allegations, and Rabbi [[michael-lerner|Michael Lerner]] of Tikkun emphasized the political repercussions of making unfounded accusations of that kind.[1]
