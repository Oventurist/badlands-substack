# Common Cause

Common Cause is an American advocacy organization active in election-related litigation and testimony, described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a "leftist lawfare outfit."[1]

## Role in the Griswold impeachment hearing

During the April 2024 [[colorado|Colorado]] House committee hearing on the attempted impeachment of Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]], two of Griswold's four witnesses came from Common Cause.[1] One of those two had served as an attorney for the petitioners in the ballot-disqualification case that the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] decided 9-0 in favor of keeping [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on the ballot.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who attended the hearing in person, noted that Griswold's witnesses "patted themselves on the back for being nonpartisan" despite that direct involvement in the underlying litigation, and that no one testified under oath during the proceeding.[1] The episode is presented as an illustration of how nominally neutral civil-society organizations function as participants in [[lawfare|lawfare]] campaigns.[1]
