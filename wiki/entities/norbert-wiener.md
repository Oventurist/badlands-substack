# Norbert Wiener

Norbert Wiener, the founder of cybernetics, is named in Badlands Media reporting as one of the theorists whose work shaped the modern [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] worldview.[1]

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the "new wisdom" that emerged in the wake of the cybernetics revolution of the 1960s asserted that technology grows with a life all its own, acting as a synthetic and deterministic *élan vital* without regard for human thought or free will.[1] Wiener is grouped with Marshall McLuhan, [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]], [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]] and [[ray-kurzweil|Ray Kurzweil]] as the intellectual ancestors of the priests of the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]].[1]

Ehret argues that the cybernetics cult replaced the older assumption that a moral direction shapes upward evolution with the claim that notions of "better" or "worse" have no meaning in a mechanistic and purposeless universe — a premise he traces through to [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]]'s declaration that soul, spirit and free will are "over".[1]
