# Jonathan Li

Jonathan Li is a Chinese financier who ran BHR, a Chinese private equity fund on whose board of directors [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] was listed.[1]

## Relationship with the Biden family

According to the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], evidence shows that between 2009 and 2017, during his tenure as Vice President and prior to later payments to Hunter Biden, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] developed a familiar relationship with Li.[1] Biden business associate [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] described how Joe Biden met Li for coffee in Beijing, had a phone call with him, and wrote college recommendation letters for his children.[1] Hunter Biden also arranged a meeting between Li and Joe Biden while the latter was Vice President.[1]

## 2019 wire transfer

On August 2, 2019 — months after Joe Biden announced his 2020 presidential candidacy on April 25, 2019 — Li wired $250,000 with Joe Biden's Delaware home listed on the wire.[1] That home was the same Wilmington property where classified documents were later found in the garage, and where Hunter Biden was living while receiving income from CCP-linked business dealings.[1] A separate $10,000 wire from Wang Xin, also listing Joe Biden's home address, had been sent on July 26, 2019.[1]

Hunter Biden's lawyer George Mesires stated on October 13, 2019 that Hunter served with BHR "only as a member of its board of directors," which was purportedly an "unpaid position."[1] During a presidential debate on October 22, 2020, Joe Biden said, "My son has not made money […] in China."[1]
