# Han Dong-hoon

**Han Dong-hoon** is the leader of South Korea's [[people-power-party|People Power Party]] (PPP), the governing party of President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]], who moved in December 2024 to sideline the president following his short-lived declaration of martial law.[1]

## Response to the martial law crisis

On Sunday, December 8, 2024, Han said that the president would be excluded from foreign and other state affairs, and that Prime Minister [[han-duck-soo|Han Duck-soo]] would manage the government.[1] The statement came as prosecutors arrested former defense minister [[kim-yong-hyun|Kim Yong-hyun]] over his alleged role in the martial law decision and as the [[south-korea|South Korean]] Justice Ministry prepared a travel ban against Yoon.[1]

Badlands commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted Han's announcement as evidence that the "traitor-president's own party" had turned against him, describing the wider Korean response — arrests of the military leaders who went along with what the writer called a coup — as an "incredibly based development."[1]
