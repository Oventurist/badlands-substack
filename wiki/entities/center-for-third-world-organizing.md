# Center for Third World Organizing

The Center for Third World Organizing is an American activist organization identified in a September 2025 [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] report as a recipient of [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] funding.[1]

## Allegations

The report paired the Center for Third World Organizing with its "militant partner" the [[ruckus-society|Ruckus Society]], stating that the two trained activists in property destruction and sabotage during the 2020 riots.[1] Both were cited as examples of U.S.-based organizations engaging in "direct actions" that the FBI defines as domestic terrorism, within a funding network the report valued at more than $80 million since 2016.[1]
