# Yitzhak Rabin

Yitzhak Rabin was the Israeli Prime Minister and retired [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] general who signed the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]] in 1993.[1]

## Oslo and assassination

Under the agreement Rabin signed, Palestine would immediately recognize the State of [[israel|Israel]], and in exchange the IDF would implement a five-year plan to incrementally withdraw from Gaza and the West Bank, culminating in recognition of Palestine as a nation.[1]

Rabin was brutally assassinated by a Jewish terrorist, an act that Badlands commentary identifies as the pivotal event enabling the rise of a new celebrity politician, [[benjamin-netanyahu|Bibi Netanyahu]].[1] Netanyahu has since bragged that upon coming to power in 1996 he intentionally subverted the Oslo Accords and ensured that the promised troop drawdown never occurred, leaving Rabin's framework effectively dead in practice long before Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] called in 2026 for its formal cancellation.[1]
