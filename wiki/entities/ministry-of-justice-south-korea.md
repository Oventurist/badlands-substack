# Ministry of Justice (South Korea)

The **Ministry of Justice** is the [[south-korea|South Korean]] government department responsible for prosecution policy, immigration, and exit controls, which in December 2024 imposed an unprecedented travel ban on a sitting president.[1]

## Travel ban on President Yoon (December 2024)

On Monday, December 9, 2024, the ministry banned President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]] from leaving the country after his brief imposition of martial law the previous week.[1] According to the Dong-A Ilbo daily, it was the first time in South Korean history that the measure had been applied against a sitting president.[1] The ban had been requested by the [[corruption-investigation-office-for-high-ranking-officials|Corruption Investigation Office for High-ranking Officials]] and was announced by a ministry official at a parliamentary hearing, according to Reuters.[1]

The measure formed part of a broader legal response in which police opened an investigation into Yoon on suspicion of "insurrection," prosecutors arrested former defense minister [[kim-yong-hyun|Kim Yong-hyun]], and the opposition filed treason complaints against Yoon, Kim, and martial law commander [[park-an-su|Park An-Su]].[1] While a sitting South Korean president enjoys immunity from prosecution, that immunity does not cover allegations of insurrection or treason.[1]
