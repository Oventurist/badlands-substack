# Brazilian Armed Forces

The Brazilian Armed Forces are the national military of Brazil and, in Badlands Media's coverage of the [[2022-brazilian-general-election|2022 Brazilian general election]], a central actor in the dispute over the credibility of the country's voting systems.[1]

## Role in electoral transparency

In an interview with the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] given before the election, President [[jair-bolsonaro|Jair Bolsonaro]] said that what made him more confident about the ongoing elections was the fact that the armed forces in Brazil had been invited to join in electoral transparency.[1] Bolsonaro added that the armed forces had told him it was impossible to grant a seal of credibility or reliability to the voting system given the very many vulnerabilities or weaknesses that the system still featured.[1] The Badlands essay treated this as evidence that Brazil's military was aware of engineered corruption in the electoral machinery and was actively monitoring it heading into the vote.[1]

## Post-election audit reports

Following the disputed result, the article reported swirling statements and reports that Brazil's military apparatus could be considering enacting full-scale audits of the nation's recent elections and of the underlying election systems, amid widespread protests and national tensions.[1] The author suggested this could constitute the first major global elections domino to shift and teeter, observed by an international and engaged public with their own elections rapidly approaching.[1]

## Presence in government

Bolsonaro also described having personally appointed his cabinet rather than accepting top-down impositions from political parties, with one third of the cabinet made up of members of the military and another third of civilian professionals keenly aware of their responsibilities.[1] The article read this pattern — populist leaders installing a mix of military and civilian figures in key leadership positions — as a common feature among nationalist leaders such as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and possibly [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], and as a possible hint of a [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1]
