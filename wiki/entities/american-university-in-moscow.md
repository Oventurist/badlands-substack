# American University in Moscow

The American University in Moscow is an institution associated with efforts to sustain dialogue and cooperation between the United States and Russia.[1] It is led by Dr. [[edward-lozansky|Edward Lozansky]], whose autobiography *Building U.S.–Russia Bridges* documents the back-channel diplomacy of the late Cold War and the plans for a US–Russian development partnership that were ultimately set aside in favor of the policies of the 1990s.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], editor-in-chief of the Canadian Patriot Review and a Badlands Media contributor, is a Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow.[1] The institution appears in the article as part of a network of writers, scholars and statesmen who argue that a US–Russian strategic alliance premised on scientific and industrial progress remains achievable.[1]
