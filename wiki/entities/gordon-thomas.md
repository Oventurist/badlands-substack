# Gordon Thomas

Gordon Thomas was a British author and investigative writer whose work documented the intelligence connections of the media mogul [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]].[1]

## Robert Maxwell, Israel's Superspy

With co-author Martin Dillon, Thomas wrote the unflattering biography *ROBERT MAXWELL, ISRAEL'S SUPERSPY: The Life and Murder of a Media Mogul*, which suggests that Maxwell died at the hands of the spy agency he worked for, Israel's [[mossad|Mossad]].[1] The book portrays Maxwell as a vain, obese man of many excesses, and argues that he helped Mossad steal intelligence-gathering software from the United States before selling it around the world.[1] It also documents the sale of the compromised [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to the military dictatorships of Chile and Argentina, where it was used to facilitate the mass murder of dissidents.[1]

Badlands Media lists Thomas, alongside [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]], among the respected journalists and investigators who have documented Maxwell's close ties to Israeli intelligence and his links to other intelligence services.[1] Thomas's research is also cited for the claim that Maxwell's Israeli holdings included companies that were official "service providers" for Mossad.[1]
