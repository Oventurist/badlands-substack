# Charlotte A. Burrows

Charlotte A. Burrows is the Chair of the U.S. [[equal-employment-opportunity-commission|Equal Employment Opportunity Commission]], the federal body responsible for enforcing workplace anti-discrimination law.[1]

## Commissioner's charge against Tesla

Burrows initiated the administrative process that led to the EEOC's September 2023 federal lawsuit against [[tesla|Tesla]] by filing a commissioner's charge — a mechanism that allows a commissioner, rather than an aggrieved employee, to trigger an agency investigation.[1] Her charge alleged that Tesla had violated [[title-vii-of-the-civil-rights-act-of-1964|Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964]] by subjecting Black employees to an unlawful hostile work environment and by retaliating against employees who opposed the harassment.[1] The resulting suit was filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California.[1]

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, Burrows's charge was cited as evidence that the action against Tesla originated inside the agency rather than with a groundswell of employee complaints, reinforcing the contributors' reading of the case as politically motivated pressure on [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]
