# Dark Storm Team

Dark Storm Team is a pro-Palestinian hacker group known primarily for targeting countries and entities that support Israel's campaign in Gaza following [[hamas|Hamas]]' surprise attack of October 7, 2023.[1]

## X outage claim (March 2025)

The group claimed responsibility, in a public Telegram post, for a distributed denial-of-service attack on [[x-corp|X]] (formerly Twitter) that caused major outages throughout a Monday in March 2025, with tens of thousands of users reporting at least three separate outages of the website and app.[1]

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] instead pointed the finger at Ukraine, suggesting in a series of posts on X that a "large, coordinated group and/or a country is involved" in the "massive" hack and noting that "we get attacked every day, but this was done with a lot of resources."[1] Musk later told Larry Kudlow in a Fox News interview that the hackers had "IP addresses originating in the Ukraine area."[1]
