# Shri Thanedar

Shri Thanedar is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Michigan who drew national attention in late April 2025 when he filed seven articles of impeachment against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Coverage of the impeachment filing quickly shifted to Thanedar's own finances and business history, which critics argued undermined his credibility as an impeachment sponsor.[1]

## Impeachment articles against Trump

Thanedar's seven articles included a claim that Trump had "violated the Supreme Court's 'order'" to return [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]] — described in the reporting as an MS-13 gang member — to the United States, an assertion the source disputed on the grounds that no such order had been violated.[1] Commentary in the Badlands News Brief treated the filing as a bid for attention by "the 'stunning and brave' new face of the Democrat Party" rather than a serious legislative effort, and questioned Thanedar's fitness to serve.[1]

## Campaign debt

Investigative journalist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] published Federal Election Commission records indicating that Thanedar carried campaign debt of $730,313.82, with reporting placing his total obligations at roughly $800,000.[1]

## AniClin Preclinical Services and abandoned animals

In 2010 Thanedar was accused of shutting down AniClin Preclinical Services, a testing laboratory in New Jersey, after its parent company Azopharma — which Thanedar owned — closed.[1] Months after the closure, local animal rights activists reported to authorities that 118 beagles had been abandoned in the facility.[1] Badlands commentary stated that monkeys and other animals were also left behind, and connected the episode to broader criticism of the lawmaker's record.[1]
