# Zack Exley

Zack Exley is an American political and technology consultant identified by Badlands Media as a figure moving through several of the organizations it associates with the [[george-soros|George Soros]] "shadow network."[1]

## Career

Exley works as a political and technology consultant for [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]].[1] He has previously held key positions at the [[wikimedia-foundation|Wikimedia Foundation]], ThoughtWorks, the New Organizing Institute, Politico, and the [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] presidential campaign.[1] Beyond domestic politics he worked for [[john-kerry|John Kerry]]'s presidential campaign and for the UK Labour Party's re-election campaign.[1]

Earlier, Exley served as organizing director at [[moveon-org|MoveOn.org]], where the article says he was caught up in a rigging scandal in favour of Howard Dean during the Dean campaign.[1] Badlands presents this episode as the "clincher" linking him to a pattern of internal manipulation of nominating contests.[1]

## Justice Democrats

In 2017 Exley joined Cenk Uygur of The Young Turks and Kyle Kulinski of Secular Talk to form the Justice Democrats PAC, framed as an effort to reform the Democratic Party and to take on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The group went on to endorse Representatives Rashida Tlaib, Ayanna Pressley and Ilhan Omar, and — in the article's account — literally recruited [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]].[1]

He is also described as a friend of [[eli-pariser|Eli Pariser]], the MoveOn PAC head who declared in 2004 that the network had bought and owned the Democratic Party.[1]

## Assessment

Exley's documented résumé is uncontested; the article's added claim is interpretive — that this résumé traces the skeleton of a single coordinated apparatus rather than an ordinary progressive career path.[1]
