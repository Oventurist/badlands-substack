# Mary Fanning

Mary Fanning is an American writer and researcher associated with the promotion of claims about the alleged [[hammer-and-scorecard|Hammer and Scorecard]] election-manipulation system attributed to [[dennis-montgomery|Dennis Montgomery]].[1]

## Role in the Lindell orbit

Testimony in [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]] in June 2025 established that broadcaster [[brannon-howse|Brannon Howse]] was the person who insisted that [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] connect with Fanning and Montgomery — the link that brought the Hammer and Scorecard material into Lindell's post-2020 election work.[1] Howse was among the plaintiffs' witnesses on the day in question, alongside reputation-repair expert Doug Bania, Lindell himself for the third day of his testimony, and election-security professor [[j-alex-halderman|J. Alex Halderman]].[1]
