# Rush Limbaugh

**Rush Limbaugh** (1951–2021) was an American conservative talk radio host whose program, the Rush Limbaugh Show, was among the most widely syndicated in the United States.[1] He appears in Badlands Media's "[[irregular-warfare|Irregular Warfare]]" as a cited source on the conflicts of interest surrounding [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] and the appointment of a Special Counsel.[1]

## Cited commentary

The article quotes a March 2018 segment in which Limbaugh explains that Justice Department regulations require a special counsel's appointment to name a specific crime, and that a special counsel who encounters conduct outside that scope must return to the appointing official for permission to expand the inquiry.[1] Limbaugh argued that none of this occurred: Rosenstein gave [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] "free rein," making the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|investigation]] a fishing expedition rather than a bounded criminal inquiry.[1] Joe Lange endorses this reading and adds that the conviction of [[paul-manafort|Paul Manafort]] for tax evasion committed between 2010 and 2014 — conduct unrelated to Russia, and connected more to Ukrainian than Russian ties — was nonetheless presented by the media as proof of collusion.[1]

In a second quoted segment, Limbaugh lays out Rosenstein's Clinton entanglements: his service on the [[whitewater-controversy|Whitewater]] prosecution team, his role in the White House travel office FBI files case, and his wife [[lisa-barsoomian|Lisa Barsoomian]]'s representation of [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] in a 1998–99 federal civil case shortly after Rosenstein cleared [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[1] Lange describes Limbaugh as "right" on both points and uses the commentary as the backbone of his argument that the Special Counsel appointment was a planned "witch hunt."[1]
