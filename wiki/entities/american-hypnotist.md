# American Hypnotist

**American Hypnotist** is the pen name of a hypnotist and Substack writer who contributes to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and publishes independently at americanhypnotist.substack.com. His work applies the technical vocabulary of clinical hypnosis, persuasion, and neuro-linguistic programming to mass media and political communication.

He is the author of the October 23, 2023 Badlands essay [[a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine|"A Hypnotist's Take on Israel, Palestine, and Q"]], in which he argues that the [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]] media's propaganda around the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]] is backfiring. In the piece he recounts being told by one of his hypnosis teachers that "if I can heighten someone's emotions then I can give them a hypnotic suggestion," and draws from this the rule that remaining un-hypnotized requires remaining as emotion-neutral as possible amid chaos. He also recalls taking a mini-course in Photoshop in 2015, from which he retained the lesson that "anybody can make anything look like anything."

His stated positions in that essay are that criminal enterprises masquerading as governments are without exception wrong, and that innocent civilians must not be held personally culpable for the actions of such governments. He explicitly refuses to take a side in the conflict absent sufficient reason. His analytical contributions to the corpus include the treatment of the [[nominalization|nominalization]] as a propaganda instrument, the "compliance ladder" as a mechanism of social conformity, and the argument that programming "short circuits" when the surrounding social environment can no longer issue coherent, executable instructions. Badlands Media appends its standard disclaimer to his work, noting that contributors' opinions do not necessarily represent the views of the outlet.

## Mid-2025 features

The August 15, 2025 Badlands News Brief roundup [[a-look-at-recent-features|"A Look At Recent Features"]] lists two of the author's pieces: "A Hypnotist Rants about Q, Epstein and the Nature of Reality Itself" (July 19, 2025) and "A Hypnotist Rants about How to Wake Up the Masses" (July 26, 2025). Both are excerpted in the roundup only by their characteristic salutations, "Greetings Frens" and "Greetings Frens and Patriots," reflecting the informal, direct-address register of the series.
