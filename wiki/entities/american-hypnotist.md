# American Hypnotist

**American Hypnotist** is the pen name of a hypnosis practitioner and contributing author who writes for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and publishes independently at the Substack newsletter *American Hypnotist*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author applies vocabulary and technique drawn from clinical hypnosis — emotional arousal, suggestion, [[nominalization|nominalizations]], and hypnotic repetition — to the analysis of mass media and political messaging.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Analytical framework

The author's central premise is that heightened emotion is the precondition for suggestibility, citing a hypnosis teacher's maxim that "if I can heighten someone's emotions then I can give them a hypnotic suggestion."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) From this the author derives a defensive rule: the key to remaining un-hypnotized is to remain as emotion-neutral as possible amid chaos.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Media manipulation is accordingly framed not merely as false information but as engineered affect — [[disinformation]] described as the blending of truth and falsehood into a web designed to stir the passions of the masses and steer them toward specific actions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

The author further argues that divisive propaganda must both activate the amygdala and provoke a *pre-programmed response*; anger is likened to rocket fuel that is useless to social engineers unless it can be aimed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) A collective loss of clarity about *where* to direct outrage is therefore read as a critical breakdown in the [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] social-engineering apparatus.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Stated position on Israel–Palestine

Writing in October 2023 about the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]], the author declined to adopt a partisan side, stating two principles: that criminal enterprises masquerading as "government" are without exception wrong, and that innocent civilians must not be held personally culpable for the actions of such governments.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The stated goal is the removal of criminal elements from governments rather than the decimation of one side by the other, and the author explicitly refuses to be goaded into a stance without sufficient reason.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author questions how a person living halfway across the world can know what is happening when propagandists from every corner of the Earth skew the story in different directions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Views on Q and media collapse

The author treats the discrediting of the mainstream press as a "survival imperative" rather than an act of malice, and argues that the broad public familiarity with [[qanon|Q]]-associated ideas — particularly the notion that events are scripted and fake — gives people "a place to go when their old narratives break down."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author predicts that a third world war will be "like a car that won't start," prompting people to look under the hood, and that the Israel–Palestine conflict will be the final straw for many.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors and not necessarily the views of the outlet.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Later features

The author remained an active [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor into 2025, with two pieces highlighted in the 15 August 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] retrospective of recent feature articles.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) "A Hypnotist Rants about Q, Epstein and the Nature of Reality Itself," published 19 July 2025, continued the author's treatment of [[qanon|Q]] alongside the Epstein affair and questions of reality construction.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) "A Hypnotist Rants about How to Wake Up the Masses," published 26 July 2025, addressed methods of breaking mass suggestibility.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Both articles open with the author's customary salutation to "Frens and Patriots."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Writing on law and civic authority

On 16 May 2023 the author published "A Startling Contradiction at the Heart of 'Our' Legal System," a Badlands Media essay arguing that the [[rule-of-law|rule of law]] cannot exist where citizens are unable to articulate the laws binding them.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) The piece contends that the demand for professional legal interpreters and the expectation of lay compliance are mutually exclusive assumptions, and that a free society is impossible when its rules are indecipherable to its members.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) It advances the claim that Title 28 §3002(15)(A) of the US Code defines the [[united-states-federal-corporation|United States as a federal corporation]], while insisting that the older chain of authority — from God through the people to the states — remains intact, citing [[chisholm-v-georgia|Chisholm v. Georgia]] and the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]] in support.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

The essay is also unusually autobiographical: the author discloses that he was "a hardcore 'lefty' until his late twenties," when these legal facts were first brought to his attention, and offers his own conversion as evidence that sharing the citation can awaken others.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) It urges readers to assist [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[qanon|Q]] team by informing themselves and stepping into their role as the de jure civilian authority, while disclaiming any recommendation of specific action.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) Readers are directed to the author's independent newsletter at American Hypnotist for further work.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## Legal commentary on the Trump indictments

In a September 2023 piece for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], American Hypnotist analysed the indictments of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]], arguing that both rest on the unprovable premise that Trump knew his election claims were false.[4] The essay walks readers through court procedure — cause, elements, and [[standing-and-jurisdiction|standing and jurisdiction]] — to argue that the dismissal of 50 of 55 election cases on standing means the evidence was never actually heard.[4]

The author also revisits his earlier reading of the film [[white-squall|White Squall]] as a [[qanon|Q]] comm, noting that the movie ends with the captain on trial and redeemed by his crew.[4] Readers are directed to his own publication for further work, and the piece carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[4]

## Commentary on lab-grown meat and the food supply (2023)

In the 1 May 2023 Badlands news brief, American Hypnotist responded to the FDA's clearance of [[lab-grown-meat|lab-grown chicken]] by arguing that real food and "imitation" food are nowhere close to the same product, and that the food supply is tainted to an extraordinary degree — to the point that avoiding a laundry list of toxins is nearly impossible in many places.[5] He urged readers to befriend a farmer and buy real food directly.[5]

He wrote that rumors of mRNA technology being engineered into lettuce and other foods were likely true, and that a reasonable person should assume nothing purchasable at a big chain grocery store or restaurant can be fully trusted.[5] At the same time he counseled hope, suggesting that human bodies may be far more resilient to the onslaught of toxins than presently understood, citing the return of vegetation around Chernobyl centuries ahead of predictions as evidence that "life may find a way," and concluding: "Your body is not a machine—it's a miracle."[5]

## Commentary on the collapse of Vice Media (2023)

In the May 3, 2023 Badlands News Brief, American Hypnotist responded to reports that [[vice-media|Vice Media]] — once valued at $5.7 billion — was preparing to file for bankruptcy, observing that while standing armies cannot stop an idea whose time has come, billions of dollars likewise cannot save a company whose time has gone.[6] He argued the collapse suggested that progressive "infotainment" lacked the staying power and popularity attributed to it, casting doubt on the claim that the modern left had successfully captured the culture.[6] Citing Walter Lippmann's *Public Opinion*, he contended that the mass proliferation of newspapers a century earlier had been engineered by moneyed interests rather than driven by organic demand, and asked whether outlets like Vice had ever risen on genuine demand or whether the money faucet had simply run dry.[6]

## "Countering an Ocean of Deceit" (2025)

On 24 June 2025 American Hypnotist published "Countering an Ocean of Deceit," subtitled "If Q is Real, What Then?", a Badlands Media essay proposing that the Q drops together comprise a "memeplex" designed to reveal the operation's true purpose through the activities of proponents and detractors alike.[7] The essay defined [[q-operation|Q]]'s legitimacy as two demonstrable propositions — that Q is a real insider with classified access, and that Q is on the side of mankind — and referred readers to his earlier pieces "Q is Real Because Math Says So" and "Q is Our Best Fren" for the underlying arguments.[7]

Drawing on David and Goliath and on Luke Skywalker's photon-torpedo strike in *Star Wars: A New Hope* — noting that both "Luke" and "photon" carry connotations of light — he argued that "a single well-placed droplet of truth can vaporize an ocean of deceit," and identified the [[q-proofs|Q proofs]] as that droplet against the [[mockingbird-media|mockingbird media]].[7] He engaged in detail with the critic [[17th-special-citizens-operations-group|17SCOG]], accepting his forensic demonstration that Q is a structured, military-grade influence operation while rejecting his conclusion that it is therefore malign or illegal, and invoking the DoD [[dod-law-of-war-manual|Law of War Manual]] against the statutory objections drawn from Title 50 § 3093, DoD Directive 3600.01 and [[executive-order-12333|Executive Order 12333]].[7]

He disclosed that he had himself faced false accusations of domestic terrorism as a Q supporter, expressed sympathy for 17SCOG's account of being rebuked by a court over his QAnon association, and maintained that responsibility for such treatment lies with the enemy rather than with Q.[7] The essay closed with the expectation that [[the-storm|the Storm]] would arrive between late summer 2025 and 4 July 2026, and with the refrain "it drips and it drips and it drips some more… and then comes the flood," applied to the coming [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]].[7] As is customary, readers were directed to his independent newsletter at American Hypnotist for more of his work.[7]

## Neuroscience and expanded thinking

In "Expand Your Thinking" (May 25, 2023), subtitled "What Neuroscience Can Teach Us About Symbolic Communication and 5G War," the author turned from hypnosis to hemispheric neurology, taking [[qanon|Q]]'s injunction to "expand your thinking" as a practical problem in cognition.[8] He argued that the brain is continuously doing two things at once — creating maps of the world and experiencing the world directly — and that expanding one's thinking means learning to update those internal maps with new information.[8] Drawing on the work of [[iain-mcgilchrist|Iain McGilchrist]], he presented [[hemispheric-specialization|hemispheric specialization]] as an established fact of neurology and left-brain dominance as the condition in which a person's [[map-and-territory|map is mistaken for the territory]].[8]

He grounded the argument in his own field by noting that a subject can be hypnotized into not perceiving another person in the room, citing Anthony Jacquin's *Reality Is Plastic* and James Tripp's Online Deep Apprenticeship, and treated this hypnotic deletion of sensory information as literal proof that internal maps edit perception.[8] The same mechanism, he wrote, operates when someone is presented with a truth that contradicts their belief system, especially under social pressure to conform.[8]

The essay closed by recommending the website [[decoding-symbolism|Decoding Symbolism]] — with an explicit refusal to endorse its conclusions — as a training ground for the associative, symbolic mode of thought he considers necessary to the [[5g-war|5G war]], on the Q premise that the [[cabal|cabal]]'s need for symbolism will be its downfall.[8] As usual, the piece carried the Badlands disclaimer that contributors' views are their own and directed readers to his newsletter American Hypnotist.[8]
