# Lunden Roberts

Lunden Roberts is the Arkansas woman who is the mother of Navy Joan Roberts, a daughter born out of wedlock to [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], son of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] In 2020 Roberts reached an agreement with Hunter Biden establishing the child's paternity and setting child support payments.[1]

## Reopened child support case

The case was reopened after Hunter Biden requested adjustments to the child support payments he was making.[1] In December 2022, Roberts's lawyers filed a motion to have the girl's last name changed to Biden.[1] Hunter Biden appeared in person in an Arkansas courtroom on Monday, May 1, 2023 for a hearing in the matter, at which his new attorney, [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]], stated that the president's son was paying $20,000 a month to the plaintiff.[1]
