# Ford F-150 Lightning

The Ford F-150 Lightning is the all-electric version of [[ford-motor-company|Ford Motor Company]]'s F-150 pickup truck, built at the Rouge Electric Vehicle Center in Dearborn, Michigan.[1] Introduced with a high-profile launch in May 2021, it was explicitly positioned by Ford chief executive [[jim-farley|Jim Farley]] as a bellwether for mainstream consumer acceptance of battery power: "I am looking at this vehicle as a test for adoption for electric vehicles," Farley told reporters, adding, "We should all watch very carefully how this does in the market."[1]

## 2023 production cuts

Ford had planned annual production capacity of roughly 150,000 Lightnings a year, or about 3,200 units a week.[1] During its third-quarter 2023 earnings call in October, the company signalled that it would "adjust" electric-vehicle production and delay about $12 billion in EV investments in response to softening demand for higher-priced premium electric vehicles.[1]

In December 2023 a memo to suppliers, first reported by Automotive News, indicated that beginning in January the company would build an average of about 1,600 Lightning trucks a week — halving its 2024 production target.[1] A Ford spokeswoman said only, "We'll continue to match production with customer demand."[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] framed the retreat as Ford failing its own declared test of EV adoption, attributing the collapse in demand to unaffordability and high costs.[1] The cut came less than a week after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] unveiled the [[tesla|Tesla]] Cybertruck.[1]

## Commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] used the production cut to describe his own revised view of Musk and the electric-vehicle push. He had previously read Tesla's technology drive as running in parallel with an establishment "green" agenda aimed at a World Economic Forum–style future of owning nothing, but argued that reality had not borne this out, as Tesla's energy projects appeared to be consistently undercutting and disrupting that agenda.[1]
