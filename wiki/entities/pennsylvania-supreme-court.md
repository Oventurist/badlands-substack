# Pennsylvania Supreme Court

The Pennsylvania Supreme Court is the highest court in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and, in Badlands Media coverage of election integrity, is cited as one of the state judicial bodies whose rulings shape how ballots are counted in a pivotal swing state.[1]

## Election integrity rulings

Chapter 19 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series points to the Pennsylvania Supreme Court's decision to disallow mail-in ballots that are unmarked, inaccurate, or unsigned as evidence that a broader push to clean up American elections is under way.[1] The author places that decision alongside the [[save-act|SAVE Act]] and the [[north-carolina-supreme-court|North Carolina Supreme Court]]'s ruling removing [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] from the ballot as examples of states making what the author calls significant progress, while adding that whether enough progress has been made "remains to be seen."[1]

The chapter situates these rulings within its wider argument that United States elections can be and have been rigged, and that ballot-handling rules in a handful of key states determine national outcomes.[1]
