# Deadwood, South Dakota

**Deadwood** is a town in South Dakota that has served as a recurring host location for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[gart|Great American Restoration Tour]] (GART) events.[1] In its promotional material the network described Deadwood as lying "pretty damn close to the actual Badlands," making it one of only two GART locations to date situated near the geographic region from which the outlet takes its name.[1]

## Badlands Media events in Deadwood

Badlands Media first held a GART stop in Deadwood in 2024, an event the organisation later described as "absolutely fantastic" and a favourite among both its hosts and its attendees.[1] The network returned to the town for [[gart-9-deadwood|GART 9]], the ninth stop of the tour, held in August 2025 and announced on July 7, 2025 by host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[1] Announcing the return, Badlands said it expected the second Deadwood event to be no different in quality from the first, and that it would likely revert to the multi-host panel format used during its previous visit after experimenting with other formats at the intervening stops.[1]

The 2025 event was scheduled to run across a full weekend, with a welcome reception and party on the Friday night followed by three days of panels, alongside the informal off-programme activity the network refers to as "shenanigans," which it said would take place as "Badlands literally takes over the town."[1] Full-weekend General Admission and virtual tickets were sold for the event, the latter including streaming access, long-term replay access and entry to an exclusive GART [[telegram]] chat.[1]

## GART 5 (August 2024)

The first Deadwood stop was the fifth event of the Great American Restoration Tour, held in August 2024.[2] In an August 11, 2024 "last call" post, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that he and his fellow Badlands hosts would travel to Deadwood the following week, and that VIP tickets for the event had already sold out, leaving only a limited number of single-day tickets for locals wishing to attend part of the Friday or Saturday festivities.[2] Virtual tickets were also sold, granting streaming access to the full slate of Friday, Saturday and Sunday panels, long-term DVR access to replays after the weekend, and entry to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat with behind-the-scenes photos and video.[2] Announced panel topics ranged from coming "Full Circle on the Q Drops" to "Futurism and Freedom Cities."[2]

## GART 12 (June 2026)

Deadwood hosted the twelfth stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] in late June 2026, the town's third time receiving the tour.[3] In a June 25, 2026 "last call" post, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that Badlands had "developed a habit of taking the town over" in Deadwood, and framed the event as the sixth GART of what the network calls the new American Golden Age.[3] A limited number of in-person tickets remained available at the time of the announcement, alongside virtual tickets covering the full Friday, Saturday and Sunday panel programme with long-term DVR replay access and entry to the event's exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat.[3]


## Third GART visit (2026)

Deadwood was chosen again for [[gart-12-deadwood|GART 12]], announced on April 26, 2026 for a start date of June 25, making it the town's third turn hosting the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]].[4] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that Deadwood "might as well be official Badlands HQ at this point."[4] VIP tickets were nearly sold out at announcement, while Full Weekend General Admission passes covering a Friday-night welcome party and three days of panels remained available, alongside virtual tickets with DVR replay access and the exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat.[4]
