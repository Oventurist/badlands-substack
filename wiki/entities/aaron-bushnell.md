# Aaron Bushnell

Aaron Bushnell was a 25-year-old active-duty member of the U.S. Air Force from San Antonio, Texas, who died after setting himself ablaze outside the Israeli Embassy in Washington, D.C., in protest of the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## Self-immolation

Bushnell walked up to the embassy shortly before 1 p.m. on Sunday, February 25, 2024, and began livestreaming on the video platform Twitch, according to a person familiar with the matter who spoke to the Associated Press.[1] Law enforcement officials believe he set his phone down, doused himself in accelerant and ignited the flames; at one point he said he "will no longer be complicit in genocide."[1] The video was later removed from the platform, but law enforcement officials obtained and reviewed a copy.[1] The Metropolitan Police Department said on Monday that Bushnell had died of his injuries.[1]
