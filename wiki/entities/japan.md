# Japan

Japan is an East Asian state whose maritime policy in the waters east of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] became a point of contention with [[china|China]] in 2026.

According to Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, Japanese Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] and Philippine President [[ferdinand-marcos-jr|Ferdinand Marcos Jr.]] announced at a May 28 summit in Tokyo that their governments would begin maritime delimitation talks concerning their exclusive economic zones and continental shelves. Chinese Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[mao-ning|Mao Ning]] responded that the waters in question lie east of Taiwan and that any delimitation negotiations there must involve China; Taiwan also objected, urging Japan and the [[philippines|Philippines]] to consult Taipei. Japanese Chief Cabinet Secretary [[minoru-kihara|Minoru Kihara]] said any agreement reached between Tokyo and Manila would not be legally binding on third parties. China subsequently launched a "special maritime law enforcement operation" east of Taiwan.
