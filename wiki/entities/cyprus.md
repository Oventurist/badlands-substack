# Cyprus

Cyprus is an island in the eastern Mediterranean, long divided between Greek Cypriot and Turkish Cypriot administrations and a persistent point of friction between [[greece|Greece]] and [[turkey|Türkiye]].

## As a watchpoint in the Badlands corpus

In the July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]], contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] names Cyprus as one of the locations readers should monitor as evidence for his thesis that a conflict involving Türkiye is being engineered. Following a Ukrainian drone strike on the Krasnodarskaya compressor station serving the [[blue-stream-pipeline|Blue Stream]] gas pipeline, he argues that [[ukraine|Ukraine]] acted on behalf of [[israel|Israel]], and lists Cyprus alongside the [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline in the Black Sea and Türkiye's Aegean west coast as potential targets of what he describes as a NATO/Israeli operation. The island's position between Türkiye, Greece and the Levant makes it, in this reading, a natural theatre for any escalation intended to bring European states into conflict with Türkiye.
