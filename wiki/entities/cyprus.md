# Cyprus

Cyprus is an island in the eastern Mediterranean identified in July 2026 Badlands commentary as a place to watch amid rising tensions between [[turkey|Türkiye]] and its regional rivals.[1]

## 2026 flashpoint analysis

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], arguing that [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s strike on Türkiye-linked gas infrastructure was carried out on [[israel|Israel]]'s behalf, urged readers to keep an eye on Cyprus alongside the [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline crossing the Black Sea.[1] Turkey's west coast in the Aegean Sea was named as a further potential target of a [[nato|NATO]]/Israeli operation.[1] The same analysis anticipated possible Ukraine–Turkey conflict with [[greece|Greece]] potentially aiding Ukraine.[1]
