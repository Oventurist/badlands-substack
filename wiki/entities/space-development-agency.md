# Space Development Agency

The Space Development Agency (SDA) is the US defense organization developing the Transport Layer, a military satellite communications network already under development as of 2026.[1]

When the [[united-states-space-force|US Space Force]] awarded [[spacex|SpaceX]] a $2.29 billion contract in May 2026 to build the [[space-data-network-backbone|Space Data Network Backbone]], officials specified that the new effort would work alongside the SDA's Transport Layer program, with both systems expected to operate together as part of a larger War Department communications architecture.[1] The pairing reflects a broader push to speed up military space acquisitions through rapid prototyping and commercial partnerships while expanding the industrial base supporting US national security space programs.[1]
