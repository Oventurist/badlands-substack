# West Virginia Republican Party

The **West Virginia Republican Party** (West Virginia GOP) is the state affiliate of the Republican Party in West Virginia.[1]

The party was a party in interest in the December 2023 litigation brought by candidate [[john-anthony-castro|John Anthony Castro]] to remove former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's presidential ballot.[1] U.S. District Judge [[irene-berger|Irene Berger]] sided with attorneys for Trump, Secretary of State [[mac-warner|Mac Warner]], and the West Virginia GOP in dismissing the suit on standing grounds.[1]
