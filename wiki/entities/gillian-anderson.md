# Gillian Anderson

Gillian Anderson is an American-British actress best known for her role in *The X-Files*, referred to in Badlands Media commentary as the "X-Files queen."[1]

## Comment on the 2023 Grammy Awards

Anderson was cited among the public figures who reacted to the [[grammy-awards|Grammy Awards]] controversy of February 2023 and to the promotional tweet posted and later deleted by [[cbs|CBS]].[1] She was quoted as saying, "You don't have to say the entertainment industry is satanic, they're doing it for you."[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] highlighted the remark as an example of how little effort the industry was making to conceal its imagery.[1]
