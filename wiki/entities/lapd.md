# Los Angeles Police Department

The Los Angeles Police Department (LAPD) is the municipal police force of Los Angeles, California, and the agency whose officers beat [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] in 1991.[1]

## The King beating and trial

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that the officers appeared on camcorder footage to be trying to kill King, and that although they had sworn an oath to protect and serve, their conduct indicated they should no longer be police officers.[1] He allows that the officers may have been driven by job pressures and that King was intoxicated, speeding, and resisting arrest.[1] Four officers were tried for the beating and acquitted by a jury a year later, an outcome that triggered the [[la-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]].[1]

Carlson later characterizes the recording of the beating by LA cops as, in his current belief, staged — the surface layer of a much deeper structure involving prison privatization, the [[crime-bill-1994|1994 Crime Bill]], and [[cia|CIA]] drug trafficking into South Central Los Angeles.[1]
