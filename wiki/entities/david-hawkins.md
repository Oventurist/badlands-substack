# David Hawkins

**David Hawkins** is the author of *Power vs. Force*, cited in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] for his framework of emotional and vibrational states.[1]

The essay uses Hawkins's work to argue that low-frequency states such as fear, anger, and shame dominate the emotional landscape under GDS and diminish access to higher-order cognitive processes including creativity, problem-solving, and critical thinking — fear triggering reactive instincts, anger fueling division, and shame enforcing passivity.[1] Conversely, in the essay's account of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]], Hawkins's research is said to show that elevated states such as courage, love, and truth dissolve the fear, shame, and anger that underpin systems of control, which the [[white-hats|White Hats]] are described as amplifying through truth-based narratives and collective action.[1]

## Cited in Structures of Control

Part 3 of Badlands Media's *Structures of Control* series invokes Hawkins's levels of consciousness, together with [[spiral-dynamics|Spiral Dynamics]], to argue that individuals aligned with higher frequencies radiate energy that uplifts others.[2] The essay contrasts this "energetic sovereignty" with the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s need to steal energy through suppression and fear, which it reads as proof of an inability to ascend (see [[authentic-5d|authentic 5D]]).[2]

## Cited in "The Bridge" (March 2025)

In the March 1, 2025 essay "The Bridge: Awakening, Healing, and Ascension," Hawkins is again cited through *Power vs. Force*, this time to describe the shift from reaction to conscious action and for the maxim "Wisdom is the discernment of truth from falsehood."[3] The author uses the quotation to argue that as awareness deepens a person can recognise what genuinely aligns with their well-being and what is merely a product of conditioning or fear-based influence.[3]
