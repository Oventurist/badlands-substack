# David Hawkins

**David Hawkins** is the author of *Power vs. Force*, cited in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] for his framework of emotional and vibrational states.[1]

The essay uses Hawkins's work to argue that low-frequency states such as fear, anger, and shame dominate the emotional landscape under GDS and diminish access to higher-order cognitive processes including creativity, problem-solving, and critical thinking — fear triggering reactive instincts, anger fueling division, and shame enforcing passivity.[1] Conversely, in the essay's account of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]], Hawkins's research is said to show that elevated states such as courage, love, and truth dissolve the fear, shame, and anger that underpin systems of control, which the [[white-hats|White Hats]] are described as amplifying through truth-based narratives and collective action.[1]
