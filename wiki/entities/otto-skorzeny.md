# Otto Skorzeny

**Otto Skorzeny** was a Waffen-SS officer of Nazi Germany described as having been very close with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]].[1]

## Postwar recruitment claims

Badlands commentary cites reporting that [[mossad|Mossad]] hired several former Nazi SS intelligence officers after the Second World War, naming Skorzeny in particular.[1] That claim is used to support a broader argument that the relationship between the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] and the former Nazi Wehrmacht establishment differs from the conventional account.[1] The same argument draws on the reported 1940–41 approach by the Jewish militia [[lehi]] to Mussolini and Hitler with an alliance proposal called "The Jerusalem Agreement," and on the founding of [[nato|NATO]] with the participation of former German military leaders after the war.[1]

## Role in the stay-behind network

In her primer on [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]] identifies Skorzeny as the officer used to provide the extensive training required by the Axis [[stay-behind-units|stay-behind]] network — instruction in firearms, explosives and communication equipment matching the contents of the hidden caches.[2] She notes his special, close relationship with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]], who chose him for the highly secretive rescue of [[benito-mussolini|Mussolini]] toward the end of the war and for other high-profile operations.[2]

Watkins further traces a direct line from Skorzeny to contemporary Ukraine, writing that the [[right-sector|Right Sector]] and [[azov-battalion|Azov]] battalions descend from Skorzeny-trained [[stepan-bandera|Stepan Bandera]] and [[andriy-melnyk|Andriy Melnyk]] of the [[organization-of-ukrainian-nationalists|Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists]], now a Ukrainian political party.[2] Skorzeny is named at the outset of the article among the seemingly unrelated subjects — with Jonestown, the Unification Church, the USS Liberty attack, and the JFK and Lumumba assassinations — that she argues share the Gladio thread.[2]
