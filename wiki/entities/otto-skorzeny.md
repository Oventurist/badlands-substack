# Otto Skorzeny

**Otto Skorzeny** was a Waffen-SS officer of Nazi Germany described as having been very close with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]].[1]

## Postwar recruitment claims

Badlands commentary cites reporting that [[mossad|Mossad]] hired several former Nazi SS intelligence officers after the Second World War, naming Skorzeny in particular.[1] That claim is used to support a broader argument that the relationship between the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] and the former Nazi Wehrmacht establishment differs from the conventional account.[1] The same argument draws on the reported 1940–41 approach by the Jewish militia [[lehi]] to Mussolini and Hitler with an alliance proposal called "The Jerusalem Agreement," and on the founding of [[nato|NATO]] with the participation of former German military leaders after the war.[1]
