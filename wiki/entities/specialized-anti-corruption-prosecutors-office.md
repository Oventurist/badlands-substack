# Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office (SAPO)

The **Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office (SAPO)** is the Ukrainian prosecutorial body that works alongside the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine]] (NABU) to prosecute high-level corruption cases.[1]

## 2025 subordination to the executive

In July 2025, SAPO and NABU were placed under executive oversight following security raids on NABU, with President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] suggesting that Ukraine's anti-corruption infrastructure was subject to Russian influence.[1] Hundreds of Ukrainians took to the streets across the country on Tuesday, July 22, 2025, to protest the crackdown, which many viewed as an authoritarian push by the Ukrainian leader toward consolidating power.[1]

Badlands commentary argued that the raids appeared to have upset Zelensky's Western backers, producing public demonstrations and "the makings of regime change operations," and noted the apparent paradox that the same Western sponsors who created these bodies in 2015 objected when Zelensky raided them and arrested alleged Russian assets.[1]

## July 2025 curtailment

The office was one of the two national anti-corruption bodies whose independence Ukrainian lawmakers voted to scuttle in July 2025, alongside the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine]].[2] The vote produced the largest protests in Ukraine since the start of the full-scale Russian invasion, with demonstrators massing outside President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s office in defiance of martial-law restrictions and curfew.[2] See [[2025-ukrainian-anti-corruption-law-protests|2025 Ukrainian anti-corruption law protests]].[2]
