# Vermont

Vermont is a U.S. state in New England. It is the only state in the union that imposes no age restriction on candidates for the office of governor, a quirk of state law that has repeatedly enabled minors to mount gubernatorial campaigns.[1]

## Youth candidacies

In December 2025, 14-year-old high school freshman [[dean-roy|Dean Roy]] announced a campaign for governor of Vermont, arguing against "elder rule" and describing Vermont as "a dying state." If elected he would be the first U.S. governor under 18.[1] Roy's bid followed that of 13-year-old Ethan Sonneborn, who ran unsuccessfully in Vermont's Democratic gubernatorial primary in 2018.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted Vermont's unique absence of an age requirement and argued that the state is "so corrupt it makes the nation fund [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]]' commie fever dreams," concluding that a teenage candidate "couldn't possibly do worse than those who came before him."[1]
