# Barbra Streisand

**Barbra Streisand** is an American singer and philanthropist whose Streisand Foundation appears in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] reporting as one of the left-of-center funders of the legal advocacy group [[citizens-for-responsibility-and-ethics-in-washington|Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington]].[1]

## Funding of CREW

In his January 1, 2024 essay on the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification case]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the research site [[influencewatch|InfluenceWatch]] in noting that CREW has received funding from left-of-center foundations including [[george-soros|George Soros]]' [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] and the Streisand Foundation.[1] He offered the funding profile as evidence that the organization litigating to remove [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from state ballots was not the nonpartisan watchdog it claims to be.[1]
