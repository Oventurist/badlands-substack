# Max Planck

**Max Planck**, described as the father of quantum theory, is quoted in "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," the fifth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series published by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on August 9, 2025.[1]

The article cites his statement, "I regard consciousness as fundamental. I regard matter as derivative from consciousness," and glosses it as meaning that the material world is downstream from awareness.[1] From this the author draws a political inference: that the system described in the series had to hijack perception first, "because whoever controls the narrative, controls the field," and that when presence returns to the body, narrative control begins to dissolve.[1] Planck's line sits alongside quotations from [[amit-goswami|Amit Goswami]] and [[bessel-van-der-kolk|Bessel van der Kolk]] in the essay's case for a [[resonance-field|resonance field]] that lies beyond measurement.[1]
