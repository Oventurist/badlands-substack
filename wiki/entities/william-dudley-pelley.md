# William Dudley Pelley

William Dudley Pelley was the American fascist leader of the Nazi [[silver-shirts|Silver Shirts]], with whom [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] openly associated during the interwar and wartime years.[1]

Pelley's Theosophical ties extended to America's earliest claimed "UFO abductee" George Adamski and to George Hunt Williamson, linking the Silver Shirts movement to the broader occult and proto-New Age currents that [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] traces through this period.[1] It was Pelley who reproduced the Protocols of Zion leaflets that were mailed out through Congressional offices in the United States under Viereck's direction during World War II.[1]
