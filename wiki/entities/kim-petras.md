# Kim Petras

Kim Petras is a pop singer, described in Badlands Media coverage as trans-identified, who shared the 2023 Grammy Award for best pop duo with [[sam-smith|Sam Smith]] for the song "Unholy."[1]

## 2023 Grammy Awards performance

Petras and Smith performed "Unholy" at the February 2023 [[grammy-awards|Grammy Awards]] in a staging that conservative outlets described as satanic.[1] Both performers and their dancers wore red; Petras danced inside a cage while Smith, wearing devil horns, was surrounded by robed dancers in a ritual circle.[1] The performance was reported by [[the-post-millennial|The Post Millennial]] and drew condemnation from Badlands Media contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]], who read it as the open veneration of Baphomet and as evidence of the hyper-sexualization of culture.[1]
