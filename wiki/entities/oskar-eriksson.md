# Oskar Eriksson

Oskar Eriksson is a Swedish curler who competed at the [[2026-winter-olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]] and triggered the Games' most widely discussed officiating dispute.[1]

During Sweden's 8–6 men's round-robin loss to Canada, Eriksson alleged that Canadian third [[marc-kennedy|Marc Kennedy]] had touched his stone after release, a violation that under [[world-curling|World Curling]] rules leads to removal of the stone.[1] After Kennedy denied the accusation profanely, Eriksson responded that he would show video after the game demonstrating the touch occurred "two meters over the hog line."[1]

Commentators reported that the Swedish team, frustrated by what it viewed as serial double-touching by the Canadians, had effectively set up a surveillance effort to document the infractions, and that other teams shared Sweden's frustration.[1] Canada subsequently accused the Swedes of improperly recording violations inside the arena.[1]
