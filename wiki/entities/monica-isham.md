# Monica Isham

Monica Isham is a Wisconsin state judge in Sawyer County who drew national attention in April 2025 when she threatened to stop holding court in response to the federal arrest of fellow Wisconsin jurist [[hannah-dugan|Hannah Dugan]].[1] Reporting described her as a "leftist" or "activist" judge, and her declaration was circulated as an email sent to judges across the state on Saturday, April 26, 2025.[1]

## The Branch 2 boycott threat

In the email — first reported by Fox News and subsequently amplified by Breitbart — Isham wrote: "If there is no guidance for us and no support for us, I will refuse to hold court in Branch 2 in Sawyer County. I will not put myself or my staff who may feel compelled to help me or my community in harms way."[1] She indicated she did not intend to cooperate with [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] agents in matters involving illegal aliens appearing before her.[1]

Isham framed the position in constitutional terms, writing: "I have no intention of allowing anyone to be taken out of my courtroom by ICE and sent to a concentration camp, especially without due process, as BOTH of the constitutions we swore to support require."[1] The reference to "BOTH" constitutions invoked her oath to the federal and Wisconsin state constitutions.[1]

## Political reaction

Representative [[tom-tiffany|Tom Tiffany]] (R-WI) posted images of Isham's email to social media and called for her removal, writing that "Monica Isham is choosing to protect illegal aliens over the law. She should resign or be removed."[1] The episode was treated in Badlands commentary as evidence of a widening confrontation between state judiciaries and federal immigration enforcement under the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], with Badlands contributor Chris Paul questioning whether the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] retained the practical power or legitimacy to resolve such conflicts.[1]
