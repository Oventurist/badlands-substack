# Bill O'Reilly

**Bill O'Reilly** is an American broadcaster and commentator who toured with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a series of live "History Tour" events in December 2021.[1]

## December 2021 tour with Trump

During the tour, Trump and O'Reilly publicly encouraged audiences to get the [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccine]], an on-stage exchange that Badlands contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] described as causing conservative "heads to explode" again.[1] Gillespie reproduces the moment as a central data point in her essay "Why Trump Pushed the Vax," treating it alongside Trump's statement that he had himself been vaccinated and boosted.[1] She argues that the apparent endorsement should be read through the lens of wartime deception rather than at face value, and questions which specific product Trump was referring to given shifting definitions of the word "vaccine."[1]
