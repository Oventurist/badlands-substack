# Sheikh Hasina

Sheikh Hasina is the former Prime Minister of Bangladesh, who led the country for 15 years at the head of the Awami League party before being ousted in the [[2024-bangladesh-government-overthrow|2024 overthrow of her government]].[1]

## Fall from power

In August 2024, weeks of student-led protests against job quotas escalated into nationwide violence that claimed more than 700 lives according to the interim government's tally, and Hasina fled the country as crowds stormed her residence.[1] Nobel Peace Prize laureate [[muhammad-yunus|Muhammad Yunus]] became chief adviser of the interim government that replaced her.[1]

## Allegations by her former minister

[[mohibul-hasan-chowdhury|Mohibul Hasan Chowdhury]], who served in Hasina's cabinet and acted as chief negotiator during the crisis, later told RT that the riots which brought down her government were backed by [[usaid|USAID]] and the family of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and that US-linked NGOs including the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] had been running campaigns against the government since 2018.[1] He described the unrest as a "carefully planned" operation bankrolled by Western interests rather than a spontaneous youth revolt.[1]
