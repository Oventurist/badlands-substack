# Chinese Communist Party

The Chinese Communist Party is the founding and ruling political party of the People's Republic of China. In the Badlands Media corpus, the CCP is framed as a central actor in a global Communist plot to subvert the United States through corporate capture, algorithmic manipulation, and economic warfare.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## TikTok and Information Operations

The June 2023 article "[[A Communist Plot to Take Over America]]" specifically identifies [[TikTok]] as a CCP-run platform whose algorithmic curation is designed to degrade Western youth culture. The source argues that the CCP uses the platform to condition users by rewarding stupidity and degradation, thereby advancing the broader Communist subversion strategy that also encompasses corporate wokeness and [[artificial intelligence]] manipulation.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

The article describes the CCP's strategy as part of a four-stage takeover plan: strip away American identity, destroy American society and values, destroy the American economy, and finally usher in a Communist state in which elites exercise total control over a two-class system.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]
