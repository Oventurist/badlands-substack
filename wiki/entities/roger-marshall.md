# Roger Marshall

Roger Marshall is a Republican United States senator and physician who was among the lawmakers pressing for rapid passage of standalone military aid to Israel in November 2023.[1] Badlands Media's news brief coverage identified him by the honorific "Dr. Marshall" and, following the wording of the cited Epoch Times report, as a senator associated with the letter designation "(R-Ky.)" in that account.[1]

## Push for standalone Israel aid

On November 7, 2023, Senate Democrats blocked a Republican-led bill that would have provided standalone aid to Israel, and Marshall was one of the Republican senators who called for quick passage of the legislation, which had already cleared the U.S. House of Representatives in a bipartisan vote.[1] The measure would have delivered $14 billion to Israel in the wake of the [[hamas|Hamas]] attack, and the House version paired the money with cuts to Internal Revenue Service funding.[1]

Speaking on the Senate floor, Marshall argued that "a timely military aid package with a unified voice from Congress showing support for Israel will not only add to Israel's stability, it will slow down and hopefully stop the evil plots of Hamas, Iran, and its proxies."[1] He emphasized that the funding level in the Republican bill matched the level requested by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in October, noting that the difference was that Biden's request also bundled in additional money for Ukraine.[1]

Democrats, led on the floor by Senate Appropriations Committee chairwoman [[patty-murray|Patty Murray]], insisted that Ukraine and Israel funding move together in a single package, producing the impasse that left neither tranche enacted.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright framed the standoff as evidence of "Congressional Paralysis," arguing that the bifurcation of Ukraine and Israel aid promised by Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] had resulted in the establishment blocking aid altogether — an outcome the writer characterized as favorable, since no fresh aid had been deployed to either theater while war hawks in both parties were left publicly frustrated.[1]
