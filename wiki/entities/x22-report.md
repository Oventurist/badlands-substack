# X22 Report

The **X22 Report** is an alternative-media podcast and video program hosted by a commentator known publicly as "Dave," distributed on platforms including Rumble, and focused on economic analysis and political commentary from a pro-Trump, anon-adjacent perspective.[1] Episodes are numbered and typically split into an "a" segment covering economics and a "b" segment covering geopolitics.[1]

## Digital wallet commentary

In episode 2949a, titled "The Economic Transition Has Begun, How Do You Introduce Digital Wallets?", Dave argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[trump-digital-trading-cards|Trump Digital Trading Cards]] NFT launch functioned as an introduction to and normalization of [[digital-wallets|digital wallets]] for the general public, saying, "I do believe this is the beginning of the transition into a different currency, and he's bringing the people with him."[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] cited the episode as independent corroboration of the thesis she had begun outlining hours earlier, and stated her agreement with Dave's reading.[1]
