# Lawrence Dunegan

**Dr. Lawrence Dunegan, M.D.** was the physician who attended a 1969 meeting of pediatric doctors in Pittsburgh and later publicized what was said there.[1]

The speaker at that meeting was Dr. [[richard-day|Richard Day]], national medical director of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], then funded by the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]]. Dunegan reported that Day predicted the future creation of hard-to-cure diseases and claimed that cures for nearly all cancers had been developed but were being withheld at the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] so that populations would not increase.[1]

Dunegan's recollection is one of the central pieces of testimony Badlands Media cites for its argument that population control, rather than public benefit, drove twentieth-century medical philanthropy.[1]
