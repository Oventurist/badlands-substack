# Coalition Provisional Authority

The Coalition Provisional Authority (CPA) was the American-run governing body in occupied Iraq, and the entity through which the cash flown from the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Federal Reserve]] to Baghdad was received.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

According to the CNBC reporting quoted in the article, a "previously unknown" CPA official was tasked with picking up the bales of billions as they were unloaded from C-17s and arranging for them to reach the [[central-bank-of-iraq|Central Bank of Iraq]] in downtown Baghdad.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The journey ran roughly seven miles over a road the U.S. military called "Route Irish," through territory frequently controlled by insurgents and exposed to rocket-propelled grenades, mortars, car bombs and IEDs — so dangerous that returning American GIs posted YouTube videos of the trip for bragging rights.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The U.S. military did not participate in this leg.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The official was described as a stocky, middle-aged naturalised American citizen of Lebanese descent born in Saudi Arabia, identified only by the first name Basel at his own request.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) He ferried cash in Baghdad for the CPA and the American embassy from 2003 until 2008, handling by his own account about $40 billion, making him the last American to see the money before it disappeared into the vaults of the Central Bank of Iraq and possibly the only person ever to oversee the movement of $40 billion in a combat zone.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) CNBC noted that no one in the U.S. government appears to have planned to put so much responsibility — and temptation — into the hands of just one man.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The article treats these details as clues rather than colour: Basel's Saudi birth and Lebanese descent mirror the biography of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]], and his prior occupation as a translator, said to have landed the job by being in "the right place at the right time," is presented as implausible.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) That he never lost a single load along one of Iraq's deadliest roads without military escort is read as evidence of protection by a powerful patron.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) CNBC also reported that Basel was planning a trip to Sudan and negotiating a contract to transport billions in cash into newly liberated Libya — an article published on 25 October 2011, five days after [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] was killed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)
