# Fidesz

Fidesz is the Hungarian governing party led by [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] for the sixteen years preceding the 2026 parliamentary election.[1]

In that election Fidesz was defeated by [[peter-magyar|Péter Magyar]]'s [[tisza-party|Tisza]] party, which led roughly 52% to 39% at the time Orbán conceded and congratulated Magyar.[1] The party's tenure in government had been marked by repeated disputes with the [[european-union|European Union]] over Russia policy, sanctions, and military support for [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Ahead of the vote, Orbán received public backing from Czech politician Andrej Babiš and Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]].[1]
