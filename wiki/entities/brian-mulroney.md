# Brian Mulroney

Brian Mulroney was the Prime Minister of Canada who, together with U.S. President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and Mexican President [[carlos-salinas-de-gortari|Carlos Salinas de Gortari]], signed the North American Free Trade Agreement in December 1992.[1] He appears in the Badlands Media account chiefly as the third signatory of the deal, with the article's attention directed at the U.S.–Mexico side of the arrangement.[1]

The article notes that Canadian public opinion, like American public opinion, was "never informed" of the Salinas presidency's documented links to organized crime, because Washington was determined not to jeopardize the agreement's approval.[1] See [[nafta]].
