# Texas Department of Public Safety

The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) is the state law-enforcement agency of Texas, active in border enforcement operations along the Rio Grande.[1]

## El Paso border riot arrests (2024)

Following a stampede that overwhelmed [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops along the Rio Grande, DPS booked about 220 people on riot charges in El Paso.[1] Of those, 39 were processed for release to [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and remained jailed on immigration detainers.[1] On March 31, 2024, magistrate judge [[humberto-acosta|Humberto Acosta]] ordered all rioting participation cases released on their own recognizance.[1] See [[el-paso-border-riot-case]].[1]
