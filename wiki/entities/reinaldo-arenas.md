# Reinaldo Arenas

Reinaldo Arenas was a Cuban writer and dissident whose memoir *Antes Que Anochezca* ("Before Night Falls") is quoted as the epigraph of the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1."[1]

The quoted passage contrasts communist and capitalist systems: "The difference between the communist system and the capitalist one is that both kick you in the ass, but in the communist one they kick you and you have to applaud, while in the capitalist one, they kick you and you can scream. I came here to scream."[1] The essay uses the line to frame its argument that the decisive difference between [[cuba|Cuba]] and the United States is not material comfort but the freedom to voice dissent — a freedom the article says Cubans lack under [[communism|communism]].[1]
