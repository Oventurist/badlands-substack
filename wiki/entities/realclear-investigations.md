# RealClear Investigations

RealClear Investigations is the long-form investigative reporting arm of the RealClear media group. It has published extensively on the origins of the Russia investigation and on mainstream media coverage of that story.[1]

## Coverage of the Durham probe

On February 15, 2023, RealClear Investigations published [[aaron-mate|Aaron Maté]]'s article "Unchastened by Russiagate, the NY Times Doubles Down in Its Special Counsel Coverage," which scrutinized the [[new-york-times|New York Times]]' claim that Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] had "come up empty" before issuing his final report.[1] The piece argued the Times had omitted countervailing information, distorted available facts, and sought to discredit the Durham probe in advance while whitewashing key [[fbi|FBI]] and Clinton campaign figures in the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] story.[1]

The article was highlighted in the February 16, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] recommended it alongside his own Substack analysis.[1]
