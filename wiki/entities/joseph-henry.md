# Joseph Henry

Joseph Henry was an American scientist described in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the father of electromagnetism in America, and depicted with his elbow near a pillar at the rear of Christian Schussele's 1862 group portrait [[men-of-progress|*Men of Progress*]].[1]

## Role in the telegraph

Henry was among the collaborators without whom [[samuel-morse|Samuel Morse]]'s telegraph would not have existed, working within a network that also included [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]]'s grandson Alexander Dallas Bache, Alexander von Humboldt, and the European scientists Carl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber.[1] The experiments of Gauss, Weber, Henry and Morse led rapidly to the development of the telegraph, which was applied first in the United States and then spread across Europe, Canada and beyond, so that by the Civil War telegraph lines crossed the United States, Europe and the Atlantic itself.[1]
