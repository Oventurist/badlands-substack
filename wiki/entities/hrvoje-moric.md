# Hrvoje Moric

Hrvoje Moric is the host of the Geopolitics & Empire interview program, described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a man he likes very much and considers a friend.[1] Moric hosted the November 2025 interview with [[peter-duke|Peter Duke]] titled "Who Rules the World and…" which prompted Ehret's essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded," published by Badlands Media on November 15, 2025.[1]

Ehret's critique was directed principally at positions advanced by Duke during the interview, but he attributed to both men the concluding thesis that the [[brics|BRICS]] / multipolar alliance is simply a different flavor of totalitarian evil — a view Ehret set out to refute by distinguishing the "lower realm" of mechanisms and banking arrangements from the higher realm of [[universal-principles|universal principles]].[1] Ehret also noted the parts of the interview he approved of, including Duke's appreciation for Executive Intelligence Review research on Lord Palmerston's Zoo and for [[epistemological-warfare|epistemological warfare]] as the decisive battleground of history.[1]
