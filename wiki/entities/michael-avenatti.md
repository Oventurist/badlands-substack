# Michael Avenatti

**Michael Avenatti** is an American lawyer who rose to national prominence as the attorney for adult film performer [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]] during her legal conflict with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and who was subsequently convicted of criminal offenses and disgraced.[1]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of April 7, 2023, the analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] pointed to Avenatti as a cautionary precedent while assessing Daniels's stated willingness to testify in the [[trump-hush-money-case|Trump hush money case]].[1] Burning Bright suggested readers review Avenatti's interviews from the period leading up to the previous attempt to use Daniels against Trump, and consider how that effort ultimately turned out for him.[1] The argument formed part of a broader claim that convictions in Daniels's orbit have fallen on figures aligned against Trump rather than with him.[1]
