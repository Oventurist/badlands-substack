# Manhattan District Attorney's Office

The Manhattan District Attorney's Office is the New York County prosecuting authority.[1]

On April 11, 2026 the office opened a criminal investigation into an allegation that Democratic Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] sexually assaulted a former staffer in New York in 2024.[1] The office was reported to be already investigating prior assault claims against Swalwell at the time the broader wave of accusations broke.[1] Swalwell denied all claims, and the allegations remain unproven in court.[1]

## The purported exculpatory FEC letter (2023)

In March 2023, as the case moved toward an expected indictment, a purported 2018 letter from a lawyer for [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] to the [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] surfaced stating that Cohen had paid Daniels from his own personal funds and that the Trump campaign was not a party to the transaction and did not reimburse him.[2] Reporting described the document as "totally exculpatory" and as capable of hobbling the DA's case.[2] An arrest widely expected on Tuesday, March 21, 2023 did not occur.[2]

Badlands commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described the office as a "blatantly corrupt Soros-backed DA" presiding over the most crime-ridden areas in the nation, argued that the underlying allegations had already failed in court — Michael Avenatti went to prison and Daniels was ordered to pay some $300,000 in legal fees — and concluded that prosecutors merely wanted "the show and the mug shot," a [[nancy-pelosi|Pelosi]]-style "wrap-up smear" designed to entrap reactionary Trump supporters.[2]

### Trump declares the case "DEAD"

By the weekend of March 25–26, 2023, [[donald-trump|Trump]] was publicly declaring the office's investigation finished, writing that "The Manhattan D.A. Witch Hunt against me is DEAD, no evidence at all, & it has been conclusively proven that I did nothing wrong!"[4] He suggested the office had been tricked by its "star" witness [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] — a disbarred lawyer and convicted felon — and said the only remaining question was whether the DA's office would sue Cohen for lying and fraud.[4] Speaking to reporters after the [[2023-waco-rally|Waco rally]], Trump said, "I think they've already dropped the case … they have absolutely nothing," calling it "a fake case."[4]

## Prosecution of Daniel Penny (2023)

On Thursday, May 11, 2023, the office confirmed that former Marine [[daniel-penny|Daniel Penny]] would be charged with second-degree manslaughter over the subway chokehold death of [[jordan-neely|Jordan Neely]], with Penny expected to surrender to authorities the following morning.[3] Legal commentary at the time anticipated that District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] would punt the charging decision to a grand jury.[3] A witness aboard the F train told the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] that Neely had been threatening passengers before the intervention, and said she was "praying" that Penny "gets treated fairly."[3] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the prosecution ignored exculpatory evidence and predicted that the case would end either in a wrongful conviction or in citywide unrest following an acquittal.[3]
