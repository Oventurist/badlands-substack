# Equalize

Equalize is a Milan-based Italian intelligence and investigations firm, staffed in part by former Italian intelligence operatives, that became the center of a major hacking scandal disclosed in late 2024.[1] Four people in Milan were arrested on November 1, 2024, over an alleged scheme to breach Italian government servers and assemble compromising material on Italian officials.[1]

## The hacking network

Reporting indicated that Equalize allegedly hacked information on thousands of people, including politicians, entrepreneurs, athletes and musicians, and that the firm is accused of working for Israeli intelligence and the [[vatican|Vatican]], according to police wiretaps leaked to Italian media.[1] Politico reported that members of the network — including Nunzio Samuele Calamucci, described by prosecutors as the orchestrator of the scheme — met two Israeli agents at the firm's Milan office in February 2023 to discuss a job worth €1 million.[1]

The task described in the leaked wiretaps was a cyber operation against Russian targets, including an unidentified "right-hand man" of President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], and tracing the financial trail running from the bank accounts of wealthy figures to the Russian mercenary outfit [[wagner-group|Wagner]].[1] The resulting information was reportedly to be passed on to the Vatican, which accounts positioned as a middleman in the arrangement.[1] In exchange, [[mossad|Mossad]] was said to be offering blackmail material concerning Qatar, Iran, and Italian gas companies.[1]
