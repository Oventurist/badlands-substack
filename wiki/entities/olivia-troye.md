# Olivia Troye

Olivia Troye is a former Trump administration White House staffer who later became a public critic of Donald Trump. In Badlands Media commentary she is presented as one of the internal "moles" whose accounts of the first Trump term are treated as fabrications rather than testimony.[1]

## Portrayal in Badlands Media commentary

In the March 2024 article "The CIA is Being Thrown Under the Bus," Erik Carlson lists Troye alongside Alexander Vindman and John Kelly as examples of hostile insiders who, in his telling, sabotaged or misrepresented the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration from within. Carlson characterizes Troye as having lied about Trump treating women badly in the White House, and complains that such claims are still widely believed.[1]

The reference to Troye appears in a broader argument that a second Trump term would be staffed differently: with what Carlson calls the moles removed, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] would no longer be able to control America from inside its own government and agencies, and would instead be forced to fight Trump from outside through NATO, the United Nations and the European Union.[1]

Her inclusion in the article is illustrative rather than biographical — the piece offers no account of her career, only her role as an alleged unreliable narrator of the Trump White House.[1]
