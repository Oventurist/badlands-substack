# Eli Pariser

Eli Pariser is an American political and internet activist best known for leading [[moveon-org|MoveOn PAC]], one of the organizations Badlands Media places inside what it terms the [[george-soros|George Soros]] "shadow network."[1]

## The 2004 declaration

On 9 December 2004, following an election cycle in which a cadre of leftist billionaires poured over $300 million into the Democratic war chest, Pariser boasted to MoveOn's members: "Now it's our party. We bought it, we own it."[1] Badlands cites this line as the plainest available admission that outside money had purchased operational control of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic Party]], and notes that Pariser is a Soros confidant.[1]

## Associations

The article links Pariser to [[zack-exley|Zack Exley]], described as a friend and fellow shadow-network operative who served as MoveOn.org's organizing director before co-founding the [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]] PAC.[1] In the article's schema, figures like Pariser occupy the operational tier of a structure whose financing sits with Soros and allied foundations, and whose output is candidate selection ahead of any popular vote.[1]

## Assessment

The characterisation of Pariser as a network operative rather than an independent organizer reflects the contributor's thesis about [[uniparty|Uniparty]] control of both major parties; the quoted 2004 remark is documented, while the broader coordination claim is interpretive.[1]
