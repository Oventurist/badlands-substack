# Support Group for Islam and Muslims (JNIM)

The Support Group for Islam and Muslims, known by its French-derived acronym JNIM, is a jihadist coalition operating in the Sahel.[1] Senegal's foreign ministry identified JNIM members, alongside Tuareg rebels, as the perpetrators of an attack carried out between 25 and 27 July 2024 in northern [[mali|Mali]] against the Armed Forces of Mali (FAMA), an assault it said produced significant civilian casualties.[1]

The same convoy carried contractors from the Russian [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]], dozens of whom were killed.[1] Ukraine's [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|GUR]] subsequently claimed a role in planning the ambush, prompting [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] to accuse Kiev of justifying terrorism.[1] Badlands commentary described the episode as evidence of a proxy war in which Western funds flow to al-Qaeda- and ISIS-linked militancy in Africa.[1]
