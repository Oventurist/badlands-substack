# Kirsty Coventry

Kirsty Coventry is the President of the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]] (IOC).[1]

## Female category policy

In March 2026, Coventry presided over the IOC's approval of a new policy restricting eligibility for women's events at Olympic competition to biological females, effective with the 2028 Los Angeles Games.[1] She said the decision was based on scientific and medical guidance and was motivated by competitive fairness and athlete safety.[1]

Under the policy, eligibility is determined by a one-time screening for the SRY gene, with athletes testing negative qualifying for the female category; the IOC stated the measure protects fairness, safety, and integrity in women's competition and would not be applied retroactively.[1] Athletes testing positive for the SRY gene, including transgender and certain intersex competitors, are ineligible for women's events but may compete in male or open divisions, with limited exceptions for conditions such as Complete Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the ruling as "a win for women," writing that athletes with Y chromosomes are no longer permitted in women's Olympic sport and reading the decision as evidence that "sanity seems to be returning to the collective mind," while arguing that the wider culture war over pediatric gender medicine remains unresolved.[1]
