# Benjamin Song

Benjamin Song is one of nine defendants convicted by a federal jury in Fort Worth, Texas for roles in the July 4, 2025 attack outside the [[prairieland-detention-center|Prairieland ICE detention center]] in Alvarado, Texas.

As reported in the March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief, drawing on the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], Song was convicted of attempted murder and firearms offenses arising from the shooting of Alvarado Police Lieutenant Thomas Gross, who was wounded during the confrontation. Prosecutors described the defendants as members of a "North Texas [[antifa|Antifa]] cell" involved in rioting, explosives offenses and providing material support to terrorists. Sentencing had not been announced at the time of reporting.
