# Unjected

**Unjected** is a Hawaii-based online platform, described in February 2023 reporting as the "world's first" dating service for unvaccinated people, associated with co-founders identified in the reporting as Thomson and Pyle.[1]

## Removal from app stores

Soon after the app's launch, Thomson and Pyle began receiving hate emails followed by negative publicity in the media.[1] [[apple|Apple]] decided to remove Unjected from its app store, claiming the service provided medical disinformation.[1] Thomson recalled that the founders had "tailored everything and played this chess game until it [met] Apple standards," but that it still was not enough, adding: "It took us until July 31 to get banned."[1] When [[google|Google]] threatened to follow suit, the founders pulled the plug on both media giants and took Unjected.com live using the web domain host GoDaddy in August 2021.[1] Thomson framed the episode as a break with the technology industry: "We decided—OK—the big-tech world is not our friend. They don't want us to exist in this realm. They'll always go out of their way to ensure we're censored or taken down."[1]

## Scope of the service

Thomson said Unjected is more than a dating service for the unvaccinated: it also operates as a blood bank database and a fertility bank for the unvaccinated.[1] The dating service alone was reported to have 110,000 subscriptions across 85 countries, adding 3,000 to 5,000 new clients per month.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] placed the venture in the frame of tribalism, arguing that for most of human history tribalism divided people along lines of belief and value, and that although it is now regarded as bigoted it served a valid purpose.[1] He observed that social psychology holds that human connection arises from common ideas and values, with physical proximity and emotional charge enabling that conceptually based connection — factors he said were undoubtedly influencing the formation of an unvaccinated dating service.[1] Deschamps further asserted a "clear and present danger" from mRNA vaccination, noting the belief among some that changes to a person's biome would be passed to their progeny, and asked whether unvaccinated blood and people would become a hot commodity in the decades to come or whether globalists would instead label such people enemies of the state.[1]
