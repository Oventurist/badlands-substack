# Will Sommer

Will Sommer is a journalist and the author of "Trust the Plan," a book about the [[qanon|QAnon]] phenomenon.[1]

## PBS News Hour appearance

In August 2025 Sommer discussed [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s relationship with QAnon in a PBS News Hour segment with William Brangham.[1] The segment characterized Trump as having a habit of courting unfounded conspiracies, including QAnon — described as the belief that a hidden figure in government is explaining how Trump is waging a secret battle against dark, nefarious forces — and argued that his amplification of the movement had fuelled speculation about the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] case.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright responded to the segment by reframing the premise: "Maybe this wasn't about waking EVERYBODY up. Maybe this was about waking SOME people up, so that those people could go forward."[1] He described the "Frame & Support" refrain as synonymous with the Truth Community, meaning different things to different people, and discussed it with Alpha Warrior on episode 32 of The Narrative.[1]
