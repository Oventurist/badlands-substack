# Wael Jalaidan

Wael Jalaidan is a Saudi figure identified in the Badlands account as a co-founder of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and a product of the American campus network run by the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] (MSA).1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## MSA background

During the 1980s Jalaidan ran the MSA chapter at the University of Arizona.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He subsequently went on to help Osama bin Laden found Al Qaeda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The article cites him alongside [[anwar-al-awlaki|Anwar al-Awlaki]] as evidence that the MSA's hundreds of North American chapters have served as incubators for Islamic supremacist ideology rather than as ordinary cultural fraternities.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Rabita Trust

Jalaidan partnered with [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] — the patron who recruited the Abedin family to Jeddah — to establish the [[rabita-trust|Rabita Trust]], an organization formally designated as a terrorist entity under United States law because of its funding of Al Qaeda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The article notes that the Rabita Trust was also a Pakistani organization, a detail it links to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s Pakistani family background.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Significance

Jalaidan's trajectory is used in the article to connect the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s university infrastructure directly to terror financing, supporting the broader claim that the [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization/stealth jihad]] strategy pursued infiltration of Western institutions and violent jihad through the same networks and the same personnel.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
