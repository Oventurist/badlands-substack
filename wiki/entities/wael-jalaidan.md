# Wael Jalaidan

Wael Jalaidan is described in the Badlands corpus as a co-founder of [[al-qaeda|al-Qaeda]] and a former campus Islamic organizer in the United States.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]], quoting PJMedia, states that in the 1980s Jalaidan ran the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at the University of Arizona, and that he "would soon go on to help [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found al-Qaeda." He is also said to have partnered with [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] — the patron of the [[huma-abedin|Abedin]] family — to establish the [[rabita-trust|Rabita Trust]], which was formally designated a terrorist organization under U.S. law for funding al-Qaeda.

Jalaidan appears in the article alongside [[anwar-al-awlaki|Anwar al-Awlaki]] as evidence for the claim, attributed to Patrick Poole, that al-Awlaki "is far from the only jihadist to hone his supremacist ideology in the MSA's friendly confines."
