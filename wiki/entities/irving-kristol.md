# Irving Kristol

Irving Kristol was an American writer and editor counted among the network of former devotees of Trotsky's brand of socialism with permanent revolution characteristics who became a driving nexus within the imperial intelligentsia of the west.[1] The article groups him with [[james-burnham|James Burnham]], [[albert-wohlstetter|Albert Wohlstetter]] and [[richard-perle|Richard Perle]] in this lineage.[1]

These ideologues, the account argues, simply did not find the switch to neoconservatism very difficult after Trotsky's plans to take control of Russia failed by 1940.[1] [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]] and [[elizabeth-gould|Elizabeth Gould]] write that the [[team-b|Team B]] approach was "developed by an inbred class of former Trotskyist intellectuals" and represented a radical transformation of America's national security bureaucracy into a new kind of elitist cult.[1]
