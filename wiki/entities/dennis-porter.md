# Dennis Porter

Dennis Porter is the co-founder and chief executive officer of the [[satoshi-action-fund|Satoshi Action Fund]], a Bitcoin policy advocacy organization.[1]

## 2024 strategic reserve claim

In July 2024 Porter said that "credible" sources had told him [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could announce [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] as a "strategic reserve asset" during a Bitcoin conference in Nashville later that month.[1] Writing on X, Porter argued that "adding #Bitcoin as a 'strategic reserve' to the US Treasury is a no brainer and once the USA does it, the paradigm will shift and the world will understand they must also have a #Bitcoin position."[1] The report, carried by Investing.com, was described as a potential "watershed moment" and fed expectations of a [[us-bitcoin-strategic-reserve|US Bitcoin strategic reserve]].[1]
