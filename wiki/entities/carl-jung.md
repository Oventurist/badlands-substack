# Carl Jung

**Carl Jung** is the psychologist whose concept of the "shadow" is invoked in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

The essay describes the shadow as the repressed and often disowned parts of collective consciousness, holding unresolved fears, anxieties, and suppressed truths that remain hidden beneath societal norms.[1] It argues that GDS capitalizes on this repression by ensuring the shadow stays unexamined, which makes individuals and communities more susceptible to manipulation.[1] By diverting attention away from these unconscious drivers, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is said to keep populations fragmented, reactive, and easily influenced by external control mechanisms, compounding the effects of the unresolved [[intergenerational-trauma|intergenerational trauma]] described elsewhere in the piece.[1]
