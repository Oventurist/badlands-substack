# American Postal Workers Union

The American Postal Workers Union (APWU) is the labor union representing employees of the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]]. Its president, [[mark-dimondstein|Mark Dimondstein]], has publicly campaigned for the restoration and expansion of postal banking.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

## Advocacy for postal banking

Through an opinion piece in The Hill, the union's president argued that the 2023 bank crisis triggered by the failures of Silicon Valley Bank and Signature Bank demonstrated that the U.S. banking system depends entirely on the federal government for deposit insurance, loan guarantees and interest rates, and that debate over banking's future should begin with the history of postal banking.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) The APWU position holds that the [[postal-savings-system|Postal Savings System]], created by an act of Congress in 1910 and operating for 55 years, became one of the largest depository institutions in the country, served as a safe harbor during the Great Depression, and delivered reliable basic banking to rural communities — making postal banking as an essential public service "an old idea whose time has come again."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

Badlands Media cites the union's advocacy as mainstream corroboration that a [[public-banking-option|public banking option]] protected Americans' savings for decades and could do so again, potentially in combination with [[fedaccounts|FedAccounts]] serviced through post office branches.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)
