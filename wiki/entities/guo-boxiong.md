# Guo Boxiong

Guo Boxiong is a former general of the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]] identified in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" among six party leaders removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule for alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

The article names Guo alongside generals [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]], former security chief [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]], [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]] and [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]].[1] It presents such removals as inseparable from Xi's assumption of unprecedented personal authority over the central command structure of the PLA, a degree of control the article says exceeds even that held by [[mao-zedong|Mao Zedong]], who had to share power with revolutionary-era marshals.[1]
