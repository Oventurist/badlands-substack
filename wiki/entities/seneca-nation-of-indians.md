# Seneca Nation of Indians

The Seneca Nation of Indians is a Native American nation whose reservation lies in western New York near the Pennsylvania border.[1]

## 2026 school robot controversy

The [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]], a small rural district located on the Seneca Nation reservation, drew national attention in July 2026 when it approved and then paused a nearly $60,000 purchase of an AI-powered humanoid robot from [[realbotix|Realbotix]] for classroom use. Parents, teachers, and state officials objected over student data privacy and the vendor's ties to a sex-doll manufacturer.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] closed her commentary on the affair with a joke that, with the pilot on hold, "the heterosexual men and boys of the Seneca Nation are ostensibly nursing disappointment," and remarked on the incongruity of such a project being attempted "so close to Amish country."[1]
