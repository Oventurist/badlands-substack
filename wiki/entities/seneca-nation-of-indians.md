# Seneca Nation of Indians

The Seneca Nation of Indians is a federally recognised Native American tribe whose territories lie in western New York State, near the Pennsylvania border. The nation is one of the constituent members of the historic Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) Confederacy and exercises substantial governmental authority over its reservation lands.

The nation features in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] as the location of the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]], whose plan to deploy an AI humanoid robot supplied by [[realbotix|Realbotix]] in its classrooms was paused after community backlash. The Brief emphasised the rural, reservation-based setting of the district in its criticism of the proposal, and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]'s commentary closed the item with a satirical reference to the community's reaction to the pilot's suspension.
