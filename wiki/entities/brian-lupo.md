# Brian Lupo

Brian Lupo is a Badlands contributor associated with election-integrity research, including the Why We Vote catalog documenting irregularities in Georgia.[1]

## Fulton County warrant commentary

In February 2026, after the unsealing of the [[fbi|FBI]] search warrant for [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] election records, Lupo remarked that the warrant reads like a review of the Why We Vote catalog on Georgia — an observation endorsed by fellow contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who noted that the categories cited by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] (missing ballot images, duplicate ballots, inconsistent totals, missing SHA hash files, tabulator tape discrepancies, and broken security seals) matched what Badlands researchers had catalogued for years.[1]

## Response to the November 2025 Epstein document release

Lupo was cited by [[npr|NPR]] in its November 2025 article "Right-Wing Media Shrugs Off Latest Epstein Document Release," which described him as a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] personality who took a slightly different view from colleague [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on his own livestream.[2] Lupo argued that the emails did not exactly say nothing, but that they showed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was informing on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and his associate [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], who is serving a 20-year prison term for helping Epstein sexually abuse underage girls.[2]
