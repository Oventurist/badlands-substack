# Brian Lupo

Brian Lupo is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor associated with the outlet's election-integrity research, particularly the Why We Vote document catalog.

In the February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cites Lupo's observation, made the previous day, that the unsealed federal search warrant for [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] "reads like a review of the Why We Vote catalog on Georgia" — that is, that the categories of records the [[fbi|FBI]] sought closely track the deficiencies independent researchers had catalogued for years. She endorses the assessment, writing "He's right," and adds that this is "what we've been saying on Badlands for literally years."
