# National Institutes of Health

The National Institutes of Health (NIH) is the principal biomedical research agency of the United States federal government, operating within the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] and running intramural laboratories including the Rocky Mountain Laboratory in Montana.

## The 2026 monkeypox smuggling case

The June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief reports as a bonus item that two NIH researchers were charged in a federal criminal complaint with conspiracy to smuggle monkeypox into the United States and making false statements to federal authorities, according to the US Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Michigan. The defendants are [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]], a Netherlands citizen and chief of the Virus Ecology Section at the Rocky Mountain Laboratory, and [[claude-kwe|Claude Kwe]], a Cameroon national and research fellow in Munster's section.

Federal authorities allege the two arrived at Detroit Metropolitan Airport on January 25, 2026 after traveling from the Republic of Congo during a monkeypox outbreak, and told [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] officers that a black case they carried contained diagnostic equipment. Investigators say it instead held 113 vials in Styrofoam coolers, of which 17 tested positive for deactivated monkeypox virus, one for chickenpox virus, and two for human DNA. The case is being investigated by the [[fbi|FBI]], Customs and Border Protection, and the HHS Office of Inspector General, and the men face up to five years in prison if convicted. The episode places NIH intramural research staff at the center of a federal biosecurity prosecution.
