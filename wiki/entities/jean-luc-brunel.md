# Jean-Luc Brunel

**Jean-Luc Brunel** was a French modeling agent whose arrest is cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as part of the wave of trafficking-related prosecutions that followed the [[q-operation|Q]] drops.[1]

## Citation in Chapter 4

Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series discusses drop #29 of November 1, 2017, in which Q is read as reporting that a fair number of trafficking operations had been stopped while others remained ongoing — an implication the author takes to mean that the conflict is continuing and will not cease until all bad actors are in custody.[1] He then directs readers to consider the names arrested after that drop, listing [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]], [[harvey-weinstein|Weinstein]], [[ghislaine-maxwell|Maxwell]], Brunel and Peter Nygard.[1] The chapter presents this sequence as evidence that [[donald-trump|Trump]] pursued such figures more aggressively than any president before him.[1]
