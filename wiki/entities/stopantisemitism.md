# StopAntisemitism

StopAntisemitism is an advocacy group identified by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as the primary moving factor in pressuring employers to fire Americans who expressed dissenting views on Israel's military actions in Gaza.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The group frequently doxxed its targets through its account on X.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Cases DeLarme attributes to this pressure campaign include [[maura-finkelstein|Maura Finkelstein]], a tenured Jewish professor at Muhlenberg College; Hesen Jabr, a nurse at NYU Langone Health fired for comparing the ethnic cleansing of Gaza by Israel to the ethnic cleansing of European Jews by the Nazi party; and Nikki Noor Aytoglu, a dental hygienist terminated after social media posts that labeled Jews "conniving" and suggested Israeli involvement in 9/11.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

[[chaya-raichik|Chaya Raichik]] of Libs of TikTok consistently amplified StopAntisemitism's posts about the firings with comments such as "Consequences for hate are necessary."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) DeLarme notes he is not arguing that employers lack the right to fire such employees; his concern is the ease with which commentators abandoned their stated ethics on [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] once the targets changed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
