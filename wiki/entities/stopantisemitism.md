# StopAntisemitism

StopAntisemitism is an American advocacy group that monitors and publicizes instances of antisemitism, including on social media. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," the organization is identified as the primary pressure group that pushed employers to fire individuals over their statements regarding Israel and the Gaza war.

## Role in the firings of Israel critics

The article states that StopAntisemitism was "the primary moving factor in pressuring the employers to fire the individuals mentioned above," referring to people terminated for dissenting views on Israel's military actions in Gaza, "often doxxing them via StopAntisemitism's X account." The individuals cited include Maura Finkelstein, a tenured Jewish professor at Muhlenberg College; Hesen Jabr, a nurse at NYU Langone Health; and Nikki Noor Aytoglu, a dental hygienist.

## Amplification and criticism

The article reports that [[chaya-raichik|Chaya Raichik]] (Libs of TikTok) consistently amplified StopAntisemitism's posts about the firings — including that of the writer Susan Abulhawa — with comments such as "Consequences for hate are necessary." [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the article's author, describes the group's doxxing of critics as evidence of how right-wing free speech advocates "will easily abandon their ethics and stance on an issue when the tables are turned."
