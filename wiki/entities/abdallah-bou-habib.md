# Abdallah Bou Habib

Abdallah Bou Habib is the Lebanese Foreign Minister who publicly criticized US handling of the escalating 2024 crisis between Israel and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[1]

## Response to Biden's UN address

On Tuesday, September 24, 2024, Bou Habib voiced disappointment with remarks by US President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on the escalating conflict, while saying he still hoped Washington could intervene to help.[1] Of Biden's speech to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] General Assembly earlier that day he said: "It was not strong. It is not promising and it would not solve this problem."[1]

Speaking in New York during a virtual event hosted by the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment for International Peace]], he added: "I (am) still hoping. The United States is the only country that can really make a difference in the Middle East and with regard to Lebanon."[1] He estimated that half a million people had been displaced inside Lebanon, and said Lebanon's prime minister hoped to meet US officials over the following two days.[1]

The remarks came as [[israel|Israel]], after almost a year of war against [[hamas|Hamas]] in [[gaza|Gaza]], shifted focus to its northern frontier with Lebanon, where [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] had been firing rockets into Israel in support of its ally.[1] In his address, Biden sought to calm tensions, telling the 193-member General Assembly that full-scale war was not in anyone's interest and that a diplomatic solution remained possible.[1]
