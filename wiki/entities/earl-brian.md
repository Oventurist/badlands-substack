# Earl Brian

Earl Brian is an American businessman and former [[cia|CIA]] covert operations officer who introduced the [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to the Israeli spymaster [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]].[1]

## Business empire and the Meese connection

By 1982 Brian had left CIA covert work and was building a business empire in which the wife of then-Attorney General [[edwin-meese-iii|Edwin Meese III]] was a major investor — a coincidence Badlands Media flags with the remark, "It's a small world after all."[1]

## The trapdoor plan

Brian and Eitan, then head of [[lekem|Lekem]], the Israeli Bureau of Scientific Relations, conceived the plan to install a "trapdoor" or backdoor in PROMIS.[1] The scheme envisioned selling the bugged software worldwide, providing Israel and allied elements of the U.S. security state with a digital peephole into the affairs of enemies and allies while generating substantial profits.[1] The article notes, as a hypothetical, that such software could also help those with access to circumvent authorities investigating financial crimes.[1]

## Failure and replacement by Maxwell

The Eitan–Brian–Meese collaboration did not go well and led to the bankruptcy of [[inslaw|Inslaw]], the software's developer.[1] Displeased with Brian and Meese, Eitan turned to the man he believed could most effectively promote the software: the media mogul [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]], whose marketing effort proved far more effective.[1]
