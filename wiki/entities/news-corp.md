# News Corp

**News Corp** is the publishing arm of the media empire built by [[rupert-murdoch|Rupert Murdoch]], operating alongside the separately listed [[fox-corporation|Fox Corp.]] under the control of the Murdoch family.[1]

## 2023 leadership transition

On September 21, 2023, News Corp. and Fox Corp. jointly announced that Rupert Murdoch, then 92 years old, would step down as chairman of both boards, with the change taking effect in November.[1] Murdoch was to be appointed chairman emeritus of each company, while his son [[lachlan-murdoch|Lachlan Murdoch]] would become sole chairman of News Corp. and continue as Fox Corp.'s executive chair and chief executive.[1]

In a note to employees announcing the transition, the elder Murdoch said the companies were "in robust health," expressed optimism about the coming years, and framed the present moment in terms of an intensifying "battle for the freedom of speech and, ultimately, the freedom of thought."[1] The announcement came after a tumultuous year at the group's television operations, shortly after Fox agreed to a $787.5 million settlement with [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] over defamation claims tied to 2020 election coverage.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the reorganization at the top of one of the world's largest media groups as significant given its timing ahead of trials involving [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the 2020 election.[1]
