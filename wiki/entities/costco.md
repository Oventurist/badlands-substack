# Costco

Costco is a large publicly traded American warehouse retailer that sells both groceries and general merchandise.[1]

## Role during the COVID lockdowns

In a February 2023 Badlands Media commentary, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited Costco alongside [[target|Target]] and [[walmart|Walmart]] as examples of big corporate stores that were permitted to remain open during the [[covid-19-lockdowns|COVID lockdowns]] because they sold groceries, while most retail stores were shut down.[1] Because grocery sales qualified them as essential, these chains could continue selling non-grocery retail goods as well.[1]

Carlson used this asymmetry to illustrate the transfer of market share from private small businesses to publicly traded corporations: a shopper could not enter a small appliance store to buy a vacuum, but could buy one at Costco even without purchasing groceries.[1] He argued this dynamic was one mechanism by which the 0.1% of American businesses represented on the stock market "cannibalized" the other 99.9%.[1]
