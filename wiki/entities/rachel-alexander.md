# Rachel Alexander

Rachel Alexander is an attorney and independent journalist who covers American election law and election-integrity litigation.[1] Badlands Media described her as "one attorney and independent journalist that does know what she's talking about," contrasting her research with commentators who repeat the claim that Donald Trump lost every 2020 election lawsuit without examining the underlying dockets.[1]

## Index of 2020 election cases

Alexander published an index of the lawsuits arising from the 2020 United States presidential election, distributed through her account on X (formerly Twitter).[1] The index drew on the extensive case report compiled by [[election-integrity-info|ElectionIntegrity.Info]] and on case summaries produced by the [[healthy-elections-project|Healthy Elections Project]], a joint Stanford–MIT effort.[1]

Her tabulation counted 92 lawsuits dealing specifically with the 2020 election. Of those, 30 were decided on the merits and 62 were disposed of on procedural or other defects such as standing, jurisdiction, timeliness, laches, or public-health considerations.[1] In the 30 merits decisions, Trump and other plaintiffs aligned with the election-integrity movement prevailed in 22 — a 73 percent success rate when evidence was actually heard by a court.[1]

Within her index Alexander quoted attorney [[robert-barnes|Robert Barnes]]'s summary of how courts handled 2020 challenges: "Not Ripe in Spring, No Standing by Summer, Laches by Fall, and Moot by Winter."[1]

## Association with Badlands Media

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted Alexander's research as "an invaluable resource" and announced that she would join the program [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] to discuss the case index and her other work.[1] The Badlands article built on her data as the factual basis for its argument that the "Trump lost all his cases" line is a media fabrication rather than a description of the litigation record.[1]
