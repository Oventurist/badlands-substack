# Wang Wenbin

Wang Wenbin is a spokesman for the Foreign Ministry of the People's Republic of China who confirmed high-level China–U.S. economic contacts in November 2023.[1]

## November 2023 confirmation of the He Lifeng visit

On November 6, 2023, Wang confirmed that Chinese Vice Premier [[he-lifeng|He Lifeng]] would visit the United States from November 8 to November 12, a trip that included economic talks with U.S. Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] on November 9 and 10.[1] The meetings preceded the [[asia-pacific-economic-cooperation|Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation]] summit in San Francisco and a potential meeting between the leaders of the world's two largest economies.[1] Chinese state media had shortly beforehand revealed that He had taken charge of two Chinese Communist Party committees created by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in March 2023 as part of a move to bring the financial system under the Party's direct control.[1]

## Balloon dispute (February 2023)

During the [[chinese-spy-balloon-incident-2023|2023 balloon controversy]], Wang told reporters on February 13 that "since last year alone, U.S. high-altitude balloons have illegally flown over China's airspace more than ten times without the approval of relevant Chinese authorities."[2] He said the United States "should change its course and reflect on itself, instead of smearing and accusing China," and reiterated Beijing's position that Washington had overreacted in shooting down a Chinese aircraft.[2] The remarks came after the US military downed four suspicious aerial objects in the space of a month, with American officials maintaining that at least one was part of a Chinese surveillance operation.[2]
