# Cho Tae-yul

Cho Tae-yul is the Foreign Minister of [[south-korea|South Korea]].[1]

Addressing a forum on security and foreign affairs on 23 April 2025, Cho argued that Seoul's engagement with [[china|China]] aligned with United States interests by contributing to regional stability.[1] He said South Korea would "continue to work toward a healthy and balanced relationship with China on the basis of mutual respect, reciprocity, and shared interests," while adding that "in areas where we see things differently, for example, in the South China Sea and the Yellow Sea, we will certainly continue to speak with candor and disagree."[1]

At the same forum former United States Deputy Secretary of State Kurt Campbell warned that confronting the China challenge single-handedly was "a recipe for failure," describing Washington's ability to work with allies and partners as its "most significant ticket to the big game."[1]
