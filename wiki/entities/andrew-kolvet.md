# Andrew Kolvet

**Andrew Kolvet** is a spokesman for [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] and the executive producer of *The Charlie Kirk Show*.[1]

## Confirmation of the Kirk donor messages

In October 2025, after commentator [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] published text messages attributed to [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] from two days before his assassination, Kolvet publicly confirmed that the messages were genuine.[1] He said he had not published them himself because they were private chats, but that he had informed government officials about them.[1] Kolvet also rejected the suggestion, which he attributed to Owens, that Turning Point does not care about why Kirk was killed.[1]

According to Kolvet, the exchange was a group text that included both Jewish and non-Jewish donors to Turning Point USA, in which one participant responded to Kirk's threat to invite Owens to speak at the organization's next conference with the remark, "That nasty free will thing that God bestowed upon us makes life frustrating sometimes..."[1] Kolvet additionally confirmed that Kirk had written a letter to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] warning that he was losing the public battle in the United States, and stated that Kirk was not someone who could be controlled.[1]

Timcast reported the confirmation in the terms that Kolvet, "Executive Producer of The Charlie Kirk Show, has confirmed that the text messages shared by Candace Owens about Charlie Kirk's evolving views on Israel are indeed authentic."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] dismissed the chain of confirmations as "a Regime operator says that another Regime operator says that another Regime operator read the authentic texts of another Regime operator," characterizing the affair as "a game of telephone featuring gossip from and about TV Characters" in which each layer added "a new psyop purveyor purveying a different version of the psyop."[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], by contrast, treated the confirmed messages as "very damning" evidence bearing on Kirk's death.[1]
