# Bob Kent

Bob Kent is an Air Force intelligence officer who attempted to organize the rescue of retired FBI agent [[bob-levinson|Bob Levinson]], the CIA-contracted operative captured in Iran in 2007.[1]

Kent recruited former federal prosecutor [[david-mcgee|David McGee]] into the effort, and contracted with Russian oligarch [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]] to pay the mission's $20 million cost in exchange for expedited green cards for Deripaska's family.[1] The FBI point man on the arrangement was [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]].[1] The rescue, planned during President Obama's first term, was called off at the last moment when the Clinton [[state-department|State Department]] became spooked.[1]

In 2021 Kent concluded that Levinson was still alive and returned to McGee with the idea of extorting Don Gaetz — father of Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] — for $25 million to finance a renewed rescue operation, a scheme that ultimately produced McGee's indictment and conviction.[1]
