# Marc Andreessen

Marc Andreessen is an American software engineer, entrepreneur, and venture capitalist. He co-authored the Mosaic web browser and co-founded Netscape before becoming a prominent Silicon Valley investor through the firm Andreessen Horowitz. In recent years he has become an outspoken advocate of accelerated technological development and a critic of regulatory constraints on artificial intelligence and software.

In March 2026 Andreessen was named among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]] by the [[white-house|White House]], as reported in the March 26 Badlands Brief. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] grouped him among the appointees associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]]'s network, a categorization she expected critics of the administration's technology policy to seize upon.
