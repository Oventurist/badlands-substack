# Bonnie Badenoch

**Dr. Bonnie Badenoch** is a therapist and author, described in the April 19, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Remembering: Fractured Selves and the Path Beyond Ego" as the author of *The Heart of Trauma*.[1]

The essay, by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], quotes her definition of integration: "Integration means that each part of us is in communication with every other part, and there is no longer a need to exile any of them for us to feel safe, loved, or whole."[1] The author uses the quotation to anchor the claim that coherence is "not sameness" but "synchronized diversity, a harmony made from difference," and that wholeness requires remembering every part of the self rather than exiling the inconvenient ones.[1] Her formulation sits at the centre of the article's treatment of [[parts-work|parts work]] and [[psychological-integration|psychological integration]].[1]
