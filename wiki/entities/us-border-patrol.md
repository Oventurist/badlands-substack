# United States Border Patrol

The United States Border Patrol is the federal law enforcement component responsible for policing the areas between ports of entry along the American land borders. It operates under [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]].[1]

## 2026 Arivaca shooting

On January 27, 2026, a Border Patrol agent shot and wounded [[patrick-gary-schlegel|Patrick Gary Schlegel]], 34, near Arivaca, Arizona, during an encounter close to the southern border.[1] Authorities said Schlegel fired at agents and at a Customs and Border Protection helicopter before agents returned fire.[1] Schlegel was airlifted to a hospital, underwent surgery, and was later reported in serious but stable condition.[1]

The [[pima-county-sheriffs-department|Pima County Sheriff's Department]] took the lead on a use-of-force investigation at the request of the [[fbi|FBI]], reflecting the standard practice of referring agent-involved shootings to an outside agency.[1] Federal prosecutors said Schlegel was expected to face charges including assault on a federal officer, human smuggling, and being a felon in possession of a firearm.[1]

## Context: assaults on immigration enforcement personnel

Badlands commentators framed the incident as part of a broader pattern of rising assaults against immigration officers and a corresponding increase in officer-involved shootings.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued on Badlands Daily with [[canncon|CannCon]] that agents "regardless of what you think of the agency or the law, are doing their jobs," and characterized organized street opposition to immigration enforcement as a [[color-revolution|Color Revolution]] rather than ordinary protest.[1]

## Killing of Agent David Maland, January 2025

On January 20, 2025 — Inauguration Day — Border Patrol agent [[david-maland|David Maland]], 44, and other agents pulled over 21-year-old [[teresa-youngblut|Teresa "Milo" Youngblut]] and German citizen [[felix-bauckholt|Felix "Ophelia" Bauckholt]] in northern Vermont near the Canadian border.[2] Youngblut opened fire on the agents and a back-and-forth shootout followed, killing Maland and Bauckholt and injuring Youngblut.[2] Youngblut was arrested in Washington state the following week and charged with using a deadly weapon while assaulting a United States Border Patrol agent and with using and discharging a firearm during that assault; a judge ordered her held without bail pending a February 7 preliminary hearing.[2] Federal investigators subsequently expanded their focus to 32-year-old [[michelle-zajko|Michelle Zajko]], who police say purchased the handguns used in the shooting, in a case police have linked to a "trans vegan cult" tied to multiple murders across the United States.[2]

## Reported luggage handling at Yuma (May 2023)

On May 15, 2023, [[real-americas-voice|Real America's Voice]] correspondent [[ben-bergquam|Ben Bergquam]] reported from Yuma, Arizona that Border Patrol agents were picking up tagged bags belonging to illegal border crossers and delivering them to the migrants' destinations, a practice [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] described on air as an "illegal alien concierge service."[3] Bergquam said the migrants he encountered overnight came from Moldova, Egypt and Sudan and that hundreds were streaming into Arizona.[3] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the account to earlier reports of migrants receiving taxpayer-funded cell phones and court dates in the 2030s, arguing the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] had reduced Border Patrol to "badged luggage porters" rather than applying limited resources to border security; she noted as countervailing news that agents had arrested a lone Afghan on the FBI terror watchlist among asylum seekers.[3]
