# Todd Eddins

Todd Eddins is an Associate Justice of the Hawaii Supreme Court, appointed by former Democratic Governor David Ige.[1]

## Criticism of the Supreme Court

In a May 2024 interview with Slate, Eddins criticized the justices of the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]], saying they were "incredibly dishonest about how law and facts are cherry-picked."[1] Newsweek grouped him with retired Judge [[david-tatel|David S. Tatel]] and U.S. District Judge [[carlton-reeves|Carlton Reeves]] as part of a growing chorus of critics of the high court emerging from within the judiciary itself, with both Eddins and Reeves still sitting judges at the time.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted in the Badlands News Brief that the three judges most prominently quoted as aligned against the Supreme Court — Tatel, Eddins, and Reeves — were all Democratic appointees, and urged readers to view the criticism as narrative management within a broader information war rather than as an objective indictment of the Court.[1]
