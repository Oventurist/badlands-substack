# Defense Information Systems Agency

The Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) is a United States Department of Defense combat support agency based in part at [[fort-meade|Fort Meade]], Maryland, composed of military personnel, federal civilians, and contractors.[1]

## Mission

DISA "provides information technology and communications support to the President, Vice President, Secretary of Defense, the military services, the combatant commands, and any individual or system contributing to the defense of the United States."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

In the December 2024 Badlands News Brief, GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited DISA while discussing the Department of Defense's counterinsurgency (COIN) doctrine, characterizing the agency as a body of "information warfare specialists who play supporting roles to the President, Vice President, and combatant commands."[1] The observation was part of a broader argument that Fort Meade concentrates the infrastructure of the information war, alongside the [[national-security-agency|NSA]], [[us-cyber-command|CYBERCOM]], the [[defense-information-school|Defense Information School]], and the [[defense-media-activity|Defense Media Activity]].[1]
