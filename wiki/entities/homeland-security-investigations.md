# Homeland Security Investigations

Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) is the principal investigative arm of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], responsible for criminal investigations spanning immigration, financial crime, and transnational offenses.[1]

## Skid Row operation

HSI personnel were among roughly twenty federal agents who conducted an operation on Los Angeles' Skid Row in June 2026 as part of the [[skid-row-voter-fraud-investigation|Skid Row voter fraud investigation]] into alleged fraud connected to the city's mayoral election and the gubernatorial primary races.[1] Witnesses described the agents, dressed in casual clothing, fanning out across the streets shortly before noon, interviewing dozens of homeless residents, taking notes, and being directed by locals toward specific areas and individuals.[1]

## Operation Metro Surge

HSI agents formed part of the roughly 2,000-strong federal deployment sent to Minneapolis and St. Paul in early January 2026 under [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]], alongside [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] and [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] personnel.[2] The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] framed the surge as an immigration enforcement operation in [[minnesota|Minnesota]], and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] later ordered an additional 1,000 CBP agents to the Twin Cities.[2]

## Sean Combs investigation

In March 2024 HSI agents based in New York led an investigation of hip-hop figure [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]], with Department of Homeland Security personnel searching his properties in Los Angeles and the Miami area on March 25.[3] The New York venue reflected a federal lawsuit filed there in November by a former girlfriend accusing Combs of sex trafficking; Combs denied wrongdoing and said his accusers sought "a quick payday."[3]
