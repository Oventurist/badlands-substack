# Egmont Group

The Egmont Group is an international network of more than 170 financial intelligence units drawn from nearly every government on earth, created so that national financial-crime intelligence bodies can share information with one another.[1] The United States is represented in the network by the [[financial-crimes-enforcement-network|Financial Crimes Enforcement Network]] (FinCEN).[1]

## Colombia dispute

Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] was sanctioned by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Department of the Treasury]] in 2025, and the sanctioning document accused him of causing Colombia's financial crimes intelligence unit to be suspended from the Egmont Group for allegedly sharing information he had collected from Egmont servers.[1]

## Criticism

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that both Petro and Nicolás Maduro have accused the most powerful banks in the United States and Europe of functioning as the cartels themselves, with Petro arguing that institutions such as the Egmont Group allow that criminality to continue and prosper by providing cover for it.[1] The group and FinCEN were the subject of several episodes of the Badlands program *Breaking History*.[1]
