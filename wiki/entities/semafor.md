# Semafor

**Semafor** is an American digital news outlet covering media and politics.[1]

## Coverage of the 2024 Washington Post non-endorsement

In October 2024, Semafor obtained internal reaction from [[washington-post|The Washington Post]] following owner [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]]'s decision not to endorse a presidential candidate.[1] A Post editorial board member told the outlet that "people are shocked, furious, surprised," and described internal discussions involving resignation, adding: "If you don't have the balls to own a newspaper, don't."[1] Semafor's reporting was among the sources aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and cited by Badlands Media in its account of the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|non-endorsement controversy]].[1]
