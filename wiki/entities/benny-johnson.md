# Benny Johnson

Benny Johnson is a prominent conservative media influencer whose coverage of the pre-trial proceedings in the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] case drew sharp criticism from Badlands Media in July 2026.[1]

## Role in the Robinson pre-trial coverage

Following the preliminary hearing in Robinson's prosecution for the killing of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], Johnson was among the prominent "conservative" influencers who left the courtroom telling their audiences that the trial — which had not yet occurred — was effectively over, and that their millions of followers should "pray for a speedy trial" so that [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]] could move on.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized Johnson's appeal as overt emotional blackmail of Christians, noting that he invoked the Bible's command to stand up for widows.[1] In her reading, the implication of Johnson's video was that the Christian thing to do is to rush the judicial process to protect Erika Kirk's feelings, and to condemn a man before his trial because the government has declared him guilty.[1]

Johnson, along with [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]] and [[brandon-tatum|Officer Tatum]], described a very clear video said to prove definitively that the shooter was Robinson.[1] The messaging point was quietly abandoned after Badlands streamer [[canncon|CannCon]] discovered that the supposedly secret video was visible on a defense laptop in the CourtTV feed and was the same footage already publicly available; Johnson and Posobiec did not respond to requests for comment.[1]

## Criticism

Ashe in America argued that Johnson and Posobiec were inverting the Constitution to sell a story, influence potential jurors and generate a public outrage mob, comparing the effort directly to the dynamics of the [[derek-chauvin|Derek Chauvin]] murder trial and describing it as what appeared to be an illegal psychological warfare campaign waged on the American people.[1] She further objected to the framing that Erika Kirk and [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] possess a right to a speedy trial — a protection belonging to the accused — and noted that Utah's victims' rights provisions cannot outweigh federally protected constitutional rights of the defendant.[1]

## The Cohen FEC letter post (2023)

On March 22, 2023 Johnson posted what he called a "bombshell document" destroying the Manhattan DA's case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]: a 2018 letter from [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]]'s lawyer to the [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] declaring that Cohen used his own personal funds to pay [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]], that the Trump campaign was not a party to the transaction and that it did not reimburse Cohen — concluding, "It's OVER."[2] The post drew roughly 11,700 reposts and 31,000 likes and was aggregated by the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as the lead item of the day.[2]

## Tenet Media indictment (2024)

Benny Johnson was named in the federal indictment unsealed on September 4, 2024 among the conservative influencers working with [[tenet-media|Tenet Media]], which prosecutors said received nearly $10 million from employees of [[russia-today|Russia Today]] in a scheme to distribute content carrying hidden Russian government messaging to US audiences.[3] The indictment specifically noted that Johnson, along with [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]], [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] and [[lauren-southern|Lauren Southern]], was unaware of the true source of the money and had been told by founder [[lauren-chen|Lauren Chen]] that it came from a private investor called "Eduard Grigoriann."[3]
