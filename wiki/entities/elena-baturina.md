# Elena Baturina

Elena Baturina is a Russian businesswoman described in congressional testimony and Republican oversight commentary as the wealthiest woman in Russia, worth over $1 billion.[1]

Rep. [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] cited her presence at one of two dinners at the Cafe Milano restaurant in Washington, D.C., attended in full by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], as evidence contradicting Biden's repeated claim that he was not involved in his family's foreign business dealings.[1] Her attendance became a recurring reference point in the [[joe-biden-bribery-allegation|Joe Biden bribery allegation]] pressed by the House Oversight and Judiciary chairmen in mid-2023.[1]

## Appearance in the August 2023 bank records

Detailed bank records released by [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight]] Republicans in August 2023 revealed links between Baturina and the Biden family as part of a broader accounting of roughly $20 million in foreign payments to the family and its partners during [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s vice presidency.[2] Her name appeared alongside disclosures about shell-company transfers involving [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] and about board payments from the Ukrainian oil company [[burisma|Burisma]].[2]
