# Mike Dickson

Mike Dickson was a prominent British sports journalist who worked for the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]], best known in alternative-media commentary for his sustained criticism of Serbian tennis player [[novak-djokovic]] over Djokovic's refusal to take a COVID-19 vaccine.[1]

## Criticism of Novak Djokovic

When Djokovic arrived for the Australian Open in early 2022 without a COVID-19 vaccination, he was removed from the tournament and had his travel visa revoked by Australian authorities, despite having a clean bill of health at the time.[1] Dickson was described as one of the lead instigators of the resulting attempt at cancellation, characterised as relentless in his criticism and reported to have called the player "arrogant and deplorable" for refusing to submit to the vaccination requirement.[1]

## Death

In January 2024, Dickson was reported to have collapsed and "died suddenly" at the age of 59 while covering the Australian Open. The cause of death was kept confidential.[1] Despite Dickson's earlier attempts to portray him as a global villain, Djokovic offered only kind words and condolences upon news of the journalist's passing.[1]

## Reception in alternative media

The death was folded into a broader alternative-media narrative about a pattern of "well-known vaccine shills suddenly dying," which commentators such as [[simon-esler]] argued was becoming difficult to ignore for those sceptical of the official COVID-19 narrative.[1] Esler acknowledged that critics correctly point out that individual causes of death cannot be established from such reports, but defended the use of inductive reasoning — drawing general conclusions from specific observations — as basic situational awareness given what he described as the legacy media's omission of mounting data on rising deaths.[1] The framing appeared in the [[badlands-news-brief]] published by [[badlands-media]], which relayed the original reporting from [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]
