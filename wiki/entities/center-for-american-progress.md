# Center for American Progress

The **Center for American Progress** (CAP) is a Washington, D.C. progressive think tank founded by [[john-podesta|John Podesta]].[1] Podesta ran the organization after leaving the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]] during George W. Bush's first term and before returning to government service in the Obama administration.[1]

## Overlap with the Podesta Group

According to the American Enterprise Institute analysis quoted by Badlands, there was "repeated and significant overlap" between CAP's policy agenda and its donors on one hand, and the Podesta Group's lobbying clients and lobbying issues on the other.[1] Because John Podesta directed CAP while his brother [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]] ran the family lobbying firm, critics viewed the pairing as an integrated influence operation spanning ideas, money and access.[1]

Badlands writer Joe Lange goes further, asserting that CAP was created "as another way to launder taxpayers money for the democrats" — an interpretive claim rather than a documented finding.[1]
