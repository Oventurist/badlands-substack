# Yukos Oil

Yukos Oil was a Russian petroleum company assembled during the privatizations of the 1990s and dismantled in the early 2000s, cited in Badlands commentary as the paradigmatic case of [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s subordination of oligarchic wealth to sovereign will.[1]

## Rise and dismantling

Yukos was once the empire of [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]], one of the tycoons who, under [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]]'s shock-therapy privatizations, acquired state assets at fire-sale prices.[1] According to an October 2025 Badlands News Brief analysis by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], Yukos was dismantled through tax-evasion charges and its assets absorbed by state-controlled [[rosneft|Rosneft]].[1]

## Significance in the Badlands reading

The Yukos case is presented as one element of a broader pattern in which [[gazprom|Gazprom]] reclaimed majority control over natural-gas assets and sectors such as aerospace and nuclear energy saw outright state takeovers, with spoils redistributed to loyalists who bent the knee rather than challenge the Kremlin.[1] In the Badlands framing, this de-privatization was the reclamation of Russia's destiny from Western-backed predators, and it supplies the template that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] are said to be running in inverted form against the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
