# Oleg Deripaska

Oleg Deripaska is a Russian metals and aluminium magnate who has been among the most prominent targets of United States sanctions and criminal enforcement directed at the Russian oligarch class.[1]

## Sanctions and enforcement

Deripaska was identified in Badlands commentary as one of the most significant targets of the 2022–2023 U.S. campaign against smuggling and money laundering networks supporting the Russian regime, alongside [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]] and Andrei Derkach.[1] That campaign, described in reporting by the [[organized-crime-and-corruption-reporting-project|OCCRP]], involved the seizure of more than $500 million in assets and the indictment of more than 30 individuals and two corporate entities for offences including sanctions evasion and money laundering.[1]

## Badlands framing

Contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] characterized Deripaska as "a KEY figure in Spygate," placing him within what the contributor called the international Swamp rather than treating him purely as a foreign target.[1] Just Human questioned whether the enforcement actions described by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] were genuinely aimed at "Russia and its enablers" or at the Swamp spanning Russia, Europe and the United States.[1]

## McGonigal indictment

In January 2023, former [[fbi|FBI]] counterintelligence official [[charles-mcgonigal|Charles McGonigal]] was accused in a five-count indictment of working for Deripaska, who had been under U.S. sanctions since 2018.[2] Prosecutors alleged that McGonigal and former Russian diplomat [[sergey-shestakov|Sergey Shestakov]] worked for Deripaska in 2021 to investigate an unnamed rival Russian oligarch in violation of those sanctions, and sought to conceal the activity.[2] Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] described Deripaska as "a KEY character in RussiaGate" and suggested that evidence could cross-pollinate between the existing case against Deripaska and matters held by [[john-durham|John Durham]].[2]
