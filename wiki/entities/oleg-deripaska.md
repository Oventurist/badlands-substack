# Oleg Deripaska

Oleg Deripaska is a Russian industrialist who built a fortune in the aluminium sector following the privatizations of the 1990s. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], he is listed among the "contemporary modern oligarchs" produced by post-Soviet liberalization.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names Deripaska alongside Boris Berezovsky, Mikhail Fridman and [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]] in Russia, and [[petro-poroshenko|Petro Poroshenko]], Rinat Akhmetov, [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]] and [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] in Ukraine, as beneficiaries of the privatization and liberalization reforms directed by [[anatoly-chubais|Anatoly Chubais]] and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] under [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]]. The article observes that many of these figures "soon purchased houses in the swank upmarket sections of London, which has come to be known as 'Moscow on Thames'."
