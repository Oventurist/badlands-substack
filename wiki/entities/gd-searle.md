# G.D. Searle

G.D. Searle was the American pharmaceutical company that originally held the patent on [[aspartame]], the artificial sweetener at the center of Badlands Media's account of regulatory capture in the food supply.[1]

## Role in aspartame's approval

A 1980 [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] Board of Inquiry composed of three independent scientists concluded that aspartame "might induce brain tumors," and the agency had banned the sweetener on that basis.[1] According to the article, that outcome was reversed after [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]], then serving as president of G.D. Searle, vowed that he would "call in his markers" to secure approval.[1]

## Acquisition by Monsanto

[[monsanto|Monsanto]] subsequently bought out Searle and, in the article's characterization, was untroubled by aspartame's clouded history.[1] The Searle-to-Monsanto transfer is presented as one link in a chain connecting political influence, agrochemical interests, and additives that remain in the American food supply.[1]
