# Permindex

Permindex is described in the Badlands account as an assassination bureau operating within the parallel state that grew up around [[nato|NATO]] in the early Cold War.[1]

## Context

With NATO in place by April 8, 1949, the structure interfaced with the newly established [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] and the [[cia|CIA]], created in 1946 and 1947 respectively.[1] Under the guiding hand of [[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]], NATO came under the management of unreconstructed Nazis, French Vichy figures and Italian fascists running what became known as "NATO's secret armies" — a globally extended parallel state managing global terrorism, assassination bureaus such as Permindex, and organized crime syndicates.[1] This apparatus is associated with the [[operation-gladio|Gladio]] stay-behind networks and with assassinations of anti-imperial figures including Aldo Moro, Olof Palme, [[enrico-mattei|Enrico Mattei]], Adnan Menderes and [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], plus repeated attempts on [[charles-de-gaulle|Charles de Gaulle]].[1]
