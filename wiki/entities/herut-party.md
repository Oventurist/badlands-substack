# Herut Party

The Herut Party was the Israeli political vehicle formed out of the membership of the [[irgun|Irgun]] after that organization was formally disbanded in 1949, following the establishment of the Israeli state.[1]

Herut carried forward the [[revisionist-zionism|Revisionist]] political tradition of [[zeev-jabotinsky|Ze'ev Jabotinsky]] into electoral politics, and eventually became the [[likud-party|Likud Party]] in 1977 — the same period in which [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was cycling out of the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] and partnering with his father to plot his political career.[1]
