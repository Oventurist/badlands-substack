# Gays Against Groomers

Gays Against Groomers is an advocacy organization made up of gay and lesbian people that has spoken out aggressively against gender-affirmative care and against what it characterizes as the indoctrination of children with [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]].[1]

## Role in narrative-warfare analysis

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cited the group as evidence complicating the narrative that the LGBTQ community as a whole is targeting children — a narrative he argued is deployed to radicalize conservatives against progressives.[1] He noted that this framing ignores the existence of large, vocal organizations such as Gays Against Groomers and LGB United, as well as the basic possibility that gay and lesbian people can hold conservative political views.[1]

Deschamps grouped Gays Against Groomers with [[lesbians-united|Lesbians United]] and the [[lgb-alliance|LGB Alliance]] as organizations that have issued statements opposing the indoctrination of children with gender ideology and the homophobia these organizations allege it fosters.[1] In his analysis, the invisibility of such groups within mainstream coverage is a product of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] serving [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric operations]], in which the amplified voices of a small radicalized minority are made to appear as a majority on the left.[1]
