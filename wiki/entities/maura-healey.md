# Maura Healey

Maura Healey is the Democratic Governor of Massachusetts. In July 2023 she became the focus of criticism over her administration's placement of newly arrived migrants in state-funded motels, and specifically over reporting that wealthy enclaves were being excluded from the program.[1]

## Migrant housing controversy

Boston Herald columnist [[howie-carr|Howie Carr]] reported that Healey was sparing "millionaire destinations" from absorbing a growing number of illegal aliens housed at taxpayer expense in Massachusetts motels.[1] State Rep. [[peter-durant|Peter Durant]], a Republican then running for state senate, asked Healey for a full accounting of the cost to Massachusetts taxpayers of housing, feeding and caring for the arrivals.[1] Healey's office responded that nearly 40 motels were then in use to house new arrivals but declined to provide a cost estimate.[1]

Carr wrote that among the towns and cities where the state was funding migrant motels, almost none of the Massachusetts "millionaire destinations" — Nantucket, Martha's Vineyard, Cambridge or Newburyport — appeared on the list.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief]], framed the episode as an illustration of hypocrisy, defined in the commentary as "the sin of pretending virtue or goodness," arguing that establishment border policy is a pretense of virtue in which the ruling class demands open borders while declining to absorb unvetted arrivals into its own communities.[1] The commentary connected the Massachusetts arrangement to the earlier Martha's Vineyard episode, in which the affluent island declared an emergency over a small number of migrant arrivals despite an abundance of empty vacation homes, and to remarks attributed to former Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] about needing migrants "to pick the crops down here."[1]
