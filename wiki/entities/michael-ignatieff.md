# Michael Ignatieff

Count Michael Grant Ignatieff is a Canadian academic and politician who served as leader of Canada's Liberal Party from 2008 to 2011.[1] He is the grandson of the Rhodes Scholar philosopher [[george-grant|George Grant]], and thus a descendant of the family line that also produced Rhodes Trust president George Parkin.[1]

Badlands Media contributor Matthew Ehret places Ignatieff's leadership tenure within a managed succession: while Ignatieff led the party, [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] was still being groomed for the eventual leadership by [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] and [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] privy council officials.[1] Ignatieff worked closely with Rhodes Scholar [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]], the former Premier of Ontario who took over as Liberal leader from 2011 to 2013 and later served as president of [[george-soros|George Soros]]' Open Society University in Hungary.[1]
