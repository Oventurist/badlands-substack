# Moshe Arbel

Moshe Arbel is the Israeli Interior Minister whose ministry controls the issuance of visas and residency permits, including those sought by foreign religious organizations seeking to operate in or visit Israel.[1]

## Dispute over Christian visas

In July 2025 Arbel became the recipient of a scathing letter from United States Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]], who blasted the Interior Ministry for refusing to grant visas to American Christian groups attempting to visit Israel.[1] Huckabee, a Baptist minister and longtime supporter of the Jewish state, expressed "profound disappointment" that an earlier meeting between the two men on the subject had produced no improvement, writing that "the situation has actually gotten worse."[1] Huckabee stressed that Evangelical Christian groups were friends of Israel but were being treated "like enemies," and threatened reciprocal steps: the embassy would be forced to announce publicly that Israel does not welcome Christian groups and harasses them, and would warn American Christians that their support was being met with hostility and that tourists should reconsider their travel plans.[1] The letter was first reported by Israel's Channel 12.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the episode as part of a broader pattern in which Christians are treated as adversaries in the Holy Land, linking the visa refusals to the ideological legacy of [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]] and the [[lehava]] movement, whose adherents the commentary placed inside [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet.[1]
