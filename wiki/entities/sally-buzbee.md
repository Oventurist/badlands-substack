# Sally Buzbee

Sally Buzbee is an American journalist who served as the first female executive editor of [[washington-post|The Washington Post]], a role she had held since 2021.[1]

On Sunday, June 2, 2024, the paper announced that Buzbee had stepped down from the position.[1] She was to be succeeded on an interim basis by [[matthew-murray|Matthew Murray]], the former editor-in-chief of [[wall-street-journal|The Wall Street Journal]], who would serve as executive editor until after the 2024 presidential election, at which point [[robert-winnett|Robert Winnett]], then deputy editor at The Telegraph, would take over.[1]

The leadership change was paired with a complete restructuring of the newsroom at the [[jeff-bezos|Bezos]]-owned publication, splitting it into a three-pronged operation: the main newsroom, the editorial page, and a "third newsroom" comprising service and social media journalism run separately from the core news operation.[1] The shakeup was reported by [[daily-caller|The Daily Caller]].[1]
