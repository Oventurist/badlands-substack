# Shari Redstone

Shari Redstone (rendered "Sheri Redstone" in the source) is the media executive who runs [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]. The Badlands Media essay A New Hollywood describes her as a [[donald-trump|Trump]] ally who took over the company from her father [[sumner-redstone|Sumner Redstone]] in 2016.

Her stewardship is presented in the article as the first stage of a pro-Trump shift in control of American entertainment, to be completed by the merger of Paramount Global with [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]]. The programming cited as evidence of this shift — *Lioness*, *Landman* and Showtime's *The Agency* — was released under her leadership.
