# Robert Shea

Robert Shea was an author and [[playboy|Playboy]] magazine colleague of [[robert-anton-wilson|Robert Anton Wilson]], with whom he co-authored the [[illuminatus-trilogy|Illuminatus Trilogy]].[1]

Badlands Media's [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series cites Shea only in connection with the trilogy, whose closing sequence — an evil cult of global influence attempting to reanimate an army of zombies held in suspended animation beneath a large body of water — is read by the author as possible disclosure about the [[koryos|Koryos]] and the [[black-sea|Black Sea]].[1]
