# Infectious Diseases Society of America

The Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) is a professional medical association that, jointly with the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]], created the COVID-19 Real-Time Learning Network, a public-facing resource on the pandemic.[1]

## Role in the mask guidance dispute

A November 2021 letter from epidemiologist [[michael-osterholm|Michael Osterholm]] and seven colleagues to the CDC warned that misrepresenting data on trusted websites such as the CDC's and the IDSA-linked COVID-19 Real-Time Learning Network would "damage the credibility of science," endanger public trust by "misrepresenting the evidence," and give the public "false expectations" that masking would protect them from SARS-CoV-2.[1] The authors specifically urged the IDSA to remove from its website the suggestion that masking prevents severe disease.[1] Osterholm described a pattern of selectively choosing data that supported the desired narrative on masks — claims he said were unsupported by the scientific evidence provided by the CDC and IDSA on their own websites.[1]

The letter surfaced publicly in August 2023 among documents obtained from the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]] and was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] as evidence that public health officials had advanced mask policy despite opposing scientific evidence received from experts.[1]
