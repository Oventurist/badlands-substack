# David Livingstone

David Livingstone is a historian and author cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] for his work *Ordo Ab Chaos*, which reconstructs the fabrication of [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]].[1]

Livingstone identifies the source text plagiarized to produce the Protocols as *Dialogue in Hell Between Machiavelli and Montesquieu*, an 1864 book by [[maurice-joly|Maurice Joly]], protégé of [[adolphe-cremieux|Adolphe Crémieux]] of the [[alliance-israelite-universelle|Alliance Israelite Universelle]].[1] He details Crémieux's positions as Grand Master of the Rite of Misraim and Grand Commander of the Supreme Council of France, and notes Joly's own Jewish background and lifelong Freemasonry, adding that Joly's book was an attack on the political ambitions of Napoleon III rather than any discussion of Jews.[1]

Livingstone further cites *Warrant for Genocide*, which established that over 160 passages in the Protocols — two fifths of the entire text — are lifted from Joly, with the borrowed passages appearing in nearly the same order as in the original.[1]
