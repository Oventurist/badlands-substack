# Check My Ads

Check My Ads is a tax-exempt organization that works on digital advertising accountability, pressuring advertisers and ad-tech intermediaries over the outlets they fund.[1]

## Litigation with Rumble

The video platform [[rumble|Rumble]] has sued Check My Ads for defamation, accusing the group of "routinely target[ing] news outlets and platforms that do not adhere to their political worldview."[1] The suit runs parallel to Rumble's separate antitrust action against [[google|Google]] and [[alphabet|Alphabet]] over digital advertising practices.[1] Within the Badlands framing, both cases are treated as fronts in a broader conflict over de-platforming and advertising-side censorship of dissident media.[1]
