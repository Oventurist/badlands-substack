# Chauntyll Louisa Allen

Chauntyll Louisa Allen is one of two individuals arrested by federal agents in January 2026 in connection with the coordinated attack on [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota.

## Arrest in the Cities Church case

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] announced on January 22, 2026 that federal law enforcement agents had taken Allen and Nekima Levy Armstrong into custody "at her direction," identifying the pair in connection with the attack on Cities Church. According to the January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] and [[fbi|FBI]] agents executed the arrests in Minnesota; Bondi said Armstrong allegedly played a key role in organizing the attack, while federal officials said they were investigating potential civil rights and other charges related to the incident.

The arrests followed a protest at the church the previous weekend in which activists disrupted services to target a pastor said to be linked to [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|US Immigration and Customs Enforcement]]. The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] had previously opened an investigation into the disruption under the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] and the FACE Act. Former CNN host [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]], alleged to have been involved in the incident, was not arrested.
