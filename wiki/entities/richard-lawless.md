# Richard Lawless

Richard Lawless was a former fifteen-year veteran of the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] who won state contracts to promote exports in Asia while [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] served as Florida's commerce secretary.[1]

He later joined with Bush and paid him to find real-estate property for Japanese investors; a single 1993 deal gave Bush a commission of $213,000.[1] The article presents the relationship as straightforward quid pro quo: state contracts awarded, then repaid through lucrative private commissions.[1]
