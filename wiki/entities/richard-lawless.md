# Richard Lawless

Richard Lawless was a former fifteen-year veteran of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] who won state contracts to promote exports in Asia while [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] served as Florida's commerce secretary.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

He later joined with Bush and paid him to find real estate property for Japanese investors; one 1993 deal alone gave Bush a commission of $213,000.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The Badlands Media account presents the sequence as straightforward quid pro quo: state contracts out, private commissions back.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
