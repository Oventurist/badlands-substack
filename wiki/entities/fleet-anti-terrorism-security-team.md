# Fleet Anti-terrorism Security Team

The Fleet Anti-terrorism Security Team (FAST) is an elite counter-terror unit of the [[united-states-marine-corps|United States Marine Corps]] tasked with reinforcing the security of American diplomatic facilities and other high-value installations during emergencies.[1]

## Deployment to Haiti, March 2024

On March 13, 2024, [[us-southern-command|US Southern Command]] announced that, at the request of the [[state-department|State Department]], a FAST element had been deployed to [[haiti|Haiti]] to bolster security at the American embassy in Port-au-Prince amid the [[2024-haiti-gang-crisis|2024 Haiti gang crisis]].[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] confirmed the deployment as urgent, citing armed gangs' takeover of much of the capital and the resignation of Prime Minister [[ariel-henry|Ariel Henry]] the previous day.[1]

The team's stated mission was twofold: to assist the Marine embassy guard already stationed at the compound in defending it should it come under attack, and to help evacuate non-essential American personnel from the country.[1] SOUTHCOM said it was "prepared with a wide range of contingency plans to ensure the safety and security of U.S. Citizens in Haiti."[1]
