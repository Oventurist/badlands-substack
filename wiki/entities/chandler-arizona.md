# Chandler, Arizona

**Chandler** is a city in the Phoenix metropolitan area of Arizona. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as the host city of the inaugural stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] (GART), staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] from April 20 to 23, 2023.[1]

## GART AZ (April 2023)

The conference took place at the [[hilton-phoenix-chandler|Hilton Phoenix Chandler]] in Chandler and was billed as Badlands Media's first in-person, ticketed event, bringing together the network's personalities, show hosts, sponsors and readers.[1] In an April 11, 2023 "Last Call" notice the outlet released a final batch of in-person tickets — including a small allocation of VIP Dinner places — and warned that in-person sales would close at 1pm EST on Monday, April 17, urging readers to "grab yours NOW if you plan to join us in the desert."[1] The desert setting of the Phoenix metropolitan area was used as shorthand for the event throughout the promotion.[1]

Badlands told readers that the last batch of tickets would "move FAST," and offered a pre-orderable exclusive live stream for those who could not travel to Chandler, inviting them to attend in person at a future stop.[1]

## Announcement of the Chandler conference (February 2023)

The Chandler stop was first announced on February 25, 2023 in a post headlined "The Great American Restoration Tour," subtitled "Join Us in Chandler, Arizona This Spring!"[2] The announcement confirmed the conference would take place at the [[hilton-phoenix-chandler|Hilton Phoenix Chandler]] in Chandler from April 20 to 23, 2023, and noted that VIP tickets — which included a private dinner event the day before the conference — were already sold out, while General Admission tickets remained available but were "moving FAST."[2] Readers planning to travel to Chandler were directed to reserve rooms at the Hilton Phoenix Chandler using a preferred [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] room rate.[2] Those unable to reach Arizona were offered a pre-orderable exclusive live stream of the event.[2] The Chandler gathering was described as the inaugural event of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] and was "powered by" [[firman-power-equipment|FIRMAN]].[2]
