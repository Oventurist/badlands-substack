# Mark Shapiro

Mark Shapiro is an executive at [[endeavor|Endeavor]] and TKO, the sports and entertainment group whose On Location subsidiary sells corporate hospitality packages for major events.[1]

## 2028 Olympics prediction

At a Morgan Stanley media conference in March 2025, Shapiro predicted the [[2028-los-angeles-olympics|2028 Los Angeles Olympics]] would be "out-of-control good" despite that year's wildfires, and that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would take credit for their success.[1] He said the "L.A. and Back" narrative would drive strong demand for On Location hospitality packages and told analyst Ben Swinburne that organizers had "had time to rebuild, and it's going to be a coming-out party."[1] Turning political unprompted, Shapiro said Trump "is going to walk out of office with the trophy that is the L.A. Olympics" and that he was "a true believer that this will be his Olympics as much as anything else."[1]

## WWE–UFC merger

In April 2023, when [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]] agreed to merge [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] with the [[ufc|UFC]], Shapiro — Endeavor's president and chief operating officer — was designated to hold the same roles at the new publicly traded company.[2] The combined entity was to be led by chief executive [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] with [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]] as executive chairman.[2]
