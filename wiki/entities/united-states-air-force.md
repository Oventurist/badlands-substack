# United States Air Force

The United States Air Force is the air warfare branch of the U.S. armed forces, organized under the Department of the Air Force alongside the Space Force.[1]

## 2024 reorientation toward China

On Monday, February 12, 2024, the Air Force announced that it would create a new forward-looking capabilities planning command, refocus its training enterprise, and rethink how airmen deploy, as part of a set of 24 initiatives intended to reorient the service to outpace China's military ambitions and prevail in future conflicts.[1] The plans were unveiled by the top civilian and uniformed leaders of the Air Force and Space Force at the Air and Space Forces Association's Air Warfare Symposium, following a months-long department-wide review that had begun in the fall of 2023.[1] Observers characterized the result as one of the most significant reorganizations of the service since the end of the Cold War.[1]

Air Force Secretary [[frank-kendall|Frank Kendall]] said the goal was to prove the department's competitive resolve to U.S. adversaries — particularly Russia and China — and to execute the plans with urgency, warning that "the risk of conflict is here now."[1]
