# Alliant International University

Alliant International University is a U.S. institution with a Los Angeles campus offering graduate clinical psychology training, including Clinical Psy.D. and Clinical Ph.D. programs.[1] It appears in Badlands Media's coverage of cult ritual abuse as the academic home of Dr. [[james-randall-noblitt|James Randall Noblitt]], a professor of clinical psychology in its Clinical Psy.D. Program and Coordinator for the Multi-Interest Option of its Clinical Ph.D. and PsyD Programs at the Los Angeles campus.[1] Noblitt joined the Alliant faculty in 2006 after relocating from Dallas, Texas, where he had run a private clinical practice since 1984.[1]

The university's relevance to the [[ritual-abuse|ritual abuse]] literature is that it provided an institutional base from which one of the field's most-cited clinicians continued to train future generations of therapists in recognizing dissociative presentations associated with [[mind-control-programming|trauma-based mind control]].[1]
