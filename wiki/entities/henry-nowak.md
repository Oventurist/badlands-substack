# Henry Nowak

Henry Nowak was an 18-year-old man who was stabbed to death in Southampton, United Kingdom, on December 3, 2025, in a case that became a focus of public outrage over British policing and media coverage.[1]

## The killing

Nowak was stabbed five times by 23-year-old [[vickrum-singh-digwa|Vickrum Singh Digwa]] while walking home after a night out.[1] He suffered wounds to his face and legs and a penetrating lung injury inflicted with an eight-inch blade.[1]

Nowak repeatedly told arriving police officers that he had been stabbed and could not breathe, but officers instead arrested and handcuffed him after Digwa claimed Nowak had launched a racist attack that knocked off his turban.[1] Nowak died about an hour later from his injuries.[1] No independent evidence supported Digwa's racism allegation.[1]

## Aftermath

Officers focused on the unverified racism claim rather than treating Nowak's wounds or immediately securing Digwa, despite a visible blood trail.[1] Digwa was convicted of murder on May 28, 2026, and sentenced to life imprisonment with a minimum term of 21 years.[1] Nearly six months after the killing, [[hampshire-police|Hampshire Police]] referred itself for an independent investigation into its handling of the incident.[1]

Nowak's family described the police treatment of their son as "unbearable" and noted that the perpetrator "was believed" while their bleeding son was not.[1] The case prompted wider discussion — including allusions to a "white George Floyd" — about police response and media coverage when the victim is white and the perpetrator is from a minority background.[1]
