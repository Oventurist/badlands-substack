# Augustin Barruel

Augustin Barruel was the author of *Memoirs Illustrating the History of Jacobinism* (1798), one of the principal sources used in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2," where it is cited under the abbreviation **AB**.[1]

Barruel documented the training of the Illuminati novice, who "must … learn how to date his letters, and be conversant with the Illuminized Hegira or Calendar; for all letters which he will receive in future will be dated according to the Persian era, caled [sic] Jezdegert and beginning A.D. 630," with the year beginning "on the first of Pharavardin, which answer to the 21st of March."[1] He listed the Persian month names substituted for the Western calendar, noting that the first month had forty-one days while the month of March, called Asphandar, had only twenty.[1] Barruel also recorded that the novice's letters to his superior were written in cipher, which "he must make himself master of … until initiated into the higher degrees, when he will be entrusted with the hieroglyphics of the Order," and reproduced both the novice cipher and the hieroglyphic cipher used in the higher Scotch Knight degrees.[1]

Writing thirteen years after the Bavarian suppression, Barruel described [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]] as "now banished from his country as a traitor to his Prince and to the whole Universe," yet enjoying asylum, a public pension and the title of Honorary Councilor at the court of Ernest Lewis, Duke of Saxe Gotha.[1] Barruel is further cited for the inventory of material seized at [[xavier-zwack|Xavier Zwack]]'s residence in 1786.[1]
