# CBS

CBS is an American broadcast television network owned by [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]. In the Badlands Media essay A New Hollywood, CBS is discussed principally as the defendant in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s $10 billion lawsuit arising from the network's *60 Minutes* interview with Kamala Harris during the most recent presidential campaign.

According to the article, it was quickly discovered after the broadcast that the interview had been edited to make Harris look better, with the editors removing "as much unintelligible word-salad as they could." Trump viewed this as election tampering and sued. The source states that Trump critics and CBS leadership laughed the lawsuit off as frivolous, but that CBS has very recently discussed settling out of court. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that the settlement talk is explained by the pending merger between Paramount Global and [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]]: Paramount, worth roughly $8 billion — less than the damages sought — does not want anything to interfere with the deal.

## The gas tax interview (May 2026)

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told CBS News chief White House correspondent [[nancy-cordes|Nancy Cordes]] in a phone interview that he plans to temporarily suspend the federal gas tax "for a period of time" before gradually restoring it once fuel prices decline, saying the tax would be phased back in "when gas goes down."
