# Mike Gonzalez

Mike Gonzalez is a senior fellow at the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]], the Washington conservative think tank, and a frequent commentator on left-wing political movements in the United States.

In the July 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, Gonzalez appears via [[fox-news|Fox News]] reporting on the wave of progressive and democratic-socialist primary victories preceding the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]]. He is quoted warning that continued socialist electoral gains could result in "communists in double digits" in the House of Representatives. The Brief situates that warning alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s July 3 Mount Rushmore speech invoking a "communist menace," and against [[associated-press|Associated Press]] reporting that experts regarded broad claims linking Democrats to [[communism|communism]] as inaccurate.
