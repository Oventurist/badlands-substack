# Alfred Milner

Alfred Milner, 1st Viscount Milner (23 March 1854 – 13 May 1925) was a British statesman and colonial administrator who played a role in the formulation of British foreign and domestic policy between the mid-1890s and the early 1920s. From December 1916 to November 1918 he was one of the most important members of Prime Minister David Lloyd George's War Cabinet, and he subsequently served as Britain's Secretary of State for War from 1918 to 1919 and Secretary of State for the Colonies from 1919 to 1921.[1]

Milner founded the [[round-table-movement|Round Table]] in 1909 with the stated purpose of uniting the English-speaking countries into a single global empire.[1]
