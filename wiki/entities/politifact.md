# PolitiFact

**PolitiFact** is an American fact-checking organization described by Badlands Media as a "weaponized 'fact checker'" used to suppress discussion of [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] and, more broadly, as a component of a [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] information ecosystem.[1]

## Role in the Pizzagate suppression

Anyone who tried to post about Pizzagate when the story broke was either censored or had their post hit by PolitiFact or a similar fact-checking outfit, in exactly the manner that [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]] describe hybrid warfare in *The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare*.[1]

## Funding

The article notes that when PolitiFact was first started in 2015 it was funded by the [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] to the tune of $382,997.[1] Badlands Media presents this single example as a microcosm — an ecosystem showing how a fifth-generation psychological operation is run, while cautioning that the full-scale operation to cover up Pizzagate was much larger and more sophisticated.[1]
