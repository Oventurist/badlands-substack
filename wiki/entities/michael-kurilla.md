# Michael Kurilla

General Michael "Erik" Kurilla is a United States Army officer who served as Combatant Commander of [[centcom|United States Central Command]], the command responsible for American military operations across the Middle East.[1]

## Role in the 2024 Israel–Hezbollah escalation

Badlands Media reporting held that the September 2024 pager attacks against [[hezbollah]] were flanked by visits from Kurilla to Israel, during which he warned Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that the US military would not assist the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] if Israel instigated a ground war with Hezbollah or any other party.[1] Colonel [[lawrence-wilkerson|Lawrence Wilkerson]] added the nuance that Kurilla's warning carried an implicit assurance that the US military would be able to determine whether the IDF had initiated the provocation.[1]

Commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read Netanyahu's subsequent release of what was described as the largest single munition ever dropped by the IDF as a defiant answer to Kurilla's warnings — "a huge middle finger to the Devolution team."[1] The same analysis invoked Kurilla as the archetype of a combatant commander intervening against civilian escalation, and asked whether comparable [[pentagon]] officials had similarly intervened in the Ukraine long-range missile decision.[1]

## Renewed usurpation claims (September 2024)

In September 2024 [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] returned to Colonel [[lawrence-wilkerson|Lawrence Wilkerson]]'s interview with Judge [[andrew-napolitano|Andrew Napolitano]], in which Wilkerson said that Pentagon officials — naming Kurilla, the Combatant Commander of [[centcom|CENTCOM]] — had usurped the authority of the [[white-house|White House]] and [[state-department|State Department]] and were conducting US foreign policy.[2] Assessing reports that the United States was surging troops to the Middle East amid the Israel–[[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] escalation, he concluded that the movements were routine rotations and a long-scheduled carrier deployment, and therefore did nothing to contradict Wilkerson's account of combatant-commander control over policy.[2]

## September 2024 warnings to Israel

In the week following the Lebanon [[pager-attack|pager attacks]], Badlands reported that Kurilla, as [[centcom|CENTCOM]] commander, travelled to [[israel|Israel]] twice to remind [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] that if they initiated a ground war with Lebanon, Syria or [[iran|Iran]], they would be on their own.[3] Retired Colonel [[lawrence-wilkerson|Lawrence Wilkerson]], speaking to [[andrew-napolitano|Judge Andrew Napolitano]], characterized the episode in terms Badlands summarized as a combatant commander having effectively usurped the [[white-house|White House]], [[state-department|State Department]] and [[cia|CIA]] to take control of U.S. diplomacy in the Middle East — a development the authors linked to the [[devolution|Devolution]] thesis.[3]

## October 2024 THAAD narrative

In October 2024, when the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] confirmed the deployment of a [[thaad|THAAD]] missile defense battery and roughly 100 troops to Israel, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the resulting alarm over "US troops to the Middle East" was manufactured and amounted to "another attempt to villainize CENTCOM and General Kurilla."[4] He noted that the roughly 40,000 other U.S. personnel in the region were mostly aboard ships on routine deployments and that he had found no evidence of any U.S. troops mobilized for offensive action on fronts bordering Israel.[4]


## Wilkerson anecdotes and the Nasrallah strike (2024)

After Israel's September 2024 killing of Hezbollah leader [[hassan-nasrallah|Hassan Nasrallah]], Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that purported anecdotes by Colonel Lawrence Wilkerson concerning Kurilla, then the CENTCOM commander, had become newly important.[5] If Wilkerson's account was accurate, he wrote, provocation of the kind represented by the strike could not be rewarded with U.S. troops — and [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] appeared to have called Kurilla's bluff.[5]

## Friction over the Lebanon escalation (September 2024)

Badlands Media wrote in September 2024 that as [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] prepared to invade [[lebanon|Lebanon]], he was "thumbing his nose" at Kurilla, then Combatant Commander of [[centcom|CENTCOM]] — presenting the Israeli escalation as proceeding against the preferences of the American regional command.[6]

## Warning invoked during the 2024 Lebanon offensive

Badlands commentary on Israel's October 2024 ground incursion into Lebanon framed [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu's]] escalation against [[hezbollah]] and [[iran]] as an "all in" wager that directly called out Kurilla's threat that the United States would not back Israel in a war against Hezbollah or Iran.[7] The move was likened to throwing oneself into a tiger pit and daring the zookeepers to mount a rescue.[7] See [[2024-israeli-ground-invasion-of-lebanon]].[7]
