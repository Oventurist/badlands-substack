# Skydance Media

Skydance Media is an American film and television production company run by [[david-ellison|David Ellison]], the son of [[oracle|Oracle]] cofounder [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]]. In the Badlands Media essay A New Hollywood, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies Skydance as the vehicle through which allies of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] are acquiring control of [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]], with which it is in the process of merging.

The article lists Skydance's film credits as *Top Gun: Maverick*, the *Mission Impossible* franchise and *Jack Reacher*, noting that the common theme among them is [[tom-cruise|Tom Cruise]]. Its television output is said to include *Reacher*, about an ex-military police major who uses his skills to solve crimes, and *Jack Ryan*, in which a [[cia|CIA]] analyst goes into the field to save America from foreign threats. Carlson reads this concentration of intelligence-agency and military-heroic programming as a signal that Trump intends to reform rather than dismantle the CIA, and as evidence that Hollywood "has already been infiltrated by Trump's people."
