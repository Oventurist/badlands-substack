# Sabrina Singh

Sabrina Singh is a deputy press secretary and spokeswoman for the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] who conducted Defense Department press briefings during the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]].[1]

## August 2024 Kursk briefing

At a daily Pentagon briefing on Thursday, August 8, 2024, Singh was asked whether Ukrainian forces' use of American weapons during the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk incursion]] was consistent with US policy governing what Ukraine may do with US-supplied arms.[1] She answered without hesitation that "yes it is consistent with our policy," explaining that Washington supported the need for "crossfire" from Ukraine back across the border onto Russian positions from which Ukraine was being attacked, even when those positions lay on Russian territory.[1] She framed the offensive in Russia's Kursk oblast, then in its fourth day, as defensive in nature.[1]

Singh maintained that it remained US policy for Ukraine to avoid striking deep into Russian territory with American arms, but when pressed on the hypothetical of a direct attack on Moscow she declined to rule it out, saying "I'm not going to put a specific range on it" and "I'm not gonna draw a circular map for you here of where they can and cannot strike," while adding that "we still don't support long-range attacks into Russia."[1] ZeroHedge characterized the exchange as a clear escalation in rhetoric from the Pentagon, in which attacking the Russian capital was discussed even theoretically.[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

Writing as [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], the Badlands Media commentator argued that the exchange displayed incompetence rather than nefarious control, calling it further evidence of the "Deep State Archipelago" thesis — that the entrenched bureaucracy and its media apparatus are more siloed than they present themselves, cut off from former information channels and "genuinely grasping in the dark" when asked direct questions.[1] The commentary held that such statements can be read bicamerally, as either threatening or confused, producing the same effect on the public: unease, anxiety and fear.[1]

## Gallant visit cancellation (October 2024)

On Tuesday, October 8, 2024, Singh, then [[pentagon|Pentagon]] deputy press secretary, confirmed to reporters that Israel had cancelled a planned Wednesday meeting between Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] and U.S. Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]].[2] "We were just informed that Minister Gallant will be postponing his trip to Washington, D.C.," Singh said, adding that "Secretary Austin looks forward to seeing him soon."[2]

The meeting had been scheduled as the United States and Israel debated how to respond to Iran's missile attack of the previous week, and Gallant had also been due to meet White House national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]].[2] Israeli media reported that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] told Gallant hours before departure that he did not approve of the trip and should wait until Netanyahu had spoken with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — the two had not spoken in nearly 50 days — and until the political-security cabinet approved Israel's planned actions against Iran.[2]
