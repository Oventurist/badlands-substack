# Kimberly Hermann

Kimberly Hermann is General Counsel of the [[southeastern-legal-foundation|Southeastern Legal Foundation]], the Georgia-based conservative organization whose lawsuit against the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] produced disclosures about [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s use of pseudonymous email accounts as vice president.[1]

## Role in the Biden pseudonym email litigation

In a joint filing disclosed late on October 30, 2023, the National Archives revealed that Biden had sent or received up to 82,000 pages of private emails across all eight years of his vice presidency, involving three shadow addresses: "robinware456@gmail.com," "JRBWare@gmail.com" and "Robert.L.Peters@pci.gov."[1]

Hermann told the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] that "The fact that as vice-president, Joe Biden sent 82,000 pages of emails from alias email addresses is shocking," adding that "The American public has a right to know what is in those emails. SLF remains hopeful that now that we have confirmed that the emails exist, NARA will fulfill its legal obligation and produce them in a timely and transparent manner."[1] It remained unclear what topics most of the [[biden-pseudonym-emails|pseudonym emails]] covered or who else was looped in on the messages.[1]
