# Li Ka-shing

Li Ka-shing is a Hong Kong billionaire, aged 96 in 2025, who controls the conglomerate [[ck-hutchison|CK Hutchison]].[1]

## Panama Canal ports sale

On March 4, 2025, CK Hutchison announced plans to sell 43 port facilities globally — including critical ports at both ends of the [[panama-canal|Panama Canal]] and near the Suez Canal — for approximately $22.8 billion to a consortium led by the US investment giant [[blackrock|BlackRock]].[1] The deal was spearheaded by BlackRock chief executive [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]], described as a longtime confidant of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and called for an agreement to be signed by April 2, 2025.[1]

China's State Administration for Market Regulation opened an investigation into potential violations of Chinese anti-monopoly laws, effectively stalling the transaction, after President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] was reportedly "angry" that Li's company had not consulted Beijing before announcing the sale.[1]
