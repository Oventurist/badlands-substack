# Hassan al-Banna

Hassan al-Banna was the Egyptian schoolteacher and organizer who founded the modern [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] in 1928 in Cairo, Egypt.[1]

## Origins and British connection

According to commentary from Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], al-Banna graduated from a Salafi (radical) Islamic school that had been set up by the British government in the 1880s under the direction of British Lord and Comptroller General of Egypt [[evelyn-baring|Evelyn Baring]].[1] Al-Banna is said to have studied under [[rashid-rida|Rida Rashid]], one of the Islamic clerics responsible for the proliferation of Salafi Islam, who was described as having been plucked from obscurity by British Intelligence and employed as head imam of the British-funded school in Cairo.[1] On this account, Rida was on the payroll of British Intelligence, and his students created the Muslim Brotherhood — leading Badlands commentators to conclude that "the Muslim Brotherhood is a creation of British Intelligence."[1]

The Brotherhood that al-Banna founded has, per reporting cited in the brief, been pushing terrorism since its founding in Egypt in the 1920s, and spawned affiliates including the organization from which Hamas was spun off in the 1980s.[1]
