# John F. Hylan

John F. Hylan was a mayor of New York City whose 1922 warning about "invisible government" is quoted in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1."[1]

Hylan stated: "The real menace of our Republic is the invisible government, which like a giant octopus sprawls its slimy length over our city, state, and nation ... At the head of this octopus are the Rockefeller—Standard Oil interests and a small group of powerful banking houses generally referred to as the international bankers [who] virtually run the U.S. government for their own selfish purposes."[1]

The quotation is used to support the article's argument that clandestine financial power — including the interests of [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] and the international bankers who funded the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] — has long operated beyond electoral accountability.[1]
