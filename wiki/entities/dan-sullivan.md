# Dan Sullivan

Dan Sullivan is a Republican United States Senator from Alaska who, alongside his Alaska colleague [[lisa-murkowski|Lisa Murkowski]], served as one of the principal public voices responding to Russian and Chinese naval activity near American waters in the North Pacific.[1]

## 2023 Russian-Chinese naval patrol near the Aleutians

In a joint statement issued on Saturday, August 5, 2023, Sullivan and Murkowski said that a total of eleven Russian and Chinese vessels had been operating near the [[aleutian-islands|Aleutian Islands]], and that four [[us-navy|US Navy]] destroyers had been deployed in response.[1] Murkowski said the two senators had been in close contact with leadership from Alaska Command for several days and had received detailed classified briefings on the foreign vessels transiting US waters in the Aleutians.[1]

Sullivan noted that Chinese and Russian vessels had come similarly close to Alaska the previous summer and were encountered at the time by a [[us-coast-guard|US Coast Guard]] ship on a routine patrol — a response he characterized as "tepid."[1] That earlier episode, he said, led him to press senior military leaders to be prepared with a more robust response should the activity recur.[1] The 2023 patrol drew a monitoring response from [[united-states-northern-command|US Northern Command]] and [[norad|NORAD]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted that the American reaction reported by [[cnn|CNN]] came from Sullivan and Murkowski rather than from the executive branch, the commander in chief, or the national security establishment, comparing the [[white-house|White House]]'s silence to its handling of the earlier Chinese surveillance balloon incident.[1]
