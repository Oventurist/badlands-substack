# Wes Moore

Wes Moore is the Democratic Governor of Maryland.[1] He was among the governors who met with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] at the [[white-house|White House]] on July 3, 2024, in a closed-door session convened as pressure mounted on Biden to leave the presidential race following his debate performance.[1]

More than twenty Democratic governors attended in person and virtually for over an hour.[1] Afterward, Moore said, "The president is our nominee. The president is our party leader," and reported that Biden "was very clear that he's in this to win it."[1] The governors, who also included [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] of Minnesota, [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] of California and [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]] of New York, described the conversation as "candid," voiced concerns about the debate, but declined to join Democrats urging Biden to withdraw.[1]

## Key Bridge funding dispute (2025)

In 2025 Moore, the Democratic governor of Maryland and a potential presidential candidate, became the target of a public funding threat from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the rebuild of Baltimore's [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|Francis Scott Key Bridge]].[2] After quarreling with Moore on social media about Baltimore's crime rate, Trump wrote on Truth Social: "I gave Wes Moore a lot of money to fix his demolished bridge. I will now have to rethink that decision???"[2] Reporting that the rebuild could exceed $5 billion — more than double the $1.9 billion estimate Maryland officials outlined after the span fell into the Patapsco River — was expected to give that threat fresh fuel.[2]
