# Osama bin Laden

**Osama bin Laden** (referred to in Q drops as "UBL," Usama Bin Laden) was the leader of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and, in Badlands Media's account, a long-protected [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] asset.[1]

## CIA asset and protection

The article states that bin Laden had worked with the CIA since fighting the Soviets in Afghanistan in the 1980s and was subsequently made the face of the never-ending [[war-on-terror|war on terror]] after the September 11 attacks.[1] Although he was the most wanted man in the world, it took a decade to find and kill him — which the author attributes not to difficulty but to protection: the government always knew where he was, and people inside it shared intelligence with contacts around him so he could evade capture.[1] Q drop 1887 is read as connecting these [[stand-down-order|stand down orders]] protecting bin Laden with Obama's later requirement of White House approval for all battlefield action, effectively a stand down order protecting [[isis|ISIS]] and Al Qaeda.[1]

## The Clinton memorandum

Former CIA officers said in the documentary *The Longest War* that they were prevented from killing bin Laden before 9/11 by a Memorandum of Notification signed by President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] in August 1998.[1] Then-CIA station chief in Islamabad Bob Grenier explained that the "lethal finding" signed by Clinton allowed "lethal activity" against bin Laden but forbade making his death the purpose of an attack.[1] At the time bin Laden was already responsible for the bombing of an American embassy in East Africa and had declared holy war on the United States.[1] The article ties this memorandum to [[sandy-berger|Sandy Berger]]'s theft of classified documents from the National Archives ahead of the [[nine-eleven-commission|9/11 Commission]].[1]

## Expendability

Bin Laden remained "untouchable" until [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s re-election prospects deteriorated; with internal polling weak, ISIS expanding and the Iraq and Afghanistan wars going badly, he became expendable and was, in this telling, sacrificed to save the campaign — while protection of ISIS continued because the weapons deals were too lucrative.[1]
