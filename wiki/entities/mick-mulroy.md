# Mick Mulroy

Mick Mulroy is a former [[pentagon|Pentagon]] official and retired [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] officer who is frequently quoted as a defense analyst on U.S. military assistance to Ukraine.[1]

## Commentary on JDAM failures in Ukraine

In April 2023, following the leak of classified Pentagon documents connected to alleged leaker [[jack-teixeira|Jack Teixeira]], Mulroy commented on reports that American precision munitions were underperforming on the Ukrainian battlefield.[1] He said: "I do think there may be concern that the Russians may be jamming the signal used to direct the JDAMs, which would answer why these munitions are not performing in the manner expected and how they perform in other war zones."[1]

His remarks supported the leaked assessment that Russian GPS jamming was interfering with the targeting process of the [[jdam|Joint Direct Attack Munition]] and other U.S. guided weapons supplied to Ukraine.[1]
