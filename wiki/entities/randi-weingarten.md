# Randi Weingarten

Randi Weingarten is an American labor leader and one of two influential union figures whose departure from the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] in June 2025 underscored the turmoil surrounding new party chair [[ken-martin|Ken Martin]].[1]

## Departure from the DNC

According to reporting in *The New York Times*, Martin had offered to keep Weingarten and fellow union leader [[lee-saunders|Lee Saunders]] as members of the DNC, but would not renew their assignments on the powerful Rules and Bylaws Committee, the body that controls how the party nominates its presidential candidates.[1] Both leaders — who together represent a combined 3.2 million workers — subsequently left the DNC, publicly questioning the party's direction under Martin.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that because the DNC's presidential nominating process had been a source of scandal since 2015, removing union leaders from that machinery was arguably a constructive move, even though it helped explain the internal scapegoating Martin faced.[1]
