# Kazatomprom

Kazatomprom is the state-run uranium agency of [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], and the seller of the mine stakes that opened the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One]] chain of transactions in the Badlands Media account.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Within days of the September 2005 Almaty visit by [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]], during which the pair dined with president [[nursultan-nazarbayev|Nursultan Nazarbayev]], Giustra's company [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy Ltd.]] signed a preliminary deal giving it stakes in three uranium mines controlled by Kazatomprom.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Those Kazakh mines were described by the *New York Times* as among the most lucrative in the world, and became the most valuable asset carried into the 2007 UrAsia–[[uranium-one|Uranium One]] merger.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
