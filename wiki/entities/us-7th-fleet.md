# U.S. 7th Fleet

The U.S. 7th Fleet is the United States Navy's forward-deployed numbered fleet in the western Pacific, responsible for naval operations across the Indo-Pacific theater including the waters around Taiwan.[1]

## April 2023 Taiwan Strait statement

Following the April 16, 2023 transit of the [[taiwan-strait|Taiwan Strait]] by the [[uss-milius|USS Milius]], the 7th Fleet issued a statement describing the passage as routine and noting that the ship had "transited through a corridor in the Strait that is beyond the territorial sea of any coastal State."[1] The transit was the first known U.S. warship passage through the strait since China completed an encirclement exercise around Taiwan, and China's [[eastern-theater-command|Eastern Theater Command]] publicly protested it.[1]

## Role in regional tensions

The 7th Fleet's transits function as the operational expression of the U.S. position that the Taiwan Strait contains international waters, a position Beijing rejects.[1] Badlands commentary framed such deployments as part of an escalating pattern — Chinese aircraft "buzzing" Taiwan, U.S. warships posturing, and resurgent Japanese, Australian and Indian activity in the region — that could culminate in a manufactured incident.[1]
