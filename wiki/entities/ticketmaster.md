# Ticketmaster

**Ticketmaster** is the largest ticket-sales platform in the United States and a subsidiary of [[live-nation-entertainment|Live Nation Entertainment]].[1]

## Eras Tour presale failure

In late 2022 Ticketmaster's presale for [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]]'s Eras Tour collapsed, with the site crashing as enormous numbers of fans competed against bots for a limited supply of tickets.[1] The episode became a national controversy and a touchstone for complaints about the company's market power.[1]

## Antitrust action

More than a year later, on May 23, 2024, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed an antitrust suit against Live Nation, with Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] declaring, "It is time to break it up."[1] The government alleged that Live Nation and Ticketmaster had inserted themselves "at the center and the edges of virtually every aspect of the live music ecosystem," freezing innovation and imposing real costs on Americans.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Ticketmaster had given ordinary concertgoers problems for years across acts like Dave Matthews Band and Dead & Company without federal intervention, and that only when Swift objected did the DOJ move.[1] She connected the action to the Biden administration's prior focus on criminalizing Ticketmaster's "junk fees."[1]
