# Ron Johnson

Ron Johnson is a Republican United States senator from Wisconsin known for oversight investigations of federal agencies and of the conduct of Trump-related prosecutions.[1]

In July 2026 Johnson joined [[chuck-grassley|Sen. Chuck Grassley]], chair of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]], in releasing a joint report built on whistleblower information and internal Justice Department records concerning former special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]].[1] The report alleged that Smith's prosecutors had obtained and reviewed text messages from 44 members of [[us-congress|Congress]], bypassing standard document-filtering protocols and directly accessing communications that fell outside the scope of their investigations.[1] The disclosures fed broader Republican criticism of the Biden-era Justice Department's handling of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] investigations, which were dropped after Trump's 2024 election victory.[1]

## Oversight of the Biden documents matter

Johnson, together with Senator [[chuck-grassley]], pressed the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]] for information about the handling of classified records associated with [[joe-biden]].[2] Their February 24 letter asked how and when the Archives learned that records had been transported to Boston; Acting Archivist [[debra-steidel-wall]] replied that NARA learned of the transfer on November 3, 2022 and disclosed for the first time that nine boxes had been retrieved from the Boston office of Biden attorney [[patrick-moore]].[2] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] credited Johnson and Grassley with diligently assembling a documentary record on the [[biden-classified-documents-scandal|classified documents scandal]].[2]

## Biden Ukraine source records

Johnson joined Senate Judiciary Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] in releasing two new FBI FD-1023 records documenting confidential-source allegations of Biden family corruption in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], part of the disclosures around the [[fbi-confidential-source-reports-on-biden-ukraine-corruption|FBI confidential source reports on Biden Ukraine corruption]].[3] [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] said there was no record the bureau had sought to thoroughly investigate the claims.[3]
