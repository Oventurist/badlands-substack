# Haemanthus

Haemanthus is an artificial-intelligence startup whose name is Greek for "blood flower."[1] The company hopes to introduce a product that can be used in medical testing and other settings.[1] It was founded by [[billy-evans|Billy Evans]], the partner of [[theranos|Theranos]] founder [[elizabeth-holmes|Elizabeth Holmes]], who has raised millions of dollars for the venture.[1]

As of May 2025 the company had not officially launched, and details of its work were known only through two sources with direct knowledge of the endeavor who could not speak publicly.[1] Those sources reported that Holmes, serving an 11-year federal sentence for misleading Theranos investors, had been providing advice to Evans on the startup from prison.[1]
