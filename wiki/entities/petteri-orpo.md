# Petteri Orpo

Petteri Orpo is the Prime Minister of [[finland|Finland]], in office during the country's 2023 border confrontation with Russia.[1]

## Border dispute with Russia

In November 2023, as Finland's Border Guard reported a sharp rise in undocumented migrants — largely originating in Africa and the Middle East — appearing at crossings along the roughly 830-mile Russian-Finnish frontier, Orpo publicly condemned what he and European Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] called Russia's "instrumentalization of migrants."[1] The Finnish military subsequently bolstered barriers along the border, which also serves as an external border of the European Union.[1] The dispute followed Russia's pledge of "countermeasures" after Finland joined [[nato|NATO]] in April 2023.[1]
