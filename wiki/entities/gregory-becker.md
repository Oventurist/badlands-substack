# Gregory Becker

Gregory Becker was the chief executive officer of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] at the time of the institution's failure in March 2023.[1]

## Share sales before the collapse

In Badlands Media commentary on the bank's failure, contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] noted that Becker sold shares in SVB before the collapse, and that he was not alone: two other SVB executives sold shares as well.[1] The bank also paid out bonuses before the government seized it.[1]

Deschamps allowed that insiders might simply have seen the writing on the wall and moved to "greedily clean out the bank before it went down," but argued that other indications — including a [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]] report that [[jpmorgan-chase|JP Morgan]] had warned certain SVB customers to move their funds over asset-safety concerns — suggested that some parties knew in advance what was coming.[1] He characterised the anticipation of collapse combined with self-enrichment as "a massive breach of fiduciary duty on the part of these trustees of the people's wealth."[1]

## Context

The share sales became a central element of the argument that the [[2023-silicon-valley-bank-run|2023 Silicon Valley Bank run]] was not a purely spontaneous panic but an industry-driven event in which insiders and large venture capital investors moved first.[1]
