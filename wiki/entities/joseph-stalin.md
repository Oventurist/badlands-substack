# Joseph Stalin

Joseph Stalin was the Soviet leader whose faction, described in Badlands Media coverage as "the saner forces," ousted [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] from power in 1927.[1]

## Struggle with Trotsky

Stalin's commitment to [[socialism-in-one-country|"socialism in one country"]] stood against Trotsky's commitment to global socialist revolution, a divide the article describes as unbridgeable between the two revolutionaries.[1] The article argues that an over-simplified reading of Russian history has prevented historians from recognizing the real stakes of the life-and-death battles fought between Trotsky and Stalin during the first twenty post-revolutionary years.[1] Historian [[grover-furr|Grover Furr]] is cited as documenting, from declassified American and Russian archival material, Trotsky's repeated attempts to return to power against Stalin with assistance from fascist forces in Britain, Japan, Ukraine and Germany until 1940.[1]

## Economic consequences

The expulsion of Trotsky coincided with the departure of [[armand-hammer|Armand Hammer]] from Russia; Hammer, who had dominated Soviet–Western business dealings under the [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]], only returned to prominence in Russia after Stalin's death in 1953.[1]
