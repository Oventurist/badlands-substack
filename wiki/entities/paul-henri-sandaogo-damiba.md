# Paul-Henri Sandaogo Damiba

Paul-Henri Sandaogo Damiba is a former President of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]], deposed by the military government now led by Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]].

## Arrest in Togo

As reported in the January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, Damiba was arrested in [[togo|Togo]] and returned to Burkina Faso amid junta allegations that he was linked to a coup and assassination plot against Traoré. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the arrest as occurring "under suspicion that he was involved in an elaborate scheme to kill Traore."

Quoting the underlying reporting, the Brief lists the charges levelled at Damiba by Burkina Faso's military government as "criminal embezzlement of public funds, illicit enrichment, corruption, aggravated receiving of stolen goods and money laundering," according to a statement by Togo's justice ministry. The arrest immediately preceded Traoré's decree dissolving all political parties in Burkina Faso.
