# James Clyburn

James Clyburn is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from South Carolina and a prominent voice in the Congressional Black Caucus.[1]

## Defense of Biden after the 2024 debate

Following the [[2024-presidential-debate|June 2024 presidential debate]], Clyburn was among the House Democrats who rallied behind President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] as calls mounted for him to leave the ticket.[1] Emerging from a closed-door Democratic caucus meeting on Tuesday, July 9, 2024 — a session that ran roughly two hours and in which the debate over Biden's future was described as lopsided in favor of keeping him as the nominee — Clyburn declared, "We're ridin' with Biden."[1] The Congressional Black Caucus, in which he is influential, had rallied aggressively behind Biden since the debate.[1]
