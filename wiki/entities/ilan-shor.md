# Ilan Shor

Ilan Shor is an Israeli-Moldovan businessman identified as a central participant in the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|billion-dollar Moldovan bank fraud]] of 2011–2014.[1] He worked closely with [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] and fellow oligarchs [[vyacheslav-platon|Vyacheslav Platon]] and [[vlad-filat|Vlad Filat]] in the scheme described as the "theft of the century".[1]

Businesses controlled by Shor and others within Plahotniuc's network received fraudulent loans extended through three major financial institutions — Banca de Economii, Unibank and Banca Sociala — that had been taken over by the Moldovan Central Bank, to the tune of roughly $1 billion.[1] Shor was charged with money laundering and embezzlement in absentia, though unlike Filat and Platon he never saw a jail cell.[1]
