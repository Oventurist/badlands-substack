# Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building

The Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, was the target of the April 19, 1995 [[oklahoma-city-bombing|Oklahoma City bombing]].[1]

## The attack

On the morning of April 19, 1995, a Ryder rental truck pulled up to the building; at 9:02 a.m. it exploded, causing a third of the structure to disintegrate from the force of the blast.[1] At least 168 people were killed and more than 680 others injured.[1] The explosion destroyed or damaged 324 other buildings spanning sixteen blocks and destroyed 86 cars, with total damage exceeding $652 million.[1]

## Aftermath and disputed handling

The Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" observes that, as with the World Trade Center complex six years later, the debris from the Murrah building was carted off and destroyed before a thorough examination could be completed, with the result that the official narrative became the account of record despite conflicting reports on the ground — including eyewitness accounts of more than one bomb found in the debris.[1] [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]] was apprehended within ninety minutes and, with [[terry-nichols|Terry Nichols]], linked to the blast by forensic evidence within days.[1] The date falls within the forty-day [[season-of-sacrifice|season of sacrifice]] described by [[mark-passio|Mark Passio]].[1]
