# Metropolitan Police

The Metropolitan Police, commonly the Met Police, is the territorial police force for London and Britain's most prominent law enforcement body. In Badlands coverage it features chiefly through the public statements of its commissioner, Sir [[mark-rowley|Mark Rowley]], on online speech during the 2024 United Kingdom riots.[1]

## Online speech enforcement, August 2024

In August 2024 Rowley warned that "keyboard warriors" could be hit with terrorism charges for inciting riots online, even if they were living abroad, in response to waves of rioting that followed the murder of three young girls at a Taylor Swift dance class in Southport.[1] He said the "full force of the law" would apply not just to those physically involved in the riots but to those making inflammatory comments about them on social media, and that the force would pursue people whether they were "committing crimes on the streets or committing crimes from further afield online."[1]

Rowley enumerated the legal tools available as offences of incitement, stirring up racial hatred, and "numerous terrorist offences regarding the publishing of material," saying all were in play against people provoking hatred and violence.[1] The exchange arose after a Sky News reporter characterized [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] as a high-profile figure "whipping up hatred," despite Musk having only asked Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] why all communities were not protected in Britain.[1]
