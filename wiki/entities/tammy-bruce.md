# Tammy Bruce

Tammy Bruce is the [[state-department|State Department]] spokeswoman in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]

## United Nations two-state solution conference (July 2025)

Bruce drew sharp criticism from Badlands commentators for her handling of the United Nations conference on reviving the two-state solution, held in New York City in late July 2025 and co-chaired by France and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1] At a State Department press briefing, minutes before the conference's signatory declaration was announced, Bruce said: "The UN is serving as host to an unproductive and ill-timed conference on the so-called 'Two-State Solution' in New York City. This is a publicity stunt that comes in the middle of delicate diplomatic efforts to end the conflict in Gaza. Far from promoting peace, the conference will prolong the war, will embolden [[hamas|Hamas]] and reward its obstruction, and undermine real world efforts to achieve real peace."[1]

Shortly afterward, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and other Arab states signed a declaration demanding that Hamas disarm, surrender its weapons to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], and end its rule in Gaza — an outcome commentators described as historic and unprecedented in Arab politics.[1]

## Criticism

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the conduct of Bruce and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] "gross" and a "complete and total disgrace," arguing they had become "hysterical" in dismissing the conference as a publicity stunt out of misplaced loyalty to Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and failing to recognize that the conference had been organized by the Saudis, described as Trump's closest Arab partner.[1] He called for Bruce to resign immediately so that a less biased and more congenial official could take her place.[1]

## Dismissal and echo by Ortagus

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described Bruce as "now-disgraced" and reported that her July 2025 statement dismissing the Saudi-French [[new-york-declaration|New York Declaration]] as a "publicity stunt" and a "gift to Hamas" was what got her fired from the [[state-department|State Department]].[2] He noted with astonishment that US Representative to the UN [[morgan-ortagus|Morgan Ortagus]] subsequently read the same statement word-for-word in response to the [[united-nations-general-assembly|UN General Assembly]]'s endorsement of the declaration in September 2025, apparently unaware of what the move had cost Bruce.[2]

## Opposition to the UN two-state conference (July 2025)

As [[state-department|State Department]] spokeswoman, Bruce issued a statement condemning the [[2025-un-two-state-solution-conference|high-level United Nations conference]] convened in New York on July 28, 2025, on the peaceful settlement of the Palestinian issue, and — in the reading of Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] — the entire concept of a [[two-state-solution|two-state solution]].[3] The commentary described Bruce as "an ardent Zionist and shameless apologist" for the Israeli government, contrasting her position with earlier American diplomatic support for Palestinian statehood.[3]


## Nomination as deputy UN ambassador (2025)

In August 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nominated Bruce, then [[state-department|State Department]] spokeswoman, to serve as the United States deputy representative to the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[4] Announcing the pick on Truth Social, Trump said Bruce, who had worked at the State Department since he took office in January, had done a "fantastic job" in the role.[4] Before entering government she had been a Fox News conservative contributor for more than twenty years and authored several books critical of liberals, including *Fear Itself: Exposing the Left's Mind-Killing Agenda*.[4] It was unclear when she would assume the post if confirmed by the Senate.[4]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the nomination as a demotion, attributing it to her attack on Saudi Arabia for spearheading a two-state-solution conference that she dismissed as a public-relations stunt rewarding [[hamas|Hamas]] — a position undercut minutes later when the conference issued a declaration calling on Hamas to surrender to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]].[4]
