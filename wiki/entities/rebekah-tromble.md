# Rebekah Tromble

Rebekah Tromble is an academic researcher in the online-disinformation field, named in Badlands Media coverage as a participant in a panel of "censorship industry insiders" discussing the sector's prospects after the dismantling of U.S. government censorship programs in 2025.[1]

## Panel remarks

The article quotes panel commentary — attributed to the assembled researchers and framed around Tromble's role — stating that "if it weren't for Europe right now, I think that I would feel pretty defeated and despondent in this moment," and that direct engagement with platform staff had become far harder "because there are simply fewer of them."[1] The speaker described years spent "building up relationships" with people inside the platforms who were "trying to do the right thing," most of whom were said to be gone, leaving researchers unsure whom to contact.[1]

The remarks concluded that without the European Union and the [[digital-services-act|Digital Services Act]] there would be little hope of "rectifying this situation," and expressed the expectation that the DSA coming into full force would lead to "a sort of re-staffing of some of these positions."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] presented these statements as an admission that American disinformation researchers were relying on a foreign jurisdiction's censorship law — enforced by fines potentially reaching billions of dollars — to pressure U.S. platforms, particularly X, into rehiring moderation staff dismissed by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1] Tromble appeared alongside [[katie-harbath|Katie Harbath]] and [[dean-jackson|Dean Jackson]].[1]
