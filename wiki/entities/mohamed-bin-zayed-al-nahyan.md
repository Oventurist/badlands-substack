# Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan

Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, commonly abbreviated MBZ, is the President of the United Arab Emirates and a central figure in Gulf geopolitics.

## February 2026 death rumors

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief devoted a segment, headed "PSYOP: Rumors of MBZ Death Greatly Exaggerated…Maybe," to a wave of online claims that bin Zayed had died. According to Arab News, Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] posted on X that Sunday that he was postponing a planned visit to Abu Dhabi because bin Zayed had a "health problem." The post, later deleted, described a phone call in which "President Erdogan expressed his sadness over the health problem experienced by (President) Al Nahyan and wished him a prompt recovery," and said Erdogan would travel to the UAE at a later date. As of the Brief's publication there was no official confirmation that bin Zayed was deceased.

The Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry described X users spending a day declaring the Emir dead, and said he could not determine whether Erdogan's deleted post initiated the story or merely accelerated it. He noted that both the Turkish and Emirati governments issued statements about the phone call but that these satisfied no one. He tied the episode to what he characterized as a concerted effort to drive a wedge between [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the UAE, with both states using the shifting dynamic to negotiate new bilateral agreements with regional partners including [[india|India]] and [[pakistan|Pakistan]]. Because he regards Erdogan as aligned with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], he speculated that the entire affair was a ruse intended to sow confusion while geopolitical arrangements were reshaped.
