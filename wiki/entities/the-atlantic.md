# The Atlantic

*The Atlantic* is an American magazine of politics, culture and ideas.

## In the Badlands critique

A Swan Song for the MSM groups The Atlantic with [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] and the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] as "mainstream, historically spook-friendly outlets" whose hiring practices favor writers already inclined to reproduce establishment narratives. [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]], the article's principal case study, previously worked for the magazine, and in the TikTok remarks quoted in the piece she cites The Atlantic alongside the Post as a billionaire-owned outlet that has nonetheless carried out layoffs.


## April 2026: the Kash Patel report

The Brief of April 20, 2026 covers the Atlantic's report alleging that FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] has a serious drinking problem, has been "unreachable" at moments, and has been intoxicated while on the job. The Brief notes attorney Jesse Binnall's dispute of the claims and a [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] opinion article arguing the magazine could face legal consequences, while Badlands commentary treats the report as a hit piece timed to blunt the "justice phase" of the [[rico-grande|RICO Grande]] narrative.

## July 2026: "Lab-Leak Payback"

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that *The Atlantic* published a piece lamenting that justice is coming for the scientists of the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]], quoted as observing: "The precise timing of these cases, which have all come to light since April, may be a coincidence. Yet all five are centered on a small community of scientists affiliated with the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, which Fauci ran for nearly 40 years." Badlands commentary (Ashe in America) reads the magazine as one of the pandemic era's complicit "central narrative deployers," recalling its October 2022 "desperate plea for amnesty," and frames the prosecutions — the debarment of [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]], the indictment of [[david-morens|David Morens]], the removal of [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]], the indictment of [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]] and Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]]'s subpoena of [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] — as the "Justice Phase" preceding a "Golden Age."
