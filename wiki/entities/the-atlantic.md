# The Atlantic

*The Atlantic* is an American magazine of politics, culture and ideas.

## In the Badlands critique

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] groups The Atlantic with [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] and the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] as "mainstream, historically spook-friendly outlets" whose hiring practices favor writers already inclined to reproduce establishment narratives. [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]], the article's principal case study, previously worked for the magazine, and in the TikTok remarks quoted in the piece she cites The Atlantic alongside the Post as a billionaire-owned outlet that has nonetheless carried out layoffs.
