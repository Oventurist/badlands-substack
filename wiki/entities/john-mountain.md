# John Mountain

John Mountain is a British-born celebrity chef based in Perth, Australia, who became the focus of international culture-war commentary in June 2023 after banning vegan customers from his restaurant.[1]

## Fyre restaurant ban

Mountain, aged 55 at the time, operated the Perth restaurant Fyre. Following a negative review left by a non-meat-eating customer, he declared "I'm done with vegans. I'm absolutely done, done, done with vegans," and responded to the reviewer with the profane remark that vegans "can f*** off."[1] He subsequently implemented a blanket ban on vegan customers at the restaurant, citing mental health reasons, a decision first reported by the U.K. Daily Mail and relayed in the United States by [[western-journal|The Western Journal]].[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the episode as a springboard for a broader argument about consumer entitlement and the rights of business owners.[1] Deschamps argued that most people no longer understand their rights or how to defend them, leaving small operators exposed to bad-faith reviews for services that were never promised or advertised.[1] He compared the situation to online retail, where dissatisfaction over a late delivery is typically directed at a listing or platform rather than the party actually responsible, producing what he described as an enormous volume of misdirected commercial grievance that harms innocent parties.[1]

Deschamps stressed that the problem was not veganism as such but entitlement — the belief that membership in a social or protected class confers a right to services, benefits, discounts, or preferential treatment.[1] He linked the ban to a wider pattern of non-compliance with what he called culturally reinforced entitlement, listing masking, mandatory vaccination, and CBDC QR codes as policies imposed through coercion rather than agreement, and suggested that earlier displays of the restaurateur's willingness to refuse might have blunted later draconian measures.[1]
