# Holtec International

Holtec International is an American energy technology company active in nuclear plant decommissioning, spent-fuel storage and reactor development, and the owner of the [[palisades-nuclear-plant]] in Michigan.[1]

## Palisades restart

In September 2024 the [[department-of-energy]] finalized a $1.5 billion loan backing Holtec's effort to revive Palisades, which would be the first restart of a closed nuclear plant in United States history.[1] The transaction was widely presented as a marker of the broader [[nuclear-renaissance]] driven by surging electricity demand from AI data centers and by policy support in Washington and Silicon Valley alike.[1]

## Palisades restart

In March 2024, the Loan Programs Office of the US Energy Department, led by [[jigar-shah|Jigar Shah]], approved a loan to Holtec to reopen the [[palisades-nuclear-plant|Palisades nuclear plant]] in Michigan.[2] Holtec had bought the facility when it closed in 2022 with plans to decommission it, but officials including Governor Gretchen Whitmer began almost immediately calling for its reopening; the company changed course and planned for the plant to resume producing power in late 2025, a shift observers expected could spur other operators to follow.[2] The restart followed a $1.5 billion federal offer to bring the shuttered plant back online, and formed part of a broader sense within industry and government that further revivals were likely.[2]
