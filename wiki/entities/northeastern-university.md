# Northeastern University

Northeastern University is an American research university in Boston. Badlands Media cites its researchers, working jointly with [[imdea-networks|IMDEA Networks]], for a study exposing security and privacy threats in [[smart-homes|smart homes]].[1]

## Smart home privacy research

Scientists from Northeastern and IMDEA Networks demonstrated a range of security and privacy threats arising from local network interactions among Internet of Things devices and mobile apps, examining 93 different IoT devices and apps. Their study, "In the Room Where It Happens: Characterizing Local Communication and Threats in Smart Homes," was presented at the ACM Internet Measurement Conference in Montreal.[1]

David Choffnes, associate professor of computer science and executive director of Northeastern's Cybersecurity and Privacy Institute, said that although people think of their homes as trusted and private, smart devices are "piercing that veil of trust and privacy — in ways that allow nearly any company to learn what devices are in your home, to know when you are home, and learn where your home is," adding that these behaviors are generally not disclosed to consumers.[1]

The researchers responsibly disclosed the vulnerabilities to affected IoT vendors and Google's Android Security Team, resulting in security updates for some products.[1]
