# American Birth Control League

The **American Birth Control League (ABCL)** was the organization founded by [[margaret-sanger|Margaret Sanger]] that eventually became [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[1]

Its birth control clinical studies were proposed by Sanger and funded by the [[bureau-of-social-hygiene|Bureau of Social Hygiene]], the body that [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] took charge of after creating the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] in 1913.[1]

Badlands Media places the league squarely within the American [[eugenics]] movement, noting Sanger's advocacy of limiting "dysgenic stocks" — Black, Hispanic, American Indian and Catholic populations — along with "slum dwellers" such as Jewish immigrants.[1]
