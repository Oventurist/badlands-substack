# César Vásquez

César Vásquez is the health minister of [[peru|Peru]] at the time of the May 2024 decree that officially categorised transgender and intersex people as "mentally ill."[1]

## Role in the 2024 decree

The health ministry under Vásquez justified the classification as the only means by which Peru's public health services could "guarantee full coverage of medical attention for mental health."[1] Amid the resulting backlash, Vásquez himself failed to comment on the row, instead publicly defending President [[dina-boluarte|Dina Boluarte]], who was under investigation for the unexplained acquisition of expensive jewellery.[1]
