# David Axelrod

David Axelrod is a Democratic political strategist and commentator, best known as a chief adviser to Barack Obama.

## Comments on Biden's diagnosis (2025)

After the announcement of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s aggressive prostate cancer in May 2025, Axelrod said he believed conversations about Biden's "mental acuity" would become more muted as a result of the news.[1] Badlands writer Ashe in America rejected that framing, writing that she would not "play along with the party of false decorum" and arguing that Axelrod should "sit this one out, since he is likely a subject of the coming investigation" into the [[autopen-scandal|autopen]] and the conduct of the Biden presidency.[1] The remarks came in the same news cycle as the release of the [[hur-report|Hur]] interview audio and the [[biden-prostate-cancer-diagnosis|cancer diagnosis]].[1]
