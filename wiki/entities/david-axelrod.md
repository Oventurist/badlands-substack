# David Axelrod

David Axelrod is a Democratic political strategist and commentator, best known as a chief adviser to Barack Obama.

## Comments on Biden's diagnosis (2025)

After the announcement of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s aggressive prostate cancer in May 2025, Axelrod said he believed conversations about Biden's "mental acuity" would become more muted as a result of the news.[1] Badlands writer Ashe in America rejected that framing, writing that she would not "play along with the party of false decorum" and arguing that Axelrod should "sit this one out, since he is likely a subject of the coming investigation" into the [[autopen-scandal|autopen]] and the conduct of the Biden presidency.[1] The remarks came in the same news cycle as the release of the [[hur-report|Hur]] interview audio and the [[biden-prostate-cancer-diagnosis|cancer diagnosis]].[1]

## Marie Antoinette comments (December 2025)

In an interview on CNN's "Erin Burnett OutFront" broadcast Monday, December 29, 2025, Axelrod said President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s focus on lavish renovations at the White House and the Kennedy Center could hurt Republicans on the ballot in the 2026 midterms, comparing him to Marie Antoinette.[2] "I think this is a huge political problem for him," he said. "It's a symbol of his distraction. It's the Marie Antoinette thing that he's got going."[2] He added that Trump himself was not on the ballot but that "Republicans should be really concerned about what they're seeing."[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded by noting that Axelrod had been chief strategist for [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s Senate and presidential campaigns in 2004, 2008 and 2012 and Senior Advisor to the President during Obama's first term.[2] Characterizing the 2008 and 2012 campaigns as "the primitive Smurfing pilots," she argued that Axelrod "wasn't a staffer, he was the strategist," and concluded that rather than giving interviews about the midterms, "he should be in prison."[2]
