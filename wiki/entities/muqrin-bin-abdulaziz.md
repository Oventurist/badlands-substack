# Muqrin bin Abdulaziz

Prince Muqrin is described in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] Chapter 14 as a member of the Saudi royal house who was killed during the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]], and as the son of former Crown Prince Abdulaziz.[1]

## Death and the Q reading

The prince died in what was reported as a helicopter crash.[1] Q drop #99, dated November 5th, 2017, asks who was killed in Saudi Arabia, and the Badlands commentary answers by distinguishing Muqrin's confirmed death from reports that Prince Aziz bin Fahd had been killed while resisting — reports the article says were later refuted.[1]

The chapter then reads the death through drop #259, which states that seven of ten plane crashes are targeted kills; the author extends this to helicopter crashes and argues Muqrin's aircraft was shot down as he attempted to escape the round-up.[1] Drop #259 arrived roughly a month after the purge drops, a sequencing the article treats as significant.[1]

## Surrounding events

The article situates the death within a short chain of incidents: the Las Vegas shooting a little over a month earlier, which the author says had Saudi involvement written all over it; a ballistic missile fired from Yemen at Riyadh the day before the crackdown, which was intercepted and which Q attributed to friendly forces; and then the crackdown itself.[1] From this the author concludes that [[white-hats|white hats]] were operating inside Saudi Arabia and that the events were a deliberate response.[1] Drop #259's reference to 9/11 alongside crashes is used to reinforce the argument, given the claimed Saudi role in the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]
