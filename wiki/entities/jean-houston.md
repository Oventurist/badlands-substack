# Jean Houston

**Jean Houston** is quoted in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites her line, "We are the ancestors of a future age," and presents it to the reader "not as theory, but as activation," arguing that "the future breathes through you" and that a person's frequency — the way they love, pause, speak and create — "sends signals into a timeline still forming."[1]

Her quotation appears in the concluding section "Living It Forward," after [[dan-siegel|Dan Siegel]] on integration, and introduces the essay's closing images of presence as "a tuning fork" and of [[coherence|coherence]] as "a doorway, not into escape, but into embodiment."[1]
