# Rick Rieder

Rick Rieder is [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s Chief Investment Officer for Global Fixed Income and Chief Bond Investment Manager.[1] In January 2026 he was reported to be among the candidates considered to replace [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] as Chair of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], on a shortlist vetted by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]].[1]

Some reports on January 29, 2026 alleged that Rieder had been informed he would not receive the nomination.[1] Like the other candidates — [[kevin-warsh|Kevin Warsh]], [[kevin-hassett|Kevin Hassett]], and [[christopher-waller|Christopher Waller]] — he was characterized as supportive of cutting interest rates.[1]
