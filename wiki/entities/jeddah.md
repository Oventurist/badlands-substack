# Jeddah

Jeddah is the Saudi Arabian city identified in the article as the birthplace of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] and flagged as a recurring node in the series' account of the network.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The author instructs readers to "remember that very important data point," adding that "a lot of roads lead to Jeddah."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The city's significance in the article derives from the convergence it represents between Saudi royal wealth and Lebanese lineage — Alwaleed's father was the Saudi royal Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud and his mother Mona El Solh was the daughter of Riad El Solh, the first Prime Minister of Lebanon.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The same Saudi-birth and Lebanese-descent combination attaches to "Basel," the [[coalition-provisional-authority|CPA]] official who received $40 billion in cash in Baghdad, which the article treats as more than coincidence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)
