# Natcast

Natcast is a Silicon Valley-based nonprofit selected by the Biden administration to lead a $7.4 billion federal semiconductor initiative, and the subject of a September 2025 funding seizure by the [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Department]] under Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]].[1]

## Mandate

Natcast brought together premier American chip companies and research universities under a multi-billion-dollar contract intended to secure the United States' place as the epicenter of semiconductor advancement.[1] The initiative was devised as the linchpin of America's semiconductor resurgence, and both President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and lawmakers of both parties continued to recognize the underlying directive as essential to the global technology race with China.[1]

## Commerce Department funding seizure

In late September 2025 the Commerce Department abruptly yanked Natcast's funding, leaving the program in limbo, triggering mass layoffs, and pushing the organization to the brink of shutdown.[1] The little-known nonprofit came to public attention when Lutnick derided it as a "semiconductor slush fund that did nothing but line the pockets of Biden loyalists with American tax dollars."[1] Lutnick grounded his incursion in the claim that the organization was not on sound legal footing, saying his goal was to keep control of federal funds.[1] Politico reported the episode after speaking with more than 50 people, many of whom requested anonymity to avoid potential retaliation, in what it described as the most extensive account to date of the fallout from the decision to reclaim billions of dollars approved under former President Joe Biden.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] argued that headlines about the seizure become comical once one accepts "switch theory" — the Badlands contention that the chips initiative was Trump's program to begin with, executed through the intervening administration.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] elaborated that the switch, discussed for years alongside [[devolution]], holds that the "Fake Biden Admin" did not stop the reset but accelerated it, allowing Trump to flip the switch to the Golden Age while the prior regime absorbed responsibility for the transition.[1]
