# Kasie Hunt

Kasie Hunt is a [[cnn|CNN]] anchor who drew widespread criticism in June 2024 after abruptly ending a live interview with a Trump campaign spokesperson who had begun discussing the network's bias.[1]

## The Leavitt interview

Days before the CNN-hosted 2024 presidential debate, Trump 2024 national press secretary [[karoline-leavitt|Karoline Leavitt]] appeared on Hunt's program.[1] Seconds after Leavitt began to discuss CNN's historical bias against her candidate — specifically the record of debate moderator [[jake-tapper|Jake Tapper]] — Hunt "ma'am'd" her, cut her microphone, and ended the interview.[1] Hunt attempted to defend her handling of the segment in a post on X, which was met with further derision on social media.[1] The Trump campaign's response was that the incident "proved our point."[1]

Aggregated reporting noted that Tapper, the colleague Hunt insisted be "respected," was himself at the center of a defamation suit brought by a consulting company alleging that the network and one of its reporters lied about the company's work; CNN's defense was that it did not intend to harm and that its language was "opinion or ambiguous."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Hunt had failed at the institutional journalist's task of suppressing bias during debate week and embarrassed herself when confronted with what she called the long-accepted truth that Tapper is a political hack.[1] Because Tapper was moderating the debate and would control a mute button, his bias was directly relevant to the story; Hunt's refusal to engage and her decision to mute her guest instead, in this reading, simply meant she was bad at her job.[1] The commentary added that the practical effect was limited because CNN's audience is small.[1]
