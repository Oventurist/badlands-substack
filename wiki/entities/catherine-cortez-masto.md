# Catherine Cortez Masto

Catherine Cortez Masto (D-Nev.) is a United States Senator who, together with Sen. [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]] (D-Ore.), introduced the [[change-corruption-act|Change Corruption Act]] in December 2025.[1] The legislation would bar any sitting or living former president from being featured on U.S. currency, thwarting the Treasury Department's plan to issue a commemorative $1 coin bearing the image of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The bill was cosponsored by Democratic Senators Ron Wyden of Oregon and Richard Blumenthal of Connecticut.[1]
