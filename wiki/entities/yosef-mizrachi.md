# Yosef Mizrachi

**Rabbi Yosef Mizrachi** is an Orthodox Jewish preacher cited in Badlands commentary for a sermon in which he openly mocks and ridicules [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionists]] who support Israel in the belief that Jewish Zionists will eventually accept Jesus as the messiah — referring to Jesus derisively as "JC Penny."[1]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry invoked the sermon as evidence that the theological expectations of Christian Zionist supporters of Israel are not reciprocated, arguing that plans for the [[third-temple|Third Temple]] are oriented not toward Jesus but toward a figure described as the Mashiach — a mortal ruler whom he characterized as resembling the [[antichrist|Antichrist]] rather than Christ.[1]
