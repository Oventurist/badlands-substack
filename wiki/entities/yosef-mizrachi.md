# Yosef Mizrachi

Yosef Mizrachi is a rabbi whose sermons have circulated in Badlands commentary on the religious dimensions of Israeli–Christian relations.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited a sermon in which Mizrachi openly mocks and ridicules [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionists]] who support Israel in the belief that Jewish Zionists will one day accept Jesus as messiah, using "JC Penny" as an epithet for Jesus.[1] The writer offered the sermon as evidence that expectations of mass conversion tied to building the [[third-temple|Third Temple]] are misplaced, and that the messiah anticipated by those practitioners is a mortal ruler he likened to [[the-antichrist|the Antichrist]].[1]
