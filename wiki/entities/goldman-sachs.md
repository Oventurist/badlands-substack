# Goldman Sachs

Goldman Sachs is a global investment bank headquartered in New York City, chaired and led in 2026 by [[david-solomon|David Solomon]].

## Role in the Singham funding network

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] reports that the GS Donor Advised Philanthropy Fund For Wealth Management Inc., described as Goldman Sachs' philanthropy arm in New York City, received $110,376,701 of the roughly $278 million that [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] allegedly moved from Shanghai into the United States. That channel, alongside two now-defunct shell companies, is said to have fed six nonprofits including [[peoples-forum|People's Forum Inc.]], [[tricontinental|Tricontinental Ltd.]] and [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]].

The fund's role has drawn direct government attention. The Brief reports that Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] met with Solomon earlier in 2026 in New York and delivered a message urging cooperation with investigators while warning of potential scrutiny of the philanthropy fund. The federal grand jury inquiry led by U.S. Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] has issued subpoenas for bank records and financial documents from organizations in the network.
