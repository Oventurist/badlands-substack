# Andrew Clyde

Andrew Clyde is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Georgia.[1]

## FairTax Day resolution

In April 2024 Clyde joined fellow Georgia Republican [[buddy-carter|Buddy Carter]] in introducing a resolution that would declare April 16 — the day after Tax Day — as "FairTax Day."[1] The resolution formed part of H.R. 25, the [[fairtax-act|FairTax Act]], legislation that would replace the federal tax code with a national consumption tax and abolish both the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] and Tax Day.[1] The sponsors argued the resolution would demonstrate a readiness to begin a process returning control over paychecks to taxpayers.[1]

## Stabilizing brace repeal resolution (2023)

Clyde was the sponsor of the resolution to repeal the [[bureau-of-alcohol-tobacco-firearms-and-explosives|ATF]]'s [[stabilizing-brace-rule|stabilizing brace rule]], which the House passed 219–210 on June 13, 2023, nearly along party lines with two Democrats in favor and two Republicans opposed.[2] The floor debate was contentious, with Republicans accusing the administration of "executive overreach" and Democrats arguing the measure would "help kill people."[2] The resolution moved to the [[united-states-senate|Senate]], which was expected to consider it within the week, though President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had promised a veto that neither chamber appeared able to override.[2]
