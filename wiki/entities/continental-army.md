# Continental Army

The Continental Army was the regular military force of the American colonies during the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]], commanded by [[george-washington|George Washington]] and, in the southern theater of 1780, by General [[horatio-gates|Horatio Gates]].[1]

## Collapse in the South, 1780

[[francis-marion|Francis Marion]] served five years as a commissioned Continental officer, rising through the ranks through hard drilling of his men and the diligent construction of coastal fortifications in anticipation that the British would return the war to the southern theater.[1] When the British took Charleston in the spring of 1780, an entire Continental Army of roughly five thousand men was trapped inside the city and spent the remainder of the war on prison ships.[1] A few months later a second Continental Army that had marched south to liberate Charleston was destroyed at the [[battle-of-camden|Battle of Camden]].[1]

With all colonial and militia forces driven from South Carolina, nothing appeared to stand between the [[british-empire|British]] and Washington in Virginia.[1] The article notes that Washington never developed a taste for cavalry, which is why the Continental Army went a considerable time without any horseback troops — a gap [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]] exploited.[1] Continental dragoons nevertheless operated in South Carolina, and it was a party of them escorting Governor [[john-rutledge|John Rutledge]] northward that joined the Virginia militia destroyed at the [[waxhaws-massacre|Waxhaws]].[1]

## Marion's break with the regular army

Marion's disillusionment is dated to a Charleston cocktail party in the spring of 1780, where he found himself surrounded by flamboyant officers in powdered wigs and tights who seemed unconcerned by the approach of [[henry-clinton|Henry Clinton]]'s army.[1] When the host locked the doors — a common practice of the day — Marion concluded that the Continental Army was not the outfit he had thought it was, excused himself upstairs, found an unlocked window and jumped, severely breaking his ankle.[1] Thereafter he operated without logistical support from any government or military authority.[1]
