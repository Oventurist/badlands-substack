# Wisconsin Elections Commission

The Wisconsin Elections Commission is the state agency that administers elections in Wisconsin. In the Badlands corpus it appears in connection with the [[fbi|FBI]]'s investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election.

## Role in the 2020 election probe

The May 2026 Badlands Brief reports that federal agents spoke with Commission Deputy Administrator [[robert-kehoe|Robert Kehoe]] as part of the investigation into Milwaukee's 2020 election administration. Wisconsin's 2020 ballots still exist despite standard retention timelines, partly due to ongoing litigation brought by a New London man who sued state and local election officials over issues tied to the 2020 election — litigation that has kept the ballots available to investigators. The probe also involves the flash-drive incident at the [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] under former Executive Director [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]].
