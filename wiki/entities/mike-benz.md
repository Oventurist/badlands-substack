# Mike Benz

Mike Benz is a commentator on United States foreign policy and the censorship apparatus, cited in the Badlands corpus chiefly for a term of art he popularized.

## "The Blob"

[[assad-day-in-damascus]] refers to "the Western foreign policy and national security monstrosity—what Mike Benz affectionately refers to as 'the Blob.'" In the article's usage, the Blob names the dense network of agencies, think tanks, contractors and non-governmental organizations — including the [[cia]], the [[state-department|State Department]], the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]] and the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]] — whose overlapping personnel and shared assumptions produce a foreign policy continuous across administrations.

The concept underpins two of the article's claims: that anyone who seeks a negotiated outcome deviating "from the wishes of the blob" is slandered as a Russian agent, as the source says happened to [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]; and that the State Department "and its NGO blob" orchestrated the 2014 [[color-revolution]] in Ukraine.
