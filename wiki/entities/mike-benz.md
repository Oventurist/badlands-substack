# Mike Benz

Mike Benz is a commentator on the Western foreign policy and national security establishment, cited by Badlands Media as the popularizer of the term "[[the-blob|the Blob]]" for that apparatus.[1]

The article credits Benz with affectionately referring to the "Western foreign policy and national security monstrosity" as the Blob, using the label to frame its discussion of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] and the network of agencies behind the December 2024 regime change in [[syria|Syria]].[1]

## Research on the marine mammal program (2026)

In May 2026, as legacy outlets promoted the [[suicide-dolphins-narrative|"suicide dolphins" story]] about [[iran|Iran]], Benz researched the underlying subject and concluded that weaponized marine mammals originated as an American program run by the [[cia|CIA]] rather than an Iranian innovation.[2] He said the agency had already trained dolphins and seals to plant bombs on underwater pipelines, a finding Badlands writers connected to speculation about the destruction of the Nord Stream pipeline.[2] Benz further described CIA special operations units killing dolphins out of suspicion that any given animal could be a threat, which [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted as the most striking detail of the episode.[2]

## Badlands interview on censorship and irregular warfare (2024)

In March 2024, Badlands Media published a special interview in which Benz answered questions from [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on censorship and irregular warfare; Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the unfiltered discussion as must-watch.[3]

## Smith-Mundt Act argument on Joe Rogan (March 2025)

Benz, a former U.S. Department of State official, appeared on [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s podcast in March 2025, where he argued that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] repealed the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]] — the statute that had barred government messaging produced for foreign audiences from being disseminated on U.S. soil.[4] Benz said the repeal allowed the use of propaganda against Americans, including the infiltration of U.S. universities, unions, media, politicians, and judges through covert means.[4]

"So the Smith–Mundt Act was always designed to say, listen, you can do this dirty stuff abroad, but it can't come home," Benz said. "We had that protection, which lasted for 70 years, and we only lost it a decade ago [during Obama's administration]."[4] The segment was clipped and posted by the account Wall Street Apes on X and reposted by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] with the caption "Restore the Smith-Mundt act!"[4] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] was less optimistic about statutory remedies, writing that anyone who believes repealing the Smith-Mundt Modernization Act will stop government propaganda "has consumed too much government propaganda."[4]

## Alternate elector revelations (2023)

As executive director of the [[foundation-for-freedom-online|Foundation for Freedom Online]], Benz laid out evidence on Twitter in July 2023 that the Biden campaign had planned to send its own slates of alternate electors in the event it lost the 2020 election, plans he said were written and overseen by [[john-podesta|John Podesta]].[5] The disclosures formed part of Benz's wider reporting, running since December 2022, on the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]] — a non-governmental effort led by Podesta and the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]] to shape the outcome of the 2020 election — which he said had spent the summer of 2020 wargaming how to overturn the results if Trump won.[5] Benz's account also placed former FBI general counsel [[james-baker|James Baker]] at the head of the National Task Force on Election Crises.[5]

## "USAID Truman Show" claim (February 2025)

In February 2025, Benz posted on X that audiences had been "living your whole [life] in a carefully constructed USAID Truman Show, where none of the institutions you meet – from the media, to public health, to universities, to NGOs, to terrorists – are the institutions you think you are."[6] [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] quoted the post, adding, "It's More Than Just USAID, but … yes."[6] The exchange circulated as the second Trump administration moved to dismantle the [[usaid|U.S. Agency for International Development]], and Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted that the claim had been dismissed as conspiracy theory only weeks earlier.[6]

## NewsGuard and the Deitz Nuutzen Hoax (2024)

Benz spent months in 2024 publicizing the activities of [[newsguard|NewsGuard]] on X, an effort Badlands writers credited when incoming [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] chairman [[brendan-carr|Brendan Carr]] made the firm a target of his "censorship cartel" inquiry in November 2024.[7] Benz also briefly fell for a viral hoax claiming that a 1721 painting by "Deitz Nuützen" had predicted a photograph of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] eating McDonald's, before pointing out that the purported artist's name was a rendering of the "deez nuts" joke.[7]

Benz also weighed in on the viral outrage over the New York seizure and euthanasia of [[peanut-the-squirrel|Peanut the squirrel]], asking on X in the final weekend before the November 2024 election, "Are we gonna win this election because of this f'ing squirrel?"[8] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] answered that from a narrative standpoint the answer was decisively yes, adding that "in US elections, the narrative is all there is."[8]
