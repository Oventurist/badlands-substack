# Atlanta Journal-Constitution

The **Atlanta Journal-Constitution** (AJC) is Georgia's principal daily newspaper and the dominant outlet covering state politics and election administration.[1]

## Coverage of the 2023 election discrepancies

The AJC's reporting on the November 2023 municipal elections became itself a subject of controversy. In an article on hand counts spreading across Georgia, the paper reported that "there were no signs of miscounts or voting machine errors in Spalding County," while noting that "deep distrust of Georgia's voting machines drove the county election board to require a hand recount this week."[1] Critics writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] characterized this as an attempt to get ahead of the questions raised by the hand-count results, and noted that the same outlet published a separate report the same day stating that all [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] elections were headed for a recount.[1]

The AJC also reported the setting of a January 9, 2024 trial date in [[curling-v-raffensperger|*Curling et al v. Raffensperger et al*]] before Judge [[amy-totenberg|Amy Totenberg]], and carried Deputy Secretary of State [[jordan-fuchs|Jordan Fuchs]]'s statement that "we don't negotiate with election deniers."[1] It was likewise the outlet to which Kristin Nabers of [[all-voting-is-local|All Voting Is Local]] gave her assessment that hand counts were "part of a push by bad-faith actors."[1]
