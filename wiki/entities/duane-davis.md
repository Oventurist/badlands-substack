# Duane Davis

Duane "Keffe D" Davis is the suspect charged in Nevada in connection with the 1996 death of the rapper [[tupac-shakur|Tupac Shakur]].[1]

## Trial postponement

In February 2025, Davis's scheduled murder trial was postponed. The decision was announced on Tuesday, February 18, 2025 by Clark County District Court Judge Carli Kierny, who cited recent defense developments and the necessity of ensuring a fair trial.[1] Initially slated for March 2025, the prosecution was rescheduled for February 9, 2026.[1]

"It looks like there are quite a few things that are left to be done to get this case prepared so that Mr. Davis can have effective assistance of counsel," Judge Kierny explained.[1] The court also mandated a status hearing over the following summer, requiring both the prosecution and defense teams to attend.[1]
