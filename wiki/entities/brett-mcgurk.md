# Brett McGurk

Brett McGurk served as the longtime Middle East negotiator for President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], acting as the administration's principal envoy in the multi-year effort to secure a ceasefire and hostage release in Gaza.[1]

## Role in the January 2025 Gaza Deal

When Qatar's prime minister announced on January 15, 2025 that a ceasefire-for-hostage deal had been struck, representatives of two American administrations were on hand in Doha.[1] McGurk had been joined in the final days of negotiation by [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], the Middle East envoy for President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], for the closing push.[1] A senior Biden administration official described the cooperation between the outgoing and incoming teams as "almost unprecedented," made possible by a rare intersection of interests between bitter rivals who both saw an opening after Trump's November victory.[1] The resulting framework was reported to involve a 42-day ceasefire and a prisoner exchange including all Israelis taken captive in the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] incursion.[1] After the announcement, both the incoming and outgoing presidents took full credit, a sign that the poisonous relationship between them endured.[1]

## Lebanon mission with Amos Hochstein (October 2024)

In late October 2024, McGurk and fellow senior Biden adviser [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]] were reported to be arriving in Israel on Thursday, October 31 to try to close a deal ending the war in Lebanon and permitting displaced civilians from both sides of the border to return home.[2] A successful agreement between Israel and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] would have amounted to the first significant de-escalation of the regional war since the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attacks.[2]

Officials on both sides said Hezbollah appeared willing at last to disconnect itself from Hamas in Gaza after the losses it had absorbed over the preceding two months, including the assassination of Hassan Nasrallah, and that the fighting could end within weeks.[2] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] convened ministers and senior figures from the Israel Defense Forces and the intelligence services on the Tuesday night before the visit to weigh the potential deal.[2]

## Regional talks, July 2024

As White House Coordinator for the Middle East and North Africa, McGurk was in the region for talks with U.S. partners at the end of July 2024, alongside CIA Director [[william-burns|Bill Burns]]'s Rome meetings, as the Biden administration pressed for a ceasefire and hostage-release deal — an effort thrown into doubt by the assassination of [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran.[3]
