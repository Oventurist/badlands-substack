# John Kiriakou

John Kiriakou is a former [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] officer known publicly for his disclosures regarding the agency's post-9/11 interrogation program and for his subsequent prosecution, which made him a recurring reference point in debates over press freedom and the treatment of national-security dissenters.

In the March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief, Kiriakou is cited from an [[rt|RT]] program aired March 15, 2026, in which he said he was "genuinely worried" about the safety of journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]. His concern followed Carlson's claim that the CIA might pursue a criminal referral under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] over communications with individuals in [[iran|Iran]] before the outbreak of war. Kiriakou's intervention is used in the Brief to lend an insider's weight to the argument that the referral threat represents an intelligence-community move against a domestic critic rather than a routine legal matter.
