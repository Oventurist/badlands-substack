# John Kiriakou

John Kiriakou is a former officer of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] who, by 2026, appeared as a commentator on international broadcast programs including RT.[1]

On an RT program aired March 15, 2026, Kiriakou said he was "genuinely worried" about the safety of journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].[1] His concern followed Carlson's claim that the CIA might pursue a criminal referral under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] relating to communications with individuals in [[iran|Iran]] before the outbreak of the war.[1] Badlands coverage presented Kiriakou's warning as corroboration, from someone with agency experience, that the pressure on Carlson was more than rhetorical.[1]
