# Gabriel Attal

Gabriel Attal is a French politician serving as prime minister in the government of [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]].[1]

In February 2024, an exclusive IFOP poll projected that Attal would lose a hypothetical 2027 second-round presidential run-off to National Rally candidate [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]] by 49 percent to 51 percent — the first poll ever to show Le Pen winning the presidency.[1] The result received front-page coverage in France.[1] Attal had previously served as education minister and was named prime minister following the resignation of Élisabeth Borne.[1]
