# CNOOC

The China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC), listed as 0883.HK and 600938.SS, is one of [[china|China's]] national oil companies and a central instrument of Chinese state energy policy.[1]

## First yuan-settled LNG trade

In late March 2023 CNOOC and the French energy major [[totalenergies|TotalEnergies]] completed China's first liquefied natural gas trade settled in yuan, executed through the [[shanghai-petroleum-and-natural-gas-exchange|Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange]].[1] Roughly 65,000 tonnes of LNG imported from the United Arab Emirates changed hands in the transaction, according to the exchange.[1] TotalEnergies confirmed to Reuters that the cargo originated in the UAE but declined further comment, while CNOOC did not immediately respond to a request for comment.[1]

The trade was reported in the context of a broader Chinese effort to settle oil and gas transactions in yuan in order to internationalise the currency and weaken the dollar's grip on world trade, an effort reinforced by [[russia|Russia's]] increasing use of the yuan under Western sanctions.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary grouped the CNOOC–TotalEnergies deal with [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia's]] move toward the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] as evidence of an emerging "Sovereign Alliance," asking whether France was breaking ranks with United States hegemony.[1]
