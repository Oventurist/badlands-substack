# Tom Tiffany

Tom Tiffany is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Wisconsin who intervened publicly in the April 2025 controversy over Wisconsin judges and federal immigration enforcement.[1]

## Response to the Isham email

After Sawyer County judge [[monica-isham|Monica Isham]] circulated an email to Wisconsin judges threatening to refuse to hold court rather than cooperate with [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], Tiffany shared images of the email in a social media post on Saturday, April 26, 2025.[1] He wrote that "Monica Isham is choosing to protect illegal aliens over the law. She should resign or be removed."[1]

The intervention placed Tiffany among the Republican officials treating state judicial non-cooperation as an obstruction problem rather than a due-process dispute, in the same news cycle as the arrests of Judge [[hannah-dugan|Hannah Dugan]] and former New Mexico judge [[joel-cano|Joel Cano]].[1]
