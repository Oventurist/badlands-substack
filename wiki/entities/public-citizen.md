# Public Citizen

**Public Citizen** is an American progressive advocacy organization named among the sponsoring groups of the coalition assembled to oppose the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] following the 2024 election.[1]

## Role in the post-2024 coalition

Public Citizen was listed alongside the [[working-families-party|Working Families Party]], [[moveon-org|MoveOn]], the [[indivisible|Indivisible Project]] and the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] as an organization that had already joined the sponsor coalition in the lead-up to the 2024 election.[1] Organizers said the effort aligned more than 100 groups and Democratic Representative [[pramila-jayapal|Pramila Jayapal]], and included a schedule of eight roughly weekly "Mass Calls" beginning November 7, 2024.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] placed the group within what he calls "NGO world" — a network of tax-exempt organizations he describes as the nervous system of the deep political establishment, executing the will of a "think tank class" that includes the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]], the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
