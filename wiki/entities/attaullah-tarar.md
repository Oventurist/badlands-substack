# Attaullah Tarar

Attaullah Tarar is Pakistan's Information Minister, who in late April 2025 publicly claimed that Islamabad possessed "credible intelligence" of an imminent Indian military strike.[1]

## April 2025 warning of Indian military action

In an unusual middle-of-the-night post on X early on Wednesday, April 30, 2025, Tarar wrote that "Pakistan has credible intelligence that India intends carrying out military action against Pakistan in the next 24-36 hours."[1] He did not elaborate on what evidence supported the claim.[1] The statement came as both the United States and China urged restraint amid sharply escalating tensions.[1]

Tarar's comments followed by one week a massacre of 26 tourists by militants in the mountainous town of Pahalgam in Indian-administered Kashmir, an attack that provoked widespread outrage.[1] India accused Pakistan of involvement in the attack, a charge Islamabad denied while offering a neutral investigation into the incident.[1] Badlands commentary responded that "the Regime will take more war wherever they can get it."[1]
