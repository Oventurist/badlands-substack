# Matt Wallace

Matt Wallace is a social media commentator active on Twitter (X) under the handle @MattWallace888.[1]

In November 2023, Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited a post by Wallace as a source that "delves into the matter" of the Panamanian mining dispute, in the context of the killing of two anti-mining protesters by [[kenneth-darlington|Kenneth Darlington]] and the contract between the Panamanian government and [[first-quantum-minerals|First Quantum Minerals]].[1] The citation appeared as part of Carlson's argument that mainstream coverage of the shooting omitted the underlying dispute over the mining concession.[1]
