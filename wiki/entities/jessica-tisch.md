# Jessica Tisch

Jessica Tisch is the police commissioner of the City of New York, heading the [[nypd|New York Police Department]].[1]

## Response to the killing of Brian Thompson

Tisch addressed reporters at a press conference on December 4, 2024, after the fatal shooting of [[unitedhealthcare]] chief executive [[brian-thompson]] outside a midtown Manhattan hotel.[1] She described the shooting as a "brazen, targeted attack" and stated that it "does not appear to be a random act of violence."[1] Tisch further told reporters, "I want to be clear at this time, every indication is that this was a premeditated, pre-planned targeted attack."[1] Her remarks were delivered alongside those of chief of detectives Joseph Kenny, who described the victim's wounds and the timeline reconstructed from surveillance footage.[1]
