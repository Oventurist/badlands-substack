# Terrence Banks

Terrence Banks is the third of the Banks brothers involved in the September 2024 New York City corruption story, and runs the Pearl Alliance, a firm that represents companies holding contracts worth hundreds of thousands of dollars with a number of city agencies.[1] According to CBS News reporting cited by Badlands, Terrence Banks is not a registered lobbyist.[1]

His brothers are [[philip-banks-iii|Philip Banks III]], the deputy mayor for public safety, and [[david-banks|David Banks]], the Commissioner of Schools, whose fiancée [[sheena-wright|Sheena Wright]] is also a deputy mayor.[1] Badlands treated the arrangement — a family member representing city contractors while two siblings held senior positions inside city government — as a central example of the nepotism and public-private corruption exposed by the federal investigation that also led to the resignation of Police Commissioner [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]].[1]
