# Anglican Communion

The Anglican Communion is the worldwide association of Anglican churches historically centered on the Church of England and the office of the Archbishop of Canterbury.[1] By 2025 the Communion was described as having an "already torn fabric," strained by long-running disputes over same-sex marriage and the authority of Scripture.[1]

## 2025 crisis over the Canterbury succession

The election of [[sarah-mullally|Sarah Mullally]] as the first female Archbishop of Canterbury in October 2025 intensified the rift.[1] The [[church-of-nigeria|Anglican Church of Nigeria]], through primate [[henry-ndukuba|Henry Ndukuba]], formally rejected the appointment, calling it "a double jeopardy" for imposing female headship and for elevating a supporter of same-sex marriage.[1]

Nigeria, as part of the Global Fellowship of Confessing Anglicans (GAFCON), reaffirmed its commitment to "the authority of the Scriptures" and rejected what Ndukuba called "the revisionist agenda" within parts of the Communion.[1] Ndukuba stated that the election confirmed "that the global Anglican world could no longer accept the leadership of the Church of England and that of the Archbishop of Canterbury," a declaration that effectively questions Canterbury's role as the Communion's focus of unity.[1]
