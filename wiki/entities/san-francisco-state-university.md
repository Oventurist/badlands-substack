# San Francisco State University

San Francisco State University (SFSU) is a public university in California that drew national criticism in April 2023 after an event featuring former collegiate swimmer [[riley-gaines|Riley Gaines]] ended in what was described as an assault on the speaker.[1]

## The Gaines event and its aftermath

Gaines had been invited to speak about her views on transgender participation in women's sports. According to accounts reported at the time, she was reportedly assaulted during the event and had to be removed by police to a locked room for several hours for her own protection.[1] The disruption was characterized as the latest example of protesters "deplatforming" speakers with opposing views by shouting them down, with the crowd becoming so unruly that police intervened to rush Gaines to safety.[1]

## Institutional response

Rather than condemning the mob or promising accountability, SFSU issued a statement that was, in the words of law professor [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]], "conspicuous in its failure to condemn the mob or promise accountability for these actions."[1] Turley argued that the statement instead appeared to express sympathy for the protesters and for those who had been exposed to Gaines's views, and that the university's posture explained why students believed they had license to engage in such disruptions.[1] He framed the episode as a straightforward test for an institution of higher education, noting that denying free expression and threatening those with opposing views is "the very antithesis of core principles."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the incident as evidence that trans-activist confrontations are "activating the sleeping giant," arguing that Gaines had become a catalyst exposing the character of the activists involved and calling for a united, non-violent conservative response built on conversation with families and communities rather than open confrontation.[1]
