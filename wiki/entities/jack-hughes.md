# Jack Hughes

Jack Hughes is an American ice hockey forward who plays for the New Jersey Devils of the National Hockey League and represented the United States at the 2026 Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics.[1]

Hughes scored the golden goal in the [[2026-winter-olympics-mens-hockey-final|2026 Olympic men's hockey final]], beating Canada 1:41 into overtime on an assist from Zach Werenski to give the United States a 2-1 win and its first Olympic gold in men's hockey since the 1980 "Miracle on Ice" era.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted that Hughes had his front teeth knocked out earlier in the same game after taking a hockey stick to the face, treating the detail as a "perfect encapsulation" of the grit he credited for the American victory.[1] Hughes's brother Quinn Hughes also played for the United States, assisting on Matt Boldy's opening goal.[1]
