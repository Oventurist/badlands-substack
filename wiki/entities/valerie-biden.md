# Valerie Biden

Valerie Biden is the younger sister of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and a member of his innermost circle of advisers.[1]

## Role in the Biden "oligarchy"

In late June 2024, after Biden's widely criticized debate performance, Axios reported that Valerie Biden, Dr. [[jill-biden|Jill Biden]] and 85-year-old [[ted-kaufman|Ted Kaufman]] — along with a small band of [[white-house|White House]] advisers — were the only people with authority over whether Biden would continue his re-election campaign.[1] Axios described the group as a decades-long kitchen cabinet operating as an extended family, council of elders and "governing oligarchy," and reported that Biden engaged in no organized process outside his family in deciding to seek a second term.[1] Badlands Media commentator Ashe in America argued that the framing conceded that voters, rather than a small family circle, were not the ones deciding the Democratic nomination.[1]
