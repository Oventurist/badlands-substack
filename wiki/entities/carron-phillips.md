# Carron Phillips

Carron Phillips is a sports and culture writer for the outlet [[deadspin|Deadspin]] who became the subject of a threatened defamation action in December 2023 over an article about a young Kansas City Chiefs fan.[1]

## Blackface accusation controversy

Phillips wrote that nine-year-old [[holden-armenta|Holden Armenta]], photographed at a Chiefs game with his face painted in team colors and wearing a Native American headdress, had "found a way to hate Black people and the Native Americans at the same time."[1] The accusation of blackface was false; the boy is of Native American descent and his face was painted half red and half black in Chiefs colors.[1]

The child's parents, Shannon and Raul Armenta, retained the law firm [[clare-locke|Clare Locke LLP]] to demand a retraction and threatened further legal action against Phillips, Deadspin, and its publishers G/O Media and Great Hill Partners.[1] Clare Locke had previously won a $787.5 million settlement from Fox News on behalf of [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]], an outcome that also preceded Tucker Carlson's departure from that network.[1]
