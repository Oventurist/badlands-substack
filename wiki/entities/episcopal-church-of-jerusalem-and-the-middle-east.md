# Episcopal Church of Jerusalem and the Middle East

The Episcopal Church of Jerusalem and the Middle East is the Anglican provincial body whose Diocese of Jerusalem oversees churches and institutions across the Holy Land, including the [[al-ahli-arab-anglican-hospital|Al-Ahli Arab Anglican Hospital]] in Gaza City.[1] According to the diocese's own account, it has existed since 1841, predating the modern State of Israel by more than a century.[1]

## July 2024 dispute with the IDF

In July 2024 the church publicly protested the closure of its Gaza City hospital, which it said resulted from Israeli military evacuation orders covering several residential districts.[1] A statement issued in the name of the Diocese of Jerusalem asserted that the hospital had been "compelled to close by the Israeli army" and described heavy drone fire in the immediate vicinity of the facility on the preceding Sunday evening.[1]

The [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] denied ordering the closure, saying it had made clear there was no requirement to shut hospitals or medical centres in evacuation zones, and the hospital director stated that patients had left of their own accord or been transferred elsewhere.[1]

Badlands Media commentary used the episode to argue that what it termed the "Zionist war machine" was indifferent to opposing religious traditions — Muslim, Christian and even Orthodox Jewish alike — and cited the October 2023 strike on a Greek Orthodox church in Gaza as precedent.[1]
