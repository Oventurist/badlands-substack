# Flexport

Flexport is one of the largest U.S. supply-chain logistics operators.[1]

## Warning on the 2024 port strike

In late September 2024, Flexport's chief executive warned that "the biggest wild card in the presidential election that nobody's talking about? The looming port strike that could shut down all East and Gulf Coast ports just 36 days before the election."[1] The remark framed the threatened walkout by dockworkers represented by the [[international-longshoremens-association|International Longshoremen's Association]] against the [[united-states-maritime-alliance|United States Maritime Alliance]] as an electoral as well as economic event.[1]

The [[port-authority-of-new-york-and-new-jersey|Port Authority of New York and New Jersey]] said it was "coordinating with partners across the supply chain to prepare for any impacts," urging both sides "to find common ground and keep the cargo flowing for the good of the national economy," and noting that $240 billion in goods move through its two ports each year, supporting more than 600,000 local jobs.[1]
