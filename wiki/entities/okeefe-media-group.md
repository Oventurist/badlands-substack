# O'Keefe Media Group

The O'Keefe Media Group (OMG) is the undercover journalism organization founded by [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] after his departure from his earlier project. In the Badlands Media corpus it is cited as an originating source of hidden-camera evidence in election-fraud stories.

## Los Angeles homeless registration investigation

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief attributes to the group a March 2026 undercover documentation of a voter registration drive for homeless people in [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], in which, per the Brief, "money exchanged and fake names" were recorded being registered. The Brief presents this footage as chronologically preceding — and implicitly prompting — the May 18, 2026 [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] indictment of [[brenda-lee-brown-armstrong|Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong]] and the June 18 [[fbi|FBI]] and [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] operation on [[skid-row|Skid Row]].
