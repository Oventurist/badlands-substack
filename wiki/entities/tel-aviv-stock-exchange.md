# Tel Aviv Stock Exchange

The Tel Aviv Stock Exchange (TASE) is [[israel|Israel]]'s principal securities market. It became a focus of international scrutiny after a 2023 academic study reported that short selling of Israeli securities listed on the exchange "increased dramatically" in the days immediately preceding the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 Hamas attack]].[1]

## Role in the October 7 trading controversy

The study by [[robert-jackson-jr|Robert J. Jackson Jr.]] and [[joshua-mitts|Joshua Mitts]] found that shorting of dozens of Israeli companies listed on the exchange significantly increased before the attack, including 4.43 million new shares shorted on a single company — [[bank-leumi|Leumi]], the country's largest bank — between September 14 and October 5.[1] The researchers concluded that the observed volumes far exceeded short selling recorded during other periods of crisis, including the post-financial-crisis recession, the 2014 Israel-Gaza war, and the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]

When contacted by [[reuters|Reuters]], the TASE directed inquiries to the [[israel-securities-authority|Israel Securities Authority]], which said the matter was known to the authority and under investigation by all relevant parties.[1] The regulator's spokesperson declined to provide further detail and Israeli police did not respond promptly.[1] The episode forms part of the wider [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|Israel October 7 foreknowledge allegations]].[1]
