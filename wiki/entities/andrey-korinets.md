# Andrey Stanislavovich Korinets

Andrey Stanislavovich Korinets is a Russian national charged by the United States [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in December 2023 in connection with an alleged state-linked spear phishing campaign against U.S. intelligence officers.[1]

## Charges

The DOJ alleged on December 7, 2023 that hackers acting on behalf of the Russian government targeted U.S. intelligence officers in a "sophisticated spear phishing campaign" designed to influence elections in the United Kingdom, stealing information used in "foreign malign influence operations designed to influence the U.K.'s 2019 elections."[1] The operation was said to have penetrated computer networks in the United States, the United Kingdom, Ukraine, and other NATO member states.[1]

A federal grand jury in San Francisco returned the indictment on Tuesday, December 5, and it was unsealed two days later.[1] Korinets was charged alongside [[ruslan-peretyatko|Ruslan Aleksandrovich Peretyatko]], an officer of the Russian [[federal-security-service|Federal Security Service]], with each facing one count of conspiracy to commit an offense against the United States and one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud.[1] Both defendants, together with unindicted co-conspirators, were identified by prosecutors as members of the [[callisto-group|Callisto Group]].[1]

## Reception

Badlands Media commentary framed the unsealing as deliberately timed narrative seeding rather than a novel revelation, arguing that state-linked targeting of intelligence personnel is a regular occurrence and that the relevant question concerns the timing of the disclosure.[1]
