# Tina Smith

Tina Smith is a Democratic United States Senator from Minnesota. She is named in the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] among the Democratic lawmakers who planned to skip President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2026 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union address]] before a joint session of [[congress|Congress]].

## The 2026 boycott

According to the Brief, Smith was one of several senators — alongside [[ed-markey|Ed Markey]], [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]], and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]] — reported to be attending alternate events or protests rather than the speech. The source describes the boycott as part of a wider disagreement within the Democratic Party over how best to protest the address, with more than a dozen Democratic congresswomen separately planning to wear white in the chamber.
