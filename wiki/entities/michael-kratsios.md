# Michael Kratsios

Michael Kratsios is the Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, serving as the president's principal adviser on science and technology policy.[1]

## Quantum initiative

In June 2026, Kratsios and other administration officials publicly highlighted quantum technologies as critical to American economic competitiveness and national security, framing them alongside artificial intelligence and biotechnology as strategic priorities.[1] The messaging accompanied an OSTP announcement of a new national effort to develop a quantum computer capable of ushering in what the office called the "era of quantum-enabled intelligence."[1] Officials tied the effort to the administration's broader "Golden Age of Innovation" theme.[1]

## Aviation deregulation rollout (2025)

As director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, Kratsios presented the June 2025 executive orders on flying cars and supersonic flight during a White House press call.[2] He said the eVTOL order would establish a pilot program working with both public and private stakeholders, remarking that "flying cars are not just for the Jetsons, they are also for the American people in the near term" and that eVTOL "promises to revolutionize transportation as well as cargo delivery and logistics ... blazing a trail to new frontiers as part of the golden age of American innovation."[2] On supersonic flight he argued that "Americans should be able to fly from New York to L.A. in under four hours," that advances in aerospace engineering, material science and noise reduction had made domestic supersonic travel safe, sustainable and commercially viable, and that "for the last 50 years, outdated and overly restricted regulations grounded supersonic passenger flight and weakened our global competitiveness in aviation," concluding, "The market is here and the technology is here."[2] See [[2025-executive-orders-on-flying-cars-and-supersonic-flight|the 2025 executive orders on flying cars and supersonic flight]].[2]

## Space policy coordination role (December 2025)

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s December 18, 2025 executive order [[ensuring-american-space-superiority|"Ensuring American Space Superiority"]] reorganized national space policy coordination under Kratsios in his capacity as the president's chief science adviser.[3] The same order restructured the [[national-space-council|National Space Council]], which an administration official said would continue under the White House's Office of Technology Policy with the president as chairman.[3]
