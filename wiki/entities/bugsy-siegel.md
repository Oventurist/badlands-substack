# Bugsy Siegel

Bugsy Siegel is described in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] figure who ran Las Vegas under [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]].[1]

## Death

Carlson writes that Siegel held that position until his good friend Lansky discovered that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money and had him killed.[1] Siegel appears alongside [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]], who ran Hollywood, as one of the two regional lieutenants in the structure Carlson places above the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]].[1]
