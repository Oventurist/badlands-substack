# Executive Office of the President

The **Executive Office of the President (EOP)** is the collection of staff agencies and support offices that serve the President of the United States directly, including the White House Office and its information-technology infrastructure.[1] In the context of the [[spygate|Spygate]] scandal, the EOP is significant because its computer servers were the source of data alleged to have been improperly obtained by contractors working for the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] campaign's network.[1]

## Role in the Spygate allegations

Evidence surfaced by Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] during the [[michael-sussmann|Sussmann]] prosecution indicated that the Clinton-aligned private cyber operation continued its collection after [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] took office, extending its surveillance from Trump Tower to the White House itself.[1] When the [[fbi|FBI]] closed its inquiry into the original [[alfa-bank-hoax|Alfa Bank Hoax]] around the time of the inauguration, the operatives revised and expanded the allegation using data freshly taken from servers inside the EOP and presented the new package to the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] in February 2017, seeking to induce a counterintelligence investigation of the sitting President.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[brian-cates|Brian Cates]] emphasised that this EOP-sourced material was taken after Trump had been sworn in as the nation's 45th President, and argued that the conduct of federal law enforcement and intelligence personnel who knowingly continued illegally predicated surveillance of a sitting President could support charges as serious as treason.[1]
