# Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office

The Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office is the county law enforcement agency serving Hillsborough County, Florida, which covers the Tampa metropolitan area.[1]

## March 2026 human trafficking operation

In March 2026 the agency announced the arrest of 89 people during a multi-week undercover operation conducted between January 1 and February 6, 2026.[1] The effort was led by the office's Human Trafficking Section and Internet Predator Unit and targeted individuals seeking to sexually exploit children and to purchase sex.[1]

The operation yielded more than 1,200 felony charges — 1,217 in total — related to human and sex trafficking.[1] Investigators also rescued a missing 17-year-old girl who had been exploited and safely recovered a 2-year-old child after receiving a tip about ongoing exploitation.[1]

Sheriff [[chad-chronister|Chad Chronister]] said of the operation: "If you are using a hotel room, a chat app, or a fake profile to pursue a child, we are there. Our detectives will follow the digital trail all the way to your door."[1]

## Context in the national enforcement surge

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] placed the Hillsborough operation within a broader wave of enforcement since President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second inauguration, citing reports of more than 5,000 trafficking-related arrests and rescues, including federal efforts such as Operations Restore Justice, Enduring Justice, and Relentless Justice, which together produced 732 arrests and the identification or rescue of more than 450 child victims.[1] An analysis of federal government accounts also credited the surge with 1,000-plus trafficking investigations, 200-plus federal indictments, 100-plus federal convictions frequently carrying sentences of 15 to 30 years or longer, and 800-plus victims identified or assisted, with the enforcement disproportionately focused on sex trafficking and [[child-trafficking|child exploitation]].[1]
