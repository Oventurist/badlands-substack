# David Kendall

**David Kendall** is the attorney representing former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] in matters before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[1]

## Epstein-Maxwell subpoena dispute

In November 2025, Kendall sought to block subpoenas tied to the Committee's [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-[[ghislaine-maxwell|Maxwell]] investigation, arguing that the panel should rely on written responses rather than question the Clintons directly.[1] In a letter sent that Friday, Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] rejected the request, writing that the Committee "disagrees" with the idea that accepting limited written information would be "the most efficient and equitable way to proceed," and adding that Kendall's request relied on assertions already "disproved by public information."[1] The Committee ordered both Clintons to appear for closed-door depositions in December.[1]

Badlands host Ashe in America commented that the depositions were doubtful to actually occur, and criticized the closed-door format, arguing that a second-hand account from "camera thirsty legislators" was a poor substitute — though "better than nothing."[1]
