# Victoria Spartz

Victoria Spartz is a Republican member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Indiana with a record of opposing her own leadership on procedural votes.[1]

## June 2026 rule failure

Spartz was named among the conservatives who drove the late-June 2026 revolt that defeated the House procedural rule 224-198 and froze the chamber's floor business.[1] The week-long impasse originated in frustration over the stalled [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s top elections priority, and expanded into demands for amendment votes on defense policy, Delphi auto parts retiree pensions, and the immigration bill HR 2.[1]

Faced with the rebellion, Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] and Majority Leader Steve Scalise cancelled remaining votes and began the July 4 recess early, deferring the annual [[pentagon|Pentagon]] policy bill and the fiscal 2027 [[state-department|State Department]] spending measure.[1]
