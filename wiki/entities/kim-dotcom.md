# Kim Dotcom

Kim Dotcom is an internet entrepreneur and prolific commentator on the social media platform X, where he frequently posts criticism of United States intelligence agencies and Western media institutions.[1]

## Commentary on the Carlson–Putin interview

In February 2024, as speculation mounted that former Fox News host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] had traveled to Moscow to interview Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], Dotcom posted that the prospective interview would be "uncensored on X" and argued that "the US deep state and the Biden administration must be in panic mode because Putin (the most censored man in the West) will expose their propaganda and lies."[1] The remark was cited in coverage of the episode as representative of the wider online enthusiasm surrounding the trip, during which "Tucker" trended on X with more than 307,000 posts in a single morning.[1]

Dotcom's framing tied the moment to a broader argument that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s acquisition of Twitter had loosened corporate and governmental influence over the platform, enabling citizen journalists to circulate reporting outside legacy media gatekeeping.[1]
