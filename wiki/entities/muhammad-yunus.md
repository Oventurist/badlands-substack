# Muhammad Yunus

Muhammad Yunus is a Bangladeshi economist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate who became chief adviser of the interim government of Bangladesh following the [[2024-bangladesh-government-overthrow|2024 ouster]] of Prime Minister [[sheikh-hasina|Sheikh Hasina]].[1]

## Interim leadership and allegations

Yunus assumed the role after August 2024 protests turned into nationwide violence that killed more than 700 people according to the interim government's own tally, and after Hasina fled the country.[1]

[[mohibul-hasan-chowdhury|Mohibul Hasan Chowdhury]], a minister in the ousted cabinet, alleged in an interview with RT that "there is a nexus between the Clinton family, and the interim Yunus regime from a very long past," claiming that clandestine NGO funding tied to [[usaid|USAID]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s family had long been directed at changing the government in Bangladesh.[1]
