# Elon Musk

**Elon Musk** is the entrepreneur who acquired Twitter, now [[x-twitter|X]], and who Badlands Media presents as a case study in how the establishment turns on figures who challenge the censorship regime.[1]

## Reversal of establishment opinion

Musk had been revered as a brilliant, quirky entrepreneur and the great tycoon of the modern age; the establishment turned on him overnight once he announced his plan to buy Twitter.[1] Badlands describes the reversal as remarkable.[1]

## Investigations and pressure

Beyond attacks from former watchdog organizations, the article notes a number of formal investigations into Musk and his companies that did not exist before he challenged the censorship regime.[1] The [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] mounted a pressure effort against Musk after he refused to satisfy its demands, which Badlands frames as part of the same coordinated offensive that recurs every four years.[1]

## Relative position

Musk commands vastly greater resources than [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]] of [[rumble|Rumble]], though Badlands assesses Rumble as challenging the regime more intensely and therefore as the more vulnerable target.[1] The establishment's aim, in the author's view, is to take out Rumble and revert Twitter to its previous state.[1]
