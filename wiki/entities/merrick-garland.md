# Merrick Garland

**Merrick Garland** served as Attorney General of the United States during the Biden administration and headed the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] at the time of the August 8, 2022 [[fbi|FBI]] search of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] residence.

## Role in the Mar-a-Lago raid

According to the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago search]] was personally approved by Garland. The same item reports that [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]], described as a longtime DOJ veteran and Garland ally who had been consulted on Trump-related matters, emailed National Security Division attorney [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] on August 10, 2022 — two days after the raid — questioning whether Trump had declassified the seized documents and observing that "the President as Commander in Chief" is not necessarily bound by standard declassification procedures. The source treats this email as evidence that senior officials under Garland harbored early internal doubts about the legal footing of an action he had authorized.

## Weaponization framing

Badlands commentary places Garland's tenure within a broader argument that the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of federal law enforcement]] during the Biden years was "official policy" rather than the work of rogue officials, and notes that the Stemler email surfaced during the Justice Department's later review of weaponization. This framing is the source's interpretation.
