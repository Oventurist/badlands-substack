# Senegal

Senegal is a West African state whose Ministry of African Integration and Foreign Affairs became involved in a 2024 diplomatic dispute with Ukraine over statements supporting an attack in neighboring [[mali|Mali]].[1]

The ministry said it was surprised to learn that the Ukrainian embassy in Dakar had published on Facebook a propaganda video of the Ukrainian army accompanied by a comment from Ambassador [[yury-pivovarov|Yury Pivovarov]] expressing unequivocal support for the terrorist attack perpetrated between 25 and 27 July 2024 in northern Mali by Tuareg rebels and members of [[jnim|the Support Group for Islam and Muslims]] against the Armed Forces of Mali, which resulted in significant civilian casualties.[1] The attack also killed dozens of [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] contractors travelling with the Malian convoy.[1] Ukraine's ambassador was summoned over the post, and [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] separately called on the international community to hold Ukraine to account.[1]
