# Gu Mu

Gu Mu was the Chinese economist who led the international exploratory missions that informed the July 6, 1978 [[state-council-of-china|State Council]] Forum on the "Principles to Guide the Four Modernizations."[1]

According to Matthew Ehret's Badlands account, Gu Mu's delegations toured advanced world economies including Japan, Hong Kong and Western Europe, and the resulting reports laid out concrete pathways toward full-spectrum economic sovereignty for China.[1] The reports placed particular emphasis on cultivating the cognitive and creative powers of a new generation of scientists capable of driving the non-linear breakthroughs China would need in order to break free of the closed-system economics that technocrats such as [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] wished the world to accept.[1] Gu Mu's findings thus fed directly into the [[four-modernizations|Four Modernizations]] program originally enunciated by [[zhou-enlai|Zhou Enlai]] and carried forward by [[deng-xiaoping|Deng Xiaoping]].[1]
