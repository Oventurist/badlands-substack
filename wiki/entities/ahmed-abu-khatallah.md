# Ahmed Abu Khatallah

Ahmed Abu Khatallah is a Libyan militant convicted in U.S. federal court for his role in the September 11, 2012 [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]] on the U.S. diplomatic mission in Libya.[1] He was one of two Libyan militants — alongside [[mustafa-al-imam|Mustafa al-Imam]] — prosecuted and convicted in American courts before the February 2026 extradition of [[zubayar-al-bakoush|Zubayar Al-Bakoush]], who became the third person charged in connection with the assault.[1]

The Badlands Brief cited Abu Khatallah's earlier conviction as context for the argument that foreign prosecutions alone have not delivered accountability for the deaths of Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|J. Christopher Stevens]], State Department information officer Sean Smith, and CIA security contractors [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]] and [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]].[1]
