# Elliot Williams

Williams was the Democrat-called witness at the first hearing of the [[house-weaponization-committee|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]], held in February 2023.[1] During the hearing he defended the [[fbi|FBI]]'s collusion with Big Tech to censor conservatives and, in the framing of the source account, the truth.[1]

## Exchange with Matt Gaetz

Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] (R-FL) questioned Williams and left him stammering as he tried to avoid acknowledging that his lobbying firm, the [[raben-group|Raben Group]], represents [[google|Google]], [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s [[open-society-foundations|Open Society]], and [[pfizer|Pfizer]].[1] Gaetz's questioning was presented as exposing the union between Washington's political class and the private institutions targeting the rights of Americans.[1] Gaetz publicized the exchange on X the evening of February 9, 2023, where it drew roughly 3,150 reposts and 9,140 likes.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that Williams "played clueless," but as a key player at Raben he could not plausibly be unaware of his clients and their actions.[1]
