# Joaquin Castro

Joaquin Castro is a Democratic member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|U.S. House of Representatives]] from Texas.[1]

## Letter on Israel's nuclear arsenal

In May 2026 Castro led a group of 30 House Democrats in a letter to Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] demanding that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] disclose information about the [[israeli-nuclear-program|Israeli nuclear program]] and related U.S. policies.[1] The letter argued that continued official U.S. silence creates serious risks in the Middle East amid the ongoing conflict with Iran, warning that "the risks of miscalculation, escalation, and nuclear use in this environment are not theoretical."[1] It called for ending long-standing ambiguity in favor of a coherent regional nonproliferation policy covering Iran's nuclear program and Saudi Arabia's nuclear ambitions.[1]

## Call to federalize the Texas National Guard (January 2024)

In January 2024, after the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruled 5-4 that the federal government could remove razor wire installed by Texas along its border with Mexico, the [[texas-national-guard|Texas National Guard]] continued making and installing the barriers in defiance of the order.[2] Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]] issued a statement vowing that authorities under his jurisdiction would not back down and invoking Article I, Section 10, Clause 3 of the Constitution as reserving to the state a right of self-defense.[2]

In response, Castro called on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to seize the Texas Guard and federalize it, a demand that fellow Texas Democrat [[greg-casar|Greg Casar]] endorsed shortly afterward.[2] The exchange became a focal point of what Badlands writers described as an escalating federal-versus-state confrontation with the potential to move from political to social and, in the worst case, kinetic terrain.[2]
