# Syrian Emergency Task Force

The Syrian Emergency Task Force (SETF) was a Washington, D.C. lobbying group that raised money for a Syrian rebel army fighting to overthrow [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]]. Its Executive Director was [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The Badlands account asserts that SETF was in substance a lobbying group for [[isis|ISIS]], and that [[congress|Congress]] was consequently giving taxpayer money to ISIS.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Through SETF, Moustafa acted as the intermediary between [[john-mccain|Senator John McCain]] and ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi, with McCain helping to fund and arm the organisation under the approval of President [[barack-obama|Obama]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The article identifies SETF as a repeat of the earlier [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]], which Moustafa had run on behalf of the insurgents fighting [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## December 2024 media prominence

In December 2024, following the fall of Damascus, the group's executive director [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] appeared on CNN in an interview with [[jim-acosta|Jim Acosta]] discussing the new Syrian order.[2] Badlands commentary characterized the Syrian Emergency Task Force as the public-relations division of [[islamic-state|ISIS]], noted that Moustafa had been photographed in Syria in 2011 alongside Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and [[abu-bakr-al-baghdadi|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi]], and pointed out that the organization is a non-governmental organization founded and based in Washington, D.C.[2] The commentary presented the group's Washington footprint and mainstream media access as evidence of what it called Uniparty kayfabe surrounding the Syrian regime change.[2]
