# Syrian Emergency Task Force

The Syrian Emergency Task Force (SETF) is a Washington, D.C. lobbying organization headed by [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] that raised money and political support for the Syrian rebel army seeking to overthrow Bashar al-Assad.[1] The Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest" characterizes it not as a humanitarian or opposition advocacy group but as "a lobbying group for [[isis|ISIS]]," through which [[congress|Congress]] channeled taxpayer money to the organization.[1]

The article states that Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] was directly connected to Moustafa and used the relationship to help fund and arm ISIS with the full approval of President Obama, with Moustafa serving as the intermediary between McCain and ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi.[1] SETF is presented as the Syrian successor to the earlier [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]], run by the same man on the same template.[1]
