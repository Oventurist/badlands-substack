# Syrian Emergency Task Force

The Syrian Emergency Task Force (SETF) is a U.S.-based advocacy and lobbying organization that claims to support the Syrian opposition. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," the SETF is identified as a lobbying group that was "actually a lobbying group for ISIS," designed to channel U.S. taxpayer money to terrorist organizations under the cover of humanitarian support.

According to the article, [[mouza-moustafa|Mouza Moustafa]] served as Executive Director of the SETF, and the group was part of a broader network including United For a Free Syria (UFS) and the Coalition for a Democratic Syria (CDS). The source claims that Moustafa was the "go-between" for [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and [[isis|ISIS leader]] [[abu-bakr-al-baghdadi|Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi]], and that the SETF was the primary mechanism through which U.S. taxpayer money was funneled to ISIS fighters in Syria.

The article asserts that [[barack-obama|President Obama]] gave these operations his "full approval and blessing," and that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was similarly complicit. It claims that the SETF was set up by "our enemies to fund the very groups we are at war with," and that the same playbook used in Libya was replicated in Syria. The source notes that Moustafa had previously led the [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]] before moving on to the SETF, suggesting a deliberate pattern of creating humanitarian-sounding organizations to conceal terrorist funding.

According to the article, the SETF's work included chaperoning McCain on his secret 2013 trip to Syria to meet with ISIS leaders, using McCain's reputation as a "war hero" to provide legitimacy to the terrorist groups the organization was funding.
