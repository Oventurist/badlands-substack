# Mario Draghi

Mario Draghi is named by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the "eco-warrior central bankers" who, alongside [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] and other technocrats, began plans to rewire the entire world economy around a program of total depopulation centred on Green New Deals and decarbonization.[1]

## Role in the climate finance thesis

In Ehret's framing, Draghi belongs to a class of monetary officials who translated the doctrines of the [[gaia-cult|Gaia cult]] into the machinery of finance, aligning capital allocation with the emissions targets promoted through the [[ipcc|IPCC]] and the United Nations sustainable development framework.[1] The article argues that this program ignores the fact that CO2 is plant food and that the carbon molecule is the driving force behind life on earth, and that reducing global CO2 emissions to "acceptable levels" would bring nothing more than chaos, famine and depopulation.[1] Draghi is thereby placed within the wider [[depopulation-agenda|depopulation agenda]] rather than treated as a conventional monetary policymaker.[1]
