# Richard Welch

Richard Welch was a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] station chief in Greece who was murdered outside his home in Athens on Christmas Day, 1975.[1]

## Effect on the Church Committee

Welch's assassination came in the middle of the [[church-committee|Church Committee]]'s investigation into intelligence abuses and, according to Badlands Media, diverted the public's attention from the committee's focus on those abuses while drumming up sympathy for the intelligence community.[1] The killing is presented alongside other headwinds the committee faced — accusations that the inquiry was a vehicle for [[frank-church|Frank Church]]'s 1976 presidential bid, and arguments that congressional investigation had undermined the security and credibility of U.S. intelligence agencies.[1]
