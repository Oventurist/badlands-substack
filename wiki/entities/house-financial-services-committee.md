# House Financial Services Committee

The House Financial Services Committee is the standing committee of the United States House of Representatives with jurisdiction over banking, securities, insurance and monetary policy, and the venue in which federal digital-asset legislation has advanced.[1]

## March 2025 stablecoin hearing

On Tuesday, March 11, 2025, the committee held a hearing on [[stablecoin|stablecoin]] legislation at which the pro-digital-assets witness panel included a senior executive from BNY and Davis Polk & Wardwell lawyer [[randy-guynn|Randy Guynn]].[1] [[coindesk|Coindesk]] characterized the lineup as a reversal of prior dynamics: after years in which crypto insiders sought unsuccessfully to attract the attention of [[us-congress|Congress]], representatives of the traditional financial system were now actively working to tip the balance toward stablecoin regulation.[1]

Guynn testified that the safeguards in the [[stable-act|STABLE Act]] should place permitted stablecoin issuers under protections analogous to banking, such that their payment stablecoins "should be as safe as insured bank deposits and central bank money."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GMONEY framed the committee's proceedings as an effort by the banking class — with [[maxine-waters|Maxine Waters]] characterized as an instrument of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] — to preserve control through permissioned issuance, arguing that crypto was exposing the centralized nature of the US dollar.[1]
