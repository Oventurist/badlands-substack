# Benita Pearson

Benita Pearson is a United States District Judge for the Northern District of Ohio who has presided over the civil litigation arising from the 2023 [[east-palestine-train-derailment|East Palestine, Ohio, train derailment]].[1]

## East Palestine settlement litigation

Pearson granted the $600 million class-action settlement in the derailment case, together with a 27 percent attorney fee, in 2024.[1] She subsequently supervised the follow-on trial, which began the week of March 31, 2025, to determine whether the railcar lessor [[gatx-corporation|GATX Corp.]] and the chemical manufacturer [[oxy-vinyls|Oxy Vinyls]] must reimburse [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern]] for part of that settlement.[1] The compensation owed to class members was unaffected by the contribution dispute; Norfolk Southern remained liable for the payout regardless of the jury's verdict.[1]
