# Fairfax Bar & Grill

The Fairfax Bar & Grill is a dive bar in Bloomington, in southern Indiana, that drew national attention in the spring of 2023 after publicly defending [[bud-light|Bud Light]] during the boycott triggered by [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch]]'s partnership with transgender influencer [[dylan-mulvaney|Dylan Mulvaney]].[1]

## The controversy

The bar declared that it supported free speech except for anyone who criticized Anheuser-Busch or Bud Light, and began ejecting patrons it deemed "intolerant."[1] Its statement read: "We are all aware of the controversy surrounding Bud Light. We support ALL people in this establishment no matter who you are of how you identify. We will continue to sell Anheiser [sic] Busch products because we don't care who they make special cans for. … If you are intolerant of other humans of any kind, we ask that you keep your opinions to yourself. Should you feel the need to discuss this matter in public you will be asked to leave. We will not tolerate intolerance here."[1]

Owner [[mckinley-minniefield|McKinley Minniefield]] told WISH-TV: "We were just dealing with a lot of hate speech, and people being uncomfortable. My bartenders were aggravated and we had customers that were leaving."[1] Following vehement and costly backlash from paying customers, the bar was reported by ZeroHedge to be begging for more business.[1]

## Commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the episode as a "Micro" example — patrons of an Indiana bar taking a stand — illustrating "how inevitable our victory over subversive globalists truly is."[1]
