# Christopher Porter

**Christopher Porter** is a United States cyber-intelligence analyst who served as National Intelligence Officer for Cyber at the [[national-intelligence-council|National Intelligence Council]] around the turn of the 2020 election cycle.

Porter is identified as the author of a January 15, 2020 assessment, declassified in 2026 by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], warning that [[russia|Russia]], [[china|China]], [[iran|Iran]], North Korea, and non-state actors had the capability to compromise U.S. voting infrastructure ahead of the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]]. The memo flagged internet-connected voter registration databases, pollbooks, and election websites as the most exposed components, and described scenarios in which altered data could block voters, create delays, or force reliance on provisional ballots. President Trump was briefed on the memo's concerns in February 2020.

Beyond authorship of the memo, the Badlands Brief coverage of April 22, 2026 provides no further biographical detail about Porter.
