# Miguel Cardona

Miguel Cardona served as United States Secretary of Education in the administration of Joe Biden.[1]

## 2023 House Judiciary subpoena

In early February 2023, the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]], under new chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] (R-Ohio), issued its first subpoenas of the Congress to a trio of Biden administration officials — Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]], [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]], and Cardona — over the short-lived 2021 Justice Department memo directing federal attention to threats against school board members.[1] The subpoenas followed more than 100 letters sent by Judiciary Republicans about the memo.[1] Coverage of the action appeared in [[the-hill|The Hill]].[1]
