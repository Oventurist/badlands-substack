# C. D. Jackson

Charles Douglas "C. D." Jackson was a United States government propagandist and senior executive of Time Inc., described as an expert on psychological warfare.[1]

In [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account of the origins of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]], Jackson is identified as another [[cia|CIA]]-connected individual who helped facilitate the rise of the Bilderbergers.[1] As publisher of Life magazine he served under President Eisenhower as a "special consultant for psychological warfare."[1] DeLarme situates him alongside [[jozef-retinger|Józef Retinger]] and the intelligence figures — [[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]], [[walter-bedell-smith|Walter Bedell Smith]], Thomas Braden and [[william-donovan|William Donovan]] — whose involvement supports the argument that the Bilderberg conferences were at least partially organised and sponsored by American intelligence.[1]

Jackson's media role also bears on DeLarme's related complaint that many in the American legacy media have met with the Bilderbergers over the years yet little or nothing is ever reported about the group's activities, which has led some writers to claim the Bilderbergers request censorship and news management.[1]
