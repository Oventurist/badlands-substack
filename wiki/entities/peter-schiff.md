# Peter Schiff

Peter Schiff is an American investor and commentator widely described as a "gold bug" for his long-standing advocacy of precious metals and his persistent criticism of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]].[1]

## Dispute with Tom Lee (September 2025)

In September 2025 Schiff renewed his critique of Bitcoin in response to Fundstrat strategist [[tom-lee|Tom Lee]]'s $200,000 year-end price target.[1] In a post on X, Schiff highlighted that gold had risen 10% over the preceding two months and reached a new high of $3,620, writing that "Markets are forward-looking. That's why gold is up 10% in advance of coming rate cuts."[1]

Schiff argued that this move showed traders anticipating easier monetary policy from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], and that Bitcoin's failure to follow gold's lead was a warning sign.[1] He noted that Lee had blamed the Fed's two-month pause for Bitcoin's flat performance even though gold rallied to a record over the same window.[1]
