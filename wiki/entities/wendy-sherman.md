# Wendy Sherman

**Wendy Sherman** is an American diplomat who served as Deputy Secretary of State in the administration of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] before retiring from the post.[1]

Her retirement, roughly seven months before March 2024, left the department's number two position vacant and triggered an internal succession contest. Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] served as acting deputy secretary in the interim and was a candidate to succeed Sherman permanently, but Biden instead nominated [[kurt-campbell|Kurt Campbell]], who assumed the office in February 2024.[1]
