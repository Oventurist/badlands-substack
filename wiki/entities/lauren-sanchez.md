# Lauren Sanchez

Lauren Sanchez is an American broadcast journalist and helicopter pilot, best known publicly as the fiancée of [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]].[1]

In April 2025 Sanchez flew aboard [[new-shepard-ns-31|New Shepard NS-31]], [[blue-origin|Blue Origin]]'s all-female suborbital tourism flight, alongside [[katy-perry|Katy Perry]], [[gayle-king|Gayle King]], Amanda Nguyen, Aisha Bowe and Kerianne Flynn.[1] Press coverage noted that Bezos, Blue Origin's founder and former Amazon boss, was sending his fiancée to space as part of the mission, which launched from the company's west Texas base.[1]

## Venice wedding (June 2025)

Sanchez married [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]] in a marathon celebration in Venice in late June 2025, attended by more than 200 guests who moved between luxury venues over a multi-day sequence of parties, ending with a retro "pajama party" at the Arsenale on June 28.[2] The couple had selected Venice in April and set a 3-million-euro ($3.5 million) gift in motion to three Venetian charities while asking guests to eschew personal gifts; Italy's tourism ministry estimated a 957-million-euro ($1.12 billion) benefit to the tourism economy.[2]
