# Lauren Sanchez

Lauren Sanchez is an American broadcast journalist and helicopter pilot, best known publicly as the fiancée of [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]].[1]

In April 2025 Sanchez flew aboard [[new-shepard-ns-31|New Shepard NS-31]], [[blue-origin|Blue Origin]]'s all-female suborbital tourism flight, alongside [[katy-perry|Katy Perry]], [[gayle-king|Gayle King]], Amanda Nguyen, Aisha Bowe and Kerianne Flynn.[1] Press coverage noted that Bezos, Blue Origin's founder and former Amazon boss, was sending his fiancée to space as part of the mission, which launched from the company's west Texas base.[1]
