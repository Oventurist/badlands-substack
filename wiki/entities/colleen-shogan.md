# Colleen Shogan

Colleen Shogan was the Archivist of the United States, heading the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]], until President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] dismissed her on Friday, February 7, 2025 — the same day he announced plans to overhaul the board of the [[john-f-kennedy-center-for-the-performing-arts|John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts]].[1] The national archivist is responsible for overseeing government records; Trump had repeatedly criticized the agency since it alerted the Justice Department to his handling of classified documents in early 2022.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands contributor Chris Paul argued that Shogan is associated with [[david-rubenstein|David Rubenstein]], whom Trump simultaneously removed as Kennedy Center chairman.[1] Paul noted that Shogan had served as Senior Vice President and Director of the [[rubenstein-center-for-white-house-history|Rubenstein Center for White House History]], and used that overlap to speculate — explicitly labelled a "working conspiracy theory" open to feedback — about whether records could have moved out of NARA into privately controlled historical institutions.[1]
