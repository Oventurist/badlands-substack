# Azov Battalion

The Azov Battalion is a Ukrainian volunteer paramilitary formation that emerged in the wake of the 2014 [[maidan-coup|Maidan Coup]] and was subsequently absorbed into Ukraine's regular security structures.

## Oligarch funding

In the April 21, 2026 Badlands Brief, Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recounts covering the Azov Battalion on the program The Book of Trump alongside a discussion of Ukrainian oligarch [[ihor-kolomoisky|Ihor Kolomoisky]]. According to that account, Kolomoisky — then governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast and a public leader of the Maidan events — created the Donbass Battalion outright and pumped money and other resources into the Azov Battalion, which had already formed, as well as into other volunteer militias. The Azov case is presented in the source as an illustration of how private oligarchic wealth in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] shaped the country's armed formations, a precedent the commentary links to fears about corruption in newly expanded European defense procurement.
