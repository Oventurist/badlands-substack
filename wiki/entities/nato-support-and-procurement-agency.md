# NATO Support and Procurement Agency

The NATO Support and Procurement Agency (NSPA) is the purchasing arm of the [[nato|NATO]] alliance. In December 2025 it became the centre of a large bribery scandal that led to the suspension of multiple NATO–Israel arms contracts and to arrests across Europe, according to reporting by La Lettre, Le Soir, Knack and Follow the Money.[1]

Investigators described a network of private operators exploiting a revolving-door system that allows former NSPA staff to become defense-industry consultants, where they prospered in "the new geopolitical situation" driven by "the explosion in European defense budgets."[1] The agency suspended several contracts with [[elbit-systems|Elbit Systems]], Israel's largest weapons producer, over evidence that the firm used a former NSPA employee to bribe former colleagues and win deals.[1] A 60-year-old Italian national, Eliau Eluasvili, was the subject of an international arrest warrant issued by a Belgian court in late September and remained at large.[1]
