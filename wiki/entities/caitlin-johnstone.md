# Caitlin Johnstone

Caitlin Johnstone is an independent writer and commentator whose work focuses on United States foreign policy, war propaganda and the conduct of Western military alliances.[1]

In September 2023, Johnstone published an item headlined "NATO Chief Openly Admits Russia Invaded Ukraine Because Of NATO Expansion," reporting on remarks by [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]] to the European Parliament's foreign affairs committee.[1] Her account reproduced Stoltenberg's statements that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had sought a written promise of no further [[nato|NATO]] enlargement in the autumn of 2021 as a precondition for not invading Ukraine, that NATO refused, and that Putin consequently "went to war to prevent NATO, more NATO, close to his borders."[1] The piece was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
