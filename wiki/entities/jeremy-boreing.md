# Jeremy Boreing

Jeremy Boreing is the co-chief executive officer of [[daily-wire|The Daily Wire]], the conservative media company that produces programs hosted by [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], Michael Knowles, and Matt Walsh, among others.[1]

## Comments on a potential Fox acquisition (December 2024)

In December 2024, after a Semafor report indicated that [[fox-corporation|Fox Corp.]] was interested in expanding into audio and had considered acquiring a number of podcast brands, Boreing addressed the possibility that The Daily Wire could be a target.[1] He said the company was not actively seeking a sale but would not rule out an offer: "We're not closed off to an offer," he told Axios. "It's easy to imagine a strategic partnership with Fox or someone like Fox, that could be mutually beneficial. I think that we complement Fox and don't compete with Fox."[1]

Other potential targets named in the reporting included Red Seat Ventures — home to former [[fox-news|Fox News]] hosts such as [[megyn-kelly|Megyn Kelly]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], and Eric Bolling — and the podcast network Audioboom.[1] Fox was already active in podcasting through its news, business, and sports brands as well as OutKick, which it purchased in 2021.[1] The Daily Wire had been valued at more than $1 billion during a 2023 fundraising round and continued to show revenue growth in 2024.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] connected the acquisition talk to simultaneous, unsubstantiated rumors that [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]] had sold his podcast operation to Shapiro and The Daily Wire, remarking on how fitting it would be if the whole of what he called "ConInc" fell under the Fox News umbrella.[1]
