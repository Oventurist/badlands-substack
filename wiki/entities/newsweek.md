# Newsweek

*Newsweek* is an American news magazine and digital outlet. It appears in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief as the source for reactions to the reported cartel drone incursion into US airspace near El Paso, Texas.

The Brief cites *Newsweek* reporting that Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] rejected the reports, saying "there is no information about the use of drones on the border," and that Representative [[tony-gonzales|Tony Gonzales]] of Texas responded to the news by describing such incursions as routine along the border: "It's a Wednesday for us." In this edition *Newsweek* functions as the outlet through which both the Mexican denial and the countervailing congressional testimony reached the Badlands audience, and the Brief presents the two quotations side by side without endorsing either.
