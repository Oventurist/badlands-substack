# Rick Hanson

**Dr. Rick Hanson** is a neuropsychologist and the author of *Buddha's Brain*, cited in the March 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Walking the Path: From Awakening to Integration" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay quotes his best-known formulation that "The mind is like Velcro for negative experiences and Teflon for positive ones."[1] The line closes the article's section on relationships after awakening, where the author argues that ties are the sharpest test of the negativity bias Hanson describes: [[psychological-integration|integration]] means deliberately choosing connections that nurture rather than clinging to old patterns that merely feel familiar.[1]

In the surrounding passage the article offers practical guidance consistent with that framing — leading by example rather than proselytising, telling a venting friend "I see it differently now" and then pausing, setting boundaries by picturing a shield of light before a difficult conversation, and asking of any relationship whether it lifts or weighs.[1] Some ties, the essay says, fade naturally while others deepen through understanding, and the path "widens as you learn to walk with grace."[1]
