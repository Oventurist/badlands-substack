# Libertarian Party

The Libertarian Party is a United States third party whose national convention became a point of contact between major-party and independent presidential candidates during the 2024 election cycle.[1]

## 2024 national convention

Both [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and independent presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] had appearance commitments at the party's national convention in May 2024.[1] Seeking to exploit that overlap, Kennedy challenged Trump to debate him at the convention, describing it as "perfect neutral territory."[1] The party's convention thus served as a proposed venue for a debate outside the control of the major-party commission structure and the mainstream broadcasters.[1]

## Context of the challenge

Kennedy's invitation opened with gratitude to Trump for spotlighting what he called major media's "rigged polling," saying his own campaign shared that concern and that Democrat-aligned pollsters used deceptive methods to pretend he was languishing in single digits.[1] He cited his campaign's own Zogby polling showing Trump beating [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] handily, Kennedy beating Biden by more, and Kennedy narrowly beating Trump head-to-head, along with CNN and Quinnipiac polls placing him above the 15% debate threshold and an Activote survey putting him at 26% among young voters.[1] He also criticised Trump for blowing up the deficit, shutting down businesses during COVID, and filling his administration with "swamp creatures."[1]

## 2024 presidential nomination

At the [[2024-libertarian-national-convention|2024 national convention]], described by the Washington Post as a gathering of the third-largest political party in the country, the party nominated [[chase-oliver|Chase Oliver]] as its presidential candidate late on Sunday, May 26, 2024.[2] The nomination followed seven rounds of voting over a long and contentious day in which factions feuded over both the ticket and party leadership positions, with fewer than 900 delegates voting and Oliver ultimately taking 60 percent against "none of the above."[2] Oliver, backed by the left-leaning Classic Liberal Caucus, defeated [[michael-rectenwald|Michael Rectenwald]], the candidate endorsed by the [[mises-caucus|Mises Caucus]] that had taken over the party in 2022.[2]

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] addressed the convention and negotiated portions of the platform in real time from the stage, drawing boos and profanity from delegates.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Libertarians spent the following Monday "trying to convince everyone they're actually relevant after proving over the weekend that they are, in fact, not," argued that the party had face-planted after being given its biggest spotlight ever, and characterized it as remaining "the party of open borders and societal licentiousness."[2] She added that Libertarians were afterward fighting each other on social media, with some asking Trump to make good on the cabinet and senior post positions they had booed at the convention.[2]
