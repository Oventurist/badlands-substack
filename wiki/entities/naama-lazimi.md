# Naama Lazimi

Naama Lazimi is an Israeli member of the Knesset representing the Democrats party.[1]

Lazimi submitted a request for information regarding allegations that [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] had sought to intimidate a witness in the corruption case against her husband, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] In response, the State Attorney's Office informed Lazimi that a police investigation had been opened against Sara Netanyahu on December 26, disclosing the existence of the probe publicly.[1]
