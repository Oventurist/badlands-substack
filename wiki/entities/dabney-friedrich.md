# Dabney L. Friedrich

Dabney L. Friedrich is a United States federal judge who ruled in litigation brought by the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] against the second Trump administration over frozen foreign-assistance funding.[1]

On August 11, 2025, Friedrich granted a preliminary injunction ordering the release of an additional $95 million to the endowment, ruling that the funding freeze had caused irreparable harm to the NED's global reputation and its programs.[1] The decision prompted pushback from some Republicans, notably Rep. [[eli-crane|Eli Crane]], who introduced an amendment to defund the organization in line with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated position.[1]
