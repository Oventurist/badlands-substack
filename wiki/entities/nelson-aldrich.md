# Nelson W. Aldrich

Nelson W. Aldrich was a United States Senator and, at the time, the leader of the Republican Party, who played the central legislative role in the creation of American central banking.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

After the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]], Aldrich set up the [[national-monetary-commission|National Monetary Commission]] to investigate a better economic system for the United States.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The Commission concluded that the European model, including the Central Bank of England, was superior, and its reports and recommendations became one of the principal bases for enactment of the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act of 1913]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

In 1910, the leaders of the [[populist-party|Populist Party]] convinced Aldrich to transform the entire monetary system of the United States, and the resulting master plan was hatched at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) Among the attendees, Aldrich is noted for having a daughter who married a Rockefeller.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
