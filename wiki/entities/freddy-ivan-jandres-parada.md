# Freddy Ivan Jandres-Parada

Freddy Ivan Jandres-Parada, also known as *Lucky De Park View*, is a senior leader of [[ms-13|La Mara Salvatrucha]] (MS-13) who was arrested in California in March 2024 after four years as a fugitive.[1]

## Arrest

Jandres-Parada was arrested on March 7, 2024 by authorities in San Diego County, though the arrest was not publicly known until later that month.[1] He was expected to be presented before a federal judge in the days that followed.[1] Court Watch, which obtained the court documents, reported that he was "one of FBI's Most Wanted," describing him as a "board of director" for MS-13 and one of the most senior leaders in the gang.[1] An indictment against him was filed in December 2020 and unsealed in January 2021.[1]

## Allegations

He was wanted for his alleged involvement in directing MS-13 activity in the United States, Mexico and El Salvador, and is described as among the most important MS-13 leaders worldwide.[1] According to [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], he has been charged with several terrorism offenses for his alleged role in numerous acts of violence against civilians, law enforcement and members of rival gangs, as well as for the distribution of drugs and for participating in extortion schemes around the world.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the case as an illustration of a government that prioritizes broad entry while proving unable to locate dangerous criminals for four years, sardonically asking whether Jandres-Parada had yet had his asylum hearing.[1] She characterized the arrest of one fugitive out of what she described as more than ten million "criminal invaders" as the regime patting itself on the back.[1]
