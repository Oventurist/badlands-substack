# Paul Keary

Paul Keary is a longtime associate of [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] and the third founding partner of [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]], alongside Kelly and [[doug-band|Doug Band]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Role in Teneo's formation

In 2009, while Kelly created his own private consulting firm shortly before taking an unpaid [[state-department|State Department]] post, Keary created two consulting firms of his own; all were later combined under the Teneo umbrella when the firm officially launched in June 2011.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) During the two-year interval imposed by Kelly's non-compete agreement, Keary gathered clients for the coming launch while Band made parallel connections through the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Clients

Many of the clients Keary signed overlapped with [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]'s activities. The USA bid committee for the World Cup hired Keary in 2010 to help make the pitch for future games; earlier that year Band had joined the committee's board of directors, and Bill Clinton personally lobbied [[fifa]] that fall to bring the tournament to the United States.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Keary also won the business of the Swiss bank [[ubs]], whose Zurich branch had come under fire in 2009 for helping wealthy Americans evade taxes. A former employee said Teneo helped arrange the "Revitalizing America" event series beginning in May 2011, at which UBS executive Bob McCann appeared on stage with Bill Clinton and [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Badlands Media credits Keary with bringing both FIFA and UBS into the orbit of Clinton Foundation donations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
