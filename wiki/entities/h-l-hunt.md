# H.L. Hunt

H.L. Hunt was a Texas oil tycoon who, in Badlands Media commentary, is identified as a financier of opposing sides in the racial conflicts of the 1960s.[1]

## Alleged funding of opposing movements

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that [[malcolm-x|Malcolm X]] became aware that people like Hunt were funding both the [[black-panther-party|Black Panthers]] and the [[ku-klux-klan|Ku Klux Klan]] in order to create chaos, unrest and hate during the turbulent 1960s, and that this discovery led to Malcolm X's murder.[1]

## Family and legacy

Carlson noted that readers may recognize Hunt's name because his family owns the [[kansas-city-chiefs|Kansas City Chiefs]].[1] He added that, according to some accounts, Hunt and President [[lyndon-b-johnson|Lyndon B. Johnson]] were aware of the true motivations behind the JFK assassination, a subject he set aside as "a story for another day."[1]
