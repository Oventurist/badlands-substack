# Thiel Capital

Thiel Capital is the investment firm associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] Its most publicly visible alumnus in the Badlands narrative is [[eric-weinstein|Eric Weinstein]], the mathematician and podcast host who served as the firm's Managing Director.[1]

## Relevance to the Epstein narrative

In July 2025, Weinstein drew attention for comments describing [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] as "a front."[1] [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted the proximity of the commentary to Thiel's own legal exposure, observing that Thiel was a defendant in the $8 billion Facebook trial then set to begin — "just sayin."[1]
