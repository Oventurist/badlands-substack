# Lynn Forester de Rothschild

Lynn Forester de Rothschild is a financier and philanthropist described in Badlands Media coverage as the effective public face of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild dynasty]].[1] She heads the [[council-for-inclusive-capitalism|Council for Inclusive Capitalism]], a project characterized by alternative-media analyst [[brandon-smith|Brandon Smith]] of Alt-Market as the culmination of decades of globalist agendas and, in his framing, open confirmation of long-dismissed "New World Order" claims about governance by elites.[1]

## Admission of ESG failure

At the B20 Summit in India in 2023, Forester de Rothschild publicly acknowledged the defeat of [[esg|ESG]] — environmental, social and governance investing — as a branded framework.[1] In the same remarks she suggested that the goal going forward would be to replace the term "ESG" with different language less recognizable to the public, an approach Alt-Market read as an intent to abandon the label while continuing the underlying program of steering corporations toward progressive policy commitments.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the admission as a scion of the Rothschilds conceding the need to rebrand ESG terminology as a new form of collectivist stakeholder capitalism, arguing that the public had come to understand "wokeism" as an engineered assault on free-market dynamics and that a forced rebrand was evidence of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] in progress.[1]
