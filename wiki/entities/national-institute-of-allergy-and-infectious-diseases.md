# National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID)

The National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) is the U.S. federal research institute that [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] directed for nearly four decades.[1] By mid-2026 it had become the institutional center of gravity for a wave of legal and administrative actions targeting American scientists over COVID-19 research and alleged efforts to obscure information about the virus's origins.[1]

## Role in the COVID origins actions

A series of debarments, indictments, arrests, leadership removals, and congressional subpoenas — all surfacing after April 2026 — were centered on a small community of scientists affiliated with NIAID.[1] Enforcement in these matters shifted toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1]

Individuals connected to the institute in these actions included [[david-morens|David Morens]], a former Fauci adviser indicted for conspiring to conceal or falsify discussions of research grants; [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]], removed from his role as acting head of NIAID; and [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]], indicted in May 2026 over alleged smuggling of mpox virus samples.[1] Virologist [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]] was separately referred for federal debarment in April 2026.[1]

[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] observed that while the precise timing of the five cases might be coincidental, all were centered on the NIAID-affiliated scientific community that Fauci had led for nearly 40 years.[1]
