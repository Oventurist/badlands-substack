# Erica Carr

Erica Carr is the acting executive director of the [[usaid|United States Agency for International Development]] who, in March 2025, instructed the agency's remaining staff to destroy documents stored at its former Washington headquarters.[1]

## The document destruction email

Carr sent an email, obtained by [[politico|Politico]], directing remaining USAID staff to convene at the agency's now-former headquarters at the Ronald Reagan Building on Tuesday, March 11, 2025, for an "all day" group effort to destroy documents, many of which contained sensitive information.[1] The materials earmarked for destruction included the contents of the agency's "classified safes and personnel documents."[1]

"Shred as many documents first, and reserve the burn bags for when the shredder becomes unavailable or needs a break," the email said.[1] Carr instructed staff to label the burn bags with the words "SECRET" and "USAID/B/IO/" — agency shorthand for "bureau or independent office" — in dark Sharpie.[1] The email provided no reason for the document destruction.[1]

The email was subsequently cited by federal employee unions in a motion filed in Washington, D.C. federal court, where Carr was identified as the agency's acting executive secretary and the instruction described as convening employees for "clearing classified safes and personnel documents."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry read the directive as behavior characteristic of "a panic-stricken organized crime syndicate that is desperate to make itself invisible," and used it to argue sarcastically that USAID and the [[state-department|State Department]] were legitimate organizations.[1] He noted that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had appointed special envoys empowered to operate outside the State Department in order to conduct real diplomacy, and pointed to a diplomatic breakthrough in Gaza around the time Trump entered office as evidence for that framing.[1]
