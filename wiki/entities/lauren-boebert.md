# Lauren Boebert

Lauren Boebert is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Colorado. Within the Badlands corpus she appears principally as the subject of speculation about which populist-aligned Republicans might be targeted for removal in primary contests following the defeat of [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]].

## Speculation about the 2026 Colorado primary

In the May 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] — who lives in Boebert's district — wrote that she was curious whether "this operation" against dissenting Republicans would continue and "now center more definitively on Lauren Boebert here in my district," noting that the Colorado primary was scheduled for June 30. She framed the question rhetorically: "Can Boebert stop what is coming?" The remark reflects Ashe's broader position, restated in the same segment, that "elections are still fake."
