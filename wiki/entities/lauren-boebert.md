# Lauren Boebert

Lauren Boebert is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Colorado, identified in Badlands commentary as a potential next target of the campaign of primary challenges that removed [[thomas-massie|Rep. Thomas Massie]] in May 2026.[1]

## 2026 primary speculation

Writing after Massie's defeat, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] — a Colorado resident — said she was "curious if this operation is going to continue, but now center more definitively on Lauren Boebert here in my district," noting that her district's primary was scheduled for June 30.[1] She closed the observation with the rhetorical question "Can Boebert stop what is coming?", echoing the framing she applied to Massie.[1]

## Questioning of Hillary Clinton (2026)

During the March 2026 congressional hearings connected to the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] inquiries, Boebert posed the questions to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] regarding [[pizzagate|PizzaGate]] and the alleged [[frazzledrip|"frazzledrip"]] video said to have been stored in an "insurance" folder on Anthony Weiner's laptop.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], a constituent of Boebert's who described herself as a frequent critic, credited her for the effort: "Boebert asked the main questions on the minds of many Americans and, judging by the reactions of Clinton's attorneys, she was over the target. It took courage."[2]

## Third district seat and the 2024 succession

After Boebert announced she would leave Colorado's third congressional district to seek election elsewhere, U.S. Air Force veteran and former state representative [[ron-hanks|Ron Hanks]] announced a run for the abandoned seat at the start of 2024.[3] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed Hanks as "an honorable man and an excellent choice for Colorado's third congressional district," and proposed his candidacy as a test of whether Fourteenth Amendment lawfare would be used against January 6 attendees.[3]

## Opposition to Scalise's speakership bid (October 2023)

Boebert was among the Republicans who refused to fall in line behind Majority Leader [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] after the conference nominated him for Speaker of the House on October 11, 2023 over Judiciary Committee Chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] by a 113–99 secret ballot.[4] Asked about the result, she said flatly, "I'm voting for Jim Jordan on the floor," a stance that contributed to the sense of uncertainty hanging over the conference given that only five Republican defections were needed to deny Scalise the gavel.[4]

## Trump's Aurora visit (October 2024)

When the [[donald-trump|Trump]] campaign announced a rally in [[aurora-colorado|Aurora]] for October 2024, Boebert — then representing Colorado's 3rd Congressional District while running to represent the state's 4th — said she was "ready and thrilled" to have the former president in Colorado.[5]
