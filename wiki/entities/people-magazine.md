# People (magazine)

People is an American celebrity and human-interest magazine, best known in popular culture for its annual "Sexiest Man Alive" designation.[1]

## 2024 Sexiest Man Alive

On November 12, 2024, People named actor [[john-krasinski|John Krasinski]] its 2024 Sexiest Man Alive, revealing the choice on an episode of *The Late Show with Stephen Colbert* and simultaneously publishing the cover image on social media.[1] The reveal segment featured host [[stephen-colbert|Stephen Colbert]] pretending to be aggrieved at being passed over, followed by an 1980s-infomercial-styled comedy sketch in which Krasinski instructed him on how to be sexy.[1]

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief covered the announcement as a symbolic media signal, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reading Krasinski's screen roles — a bureaucratically hapless office worker, a father resisting an alien invasion during a period of UAP declassification hearings, and a Jack Ryan arc ending in public whistleblowing on intelligence-community corruption — as suggestive of the political moment, and contrasting the pick favorably with the magazine's earlier cover choices.[1]
