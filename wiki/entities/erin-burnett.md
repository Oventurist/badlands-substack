# Erin Burnett

Erin Burnett is an American television news anchor employed by [[cnn|CNN]], where she hosts the network's evening news and interview programming.[1]

## May 2024 interview with Joe Biden

Burnett drew attention in May 2024 for an interview with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] that aired on CNN on Wednesday, May 8, 2024, and which critics characterized as a "train-wreck" for the president.[1] During the exchange, Burnett recited a list of statistics describing the condition of the American economy and noted that "voters, by a wide margin, trust Trump more on the economy."[1] Among the underlying causes she cited were a doubling of the cost of buying a home, a decline in real income after accounting for inflation, economic growth falling well short of expectations, and consumer confidence sitting near a two-year low.[1]

Burnett then asked Biden whether he was worried that he was running out of time to turn the economy around before the November election.[1] Biden responded that "we've already turned it around," and asserted that "the polling data has been wrong all along."[1] The Badlands News Brief highlighted the exchange as an instance of the president denying observable economic reality, contrasting his claim with the fact that inflation surged after he took office to rates unseen since the early 1980s, peaking at an annual rate of 9.1 percent in June 2022.[1]

The interview was framed by aggregators such as [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] under the headline "Look at his face," referring to Biden's visible reaction as Burnett read the economic figures.[1]
