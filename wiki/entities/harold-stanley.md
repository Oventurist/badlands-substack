# Harold Stanley

Harold Stanley was an American investment banker and the founder of [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]].[1] He is listed in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" among the most notable public-facing members of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, cited as evidence of the Order's reach into high finance alongside [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]] and [[guaranty-trust-company|Guaranty Trust]].[1]
