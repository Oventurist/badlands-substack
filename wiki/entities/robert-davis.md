# Robert Davis

Robert Davis is the young man accused of killing Philadelphia journalist [[josh-kruger|Josh Kruger]] in October 2023.[1] He was 19 years old at the time a warrant was issued for his arrest.[1]

## The Kruger killing

Kruger, 39, was shot seven times at the base of the stairs in his Point Breeze home in the middle of the night.[1] Surveillance video near the scene, together with tips from Kruger's friends and family, led detectives to Davis, and a warrant for his arrest was issued days later.[1] Police described the two as acquaintances and said Kruger "was trying to help [Davis] get through life."[1]

## Family account

In interviews with [[philadelphia-inquirer|The Philadelphia Inquirer]], Davis's mother and older brother said that Davis was just 15 when he and Kruger began a years-long relationship involving drugs, and that Davis had told them Kruger was threatening to post sexually explicit videos of him online before the shooting.[1] Their account, they said, was drawn from recent conversations with Davis and from years of watching his life unravel as he tried to conceal both the relationship and his drug addiction.[1]

Those assertions added troubling complexities to a killing that had already drawn national attention.[1] Separately, detectives discovered what multiple law-enforcement sources described as explicit photos and messages on Kruger's phone; the sources, speaking anonymously about an ongoing investigation, called the material "disturbing" and said it had been turned over to the police department's Special Victims Unit for further analysis, without stating whether it was connected to Davis.[1]
