# Angelina Jolie

Angelina Jolie is a Hollywood actress and former United Nations humanitarian ambassador whose November 2025 visit to Ukraine became an international news story after Ukrainian draft enforcement officers detained and conscripted a member of her travelling party.[1]

## 2025 Ukraine visit and conscription incident

Jolie made an unannounced visit to Ukraine in early November 2025. Her convoy was travelling toward Kherson when it was stopped at a military roadblock near the city of Yuzhnoukrainsk in the Nikolaev Region on Tuesday, November 4, 2025.[1] Despite a member of the entourage telling officials that he was transporting an "important person," military recruitment officers detained the man — identified in some reports as Jolie's driver and in others as a bodyguard — and conscripted him into the Ukrainian armed forces.[1]

According to footage circulating on Telegram, Jolie personally went to the draft center to attempt to secure his release, and TASS sources claimed she also tried to contact the office of Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] to resolve the matter.[1] The episode was reported by [[rt|RT]] and was widely circulated as an illustration of the coercive character of Ukraine's mobilization system.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the incident as darkly comic, questioning what Jolie was doing in Ukraine at all and mocking her apparent instinct to invoke her celebrity status at the recruitment depot.[1] The commentary paired the Jolie story with reporting on mass Ukrainian desertions, arguing that ordinary men are dragged from their homes and vehicles to die in what the author called a pointless war, and joking that members of Congress who voted to fund Ukraine should themselves be conscripted.[1] The pairing situated Jolie's visit within the broader [[ukrainian-mobilization-crisis|Ukrainian mobilization crisis]].[1]
