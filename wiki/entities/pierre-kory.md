# Pierre Kory

Pierre Kory is an American physician specialising in emergency care and pulmonology who became a prominent figure in medical-dissident circles.[1] In March 2025 he participated in a review of the medical records of a six-year-old Texas girl whose death had been widely attributed to measles.[1]

## The Texas measles death review

[[childrens-health-defense|Children's Health Defense]] obtained access to the girl's medical records at the specific request of her family, who asked that the findings be shared and reported as a warning to other parents.[1] The review was presented on CHD.TV by Polly Tommey, CHD Chief Scientific Officer Brian Hooker, Kory, and Dr. Ben Edwards.[1]

Hooker explained that the girl had actually been recovering from measles.[1] Her pediatrician had given cough syrup and recommended Tylenol; after the rash began fading she developed problems with coughing and breathing, and her parents took her to the emergency room where antibiotics were administered.[1] The girl showed no progress after two and a half days, ended up in the ICU, and died 36 hours later.[1]

Kory, reviewing the records in his capacity as a critical-care and pulmonology specialist, concluded: "This case was tragic. But she did not die of measles by any stretch of the imagination. In fact, she died of a pneumonia."[1] The item ran as a bonus story in the Badlands News Brief of March 20, 2025.[1]
