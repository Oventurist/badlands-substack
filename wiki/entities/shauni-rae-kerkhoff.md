# Shauni Rae Kerkhoff

Shauni Rae Kerkhoff is a former officer of the [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] who was identified in a November 2025 Blaze News investigation as a forensic gait match to the unidentified suspect in the [[january-5-2021-pipe-bombs|January 5, 2021 pipe bomb]] case.[1]

## Blaze News gait analysis

A software algorithm that analyzes walking parameters — including flexion (knee bend), hip extension, speed, step length, cadence and variance — rated Kerkhoff, 31, of Alexandria, Virginia, as a 94% match to the pipe-bomb suspect shown on video from January 5, 2021.[1] The veteran analyst who ran the analysis for Blaze News said that, taking into account visual observations the program can struggle with, he personally assessed the match at closer to 98%.[1] Several intelligence sources confirmed the investigation's findings to the outlet, and a source close to a congressional investigation of January 6 said evidence had recently emerged pointing toward possible law enforcement involvement in the planting of the devices.[1]

## Career

Kerkhoff served as a Capitol Police officer for four and a half years before leaving the department in mid-2021 for a security detail at the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]], according to sources cited by Blaze News.[1] CIA spokeswoman Liz Lyons stated that the subject worked in campus security.[1]

## Surveillance of her residence

Kerkhoff's residence in Alexandria appeared to be under the watch of law enforcement officers on the Friday night before the report's publication.[1] Blaze News editor in chief [[christopher-bedford|Christopher Bedford]] was pulled over by local police after stopping to observe the home, and was subsequently allowed to leave.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[canncon|CannCon]] described the report as "definitely huge" and as another case in which "the conspiracy theorists were right again," adding that the untold story of the 2020 election had still to reach the mainstream.[1]
