# Lisa Murkowski

Lisa Murkowski is a Republican United States Senator from Alaska who, in March 2026, publicly declined to support the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]], placing her at odds with [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and most Senate Republicans on federal election legislation.[1]

## Opposition to the SAVE America Act

Speaking to a LindellTV reporter on Monday, March 9, 2026, Murkowski said she would not vote for the SAVE America Act because implementing documentary proof of citizenship and voter identification requirements would "disenfranchise" rural Alaskans.[1] The bill, which passed the House in February 2026, would require documentary proof of US citizenship to register to vote in federal elections and direct states to remove non-citizens from voter rolls.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized Murkowski's reasoning as a progressive, expansionist talking point about the electoral franchise and argued it was itself demeaning: between 60 and 70 percent of Alaska's population is indigenous, Native Alaskan, or American Indian, and roughly 20 percent lives in rural communities, so the claim amounted to alleging that those constituents could not obtain identification or prove their citizenship.[1]

## Elections and ranked choice voting

Ashe in America argued that Murkowski was never a plausible vote for the bill, describing her as "a progressive when it comes to elections" and noting her support for [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked choice voting]], which the commentary called un-auditable and unverifiable.[1] Because of ranked choice voting in Alaska, the piece contended, voters would be hard pressed to hold Murkowski accountable at the ballot box.[1]

The commentary expressed hope that every senator voting "no" on the SAVE America Act would be put on the record insulting their constituents as incapable of lawfully proving their identity, predicting the episode had "all the classic earmarks of an excellent boomerang" heading into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]].[1]

## Chinese and Russian naval patrol off Alaska

In August 2023, Murkowski and fellow Alaska Republican Senator [[dan-sullivan|Dan Sullivan]] issued a joint statement disclosing that a total of eleven Russian and Chinese vessels had been operating near the [[aleutian-islands|Aleutian Islands]], and that they were met in response by four [[us-navy|US Navy]] destroyers.[2] Murkowski said she and Sullivan had been in "close contact with leadership from Alaska Command for several days now and received detailed classified briefings about the foreign vessels that are transiting U.S. waters in the Aleutians."[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that the most notable feature of the episode was that the American response, as reported by [[cnn|CNN]], came from the two Alaskan senators rather than from the executive branch or the national security establishment, with no [[white-house|White House]] statement more than twelve hours after the incident was reported.[2]

## Vote against Kash Patel (February 2025)

Murkowski was one of two Republicans whose opposition held the February 20, 2025 confirmation of [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] as [[fbi|FBI]] director to a 51-49 margin.[3] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentary by Brad Cologero Getz urged readers to bookmark the vote, noting that Murkowski's primary falls in early 2028 and that of fellow holdout [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]] in early 2026, and calling for strong America-First candidates to run against them both.[3]
