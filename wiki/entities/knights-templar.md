# Knights Templar

The Knights Templar were a medieval Christian military order described in Badlands Media commentary as the group responsible for organizing the Crusades.[1] Beginning in 1307 and continuing throughout the 14th century, Templars were routinely arrested amid allegations that they conducted occult rituals venerating [[baphomet|Baphomet]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the Templars as deeply associated with occult mysticism and as an order "beloved by families such as the Rothschild," extending the same claim to other Crusader-era military orders including the Knights of Malta.[1] The commentary cited a photograph of Nathaniel Philip Rothschild's $87 million yacht — put up for sale in mid-2024 — and the flag flown on its stern, credited to *Breaking History* co-host [[matt-ehret|Matt Ehret]], as visual evidence of that asserted continuity.[1] See also the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]].[1]

## Origins in Ehret's account

In Part 7 of his "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler" series, Matthew Ehret writes that the Templar cultists — described as Baphomet worshippers — were created by the grand strategist [[bernard-de-clairvaux|Bernard de Clairvaux]] of the Cistercian order, an enemy of the Augustinian Platonist Peter Abelard who placed at least two popes into power.[2] Clairvaux established the constitution of what Ehret calls a gnostic "Christian mercenary" cult that would soon control the majority of banking operations and inaugurate a new era of Christian-versus-Muslim and Christian-versus-Jewish religious violence.[2]

When Venice and the Ultramontane Papacy launched the First Crusade in 1095, the trade routes established by [[radhanite-traders|Radhanite Jews]] were the first things destroyed in Europe by the Crusaders, whom the Templars organized and who then seized that infrastructure to wage war.[2] Ehret ties the Templar fixation on Solomon's Temple — their headquarters lay beneath its presumed site — and their obsession with the Grail myth of a royal bloodline descended from Jesus and Mary Magdalene to the later emergence of Rosicrucianism, modern masonry and political Zionism.[2]

## Origins, Mithraism, and the Solomon's Temple project

Part 6 of the same series describes the Templars as a mercenary cult established by the Cistercian grand strategist Bernard of Clairvaux in 1118 CE, officially titled "The Poor Fellow-Soldiers of Christ and of the Temple of Solomon."[3] Failing to live up to their aspirations of poverty, the order became the dominant financial empire across Europe and the Mediterranean and oversaw a network of Mithraic mystery cults stretching from Russia to England and the Middle East.[3] The Templars frequently managed the [[kingdom-of-jerusalem]] (1099–1291), and their Jerusalem headquarters lay in elaborate crypts beneath the Al-Aqsa Mosque, the supposed site of Solomon's Temple — which the article suggests may have housed a Mithraeum.[3]

[[albert-pike]] wrote in *Morals and Dogma* (1871) that the nine knights crusaders of 1118, among them Geoffroi de Saint-Omer and Hugues de Payens, took an oath before the Patriarch of Constantinople, and that while their avowed object was to protect Christian pilgrims, "their secret object was the re-building of the Temple of Solomon on the model prophesied by Ezekiel."[3] Pike also judged that "the Templars were unintelligent and therefore unsuccessful Jesuits," and that the [[jesuits|Jesuit Order]] was itself a reconstructed and more disciplined Templar Order.[3]

The Jerusalem crosses became affiliated with the Templars before the order was publicly dissolved, and reappeared on the insignia of the [[equestrian-order-of-the-holy-sepulchre]].[3] Grand Master Jacques de Molay, burned at the stake in 1314, survives in [[aleister-crowley]]'s tenth-degree "elevenfold de Molay cross."[3] The article further argues that [[cecil-rhodes]]' secret society, based on the Grail myths of the Round Table, was a revival of the Templar-managed Crusader project.[3]

## Part 1: Baphomet beneath the Temple Mount

Part 1 of the series recalls that the Gnostic order of Templars was caught worshipping a demonic entity in the form of Baphomet beneath the [[temple-mount|Temple Mount]] during the early 14th century.[4] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] connects this to Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]]'s 19th-century excavation of the Templar headquarters in Jerusalem, where Warren mapped caverns and tunnels he believed had been designed to infuse the soil of Jerusalem with sacrificial blood in a pact with dark spirit forces.[4] Ehret also lists the Crusader Knights among the historically anti-Jewish movements that a simplistic "Jews are the cause of all evil" thesis would retroactively rehabilitate.[4]
