# Paul Finnegan

**Paul Finnegan** is a private equity executive named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as a member of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article identifies him as co-CEO of [[madison-dearborn-partners|Madison Dearborn]], which it calls "a private equity engine linked to Fed governance and infrastructure control."[1] He is listed among the HMC figures the author cites to argue that Harvard's endowment governance constitutes a cartel binding university capital to private equity, [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]-adjacent networks and infrastructure holdings within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
