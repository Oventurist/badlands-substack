# Chris Wright

Chris Wright is the United States Secretary of Energy in the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], heading the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]].

## Nuclear microreactor air transport

The February 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]] reports that Wright publicized the first air transport of a [[nuclear-microreactor|nuclear microreactor]] prototype, conducted jointly by the Department of Energy and the Department of War as part of a Defense Department demonstration program run through the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]. Writing on X, Wright said: "President Trump promised the American people that he would unleash American energy dominance. Yesterday, @ENERGY and @DeptofWar partnered to move a nuclear reactor by air for the very first time. This is the next chapter for U.S. energy."

The Department of Energy separately framed the demonstration as a step in fulfilling an executive order Trump signed on May 23, 2025, directing the deployment of advanced nuclear reactor technologies for national security purposes. The Brief pairs the announcement with commentary from Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] on nuclear modernization following the expiration of the [[new-start-treaty|New START Treaty]], while noting that no new nuclear weapons policy had been announced.


## Planned Venezuela visit (February 2026)

The February 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-4ec|Badlands Brief]] reported, citing an exclusive [[politico|Politico]] interview, that Wright said he would soon travel to [[venezuela|Venezuela]] to meet the country's leaders and discuss the future of its state-run oil sector. The Brief situated the planned visit within the administration's effort to reshape ties with Caracas and to address production and management problems at [[pdvsa|PDVSA]].
