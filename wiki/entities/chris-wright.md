# Chris Wright

Chris Wright is the United States Secretary of Energy in the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], heading the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]].

## Nuclear microreactor air transport

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Wright publicized the first air transport of a [[nuclear-microreactor|nuclear microreactor]] prototype, conducted jointly by the Department of Energy and the Department of War as part of a Defense Department demonstration program run through the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]. Writing on X, Wright said: "President Trump promised the American people that he would unleash American energy dominance. Yesterday, @ENERGY and @DeptofWar partnered to move a nuclear reactor by air for the very first time. This is the next chapter for U.S. energy."

The Department of Energy separately framed the demonstration as a step in fulfilling an executive order Trump signed on May 23, 2025, directing the deployment of advanced nuclear reactor technologies for national security purposes. The Brief pairs the announcement with commentary from Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] on nuclear modernization following the expiration of the [[new-start-treaty|New START Treaty]], while noting that no new nuclear weapons policy had been announced.


## Planned Venezuela visit (February 2026)

The February 10, 2026 Badlands Brief reported, citing an exclusive [[politico|Politico]] interview, that Wright said he would soon travel to [[venezuela|Venezuela]] to meet the country's leaders and discuss the future of its state-run oil sector. The Brief situated the planned visit within the administration's effort to reshape ties with Caracas and to address production and management problems at [[pdvsa|PDVSA]].

## CERAWeek and the Iran-driven price surge (March 2026)

The March 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, Alternative Annexations & New(ish) Regime Change, reports that Wright called for increased domestic oil production to offset global supply disruptions caused by the conflict with Iran, as US gasoline prices approached the roughly $4-per-gallon "psychological threshold" at which consumer driving demand historically declines. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] notes that Wright appeared at [[ceraweek|CERAWeek]] in Houston urging oil and gas leaders to raise output immediately, while the [[white-house|White House]] held direct meetings with executives and diplomats to coordinate higher domestic production. He reads the push as a revival of the "Drill, Baby, Drill" mandate that is in his view only the first stage toward sovereign energy independence and accelerated nuclear power.
