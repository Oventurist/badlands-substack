# Josh Paul

Josh Paul is a former United States [[state-department|State Department]] official who resigned in protest over American policy of supplying lethal arms to Israel for use in Gaza.[1] After leaving government he became one of the more prominent insider critics of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's handling of the war.[1]

## Criticism of the Biden administration

Commenting on the January 2025 [[gaza-ceasefire|Gaza ceasefire]] to The Independent, Paul said the arrangement was "a deal that, in its basic form, has been on the table for many months," and called it "an absolute travesty that the Biden administration never used any of the massive leverage it had to push it over the finish line."[1]

Badlands Media cited Paul, alongside fellow resignees Annelle Sheline and Tariq Habash, as credentialed corroboration for its central claim: that the ceasefire terms had long been achievable and that the delay reflected a political choice by Washington rather than the intransigence of the parties.[1] The article contrasted this record with the aggressive approach taken by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], who pressed [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to accept a framework he had repeatedly rejected.[1]
