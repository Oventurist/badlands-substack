# Matthias Warnig

**Matthias Warnig** is a German businessman and the former executive director of the Swiss-based operating company for the [[nord-stream-2|Nord Stream 2]] natural gas pipeline.[1]

In March 2025 the Financial Times reported that Warnig was setting up a deal, backed by unnamed U.S. investors, to activate the never-commissioned pipeline — a move the paper described as once unthinkable and as evidence of the breadth of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rapprochement with Moscow.[1] The plan was framed as potential leverage in the ongoing Ukraine peace negotiations.[1]
