# Children's Investment Fund Foundation

The **Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF)** is a London-based philanthropic foundation attached to the hedge fund The Children's Investment Fund Management, founded and run by Sir Chris Hohn.[1]

According to a 2019 study by Dean Andromedas published by Executive Intelligence Review and cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], CIFF gave no less than £121,140 (about $155,000) to [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]].[1] The foundation holds an endowment of over £2 billion, and its chief executive is Kate Hampton, who also serves as vice chair of the European Climate Foundation and as a board member of the Carbon Disclosure Project.[1]

The same study reported that the financier [[george-soros|George Soros]] topped Extinction Rebellion's list of donors in documents obtained by [[breitbart-news|Breitbart News]], although the amount he gave was redacted.[1]
