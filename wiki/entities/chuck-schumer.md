# Chuck Schumer

Chuck Schumer is a United States Senator from New York who served as Senate Majority Leader. He appears in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as evidence of American efforts to unseat [[benjamin-netanyahu]].

The article cites Schumer's March 2024 call for new elections in [[israel]], reported by NPR, describing it as "essentially calling Netanyahu illegitimate." It groups the episode with the ongoing corruption probe against Netanyahu and [[usaid]]-funded protests over judicial reform in 2023 as components of an attempted Israeli regime change that "ended abruptly after October 7th."

## The "Election Integrity Group" (May 2026)

In his May 12, 2026 [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement announcing the [[election-integrity-army|Election Integrity Army]] for the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attacked Schumer — referring to him as "Palestinian Chuck Schumer" — for hiring former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]] as part of a Democrat-led "Election Integrity Group" that, in Trump's words, "will no doubt try to suppress Republican voters, and interfere in our Elections." Trump also named the election lawyer [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]] as part of the group. The statement continues a pattern, already documented on this page, of Schumer convening Democratic election-law experts ahead of the midterms; an earlier June 2026 report in the corpus described a similar "slate of experts" he assembled around the same elections.
