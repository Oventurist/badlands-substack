# David Rubenstein

David Rubenstein is an American financier and philanthropist who, as of February 2025, served as Chairman of the Board of Trustees of the [[john-f-kennedy-center-for-the-performing-arts|John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts]] in Washington, D.C., until President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he was terminating multiple trustees and installing himself as chairman.[1] Badlands contributor Jon Herold noted that Rubenstein was the individual Trump replaced as chairman, and pointed to a December 2021 article he had written that focused entirely on Rubenstein.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands writers characterize Rubenstein as "an elite even among the global Regime elite," a "philanthropist" whose institutional footprint spans American historical memory.[1] Commentator Chris Paul highlighted that Rubenstein founded the [[rubenstein-center-for-white-house-history|Rubenstein Center for White House History]], whose Senior Vice President and Director was [[colleen-shogan|Colleen Shogan]] — the Archivist of the United States dismissed by Trump on the same day the Kennedy Center shake-up was announced.[1]

Paul advanced what he labelled a "working conspiracy theory": given the precedent of presidential records held at presidential libraries and Joe Biden's Senate papers held at the University of Delaware, he asked whether the Rubenstein Center might serve a comparable function for records moved out of the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]].[1] He acknowledged the mainstream view rejects this, arguing that if NARA documents were ever found at the Center for White House History it would be dismissed as "a simple mistake, cleared up quickly by commie bureaucrats."[1]

Paul further observed, crediting Jon Herold, that Rubenstein maintains his own Holocaust Museum collection, and framed the pattern as an attempt to "own history" and to write an "Official History."[1] He also noted that Rubenstein received a Presidential Medal of Freedom from Joe Biden, remarking pointedly that "Joe Biden is not a fake president in David Rubenstein's history."[1]
