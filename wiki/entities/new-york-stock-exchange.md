# New York Stock Exchange

The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) is the principal American securities exchange, located on Wall Street in New York City, and the venue at which listed companies mark their public debuts by ringing the opening bell.[1]

## Public Square listing (2023)

On Thursday, July 20, 2023, the conservative online marketplace [[public-square|Public Square]] began trading on the NYSE under the ticker $PSQH.[1] The floor was described as unusually lively, with participants chanting "USA! USA!" as [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]], [[kim-guilfoyle|Kim Guilfoyle]] and chief executive [[michael-seifert|Michael Seifert]] stood at the podium.[1] CNBC personnel broadcasting from the floor -- [[jim-cramer|Jim Cramer]] and "Squawk on the Street" host [[david-faber|David Faber]] -- were reported to have reacted with visible disapproval, with Faber covering his ears while the broadcast turned to home prices.[1]
