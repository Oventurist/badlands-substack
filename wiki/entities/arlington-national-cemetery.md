# Arlington National Cemetery

**Arlington National Cemetery** is the United States military cemetery in Arlington, Virginia. It appears in the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series as the site of a December 2024 visit by [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] that the author reads as part of a cluster of deliberate signals surrounding the Army/Navy football game.[1]

## The Kash Patel visit

According to the article, Patel visited Arlington on the same day as the Army/Navy game, and specifically visited the gravesite associated with [[extortion-17|Extortion 17]], the Chinook shot down in Afghanistan in August 2011 while carrying seventeen Navy SEALs.[1] The author treats the timing and the choice of gravesite as intentional communication rather than coincidence, placing it alongside [[palantir|Palantir]]'s game-day advertising and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appearance at the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] as evidence of coordinated messaging.[1]

Patel is also described as retweeting an article featuring the number 17 and discussing "spy games" at the [[fbi|FBI]], which the author folds into the same numerical pattern.[1]

## Significance in the chapter

Arlington's role in the argument is symbolic rather than institutional: the cemetery is the physical location that ties the contemporary signalling to the unresolved questions the series raises about the Extortion 17 shootdown and the announced death of [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] a few months earlier.[1]
