# Texas Commerce Bank

Texas Commerce Bank was a Houston, Texas banking institution formed in 1964 by the merger of the National Bank of Commerce and [[texas-national-bank|Texas National Bank]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The Badlands Media account treats it as a CIA bank from its inception and as the destination of Mexican cartel drug proceeds.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

## Origins and the Bush relationship

A vanity book marking the bank's centenary in 1986, commissioned by bank president Ben Love in 1981, describes its "cozy relationship" with [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and his [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Off-Shore Company]], which was linked to the CIA's abortive [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion of Cuba in 1961.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The book records that the bank's "major predecessor," Texas National Bank, played a "significant role in helping finance the early development of Zapata Off-Shore Company," whose loans for drilling rigs and tending vessels made it one of the bank's most significant oil-related accounts of the late 1950s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The bank was founded by the family of [[james-baker|James Baker]], the close Bush friend and administration official who arranged for [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] to be hired into its international division as a CIA non-official cover officer.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Jeb was posted to Caracas, Venezuela in 1977 as branch manager and vice president at 24.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

## Board and ownership

In 1977 the bank's board included Lady Bird Johnson and the recently defeated President Gerald Ford — who had appointed Bush to lead the [[cia|CIA]] in 1976.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) In the 1980s [[kenneth-lay|Kenneth Lay]], founder of the CIA-connected Enron, joined the board.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Howard Hughes's CIA-linked Summa Corporation used the bank to purchase a number of properties on the Las Vegas strip.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The bank was bought by Chemical Bank in 1987 and is now part of JPMorgan Chase.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

## Cartel deposits

Beginning with a $40,000 deposit in December 1993, [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] made 25 further cash deposits at the bank through February 1995, in sums ranging from $98,000 to $800,000, carried from Mexico to Texas in cardboard boxes and suitcases on commercial flights by his bagman Jorge Stergios.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) A Houston federal judge and jury confiscated $9 million from the account in 1997 as drug-trade proceeds.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article describes Texas Commerce Bank as the bank of choice for Latin American drug cartels and notes it was found to have stashed $7 million in [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] drug profits, yet faced no legal consequence — protection it attributes to the shadow government.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
