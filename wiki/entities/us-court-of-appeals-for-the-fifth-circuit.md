# US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit

The US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit is a federal appellate court that in February 2026 issued a 2-1 decision backing the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's reading of federal immigration detention law.[1]

## Immigration detention

The Fifth Circuit's ruling was the administration's first appellate win on the mandatory-detention question and was followed the next month by a parallel 2-1 decision from the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-eighth-circuit|US Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit]], which held that federal law mandates rather than merely permits detention in most deportation cases.[1]
