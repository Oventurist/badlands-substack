# US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit

The United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit is the federal appellate court based in New Orleans, Louisiana, with jurisdiction over federal district courts in Louisiana, Mississippi, and Texas. Because of its geographic coverage of much of the southern border, it hears a substantial volume of immigration litigation and is frequently the venue in which federal immigration policy is tested.

The March 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]] notes that in February 2026 the Fifth Circuit issued a 2-1 decision upholding the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's position that federal law mandates detention in most deportation cases. That ruling was followed the next month by a parallel 2-1 decision from the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-eighth-circuit|Eighth Circuit]], giving the administration two appellate victories on the same statutory question.
