# Aston Mack

Aston Mack is a 35-year-old [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] activist known for his role in protests in Florida who was arrested on May 13, 2025 in a child sex sting operation.[1]

## Arrest

According to reporting aggregated in the Badlands News Brief, Mack allegedly sent explicit messages to an undercover officer posing as a 14-year-old girl on the platform "Skip The Games" and arranged a meeting for sex.[1] When confronted by police and reporter Chris Hansen, Mack allegedly attempted to flee.[1] He faces charges of soliciting a minor and traveling to meet a child, and was held in custody on $250,000 bail.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Mack had been positioned as a role model and "globally celebrated for being virtuous and good and focused on social justice and equitable rights," pointing to the English football club Arsenal having worn a kit honoring Mack and Black Lives Matter — a promotional post the club later deleted, though archived copies survived.[1] She argued that the criminal impulse involved cannot be rehabilitated, that the social-justice premise that it can has emboldened predators, and that such offenders should be removed from society.[1] She tied the case to a broader claim that "many of those in power are also monsters that prey on children," but that the pattern was "coming to light faster now."[1]
