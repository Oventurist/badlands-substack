# Colin Powell

**Colin Powell** was a retired United States Army general, former Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and former Secretary of State.[1]

## June 2020 denunciation of Trump

In Badlands Media's account of the alleged [[purple-revolution|Purple Revolution]], Powell appears as the last of four senior military figures to break publicly with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the first week of June 2020, denouncing him on June 7, 2020.[1] The sequence began with Trump's June 1 announcement that he might deploy federal troops against nationwide rioting, followed by former president [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]'s June 2 endorsement of the protests, General [[james-mattis|James Mattis]]'s June 3 statement that Trump was a threat to the Constitution together with Secretary of Defense [[mark-esper|Mark Esper]]'s public opposition to the troop plan, and General [[john-kelly|John Kelly]]'s June 5 denunciation.[1]

The article treats this cluster as evidence that former and serving military leadership had been infiltrated at the highest levels and was engaged in what it calls literal mutiny, with some participants invoking the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]].[1] Powell is placed within the category of "TV Generals" — figures the author argues were elevated and rewarded to sustain the military-industrial complex and oppose Trump's stated aim of avoiding foreign wars.[1]
