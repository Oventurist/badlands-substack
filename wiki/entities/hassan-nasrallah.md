# Hassan Nasrallah

Hassan Nasrallah was a Secretary-General of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] who was assassinated by [[israel|Israel]] in late September 2024.[1]

## Commemoration and Israeli strikes (September 2025)

In September 2025, Hezbollah was preparing to commemorate the first anniversary of the assassinations of its two Secretaries-General, Nasrallah and Hashem Safieddine.[1] On the evening of Thursday, 18 September 2025, the Israeli military launched a series of airstrikes on areas south and north of the [[litani-river|Litani River]] in Lebanon, after issuing urgent warnings for residents to stay away from six designated locations.[1]

A Lebanese security source told Arab News that "the timing of the attacks is striking, as it coincides with Hezbollah's preparations to commemorate the first anniversary of the assassinations of its two Secretary-Generals Hassan Nasrallah and Hashem Safieddine," describing the strikes as "an attempt to remind the party that Israel continues to pursue it."[1] The same source expected Israeli attacks to escalate up to the first anniversary of Nasrallah's death on 27 September.[1]


## Death in Dahieh (September 2024)

Nasrallah was killed on the evening of Friday, September 27, 2024, when the Israeli air force struck an underground complex hidden in the southern Beirut suburb of Dahieh using roughly a hundred munitions, believed to include U.S.-made 2,000-pound bombs, in the operation documented at [[killing-of-hassan-nasrallah|the killing of Hassan Nasrallah]].[2] Careful to the point of paranoia about his security and rarely appearing in public, he would have given little notice of his plan to make the trip, yet Israeli intelligence penetration of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] was deep enough that Israel knew he and other surviving members of the group's decimated leadership would be at the supposedly secret location.[2] [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] gave permission for the strike from New York, where he had just addressed the UN General Assembly.[2] An unsubstantiated report in Le Parisien claimed the mole who alerted the Israelis was Iranian, a striking detail given that [[iran|Iran]] is Hezbollah's principal backer.[2]
