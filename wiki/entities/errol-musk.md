# Errol Musk

Errol Musk is the father of Tesla and SpaceX chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and an occasional public commentator on his son's affairs.[1]

## Comments on the Trump–Musk feud

Speaking to journalists in Moscow in June 2025, where he was attending a technology forum, Errol Musk weighed in on the public falling-out between his son and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], suggesting the two former allies might still find common ground.[1] He characterized his son's sharp response as rooted in principle while allowing that compromise was possible, saying, "I think Elon might agree to step down a little … because he's basically standing up for his principles."[1]

The dispute had escalated over Trump's "Big Beautiful" tax and spending bill, which Elon Musk criticized as undermining the budget-trimming work of [[doge|the Department of Government Efficiency]].[1] Badlands commentary treated the elder Musk's Moscow remarks as a public signal that a reconciliation between the two men was already in motion.[1]
