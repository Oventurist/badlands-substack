# Why We Vote

Why We Vote is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program hosted by [[canncon|CannCon]], airing Tuesdays at 5pm ET as of January 2023.[1] Each week the host interviews voting experts from across the country, exploring why the network regards the electoral foundation of the American republic as requiring continued focus from patriots despite what it characterizes as repeated attempts at fraud and election theft.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Why We Vote on Tuesdays at 5pm ET, with CannCon interviewing voting experts nationwide about keeping election integrity central to the Republic despite repeated attempts at fraud and election theft.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Why We Vote in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]

## December 2025 episodes

Ashe in America announced that she and CannCon would host Patrick Byrne on Why We Vote to discuss the Venezuelan election fraud story emerging from Tina Peters' First Amendment appeal filings, a Lara Logan interview, and Emerald Robinson's reporting.[5] She also pointed to an earlier episode in which she and Colonel Shawn Smith went through the official narrative on the Peters-commissioned Mesa County reports.[5]
