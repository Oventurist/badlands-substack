# Jasmine Crockett

Jasmine Crockett is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas's 30th congressional district who ran unsuccessfully for the Democratic nomination for a Texas US Senate seat in 2026.[1]

## 2026 Senate primary defeat

Crockett lost the Democratic Senate primary to state Rep. [[james-talarico|James Talarico]] in the [[2026-texas-primary-elections|March 2026 Texas primaries]], a closely watched contest given her national profile.[1]

## Disenfranchisement claims

On election night, Crockett told supporters in Dallas that voters had been "disenfranchised," stating that her campaign received calls and emails from people who reported being denied entry to polling locations or experiencing confusion about where they were allowed to vote.[1] The confusion was attributed to procedural changes requiring some voters to cast ballots at assigned precinct locations rather than at any polling site.[1] A Dallas County judge initially ordered polling places to stay open longer in response, but the [[texas-supreme-court|Texas Supreme Court]] blocked the extension of voting hours.[1]

Crockett said the reports led her to believe some people were unable to cast ballots, though no formal findings of election fraud were announced.[1] Badlands commentators highlighted the episode as an instance of a Democratic candidate raising election-administration objections of the sort ordinarily branded [[election-denial|election denialism]] when raised by Republicans.[1]

## Objection to the 2025 Iran strikes

On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, Crockett voiced disapproval of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s decision to attack nuclear sites in [[iran|Iran]] without congressional approval.[2] Writing on X, she argued that "Congress must authorize military force before any president—including Trump—launches a strike."[2] She doubled down in a widely circulated video, saying, "And to understand enough about the Constitution to the extent that I'm the one that's supposed to make the f---ing decision, or at least get a vote," a remark that drew applause from those gathered in the room.[2]

Her comments came amid broader Democratic outrage over the strikes, which led Rep. [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] to suggest Trump could be impeached.[2] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote that "Agent Crockett is crushing it," arguing that she was making it impossible for congressmen and senators to sell the narrative that "we don't know anything."[2]
