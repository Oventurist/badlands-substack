# United States Mint

The United States Mint is the federal body responsible for producing the nation's circulating coinage. In December 2025 the Mint was expected to announce, potentially within the week, a decision on issuing a commemorative $1 coin featuring President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to coincide with the nation's 250th birthday.[1] A draft rendering circulated by the Treasury Department showed Trump's profile superimposed over the motto "Liberty."[1]

The plan prompted Senate Democrats to introduce the [[change-corruption-act|Change Corruption Act]], which would prohibit any living or sitting president from appearing on U.S. currency, citing the historical precedent against doing so.[1]
