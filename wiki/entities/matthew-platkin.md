# Matthew Platkin

**Matthew Platkin** is the Attorney General of New Jersey. In the Badlands corpus he appears as the state official whose subpoena to [[first-choice-womens-resource-centers|First Choice Women's Resource Centers]] triggered a 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruling on access to federal courts.

## The First Choice investigation

Platkin's office issued a subpoena to First Choice demanding donor and operational records as part of a state investigation into the crisis pregnancy center network. First Choice sued in federal court, arguing the demand chilled its [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] speech and associational rights. The Supreme Court ruled that the group may pursue those claims in federal court, quoting the principle that "an official demand for private donor information is enough to discourage reasonable individuals from associating with a group." The Court did not rule on the validity of the subpoena itself.

Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterizes the episode as part of a pattern of state harassment of pro-life pregnancy centers, and the ruling as a check on that practice. First Choice executive director Aimee Huber warned that if New Jersey's attorney general could bully her organization, officials in other states could do the same.
