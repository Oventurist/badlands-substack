# Matthew Platkin

**Matthew Platkin** is the Attorney General of [[new-jersey|New Jersey]].[1]

## First Choice subpoena

Platkin issued a subpoena to [[first-choice-womens-resource-centers|First Choice Women's Resource Centers]], a [[crisis-pregnancy-centers|crisis pregnancy center]] network, seeking donor and operational records as part of a state investigation.[1] The center argued the demand could chill speech and association rights protected under the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and challenged it in federal court.[1]

In 2026 the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] held that First Choice may proceed with its federal suit, finding that the attorney general's demand for private donor information injured the group's First Amendment associational rights, though the Court did not rule on the merits of the subpoena.[1] Commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the office's conduct as harassment of reproductive centers that decline to provide abortion.[1]
