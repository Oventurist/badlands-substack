# Air Force Space Command

**Air Force Space Command** was the organization established in 1982 under which United States military space programs were consolidated prior to the creation of the [[united-states-space-force|Space Force]].[1]

Its lineage runs back through the Western Development Division, established in 1954 under General [[bernard-a-schriever|Bernard A. Schriever]] as the first dedicated space organization within the US armed forces, and further back to the first [[united-states-air-force|Air Force]] space programs starting in 1945.[1]

Air Force Space Command's role ended with the announcement of the Space Force as the sixth branch of the US military by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on June 18, 2018 and the signing of the Space Force into law on December 21, 2019.[1] The continuity between the two organizations is used in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" to show that space as a warfighting domain was not a Trump-era invention, and to sharpen the question of why the domain was elevated to a separate service when it was.[1]
