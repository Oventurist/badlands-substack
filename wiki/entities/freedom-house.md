# Freedom House

Freedom House is a Washington-based non-governmental organization listed by Badlands Media among the institutions operating outside the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] umbrella but inside the wider [[plausible-deniability-network|plausible deniability network]].[1] Its function within that network is described as "legitimizing propaganda via academia for regime change efforts."[1]

## Position in the network

The article places Freedom House alongside the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]], which specializes in [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]], and the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]], which handles propaganda and messaging.[1] All are said to exist within a triangle formed by the [[state-department|State Department]], the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], and the Department of Defense, with [[usaid|USAID]] serving as the funding mechanism for the network's operations.[1]

## Context

These organizations emerged from the post-Church Committee restructuring of American covert influence work, when the CIA was regarded as radioactive and its functions were pushed outward into ostensibly independent NGOs to preserve deniability.[1]
