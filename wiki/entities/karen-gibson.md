# Karen Gibson

Karen Gibson is the [[senate-sergeant-at-arms|Senate Sergeant at Arms]], having taken over the post shortly after the assault on the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021.[1]

In testimony before the [[senate-appropriations-committee|Senate Appropriations Committee]] in April 2023, Gibson said satellite communication was being deployed "to ensure a redundant and secure means of communication during a disruptive event."[1] She described the satellite phones — issued to more than 50 senators and offered to all 100 — as a security backstop in the event of an emergency that "takes out communications" in part of America, with federal funding covering the required satellite airtime.[1] The program drew scrutiny from Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], who questioned what had prompted the sudden ramp-up in communications redundancy and whether it related to [[devolution|devolution]] or to the grid-collapse scenario associated with the [[great-reset|Great Reset]].[1]
