# Europol

Europol, formally the European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Cooperation, is the European Union's police coordination body, which pools investigative resources across member states and partner countries rather than making arrests directly itself.[1]

## Kidflix takedown

On April 2, 2025, Europol announced the shutdown of the darknet child sexual abuse platform [[kidflix|Kidflix]], describing it as a massive international pedophile network spanning more than 30 countries.[1] The operation was carried out under the direction of the Bavarian State Criminal Police Office in Germany, with investigations coordinated across 35 countries.[1] Europol said the platform had an estimated 1.8 million users worldwide; 79 people had been arrested at the time of the announcement and nearly 1,400 further suspects had been identified, more than one hundred of them located in Germany.[1]

## Commentary and criticism

The announcement drew scrutiny from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators. [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded to the reported scale of the network by writing that "we are going to need more millstones," and questioned whether the timing of the announcement on the same day as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "Liberation Day" tariff rollout was coincidental.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that arresting 79 people out of roughly two million documented users amounted to roughly 0.004 percent of the identified consumer base, and asked why the agency chose to burn the operation and publicize it — thereby notifying the remaining users — rather than pursue a larger share of the suspects identified through system log evidence.[1] She further speculated about who might appear on a worldwide user list in Washington, D.C.[1]
