# Alan Shaw

Alan Shaw is the chief executive officer of the [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern Railway]], the freight rail operator whose train derailment in East Palestine, Ohio in February 2023 triggered a major chemical disaster and an ensuing public relations crisis.[1]

## East Palestine derailment response

In the weeks after the [[east-palestine-train-derailment|East Palestine derailment]], Shaw published a public letter pledging that the company would remain engaged with the affected community, writing, "We will not walk away, East Palestine."[1] The pledge was undercut almost immediately when Norfolk Southern officials declined to attend a town hall meeting with worried residents who were seeking answers about the derailment of a 150-car freight train, twenty of whose cars were carrying hazardous materials.[1]

Commentators noted that Shaw's company was "not off to a great start" in managing the aftermath, as reports mounted of short-term health impacts among residents following the derailment and the controlled burn of the toxic chemical vinyl chloride, with concern extending as far as Pittsburgh.[1] A class action lawsuit filed against the railway alleged that East Palestine residents "may already be undergoing DNA mutations" as a result of the exposure.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] placed the corporate response within a wider argument that the railroad industry had changed its policies in ways that effectively guaranteed derailments, and that the cleanup was not a genuine remediation effort at all.[1]
