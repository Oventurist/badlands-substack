# Cedric Richmond

Cedric Richmond is an American Democratic politician and political operative who served as a White House aide in the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] before moving to a senior role on Biden's re-election effort, where he held the position of campaign co-chair.[1]

## Role in the 2024 campaign

In June 2024, Richmond was among the senior campaign aides and longtime advisers reported to be present at [[camp-david|Camp David]] for the week-long preparation session ahead of Biden's first debate with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], scheduled for June 27 in Atlanta.[1] The preparation effort was led by Biden's former chief of staff [[ron-klain|Ron Klain]].[1] Reporting on the session noted that Biden, if elected, would be 86 at the completion of a second term, the oldest president ever to serve.[1]
