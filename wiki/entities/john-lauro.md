# John Lauro

John Lauro is an American defense attorney who joined former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s legal team to defend him against the federal charges arising from Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigation of the 2020 presidential election and the events of January 6, 2021.[1]

## Arraignment appearance

On August 3, 2023, Lauro sat at the defense table to Trump's left, alongside co-counsel [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], as the former president was arraigned before Magistrate Judge Moxila Upadhyaya in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia.[1] After Trump was sworn in and the judge read aloud the charges and potential penalties, Lauro went to the podium and formally waived the reading of the full 45-page indictment.[1] Trump then entered a plea of not guilty.[1]

Lauro's role at the arraignment placed him at the center of the defense against a four-count indictment charging conspiracy to defraud the United States, obstruction of the certification of the electoral vote, and conspiracy against citizens' right to vote.[1] Coverage of the hearing by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] emphasized Trump's composed demeanor throughout the proceeding, a detail highlighted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators as evidence that the defense saw strategic opportunity in the case.[1]

## Support for televised trials

Appearing on "Fox News Sunday" in early August 2023, Lauro said he would support having Trump's federal trials televised, telling the program "I would love to see that" and adding, "I'm shocked, actually, that all the networks haven't lined up and filed pleadings already objecting to this."[2] His comments placed him alongside House Democrats and constitutional law experts pressing to relax the long-standing prohibition on cameras in federal court, an issue treated at length in [[cameras-in-federal-court|the debate over cameras in federal court]].[2]
