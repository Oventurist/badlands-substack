# Pete Hoekstra

Pete Hoekstra is a former Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Michigan who served as U.S. ambassador to the Netherlands during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term.[1]

In November 2024, Trump announced that he had chosen Hoekstra to serve as his incoming administration's ambassador to Canada. The announcement came the same day Trump named [[matthew-whitaker|Matt Whitaker]] as U.S. ambassador to [[nato|NATO]].[1]
