# Byron Donalds

Byron Donalds is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Florida (R-FL). In the Badlands Media corpus he is quoted as part of the February 2026 debate over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s push for federal election reform.[1]

## Position on ballot handling

Donalds framed the issue in terms of chain-of-custody rather than an outright ban on mail voting, telling Politico: "In Florida, we treat ballots like they're evidence in a trial. Other states need to follow that. … I think that's what the White House is referencing. You just can't have ballots out there in the ether."[1] His comment is notable within the Brief's account because it attempts to reconcile the President's rhetoric with a workable administrative standard — treating ballots as evidentiary material subject to strict custody rules.[1]

## Significance in the corpus

The Brief used Donalds's remarks, together with those of [[mike-lawler|Mike Lawler]], [[cory-mills|Cory Mills]] and [[derrick-van-orden|Derrick Van Orden]], to illustrate that Republicans in [[congress|Congress]] were interpreting rather than adopting Trump's position that mail voting is inherently "a corrupt system."[1] The Brief noted that election-related bills were clearing the House but were expected to stall in the [[senate|Senate]].[1]
