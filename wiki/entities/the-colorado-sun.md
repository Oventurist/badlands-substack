# The Colorado Sun

**The Colorado Sun** is a Colorado-based online news outlet covering state politics, courts, and public affairs.[1]

## Coverage of the ballot disqualification case

The Sun provided sustained reporting on the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|lawsuit seeking to bar]] [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from Colorado's 2024 Republican presidential primary ballot, including its coverage of closing arguments before a [[denver-district-court|Denver District Court]] judge on November 15, 2023.[1] Its reporting noted that the case stemmed from a suit filed by a liberal political nonprofit based in Washington, D.C., that it was likely to advance to the U.S. Supreme Court, and that similar suits brought in other states had to that point all been unsuccessful.[1] The outlet summarized the legal theory at issue: that Trump's role in the January 6, 2021 riot at the U.S. Capitol disqualified him under the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

Its coverage was excerpted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in the Badlands News Brief.[1]
