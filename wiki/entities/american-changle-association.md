# American Changle Association

The American Changle Association is a New York non-profit organization that provides assistance to Chinese nationals living in New York City.[1]

## 2022 FBI raid

In the fall of 2022, [[fbi|FBI]] agents raided the association's Manhattan headquarters, which was allegedly being used as a secret Chinese police station staffed by "illegal cops" who purported to solve crimes.[1] The clandestine station occupied the third floor of the organization's building and was linked to a unit code-named 110 Overseas, based in China's Fujian province.[1] The US Attorney's Office, which participated in the raid alongside the FBI, declined to comment when asked by DailyMail.com to authenticate the underlying New York Times reporting.[1]

The association's former chairman was Lu Jianshun, who in 2021 donated $4,000 to [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]]' mayoral campaign, according to The New York Times.[1] The raid was reported by the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]].[1]
