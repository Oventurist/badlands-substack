# Star News Digital Media

Star News Digital Media is a conservative digital news company operating a group of state-level outlets, among them *The Arizona Sun Times*. Its CEO and Editor-in-Chief is [[michael-patrick-leahy|Michael Patrick Leahy]].[1]

The organization figures in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s coverage of the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]] litigation through Leahy's December 2022 *War Room* appearance, in which he summarized [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s 70-page election contest and commented on the judge assigned to the case in [[maricopa-county-superior-court|Maricopa County Superior Court]].[1]
