# Andrew McCabe

Andrew McCabe is a former [[fbi|FBI]] deputy director who moved into a media career as a [[cnn|CNN]] commentator, and who in May 2023 publicly linked the [[sai-varshith-kandula|U-Haul incident]] at the [[white-house|White House]] to the January 6 Capitol riot.[1]

## The U-Haul commentary

Following the May 22, 2023 incident in which a U-Haul truck struck White House barricades, McCabe said on CNN: "And I think you have to draw a line from this apparent attack on the White House by someone bearing a Nazi flag to at least some of the people, it's hard to say how many, but some of the people involved in the January 6th attack on the Capitol."[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] singled out the remark, emphasizing that it was "that Andrew McCabe" and that he "did use his new career in media to link the alleged brown white supremacist U-Haul driver to January 6 defendants," concluding that "FBI psy-op habits die hard."[1] Her broader argument was that the subsequent downgrading of Kandula's charges to a single count of depredation of federal property undercut the narrative McCabe had advanced.[1]
