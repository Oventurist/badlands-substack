# Stephen Yekel

Stephen Yekel was a Georgia state court judge who died by suicide inside his courtroom in Effingham County State Court on the morning of Tuesday, December 31, 2024.[1]

## Death

Yekel, 74, was found by a deputy at around 10 a.m., though authorities believed he had shot himself late Monday night or early Tuesday morning.[1] The day he was discovered would have been his last on the bench, having recently lost a bid for re-election.[1] Effingham County Sheriff Jimmy McDuffie confirmed the circumstances of the discovery, and the Georgia Bureau of Investigation opened a probe into the incident.[1]

## Attempted resignation

Yekel had been appointed to the state court in 2022.[1] He had tried to resign from his position but was prevented from doing so by Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]].[1] McDuffie said he understood that Yekel had sent a note to the Governor's office regarding the resignation, but did not disclose its contents.[1]

The story ran as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief of January 2, 2025, drawn from Daily Mail reporting.[1]
