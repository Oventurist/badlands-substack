# Rex Tillerson

Rex Tillerson is a former ExxonMobil chief executive who served as [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s first Secretary of State. Badlands analysis casts him as the originator of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] campaign against [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## ExxonMobil and the Venezuela campaign

According to Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry — expanding on the argument in the Book of Trump episode "Hugo Chavez, Part Two" — it was Tillerson, as ExxonMobil CEO, who started the Deep State's crusade against Venezuela in 2007, ten years before he became Secretary of State.[1] The dispute traces to the nationalization policies of [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]] and the oil major's exposure in the country.[1]

## Secretary of State and dismissal

During Trump's first year in office, Tillerson "flexed" on [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], leaning on Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steve Mnuchin]] to issue sanctions against the Venezuelan leader.[1] Trump fired Tillerson in March 2018 — the same month that Maduro's lawyer [[david-boies|David Boies]] filed lawsuits against 42 entities in a RICO-styled corruption case, in the Southern District of Florida and in Geneva, Switzerland.[1] Badlands treats the timing as significant, linking Tillerson's removal to the sequence that produced the raids on [[helsinge|Helsinge]], the arrest of banker [[mathias-krull|Mathias Krull]], and [[executive-order-13850|Executive Order 13850]].[1]
