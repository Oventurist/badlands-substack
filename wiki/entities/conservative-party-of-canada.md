# Conservative Party of Canada

The **Conservative Party of Canada** is the principal opposition party in Canadian federal politics, led during the [[2025-canadian-federal-election|2025 federal election]] by [[pierre-poilievre|Pierre Poilievre]].[1]

## 2025 federal election

The party entered the 2025 campaign as the heavy favourite, having led the polls for more than a year and at one point standing 27 points ahead of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]].[1] That advantage evaporated over the course of the campaign, and the Conservatives lost to Liberal leader [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] in what was described as a stunning reversal of fortunes for Poilievre, a 45-year-old career politician.[1] The vote was widely seen as a decision about which leader could best handle U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], whose threats to annex Canada and stiff tariffs on the country sparked a wave of Canadian nationalism that worked against the Conservatives.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] compared the collapse of the Conservative campaign to the fate of a front-runner who squanders a commanding lead, quipping that "Canadian Ron! DeeSantis goes the way of the standard Ron! DeeSantis, minus the high heels."[1]
