# Senkaku Islands

The Senkaku Islands are a group of geopolitically sensitive islands in the East China Sea administered by [[japan|Japan]] and claimed by [[china|China]], which calls them the Diaoyu Islands.[1] The chain is a long-standing flashpoint between the two historic rivals, and lies slightly less than 100 miles northeast of [[taiwan|Taiwan]], making it a focal point of any conflict over the island.[1]

## December 2025 coast guard standoff

On Tuesday, December 2, 2025, Chinese coast guard vessels attempted to run off a Japanese fishing boat accused of operating inside waters China claims around the islands.[1] A Japanese Coast Guard ship that had been accompanying the fishing vessel in turn expelled two Chinese Coast Guard ships as they approached and tried to enforce Beijing's expansive maritime claims over the territory.[1]

The incident occurred against the backdrop of the diplomatic crisis triggered by Japanese Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]]'s statement the previous month that Japan would militarily aid Taiwan's defense in the event of a Chinese invasion, a dispute that observers warned was becoming a potential military standoff.[1] Japan had already deployed medium-range missiles to Yonaguni, a remote island near China and the closest Japanese territory to the likely theatre of action.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the confrontation as part of a scripted escalation cycle dating to Nancy Pelosi's 2022 Taiwan visit, noting that Beijing's foreign ministry spokesperson warned that right-wing forces in Japan were leading the country and the region toward disaster, while Taipei distributed civil defense handbooks to households.[1]
