# Lordstown Motors

Lordstown Motors is an American electric-truck manufacturer that went public during the early-2020s electric-vehicle boom and subsequently filed for bankruptcy.[1]

A December 2023 Wall Street Journal analysis named Lordstown Motors, alongside Proterra and Electric Last Mile Solutions, as one of three formerly public EV companies to have filed for bankruptcy protection.[1] The failures came within a wider [[2023-electric-vehicle-startup-shakeout|EV startup shakeout]] in which at least 18 listed EV and battery firms — including [[nikola|Nikola]] and [[fisker|Fisker]] — were judged at risk of running out of cash by the end of 2024, battery maker Romeo Power and charging firm Volta were sold for a fraction of their listing valuations, and the median stock in the group had lost more than 80% of its value since its market debut.[1]
