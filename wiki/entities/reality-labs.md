# Reality Labs

Reality Labs is the [[meta-platforms|Meta]] division responsible for the company's virtual reality, mixed reality and metaverse products, including [[horizon-worlds|Horizon Worlds]].[1]

## Funding and losses

Meta said in March 2026 that it will continue to fund Reality Labs for the foreseeable future even as it shifts its metaverse experiences to mobile and to mixed reality rather than fully immersive virtual worlds.[1] Reality Labs has accumulated tens of billions of dollars in losses, but Meta leadership maintains the initiative is a long-term investment.[1] [[andrew-bosworth|Andrew Bosworth]] stated that neither Horizon nor VR is dead and that the company is "continuing to invest tremendously."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[matthew-trump|Matthew Trump]] characterized the episode as [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] frantically changing course to catch up with rivals in the artificial intelligence space, while cautioning that Zuckerberg remains formidable because of the "Himalaya Range of personal data at his disposal."[1]

## Custom silicon and the 2023 layoffs

Reality Labs housed the [[facebook-agile-silicon-team|Facebook Agile Silicon Team]] (FAST), a roughly 600-person unit developing custom chips intended to let Meta's devices perform unique tasks and operate more efficiently than competing AR/VR hardware.[2] In October 2023 Meta moved to lay off employees in that unit, having struggled to produce chips competitive with external suppliers and having turned to [[qualcomm|Qualcomm]] for the silicon in its shipping products; a restructuring had been expected since the spring, when a new executive was hired to lead FAST.[2]
