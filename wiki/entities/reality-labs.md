# Reality Labs

Reality Labs is the division of [[meta-platforms|Meta]] responsible for the company's virtual reality, mixed reality and metaverse hardware and software, including the [[horizon-worlds|Horizon Worlds]] platform. It is the organizational vehicle for [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s long-term wager that immersive computing will succeed the smartphone as the dominant consumer platform.

## Financial position

As reported in the Badlands Brief edition [[badlands-brief-52b|"Golgotha & The Metaverse"]] (March 20, 2026), Reality Labs has accumulated tens of billions of dollars in losses. Meta leadership has consistently defended the division's spending as a long-term investment rather than a near-term profit center.

## 2026 strategic pivot

In March 2026, Meta announced it would continue funding Reality Labs for the foreseeable future even as it reoriented its metaverse products — shifting emphasis from fully immersive virtual worlds toward mixed reality experiences and mobile delivery. The company simultaneously reversed a plan to discontinue Horizon Worlds in VR. Meta technology chief Andrew Bosworth publicly rejected the characterization that either Horizon Worlds or VR generally was "dead," stating that the company was "continuing to invest tremendously."

Badlands contributor [[matthew-trump|Matthew Trump]] framed the pivot as a "frantic effort to catch up to his rivals in the AI space," while cautioning that Zuckerberg's competitive position in artificial intelligence is underwritten by the vast reservoir of personal data Meta's users have supplied over two decades.
