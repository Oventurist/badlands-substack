# Ken Howery

Ken Howery is a cofounder of PayPal who was nominated by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in December 2024 to serve as United States Ambassador to [[denmark|Denmark]].[1]

## Nomination and Greenland

Trump announced the Howery nomination in the same [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement in which he declared that "for purposes of National Security and Freedom throughout the World, the United States of America feels that the ownership and control of [[greenland|Greenland]] is an absolute necessity."[1] Greenland, the world's largest island, is an autonomous territory of Denmark, a key U.S. ally and [[nato|NATO]] member, making the ambassadorship a focal point of Trump's revived acquisition proposal.[1]
