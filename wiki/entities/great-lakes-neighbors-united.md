# Great Lakes Neighbors United

Great Lakes Neighbors United is a Wisconsin nonprofit organization formed in October 2025 by a grassroots group of about a dozen residents of [[port-washington|Port Washington]], Wisconsin, to contest large-scale data center development in their community.[1]

The group's members connected on Facebook and began protesting at city council meetings before incorporating as a nonprofit.[1] They gathered the roughly 1,000 signatures needed to place a measure on the municipal ballot in just ten days, producing what could be the first municipal referendum in the United States aimed at restricting data center development.[1] The measure would require voter approval before city leaders could grant tax incentives to major projects, and was aimed at a proposed $15 billion, 1.3-gigawatt AI campus tied to the [[project-stargate|Stargate]] megaproject backed by [[openai|OpenAI]], [[oracle|Oracle]] and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Organizers, including former nurse practitioner Carri Prom and co-founder Christine Le Jeune, said they were neither anti-development nor anti-tech but wanted "responsible" growth amid concerns over noise pollution, freshwater consumption, energy costs, and a lack of transparency.[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the campaign as a largely social-media-organized effort amplified by NGOs and influencers rather than an organic uprising.[1]
