# Matt Klein

Matt Klein is a Democratic candidate in a 2026 Minnesota primary race who was one of three candidates suspended by [[kalshi|Kalshi]] in the platform's first insider trading enforcement action.[1]

## Kalshi ban

Klein had wagered on his own election outcome on the Kalshi prediction market platform, triggering newly implemented safeguards that block political candidates from trading on their own races.[1] Kalshi's head of enforcement stated that any trade by a candidate who can influence a market outcome violates the rules regardless of size and described the cases as political insider trading; the matters were not referred to the [[commodity-futures-trading-commission|Commodity Futures Trading Commission]] or the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1]
