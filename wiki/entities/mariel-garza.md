# Mariel Garza

Mariel Garza is an American journalist who served as editorials editor of the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] until her resignation in October 2024.[1]

## Resignation over the 2024 non-endorsement

Garza resigned after owner [[patrick-soon-shiong|Patrick Soon-Shiong]] decided that the paper would issue no endorsement in the 2024 presidential race, a decision reported by the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] on October 23, 2024.[1] In an interview with the [[columbia-journalism-review|Columbia Journalism Review]] she said: "I want to make it clear that I am not OK with us being silent. In dangerous times, honest people need to stand up. This is how I'm standing up."[1]

Garza said the editorial board had planned to endorse [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] before the owner intervened, and noted that the paper did not explain to readers why it was withholding an endorsement.[1] Her departure was among the first resignations in the wider wave of newsroom protest surrounding the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
