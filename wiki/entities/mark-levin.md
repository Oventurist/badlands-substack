# Mark Levin

Mark Levin is an American conservative talk-radio host and commentator identified in Badlands coverage as a leading voice in the pro-Israel faction of the American right during the March 2026 dispute over speech and the war with [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Role in the Carlson affair

Levin posted a Mediaite article highlighting Republican Congressman [[max-miller|Max Miller]]'s call for [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] to be investigated and imprisoned as an alleged Iranian agent.[1] Badlands writers noted that Carlson was one of Levin's most prominent public critics, making the amplification pointed.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described a widely circulated "Mark Levin love letter" post — with a cameo from Sean Hannity — as so effusive that it raised the question of who its intended audience was, and used it to illustrate the "final boss" thesis that the public must stop waiting for a political savior.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that [[donald-trump|President Trump]] was deliberately baiting Levin, [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] and their allies into overreaching on demands that Americans be jailed for hate speech, thereby exposing the campaign against the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] Of the escalating rhetoric he wrote simply: "hubris and ego will blind thee."[1]

## Break with Trump over Netanyahu (June 2026)

By June 2026, Badlands writers described Levin as "now beginning to turn on President Trump" following Trump's intervention to stop an Israeli strike on Iran.[2] Levin accused Trump of being "desperate for a deal" and mocked him as unable to negotiate, then defended [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] while disparaging Trump: "I don't know why he keeps bashing the Prime Minister of Israel! …Why the constant beating up of Netanyahu?!"[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the outburst as evidence of "trouble in paradise," arguing that Levin understood the United States had already decided to abandon Israel's military campaign in the Middle East and was throwing shade at Trump as a result.[2]

## "Get Khamenei" posts and the Qatar rebuke (February 2026)

In February 2026 Badlands wrote that Levin had "been acting like a mental patient for the past month," posting "Get Khamenei!" multiple times a day in what [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described as an apparent rally cry to assassinate the leader of [[iran|Iran]].[3] Levin was also named, alongside [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], among those [[donald-trump|President Trump]] indirectly rebuked at the first [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] meeting in Washington for their persistent attacks on Emir Tamim of [[qatar|Qatar]].[3] The Brief grouped him with [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] as figures characterized as "bloodthirsty sociopaths fawning for war" rather than earnest seekers of peace.[3]

## Reaction to the June 2026 Netanyahu call leak
Following [[axios|Axios]]'s report of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s expletive-filled June 1, 2026 call with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], Levin called for an FBI investigation to identify the leaker.[4] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read that reaction — alongside [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]]'s complaints about disloyalty in Trump's camp — as circumstantial evidence the call was real, arguing that if it had not happened they would simply have brushed the story off as fake news rather than demanding "heads on pikes."[4]

## Reaction to the February 2026 peace track

During the [[2026-witkoff-abu-dhabi-and-istanbul-talks|February 2026 Witkoff diplomatic round]] in Abu Dhabi and [[istanbul|Istanbul]], Levin reacted publicly with frustration, asking "Who the hell is in charge of this?"[5] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] answered in the Badlands Brief that the person in charge was President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and described Levin as "crashing out completely" at the prospect of peace.[5] The commentary argued that Levin supported Trump so long as the President appeared to be delivering war with [[iran|Iran]], but turned hostile once peace looked likely, and speculated that Iranian, Turkish, Qatari, and Saudi officials meeting in [[turkey|Turkey]] were game-planning how to deal with Benjamin Netanyahu and Levin.[5]


## June 2026 Iran commentary

During the confused June 2026 sequence of U.S. strikes, ceasefire claims and Israeli escalation, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that Mark Levin read the situation as a great victory for Zionist warmongers, while anti-Trump and anti-Zionist commentators read the same events as a sign of [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s weakness — an illustration, in his telling, of how thoroughly the battlespace had been muddled.[6]

## Jerusalem News Syndicate summit and June 2026 broadcasts

Levin spoke at the [[jerusalem-news-syndicate|Jerusalem News Syndicate]]'s 2026 International Policy Summit, where Badlands Media sarcastically noted he "wasn't unhinged at all" while quoting him warning the audience, "They will murder every single one of you... every woman in this room, they will rape you."[7] The outlet said his Friday evening show was the more notable performance, in which he attacked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and told the administration to stop belittling [[israel|Israel]].[7]

Badlands grouped Levin's remarks with those of [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]], [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and [[ben-sabti|Ben Sabti]] as evidence that the GOP, what it termed Mossad Media, and Israel's proxies were spinning out over the peace deal Trump signed with [[iran|Iran]].[7]

## February 2026 Iran and Turkey posts

During the early 2026 standoff over Iran, Levin — described by Badlands as serving in the Trump administration in an antisemitism-monitoring capacity — called Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] "evil" in a quote post addressing the nuclear deal [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed with Erdogan, in which Trump promised Turkey F-35 fighter jets.[8] The post appeared to respond to a viral video of [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]] describing the Trump–Erdogan relationship as a "bromance."[8]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized Levin's output as subversion of the president, noting a further post implying Trump was being fooled and that failure to go to war with Iran would be catastrophic.[8] Citing a *Jerusalem Post* op-ed titled "The silence of the graveyard: Why the West abandons Iran to the ayatollahs," Levin called Trump's reluctance to start a war in which American soldiers would die "a disgrace."[8] Badlands framed such statements as vindication of Trump's decision to appoint NeoCons and Zionists to his administration so the public could see how they would wield power, predicting they would soon turn on him.[8]

## Opposition to the 2026 Iran framework

During the May 2026 [[2026-iran-deal-negotiations|U.S.–Iran negotiations]], Levin was singled out in the Badlands Brief as the leading example of what the writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called self-revealing subversion within the MAGA coalition.[9] In sequential posts, Levin first reminded followers that Saudi Arabia was responsible for the September 11 attacks — an assertion the Brief characterized as a lie told by criminals in the [[cia|CIA]] — and then quoted a Jerusalem Post article about the rumored deal, calling it a "big problem."[9] He subsequently posted a podcast clip in which the host and guest questioned why more people were not treating President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s near-daily conversations with Gulf Arab leaders as "foreign influence," adding the comment "Great question," which the Brief labeled "absolute subversion."[9]

The same article grouped Levin with [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] as figures who had been calling for the removal of Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] for months and were "spiking the football" upon news of her resignation.[9]

## Gaza casualty denial (October 2025)

In October 2025 Levin argued on air that "it's not possible Israel killed so many women and children in Gaza," adding that "during the Holocaust, we had film after film—you don't have that in Gaza."[10] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] highlighted the remark alongside an essay on collective denial in Israel, asking whether Levin was in effect claiming that it is possible to fake a genocide, and noting that such a claim "certainly opens new territory for exploration."[10]

## Call to move past the Epstein controversy (July 2025)

In a Sunday evening interview in July 2025, Levin urged Speaker of the House [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] to move on from the controversy surrounding the Trump administration's handling of the Jeffrey Epstein case, deeming the matter a "waste of time."[11] His comments followed Johnson's emphasis on the importance of Republicans holding Congress in the 2026 midterms, which Johnson warned would otherwise bring immediate impeachment proceedings against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[11] Badlands commentator Chris Paul dismissed the exchange, writing that "elections are fake" and that anyone using the midterms as leverage for pushing an agenda is "too stupid or dishonest to take seriously."[11]

## "Thrice Jewish president" remark (December 2025)

At the [[white-house|White House]] Hanukkah party in December 2025, Levin appeared on stage with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and reminded him that he had called Trump "the first Jewish president" back in 2019, before doubling down and describing him as "the first two-time—non-consecutive Jewish president."[12] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the exchange was not really about Jews or Jewishness at all but about raw political power and the ability of a government to shield itself from fair criticism.[12]

The same commentary contended that ever since Trump's May visit to the Gulf Arab states, Levin has been engaged in subversive social media activity against the president, openly ridiculing moves to normalise relations with the Islamic world as well as with [[russia|Russia]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[12] It cited a post Levin reposted on the Monday before appearing with Trump — insinuating that Trump is a traitor because of his relationship with [[qatar|Qatar]] — as evidence that Levin does not view the president with the reverence he publicly claims, and that Trump knows it.[12]
