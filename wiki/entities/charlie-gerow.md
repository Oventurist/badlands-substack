# Charlie Gerow

Charlie Gerow is a conservative political strategist based in Pennsylvania.[1]

Commenting to [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] in January 2024 on the wave of Pennsylvania Democrats changing their party registration, Gerow said that "as the Democrat Party tilts further to the progressive left, more historically traditional, working-class families are moving to the Republican Party, both in terms of how they vote and how they're registered."[1] He characterized the movement as a significant shift in the base of the party.[1] His remarks framed coverage of the [[2023-pennsylvania-voter-registration-shift|2023 Pennsylvania voter registration shift]].[1]
