# Preet Bharara

Preet Bharara is an American lawyer who served as United States Attorney for the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]] until he was fired by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] early in Trump's first term.[1]

In the Badlands Media account by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], Bharara's removal was a necessary precondition for the broader operation: firing him allowed Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] to install [[geoffrey-berman|Geoffrey Berman]] as head of the SDNY, an office overseeing what Lange describes as some of the most important investigations of the conflict — the Weiner/[[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] laptop, Epstein and Maxwell, and later [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] and [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]].[1]

Q drop 1370 (May 15, 2018) opened with the questions "Why was Preet Bharara fired? Why was the NY AG just removed? Why did RUDY recently join POTUS' legal team after being 'quiet' for so long? What must be cleaned first?"[1] Lange reads the sequence as evidence that the New York legal apparatus had to be "cleaned" before Giuliani could join Trump's legal team and before evidence could be legally introduced into ongoing criminal investigations.[1] This interpretation reflects Badlands Media commentary rather than documented fact.
