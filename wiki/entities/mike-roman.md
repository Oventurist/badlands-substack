# Mike Roman

Mike Roman is a Republican political operative and a co-defendant of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the Georgia election-interference prosecution brought by Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]].[1]

## Motion to disqualify Fani Willis

Roman is credited with first surfacing, in a court filing, the allegation that Willis had hired her romantic partner [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] as a top prosecutor on the Trump case. In a 127-page filing in Trump's 2020 election trial in Georgia, Roman argued that Wade's involvement rendered the indictment "fatally defective," and asked the court to dismiss the charges against him and to bar the Fulton County District Attorney's office from any further involvement in the case.[1]

The filing asserted that Wade had been hired without proper approval, that he was a private attorney in the midst of a divorce, and that he had "little to no experience trying felony cases, much less complex RICO actions." It further alleged that Wade collected nearly $700,000 from Fulton County taxpayers, money with which he allegedly took Willis on lavish vacations, and that he billed taxpayers $2,000 for time spent conferring with the Biden White House about prosecuting the sitting president's political opponent.[1]

The allegations prompted Judge [[scot-mcafee|Scot McAfee]] to order Willis to answer the misconduct claims in writing by February 2, 2024, with an evidentiary hearing scheduled for February 15.[1]

## Willis conspiracy allegation

In a January 2024 court filing, Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] accused Roman of "conspiring" with Joycelyn Wade, the estranged wife of special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], in an attempt to have the charges against him dismissed and to undermine her office ahead of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] trial.[2] Roman, who worked for Trump's 2020 campaign, was the defendant whose earlier filing alleged that Willis and Nathan Wade were romantically involved, and who sought Willis's disqualification from the case along with dismissal of the charges against him.[2] A Georgia judge gave Willis until February 2, 2024 to respond formally to those allegations.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Willis's conspiracy claim depended entirely on the timing of disclosures in Nathan Wade's parallel divorce case — a schedule set by the court — and doubted that Willis possessed evidence Roman knew of the affair before those filings became public.[2]
