# David Williams (Deutsche Bank)

David Williams is a managing director at [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]] who worked directly on at least one of the several loans the bank extended to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the course of decades.[1]

## Testimony in the New York civil fraud trial

On Tuesday, November 28, 2023, Williams testified in Manhattan in the civil fraud trial brought by New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]]. He said it was "atypical, but not entirely unusual" for a bank to internally cut a client's stated asset values by 50% and approve the loan anyway, as Deutsche Bank had done with Trump. "It just depends on the circumstances," Williams said.[1]

Bank credit memos showed Deutsche Bank had reduced Trump's stated net worth in 2011 and 2012 from roughly $4.2 billion to $2.3 billion while still approving loans for properties in Miami, Chicago and Washington, because it expected the loans to be profitable given Trump's development history and other criteria.[1]

Coverage by ZeroHedge argued the testimony "completely undermines" James' premise that Trump had defrauded the German lender, which the state had cast as his biggest victim.[1]
