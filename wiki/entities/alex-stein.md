# Alex Stein

Alex Stein is an American comedian and political provocateur active in online right-of-center media.[1]

## TimCast appearance

Over the weekend of August 30–31, 2025, Stein appeared on [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]]'s TimCast program.[1] According to the Badlands News Brief, the discussion became heated once it turned to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and his alleged connections to Israel.[1] The brief cited the exchange as evidence that the taboo around criticizing Israeli influence had broken down across conservative broadcast platforms, alongside the departure of [[owen-schroyer|Owen Schroyer]] from [[infowars|Infowars]] and the controversy over [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]].[1]
