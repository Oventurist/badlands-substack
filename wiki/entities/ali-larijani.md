# Ali Larijani

Ali Larijani is a senior Iranian political figure, formerly Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly and a long-serving official in the country's security and nuclear-negotiation apparatus.

In the March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Larijani is quoted from Times of Israel reporting as saying that the war will not end before [[iran|Iran]]'s enemies are made "sorry for their grave miscalculation." His statement appears in the Brief's roundup of Israeli and Iranian messaging, immediately after the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] said it had struck dozens of targets in Tehran as part of a campaign to "deepen the blow against the regime," and is presented as Tehran's refusal of any near-term off-ramp.


## Reported death (March 2026)

The March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1cd|Badlands Brief]] reports that Iranian state media confirmed Larijani's death as Secretary of the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]], killed along with his son, a deputy and bodyguards in U.S.-Israeli airstrikes at his daughter's home. [[israel|Israel]] separately announced the elimination of Basij commander [[gholamreza-soleimani|Gholamreza Soleimani]] in the same wave of strikes.

Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes Larijani as the Iranian official responsible for implementing a form of "[[devolution|Devolution]]" — [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] — in August 2025 after the 12-Day War, and argues that this protocol makes a successful decapitation strike unlikely because authority had already been devolved to lower-level commanders. He notes that Iran was slow to affirm the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s claim and by some reports initially denied it, that someone later took control of Larijani's X account to post about the death, and that one explanation for a denial would be to protect Larijani by moving him into hiding.
