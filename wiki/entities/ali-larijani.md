# Ali Larijani

Ali Larijani is an Iranian official of the National Supreme Security Council, presented in Badlands commentary as the architect of the arrangement that displaced the Supreme Leader from [[iran|Iran]]'s operational chain of command.[1]

## Continuity of Government plan

Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] states his understanding that Larijani implemented a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] plan in August 2025 which removed the Ayatollah from the chain of command, rendering the office "an irrelevant figure head."[1]

On this reading, the intense public attention to whether [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] — successor to [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] — is alive, gay, or "made of cardboard" misses the operative question of who actually leads Iran during the 2026 conflict.[1] The framing places Larijani, rather than the clerical leadership, near the center of Iranian decision-making.[1]
