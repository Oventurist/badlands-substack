# The Misfits

The Misfits are a seminal American horror-punk band, described by Badlands Media as one of the defining acts of the [[punk-rock]] underground.[1] [[glenn-danzig|Glenn Danzig]] served as the band's original frontman before departing in 1983 to pursue a solo career.[1]

The group is cited in Badlands Media's argument that the founding figures of punk frequently held views — including support for immigration vetting and skepticism of mainstream narratives — that contradict the left-progressive identity now attached to the genre.[1]
