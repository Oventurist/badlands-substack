# League for Social Reconstruction

The League of Social Reconstruction was the organizational form taken by the self-professed "Fabian Society of Canada," set up at the height of the Depression by five [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholars]] in order, according to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], to create a Canadian fascist regime in 1932.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

The League established a political party called the Cooperative Commonwealth Federation (CCF), which later changed its name to the New Democratic Party (NDP) in 1961.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret argues that while many decent people have belonged to the NDP and the Liberals over the years, the lineage retains a rotten core tied to the highest echelons of the British oligarchy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) The [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] proper is presented as having worked alongside the Rhodes network for more than a century.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
