# An Garda Siochana

An Garda Siochana — the Gardai — is the national police service of Ireland.[1] Following the November 2023 [[2023-dublin-riots|riots in Dublin]], the Irish Justice Minister promised a crackdown on crime, and proposed legislation would empower the Gardai to access and intercept private conversations on social media platforms.[1]

## Surveillance powers and hate speech legislation

Reporting relayed by Modernity News, citing the Irish Times, stated that under the new legislation the Gardai would be able to access private social media conversations, effectively placing Irish citizens who share memes in private WhatsApp groups under government surveillance if the accompanying [[hate-speech|hate speech]] measures pass.[1] The legislation would also allow authorities to demand "cell site location data" from cellphone companies in order to locate dissidents.[1]

The riots followed the stabbing of three children outside a Dublin primary school by an Algerian migrant who, according to the account, should have been deported two decades earlier and had previously been released after an arrest for carrying a knife.[1] Critics argued that authorities reacted with more outrage toward the protesters than toward the attacker.[1]

Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the Irish measures as part of a global pattern rather than a national anomaly, arguing that "the regime has all this info on you already, whether you're in Ireland or Iowa," and noting the timing one day after the release of the [[ctil-files|CTIL Files]] by [[michael-shellenberger|Michael Shellenberger]] and [[matt-taibbi|Matt Taibbi]].[1] The commentary observed that Irish residents nonetheless retain greater data privacy protections than Americans because of GDPR and other EU regulations.[1]
