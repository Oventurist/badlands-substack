# Frank Luntz

Frank Luntz is an American pollster and political consultant best known for his work on Republican message testing and for teaching political communication at the university level.[1]

## Connection to Ron Dermer

A July 2024 Badlands News Brief profile of Israeli strategic affairs minister [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] noted that Dermer studied under Luntz at the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania, and that Luntz described Dermer as "the most talented student I've ever had."[1] The Badlands commentary used the Luntz connection as one of several data points establishing Dermer's deep integration into American Republican political circles — alongside the claim, sourced to Politico, that had Dermer remained in the United States he might plausibly have risen to chair the Republican National Committee or to manage presidential campaigns.[1]
