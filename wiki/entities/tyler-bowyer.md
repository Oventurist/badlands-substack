# Tyler Bowyer

Tyler Bowyer is the Chief Operating Officer of [[turning-point-action|Turning Point Action]], the political arm of [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]].[1]

## 2023 DeSantis invitation dispute

In July 2023, after [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]] reported that Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] had declined an invitation to speak at Turning Point Action's West Palm Beach conference, Bowyer retweeted Posobiec's report and publicly questioned the strategy of the DeSantis presidential campaign.[1] His reaction, alongside [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]'s terse "Too bad!", was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a sign of friction between DeSantis and a movement organization that had previously platformed him.[1]
