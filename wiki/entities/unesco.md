# UNESCO

**UNESCO**, the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, appears in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account of the green agenda through its founder Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]], who was also a president of the Eugenics Society.[1]

Ehret quotes Huxley's 1946 statement that "Political unification in some sort of world government will be required," and that although "any radical eugenic policy will be for many years politically and psychologically impossible, it will be important for UNESCO to see that the eugenic problem is examined with the greatest care, and that the public mind is informed of the issues at stake so that much that now is unthinkable may at least become thinkable."[1] In Ehret's framing this establishes UNESCO's founding purpose as the gradual normalization of eugenic thinking under the banner of international education.[1]
