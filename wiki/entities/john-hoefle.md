# John Hoefle

John Hoefle is a historian and financial analyst cited by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] for a 2009 study published in *[[executive-intelligence-review|Executive Intelligence Review]]* on the defense of national sovereignty.[1]

In the passage Ehret quotes, Hoefle argues that the [[house-of-morgan|House of Morgan]] "was, in truth, a British operation from its inception," tracing its lineage from George Peabody & Co., founded in London in 1851, through J.S. Morgan & Co. to J.P. Morgan & Co. in New York.[1] Hoefle describes the bank as moving from helping the British gain control of American railroads to becoming "a leading force in the oligarchy's war against the American System," dominant in finance, steel, automobiles, railroads and electricity generation.[1]
