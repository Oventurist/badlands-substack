# Honest Elections Project Action

**Honest Elections Project Action (HEPA)** is an American advocacy organization focused on election-integrity policy, led by executive director Jason Snead.[1]

## D.C. noncitizen voting vote

Following the House vote to overturn the [[dc-noncitizen-voting-law|D.C. noncitizen voting law]] in February 2023, Snead issued a statement applauding the [[us-house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] "for acting to ensure that foreign spies and illegal immigrants are not voting in the nation's capital."[1] He argued that because the District is not a state, the House had acted properly under its constitutional authority to stop what he called an "extreme and antidemocratic measure."[1]
