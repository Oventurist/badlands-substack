# Donetsk People's Republic

The Donetsk People's Republic (DPR) is a formerly Ukrainian region in the Donbass that has joined the Russian Federation, and is headed by [[denis-pushilin|Denis Pushilin]].[1]

## Role in the 2025 minerals negotiations

In February 2025 the DPR became central to the dispute over Ukraine's offer of natural-resource access to the United States. Pushilin said that most of the reserves being offered by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]]'s team lie in the Donbass, and specifically that most of the lithium and titanium sits within DPR territory that Russian forces had "already liberated."[1] He pledged that those reserves would benefit the region's own people and Russia as a whole.[1]

The claim directly complicated the arrangement sought by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, which had pressed for U.S. rights to Ukrainian natural resources, including rare earth minerals, as a guarantee for further American assistance.[1] Trump asserted that Kiev had agreed to relinquish $500 billion worth of rare-earth minerals to offset aid provided under the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1] Broader estimates of Ukraine's raw-material wealth cited coal, iron ore, lithium, titanium, graphite and uranium.[1]

Badlands commentators suggested the territorial ownership of the deposits could become a bargaining chip in future negotiations between Trump and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]
