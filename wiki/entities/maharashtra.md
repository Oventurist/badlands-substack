# Maharashtra

**Maharashtra** is an Indian state whose government handled the expulsion of Pakistani short-term visa holders following the [[pahalgam-terror-attack|Pahalgam terror attack]] of April 2025.[1]

## Expulsion of Pakistani visa holders

Chief Minister [[devendra-fadnavis|Devendra Fadnavis]] said on 28 April 2025 that there were no Pakistanis unaccounted for on the list of short-term visa holders who had been ordered to leave in the wake of the attack.[1] His statement came a day after Deputy Chief Minister [[eknath-shinde|Eknath Shinde]] said 107 were unaccounted for.[1] Fadnavis's home department sought to clear up the confusion, reporting that all but 14 of the 55 Pakistanis in the state on short-term visas had exited in accordance with the central government directive, and that the state had tracked down the remaining 14, some of whom were present on medical visas.[1] An official said those on short-term visas had been asked to leave by Sunday, while those on medical visas would have to exit by Tuesday.[1]

Departmental data further recorded 5,023 Pakistanis living in various parts of the state excluding Mumbai, with 2,740 on long-term visas and 2,088 on extensions to long-term visas.[1] Another 34 had overstayed long-term visas and were living in the state illegally, three of them in prison.[1]
