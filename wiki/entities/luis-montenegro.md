# Luís Montenegro

Luís Montenegro leads Portugal's Social Democratic Party, the largest component of the center-right Democratic Alliance (AD) coalition that narrowly won the March 2024 Portuguese parliamentary election, ending the Socialist Party's eight-year hold on power.[1] With 99% of votes tallied, the AD held 79 seats to the Socialists' 77, short of a majority in the 230-seat parliament.[1]

Montenegro repeatedly promised he would not form a ruling coalition with the surging populist party [[chega|Chega]], whose positions he characterized as "often xenophobic, racist, populist and excessively demagogic."[1] He forcefully reiterated the pledge after the results were posted, saying, "Of course I will keep my word," and, "I would never do such evil to myself, my party and my country as to not fulfill the commitments I made so clearly."[1]

Chega leader [[andre-ventura|André Ventura]] argued that voters had endorsed an AD–Chega government.[1] Analysts noted the Socialists could allow Montenegro to govern by abstaining from the investiture vote, but that a minority government would likely prove fragile: only two minority governments have lasted a full term in Portugal's 50 years of democracy.[1]
