# Chao Shao

Chao Shao is the name given to a researcher associated with the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] who, in an interview conducted by the Chinese-born activist and author [[jennifer-zeng|Jennifer Zeng]] between March and April 2020, claimed that China had engineered the virus responsible for COVID-19 as a "bioweapon."[1]

## Claims

According to an ANI report summarised by CNBC-TV18, Chao Shao told Zeng that he and his colleagues had been given four strains of the coronavirus in order to determine which strain could spread most effectively.[1] He recounted an incident in which another researcher at the institute, identified as Shan Chao, admitted that a superior had provided the four strains so the team could test their infectivity across various species, including humans.[1] Chao Shao explicitly referred to the coronavirus as a "bioweapon."[1]

The account is single-sourced and was circulated years after the interview was recorded, resurfacing in June 2023 amid renewed mainstream attention to the laboratory-origin hypothesis.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] declined to treat the claim as conclusive, saying he did not know whether COVID-19 was an engineered bioweapon or a psychological operation built on an ordinary respiratory illness, and suspected elements of both.[1] He argued that the more consequential fact was the trauma inflicted by "the Deep State's governmental overreach during the ensuing lockdowns," and that the steady mainstreaming of origin questions suggested a shift in permissible public discourse.[1]
