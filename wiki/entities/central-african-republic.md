# Central African Republic

The Central African Republic is a landlocked state in central Africa led by President [[faustin-touadera|Faustin Touadéra]] and a principal theatre of Russian [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] operations on the continent.[1]

## Wagner deployment and 2023 referendum

Hundreds of Wagner mercenaries arrived in the Central African Republic in mid-July 2023, according to local reports.[1] Their arrival preceded a constitutional referendum held that month which could abolish presidential term limits and thereby permit Touadéra to seek another term.[1]

At a Russia-Africa summit in St. Petersburg hosted by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in late July 2023, Wagner chief [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] was photographed shaking hands with a suited African delegate identified by the watchdog All Eyes on Wagner as Touadéra's chief of protocol.[1] The appearance came just a month after Prigozhin's failed mutiny in Russia, and coincided with a Wagner-branded Telegram channel claiming credit for the [[2023-niger-coup|coup in Niger]] that took place on the eve of the summit — a claim that could not be independently verified.[1]
