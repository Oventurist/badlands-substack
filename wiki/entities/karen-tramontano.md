# Karen Tramontano

Karen Tramontano is a co-founder of [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]] alongside [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]].[1] Like Painter, she appears in emails recovered from [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop]] in connection with the firm's work.[1]

Tramontano is referenced directly in the November 2015 negotiations over Blue Star's contract with [[burisma|Burisma]]: reassuring [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]] that the parties were aligned on the engagement's real objectives, [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] wrote, "I trust Sally and Karen implicitly so I believe we are all aligned."[1] The contract, whose "ultimate purpose" Pozharskyi had described as closing down "any cases/pursuits" against [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]] in Ukraine, was signed in mid-November 2015.[1] Blue Star was later investigated for illegal lobbying, an inquiry closed in May 2022 with no finding of wrongdoing.[1]
