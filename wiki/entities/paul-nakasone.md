# Paul Nakasone

**General Paul M. Nakasone** is a United States Army officer who served as Director of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] and commander of US Cyber Command.[1]

## October 2020 meeting with Milley

In Badlands Media's [[purple-revolution|Purple Revolution]] analysis, Nakasone appears principally through a meeting held on October 1, 2020 — one month before the presidential election — at which he and General [[mark-milley|Mark Milley]] discussed securing the democratic process.[1] The article raises the meeting as an open question rather than a settled fact, asking whether Nakasone and Milley were working with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] or against him.[1]

The ambiguity is framed by the author's broader argument that Trump did not obtain full control of military intelligence until roughly a week after the November 3, 2020 election, when a rapid series of Defense Department replacements installed [[christopher-miller|Chris Miller]], [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], [[anthony-tata|Anthony Tata]] and [[ezra-cohen-watnick|Ezra Cohen-Watnick]].[1] Nakasone's October position therefore falls in the period the article treats as still compromised at the top of the chain of command.[1]
