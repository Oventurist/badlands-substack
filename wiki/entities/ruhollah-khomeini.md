# Ruhollah Khomeini

Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini was the anti-American cleric who was eventually put into power in [[iran|Iran]] after [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|the Shah]] was ousted during the [[iranian-revolution|Iranian Revolution]] of 1979.[1]

In [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s July 2023 essay for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], Khomeini's ascent is presented as the moment the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] lost the overt control over Iran it had exercised through the Shah for the 26 years following the [[1953-iranian-coup|1953 coup]].[1] It was during this period that 52 American diplomats were taken hostage by militant Iranian students in November 1979, in the episode known as the [[iran-hostage-crisis|Iran Hostage Crisis]].[1] Carlson's larger argument is that the loss of the Shah's government did not end CIA involvement in Iran but changed its form: having lost formal control, the agency, in his account, set out to make Iran the world's new enemy and to infiltrate the post-revolutionary state, so that both genuine anti-American factions and CIA-controlled factions operate within it.[1]
