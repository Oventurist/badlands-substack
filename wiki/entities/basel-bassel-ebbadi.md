# Basel Bassel Ebbadi

Basel Bassel Ebbadi is a Lebanese national, aged 22 at the time of his arrest, who was apprehended after illegally entering the United States from Mexico near El Paso, Texas, and who reportedly identified himself to agents as a [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] terrorist.[1]

## Arrest and statements

Agents of the [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] captured Ebbadi on March 9, 2024, after he crossed from Mexico into Texas, and transported him to the El Paso hardened facility for processing and investigation.[1] Two days later he told agents he intended to build a bomb; asked why he had come to the United States, he said, "I'm going to try to make a bomb," according to an exclusive report by the New York Post.[1] He reportedly stated he was headed to New York.[1] Agents moved him into isolation and contacted the Tactical Terrorism Response Team to conduct an interview.[1]

## Commentary

[[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] presented the case as a direct consequence of what he described as the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's effectively non-existent border policy, writing that "elections have consequences" and "stolen elections have even larger consequences."[1] He recalled [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s warnings that terrorists and career criminals would exploit border weakness, and asked how many additional Hezbollah, Hamas, or ISIS-linked individuals had crossed undetected.[1]
