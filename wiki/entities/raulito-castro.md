# Raulito Castro

**Raulito Castro**, a grandson of longtime Cuban leader [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]] and known by the nickname "El Cangrejo" (The Crab), emerged in March 2026 as a figure at the center of reported negotiations between [[cuba|Cuba]] and the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]

## March 2026 press conference

On a Thursday evening in mid-March 2026, sitting Cuban President [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]] announced that he would host an emergency press conference the following morning to disclose something important.[1] At that appearance Díaz-Canel stood alongside Raulito Castro and said that Cuba was in open negotiations with the Trump administration, providing no further details.[1]

## Reported succession role

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the joint appearance all but confirmed Axios reporting by Marc Caputo that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and Castro had been engaged in secret negotiations over Castro potentially taking over as Cuba's leader.[1] The commentary noted that Díaz-Canel had been the first non-Castro family member to lead Cuba since Fidel Castro assumed power in 1959, and that [[neoconservatives|NeoCon]] demands had historically called for the Castro family to be expelled from Cuba or arrested for war crimes.[1] On that reading, President [[donald-trump|Trump]] appeared instead to be preparing to install a Castro as America's preferred Cuban leader, following a template previously seen in [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## May 2026 succession reporting

Following the unsealing of a US federal indictment against [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]] over the 1996 [[brothers-to-the-rescue|Brothers to the Rescue]] shootdown, Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Raulito remained in constant contact with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and that all indications pointed toward his being installed as Trump's preferred leader in Cuba.[2] Such an outcome was characterized as a "rugpull" against the Hispanic NeoCon bloc, which had backed pressure on Havana in the expectation of seeing the Castro family removed rather than perpetuated.[2]

## Nickname and "El Cangrejo" reporting

In commentary published March 2, 2026, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described Raulito as the favorite grandson of [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]], also named Raúl, who goes by the nickname El Cangrejo, or "The Crab" — a name he called an "epic TrumpaMania stage-name."[3] He noted reports attributing the nickname to a deformity involving one of Castro's hands, but wrote that alleged photographs of him showed apparently normal hands, expressing disappointment while allowing that the image might be old.[3] He concluded with a call to negotiate rather than escalate.[3]

The same commentary framed [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s February 27, 2026 "friendly takeover" remarks about [[cuba|Cuba]] as pointing toward a "Venezuela 2.0" outcome for the Castro regime, minus the arrest or extraction of the incumbent leadership — an anticipated "major rugpull" for audiences hoping for hardcore regime-change operations, whose appetite had temporarily been satisfied by the opening of the war with Iran.[3]
