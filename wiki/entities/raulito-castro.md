# Raulito Castro

Raulito Castro is a Cuban political figure, grandson of [[raul-castro|Raul Castro]], known by the nickname "El Cangrejo" — the Crab. The Badlands corpus identifies him as the figure reportedly positioned to assume leadership of [[cuba|Cuba]] in an arrangement negotiated with the United States.

The March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] reports that he appeared alongside President [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Diaz-Canel]] at a hastily announced Friday morning press conference in Havana at which Diaz-Canel disclosed that Cuba was in open negotiations with the Trump administration, without providing further detail. Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read his presence as confirmation of [[marc-caputo|Marc Caputo]]'s [[axios|Axios]] reporting that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and Castro had been engaged in secret talks about Castro taking over as leader — a "template," in the Brief's phrase, resembling what has played out in [[venezuela|Venezuela]].
