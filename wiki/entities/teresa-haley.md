# Teresa Haley

Teresa Haley is the former president of the Illinois state conference of the [[naacp|NAACP]], who in December 2023 apologized after video captured her describing migrants as rapists and "like savages" during a statewide meeting.[1]

## The remarks and apology

Haley's comments, recorded on video, came as she objected to migrants receiving shelter and clothing while, in her account, Black people in need had long gone without comparable assistance.[1] She subsequently apologized "to anyone who may have been hurt or offended," while Illinois branch presidents said they "unanimously supported" her leadership.[1]

In a news release, Haley wrote: "I love and value all members of our communities — including immigrants. I have worked tirelessly to advocate for the underserved and the voiceless. I remain focused on denouncing injustices, racism, and discrimination. I am empathetic to the plight of all people, and I proudly serve as a beacon of hope to the hopeless."[1] She closed by restating the NAACP mission of achieving "equity, political rights, and social inclusion" and accelerating the well-being of Black people and all persons of color.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the episode alongside the contemporaneous troubles of [[claudine-gay|Claudine Gay]] at [[harvard-university|Harvard]] as evidence of what he called a "Marxist death spiral or circular firing squad pattern" visible across multiple institutional theaters at once.[1] He characterized the NAACP as a narrative power-projection vector of the cultural arm of the Deep State, and argued there was "something delightfully delicious" about watching leaders taken down by the very social power structures they helped erect.[1]
