# Condoleezza Rice

Condoleezza Rice is named in "A Bright Red Line" as one of the Bush-administration figures who, together with [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], affirmatively discussed the expansion of [[nato|NATO]] to include Ukraine specifically.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) The article uses her involvement to date the policy's origins to the mid-2000s, well before the events of 2014 and 2022, and to argue that the risk of provoking Russia was understood at the time it was adopted.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) That same period produced the February 2008 cable by [[william-burns|William Burns]] warning that Ukrainian NATO entry was the brightest of all Russian redlines.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

## Cited in criticism of Fox News war-crimes coverage

In "The Exploitation of Charlie Kirk," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] recalled that [[fox-news|Fox News]] host [[harris-faulkner|Harris Faulkner]] had hosted Rice to discuss war crimes following Russia's invasion of Ukraine, calling it ironic that the network which most fueled support for the [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]] gave the former National Security Advisor — who played a central role in overseeing the U.S. invasions of Iraq and Afghanistan — a platform to lecture viewers on the illegality of invading a sovereign nation.[2] DeLarme contrasted that segment with Faulkner's later, unchallenging interview of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] after the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[2]
