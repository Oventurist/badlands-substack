# Brown-Forman Corporation

Brown-Forman Corp. is the American spirits company that owns the [[jack-daniels|Jack Daniel's]] whiskey brand.[1] In August 2024 it became a subject of the anti-DEI campaign waged by commentator and filmmaker [[robby-starbuck|Robby Starbuck]].[1]

## 2024 DEI retrenchment

Starbuck announced in an overnight post on X that Jack Daniel's would be his next target for exposing internal diversity, equity and inclusion programs.[1] Unlike his previous reveals — which had pushed Harley-Davidson Inc., Tractor Supply Co. and Deere & Co. to reverse or eliminate [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] and related programs — Brown-Forman had been tipped off well in advance and moved first, cutting woke training before the exposé landed.[1]

"They must have been tipped off by us going through employee LinkedIn pages," Starbuck said, posting an email from company executives to employees describing the "evolving" situation surrounding the company's DEI program.[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which covered the episode, framed it within its argument that "the toxic woke mind virus has infected corporate America, transforming companies into left-wing activists."[1]

Badlands commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the episode to argue that consumer pressure had reversed what once seemed an unstoppable societal transformation, citing a March 2024 Rasmussen survey finding that only 25% of American adults think DEI programs make companies better.[1]
