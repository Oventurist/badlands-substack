# Unite the Kingdom

Unite the Kingdom was a London rally held in May 2026 and organized by British activist [[tommy-robinson|Tommy Robinson]], drawing tens of thousands of demonstrators according to police estimates. It became a flashpoint in the debate over mass migration in the United Kingdom and drew both international support and official condemnation.

## The rally and its fallout

Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] publicly backed the rally's activists, saying it was "OK to defend your culture" and encouraging opponents of mass migration in the UK to "keep on going," framing tighter border controls as both an economic and cultural issue. British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] condemned the event as promoting "hatred and division," while police reported dozens of arrests, including several tied to alleged hate-crime offenses.

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the coverage of the rally and of reported British and French neighborhood patrols as "storytelling" — a character assassination of citizens reclaiming their homelands — and attributed the underlying "Great Migration" to a transformational change program pushed by the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and its partners over two decades.
