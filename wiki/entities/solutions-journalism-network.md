# Solutions Journalism Network

The **Solutions Journalism Network** is a nonprofit named by Badlands Media in its list of organizations "policing journalism."[1]

The article places it with the [[pulitzer-center-on-crisis-reporting|Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting]], the [[national-press-foundation|National Press Foundation]], the [[international-center-for-journalists|International Center for Journalists]] and the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]], stating that all of them are connected to individuals belonging to one or multiple of the clandestine think tanks examined in the series, and citing them as evidence of the media reach of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]].[1]
