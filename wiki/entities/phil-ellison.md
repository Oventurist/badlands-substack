# Phil Ellison

Phil Ellison is a Michigan attorney who, alongside the [[pacific-legal-foundation|Pacific Legal Foundation]], represents the Pung family in [[pung-v-isabella-county|Pung v. Isabella County]].[1]

The case, before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] with a decision expected in 2026, challenges how counties nationally handle property tax foreclosures and asks whether local governments must compensate homeowners based on fair market value rather than retaining surplus value beyond the tax debt owed.[1]
