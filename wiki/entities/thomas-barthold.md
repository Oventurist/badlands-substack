# Thomas Barthold

Thomas Barthold is the chief of staff of the [[joint-committee-on-taxation|Joint Committee on Taxation]], the nonpartisan congressional body that scores tax legislation.[1]

## Background

Barthold joined the Joint Committee staff as a staff economist in 1987, during the Reagan administration.[1] Before that he taught economics at Dartmouth College, having earned a doctorate in economics from Harvard University.[1]

## Testimony on the 2025 reconciliation bill

In May 2025, before the House of Representatives passed [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s "big, beautiful bill," Barthold answered questions from Representative [[tom-suozzi|Tom Suozzi]] about the reconciliation package's treatment of tips, overtime and Social Security taxes.[1] Asked directly whether the bill continued the payroll tax on tips despite Trump's promise that "your tips will be 100 percent yours," Barthold replied, "Uh, yes, yes it does."[1] The answer was widely cited as evidence that the legislation fell short of the campaign pledges on [[tax-free-tips|tax-free tips]].[1]
