# CLINTEL (Global Climate Intelligence Group)

CLINTEL, formally the Global Climate Intelligence Group, is an international organization of scientists and professionals that publicly disputes the existence of a climate emergency.[1] The group is best known for its World Climate Declaration, a short statement circulated for signature among scientists and technical professionals worldwide.[1]

## World Climate Declaration

In August 2023 CLINTEL made public a version of its World Climate Declaration, which opens with the assertion that "there is no climate emergency" and argues that carbon dioxide is beneficial to the Earth, contrary to what the declaration characterizes as the popular alarmist narrative.[1] The declaration calls for climate science to be "less political" while climate policies should be "more scientific," urging scientists to address openly the uncertainties and exaggerations in their predictions of global warming and politicians to count dispassionately the real costs as well as the imagined benefits of their policy measures.[1]

By the time the August 2023 version was released, a total of 1,609 scientists and professionals from around the world had signed the declaration, including 321 signatories from the United States.[1]

## Scientific arguments advanced

The coalition argues that the Earth's climate has varied for as long as the planet has existed, with several distinct cold and warm phases over geological history.[1] CLINTEL notes that the Little Ice Age ended as recently as 1850, and therefore concludes that it is "no surprise that we now are experiencing a period of warming."[1] The declaration further holds that observed warming is happening "far slower" than the rate predicted by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.[1]
