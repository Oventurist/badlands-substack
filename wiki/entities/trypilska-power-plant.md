# Trypilska Power Plant

The Trypilska thermal power plant is an electricity generating station located south of Kyiv and described as the largest power plant in the Kyiv region.[1]

## April 2024 destruction

On April 11, 2024, during a large [[russia|Russian]] missile and suicide-drone offensive against [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s energy system, six missiles struck the Trypilska plant according to a person familiar with the strike cited by Bloomberg.[1] The attack formed part of a broader campaign that damaged power plants near Kyiv, in the Kharkiv region in the east, Zaporizhzhia in the south, Lviv in the west, and Odesa, as reported by Energy Minister [[german-galushchenko|German Galushchenko]] and President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1]

Russia air-launched six hypersonic Kinzhal missiles in the same offensive, none of which Ukrainian air defenses were able to intercept; of 42 slower conventional missiles, 18 were shot down, and only one of 40 drones was downed.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the failure of U.S. and [[nato|NATO]]-provided air defenses against hypersonic weapons should alarm [[israel|Israel]], given that [[iran|Iran]] possesses similar technology and enjoys Russian backing.[1] The writer characterized [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s targeting of critical energy infrastructure as a deliberate strategy to force Ukraine to the negotiating table, calling it a mercy relative to Ukraine's newly passed conscription measures intended to add 500,000 men to its forces.[1]
