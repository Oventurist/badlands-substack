# North Dakota Supreme Court

The **North Dakota Supreme Court** is the court of last resort in the state of North Dakota. In November 2025 it reinstated the state's abortion ban, overturning a district court ruling that had found the ban unconstitutional.[1]

## 2025 Abortion Ban Ruling

The decision makes it a felony crime to perform an abortion in North Dakota, with providers facing as many as five years in prison and a $10,000 fine; patients are protected from prosecution.[1] North Dakota initially moved to ban abortion after the Supreme Court's [[dobbs-v-jackson-womens-health-organization|Dobbs]] decision overturned [[roe-v-wade|Roe v. Wade]], a step that prompted the state's only abortion provider, the Red River Women's Clinic, to relocate from Fargo, North Dakota, to Moorhead, Minnesota.[1] The state passed a near-total ban in 2023, which was struck down by State District Judge Bruce Romanick on the ground that it was unconstitutionally vague — the ruling the state Supreme Court reversed.[1]
