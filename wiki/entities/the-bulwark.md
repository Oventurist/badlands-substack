# The Bulwark

The Bulwark is a conservative anti-[[donald-trump|Trump]] news and commentary outlet associated with the network of establishment Republican writers around [[william-kristol|Bill Kristol]] and [[defending-democracy-together|Defending Democracy Together]].[1] In Badlands Media commentary it functions as a publishing venue for former Trump officials who turned against the president.[1]

## Troye's coronavirus intelligence essay

In May 2021, nine months after leaving the White House, [[olivia-troye|Olivia Troye]] published "How the Trump Administration Twisted Coronavirus Intelligence" in The Bulwark, arguing that rather than using intelligence to inform the pandemic response, the administration bent facts for political ends.[1] The essay described how a January 26, 2020 [[washington-times|Washington Times]] article tying the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute]] to Beijing's covert bio-weapons program circulated in the White House, how Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] and his chief of staff repeatedly questioned the intelligence community about the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]] through February 2020, and how the administration appeared to seek through the [[state-department|State Department]] what the intelligence community would not supply.[1] It further recorded [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]]'s March 2020 claim of "enormous evidence" for a lab origin and Trump's subsequent claim of a "high degree of confidence," concluding that the administration's first instinct was to find a scapegoat rather than formulate a response.[1]

Erik Carlson's "The Sabotage of Trump's Administration" (December 27, 2023) quotes the piece at length but reads it as inadvertently confirming that Trump's inner circle, and not the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], was the party genuinely interested in COVID's origin.[1]
