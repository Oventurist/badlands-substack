# Wall Street Silver

Wall Street Silver is a financial and political commentary account on social media whose posts circulate widely in alternative-media and "Truth Community" circles.[1]

In August 2024, a Wall Street Silver post went viral for pointing out what it characterized as an obvious propaganda push behind the monkeypox coverage of the preceding weeks, coinciding with the [[2024-mpox-global-health-emergency|2024 mpox global health emergency]] declared by the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1] The post prompted the anonymous researcher [[julians-rum|Julian's Rum]] to resurface a 2020 [[qanon|Q]] drop about a last-minute change to the Democratic convention, linking it to contemporaneous calls by some officials to delay or cancel the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] for public-health reasons.[1]

## Mockery of the trillion-dollar coin, 2023

In early May 2023 Wall Street Silver posted a widely shared reaction to economist [[paul-krugman|Paul Krugman]]'s revival of the [[trillion-dollar-coin|trillion-dollar coin]] proposal: "They gave this guy a Nobel Prize ... We can just mint six of the $1 trillion coins and pay all of the govt bills for a year. No taxes needed. It's magic."[2] The post was quoted by Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as the entry point for her critique of the scheme and of the [[nobel-prize-in-economic-sciences|Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences]] that lent it credibility.[2] The account's framing was consistent with its broader hard-money orientation, treating fiat money creation by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] as a form of [[currency-debasement|currency debasement]].[2]
