# Lance Twiggs

Lance Twiggs is a witness in the prosecution of [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] over the killing of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1]

Conservative influencers covering the July 2026 preliminary hearing pointed to Twiggs's "damning" testimony as evidence that the case was effectively concluded before trial.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] countered that the Twiggs testimony took the form of a video deposition and that the defense had no opportunity to cross-examine the witness, making it an unsound basis for pronouncing the outcome settled.[1] The episode was cited as an example of how commentary around the case advanced conclusions ahead of any adversarial testing of the evidence.[1]
