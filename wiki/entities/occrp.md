# Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project

The Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project (OCCRP) is an international investigative journalism consortium specializing in cross-border corruption and financial crime reporting. It is cited in [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] for its 2022 Suisse Secrets project, in particular an article on Venezuelan elites who hid stolen oil money in [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] accounts.

The Badlands commentary draws on that reporting for two purposes: to document the presence of [[pdvsa|PDVSA]]-linked accounts at Credit Suisse, and to note that the cooperating witness [[mathias-krull|Mathias Krull]] appears in the piece providing insight into former PDVSA official [[nervis-villalobos|Nervis Villalobos]]. The Suisse Secrets material is used alongside Argentine press reporting to argue that Credit Suisse functioned as a repository for both white-collar and cartel wealth, reportedly encompassing more than 18,000 accounts and over $100 billion in assets.
