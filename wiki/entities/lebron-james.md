# LeBron James

LeBron James is an American professional basketball player cited by Badlands Media as an example of athlete activism the outlet regards as hypocritical.[1]

In the essay "Sports in Society," James is described as "virtue signaling about human rights while being completely fine with accepting Chinese money."[1] He appears in a list of prominent sports figures — alongside Steve Kerr, Gregg Popovich, Serena Williams, Lewis Hamilton, and much of the [[national-football-league|NFL]] — accused of pushing the Black Lives Matter agenda on audiences who wanted only to watch athletic competition.[1] The article treats such conduct as one of the reasons many viewers have soured on professional sports, while arguing that a "multitude of athletes doing the right thing" outweighs the conduct of these figures.[1]
