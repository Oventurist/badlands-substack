# National Education Association

The National Education Association (NEA) is the largest labor union representing public school educators in the United States. In 2025 it served as the principal litigant opposing the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]]'s anti-DEI certification policy.[1]

## Litigation against the 2025 anti-DEI policy

The NEA challenged the Department of Education policy, first announced in a February 2025 Dear Colleague letter, that threatened to withhold federal funding from schools engaging in [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|diversity, equity and inclusion]] programming or otherwise incorporating race into student life.[1] Under the policy, schools receiving federal funds would from April 2025 have been subject to certification mandates requiring disclosure of their compliance with the administration's prohibitions.[1]

United States District Judge [[landya-mccafferty|Landya McCafferty]] concluded that the NEA was likely to succeed in its claims that the policy was unconstitutionally vague and that the department had failed to follow legally required procedural steps in adopting it.[1] She separately characterized the policy as "textbook viewpoint discrimination" likely violating the First Amendment.[1] The NEA's win was one of three rulings issued on April 24, 2025 by separate judges — two of them [[donald-trump|Trump]] appointees — against the policy.[1]
