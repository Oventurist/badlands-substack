# Eurasian Economic Union

The Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) is a Eurasian economic bloc described in Badlands Media commentary as a central component of the emerging multipolar financial architecture.[1]

## Role

The [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] is described as tied increasingly to the Eurasian Economic Union, which is "quickly preparing the groundwork for a fully integrated new financial architecture founded upon real world values" as outlined on numerous occasions by the Russian economist Sergey Glazyev.[1] Through this arrangement, the article argues, a healthier compact of civilizational forces has made clear its desire both to survive and to thrive in the twenty-first century and beyond.[1]

The EAEU is grouped with the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] and its proposed SCO Development Bank as institutional expressions of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]].[1]
