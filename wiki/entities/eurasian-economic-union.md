# Eurasian Economic Union

The Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) is the Eurasian integration bloc described in Badlands Media commentary as increasingly tied to China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Role in a new financial architecture

The EAEU is said to be quickly preparing the groundwork for a fully integrated new financial architecture founded upon real-world values, as outlined on numerous occasions by [[sergey-glazyev|Sergey Glazyev]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Together with the BRI, this constitutes what the author calls a healthier compact of civilizational forces determined to survive and thrive in the 21st century.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Relation to the multipolar alliance

The EAEU forms part of the broader multipolar alliance whose leaders convened at the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|SCO]] summit in Tianjin and endorsed the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Ukraine's 2013 turn

In October 2013 Ukrainian President [[viktor-yanukovych|Viktor Yanukovych]] chose to throw Ukraine's destiny into the Eurasian Economic Union rather than sacrifice the country on the altar of the collapsing European Union, a decision that in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account reclassified him from a "good" plutocrat to a bad one in the estimation of figures such as [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) The 2014 coup in Ukraine followed, and Ehret treats it as the point after which the unipolar script began visibly failing.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
