# Oprah Winfrey

Oprah Winfrey is an American television host and media proprietor named in Badlands Media commentary among figures alleged to have ties to [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]].[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] asked "Who else has ties to P Diddy?" and answered with a short list: "The Obamas, Kamala, Oprah — the list of Deep State players goes on and on."[1] The essay speculated that Combs may have turned state's witness and be "singing like a bird," and presented his arrest as "the priming of the pump" for anticipated mass arrests.[1]

## Context

Winfrey's inclusion places her within the essay's category of [[deep-state|Deep State]] figures whose exposure the author expected to follow the Combs prosecution, alongside [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] The claims are presented as assertion without documentary support; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
