# Lugansk People's Republic

The Lugansk People's Republic is a territory in the eastern Donbas region administered by [[russia|Russia]] and treated in Russian official accounts as Russian territory during the [[russian-invasion-of-ukraine|war in Ukraine]].[1]

## Starobelsk dormitory attack

On May 22, 2026, a Ukrainian drone struck a college dormitory in Starobelsk, within the Lugansk People's Republic, late at night while students were asleep.[1] The [[starobelsk-dormitory-attack|attack]] reportedly killed 21 people, most of them teenage girls, and injured 70 others.[1] Russia treated the strike as a terrorist attack on its own territory and responded with a large-scale overnight missile and drone campaign against Ukrainian military and industrial targets, coordinated by the [[russian-ministry-of-defence|Russian Ministry of Defence]].[1]
