# Georgia Senate Committee on Special Investigations

The Georgia Senate Committee on Special Investigations is a standing investigative committee of the [[georgia-senate|Georgia State Senate]] that in 2024 conducted an inquiry into the conduct of Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and her appointment of special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]].[1]

## March 2024 hearing

On Wednesday, March 6, 2024, defense attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] testified before the committee, producing what contemporaneous coverage described as substantial new information about how the defense first became aware of the improper relationship between Willis and Wade.[1] Merchant's testimony was described as based largely on open records information and her own independent investigation.[1]

Over the course of the hearing Merchant laid out the defense's theory of the case: that Willis had been deceptive in obtaining county funding used to hire Wade; that Wade's billing vastly exceeded that of the other outside prosecutors, [[anna-cross|Anna Cross]] and [[john-floyd|John Floyd]], despite little demonstrable work product; and that the District Attorney's office was obstructing her open records requests.[1] She also alleged witness tampering directed at [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]], including a September 2023 call from Willis witnessed by Cobb County prosecutor [[cindi-lee-yeager|Cindi Lee Yeager]] and a later call from attorney [[gabe-banks|Gabe Banks]].[1] The committee chairman responded to this account by stating, "Sounds like tampering with witnesses."[1]

## Status and possible outcomes

At the close of the March 2024 hearing the Senate investigation remained ongoing, and the committee indicated it might recall Merchant to testify at a future date.[1] Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argued that the committee ought to recommend criminal charges arising from the conduct described in the testimony, in parallel with the disqualification question then before Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]].[1]

Badlands Media special coverage of the hearing was carried live, reflecting the degree of independent-media attention the proceeding attracted.[1]
