# Mises Caucus

The Mises Caucus is a right-wing faction within the United States [[libertarian-party|Libertarian Party]] that took over the party's leadership in 2022.[1]

## Role in the 2024 convention

The caucus endorsed [[michael-rectenwald|Michael Rectenwald]] for the party's 2024 presidential nomination, and he led through most of the balloting before losing to [[chase-oliver|Chase Oliver]], whose candidacy was backed by the left-leaning Classic Liberal Caucus and functioned in part as a protest against the ruling caucus.[1] The nomination required seven rounds of voting amid open factional feuding over both the ticket and party leadership posts.[1]

Dave Smith, a comedian and Mises Caucus leader, characterized the fight as broadly representative of American politics, saying, "In a weird way, this is a microcosm of where the country is headed. Things are so polarized."[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the caucus's reform claims, writing that Libertarians remained "the party of open borders and societal licentiousness, regardless of what the Mises Reformers say."[1]
