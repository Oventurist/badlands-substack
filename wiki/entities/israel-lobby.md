# Israel Lobby

The "Israel Lobby" is the term used in Badlands Media commentary for the network of political donors, advocacy organizations and allied media figures said to exert influence over U.S. policy toward [[israel|Israel]] and the wider Middle East.[1]

## Role in the US-Iran peace deal

Following the announcement of the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding|U.S.–Iran peace framework]] in June 2026, Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry wrote that "it's very clear now that the Israel Lobby's plan is to use their considerable influence over [[congress|Congress]] to block this peace deal from being finalized," and predicted that this would be the point at which the GOP turned on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He argued that the resulting fight would expose "MAGA infiltrators" seeking their pound of flesh, and pointed to reactions from commentators he described as "Mossad Media" — including [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] and [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] — as well as [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] and Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1]

## Strategic framing

In the same commentary the lobby's defeat is presented as a necessary condition both for thwarting the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel Project]] and for liberating the American people from its control, with Trump's decision to negotiate a peace that explicitly excludes Israel treated as the mechanism that forces the network into the open.[1]
