# Elbit Systems

Elbit Systems is Israel's largest weapons producer.[1] In December 2025 the [[nato-support-and-procurement-agency|NATO Support and Procurement Agency]] (NSPA) suspended multiple contracts with the company over mounting evidence that Elbit had used a former NSPA staff member to bribe ex-colleagues in order to secure deals.[1]

The suspensions formed part of a wider bribery scandal at the heart of the [[nato|NATO]] alliance's purchasing arm that had already triggered multiple arrests across Europe, as reported by La Lettre, Le Soir, Knack and Follow the Money.[1] Investigators described a shadowy network of private operators exploiting a revolving-door system in which former NSPA staff become defense-industry consultants, thriving amid "the explosion in European defense budgets."[1] A 60-year-old Italian national, Eliau Eluasvili, had been on the run since late September, when a Belgian court issued an international arrest warrant against him in connection with the multi-nation investigation.[1]

Beyond guided mortars, communications equipment and microchips, Elbit is best known for aerial drones and other unmanned autonomous vehicles, which Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted have been used extensively by the Ukrainian military, including in an attempted assassination of Vladimir Putin.[1] The same commentary linked the scandal to the broader web of Ukrainian corruption and to reported Israeli military ties to Ukrainian formations.[1]
