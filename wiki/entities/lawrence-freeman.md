# Lawrence Freeman

Lawrence Freeman was a Miami lawyer and convicted money launderer for drug runners, identified by journalist Pete Brewton as one of the men who put together the Florida land deal that destroyed two thrifts during the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]].[1]

The deal drew a $20 million loan from [[robert-corson|Robert Corson]]'s [[vision-banc-savings|Vision Banc Savings]], which lost $17 million of it, and $80 million from [[hill-financial-savings|Hill Financial Savings]] of Red Hill, Pennsylvania, which lost $40 million.[1] Brewton said the Pennsylvania thrift, since bankrupt, had ties to the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and organised crime, as did Freeman himself.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] characterizes the transaction as a CIA-linked land deal set up by a money launderer connected to drug traffickers, from which the [[shadow-government|shadow government]] and its drug trafficking partners profited while both thrifts collapsed.[1]
