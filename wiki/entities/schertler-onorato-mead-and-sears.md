# Schertler, Onorato, Mead & Sears

Schertler, Onorato, Mead & Sears is a Washington-area law firm that defended [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] against the five false-statement counts brought by Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] in the Eastern District of Virginia in 2022.[1]

## Representation of Danchenko

Danchenko was represented at trial by firm attorneys [[stuart-sears|Stuart A. Sears]] and [[danny-onorato|Danny Onorato]], assisted by three paralegals — Charlie Tent, Grace McMahon and Matt Milzman — who sat at the defence table before Judge [[anthony-trenga|Anthony J. Trenga]].[1] The Badlands account notes as a "fun fact" that this was the same firm that had represented Hillary for America in the [[michael-sussmann|Michael Sussmann]] case, the other Durham prosecution arising from the origins of [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]].[1]

## Positions taken at trial

The firm's trial strategy, as it emerged on the opening day, rested on the contention that Danchenko had given truthful answers to ambiguous questions and that the interviewing [[fbi|FBI]] agents had failed to make clear what they were asking.[1] It sought to exclude any mention of the earlier counterespionage investigation of Danchenko, to admit contemporaneous news articles showing that the information [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles Dolan]] passed to Danchenko was publicly available, and to introduce Amtrak records and a Facebook message corroborating Danchenko's account of his July 2016 trip to New York.[1] It also invoked the rule of completeness over an excised email in a chain between [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]] and a Mr. Zlodorev, and argued that admission of the Dolan and Millian emails would allow the government to smuggle in a concealment theory where no duty to disclose existed.[1] The government responded by relying on Danchenko's [[danchenko-immunity-agreement|immunity agreement]].[1]
