# Sable Offshore

Sable Offshore is a United States petroleum company identified as the operator directed by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to restart shuttered oil production operations in California under authorities delegated by the [[defense-production-act|Defense Production Act]].[1]

## Santa Ynez Unit restart

In March 2026, the White House invoked Defense Production Act authorities to address energy supply concerns tied to instability involving [[iran|Iran]], directing a US oil company to restart petroleum production operations in California that had previously been halted.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] identified the operator as Sable Offshore and the assets involved as the Santa Ynez Unit and its associated pipeline system, describing the restart as boosting in-state production by roughly 50,000 barrels per day.[1]

In that framing, the restart displaces foreign crude imports that had been routed through maritime chokepoints such as the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], which were under narrative and physical pressure during the Iran conflict.[1] Burning Bright characterized the action as "the codification (literally) of sovereign mandate," arguing that energy independence had been converted from a campaign slogan into an explicit national security posture.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary positioned the Sable Offshore restart as phase one of what Burning Bright calls "Sovereign Disentanglement" — the deliberate decentralization of American energy capacity under cover of foreign crisis, with the geopolitical drama functioning as a shield rather than the mission itself.[1]
