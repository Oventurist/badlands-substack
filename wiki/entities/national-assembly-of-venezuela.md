# National Assembly of Venezuela

The National Assembly is the unicameral legislature of [[venezuela|Venezuela]]. As of February 2026 it remained dominated by the ruling party and functioned as the principal instrument for institutional appointments and legal reform under the country's transitional government.[1]

## February 2026 institutional reshuffle

The Assembly accepted the resignation of Attorney General [[tarek-william-saab|Tarek William Saab]] after nearly nine years in office and immediately appointed him acting Ombudsman, following the resignation of the previous ombudsman.[1] It designated Larry Devoe, head of the National Council of Human Rights, as acting Attorney General.[1] The legislature also formed a 13-member parliamentary commission, granted a 30-day deadline, to evaluate candidates and define the permanent heads of the Attorney General's Office and the Ombudsman's Office.[1]

## Amnesty legislation

In the same month the Assembly approved a limited [[venezuela-amnesty-law|amnesty bill]] after a second debate headed by [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]], brother of President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]].[1] Human rights organizations condemned the parallel appointment of Saab as a setback for justice reform and questioned the independence of the country's prosecutorial and human rights institutions.[1]
