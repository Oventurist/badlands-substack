# Alliance Defending Freedom

**Alliance Defending Freedom** is an Arizona-based legal organization that litigates religious-liberty and free-expression cases in American courts.[1] The firm represented Colorado baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] in the transgender-cake case brought by attorney [[autumn-scardina|Autumn Scardina]] against his bakery, [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]].[1]

After the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] dismissed the case on procedural grounds in a 4–3 decision on October 8, 2024, Phillips's attorney Jake Werner of the organization said his client had been pursued and mocked for years by those who disagreed with him, adding: "Enough is enough. Jack has been dragged through courts for over a decade. It's time to leave him alone."[1]

## Oregon adoption case (2023)

In April 2023 Alliance Defending Freedom filed suit on behalf of a woman whose application to adopt was denied by the State of Oregon because of her Christian faith and her refusal to agree to support gender transition for a child who might identify as transgender.[2] The organization argued that Oregon officials had put politics above children's welfare, and commentary accompanying the reporting described the denial as a backdoor method of discriminating against people of faith.[2] The dispute is covered further at [[oregon-adoption-religious-discrimination-case|the Oregon adoption religious discrimination case]].[2]
