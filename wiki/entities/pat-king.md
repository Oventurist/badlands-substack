# Pat King

Pat King is a Canadian activist and one of the organizers of the 2022 [[freedom-convoy|"Freedom Convoy"]] trucker protests against Canadian COVID-19 policies.[1] In late November 2024 he was convicted on five charges arising from his role in the demonstrations, which had blockaded downtown Ottawa.[1]

## Conviction

Justice Charles Hackland held that "Mr. King was not merely engaging in political speech... Rather, he was inciting the protesters to continue their ongoing blockade of downtown Ottawa."[1] Badlands Media noted that the blockade was, in its assessment, an entirely peaceful protest that caused no property damage and did no harm beyond frustrating the government of [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]].[1] King faces up to five years.[1]

## Contrast with the Montreal riots

The conviction was handed down the same Saturday that pro-Palestinian, anti-NATO demonstrators rioted in Montreal during the 70th Annual Session of the [[nato|NATO]] Parliamentary Assembly, smashing shop windows, setting vehicles on fire, burning an effigy of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and assaulting police; three protesters were arrested.[1] Badlands used the juxtaposition to argue that the Canadian government treats protesters asymmetrically according to the content of their political message, and observed that Canada lacks an equivalent to the American First Amendment, under which content-based restrictions on speech are presumptively invalid and peaceful incitement to continue protesting would be protected speech and assembly.[1] The commentary predicted that the Montreal rioters would receive, at most, a slap on the wrist.[1]
