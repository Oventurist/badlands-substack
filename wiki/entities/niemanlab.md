# Nieman Journalism Lab

The Nieman Journalism Lab (NiemanLab) is a journalism research and analysis publication affiliated with the Nieman Foundation at Harvard University, covering innovation and change in the news media. In the Badlands corpus it appears as the outlet whose reporter exposed *[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]]*'s fictional measles story.

## The measles fiction exposé (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that NiemanLab published [[laura-hazard-owen|Laura Hazard Owen]]'s piece on [[elizabeth-bruenig|Elizabeth Bruenig]]'s fictional Atlantic essay about an unvaccinated child dying from measles — headlined by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] as "The Atlantic Busted Fabricating Dead Kid Measles Story." Owen wrote that at the end of Bruenig's piece an editor's note claimed the story was "based on extensive reporting and interviews with physicians," and that her own reaction was to flag the piece to her mom group as fiction. Multiple journalists questioned the story's truthfulness, which reportedly led to the disclaimer.
