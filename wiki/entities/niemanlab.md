# NiemanLab

NiemanLab is the journalism-industry publication of the Nieman Foundation at Harvard University, covering the business, ethics and technology of news.[1]

## The Atlantic measles fiction story

In February 2026, NiemanLab writer [[laura-hazard-owen|Laura Hazard Owen]] published the account that revealed a viral [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]] essay by [[elizabeth-bruenig|Elizabeth Bruenig]] — narrated as a mother's first-hand experience of her unvaccinated 11-month-old dying of measles — to be a work of fiction built on a composite character.[1] Owen described her own conflicted reaction and questioned whether vaccine-skeptical readers would dismiss the piece as fake.[1] Coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] carried the finding into wider circulation.[1]

The episode was cited by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as a rare case of mainstream, institution-aligned journalists holding one of their own to account, even while she criticized the underlying assumption that such essays could change minds.[1]
