# Arun Subramanian

Arun Subramanian is a United States federal judge who presided over the criminal case against music mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]].[1]

## Bail rulings in the Combs case

On Monday, 4 August 2025, Subramanian denied Combs' bail, marking the fifth time the defendant had been refused release from the Brooklyn Metropolitan Detention Center, where he had been held since his arrest in September 2024.[1]

In his ruling, Subramanian wrote that the court must "find by clear and convincing evidence that the person is not likely to flee or pose a danger to the safety of any other person or the community if released," and concluded that Combs posed a risk of "flight or danger."[1] The judge also addressed the conditions that would warrant release, describing them as "'exceptional reasons' warranting release where it was undisputed that the defendant posed no risk of flight or danger, and where 'unique circumstances' counseled release, including the defendant's advanced 'age and serious medical conditions, which require care that the MDC may not be able to provide.'"[1]

## Combs superseding-indictment arraignment (March 2025)

On Friday, March 14, 2025, [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] appeared before U.S. District Judge Subramanian in Manhattan federal court to plead not guilty to two new superseding indictments unveiled in January and earlier that month.[2] It was the first time Combs directly addressed the court since his September 2024 arraignment on sex trafficking, racketeering conspiracy and transportation to engage in prostitution charges, and he spoke confidently during the hour-long hearing.[2]
