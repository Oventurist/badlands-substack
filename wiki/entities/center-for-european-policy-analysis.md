# Center for European Policy Analysis (CEPA)

The Center for European Policy Analysis is a transatlantic policy think tank whose May 2026 analysis of the Russia–China relationship was covered — and criticized as "globalist" — in the Badlands Brief.[1]

## Analysis of the Putin–Xi relationship

Writing as [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] traveled to Beijing to meet [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] directly on the heels of a [[donald-trump|Trump]]–Xi summit, CEPA argued that the Putin–Xi relationship has evolved far beyond a tactical "marriage of convenience" into a mature alliance rooted in a "shared consciousness."[1] The analysis noted that since 2012 the two leaders have met nearly 50 times — more than any other world leader has met Xi — culminating in their February 2022 "no limits" friendship declaration issued days before Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine.[1]

CEPA held that Beijing has since sustained Moscow's war effort through dual-use exports, coordinated information operations, and economic lifelines, that both powers back [[iran|Iran]], and that they pursue a vision privileging state power over individual liberty while seeking to reorder the international system against the West.[1] On that basis, CEPA and allied think tanks urged the Trump administration to abandon hopes of splitting the pair and instead strengthen democratic alliances against this "meta threat."[1]

## Badlands critique

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the CEPA framing as part of the Media Industrial Complex's misreading of events, arguing that the apparent escalation among Trump, Putin, and Xi is a "cover story" or "translation layer" enabling a shared pivot toward decentralized, sovereign national development — a dynamic he calls the Multipolar War waged jointly against the Hegemon rather than against each other.[1]
