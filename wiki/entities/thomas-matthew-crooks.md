# Thomas Matthew Crooks

Thomas Matthew Crooks was the 20-year-old gunman identified as having opened fire at a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania, on 13 July 2024, in what became known as the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]].[1]

The shot narrowly missed Trump's skull and instead pierced his right ear as he turned to look at an immigration chart displayed on a screen at the event.[1] In recounting the shooting during his nomination speech at the 2024 Republican National Convention, Trump said that had he not moved his head at the last instant, "the assassin's bullet would have perfectly hit its mark, and I would not be with you tonight."[1] Trump also described how rally attendees did not "run for the exits or stampede" but stood in place, with many pointing toward the shooter's position — behavior he credited with saving lives.[1]

## Identification and death

The [[fbi|FBI]] identified Crooks early on the Sunday morning after the shooting as a 20-year-old resident of Bethel Park, Pennsylvania, and said the investigation remained active and ongoing.[2] According to the [[us-secret-service|Secret Service]], Crooks attacked from an elevated position outside the rally venue at a farm show in Butler and was fatally shot by agents returning fire.[2] One rally attendee was killed and two spectators critically injured in the barrage; all three were identified as men.[2] Badlands commentary treated the "cascade of security failures" that allowed Crooks to reach that position as itself an answer for those paying attention.[2]
