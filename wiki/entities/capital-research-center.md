# Capital Research Center

The Capital Research Center is an American research organization that investigates the funding networks of nonprofit and advocacy groups. In September 2025 it published an exclusive report alleging that the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] had directed more than $80 million to groups tied to terrorism or extremist violence.[1]

## September 2025 Open Society report

The report stated that since 2016 the Open Society Foundations — founded by [[george-soros|George Soros]] and by then run with his son [[alex-soros|Alexander Soros]] — had poured over $80 million into organizations linked to terrorism or extremist violence, including U.S.-based groups engaged in "direct actions" that the FBI defines as domestic terrorism.[1]

Among the recipients identified were the [[center-for-third-world-organizing|Center for Third World Organizing]] and its militant partner the [[ruckus-society|Ruckus Society]], which the report said trained activists in property destruction and sabotage during the 2020 riots, and the [[sunrise-movement|Sunrise Movement]], which endorsed the Antifa-linked [[stop-cop-city|Stop Cop City]] campaign.[1] The report noted that activists connected to that campaign faced more than 40 domestic terrorism charges and 60 racketeering indictments.[1] It further stated that Open Society awarded $18 million to the [[movement-for-black-lives|Movement for Black Lives]], which co-authored a guide the report described as glorifying Hamas's October 7 massacre and instructing activists in the use of false IDs, blockades, and economic disruption.[1] Internationally, the report said more than $2.3 million had been funneled to [[al-haq|Al-Haq]], a West Bank NGO long accused of ties to the [[popular-front-for-the-liberation-of-palestine|Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine]].[1]

## Board connections and commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright highlighted that one of the Capital Research Center's board members, [[edwin-meese-iii|Edwin Meese III]], had been awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2019 for his "contributions to conservative principles and public service."[1] The commentary observed that the report was published on the same day Trump announced he was designating [[antifa|Antifa]] a domestic terror organization, and argued it could serve as a predicate for that designation as well as a fulcrum for RICO charges against Soros and similar funders.[1] Burning Bright also read the Meese connection as evidence that a long-running civilian-military alliance had been operating behind the scenes, with effects surfacing on a delay.[1]
