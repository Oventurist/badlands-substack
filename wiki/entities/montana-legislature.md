# Montana Legislature

The Montana Legislature is the bicameral state lawmaking body of Montana, comprising a Senate and a House of Representatives. In April 2023 it drew national attention when its House voted to remove Democratic representative [[zooey-zephyr|Zooey Zephyr]] from the chamber floor for the remainder of the 2023 session, after the member was first censured over a series of disruptions tied to debate on transgender-related legislation.[1]

## 2023 session and transgender legislation

During the 2023 session the legislature took up a number of bills that supporters described as measures to protect children — restricting what they characterized as trans indoctrination in schools and sexualized drag shows, and banning sex-change surgeries and hormone therapies for minors.[1] Among these was SB 99, a bill to ban gender-affirming care for minors that received a House committee hearing in March 2023.[1] Coverage aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed Montana as one of many states acting to contain what it described as the political indoctrination of children by trans activists and the far left.[1]

## Zephyr censure

Zephyr, who represents District 100 around Missoula, warned lawmakers that they would have "blood on your hands" if such bills passed, language that legislative leadership treated as an incitement.[1] After further floor disruptions the chamber voted to bar the representative from the House floor for the rest of 2023, a sanction that allowed remote voting but not participation in floor debate.[1]
