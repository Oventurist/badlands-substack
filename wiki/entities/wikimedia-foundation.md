# Wikimedia Foundation

The Wikimedia Foundation is the nonprofit organization that operates Wikipedia. Badlands Media raises it in the context of perceived editorial bias, arguing that there are important links between [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the foundation's governance.[1]

## Alleged Soros connections

Melissa Hagemann, a senior program manager of the [[open-society-foundations|Soros Open Society Foundations']] Information Program, has previously sat on the advisory board of the Wikimedia Foundation.[1] Hagemann has also worked with the [[bill-and-melinda-gates-foundation|Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation]] — an organization that, like Soros, has invested heavily in the drug industry over the years and which, according to the Los Angeles Times, reaps vast financial gains from investments that contravene its stated charitable purposes.[1]

Ethan Zuckerman, another former member of the Wikimedia advisory board, is likewise described as having connections to Soros and as having sat on the Global Board of his Open Society Foundations.[1]

## Context

These personnel overlaps are offered as an answer to the rhetorical question of why Wikipedia "seems so biased," fitting the article's broader model in which the network purchases and influences media outlets across every front — television, radio, print, internet, social media and pop culture — in order to shape narratives and frame reality.[1] The advisory-board appointments themselves are matters of record; the inference that they translate into editorial control of Wikipedia is the contributor's own.

[[zack-exley|Zack Exley]], another figure in the article, is noted to have held a key position at the Wikimedia Foundation.[1]
