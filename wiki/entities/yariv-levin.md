# Yariv Levin

Yariv Levin is the Israeli Justice Minister. In late December 2024 he was designated to serve as caretaker prime minister while Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was incapacitated during surgery to remove his prostate, according to reporting cited by Badlands Media from The Jerusalem Post.[1] Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] was designated to chair the political-security cabinet during the same period.[1]

Levin's temporary elevation placed the architect of Israel's contested judicial overhaul in the premiership at the very moment Netanyahu's attorney, Amit Hadad, obtained approval from the Attorney General's Office to cancel the prime minister's scheduled testimony in his ongoing corruption trial.[1] Badlands commentary tied the episode to a broader pattern of delay in that prosecution, including an earlier effort to reorganize the judicial system so that Netanyahu's ruling party could stack the Supreme Court with justices of its choosing.[1]

## Call for West Bank sovereignty (July 2025)

In comments reported on July 2, 2025, Levin, serving as [[israel|Israel]]'s Justice Minister, said "the time has come" for Israel to apply sovereignty across the occupied West Bank.[2] The remarks drew a strong condemnation from [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], whose Ministry of Foreign Affairs said such a step would violate international law and reiterated the Kingdom's rejection of settlement expansion on Palestinian land.[2]
