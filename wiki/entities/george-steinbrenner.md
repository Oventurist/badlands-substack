# George Steinbrenner

George Steinbrenner was the owner of the [[new-york-yankees|New York Yankees]] for nearly 40 years until his death.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told Yankees players and coaches during his September 11, 2025 clubhouse visit that he had been friends with the late Steinbrenner.[1]
