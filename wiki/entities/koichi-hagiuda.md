# Koichi Hagiuda

**Koichi Hagiuda** is a senior figure in Japan's ruling [[liberal-democratic-party|Liberal Democratic Party]].[1]

## December 2025 Taiwan visit

Hagiuda met with [[taiwan|Taiwan]]'s President [[lai-ching-te|Lai Ching-te]] in December 2025 as part of a wave of visits to Taiwan by Japanese lawmakers, describing relations between Tokyo and Taipei as "the best they have ever been," according to a statement from Taiwan's Presidential Office.[1] Lai said he hoped to further deepen cooperation and that the two sides could work together to advance a "free and open Indo-Pacific."[1]

Although visits by Japanese lawmakers to Taiwan are not unusual, this round came amid heightened tensions between Japan and [[china|China]] over Taiwan, after Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] said the previous month that Japan's military could theoretically be deployed if China attacked Taiwan — prompting a fierce response from Beijing that included limits on Chinese tourists traveling to Japan and public criticism of Takaichi.[1]
