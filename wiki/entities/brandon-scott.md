# Brandon Scott

Brandon Scott is the mayor of Baltimore, Maryland, who drew national attention in March 2024 after responding to critics who labeled him the "DEI Mayor" in the aftermath of the [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse]].[1]

## MSNBC appearance

Appearing on [[joy-reid|Joy Reid]]'s MSNBC program, Scott asserted that white conservatives "should be afraid" of the consequences of calling him the "DEI Mayor" and said his "purpose in life" was to make them uncomfortable.[1]

Reid framed the criticism as racially motivated, asserting that when right-wingers mock DEI they actually mean "black people," and claiming, "It's the reason they complained about [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]], it's not fashionable to be openly racist anymore in America."[1] Modernity News, in coverage carried by Badlands, described Reid as a "professional race-bator" and characterized Scott as dressing and acting "like a Black Lives Matter activist."[1]

Scott complained that "young black men" were being demonized and treated as the "boogeymen" by conservatives who, in his telling, believe that "only straight, white wealthy men" should hold power.[1]
