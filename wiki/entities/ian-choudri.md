# Ian Choudri

Ian Choudri is the chief executive of California's bullet train project, a rail and infrastructure veteran appointed to lead the [[california-high-speed-rail-authority|California High-Speed Rail Authority]] through a period of severe financial and political pressure.[1]

As of July 2025 Choudri was developing a new business plan intended to lower costs, accelerate construction and add private sector partners to the project.[1] His appointment coincided with Transportation Secretary [[sean-duffy|Sean Duffy]]'s decision to claw back $4 billion in federal grants and with Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]]'s lawsuit against the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration over that decision.[1]

Badlands commentary singled out Choudri's private-sector orientation as the most encouraging element of the story, while arguing that the deeper question — why no functional trains exist decades after the 1982 approval, and where the money went — remained unanswered.[1]
