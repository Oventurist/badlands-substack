# The Boring Company

The Boring Company is a tunneling and infrastructure firm associated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]. It appears in Badlands Media coverage as the cost-reduction premise behind proposals for a fixed link across the Bering Strait.[1]

## Relevance to the Bering Strait tunnel

Kremlin special envoy [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]] first floated the tunnel concept in October 2025, estimating that a conventional build could exceed $65 billion.[1] He suggested that costs could fall below $8 billion if construction methods associated with The Boring Company were employed — an order-of-magnitude reduction that has been central to arguments for the project's feasibility.[1] On June 5, 2026 Russia and the United States were reported to be signing an agreement to advance the design phase of the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait tunnel]] connecting Russia's Chukotka region with Alaska.[1]
