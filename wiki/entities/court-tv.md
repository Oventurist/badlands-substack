# Court TV

Court TV is an American cable television network that broadcasts courtroom proceedings.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Badlands host [[canncon|CannCon]] discovered that the "super secret" video in the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] case — touted by figures around the trial as decisive evidence that would make the trial "over" — was actually the same footage captured in the Court TV stream, which [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] then played on her show. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the discovery as evidence that the "4K video" drama was engagement farming aimed at influencing the jury pool in the murder case of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].
