# Malachi Martin

Malachi Martin was a former Jesuit historian cited by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a critical witness to the character and doctrine of [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]].[1]

Martin wrote that "Teilhard was not overly shocked by bloodshed, regarded violence as a necessary concomitant of Evolution, and seemed to have enjoyed war — what he saw of it," adding that death, bloody or otherwise, was what Chardin called a "mutation."[1] Ehret uses Martin's testimony to argue that Chardin's system, by dissolving evil into statistics and complexity, removed any moral barrier to war, [[eugenics|eugenics]] and the sacrifice of what Chardin termed "life's rejects."[1]
