# William Temple Franklin

William Temple Franklin was the grandson of [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]], whom Franklin was training in statecraft.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

He appears as the young man in [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]]'s unfinished 1783 painting *The Peace of Paris*, alongside the rest of the American delegation that came to Paris to sign the treaty officially ending America's war with Great Britain — John Jay, John Adams, Benjamin Franklin and Henry Laurens.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) The painting was never completed because the British delegation never showed up for the portrait sitting, the British having never really accepted that they lost the war.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats the half-finished canvas as fitting, since the revolution itself was never finished and British oligarchist influence within the United States was never extracted, remaining behind as a powerful fifth column over the following two centuries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
