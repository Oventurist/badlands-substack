# Gianni Infantino

Gianni Infantino is the president of [[fifa|FIFA]], world football's governing body. In May 2025 he appeared alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the first meeting of the administration's [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 World Cup]] taskforce.[1]

## 2025 World Cup taskforce exchange

At that meeting Trump suggested that the opportunity for [[russia|Russia]] to play at the 2026 World Cup could serve as an "incentive" for Moscow to end the war in Ukraine, apparently unaware that the Russian national team had been banned from international competition by FIFA and UEFA since the 2022 invasion.[1] "I didn't know that. Is that right?" Trump asked; Infantino replied, "That is right," adding that the Russians "are banned for the time being from playing but we hope that something happens and peace will happen so that Russia can be readmitted."[1] Trump responded, "That's possible. Hey, that could be a good incentive, right?"[1]

Under the rules in force at the time, Russia would not appear at the 2026 tournament co-hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico; qualification had begun in September 2023, with 45 places available in addition to the three host nations.[1] Badlands commentator Burning Bright framed the exchange as evidence that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] does not need to force peace but merely to "untangle Hegelian doom loops and let peace arise."[1]
