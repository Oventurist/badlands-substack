# Danny Werfel

Danny Werfel is an American public administrator who served as Commissioner of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] and, in that capacity, announced the agency's expanded enforcement programme in September 2023.[1]

## IRS enforcement initiative (September 2023)

In a statement issued on 8 September 2023, Werfel announced what the agency described as a "sweeping, historic" tax enforcement initiative, funded by a boost in appropriations tied to the Inflation Reduction Act and built around artificial intelligence and other advanced technologies for identifying tax evaders.[1] "There is a sea change taking place at the IRS in every aspect of our operations," Werfel said, describing the completion of a top-to-bottom review of the agency's enforcement efforts and a renewed focus on those "abusing the nation's tax laws."[1]

Werfel framed the technological component as both a detection tool and a fairness measure: "The changes will be driven with the help of improved technology as well as Artificial Intelligence that will help IRS compliance teams better detect tax cheating, identify emerging compliance threats and improve case selection tools to avoid burdening taxpayers with needless 'no-change' audits."[1] Badlands Media carried the announcement as a bonus item in its news brief covering the news cycle of 12 September 2023, in a issue otherwise dominated by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] impeachment inquiry and intelligence-community disclosures.[1]
