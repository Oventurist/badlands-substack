# 2024 YR4

**2024 YR4** is an asteroid estimated to have a 4% chance of striking the Moon in 2032, and the subject of a 2025 research paper examining whether humanity should deflect or destroy it.[1]

## Research paper

More than a dozen researchers, including several [[nasa|NASA]] scientists, explored the question in a paper uploaded to the preprint website arXiv on September 15, 2025, which had not yet been peer-reviewed.[1] The researchers wrote that a lunar collision could produce debris "up to 1,000 times above background levels over just a few days, possibly threatening astronauts and spacecraft" in low-Earth orbit.[1] To avoid creating that potentially dangerous debris field, one option discussed in the paper is to nuke the asteroid — to trigger what the scientists call a "robust disruption" — before it reaches the Moon.[1] Media coverage framed the scenario with references to the film "Armageddon."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America read the story as narrative preparation rather than science, asking whether the reference point was "Armageddon" or "Don't Look Up."[1] She questioned whether it was coincidental that "regime-darlings" Leonardo DiCaprio and Jennifer Lawrence had starred four years earlier in a film mocking conspiracy theorists who disbelieved in an imminent, earth-ending meteor — a film in which, she noted, everyone dies.[1]

She predicted both movies would be deployed "on the hearts and minds of the masses": "Armageddon" to signal bravery in fighting the threat, and "Don't Look Up" to mock anyone who asks too many questions.[1] She described the dynamic as the "hot war" version of the climate change deployment, in which dissenters are cast as existential enemies of both humanity and earth, and concluded bluntly that "[t]he meteor scare is bullshit."[1]
