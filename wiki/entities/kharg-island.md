# Kharg Island

Kharg Island is an Iranian island in the northern Persian Gulf and the country's principal crude oil export terminal. The March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] describes it as a strategic hub handling about 90 percent of [[iran|Iran]]'s oil shipments, which makes it simultaneously the most valuable and the most exposed node in the Iranian export system.

## The 2026 strikes

According to the Brief, the United States carried out strikes on Iranian military targets on Kharg Island during the March 2026 phase of the war. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said the attacks destroyed military facilities on the island but deliberately avoided its oil infrastructure, and warned that broader strikes could follow if Iran interfered with shipping in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]]. Iran threatened retaliation, warning that energy infrastructure tied to the United States in Gulf countries could become targets.

The Brief's commentators treat the calibrated nature of the Kharg strikes — military targets hit, export terminals spared — as evidence for their broader reading that the energy dimension of the war is being managed rather than merely escalated, with disruption falling selectively on import-dependent economies rather than on the energy-independent belligerents.
