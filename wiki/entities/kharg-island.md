# Kharg Island

Kharg Island is an Iranian island in the Persian Gulf functioning as the country's principal oil export terminal, handling roughly 90 percent of [[iran|Iran]]'s oil shipments.[1]

## March 2026 strikes

The United States carried out strikes on Iranian military targets on Kharg Island during the 2026 war.[1] [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] said the attacks destroyed military facilities on the island but deliberately avoided the oil infrastructure, warning that broader strikes could follow if Iran interfered with shipping in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

Iran threatened retaliation after the strikes, warning that energy infrastructure tied to the United States in Gulf countries could become targets.[1] The escalation disrupted maritime traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, the route carrying about one-fifth of the world's oil supply.[1]

## Analysis

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the selective targeting — military facilities struck, export capacity spared — as evidence of a deliberate, calibrated campaign in which energy is both the arena and the prize, with the pressure falling hardest on import-dependent Europe rather than on the [[multipolar-world|multipolar]] bloc.[1]
