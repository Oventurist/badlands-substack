# William Collins Whitney

William Collins Whitney (Yale class of 1863) was a member of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] and served as United States Secretary of the Navy.[1] He married Flora Payne of the Standard Oil Payne dynasty.[1]

In *America's Secret Establishment*, [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]] presents the Whitney–Stimson–Bundy sequence as the model case of the Order's "vertical chain" of influence: Whitney's attorney was Elihu Root, Root hired [[henry-stimson|Henry Stimson]] (Yale 1888) out of law school, Stimson took over from Root as Secretary of War in 1911 under fellow Bonesman [[william-howard-taft|William Howard Taft]], and Stimson's special assistant Hollister Bundy (Yale 1909) fathered the Bonesmen [[william-bundy|William Bundy]] and [[mcgeorge-bundy|McGeorge Bundy]].[1]
