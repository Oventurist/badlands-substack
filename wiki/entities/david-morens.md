# David Morens

David Morens is a former senior adviser to [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] at the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]].[1] In 2026 he was indicted for conspiring to conceal or falsify discussions of research grants and for using private email accounts to evade public records requests.[1]

Morens was arrested at his home by armed federal agents.[1] His indictment formed part of a broader cluster of legal and administrative actions taken after April 2026 against NIAID-affiliated scientists over COVID-19 research and alleged efforts to obscure the virus's origins, with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1] Shortly after the Morens case surfaced, [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]] was removed as acting head of NIAID.[1]

## April 2026 indictment

On Tuesday, April 28, 2026, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced that Morens, then 78 years old and described as a senior adviser to Dr. [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] from 2006 to 2022, had been charged with conspiracy against the United States, destruction and falsification of federal records, concealment of documents, and aiding and abetting.[2] Prosecutors allege that he deliberately withheld, altered, and concealed information during federal probes into COVID-19 origins in order to suppress alternative theories about the pandemic's beginnings.[2] Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] described the conduct as a "profound abuse of trust" at the height of the global health crisis.[2] The indictment was announced the same day as a second indictment of former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]] had until recently been a canceling offense, but argued that it is itself a secondary narrative obscuring her view that COVID-19 was an intentional deployment; she said the indictment could mark the start of justice while warning it could equally be an attempt to offer "a story of justice — at the expense of justice itself."[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] responded that in his framing COVID was a narrative deployment masquerading as an actual event, and said the unraveling of the central COVID narrative could be seismic for bipartisan and international mindshare.[2]

## The 2021 FOIA-evasion email

Years before his indictment, Morens surfaced in congressional oversight of federal COVID-19 records practices. In a 2021 email to former [[ecohealth-alliance|EcoHealth Alliance]] president [[peter-daszak|Peter Daszak]], Morens wrote that he could route material to [[anthony-fauci|Fauci]] outside official channels to defeat Freedom of Information Act requests: "I can either send stuff to Tony on his private gmail, or hand it to him at work. He is too smart to let colleagues send him stuff that could cause trouble."[3] On May 29, 2024, citing that email and a separate message in which Morens alluded to a "secret back channel," [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House COVID-19 Committee]] Chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] requested access to Fauci's personal email and cellphone records ahead of Fauci's testimony and opened an investigation into the arrangement.[3]
