# Asia Society

The **Asia Society** is an international policy and cultural organization named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as a board affiliation of [[michael-chae|Michael Chae]], the [[blackstone|Blackstone]] chief financial officer said to sit on the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article notes that Chae serves on the Asia Society board alongside Saudi intelligence chief [[turki-al-faisal|Prince Turki al-Faisal]], presenting the overlap as an illustration of how Harvard's endowment governance connects to foreign intelligence and foreign policy circles within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
