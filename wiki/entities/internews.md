# Internews

Internews is an international media-development nonprofit that has featured prominently in criticism of United States foreign-aid-funded media networks. In February 2025, [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] published a widely circulated thread identifying Internews as the hub of a sprawling network of media outlets funded through [[usaid|USAID]].[1]

## WikiLeaks allegations

According to the WikiLeaks item republished in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Internews head [[jeanne-bourgault|Jeanne Bourgault]] is "secretly married" to [[ray-jennings|Ray S. Jennings]], described as a high-profile regime-change specialist for USAID.[1] WikiLeaks stated that Bourgault paid herself $451,000 while telling her alma mater's alumni magazine that she goes to the office "once or twice a month," and that Jennings had authored numerous papers recommending funding for his wife's methods.[1]

WikiLeaks further asserted that the relationship between Bourgault and Jennings appears to have only one obscure reference on the entire internet — a University of Washington alumni magazine feature titled "Alumna brings an information revolution to the developing world" — and that it had not been disclosed by USAID, Internews, or the 4,291 media outlets connected to the organization.[1] Badlands presented the item under the heading "The Ultimate USAID Couple?" as an example of undisclosed conflicts of interest inside the US-funded media ecosystem.[1]
