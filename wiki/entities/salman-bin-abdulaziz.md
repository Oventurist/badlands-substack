# Salman bin Abdulaziz

Salman bin Abdulaziz is the King of Saudi Arabia.[1]

## 2017 succession decision

In June 2017, King Salman elevated his son, then Deputy Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], to the position of Crown Prince, displacing [[mohammed-bin-nayef|Mohammed bin Nayef]].[1] Badlands Media dated the move to one month after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s visit to Riyadh and the sword-dance ceremony, reading the reshuffle as a power projection by an emerging "Sovereign Alliance."[1] The elevation was followed shortly afterward by the Saudi-led embargo of [[qatar|Qatar]].[1]

## Accession and the 2017 purge

Chapter 9 of the Badlands series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] recounts that after the death in 2015 of [[abdulaziz-bin-abdul-rahman-al-saud|Abdulaziz]], described there as the former King of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], Prince Salman acceded to the throne.[2] The chapter treats the most important consequence of that accession as the elevation of his son, [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], to Crown Prince in 2017.[2]

It is after this appointment, the chapter says, that Salman set in motion the plan to purge the Saudi government and royal family — the operation that became the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]], which the same chapter elsewhere attributes operationally to the Crown Prince.[2]
