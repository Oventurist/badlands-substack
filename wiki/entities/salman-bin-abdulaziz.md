# Salman bin Abdulaziz

Salman bin Abdulaziz is the King of Saudi Arabia.[1]

## 2017 succession decision

In June 2017, King Salman elevated his son, then Deputy Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], to the position of Crown Prince, displacing [[mohammed-bin-nayef|Mohammed bin Nayef]].[1] Badlands Media dated the move to one month after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s visit to Riyadh and the sword-dance ceremony, reading the reshuffle as a power projection by an emerging "Sovereign Alliance."[1] The elevation was followed shortly afterward by the Saudi-led embargo of [[qatar|Qatar]].[1]
