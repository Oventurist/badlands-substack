# Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud

Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud is the King of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]. Before his accession he served for decades as governor of Riyadh, a tenure the Badlands corpus describes as lifelong, and in 2011 he was appointed Crown Prince by his brother, King Abdullah.

## Succession and the rise of Mohammed bin Salman

The Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026 traces a sequence of events from that 2011 appointment. Salman became King in January 2015 and named his son [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed]] Deputy Crown Prince and Defense Minister; according to [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the son "immediately went to work dismantling and rebuilding the government, on paper," installing a new government within ten days. Mohammed was elevated to Crown Prince in June 2017, one month after [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s first visit to Riyadh, and in November of that year Salman named him acting sovereign of the Kingdom — after which, in the commentary's account, Mohammed arrested relatives alleged to be corrupt and working with the [[cia|CIA]] and, by extension, Mossad.

The Brief presents this succession as the mechanism by which Saudi Arabia broke from what it calls [[deep-state|Deep State]] control established after the 1975 assassination of [[king-faisal|King Faisal]], enabling the [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] program and the regional realignment that followed.
