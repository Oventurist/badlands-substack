# Detroit

Detroit is the largest city in Michigan and, in the Badlands corpus, a recurring focus of election-integrity disputes centered on its ballot handling and record retention.

## The 2026 DOJ ballot demand

The April 21, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] requested access to roughly 865,000 Detroit ballots as part of a federal review of election procedures, warning that legal action could follow if local officials do not comply. The demand is tied to federal record-retention and election-oversight laws, including Civil Rights Act provisions that require jurisdictions to preserve election materials and make them available for inspection.

Detroit officials responded with concerns about the logistical challenges and implications of turning over such a large volume of material, citing storage requirements, chain-of-custody questions, and administrative burden. The Brief presents the demand as part of a wider federal election-oversight posture that also includes the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]]'s findings on [[actblue|ActBlue]] and the appointment of [[joseph-digenova|Joseph DiGenova]] to the [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] investigation.
