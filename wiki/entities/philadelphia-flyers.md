# Philadelphia Flyers

The **Philadelphia Flyers** are a professional ice hockey franchise of the [[national-hockey-league|National Hockey League]] based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.[1]

## 2023 Pride Night

On Tuesday, January 17, 2023, the Flyers staged their annual "Pride Night" promotion at the Wells Fargo Arena, a event that included a nonbinary youth participating with the team and players wearing rainbow uniforms during pregame warmups.[1] Flyers defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] declined to take part in the warmup, citing his Russian Orthodox Christian faith, triggering a national controversy and a wave of activist criticism directed at the player, the club, and the league.[1] The team's handling of the episode became a reference point in wider American debates over corporate LGBT advocacy and [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] in professional sports.[1]
