# Philadelphia Flyers

The **Philadelphia Flyers** are a professional ice hockey franchise of the [[national-hockey-league|National Hockey League]] based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.[1]

## 2023 Pride Night

On Tuesday, January 17, 2023, the Flyers staged their annual "Pride Night" promotion at the Wells Fargo Arena, a event that included a nonbinary youth participating with the team and players wearing rainbow uniforms during pregame warmups.[1] Flyers defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] declined to take part in the warmup, citing his Russian Orthodox Christian faith, triggering a national controversy and a wave of activist criticism directed at the player, the club, and the league.[1] The team's handling of the episode became a reference point in wider American debates over corporate LGBT advocacy and [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] in professional sports.[1]

The opponent that evening was the Anaheim Ducks, and both clubs were scheduled to wear LGBTQ-support jerseys during pregame warmups.[2] In response to the ensuing controversy the organization stated: "The Philadelphia Flyers organization is committed to inclusivity and is proud to support the LGBTQ+ community. Many of our players are active in their support of local LGBTQ+ organizations, and we were proud to host our annual Pride Night again this year. The Flyers will continue to be strong advocates for inclusivity and the LGBTQ+ community."[2] The statement made no mention of Provorov's Russian Orthodox faith, which Badlands characterized as the club declining to defend its player's right to religious freedom.[2]

Head coach [[john-tortorella|John Tortorella]] took the opposite approach, publicly backing Provorov, refusing to bench him, and saying the player had "done nothing wrong."[2] Badlands reported that "cancel culture vultures" subsequently swarmed around Provorov, Tortorella and the Flyers organization alike.[2]
