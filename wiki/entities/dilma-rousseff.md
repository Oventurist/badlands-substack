# Dilma Rousseff

Dilma Rousseff is a Brazilian politician and former president of Brazil who, by April 2023, had become chief executive of the [[new-development-bank|New Development Bank]], the Shanghai-based lender established by the [[brics|BRICS]] countries.[1]

## Role at the New Development Bank

Rousseff's appointment placed a former Brazilian head of state at the head of the BRICS-founded institution at the moment it became a platform for [[de-dollarization|de-dollarization]] advocacy.[1] Her tenure began as Brazilian president [[luiz-inacio-lula-da-silva|Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]] visited the bank and publicly urged BRICS nations to create an alternative to the dollar for foreign trade, shortly before Lula's meeting with Chinese president [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in Beijing.[1]

## Significance

Because the bank's membership spans Brazil, China, Russia, India and South Africa, Rousseff's leadership was read by commentators as symbolising the political alignment between Brazil's returning left-wing government and the wider BRICS project of reducing reliance on the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] and Western financial institutions.[1]
