# American Institute for Free Labor Development

The American Institute for Free Labor Development (AIFLD) was an [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] entity founded in 1962 and bankrolled by [[usaid|USAID]] and the [[cia|CIA]], which trained over 25,000 Latin American unionists in "anti-communist" tactics at camps in Virginia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) It was one of four U.S.-government-funded labor institutes created during the Cold War, and in 1997 was folded, along with the [[asian-american-free-labor-institute|Asian-American Free Labor Institute]] and two others, into the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

## Operations

AIFLD graduates led strikes that paralyzed leftist governments. In Guyana in 1962–63, CIA-funded AFL-CIO actions against [[cheddi-jagan|Cheddi Jagan]] triggered a three-month general strike that paved the way for U.S.-backed regime change.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) In [[brazilian-coup-1964|Brazil's 1964 coup]], AIFLD alumni in the metalworkers' union provided intelligence to the CIA and the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]], helping justify military intervention against President [[joao-goulart|João Goulart]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The institute's documented role in covert U.S. statecraft also extended to Guatemala, Honduras, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador and Chile, using propaganda dissemination, union splitting, training of anti-communist leaders, and direct support for regime change operations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) In [[chilean-coup-1973|Chile]], according to Tim Shorrock's 2003 piece in The Nation, AIFLD channeled millions of dollars from 1971 to 1973 to right-wing unions and political parties opposed to Salvador Allende's socialist agenda — aid that financed the revolt by Chile's professional class and fanned the unrest used as pretext for Augusto Pinochet's crackdown and seventeen-year dictatorship.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

During the [[lane-kirkland|Lane Kirkland]] era, AIFLD was used to train Salvadoran death-squad affiliates as U.S. unions backed Reagan's counterinsurgency in Central America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
