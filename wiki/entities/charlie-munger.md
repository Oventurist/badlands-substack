# Charlie Munger

Charlie Munger was a longtime executive of Berkshire Hathaway and a well-known critic of cryptocurrency. In Badlands Media commentary he is presented as an emblematic figure of legacy finance whose hostility to Bitcoin is read as evidence of the threat the protocol poses to entrenched monetary power.[1]

## Depiction in Badlands commentary

A Badlands Brief essay published on January 3, 2024 — the fifteenth anniversary of the Bitcoin Genesis Block — referenced a February 2, 2023 NOSTR post by Edward Snowden that named Munger, describing him as a "Berkshire Hathaway Bankster."[1] The essay reported that Snowden's post argued Bitcoin's technology had "arrested" the banker, and that this arrest occurred incrementally, block by block, rather than through any conventional legal proceeding.[1]

Munger was grouped in the essay with other public critics of Bitcoin, including Senator Elizabeth Warren and JPMorgan Chase chief executive Jamie Dimon, under the rhetorical heading that "those who scream the loudest" are those most threatened by a monetary system they cannot control.[1]
