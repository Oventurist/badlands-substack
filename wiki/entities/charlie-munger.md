# Charlie Munger

Charlie Munger was a longtime executive of [[entities/berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] and a well-known critic of cryptocurrency. In Badlands Media commentary he is presented as an emblematic figure of legacy finance whose hostility to [[concepts/bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is read as evidence of the threat the protocol poses to entrenched monetary power.[1]

## Depiction in Badlands commentary

A Badlands Brief essay published on January 3, 2024 — the fifteenth anniversary of the Bitcoin Genesis Block — referenced a February 2, 2023 NOSTR post by [[entities/edward-snowden|Edward Snowden]] that named Munger, describing him as a "Berkshire Hathaway Bankster."[1] The essay reported that Snowden's post argued Bitcoin's technology had "arrested" the banker, and that this arrest occurred incrementally, block by block, rather than through any conventional legal proceeding.[1]

Munger was grouped in the essay with other public critics of Bitcoin, including [[entities/elizabeth-warren|Senator Elizabeth Warren]] and [[entities/jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] chief executive [[entities/jamie-dimon|Jamie Dimon]], under the rhetorical heading that "those who scream the loudest" are those most threatened by a monetary system they cannot control.[1]
