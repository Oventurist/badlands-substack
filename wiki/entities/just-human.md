# Just Human

Just Human is a pseudonymous analyst affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. He co-hosts **Defected** with [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], airing Sundays at 9pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

Defected examines current events through the combined prism of [[narrative-warfare|Narrative Warfare]], game theory, and fifth-generation strategy, with the stated goal of helping viewers escape what the hosts describe as an external system of control and instead "program their own minds."[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## Badlands News Brief commentary (February 2023)

Just Human contributed two short "Our Take" segments to the February 13, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[3] On the intensifying special-counsel investigation led by [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], he wrote that the media would hope "the walls are finally closing in on Trump," but that in fact "the walls are closing in on the Swamp."[3] On the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|North American aerial object shootdowns]], his entire comment was "We agree with Flynn," a reference to [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]].[3]

## Commentary in the Badlands News Brief

In the April 2023 edition titled "Real Leaks & Fake News," Just Human contributed two analyses.[4] The first addressed [[new-york-times|New York Times]] reporting on the [[trump-classified-map-investigation|classified map]] said to have been shown by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after leaving office, arguing the story's release collided with the [[discord-classified-documents-leak|Discord leak]] of more than 100 real classified documents and reading it as an intentional but mistimed narrative sync — "Simulation winking at us, or an intentional sync?"[4]

The second examined the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]]'s decision to part ways with [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], calling it unlikely on its face given his electioneering record and the approach of 2024.[4] He offered three explanations — caseload, greater usefulness outside the DNC, or looming legal trouble arising from [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s grand jury work — and named the third both preferred and probably most likely.[4]

## On the Biden documents scandal (2023)

Writing in the March 9, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief]], Just Human credited Senators [[chuck-grassley]] and [[ron-johnson]] with diligently building a stack of letters and documents evidencing the crimes of the Bidens.[5] He observed that the [[biden-classified-documents-scandal|Biden classified documents scandal]] kept expanding — from a few misplaced pages in a closet, to a garage, to searches of a beach home and the University of Delaware, and finally to boxes of documents and handwritten notes dating from [[joe-biden]]'s vice presidency back to his Senate years.[5]

He noted that the media barely covered the story despite what he called a monster scandal brewing, with Joe, James and [[hunter-biden]] under investigation by U.S. Attorney David Weiss and Special Counsel [[robert-hur]], and the [[department-of-justice]] Inspector General being notified of developments as they occurred.[5]

## Kabul withdrawal analysis cited (2023)

In the November 8, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that he began following Just Human in August 2021 not only for his calm, reasoned takes on the Kabul Withdrawal and the ensuing disaster — both for troops on the ground and narratively for the Biden administration — but because of the way he connected the chaos to the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[6] In that reading, the agreement promised cooperation with the [[taliban|Taliban]] to create a de facto Region of Responsibility permitting U.S. troops to withdraw from a war zone crafted under the Bush administration and perfected under the Obama administration.[6]


## Credit Suisse commentary (March 2023)

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] for March 14, 2023, Just Human supplied the financial analysis, calling [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] "the International Swamp's favorite bank" and noting it had been in scandals for years, producing multiple investigations with some still ongoing.[7] He highlighted that the bank's shares had hit an all-time low of $2.44 that week against $1.29 trillion in assets under management as of 2022, and warned that if Credit Suisse fell it "will be catastrophic to [their] systems."[7]

## February 2023 commentary

In the February 22, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Just Human contributed three items.[8] On the [[january-6-capitol-security-footage-release|January 6 Capitol security footage release]], the writer stressed that [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] appeared to have access to rather than possession of the footage — an important distinction because airing it "may require approval from the Capitol Police Board" — and noted that Capitol Police Chief [[thomas-manger|Thomas Manger]] and House Sergeant at Arms [[william-mcfarland|William McFarland]] learned of the arrangement only from the news.[8] On the [[2023-defense-department-email-server-exposure|Defense Department email server exposure]], Just Human wrote that the lapse "could be the result of human error or ... not," raising the possibility of an attempt to compromise [[united-states-special-operations-command|USSOCOM]]'s civilian network or a covert transfer of data.[8] On the [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]] subpoena, the writer catalogued the revival of [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-related legal activity — unsealings, emails, suits against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] and [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]], a [[ghislaine-maxwell|Maxwell]] appeal — concluding simply: "Excellent."[8]

## SCIF analysis, August 2023

On an episode of the [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]] preceding the August 16, 2023 news brief, Just Human laid out what [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] called "the general absurdity" of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] potentially being permitted to discuss his legal defense in a [[sensitive-compartmented-information-facility|SCIF]] when [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s case turns on the claim that documents at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] were held in a facility certified only for his presidential term.[9]

## Commentary on the Pence document search (2023)

In the February 3, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Just Human addressed the anticipated [[fbi|FBI]] search of [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]]'s Indiana home for classified material, comparing it to the searches of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s office and homes.[10] He highlighted the argument advanced by Pence's lawyers that the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] had "bypassed the standard procedures" by taking the documents directly from Pence rather than routing them through the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]], adding that there "might be something to that."[10]

## Commentary, January 2023

In the January 20, 2023 Badlands News Brief, Just Human argued that although President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Delaware home kept no visitor log, the [[us-secret-service|Secret Service]] ran checkpoints and screened visitors and therefore held records, and read the anonymously sourced Fox News report as a signal that House Republicans would receive them on request.[11] On the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s inconclusive leak report he advanced the view that the *Dobbs* draft had been leaked deliberately by "patriots" or "white hats" so that a potentially violent left-wing response could be assessed and neutralised ahead of the opinion's release in June 2022.[11]

## Commentary in "The Wheels of Justice Roll On" (February 2023)

Just Human supplied three "Our Take" segments to the February 27, 2023 Badlands News Brief.[12] On the arrest of thirteen senior [[ms-13|MS-13]] leaders, he identified [[joint-task-force-vulcan|Joint Task Force Vulcan]] as "a Trump and Barr-created effort" launched in August 2019 with the mission to "go to war against MS-13," noted that its director [[john-j-durham|John J. Durham]] is a son of Special Counsel [[john-durham|John H. Durham]], and concluded that "the War on MS-13 continues."[12]

Commenting on a USA Today report about classified documents turning up in odd places, he observed that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had posted the article on Truth Social and tied it to "Mad Dog Psycho" Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], remarking that Smith "at a minimum, should give Biden, Obama, and all of the others the same treatment!" — which Just Human read as "foreshadowing."[12]

On the civil forfeiture complaint against six luxury properties owned by [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], he described the oligarch as a long-time donor to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], a friend of the Clintons and a key figure in the Skolkovo and [[uranium-one]] scandals whose $90 million yacht had already been seized, concluding that "'Drain the Swamp' is real."[12]

## Color revolution thesis on Israel (March 2023)

In the March 28, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] credited Just Human with positing that the mass protests in Israel against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s [[israeli-judicial-overhaul|judicial overhaul]] were another [[color-revolution|color revolution]], comparable to the 2014 Ukraine coup, the January 6 "FEDsurrection," and the 2023 "insurrection" in Brazil.[13]

## Commentary on the Russia sanctions campaign

In March 2023, Just Human examined the United States effort against smuggling and money laundering networks supporting the Russian regime, singling out [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]], [[andrei-derkach|Andrei Derkach]] and [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]] as "members of the Swamp, the international Swamp."[14] He noted that Vekselberg was "a Clinton buddy and Foundation donor" and that Deripaska was "a KEY figure in Spygate."[14] Quoting the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] fact sheet describing the actions as a response to "Russia's unprovoked, full-scale invasion of Ukraine" and a use of "all tools at [their] disposal," he asked whether the [[us-sanctions-campaign-against-russian-oligarch-networks|campaign]] was aimed at Russia and its enablers or at the Swamp in Russia, Europe and the United States.[14]

## Framing of the 2023 Trump indictment

In the June 8, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] credited Just Human with the argument that a further indictment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would provide narrative shielding to the [[justice-department|DOJ]] for future indictments of former heads of state, while establishing legal and psychological templates for such prosecutions.[15]
