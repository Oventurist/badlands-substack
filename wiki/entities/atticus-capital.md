# Atticus Capital

**Atticus Capital** is a hedge fund co-founded by [[timothy-barakett|Timothy Barakett]] and [[nathaniel-rothschild|Nathaniel Rothschild]], cited in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the vehicle linking Harvard's endowment leadership to Rothschild dynastic finance.[1]

The article describes Barakett's partnership with Rothschild at Atticus as having "positioned him as a financial control agent who would later chair the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]]," offering the sequence as evidence of intergenerational elite continuity inside the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
