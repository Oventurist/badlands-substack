# Carl Icahn

Carl Icahn is an American billionaire investor described in Badlands coverage as a "legendary investor" and a long-standing ally of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Atlantic City and the Trump Plaza

Trump entered the hotel and casino business in Atlantic City in the early 1980s and remained in it for many years, but the [[2008-financial-crisis|2008 financial crisis]] caused a sudden decline in the sector and within five years he was looking to exit the casino space altogether.[1] In 2013 Trump sought to sell the Trump Plaza Hotel and Casino, but the sale was blocked by Icahn, who was the senior lender on the property's mortgage.[1] The Plaza closed its doors in 2014; Icahn bought the deed to the land in 2018 and had the property demolished in 2021.[1]

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observed that Icahn both financed the property and later acquired its deed, and argued that one could read this as Icahn bailing Trump out during a period of financial difficulty, consistent with his reputation as a Trump ally over the years.[1]

## Advisory role in the first Trump administration

After Trump won the 2016 presidential election, Icahn became a special advisor to the President on regulatory matters.[1] He stepped down from that role at around the same time as Steve Bannon, having been criticised by Democrats for exercising too much influence over how regulation affected his own investments.[1]

## Disney takeover attempt

Carlson noted that Icahn and fellow billionaire investor [[nelson-peltz|Nelson Peltz]] had both recently been in the news for unsuccessfully attempting to take control of [[disney|Disney]], which he characterised as a "money-bleeding and woke corporation."[1] In Carlson's framing, figures such as Icahn and Peltz illustrate how Trump's wealthy allies must publicly downplay their support in order to operate within a [[deep-state|Deep State]]-controlled financial system.[1]
