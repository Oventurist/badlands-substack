# El Paso County GOP

The El Paso County GOP is the Republican Party organization of El Paso County, Colorado, described by Badlands Media as the largest county party in the state and the only county in the nation home to five military commands.[1]

Its vice chairman, [[todd-watkins|Todd Watkins]] — a veteran and retired US Border Patrol Chief — sent a letter on August 21, 2023 to the [[colorado-republican-party|Colorado Republican Party]] urging assistance in contacting legislators about the treatment of Americans arrested or charged in connection with the January 6, 2021 incident at the US Capitol.[1] More than 200 Coloradans, many of them Republican officials, signed on in support.[1] The letter drew a dismissive public rebuttal from Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]], who accused Republican leaders of "misdirecting the energy and resources of Republican activists."[1]
