# Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine

The Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine (PFLP) is a Palestinian militant organization designated as a foreign terrorist organization by both the European Union and the United States.[1]

## Alleged NGO linkages

The PFLP featured in a September 2025 [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] report on the grantmaking of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[1] That report noted that Open Society had funneled more than $2.3 million into [[al-haq|Al-Haq]], a West Bank nongovernmental organization long accused of ties to the PFLP.[1] The report used this relationship to argue that funding risks associated with the foundation extended beyond American domestic activism into internationally designated terrorist networks.[1]
