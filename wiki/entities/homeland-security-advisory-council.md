# Homeland Security Advisory Council

The Homeland Security Advisory Council (HSAC) is an advisory body within the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] tasked with providing real-time advice to the Secretary of Homeland Security.[1] Under Secretary [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]], the council was reconstituted in March 2022, when DHS appointed 33 new members ahead of the council's first meeting of his tenure.[1]

## Bookbinder appointment and the Colorado case

Among those appointed was [[noah-bookbinder|Noah Bookbinder]], president and CEO of [[citizens-for-responsibility-and-ethics-in-washington|Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington]], the organization that brought the Colorado ballot-eligibility suit against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Reporting highlighted by Badlands Media presented the council seat as a "direct line" between the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and the litigation that produced the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]]'s 4–3 decision removing Trump from the state's 2024 Republican primary ballot.[1] Badlands commentary treated the overlap as evidence that the case was an exercise in politically directed lawfare rather than neutral adjudication.[1]
