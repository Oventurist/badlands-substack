# Vladimir Padrino

Vladimir Padrino is the Defense Minister of [[venezuela|Venezuela]], serving under President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

## 2025 anti-drug military buildup

In September 2025 Padrino clarified the Venezuelan military's role in a social media video accompanying a sweeping military buildup along the country's coasts, framed as a sovereign counter-narcotics campaign rather than a response to United States pressure.[1] "No one is going to come and do the work for us. No one is going to step on this land and do what we're supposed to do," he said.[1]

The deployments came amid Washington's Caribbean operations, including the movement of ten additional fighter jets to Puerto Rico and a strike on a Venezuelan vessel said to be carrying drugs.[1]
