# Alliance for Global Justice

The Alliance for Global Justice (AFGJ) is an Arizona-based nonprofit organization that acts as a fiscal sponsor for activist groups lacking their own tax-exempt status.[1] It came to attention in December 2025 through a federal indictment involving [[black-lives-matter-okc|Black Lives Matter OKC]], a group AFGJ sponsored.[1]

## Fiscal sponsorship role

Because BLMOKC was not itself a registered tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, it accepted charitable donations through its affiliation with AFGJ.[1] As fiscal sponsor, AFGJ required BLMOKC to use funds only in ways permitted under Section 501(c)(3), to account fully upon request for the disbursement of all funds received, and to refrain from purchasing real estate without AFGJ's consent.[1]

## Federal case

A federal grand jury indictment unsealed by U.S. Attorney Robert J. Troester charged [[tashella-dickerson|Tashella Sheri Amore Dickerson]], BLMOKC's executive director, with wire fraud and money laundering, alleging she embezzled funds from the organization's accounts for personal benefit between June 2020 and at least October 2025.[1] The arrangement with AFGJ formed the legal backdrop for the charged conduct, since the donations at issue flowed through the sponsorship relationship.[1]
