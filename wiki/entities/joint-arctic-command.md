# Joint Arctic Command

The **Joint Arctic Command** is the Danish military command responsible for defense and surveillance operations in and around [[greenland|Greenland]] and the Faroe Islands.[1] It became a point of reference in January 2026 during the [[greenland-tariff-dispute-2026|Greenland tariff dispute]], when a Danish general speaking for the command stated that there were no immediate Chinese or Russian naval threats near Greenland waters.[1]

The statement was significant because it undercut one of the stated security rationales for aggressive American action on the island, while simultaneously reaffirming that routine [[nato|NATO]] collaboration between [[denmark|Denmark]] and the United States continued despite the political rupture over tariffs and sovereignty.[1] The command's posture illustrated the split between the diplomatic confrontation playing out in public and the operational military relationship that persisted underneath it.[1]
