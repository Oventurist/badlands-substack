# Joint Arctic Command

The Joint Arctic Command is the [[denmark|Danish]] military command responsible for operations in and around [[greenland]] and the Faroe Islands. It appears in the January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief as the source of an official assessment issued during the American–European confrontation over Greenland's status.

According to Reuters reporting summarised in the Brief, a Danish general speaking for the Joint Arctic Command stated on January 16, 2026 that there were no Chinese or Russian ships operating near Greenland and therefore no immediate naval threat in Greenland waters. The command reaffirmed ongoing [[nato|NATO]] collaboration with the United States. The Brief presents this statement as evidence that practical military cooperation persisted even as broader strategic disagreements between Washington and Copenhagen — including [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s tariffs on eight European states — continued to escalate.
