# Nicol Suarez

**Nicol Suarez** is a 30-year-old Colombian national and illegal alien who appeared in New York City criminal court in late July 2025 on child rape charges.[1] Suarez, described in reporting as biologically male but presenting as a woman, was charged with raping a 14-year-old boy in February in the bathroom of a bodega near Thomas Jefferson Park in East Harlem.[1]

## Case

According to *Breitbart News*, Suarez allegedly stalked the boy inside the bodega and followed him into the bathroom; after the alleged assault the boy ran to tell nearby witnesses, and police were called.[1] At the late-July hearing the judge set a pre-trial hearing for September, after which Suarez was ushered into a holding cell and returned to Rikers Island, where he remained held on a quarter-of-a-million-dollar bond.[1]

## Context

The case was carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, alongside an announcement by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] that it had arrested 214 migrants charged with or convicted of sex crimes against minors in the Houston area over six months.[1] Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed New York's sanctuary-city status and immigration-related legal challenges — which she attributed to coordination by the [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] — as enabling such cases.[1]
