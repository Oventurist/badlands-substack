# Nursultan Nazarbayev

Nursultan A. Nazarbayev was the authoritarian president of [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] and, in the Badlands Media account of the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]], the counterparty to the first transaction in the chain.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

In September 2005, [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and the Canadian mining financier [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]] flew aboard Giustra's private jet to Almaty and dined with Nazarbayev.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Citing the *New York Times*, the article states that Clinton "handed the Kazakh president a propaganda coup" by expressing support for Nazarbayev's bid to head an international elections-monitoring group, thereby undercutting American foreign policy and the criticism of Kazakhstan's poor human rights record voiced by others including his wife, then a senator.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Within days of the meeting, Giustra's company [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy Ltd.]] signed a preliminary agreement granting it stakes in three uranium mines controlled by the state-run agency [[kazatomprom|Kazatomprom]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Badlands frames this as Nazarbayev agreeing to sell portions of state-owned uranium mines "for a kickback," a transaction that transformed Giustra's small firm into a major uranium industry player and set the stage for the eventual Russian acquisition.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
