# Jake Sullivan

Jake Sullivan is an American foreign policy official identified in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as one of the architects of the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Nuclear Deal]], operating "at the policy level."

The article associates him with a cluster of establishment affiliations: the so-called 7th Floor Group at the [[state-department]], the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace]], the [[trilateral-commission]], and a Rhodes Scholarship. It describes him as having been the top cabinet official for [[joe-biden]] — serving as National Security Advisor — and as a significant behind-the-scenes figure in [[hillary-clinton]]'s presidential campaign.

Sullivan worked under [[tom-donilon]], who was "technically Jake Sullivan's boss at the time of the Iran Deal." Alongside [[william-burns]], the article characterises the pair as "two certified swamp creatures in high places," presenting them as recurring characters in what it calls the geopolitical play driving Western policy toward [[iran]].
