# Josh Rogin

Josh Rogin is a Washington Post columnist who covers foreign policy and national security.[1]

## Clash with Bill Maher

In April 2025 Rogin appeared on *Real Time* and accused host [[bill-maher|Bill Maher]] of having "fallen into the trap" by accepting a meeting with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the [[white-house|White House]] the previous week.[1] Maher, a longtime Trump critic who has nonetheless distinguished himself on the late-night circuit by criticizing progressive excess, described Trump as "gracious and measured" during the visit while acknowledging that his warm words would not sit well with progressives.[1]

Rogin argued the dinner had been a "PR stunt" and told Maher, "I think you've fallen into the trap, and I think I represent 99% of the internet when I say this: you've played the game of proximity is principle. I'm not questioning your motivation, I'm questioning Trump's, OK?"[1] He stressed that he and the audience accepted Maher had met Trump in good faith while questioning the president's motives — a framing that struck a nerve with Maher, who replied, "You don't have to patronize me, dude. I don't know you. I've never met you."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentary read the segment not as a defense of establishment media but as capitulation to it: a "well thought-out, detailed, subtly subversive capitulation" in which nothing Maher learned at the dinner was new to him, though it was new to his viewers.[1] The conclusion offered was that in the "Mind War," retreat is defeat.[1]
