# Mexican Drug Cartel

The Mexican drug cartel appears in "A New Hollywood" both as the antagonist of Paramount+'s [[lioness|Lioness]] and as a real-world target [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] expects [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to act against.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

In the second season of *Lioness*, a [[cia|CIA]] military crew rescues an American politician — and, in a plot turn Carlson flags as a spoiler, trafficked children — from a Mexican drug kingpin.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) The series shows how ruthless the cartel is and the means it uses to stay in control, "the carrot or the stick": politicians and government officials are offered large sums of money to protect the cartel, and if they turn down the deal, their whole family will be killed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Carlson draws the lesson that corruption "is not as simple as corrupt people taking money to do corrupt things."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

On the strength of this programming, and his broader thesis that Hollywood is now preparing audiences for Trump's agenda, Carlson states plainly: "I believe Trump will take down the Mexican drug cartel."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
