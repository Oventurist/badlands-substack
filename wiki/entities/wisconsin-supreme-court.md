# Wisconsin Supreme Court

The Wisconsin Supreme Court is the highest court in the state of Wisconsin, exercising among other powers supervisory and disciplinary authority over the state's judges.[1]

## Suspension of Judge Hannah Dugan

On Tuesday, April 29, 2025, the court announced that it was in the public interest to relieve Milwaukee County Circuit Judge [[hannah-dugan|Hannah Dugan]] of her duties while she faced two federal charges.[1] Dugan had been taken into custody by the [[fbi|FBI]] the previous Friday morning at the county courthouse on charges of concealing an individual to prevent his discovery and arrest, and of obstructing or impeding a proceeding.[1]

According to an FBI affidavit filed in court, Dugan escorted the migrant [[eduardo-flores-ruiz|Eduardo Flores-Ruiz]] and his lawyer out of her courtroom through the jury door on April 18 in order to help him avoid arrest by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] authorities.[1] Flores-Ruiz, a Mexican national, had been charged with battery for allegedly punching someone thirty times in the face after the person complained about his loud music.[1] The temporary suspension was reported in the independent-media ecosystem as a humiliating blow to the judge and as a marker of the friction between state courts and federal immigration enforcement.[1]
