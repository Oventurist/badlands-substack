# Center for Renewing America

The Center for Renewing America is a Trump-aligned policy organization that has been among the leading critics of continued federal funding for the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1]

Together with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], the center has described the NED as a "partisan political weapon" or outright "scam," alleging that it meddles in foreign affairs and underwrites anti-Trump agendas.[1] Its opposition sits on one side of an intra-Republican divide over the fiscal year 2026 appropriations bill, in which House Republicans proposed $315 million for the endowment despite President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s request that it receive nothing.[1]
