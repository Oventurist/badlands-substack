# Liz Crokin

**Liz Crokin** is an award-winning author and journalist known for reporting on alleged elite child sex trafficking and Hollywood abuse networks.[1]

## Diddy–Epstein claims, 2024

In early March 2024, Crokin shared what Evie Magazine described as a bombshell: the suggestion that the music mogul [[sean-combs|P. Diddy]] may have been part of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s sex trafficking ring, and that the singer Britney Spears may have been one of their victims.[1]

Evie Magazine argued that such theories did not seem far-fetched given that Epstein's circle was full of rich and powerful people, that a crime syndicate within Hollywood was not hard to believe in, and that its existence would explain why so many abuse cases go unreported.[1] The claims were published alongside a survey of the then-current allegations against Combs.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] situated Crokin's claims within a broader argument that the music industry hosts a large-scale mind control and entrapment operation, invoking [[kanye-west|Kanye West]]'s exposure of his handler [[harley-pasternak|Harley Pasternak]] as a precedent that had red-pilled millions about MKUltra-style techniques in celebrity industries.[1]
