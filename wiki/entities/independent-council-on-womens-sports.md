# Independent Council on Women's Sports

The **Independent Council on Women's Sports** (ICONS) is an athlete activist group that campaigns against the inclusion of transgender women in female athletic categories.[1]

## Role in the 2023 Alberta powerlifting protest

In March 2023 ICONS published video of [[avi-silverberg|Avi Silverberg]], the head coach of Team Canada Powerlifting, entering the women's division of the Heroes Classic tournament in Lethbridge, Alberta, after declaring that he identified as a female.[1] The footage showed Silverberg approaching the platform fully bearded and in a men's singlet before bench-pressing nearly 370 pounds, surpassing the Alberta women's record of 275 pounds held by transgender lifter [[anne-andres|Anne Andres]] by almost 100 pounds.[1]

ICONS also supplied the competitive statistics that framed the story, noting that Andres had won eight of nine competitions entered in the women's category over the previous four years.[1] The organization's distribution of the video drove mainstream coverage of the incident, including by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]], and made the group a central actor in the wider argument over [[transgender-athletes-in-womens-sports|transgender athletes in women's sports]].[1]
