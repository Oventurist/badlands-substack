# Tristan Harris

Tristan Harris is a technology critic and co-founder of the [[center-for-humane-technology|Center for Humane Technology]], best known in the Badlands corpus as co-presenter of *The AI Dilemma*.[1]

In March 2023, Harris and [[aza-raskin|Aza Raskin]] delivered *The AI Dilemma* to a private gathering of leading technologists and decision-makers in San Francisco on March 9, 2023, before the launch of GPT-4. The presentation argued that existing [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] capabilities already pose catastrophic risks to a functional society, that AI companies are caught in a race to deploy as quickly as possible without adequate safety measures, and that institutions must be upgraded for a post-AI world.[1] The published video urged viewers to call their political representatives to advocate for hearings on AI risk and for adequate guardrails, and had been watched by more than 2.7 million people.[1]

Harris and Raskin concluded the presentation by urging AI researchers to pause, slow the public release of new technologies, and consider the future of humanity before proceeding. Badlands' treatment describes this as a nice thought but incredibly naïve, since the remedy requires a complete rewrite of human nature.[1] Harris and Raskin are also cited for the claim that AI will soon be capable of dream mapping and dream interpretation through rapidly improving analysis of functional MRI data, allowing a machine to read brain activity and render a visual or written representation of dreams — a capability associated with [[reality-collapse|reality collapse]].[1]

Harris is listed among the signatories of the [[ai-pause-open-letter|open letter of March 22, 2023]] calling on all AI labs to pause for at least six months the training of systems more powerful than GPT-4, alongside Raskin, [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]], and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]
