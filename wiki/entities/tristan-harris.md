# Tristan Harris

Tristan Harris is an American technology critic and co-founder of the [[center-for-humane-technology|Center for Humane Technology]]. He is best known in the Badlands Media corpus for his role, alongside [[aza-raskin|Aza Raskin]], in producing and delivering [[the-ai-dilemma|The AI Dilemma]], a presentation released in March 2023 warning that existing artificial intelligence capabilities already pose catastrophic risks to a functional society.

## The AI Dilemma presentation

According to the July 2023 Badlands Media article "[[artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason]]" by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], Harris and Raskin delivered their presentation at a private gathering in San Francisco on March 9, 2023, before an audience of leading technologists and decision-makers, and before the public launch of GPT-4. The recorded version had been viewed more than 2.7 million times by the time of the article's publication.

The article characterizes the objective of the presentation as two-fold: raising the collective understanding of AI researchers and the general viewing public, and framing the public conversation "in a societally constructive way." Harris and Raskin conclude by urging AI researchers to pause, to slow the public release of new systems, and to consider the future of humanity before proceeding. The source describes this appeal as "a nice thought, even though it's incredibly naïve," on the grounds that the proposed solution "requires a complete rewrite of human nature."

Harris is also credited in the article, with Raskin, for the claim that AI will soon be capable of dream mapping and dream interpretation through rapidly improving analysis of functional MRI (fMRI) data — that is, reading brain activity and rendering a visual or written representation of a subject's dreams.

## Signatory to the pause letter

Harris was among the signatories of the [[pause-giant-ai-experiments-open-letter|open letter of March 22, 2023]] calling on all AI labs to immediately pause for at least six months the training of AI systems more powerful than GPT-4. Other named signatories cited in the article include Raskin, [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]], and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].

## Treatment in the corpus

The Badlands treatment of Harris is sympathetic to his technical warnings but skeptical of the political frame around them. The article accepts the substance of the risks he describes — synthetic media, [[reality-collapse|reality collapse]], the collapse of verifiable evidence — while arguing that "heightened hysteria is very useful to the current global corporate communist takeover," and that calls for restraint from the commercial sector coexist comfortably with unrestrained development by governments, research firms, and defense contractors.
