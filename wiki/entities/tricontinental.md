# Tricontinental

Tricontinental Ltd. is a Massachusetts 501(c)(3) organization founded by Marxist activist [[vijay-prashad|Vijay Prashad]].[1]

## Role in the Singham network

Tricontinental was named as one of six key nonprofits receiving funds in the alleged [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] financing structure under federal grand jury investigation in 2026, with $16,760,000 attributed to it.[1] The wider network described by prosecutors and prior [[fox-news|Fox News]] reporting also encompassed [[peoples-forum|People's Forum Inc.]], [[codepink|CodePink]], Justice and Education Fund Inc., People's Support Foundation Ltd., and Breakthrough BT Media Inc.[1]
