# Tricontinental

Tricontinental Ltd. is a Massachusetts-registered 501(c)(3) research organization founded by the Marxist activist and historian [[vijay-prashad|Vijay Prashad]].

## Funding from the Singham network

According to [[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]], Tricontinental received $16,760,000 as one of six key nonprofits in the funding architecture attributed to [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]. The money is described as originating from Singham's Shanghai base and reaching recipients through three primary channels — two now-defunct shell companies and the donor-advised philanthropy fund of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]]. Along with [[peoples-forum|People's Forum Inc.]], [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]] and others, Tricontinental is reported to be within the scope of subpoenas issued by the federal grand jury in Manhattan operating under U.S. Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] and authorized by Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].
