# Israel Nature and Parks Authority

The Israel Nature and Parks Authority is the Israeli government body responsible for protected areas and wildlife management. As reported in the Badlands Brief, the authority objected to the reclassification of the Nile crocodile carried out by Environmental Protection Minister [[idit-silman|Idit Silman]], which was intended to enable the keeping of the animals by security agencies at prison facilities. The Environmental Protection Ministry's own legal adviser joined the objection, arguing that the proposal — advanced by National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]] and modeled on Florida's [[alligator-alcatraz|"Alligator Alcatraz"]] — lacked sufficient legal and professional justification. The reclassification proceeded despite those objections, though reporting indicated it enabled consideration of a pilot program rather than any actual deployment by the [[israel-prison-service|Israel Prison Service]].
