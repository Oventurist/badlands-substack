# Louis Jolyon West

Dr. Louis Jolyon West was an American psychiatrist who, in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI," is identified as the physician who treated [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]] for anxiety following Ruby's killing of [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]].[1] The article reports that West is alleged by some theorists to have been a [[cia|CIA]] [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] doctor.[1]

## Significance in the patsy thesis

West's relevance to the article is structural rather than biographical. His appearance at Ruby's bedside supplies the link that allows author [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] to connect two of the principal figures of the [[john-f-kennedy|Kennedy]] assassination story to the same covert program: Oswald through his posting at the Atsugi Naval Air Facility, described in the article as a hub of CIA psychedelic-drug research, and Ruby through West's psychiatric care.[1] Carlson frames this convergence as a question — "Coincidence?" — rather than a proven finding, noting that both men "were said to be connected" to MK ULTRA.[1]

The article characterises MK ULTRA itself as the CIA's alleged mind-control system, employing LSD along with physical and psychological torture to break subjects' minds and reprogram them as intelligence assets.[1] Within that framework, a treating psychiatrist with alleged program ties is presented as the mechanism by which an ordinary citizen could be turned into a controlled participant in a political killing.[1] The article offers no documentary evidence for West's alleged program role and attributes the claim to theorists rather than to records.[1]
