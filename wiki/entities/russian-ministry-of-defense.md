# Russian Ministry of Defence

The Russian Ministry of Defence is the federal executive body responsible for the armed forces of the Russian Federation, and during the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]] it became the target of sustained and highly public criticism from [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] and his [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] over ammunition supply and the conduct of the [[battle-of-bakhmut|Battle of Bakhmut]].[1]

## The Prigozhin feud as kayfabe

Writing in May 2023, Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] reported that many of the more plugged-in and openly Russia-aligned citizen journalists regarded the ongoing feud between Wagner and the defence ministry as obvious [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] — staged conflict — reasoning that Prigozhin "would be in a gulag if his continued (and highly publicized) tirades were not directly approved by the [[kremlin|Kremlin]]."[1] On that reading the ministry's public disputes with Wagner served a narrative rather than an operational function.[1]

## Maneuver defense and expected counter-strike

The same commentary characterized Russian military practice as resting on "maneuver defense," described as highly advanced territorial counter-striking, and argued that the ministry and the wider Russian force posture were "winding up" for an emphatic counter-assault with public backing after Wagner pressure.[1] Contemporaneous headlines cited in support included Russian strikes on a U.S.-supplied Patriot system in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], efforts to overwhelm Ukrainian anti-air defenses, and Moscow's consideration of severing ties with [[poland|Poland]] as U.S.-supplied HIMARS were deployed near Kaliningrad.[1] Burning Bright argued that an aggressive Russian counter-assault, though widely feared, would be the best available outcome for Europe because it would foreclose the protracted "New Forever War" sought by the Western [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]], and that by crushing what remained of the [[azov-battalion|Azov Battalion]] in Bakhmut [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] could end the conflict.[1]
