# Pacific Legal Foundation

The Pacific Legal Foundation (PLF) is a public-interest law firm known for litigating property-rights cases, including challenges to government property seizures.[1]

## Pung v. Isabella County

PLF, together with attorney [[phil-ellison|Phil Ellison]], represents the Pung family in [[pung-v-isabella-county|Pung v. Isabella County]], a Michigan tax-foreclosure dispute taken up by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] and expected to be decided in 2026.[1] The case asks whether local governments must compensate homeowners at fair market value, and whether governments may seize properties worth far more than the underlying tax debt.[1] PLF Senior Attorney [[christina-martin|Christina Martin]] gave an exclusive interview on the case to The Center Square.[1]
