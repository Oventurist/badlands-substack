# Palestinian Center for Policy and Survey Research

The **Palestinian Center for Policy and Survey Research (PCPSR)** is a Palestinian research institution known for its regular opinion polling of Palestinians in Gaza and the West Bank.[1]

## March 2024 Gaza poll

A March 2024 PCPSR opinion poll found that support for [[hamas|Hamas]] among Gazans topped 50%, a 14-point rise since December 2023.[1] The figure was cited by Audrey Kurth Cronin, director of the Carnegie Mellon Institute for Strategy and Technology, in a Foreign Affairs op-ed arguing that Israel's military operation in Gaza had been a "strategic disaster" because it had increased rather than eroded popular backing for the movement.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the same numbers to argue the opposite conclusion — that if the intent of the campaign was to inflate Hamas support and thereby justify a prolonged war under [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], the results marked success rather than failure.[1]
