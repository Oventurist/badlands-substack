# Don Bacon

**Don Bacon** is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Nebraska.[1]

## NSA deputy director nomination

On Friday, December 12, 2025, Bacon publicly disclosed that the [[white-house|White House]] was withdrawing the nomination of [[joe-francescon|Joe Francescon]] as deputy director of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]], a withdrawal that came just as the agency's acting deputy director was set to retire at the end of the month.[1] The White House did not comment on the decision, which followed [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]]'s revelation that Francescon had donated to the 2023 campaign of Colorado Democrat [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]].[1]
