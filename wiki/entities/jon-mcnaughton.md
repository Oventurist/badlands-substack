# Jon McNaughton

**Jon McNaughton** is an American painter known for politically themed works celebrating conservative and pro-Trump subjects.[1]

## Appearance in Badlands Media

McNaughton's painting *MAGA Ride* is used as the lead illustration for the Badlands Media feature "Trump Did It" by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], a speculative retrospective written from the vantage point of the year 2050.[1] The choice of image reinforces the article's opening conceit that, in the imagined post-globalist future, "images of Trump and the heroes who helped restore true freedom are gloriously displayed for all to see," with [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s likeness placed "next to famous patriots and regular Americans."[1]

The article describes an iconic image of Trump captioned with the phrase "Without the true rule of law, there can be no freedom," a slogan the narrative attributes to Trump's presidency and to his fictional 2038 address before a new global body for preserving unalienable rights.[1] McNaughton's heroic, allegorical style is characteristic of the visual idiom [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] draws on for such features.[1]
