# McKinsey & Company

McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as a principal architect — not merely an observer — of the labor disruption described in [[a-time-to-strike|A Time to Strike]] (October 11, 2023), where [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] calls it "essentially the world's strategy firm."

## Reach

The article describes McKinsey and its peer large and medium professional services firms as delivering "solutions" to "problems" in every sector of every industry in every geography in the world. It places the firm in automotive and manufacturing, media and entertainment, healthcare, retail, forestry, aerospace and agriculture, noting that its client list includes the top players in every industry, who hire it to solve every conceivable problem. The essay emphasizes that McKinsey is the strategic partner of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and that its influence "touches every aspect of human life."

## The future of work research

The source quotes McKinsey's July 2020 report "The future of work: reskilling and remote working to recover in the next normal": "Even before the current crisis, AI, automation, and other technologies were already creating major shifts in required workforce skills. In 2017, our scenarios suggested that by 2030, up to 375 million workers (14 percent of the global workforce) would have to switch occupational categories." Although the report reads on its surface as a response to Covid-19 and remote work, the author urges readers to look past that framing.

Her interpretive claim is that McKinsey was measuring occupational displacement in 2017 because it was studying it, and was studying it because it knew its own recommendations would bring about those consequences: the world that produces these impacts on the global talent landscape is the world the firm is designing. A follow-up McKinsey publication of February 2021, "The future of work after COVID-19," is cited as revising the figures eight months later. On this reading, the [[future-of-work|future of work]] discussion running inside global corporations for nearly a decade signals a settled conclusion — that there will be fewer jobs than people — and the appropriate moment for organized labor to resist would have been before that infrastructure was laid, in the author's view 2017 at the latest.
