# Soo Kim

Soo Kim is a former CIA analyst and policy practitioner associated with the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] whose name drew attention from Badlands-aligned researchers in September 2024 because she appeared among the accounts followed by [[ryan-wesley-routh|Ryan Wesley Routh]], the suspect in the [[2024-trump-golf-course-assassination-attempt|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]] at his West Palm Beach golf course.[1]

## Attention after the Routh follower list

Badlands contributor [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]] produced a video cataloguing Routh's followers; [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the name standing out to him and to other researchers was the last on the list, Soo Kim.[1] Her posts had been protected by the time of writing, but her public biography remained visible and listed affiliations including former [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] service, the RAND Corporation, and Yale — which the author characterized as a "NeoCon haven."[1]

The bio also listed acronym-based affiliations that the author unpacked: LMI, a consulting firm describing itself as "dedicated to improving the management of government," whose pinned post described running a training program across the Department of Defense; and the Philip Merrill Center for Strategic Studies at the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies, which describes its mission as exploring "politics and the use of force in international relations."[1] Badlands treated these affiliations as evidence of a Beltway consultancy and think-tank ecosystem that extracts taxpayer funding while imposing its preferences on government policy.[1]

Note: the article's treatment of Kim is speculative commentary derived from a follower list and a public biography; it does not allege or establish any operational connection to the events in Florida.[1]
