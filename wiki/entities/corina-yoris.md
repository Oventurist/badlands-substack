# Corina Yoris

Dr. Corina Yoris is a Venezuelan academic who was designated as the substitute presidential candidate of the opposition after [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] was barred from the ballot ahead of Venezuela's July 2024 election.[1]

## Blocked candidacy

The government of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] blocked the Democratic Unitary Platform (PUD) opposition coalition from registering its candidate before a midnight deadline in the early hours of March 26, 2024, and Yoris — Machado's chosen successor — was also blocked.[1] The obstruction was executed through Venezuela's National Electoral Council.[1]

## International response

At a press briefing aboard Air Force One on the afternoon of March 26, 2024, [[white-house|White House]] Press Secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] said the administration was "deeply concerned by the National Electoral Council's decision to prevent the registration of … the democratic opposition party's candidate, Dr. Corina Yoris," and stated that the United States was working with other members of the international community to ensure Venezuelans could participate in inclusive and competitive elections.[1] She suggested Venezuela could lose sanctions relief if the Maduro regime did not provide free and fair elections.[1]

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted what she called palpable hypocrisy in the administration's posture, noting that officials condemning the exclusion of Venezuela's leading opposition candidate were simultaneously engaged in legal actions against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], the leading candidate in the United States.[1]
