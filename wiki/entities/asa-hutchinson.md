# Asa Hutchinson

Asa Hutchinson is a former Governor of Arkansas who qualified for a place on the stage at the [[first-gop-presidential-debate-2023|first Republican presidential debate]] of the 2024 primary cycle, held in Milwaukee on the night of Wednesday, August 23, 2023.[1]

## 2024 presidential campaign

Hutchinson was one of eight candidates to meet the qualification threshold for the Milwaukee debate, alongside North Dakota Governor [[doug-burgum|Doug Burgum]], former New Jersey Governor [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]], Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], former U.N. Ambassador [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]], entrepreneur [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] and South Carolina Senator [[tim-scott|Tim Scott]].[1] Absent from the event was the primary's early front-runner, former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who declined to participate and instead released a pre-recorded interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].[1]

## Badlands assessment

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] ranked Hutchinson among the losers of the evening, grouping him with Pence and Haley in the bottom tier of performances.[1] She also credited him with "the best gaffe of the evening," when he said: "Abortion, er excuse me, adoption services."[1]
