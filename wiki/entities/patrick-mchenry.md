# Patrick McHenry

Patrick McHenry (R-N.C.) is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives who served as Speaker Pro Tempore during the leadership vacuum that followed the removal of Kevin McCarthy as Speaker in October 2023.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership crisis

Following the ouster of [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], McHenry — a longtime McCarthy ally — assumed the position of Speaker Pro Tempore, a caretaker role with narrowly defined powers that left the chamber effectively unable to conduct legislative business.[1] Amid the GOP infighting that froze the [[us-house-of-representatives|House]], a group of mainstream Republicans and Democrats called for a temporary expansion of McHenry's authority so that the House could vote on funding measures before a permanent Speaker was chosen.[1] The proposal contemplated extending McHenry's expanded powers through January.[1]

[[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] had been expected to support the McHenry empowerment effort ahead of his third speakership ballot, but backtracked hours later after multiple Republicans objected to the plan, and the back-up plan subsequently fell apart.[1]

McHenry's office also served as the formal recipient of executive branch correspondence during the interregnum: Office of Management and Budget Director [[shalanda-young|Shalanda Young]] addressed the Biden administration's $105 billion national security supplemental request to McHenry in his capacity as Speaker Pro Tempore.[1]
