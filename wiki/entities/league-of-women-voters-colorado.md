# League of Women Voters Colorado

The League of Women Voters of Colorado is the state affiliate of the League of Women Voters and was one of three plaintiff organizations in [[naacp-v-useip|*NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.*]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) Alongside the [[naacp|NAACP]]'s CO/MT/WY Area Conference and [[mi-familia-vota|Mi Familia Vota]], it sought to hold [[useip|USEIP]] and named activists liable for voter intimidation under Section 11(b) of the [[voting-rights-act-of-1965|Voting Rights Act of 1965]] and the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act of 1871]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

Defendants noted an inconsistency in the League's position: on November 23, 2016 the organization published a piece titled "The 2016 Presidential Election was Rigged," itself an instance of the [[election-denial|election-questioning]] speech it later characterized as intimidating when practiced by Trump supporters.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) Defendants also observed that all three plaintiff organizations engage in political [[canvassing]] as a matter of routine business, since grassroots organizing requires it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

The court entered judgement against the plaintiffs on partial findings in July 2024 and awarded costs to the defendants.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)
