# Stephen Hawking

Stephen Hawking (1942–2018) was a British theoretical physicist and cosmologist. He appears in the Badlands Media corpus as a late-career witness to the risks of self-improving artificial intelligence.

The July 2023 article "[[artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason]]" quotes Hawking's last televised interview, given to Piers Morgan in 2017:

> "The great danger from artificial intelligence is if we let it self-design for then it can improve itself rapidly and we may lose control."

The article treats the warning as already overtaken by events, responding simply: "We are already there. AI has learned to code and can create – not just artifacts, but other AIs." That capacity underpins the source's rejection of nuclear-arms analogies as inadequate, summarized in the line "Nukes can't make more nukes. But AIs can create more AIs." Hawking's caution is thus presented not as a forecast to be debated but as a threshold already crossed, consistent with the article's broader argument about the [[technological-singularity|singularity]].
