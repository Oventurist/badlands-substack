# National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence

The National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence (NSCAI), also referred to as the "A.I. Commission," is a United States government body charged with advising on the national-security implications of artificial intelligence.[1] In the Badlands Media article "Dark Equation," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted — citing a February 2021 report from The Last American Vagabond — that the Biden administration placed former [[google|Google]] chief executive [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]] at the head of the commission.[1]

The article presented the commission as a key mechanism through which private technology executives obtained direct influence over federal artificial-intelligence policy, alongside reporting that Schmidt was personally funding the salaries of more than two dozen Biden administration employees.[1] For DeLarme, this arrangement illustrated how the debate over regulating [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] was being shaped from within by the same industry figures who stood to benefit from its outcome.[1]
