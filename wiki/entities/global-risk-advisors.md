# Global Risk Advisors

Global Risk Advisors is a private intelligence and security consultancy associated with former [[cia|CIA]] officer [[kevin-chalker|Kevin Chalker]], known in alternative-media accounts for contract work performed on behalf of Qatari clients.[1]

## 2017 Doha presentation

Badlands Media commentary references a 2017 presentation given by Chalker and Global Risk Advisors to Qatari figures in Doha, in which the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] was named as a Muslim Brotherhood media asset.[1] That claim is used by Badlands writers as the evidentiary basis for treating the Times as an intelligence-linked outlet whose reporting should be read as narrative management rather than journalism.[1]

The specific occasion was the paper's January 22, 2025 article arguing that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order designating Mexican cartels as foreign terrorist organizations could damage the U.S. economy, since cartel operations are interwoven with legal sectors such as agriculture and tourism and Mexico is the United States' largest trading partner in goods.[1] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] countered by citing the Military Commissions Act of 2006 and its definition of "unlawful enemy combatant," under which anyone providing material support to such a combatant risks the same designation.[1]
