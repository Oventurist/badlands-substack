# Basij

The Basij is an Iranian volunteer paramilitary militia organized under the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, used for internal security, mobilization and domestic mass organization.

In the March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, the Basij appears through its longtime commander [[gholamreza-soleimani|Gholamreza Soleimani]], whose elimination was announced by [[israel|Israel]] in the same wave of strikes that reportedly killed [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]]. The targeting of the militia's commander alongside the secretary of the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]] is presented by the source as part of a campaign against the upper tier of [[iran|Iran]]'s security leadership.
