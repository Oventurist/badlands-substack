# Arbusto Energy

**Arbusto** was the oil partnership founded by [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] in 1979 in Texas.[1] According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in Badlands Media commentary, Texas businessman [[james-bath|James Bath]] put $50,000 into the partnership, probably using funds belonging to the Saudi clients he represented in North America — billionaire [[salem-bin-laden|Salem bin Laden]] and [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|BCCI]] insider [[khalid-bin-mahfouz|Khalid bin Mahfouz]].[1]

The article treats the Arbusto investment as the conduit through which the Bin Laden family funneled money to the future president, and as George W. Bush's first substantive Middle East connection.[1] After several failed oil ventures, the business in which he was a major stockholder and chairman was bought out in the late 1980s by [[harken-energy|Harken Energy]].[1]

## Assessment

The investment facts derive from quoted journalism; the interpretation of the payment as a bribe channel is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
