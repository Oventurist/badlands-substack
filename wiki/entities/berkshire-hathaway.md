# Berkshire Hathaway

Berkshire Hathaway is the American holding company built by Warren Buffett, with large equity stakes across the U.S. economy.

## Role in the media ownership argument

A Swan Song for the MSM includes Berkshire Hathaway among the institutional investors — with [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[state-street|State Street]] — whose mutual holdings in the six dominant media conglomerates are offered as evidence that [[mainstream-media|centralized media]] competition is illusory. The article situates the representatives of these firms within transnational policy forums such as the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], treating financial concentration and narrative concentration as two faces of the same structure.

## Position in 2010

Erik Carlson's America Has a CIA Run Economy lists Berkshire Hathaway among the five largest American companies by market capitalization in 2010, alongside Exxon, [[apple-inc|Apple]], [[microsoft]], and Walmart. It does not appear in the essay's 2020 or 2025 rankings, illustrating the article's argument that the top of the [[sp-500|S&P 500]] has been progressively taken over by big technology and AI firms.
