# Berkshire Hathaway

Berkshire Hathaway is an American holding company named by Badlands Media among the world's largest institutional investors whose stakes span the mainstream media conglomerates.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

Together with [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|the Vanguard Group]] and [[state-street|State Street]], Berkshire Hathaway is presented as part of the ownership layer that renders the "Big Six" a single [[centralized-media|centralized media]] bloc rather than competing enterprises, with representatives of these firms described as regular fixtures at the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)
