# Berkshire Hathaway

Berkshire Hathaway is the American holding company built by Warren Buffett, with large equity stakes across the U.S. economy.

## Role in the media ownership argument

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] includes Berkshire Hathaway among the institutional investors — with [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[state-street|State Street]] — whose mutual holdings in the six dominant media conglomerates are offered as evidence that [[mainstream-media|centralized media]] competition is illusory. The article situates the representatives of these firms within transnational policy forums such as the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], treating financial concentration and narrative concentration as two faces of the same structure.
