# Rafael Ángel Calderón Fournier

Rafael Ángel Calderón Fournier is a former president of [[costa-rica|Costa Rica]] mentioned in Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s "Illusions of Democracy" in connection with a pair of symbolic votes by the [[legislative-assembly-of-costa-rica|Legislative Assembly]].[1]

## The traitor title

Citing CulturaCR.net, the article reports that the Assembly approved bill 22,289 declaring [[jose-figueres-ferrer|José Figueres Ferrer]] "Hero of peace," and notes that the measure was passed "in a convenient way just two months after these same deputies eliminated the title of traitor of the Homeland that weighed on former President Rafael Ángel Calderón Fournier."[1] The juxtaposition is presented as context for the political symbolism of the Figueres honour; only deputies José María Villalta of the Frente Amplio and Paola Vega of the PAC voted against the Hero of Peace bill.[1] The article does not elaborate further on Calderón Fournier's record or on the origin of the traitor designation.[1]
