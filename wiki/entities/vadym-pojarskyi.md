# Vadym Pojarskyi

Vadym Pojarskyi (rendered "Vadim Pojarskii" in FBI documentation) was a senior executive of the Ukrainian natural gas company [[burisma|Burisma Holdings]] during the period in which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] served on the company's board.[1] He is named in an FBI FD-1023 confidential human source report released in July 2023 by Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] as the Burisma official who explained the commercial logic of the Biden hire to a trusted FBI informant.[1]

## The FD-1023 allegation

According to the informant's account to the Bureau, recorded in 2020 but describing a meeting held while [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was still vice president, Pojarskyi stated: "They hired Hunter Biden to protect us, through his dad, from all kinds of problems."[1] The informant further reported that Burisma had asked Hunter Biden to help sideline Ukraine's top prosecutor, [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]], who was investigating the firm for corruption and was said to be interfering with Burisma's attempt to acquire a United States company.[1]

The document's release by Grassley on Thursday, July 20, 2023, made Pojarskyi's reported remarks a central exhibit in congressional Republicans' case that the younger Biden's board seat was purchased for its access to the vice president rather than for any energy-sector expertise.[1] Commentators associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the memo as an instance of what they term Disclosure — documentary evidence entering the congressional record — rather than mere scandal-driven Exposure.[1]
