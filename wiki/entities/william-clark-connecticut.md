# William Clark (Connecticut judge)

William Clark is a Connecticut Superior Court judge who in November 2023 ordered a new Democratic mayoral primary in Bridgeport after finding that video evidence of ballot fraud tainted the original contest.[1]

## Bridgeport ruling

Clark's 37-page ruling, issued Wednesday, November 1, 2023, rested on 180 pieces of evidence presented by counsel for mayoral candidate [[john-gomes|John Gomes]], including footage that appeared to show Bridgeport Democratic Town Committee vice chair [[wanda-geter-pataky|Wanda Geter-Pataky]] inserting stacks of ballots into a drop box.[1] Clark wrote that "the videos are shocking to the court and should be shocking to all the parties," and observed that incumbent Mayor [[joe-ganim|Joe Ganim]] had been right to express shock when shown the clips during his testimony.[1]

The decision was widely cited in election-integrity commentary, including by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]
