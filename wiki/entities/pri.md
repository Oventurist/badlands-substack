# Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI)

The Institutional Revolutionary Party, known by its Spanish initials PRI, was the party that ruled Mexico during the events described in the Badlands Media account.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Its secretary general and second-ranking official, [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]], was assassinated in September 1994 in a killing whose "intellectual author" a federal judge found to be [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]], brother of the sitting PRI president Carlos Salinas de Gortari.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) When [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] resigned as special prosecutor over the case, he said his investigation was being blocked by ruling-party power brokers — placing the party apparatus itself inside the cover-up.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article presents the episode as an illustration of the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]]'s capture of Mexican state institutions on behalf of the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
