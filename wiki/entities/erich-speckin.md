# Erich Speckin

Erich Speckin is a forensic document examiner who served as the plaintiffs' handwriting and signature verification expert during the second phase of [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s election challenge trial in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona.[1]

## Testimony in Lake v. Hobbs

On Thursday, May 18, 2023 — day two of the trial in [[lake-v-hobbs|Lake v. Hobbs et al.]] — Speckin testified regarding the speed at which Maricopa County reviewers processed mail-in ballot signature comparisons.[1] In his opinion, the 45,670 signatures approved by the reviewers identified in county data as User 26 and User 9 should be removed from the pool of 321,495 signatures compared in under three seconds, because those "comparisons" were completed so quickly that they could not conceivably have been performed by a human being.[1]

Speckin's testimony was central to the plaintiffs' theory of the case: that if machines rather than human reviewers were effectively conducting [[signature-verification|signature verification]], then verification as required by Arizona statute did not take place at all.[1]
