# Vijay Gandhi

**Vijay "Jay" Gandhi** is a former United States magistrate judge who served in the Central District of California.[1]

## Palisades fire litigation

Gandhi was one of two federal judges who lost their Pacific Palisades homes in the January 2025 firestorm and subsequently sued the [[los-angeles-department-of-water-and-power|Los Angeles Department of Water and Power]], joining hundreds of their neighbors in claiming the utility failed to properly prepare for the wildfire and to respond once it broke out.[1] He filed the suit in late April 2025 along with his family and with U.S. District Judge [[dean-pregerson|Dean Pregerson]], who currently sits on the same court.[1] The action, brought in Los Angeles Superior Court, alleges that the [[palisades-fire|Palisades conflagration]] "was caused by both LADWP's water and power assets, specifically empty reservoirs and energized powerlines."[1]

The complaint relies in part on Los Angeles Times reporting that the Santa Ynez Reservoir in the Palisades sat empty during the firefight, having been closed months earlier for repairs, and argues that LADWP was unprepared despite National Weather Service warnings of critical fire weather.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the involvement of judges as plaintiffs meant the filings had a "baked in legal review," but predicted the litigation would likely settle rather than reach discovery, leaving the workings of public-private-partnership utility governance sealed.[1]
