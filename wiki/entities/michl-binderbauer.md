# Michl Binderbauer

Dr. Michl Binderbauer is the chief executive officer of [[tae-technologies|TAE Technologies]], a US fusion energy company developing beam-driven fusion technology.[1]

Binderbauer is a central figure in TAE's $6 billion all-stock merger with [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]], upon whose completion in mid-2026 he is slated to serve as co-CEO of the combined entity alongside [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]].[1] Announcing the site selection process for a first commercial 50 MWe fusion power plant, Binderbauer highlighted fusion's timely momentum amid growing electricity needs driven by data centers and artificial-intelligence workloads.[1]

## Merger with Trump Media

Under the all-stock merger valued at more than $6 billion announced on December 18, 2025, Binderbauer, chief executive and director of [[tae-technologies|TAE Technologies]], is expected to serve as co-CEO of the combined company alongside [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]], chairman and chief executive of [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]].[2] The merged entity plans to select a site in 2026 and begin construction on a 50-megawatt utility-scale fusion power plant, with 350–500 megawatt facilities envisioned in later phases.[2]
