# House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight

The House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight is a panel of the U.S. House of Representatives Committee on House Administration.[1] In March 2024 it assumed a central role in reexamining the work of the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]].[1]

## Preliminary findings on the January 6 Committee

On Monday, March 11, 2024, the subcommittee released preliminary findings on the January 6 Committee, described by Badlands writers as damning.[1] Key findings included that the committee hired Hollywood producers to assist with its prime-time hearings; that it refused to adopt rules, allowing it to operate without limits; and that [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] served as Vice Chair — a position reserved for the majority under House rules — after being named by Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] as one of her eight majority appointments, rather than as minority ranking member.[1] The subcommittee concluded the minority party was therefore not meaningfully represented on the committee.[1]

The findings also stated that committee leadership failed to turn over evidence used in the prime-time hearings, hid multiple transcribed interviews and other evidence that contradicted its narrative, promoted [[cassidy-hutchinson|Cassidy Hutchinson]]'s sensational testimony despite contradiction by the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] agent driving the presidential SUV, and colluded with [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] on her prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The subcommittee characterized these as preliminary conclusions, with further work to follow.[1]
