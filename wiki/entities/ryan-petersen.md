# Ryan Petersen

Ryan Petersen is the chief executive officer of [[flexport]], a freight forwarding and supply-chain logistics company, and a frequent commentator on global shipping conditions.[1]

## Warning on the 2024 port strike

In September 2024, ahead of the expiry of the contract between the [[international-longshoremens-association]] and the [[united-states-maritime-alliance]], Petersen wrote on X that "the biggest wild card in the presidential election that nobody's talking about" was "the looming port strike that could shut down all East and Gulf Coast ports just 36 days before the election."[1] His warning was cited alongside Goldman Sachs analysis estimating that upwards of $4.9 billion per day in international trade was at risk along the East and Gulf coasts, with congestion likely to revive transport price inflation.[1]
