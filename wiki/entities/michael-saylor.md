# Michael Saylor

Michael Saylor is the founder and chairman of Strategy, formerly MicroStrategy, an aggressive corporate acquirer of [[bitcoin|bitcoin]] and one of the most prominent institutional advocates for the asset.[1]

## March 2025 market commentary

In remarks reported on March 17, 2025, Saylor predicted that bitcoin would rally decisively once investor confidence returned to markets.[1] The asset was down roughly 10 percent on the year and had been consolidating for several weeks as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariffs intensified global economic concerns and kept investors out of risk assets.[1]

"The market is really skittish based upon concerns about tariffs," Saylor said.[1] He pointed to the disposition of the U.S. economy and the interest-rate forward curve, noting that "the interest rates have pulled in 30, 40, 50 basis points. And we're in this macro risk off zone. When that flips, I think Bitcoin will rip forward with a vengeance."[1] He added that "if you're going to put a billion dollar bet on right now, the only thing you would do is put a billion dollar bet on bitcoin as a long term store of value, that is clear."[1] Though laser-focused on bitcoin, Saylor was reported to view it as only one tier of several composing the broader digital economy.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writers used Saylor's remarks as a springboard for a monetary argument: that the "Great Awakening" is about moving from a false reality into the real, that the false is made possible only through incentive structures anathema to human conscience, and that those incentives are rewarded via fiat — concluding, "He who makes the money makes the world. Who makes the money?"[1]


## MicroStrategy Bitcoin Purchases (April 2025)

[[microstrategy|MicroStrategy]] disclosed that it acquired 6,556 bitcoin for $555.8 million in cash between April 14 and April 20, 2025, at an average price near $84,785, while simultaneously selling 1.755 million MSTR shares for net proceeds of $547.7 million — prompting the Badlands quip that "Michael loves his ATM."[2] Analysts at TD Cowen said that if the company stopped buying [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] entirely it would have little to no impact on the cryptocurrency's price.[2]

## Strategy rebrand (February 2025)

On February 5, 2025, Saylor's [[microstrategy|MicroStrategy Incorporated]] (Nasdaq: MSTR) announced it would do business as Strategy, presenting itself as the world's first and largest Bitcoin Treasury Company alongside its business intelligence operations.[3] The new identity used a stylized "B" and an orange primary color to signal the company's Bitcoin position.[3] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the rebrand as consistent with the view that Saylor's vehicle exists to compel the fiat currency class to finance its own obsolescence.[3]
