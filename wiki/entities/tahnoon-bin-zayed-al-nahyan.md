# Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan

Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan is the national security adviser of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] and chairman of two of Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth funds. He is the brother of UAE President [[mohamed-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan]].[1]

## Role in the 2025 US investment pledge

During President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 2025 Gulf tour, Trump referenced Sheikh Tahnoon's visit to Washington weeks earlier, telling Sheikh Mohamed: "Your wonderful brother came to Washington a few weeks ago and he told us about your generous statement as to the 1.4 trillion."[1] The reference was to a UAE pledge to invest $1.4 trillion in the United States over ten years.[1] Trump responded, "And all I can say is thank you very much. We will work very hard to deserve it."[1]
