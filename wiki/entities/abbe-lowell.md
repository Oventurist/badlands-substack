# Abbe Lowell

Abbe Lowell is an American defense attorney who joined [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s legal team as his new counsel ahead of the May 2023 Arkansas child support proceedings.[1] He had already been acting for Hunter Biden earlier that year in correspondence with federal and state authorities over the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]].[3]

## Laptop letters (February 2023)

On February 1, 2023, Lowell sent letters to the Delaware attorney general, the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s National Security Division and the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] urging investigations into what he characterized as attempts by allies of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to "weaponize" the contents of the laptop.[3] The letters constituted the first public acknowledgment by Hunter Biden and his lawyers that the personal data on the machine was in fact his.[3]

## Role in the Roberts child support case

At the hearing held Monday, May 1, 2023 in an Arkansas courtroom, Lowell told the court that the president's son was paying $20,000 a month to the plaintiff, [[lunden-roberts|Lunden Roberts]], mother of Hunter Biden's four-year-old daughter Navy Joan Roberts.[1] The case had been reopened after Hunter Biden sought adjustments to the payment schedule agreed in 2020, and Roberts's lawyers had moved in December to have the child's surname changed to Biden.[1]

Lowell's appearance was read by Badlands Media commentators as part of an escalating legal exposure surrounding the Biden family, with the case described as one strand of the continuous "Hunter news" cycle.[1]

## Badlands characterization

Following the October 2025 indictment of former national security adviser [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] remarked that "Abbe Lowell is in charge of all the psyop cases," positioning Lowell as the recurring defense counsel in politically charged federal prosecutions that Badlands writers read as narrative operations rather than ordinary criminal matters.[2]

## September 2024 plea reversal

On September 5, 2024, Lowell told a federal court in California that his client [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] intended to switch his plea and plead guilty to all nine tax charges brought by special counsel [[david-weiss|David Weiss]], having initially pleaded not guilty.[4] The announcement shocked federal prosecutors, with [[leo-wise|Leo Wise]] saying it was the first the government had heard of it.[4] The reversal came after Lowell's defense team had prepared to argue that Hunter Biden was too high or drunk to pay his taxes, and Judge [[mark-scarsi|Mark Scarsi]] set sentencing for December 16, 2024.[4]
