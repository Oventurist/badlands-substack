# Ben Sasse

Ben Sasse is a former Republican U.S. senator from Nebraska, described in Badlands coverage as a "Never Trumper."[1]

## Donor network reporting

July 2023 reporting by [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]], summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], noted that the Iranian American Political Action Committee run through the [[public-affairs-alliance-of-iranian-americans|Public Affairs Alliance of Iranian Americans]] directed the comparatively small share of its giving that reached Republicans toward groups supporting [[paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]], [[dan-crenshaw|Dan Crenshaw]], and Sasse.[1] The same PAC network included donations from [[morteza-hosseini|Morteza Hosseini]], a major benefactor of Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]].[1]

## University of Florida presidency

Sasse resigned his Senate seat in early 2023 to become president of the [[university-of-florida|University of Florida]], a move Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] supported using the influence of his office, with DeSantis chief of staff [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] serving as Sasse's "sherpa" through the university search process.[2] His departure from Congress opened the Senate seat that Nebraska Governor Jim Pillen filled by appointing former Governor [[pete-ricketts|Pete Ricketts]], who had chaired the Republican Governors Association when it donated $20 million to DeSantis.[2] Sasse quit the university presidency in mid-2024, citing the need to care for his wife, who has epilepsy, and shortly afterward came under investigation for financial issues during his tenure, with DeSantis calling for an inquiry.[2] The sequence is described in detail at [[ben-sasse-university-of-florida-spending-investigation|Ben Sasse University of Florida spending investigation]].[2]
