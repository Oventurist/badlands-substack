# Akhmad Kadyrov

Akhmad Kadyrov was the Chief Mufti of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria who switched sides during the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]], offering his services and his men to Vladimir Putin and the Russian government.[1] His defection split the Chechen forces, with roughly half aligning with Moscow.[1]

Kadyrov was named President of Chechnya in 2003.[1] In March 2004, during a memorial parade, he was assassinated by a bomb planted by the same radical Islamist network that had triggered the war.[1]

In 1999, at the onset of the Second Chechen War, Kadyrov formed the 141st Special Motorized Regiment — the formation later known as the [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]] or "Kadyrovites."[1] Because that unit was protecting him at the time of his death, his son [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]] disbanded it and folded the few men he trusted into Russian law enforcement.[1] Ramzan subsequently tasked [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]] with avenging his father by eliminating [[movladi-baisarov|Movladi Baisarov]], a former member of Akhmad's own security detail believed to have played a role in the assassination.[1]
