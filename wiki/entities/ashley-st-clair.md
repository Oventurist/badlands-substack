# Ashley St. Clair

Ashley St. Clair is a commentator and user of the social media platform X who figured in the December 2023 [[phoenix-airport-migrant-bussing-story|Phoenix airport migrant bussing story]].[1]

## Phoenix airport claims

On Tuesday, December 19, 2023, a post from St. Clair sparked the first major public concern that the government and/or non-governmental organizations were flying migrants from border processing centers onto Delta Air Lines planes at Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport and then across the nation.[1] The claim was contested: X's Community Notes feature responded that "there is absolutely no evidence of the people at the gate being 'migrants.'"[1]

The episode was part of a broader surge in citizen journalist reporting on X that week, later reinforced when [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] of [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]] filmed a bus driver at the Phoenix Sky Harbor drop-off terminal confirming that the passengers he hauled were migrants from the border.[1] The item was aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Child support dispute with Elon Musk

Weeks after announcing that she had given birth to the child of Tesla chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] — reported as his 13th — the conservative influencer accused him of scaling back support payments for their son.[2] In a video published by the Daily Mail on March 31, 2025, St. Clair was seen handing over the keys to her $100,000 Tesla outside her Manhattan apartment, telling a reporter, "I need to make up for the 60 percent cut that Elon made to our son's child support."[2] Asked whether she felt Musk was being "vindictive," she said, "Well, that's his modus operandi, when women speak out," adding, "You can check the stocks, I'm not the only one who is cleaning up after his messes"; she said her last attempt to speak with Musk had been on February 13.[2]

Musk responded the same day to a clip of the interview on X, writing, "I don't know if the child is mine or not, but am not against finding out. No court order is needed."[2] While he did not address whether he had reduced payments, he said he was still supporting the infant financially: "Despite not knowing for sure, I have given Ashley $2.5M and am sending her $500k/year."[2] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] mocked the arrangement in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] brief as a "$2.5m signing bonus and a $500k a year salary for practice squad talent at best," joking that Jerry Jones must have negotiated Musk's child support.[2]
