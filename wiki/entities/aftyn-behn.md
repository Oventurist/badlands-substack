# Aftyn Behn

Aftyn Behn is a Democratic politician who ran in the December 2025 Tennessee U.S. House special election, losing to Republican [[matt-van-epps|Matt Van Epps]] but substantially narrowing the Republican margin in a deep-red district.[1]

## Special election performance

Although Behn lost the seat on Tuesday, December 2, 2025, her showing continued a streak of Democratic overperformances across special elections that year, and national outside groups poured millions of dollars into the race as Republicans grew concerned about a possible upset.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had carried the district by 22 points the previous fall, but voters' dissatisfaction with the cost of living helped Behn cut into that margin.[1] Commentary treated the result as a signal ahead of the midterm elections, while Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] rejected that framing and argued the deeper problem was the integrity of the electoral system itself.[1] The contest is described further at [[2025-tennessee-7th-congressional-district-special-election|the 2025 Tennessee special election]].[1]
