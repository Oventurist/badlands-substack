# Bank for International Settlements

The Bank for International Settlements (BIS) is the international institution that serves as a bank for central banks and a coordinating body for global monetary policy. Badlands Media covered the BIS in February 2023 after its head, [[agustin-carstens|Agustin Carstens]], declared that fiat money had defeated cryptocurrency.[1]

## Carstens' declaration

In an interview with Bloomberg TV, Carstens said of the contest between crypto and state-issued money: "That battle has been won … A technology doesn't make for trusted money," adding that "only the legal, historical infrastructure behind central banks can give great credibility" to money.[1] He anticipated a "strong statement" from the G20 nations calling for stronger regulation of digital assets, and said crypto as a financial activity could exist only "under certain conditions."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that monetary policy is one of the few domains in which "a mere declaration is enough to have an effect on an entire industry," and characterized the BIS as part of a "self-declared and government endorsed international banking cartel" repeating the strategy it used before the creation of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] in 1913.[1] He contended that a long free-banking and tighter-controls period functioned as a decades-long psychological operation to strip public confidence in citizen-based currency systems, contrasting it with the debt-free and interest-free [[colonial-scrip|Colonial Scrip]] used in the American colonies.[1] In his view cryptocurrency represented a return to citizen-produced money, and the central bankers waging war on it were "winning—at least for now."[1]
