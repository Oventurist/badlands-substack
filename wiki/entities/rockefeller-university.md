# Rockefeller University

**Rockefeller University** is a New York biomedical research institution referenced in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" through its former chief investment officer, [[amy-falls|Amy Falls]], who later appears among the figures associated with the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

The article uses that career path to argue that Harvard's endowment governance is interlocked with Rockefeller-affiliated institutions and legacy Wall Street networks, part of a broader claim that the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and related bodies sit at the financial and philanthropic core of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
