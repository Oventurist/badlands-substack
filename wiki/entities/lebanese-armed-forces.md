# Lebanese Armed Forces

The Lebanese Armed Forces (LAF) are the national military of Lebanon and the designated state authority in the US-backed [[israel-lebanon-ceasefire|Israel-Lebanon ceasefire framework]] announced in June 2026.[1]

## Role in the 2026 framework

Under the framework — announced jointly by the United States, [[israel|Israel]] and Lebanon after negotiations in Washington — [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] is required to halt attacks on Israel and withdraw its operatives from areas south of the [[litani-river|Litani River]], while security zones in southern Lebanon come under the control of the Lebanese Armed Forces.[1] Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] said the goal is a security arrangement in which armed non-state actors no longer operate in the designated zones.[1]

The arrangement reflects a long-standing international position that Hezbollah's armed presence south of the Litani violates earlier ceasefire arrangements and should be replaced by Lebanese state authority; previous agreements likewise envisioned Hezbollah forces withdrawing north of the river while Lebanese troops deployed in the area.[1] The broader objective of the emerging framework is thus to substitute the authority of the Lebanese state for Hezbollah's military presence in the south — an outcome Hezbollah has resisted, and one complicated by continued rocket launches, drone incidents and Israeli strikes after the announcement.[1]
