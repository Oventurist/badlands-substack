# Lebanese Armed Forces

The Lebanese Armed Forces (LAF) are the national military of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and the institution designated in successive international ceasefire arrangements to assume security control over southern Lebanon in place of non-state armed groups.

## Role in the 2026 ceasefire framework

According to the June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief, the United States is backing an [[israel|Israel]]–Lebanon ceasefire framework under which [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] would halt attacks on Israel and withdraw its operatives from areas south of the [[litani-river|Litani River]], with the resulting security zones in southern Lebanon coming under the control of the Lebanese Armed Forces. The framework was announced jointly by the United States, Israel, and Lebanon following negotiations in Washington.

The Brief situates this arrangement within a long-standing international position that Hezbollah's armed presence south of the Litani violates earlier ceasefire agreements and should be replaced by Lebanese state authority; previous agreements similarly envisioned Hezbollah forces withdrawing north of the river while Lebanese troops deployed in the area. The article characterizes the emerging US-backed framework as moving beyond a simple ceasefire toward the broader objective of substituting the authority of the Lebanese state for Hezbollah's military presence — an outcome whose viability depends on Hezbollah's acceptance, given that the group was not a direct signatory and that rocket launches, drone incidents, and Israeli strikes continued after the announcement.
