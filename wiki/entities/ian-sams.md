# Ian Sams

Ian Sams served as spokesman for the [[white-house|White House]] Counsel's Office during the Biden administration and was the official who fronted the administration's response to the [[hur-report|Hur report]] in February 2024.[1]

At the first White House briefing to address the report, Sams argued that the inquiry's purpose had been narrow and that its outcome vindicated the president: "The special counsel's assignment when he was appointed was to determine whether any criminal conduct occurred. He found it didn't. That was the finding. The case is closed."[1] He simultaneously dismissed [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s observations about President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s memory as "gratuitous comments" that were "inappropriate" and should not distract from the conclusion that the case was closed.[1]

Badlands commentary characterized the briefing as a "spin cycle," noting the difficulty of praising a report's legal findings while denouncing its factual observations about the president's mental state.[1]
