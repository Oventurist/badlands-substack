# J. Christopher Giancarlo

**J. Christopher Giancarlo** is an American regulator and blockchain advocate who chaired the [[commodity-futures-trading-commission|Commodity Futures Trading Commission]] during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration. He is profiled in the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" as a second key figure, alongside [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], behind the American push for a [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Career

Having served as acting CFTC chair since Trump's inauguration on January 20, 2017, Giancarlo was nominated as full-time chairman on March 14, 2017 and confirmed by the U.S. Senate on August 3, 2017.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) He is described as a blockchain technology advocate and contributor to global discourse on cryptocurrencies and digital assets, the founder of the [[digital-dollar-project|Digital Dollar Project]], and a former director of the financial services company BlockFi.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) His congressional testimony in favor of crypto and blockchain earned him the nickname "Crypto Dad" in cryptocurrency circles, and he authored a book of the same name.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Contacts with Treasury

According to CNBC reporting cited in the article, before launching the Digital Dollar Project Giancarlo was among the crypto-progressive voices in the ear of Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) A July 2018 email exchange shows Giancarlo, through his executive assistant, pressing insistently for an in-person meeting with the Treasury Secretary.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Asked by CNBC about a White House meeting, Giancarlo said he could not recall that specific request but that throughout 2018 he "routinely apprised Secretary Mnuchin of ongoing operation and supervision of the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] Futures market launched under CFTC oversight in December 2017."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Views on money

In a Forbes interview quoted at length, Giancarlo said the lesson of his research was that "money is too important to be left to central bankers," an adaptation of Georges Clemenceau's remark that war is too important to be left to the generals.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) He argued that a free society has much to gain from crypto-driven modernization of the financial system but much to lose if it has no say in the values encoded into the future of money, whether non-sovereign money such as Diem or sovereign money in the form of a digital dollar.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The values he enumerated — free enterprise, free economic activity, individual privacy, and freedom from government censorship of legal activity — are what he said motivated the launch of the Digital Dollar Project.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents this list as the substance of what Trump is fighting for behind the scenes: American constitutional values embedded in the digital dollar.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)
