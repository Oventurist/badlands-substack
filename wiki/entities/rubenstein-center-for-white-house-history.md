# Rubenstein Center for White House History

The Rubenstein Center for White House History is a historical research institution founded by financier [[david-rubenstein|David Rubenstein]].[1] Its Senior Vice President and Director was [[colleen-shogan|Colleen Shogan]], who subsequently became Archivist of the United States before being dismissed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in February 2025.[1]

## Badlands framing

In the Badlands News Brief of February 10, 2025, commentator Chris Paul raised the Center as the hinge of a self-described "working conspiracy theory": drawing an analogy to presidential records housed at presidential libraries and Joe Biden's Senate papers at the University of Delaware, he asked whether the Rubenstein Center might serve a similar custodial function for records moved out of the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]].[1] Paul framed the broader question as whether Rubenstein's accumulation of historical institutions — including a Holocaust Museum collection — amounts to an effort to own and rewrite official history.[1]
