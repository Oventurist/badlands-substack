# 4th Psychological Operations Group

The 4th Psychological Operations Group is a United States Army psychological operations formation frequently invoked in the Badlands Media corpus as the presumed instrument behind large-scale narrative management. It appears in the April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks", in commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] on the U.S. government's request that [[planet-labs|Planet Labs]] withhold satellite imagery of [[iran|Iran]].

The commentary asks what would be required if the group were running an information operation not merely against foreign publics but against [[israel|Israel]]'s own security services — specifically [[mossad|Mossad]] and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] — so that they too believe the United States is fighting an earnest war against Iran. The answer he offers is manipulated intelligence, manipulated satellite imagery, and Iranian officials providing affirmation of U.S. and [[donald-trump|Trump]] claims. In this reading, Mossad and the IDF continue supplying "critical" intelligence to Washington while wrongly believing they are the ones in control and manipulating Trump, and Trump's deliberately cartoonish public rhetoric about the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] ultimatum serves to induce his adversaries to underestimate him. The corpus offers this as speculation rather than documented fact, but treats the group as a plausible mechanism for the "[[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]]" it sees running through the 2026 conflict.
