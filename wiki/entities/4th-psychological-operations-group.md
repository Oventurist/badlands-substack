# 4th Psychological Operations Group

The 4th Psychological Operations Group is a U.S. Army psychological operations unit frequently invoked in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as an instrument of narrative warfare.[1]

Discussing the satellite imagery blackout imposed on [[iran|Iran]] war coverage, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] speculated that the 4th Psyop Group could be running an information operation against [[mossad|Mossad]] and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] themselves, such that even Israeli services believe the U.S. military is fighting an earnest war against Iran.[1] He asked what such an operation would require — manipulated intelligence, manipulated satellite imagery, and Iranian officials affirming U.S. and [[donald-trump|Trump]] claims — while Israeli services continue supplying "critical" intelligence and believe they are the ones manipulating Trump.[1] In this reading, Trump's cartoonish public rhetoric functions as bait to have his genius taken for granted.[1]

## "Ghosts in the Machine" recruiting video

In 2022 the 4th Psychological Operations Group — self-described as the "Masters of Influence" and practitioners of information warfare — released a highly controversial recruiting video titled "[[ghosts-in-the-machine|Ghosts in the Machine]]" that instantly went viral.[2] Crafted to look like a psychological thriller with unsettling imagery and references to real-life uprisings, and described as designed to target psychological vulnerabilities and create confusion in adversary organizations, the clip asked: "Who's pulling the strings?"[2] Coverage characterized it as a call to join an elite, secretive team functioning as a puppeteer over hot-button national security threats such as the Chinese and Russian governments, confirming that "the war of tomorrow is fought entirely within the human mind."[2] Badlands contributor [[gmoney|GMONEY]] noted a Pepe image appearing on the "KOKO the Clown" figure at the 57-second mark of the 1:17 clip and riffed on its tagline "Words are our weapon."[2]
