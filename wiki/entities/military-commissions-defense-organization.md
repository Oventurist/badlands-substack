# Military Commissions Defense Organization

The Military Commissions Defense Organization (MCDO) is a component of the United States Department of Defense that provides legal defense to detainees facing military tribunals at Guantanamo Bay, including men accused of conspiring to carry out the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] The Office of Military Commissions, within which MCDO investigators work, employed [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]], whose July 20, 2021 sworn declaration collected allegations of a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and [[fbi|FBI]] coverup surrounding the attacks.[1]

## Role in the al-Baluchi defense

Canestraro served on the defense team for Guantanamo detainee [[ammar-al-baluchi|Ammar al-Baluchi]], a Pakistani citizen awaiting trial alongside four other men accused of planning the attacks.[1] Because MCDO defense teams receive government discovery material and may conduct their own investigations on behalf of clients, Canestraro was able to combine documentary discovery with interviews of eleven former FBI agents, two former CIA officers, a CNN investigative journalist, former deputy National Security Advisor Richard Clarke, and former Senator Bob Graham.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] emphasized that the investigative work emerged from inside the Department of Defense's own defense apparatus rather than from outside researchers, which the outlet treated as strengthening the credibility of the resulting declaration.[1]
