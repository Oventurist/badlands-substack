# National Center for Public Policy Research

The **National Center for Public Policy Research** is a conservative American think tank.[1]

Its senior fellow [[horace-cooper|Horace Cooper]], a former constitutional law instructor at George Mason University, provided widely cited analysis in December 2023 predicting that the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] would reverse the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]]'s removal of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state ballot while sidestepping the question of whether Trump had engaged in insurrection under the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

## Free Enterprise Project and the anti-ESG campaign

The organization operates the [[free-enterprise-project|Free Enterprise Project]], a conservative shareholder activist group directed by fellow [[scott-shepard|Scott Shepard]].[2] In June 2023, Shepard told The Epoch Times that opposition to [[esg|ESG]] had broadened well beyond the usual conservative constituency, saying, "it's not just the conservatives, who are always interested in this sort of thing, it's the whole country."[2] He argued that ESG had hardened from guidelines into heavy-handed mandates advancing controversial "social justice" ideologies, and that resulting breaches of fiduciary responsibility to shareholders would expose companies to legal action of the kind his organization had begun to file.[2] Consumer boycotts of [[target|Target]] and [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch]] were credited with supplying the traction that made such litigation viable.[2]
