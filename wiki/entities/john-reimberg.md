# John Reimberg

John Reimberg is Ecuador's interior minister under President [[daniel-noboa|Daniel Noboa]].[1]

Commenting on the opening of the first [[fbi|FBI]] office in [[ecuador|Ecuador]] in March 2026, Reimberg said the key change was the permanent presence of FBI agents working directly with a newly formed unit within Ecuador's national police.[1] The office is intended to expand cooperation on investigations involving drug and weapons trafficking, money laundering, and the financing of terrorism.[1]
