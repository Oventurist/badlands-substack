# Julia Wendell

Julia Wendell, also known as Julia Faustyna, is a Polish woman who gained international attention in early 2023 by publicly claiming that she might be [[madeleine-mccann|Madeleine McCann]], the British child who vanished in Portugal in 2007.[1]

## Public claim and social media

Wendell, 21 years old at the time, made her claim on Instagram and TikTok under the handle @iammadeleinemccann, citing similarities in age and appearance to the missing girl, and accumulated tens of thousands of followers on both platforms.[1]

## Forensic testing

In March 2023 she submitted samples for three different forensic examinations designed to outline her DNA sequence, together with a 23andMe-style genetic test intended to establish her ancestry.[1] The submissions were disclosed by private investigator Dr. Fia Johansson, who said that if the results indicated British or regional origin the investigation into the McCann disappearance would continue and detectives in Portugal would be contacted.[1] Johansson had relocated Wendell from Poland to a safe house in the United States after a series of death threats against her.[1]
