# Vasyl Maliuk

**Vasyl Maliuk** is the head of the [[security-service-of-ukraine|Security Service of Ukraine]] (SBU), Ukraine's principal domestic intelligence and counterintelligence agency.[1]

## Role in Operation Spiderweb

Maliuk served as the public face of [[operation-spiderweb|Operation Spiderweb]], the June 2025 Ukrainian drone strike on Russian strategic aviation.[1] He disclosed that the drones used in the attack had been smuggled into Russia and concealed inside shipping containers that were later loaded onto trucks.[1] Once the trucks were positioned near Russian air bases, the container roof panels were lifted off by a remotely activated mechanism, releasing first-person-view drones that were piloted remotely against the parked aircraft.[1] The operation, prepared over roughly eighteen months, destroyed billions of dollars' worth of Russian aircraft at bases as far away as Siberia.[1]
