# John McCone

John McCone was a senior United States intelligence official, referred to in "The Harvard Control Grid" as a CIA Deputy Director.[1] The article states that in the 1970s, then-Dean [[henry-rosovsky|Henry Rosovsky]] reportedly enlisted McCone to advise [[harvard-university|Harvard]] on managing its Arab investments during the oil embargo.[1]

Badlands Media presents the consultation as a precedent showing that Harvard has historically drawn on intelligence leadership for economic positioning, alongside later examples such as [[robert-gates|Robert Gates]] on the Board of Overseers, [[ash-carter|Ash Carter]] at the [[belfer-center|Belfer Center]], and [[james-woolsey|James Woolsey]] in Harvard-linked symposiums.[1]
