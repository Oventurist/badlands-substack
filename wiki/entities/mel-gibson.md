# Mel Gibson

Mel Gibson is an American-Australian actor and director named in the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]] as one of three "ambassadors to Hollywood" recently appointed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], together with [[jon-voight|Jon Voight]] and [[sylvester-stallone|Sylvester Stallone]].

The article uses the appointments to argue that Trump sees the film industry as salvageable — that he recognizes "the destructive force Hollywood has become" yet declines to "burn it to the ground," because he understands that it can again be used "to inspire and shape while entertaining."
