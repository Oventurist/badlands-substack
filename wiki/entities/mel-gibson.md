# Mel Gibson

Mel Gibson is the actor and director appointed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], together with [[jon-voight|Jon Voight]] and [[sylvester-stallone|Sylvester Stallone]], as an ambassador to [[hollywood|Hollywood]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reads the trio of appointments as evidence that Trump regards Hollywood as salvageable rather than irredeemable, and as an institution that could again be used to inspire and shape audiences while entertaining them.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Gibson's *Road Warrior* is among the films Carlson lists as formative to his own Generation X moviegoing.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## The Passion of the Christ and the Hollywood backlash

In April 2026, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] invoked Gibson alongside [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] as figures who created iconic works glorifying God despite what the outlet described as a Satanic cabal controlling American culture.[2] The treatment Gibson received over *The Passion of the Christ*, released in 2004, was cited as proof that Hollywood and the broader [[deep-state|Deep State]] establishment despise Jesus Christ and have worked to erase Him from American culture.[2] Badlands placed Gibson and West together in a "pantheon of modern heroes" for spreading the Gospel to audiences that might otherwise never have encountered it.[2]

## 9/11 disclosure calls (2025)

In April 2025 Gibson, a vocal supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], urged the American government to reveal the "truth" about the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[3] He shared a clip from [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s interview with former Congressman [[curt-weldon|Curt Weldon]] and wrote on X that the tragedy "might be the biggest scandal in American history."[3] Gibson argued that "three buildings collapsed as if they were programmed to do so. Not two – three. To this day, no one has explained how Building 7 fell, even though no plane hit it," and that the towers "were designed to withstand airplane impacts."[3] He concluded: "What really happened that day? And why is no one allowed to talk about it? Enough with the fear. It's time for the truth."[3]

## Hollywood special ambassador (2025)

On January 16, 2025, President-elect Donald Trump named Gibson, alongside Sylvester Stallone and Jon Voight, as one of three [[hollywood-special-ambassadors|Hollywood special ambassadors]] who would serve as his "eyes and ears" in the entertainment capital.[4] The appointment surprised Gibson himself; in a statement distributed hours later by his publicist he said, "I got the tweet at the same time as all of you and was just as surprised. Nevertheless, I heed the call."[4] He added, "Any chance the position comes with an Ambassador's residence?" — a reference to the destruction of his Malibu, California home in the recent California wildfires.[4]

## Pardon Attorney Dismissal (2025)

Gibson featured indirectly in a 2025 controversy at the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]], when [[elizabeth-oyer|Elizabeth Oyer]], the department's top pardon lawyer, was fired after refusing to grant the actor restored gun ownership rights.[5] Oyer subsequently became a prominent critic of Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], describing the department as taking direction from the [[white-house|White House]].[5]
