# Foreign Affairs

**Foreign Affairs** is an American magazine of international relations and U.S. foreign policy, widely regarded as a house organ of the Western foreign-policy establishment.[1]

## June 2024 Gaza op-ed

In June 2024 the magazine published an op-ed by Audrey Kurth Cronin, director of the Carnegie Mellon Institute for Strategy and Technology, arguing that Israel's military operation in Gaza was a "strategic disaster" because support for [[hamas|Hamas]] among Gazans was rising rather than collapsing.[1] The piece, summarized by [[tass|TASS]] and carried in the Badlands News Brief, cited Palestinian Center for Policy and Survey Research polling showing Hamas support above 50% in March 2024, up 14 points since December 2023, and attributed the shift to mass civilian casualties, restrictions on humanitarian aid and the Israeli operations in [[rafah-invasion|Rafah]].[1]

Badlands treated the appearance of such an assessment in an establishment journal as notable in itself — a sign that criticism of the campaign had migrated from dissident outlets into mainstream foreign-policy discourse.[1]
