# Lockheed Martin

**Lockheed Martin** is a United States weapons manufacturer and one of the principal contractors of the American [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]]. It is the prime contractor for the Terminal High Altitude Area Defense ([[thaad|THAAD]]) missile defense system.[1]

## Saudi manufacturing agreements

In February 2024, Lockheed Martin signed agreements permitting Saudi Arabian companies to manufacture parts of the THAAD system, announced on the sidelines of the World Defense Show in Riyadh.[1] The company said in a statement issued Monday, February 5, 2024, that the sub-contracts would enhance manufacturing capability inside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and transfer expertise in order to strengthen the kingdom's domestic defense industry.[1] State-owned [[saudi-arabian-military-industries|Saudi Arabian Military Industries]] signed eleven agreements at the same event, according to the Saudi state news agency.[1] Reporting also indicated that [[qatar|Qatar]] would likewise manufacture parts.[1]

The contracts were concluded amid rising regional tension, as the United States continued military operations in the Middle East against Iran-backed groups; the United States and Britain had struck 36 Houthi targets in Yemen on Saturday, February 3, one day after American forces hit Tehran-backed groups in Iraq and Syria in retaliation for a deadly attack on US troops in Jordan.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] interpreted the arrangement as evidence that the Saudi and [[brics|BRICS]] posture had functioned as a negotiating tactic contributing to the dismantling of the military-industrial complex, and as a "Devo proof" that unipolar hegemony had ended.[1] The argument held that if Middle Eastern states can manufacture their own military equipment, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] can no longer dangle such weapons systems over foreign governments as leverage — noting that the Biden administration had withdrawn THAAD systems that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] deployed to Saudi Arabia in 2019.[1]
