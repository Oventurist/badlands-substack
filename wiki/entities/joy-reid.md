# Joy Reid

Joy Reid is an American television host and commentator who anchored a primetime program on [[msnbc|MSNBC]].

## In the Badlands critique

A Swan Song for the MSM cites Reid, together with [[jen-psaki|Jen Psaki]], as evidence of what it calls the rampant mediocrity of cable news, inviting the reader to "try to sit through five minutes of Joy Reid or Jen Psaki's god awful MSNBC shows." The author notes that he attempts this exercise "semi regularly" and that "it takes a strong stomach." Reid's program is offered as a specimen of programming whose emphasis is on "highlighting our differences and smugly asserting why 'our team' is better than 'their team'" rather than on the adversarial function the corpus assigns to genuine journalism.
