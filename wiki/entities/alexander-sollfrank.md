# Alexander Sollfrank

Lieutenant-General Alexander Sollfrank is a German officer who heads [[nato|NATO]]'s logistics command.[1]

In September 2024 Sollfrank confirmed that the alliance was developing mass evacuation and rescue plans for a possible future war with Russia, working to ensure it has the operational capability to extract large numbers of wounded troops from the front lines.[1] He warned that, unlike allied experience in Afghanistan and Iraq, an all-out war with Russia would likely see NATO incur heavy losses across a vast battlefield, and that Russia's air force and its missile and rocket stockpiles would make medical evacuation by aircraft too risky — potentially forcing NATO to move casualties by "hospital trains."[1] "The challenge will be to swiftly ensure high-quality care for, in the worst case, a great number of wounded," he said.[1] His warning coincided with German military assessments that Russia could be capable of attacking a NATO country as soon as 2029.[1]

Badlands commentary responded by calling for NATO to be abolished outright and for its leaders to be investigated and prosecuted for crimes against humanity.[1]
