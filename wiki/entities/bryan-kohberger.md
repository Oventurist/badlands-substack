# Bryan Kohberger

**Bryan Kohberger** is the man convicted of the November 2022 murders of four University of Idaho students — Ethan Chapin, Xana Kernodle, Madison Mogen, and Kaylee Goncalves — at a residence in Moscow, Idaho.[1]

## Crime and arrest

The killings occurred in the early hours of November 13, 2022.[1] Kohberger was arrested six weeks later at his parents' home in Pennsylvania.[1] Prosecutors said DNA evidence, surveillance footage, online purchase history, and cellphone data connected him to the crime.[1]

## Plea and sentencing

Kohberger pleaded guilty as part of a plea deal that spared him the death penalty.[1] On July 23, 2025, at a sentencing hearing held in Boise, Idaho, he received four life terms without parole plus an additional ten years for a burglary charge.[1] The hearing featured emotional victim impact statements from the families of the deceased; Kohberger declined to make any statement, prompting murmurs from the victims' families.[1]
