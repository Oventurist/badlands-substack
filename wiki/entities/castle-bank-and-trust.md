# Castle Bank & Trust

Castle Bank & Trust (Bahamas) Ltd. was a privately owned, Nassau-based offshore bank set up and principally controlled by the CIA lawyer-banker [[paul-helliwell]], and identified in Joe Lange's account as the model for the agency's later money laundering operations.[1] Helliwell served as its president while also holding the directorship of Sea Supply, Inc. and helping establish the CIA proprietary airline Civil Air Transport.[1]

## The tax evasion case

For years Internal Revenue Service agents had tried without much success to obtain documentary proof that Americans were using offshore banks in the Caribbean to hide income and evade taxes; Castle Bank was one of the suspect institutions.[1] In what The Washington Post called "the great Castle Bank caper," two IRS agents outdid themselves as sleuths only to have their chief throw out their hard-gained evidence of possible massive tax evasion as inadmissible.[1]

The Post concluded that it now appeared that pressure from the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], rather than any legal problem, was what caused the [[justice-department]] to drop what could have been the biggest tax evasion case of all time — and that the supposed legal obstacle to using the Castle Bank depositors' list was questionable at best, since the government already possessed the same list, legally obtained.[1]

The CIA's argument was that pursuit of the bank would endanger national security, because Castle Bank — besides its possible use as a haven for tax evaders — was the conduit for millions of dollars earmarked by the agency for funding clandestine operations against Cuba and other covert intelligence operations directed at countries in Latin America and the Far East.[1] Lange treats this as an archetypal instance of a recurring pattern in which "national security" is used as a shield to protect untouchable operatives from prosecution.[1]

## Aftermath

Castle Bank closed its operations in the Bahamas and Cayman Islands in 1977 and was subsequently based in Panama, though apparently with no offices there.[1] Helliwell went on to help set up the [[nugan-hand-bank]], and Lange argues Castle Bank became the template for later operations such as BCCI — as well as, he suspects, the training ground for [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]].[1] The Bahamas was one of the jurisdictions Milian Rodriguez named in testimony about where he laundered [[medellin-cartel]] proceeds.[1]
