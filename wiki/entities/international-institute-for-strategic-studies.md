# International Institute for Strategic Studies

The International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS) is a London-based think tank focused on defense and strategic affairs.[1]

## Kasyanov presentation

In 2006, former Russian Prime Minister [[mikhail-kasyanov|Mikhail Kasyanov]] gave a presentation to the IISS in which he claimed — as a former member of [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s administration — that Putin had restructured the Russian government and consolidated power, effectively branding him an autocrat.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] speculated that this presentation may have been an origin point for the "Putin is a strongman dictator" narrative in Western discourse.[1]

## Funding criticism

The same commentary pointed to a published list of companies funding the IISS, characterizing the donor base as the British and American military-industrial complex and arguing that this funding profile explains the institute's strategic orientation.[1]
