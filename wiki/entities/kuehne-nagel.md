# Kuehne + Nagel

Kuehne + Nagel International AG is a global transport and logistics company whose December 2023 reporting documented the scale of commercial shipping's withdrawal from the Red Sea.[1]

The firm reported that more than 100 container ships had been rerouted from the Red Sea around Africa to avoid Iran-backed [[houthis|Houthi]] militants in Yemen, who were attacking commercial vessels with missiles and drones.[1] Bloomberg carried two headlines early on Wednesday, December 20, 2023 detailing the company's update: 103 container ships had detoured around the Cape of Good Hope, lengthening voyages by one to two weeks, and Kuehne + Nagel expected the number of detours to rise in the following days.[1]

For vessels still transiting the waterway connecting to the Suez Canal, war-risk insurance costs jumped that week from roughly 0.1%–0.2% of hull value to 0.5%, meaning a $100 million vessel would pay about $500,000 per voyage.[1] Higher insurance premiums combined with longer routings around the Cape pointed toward snarled supply chains and rising goods prices, part of the wider [[red-sea-shipping-crisis|Red Sea shipping crisis]].[1]
