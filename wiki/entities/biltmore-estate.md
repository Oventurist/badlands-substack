# Biltmore Estate

The Biltmore Estate, also called the Biltmore House, is a large private estate in Asheville, North Carolina. Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] Chapter 14 identifies it as the property of the [[vanderbilt-family|Vanderbilt family]] and makes it the focus of a speculative section of the chapter.[1]

## Claims about the pool

The article presents photographs of the estate's drained indoor pool and highlights three features: ropes running along the side, the absence of drains, and rust accumulated at the bottom.[1] It juxtaposes these images with artwork the author attributes to the Podesta family — see [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] — and argues the comparison hints at what the pool "was actually used for once upon a time."[1]

The chapter goes on to claim that one of the compared images appears to depict a younger [[anderson-cooper|Anderson Cooper]], and that a family photograph of Cooper, his brother and [[gloria-vanderbilt|Gloria Vanderbilt]] carries a disturbing backdrop.[1] All of these readings are the author's inference from imagery rather than documented findings.[1]
