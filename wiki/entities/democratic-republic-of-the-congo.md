# Democratic Republic of the Congo

The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) is a central African state whose eastern mineral provinces are the site of one of the three major African conflicts tracked by the Badlands corpus. It appears in the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]] in connection with the United States–DRC minerals partnership, the [[m23-movement|M23]] insurgency, and the reported death of former president [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]].

## US–DRC minerals partnership

The Brief reports that [[corneille-nangaa|Corneille Nangaa]], leader of the Rwanda-linked [[alliance-fleuve-congo|Alliance Fleuve Congo]], publicly denounced the US–DRC minerals partnership as unconstitutional — a claim the DRC presidency rejected. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the arrangement as "Conflict B," the theatre in which President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is directly involved, and noted that Nangaa was accusing both Trump and President [[felix-tshisekedi|Félix Tshisekedi]] of violating the law through the implementation of their legal framework.

## Eastern conflict and Kabila

Militias crossing into eastern Congo from [[rwanda|Rwanda]] have seized control of mining towns. In 2025 Kabila was accused of aiding and abetting these rebels, charged with treason, and convicted in absentia of a failed coup, with the prosecutor obtaining the death penalty. The commentary recalls Kabila's presidency as the period in which the mining financier [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] "bribed his way into a relationship" with the Congolese head of state.

The Brief also cites Agenzia Fides reporting that Kabila was killed on January 25, 2026 in an explosion during a meeting with representatives of a guerrilla group, said to have killed around 40 people, though no official source had confirmed the death.

## Resource geopolitics

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry links the Congolese situation to broader minerals realignment, noting that [[china|China]] was planning to move 100,000 tons of copper to the United States, and to congressional discussion of sanctions against Rwanda. He concludes that "the Sovereign Alliance lives, and that doesn't bode well for globalist wannabes," placing the DRC within the corpus's [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework.
