# Thomas Jolly

Thomas Jolly is a French theatre director who served as artistic director of the opening ceremony of the [[2024-paris-summer-olympics|2024 Paris Summer Olympics]].[1]

## Paris 2024 opening ceremony

Described in coverage cited by Badlands as a "queer artistic director," Jolly said of his production that he "wants everyone to feel represented."[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] treated the remark as "yet another example of gay activists unable to control their impulse to project their sexual preferences on everything, even sporting events," and reported that the ceremony he planned featured performances by a horde of drag queens, sexualized dancing, and a segment widely described as an LGBT recreation of The Last Supper.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that she turned off what she called the "pornographic runway" and the segment featuring a blue-painted performer she read as an attempt to mock God, while praising other portions of Jolly's staging — the metal band performing beside a castle while Marie Antoinette held her head, the parade of athletes on boats down the Seine, and the Eiffel Tower light show — as so well executed they had to be appreciated.[1]
