# Defense One

**Defense One** is an American national-security and defense news outlet.[1] It appears in the Badlands Media corpus chiefly as the publisher of a December 21, 2021 article titled "US Army Creates Single Vaccine Against All COVID & SARS Variants, Researchers Say."[1]

## The SpFN report

The article reported that scientists at the [[walter-reed-army-institute-of-research|Walter Reed Army Institute of Research]] expected within weeks to announce a vaccine effective against COVID-19 and all its variants, including Omicron, as well as previous SARS-origin viruses that had killed millions worldwide.[1] It described the achievement as the product of nearly two years of work, noted that the [[spike-ferritin-nanoparticle-vaccine|SpFN]] candidate had completed animal trials and Phase 1 human trials with positive results, and quoted Dr. Kayvon Modjarrad of the institute's infectious diseases branch in an exclusive interview.[1] It also explained the candidate's distinctive 24-faced protein architecture.[1]

Badlands contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] treats the Defense One piece as authoritative on the grounds that the Army would not have permitted such a report to be published, and remain published, unless it were accurate.[1]
