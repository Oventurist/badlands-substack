# Erin Ward

Erin Ward is an Omaha, Nebraska substitute teacher who in April 2024 was charged after being caught having sex with a 17-year-old student, a case that drew national attention because she is the wife of [[doug-ward|Doug Ward]], a high-ranking official at the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]].[1]

## Charges

Ward, 45, admitted to having sex with the student in her family vehicle.[1] Because the age of consent in Nebraska is 16, she was charged only with felony sexual abuse by a school employee rather than statutory rape, though the count carries a possible sentence of up to 20 years in prison.[1] Reporting cited by [[the-post-millennial|The Post Millennial]] noted that Ward and her 53-year-old husband have three children together, including a daughter roughly the same age as the victim, and that the couple had posted numerous photographs together on social media.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], called the story "bad before it involved the Biden Administration, and now it's worse. And it's weird," linking it to the earlier case of a transgender nuclear official in the same administration who was caught stealing luggage and asking what other conduct might be found "in the Biden nuclear domain."[1] She described the most heartbreaking aspects as the harm to the 17-year-old victim and to the Wards' own children, whose childhoods she said would be marked by their mother's conduct, and cited a Centers for Disease Control estimate that the total lifetime economic burden of child sexual abuse in the United States in 2015 was at least $9.3 billion.[1]

Ashe argued that the apparent increase in such stories reflects not a rise in the underlying crimes but a decline in the mechanisms that once shielded them from public view, calling it encouraging that the story surfaced at all rather than being buried to protect a Defense Department official, and summarizing her position as "Exposure: Accelerate."[1]
