# White House Environmental Justice Advisory Council

The White House Environmental Justice Advisory Council is a federal advisory body attached to the [[white-house|White House]] that counselled the executive branch on environmental justice policy and grantmaking during the Biden administration.[1]

## Role in the green grant referrals

The council featured in criminal referrals made by [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]] Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]] to the agency's inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] alleging self-dealing in the distribution of Biden-era green energy grants funded by the [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]].[1] Among the cited cases was a $20 million award to [[young-gifted-and-green|Young, Gifted & Green]], whose chief executive applied for the funding while simultaneously serving as a member of the advisory council.[1]

Zeldin argued more broadly that such funding flowed to "pass-through entities" connected to former Obama-Biden officials, Democratic donors and politically connected individuals, describing the rushed 2024 distribution as "blatant waste and abuse" that "feels criminal" and centred on "self-dealing."[1]
