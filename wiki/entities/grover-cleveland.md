# Grover Cleveland

Grover Cleveland was the American president who succeeded [[benjamin-harrison|Benjamin Harrison]] and who, in Badlands commentary, was unfairly blamed for an economic collapse engineered by his predecessor.[1]

Cleveland was inaugurated days after a stock market crash triggered, in this account, by Harrison's deliberate efforts to damage the incoming administration; the resulting [[panic-of-1893|Panic of 1893]] produced a depression lasting through 1897 for which Cleveland bore the political cost.[1] Commentators noted that Cleveland, like [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], served two presidential terms with a "pause" in the middle — a parallel used to frame the 2024 transition.[1]
