# Mohammed Deif

Mohammed Deif was a senior [[hamas|Hamas]] military commander named among the individuals for whom the Prosecutor of the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] sought arrest warrants in May 2024.[1]

## ICC arrest warrant application

In a statement issued on Tuesday, May 21, 2024, [[european-union|European Union]] foreign policy chief [[josep-borrell|Josep Borrell]] took note of the ICC Prosecutor's decision to apply for warrants of arrest before Pre-Trial Chamber I against [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]], Mohammed Deif, [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]], Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]].[1]

Borrell emphasized that the mandate of the ICC as an independent international institution is "to prosecute the most serious crimes under international law," and that all states that have ratified the ICC statutes are bound to execute the court's decisions.[1] With the exception of Ukraine and Turkey, all of Europe is a signatory to the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]], obliging those states to apprehend individuals wanted by the court.[1]

The simultaneous listing of Hamas commanders alongside sitting Israeli officials was central to commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], which read the move as evidence that globalist institutions were beginning to be turned against the very actors who had built them.[1]
