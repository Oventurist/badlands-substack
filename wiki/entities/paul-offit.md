# Paul Offit

Dr. Paul Offit is a physician and vaccine advocate who serves as a member of the [[vaccines-and-related-biological-products-advisory-committee|Vaccines and Related Biological Products Advisory Committee]] (VRBPAC), the advisory panel to the [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] that selects annual influenza vaccine strains.[1]

## Publicizing the 2025 VRBPAC cancellation

Offit told CNBC on February 26, 2025 that he had received an email at 4:18 p.m. ET that day announcing the cancellation of the committee's March 13 meeting, with no explanation and no indication of rescheduling.[1] He publicly questioned who had cancelled the meeting and why, and asked whether "manufacturers now turn to the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] to determine strains for this year's influenza vaccines."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer Ashe in America treated Offit's reaction as overwrought, describing his objection as anger at losing "his government paid vacation and catered meals" and a belief that legacy media could still mount a pressure campaign against the Trump administration.[1] She argued the answer to his WHO question was plainly no, since Trump had withdrawn the United States and its funding from the WHO on Inauguration Day and since VRBPAC is an advisory body whose recommendations are non-binding on the FDA.[1]
