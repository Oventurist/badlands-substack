# Summer Lee

Summer Lee is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Pennsylvania who sits on the House Oversight Committee's Federal Law Enforcement Subcommittee.[1]

## Role in the Epstein records push

On July 23, 2025, the Federal Law Enforcement Subcommittee of the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] adopted a motion offered by Lee, by an 8–2 vote, directing the release of [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] records related to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] Her motion was paired with a separate voice-vote motion by Rep. [[scott-perry|Scott Perry]] (R-Pa.) authorizing deposition subpoenas for eleven current and former officials.[1]

The combined bipartisan action set the stage for Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] to issue formal subpoenas on August 5, 2025, targeting figures including [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] and a series of former Attorneys General.[1] Lee's participation is notable in Badlands coverage because it demonstrated cross-party support for forcing disclosure of the Epstein files.[1]
