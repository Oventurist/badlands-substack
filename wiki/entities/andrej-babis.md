# Andrej Babiš

Andrej Babiš is a Czech politician who publicly backed Hungarian Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] ahead of Hungary's 2026 parliamentary election.[1]

His endorsement, alongside that of Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]], aligned him with Orbán's positions in the bloc's disputes with the [[european-union|European Union]] over Russia policy, sanctions, and military support for [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Orbán nonetheless conceded defeat to [[peter-magyar|Péter Magyar]]'s [[tisza-party|Tisza]] party.[1]
