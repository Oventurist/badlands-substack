# Andrej Babiš

Andrej Babiš is a Czech politician who publicly backed Hungarian Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] ahead of Hungary's 2026 parliamentary election.[1]

His endorsement, alongside that of Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]], aligned him with Orbán's positions in the bloc's disputes with the [[european-union|European Union]] over Russia policy, sanctions, and military support for [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Orbán nonetheless conceded defeat to [[peter-magyar|Péter Magyar]]'s [[tisza-party|Tisza]] party.[1]

## 2025 Czech election victory

Babiš, a populist billionaire dubbed "the Czech Trump" by The Economist, led his ANO party to first place with 35% of the vote in the [[2025-czech-parliamentary-election|2025 Czech parliamentary election]] held on October 3 and 4, 2025.[2] The campaign was unusually bitter: in September he was struck on the head with a crutch by a pensioner and briefly hospitalized, and he traded insults with incumbent prime minister [[petr-fiala|Petr Fiala]] over subjects from foreign policy to the price of butter, once calling him a "fool" whose brain was clouded by "anti-Babisism."[2] Fiala's SPOLU alliance finished second with 23% and ruled out a coalition with him, leaving Babiš needing one or two other partners to form the ANO-led government widely expected to follow.[2]
