# Dawn Engoron

Dawn Engoron is the wife of New York judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]], who presided over former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s civil business fraud trial.[1]

## 2023 social media controversy

In November 2023, conservative activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] accused Dawn Engoron of running an X account that frequently posted anti-Trump messages against the former president.[1] Because her husband was overseeing Trump's civil fraud trial — and because the judge had already faced criticism of his own — Loomer's claims prompted calls for a mistrial in the case.[1]

*Newsweek* was unable to verify that the account belonged to Dawn Engoron, and on the Wednesday of that week she issued a firm denial in a statement to the outlet.[1] "I do not have a Twitter account. This is not me. I have not posted any anti Trump messages," she said.[1] The exchange was reported by [[newsweek|Newsweek]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of November 10, 2023.[1]
