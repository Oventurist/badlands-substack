# Christina Martin

Christina Martin is a Senior Attorney at the [[pacific-legal-foundation|Pacific Legal Foundation]], where she works on property-rights litigation.[1]

In December 2025 Martin gave an exclusive interview to The Center Square regarding [[pung-v-isabella-county|Pung v. Isabella County]], the Michigan tax-foreclosure case accepted by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] and due to be decided in 2026.[1] The case asks whether local governments must compensate foreclosed homeowners at fair market value and whether they may seize property worth far more than the tax debt at issue.[1]
