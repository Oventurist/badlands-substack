# Craig Mason

Craig Mason is a host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. He co-hosts **The Friggin' Friday Night Show!** with [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]], which aired Fridays at 9pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

The program is described by the network as taking viewers "to the place where Logic and Reason intersect," reviewing the news of the preceding week in an entertaining and light-hearted manner, and functions as one of the network's weekly wrap-up shows alongside The Liberty Den.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]
