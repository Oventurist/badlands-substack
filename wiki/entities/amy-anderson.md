# Amy Anderson

Amy Anderson is an FBI Supervisory Special Agent who testified as a prosecution witness on the final day of the trial of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] in October 2022.[1]

## Career

Anderson began work at the [[fbi|FBI]] in 2010, starting in San Diego before transferring to New York.[1] In 2016 she was promoted to supervisory special agent and assigned to FBI Headquarters in Washington, D.C., where she worked in the Counterintelligence Division, Counterespionage Section.[1] In April 2017 she was placed on the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] / [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller Special Counsel]] team, moving back to FBI Headquarters in January 2018.[1] She has done counterintelligence work — specifically Russian counterintelligence — for her entire career, and in January 2022 was promoted to assistant legal attaché (ALAT) at the U.S. Embassy in Ottawa.[1]

## Role in validating the dossier

Anderson's first assignment on the Crossfire Hurricane team was to "validate the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]]," explaining that "there was a lot of reporting out there that we needed to determine if it was true or not."[1] Working alongside analyst [[brittany-hertzog|Brittany Hertzog]], she developed interest in Danchenko's contacts [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]] and [[olga-galkina|Olga Galkina]], and she supplied Special Agent [[kevin-helson|Kevin Helson]] with specific questions to pose to Danchenko about Dolan.[1] Helson asked those questions in a recorded interview on June 15, 2017, and obtained nothing; Danchenko did not disclose his messages or meetings with Dolan, nor that information from Dolan had ended up in the dossier.[1] Anderson submitted a memorandum on Dolan to her superiors in the hope that investigative steps would be taken, but the article's author concluded that those superiors were nonresponsive.[1]

## Testimony

DOJ Trial Attorney Brittain Shaw handled the direct examination.[1] The defense had objected to Anderson testifying at all on the ground that her account would be cumulative of Hertzog's; Judge Anthony Trenga elected to hear from her but advised [[john-durham|John Durham]] to confine questioning to "what we haven't already heard."[1] Portions of Anderson's answers describing what Galkina had told her about Dolan were struck as hearsay after a sidebar.[1] Defense counsel Danny Onorato's cross examination used Anderson to attack the FBI personnel who had prepared the FISA warrants on [[carter-page|Carter Page]], emphasizing that the purported Russian disinformation had in fact come from Dolan.[1] The Badlands writer treated Anderson's testimony as strong evidence that Danchenko had "flipped" on Dolan, and observed that prosecutors' repeated interest in the physical location of figures in the alleged "joint venture conspiracy" might foreshadow a future case in the Eastern District of Virginia, where Dolan and Danchenko met in a park near Falls Church on January 1, 2017.[1]

## Dossier validation described on day one

On the opening day of the trial, Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] testified that Anderson came onto the team with the transition into the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller Special Counsel]] and that her specific task was what the team called "dossier validation," in which she was assisted by intelligence analyst [[brittany-hertzog|Brittany Hertzog]].[2]

## Tasking of Danchenko's handler

On the third day of the trial, Agent [[kevin-helson|Kevin Helson]] testified that he first raised [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles Dolan]] with [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] at the recorded June 15, 2017 meeting at the request of Auten and Special Supervisory Agent Anderson.[3] Anderson thus appears in the trial record as one of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] officials who directed the source handler's questioning without herself meeting the source, a pattern of compartmentalisation that ran through the day's testimony.[3]
