# Tom Donilon

**Tom Donilon**, former U.S. National Security Advisor, is described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as "another card-carrying deep state operator" involved in the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

## Political career

Donilon has been active in this world for a long time.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) He advised the presidential campaigns of Jimmy Carter, Walter Mondale, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], Michael Dukakis, [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], designing policy, managing conventions, preparing candidates for debates and overseeing presidential transitions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

## Think tank and finance roles

He has been a regular fixture at deep political forums including [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]], where he sat on the steering committee, the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) DeLarme stresses in particular his role as chairman of the BlackRock Investment Institute — essentially the "brain" of [[blackrock|BlackRock Inc.]] — noting that BlackRock holds considerable, if not majority, equity stakes in [[chevron|Chevron]], Exxon, BP and Shell, all of which stood to gain tremendously from the Iran deal.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

## Role in the Iran Deal

Donilon was technically [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]]'s boss at the time of the deal and played a key policy role.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) When [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pulled the United States out of the agreement, Donilon was quoted saying it was "the worst move in the Middle East since the Iraq War."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) DeLarme notes that Biden originally tapped Donilon to be [[cia|CIA]] Director, but that he went to BlackRock instead — "not a small fish."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

## Appointment as NYPD Commissioner

In September 2024, Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] appointed Donilon to replace [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]] as Commissioner of the [[new-york-city-police-department|New York City Police Department]], after Caban resigned in the wake of federal raids on the homes of senior city officials.[2] Badlands flagged the appointment pointedly in light of Donilon's establishment pedigree, presenting it without further comment as a data point about who fills vacancies created by federal corruption probes.[2]
