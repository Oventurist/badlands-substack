# Alexander Novak

Alexander Novak is the Russian Energy Minister and a central figure in Moscow's pipeline diplomacy with [[china|China]] and Central Asia.[1]

## Trans-Kazakh pipeline update

At the turn of 2025 Novak shared an update on the proposed Russian gas pipeline to China routed through [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], confirming that "this process, so to speak, is underway" and that estimates, the feasibility study, and negotiations were all in progress.[1] Analyst [[andrew-korybko|Andrew Korybko]] cautioned that the remark should not be read, as RT's report implied, as meaning the project was a done deal; at that stage it functioned more as a message directed at the United States.[1]

The context was the unresolved Sino-Russian pricing dispute over the [[power-of-siberia-2|Power of Siberia II]] pipeline, in which China had been demanding bargain-basement prices reportedly equivalent to Russia's domestic rates while [[russia|Russia]] sought better terms.[1] With that impasse still unbroken, Korybko argued that treating the trans-Kazakh proposal as an agreed rerouting of Power of Siberia II — as Asia Times' Yong Jian had — was a premature conclusion.[1]
