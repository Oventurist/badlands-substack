# Roberta Kaplan

Roberta Kaplan is the attorney who represented [[e-jean-carroll|E. Jean Carroll]] in her litigation against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Concession on outside funding

Carroll had previously denied receiving outside funding for her lawsuit.[1] In April 2023, however, Kaplan conceded to Trump attorney [[alina-habba|Alina Habba]] that Carroll "remembered just recently that she had been told at some point about outside funding by a nonprofit and that it was irrelevant to her claim anyway."[1] Reporting by Bloomberg tied that funding to LinkedIn founder and Democratic mega-donor [[reid-hoffman|Reid Hoffman]].[1]

Following the concession, Habba wrote to Judge [[lewis-kaplan|Lewis Kaplan]], who was overseeing the lawsuit, raising significant concerns as to "the plaintiff's bias and motive in commencing the litigation."[1]
