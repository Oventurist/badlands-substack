# Michael Beschloss

Michael Beschloss is a presidential historian and frequent commentator on [[msnbc|MSNBC]].[1]

## March 2024 "dictatorship" remarks

While previewing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s State of the Union address on Thursday, March 7, 2024, Beschloss compared Biden's delivery to [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin D. Roosevelt]] warning Americans about the rise of fascism.[1] Asked by anchor Andrea Mitchell to "zoom out" on what was at stake, Beschloss called the moment "a real historical moment" and declared: "We could be a dictatorship next year if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is elected and carries through on his threats to suspend the Constitution. That's what's at stake."[1] He added that "this is a year when we Americans have to choose whether we're going to live as a democracy, as a republic, or as an authoritarian system," drawing an analogy to Roosevelt's 1941 Four Freedoms speech delivered as "Nazis, fascists, Imperial Japanese were rampaging around the world."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the segment as escalating hysteria and "straight up projecting," arguing that the Biden administration itself represented the closest thing to a dictatorship in her adult lifetime through the prosecution of political opponents, expansion of the surveillance state and weaponization of government.[1] She predicted the rhetoric would intensify through November and attributed its reception to "Trump Derangement Syndrome," which she called a mass psychosis afflicting a loud minority concentrated in the institutions of power.[1]
