# Phinance Technologies

Phinance Technologies is a research firm founded by former [[blackrock|BlackRock]] portfolio manager [[ed-dowd|Ed Dowd]] together with partners Carlos Alegria and Yuri Nunes.[1] Alongside traditional macroeconomic analysis, the firm produces a series of reports — grouped under the heading of its "Humanity Projects" — that examine pandemic-related disabilities and excess deaths using official government data sets.[1]

## Disability and excess-death research

Phinance's multi-part studies of United States disability figures and excess mortality became a central reference point for critics of the mRNA COVID-19 vaccine rollout.[1] An analysis released by the firm reported that the rate of Serious Adverse Events recorded in the mRNA COVID-19 vaccine clinical trials closely tracked the spike in disabilities reported in the population after the vaccines were deployed at scale.[1] The implication drawn from the correlation — summarised in the headline framing that "the trials should have been halted" — was that a safety signal visible in trial data was reproduced at population scale once the products were distributed to hundreds of millions of people.[1]

The work was publicised by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which contrasted the firm's use of official data with the eighteen months during which vaccine skeptics faced ostracism, deplatforming and contested fact-checks.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] relayed the reporting in its [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that whatever the final truth proved to be, it would not reflect kindly on [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]].[1]
