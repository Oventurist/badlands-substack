# Alou Boi Diarra

Alou Boi Diarra is the air force chief of [[mali|Mali]]'s armed forces.[1]

## Russian defense talks, June 2024

Diarra took part alongside Malian Defense Minister [[sadio-camara|Sadio Camara]] in talks with a [[russian-defense-ministry|Russian Defense Ministry]] delegation headed by Deputy Minister [[yunus-bek-yevkurov|Yunus-Bek Yevkurov]] during the delegation's early-June 2024 visit to Bamako, as announced in a press release on the Malian armed forces' website.[1] The visit was part of a wider Russian outreach to Sahel governments that also included [[niger|Niger]], where transitional leader [[abdourahmane-tchiani|Abdourahamane Tchiani]] met the same delegation in Niamey to discuss military, security, and economic cooperation.[1]
