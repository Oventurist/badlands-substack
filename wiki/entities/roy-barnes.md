# Roy Barnes

Roy Barnes is a former Governor of Georgia who testified in the February 2024 evidentiary hearing over the disqualification of Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] from the state's election interference prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Testimony in the Willis disqualification hearing

On the Friday of the hearing week, in place of the anticipated recall of Willis to the stand, the court instead heard from Barnes, who testified that he had been offered the special prosecutor role ultimately filled by [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] but declined it.[1] Barnes said he turned down the appointment for what Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized as "creative reasons" — he did not want the attention and spotlight that would accompany what she called a "novel legal/political fever dream."[1] Ashe in America described Barnes on the stand as "a southern gentleman and an antebellum meme."[1]

His testimony was significant to the defense because it established that Willis had sought out at least one prominent, experienced alternative before hiring Wade, whose qualifications and compensation were central to the disqualification motion.[1]
