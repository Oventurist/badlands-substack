# Joko Widodo

Joko Widodo, commonly known as Jokowi, was the president of [[indonesia|Indonesia]] during the period covered by this article.[1]

## Push away from Western payment networks

In March 2023 Widodo pressed local agencies and authorities to stop using international payment systems and to move instead to cards authorised by domestic Indonesian banks.[1] He argued that Indonesia needed to insulate itself against geopolitical and international upheaval, pointing to the financial-sector sanctions imposed on [[russia|Russia]] by the United States, the European Union and their allies over the war in Ukraine as the cautionary example.[1] The initiative accompanied preparations by [[bank-of-indonesia|Bank Indonesia]] to transition away from [[visa|Visa]] and [[mastercard|Mastercard]] while developing an indigenous payment platform, as reported by the Antara news agency.[1] Widodo characterised the two card networks as a "problem" for the country.[1]

## Significance

The Badlands sponsor and financial analyst [[kirk-elliott|Dr. Kirk Elliott]] argued that Widodo's decision represented another domino falling after the United States pushed Russia out of the dollar system, noting that a country of 278 million people would no longer use the American financial architecture and that dollar-denominated savings were becoming correspondingly riskier.[1]
