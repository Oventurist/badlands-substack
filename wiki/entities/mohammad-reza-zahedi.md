# Mohammad Reza Zahedi

Mohammad Reza Zahedi was a senior commander in the [[quds-force|Quds Force]], the elite foreign espionage and paramilitary arm of [[iran|Iran]]'s [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

He was killed on Monday, April 1, 2024, when suspected Israeli warplanes bombed Iran's embassy compound in Damascus, [[syria|Syria]].[1] The IRGC said seven Iranian military advisers died in the strike, including Zahedi and two other senior commanders.[1] The attack marked a major escalation in [[israel|Israel]]'s war with its regional adversaries and was the first Israeli strike on the embassy compound itself.[1] See [[2024-israeli-strike-on-iranian-embassy-in-damascus]].[1]
