# Brad Getz

Brad Getz is a host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], appearing on two programs in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

He co-hosts **Altered State** with [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]] on Wednesdays at 9pm ET. The program examines what its hosts describe as the slow, engineered degradation of American society, spotlighting historical psychological operations while attempting to point audiences toward a more sovereign and hopeful future.[1]

Getz also co-hosts **The Friggin' Friday Night Show!** with Craig Mason on Fridays at 9pm ET, billed as the place "where Logic and Reason intersect," parsing the week's news in an entertaining format.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

In "President Trump's Call to Arms" (March 7, 2023), Getz analysed the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]], describing it as a "quotable masterpiece" marking "the beginning of the next stage in this war."[4] The essay is unusually autobiographical: Getz described himself as "a near two-decade long supporter of GWB and the Bush family in general" who found [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s 2016 demolition of [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] "jarring to behold" but credited it with laying "the groundwork for my true Awakening."[4] He argued that [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] had blurred party lines and produced the [[rino|RINO]] faction, traced the pattern back through the Bush family to [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] and their "fellow CFR infiltrators," and summarized it as "infiltration over invasion."[4] Getz linked Trump's rejection of endless wars to [[qanon|Q]] drops 119 and 489 on the auditing of war spending, framed the 2024 election as "a Battle for the True United States of America," and closed by pledging that Trump "intends to finish what he started, and I intend to help him."[4] The piece carried the standard disclaimer that Badlands features represent the opinions of contributing authors, and directed readers to more of his work at The Logical Faction Press.[4]

## Commentary on Trump's Meta reinstatement (January 2023)

In a January 2023 Badlands News Brief, Getz commented on [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s announcement that it would restore [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[facebook|Facebook]] and [[instagram|Instagram]] accounts, calling it "a symbolic admission of wrongful suspension in light of the Congressional Jan 6th Witch Hunt" and describing the hearings as a dud that mainly highlighted their organizers' vindictive nature.[3] He likened the platforms' shifting posture to the belated remorse expressed by judges after the Salem witch trials, while stating that he did not believe Meta president [[nick-clegg|Nick Clegg]] or other Facebook executives felt any remorse themselves.[3] Getz tied the reversal to the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] disclosures of government meddling — a violation he suspected Meta shared — and predicted the company would use the reinstatement's restrictions and suspension threats "to the fullest and in their typical vindictive manner."[3]
