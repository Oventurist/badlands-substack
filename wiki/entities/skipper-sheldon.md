# Skipper Sheldon

**Skipper Sheldon** is the captain character in the film [[white-squall|White Squall]], treated in Badlands Media commentary as an archetypal stand-in for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] within the [[qanon|Q]] "comm" surrounding the film.[1]

## Role in the film

In the plot, Skipper Sheldon leads a rag-tag group of boys invited to go on an adventure at sea.[1] Out at sea they must learn to work together and overcome the challenges thrown at them by the forces of nature, finding themselves in unknown territory where the only way home is to coordinate sufficiently among themselves to run their ship.[1] Each boy must overcome his own internal challenges in order to become part of the team, and they must act as a team or perish; Skipper enables this by serving as captain of the crew, providing marching orders and guidance.[1]

## As an archetype for Trump

The April 25, 2023 essay "The Significance of White Squall" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] argues that [[anons|anons]] needed a sufficiently strong leader to rally around in the same way the boys needed Skipper, and that it is no coincidence that the archetypes of Trump and Skipper bear a great resemblance to each other.[1] The essay adds that the [[4chan|4chan]] board where Q posted was a markedly masculine space in which nothing short of a strong masculine leader would do.[1]

## The trial

At the end of the film Skipper — like Trump — finds himself on trial, and is ultimately rescued by his crew, who continue to stand by him even though "polite society" wishes to penalise him for taking the boys on the adventure.[1] The essay reads this as symbolising both that society is not yet ready to partake in the gifts anons have to offer and as a blatant prediction of Trump's own legal predicament.[1] Anons are said to stand by Trump not literally in a courtroom but by assembling [[q-proofs|Q proofs]], researching his presidency and publishing their findings, and it is that unity of mission which redeems both Skipper and the voyage itself.[1]
