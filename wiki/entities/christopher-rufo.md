# Christopher Rufo

Christopher Rufo is described as one of the more influential right-wing personalities in the American culture war and is cited as an example of conservative anti-[[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] advocacy.[1]

## Tony Kinnett tweet

On December 20, 2021, Rufo tweeted in response to the firing of Tony Kinnett, an Indianapolis teacher who had posted a video about critical race theory being taught in public schools: "Outrageous: this brave teacher stood against critical race theory indoctrination in his school district—and they fired him."[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] reads the tweet as correctly highlighting the absurdity of a school terminating an employee for holding a viewpoint on a contentious issue shared by roughly half the nation.[1]

## Assessment

DeLarme states he agrees with Rufo — as with [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] and [[bari-weiss|Bari Weiss]] — in these specific instances, holding that people should be allowed opinions no matter how unpalatable and that it is dangerous for employers to enforce beliefs through threat of firing.[1] The distinction he draws is that he did not stop believing this after October 7, 2023, unlike the right-wing free-speech advocates who fell silent when Americans were fired over criticism of Israel.[1]
