# Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer who died in New York in 1943.[1] Upon his death, his papers were taken into federal custody, and the engineer [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] — an uncle of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — was tasked with reviewing some of them.[1]

## Role in contemporary technology narratives

Tesla's unaccounted-for research materials remain a recurring theme in alternative-media speculation about suppressed or classified technology. Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] noted in June 2026 that some of the trunks of Tesla papers are still unaccounted for, and suggested that technologies rolled out by the Trump administration under the banner of [[quantum-computing|quantum computing]] might in fact draw on previously hidden capabilities.[1] The speculation followed Trump's reference to his uncle during the June 2026 quantum executive order signing.[1]

## Seizure of Tesla's papers and the Trump connection (Carlson, 2024)

A June 15, 2024 Badlands feature by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounted that in early 1943, two days after Tesla's death, the [[fbi|FBI]] ordered the Office of Alien Property Custodian to seize his belongings, and that MIT physicist [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] — uncle of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — was called in to analyse the artifacts, concluding after a three-day investigation that nothing in them would constitute a hazard in unfriendly hands.[2] Carlson argued that the assessment deliberately understated the material's significance, observing that the Van de Graaff generators John G. Trump helped develop afterwards closely resemble the Tesla coil, and that his lab director's remark about being able to make "death rays" echoed what Tesla was said to be working on before his death.[2]

Carlson speculated that John G. Trump passed on to his nephew what he had learned from Tesla's materials — miraculous medical procedures, cheap or free energy and powerful weapons — and that Donald Trump has withheld public discussion of it to avoid alerting the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[2] He extended the thread to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], noting that Musk named his company after Tesla, became the leading military contractor of Trump's presidency, and then purchased Twitter.[2]
