# Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer who died in New York in 1943.[1] Upon his death, his papers were taken into federal custody, and the engineer [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] — an uncle of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — was tasked with reviewing some of them.[1]

## Role in contemporary technology narratives

Tesla's unaccounted-for research materials remain a recurring theme in alternative-media speculation about suppressed or classified technology. Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] noted in June 2026 that some of the trunks of Tesla papers are still unaccounted for, and suggested that technologies rolled out by the Trump administration under the banner of [[quantum-computing|quantum computing]] might in fact draw on previously hidden capabilities.[1] The speculation followed Trump's reference to his uncle during the June 2026 quantum executive order signing.[1]

## In "Soul Governance"

The Badlands Media essay [[soul-governance|Soul Governance]] cites Tesla's preoccupation with the numbers 3, 6 and 9, asserting that "he wasn't theorizing, he was pointing to the divine coil of life itself."[4] The essay groups this with [[walter-russell|Walter Russell]]'s "universal heartbeat," spiral mathematics, sacred geometry and Phi-ratio symmetry as evidence that the [[logos|Logos]] is encoded in the human body, framing the whole as "the physics of remembrance" rather than metaphor.[4]

## Cited in Structures of Control

Part 3 of Badlands Media's *Structures of Control* series cites Tesla's advancements in wireless energy as having been suppressed "to preserve centralized power structures," offering him as the archetype of its claim that breakthroughs in energy, medicine and cognitive enhancement are withheld or repurposed by the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[6] The reference sits alongside [[mk-ultra|MKUltra]], the [[project-stargate|Stargate Project]] and the [[gateway-process|Gateway Process]] in the essay's catalogue of technological suppression underpinning the [[manufactured-5d|manufactured 5D]].[6]

## Seizure of Tesla's papers and the Trump connection (Carlson, 2024)

A June 15, 2024 Badlands feature by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounted that in early 1943, two days after Tesla's death, the [[fbi|FBI]] ordered the Office of Alien Property Custodian to seize his belongings, and that MIT physicist [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] — uncle of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — was called in to analyse the artifacts, concluding after a three-day investigation that nothing in them would constitute a hazard in unfriendly hands.[2] Carlson argued that the assessment deliberately understated the material's significance, observing that the Van de Graaff generators John G. Trump helped develop afterwards closely resemble the Tesla coil, and that his lab director's remark about being able to make "death rays" echoed what Tesla was said to be working on before his death.[2]

Carlson speculated that John G. Trump passed on to his nephew what he had learned from Tesla's materials — miraculous medical procedures, cheap or free energy and powerful weapons — and that Donald Trump has withheld public discussion of it to avoid alerting the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[2] He extended the thread to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], noting that Musk named his company after Tesla, became the leading military contractor of Trump's presidency, and then purchased Twitter.[2]

## The Long Island towers and wartime intelligence

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] records that Tesla's towers were financed by J.P. Morgan together with [[thomas-fortune-ryan|Thomas Fortune Ryan]], the manager of New York's [[sleepy-hollow-club|Sleepy Hollow Club]], before the outbreak of World War I.[3] In 1915 [[william-james-flynn|William James Flynn]] tracked down the Long Island wireless station transmitting to German intelligence, and his interception of messages to the German high command supplied the legal justification for the United States government's seizure and destruction of both the [[wardenclyffe-tower|Wardenclyffe Tower]] and the Telefunken Wireless Station at Sayville during the war.[3] By July 1917 the Navy, under the leadership of [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]], ordered the demolition of Wardenclyffe on the basis of naval intelligence reports that German spies were using the tower to communicate.[3] Ehret raises the speculation that the 186-foot tower was the "mysterious means" of transatlantic communication referred to in a 1917 Office of Naval Intelligence report on [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], noting Crowley's "magickal workings" on Long Island and his association with Tesla's intimate friend [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]].[3]
