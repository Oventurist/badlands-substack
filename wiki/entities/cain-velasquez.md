# Cain Velasquez

Cain Velasquez is a former UFC heavyweight champion who, in March 2025, was sentenced to five years in prison after pleading no contest to charges including attempted murder stemming from a 2022 shooting.[1]

## Sentencing

Velasquez, then 42, appeared at a sentencing hearing on Monday, March 24, 2025, at the Santa Clara County Hall of Justice in San Jose, California, where he received a five-year sentence minus time served.[1] Five years from the day of his arrest would be February 28, 2027.[1] According to multiple reporters in the courtroom, including veteran MMA journalist Josh Gross, Judge Arthur Bolcanegra said Velasquez would be credited with 1,283 days, meaning he would have to serve 542 additional days behind bars.[1]

Roughly seventy spectators attended the hearing.[1] Impact statements were read from Velasquez, his 15-year-old daughter, and his wife, in addition to statements from shooting target Harry Goularte and Goularte's family.[1]

## Background of the 2022 incident

The 2022 incident involved a high-speed chase during which Velasquez fired a handgun at a fleeing vehicle carrying a man who worked at a daycare and who allegedly molested Velasquez's four-year-old child.[1] That underlying molestation trial had not been decided as of the sentencing and was scheduled for June; the daycare was forced to close over the allegations.[1]

## Reaction

Reactions divided sharply. Some argued Velasquez got off easy, noting the charges typically carry a sentence of 30 years to life, while many in the MMA community saw a just man unjustly sentenced for stepping outside the bounds of the law where the law had failed.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that five years with time served was "orders of magnitude better than many around Velasquez feared," observing that the 42-year-old father and all-time great athlete would be released before he turned 45.[1] Burning Bright described Velasquez as a stoic, immovable fighter during his time atop the UFC heavyweight division and added, "I suspect any kid would be lucky to call him dad."[1]
