# Derrick Jensen

**Derrick Jensen** is an American author and anti-civilization activist who corresponded with [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]] and went on to co-found the eco-anarchist movement [[deep-green-resistance|Deep Green Resistance]].[1]

## Correspondence with Kaczynski

In a 1998 letter to Jensen, Kaczynski wrote: "I agree with you that civilization is a curse and should be eliminated — if possible. But unlike you, I am not confident that civilization will go down in the reasonably near future… I strongly disagree with your statement that 'it is our primary task' to see that civilization goes down 'at a minimum cost to human…life.' I think we have a desperate struggle ahead of us, and if we pull our punches, we are sure to lose."[1] In the same letter Kaczynski made his [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] convictions explicit, arguing that the human race "will have to pass through fire" and be subject to the same population-collapse dynamics as any over-numerous species.[1]

## Deep Green Resistance

Jensen became a guru of the ecology movement in his own right, publishing some fifty books promoting eco-activism.[1] In 2011 he co-founded Deep Green Resistance, a California-based eco-anarchy movement that [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as having deep penetration into Canada.[1] The organization advocates the destruction of major infrastructure worldwide in a bid to "save nature from humanity," and its "Decisive Ecological Warfare" doctrine divides adherents into "above grounders" who work publicly and "under grounders" who "engage in limited attacks on infrastructure, especially energy infrastructure."[1]
