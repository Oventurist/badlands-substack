# Constellation Energy

Constellation Energy is a large American electricity generator and the operator of the country's biggest fleet of nuclear reactors.[1]

## Three Mile Island restart deal

In late September 2024, Constellation and [[microsoft]] unveiled a $1.6 billion power purchase agreement to restart a dormant reactor at Pennsylvania's [[three-mile-island-nuclear-power-plant|Three Mile Island]] plant in 2028, supplying power for Microsoft's data center operations.[1] The deal was reported alongside the [[department-of-energy]] loan for the [[palisades-nuclear-plant]] as evidence of a dawning new U.S. nuclear age after years of false starts, driven by the energy demands of the AI buildout.[1]
