# Charles Lee

General Charles Lee was a [[continental-army|Continental Army]] general captured by [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]] in December 1776, an episode the Patriot Chronicles treats as inadvertently decisive for the American cause.[1]

## Capture in New Jersey

Leading a scouting party in New Jersey in December 1776, Tarleton came upon a farmhouse occupied by Lee.[1] Lee was caught in his pajamas with no guard posted — a demonstration of his ineptitude, in the article's judgment — and Tarleton threatened to burn the house down with everyone inside unless Lee surrendered, which he did.[1]

## Rivalry with Washington

Unbeknownst to Tarleton, Lee was a hated rival of [[george-washington|George Washington]] with plans in place to usurp him as commander of the Continental Army.[1] Had Lee's coup succeeded, the article argues, Washington's bold tactics at Trenton one year later would never have transpired and the Revolution could have collapsed just six months after the signing of the Declaration — from which perspective the author suggests Tarleton's capture of Lee made him, in that one act, a great patriot.[1] Tarleton was promoted to lieutenant colonel by [[henry-clinton|Sir Henry Clinton]] shortly afterward.[1]
