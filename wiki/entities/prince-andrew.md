# Prince Andrew

**Prince Andrew** is the British royal figure whose entanglement in the Jeffrey Epstein affair is treated in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] corpus as a pivotal moment in the public unravelling of that story.

## Treatment in the corpus

In Badlands Blitz Episode 7, published February 21, 2026, the hosts open the episode with what they describe as "the Epstein Web's expansion and subversion that led to the Prince Andrew inflection point." Three claims are compressed into that phrase. The first is that the Epstein network — the "Web" — has been expanding in public view. The second is that this expansion has been accompanied by subversion, implying that the widening of the story is being managed or redirected as it unfolds. The third is that Prince Andrew constitutes the "inflection point" of that process: the juncture at which the trajectory of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein Narrative]] changed direction.

The source does not spell out in the written post what the inflection consists of, the substantive discussion having taken place in the episode's video segment. Within the analytical vocabulary of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], however, the placement is significant. A figure of Prince Andrew's standing being surrendered to the story is the kind of event the programme would ordinarily read either as a genuine "Actual" — a real loss inflicted on entrenched interests — or as a controlled concession, a high-value but ultimately expendable figure offered up to satisfy public appetite while the network's more consequential members remain shielded. The episode's subtitle, which speaks of "[[deep-state|Deep State]] Burn Cards," suggests the latter reading is at least in play.
