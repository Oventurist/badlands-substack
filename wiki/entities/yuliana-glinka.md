# Yuliana Glinka

Yuliana Glinka (1844–1918) was a Russian mystic identified by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a central figure in the introduction of [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] into Russian political life.[1] She was a close colleague of the occultist [[gerard-encausse|Gerard Encausse]] (Papus), who became a trusted consultant to Czar [[nicholas-ii|Nicholas II]] in 1901 and introduced the Czar and his wife Alexandra to the Protocols as evidence of an evil Jewish conspiracy to take over the world.[1]

The Russian publicist and Theosophist [[mikhail-menshikov|Mikhail Menshikov]], the first to publish the Protocols in 1902, described Glinka's method of transmission: the "lady of fashion" invited him to her house to see a document of vast importance, received him in an elegant apartment speaking perfect French, told him she was in direct contact with the world beyond the grave, inducted him into the mysteries of [[theosophy|Theosophy]], and only then "initiated him into the mysteries of the Protocols."[1] Ehret presents this episode as evidence that the forgery emerged from occult and Theosophical circles rather than from any authentic documentary source.[1]
