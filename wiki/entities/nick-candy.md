# Nick Candy

Nick Candy is a British businessman who serves as treasurer of [[reform-uk|Reform UK]].[1]

## Mar-a-Lago meeting

On Monday, December 16, 2024, Candy joined Reform leader [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]] for an hour-long meeting with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] at [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Mar-a-Lago club in Florida, amid rumours that Musk was preparing to donate $100 million to the party — which would be by far the largest donation in British electoral history.[1] In a joint statement Farage and Candy described the meeting as "great," said they had "learned a great deal about the Trump ground game," and indicated they would have "ongoing discussions" with the Tesla boss on other areas.[1] They added: "We only have one more chance left to save the West and we can do great things together," thanking Trump for the use of Mar-a-Lago and declaring "the special relationship is alive and well."[1]

Neither man mentioned money.[1] According to *The Sunday Times*, leading businessmen and Conservative Party officials believed Musk could hand over the cash as a "f*** you Starmer" payment amid his feud with the prime minister, [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]].[1]
