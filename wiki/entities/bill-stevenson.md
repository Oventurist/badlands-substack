# Bill Stevenson

Bill Stevenson is a Delaware businessman and the first husband of [[jill-biden|Jill Biden]].[1]

On Sunday, December 28, 2025, Stevenson called 911 to report a domestic dispute at his Oak Hill home in Delaware and was present when authorities arrived, according to law enforcement accounts reported by TMZ.[1] Responding officers found his wife, 64-year-old Linda Stevenson, unresponsive in the living room; life-saving measures were attempted before she was pronounced dead at the scene.[1] Dispatch audio from the incident referenced "cardiac arrest."[1] The item was carried as a bonus story in the Badlands News Brief without further commentary.[1]
