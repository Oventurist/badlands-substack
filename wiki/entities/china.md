# China

## Open-source AI strategy (2026)

In the July 2026 dispute over [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]], Trump-aligned technologists argued that restricting open-source AI development would weaken US competitiveness while benefiting foreign rivals, particularly China.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] went further, writing that China was "continuing to rug-pull the closed-source and closed-weight American AI industry by timing strategic releases of open-source and open-weight models" that simultaneously accelerate the industry as a whole while blunting Western attempts to slow it down.[1] On his account, these releases grant President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the narrative shielding required to resist the regulation push and continue the acceleration path — meaning China is "actually HELPING protect the future of an open-source, open-weight, FREE AI world" while the biggest voices in the American AI industry try to get Western governments to stop it.[1] He concluded: "Good thing Trump and [[xi-jinping|Xi]] are Sovereign Bros."[1]
