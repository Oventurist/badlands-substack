# Larry Diamond

Larry Diamond is an American political scientist and a longtime advisor to the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]], where he is associated with the study and promotion of democratization.[1]

Diamond is cited, alongside fellow NED advisor Rachel Kleinfeld and scholars Steven Levitsky and Lucan Way, as among the endowment-linked experts who have publicly criticized [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the Republican Party as antidemocratic.[1] Levitsky and Way, for their part, described the GOP as an "extremist, antidemocratic force" in 2022.[1] Critics of the NED point to this cluster of statements as evidence that the organization's intellectual leadership has adopted an explicitly anti-Trump posture.[1]
