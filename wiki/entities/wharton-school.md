# Wharton School

The **Wharton School** is the business school of the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]]. In the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" it is identified as the incubator of financial technocrats within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]] compliance arm.[1]

## Alleged role

The article says Wharton "births the foot soldiers of ESG enforcement and DEI policy," producing financial technocrats tied to [[blackrock|BlackRock]], Davos and Biden-aligned foundations.[1] Alongside the fraternity [[zeta-psi|Zeta Psi]] and a "Wharton Inner Circle," it is described as a staging ground for BlackRock-trained executives later embedded in ESG compliance, central bank digital currency strategy and [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] think tanks.[1] BlackRock CEO [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]]'s association with Wharton and Penn alumni is offered as evidence that "academic grooming leads to economic enforcement — not by accident, but by design."[1]

## Alleged unravelling

The article reports that Wharton's association with Fink and ESG enforcement came under attack from within its own alumni base, with donors issuing ultimatums to abandon the ideology or forfeit legacy capital, and that Wharton paused its ESG strategy workshops indefinitely as part of a broader institutional retreat.[1]
