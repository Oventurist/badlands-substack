# Kurdistan Workers Party

The Kurdistan Workers Party (PKK) is a Kurdish militant organisation that has fought for self-rule inside Turkey since 1984.[1] It is designated a terrorist organisation by both the United States and the European Union.[1]

## Role in Turkish policy toward Syria

The PKK is central to Ankara's objections to United States policy in northern Syria. Turkey equates the American-allied, Kurdish-led [[syrian-democratic-forces|Syrian Democratic Forces]] with the PKK and deems the SDF a top national security threat.[1] On that basis Turkish Foreign Minister [[hakan-fidan|Hakan Fidan]] reiterated in January 2025 his country's demand that Washington end its alliance with the SDF, while offering that Turkey was ready to take control of the roughly one dozen [[islamic-state|ISIS]] prisons and detention camps located in SDF-controlled areas of northern Syria.[1] Fidan argued that the SDF's contribution to the war against ISIS had been limited to managing those prisons.[1]
