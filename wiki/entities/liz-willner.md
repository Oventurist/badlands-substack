# Liz Willner

Liz Willner is the founder of [[openvaers|OpenVAERS]], a project that republishes reports submitted to the U.S. Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System in a publicly searchable form.[1]

## Statements on long-term vaccine surveillance

Following a February 15, 2024 hearing of the House [[select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]], at which [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] and [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] officials were asked whether active extended surveillance of early [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccine]] recipients was under way, Willner told [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]]: "There is no system in place for long-term vaccine safety surveillance in this country."[1]

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted that Willner's statement was deliberately general — referring to vaccines as a category rather than only to COVID-19 or mRNA products — and argued that OpenVAERS' work established that officials who dismissed concerned parents as "anti-vaxxers" were themselves not tracking long-term outcomes of mandated products.[1]
