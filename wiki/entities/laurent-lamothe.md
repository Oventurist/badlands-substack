# Laurent Lamothe

Laurent Lamothe is a Haitian businessman and politician who served as Prime Minister of [[haiti|Haiti]].[1]

## Commentary during the 2024 crisis

In March 2024, as gangs attacked electrical stations and plunged Haiti into darkness overnight, Lamothe called for law and order to be restored on the island, describing a "total void of law and order" amid the armed campaign led by [[jimmy-cherizier|Jimmy "Barbecue" Cherizier]].[1]

Lamothe also drew attention to the handling of international assistance to Haiti, stating that "the international community promised $9 billion in aid but Haiti only received half of it, and out of the half most of it went to the US military."[1] Badlands Media commentary seized on this claim, speculating whether by "military" Lamothe might have meant the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], and framing the unrest as revealing new allegations about the disposition of aid provided to the nation.[1]
