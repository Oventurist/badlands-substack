# Balenciaga

Balenciaga is a popular luxury fashion brand that faced widespread backlash in late 2022 over advertising campaigns critics said normalized child sexual abuse.[1]

## 2022 advertising scandal

The campaigns included photographs of children holding teddy bears dressed in bondage harnesses, as well as strategically placed court documents referencing child sexual abuse material — the term anti-exploitation advocates prefer to "child pornography."[1] The public moved quickly to condemn the brand, and the controversy eventually produced an apology from Balenciaga's creative director.[1]

## Aftermath

The [[national-center-on-sexual-exploitation|National Center on Sexual Exploitation]] observed that only a few months after the Balenciaga scandal, mainstream fashion again welcomed sexual abuse and exploitation, pointing to designer [[luis-de-javier|Luis De Javier]]'s [[new-york-fashion-week|New York Fashion Week]] collaboration with [[pornhub]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[absolutetruth1776]] opened his commentary on that collaboration with the line "Move over Balenciaga," arguing the pattern showed elite culture "outing themselves nearly everyday."[1]
