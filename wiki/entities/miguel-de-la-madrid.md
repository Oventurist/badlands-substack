# Miguel de la Madrid

Miguel de la Madrid was the president of Mexico who preceded [[carlos-salinas-de-gortari|Carlos Salinas de Gortari]] and, as leader of the [[institutional-revolutionary-party|PRI]], presided over the [[1988-mexican-presidential-election|1988 presidential election]].[1] His significance in the Badlands Media account rests on an autobiography that began circulating in Mexico in March 2004, in which, across 850 pages, he gave what the New York Times called "the firmest confirmation to date" that the 1988 elections were rigged.[1]

De la Madrid recounted that on election night the secretary of the interior advised him that initial results were running heavily against the PRI, with Salinas losing badly to opposition leader Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas in areas around the capital.[1] With the public demanding returns, the government instead lied and said the computer system tabulating the votes had crashed, beginning what the Times described as "the frantic staging of a fraudulent victory."[1] Political analysts said the revelations were not new, but the memoir put a former president's name to them.[1]
