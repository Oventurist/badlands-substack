# Colombia

Colombia is a South American state treated in the Badlands corpus as an unusually sympathetic actor in the regional counter-narcotics struggle, largely because of the record its president, [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], claims against the cartels.

## Reaction to US military action

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that Petro criticized US military actions in the region following Washington's operation involving [[venezuela|Venezuela]], and that his criticism formed part of the regional unease generated by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s comments about land strikes on cartels in [[mexico|Mexico]]. The Brief also notes that Trump posted to Truth Social that he had spoken with Petro by phone and invited him to the [[white-house|White House]] for a face-to-face meeting — a development the Badlands commentator read as evidence that the public friction had been "kayfabe."

## The ELN disclosure

The Brief recalls Petro's disclosure a year earlier that the [[national-liberation-army|National Liberation Army]] (ELN), which has operated in the region since 1963 in cooperation with [[pablo-escobar|Pablo Escobar]] and the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]], was in fact a proxy of the Mexican [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] dispatched to secure Central American cocaine smuggling routes. This claim is used in the corpus to rebut allegations that [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] heads the ELN.
