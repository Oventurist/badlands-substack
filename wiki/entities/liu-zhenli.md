# Liu Zhenli

Liu Zhenli is a senior Chinese military officer named in January 2026 social-media reports alleging a wave of arrests at the top of the People's Liberation Army.[1]

## Alleged January 2026 arrests

Reports circulating on social media, amplified by an account quoted by General Michael Flynn, claimed that [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]], Liu Zhenli and [[zhong-shaojun|Zhong Shaojun]] had been arrested simultaneously, and that all of Zhang Youxia's family members had also been taken into custody.[1] The reports said the news had circulated among the top echelons of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] leadership.[1] A follow-up post alleged that seventeen people were detained in a single sweep, carried out by [[wang-xiaohong|Wang Xiaohong]]'s special service bureau.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the three named generals come from the "red families" — the original founding families of the CCP, which control most of the state-owned natural resources and infrastructure — and framed the episode as a failed military coup against [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1] The source account itself cautioned that the information could not be verified and suggested waiting to see whether Zhang Youxia appeared at the Spring Festival group meeting.[1]

## Official investigation

Chinese authorities subsequently announced an investigation into Liu Zhenli, alongside Central Military Commission Vice Chairman Gen. [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]], for "serious violations of discipline and law" — a phrase conventionally indicating corruption.[2] The probes were revealed over the weekend of January 25–26, 2026, and the People's Liberation Army Daily accused the pair of "seriously betraying" the Communist Party's trust, "trampling on and undermining" the [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]], and threatening the authority of its chairman, [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[2] The removals left the CMC with only Xi and one other member, contributing to a leadership void in the senior [[peoples-liberation-army|PLA]] ranks.[2] See [[china-military-purge-2026|2026 Chinese military purge]].[2]
