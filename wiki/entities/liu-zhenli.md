# Liu Zhenli

Liu Zhenli is a senior general of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and a member of the Central Military Commission, the party organ that directs China's armed forces. He appears in the Badlands corpus solely in connection with reports of a purge of the Chinese military high command in January 2026.

## Reported arrest, January 2026

The January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] ([[badlands-brief-577|"ICE Getting Boiled, Chinese Coup Chaos, & Carney Bends The Knee"]]) relays a widely circulated social-media claim, quoted by Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry from an X account cited by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]], that "Zhang Youxia, Liu Zhenli, and Zhong Shaojun have been arrested simultaneously." The Brief's commentary states that seventeen officials were ultimately taken in a single sweep, that the three named generals belong to the "red families" descended from the founding lineages of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], and that those families control most of China's state-owned natural resources and infrastructure.

In the commentary's reading, the arrests represent [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] moving decisively against a rival faction rather than a sign of regime weakness, part of a broader pattern in which — according to reporting attributed to the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] and the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] — more than fifty senior officers and defense-industry executives have been investigated or removed since the summer of 2023 across the army, air force, navy, strategic-missile force, paramilitary police, and major theater commands including the one focused on Taiwan. As with the other names in this episode, the reports are unverified and the source itself notes that the information could not be confirmed. See also [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and Wang Xiaohong.
