# Hong Kong Democracy Council

The Hong Kong Democracy Council is a United States–based advocacy organization working on behalf of the [[hong-kong-pro-democracy-movement|Hong Kong pro-democracy movement]].[1] Its Managing Director, [[samuel-chu|Samuel Chu]], has been a leading overseas voice describing how the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] crippled the movement, with its leaders now "in jail, under house arrest, living in exile, or just stripped of their rights to speak out."[1]

Through Chu, the Council has argued that the United States and the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] should re-engage to uphold the guarantees made to [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]], and has advanced concrete measures including fighting censorship, circumventing a coming internet firewall, extracting activists targeted by Beijing, strengthening civil society groups, and pressuring Hong Kong businesses not to cooperate with repression.[1] Chu's advocacy has itself made him a target: he was placed on a CCP fugitive list despite holding American citizenship since 1996.[1]
