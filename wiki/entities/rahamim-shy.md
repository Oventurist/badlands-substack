# Rahamim "Rami" Shy

Rahamim "Rami" Shy is a former senior policy adviser to the Obama administration who, at age 46, appeared before a British court charged with child sex offences.[1] Court documents reported by the Daily Mail state that Shy — who coordinated the United States government's strategy to combat terrorists from [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and the [[taliban]] — is accused of arranging the commission of a child sex offence.[1] He is additionally charged with possession of two category C indecent images of children and with possessing a prohibited image of a child.[1]

## Background and government service

Shy is a US citizen resident in New Jersey who worked for the [[white-house]] under President [[barack-obama]] and Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton]].[1] Commentary published in the [[badlands-news-brief]] describes his portfolio as part of the Obama administration's plan to "keep an eye on" the Taliban and Al Qaeda, a role he is said to have filled both as a high-level consultant to the [[nato]]-led [[international-security-assistance-force|International Security Assistance Force (ISAF)]] and in a senior position at the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|US Treasury Department]] tasked with ending the financing of terrorism.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] catalogued the network of people and institutions to which Shy is connected, listing not only Barack Obama but also Hillary Clinton, [[john-podesta]], NATO's military structures, Citi Bank Group, the US Treasury Department, and a set of high-profile radical Islamist organizations.[1]

## Arrest and prosecution

Shy was arrested in late February by Bedfordshire Police and charged the following day.[1] He appeared at Luton Crown Court by video link from HMP Bedford wearing a grey prison-issue tracksuit, was not asked to enter a plea to any of the charges, and was remanded in custody ahead of a further hearing scheduled for June.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The Badlands treatment of the case placed it within the outlet's recurring "Shark Fin" template associated with writer [[burning-bright]], with GhostofBasedPatrickHenry calling Shy "a pedophile shark."[1] The commentary argued that during Shy's tenure in counterterrorism roles between 2008 and 2014 radical Islamic terrorism grew to unprecedented levels and [[isis]] emerged, establishing a territorial claim across Syria and Iraq larger in area than many small countries.[1] It further asserted a connection between Shy's work and Hillary Clinton's role in the overthrow of the Libyan government and the killing of [[muammar-gaddafi]], whose looted private armory was said to have been used by the CIA to arm ISIS — the substance, in this telling, of the 2012 Benghazi scandal.[1]

The same commentary restated the Badlands thesis that ISIS was created in order to overthrow Syrian President [[bashar-al-assad]], who had rebuffed Qatari (described as Muslim Brotherhood/CIA-aligned) requests to build a pipeline running from Qatar through Syria to compete with the Iran–Russia route, with Assad and Vladimir Putin cast as enduring allies; the Qataris were said to have turned to Secretary of State Clinton for assistance.[1] Invoking Colonel Towner-Watkins' analysis on Breaking History and The AlphaWarrior Show, the writer characterized the whole affair as bearing the signature of [[operation-gladio]] and dubbed Shy "the new Gladio-poster-boy."[1]
