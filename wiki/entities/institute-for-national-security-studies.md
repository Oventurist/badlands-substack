# Institute for National Security Studies (INSS)

The Institute for National Security Studies is an Israeli think tank based at Tel Aviv University, whose Iran and Shiite Axis program is directed by [[raz-zimmt|Raz Zimmt]].[1]

## 2026 Iran analysis

In May 2026 the institute's analysis, delivered by Zimmt, held that [[iran|Iran]] had greater incentive to resist U.S. demands after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] paused planned renewed airstrikes at the request of Gulf allies.[1] The assessment concluded that Tehran read the pause as evidence that Washington and its partners fear the fallout of renewed war more than Iran does, and that Trump's statements are unreliable — so Iran should hold firm on nuclear enrichment and demand upfront guarantees including an end to the conflict, sanctions relief, and recognition of Iranian control over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the publication of such analysis as a sign that the Israel lobby is losing patience with Trump, whom he credited with locking the region into a "perpetual holding pattern that allows neither peace nor war to break out."[1]
