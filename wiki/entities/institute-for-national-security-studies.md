# Institute for National Security Studies

The Institute for National Security Studies (INSS) is an Israeli think tank affiliated with Tel Aviv University, producing analysis on regional security and Iranian affairs.

## Analysis of Iranian negotiating leverage

The May 20, 2026 Badlands Brief summarizes an INSS analysis by [[raz-zimmt|Raz Zimmt]], director of the institute's Iran and Shiite Axis program, arguing that [[iran|Iran]] gained incentive to resist U.S. demands after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] paused planned renewed airstrikes at the request of Gulf allies hoping to revive talks. According to Zimmt, Tehran concluded both that the United States and its partners fear the fallout of renewed war more than Iran does, and that Trump's statements are unreliable — leading Iran to withhold concessions on its nuclear enrichment infrastructure and to demand upfront guarantees including an end to the conflict, sanctions relief or unfrozen assets, and arrangements recognizing Iranian control of the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the publication of such analysis as a sign that the [[israel-lobby|Israel lobby]] and Israeli establishment were losing patience with Trump's approach.
