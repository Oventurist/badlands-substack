# Colossal Biosciences

Colossal Biosciences is an American biotechnology company that describes its mission as bringing back lost species through genetic engineering, a practice its promoters market under the label [[de-extinction]].[1] The company reached mainstream prominence in April 2025 when it announced that three genetically engineered wolf pups, presented as resembling the extinct dire wolf, were living in an undisclosed secure location in the United States.[1]

## The 2025 dire wolf announcement

Researchers at Colossal Biosciences reported on Monday, April 7, 2025 that the wolf pups — ranging in age from three to six months — had long white hair, muscular jaws, and already weighed roughly 80 pounds, with projections that they would reach about 140 pounds at maturity.[1] Dire wolves, which went extinct more than 10,000 years ago, were substantially larger than gray wolves, their closest living relatives.[1] The animals were described as trotting, sleeping and howling at the company's secure facility.[1]

Independent scientists cautioned that the achievement did not mean dire wolves were returning to North American grasslands.[1] Vincent Lynch, a biologist at the University at Buffalo who was not involved in the research, said that "All you can do now is make something look superficially like something else" rather than fully revive an extinct species.[1]

## Reception in independent media

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated the coverage itself as the story, noting that the Associated Press headline — which described the animals as genetically engineered wolves rather than resurrected dire wolves — was markedly more skeptical than the framing used by peer outlets that adopted the "de-extinction" terminology wholesale.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the announcement as "just marketing" and predicted the venture would "probably going to end badly."[1]
