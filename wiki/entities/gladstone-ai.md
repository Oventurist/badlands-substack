# Gladstone AI

Gladstone AI is the firm that produced a U.S. government–commissioned action plan on the national security risks of advanced artificial intelligence, published in March 2024 and reported by [[time-magazine|TIME]].[1]

## Action plan and findings

The report argued that the U.S. government must move "quickly and decisively" to avert substantial national security risks stemming from [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]], which in the worst case could pose an "extinction-level threat to the human species."[1] It stated that "current frontier AI development poses urgent and growing risks to national security," and that the rise of advanced AI and [[artificial-general-intelligence|artificial general intelligence]] had the potential to destabilize global security in ways reminiscent of the introduction of nuclear weapons.[1]

Its three authors worked on the report for more than a year, speaking with more than 200 government employees, experts, and workers at frontier AI companies including [[openai|OpenAI]], Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta.[1] Accounts from some of those conversations suggested that many AI safety workers inside cutting-edge labs were concerned about perverse incentives driving decision-making by the executives who control their companies.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] noted that the report appeared shortly after General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] published an article describing AI as a nuclear-level threat and emphasized the subject at the Patriotic Podcasters conference in Tulsa.[1] Esler criticized the report for omitting what he described as real-time, AI-based psychological warfare run by foreign adversaries, and questioned why it promoted policies that would cripple the U.S. AI industry while enemies used the same technology offensively.[1]
