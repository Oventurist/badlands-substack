# MSNBC

MSNBC is an American cable news network owned through NBCUniversal by [[comcast|Comcast]].

## In the Badlands critique

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] groups MSNBC with [[cnn|CNN]] as a network that had been losing viewers before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rise and was temporarily revived by liberal political obsession during his presidency — a recovery the article dismisses as a temporary sugar high.

The essay singles out MSNBC's programming as an illustration of what it calls the rampant mediocrity of contemporary journalism, describing the network's output as "substance-free affirmations of the modern liberal cult-of-personality talking points" and inviting readers to "try to sit through five minutes of [[joy-reid|Joy Reid]] or [[jen-psaki|Jen Psaki]]'s god awful MSNBC shows." The author notes that he attempts this "semi regularly" and that "it takes a strong stomach," and adds that the same criticism applies to most [[fox-news|Fox News]] hosts.
