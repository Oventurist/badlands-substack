# Los Angeles County District Attorney

The Los Angeles County District Attorney's office is the prosecuting authority for Los Angeles County, California. It appears in Badlands Media's election-integrity coverage as the office that brought, and then abandoned, criminal charges against [[eugene-yu|Eugene Yu]], the chief executive of the election-worker software company [[konnech|Konnech]].[1]

## The Konnech case

In October 2022 the office alleged that Yu had stolen personal information belonging to United States election workers and stored it illegally on servers in the People's Republic of China, in violation of contractual requirements that the data remain in the United States and be accessible only to citizens and permanent residents.[1]

The office subsequently asked the court to dismiss the case without prejudice. Tiffany Blacknell, Director of the Bureau of Communications for the office, said prosecutors were "concerned about both the pace of the investigation and the potential bias in the presentation and investigation of the evidence," and that the office wished to "alert the public in order to ensure transparency in this process."[1] Because the dismissal was without prejudice, the office retains the ability to recharge Yu, as may prosecutors in other jurisdictions where Konnech held contracts.[1]
