# Aleksandr Bogomaz

**Aleksandr Bogomaz** is the governor of Russia's Bryansk Region, a border region adjacent to Ukraine and to the Kursk Region.[1]

## 2024 border incursion announcement

On Sunday, October 27, 2024, Bogomaz announced on Telegram that the Russian military, together with border guards and National Guard units, had repelled an [[2024-bryansk-region-incursion-attempt|attempted incursion]] into the Bryansk Region.[1] He said an "armed group" had sought to cross the border near the long-abandoned village of Manev, and that Russian forces opened fire and forced the intruders to retreat.[1] Bogomaz did not disclose the size of the incursion force or its losses and reported no Russian casualties, stating that the situation in the region remained stable and that the border area was controlled by the regional operations headquarters.[1]
