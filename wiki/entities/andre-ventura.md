# André Ventura

André Ventura is the leader of the Portuguese populist party [[chega|Chega]], which surged to third place in Portugal's March 2024 parliamentary election less than five years after its founding.[1]

Citing the results, Ventura said his party had a rightful place in a new ruling coalition. "We are available to provide a stable government in Portugal," he said, adding that "AD asked for a majority. Today the Portuguese spoke out and said they want a two-party government from AD and Chega."[1] His overture was rebuffed by [[luis-montenegro|Luís Montenegro]], leader of the victorious center-right Democratic Alliance coalition, who had characterized Chega's positions as "often xenophobic, racist, populist and excessively demagogic" and pledged never to govern with the party.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary framed the establishment's descriptions of Ventura's party as narrative framing rather than accurate description, arguing that terms like "populist" and "xenophobic" are deployed against leaders advancing first-principles positions.[1]
