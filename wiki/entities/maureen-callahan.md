# Maureen Callahan

Maureen Callahan is an American columnist who wrote for the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] and who, in 2016, corresponded by email with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] accuser [[sarah-ransome|Sarah Ransome]].[1] That correspondence became part of the court record in the Epstein-related civil litigation and was made public in the batch of documents unsealed on Monday, January 8, 2024.[1]

## The Ransome emails

In the 2016 emails to Callahan, Ransome alleged that an unnamed friend had "sexual relations" with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Epstein's New York home "on regular occasions," and claimed to hold copies of tapes Epstein had recorded of Trump, former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] and [[richard-branson|Richard Branson]] with an unnamed woman.[1] Ransome subsequently retracted the claims in an October 23, 2016 email to Callahan, writing, "I would like to retract everything I have said to you and walk away from this."[1]

Because the exchange ran through a working New York Post columnist, the emails placed a mainstream newsroom directly inside the documentary record of the Epstein case when the filings were unsealed nearly eight years later.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the reporting on the Callahan emails to argue that outlets across the political spectrum were engaged in what he called a conflation campaign designed to attach Epstein-related allegations to Trump, noting that coverage frequently led with the allegations rather than the retraction.[1]
