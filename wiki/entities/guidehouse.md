# Guidehouse

Guidehouse is a professional services and technology vendor that stored corporate data for [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]].[1] In January 2021 a data breach at Guidehouse exposed sensitive Morgan Stanley client information, including Social Security numbers.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

The breach is cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] because [[jen-easterly|Jen Easterly]] was Morgan Stanley's global head of cybersecurity at the time, and in that role would have had oversight of the security of the vendor ecosystem in which Guidehouse operated.[1] Five months after the incident, Easterly was appointed to lead the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]], the agency charged with securing U.S. critical infrastructure — a sequence the Brief presents as undercutting her later categorical assurances about election security.[1] Morgan Stanley went on to have additional cybersecurity scandals after her departure.[1]
