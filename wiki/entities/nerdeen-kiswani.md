# Nerdeen Kiswani

Nerdeen Kiswani is an American pro-Palestinian activist and the founder of [[within-our-lifetime|Within Our Lifetime]] (WOL), a New York City–based protest organization.[1]

## Grand Central Terminal protest

On April 7, 2025, Kiswani posted video to X showing dozens of WOL demonstrators chanting and waving flags inside and at the entrance of New York City's Grand Central Terminal.[1] The action began outside United Nations headquarters at 4:00 p.m., timed to World Health Day and to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s White House visit.[1] Kiswani wrote that the group "took over Grand Central Station," that the NYPD shut down multiple entrances and brought "the heart of NYC to a halt," and that after protesting in front of the UN and the Egyptian embassy, "We are just getting started."[1]
