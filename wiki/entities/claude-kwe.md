# Claude Kwe

Claude Kwe is a Cameroon national and research fellow in the Virus Ecology Section at the Rocky Mountain Laboratory in Montana, a [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]] facility headed by [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]].

The June 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2c0|Badlands Brief]] reports that Kwe and Munster were charged in a federal criminal complaint by the US Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Michigan with conspiracy to smuggle monkeypox into the United States and making false statements to federal authorities. The pair are alleged to have arrived at Detroit Metropolitan Airport on January 25, 2026, after travel from the Republic of Congo during a monkeypox outbreak, carrying a black case they told [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] officers contained diagnostic equipment; investigators say it held 113 vials in Styrofoam coolers, 17 of which contained deactivated monkeypox virus, one chickenpox virus, and two human DNA. The investigation involves the [[fbi|FBI]], Customs and Border Protection, and the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] Office of Inspector General. Kwe faces up to five years in prison if convicted.
