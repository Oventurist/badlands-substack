# Vince Fong

Vince Fong is an American Republican politician from California who served in the California State Assembly before winning a May 2024 special election to the United States House of Representatives.[1]

## 2024 special election

On Tuesday, May 21, 2024, Fong won a special election to complete the remainder of the term of deposed former House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], a term running through January.[1] A McCarthy protégé who also carried the former speaker's endorsement, Fong defeated fellow Republican and Tulare County Sheriff [[mike-boudreaux|Mike Boudreaux]] in California's 20th Congressional District, in the state's Central Valley farm belt.[1]

Fong was backed by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and because of Trump's involvement the race was watched as a possible proxy measure of the former president's clout as he headed toward an all-but-certain November matchup against President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Stated priorities

"With the campaign over, the real work now begins," Fong said in a statement after the win, adding that he would focus on border security, supporting small business, and investing in the water storage critical to the agriculture of his region.[1]
