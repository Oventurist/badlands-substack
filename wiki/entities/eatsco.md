# EATSCO

EATSCO — the Egyptian American Transport and Services Corporation — was a company owned by former [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] agents [[thomas-clines|Thomas Clines]], [[ted-shackley|Theodore Shackley]] and [[richard-secord|Richard Secord]].[1]

[[farhad-azima|Farhad Azima]] supplied air and logistical support to EATSCO beginning in the late 1970s, the origin of his own relationship with the agency.[1] EATSCO was prominently involved in the activities of former CIA agent [[edwin-wilson|Edwin Wilson]], who shipped arms illegally to Libya.[1] The firm appears in Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s account as one of the intelligence-linked ventures whose personnel connected covert arms logistics to the banking failures of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]], notably the collapse of [[indian-springs-state-bank|Indian Springs State Bank]].[1]
