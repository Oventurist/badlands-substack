# Waverly Crenshaw

Waverly Crenshaw is a United States District Judge in the Middle District of Tennessee who dismissed the federal human-smuggling case against [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]] on May 22, 2026.[1]

## The vindictive prosecution ruling

Crenshaw granted Abrego Garcia's motion alleging vindictive prosecution, and [[abc-news|ABC News]] reported that he found the government had not rebutted a presumption of vindictiveness.[1] The dismissal ended a case built on a May 21, 2025 indictment alleging a conspiracy running from about 2016 to about 2025 to bring undocumented immigrants into the United States.[1]

The ruling had immediate consequences beyond the defendant: a public interest group subsequently filed an ethics complaint against Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] over the handling of the prosecution, as noted in a May 31 commentary by [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]].[1]
