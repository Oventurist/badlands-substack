# Waverly Crenshaw

Waverly Crenshaw is a United States District Judge for the Middle District of Tennessee, the federal trial court based in Nashville.

## Dismissal of the Abrego Garcia case

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Judge Crenshaw dismissed the Tennessee human-smuggling case against [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]] on May 22, 2026, after granting the defendant's motion alleging vindictive prosecution. Per [[abc|ABC News]], Crenshaw found that the government had not rebutted a presumption of vindictiveness. The indictment, returned May 21, 2025, had alleged a conspiracy from about 2016 to about 2025 to bring undocumented immigrants into the United States and identified Abrego Garcia as an El Salvador citizen and a member or associate of [[ms-13|MS-13]].

The dismissal triggered an ethics complaint against Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], filed by a public interest group and reported in a May 31, 2026 commentary by [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] — a development invoked in Badlands commentary on the "ending" of the bar associations.
