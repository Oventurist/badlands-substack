# Rogue Hypnotist

**Rogue Hypnotist** (RH) is the pen name of an anonymous author credited in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — What is Clown World?" with putting the [[koryos|Koryos]] "on the map" and making other researchers aware of their existence.[1] The Badlands author says RH deserves to be seen as one of the great researchers of our time and points readers to his book series on the subject.[1]

The specific finding drawn from RH in this installment is his report that the early Koryos werewolf cult were described as giant red-haired albinos — the detail on which the essay builds its proposed link between the cult and the powder-white, red-haired clown costume.[1]

## Trilogy on vampire, werewolf and zombie cults

The June 21, 2023 essay "The Zombie Apocalypse vs. The Great Awakening" describes Rogue Hypnotist as an anonymous author who has written a groundbreaking trilogy on vampire-, werewolf- and zombie-themed cults said to have existed since antiquity and to remain in existence today.[3] From that trilogy the essay takes the definition of a "zombie" as a human being who does not possess a will of his or her own — neither truly dead nor alive, retaining rudimentary motor function and wandering the Earth perpetually hungry.[3] He is also cited among researchers holding that at least one purpose of [[blood-sacrifice|blood-ritual sacrifice]] is to "give the middle finger" to God.[3]

## Recommended in The Werewolf Game

The opening installment of [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] series, published March 18, 2024, recommends Rogue Hypnotist's book [[psychopath-proof|*Psychopath Proof*]] for a thorough treatment of psychopathy, calling it "the single most insightful and useful book I've ever read on the subject."[2] The recommendation appears in a section arguing that learning to profile psychopaths is essential self-defense and that the masses cannot protect themselves against a phenomenon they cannot recognise.[2]


## Epigraph in Part 5

Part 5 of the series, "The Ancient Origin of Modern Conflict," opens with the epigraph "Words are archeological artifacts," attributed to Rogue Hypnotist, a line the essay uses to frame its argument that etymology is the surest route to recovering suppressed history.[4] The same installment names [[wicked-teeth|*Wicked Teeth: the Secret History of Werewolves*]] as one of the few detailed sources available on the [[koryos|Koryos]].[4]

## *Wicked Teeth* on child recruitment

In the seventh installment of the series, "Hell is Organized" (August 8, 2024), Rogue Hypnotist is cited by name as the anonymous author of *Wicked Teeth: the Secret History of Werewolves*, the book from which the Badlands author draws his account of how the [[koryos|Koryos]] recruited children.[5] According to that account the cult groomed recruited children into killers through various drug-induced, ritualized means, described by the Badlands author as "a bit like a 'proto [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]]' program for kids," and used children, whether corporeal or otherwise, as weapons.[5] The essay adds that the children so conditioned were known to become absolutely ferocious killers.[5]
