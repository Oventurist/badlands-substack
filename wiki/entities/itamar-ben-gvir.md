# Itamar Ben Gvir

Itamar Ben Gvir is Israel's National Security Minister and a figure associated with the hardline wing of the Israeli government. In the Badlands Brief he appears as the sponsor of a proposal to surround prisons holding Palestinian security detainees with Nile crocodiles as a deterrent against escape attempts.

Ben Gvir has said the concept was inspired by Florida's [[alligator-alcatraz|"Alligator Alcatraz"]], and the [[israel-prison-service|Israel Prison Service]] previously sent officials to a crocodile farm to examine the practical aspects of the plan. Environmental Protection Minister [[idit-silman|Idit Silman]] subsequently reclassified the Nile crocodile into a category permitting security agencies to keep the animals under specified conditions, a step Israeli media reported was intended to facilitate Ben Gvir's proposal. The reclassification drew objections from the [[israel-nature-and-parks-authority|Israel Nature and Parks Authority]] and the Environmental Protection Ministry's legal adviser. As of the reporting, the change enabled consideration of a pilot program only; no crocodiles had been deployed at [[israel|Israeli]] prison facilities.


## Global Sumud Flotilla video (May 2026)

The May 22, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Ben-Gvir posted video of detained Gaza flotilla activists kneeling and handcuffed after Israel intercepted the [[global-sumud-flotilla|Global Sumud Flotilla]], which reportedly carried about 430 international volunteers trying to reach Gaza with humanitarian aid. Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] defended the interception but distanced himself from Ben-Gvir's treatment of the detainees and ordered deportations expedited. Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] accused Ben-Gvir of acting "like a true Nazi," and Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the video as an example of "hubris" among Israeli leaders.
