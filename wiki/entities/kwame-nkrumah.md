# Kwame Nkrumah

Kwame Nkrumah was the President of Ghana whom United States President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] assisted in building the largest hydroelectric dam in Africa, the Volta River Dam.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands account

The Kennedy–Nkrumah collaboration is presented as evidence that Kennedy's anti-colonial rhetoric was matched by concrete action: he "wasn't only using nice words," but put effort into supporting the industrial development aspirations of nations around the world.[1] The episode is cited alongside the Peace Corps and efforts to expand industrial growth in South America for the benefit of local populations rather than Western cartels — all described as normal expressions of a better US foreign policy toward poor nations prior to the 1970s, before the shift marked by [[nssm-200|NSSM-200]].[1]
