# Cedric Lodge

Cedric Lodge is a former manager of the [[harvard-medical-school|Harvard Medical School]] morgue who pleaded guilty to a federal charge of interstate transport of human remains in May 2025.[1]

Lodge, 57 at the time of his plea, was morgue manager when he committed the offenses, according to the United States Attorney's Office for the Middle District of Pennsylvania.[1] Over a five-year period he stole and sold "head, brains, skins, bones, and other human remains" taken from the morgue at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]], according to a federal indictment.[1] A man in Pennsylvania paid Lodge's wife Denise $37,355.16 over three years after she allegedly shipped him body parts her husband had taken; the indictment states the payments were sent via PayPal and included memos such as "head number 7" and "braiiiiiiins."[1]

Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] noted the coincidence that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posted about Harvard on Truth Social the same night the story was being discussed on air with [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]].[1]
