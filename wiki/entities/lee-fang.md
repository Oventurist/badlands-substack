# Lee Fang

Lee Fang is an American investigative journalist who publishes independently on Substack.[1] His reporting on the finances of former United Nations ambassador [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] was quoted at length by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] in a November 2023 essay on Haley's standing with Republican donors.[1]

## Reporting on Haley's sudden wealth

In the piece cited by DeLarme, Fang reported that over the preceding year Haley and her husband had disclosed a large investment stock portfolio and $12 million of income, and that Haley had become wealthy in large part through ties to a network of defense interests and hawkish advocacy organizations connected to U.S. and Israeli intelligence officials.[1] Fang noted that among Haley's first reported private-sector positions after leaving government was a seat on the board of [[boeing|Boeing]], a defense contractor, paying roughly $300,000 a year in cash and stock, and that financial disclosures showed she still owned up to $250,000 in Boeing stock.[1] DeLarme contrasted these figures with reports that on leaving her post Haley held a bank account with less than $15,000 in 2017 and up to $1 million in debt.[1]
