# World Jewish Congress

The World Jewish Congress (WJC) is an international Jewish organization whose president, [[ronald-lauder|Ronald Lauder]], publicly criticized Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] at a WJC conference held in Jerusalem in May 2025.[1]

The conference was attended by various Jewish delegations from around the world as well as Israeli Foreign Minister [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]].[1] Lauder told the gathering that "all the good things that Israel is doing are being destroyed by Smotrich," because the finance minister's statements about starving the population of Gaza and destroying the enclave "were played all over the world," and asked why Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had not stopped him.[1] Much of the audience applauded, while Sa'ar responded that he "took note" of the criticism.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the episode — alongside the shooting incident involving foreign diplomats in the West Bank the same week — as evidence that Israel's international reputation was suffering and that pressure was tightening on Netanyahu.[1]
