# Fritz Thyssen

Fritz Thyssen was the German industrial magnate whose banking and steel interests connected the Nazi war economy to Wall Street, and who is famous for writing the book *I Paid Hitler*.[1]

## Banking network

Thyssen controlled the Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart of the Netherlands, an institution deeply enmeshed with the [[union-banking-corporation|Union Banking Corporation]] directed by [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]].[1] That relationship formed the basis of the 1942 U.S. federal investigation of Nazi banking operations in America and of Bush's conviction for "trading with the enemy."[1]

## Industrial power

The bank was tied to the Steel Works of the German Steel Trust, a combine controlling 50.8% of Nazi Germany's pig iron, 41.4% of its universal plate, 38.5% of its galvanized steel, 45.5% of its pipes and 35% of its explosives.[1]
