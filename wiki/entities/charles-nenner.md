# Charles Nenner

Charles Nenner is a geopolitical and financial cycle analyst known for forecasting market and conflict cycles.[1] In coverage cited in October 2023, Nenner had warned that the world was teetering on the edge of a war cycle that could last many years — a forecast presented as increasingly plausible given that the war in Ukraine had by then been ongoing for roughly 1.7 years and [[hamas|Hamas]] had just invaded Israel, opening a second major geopolitical flashpoint.[1] His war-cycle framing accompanied a related warning about severe downside for equity indices.[1]

Nenner's forecast was invoked by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] in reporting that Google searches for "Will I get drafted to war?" had risen above the February 2022 peak recorded when Russia invaded Ukraine, reaching their highest level since the 2007 Iraq troop surge.[1]
