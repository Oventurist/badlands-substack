# Catherine M. Salinas

Catherine M. Salinas is a United States Magistrate Judge serving in the Northern District of Georgia.[1]

## 2026 Fulton County election records warrant

Salinas signed the federal search warrant that authorized the FBI to search the Fulton County Election Hub and Operations Center in Union City, Georgia in late January 2026 and to seize a broad range of materials from the 2020 General Election.[1] The warrant's scope covered all categories of physical ballots, absentee ballot envelopes, provisional ballots, ballot images, tabulator tapes from scanners, and 2020 voter rolls.[1]

The judicial authorization is significant because it represented a federal court finding of probable cause sufficient to justify seizing election materials that [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] had, according to critics, declined to produce in response to state-level demands since 2020.[1] Neither the FBI nor the Justice Department publicly disclosed the underlying affidavit, the investigative theory, or the identities of any targets; the investigation was described as ongoing.[1]
