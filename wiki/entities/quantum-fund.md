# Quantum Fund

The Quantum Fund is the hedge fund through which [[george-soros|George Soros]] built his fortune.[1]

## Origins as Double Eagle

Soros started a hedge fund called Double Eagle in 1969.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] dwells on the symbolism of the name: in Byzantine heraldry and vexillology the double-headed eagle is a charge associated with the concept of Empire, its two heads representing the emperor's dual sovereignty in secular and religious matters and/or dominance over both East and West.[1] Carlson calls the choice "interesting, to say the least."[1]

## Renaming and growth

In 1970 [[soros-fund-management|Soros Fund Management]] was established and Double Eagle was renamed the Quantum Fund.[1] The fund began with $12 million and grew to $25 billion by 2011.[1]

## Advisory board

In the 1970s Soros invited [[nils-taube|Baron Nils Otto von Taube]] — later the principal investment manager of [[jacob-rothschild|Jacob Rothschild]] — to sit on the advisory board of his Quantum Group hedge funds.[1] Carlson treats this overlap as evidence of the fund's embedding in a wider British financial network.[1]
