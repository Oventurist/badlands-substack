# Eric Swider

Eric Swider is the chief executive officer of [[digital-world-acquisition-corp|Digital World Acquisition Corp]] (DWAC), the special purpose acquisition company formed to merge with [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]], the parent of [[truth-social|Truth Social]].[1]

## SEC approval of the TMTG merger

In February 2024, after the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] approved the long-delayed merger plan, DWAC shares spiked and Swider said in a statement that "this achievement marks a significant milestone for us."[1] TMTG chief executive [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] thanked "DWAC and its CEO Eric Swider" alongside DWAC shareholders, the TMTG board and staff, and Truth Social users in his own statement on the approval.[1]
