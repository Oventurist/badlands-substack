# Career Colleges and Schools of Texas

Career Colleges & Schools of Texas is a trade association that leads more than seventy for-profit higher education institutions in Texas.[1]

## Litigation against the borrower defense rule

In April 2023 the group filed suit against the [[department-of-education|Education Department]] challenging new federal rules governing student loan discharges.[1] The regulations, issued by the administration of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], took effect in July 2023 and were designed to expand the circumstances under which borrowers misled or defrauded by a university could have their debts relieved.[1]

In August 2023 a three-judge panel of the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-fifth-circuit|US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit]] granted the group's opposed emergency motion for an injunction pending appeal of the borrower-defense and closed-school provisions of the rule.[1] The two-page order did not set out the panel's reasoning but indicated that the case would be heard that fall, leaving the [[biden-borrower-defense-student-debt-rule|borrower defense rule]] blocked in the interim.[1]
