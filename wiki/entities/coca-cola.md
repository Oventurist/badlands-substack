# Coca-Cola

The Coca-Cola Company is a multinational beverage corporation whose brands include Sprite.[1] In October 2023 it became a focal point in the debate over corporate political signaling when it quietly removed website language supporting [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]].[1]

## 2023 removal of BLM language

Sen. [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] (R-Texas) criticized Coca-Cola on his podcast, *The Verdict with Ted Cruz*, for its support of Black Lives Matter after the organization's Chicago chapter posted — and later deleted — an image of a paraglider captioned "I stand with Palestine," a reference to the [[hamas|Hamas]] attackers who paraglided into an Israeli music festival and killed hundreds of attendees.[1] The morning after the October 18 episode aired, Coca-Cola deleted the relevant language from its website.[1]

An archived version of the page had read that Sprite "announced a $500,000 contribution to the Black Lives Matter Global Network in a social post committing action in the fight for racial justice," and described a 60-second commercial titled "Dreams Realized" that debuted during the 2020 BET Awards telecast.[1] The current version of the page omits that paragraph.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the deletion did not reflect any corporate reckoning with the substance of the BLM movement — noting that [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s revelations about the [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] narrative were not what prompted the change.[1] Rather, in this reading, BLM's refusal to side with Israel after the October 7 Hamas attack crossed a line for the company, and "the communist corporations aren't waking up. They're evolving with the narrative."[1] The commentary framed the 2020 corporate donation wave as part of an American [[color-revolution|color revolution]], and urged readers to "starve global business" of their dollars by shopping local and American-made.[1]
