# Narendra Modi

Narendra Modi is the Prime Minister of India. In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies him — referred to in the text as one of the "Presidents Modi, Xi and Putin" — as one of the three leaders representing the founding nations of the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] at its Tianjin summit of August 31 to September 1, 2025.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

The article places India among the states of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]] that, in the author's assessment, will not abandon their sovereignty or their pursuit of win-win cooperation in favour of a Great Reset managed by supranational technocratic institutions. Modi's presence alongside [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] is treated as symbolically significant evidence that the emerging architecture described in the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]] spans the major Eurasian powers rather than being a purely Sino-Russian project.
