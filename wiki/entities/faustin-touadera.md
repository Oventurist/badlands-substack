# Faustin-Archange Touadéra

Faustin Touadéra is the president of the [[central-african-republic|Central African Republic]].[1]

## Wagner Group ties

In July 2023, photographs circulated showing [[wagner-group|Wagner]] chief [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] at a Russia-Africa summit in St. Petersburg shaking hands with an African delegate identified by the watchdog All Eyes on Wagner as Touadéra's chief of protocol.[1]

Hundreds of Wagner mercenaries had arrived in the Central African Republic the previous week, according to local reports, ahead of a constitutional referendum scheduled for that Sunday which could abolish presidential term limits and thereby allow Touadéra to run again.[1]
