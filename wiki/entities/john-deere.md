# John Deere

John Deere is an Illinois-based agricultural machinery manufacturer that sells tractors, crop harvesters and other large equipment, and which became a target of tariff threats from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## 2024 tariff threat

Speaking at an event with farmers in Pennsylvania in September 2024, Trump threatened the company with a 200 percent tariff on everything it sought to sell into the United States if it followed through on a previously announced plan to move a substantial part of its manufacturing to Mexico.[1] "I just noticed behind me John Deere tractors. I know a lot about John Deere, I love the company," Trump said, before adding that he was "just notifying John Deere right now" that outsourcing would trigger the tariff.[1] Trump conceded that the company might not have made a final decision, but said he believed it had.[1] The episode was read as an indicator that Trump would use tariffs aggressively if he won the November 2024 election.[1]

Fox Business Network had reported in June 2024 that John Deere was laying off roughly 600 staff members at plants in Illinois and Iowa as part of a production shift toward Mexico.[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the threatened tariffs as the "stick" counterpart to the "carrot" of tax incentives, arguing that behavioral change in corporate siting decisions depends on both.[1] She disputed the standard expert claim that tariffs simply pass through to consumers as inflation, contending that such analysis assumes tariffs would have no effect on John Deere's own behavior and ignores the company's understanding of price thresholds among its farming customer base.[1]

The commentary also argued that the company's significance to the American food supply extends well beyond tractors and harvesters into agricultural chemical infrastructure, citing John Deere's March 2022 announcement that double-digit units of its "green-on-green" See & Spray Ultimate targeted spraying system would be available to order.[1] Ashe in America characterized this as automating chemical delivery across the full cycle of crop growth, and attributed weak regulation of such firms to the company's lobbying spending, closing with a call to "make America healthy again" even at the cost of putting certain corporations out of business.[1]
