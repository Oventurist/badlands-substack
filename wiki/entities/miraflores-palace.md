# Miraflores Palace

Miraflores Palace in Caracas is the official seat of the presidency of [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

Following the removal of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] under [[operation-absolute-resolve|Operation Absolute Resolve]], Vice President and Oil Minister [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] was sworn in at the palace as interim president in January 2026.[1] Gunfire was reported at Miraflores following the swearing-in; according to some accounts, security forces at the palace were firing at a drone.[1]
