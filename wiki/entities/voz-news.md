# VOZ News

VOZ News is a bilingual news outlet cited by Badlands Media for its coverage of ideological bias in American technology companies.[1]

## Gemini coverage

In March 2024, VOZ reported that [[google-gemini|Gemini]], [[google|Google]]'s artificial intelligence tool, had generated controversy in the United States over its pronounced progressive tendencies on political, historical, racial and gender questions, and had previously been "exposed for its discrimination" in declining to show favorable information or images of white people.[1] The outlet relayed accounts from former Google employees, published by The Free Press, asserting that the model's ideological design was deliberate rather than accidental.[1]
