# Buffalo Bills

The Buffalo Bills are a professional American football franchise competing in the [[nfl|National Football League]]. In Badlands Media commentary the team appears principally as the employer of safety [[damar-hamlin|Damar Hamlin]], whose on-field cardiac arrest during a nationally televised game became a focal point of the outlet's writing on vaccine questions and narrative management.[1]

## The Hamlin collapse

Writing on January 4, 2023, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described Hamlin as an extremely fit 24-year-old football player for the Buffalo Bills who, during a Monday Night Football game, made a routine and mundane tackle that did not involve helmet-to-helmet contact.[1] Seconds after the tackle Hamlin stood up and collapsed, went into cardiac arrest, and was in critical condition at the time Carlson wrote.[1]

Carlson used the incident to argue that the reaction to it — immediate attribution to a rare condition in which blunt force trauma to the chest induces cardiac arrest, coupled with the demonization of anyone raising [[covid-19-vaccines|vaccines]] as a possible contributing factor — reflected a narrative being constructed rather than a straightforward medical assessment.[1] He observed that the episode fell in the same week [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had said he would release [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] exposing [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], and suggested that the resulting taboo on questioning vaccines conveniently served the interests of those who had promoted them.[1]

Carlson explicitly declined to assert that the collapse was staged or that a vaccine had caused it, saying only that the media and social media reaction "seems to be less than genuine."[1]
