# Rupert Sheldrake

**Rupert Sheldrake** is described as a biologist and field theorist in "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," the fifth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series published by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on August 9, 2025.[1]

The article quotes Sheldrake's statement that "Memory is inherent in nature. Everything has a kind of memory," using it to support its argument that what readers experience when a shared recollection surfaces is not trauma bonding but memory reactivation.[1] Sheldrake's work is the basis for the essay's appeal to [[morphic-field|the morphic field]], which the author describes as a field that does not ask for belief but for listening, and which strengthens as more people speak and their signals align.[1] The concept underpins the article's wider account of a [[resonance-field|resonance field]] in which coherence spreads through recognition rather than persuasion.[1]

## Cited in "The Shift" (March 2025)

Sheldrake's [[morphic-resonance|morphic resonance]] is cited in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for the proposition that thoughts and emotions do not stay in the head but ripple into a collective field.[2] The essay builds its section on [[conscious-creation|conscious creation]] on that claim, comparing the effect to dropping a stone in a pond in which one person's coherence strengthens a pattern others can unconsciously tune into, and describing the mechanism as "physics meeting consciousness" rather than wishful thinking.[2]
