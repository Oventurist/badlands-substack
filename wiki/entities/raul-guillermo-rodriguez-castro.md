# Raúl Guillermo Rodríguez Castro

Raúl Guillermo Rodríguez Castro is the grandson of former Cuban leader [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]] and serves as a close aide and bodyguard to his grandfather. Though he holds no formal government position, he emerged in July 2026 as an unofficial interlocutor in the question of [[cuba|Cuba]]'s relations with the United States.[1] He is known by the nickname "El Cangrejo" ("The Crab").[1]

## Offer to negotiate with Trump

In an interview with *USA Today* published on or about July 6, 2026, Rodríguez Castro said he was prepared to negotiate directly with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the future of US–Cuba relations.[1] He indicated that Cuba could consider measures such as the release of political prisoners if broader negotiations with Washington were to take place.[1] The remarks came while the Trump administration continued to maintain economic and diplomatic pressure on the Cuban government.[1]

Hours after the interview was published, Cuba suffered another nationwide electrical grid collapse — the island's eighth since October 2025 — leaving much of the country without power while authorities prioritized restoring hospitals and other critical infrastructure.[1] Cuban officials said the cause was not immediately known.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] criticized online enthusiasm for military action against Cuba and argued for testing whether the younger Rodríguez Castro is "a reasonable guy with whom we can negotiate and trade," calling almost anything preferable to the seventy-year standing threat of war over the island.[1] The commentary situated the offer alongside the May 20 unsealing of an indictment against the elder Raúl Castro for conspiracy to kill four US nationals in a 1996 aircraft shootdown.[1]
