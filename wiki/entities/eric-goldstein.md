# Eric Goldstein

**Eric Goldstein** served as executive assistant director for cybersecurity at the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]], part of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

At the RSA Conference in April 2023 Goldstein appeared on a panel alongside Maj. Gen. [[william-hartman|William Hartman]], chief of the Cyber National Mission Force, where the two officials detailed two previously unknown incidents in which the military and civilian cyber agencies had jointly blunted state-linked attacks.[1] Reporting by [[axios|Axios]] described the disclosures as among the first clear public examples of how [[us-cyber-command|Cyber Command]] and CISA cooperate during an active event.[1]
