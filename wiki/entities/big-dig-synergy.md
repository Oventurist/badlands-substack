# Big Dig Synergy

Big Dig Synergy is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program listed for Wednesdays at 7:30pm ET in the January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] and a co-host identified only as L.[1] The show is framed around tearing apart what its hosts call the fake news with a take-no-prisoners attitude, and around demonstrating how the decentralized citizen intelligence apparatus works by observing it in action.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Big Dig Synergy on Wednesdays at 7:30pm ET, with Patrick Gunnels and co-host L dissecting what they called the Fake News and demonstrating how the decentralized citizen intelligence apparatus operates.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Big Dig Synergy in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]
