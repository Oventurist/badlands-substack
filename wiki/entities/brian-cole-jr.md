# Brian Cole Jr.

Brian Cole Jr. is the man accused of planting pipe bombs outside the headquarters of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] and the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] on the eve of January 6, 2021.[1]

## Charges

Cole was charged in December with transporting and placing the two improvised explosive devices.[1] A superseding indictment unsealed on a Wednesday in April 2026 added two additional felony counts: attempting to use weapons of mass destruction and committing an act of terrorism while armed.[1]

## Statements to investigators

In court filings, prosecutors said Cole told investigators he believed the 2020 election had been tampered with and that "someone needs to speak up."[1] During a 90-minute interview, officials said he walked agents through in detail how he constructed, transported and planted the pipe bombs, and alleged that he purchased bomb-making materials between 2018 and 2020.[1]

Cole pleaded not guilty to the original charges and had not yet been arraigned on the new indictment at the time of reporting.[1]

## Arrest and charges (December 2025)

Cole, aged 30, was arrested on Thursday, December 4, 2025, and charged with two counts tied to the two pipe bombs planted outside the Democratic and Republican National Committee offices on the eve of the January 6, 2021 Capitol attack.[2] He was the first suspect publicly identified by law enforcement in a case that had gone nearly five years without an arrest despite clues such as the suspect's height and distinctive Nike shoes.[2] As investigators disclosed new evidence, the Justice Department prepared for prosecution.[2] Badlands commentary treated the case as a manufactured sequel to what it regards as a fabricated original event.[2] See [[january-5-2021-pipe-bombs|the January 5, 2021 pipe bombs]].[2]
