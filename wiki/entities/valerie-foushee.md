# Valerie Foushee

Valerie Foushee is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from North Carolina.

She appears in the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] as one of the incumbents tested in the March 3, 2026 primaries, facing a challenge from [[nida-allam|Nida Allam]], who was backed by [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]]. The Brief treats the race as an early indicator in the Democratic Party's expensive internal contest ahead of the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]], in which thirty incumbents faced well-funded challengers motivated largely by generational change and anti-establishment sentiment.
