# Julio Rivera

Julio Rivera is a conservative commentator who, in a July 22, 2026 opinion piece for the outlet American Greatness, argued that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] should back American open-source artificial intelligence.[1]

## The American Greatness argument

Rivera warned that China is rapidly gaining ground with freely available models that could shape global AI standards, and contended that Washington cannot afford to let the United States lose what he framed as the open-source AI war.[1] The piece appeared as major US firms including [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[anthropic|Anthropic]] pressed policymakers to scrutinize or restrict Chinese open-weight models on national-security grounds, and as critics warned that heavy regulation would entrench incumbents at the expense of smaller developers.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the timing of Rivera's op-ed as confirmation of an argument made the previous evening on the Devolution Power Hour: that the US–China AI arms race would be used to halt the centralization of the American AI industry under the banner of national security, even if outlets publishing to that effect did not recognize the arms race as narrative and coordinated.[1] The debate forms part of the wider policy fight over [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]].[1]
