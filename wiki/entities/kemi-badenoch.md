# Kemi Badenoch

Kemi Badenoch is a British politician who leads the Conservative Party, the United Kingdom's principal opposition party.[1] In January 2025 she became a central figure in the parliamentary fight over a fresh national inquiry into the United Kingdom's [[grooming-gangs|child rape grooming gangs]] scandal.[1]

## Parliamentary clash over the grooming gangs inquiry

In the first exchange in Parliament on child sex gangs after the matter regained public prominence in early January 2025, Badenoch argued that the country still does not know how bad the abuse has been because the necessary research has simply never been done.[1] She used that point to underline the case for a new, more comprehensive national inquiry into grooming gangs, telling the House that "there is still work to be done."[1]

Her intervention came in response to Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], whose stated position was that Britain faced a binary choice between acting on recommendations already produced by earlier, less comprehensive reports and launching a fresh multi-year investigation — and that doing both was somehow impossible.[1] The exchange formed part of a wider and bitter argument between the UK's two legacy parties of government, Labour and the Conservatives, over which of them bore responsibility for the decades-long scandal of industrial-scale child rape by migrant-heritage gangs.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Badenoch's warning while arguing that British authorities had looked the other way for decades as the gangs operated, and that both the rapists and the politicians who enabled them out of cowardice or political expediency should be held accountable.[1]
