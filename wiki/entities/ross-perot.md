# Ross Perot

Ross Perot was an American businessman who ran as a third-party candidate in the 1992 United States presidential election.[1]

## Role in Badlands media criticism

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recounted that as a young teen she recognized the overt bias of news organizations during the 1992 campaign, when authoritative media mocked voters who were considering Perot, insisting that "only a fool would throw away their vote on a third party candidate."[1] Her parents voted for Perot, and the episode became a formative moment that solidified her desire to become a journalist.[1]

She recalled arguing in a 1995 journalism-school entrance essay that Headline News would prove the downfall of an informed American populace, because once audiences are convinced they can get the truth from a headline, whoever writes the headlines defines what is true.[1]
