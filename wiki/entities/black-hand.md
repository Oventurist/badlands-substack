# Black Hand

The **Black Hand** is described in Badlands Media commentary as an anarchist terror cell from Serbia that was deployed to kill [[archduke-franz-ferdinand|Archduke Franz Ferdinand]] of Austria on June 28, 1914.[1]

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the assassination triggered a chain of events that led a sleep-walking world into the slaughterhouse of the First World War, because the military pacts of 1914 — Russian backing for Serbia and German backing for Austria — were already well known to every geopolitician of the day.[1] The article situates the killing within the broader architecture of the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] and [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]], and within an age of assassinations between 1880 and 1914 that it attributes to London-directed anarchist networks.[1]
