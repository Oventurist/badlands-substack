# California Public Utilities Commission

The California Public Utilities Commission is a California state regulatory body whose remit includes rail safety oversight in the state.[1]

## 2023 missing ammonium nitrate shipment

The commission was among the agencies investigating the disappearance of roughly 30 tons of ammonium nitrate shipped by rail from Cheyenne, Wyoming, to a rail stop in the Mojave Desert in California in April 2023.[1] The shipment was handled by [[dyno-nobel|Dyno Nobel]], and the investigation also involved the [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]] and [[union-pacific|Union Pacific]], with the empty railcar sent back to Wyoming for inspection.[1]
