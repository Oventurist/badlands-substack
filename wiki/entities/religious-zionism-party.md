# Religious Zionism Party

The Religious Zionism party is the far-right Israeli political faction led by Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]].[1]

## Platform for the next government

Speaking at a gathering of the party, Smotrich announced that the next Israeli government must cancel the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]], extend Israeli sovereignty across the [[west-bank-annexation|West Bank]], and encourage Palestinians to leave both the West Bank and the Gaza Strip.[1] He described these steps as essential to foreclosing any possibility of a Palestinian state and insisted that no other lasting solution to the conflict exists.[1] The party thus functions as the principal organized vehicle within Israeli politics for the formal annexationist program long associated with the [[israeli-settler-movement|settler movement]].[1]

Badlands commentary treated the announcement as a moment when "the mask has finally been lifted" regarding the intentions of the governing coalition in Jerusalem, framing the party's program as the culmination of a decades-long effort — begun under [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] after 1996 — to hollow out the Oslo framework rather than implement it.[1]

## Coalition leverage (May 2025)

Badlands analysis in May 2025 emphasized the party's structural leverage over [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]: led by [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], it controls 14 seats in the [[knesset|Knesset]], and its withdrawal from the coalition would push Netanyahu's 68-seat majority below the 60 seats needed to remain Prime Minister.[2] By comparison, [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s Jewish Power Party controls only six seats, leaving Netanyahu a 62-seat majority when that faction resigned in January before later returning.[2] The party is described as far-right, and Smotrich as having a history of inflammatory remarks about Palestinians and the Gaza Strip.[2]
