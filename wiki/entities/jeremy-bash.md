# Jeremy Bash

Jeremy Bash served as Chief of Staff at the U.S. Department of Defense under Secretary of Defense [[leon-panetta|Leon Panetta]]. He figures in Badlands Media's Benghazi reporting as documentary evidence that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] moved to help on the night of the attack while the [[state-department|State Department]] did not.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## The "spinning up as we speak" message

A Freedom of Information Act lawsuit revealed that on the night of September 11, 2012, Bash immediately offered assistance to the State Department, stating that forces could move to Benghazi and "they are spinning up as we speak."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Badlands presents this message as directly contradicting the claim by former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the State Department that nothing more could have been done to save Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The Bash message also anchors the timeline of the 7:30 p.m. "Deputies Meeting" convened by the White House shortly after State learned the Pentagon was spinning up — a meeting attended by Clinton and deputies from State, Defense, and the White House, with both the President and the Secretary of Defense absent.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) In Badlands' account, forces were being scrambled and were never given the order, making Bash's contemporaneous offer of assistance a key data point in the case that the failure to respond was a deliberate [[dereliction-of-duty|dereliction of duty]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
