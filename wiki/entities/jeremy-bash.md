# Jeremy Bash

Jeremy Bash served as Chief of Staff at the United States Department of Defense under Secretary of Defense [[leon-panetta|Leon Panetta]] during the Obama administration, having previously held the same role at the [[cia|CIA]] while Panetta was director.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

Bash appears in [[a-dereliction-of-duty|A Dereliction of Duty]] as documentary evidence against the official position that no military help could have reached [[benghazi|Benghazi]] in time. Citing Fox News, the article reports that a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit revealed that Bash "immediately offered assistance to the State Department on the night of Sept. 11," writing that forces could move to Benghazi and that "they are spinning up as we speak."

The article uses this message to contradict the claim by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[state-department|State Department]] that nothing more could have been done, and to undercut the State Department Accountability Review Board's conclusion that the interagency response was "timely and appropriate." In the article's reconstruction, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] was already generating options and moving forces when the "Deputies Meeting" chaired by Clinton shifted the discussion to whether the Libyan government would consent to a deployment — a delay the article characterises as deliberate obstruction rather than caution.
