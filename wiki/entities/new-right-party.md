# New Right (Israel)

The New Right is an Israeli political party led by former Prime Minister [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]].[1]

## Founding and positioning

Founded at the end of 2018, the party branded itself as the "most right-wing" party in Israel in an attempt to supplant [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s [[likud|Likud]], which had expanded over the years to include many political moderates and centrists.[1] When Bennett ran for Prime Minister in 2021, he branded himself as "10% more right-wing than Netanyahu."[1]

While Bennett drew the most right-wing factions of Likud into the New Right, he rejected the Kahanists of [[otzma-yehudit|Otzma Yehudit]] ("Jewish Power"), led by [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], whom he considered too extreme to call allies; Netanyahu was subsequently forced to ally with those radicals for political survival.[1]
