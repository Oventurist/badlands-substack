# Thell Reed

Thell Reed is a veteran Hollywood firearms armorer and the father of *Rust* armorer [[hannah-gutierrez-reed|Hannah Gutierrez-Reed]].[1] He was 81 years old at the time of the July 2024 developments in the *Rust* prosecutions.[1]

## Reaction to the Baldwin dismissal

After a New Mexico judge threw out the involuntary manslaughter case against [[alec-baldwin|Alec Baldwin]] mid-trial on July 12, 2024 for prosecutorial suppression of evidence, Reed told the *Daily Mail*, "Well, I think that's good," and added, "I think Hannah's case should also be dismissed."[1] He argued that "there's a lot of evidence that hasn't been heard at all," saying prosecutors "suppressed all the important evidence" and that Baldwin's attorneys had surfaced it.[1] His daughter had been sentenced in April 2024 to 18 months in custody for bringing the bullets that killed cinematographer [[halyna-hutchins|Halyna Hutchins]] to the set.[1]
