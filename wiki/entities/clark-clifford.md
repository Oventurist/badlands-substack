# Clark Clifford

Clark M. Clifford was a prominent Washington lobbyist and lawyer who ran [[first-american-bankshares|First American Bankshares Inc.]], described as Washington's leading banking institution.[1]

## Role in the BCCI affair

Clifford represented the [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|Bank of Credit and Commerce International]] (BCCI) when the bank first began buying banks in the United States.[1] The Federal Reserve Board later determined that BCCI's use of front men had enabled it to acquire control of First American Bankshares, the institution Clifford headed.[1] That covert acquisition sits at the center of the American dimension of the BCCI scandal, which Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] frames as a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] money-laundering operation with ties to [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]].[1]
