# Dianne Feinstein

Dianne Feinstein was a long-serving Democratic United States Senator from California and a former chair of the Senate Intelligence Committee. In the Badlands corpus she is referenced chiefly for the reporting that a staff member who served for many years as her office driver and liaison in California was later determined to have been reporting to Chinese intelligence.

The July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief cites "Feinstein's driver" alongside the [[eric-swalwell|Swalwell]]–[[fang-fang|Fang Fang]] affair as one of "the many other examples of staffers being bad actors." The point of the citation is institutional rather than biographical: [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] uses these cases to argue that the absence of mandatory criminal background checks for congressional employees — the gap [[kat-cammack|Kat Cammack]]'s H.Res. 500 would close — represents a failure [[congress|Congress]] has been on notice about for years and has nonetheless left unaddressed.
