# Myong Joun

Myong Joun is a U.S. District Judge in Massachusetts.[1]

## Department of Education reduction-in-force order

In May 2025, Judge Joun issued an order requiring the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] to reinstate nearly 1,400 employees who had been fired earlier that year as part of the department's effort to reduce the size of its workforce.[1] In his ruling he concluded that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s "true intention is to effectively dismantle the Department," even though in his view it lacked the power to do so.[1]

The [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] granted the administration's request to temporarily pause Joun's order on Monday, July 14, 2025, in a brief unsigned ruling, clearing the way for the workforce reduction to proceed.[1] The underlying dispute had begun shortly after the department's March 11 announcement of a reduction in force involving 1,378 employees.[1]
