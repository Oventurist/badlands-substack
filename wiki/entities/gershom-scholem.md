# Gershom Scholem

Professor Gershom G. Scholem of the Hebrew University, Jerusalem, is described as one of the greatest living authorities on the mystical [[kabbalah|Kabbalistic]] literature.[1] He is credited with bringing to light the eerie connection between the [[frankism|Frankists]] and the [[jacobins|Jacobins]], and between the Frankists and the Reform movement.[1]

His work is cited alongside the testimony of [[john-robison|John Robison]] as evidence of the continuum that prominent Judaica scholars accept between Jewish-born heretics who followed the false Messiah [[sabbatai-zevi|Shabbetai Tzvi]] (1626–1676), the radical Sabbatian practitioners known as Frankists, the Jacobins, and the Reform movement — a lineage the article uses to argue for a relationship between the [[bavarian-illuminati|Illuminati]] and the Communists from the Jewish historical perspective.[1]
