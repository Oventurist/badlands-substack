# Morteza Hosseini

Morteza "Mori" Hosseini is a Florida homebuilding executive and Republican donor described as a major benefactor of Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]].[1] He and his wife, [[forough-hosseini|Forough Hosseini]], became the subject of reporting by [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]] in July 2023 that examined their political giving history.[1]

## Political donations

According to that reporting, the Hosseinis donated tens of thousands of dollars to a political action committee that funded Democratic Party leaders, including former House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]], the lead impeachment manager against former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Federal Election Commission records cited in the report show Hosseini's donations to the [[public-affairs-alliance-of-iranian-americans|Iranian American Political Action Committee]] spanning 2004 to 2011, filed under his own name as well as the aliases "Morteza Hosseini Kargar" and "Morteza Hosseini-Kargar."[1]

During the same period the PAC funded various Democratic groups, including Friends of Hillary — associated with [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] — and Friends of Weiner, associated with [[anthony-weiner|Anthony Weiner]].[1] The comparatively smaller sums directed to Republicans went to groups supporting former Republican leader [[paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]], [[dan-crenshaw|Dan Crenshaw]], and [[ben-sasse|Ben Sasse]].[1] The PAC was also reported to have backed the Obama and Biden administrations' Iran nuclear deal.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that "follow the money" remained good direction and that the revelations about DeSantis's Iranian donors looked bad for the Florida governor.[1] She noted the unusual breadth of the Hosseinis' recipient list, which ranged from Pelosi and Raskin to Ryan, Crenshaw, Sasse, and DeSantis himself.[1]
