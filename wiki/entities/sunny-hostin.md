# Sunny Hostin

Sunny Hostin is an American television host and legal commentator, best known as a co-host of the ABC daytime program [[the-view|The View]].[1]

## Post-2024 election remarks

On the Wednesday, November 6, 2024 episode of The View, following [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election victory, Hostin suggested that Latino men had supported Trump in order to "deport" themselves — a remark critics characterized as invoking a racist stereotype about Hispanics and legal status.[1] In the same segment she referred to pro-Trump white women as "uneducated."[1]

Her comments were cited as a leading example of a broader wave of attacks directed at Hispanic American voters after Hispanic-majority counties shifted toward Trump by an average of 10 percentage points.[1] Badlands Media commentary presented the episode as evidence that "the anti-racists have always been the racists," arguing that Democrats responded to hemorrhaging minority support by recasting Latino men as white supremacists.[1]
