# Human Artistry Campaign

The Human Artistry Campaign is an advocacy coalition representing creative-industry interests in disputes over generative artificial intelligence, particularly the use of copyrighted works as training material and the production of synthetic likenesses and voices.

## Response to Seedance 2.0

According to the February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief, the campaign joined the studio backlash against [[bytedance|ByteDance]]'s Seedance 2.0 video generator. In a statement reported by *Deadline*, the group said: "The launch of Seedance 2.0 is an attack on every creator around the world. Stealing human creators' work in an attempt to replace them with AI-generated slop is destructive to our culture: stealing isn't innovation. These unauthorized deepfakes and voice clones of actors violate the most basic aspects of personal autonomy and should be deeply concerning to everyone. Authorities should use every legal tool at their disposal to stop this wholesale theft."

The statement placed the campaign alongside the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]] and [[paramount-pictures|Paramount]] in urging enforcement action, and connects the organization to broader corpus themes concerning [[deepfake|deepfakes]] and the disruptive potential of artificial intelligence in the creative economy.
