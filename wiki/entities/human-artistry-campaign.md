# Human Artistry Campaign

The Human Artistry Campaign is an advocacy coalition representing creators and performers in disputes over generative artificial intelligence and intellectual property.[1]

## Opposition to Seedance 2.0

Following [[bytedance|ByteDance]]'s release of the AI video model [[seedance-2-0|Seedance 2.0]] in February 2026, the campaign issued a statement reported by *Deadline* declaring that "the launch of Seedance 2.0 is an attack on every creator around the world."[1] It continued: "Stealing human creators' work in an attempt to replace them with AI-generated slop is destructive to our culture: stealing isn't innovation. These unauthorized [[deepfake|deepfakes]] and voice clones of actors violate the most basic aspects of personal autonomy and should be deeply concerning to everyone. Authorities should use every legal tool at their disposal to stop this wholesale theft."[1]

The statement amplified cease-and-desist letters already sent to ByteDance by [[disney|Walt Disney]] and [[paramount-global|Paramount]].[1]
