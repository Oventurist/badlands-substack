# Society for UAP Studies

The Society for UAP Studies is an academic body organized to establish the formal scientific study of unidentified anomalous phenomena, the term that has largely replaced "UFO" in official and scholarly usage.[1]

## Aims and activity

The society leads a group of researchers seeking to build UAP research into a recognized academic discipline, and convened an international conference to discuss methodologies and frameworks for the new field.[1] Co-founder [[michael-cifone|Michael Cifone]] has emphasized a rigorous academic approach to studying [[unidentified-anomalous-phenomena|UAPs]] alongside a range of other unexplained phenomena.[1]

The group focuses on phenomena that have historically been overlooked by mainstream science and acknowledges the difficulty of formalizing such research.[1] It remains officially neutral on whether UAPs indicate extraterrestrial life, stating "we're not necessarily taking a position," with the stated goal of attracting serious scholarly attention and a deeper understanding of the anomalies.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the society's formation as an illustration of institutional narrative capture: "This is literally how it works. They invent their own authorities."[1] She predicted that having invented the academic discipline, its proponents would next create awards and credentials to confer authority and govern the resulting "science," on a subject about which, in her view, "all the governments of the world have always agreed to comprehensively lie to their people."[1]
