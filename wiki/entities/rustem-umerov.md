# Rustem Umerov

Rustem Umerov is the Ukrainian national security adviser who led Kyiv's delegation in United States–brokered talks on ending the [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]].[1]

## Miami talks (December 2025)

Over the weekend of December 20–21, 2025, Umerov met in Miami with White House envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], who separately met Russian envoy [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]].[1] Witkoff called the discussions "productive and constructive" but reported no clear breakthrough toward ending the war, and stated in a readout that "Ukraine remains fully committed to achieving a just and sustainable peace," with the shared priority being "to stop the killing, ensure guaranteed security, and create conditions for Ukraine's recovery, stability, and long-term prosperity."[1]

The Florida meetings came weeks after the [[trump-ukraine-peace-plan|Trump administration's proposed plan]] to end the war, an early draft of which Kyiv officials and European Union backers called unworkable.[1] Negotiators for Russia, Ukraine, Europe and the United States continued to exchange proposals in the following weeks, but Ukraine and Russia did not negotiate directly, each side meeting separately with US officials instead.[1] Representatives of both countries shared Witkoff's remarks on social media without offering further detail on any progress.[1]

## The September 2023 deputy minister firings

Shortly after Umerov's appointment as defense minister — following the sacking of [[oleksii-reznikov|Oleksii Reznikov]] over corruption — six deputy defense ministers were dismissed in the [[2023-ukrainian-defense-ministry-purge|September 2023 defense ministry purge]].[2] Neither Umerov nor Cabinet of Ministers representative Taras Melnychuk, who announced the firings on Telegram, explained the reason for the terminations.[2] Because the ministry's role is primarily logistics — procurement, salaries and benefits — rather than battlefield tactics, the turnover was not expected to have an immediate effect on the course of the war.[2]
