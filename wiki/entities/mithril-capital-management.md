# Mithril Capital Management

Mithril Capital Management is a venture capital firm associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] It forms part of the network of Thiel-linked investment vehicles that has supplied personnel to senior positions in the U.S. health bureaucracy.[1]

[[jim-oneill|Jim O'Neill]] served as a Managing Director of Mithril from 2012 to 2019, following four years at [[clarium-capital|Clarium Capital]].[1] After leaving Mithril he headed an anti-aging nonprofit and served as a board member and/or paid advisor to multiple health-tech and biotech companies before being appointed Deputy Secretary of the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] and Acting Director of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]].[1]
