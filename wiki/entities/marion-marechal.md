# Marion Maréchal

Marion Maréchal is a French right-wing politician and the niece of [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]].[1] Rather than remaining within the family's [[national-rally|National Rally]], she aligned with [[eric-zemmour|Éric Zemmour]]'s [[reconquete|Reconquête]] and served as that party's lead candidate for the June 2024 European Parliament elections.[1]

## 2024 campaign context

Maréchal's candidacy did not arrest Reconquête's slide among young voters: Ipsos polling published in March 2024 put the party at 3 percent among French 18-to-24-year-olds, down from 8 percent in December 2023, while [[jordan-bardella|Jordan Bardella]]'s National Rally climbed to 31 percent in the same cohort.[1]
