# Curt Weldon

Curt Weldon is a former U.S. Congressman known for raising questions about pre-9/11 intelligence.[1]

## Tucker Carlson interview

On Monday, April 14, 2025, journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] published an interview with Weldon addressing the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] A clip from the interview was shared on X by actor [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]], who wrote that the tragedy "might be the biggest scandal in American history" and called for the U.S. government to reveal the truth about the attacks.[1]

## Advocacy of a US-Russian partnership

Weldon, a Republican congressman from Pennsylvania, led a call endorsed by more than 100 elected representatives for a fundamental reset of relations with Moscow, commissioning the report [[us-russia-partnership-a-time-for-new-beginnings|US-Russia Partnership: A Time for New Beginnings]].[2] Published in early 2001, the dossier set out a coherent vision touching on every aspect of US–Russian relations, including cultural diplomacy, the teaching of Russian in American schools, agricultural assistance, full-spectrum energy development, space exploration, defense cooperation, asteroid defense and fusion research.[2]

The report warned that America and Russia must forge a mutually beneficial alliance or face the near certainty of a new Cold War, and argued that terrorism and WMD proliferation as shared threats made the two nations natural allies.[2] In Ehret's account, the win-win cooperation envisioned by Weldon and his allies stood in total opposition to the unipolarist program outlined in the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]]'s [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]], and it was the latter doctrine, not Weldon's, that the administration of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] ultimately adopted.[2]
