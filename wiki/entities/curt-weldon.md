# Curt Weldon

Curt Weldon is a former U.S. Congressman known for raising questions about pre-9/11 intelligence.[1]

## Tucker Carlson interview

On Monday, April 14, 2025, journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] published an interview with Weldon addressing the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] A clip from the interview was shared on X by actor [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]], who wrote that the tragedy "might be the biggest scandal in American history" and called for the U.S. government to reveal the truth about the attacks.[1]
