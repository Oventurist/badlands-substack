# Peter Durant

Peter Durant is a Republican Massachusetts state representative who in July 2023, while running for state senate, pressed Governor [[maura-healey|Maura Healey]] for a full accounting of the cost to Massachusetts taxpayers of housing, feeding and caring for the growing number of illegal aliens arriving in the state.[1]

Healey's office replied that nearly 40 motels were being used by the state to house new arrivals but refused to provide a cost estimate to taxpayers.[1] Durant told the Boston Herald that the issue was "about what we're spending in taxpayers' dollars to house, feed, secure those who are in the country illegally," adding that although some good information had been obtained, it remained unclear how many of the roughly 1,300 families involved were in the country illegally.[1] His inquiry supplied the underlying data for [[howie-carr|Howie Carr]]'s reporting that wealthy Massachusetts towns were largely absent from the list of migrant-motel host communities.[1]
