# David Pekoske

**David Pekoske** is the administrator of the [[transportation-security-administration|Transportation Security Administration]] who, in the reading advanced by the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series, sits at the center of Q Drop #67's questions about private aviation and the movement of politically connected travelers.[1]

## Background

Pekoske was head of the TSA at the time of the November 2017 drops, and had previously served as a Vice Admiral in the United States Coast Guard, where the article describes him as very highly decorated.[1]

## Role in the "Q for Dummies" reading

Drop #67 asks anons to consider the difference between commercial and private security as it pertains to boarding an airplane, and the series answers that private flights involve much more relaxed security measures — so that a traveler such as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] or [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] would face few questions about where they were going, where they had been, or whom they had met.[1] Because the TSA administrator has access to flight logs and other flight data unavailable to others, the series speculates that Pekoske may have been a "Patriot" reporting on [[deep-state|Deep State]] flights and movements.[1]

Supporting that reading, the article notes that Pekoske was nominated by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and then nominated again under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — a continuity the author suggests may be additional circumstantial support for the [[devolution|Devolution]] theory.[1] The author states he was unable to find any information on which political party Pekoske donated to.[1]
