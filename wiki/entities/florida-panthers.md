# Florida Panthers

The Florida Panthers are a National Hockey League franchise that in June 2025 won its second consecutive Stanley Cup.[1]

## 2025 Stanley Cup

The Panthers defeated the [[edmonton-oilers|Edmonton Oilers]] 5-1 in Game 6 on Tuesday, June 17, 2025 to repeat as champions.[1] *The Wall Street Journal* characterized the team as lacking the fastest skaters or the most prolific offense, but having perfected "dirtbag hockey" — trash-talking, hard checking, and an ability to goad rivals into losing their cool on the ice — a scrappy reputation the club treats as a point of pride.[1] Former NHL enforcer and TNT analyst Paul Bissonnette said of the style, "That's an art in itself. You wanna kill them. But you can't, because it's going to cost your team."[1] The more annoying the Panthers are, the more likely their opponents are to become distracted.[1]

Badlands host [[canncon|CannCon]] dismissed the piece with the remark that the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] "continues their garbage reporting."[1]
