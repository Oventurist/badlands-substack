# Ben Rhodes

Ben Rhodes is a former national security official in the [[obama-administration|Obama administration]], known for the remark that "we can tell journalists anything and they'll believe it," a formulation associated with what critics call the administration's Iran policy "echo chamber."[1]

## Criticism of the Soleimani strike

Following the January 2020 [[assassination-of-qasem-soleimani|killing of Qasem Soleimani]], Rhodes published an editorial in The Atlantic attacking President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s dealings with [[iran|Iran]].[1] The Federalist responded with a piece headlined "Ben Rhodes Attacks Trump To Obscure Obama's Complicity In Iran's Forever War," characterising Rhodes as peevish and describing Soleimani — the target of what it called a game-changing retaliatory strike — as an Obama administration partner and the leading terrorist of the leading terror force of the world's leading terror regime.[1]

Badlands Media places Rhodes among the "usual puppets from the Obama administration and in the media" who criticised Trump's action, and argues that the panic among such figures stemmed from two things in particular: the Iran nuclear deal and the pallets of cash sent to Iran.[1] The author contends that the underlying concern was exposure of a money-laundering operation rather than genuine fear of escalation.[1]
