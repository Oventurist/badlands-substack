# Ché Alexander

Ché Alexander is the Fulton County, Georgia Court Clerk and, in that capacity, the legal custodian of the county's election records.[1]

## Role in the 2026 FBI seizure of 2020 election materials

When the FBI executed a search warrant at the Fulton County Election Hub and Operations Center in Union City, Georgia in late January 2026, Alexander was present at the facility and participated in inventorying the items federal agents removed.[1] Because the court clerk is the custodian of record for ballots and related election materials in [[fulton-county|Fulton County]], her involvement was procedurally necessary to document the chain of custody for the approximately 700 boxes of ballots and records taken by agents.[1]

The materials covered by the warrant included all physical ballots — absentee, advance, provisional, in-person Election Day, emergency, damaged, and duplicated — along with absentee ballot envelopes, ballot images, tabulator tapes from scanners, and 2020 voter rolls.[1] The seizure followed years of litigation and administrative disputes over access to those same records, including subpoenas issued by the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]].[1]
