# Nika Soon-Shiong

Nika Soon-Shiong is an American activist and the daughter of [[patrick-soon-shiong|Patrick Soon-Shiong]], the billionaire owner of the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]].[1] Aged 32 at the time of the 2024 election, she is described as being very open about her politics.[1]

## Role in the 2024 non-endorsement

After the Los Angeles Times declined to endorse a presidential candidate in October 2024 without explaining itself to readers, Nika Soon-Shiong published a thread of social media posts supplying the reasoning, as reported by the [[hollywood-reporter|Hollywood Reporter]].[1] She attributed the decision to opposition to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s position on the war in Gaza, and recalled that her father, a South African transplant surgeon, had worked as an emergency surgeon at Baragwanath Hospital in Soweto during apartheid, writing: "For my family, Apartheid is not a vague concept."[1]

While maintaining that the endorsement decision itself had been the editorial board's, she added: "This is not a vote for Donald Trump. This is a refusal to ENDORSE a candidate that is overseeing a war on children."[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted that her explanation was ignored by the celebrities and Democratic operatives criticising the paper, because it contradicted the claim that the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|non-endorsements]] were capitulation to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and observed that the Hollywood Reporter had shown more traditional journalistic acumen than the Times itself in surfacing it.[1]
