# Michael Benz

Michael Benz is a former [[state-department|State Department]] official who served during the Trump administration and whose research on government-linked influence and censorship operations has been cited in congressional hearings.[1]

## Taylor Swift psychological operations claim

In January 2024 Benz posted on X a video dating from 2019 in which the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s psychological operations research arm discussed the idea of turning the singer [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]] into an asset for combating "disinformation."[1] The clip was central to a [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] article asking whether the saturation of corporate media coverage of Swift — which Bloomberg data showed rising sharply through 2023 to record highs — constituted a psychological operation, and to the surrounding speculation over whether Swift had become a [[nato|NATO]]-aligned asset.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], treated the resulting mainstream discussion as a net positive, arguing that it forced ordinarily disengaged audiences to confront the concept of government-sponsored influence operations during the 2024 election cycle.[1]
