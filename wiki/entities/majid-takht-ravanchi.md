# Majid Takht-Ravanchi

Majid Takht-Ravanchi is a Deputy Foreign Minister of [[iran|Iran]].[1]

In March 2026, amid the [[iran-war|war]] with the United States and [[israel|Israel]], Takht-Ravanchi stated that Tehran's sole condition for halting hostilities was "no further aggression" by Israel and the United States.[1] He also said that [[russia|Russia]], [[communist-china|China]], and [[france|France]] had contacted Iran in recent days seeking to ease tensions and encourage de-escalation.[1]

His remarks accompanied the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] offer of safe and unrestricted passage through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] to any Arab or European country that expelled American and Israeli ambassadors from its territory.[1]
