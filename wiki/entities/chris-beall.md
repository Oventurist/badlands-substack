# Chris Beall

Chris Beall is Deputy Secretary of State of Colorado, serving under Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]].[1]

According to [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who cross-examined Beall during her own trial in July 2024, Beall runs every aspect of Griswold's office and admitted under oath to details about Colorado's relationship with the [[electronic-registration-information-center|Electronic Registration Information Center]].[1] Those admissions, described as part of the official trial record, concerned the 2022 episode in which Griswold's office "mistakenly" mailed postcards to roughly 30,000 foreign nationals encouraging them to vote — officially attributed to a data coding mismatch between the Colorado DMV database and ERIC — and the practice of ERIC routing non-citizen records to member states without identifying them as non-citizens.[1]
