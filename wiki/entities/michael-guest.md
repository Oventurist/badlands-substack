# Michael Guest

Michael Guest is a Republican member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Mississippi who serves as chairman of the [[house-ethics-committee|House Ethics Committee]].[1]

## Role in the 2026 sexual-misconduct settlement disclosure vote

Guest featured in the July 2026 controversy over congressional use of taxpayer funds to settle sexual misconduct allegations. After the House passed a resolution offered by Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] by a near-unanimous 420-0 vote, directing the Ethics Committee and the Office of Congressional Workplace Rights to release within 60 days all records identifying which current or former members had used taxpayer money to settle such claims, Guest voiced support for making the information "readily available."[1] As Ethics chairman he was among the officials responsible for the mandated disclosure, and the committee under his chairmanship has stated that it does not itself handle sexual harassment lawsuits or the settlements arising from them.[1]

His posture contrasted with that of Rep. [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]], who voted "present" and characterized the timing of the resolution as "political theater," noting that her own earlier subpoena efforts had already compelled the production of related materials.[1]


## Named in the Gaetz Lawsuit

As chair of the [[house-ethics-committee|House Ethics Committee]], Rep. Michael Guest (R-Miss.) was named as a defendant, alongside the committee itself, in the federal lawsuit former Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] filed on December 23, 2024 in an attempt to block release of the committee's report into his conduct.[2] The committee published the report the same day.[2]
