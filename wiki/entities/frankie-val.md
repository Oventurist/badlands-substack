# Frankie Val

Frankie Val is a host in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective.[1]

## Badlands Media role

He co-hosts [[taking-it-back|Taking It Back]] alongside [[412-adel-nero|412 Adel Nero]] and [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], a Thursday 4pm ET program introduced in the network's January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] The show frames current events through the America First movement and its stated goal of reclaiming the Republic from the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
