# Vyacheslav Platon

Vyacheslav Platon is a Moldovan oligarch named as one of the collaborators of [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] in the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|"theft of the century"]], the roughly $1 billion fraud carried out against [[moldova|Moldova]] between 2011 and 2014.[1] In April 2017 Platon was found guilty and sentenced to 18 years in prison, while Plahotniuc himself never faced a single charge inside Moldova.[1]
