# Abdel Fattah al-Burhan

General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan is a Sudanese military officer who serves as head of Sudan's Sovereign Council and as commander of the Sudanese armed forces.[1] In Badlands Media's coverage of the April 2023 outbreak of fighting in [[sudan|Sudan]], al-Burhan is presented as one of two generals contending for control of the country's armed factions, the other being [[mohamed-hamdan-dagalo|General Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]], commonly known as "Hemedti".[1]

## Rivalry with Hemedti

Badlands commentary framed the Sudanese crisis as a question of whether the [[rapid-support-forces|Rapid Support Forces]] were "a force divided", with al-Burhan and Hemedti apparently pursuing separate objectives.[1] A key data point cited in support of that reading was Hemedti's February 23, 2022 visit to Moscow, from which al-Burhan was "noticeably absent" — an absence that fueled speculation that the two men were aligned with different external patrons.[1]

## Geopolitical context

Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that coups and civil strife in countries such as Sudan are "rarely naturally or locally created events", and that larger powers typically facilitate the growth and equipping of militant factions.[1] In that framing, al-Burhan's position atop the Sovereign Council and the formal military establishment made him a focal point for competing outside interests, including a Russian effort to secure a naval base on Sudan's Red Sea coast that the United States was said to oppose.[1]
