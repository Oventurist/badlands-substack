# Henry L. Stimson

Henry Lewis Stimson was an American statesman and a member of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] (Yale class of 1888), named in the Badlands Media series "The Men Behind the Curtain" as one of the most publicly consequential Bonesmen.[1]

## Career

Stimson was a partner in the Wall Street law firm of Root and Stimson; he served as Secretary of War under President [[william-howard-taft|William Howard Taft]] (1908–1912), as Governor General of the Philippines (1926–1928), as Secretary of State under President Herbert Hoover (1929–1933), and again as Secretary of War under Presidents Franklin Delano Roosevelt and Harry S. Truman (1940–1946).[1] He had been hired out of law school by the attorney Elihu Root, who in turn had served as attorney to [[william-collins-whitney|W. C. Whitney]]; Stimson then took over from Root as Secretary of War in 1911, appointed by his fellow Bonesman Taft.[1]

## Place in Sutton's "vertical chain"

In *America's Secret Establishment*, [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]] cites the Whitney–Stimson–Bundy links as the exemplary case of what he calls the "vertical chain" of influence by which the Order maintained continuity across generations.[1] Stimson's special assistant and point man in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] for the [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]] was Hollister Bundy (Yale 1909), whose sons [[william-bundy|William Bundy]] and [[mcgeorge-bundy|McGeorge Bundy]] were likewise initiated into the Order.[1]
