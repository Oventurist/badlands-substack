# Chabad

Chabad is a Hasidic movement within Judaism whose kabbalist leaders feature prominently in Badlands Media commentary on Israeli territorial ambitions.[1]

In September 2024, Rabbi [[yitzchak-ginsburgh|Yitzchak Ginsburgh]] of the Chabad movement published a letter calling for the conquest and annexation of southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] by the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]], joining other vocal kabbalist leaders who argue that conquering Lebanon will hasten the arrival of the Jewish messiah before the end of the Jewish calendar in 2027.[1] Badlands writers described the territorial program advanced by these Chabad rabbis as [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], and characterized it as a core tenet of their specific sect.[1] The commentary argued that whether Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] personally shares this fanaticism is largely irrelevant, since he is beholden to those who do while clinging to power to postpone corruption prosecution.[1]
