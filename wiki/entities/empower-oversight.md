# Empower Oversight

Empower Oversight is a whistleblower-advocacy organization, headed by president [[tristan-leavitt|Tristan Leavitt]], that represents [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] whistleblower [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]].[1]

## Role in the Hunter Biden whistleblower dispute

In July 2023 the organization pressed the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] to state publicly that Shapley had never been a source for its reporting, after [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s attorneys accused Shapley of illegally leaking material for the paper's October 2022 story on a potential chargeable tax and gun-purchase case against the president's son.[1] Leavitt framed the paper's silence as making it an accessory to Hunter Biden's legal defense.[1]

Shapley, whom Empower Oversight represents, testified to [[congress|Congress]] in May 2023 about an alleged [[justice-department|Justice Department]] conspiracy to protect the first son during a criminal probe, and told [[cbs-news|CBS News]] that he had been stopped from investigating money trails involving "dad" or "the big guy."[1]
