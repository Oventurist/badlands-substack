# Rebecca Holt

Rebecca Holt is a Wake County Superior Court judge in North Carolina who ruled against [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] in his 2024 effort to have his name removed from the state's presidential ballot.[1]

## 2024 ballot litigation

Kennedy sued after the Democratic-led [[north-carolina-state-board-of-elections|North Carolina State Board of Elections]] denied his request to strike his name from ballots, a denial the board justified by pointing to the timing of ballot printing and tabulation machine coding.[1] Holt denied Kennedy's effort on Thursday, September 5, 2024, a ruling that led directly to his appeal.[1]

The following day, the North Carolina Court of Appeals reversed course on the practical question, granting Kennedy's request to halt the mailing of ballots carrying his name and ordering the board to issue replacements without it.[1] The appellate decision did not provide a legal explanation.[1]
