# Herzi Halevi

Lt. Gen. Herzi Halevi is the Chief of Staff of the [[israel-defense-forces]].[1] The resignation of Military Intelligence Directorate chief Maj. Gen. [[aharon-haliva]] over the failures preceding the [[hamas]] attack of October 7 was coordinated with Halevi and approved by Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant]].[1]

Halevi is among the senior Israeli defense officials who have publicly said they bear responsibility for the deadly invasion carried out by Hamas on October 7, alongside the head of the [[shin-bet]] security agency.[1] As of the reporting, none of those officials other than Haliva had announced plans to resign, though many were expected to do so once the security situation stabilized.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] situated these pending departures within a wider pattern of dissension in the Israeli cabinet and mounting domestic protest against the government of [[benjamin-netanyahu]].[1]

## August 2024 warning over hostage-deal terms

Halevi, as IDF chief of staff, was reported in early August 2024 to have joined Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] in telling Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that his new conditions would sabotage the ceasefire-hostage deal then under negotiation.[2] Channel 12 quoted both men accusing Netanyahu of being aware that the demands — absent from the Israeli proposal of May 27 that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had publicly revealed — would doom the agreement.[2] Netanyahu replied that [[hamas|Hamas]] was the party adding demands.[2]

## Response to the Sde Teiman base storming

On July 29, 2024, Halevi condemned the mass break-ins at two [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] installations by far-right activists protesting the arrest of nine soldiers suspected of the serious abuse of a Palestinian detainee.[3] He described the scenes as "bordering on anarchy" and said the break-ins harmed the army, Israel's security, and the war effort.[3] Halevi joined Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]], and President [[isaac-herzog|Isaac Herzog]] in harshly criticizing the rioting outside the [[sde-teiman-detention-facility|Sde Teiman]] base and appealing for calm, but the appeals failed to prevent a second mob from breaking into the IDF's Beit Lid base in central Israel later that evening.[3] Several members of Netanyahu's ruling coalition were reported to have fomented the break-ins, with some taking part personally.[3]
