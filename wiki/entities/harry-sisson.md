# Harry Sisson

Harry Sisson is a young pro-Democratic Party social media personality described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as "the DNC's industrious zoomer mouthpiece."[1]

In "The Candidate Who Isn't There," published August 24, 2024, DeLarme cited a recent [[cnn|CNN]] appearance in which Sisson said of the [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] campaign, "It feels like this energy has been injected into the Democratic party…"[1] DeLarme used the quotation as an entry point for his central question about the Harris candidacy: where that energy had been in 2020, when Harris campaigned without the full weight of the [[democratic-party|Democrat]] establishment behind her and at one point polled at three percent before dropping out in sixth place.[1]

DeLarme treated Sisson's commentary as an example of an "artificial signal boost" rather than organic enthusiasm — part of a broader pattern in which pundits, talking heads and internet influencers who had propped up [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] until the moment he withdrew pivoted without hesitation to praising Harris.[1]
