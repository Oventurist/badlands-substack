# Keith Gill

Keith Gill is an American retail investor and online influencer better known by the handles "Roaring Kitty" and "DeepF—Value," and is described as the leader of the meme-stock movement centered on [[gamestop|GameStop]].[1]

## 2024 GameStop resurgence

Shortly before Gill reignited a meme-stock craze in May 2024, he purchased a large volume of GameStop options through the brokerage [[e-trade|E*Trade]].[1] In early June 2024 he posted screenshots of an E*Trade account showing GameStop shares then valued at approximately $140 million along with a new set of options expiring later that month; he reported total gains on the positions of $85.5 million late on Monday, June 3, 2024, indicating that his account remained in operation at that time.[1]

GameStop stock surged again on the strength of those posts, rising 21 percent on Monday, June 3, and more than 60 percent since Gill's reappearance — a demonstration of his influence over retail trading flows.[1]

## Platform and regulatory scrutiny

E*Trade and its owner [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]] were reported to be considering telling Gill he could no longer use the platform, out of concern that his posting and trading could amount to stock manipulation and that he could pump up a security for his own benefit.[1] Internal deliberations reportedly weighed whether the firm was willing to risk drawing the attention of Gill's "meme army" by removing him; no decision had been made, and the firm could conclude that no action was needed.[1] Separately, the Massachusetts securities division confirmed on June 3, 2024, that it was looking into Gill's activities.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the episode as a persuasion battleground in the information war, arguing that while tens of millions of ordinary investors had been "red pilled" as to the rigged nature of the financial system, the retail "Apes" were still reacting within a system whose rules the establishment controls.[1] The commentary held that the solution was defection from the rigged game entirely toward something sound, gesturing at decentralized, finite and fungible money — "maybe, probably [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]]."[1] The item also noted a reaction from [[dave-portnoy|Dave Portnoy]] of Barstool Sports.[1]
