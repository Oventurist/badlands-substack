# Group of Seven

The Group of Seven (G7) is an informal forum of advanced industrial democracies — Canada, France, [[germany|Germany]], [[italy|Italy]], Japan, the United Kingdom, and the United States — whose leaders meet annually to coordinate economic and foreign policy positions.

## The 2018 Canada summit

The [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]] recalls the G7 summit held in Canada on June 9, 2018, at which President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] warned German Chancellor [[angela-merkel|Angela Merkel]] and other European leaders about their dependence on [[russia|Russian]] energy. Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the summit — and the widely circulated photograph from it — as the moment Trump identified the contradiction later borne out by the [[european-energy-crisis|European energy crisis]]: that United States taxpayers were being asked to fund the defense of a continent reliant for energy on the adversary it was being defended against.
