# Vigilant News

Vigilant News is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Mondays and Fridays at 3pm ET as of January 2023, hosted by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] and [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]].[1] The hosts cover current events, politics and, in the network's framing, all matters affecting the ability of viewers to live free and prosperous lives, presented as an ongoing truth-focused series.[1]

In the December 19–25, 2022 schedule Vigilant News aired Mondays and Fridays at 3pm ET, with hosts Justin Deschamps and Ryan Delarme covering current events, politics and issues affecting the ability to live free and prosperous lives.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Vigilant News in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
