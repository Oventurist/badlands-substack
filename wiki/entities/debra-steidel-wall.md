# Debra Steidel Wall

Debra Steidel Wall is an American records official who served as Acting Archivist of the United States, heading the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] during the period in which classified documents were recovered from locations associated with President [[joe-biden]].[1]

## Correspondence with Senate investigators

Wall authored the NARA response letter to Senators [[ron-johnson]] and [[chuck-grassley]] that first disclosed the number of boxes removed from the Boston office of Biden attorney [[patrick-moore]]. Responding to a February 24 letter asking how and when the Archives learned that records had been transported to Boston, Wall stated that the agency learned of the move on November 3, 2022.[1]

Wall wrote that when NARA contacted Biden's personal counsel that day to arrange collection of boxes from the Penn Biden Center in Washington, D.C., counsel disclosed that Moore had moved other boxes to his Boston law firm.[1] She confirmed that "NARA staff retrieved nine boxes from Mr. Moore's Boston office," material that was secured at the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library on November 9 after the [[department-of-justice]] Office of Inspector General was notified on November 4.[1] The letter was first obtained by Fox News Digital and was highlighted in the [[badlands-news-brief]] as evidence of the steadily widening scope of the documents matter.[1]
