# Declan Kelly

Declan Kelly is an Irish-American businessman, co-founder and CEO of [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]], and a former fundraiser for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) When Clinton became Secretary of State she personally sought him out, then 41 years old, to be her economic envoy to Northern Ireland, giving him a special status outside normal diplomatic channels.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## State Department role

Kelly served as Special Envoy to Northern Ireland while simultaneously working as a private consultant to global corporations, earning roughly $2.4 million from Dow Chemical — a longtime client and one of the firms that participated in Clinton's Ireland initiative.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Like [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] he held "special government employee" status, which allowed him to retain outside income.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He took no salary from the [[state-department|State Department]] and hired his own staff; the article argues this made it easier to conceal how he was being compensated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Politico reported that despite job titles that sounded official and regular interaction with State Department staff and Irish diplomats, none of Kelly's envoy office employees were official government employees, and therefore faced no constraints on outside activities. A State Department statement said the Department "does not have a record of these individuals being employed by the Department."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Several of those staffers listed Declan Kelly Consulting on their résumés and became among the first Teneo employees, escaping the oversight normally applied to State employees.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Teneo

Kelly had been a top executive at FTI Consulting; upon stepping down in July 2009 he signed a two-year non-compete agreement, which the article identifies as the reason Teneo did not launch until June 2011.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The agreement allowed him to keep consulting part-time through FTI for Dow Chemical and Coca-Cola, both of which became early and major Teneo accounts.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Together with [[doug-band|Doug Band]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]], Kelly built a firm that employed numerous Clinton associates and, for a period, Bill Clinton as honorary chairman.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Badlands Media presents Kelly's envoy position as a mechanism for generating lucrative corporate deals whose participants then funneled money into the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
