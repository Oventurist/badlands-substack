# National Football League

The National Football League (NFL) is the governing body of professional American football in the United States. In [[badlands-media|Badlands]] coverage it appears chiefly as an institution that activist sports journalists attempt to enlist in cultural disputes.[1]

## 2023 Chiefs fan controversy

In November 2023, [[deadspin|Deadspin]] writer [[carron-j-phillips|Carron J. Phillips]] published an article demanding that the NFL "speak out" against a child [[kansas-city-chiefs|Kansas City Chiefs]] fan who wore a Native American–style headdress and painted his face in the team's red and black colors. Phillips accused the boy of wearing "black face" using a cropped photograph; the child was subsequently reported to be Native American, the son of a tribal board member.[1]

## Super Bowl 2025 officiating narrative

In the run-up to [[super-bowl-lix|Super Bowl 2025]] a widespread perception took hold — amplified by national media coverage through the postseason — that the NFL wanted officials to call games in favor of the Kansas City Chiefs.[2] The issue faded during the game itself: the officiating crew made questionable calls that went both ways, and the lopsided 40–22 Philadelphia Eagles win rendered them largely inconsequential.[2] Chiefs receiver [[deandre-hopkins|DeAndre Hopkins]] afterward challenged reporters over the calls that had gone against his team.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the arc as an institutional legitimacy operation, writing that the league was "running the same op in support of its own legitimacy as the Regime did with the 2024 election."[2]
