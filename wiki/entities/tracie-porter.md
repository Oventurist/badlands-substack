# Tracie Porter

**Tracie Porter** is a judge of the Cook County Circuit Court in Illinois who in early 2024 issued the order disqualifying former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's Republican presidential primary ballot, and who subsequently clarified the stay that kept him on it pending appeal.[1]

## Disqualification order and stay

Porter ruled that Trump was "disqualified" and that any votes cast for him would be void if her order took effect.[1] She initially stayed that removal order only until March 1, 2024, anticipating an appeal to the Illinois Appellate Court, First District, or to the Illinois Supreme Court, and/or a ruling by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] in the parallel case arising from [[colorado]].[1]

## Clarification

The language of Porter's original stay was deemed vague, prompting Trump's attorneys to seek clarification on Thursday, February 29, 2024, and to request a ruling by 12 p.m., failing which they said they would file an emergency motion to stay the ruling in the appeals court.[1] In response to the notice of appeal filed by Trump's attorneys, Porter modified her original stay that same day, providing that the removal order was stayed until the appeal is "finally resolved by the Illinois Appellate Court, First District, the Illinois Supreme Court, and/or the U.S. Supreme Court."[1] The effect of the modified order was that Trump would remain on the Illinois primary ballot, scheduled for March 19, 2024, while the appeal proceeded.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the stay as evidence that the ballot-removal campaign would ultimately fail, characterizing the effort as election interference conducted through what the writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called "trusted institutions."[1] See [[illinois-ballot-disqualification-case|Illinois ballot disqualification case]].
