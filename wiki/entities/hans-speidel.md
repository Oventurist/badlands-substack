# Hans Speidel

Hans Speidel was a Nazi general during the Second World War who, after the war, became a founder of the West German Bundeswehr and oversaw its integration into [[nato|NATO]].[1] From 1957 to 1963 he served as Supreme Commander of NATO's ground forces in Central Europe.[1]

## Context

Speidel is one of several former Nazi officers documented as holding senior NATO commands between 1950 and the 1980s, a roster that includes [[adolf-heusinger|Adolf Heusinger]], [[johannes-steinhoff|Johannes Steinhoff]], Johann von Kielmansegg, Jurgen Bennecke, Ernst Ferber, Karl Schnell, Franz Joseph Schulze and Ferdinand von Senger und Etterlin.[1] The post of NATO Commander in Chief of Allied Forces Central Europe was filled solely by former Nazis for sixteen consecutive years, from 1967 to 1983.[1]
