# Mollie Hemingway

Mollie Hemingway is the Editor-in-Chief of [[the-federalist|The Federalist]], a conservative online magazine, and a frequent commentator on Fox News.[1]

## Commentary on the Russiagate declassifications (July 2025)

In July 2025, during an interview on Fox News' *America Reports*, Hemingway argued that the newly released [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] documents indict the press itself, and that this is precisely why major outlets were declining to cover them.[1] "The media are hiding [the new Russiagate bombshells], and why? Because they were complicit in the crimes that were committed," she said.[1]

She continued that the outlets "can't very well report what actually happened because the whole conspiracy would have gone nowhere if the media hadn't been so willing to just republish these lies from Obama officials."[1] Her remarks were carried in the Badlands News Brief alongside [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]]'s statements on the statute of limitations for potential charges against [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and his intelligence chiefs.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] extended the argument, writing that it is "entirely likely" that at least some media organizations — which she termed "mockingbirds" — will eventually be named co-conspirators in a RICO case.[1] She noted that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been establishing that precedent for a couple of years, clarifying legal boundaries for the press through his defamation lawsuits, and asked pointedly why several media companies had been so quick to settle and what may have surfaced in discovery.[1]
