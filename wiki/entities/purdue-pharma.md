# Purdue Pharma

Purdue Pharma is an opioid manufacturer whose conduct has been the subject of federal criminal enforcement.[1]

## 2026 sentencing

In the week ending May 2, 2026, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced that Purdue Pharma had been sentenced for fraud and kickback conspiracies, listing the outcome among its weekly accomplishments on X alongside the [[comey-8647-indictment|Comey 8647 indictment]] and other actions.[1] The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] reproduced the department's summary in its coverage of Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]]'s public appearances.[1]

## 2025 opioid settlement

In late January 2025, Virginia Attorney General [[jason-miyares|Jason Miyares]] announced a $7.4 billion multistate settlement in principle with members of the [[sackler-family|Sackler family]] and Purdue Pharma over their leading role in creating and fueling the national opioid crisis.[2] Virginia was projected to receive up to approximately $107.4 million over fifteen years.[2] Under the Sacklers' leadership, Purdue invented, manufactured and aggressively marketed opioid products for decades, fueling waves of addiction and overdose deaths across the country.[2] The settlement ends the Sacklers' control of Purdue and their ability to sell opioids in the United States, and directs funding to communities over fifteen years for opioid addiction treatment, prevention and recovery programs.[2] Badlands commentary called the outcome a milestone against Big Pharma while faulting the absence of criminal prosecutions of anyone in the family or the Purdue organization.[2]
