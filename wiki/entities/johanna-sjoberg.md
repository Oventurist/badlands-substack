# Johanna Sjoberg

Johanna Sjoberg is a former employee of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] whose 2016 deposition was among the material released in the first batch of documents unsealed in January 2024 from the civil litigation connected to Epstein.[1]

## Deposition testimony

Sjoberg described [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] touching her breast "in a joking manner" while photographs were being taken.[1] Although her account had already circulated publicly, the January 3, 2024 release marked the first time her deposition itself was unsealed.[1] Sjoberg said she worked for Epstein at times and that he pressured her to go beyond her comfort level in giving sexualized massages.[1]

She also recounted a remark attributed to Epstein about [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]: "He said one time that Clinton likes them young, referring to girls."[1] That line, drawn from her deposition, became one of the most widely circulated items from the first tranche of unsealed filings.[1]

## Context

Sjoberg's deposition appeared alongside excerpts from depositions of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] and Virginia Roberts Giuffre in the same release, part of an unsealing process ordered by the court on December 18, 2023 and expected to name nearly 200 people over subsequent weeks.[1]
