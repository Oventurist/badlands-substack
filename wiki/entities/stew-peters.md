# Stew Peters

Stew Peters (rendered "Stu Peters" in the source) is an American alternative-media figure described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a "former mercenary-turned rapper-turned conspiracy guru."[1]

Ehret cites Peters' documentary *Occupied* as a recent example of pro-Nazi propaganda entering the alternative media space, grouping it with the ten-part neo-Nazi series [[europa-the-last-battle|*Europa: The Last Battle*]].[1] He argues that such films depend on [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] — a demonstrable forgery — as their evidentiary basis for the claim that Jews running the world are the source of all evil.[1]
