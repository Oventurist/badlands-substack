# Arizona House Ad Hoc Committee on Oversight, Accountability, and Big Tech

The Arizona House Ad Hoc Committee on Oversight, Accountability, and Big Tech is a special committee of the Arizona House of Representatives created in 2023 to investigate free speech violations by large technology companies and by government officials.[1]

## Formation

The committee was established after reporting that then-Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] had engaged with social media platforms to censor her critics and to suppress reports of fraud in the 2020 election on Twitter.[1] [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which covered the committee's proceedings, framed the underlying conduct as one element of a broader pattern of election manipulation.[1]

## September 2023 hearing

The committee held a hearing on September 5, 2023, examining censorship carried out by big tech firms in coordination with government actors.[1] The hearing became widely known for an exchange in which Democratic state representative [[cesar-aguilar|Cesar Aguilar]] told an expert witness that free speech was "very dangerous" while questioning why the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] does not permit censorship.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the hearing to argue that Hobbs, having presided over the disputed 2022 Arizona midterm elections and the "Drawer Three" controversy, had become "the perfect mascot for 2023's weaponized government and the persecution of political opponents," and that Arizona courts had already shielded her from accountability.[1]
