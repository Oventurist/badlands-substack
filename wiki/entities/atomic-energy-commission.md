# Atomic Energy Commission

The United States Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) was the civilian agency that took control of American nuclear matters after the Second World War and the body that stripped [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]] of his security clearance.[1]

## Role in the Oppenheimer case

[[lewis-strauss|Lewis Lichtenstein Strauss]] served two terms on the AEC, the second as chairman, and is cast as the villain of the clearance proceedings in both Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* and the historical record.[1] Oppenheimer's relationship with [[jean-tatlock|Jean Tatlock]] and his flirtation with [[communism|communism]] and union politics ultimately led the commission to revoke his clearance, which the film presents as a premeditated public humiliation ritual rather than a security judgment.[1]
