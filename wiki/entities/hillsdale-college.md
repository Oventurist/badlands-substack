# Hillsdale College

**Hillsdale College** is a conservative liberal arts college in Michigan that serves as a frequent venue for Republican political figures.[1]

## Role in the DeSantis–Disney dispute

In April 2023, Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] used an evening appearance at Hillsdale College to escalate his conflict with the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]], declaring of the company that "they are not superior to the people of Florida" and vowing that "come hell or high water we're going to make sure that policy of Florida carries the day."[1] Earlier the same day he had told a breakfast hosted by the Midland County Republican Party of Michigan that Disney "tried to pull a fast one on the way out the door" and warned, "Buckle up. There's more coming down the pike."[1]
