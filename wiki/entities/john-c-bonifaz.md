# John C. Bonifaz

John C. Bonifaz is an American attorney and election-law advocate who appears in the Badlands corpus in two distinct capacities: as an early critic of foreign influence in American voting technology, and as an adversary in litigation against Badlands writers.

In [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] quotes a letter Bonifaz sent to the [[national-institute-of-standards-and-technology|National Institute of Standards and Technology]] on behalf of Voter Action, which argued: "Dominion, Now the Largest or Second Largest Voting System Company, Is Foreign Controlled and Depends Upon Secret Source Code Created and Owned by Smartmatic, a Foreign Controlled Company With Ties to The Venezuelan Government Led by Hugo Chavez… Dominion is reported by Dun & Bradstreet to be a Toronto-based company with one listed key official, board members and employee, John Poulos, and under $18 million in sales for which the company obtains no income."

Ashe in America notes parenthetically that Bonifaz "separately is one of the masterminds in the lawfare against me and my co-defendants," presenting the juxtaposition as evidence that arguments about [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] and [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] code lineage were once advanced by figures now aligned against election-integrity researchers.
