# Jim Farley

Jim Farley is the chief executive officer of [[ford-motor-company|Ford Motor]].[1]

On Thursday, September 14, 2023, ahead of the start of the [[2023-united-auto-workers-strike|2023 United Auto Workers strike]], Farley said the [[united-auto-workers|UAW]]'s contract proposals would push Ford into bankruptcy and that there was "nothing going on" in negotiations.[1] Two days later the union described its talks with Ford as "reasonably productive," a markedly warmer characterization than its assessment of the other Detroit Three negotiations.[1]
