# Guam

Guam is a US island territory in the western Pacific that hosts significant American strategic assets, located roughly 3,400 kilometres from Pyongyang.[1] Its proximity to [[north-korea|North Korea]] makes it a recurring reference point in reporting on North Korean missile development.[1]

## January 2025 missile test coverage

After North Korea fired a ballistic missile with a suspected hypersonic warhead into the East Sea in January 2025 — launched from the Pyongyang area and travelling about 1,100 kilometres — South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff suggested the weapon could carry a hypersonic warhead of the type tested in 2024, and Yonhap News Agency reported that such a missile could potentially be used to strike US military bases on Guam.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] dismissed the reporting, mocking the notion of a strike on Guam and questioning both the existence of North Korean troops reportedly deployed in Ukraine and the credibility of North Korean missile stories generally.[1]
