# Ekrem Imamoglu

Ekrem Imamoglu is the opposition mayor of Istanbul and the principal political rival of Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]].[1]

## 2025 arrest and protests

In March 2025 Imamoglu was arrested on corruption charges, which he denies.[1] His arrest triggered mass anti-government protests across Turkey; demonstrators characterized the case as politically motivated and designed to end his prospects of becoming the country's next president.[1] Turkish authorities banned the protests but proved unable to stop them.[1]

The crackdown extended to the press: seven journalists, including AFP photojournalist [[yasin-akgul|Yasin Akgul]], were detained in dawn raids on 23 March while covering the demonstrations, prompting warnings that press freedom and democratic norms in Turkey were eroding.[1]
