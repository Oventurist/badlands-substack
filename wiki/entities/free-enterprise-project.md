# Free Enterprise Project

The Free Enterprise Project is a conservative shareholder activist program of the [[national-center-for-public-policy-research|National Center for Public Policy Research]], directed by [[scott-shepard|Scott Shepard]].[1]

The project uses shareholder pressure and litigation to push back against environmental, social, and governance ([[esg|ESG]]) commitments at large American corporations.[1] Its argument is that ESG has evolved from a set of voluntary guidelines into heavy-handed mandates advancing contested "social justice" ideologies, and that pursuing those mandates at shareholders' expense can amount to a breach of fiduciary duty exposing companies to legal action — actions the organization had already begun filing by mid-2023.[1]

The group credited consumer boycotts of companies such as [[target|Target]] and [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch]] with supplying the leverage that made these legal challenges viable, since customer defections demonstrated concrete financial harm from activist corporate positioning.[1]
