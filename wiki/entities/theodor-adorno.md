# Theodor Adorno

**Theodor Adorno** was a musicologist and social theorist described as a leading member of the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock]]/[[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]] orbit.[1] Former MI6 officer [[john-coleman|John Coleman]] claims that Adorno wrote all of [[the-beatles|the Beatles']] music and lyrics as part of a program to manufacture the [[1960s-american-counterculture-movement|1960s American counterculture]].[1]

Adorno died in 1969.[1] In the same year, the Beatles' music rights, held by Northern Songs, were sold out from under Paul McCartney and [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] to Associated Television (ATV); in the spring of 1970 McCartney announced the band had broken up, and in 1985 Michael Jackson bought the Beatles catalog from ATV.[1] The Badlands account places Adorno's death within a sequence of deaths and transfers around the band, alongside the 1967 suicide of manager Brian Epstein and the 1968 suicide of their lawyer David Jacobs.[1]
