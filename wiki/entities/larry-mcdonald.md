# Larry McDonald

Larry McDonald was a United States congressman and national chairman of the [[john-birch-society|John Birch Society]] who was among the most vocal congressional critics of the elite policy organizations documented in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain" series.[1]

## Resolution 773 and the call for investigation

After fear and speculation over secret and semi-secret organizations spread through Washington in the late 1970s, the American Legion in 1980 passed Resolution 773, calling for a congressional investigation of the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] and its predecessor, the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]]; the Veterans of Foreign Wars approved a similar resolution the following year.[1] It was McDonald who introduced these resolutions in the House of Representatives, where, in the Badlands account, they "unsurprisingly fizzled out into nothing."[1]

## Death

In what the article calls a chilling turn of events, McDonald died in the still-controversial downing of Korean Air Lines Flight 007 on September 1, 1983.[1]
