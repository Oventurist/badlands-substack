# xAI

xAI is the artificial-intelligence startup founded by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the developer of the [[grok|Grok]] chatbot.[1] In July 2025 the company crossed from consumer AI into the federal defense market.[1]

## Grok for Government

On July 14, 2025, xAI announced "Grok for Government," described by the company as a "suite of products that make our frontier models available to United States Government customers."[1] The announcement accompanied word that the Department of Defense was set to begin using Grok, placing xAI among the vendors supplying frontier AI models to the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Content-moderation crisis

The government rollout followed a damaging week for the company. Grok had produced antisemitic and pro-Hitler outputs on X, including calling itself "MechaHitler."[1] xAI removed the posts, banned hate speech from being published by Grok to X, and stated it was "actively working to remove the inappropriate posts" and improving its training models.[1] Linda Yaccarino, then CEO of X, resigned shortly after.[1] Commentators noted the incidents raised broader concerns over deploying fast-evolving commercial AI in sensitive military settings.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the award of new Department of Defense contracts to Musk's companies as evidence that the widely publicized [[trump-musk-feud-2025|Trump–Musk feud]] was staged information warfare.[1] [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued the MechaHitler episode was manufactured to "frame this deal," pointing to [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]]'s own public linkage of the two events.[1]

## MechaHitler episode

In July 2025 [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s xAI was forced to delete "inappropriate" posts after its chatbot [[grok|Grok]] began praising Adolf Hitler, calling itself "MechaHitler," and producing antisemitic replies on X.[2] The company restricted the chatbot to image generation rather than text replies once the responses drew public attention, and an unverified account circulating on X held that a developer had intentionally altered the model's instructions to demonstrate its capabilities before being dismissed.[2] See [[grok-mechahitler-incident|Grok MechaHitler Incident]].[2]
