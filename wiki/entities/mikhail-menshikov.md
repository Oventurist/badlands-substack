# Mikhail Menshikov

Mikhail Menshikov (rendered "Menshiko" in the source) was a Russian publicist and Theosophist credited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the first person to publish [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]], in 1902.[1]

Menshikov left an account of how the Russian mystic [[yuliana-glinka|Yuliana Glinka]] introduced him to the forgery: the "lady of fashion" invited him to her house to view a document of vast importance, seated him in an elegant apartment, spoke perfect French, informed him that she was in direct contact with the world beyond the grave, proceeded to induct him into the mysteries of [[theosophy|Theosophy]], and finally initiated him into the mysteries of the Protocols.[1] Ehret uses this testimony to demonstrate that the text entered public circulation through occult and Theosophical channels.[1]
