# David Rockefeller Center for Latin American Studies

The David Rockefeller Center for Latin American Studies is Harvard University's Latin America research arm, funded and named for [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]].[1] "The Harvard Control Grid" cites the center as one of the institutional bridges between Harvard and the Rockefeller dynasty.[1]

[[martin-escobari|Martín Escobari]], co-president of [[general-atlantic|General Atlantic]] and a member of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board, sits on the center's advisory board — a role the article says places him inside Harvard's Latin America arm and adjacent to Latin American business and political networks.[1]
