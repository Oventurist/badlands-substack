# Columbia Journalism Review

The Columbia Journalism Review (CJR) is an American magazine of press criticism published by the Columbia University Graduate School of Journalism.[1]

## In Badlands commentary

In March 2024, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] pointed readers to a CJR opinion piece about media coverage of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Ohio "bloodbath" speech, describing it as a "very silly OpEd" in which journalists lament that each attempt to stop Trump only makes him stronger.[1] The citation was offered as evidence that establishment press institutions were beginning to acknowledge the counterproductive effects of their own coverage and of the broader [[weaponization-of-government]] campaign against Trump.[1]

## Indictment of New York Times Russiagate coverage (2023)

In late January 2023, the Columbia Journalism Review issued a scathing indictment of the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] for yellow journalism during the Trump-Russia saga, with the finding summarized as the hyper-partisan "paper of record" having operated in bad faith.[2] Coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] argued that CJR's findings were not confined to the Times but accurately reflected the conduct of the press generally during [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]].[2] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the review's willingness to turn on prestigious outlets as evidence of a broader shift, with media institutions positioning themselves ahead of coming accountability.[2]
