# Andrew Lobaczewski

**Andrew Lobaczewski** is described in the opening installment of the Badlands Media series [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] as a brilliant psychologist and the author of [[political-ponerology|*Political Ponerology: the Science of Evil*]].[1] The essay notes that he was an escapee from communist Poland and that he had many close encounters with psychopathic personality types over the course of his life.[1]

## Account of psychopathy

According to the summary given in the article, Lobaczewski found that psychopaths feel *oppressed* by the common morality of ordinary people, simply because they see no reason for it.[1] They often quite literally regard themselves as a "more evolved" version of humanity, on the grounds that they are unburdened by what they consider the silly moral considerations of normal folk.[1] The author extends this observation with the claim that psychopathic personality types frequently wish to take revenge on the populace whose morality prevents them from satisfying their appetites without impediment.[1]

## Role in the argument

Lobaczewski's work is cited alongside that of the clinical psychologist [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]], author of [[shadow-men|*Shadow Men*]], in support of the essay's central contention that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "amounts to organized psychopathy."[1] The article treats accurate knowledge about the nature of psychopathy as one of the master keys not only to defeating the Deep State but to creating a world in which it cannot re-emerge, framing this material as a practical component of [[information-warfare|information warfare]] and of the broader analysis of [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathy and parasitism]].[1]
