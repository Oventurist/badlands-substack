# Shangela

Shangela is the drag persona of Darius Jeremy ("DJ") Pierce, a Texas performer who became one of the best-known contestants in the *RuPaul's Drag Race* franchise before facing multiple public accusations of sexual assault.[1]

## Career

Shangela was a breakthrough star of *RuPaul's Drag Race*, described on the show as "the debutantess of the Deep South" and explaining the name as "like 'Angela' with an 'S-H.'"[1] Despite an early elimination in Season Two, when the performer was in her late twenties, a more polished version of the character returned for two further installments of the franchise.[1] Shangela subsequently built a public image as a self-affirming queer activist and crossed over into mainstream television and film.[1]

## Allegations

In March 2024, *[[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]]* published an investigation, begun in November 2022 and based on 16 months of research, court documents and interviews with several accusers.[1] Four people alleged that Pierce either sexually assaulted them or attempted to have sex with them when they were too inebriated to consent; a fifth said Pierce attempted anal penetration in a bathroom closet after his advances were rejected.[1] The reporting uncovered a previously unreported police complaint filed in Los Angeles as well as multiple independent accusations spanning several years.[1]

## Commentary

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], characterized the story as shallower than expected, describing much of it as a dispute over what consent means between heavily intoxicated adults.[1] He noted the article's recurring emphasis on the danger that "conservatives may weaponize it against the queer community," and pointed to one alleged victim who declined to report the abuse out of certainty that police are racist and transphobic.[1] Esler argued that the fear expressed in the piece was largely ideologically self-generated and that no conservative response would be treated as anything other than hate.[1]
