# Andrew Bates

Andrew Bates is a [[white-house]] deputy press secretary in the administration of [[joe-biden]] who issued the administration's condemnation of campus protest rhetoric during the [[2024-columbia-university-protests]].[1]

In a statement shared with The Hill, Bates said that while every American has the right to peaceful protest, "calls for violence and physical intimidation targeting Jewish students and the Jewish community are blatantly Antisemitic, unconscionable, and dangerous — they have absolutely no place on any college campus, or anywhere in the United States of America."[1] He continued that "echoing the rhetoric of terrorist organizations, especially in the wake of the worst massacre committed against the Jewish people since the Holocaust, is despicable," concluding that the White House condemned those statements in the strongest terms.[1] The statement was issued on a Sunday as protests continued at colleges across the country, including at [[columbia-university]].[1]

## Response to Trump's NATO remarks (February 2024)

After [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said at a February 10, 2024 rally that he would let Russia "do whatever the hell they want" to [[nato|NATO]] allies that fail to contribute sufficiently to collective defense, Bates, speaking for the [[white-house|White House]], denounced the comments as "unhinged."[2] The rebuke did not prevent the remarks from resonating by the following morning with European commentators who argue that Europe cannot depend on the United States to deter Russia.[2]
