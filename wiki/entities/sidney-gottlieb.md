# Sidney Gottlieb

Sidney Gottlieb was the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] chemist who ran the agency's [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] mind-control program. Badlands Media reports that the [[harvard-psilocybin-project|Harvard Psilocybin Project]], conducted from 1960 to 1962 by [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] with the blessing of [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]], was overseen directly by Gottlieb.[1]

That period coincides with the years in which [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]] was a subject of Murray's own experiments at [[harvard-university|Harvard]], and the article uses Gottlieb's supervisory role to argue that the psychedelic experimentation on students was not an academic curiosity but an intelligence operation aimed at behavior modification on a mass scale.[1]
