# National Press Foundation

The **National Press Foundation** is a journalism-training nonprofit that Badlands Media includes in its list of bodies "policing journalism."[1]

The article groups it with the [[pulitzer-center-on-crisis-reporting|Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting]], the [[international-center-for-journalists|International Center for Journalists]], the [[solutions-journalism-network|Solutions Journalism Network]] and the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]], stating that all of them are connected to individuals belonging to one or multiple of the clandestine think tanks covered in the series — an example, in the article's framing, of how [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] extend into the media.[1]
