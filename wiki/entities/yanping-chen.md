# Yanping Chen

Yanping Chen is a Chinese American scientist who became the plaintiff in litigation seeking to unmask the confidential source behind reporting on an FBI investigation into her.[1]

## Litigation against Catherine Herridge's source

Chen sued over reporting produced by [[catherine-herridge|Catherine Herridge]] while Herridge was with [[fox-news|Fox News]], which drew on information supplied by a confidential source about an [[fbi|FBI]] investigation into Chen.[1] In August 2023 a court ordered Herridge — by then a reporter for [[cbs-news|CBS News]] — to disclose the identity and motive of the source.[1] During a subsequent deposition, Chen's lawyers stated in a filing, Herridge "refused to answer questions regarding the identity of her confidential source(s) and other aspects of her reporting process and editorial decision-making," exposing the journalist to a contempt charge.[1]

The dispute was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of November 1, 2023.[1]
