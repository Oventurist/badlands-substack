# Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

The **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)** is the United States federal public health agency responsible for disease surveillance, outbreak response and health guidance.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the CDC is named in the headline of an item on the 2026 [[ebola|Ebola]] outbreak in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]] and [[uganda|Uganda]], which characterizes the agency and aid groups such as the [[international-rescue-committee|International Rescue Committee]] as "fearmongering" over the epidemic. The accompanying commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that repeated pandemic warnings following think-tank predictions of increasingly frequent pandemics are suspect, and that in the wake of the affirmation of the lab leak theory such official messaging is "less believable than in prior years." This adversarial framing of the CDC as a narrative instrument rather than a neutral health authority is characteristic of the corpus and is contested.
