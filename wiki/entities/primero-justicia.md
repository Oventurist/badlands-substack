# Primero Justicia

**Primero Justicia** (Justice First) is a Venezuelan opposition organization identified by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a recipient of United States funding during the campaign against [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]].[1]

Following Chávez's survival of the [[2002-venezuelan-coup-attempt|April 2002 coup]] and the collapse of the business elite's owners' strike, the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] quadrupled its Venezuela budget and channeled millions to opposition NGOs including Primero Justicia and [[sumate|Súmate]].[1] DeLarme presents this funding stream, alongside the revival of [[plan-avila|Plan Ávila]] by U.S. military advisors, as the civilian half of Washington's response to Chávez's control of [[pdvsa|PDVSA]].[1]
