# Cause of America

Cause of America is an election-integrity organization referenced in Badlands Media coverage as a source of practical tools for citizens seeking to scrutinize local election administration.[1]

In her March 23, 2023 article "Rooting Out Election Corruption," [[holly-at-altitude|Holly at Altitude]] directed readers who wanted help with budgeting and tools to causeofamerica.org, in the context of a proposed campaign to challenge local and state election equipment budgets.[1] That proposal asked citizens to identify who controls election budgets in their jurisdiction — most likely the county commissioners — obtain contracts and financial records for election equipment, maintenance, and operation, and build a comparison budget between the cost of electronic voting equipment and the cost of conducting the same election with [[hand-counted-paper-ballots|hand-counted paper ballots]].[1] The resulting cost difference was to be presented publicly to elected officials at town halls, meetings, and in local papers, framing the elimination of electronic voting equipment as a fiscal issue and rolling it into a campaign issue.[1]
