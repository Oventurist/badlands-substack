# Avigdor Liberman

Avigdor Liberman is an Israeli politician who heads the opposition party Yisrael Beytenu and previously served as Israel's defense minister.[1] He re-entered international headlines in June 2025 when he publicly disclosed that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had unilaterally approved the transfer of weapons to the [[abu-shabab-clan|Abu Shabab clan]], an armed criminal gang operating in the Gaza Strip in opposition to [[hamas|Hamas]] rule.[1]

## The Gaza weapons disclosure

Liberman made the allegation in a June 5, 2025 interview with the Kan public broadcaster, saying that Netanyahu had authorized the arms transfer without cabinet deliberation.[1] Israeli defense sources subsequently confirmed the substance of the report, and Netanyahu himself later acknowledged the policy, arguing that arming anti-Hamas factions had saved the lives of Israeli soldiers.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized Liberman as a whistleblower rather than merely a political rival, noting that Liberman had resigned as defense minister after learning that Netanyahu was permitting suitcases of cash to be delivered into Gaza for Hamas.[1] In the Badlands reading, Liberman's June 2025 revelation reinforced a long-argued thesis that Israeli policy had deliberately sustained Islamic militant factions in Gaza as instruments of political management.[1] His disclosure was placed alongside criticism from [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]], leaving Netanyahu politically flanked from both the center-left and the ultra-Orthodox right at the same time.[1]
