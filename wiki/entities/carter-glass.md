# Carter Glass

Carter Glass was a United States Senator and a co-author of the banking legislation that bears his name.[1] In November 1929 Glass named [[charles-e-mitchell|Charles E. Mitchell]] of [[national-city-bank|National City Bank]] as one of the main people behind the market crash, saying, "Mitchell more than any 50 men is responsible for this stock crash."[1] The [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall Act]], enacted as part of the Banking Act of 1933, followed from the abuses Glass and his colleagues identified.[1]
