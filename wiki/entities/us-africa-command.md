# US Africa Command

US Africa Command (AFRICOM) is the unified combatant command of the United States military responsible for operations across the African continent.[1] In December 2025 it was identified as one of the headquarters that would be downgraded under a sweeping [[pentagon|Pentagon]] overhaul of American command structures.[1]

## Proposed reorganisation

Under the draft plan reported by the Washington Post, the standing of Africa Command, [[us-central-command|US Central Command]] and [[united-states-european-command|US European Command]] would be diminished by bringing these theatres under a newly created entity called [[us-international-command|US International Command]].[1] The restructuring is being driven in large part by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]]'s pledge to "break the status quo" and to reduce the number of four-star generals across the armed forces, alongside his long-stated intention to purge the "woke agenda" from within military ranks.[1] A companion entity, [[us-americas-command|US Americas Command]], would consolidate hemispheric responsibilities.[1]
