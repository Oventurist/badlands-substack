# Henry Zhao

Henry Zhao is described in United States congressional testimony as a Chinese Communist Party official who was a recipient of business communications from [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1]

## The July 2017 WhatsApp message

IRS whistleblower [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] pointed to a WhatsApp message sent by Hunter Biden to Zhao on July 30, 2017, in which Biden pressed Zhao to follow his "orders" and stated that his father was sitting with him.[1] Shapley alleged that he raised concerns about the message with Justice Department prosecutors, who rebuffed him.[1]

The message became a central exhibit in Republican arguments that the Biden family traded on the vice president's and later president's name, and it featured in the case House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] made in June 2023 for opening an impeachment inquiry into Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]].[1]
