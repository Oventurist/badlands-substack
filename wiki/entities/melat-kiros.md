# Melat Kiros

Melat Kiros is a progressive Democratic candidate in Colorado who defeated incumbent Rep. [[diana-degette|Diana DeGette]] in the 2026 Democratic primary, running with the backing of [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]].[1]

## 2026 primary upset

Kiros's victory over a long-serving incumbent rattled [[house-of-representatives|House]] Democrats, who reassessed their political strategy in its wake and described the result as evidence that even established progressive incumbents can be unseated from the left.[1] Progressive organizations announced plans to continue targeting additional Democratic incumbents.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized Kiros as part of a wider socialist surge inside the Democratic Party, referring to "the commie Kiros that unseated Diana DeGette," and predicted that the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]] would be "hilarious."[1] The commentary linked the result to a broader [[2026-democrat-primary-civil-war|intra-party conflict]] in which the party's leadership structures survived while down-ballot voters chose insurgents.[1]
