# Listen to Michigan

Listen to Michigan is an activist campaign formed ahead of Michigan's February 27, 2024 presidential primary to urge Democratic voters to cast "uncommitted" ballots as a protest against President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## Organization and aims

The campaign was made up of more than 30 Michigan officials who pledged to vote "uncommitted" in the state's presidential primary in order to demand that Biden support a ceasefire in Gaza.[1] On the Saturday before the primary week, the group posted a video on X featuring Rep. [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]], the first Palestinian American woman to serve in Congress, who told residents: "We feel completely neglected and just unseen by our government. If you want us to be louder, then come here and vote uncommitted."[1]

## Political context

Michigan is home to one of the nation's largest Arab American and Muslim populations, and Democrats feared that alienating that constituency could damage Biden in a battleground state central to his 2020 victory and expected to be close again in November 2024.[1] The effort formed part of the wider [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]] and unfolded amid chants of "Genocide Joe" and "ceasefire now" following Biden on the campaign trail.[1]

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the tactic as a gimmick designed to demonstrate that the Palestinian-aligned contingent in Michigan could be outcome-determinative in November, and placed it alongside union defections over electric-vehicle policy as evidence of a fracturing Democratic coalition.[1]

## February 2024 primary result

The Listen to Michigan effort, championed by Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] and allied with the nationwide [[abandon-biden|Abandon Biden]] campaign, culminated in the [[2024-michigan-democratic-primary|Michigan Democratic primary]] of February 27, 2024, in which more than 100,000 Democrats voted "uncommitted" — 13.3% of the statewide total and more than nine times [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 margin of victory in the state.[2] In Wayne County, home to Dearborn and its large Muslim population, 21% voted uncommitted with 75% of the count completed.[2]
