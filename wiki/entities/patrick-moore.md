# Patrick Moore

Patrick Moore is an attorney for President [[joe-biden]] whose Boston law-firm office became a focus of the investigation into Biden's handling of classified records. Moore was the lawyer who moved boxes of documents out of the Penn Biden Center in Washington, D.C. before the initial trove of classified material was discovered at that think tank.[1]

## Role in the classified documents matter

According to a letter from the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] to Senators [[ron-johnson]] and [[chuck-grassley]], NARA contacted Biden's personal counsel on November 3, 2022 to arrange the collection of boxes from the Penn Biden Center and was told at that point that Moore had already moved other boxes to his law firm in Boston.[1] The Archives notified the [[department-of-justice]] Office of Inspector General on November 4 that the documents had been relocated.[1]

The materials were collected on November 9 and secured at the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library in Boston.[1] Acting Archivist Debra Steidel Wall wrote that "NARA staff retrieved nine boxes from Mr. Moore's Boston office" — a figure that had not previously been made public.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] treated the disclosure as another expansion of a scandal that had grown from a handful of misplaced pages to boxes of documents and handwritten notes spanning Biden's Senate and vice-presidential years.[1]
