# United States Military

The U.S. armed forces are treated in "A Bright Red Line" as the resource ultimately at stake in Ukraine's bid for [[nato|NATO]] membership.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Under [[article-5|Article 5]], accession would obligate the United States to respond to attacks on Ukraine as attacks on itself, meaning that if Ukraine joins — which the article says it essentially must to sustain the war of attrition — it could well be American troops shipping to the front lines.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

The article finds an irony in this: many of the same young people who plastered social media with Ukrainian flags and filmed tearful TikToks are now serving in the military.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) While declining to disrespect those who serve and acknowledging many capable fighters willing to lay down their lives, the author reports a general global consensus that the U.S. military is "quickly becoming the softest fighting force on earth," a picture that looks bleaker still when juxtaposed against the training of Russian Spetsnaz forces.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) With recruitment at an all-time low, the article suggests the force in its current form could become the cannon fodder [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] requires.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) It closes by asking what would happen if service members who enlisted for money suddenly faced the real prospect of deployment to the Middle East or Ukraine.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

## Restrictions on battlefield command

Badlands Media's Benghazi reporting presents the military as an institution deliberately handcuffed by political leadership.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Citing Q drop 2640, the article asks why President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] placed restrictions on U.S. military command's ability to attack targets at will by requiring direct White House approval for each action, and argues that this micromanagement protected weapons sales and money laundering while allowing [[isis|ISIS]] to expand.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] is identified as the White House official at the top of that chain of command.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) By contrast, the article credits [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with immediately removing the [[white-house|White House]] from the battlefield chain of command, after which the unleashed military destroyed ISIS in less than a year.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

On the night of the Benghazi attack, a squadron member at Aviano Air Base in northeastern Italy described planes being armed and a flight line full of personnel ready to go: "The whole night we were told that we are waiting on a call," he said, and the order never came.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) He added, "At our level, we were doing everything we were supposed to be doing. At everybody else's level above us, it was political."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article also notes that CIA officers who tried to warn about looted shoulder-fired missiles were ignored, and stresses that not everyone in the three-letter agencies or the armed forces was complicit.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Intervention in the 2016 transition

"Bad Actors" claims that [[barack-obama|Obama]] intended to prevent [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from taking office after the 2016 election and was stopped by the military.[3] Citing Q drop 36 of November 2, 2017, Joe Lange writes that the military was prepared to do "the UNTHINKABLE" — a planned coup to remove the Obama administration along with corrupt politicians — and that this plan was purposely leaked to Obama and his officials as a warning, at which point they backed down.[3] [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was, in this telling, never going to be allowed to become president: "The military was going to make sure of that. They valued their oath and deserve credit for that."[3]

Lange draws a deliberate contrast with 2020. The military acted behind the scenes in 2016 but, with Trump as Commander in Chief in 2020, allowed the election to be taken despite having the power to prevent it — because the plan was always to let [[joe-biden|Biden]] be installed so that he would become a millstone around the Democrats' neck and expose corruption across every system, including elections and the justice system.[3] Because the cabal lost control of the presidency in 2016, Lange argues, it no longer commands the greatest military in the world and is more vulnerable than ever; the military is now said to be executing a plan to remove the insurgency and the puppet masters controlling it.[3]
