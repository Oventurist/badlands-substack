# Phoenix Ikner

Phoenix Ikner is the 20-year-old student identified by police as the suspected gunman in the April 17, 2025 shooting at [[florida-state-university|Florida State University]] in Tallahassee.[1]

## The shooting

The shooting began at approximately 11:50 a.m. on Thursday, April 17, 2025, killing two people and injuring six others.[1] At an afternoon press conference, authorities named Ikner, a student at the school, as the suspected shooter.[1] He was taken to an area hospital after being "neutralized" by responding officers.[1]

Police stated that Ikner's mother, Jessica Ikner, is a deputy with the [[leon-county-sheriffs-office|Leon County Sheriff's Office]] and that Ikner used his mother's weapon in the killings.[1] Authorities said Jessica Ikner had served as an officer for more than 18 years and had done a "tremendous job" in her position.[1]
