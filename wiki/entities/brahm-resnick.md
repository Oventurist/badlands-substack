# Brahm Resnick

Brahm Resnick is a political anchor and reporter for 12 News (KPNX) in Phoenix, Arizona, described in Badlands coverage as a far-left member of the Arizona press corps.[1]

## May 2023 Kari Lake press conference

At a press conference held by [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] on Tuesday, May 23, 2023 — the day after Judge Thompson dismissed Lake's election challenge — Resnick asked what The Gateway Pundit characterized as a nasty question about Lake's attorneys.[1] Lake responded by telling the assembled crowd that Resnick had been wrong on every major story and that he had wanted masks on children.[1] Resnick reacted by shouting, "You make up sh*t!", drawing loud boos from the crowd.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the real story of the press conference was the conduct of the press corps rather than the exchange itself, writing that reporters yelling profanities at the subject of their reporting is bad form.[1] She noted a separate moment in which a reporter referred to The Gateway Pundit's Jordan Conradsen as Lake's "PR guy" after he restated facts of the case before asking his question, and observed that Lake's ability to trigger the press is second only to that of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — with the implication that when journalists are triggered, they reveal themselves.[1]
