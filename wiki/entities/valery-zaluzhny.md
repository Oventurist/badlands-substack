# Valery Zaluzhny

Valery Zaluzhny is a Ukrainian general and diplomat who served as commander-in-chief of Ukraine's armed forces before being removed from the post and reassigned as Kiev's ambassador to the United Kingdom.[1]

## Military career

Zaluzhny was promoted to general in 2017, two years before Vladimir Zelensky took office as president of Ukraine, and it was under Zelensky that he was named commander of all ground forces of Ukraine.[1] Amid growing tensions between the two men, Zelensky removed the general from his command in February 2024 and dispatched him to London to serve as Ukraine's ambassador to Britain.[1]

## Reported presidential ambitions

In August 2025 The Guardian reported an "increasing belief" in Kiev that Zaluzhny was preparing to challenge Zelensky in a potential presidential race.[1] The newspaper claimed that while Zaluzhny had painstakingly concealed any political ambition, "many assume he is just biding his time before entering the fray," and cited the general-turned-envoy's supposed musings about how he would present himself to Ukrainian voters and what platform he would run on.[1] The outlet further stated that Zaluzhny had been receiving a steady flow of Ukrainian and Western dignitaries both at the embassy in London and in Kiev earlier that year.[1]
