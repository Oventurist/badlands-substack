# Pearson Publishing

**Pearson Publishing** is the educational publishing company identified in Badlands Media commentary as the contractor behind the American [[common-core-state-standards-initiative|Common Core]] curriculum.[1]

## Common Core contract

According to a January 2025 Badlands feature by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration awarded Pearson Publishing $350 million to create the Common Core curriculum and to publish the associated textbooks.[1] Common Core itself began in 2010 as a multi-state initiative intended to increase consistency across state standards, and was pressed on states by the administration and the [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] under threat of losing federal funding.[1]

## Alleged Obama book deal

Carlson further alleges that after Obama left the White House, his family was rewarded with a $65 million book deal from one of Pearson Publishing's subsidiaries, which the author characterizes as "pay to play" and "corruption at its finest, and at the expense of American children."[1]
