# Norman Schwarzkopf Jr.

General Norman Schwarzkopf Jr. was the United States Army commander called upon by President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] to lead Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm, protecting Saudi Arabia from Saddam Hussein and removing Iraqi forces from Kuwait.[1]

## Career

Schwarzkopf also played a part in the invasion of Grenada under President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] and in the invasion of Panama under Bush to remove Manuel Noriega.[1] He rose to the top of the United States military under George H.W. Bush.[1]

## Family connection

He was the son of Major General [[norman-schwarzkopf-sr|Herbert Norman Schwarzkopf]], the CIA officer who trained the first generation of Iran's [[savak|SAVAK]] secret police.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites the father-and-son sequence as an illustration of Q's claim in post #350 that power was promised to those who "played the game," including power over the U.S. military for worldwide dominance and the installation of like-kind regimes; in Lange's framing the Schwarzkopfs were members of the "club" that staffs the [[shadow-government|shadow government]].[1]
