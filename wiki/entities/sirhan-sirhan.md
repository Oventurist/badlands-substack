# Sirhan Sirhan

Sirhan Sirhan is the young Palestinian man convicted of the June 1968 killing of Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles, and described in Badlands Media coverage as having been "made the lone scapegoat" for a far more complex plot.[1]

## The case against the official account

Although Sirhan confessed to the crime, he also stated that he had no memory of what happened, a claim that has fed decades of argument that he was a programmed or hypnotically conditioned patsy rather than an independent assassin.[1] Declassified reports have shown that Sirhan joined a California Rosicrucian temple in 1966, two years before the shooting — a detail Badlands cites as evidence of occult and intelligence-linked influence on his formation.[1]

The strongest physical objection to the official narrative comes from the forensic record. Los Angeles County medical examiner and coroner Thomas Noguchi determined that the fatal bullet entered behind Kennedy's right ear, fired from a distance of roughly one to one and a half inches, and travelled from the back of the neck rather than from the front.[1] Witnesses, however, placed Sirhan no closer than about eighteen inches from Kennedy and in front of him, leading some researchers to suggest that a security guard fired the killing shot.[1]

Sirhan's attorneys William Pepper and Laurie Dusek maintained in a 2011 filing supporting a further appeal that the bullet that struck Kennedy's neck did not come from Sirhan's gun, and that the bullet entered into evidence had been switched for another.[1] Journalist Sherifa Zuhur summarised these discrepancies in a 2018 essay for Al Jazeera.[1]

## Wider conspiracy claims

In 2021, Covert Action Magazine writer Jeremy Kuzmarov published an analysis titled "New Evidence Implicates CIA, LAPD, FBI and Mafia as Plotters in Elaborate 'Hit' Plan to Prevent RFK From Ever Reaching White House," which Badlands describes as the best available reconstruction of who carried out the murder and cover-up and why.[1] That account places Sirhan at the visible edge of an operation involving the [[cia|CIA]], the [[lapd|LAPD]], the [[fbi|FBI]] and organised crime.[1]
