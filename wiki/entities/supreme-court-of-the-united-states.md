# Supreme Court of the United States

The Supreme Court of the United States is the nation's highest court and the expected final arbiter of the 2026 litigation over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order on election administration.[1]

## Trump election order

After a federal appeals court blocked key portions of the order in 23 Democrat-led states, reporting described the decision as setting up a likely Supreme Court showdown over the scope of presidential authority in regulating federal elections and the balance of power between the federal government and the states.[1] Badlands commentary observed that the Court would be deciding "dangerously close to the midterms" — exactly the drama expected from what the Brief calls fake election narratives — and noted that history suggests the justices decline to make changes so close to an election, while allowing that "they've surprised us before."[1] The underlying question posed was constitutional: if the states lack authority over the [[united-states-postal-service|US Postal Service]] and the President does too, who is authorized to exercise its decision rights.[1]
