# Thomas Bollyky

Thomas Bollyky is the director of the global health program at the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], a Washington think tank.[1]

## Commentary on global health funding

In October 2025 reporting by [[politico|Politico]] on cuts to global health funding, Bollyky observed that "there's not a lot one can see geopolitically that would give people confidence that this is a great moment for cooperation or significant reinvestment in global health," setting aside the polarization that emerged around public health during the Covid pandemic.[1] The article noted that nine European countries, including the biggest donors, were slashing foreign aid with no one stepping up to fill the void, and that overall global health funding had dropped to a 15-year low in 2025.[1]

## Badlands response

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the Council on Foreign Relations was not truly lamenting the death of health cooperation and investment but the death of globalism, upon which the organization's existence depends.[1] The commentary added that those providing expert analysis in the article were themselves impacted entities, and that the [[invisible-enemy|invisible enemy]] was becoming visible in time for observers to watch its demise.[1]
