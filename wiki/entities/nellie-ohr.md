# Nellie Ohr

Nellie Ohr is a researcher and the wife of Justice Department official [[bruce-ohr|Bruce Ohr]].[1] In Badlands Media's telling of the Russian collusion story, she worked for [[crowdstrike|CrowdStrike]] gathering the evidence that was folded into the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]], the opposition-research file funded by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and compiled by [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]].[1]

## Significance in the Spygate narrative

The article treats the Ohr partnership as the mechanism by which unverified dossier material was laundered into the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[us-department-of-justice|Justice Department]], and ultimately into the [[carter-page|Carter Page]] FISA applications.[1] A Q drop quoted in the piece repeatedly cycles the names "Bruce Ohr" and "Nellie Ohr" alongside CrowdStrike to underline that chain.[1] Badlands further asserts that Bruce Ohr's later cooperation with investigators was undertaken "in effort to save Nellie Ohr."[1]
