# Douglas J. Bennet

Douglas J. Bennet was an American foreign-policy official and the father of Colorado Senator [[michael-bennet|Michael Bennet]].[1] Badlands Media cited his career as evidence that his son belongs to an entrenched establishment political family rather than to the ranks of citizen-legislators.[1]

Bennet served as Assistant Secretary of State for International Organization Affairs and in that capacity attended the 1994 Bilderberg meeting.[1] He was also associated with the "American Council on Germany/Young Leaders" program, which Badlands Media described as populated by intelligence figures and deep-political operators of the kind at home in Davos.[1]

The article invoked this lineage to contrast Senator Bennet's background with the military service records of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and [[edward-snowden|Edward Snowden]], both of whom enlisted during the early 2000s while the Senate's political elite supported deploying troops to Iraq and Afghanistan.[1]
