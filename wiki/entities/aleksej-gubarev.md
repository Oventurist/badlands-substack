# Aleksej Gubarev

Aleksej Gubarev is a Russian entrepreneur who owns [[xbt-holdings|XBT Holdings SA]], the Cyprus-based web-services company that operated the Webzilla internet hosting business.[1]

## Role in the dossier allegations

Gubarev is mentioned in the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]] as a "hacking expert."[1] The dossier's claim that Webzilla played a key role in the early-2016 hacking of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] originated with [[olga-galkina|Olga Galkina]], who worked at XBT in Cyprus in 2016 and who fed the allegation to [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] for onward transmission to [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]].[1] The trial correspondent characterized the allegations attributed to Galkina as complete nonsense, noting that everyone implicated denied them and that the Mueller Special Counsel found no supporting evidence.[1]
