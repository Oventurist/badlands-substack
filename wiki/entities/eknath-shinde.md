# Eknath Shinde

**Eknath Shinde** is the deputy chief minister of the Indian state of [[maharashtra|Maharashtra]].[1]

## Statement on missing Pakistani visa holders (April 2025)

In April 2025, following the central government directive ordering [[pakistan|Pakistani]] short-term visa holders to leave [[india|India]] in the wake of the [[pahalgam-terror-attack|Pahalgam terror attack]], Shinde said that 107 such individuals were unaccounted for in the state.[1] His claim was contradicted the following day by chief minister [[devendra-fadnavis|Devendra Fadnavis]], who said no Pakistanis were unaccounted for, with the state home department reporting that all but 14 of the 55 Pakistanis in Maharashtra on short-term visas had exited and that the remaining 14 had been tracked down.[1]
