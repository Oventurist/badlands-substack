# Al-Malika

Al-Malika is the Iraqi political figure identified in Badlands Media coverage as the favourite to emerge as Prime Minister of [[iraq|Iraq]] following the November 2025 Iraqi parliamentary elections.[1]

## Government formation deadlock

More than four months after the conclusion of the parliamentary elections, the winning bloc had still not formed a government.[1] According to reports cited in the Brief, Al-Malika is rumoured to be running much of the Iraqi government in practice despite lacking formal authority, an arrangement that leaves the country governed by an informal executive during the [[iran-war|Iran War]].[1]

## Relations with Trump and Syria

Two months before the April 2026 Brief, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly attacked Iraq's new government on [[truth-social|Truth Social]], singling out Al-Malika.[1] Badlands assesses that the attacks had the opposite of their apparent intended effect: Iraqis rallied around a figure they perceived as an adversary of the Zionist West.[1]

A month before the Brief, Al-Malika publicly described Syrian President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]] as "a brother" and offered to restore ties with [[syria|Syria]] after years without diplomatic relations caused by the Syrian Civil War.[1] That opening preceded Iraq's April 2 announcement that it had restarted pipeline oil shipments to Syria, a step that side-steps [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s proposal to route Persian Gulf pipelines through [[israel|Israel]].[1]

Because Iraq is simultaneously shipping oil to [[turkey|Turkey]] via the [[baku-tbilisi-ceyhan-pipeline|BTC pipeline]], Badlands concludes that Al-Malika's government and President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] are cooperating, and that Trump's public aggression toward Iraq is therefore [[kayfabe]] — staged conflict masking a coordinated [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] plan.[1]
