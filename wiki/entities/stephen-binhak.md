# Stephen Binhak

Stephen Binhak is a defense attorney who spearheaded the effort to send Arizona's 2020 Republican electors case back to a grand jury.[1]

Following Maricopa County Superior Court Judge Sam J. Myers's May 2025 ruling that prosecutors had failed to present the grand jury with the text of the 1887 [[electoral-count-act|Electoral Count Act]], Binhak said, "We are extremely pleased with the court's ruling, and we think the judge got it exactly right."[1] The decision was described as a major setback for Arizona Attorney General [[kris-mayes|Kris Mayes]], who pledged to appeal in order to keep the prosecution alive.[1] Attorneys in the matter said the ruling would require Mayes to assemble a new grand jury and present the case together with a copy of the statute; the initial presentation had taken weeks.[1] See [[arizona-fake-electors-case|Arizona fake electors case]].[1]
