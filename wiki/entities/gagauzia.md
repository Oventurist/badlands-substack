# Gagauzia

Gagauzia is an autonomous region located inside [[moldova|Moldova]], a European Union candidate state bordering Ukraine.[1] The region has become a focal point of Moldova's internal struggle between pro-EU central authorities and Euroskeptic regional politics.[1]

## The Gutsul case

Gagauzia's elected leader, [[evgenia-gutsul|Evgenia Gutsul]], was sentenced by a Moldovan court to seven years in prison on charges of illegally financing the Euroskeptic SOR party, which Moldova banned in 2023.[1] She was additionally ordered to pay 40 million Moldovan lei ($2.3 million) to the state, and denied the charges as politically motivated.[1]

Badlands commentary presented maps illustrating Gagauzia's position within Moldova and Moldova's proximity to Ukraine, arguing the prosecution of a regional leader who had won election demonstrated the coercive character of the European integration project in the post-Soviet space.[1]
