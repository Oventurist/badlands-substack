# James Forrestal

James Forrestal was United States Secretary of the Navy who, with [[allen-dulles]] and officials of the [[office-of-naval-intelligence]], pressured New York Governor [[thomas-dewey]] to release [[lucky-luciano]] by commuting his sentence on condition that he be deported to Italy.[1] Three years after this occurred, the Badlands Media account states, Forrestal was assassinated when he was thrown from the sixteenth floor of the Bethesda Naval Hospital, where he was a patient undergoing mental evaluation.[1]

## Anti-communism and mentorship of Kennedy

A separate Badlands Media essay describes Forrestal as Secretary of Defense and as an anti-communist who allegedly jumped from the sixteenth floor of [[walter-reed-national-military-medical-center|Walter Reed National Military Medical Center]] in Bethesda, Maryland, on 22 May 1949.[2] That account emphasises that he was the mentor of the future president [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], establishing a mentor-protégé pairing the article treats as parallel to [[roy-cohn|Roy Cohn]]'s mentorship of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

The same essay notes that Senator [[joseph-mccarthy|Joseph McCarthy]] died at the same military hospital eight years later, on 2 May 1957, and that Kennedy's body was examined there after his assassination, with some believing the corpse was altered and tampered with while in the facility.[2] Forrestal is placed in a sequence of figures who fought [[communism|communism]] and were destroyed: Forrestal was "labelled mentally unwell" and died mysteriously, McCarthy was demonized by the press and government and died mysteriously, [[george-s-patton|General George S. Patton]] was attacked by the press and died mysteriously, and Kennedy was assassinated and then subjected to posthumous character assassination.[2] The article argues that all four were likely victims of actual assassination as well as of character assassination.[2]

## Convening the Pearl Harbor Naval Court of Inquiry

In April 1944, Forrestal ordered that a Naval Court of Inquiry be convened to investigate the facts surrounding the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor and to assess any culpability borne by members of the Navy.[3] The court convened on July 24, 1944 and held daily sessions in Washington, D.C., San Francisco and Pearl Harbor, completing its work on October 19, 1944 after interviewing numerous witnesses.[3] Its report largely exonerated Admiral [[husband-kimmel|Husband Kimmel]], finding his decisions correct given the limited information available to him, and was withheld from the public until after the war because its findings implicitly revealed that American cryptographers had broken the Japanese codes.[3] Badlands writer [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats this as evidence that Washington knew the [[attack-on-pearl-harbor|attack on Pearl Harbor]] was coming.[3]

Carlson notes that in 1949 Forrestal, by then the former Secretary of Defense, was forcibly admitted to Bethesda Naval Hospital for psychiatric treatment and was later reported to have jumped from the sixteenth-floor window of his hospital room, killing himself — a death he suggests, as with that of [[george-s-patton|General Patton]], may have been an assassination by his own government.[3]
