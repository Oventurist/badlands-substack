# Silvergate Bank

**Silvergate Bank** was a US bank closely associated with the cryptocurrency industry.[1]

After [[facebook|Facebook]]'s stablecoin project [[diem|Diem]] (formerly Libra) was halted under pressure from the first Trump administration, the project's assets were sold to Silvergate Bank.[1] Silvergate was itself shut down in March 2023.[1]

The bank's role in absorbing the remains of Diem is cited in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" as part of the sequence by which a corporate rival to [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] was terminated during the period the article examines.[1]
