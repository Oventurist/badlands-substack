# Michael Rectenwald

Michael Rectenwald is an American writer and former [[new-york-university|New York University]] professor who sought the 2024 presidential nomination of the [[libertarian-party|Libertarian Party]] as the candidate endorsed by the [[mises-caucus|Mises Caucus]].[1]

## Academic background

Rectenwald left his New York University position after facing backlash for inviting the controversial far-right commentator Milo Yiannopoulos to speak to his class.[1]

## 2024 presidential bid

Endorsed by the Mises Caucus, which had taken control of the Libertarian Party in 2022, Rectenwald was the front-runner through most of the multi-round balloting at the party's May 2024 convention.[1] He was ultimately overtaken by [[chase-oliver|Chase Oliver]] in the final head-to-head round, in which Oliver won 60 percent against "none of the above" after Rectenwald was eliminated.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Rectenwald had undermined his own campaign, citing reporting that the candidate "struggled to articulate himself, then abandoned the event midway," and later told Washington Post reporter Meryl Kornfield that he had consumed an edible cannabis product before speaking.[1] The commentary mocked the admission as a sign the party was not serious, noting that "before speaking" meant before making his pitch to be President of the United States.[1]
