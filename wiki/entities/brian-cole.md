# Brian Cole

**Brian Cole** is the man identified as the suspect arrested by the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] on Thursday, December 4, 2025, in connection with the [[january-5-2021-pipe-bombs|January 5, 2021 pipe bomb]] case.[1] Two senior law enforcement officials familiar with the matter named Cole as the person taken into custody, and an FBI official confirmed the arrest occurred that morning, with a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] news conference scheduled later the same day to formally announce it.[1]

The arrest marked the first time investigators had publicly identified a suspect in the nearly five-year inquiry into who planted pipe bombs in Washington on the eve of the January 6, 2021 Capitol riot — a case whose lack of resolution had fueled widespread speculation.[1] The devices were discovered on the evening of January 5, 2021, near the headquarters of the Democratic and Republican national committees in Washington, D.C.; no one was injured, though the FBI had previously said both devices were capable of causing deadly harm.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded to the arrest by rejecting the premise of the case altogether, asking when observers would accept that there were no functioning "pipe bombs" anywhere on January 6.[1]
