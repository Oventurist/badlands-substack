# Austan Goolsbee

**Austan Goolsbee** is an American economist listed in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" among the alumni of [[yale-university|Yale]]'s [[skull-and-bones|Skull & Bones]] society.[1]

The article names him alongside [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] as evidence that Skull & Bones functions as "a direct pipeline to the CIA, U.S. State Department, and global economic hitman networks" rather than a mere social club.[1] His inclusion supports the piece's characterization of Yale as the covert recruitment center of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]], supplying personnel to intelligence, diplomacy and economic policymaking.[1]
