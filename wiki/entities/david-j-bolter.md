# David J. Bolter

David J. Bolter is an employee of the Milwaukee County Election Commission whose sworn affidavit concerning the 2020 presidential election has been cited in reporting on ballot handling in Milwaukee.[1]

## Affidavit

Reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief in May 2026 cited a sworn affidavit attributed to Bolter describing an announcement made on November 4, 2020 regarding the arrival of additional ballots at Milwaukee's [[milwaukee-central-count-facility|Central Count facility]].[1] The affidavit was referenced alongside the flash drive episode involving former [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] director [[claire-woodall-vogg|Claire Woodall-Vogg]] in the context of a reported [[fbi|FBI]] inquiry into the administration of the 2020 election in Wisconsin.[1] Such affidavits form part of the documentary record cited in [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
