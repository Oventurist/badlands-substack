# Eric Olsen

Eric Olsen is the attorney who argued against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s ballot eligibility before the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] in [[anderson-v-griswold|Anderson v. Griswold]] in December 2023.[1]

## Argument in Anderson v. Griswold

At the December 6, 2023 hearing, Olsen argued passionately that Trump's inclusion on the Republican primary ballot would dilute the votes cast for "qualified" candidates.[1] On the second question before the court — whether the events of January 6 constituted an insurrection at all — Olsen maintained that Judge Sarah Wallace's civil-court ruling that Trump intentionally incited insurrection should stand, a position Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized as asking the court to preserve a criminal finding delivered in a civil domain.[1] His opposing counsel, [[scott-gessler|Scott Gessler]], argued that January 6 was better understood as a riot; Ashe judged the arguments on both sides weak.[1] The case turned in part on whether Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]] applies to the president as an "Officer," and was expected to be appealed to the U.S. Supreme Court regardless of outcome.[1]
