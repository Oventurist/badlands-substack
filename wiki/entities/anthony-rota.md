# Anthony Rota

Anthony Rota is a Canadian politician who served as Speaker of the House of Commons and who, in September 2023, publicly apologized for having honored a veteran of a Nazi military unit during a session of Parliament attended by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.[1]

## The September 2023 recognition

Immediately after Zelensky's address to Canadian lawmakers on Friday, September 22, 2023, Rota drew attention to 98-year-old [[yaroslav-hunka|Yaroslav Hunka]] in the visitors' gallery and introduced him as a war hero who had fought for the [[first-ukrainian-division|First Ukrainian Division]].[1] The chamber responded with a standing ovation.[1]

On Sunday, September 24, Rota issued a statement of regret: "In my remarks following the address of the President of Ukraine, I recognized an individual in the gallery. I have subsequently become aware of more information which causes me to regret my decision to do so."[1] He emphasized that his fellow members of Parliament and the Ukrainian delegation had not been made aware of his plan to recognize Hunka, and that Hunka was a resident of his own district.[1] The apology was reported by [[associated-press|AP News]].[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands Media commentators treated Rota's apology as damage control rather than accountability, arguing that the incident revealed the alignment of Western political leadership with figures the public would otherwise consider disqualifying.[1] [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] wrote that "they really aren't hiding what and who they are anymore," while [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the ovation as an unforced propaganda defeat unfolding "in front of the world."[1]
