# America PAC

America PAC is a political action committee affiliated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

During the 2025 Wisconsin Supreme Court race, America PAC offered $100 to voters who signed a petition opposing "activist judges" or who referred others to sign it.[1] Musk and affiliated groups, including the PAC, spent at least $20 million supporting Republican-backed candidate [[brad-schimel|Brad Schimel]] in that election.[1] The broader spending effort formed the backdrop to the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]] panel's finding of probable cause that Musk likely violated the state's election bribery law by offering $1 million to voters.[1]

## 2024 origins and the Musk donation

America PAC first drew national attention in July 2024, when Bloomberg reported that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had donated to the then low-profile super political action committee working to elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], in what was characterized as a major gambit by the world's richest person to make his imprint on the U.S. political landscape.[2] The amount was not disclosed at the time, but sources described it as sizable, and the PAC was next required to disclose its donor list on July 15, 2024.[2] Reporting framed the donation as evidence of Musk's shift from self-described political independent to a figure regularly using his X platform to espouse right-leaning views.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the money went to a political action committee and, per reporting, would fund get-out-the-vote efforts — arguing that since maximizing turnout is the stated top metric of "our democracy," progressive outrage over the donation was confounding, and might presage leftist calls to suppress votes for Trump.[2]

## The $45 million monthly pledge

On July 16, 2024 the *Wall Street Journal*, cited by ZeroHedge, reported that a source familiar with the matter had revealed [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s commitment of roughly $45 million a month to the new pro-Trump super political-action committee.[3] The group was described as deploying large sums of money and other resources in critical swing states specifically to counter Democratic "get out the vote" campaigns.[3] America PAC's reported focus was registering voters and persuading constituents to vote early and to request mail-in ballots across the swing states.[3]

Besides Musk, the PAC was reported to be supported by Palantir Technologies co-founder [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss, and former US ambassador to Canada Kelly Craft together with her husband Joe, chief executive of the coal producer Alliance Resource Partners.[3]

Badlands contributor Burning Bright acknowledged that members of the Truth Community approached major public figures involved in America PAC with trepidation, calling that caution healthy, but argued that the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on Trump's life]] had granted social permission for such figures to align openly with the campaign — a development he read as "either capitulation or coordination," both of which favored awakening.[3]

## Democratic scrutiny (May 2025)

In May 2025 House Democrats named America PAC — described as Elon Musk-affiliated — among the Republican and Trump-aligned fundraising vehicles for which they asked the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] to produce suspicious activity reports, alongside [[winred|WinRed]].[4] The request from [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]], [[joe-morelle|Joe Morelle]], and [[gerald-connolly|Gerald Connolly]] was framed as a counter to Republican pressure on [[actblue|ActBlue]] over alleged illegal foreign donations.[4]
