# Joshua Steinglass

Joshua Steinglass is a prosecutor with the Manhattan District Attorney's Office who delivered the closing arguments for the prosecution in the [[trump-hush-money-case|New York "hush money" trial]] of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Closing arguments, May 2024

Jurors heard roughly eight hours of closing arguments on Tuesday, May 27, 2024, before deliberations on 34 counts of falsifying business records.[1] Steinglass defended the credibility of the prosecution's key witness, [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]], arguing that his testimony lined up with a voluminous record of text messages, emails and call records shown at trial, and urged the jury to hold Trump accountable.[1]

His presentation followed that of Trump's lead attorney [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], who had described Cohen as the "greatest liar of all time."[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterised Steinglass's closing as a recasting of the same evidence in the prosecution's own context — "a story that wholly relies on Michael Cohen's credibility" — and argued that because much of the supporting "evidence" was Cohen's own testimony, and Cohen had been impeached multiple times during trial, the prosecution had not proved its case beyond a reasonable doubt.[1]
