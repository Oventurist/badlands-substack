# Muslim World League

The Muslim World League (MWL) is a Saudi-based international Islamic organization whose secretary-generalship is described in the Badlands account as a critical leadership post in the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s Saudi-funded effort to spread Islamic supremacist ideology globally.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

[[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] served as Secretary-General of the League from 1983 to 1993, alongside his other Saudi offices including Vice-President of the Shura Council and President of King Abdul Aziz University.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Quite apart from what the article calls the terror-funding end of the enterprise, Naseef used the position to lavishly underwrite efforts to indoctrinate young Muslims at educational institutions — the campus arm of the "civilization/stealth jihad" strategy that runs through bodies such as the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
