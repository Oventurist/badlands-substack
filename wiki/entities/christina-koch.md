# Christina Koch

Christina Koch is a [[nasa|NASA]] astronaut serving as a mission specialist on [[artemis-ii|Artemis II]], the first crewed lunar flyby since the Apollo program.[1] She repaired the Orion spacecraft's redesigned space toilet after its urine function went offline on day one of the mission, working with guidance from Mission Control.[1] The toilet reportedly failed again over the Easter 2026 weekend, drawing coverage that Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued overshadowed the flyby itself.[1]
