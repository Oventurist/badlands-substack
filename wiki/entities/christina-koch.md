# Christina Koch

Christina Koch is a [[nasa|NASA]] astronaut serving as a mission specialist aboard [[artemis-ii|Artemis II]], the first crewed mission to travel around the Moon since the Apollo program. She appears in the April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks" in its coverage of the mission's most publicised technical problem.

Quoting a Space.com feature, the Brief reports that the Orion spacecraft's redesigned space toilet — intended for long-duration spaceflight — had been part of the Artemis II conversation "since day one of the mission, when its urine function went offline and was fixed by mission specialist Christina Koch, with guidance from Mission Control." Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] notes that the toilet allegedly broke again over the weekend and uses the episode to question why a hardware malfunction was overshadowing what NASA presented as a historic lunar flyby, and why the mission involves no landing.
