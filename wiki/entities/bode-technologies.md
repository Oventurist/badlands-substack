# BODE Technologies

BODE Technologies was the vendor holding the [[customs-and-border-protection|US Customs and Border Protection]] contract for familial DNA testing at the US–Mexico border.[1]

## End of the CBP familial DNA contract

According to a CBP memo obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] and sent to frontline border agents over the weekend of May 20–21, 2023, "The U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) familial DNA contract with BODE Technologies will end on May 31, 2023 and all familial DNA testing will conclude on that date."[1] The testing program had been implemented during the Trump era following a court order related to the separation of migrant children from their families and evidence that drug cartels were using children to create fake family units to move illegal immigrants across the border.[1] Reporting characterized the program as a key deterrent to fraudulent entry and to [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]].[1]
