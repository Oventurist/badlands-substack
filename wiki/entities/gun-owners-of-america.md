# Gun Owners of America

Gun Owners of America (GOA) is a United States gun-rights advocacy organization that, in the mid-2020s, became a significant source of disclosures about federal domestic-extremism categorization through litigation and public-records work directed at the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]

## FBI Documents and FOIA Litigation

In September 2022 a GOA lawyer provided documents to AmmoLand News showing that the FBI was characterizing so-called "militia violent extremists" (MVEs) by reference to symbols including the Betsy Ross flag and an image of a minuteman.[1] The leak prompted GOA to file a Freedom of Information Act request seeking the underlying records.[1]

The FBI initially refused to turn over any documents to the group.[1] GOA responded by suing the Bureau to compel production; rather than await a court order, the FBI relented and released the material.[1]

## Content of the Disclosures

GOA subsequently furnished AmmoLand News with an exclusive look at the documents, which AmmoLand said it would cover in a series of articles.[1] The records indicated that the Bureau's targeting was not confined to the Three Percenters and Oath Keepers but extended to additional groups, with some threat assessments appearing credible and others appearing to be a reach.[1] The first tranche addressed "racially or ethnically motivated violent extremists" (RMVEs) and Islamist extremists, and specifically the prospect of those two ideological currents cooperating toward a shared goal of collapsing Western democracies.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands Media writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the disclosure as evidence that federal agencies were pre-emptively delegitimizing an Islamo-Christian alliance he regarded as both inevitable and desirable, tying the material to his [[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]] essays.[1]
