# World Trade Organization

The World Trade Organization is one of the institutions [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies as constituting the unipolar world order that followed the collapse of the Soviet system in the early 1990s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) In his sequence, that collapse ushered in a unipolar order built around the European Union and [[nafta|NAFTA]], followed soon thereafter by the WTO and the 1999 destruction of [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

The effect attributed to this architecture is that the trans-Atlantic was converted into a cage of "post-sovereign nations" that no longer had actual control of their own powers of credit generation, while under [[nato|NATO]] even national militaries were subject to technocratic control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret describes the arrangement as perfect for a governing elite "scientifically managing" from above while the little people bickered over their diminishing employment and standards of living from below.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
