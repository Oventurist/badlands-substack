# Michael Tyler

Michael Tyler is a Democratic communications operative who served as communications director for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 2024 re-election campaign.[1]

## Role in the 2024 campaign

Tyler became the public face of the Biden campaign's late-May 2024 decision to abandon six weeks of deliberate distance from the Manhattan courthouse at 100 Centre Street, where [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[trump-hush-money-case|hush money trial]] was being held.[1] On Tuesday, May 28, 2024, the campaign staged its own event outside the courthouse, mirroring the surrogate operations Trump's allies had been running in front of the assembled television cameras throughout the trial.[1]

Asked by a reporter why the president's re-election campaign had chosen to hold an event at his opponent's criminal trial, Tyler gave a blunt answer: "Because you all are here."[1] He elaborated that the press had been "incessantly covering this, day in and day out," and said the campaign intended to use the attention to remind voters of "the stakes of this election."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the courthouse pivot as a "guerrilla-style" escalation driven by what he described as establishment "Dem Panic," arguing that the campaign was doubling down on support for a prosecution that had in his view boosted Trump's polling among an increasingly bipartisan audience.[1] He further argued that the framing of mainstream coverage — treating the Biden operation as relevant chiefly in comparison to Trump — was itself an admission of the incumbent's diminished standing.[1]
