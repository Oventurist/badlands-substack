# CIA

The Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) is the United States government's foreign intelligence service. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," the CIA is framed as the operational arm of a "shadow government" that has deliberately created, funded, and manipulated terrorist organizations for decades, while simultaneously running large-scale operations to steal U.S. taxpayer money through military interventions.

According to the article, the CIA created and funded the Mujahideen in Afghanistan as a "private army" to protect its heroin trade. It claims the agency has been "arming and training the very terrorists that we have been at war with since the 1980s," and that U.S. troops have been "purposely sacrificed on the battlefield" to sustain a never-ending war on terror designed to generate profit for hidden networks. The source specifically claims that [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhaj]] was a CIA asset who was picked up after 9/11, interrogated in CIA prisons, and then sent back to Libya as part of a deal with [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]].

The article asserts that the CIA controls the U.S. media, which it uses to paint targets such as Gaddafi as "the worst enemies in the world" while protecting allies and assets. It claims that the CIA relocated Al-Qaeda training camps from Afghanistan to Benghazi after 9/11 so it could "keep feeding a constant supply of new fighters to kill our soldiers." The source also claims that the CIA had strong ties to [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed]], who in turn had strong ties to the Bush family.

In the article's framework, the CIA is not a neutral intelligence service but a criminal organization that uses war and terrorism as cover for theft, with the ultimate goal of maintaining control over global financial flows.

## Partnership with labor unions

The Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]] (October 2025) by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes the CIA's secret partnership with the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]], "earning it the nickname 'AFL-CIA.'" The article states that the relationship traces back to the WWII-era [[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]], the CIA's precursor, which collaborated with American unions to counter communist influences in Europe. Recognizing unions' credibility among workers, the CIA forged the partnership because "workers in other countries would be more likely to trust fellow union members." By 1949 the CIA funneled millions through the AFL's Free Trade Union Committee to split European labor federations along Cold War lines, funding non-communist rivals like [[force-ouvriere|Force Ouvrière]]. The article further describes CIA funding of the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development (AIFLD)]] in Latin America, attributing to it strikes against [[cheddi-jagan|Cheddi Jagan]] in Guyana and the destabilization of [[salvador-allende|Salvador Allende]]'s Chile, and claims that these labor operations were institutionalized after 1997 through the AFL-CIO's [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]] funded by the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]].

## Domestic operations in "A Journey of Ever-changing Truths"

The April 2025 essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]] by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] extends these claims to the domestic sphere. It states that the CIA, together with the [[fbi|FBI]], was "running record labels" in the late 1980s and engineered the rise of [[gangster-rap|gangster rap]] to influence young black men toward criminality at a time when falling crime rates threatened the profitability of [[prison-privatization|newly privatized prisons]].

The article further alleges that the agency "smuggled drugs in from South and Central America to sell on the South-Central streets of Los Angeles," where [[rick-ross|Rick Ross]] grew wealthy, having obtained those drugs by trading guns in Central America. Carlson summarizes the resulting cycle — create racial division, sell guns and drugs to promote foreign wars, promote a hood mentality in the inner cities, and fill prison cells while being compensated for it — with the remark that "no one can accuse the CIA of not being good at their job."

On organized crime, the essay places the CIA above the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] in the hierarchy, describing [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]] as "their guy" who ran agency drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War, and reading [[the-godfather|*The Godfather*]] as a narrative operation that concealed both layers behind the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]]. In the same essay's global hierarchy, the CIA and [[mossad|Mossad]] are themselves subordinate to British [[mi6|MI6]] and ultimately to the [[city-of-london|City of London]].
