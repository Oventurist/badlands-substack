# CIA

The Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) is the United States government's foreign intelligence service. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," the CIA is framed as the operational arm of a "shadow government" that has deliberately created, funded, and manipulated terrorist organizations for decades, while simultaneously running large-scale operations to steal U.S. taxpayer money through military interventions.

According to the article, the CIA created and funded the Mujahideen in Afghanistan as a "private army" to protect its heroin trade. It claims the agency has been "arming and training the very terrorists that we have been at war with since the 1980s," and that U.S. troops have been "purposely sacrificed on the battlefield" to sustain a never-ending war on terror designed to generate profit for hidden networks. The source specifically claims that [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhaj]] was a CIA asset who was picked up after 9/11, interrogated in CIA prisons, and then sent back to Libya as part of a deal with [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]].

The article asserts that the CIA controls the U.S. media, which it uses to paint targets such as Gaddafi as "the worst enemies in the world" while protecting allies and assets. It claims that the CIA relocated Al-Qaeda training camps from Afghanistan to Benghazi after 9/11 so it could "keep feeding a constant supply of new fighters to kill our soldiers." The source also claims that the CIA had strong ties to [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed]], who in turn had strong ties to the Bush family.

In the article's framework, the CIA is not a neutral intelligence service but a criminal organization that uses war and terrorism as cover for theft, with the ultimate goal of maintaining control over global financial flows.
