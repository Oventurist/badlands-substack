# Eastern Economic Forum

The **Eastern Economic Forum** is an annual economic conference held in Vladivostok in Russia's far east, used by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as a platform for major economic and geopolitical announcements.[1]

## September 2024 forum

At the forum in early September 2024, Putin addressed both the American presidential race and the largest US–Russia prisoner exchange since the Cold War.[1] Asked how he viewed the election, he said the outcome was the choice of the American people, then added that because [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had recommended his supporters back [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], "we will do the same, we will support her."[1] Putin and the moderator were both smiling as he made the remark, which drew applause from the audience; expanding on his view of Harris, he said, "She laughs so expressively and infectiously that it means that everything is fine with her."[1] He concluded, "Ultimately, the choice is up to the American people, and we will respect that choice."[1]

Speaking at the same forum, Putin thanked Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] for his role in arranging the [[2024-us-russia-prisoner-swap|2024 US–Russia prisoner swap]], and thanked Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] for providing the venue for the exchange.[1]
