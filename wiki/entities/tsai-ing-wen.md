# Tsai Ing-wen

Tsai Ing-wen served as President of Taiwan, the self-governing island whose status is the central flashpoint in U.S.–China relations.[1]

## April 2023 McCarthy meeting

On April 5, 2023, Tsai met U.S. House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] in California.[1] China responded with large-scale air and sea drills in the [[taiwan-strait|Taiwan Strait]], concluding them the following week and describing the exercises — which simulated an encirclement of Taiwan — as a "serious warning" to pro-independence politicians on the island and to their foreign supporters.[1]

The meeting and China's response formed the immediate backdrop to the [[uss-milius|USS Milius]] transit of the strait on April 16, 2023, the first known U.S. warship passage since the drills.[1]

## Broader context

China had already escalated military pressure on Taiwan in the preceding years, flying fighter jets and sailing naval vessels toward the island on a near-daily basis.[1] After former House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] visited Taiwan in August 2022, Chinese vessels increasingly crossed the midline of the Taiwan Strait, an unofficial boundary that had been observed for decades.[1] That escalating pressure drew greater global attention to Taiwan's position.[1]
