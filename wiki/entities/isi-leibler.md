# Isi Leibler

**Isi Leibler** was the former vice president and board of trustees chairman of the [[world-jewish-congress|World Jewish Congress]], and a critic of the direction taken by the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] under [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]].[1]

Leibler faulted Greenblatt for "tilting the ADL policy away from its primary mandate of combating anti-Semitism and steering it toward partisan social action issues."[1] Badlands Media cited his criticism, alongside that of other Jewish figures, to argue that objections to the ADL's conduct are not inherently anti-Semitic despite the organisation's tendency to characterise them that way.[1]
