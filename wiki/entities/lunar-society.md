# Lunar Society

The British Lunar Society is described in the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin" as an institution created by [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] while acting as colonial representative to Britain in 1757, as part of his belief that the British Empire could be reformed from within rather than merely overthrown.[1]

The group featured such scientists as Matthew Boulton, Josiah Wedgewood and Erasmus Darwin, and the essay credits it with uniquely driving the advancement of internal improvements — roads, canals, bridges, steam power and sewage — alongside industrial growth and rising living standards in Britain.[1] The society is presented as one arm of Franklin's wider effort to kindle the republican spirit of Thomas More, Erasmus and Shakespeare and transform the Empire from a Satanic [[hellfire-club|Hell-Fire Club]] operation into something in harmony with humanity's well-being.[1]
