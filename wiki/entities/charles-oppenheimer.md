# Charles Oppenheimer

Charles Oppenheimer is an American nuclear energy advocate and the grandson of physicist J. Robert Oppenheimer. In 2023 he became publicly associated with arguments for expanding nuclear power's role in a net zero-carbon economy, including through a Time magazine feature that circulated widely and prompted [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] to advocate for greater nuclear use on X.[1]

## Argument on nuclear safety and perception

Oppenheimer has argued that nuclear energy's unpopularity stems less from its actual performance than from guilt by association: "It's also important to underscore that nuclear energy became unpopular in part due to its association with nuclear weapons and fears about its safety. But the actual safety record shows it is one of the safest sources of energy, and it is becoming more popular to be an environmentalist and pro-nuclear."[1] His remarks were cited alongside [[pew-research-center|Pew Research Center]] polling published in August 2023 showing that about 57% of Americans favored more nuclear power plants, a 14-point increase since 2020, though still behind solar (82%) and wind (75%).[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the same polling differently, treating the swing toward nuclear as evidence that the American public was abandoning a green agenda "powered by batteries and wind farms" and increasingly viewing climate policy as a vehicle for a worldwide power grab.[1]
