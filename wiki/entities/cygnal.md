# Cygnal

Cygnal is a polling firm whose survey work is cited in Badlands Media commentary on American public opinion, particularly opinion within American Muslim communities following the October 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attacks on [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## The 2023 poll on American Muslim opinion

In an article published on October 25, 2023, Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited "a recent Cygnal poll" reporting that 58% of Muslim Americans believed Hamas was justified in attacking Israel.[1] Carlson paired that figure with the observation that 58% of American Muslims were born in another country, and argued that a majority-level endorsement of an attack involving the killing, rape and kidnapping of women, children and the elderly could not reasonably be described as a "moderate" position.[1] He wrote that a 5% figure would have been worrying and that 58% was "beyond concerning," and said he would be curious what share of respondents would view identical acts as justified if committed inside America against American Jews or American Christians.[1]

## Use in the immigration argument

Carlson used the Cygnal result as evidence against the claim, which he attributed to the [[obama-administration|Obama administration]], that only "moderate" Muslims were being admitted to the United States as refugees and asylum seekers.[1] In his framing, the poll demonstrated that decades of refugee and asylum policy had settled in the United States a population segment whose sympathies lay with organizations Washington designates as terrorist, and that this outcome was intended rather than accidental.[1] He returned to the poll at the close of the article, writing that if it accurately showed that a majority of American Muslims were not in fact moderate, "America has a real problem on its hands."[1]
