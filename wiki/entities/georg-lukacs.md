# Georg Lukacs

Georg Lukacs was Cultural Minister of the short-lived Bolshevik Hungarian government of 1919 and an associate of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]].[1] He is identified as one of the figures who led post-modernist reforms in Bolshevik Hungary and who subsequently became a leading light of the [[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]].[1]

Together with [[willi-munzenberg|Willi Munzenberg]], Lukacs founded the Institute for Social Research in Frankfurt, Germany in 1923 — the body that became known as the Frankfurt School.[1] Both men were radical Bolsheviks.[1] Matthew Ehret writes that many of the Hungarian reformers of this circle were absorbed by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] in the wake of the Second World War, and that the group played a much greater role in history than is generally realized, helping to set the stage for the European Union.[1]
