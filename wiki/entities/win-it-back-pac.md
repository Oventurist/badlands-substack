# Win It Back PAC

Win It Back is a well-funded political action committee formed by anti-Trump conservatives and closely tied to the fiscally conservative group [[club-for-growth|Club for Growth]].[1] Its purpose during the 2024 Republican presidential primary was to reduce support for former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] among Republican voters through paid advertising in early-voting states.[1]

## Advertising campaign and the 2023 donor memo

The committee spent more than $4 million attempting to lower Trump's support in Iowa and nearly $2 million more seeking to damage him in South Carolina.[1] In a memo dated September 28, 2023 and obtained by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]], the head of the PAC, [[david-mcintosh|David McIntosh]], candidly told donors that after extensive testing of more than forty anti-Trump television advertisements, "all attempts to undermine his conservative credentials on specific issues were ineffective."[1] The document was read as offering little reassurance to Trump's remaining rivals that any message existed capable of deflating his support.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], treated the memo as a self-indictment of the Republican establishment, noting that the $6 million acknowledged in the reporting was likely an understatement of the true cost.[1] She argued that party resources spent persuading Trump supporters to abandon him would have been better directed at election integrity work for 2024, and that the effort chiefly destroyed what remained of the GOP's national brand equity: "They've taken those donor dollars, formed them into an effigy of Trump, and set them on fire."[1] The episode was folded into the broader [[america-first-movement|America First]] argument that the establishment controls institutional power while the movement holds the loyalty of the voters.[1]
