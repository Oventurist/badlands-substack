# Mike Rounds

**Mike Rounds** is a Republican United States senator from South Dakota and a member of the [[senate-armed-services-committee|Senate Armed Services Committee]].[1]

## Confirmation of Ukrainian strikes inside Russia (June 2024)

In early June 2024 Rounds publicly confirmed that Ukraine had used U.S.-supplied weapons to strike targets inside [[russia|Russia]], though he did not say how he had been briefed.[1] His confirmation, reported by the [[associated-press|Associated Press]] alongside an account from an unnamed Western official, was among the first on-the-record acknowledgements that Ukrainian forces had acted on recently approved guidance from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] permitting American arms to be used against Russian territory for the limited purpose of defending Kharkiv.[1]

The guidance Rounds described allowed U.S.-supplied weapons to be used against Russian forces attacking or preparing to attack, while leaving in place the policy barring Ukraine from using [[atacms|ATACMS]] and other long-range munitions for offensive strikes inside Russia.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the disclosure as a marker of significant "red line" escalation between the West and Moscow.[1]
