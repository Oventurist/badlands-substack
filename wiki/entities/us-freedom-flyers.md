# US Freedom Flyers

**U.S. Freedom Flyers** is an American advocacy organisation formed by aviation-industry workers to oppose COVID-19 vaccine mandates in the airline sector.[1] Its president is [[josh-yoder|Josh Yoder]].[1]

## Unvaccinated private-jet crews claim

In a July 2022 interview with Maria Zeee of Australia's Red Voice Media, Alan Dana, a former pilot for Australia's Jetstar, said that Yoder had reported receiving calls "from wealthy businessmen and companies to fly their executives around on business jets with unvaccinated crew."[1] The claim, recirculated in January 2023, was presented as evidence that wealthy executives privately shared concerns about the potential cardiovascular effects of COVID-19 vaccination on flight crews even as those concerns were publicly dismissed.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] urged caution, noting that the claim about elite demand for unvaccinated pilots was unverified, while pointing separately to documented developments such as pilots being trained to handle sudden in-flight medical incapacitation and a pilot who died mid-flight after departing a Chicago airport in 2022.[1] He advised readers to examine each thread and datapoint carefully, arguing that legacy media fact checks were themselves being used to discredit valid reporting.[1]
