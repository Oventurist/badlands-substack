# Emily A. Johnson

Emily A. Johnson is a federal prosecutor with the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]] who argued against pretrial release for music mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] at his September 2024 bail hearing.[1]

## Role in the Combs prosecution

In asking the magistrate to deny Combs's bail application, Johnson characterized the defendant as a "serial abuser and a serial obstructer," arguing that he represented a threat to the community and that his substantial wealth would make it easy for him to flee undetected.[1] She noted that law enforcement had recovered what they suspected to be narcotics — in the form of a pink powder — in Combs's Manhattan hotel room after his arrest.[1] Magistrate Judge [[robyn-tarnofsky|Robyn F. Tarnofsky]] accepted the government's arguments and ordered Combs detained pending trial.[1]
