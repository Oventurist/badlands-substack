# Dmitry Peskov

Dmitry Peskov is the Kremlin spokesman for the government of [[russia|Russia]], serving as the principal public voice for the Russian presidency.

## Response to the Krasnodarskaya strike

In the July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]], Peskov is reported to have condemned a Ukrainian drone attack on the Krasnodarskaya compressor station — a facility serving the [[blue-stream-pipeline|Blue Stream]] pipeline operated by [[gazprom|Gazprom]] — as a threat to critical international energy infrastructure. He said Russia expected the countries affected by the pipeline, including [[turkey|Türkiye]], to respond to the attack.

His statement is significant in the Badlands reading of the episode because it explicitly internationalises the incident, framing damage to Russian export infrastructure as an injury to Türkiye and to European consumers served by the parallel [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] route rather than to Russia alone. The remarks came as [[ukraine|Ukraine]] expanded long-range attacks on Russian energy infrastructure and Russia continued missile and drone strikes on Ukrainian cities and energy facilities.
