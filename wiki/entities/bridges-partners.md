# Bridges Partners LLC

Bridges Partners LLC is a Delaware-registered consultancy hired to manage a U.S. social-media influencer network on behalf of the government of [[israel|Israel]] under a campaign code-named the [[esther-project|Esther Project]].[1]

## Ownership and formation

The firm is owned by Israeli consultants Uri Steinberg and Yair Levi and was formed in June 2025 in Delaware.[1] Shortly after its formation it received nearly $200,000 to recruit and coordinate U.S.-based social media influencers.[1]

## FARA disclosure

Bridges Partners' role became public through records filed with the U.S. Department of Justice under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]], reported by [[jerusalem-post|The Jerusalem Post]] on 1 October 2025.[1] In its disclosure the firm said its work was intended to "assist with promoting cultural interchange between the United States and Israel," and specified that the engagement runs through a German division of the global PR firm Havas.[1] The filings depicted a two-track Israeli influence operation combining big-budget political advertising with grassroots-style influencer campaigns.[1]
