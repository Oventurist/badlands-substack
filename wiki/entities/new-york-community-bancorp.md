# New York Community Bancorp

New York Community Bancorp (NYCB) is an American regional banking company that became the focus of market anxiety in early 2024 over the health of mid-sized U.S. lenders.[1]

## March 2024 capital raise

On Wednesday, March 6, 2024, NYCB announced that it had raised $1 billion from a group of investors that included former U.S. Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]]'s [[liberty-strategic-capital|Liberty Strategic Capital]], and that it had named former Comptroller of the Currency [[joseph-otting|Joseph Otting]] as its new chief executive officer.[1] Other participants in the equity investment included Hudson Bay Capital, Reverence Capital Partners, Citadel Global Equities, additional institutional investors, and certain members of the bank's own management.[1] Mnuchin joined the bank's expanded board, stating that "in evaluating this investment, we were mindful of the bank's credit risk profile."[1]

The bank's shares experienced an extremely volatile session, falling 45 percent before the announcement, rebounding roughly 30 percent afterward, and ultimately closing 7.4 percent higher.[1]

## Analyst reaction

KBW analyst Chris McGratty wrote to clients that the capital raise was painful for existing shareholders but should reduce fear of systemic risk, calling it "a good solution for the broader system" that showed the bank's ability to attract private capital and noting that the management and board overhaul provided credibility with investors.[1] McGratty expected NYCB might still sell assets and transfer credit risk to push its capital ratio above the then-current 10 percent target.[1] Kevin Heal, a banking analyst at Argus Research, said the deal should allay depositor fears and observed that, unlike [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] — whose uninsured deposits were "blown off the door in billions" — NYCB did not carry the same level of uninsured deposits.[1]

## Commentary in the Badlands corpus

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] described the NYCB headline as containing "a metric ton of signal," reading the involvement of two former [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration financial officials as evidence that consequential moves in the ongoing information conflict were being made in the U.S. financial sector.[1] He connected the episode to the 2023 collapses of Silicon Valley Bank and [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]] — a "mini Crisis Cascade" he attributed in part to the actions of Trump ally [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] — and asked whether the pattern represented pieces being deliberately put in place.[1]
