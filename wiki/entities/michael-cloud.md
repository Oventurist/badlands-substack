# Michael Cloud

Michael Cloud is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas.[1]

During the June 3, 2024 hearing of the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]], Cloud engaged in a heated exchange with former [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|NIAID]] director [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] over pandemic-era measures, at one point pressing Fauci into admitting that the COVID-19 vaccine does not stop transmission.[1]

## Badlands interview on election integrity (2025)

In May 2025 Badlands host Jon Herold interviewed Cloud on his show, opening with a question about what Herold called America's fraudulent election system; the interview aired the same day an appellate court upheld the [[new-york-even-year-election-law|New York Even Year Election Law]].[2]
