# Debbie Dingell

Debbie Dingell is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Michigan, long associated with trade and manufacturing policy in an auto-industry district.[1]

## Cooperation with Trump on trade

During a Saturday appearance on Fox News Live in early January 2025, Dingell said Democrats would not necessarily automatically oppose policies supported by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and that she was in fact "looking forward" to working with him on trade in particular.[1] Newsweek, which reported the remarks, said it had reached out to Dingell's and Trump's communications teams for comment.[1] The outlet framed the significance in terms of a broader shift: Democrats had been sharply critical of Trump during his first term and throughout the campaign, but since his November victory some Democratic lawmakers had begun identifying areas of possible common ground with the president-elect.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read Dingell's posture as one pole of a divided opposition — federal Democrats seeking accommodation "because nothing can stop what is coming," against state-level figures such as Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] and Senator [[chris-murphy|Chris Murphy]] still campaigning on "danger to democracy."[1]
