# Olivér Várhelyi

Olivér Várhelyi is a European Union official identified in Badlands coverage as one of the EU figures involved in an August 2024 controversy over remarks made to Georgian Prime Minister [[irakli-kobakhidze|Irakli Kobakhidze]].[1]

## Georgia threat controversy

According to the account relayed in the Badlands News Brief, EU officials threatened Kobakhidze "with the same fate of [Slovakian Prime Minister] [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]]," a warning delivered roughly a week after Fico was shot five times in the chest.[1] Várhelyi subsequently came forward to assert that the comments had been "taken out of context."[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] rejected that framing, asking in what universe telling a European leader that he had better be careful or he would face the same fate as a leader shot in the chest a week earlier could be anything other than an assassination threat, and denouncing Várhelyi in unusually blunt personal terms.[1] The episode was presented as evidence of EU pressure on [[georgia-country|Georgia]] as the country drifted out of the Western orbit.[1]
