# TurkStream Pipeline

TurkStream is a natural gas pipeline that carries Russian gas through [[turkey|Türkiye]] and onward to several European countries.[1] It is separate from the [[blue-stream-pipeline|Blue Stream]] pipeline, which delivers Russian gas to Türkiye itself across the Black Sea.[1]

## Strategic significance

Because TurkStream links [[russia|Russian]] supply to European consumers via Turkish territory, it sits at the intersection of the Ukraine war's energy dimension and Turkey's regional position.[1] Following the July 2026 Ukrainian drone strike on the Krasnodarskaya compressor station serving Blue Stream, Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] framed attacks on such infrastructure as threats to critical international energy systems and said Russia expected affected countries to respond.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified the TurkStream route across the Black Sea — along with the island of [[cyprus|Cyprus]] and Turkey's Aegean west coast — as places to watch for a possible [[nato|NATO]]/[[israel|Israeli]] operation amid escalating rhetoric toward Turkey.[1]
