# National Institute for Health and Care Excellence

The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) is the United Kingdom body that evaluates clinical evidence and issues guidance on the safety and effectiveness of medical treatments for the [[nhs|National Health Service]].[1]

## Evidence review on puberty blockers

The NHS commissioned NICE to review the published evidence on gonadotrophin releasing hormone analogues (GnRHa), the drugs commonly known as [[puberty-blockers|puberty blockers]], which prevent the body from producing sex hormones.[1] NICE completed that review in 2020.[1]

In a policy document published on March 12, 2024, [[nhs-england|NHS England]] stated that it had "carefully considered the evidence review conducted by NICE (2020)" together with any further published evidence available since, and concluded that there was not enough evidence to support the safety or clinical effectiveness of puberty suppressing hormones to make the treatment routinely available.[1] The determination ended routine prescription of puberty blockers to children in the UK and drew objections from transgender advocacy organizations.[1]
