# Liu Pengyu

Liu Pengyu is the spokesperson for China's Embassy in Washington, DC.[1]

## Comment on joint air patrols

Liu told [[newsweek|Newsweek]] that the expanding [[russia-china-joint-air-patrols|joint air patrols]] flown by the Russian and Chinese militaries were part of annual cooperation intended to demonstrate the two sides' determination and capability to jointly address regional security challenges.[1] His comments accompanied reporting on bomber flights — including nuclear-capable aircraft — that have ranged beyond East Asia into the broader Pacific and near Alaska since 2019.[1]
