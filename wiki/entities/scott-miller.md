# Scott Miller

**Scott Miller** is the United States ambassador to Switzerland under the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and a co-chair emeritus of the [[gill-foundation|Gill Foundation]]. He is married to the foundation's chair, [[tim-gill|Tim Gill]].[1]

Miller was named by Biden at the June 2023 [[white-house|White House]] Pride Month celebration, which the president said the couple had made possible; Biden said Miller was "doing a terrific job as my Ambassador to Switzerland."[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary drew attention to the Swiss posting as a placement in what it called "the epicenter of globalism that houses Davos and Geneva," and criticized the event for a display it said violated the [[us-flag-code|United States Flag Code]].[1]
