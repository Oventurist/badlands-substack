# International Center for Journalists

The **International Center for Journalists (ICFJ)** is a media-development nonprofit listed by Badlands Media among organizations it describes as "policing journalism."[1]

It is named together with the [[pulitzer-center-on-crisis-reporting|Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting]], the [[national-press-foundation|National Press Foundation]], the [[solutions-journalism-network|Solutions Journalism Network]] and the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]]; the article asserts that all are connected to individuals belonging to one or multiple of the clandestine think tanks it covers, and treats the cluster as an arm of the wider network of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]].[1]
