# Raytheon

Raytheon is a major American defense contractor, frequently cited alongside [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] in Badlands Media commentary as a principal beneficiary of the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]].[1]

In an August 2023 Badlands News Brief item on plans by the Biden administration and European allies to establish long-term military assistance for Ukraine, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] closed his commentary with the remark, "At least Raytheon and Lockheed will be happy!" — framing multi-year Western aid commitments as principally a windfall for weapons manufacturers.[1] DeLarme argued that devotion to the MIC is "the truest hallmark of a deep-state stooge" and transcends the partisan divide between establishment Democrats and Republicans.[1]
