# AND Magazine

AND Magazine is an independent Substack publication covering national security, intelligence and foreign policy, and is cited by Badlands Media as a source in its news briefs.[1]

## Ukraine funding coverage

In April 2023, AND Magazine published a piece titled "Before We Send Any More Money To Ukraine - Can We Find Out Where The Rest Of Our Cash Went?" summarizing [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]]'s reporting that Ukrainians had embezzled at least $400 million of U.S.-provided funds, and that [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]], confronted with the evidence by CIA Director [[william-burns|Burns]], responded by dismissing a handful of officials while taking no serious action.[1]

The piece placed the allegations in context, noting that Ukraine has long been one of the world's most corrupt nations, citing [[transparency-international|Transparency International]] findings that 38 to 42 percent of Ukrainian households reported paying bribes for basic public services and that only Russia ranked as more corrupt in Europe.[1] It also cited a [[security-service-of-ukraine|Security Service of Ukraine]] disclosure of an embezzlement scheme in which funds for military food supply were routed to firms lacking any production base or technological equipment.[1]

## Badlands assessment

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted the author's characterization of [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as a "thug" as an example of an author's bias that readers should weigh, while still engaging the underlying logic and net effects of the reporting.[1] He argued that prominent left-leaning writers advancing Ukraine corruption headlines were pushing the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] back toward objective analysis of the funding sent to what he called the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s latest forever-war quagmire.[1]
