# Michael Simon

Michael Simon is a United States district judge in Portland, Oregon.[1]

## Portland ICE facility restraining order

Simon issued a temporary restraining order limiting [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]]'s use of tear gas and projectile munitions against protesters at a Portland ICE facility.[1] The order permits those tactics only in situations involving an imminent physical threat, and followed a period of recurring protests at the facility.[1]
