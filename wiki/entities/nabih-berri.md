# Nabih Berri

Nabih Berri is the Speaker of the Parliament of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and a central Lebanese interlocutor in the negotiations that led to the November 2024 [[2024-lebanon-ceasefire|Lebanon ceasefire]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 ceasefire negotiations

In the week before the ceasefire was approved, Newsweek quoted commentary by Berri given to the Lebanese newspaper *Ad-Diyar* explaining the growing optimism on the Lebanese side.[1] According to Berri, that optimism stemmed from U.S. envoy [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]]'s confirmation, in his most recent communication, that he had "received the green light from U.S. President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to push the talks forward and reach a ceasefire."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated Berri's account as corroboration — alongside Israeli media reporting — that Hochstein was acting at Trump's direction rather than solely on behalf of the outgoing [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]

## Rejection of Israeli conditions (November 2024)

When Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] demanded freedom to conduct military operations in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] as a condition of any ceasefire, Berri, as speaker of the Lebanese parliament, predictably rejected the condition, contributing to the impasse that led US envoy [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]] to threaten withdrawal from the mediation effort.[2]
