# Reedy Creek Improvement District

The Reedy Creek Improvement District was the special governmental district created in 1967 to govern the Florida land on which Disney World was built.[1] Its existence gave [[disney]] a unique self-governing status that placed the property outside ordinary Florida county or city government, allowed the company to appoint the district's board members, and conferred various tax exemptions.[1]

## Abolition

In February 2023, Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] signed legislation abolishing the district and replacing it with the [[central-florida-tourism-oversight-district|Central Florida Tourism Oversight District]], whose members he appointed.[1] DeSantis justified the change by noting that no other business in Florida enjoyed such a "sweetheart deal," under which the regulated company effectively selected its own regulator.[1] The move followed Disney's public opposition to his Parental Rights in Education bill.[1]

## Legacy

The successor district's August 2023 announcement eliminating all diversity, equity, and inclusion programs was framed as a response to Reedy Creek's record, with the district stating that the prior body had implemented hiring and contracting programs that discriminated on the basis of gender and race at a cost of millions of dollars to taxpayers.[1]
