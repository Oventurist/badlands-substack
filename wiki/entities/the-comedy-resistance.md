# The Comedy Resistance

The Comedy Resistance (TCR) was an activist organization formed within the broader anti-Trump [[the-resistance|Resistance]] movement that recruited working comedians for political organizing and publicity work.[1]

## Founding

TCR was founded by the filmmaker Stephen Kessler together with [[heather-podesta|Heather Podesta]], the former wife of [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]] and one of the most successful lobbyists in Washington, D.C.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] reported having had a front-row seat to the group's creation, as he was living in Hollywood with a roommate employed as a video editor for TCR alongside its founders.[1]

## Activities

The organization enlisted comedians including Bob Odenkirk, David Cross, Sarah Silverman, Mike Birbiglia, Patton Oswalt and Reggie Watts to perform a variety of tasks and photo opportunities, among them registering voters at comedy shows and handing out masks during the COVID-19 period.[1]

## Affiliation

DeLarme characterized TCR as an extension of the Resistance movement and specifically as a project of the left-of-center [[action-network|Action Network]], the digital organizing platform created in 2012 by [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] and Brian Young.[1] In his account this placed TCR inside the same tax-exempt NGO infrastructure he associates with the wider [[deep-state|deep state]] apparatus.[1]
