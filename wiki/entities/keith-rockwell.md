# Keith Rockwell

Keith Rockwell is a former director at the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]] and a trade commentator.[1]

## Assessment of Trump's tariff threat

Responding to President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s November 2024 announcement that he would impose tariffs on all exports from [[canada|Canada]], [[mexico|Mexico]] and [[china|China]] until those countries reduced migration and drug flows into the United States, Rockwell predicted the move could spark a trade war.[1] "The United States exports hundreds of billions of dollars worth of goods to these countries," he said. "Anyone who expects that they will stand pat and not retaliate has not been paying attention."[1]
