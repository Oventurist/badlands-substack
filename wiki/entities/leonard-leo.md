# Leonard Leo

Leonard Leo is a conservative legal activist who built the [[federalist-society|Federalist Society]] into a political powerhouse and helped [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] create the Supreme Court majority that, in 2022, erased federal protections for abortion.[1]

## Standing in the conservative legal movement

Leo's stature within the movement is such that, at a Texas Federalist Society gathering during Trump's first term, he "needed no last name" in his introduction as he took a seat opposite Justice [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]].[1] Though Thomas was the featured speaker, Leo was arguably the most important person in the American legal system in the room.[1] Judge Edith Jones of the Fifth Circuit, introducing the evening, credited the quality of the court's newly appointed conservative judges to "the Federalist Society, to Leonard."[1]

## The Planned Parenthood case

Leo's influence remains visible in litigation that seeks to strike a further blow to abortion rights and could bankrupt [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], one of the nation's leading providers of women's healthcare.[1] The suit was brought by an anti-abortion activist tied to Leo, and has been heard by judges from the lower courts through the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals who are also linked to him.[1]
