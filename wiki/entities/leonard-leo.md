# Leonard Leo

Leonard Leo is a conservative legal activist who built the [[federalist-society|Federalist Society]] into a political powerhouse and helped [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] create the Supreme Court majority that, in 2022, erased federal protections for abortion.[1]

## Standing in the conservative legal movement

Leo's stature within the movement is such that, at a Texas Federalist Society gathering during Trump's first term, he "needed no last name" in his introduction as he took a seat opposite Justice [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]].[1] Though Thomas was the featured speaker, Leo was arguably the most important person in the American legal system in the room.[1] Judge Edith Jones of the Fifth Circuit, introducing the evening, credited the quality of the court's newly appointed conservative judges to "the Federalist Society, to Leonard."[1]

## The Planned Parenthood case

Leo's influence remains visible in litigation that seeks to strike a further blow to abortion rights and could bankrupt [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], one of the nation's leading providers of women's healthcare.[1] The suit was brought by an anti-abortion activist tied to Leo, and has been heard by judges from the lower courts through the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals who are also linked to him.[1]

## $1 billion campaign to "crush" leftist media (September 2024)

In September 2024 it was reported that Leo — described as a conservative billionaire activist who had been the driving force behind the movement to reshape the American legal system — had launched a $1 billion campaign to "crush liberal dominance" in American corporations, mass media and the entertainment industry.[2] Leo, who became widely known for helping to make the Supreme Court conservative during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term, said his non-profit group, the [[marble-freedom-trust|Marble Freedom Trust]], was prepared to make both the government and the private sector conservative.[2]

"We need to crush liberal dominance where it's most insidious, so we'll direct resources to build talent and capital formation pipelines in the areas of news and entertainment, where leftwing extremism is most evident," Leo told the Financial Times.[2] He said the effort would work by helping anti-woke organizations: "Expect us to increase support for organizations that call out companies and financial institutions that bend to the woke mind virus spread by regulators and NGOs so that they have to pay the price for putting extreme leftwing ideology ahead of consumers."[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] praised the initiative as "the way," arguing that reclaiming institutions is a noble goal many in the movement had abandoned and that journalism remains a noble institution even when its practice is corrupted.[2] The commentary paired Leo's ambition with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s push to shrink government and eliminate waste, suggesting that a Fourth Estate reclaimed on conservative principles would keep the surviving institutions necessary and constitutional.[2]
