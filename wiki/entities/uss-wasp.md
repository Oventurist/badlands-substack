# USS Wasp

The USS Wasp is a United States Navy amphibious assault ship whose deployments place US Marines and sailors in ports across the Mediterranean and Middle East.[1]

## Izmir port call, September 2024

On Monday, 2 September 2024 the Wasp made a port call at Izmir, in western Turkey, and personnel attached to the ship went ashore on liberty.[1] While walking the streets of the city, US service members were set upon by a group of Turkish men in what became the [[2024-izmir-assault-on-us-soldiers|Izmir assault]].[1] Social media video showed a group surrounding and violently restraining one soldier and placing a white bag over his head, before several other US troops intervened to push the assailants off.[1]

Reuters reported, citing the US Embassy in Turkey and the local governor's office, that a nationalist Turkish youth group had physically assaulted two US soldiers and that fifteen assailants had been detained; five other US personnel came to their comrades' aid before local police intervened.[1] The embassy confirmed on X that "U.S. service members embarked aboard the USS Wasp were the victims of an assault in Izmir today, and are now safe," with no mention of injuries.[1]

## Continued deployment and the Izmir protests

In early September 2024 the Wasp remained in regional waters as part of efforts to "deter potential threats to Israel" amid the Gaza war and the looming potential for escalation with Hezbollah and Iran.[2] Following the assault on its crew members, Turkish citizens in Izmir vowed to continue protesting at the port until the ship departed, warning the Izmir Governorship that they would remain there until it left, and condemning the United States for its role in causing suffering and violence in Iraq, Syria and the broader West Asia region.[2] See [[2024-izmir-assault-on-us-soldiers]].
