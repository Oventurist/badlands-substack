# Saul Alinsky

Saul Alinsky was an American community organizer and author best known for the book *Rules for Radicals*, a manual of confrontational organizing tactics that is frequently invoked in American conservative commentary as shorthand for deliberate destabilization of institutions.[1]

## Invocation in Badlands commentary

In a November 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] argued that the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "abiding by Saul Alinsky's *Rules For Radicals*" in its handling of the southern border, contending that the absence of border enforcement was intentional rather than negligent.[1] Fleuret's commentary accompanied reporting on a migrant caravan of several thousand people that departed Tapachula, Mexico, for the United States in late October 2023.[1] He wrote that legal immigration was welcome but that mass illegal entry served what he called a "nefarious purpose" of stressing American systems to the point of collapse.[1]
