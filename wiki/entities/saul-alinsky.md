# Saul Alinsky

Saul Alinsky was an American community organizer and author best known for the book *Rules for Radicals*, a manual of confrontational organizing tactics that is frequently invoked in American conservative commentary as shorthand for deliberate destabilization of institutions.[1]

## Invocation in Badlands commentary

In a November 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] argued that the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "abiding by Saul Alinsky's *Rules For Radicals*" in its handling of the southern border, contending that the absence of border enforcement was intentional rather than negligent.[1] Fleuret's commentary accompanied reporting on a migrant caravan of several thousand people that departed Tapachula, Mexico, for the United States in late October 2023.[1] He wrote that legal immigration was welcome but that mass illegal entry served what he called a "nefarious purpose" of stressing American systems to the point of collapse.[1]

## Mentor to the "shadow network"

A June 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cast Alinsky as the intellectual origin point of what it called the [[soros-shadow-network|Soros shadow network]], describing him as an early mentor of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] as well as many other network operatives.[2] The essay credited Alinsky with identifying for his adherents the real pathways to power in American politics, arguing that radicals could achieve wide-sweeping revolutionary change without the support of the majority so long as they understood the rules of the game and how to exploit them.[2] Its summary formulation was that "Alinsky wrote the rules, and Soros mastered the art," positioning [[george-soros|George Soros]] as the financier who operationalized *Rules for Radicals* across foundations, PACs and media.[2]

The same article identified the PICO National Network — a group it described as Alinskyite, founded by the liberal Jesuit Father John Baumann — among the organizations that lobbied the Vatican ahead of [[pope-francis|Pope Francis]]' 2015 visit to the United States, extending the Alinsky lineage into religious organizing.[2]

## Mentorship of Obama and Clinton in Q drop #117

Chapter 17 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series lists Alinsky among the mentors of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], alongside [[jeremiah-wright|Jeremiah Wright]] and [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]], characterizing the group as figures unsympathetic to America and its way of life.[3] The chapter adds that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was also mentored by Alinsky, and argues that reading his [[rules-for-radicals|Rules for Radicals]] makes it plain what such figures have been doing to the country.[3]
