# X (Twitter)

**X**, formerly Twitter, is the social media platform acquired by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and, together with [[rumble]], described by Badlands Media as one of the two frontlines of the information war heading into 2024.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Target of the censorship regime

The article states that the establishment needs Rumble taken out and Twitter reverted to its previous state — that is, to the moderation posture that prevailed during earlier mass-purge events such as the suppression of the Hunter Biden laptop story before the 2020 election.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Musk's announcement that he would buy Twitter is identified as the moment the establishment turned on him, after which formal investigations into him and his companies proliferated.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Comparison with alternatives

X is contrasted with Truth Social, which the article regards as useful but largely an echo chamber, and with Rumble, whose chief executive [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]] is said to be resisting the regime more intensely with far fewer resources.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Both X and Rumble form part of the parallel media system built after 2020, alongside Brave, in response to suppression by Facebook, YouTube and Google.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)
