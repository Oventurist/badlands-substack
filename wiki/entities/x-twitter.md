# X (Twitter)

**X**, formerly Twitter, is the social media platform acquired by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and identified by Badlands Media, alongside [[rumble|Rumble]], as one of the two frontlines of the information war heading into 2024.[1]

## Before the acquisition

Prior to Musk's purchase, Twitter was part of the platform ecosystem — with Facebook, YouTube and Google — that alternative media were at the mercy of, most conspicuously during the suppression of the Hunter Biden laptop story in the lead-up to the 2020 election.[1]

## After the acquisition

The announcement of Musk's plan to buy Twitter triggered an overnight reversal in establishment sentiment toward him, followed by watchdog-group campaigns and formal investigations.[1] Badlands argues the establishment's objective is to have Twitter "reverted to its previous state" while Rumble is removed altogether.[1] The platform is contrasted with Truth Social, which the author regards as merely an echo chamber.[1]
