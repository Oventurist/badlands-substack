# Sheena Wright

Sheena Wright is a lawyer and Deputy Mayor of New York City in the administration of [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] whose home was raided by federal agents in September 2024 alongside those of other senior city officials.[1]

Wright previously served on the city's Education Sector Advisory Council and on the state's New York Forward Re-Opening Advisory Board.[1] She is engaged to [[david-banks|David Banks]], the Commissioner of Schools and brother of Deputy Mayor for Public Safety [[philip-banks-iii|Philip Banks III]].[1]

Badlands cited Wright's combination of deputy-mayoral authority, advisory-board service, and family ties to the Banks brothers as part of the pattern of NGO-and-government overlap it described as corruption within public-private partnership in New York City, a scandal that also produced the resignation of Police Commissioner [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]].[1]
