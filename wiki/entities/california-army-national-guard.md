# California Army National Guard

The California Army National Guard is the state's Army National Guard component, ordinarily under the command of the governor of California but subject to federalization by the President.[1]

## June 2025 federalization in Los Angeles

Amid protests over immigration enforcement raids in Los Angeles in June 2025, about 2,000 California Army National Guard soldiers were placed under federal command to help protect federal personnel and property in the greater Los Angeles area, according to a statement from [[united-states-northern-command|U.S. Northern Command]].[1] Roughly 300 Guard members from the 78th Infantry Brigade Combat Team were deployed at locations in Los Angeles, Paramount and Compton.[1] The federalization proceeded in parallel with the placement of some 500 [[united-states-marine-corps|Marines]] from Twentynine Palms in a prepared-to-deploy status.[1]

Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] objected that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was escalating the situation by threatening a Marine deployment into the city.[1] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the episode in terms of game theory, arguing that patriots provoke a known enemy playbook in order to map its counters, and that the resulting chaos carried "a decidedly orange tint."[1]

## Ninth Circuit stay (June 2025)

On June 12, 2025, Judge [[charles-breyer|Charles Breyer]] ordered that control of the thousands of California National Guard troops deployed in Los Angeles be returned to Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] by noon the following day, finding that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s mobilization had been illegal.[2] Hours later the [[united-states-court-of-appeals-for-the-ninth-circuit|9th Circuit Court of Appeals]] paused that order in a late-night emergency stay and scheduled a hearing for the following Tuesday, meaning the troops remained federalized through the weekend.[2]

## MacArthur Park deployment (July 2025)

On Monday, July 7, 2025, members of the California National Guard accompanied dozens of federal immigration agents in tactical gear who deployed to a mostly empty MacArthur Park in Los Angeles.[3] It was unclear whether anyone was taken into custody during the operation, which drew outrage from local residents and was condemned by Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] as "outrageous" and "an attempt to spread fear."[3] The operation is covered at [[macarthur-park-immigration-operation|the MacArthur Park immigration operation]].[3]
