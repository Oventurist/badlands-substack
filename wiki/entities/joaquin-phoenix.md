# Joaquin Phoenix

Joaquin Phoenix is the American actor who portrayed the title character in the *Joker* films.[1]

In Badlands Media commentary by Erik Carlson, Phoenix's performance is treated as central to the film's political resonance. Carlson notes that the role did not call for Phoenix to become so emaciated, but that the actor chose to do so anyway, producing an emaciated, emotionally disturbed and violent character living with his mother who goes on to incite rioting and revolt in the city.[1] Carlson argues that it is difficult to view Phoenix's character as anything other than "the posterchild for Antifa," and reads the [[joker-film-series|Joker films]] as [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]] that granted the audience advance permission to riot.[1]
