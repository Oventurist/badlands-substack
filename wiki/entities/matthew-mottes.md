# Matthew Mottes

Matthew Mottes is the chairman of the [[municipal-water-authority-of-aliquippa|Municipal Water Authority of Aliquippa]] in western Pennsylvania.[1]

In late November 2023, the authority under Mottes's chairmanship discovered that Iran-affiliated hackers had infiltrated its systems by targeting an Israeli-made industrial control device.[1] Federal officials subsequently informed Mottes that the same hacking group had also compromised four other utilities and an aquarium.[1] His utility's breach was cited in a joint advisory from the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]], the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] and [[israel-national-cyber-directorate|Israel's National Cyber Directorate]] describing victims spanning multiple U.S. states.[1]
