# The Western Journal

The Western Journal is an American conservative news and commentary website whose reporting is frequently excerpted by independent aggregators in the alternative-media space, including [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## June 2023 White House transgender-activist report

The Western Journal supplied the source reporting for a June 13, 2023 item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] concerning the [[white-house|White House]] decision to bar trans influencer [[rose-montoya|Rose Montoya]] from the grounds.[1] The outlet reported that the White House had confirmed the ban to the New York Post after Montoya filmed a TikTok video at a White House LGBT "pride" event in which Montoya appeared topless, covering the nipples by hand, while another individual displayed scars from breast-removal surgery.[1] The Western Journal framed the episode with the observation that "even some acts of disrespect at the White House are too much for President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]."[1]

Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] used the Western Journal account as the basis for a commentary arguing that the ban demonstrated selective enforcement and hypocrisy rather than principle on the part of the Biden administration.[1]

## Credit Suisse secrecy report (2023)

In July 2023 The Western Journal relayed Reuters reporting that the Swiss parliamentary investigation into the collapse of [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] would keep its files closed for 50 years, twenty years longer than the standard 30-year moratorium in comparable cases.[2] The outlet framed the decision as a transparency problem, noting that the secrecy had triggered concern among Swiss historians.[2] The item was picked up in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used it to argue that trust in banking institutions requires vetting that a half-century seal makes impossible.[2]
