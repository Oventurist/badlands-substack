# Ray Donovan (DEA)

Ray Donovan is the former [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] official credited with helping bring down Mexican cartel leader Joaquín "El Chapo" Guzmán, described in July 2024 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as the "supposed bane of 'El Chapo'."[1]

## Cartel plan of attack

In July 2024 Donovan appeared publicly with a former Trump administration official to discuss a "plan of attack" against the Mexican cartels and their Chinese partners, presented amid congressional efforts on Capitol Hill to address the [[fentanyl-crisis|fentanyl crisis]].[1] The proposed strategy involved cutting off and prosecuting domestic and foreign banks and combining terrorist designations with RICO charges against trafficking networks.[1]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the plan echoed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s projected stance of sending Tier 1 "kill teams" south of the border upon retaking office, and that the convergence created an opportunity to link the southern border crisis to the broader [[deep-state|Deep State]] cabal in the public mind.[1] He also noted the "strange narrative convergence" that "Ray Donovan" is likewise the name of a well-known television series about a Hollywood "fixer" confronting organized crime.[1]
