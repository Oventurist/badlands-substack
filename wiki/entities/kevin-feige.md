# Kevin Feige

Kevin Feige is the head of [[marvel-studios|Marvel Studios]] and the public face of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.[1] For over a decade his principal deputy was [[victoria-alonso|Victoria Alonso]], who as President of Physical, Post Production, VFX and Animation served as one of Feige's primary right hands, overseeing the physical and digital production pipelines of the studio's films.[1]

## Role in the Alonso firing

When [[disney|Disney]] fired Alonso abruptly and "for cause" in March 2023, the decision was made by a committee of Disney executives rather than by Feige himself; trade reporting stated that Feige ultimately chose not to intervene in the outcome.[1] The firing, and the litigation threats that followed, unfolded alongside the arrest of [[jonathan-majors|Jonathan Majors]] and mounting financial losses on Marvel films and the Disney+ service — a cluster of events Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed as a broad reversal for "woke" entertainment brands.[1]
