# John Case

John Case is the attorney representing former Mesa County Clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] in her appeals and clemency efforts in Colorado.[1]

## March 2026 statement

On Wednesday, March 4, 2026, Case shared a statement with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s Ashe Epp responding to Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]]' public signal that he was considering clemency.[1] The statement said Peters was grateful to Polis for considering her request and quoted the governor's own line that "Justice in Colorado and America needs to be applied evenly."[1]

Case contrasted Peters' imprisonment with the treatment of another public official convicted of the same offense who paid a fine and performed 150 hours of public service — "less than one month of work."[1] He named Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]], Attorney General [[phil-weiser|Phil Weiser]], and [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]]-aligned election official Matt Crane as "Tina's political enemies" who had released statements demanding Polis show no mercy, adding that "it will take great courage for Governor Polis to grant Tina clemency, and Tina thanks him."[1]

When pressed on whether a clemency application had actually been submitted, a member of the legal team responded only, "There is no missing paperwork."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that this phrasing might reflect the team's theory that a presidential pardon alone is sufficient to secure Peters' release, and that they had never explicitly stated an application was filed with Polis.[1]
