# Ted Kaczynski

**Theodore "Ted" Kaczynski**, known as the Unabomber, is the subject of a two-part treatment by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

Ehret introduced Kaczynski in an earlier feature and returned to him in "On Ted Kaczynski and False Prophets," published 25 October 2025 and later highlighted in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] retrospective.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The organizing claim of that treatment is that Kaczynski shared common axioms with the leading oligarchists running the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] — that is, that the Unabomber's anti-industrial philosophy converges with the outlook of the technocratic elite rather than opposing it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The "false prophets" framing positions Kaczynski as one of several figures whose apparent radicalism serves, in Ehret's reading, the same anti-industrial agenda he associates with [[transhumanism|transhumanism]] and [[eco-terrorism|eco-terrorism]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Invoked as a comparison to Israeli device attacks

In September 2024, Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked Kaczynski as a benchmark when assessing the [[2024-lebanon-pager-attacks|Israeli pager and walkie-talkie attacks]] in [[lebanon|Lebanon]], asking how the operations differed objectively from Kaczynski's mailing of bombs to people between 1978 and 1995 in an attempt to foment chaos and anarchy.[2] He noted that Kaczynski killed three people and injured 23 across that seventeen-year period, a toll he described as "lackluster" compared with the casualties produced in Lebanon within 24 hours.[2]
