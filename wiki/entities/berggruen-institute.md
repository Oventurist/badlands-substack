# Berggruen Institute

The Berggruen Institute is a think tank where [[nils-gilman|Nils Gilman]], co-founder of the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]], serves as Senior Vice President of Programs.[1]

## Described activities

In the Badlands Media account by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the Berggruen Institute is described as creating biotechnology that rearranges DNA to create new life forms, and as being engaged in artificial intelligence and transhumanism.[1] The institute is linked in the same account to Noema Magazine, a publication described as partnered with the Washington Post, and Gilman is further described as having ties to the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]].[1]

## Significance in the TIP narrative

The institute appears in Badlands coverage chiefly as evidence of the elite institutional network behind TIP's 2020 election war games — a network the author places alongside the Soros-funded [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], with which TIP co-founder [[rosa-brooks|Rosa Brooks]] was associated.[1]
