# League of Women Voters

The League of Women Voters is a long-established American civic organization focused on voter education, registration, and election administration. Its Colorado affiliate, the League of Women Voters of Colorado, was a plaintiff in [[naacp-v-useip|NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.]] alongside the [[naacp|NAACP]]'s Colorado/Montana/Wyoming Area Conference and [[mi-familia-vota|Mi Familia Vota]].

The essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]] by defendant [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] notes that the League itself published a piece titled "The 2016 Presidential Election was Rigged" on November 23, 2016, and argues that this undercuts the plaintiffs' position that questioning an election outcome is inherently intimidating conduct. The article also observes that the League, like the other plaintiff organizations, engages in political canvassing as a routine part of its work — the same activity the suit sought to characterize as voter intimidation when performed by the [[useip|United States Election Integrity Plan]].
