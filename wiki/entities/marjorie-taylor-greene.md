# Marjorie Taylor Greene

Marjorie Taylor Greene is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Georgia, commonly referred to by the initials MTG. In the Badlands Media corpus she appears as a leading non-interventionist voice within the Republican conference, aligned with the [[maga|MAGA]] base's opposition to new Middle East wars.

## Opposition to war with Iran

The March 10, 2026 Badlands Brief records that Greene opposed war with [[iran|Iran]] and that Congressman [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]] responded by claiming Iran was planning to nuke the United States and accusing Greene of "wanting to see America get nuked."

The Brief's commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the exchange as an illustration of how "deranged and violent these people are, once the mask drops," arguing that the attack on Greene exemplified the projection of aggressive intent onto Muslims by legislators he characterizes as Christian Dispensationalists — a strand of [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionism]] the corpus links to pressure for strikes on Iran. Greene's position is presented in the piece as consistent with the antiwar demands of the base that elected President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].
