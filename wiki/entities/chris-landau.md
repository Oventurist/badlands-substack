# Chris Landau

Chris Landau is the Deputy Secretary of State in the second [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] administration, serving as the second-ranking official at the [[state-department|State Department]] under Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[1]

## Venezuela policy controversy (July 2026)

In July 2026 Landau became the subject of internal criticism after senior Trump administration officials accused him of twice miscommunicating United States policy regarding the efforts of exiled Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] to return to Venezuela following the June 24 earthquakes.[1] According to reporting by [[axios|Axios]], Landau appeared to signal support for Machado's travel in conversations with Dutch and Panamanian officials, despite the administration's stated position that it would not facilitate her return.[1]

The reported miscommunications triggered diplomatic confusion, including the reversal of one of Machado's travel plans after Secretary Rubio personally reiterated US policy to her.[1] Landau denied contradicting official policy, saying he had also communicated that the United States was neutral regarding Machado's travel.[1] A State Department spokesperson dismissed reports of internal conflict, stating that the department remained aligned with President Trump and Secretary Rubio's foreign policy agenda.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the factual accuracy of the "rogue diplomat" story mattered less than the narrative it established: that Rubio and the Trump administration oppose Machado's return and are blocking her from taking power in Venezuela, while recasting the [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]]/[[delcy-rodriguez|Rodríguez]] government as a cooperative partner.[1] The commentary framed the episode as evidence that the State Department is populated by actors inclined to install preferred outsiders in foreign governments.[1]
