# Black Basta

**Black Basta** is both a strain of ransomware and the name of the broad criminal group that deploys it.[1] According to the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]], the group includes Russian-speaking members.[1] Ransomware operations of this kind typically lock victims' computers and steal data for extortion.[1]

Black Basta has been used repeatedly against health care organizations in recent years.[1] In May 2024, four sources briefed on the investigation told [[cnn|CNN]] that the cyberattack that forced [[ascension|Ascension]] — a 140-hospital nonprofit network — to divert ambulances was a Black Basta ransomware attack.[1]

On the Friday following the Ascension disruption, the [[health-information-sharing-and-analysis-center|Health Information Sharing and Analysis Center]], a cyber threat sharing group for large health care providers worldwide, published an advisory warning that hackers using Black Basta ransomware had "recently accelerated attacks against the healthcare sector."[1] The advisory cited at least two health care organizations in Europe and the United States that had suffered "severe operational disruptions" from Black Basta in the preceding month, without naming them.[1]
