# Winsome Earle-Sears

Winsome Earle-Sears is the Lieutenant Governor of Virginia and was the Republican nominee in the 2025 Virginia gubernatorial election, running to succeed term-limited Republican Governor Glenn Youngkin in the November 4 contest.[1] She is a Black woman and a former United States Marine, a biography that Badlands contributors treated as politically significant in the framing of the race.[1]

## 2025 gubernatorial campaign

Earle-Sears faced Democratic nominee [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]], a former member of Congress and former CIA officer, in what was the only scheduled debate between the two candidates ahead of the election.[1] The debate, held in October 2025, was described as chaotic: Earle-Sears repeatedly interrupted Spanberger, who was the race's front-runner and who largely declined to engage, often staring straight ahead and remaining silent when questioned directly by her opponent.[1]

Central to Earle-Sears's debate strategy was the texting scandal engulfing [[jay-jones|Jay Jones]], the Democratic nominee for Virginia attorney general, who had been revealed to have sent text messages using violent language threatening a political opponent and that opponent's family.[1] Earle-Sears repeatedly called on Spanberger to demand that Jones drop out of the attorney general race, and the fallout from the messages continued to reverberate through the fall elections a week after first coming to light.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] characterized the matchup as "the ultimate narrative destroyer," contrasting "a literal CIA agent running for the democrats against a Black woman and Marine for the GOP."[1] The framing reflects a recurring Badlands theme in which intelligence-community backgrounds among Democratic candidates are treated as evidence of institutional capture, while Earle-Sears's military service and biography are presented as a rebuttal to conventional media narratives about the Republican Party.[1]
