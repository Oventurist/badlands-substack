# Nesta Webster

Nesta Webster was a British writer of conspiracy literature listed by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] among the occultists who advanced narratives of "Jewish bankers' conspiracies undermining the noble honest royal families of Europe."[1]

Ehret groups her with [[gerard-encausse|Papus]], [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], [[alice-bailey|Alice Bailey]], and [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]], and argues that the same narrative is now promoted by the pro-Nazi film series [[europa-the-last-battle|*Europa: The Last Battle*]].[1] In his framing, these writers illustrate a broader pattern in which pagan occultists striving to create a new fascist world order under a restored pagan priesthood directed intense hostility at Jews, whose scriptures had intervened against occultism and human sacrifice.[1]
