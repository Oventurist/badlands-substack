# Hadas Klein

Hadas Klein is a key prosecution witness in one of the corruption cases in which Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] is standing trial.[1]

## Alleged intimidation campaign

According to a report by Channel 12's "Uvda" program, [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] ordered Hanni Bleiweiss, her husband's late aide, to orchestrate protests and an online campaign against Klein.[1] Following that report, Attorney General [[gali-baharav-miara|Gali Baharav-Miara]] and State Attorney [[amit-aisman|Amit Aisman]] announced on December 26 that they had ordered an investigation on suspicion of witness harassment and obstruction of justice.[1]

The resulting criminal investigation of Sara Netanyahu is being conducted by the Israel Police together with the cybercrimes department of the State Attorney's Office.[1]
