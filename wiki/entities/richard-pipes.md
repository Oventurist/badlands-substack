# Richard Pipes

Richard Pipes was an American historian of Russia counted among the neoconservative right-wingers who grew in power and prestige during the presidencies of [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]], [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] and [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]].[1]

Together with [[paul-nitze|Paul Nitze]], [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]], [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[richard-perle|Richard Perle]] and [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]], Pipes held that the détente-era push toward trust and cooperation with the Soviet Union had to stop.[1] This cast of characters was grouped together in [[team-b|Team B]] to promote a counter-argument to the official National Intelligence Estimate, asserting the existence of an evil empire committed to becoming a global Soviet hegemon.[1]
