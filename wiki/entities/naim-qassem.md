# Naim Qassem

Naim Qassem is the Secretary-General of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], the Lebanese Shi'ite movement, who in February 2025 issued ultimatums to Israel over the withdrawal of its forces from [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[1]

## February 2025 withdrawal ultimatum

In a televised address on Sunday, February 16, 2025, cited by France 24, Qassem warned that if Israel did not withdraw its forces from Lebanon by February 18, "we will know how to deal with it."[1] "Israel must fully withdraw on February 18, it has no excuse," he said, adding that "it is the responsibility of the Lebanese state to take a firm stance and force Israel to withdraw," and that if Israeli forces remained in southern Lebanon, "everyone understands how [Hezbollah] will deal with the occupation."[1]

This was not his first such warning: on January 4 he had announced that his group's patience "might run out" over alleged Israeli violations of the truce terms, saying, "When we decide to act, you will see it immediately."[1] On the evening of February 16, the Israeli Air Force struck several Hezbollah sites in Lebanon housing rocket launchers and weapons, according to the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that under the original ceasefire deal between the IDF and Hezbollah, Israel had until the end of January to withdraw; when Israel said it could not comply, the Trump White House negotiated an extension to February 18.[1] He cited reports from figures such as Colonel Douglas Macgregor and Colonel Lawrence Wilkerson that Hezbollah has dominated the IDF in ground engagements in South Lebanon, while Israel leveraged its relationships with Europe and the United States to obtain drones and missiles enabling strikes from the safety of Tel Aviv — assassinating the top 25 Hezbollah leaders and burying the movement's underground headquarters under bombardment.[1] He also cited the [[pager-attack|pager attack]] and the following day's walkie-talkie attack, which killed 12, including children, and wounded over 3,000, and argued Israel cut Hezbollah's land bridge to [[iran|Iran]] by deploying Al Jolani and allied ISIS/Al Qaeda fighters to overthrow the Assad government in Syria.[1]

Despite this, the commentary held, Hezbollah stands ready to fight — cornered, with its leader of 32 years assassinated and its homes bombarded — against an exhausted, short-handed and demoralized enemy it has yet to lose to in a gunfight, invoking Sun Tzu's warning that he who seeks to completely annihilate his enemy will end up destroying himself.[1]

## Position on the 2024 US ceasefire proposal

In November 2024, as secretary-general of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], Qassem said the group had submitted its comments on the American ceasefire proposal mediated by [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]], stating that it was "negotiating under two conditions: first, the complete and comprehensive cessation of Israeli aggression and, second, the preservation of Lebanese sovereignty."[2]
