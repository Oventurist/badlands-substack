# Bola Tinubu

Bola Tinubu is the President of [[nigeria|Nigeria]].[1]

## Joint counterterrorism operation with the United States (May 2026)

In May 2026, Tinubu and U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] both announced that the Islamic State figure [[abu-bilal-al-manuki|Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki]] had been killed in northeastern Nigeria, according to Reuters.[1] Nigeria's State House said on 16 May that Nigerian and U.S. forces had carried out a joint counterterrorism operation and that early assessments confirmed Al-Manuki and several lieutenants were killed in a strike on his compound in the Lake Chad Basin.[1]

The Nigerian Army said the strike took place in Metele, Borno State, in close coordination with U.S. [[africom|Africa Command]], beginning around 12:01 a.m. and ending near 4 a.m. on Saturday with no casualties or asset losses.[1] A U.S. official speaking anonymously to the Associated Press said Al-Manuki had been involved in Islamic State organizing and finance and had been plotting attacks against the United States and its interests.[1]

## Regional context

Commentary published in the Badlands Brief situated the operation within the wider West African conflict between the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]] and [[ecowas|ECOWAS]], arguing that destroying such proxy groups aided Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] and his Sahelian allies even though mainstream coverage would frame it as U.S. alignment with ECOWAS.[1]
