# Bola Tinubu

Bola Tinubu is the President of [[nigeria|Nigeria]]. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears in connection with the joint Nigerian-American counterterrorism campaign against [[isis|Islamic State]]-affiliated militancy in the Lake Chad Basin.

## Confirmation of the Al-Manuki strike

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, Tinubu and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] both said that Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki — also known as Abu-Mainok — was killed in northeastern Nigeria, per [[reuters|Reuters]]. The strike on Al-Manuki's compound in the Lake Chad Basin, which also killed several of his lieutenants, was announced by Nigeria's State House on May 16, 2026 and took place in Metele, Borno State, in close coordination with [[africom|U.S. Africa Command]], according to the [[nigerian-army|Nigerian Army]].
