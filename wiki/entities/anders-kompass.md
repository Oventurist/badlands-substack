# Anders Kompass

Anders Kompass is a former [[united-nations|United Nations]] aid worker who became a whistleblower.[1]

## Disclosure

Badlands commentary recounts that the French and UN occupation of North Africa led to a major scandal when Kompass exposed a pedophile ring run by French soldiers.[1] The episode is cited as evidence in that commentary that the interventions justified by militant activity in the Sahel produced their own abuses, alongside the argument that terrorist organizations in the region benefit the [[state-department|State Department]] and its partners.[1]
