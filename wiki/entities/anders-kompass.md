# Anders Kompass

Anders Kompass is a former United Nations human rights official who became a whistleblower. In the Badlands corpus he is invoked in connection with allegations of sexual abuse by French soldiers during the UN and French military presence in the Sahel.

## Whistleblower episode in the Sahel

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recalls that the "French/UN occupation of North Africa led to a huge scandal when UN aid worker Anders Kompass turned whistleblower and exposed a pedophile ring run by French soldiers." The commentary cites the episode as part of its broader argument about the conduct of foreign forces in the region, alongside claims that terrorist organizations in the Sahel have been used to destabilize the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]] so that [[ecowas|ECOWAS]] and the [[united-nations|UN]] can justify intervention.
