# Wally Adeyemo

Wally Adeyemo served as Deputy Secretary of the Treasury in the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], acting as the department's principal interlocutor with Congress on sanctions and financial-restriction questions.[1]

## Role in the frozen Iranian funds dispute

In October 2023 Adeyemo told House Democrats in a closed-door briefing that $6 billion in Iranian oil assets, unfrozen earlier as part of a prisoner swap, "isn't going anywhere anytime soon."[1] His remarks reflected an agreement reached between the United States and [[qatar|Qatar]] to prevent [[iran|Iran]] from accessing the funds.[1]

The agreement came as Washington scrutinized Iran's role in backing [[hamas|Hamas]] following the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]] on Israel.[1] The administration maintained there was no evidence Iran had played a direct role in the assault, while the [[white-house|White House]] argued Tehran was nonetheless complicit given decades of support for the group.[1] Lawmakers of both parties had pressed the administration to re-freeze the money in the wake of the violence, making Adeyemo's briefing the administration's principal response to that pressure.[1] The episode is treated in more detail at [[iran-6-billion-fund-release]].[1]
