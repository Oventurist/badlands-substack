# Jean-Claude Juncker

Jean-Claude Juncker is the former President of the [[european-commission|European Commission]], serving as "boss at the Berlaymont in Brussels from 2014 to 2019."[1]

## Remarks on Ukrainian corruption

In an interview with the German outlet Augsburger Allgemeine published in October 2023 and reported by [[politico|Politico]], Juncker slammed the possibility of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] joining the [[european-union|European Union]], lambasting the country as massively "corrupt."[1]

"Anyone who has had anything to do with Ukraine knows that this is a country that is corrupt at all levels of society," Juncker said. "Despite its efforts, it is not ready for accession; it needs massive internal reform processes."[1] He added that "making false promises" to Ukrainians regarding EU accession "would be neither good for the EU nor for Ukraine," and that "you shouldn't make false promises to the people in Ukraine who are up to their necks in suffering."[1] Juncker said he was "very angry about some voices in Europe who are telling Ukrainians that they can become members immediately."[1]

The remarks came as Kyiv continued fending off [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s full-scale invasion, which began in February 2022, and were carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of October 9, 2023.[1]
