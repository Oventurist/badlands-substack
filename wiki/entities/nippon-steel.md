# Nippon Steel

Nippon Steel is the principal Japanese steelmaker, known in American political discourse chiefly for its attempted acquisition of [[us-steel|US Steel]].[1]

## US Steel transaction

The proposed purchase of US Steel was blocked in January by President Joe Biden, then reopened and restructured by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a negotiation that Badlands commentary describes as so favorable to the American side that the resulting arrangement makes Nippon Steel appear to be owned by US Steel and the US government rather than the reverse.[1]

Badlands commentary further alleges that then–Prime Minister [[shigeru-ishiba|Shigeru Ishiba]] was working on Nippon Steel's behalf, and that his early-February trip to the [[white-house|White House]] — the first by a foreign leader after Benjamin Netanyahu — was undertaken at the company's and allied globalist interests' behest.[1]
