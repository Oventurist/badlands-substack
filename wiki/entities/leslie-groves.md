# Leslie Groves

Leslie Groves was the US Army engineer who directed the [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]], played by Matt Damon in Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer*.[1]

In the film, shortly before the [[trinity-test|Trinity test]], Groves worries aloud about destroying the world; when [[j-robert-oppenheimer|Oppenheimer]] says the chance of annihilating all life on Earth is near zero, Groves splutters that zero would be nice.[1] Badlands notes, via ScienceFocus.com, that the concern historically belonged to physicist [[edward-teller|Edward Teller]] rather than to Groves.[1]
