# Diem

**Diem**, formerly known as Libra, was a stablecoin cryptocurrency project developed by [[facebook|Facebook]].[1] Before the involvement of David Marcus and Kevin Weil, Morgan Beller was the sole person working on a Facebook blockchain initiative, spending months during 2017 researching the technology and meeting people in the industry.[1]

The article "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" raises the question of whether the effort was a means of testing and onboarding an unassuming population onto a central bank digital currency.[1]

The project was blocked by the first Trump administration. On July 15, 2019, Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steve Mnuchin]] stated that he was "not currently comfortable with Facebook launching its Libra cryptocurrency platform," adding that the administration had held multiple meetings with regulators and Facebook representatives in which the US government expressed its concerns.[1] Facebook's efforts were ultimately stopped and the project's assets were sold to [[silvergate-bank|Silvergate Bank]], which was itself shut down in March 2023.[1]

The suppression of Diem is presented in the source article as one leg of a pattern in which competitors to [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and to [[starlink|Starlink]] were removed while Bitcoin custody was simultaneously legalised for banks.[1]
