# Rahmanullah Lakanwal

Rahmanullah Lakanwal is an Afghan national identified by law enforcement as the suspect in the November 26, 2025 shooting of two [[national-guard|National Guard]] members in Washington, D.C. He was 29 years old at the time of the attack, according to four senior law enforcement sources briefed on the investigation.[1]

## Background in Afghanistan

Relatives and officials stated that Lakanwal served alongside U.S. troops during the American war in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1] Reporting subsequently established a more specific affiliation: he had worked with the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] during the U.S. occupation, serving as a member of a partner force in Kandahar.[1] [[john-ratcliffe|CIA Director John Ratcliffe]] confirmed on the Thursday following the shooting that Lakanwal had been admitted to the United States in September 2021 "due to his prior work with the US Government, including the CIA, as a member of a partner force in Kandahar, which ended shortly following the chaotic evacuation."[1]

## Entry into the United States

Officials said Lakanwal entered the country under a special program established to evacuate vulnerable Afghans — including those who had worked with Western forces — after the Taliban recaptured Afghanistan in 2021.[1] The resettlement pathway that admitted him became a focal point of subsequent commentary, particularly because Colorado Representative [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]] had been among the most prominent advocates for the 2021 resettlement of Afghans in the United States.[1]

## The shooting

At approximately 2:15 p.m. on Wednesday, November 26, 2025, Lakanwal allegedly opened fire at close range a short distance from the White House on two West Virginia National Guard members who were on patrol.[1] U.S. Army Specialist [[sarah-beckstrom|Sarah Beckstrom]] died of her injuries the following day; U.S. Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe remained in critical condition.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands hosts treated the suspect's intelligence-service background as the central fact of the story. [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted that [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] had predicted on his show two weeks earlier that elements of the CIA would activate Islamic sleeper cells inside the United States in the coming months, characterizing the disclosure of Lakanwal's CIA ties as "spooks admitting their psyops."[1] [[canncon|CannCon]] argued the episode was an attempted [[color-revolution|color revolution]] provoked to justify investigation and escalation against the administration.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the shooting to [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]]'s forecast of violence involving the National Guard less than a month earlier and to [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]'s earlier public posture, writing that the convergence "makes you think."[1]

## Arraignment (December 2025)

On Tuesday, December 2, 2025, Lakanwal, 29, appeared virtually from his hospital bed and pleaded not guilty to all charges arising from the shooting that killed one National Guardsman and severely injured another in Washington, D.C., the previous week.[2] Terrence Austin, one of his lawyers, argued that his client merited release, citing the Justice Department's delay in bringing charges and Lakanwal's clean criminal history; his wife and five children, all boys, were reported to be living in Bellingham, Washington.[2] Lakanwal was seen lying under a blanket in a hospital gown, his eyes fluttering as he spoke through a Pashto interpreter during the brief hearing in D.C. Superior Court.[2] The judge ordered him held without bond, describing the ambush-style attack as an act that spread terror throughout the nation's capital.[2]
