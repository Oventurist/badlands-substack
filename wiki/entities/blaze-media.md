# Blaze Media

**Blaze Media**, publisher of *The Blaze* and Blaze News, is an American conservative media company whose investigative reporting on the January 6, 2021 Capitol events features prominently in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] coverage.[1]

## January 6 footage reporting

Blaze Media investigative journalist [[steve-baker|Steve Baker]] used newly released Capitol closed-circuit television clips to report that [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] Special Agent [[david-lazarus|David Lazarus]] gave false testimony in the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] trial.[1] The outlet published an analysis in October 2023 asking whether the security chief for then-Speaker Nancy Pelosi had perjured himself, based on time-stamped footage that placed Lazarus elsewhere in the Capitol complex at the time he said he observed then-officer [[harry-dunn|Harry Dunn]] interacting with Oath Keepers.[1]

Blaze News reporters were able to view the footage only on House subcommittee terminals in Washington, D.C., in a restricted capacity, and at the time of the October 4 analysis were permitted to publish just one screenshot after video permissions were placed on hold following the ouster of House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1] Rep. [[barry-loudermilk|Barry Loudermilk]] subsequently released additional screenshots to Baker, saying the allegation of a Capitol Police officer lying under oath "must be fully investigated."[1]
