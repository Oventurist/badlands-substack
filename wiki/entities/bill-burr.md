# Bill Burr

Bill Burr is an American stand-up comedian whose December 2023 remarks about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on late-night television drew attention in independent media.

## Kimmel interview

Speaking to talk show host [[jimmy-kimmel|Jimmy Kimmel]], Burr said liberals were "fucking stupid" for the way they had handled Trump and had turned him into a martyr, predicting "he's coming back" for revenge.[1] Burr framed the comments within a discussion of how he would teach his children about narcissists and sociopaths, describing Trump as "a narcissist – neutral energy – neutral."[1]

When Kimmel wondered whether Trump would "go away" if leftists ignored him, Burr replied that Trump had been fading until "you idiot liberals…indicted him and now he's a martyr," adding, "He's coming back, Jimmy! He's coming back! It's gonna be great for comedy."[1] Burr also said he wanted to vote for a president in their forties who would have to live with their decisions, and quipped of Trump and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] that "with any luck, they'll both die of natural causes before the election."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the exchange as two figures "pretending to have original takes on President Trump," and objected to Burr and Kimmel labeling anyone else a narcissist.[1] Her substantive concern was the pattern: mainstream influencers, leftist podcasters, and legacy outlets simultaneously invoking "Trump Dead" talking points, which she read as narrative deployment from a shared song book and as evidence of escalation born of panic.[1]
