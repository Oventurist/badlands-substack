# Fanil Sarvarov

Lieutenant General Fanil Sarvarov was a Russian military officer who served as head of operational training at the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces.[1] He was assassinated in Moscow on Monday, December 22, 2025.[1]

## Assassination

Russia's Investigative Committee reported that Sarvarov was killed by a car bomb in the southern part of Moscow, after an explosive device planted beneath the vehicle he was travelling in detonated on Monday morning.[1] The blast damaged several other vehicles and seriously injured Sarvarov's driver.[1] Russian officials said one line of investigation was an assassination carried out by Ukrainian intelligence services, noting that Kiev had previously used explosive devices in targeted killings of officials and public figures — including the December 2024 killing of Lt. Gen. [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]], commander of Russia's Nuclear, Chemical, and Biological Defense Forces, and his aide, by a bomb hidden in an e-scooter.[1]

## Career

According to accounts cited in Badlands coverage, Sarvarov took part in combat operations in the East Prigorodny conflict and in the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]] in the late 1990s and early 2000s, and led Russian operations in the Syrian civil war between 2015 and 2016.[1] The "East Prigorodny conflict" refers to the Ossetian–Ingush conflict of the early 1990s, which Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] has described as a prelude to the [[2008-russo-georgian-war|2008 Russo-Georgian War]] — the point at which Russia signalled it would not tolerate the post-Cold War security architecture associated with George H. W. Bush's "New World Order."[1] By virtue of his Syrian service, Sarvarov was described as having served alongside the Kadyrovite formations, which along with the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] became among the most notorious elements of the Russian military.[1]
