# John Johnson Jr.

John Johnson Jr. is the executive director of the [[michigan-department-of-civil-rights|Michigan Department of Civil Rights]].[1]

In a briefing with reporters following the department's 15 November 2023 discrimination charge against salon owner [[christine-geiger|Christine Geiger]] and her business Studio 8 Hair Lab, Johnson said: "The truth is, based on a thorough investigation, that Studio 8 and its owner Christine Geiger, openly and repeatedly violated the [[elliott-larsen-civil-rights-act|Elliott-Larsen Civil Rights Act]]."[1] The charge followed complaints over Geiger's Facebook posts stating that people identifying as anything other than a man or woman should "seek services at a local pet groomer."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the department's action as an instance of [[lawfare|lawfare]], arguing that the process itself functions as the punishment and that each such case serves as a potential building block toward new legal precedent.[1]
