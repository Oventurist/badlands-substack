# Matt Trump

Matt Trump is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host who co-presented [[spellbreakers|Spellbreakers]] with [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] on Wednesdays at 7:30pm ET as of the January 2023 schedule, a program devoted to exposing what the hosts called flimflam and pseudoscience across the media and academia.[1]
