# John Tortorella

**John Tortorella** is an American ice hockey coach who was head coach of the [[philadelphia-flyers|Philadelphia Flyers]] at the time of the January 2023 Pride Night controversy involving defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]].[1] Described as "no stranger to controversy himself," Tortorella has repeatedly waded into sociopolitical disputes over the course of his coaching career and is familiar with the professional consequences of standing his ground.[1]

## Defense of Ivan Provorov

After Provorov declined to wear a rainbow warmup jersey before the Flyers' January 17, 2023 home game against the Anaheim Ducks, Tortorella publicly defended his star defenseman.[1] He said the organization had "sent out a release regarding the beliefs that we have," called it "really a great night," and added of Provorov that "he is being true to himself and to his religion. This has to do with his beliefs and his religion. That is one thing I respect about Provy; he is always true to himself, so that's where we're at with that."[1]

Tortorella further stated that he did not believe Provorov's decision warranted benching the player, and said he was proud of him for standing his ground.[1] Speaking again on the Friday following the game, he said, "As I said, Provy did nothing wrong. Just because you don't agree with his decision doesn't mean he did anything wrong."[1]

## Earlier controversies

Tortorella had previously been outspoken against players kneeling during the National Anthem, the protest form popularized by former NFL quarterback [[colin-kaepernick|Colin Kaepernick]] to protest perceived racial injustice toward the black community in America.[1] He later reversed his hard-line stance against kneeling, though he never endorsed the practice, instead leaving the matter to individual player choice.[1]

## Reception

Badlands Media coverage presented Tortorella's defense of Provorov as a rare instance of an authority figure inside professional sports refusing to abandon a player targeted by [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]], noting that "cancel culture vultures" swarmed around Provorov, Tortorella and the Flyers organization alike after the incident.[1]
