# Jimmie Gardner

Jimmie Gardner is the brother-in-law of former Georgia gubernatorial candidate [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]] who was arrested in Tampa, Florida, in November 2023 on human trafficking allegations.[1]

## 2023 arrest

The [[tampa-police-department|Tampa Police Department]] said it arrested Gardner, then 57, on a Friday for allegedly attempting to engage in sexual acts with a 16-year-old girl.[1] According to a police release, Gardner met the minor at 1:43 a.m. and invited her to his room at the Renaissance Hotel at International Plaza; the girl accepted the invitation and, once there, he allegedly offered her money for sex.[1]

"The victim initially agreed but later told Gardner that she no longer wanted to engage and he became angry," the release stated.[1] "Gardner advised the victim that she needed to leave his hotel room. The two got involved in a verbal altercation that escalated to a physical dispute after Gardner placed his hands around the victim's neck, impeding her breathing. After the dispute, Gardner left the hotel room, and the victim called 911."[1] When officers arrived, Gardner had already left the hotel, but they located the victim at the scene.[1]

The arrest was presented by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as the "shot" paired with a "chaser" item on the arrest of a former [[cnn|CNN]] and Recount editor on child pornography charges, as part of a broader argument about the prevalence of such cases among politically connected and media figures.[1]
