# Interpublic Group

Interpublic Group (IPG) is a global advertising and media-buying holding company and one of the industry's "Big Six" firms.[1]

## Omnicom merger and FTC conditions

IPG agreed to merge with [[omnicom|Omnicom]], a transaction approved by the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] in June 2025 under conditions announced by Chairman [[andrew-ferguson|Andrew Ferguson]].[1] Together the two firms represent about one third of the global "Big Six" advertising market.[1] As part of the merger agreement, both firms committed not to agree to or maintain any practice discriminating against outlets on political or ideological grounds.[1]

## GARM membership

The FTC identified IPG as a founding member of [[global-alliance-for-responsible-media|GARM]], the cross-industry body through which the agency said collusion occurred to blacklist center-right media such as Breitbart News, Fox News, and the Daily Wire.[1] The agency argued that this documented history of collusion in media-buying services made a behavioral remedy appropriate in a context where structural remedies are the norm.[1]
