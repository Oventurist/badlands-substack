# Caspian Pipeline Consortium

The Caspian Pipeline Consortium (CPC) is an international oil transport venture operated by Russia, Kazakhstan, the United States, and several Western European nations, whose crude export infrastructure includes a hub on Russia's Black Sea coast.[1]

## Drone attack (November 2025)

On Saturday, November 29, 2025, a drone attacked the CPC crude hub on Russia's Black Sea coast.[1] The strike came amid a broader Ukrainian sea-drone campaign in the Black Sea that also hit two Gambian-flagged tankers, the Kairos and the Virat, off the Turkish coast while they were en route to the Russian port of Novorossiysk.[1] Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] characterized the tanker attacks as an "outrageous" infringement of Turkish sovereignty and said the Kremlin viewed the incidents as serious, with possible implications for ongoing diplomatic efforts.[1] Because the consortium's ownership spans Russian, Kazakh, American, and European interests, attacks on its facilities carry multinational commercial exposure beyond the Russian state.[1]
