# The Kate Awakening

The Kate Awakening is a pseudonymous host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], appearing on two weekend-adjacent programs in the network's December 2022 lineup.[1]

She co-hosts **The Liberty Den** with Badlands founder [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on Fridays at 10:30pm ET. The program closes out the broadcast week with what the network describes as a fun, funny, and casual conversation focused on positivity, frequently featuring a special guest.[1]

She also co-hosts **Raising a Nation** with Defender of the Republic on Saturdays at 9pm ET, a weekly discussion of Great Awakening themes from the perspective of parents attempting to raise what the show calls the next generation of sovereign citizens in an uncertain world.[1]

Her programming contributes the network's family- and morale-oriented content, contrasting with the research-heavy analysis blocks that dominate the weekday schedule.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]
