# The Telegraph

The Telegraph is a British national newspaper and news website. In Badlands Media coverage it appears as an example of legacy Western press behavior, particularly regarding reporting on the Middle East.

## March 2026 retraction

The March 26, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that The Telegraph removed an article that had documented alleged cooperation between Christian villages in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]. Third-party traces cited in the coverage indicate the piece was published on March 23 under the headline "Christians and Hezbollah unite against 'Epstein empire'" and was taken down roughly a day later. No public explanation was given for the removal, and the original text was no longer accessible for independent review.

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the retraction to argue that Western outlets lack "the decency or courage" to stand behind reporting that complicates the standard framing of Hezbollah as an anti-Christian terrorist organization, particularly at a moment when Christian and Muslim communities in southern Lebanon were, in his account, uniting against Israeli military operations.

## The April 2026 Trump interview on NATO

The Badlands Brief of April 2, 2026 reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said in an April 1 interview with The Telegraph that he is "strongly considering" pulling the United States out of [[nato|NATO]], with multiple major outlets independently matching the substance of the claim, per Reuters. Trump tied the threat to NATO allies' refusal to back U.S. military action and maritime operations connected to the war with [[iran|Iran]], including pressure around the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], and described NATO as a "paper tiger." The interview is the anchor of the Brief's coverage of the alliance dispute.
