# John Hostettler

John Hostettler is a former Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Indiana, known for dissenting from mainstream Republican foreign policy positions.[1]

## Criticism of U.S.-Israel policy

In "The Exploitation of Charlie Kirk," Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] named Hostettler alongside [[pat-buchanan|Pat Buchanan]] and [[ron-paul|Ron Paul]] as exceptions to the expectation that a thriving career in American media or politics requires outspoken and unwavering support for [[israel|Israel]].[1] DeLarme wrote that taking such a stance is fraught with risk, since challenging Israel and its lobby can derail a career, citing Earl Hilliard, [[cori-bush|Cori Bush]], and [[jamaal-bowman|Jamaal Bowman]] as members who lost their seats after backlash from pro-Israel groups.[1] He argued the same dynamic operates in the media space, and that it was beginning to fracture among younger portions of the American right.[1]
