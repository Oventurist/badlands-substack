# Hugh Evans

Hugh Evans is an [[fbi|FBI]] Special Agent assigned to the Bureau's Atlanta Field Office and the author of the search-warrant affidavits underpinning the federal investigation into [[fulton-county|Fulton County]]'s handling of ballots from the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Evans's affidavits, filed in federal court by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] on February 10, 2026, detail alleged deficiencies in vote counting and record-keeping including missing ballot images, inconsistent totals across recounts, chain-of-custody documentation issues, duplicate ballot images, and absentee ballots lacking the creases typically expected of ballots folded and mailed in envelopes. The affidavits explain that access to the physical ballots is required to determine whether these issues stemmed from administrative errors or intentional acts.

Evans emphasised that any deliberate interference with election records would constitute a violation of federal law even if it did not alter the outcome of Georgia's 2020 presidential race, which [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] lost by a narrow margin. Among the allegations recorded in the affidavits are discrepancies between ballot images and reported vote totals raised by a redacted witness described as a chemical engineer — claims that the source notes had previously been examined in the manual recount ordered by Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]] and reviewed by the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]].
