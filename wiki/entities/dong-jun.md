# Dong Jun

Dong Jun is the Chinese Defense Minister.[1]

## Meeting with Pete Hegseth (October 2025)

On Friday, October 31, 2025, Dong held talks with U.S. Secretary of Defense [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] at a gathering of Association of Southeast Asian Nations defense chiefs in Kuala Lumpur.[1] No readout of the discussions was provided at the time of reporting.[1]

The meeting followed [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s Thursday summit with Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in South Korea, where the two broadly agreed to reduce trade tensions sparked by American tariffs and to stabilize relations between the world's two largest economies.[1] Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the de-escalation as predicted and as hinting at joint economic projects of the kind already in the works with Russia, arguing that Trump and Putin had NATO "in a pincer" while Trump and Xi had the EU in one.[1]
