# Joycelyn Wade

Joycelyn Wade is the estranged wife of [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], the special prosecutor hired by Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] to oversee the Georgia election-interference prosecution of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The couple had been married for 26 years.[1]

## Role in the Willis–Wade controversy

In a court filing reported in January 2024, Willis accused Joycelyn Wade of "interfering" in the prosecution of Trump and of "conspiring" with Trump co-defendant [[mike-roman|Mike Roman]] in an attempt to have the charges against Roman dismissed.[1] Roman, who worked for Trump's 2020 campaign, had filed the motion alleging that Willis and Nathan Wade were romantically involved and seeking Willis's disqualification.[1] Willis argued that Joycelyn Wade and Roman were acting in concert to undermine her office in advance of the Trump trial.[1]

The divorce proceeding brought by Joycelyn Wade ran in parallel with Willis's RICO prosecution, and disclosures made in that case supplied much of the documentary material underlying the allegations against Willis.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], argued that Willis's conspiracy theory rested purely on timing and was therefore flimsy, since the pace of disclosure in the divorce case was being driven by the court rather than by Roman.[1]
