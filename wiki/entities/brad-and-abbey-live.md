# Brad & Abbey LIVE

Brad & Abbey LIVE is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Tuesdays at 7:30pm ET in the January 2023 schedule.[1]

## Format

Hosted by Brad and Abbey, the show is promoted as an honest look at the world combined with humor and original content the network says viewers will not find elsewhere.[1] It occupies the Tuesday prime-time slot ahead of [[badlands-story-hour|Badlands Story Hour]] and [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye of the Storm]].[1]

## Distribution

Live streams and replays are available on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
