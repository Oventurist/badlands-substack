# Plato

Plato was the Greek philosopher, active sometime between 428 and 347 B.C.E., whose *Republic* is presented as the structural model for the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]].[1] [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] writes that [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]] was guided by the Republic as Plato laid it out, and that the initiates of the mystery religion of the cabal have all maintained an affinity for Plato as well as Pythagoras.[1]

The Illuminati was carefully structured and divided into three main classes — coincidentally matching Plato's own three classes of ruling (mystery) class, working class and military.[1] The Order's highest class was a mystery class comprising the grades Priest, Regent, Magus and King; versions of this hierarchical system exist within various occult groups throughout history and are carried on today within groups such as the Rosicrucians and Ordo Templi Orientis.[1]

Plato also called for the complete elimination of marriage and the family, so that all women would belong to all men and all men to all women.[1] Under Weishaupt's hypothetical utopia, children born from promiscuous unions would be raised anonymously by the state, and the state would eliminate all defectives — an idea DeLarme says was carried on by later globalists such as John D. Rockefeller.[1] He adds that in the modern Communist Party these three classes still exist, with the general public failing to realize that very powerful, wealthy, multinational men living in free societies are its kings and priests.[1]
