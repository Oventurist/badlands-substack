# Mark Paoletta

Mark Paoletta is a lawyer who served as general counsel at the White House [[office-of-management-and-budget|Office of Management and Budget]] during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Role in the CFPB shutdown

In February 2025 Paoletta figured in the abrupt wind-down of activity at the [[consumer-financial-protection-bureau|Consumer Financial Protection Bureau]]. According to a CFPB employee familiar with the situation who requested anonymity, Paoletta placed supervision director [[lorelei-salas|Lorelei Salas]] and enforcement director [[eric-halperin|Eric Halperin]] on leave shortly before both resigned on 11 February 2025.[1] Reporting at the time could not establish why the two officials were placed on leave.[1] Paoletta's involvement reflected the tight institutional link between OMB and the bureau at that moment, since OMB Director [[russ-vought|Russell Vought]] was simultaneously serving as the CFPB's acting director and had issued the stop-work orders the departing officials cited.[1]
