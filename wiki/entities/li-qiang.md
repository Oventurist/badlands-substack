# Li Qiang

Li Qiang is China's premier, described in September 2023 as the country's new head of government.[1]

## G20 New Delhi summit

Li represented China at the [[g20|G20]] summit in New Delhi in place of President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], who skipped the gathering.[1] Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] likewise stayed away, with Foreign Minister [[sergei-lavrov|Sergei Lavrov]] attending on Moscow's behalf.[1] Reporting suggested that the absence of both leaders relieved Western heads of state, since sensitive agenda items could more easily reach consensus without them — among them a push by [[european-union|EU]] officials to endorse the [[african-union|African Union]]'s bid for permanent G20 membership and to present Europe as the more stable partner for African investment.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the maneuvering as part of a broader tug of war over Africa, arguing the continent is treated as the prize of the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and the [[new-world-order|New World Order]], and predicted that Africans would ultimately reclaim sovereignty from both suitors.[1]
