# Rahm Emanuel

Rahm Emanuel is a veteran Democratic Party operative and officeholder who served as White House chief of staff to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and later as U.S. ambassador to Japan under President Joe Biden.[1] In February 2025 he was hired by [[cnn|CNN]] as a global affairs and political commentator, with the network announcing that "Emanuel will bring his extensive knowledge of American politics and international affairs to CNN's on-air programming."[1]

## Political career

A Democrat Party stalwart, Emanuel served as a member of the U.S. House of Representatives from 2002 to 2008, representing Illinois' Fifth Congressional District, and chaired the House Democratic Caucus during that period.[1] He was subsequently mayor of Chicago from 2011 to 2019.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] framed the CNN hire in terms of talent-agency capture of cable news, wondering whether Emanuel would now become a media client at WME, the talent agency run by his brother [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]], and observing that "most cable news TV characters are." Paul suggested someone should count how many on-air personalities at Fox News are Ari Emanuel's clients.[1]

## Family ties in Badlands commentary (2025)

In March 2025 commentary on [[endeavor|Endeavor]] and the [[2028-los-angeles-olympics|2028 Los Angeles Olympics]], Badlands contributor Chris Paul noted that Endeavor is run by Emanuel's brother [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]], and described Rahm as Barack Obama's former Chief of Staff and Ambassador to Japan under what he called the fake and illegitimate Biden administration.[2] The commentary also referenced their brother [[ezekiel-emanuel|Ezekiel Emanuel]] and the family's connection to the [[irgun|Irgun]].[2]
