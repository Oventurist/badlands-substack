# Defending Democracy Together

Defending Democracy Together is a conservative, anti-[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] political group founded in 2019 by [[william-kristol|Bill Kristol]], Mona Charen, Linda Chavez, Sarah Longwell and Andy Zwick.[1] Its principal initiative is [[republicans-for-the-rule-of-law|Republicans for the Rule of Law]], and [[olivia-troye|Olivia Troye]] became an adviser to the organisation after leaving the Trump White House in 2020.[1]

## Profile

Erik Carlson's December 27, 2023 article "The Sabotage of Trump's Administration" cites the group as part of the network of establishment Republican organisations that absorbed former Trump officials who turned against the president.[1] Carlson describes Troye's association with it as one of several markers — alongside her Bush-era appointments and her self-description as a "[[john-mccain|John McCain]] Republican" — of what he characterises as a [[rino|RINO]]/[[neoconservatives|Neocon]] political identity.[1] In his framing, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] treats such groups as more effective messengers against Trump than Democrats, because their criticism appears to come from within his own coalition.[1]
