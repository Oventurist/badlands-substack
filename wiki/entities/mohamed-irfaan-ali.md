# Mohamed Irfaan Ali

Dr. Mohamed Irfaan Ali is the President of the Co-operative Republic of [[guyana|Guyana]].[1]

## Relations with Russia

On August 6, 2025, Ali received the letters of credence of [[andrey-pritsepov|Andrey A. Pritsepov]], Ambassador of [[russia|Russia]] to Guyana.[1] During the conversation, Ali highlighted the profound respect that the Guyanese people hold for the Russian people and for President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] personally, stressing that bilateral relations rest not only on a rich history but also on shared values.[1] He called for joint efforts to enrich cooperation in higher education, agriculture, energy and air services with concrete results, and expressed hope for the implementation of the outlined plans for Russian–Guyanese cooperation.[1]

The meeting took place while Guyana was serving as a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council, a status the Russian side cited in urging closer coordination within the United Nations system.[1]
