# Arizona State Senate

The Arizona State Senate is the upper chamber of the Arizona Legislature. In Badlands Media's election-integrity coverage it is treated as the single most consequential state legislative body in the post-2020 fraud narrative, because it was, in contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s account, the only state legislature in the country to take concrete action on alleged fraud in the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## The 2020 audit

After winning a legal battle over access to tabulation machines and roughly 2.1 million ballots from [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s 2020 general election, the Republican-led Senate hired four out-of-state firms — Wake Technology Services, [[cyber-ninjas|Cyber Ninjas]], CyFIR and Digital Discovery — to carry out a forensic audit and full hand recount.[1] The resulting [[maricopa-county-forensic-audit|Maricopa County forensic audit]] draft report identified 49,718 questionable votes and asked the state attorney general to investigate.[1]

## 2022 midterms

Following the [[2022-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], the Arizona legislature again pressed Maricopa County, demanding evidence relating to Election Day problems and requiring a response by the Friday before the scheduled date of certification.[1] Lange framed these demands, together with delays in county-level certification and the involvement of the [[arizona-attorney-generals-office|Arizona Attorney General's office]] Election Integrity Unit, as converging pressure that would ultimately force the fraud question into a courtroom.[1]
