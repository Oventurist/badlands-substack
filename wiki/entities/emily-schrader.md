# Emily Schrader

Emily Schrader is an Israeli commentator and activist active on social media in English, Hebrew, and Persian.[1] On December 30, 2025, amid protests spreading across [[iran|Iran]] over inflation and the collapse of the rial, she publicly called on her government to intervene on the protesters' behalf.[1]

"As an Israeli, I'm officially calling on my government, led by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]], to immediately authorize practical, technical, logistical and even kinetic support for the Iranian protesters who are currently risking their lives to take to the streets of Iran," she wrote in a post that drew roughly 147,000 views, 857 replies and 12,100 likes.[1]

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] cited the post as explaining "why they've been asking Trump about whether he would be happy to bomb, bomb Iran," adding the sardonic aside, "She is wearing an Iranian pin, this time it is serious!"[1]
