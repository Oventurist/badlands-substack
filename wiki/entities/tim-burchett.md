# Tim Burchett

Tim Burchett is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives cited in Badlands commentary as an example of GOP reluctance to confront Israeli influence in [[congress|Congress]].[1]

## Daily Herold appearance

In May 2026, Burchett appeared on the Daily Herold and told host Jon that he considered claims of Israel exercising disproportionate influence over Congress to be exaggerated — a position he maintained even after the host raised concerns about the [[lankford-rosen-antisemitism-bill|Lankford–Rosen antisemitism bill]] and its implications for the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the exchange to argue that "super-based-and-redpilled Republicans" would not intervene against the legislation.[1]

## UFO cover-up allegations (January 2023)

In a January 2023 NewsNation segment reported by Newsweek, Burchett alleged a "huge" UFO cover-up by the U.S. government, saying "we've been covering this up since the 40s" and that he did not trust the government, which he described as having "an arrogance about it."[2] He called on officials to "release everything," said the American public could handle the truth, and stated that at some point "we have obtained some materials that are not of this world that are being studied by different members of industry I've been told."[2] He closed by urging the government to "stop with all the shenanigans."[2]

[[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] described the remarks as a bombshell but argued that the mainstream UFO disclosure of the preceding five years was a limited hangout — a watered-down release designed to distract the public and concede only a fraction of the truth.[2] Sather claimed evidence showed the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] had possessed electrogravitic propulsion systems and working levitation craft for decades, and that admitting the full truth would cost the "Powers that Be" money and control, while maintaining that "humanity does deserve the truth."[2]

## Alleged McCarthy elbowing incident (November 2023)

On Tuesday, November 14, 2023, former Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] was accused of elbowing Burchett in the kidneys in the Capitol, allegedly in retaliation for Burchett's vote to remove McCarthy as Speaker.[3] McCarthy claimed the contact was unintentional and the product of a crowded corridor.[3] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] paired the allegation with the same-day confrontation between Senator [[markwayne-mullin|Markwayne Mullin]] and [[teamsters|Teamsters]] president [[sean-obrien|Sean O'Brien]], asking rhetorically whether any of the legislators involved would be investigated for violence in the Capitol.[3]
