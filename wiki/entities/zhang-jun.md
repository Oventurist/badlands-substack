# Zhang Jun

Zhang Jun is a Chinese diplomat who served as China's permanent representative to the United Nations.[1]

## Response to the US veto on Gaza (February 2024)

Following a United States veto of a [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] draft resolution that would have demanded an immediate humanitarian cease-fire in Gaza, Zhang said in February 2024 that objecting to a cease-fire was "nothing different from giving the green light to the continued slaughter," and expressed China's "strong disappointment at and dissatisfaction with the US veto."[1]

The draft resolution, tabled by Algeria on behalf of the Arab states, won 13 votes in favor among the Security Council's 15 members, with Britain abstaining and the United States casting the lone veto.[1] The text demanded an immediate cease-fire in Gaza, the immediate release of all hostages, guaranteed access to humanitarian supplies, and the rejection of forced displacement — measures Zhang described in his explanation of vote as based on "the minimum requirements of humanity" and therefore deserving the support of all Council members.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the episode as evidence of accumulating narrative damage to the establishment — represented, in his framing, by the Biden administration and the State Department — noting that "even China and Russia are piling on" over Gaza.[1]
