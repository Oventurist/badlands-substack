# House of Orange

The **House of Orange** is the Dutch princely dynasty that later became the Dutch royal family. In Erik Carlson's Badlands Media account of the history of global financial control, the House of Orange is described as having been taken over by the [[venetian-bankers|Venetian Bankers]] around the time those bankers relocated from Venice to Holland and formed the [[dutch-east-india-company|Dutch East India Company]] in 1602.[1]

Carlson writes that William of Orange and the House of Orange became powerful through their counseling and governing under the rule of the "incestuous" [[habsburg-dynasty|Habsburg]] family that ruled half of Europe for centuries.[1]

## Role in the 1688 settlement

In the article's telling of 1688, the Dutch East India Company created the [[city-of-london|City of London]] by making a deal with the British royal family to pay off its debt, in exchange for which the monarchy abdicated real power to the newly formed City.[1] Britain's King James II gave up his power to his daughter Mary II, who married William of Orange; together they jointly ruled Britain while the British parliamentary system was born and the Crown was left without genuine authority.[1]

## Symbolism of the color orange

Carlson draws attention to the recurrence of the color orange in contemporary events, noting that the Orange Revolution was a color revolution that began in Ukraine roughly twenty years before the article's writing, that orange represents [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is popularly known as the "Orange Man."[1] These associations are offered as suggestive symbolism rather than documented linkage.
