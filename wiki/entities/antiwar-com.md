# Antiwar.com

**Antiwar.com** is a news and commentary site focused on US foreign policy and military intervention, publishing a news wire under the banner news.antiwar.com.[1]

The outlet was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] for its coverage of reporting by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] that the United States withheld from Russia part of the intelligence it held about the plot behind the [[crocus-city-hall-attack|Crocus City Hall attack]], owing to the adversarial relationship between Washington and Moscow and concern for protecting sources and methods.[1]
