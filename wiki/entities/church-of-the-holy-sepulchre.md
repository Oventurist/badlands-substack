# Church of the Holy Sepulchre

The Church of the Holy Sepulchre is a Christian church complex in the Old City of Jerusalem, traditionally recognized as the site of both the crucifixion and the burial of Jesus. It is one of Christianity's holiest sites and, according to the Badlands Brief edition ["Golgotha & The Metaverse"](../concepts/badlands-brief-52b.md), typically draws up to four million visitors annually.

## Layout and tradition

As described in [[badlands-brief-52b|the March 2026 Brief]], the church encloses both the traditional site of the crucifixion and the empty tomb within a single large complex, the two locations separated by only a few dozen meters. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] anchored the tradition in John 19:41: "At the place where Jesus was crucified, there was a garden, and in the garden a new tomb, in which no one had ever been laid."

## 2026 closure

In March 2026, church authorities closed the site to the public, citing security concerns ahead of Easter. The closure was described as rare and precautionary, occurring amid the escalating [[iran|Iran]]–[[israel|Israel]] war and heightened security risks in Jerusalem. The Brief emphasized that the church "is almost never closed," and that the shutdown came only weeks before Easter.

Commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the previous closure occurred in 2018, when the church shut its doors in protest after the Jerusalem municipal government imposed taxes it had not previously faced. He cited that history as evidence of longstanding "animus between the Jerusalem (Israeli) government and the church," and read the 2026 closure as one front in what he described as a spiritual dimension of the broader conflict.
