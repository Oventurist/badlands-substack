# Brian Schwalb

Brian Schwalb is the Attorney General of Washington, D.C.[1] In September 2025 he filed a lawsuit on behalf of District officials against the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration over the deployment of thousands of [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops into the city without the consent of local leaders.[1]

## Lawsuit over the National Guard deployment

The suit, filed on a Thursday in early September 2025, accused the president of violating the Constitution and federal law.[1] It claimed that the troops — many drawn from out of state — had been deputized by the U.S. Marshals office and were patrolling neighborhoods, conducting searches and making arrests, despite federal laws that generally bar the military from acting as local police.[1]

The complaint further argued that the deployment undermined the city's autonomy, eroded trust between residents and law enforcement, and damaged the local economy by discouraging tourism and hurting businesses.[1]

Schwalb said in a statement that "Deploying the National Guard to engage in law enforcement is not only unnecessary and unwanted, but it is also dangerous and harmful to the District and its residents," adding, "It's DC today but could be any other city tomorrow. We've filed this action to put an end to this illegal federal overreach."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] described the filing as the "greatest lawsuit ever," arguing it should force a resolution of the District's status — either ending the federal city or confirming that it is federal.[1]
