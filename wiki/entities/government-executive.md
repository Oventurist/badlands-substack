# Government Executive

**Government Executive** is a trade publication covering the United States federal bureaucracy. In "The Schedule F Imperative - Part 2" (March 13, 2023), Badlands Media contributor [[jb-white|J.B. White]] cited the outlet's own "About" page description of itself as "government's business news daily and the premier digital destination for senior leaders in the federal government's departments and agencies," whose readers are "the high-ranking civilian and military officials who are responsible for defending the nation and carrying out the laws that define the government's role in our economy and society."[1]

## Role in White's argument

White presented Government Executive as an example of the publications favored by what he termed the [[deep-state|Deep State]], arguing that a "sovereign individual" running his own private intelligence service should periodically read the enemy's own trade press in order to learn what genuinely alarms it.[1] He reported that entering the search term "[[schedule-f|Schedule F]]" into the site's search field returned a wide range of articles, which he read as evidence that Schedule F is "a subject of existential interest to our overlords hovering around the federal government."[1] By contrast, White said an identical search on the website of the libertarian magazine Reason returned no results, a discrepancy he flagged with the aside "My, my, my."[1]

## Headlines cited

White reproduced a chronological list of Government Executive headlines about Schedule F, which together trace the policy's arc from implementation to revocation to attempted revival:[1]

- November 23, 2020 — "OMB Reportedly Designates 88% of Its Employees for Schedule F," reporting a former official's view that the decision would make it harder, not easier, for presidents of both parties to implement policy agendas.[1]
- December 14, 2020 — "As White House Steps Up Schedule F Implementation, 'Lawmakers Don't Get It'," describing moves by the [[office-of-personnel-management|Office of Personnel Management]] that stoked fears the administration was accelerating plans to politicize federal jobs as the window for Congress to block the move closed.[1]
- January 22, 2021 — "Biden Signs Executive Order Killing Schedule F, Restoring Collective Bargaining Rights," which also noted the naming of Federal Labor Relations Authority member Ernest DuBester as the agency's chairman.[1]
- July 27, 2022 — "Trump Has Endorsed a Plan to Purge the Civil Service of 'Rogue Bureaucrats'," reporting that former Trump staffers said they had identified 50,000 federal employees who would be ousted under a new iteration of Schedule F in a future Republican administration.[1]
- August 2, 2022 — "Senate Dems Have Joined the Push to Block Schedule F."[1]
- November 2, 2022 — "Sen. Kaine is 'Optimistic' an Anti-Schedule F Bill Will Get a Floor Vote This Year," in which [[tim-kaine|Tim Kaine]] called Republican arguments that the effort targeted poor performers "not honest."[1]
- December 7, 2022 — "Lawmakers Left Anti-Schedule F Legislation Out of the Compromise Defense Policy Bill," noting grim prospects after its exclusion from must-pass legislation.[1]
- December 28, 2022 — "Year of the Living Dead: How Schedule F Continued to Threaten to Upend the Civil Service in 2022."[1]
- February 13, 2023 — "Lawmakers Are Doubling Down on the Effort to Prevent the Next Schedule F."[1]
- February 17, 2023 — "A Broken Civil Service System Could Break Government," a podcast episode featuring Jeffrey Neal on the pace of change in modernizing the federal workforce.[1]
