# Committee to Investigate Russia

The Committee to Investigate Russia was an advocacy effort spearheaded by film director [[rob-reiner|Rob Reiner]] following the 2016 election of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] characterised the committee as part of a "treasonous attempt to overthrow the government" after Trump's victory.[1]

The committee produced promotional material featuring former Director of National Intelligence [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] and former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], including a short film in which Reiner appeared alongside both men.[1] Sather circulated a 22-minute clip of that material when discussing Reiner following his death in December 2025.[1]
