# Mikhail Brodsky

Mikhail Brodsky is the Israeli Ambassador to Kiev.[1] He drew attention in June 2025 when he publicly confirmed that [[israel|Israel]] had transferred US-made [[patriot-missile-system|Patriot]] air defense systems to [[ukraine|Ukraine]], contradicting Israel's earlier position that it was providing only humanitarian assistance.[1]

## Disclosure on Patriot transfers

In an interview with Ukrainian journalist Marichka Dovbenko, Brodsky said: "The Patriot systems we once received from the United States are now in Ukraine."[1] He explained that these were Israeli systems deployed in the early 1990s and that Israel had agreed to transfer them, adding that the matter "hasn't been widely discussed" but that suggestions Israel had not helped Ukraine militarily were "simply not true."[1] Asked about reports that Israel had been routing military equipment to Ukraine through third countries, Brodsky described the subject as "a sensitive issue" that should not be discussed publicly.[1]

Brodsky's remarks were read against Israel's longstanding balancing act between Kiev and Moscow, given Russia's naval facility and airbase in neighboring Syria.[1] Badlands commentary from [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the ambassador's confirmation as evidence of an operative Israel–Ukraine military alignment.[1]
