# Brightcore

Brightcore is a supplement company that became a sponsor of the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack newsletter in September 2024.[1] Its Kimchi One fermented product was promoted to readers of the Badlands News Brief with a 25% discount offered under the promotional code "Badlands."[1] Sponsorships of this kind form part of the revenue model that allows Badlands Media to distribute its written content free of charge while soliciting paid newsletter subscriptions.[1]

## Sponsorship of the Badlands Substack (2024)

Brightcore was introduced in the August 23, 2024 Badlands News Brief as a new sponsor of the Badlands Substack, promoting Kimchi One, a fermented product, with an exclusive offer for readers.[2]
