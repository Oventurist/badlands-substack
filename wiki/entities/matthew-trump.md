# Matthew Trump

Matthew Trump is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] whose commentary appears in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], the outlet's daily digest of takes on narratives in the information war.

## Commentary

In the March 20, 2026 Brief, [[badlands-brief-52b|"Golgotha & The Metaverse"]], Matthew Trump wrote on [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s reversal of its decision to discontinue [[horizon-worlds|Horizon Worlds]] in virtual reality. He described [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s metaverse bet as perhaps the most "mock-worthy" venture of the social media era and speculated that the reprieve may have been granted out of spite toward critics who had greeted the shutdown announcement with joy.

His commentary also touched on Zuckerberg's political trajectory, noting that few Trump supporters have any affection for a figure who, in his account, "openly helped finance the theft of the 2020 election in swing states" before accommodating himself to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after the subsequent election. He predicted the virtual-world concept would eventually return in a more palatable form and warned that Zuckerberg should not be dismissed in the artificial-intelligence race, given "the Himalaya Range of personal data at his disposal — a motherlode we have given him so freely over the years."
