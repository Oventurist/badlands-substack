# Matthew Trump

Matthew Trump is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] whose commentary appears in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1]

## Commentary on Meta and the metaverse

In the March 20, 2026 Brief, Matthew Trump wrote on [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s reversal of its plan to shut down [[horizon-worlds|Horizon Worlds]] in VR, asking whether anything in the social media era was more mock-worthy than [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s attempt to stake his company's future on a virtual reality avatar world that almost no one wanted to explore.[1] He suspected the reprieve was granted partly out of spite for critics who celebrated the shutdown, and noted that few [[donald-trump|Trump]] supporters have affection for Zuckerberg, who he said openly helped finance the theft of the 2020 election in swing states before bending the knee to Trump after the last election.[1] He predicted the virtual world concept will return in a less repulsive form and be remembered as a pioneering experiment that failed because it was ahead of its time, while warning that Zuckerberg's real advantage in the AI race is the enormous store of personal data users have given him freely over the years.[1]
