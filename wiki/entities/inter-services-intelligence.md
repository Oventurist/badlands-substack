# Inter-Services Intelligence

The Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI) is [[pakistan]]'s principal intelligence agency, described in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as "Pakistan's CIA." The article states that "it's an open secret that they are kept on a tight leash by the US, and that they run Pakistan."

## Role in the removal of Imran Khan

[[imran-khan]] was at first perceived as having ties to the military establishment including the ISI, particularly during his ascent to political prominence, and may initially have benefited from its support. His relationship with the agency deteriorated significantly after his ouster by parliamentary no-confidence vote in April 2022, which he attributed to military and ISI interference; he openly accused then-ISI chief nadeem anjum and former army chief General qamar javed bajwa of orchestrating his removal. With Khan gone, the article says, "[[cia|CIA]]/MIC/deep state-friendly assets are once again running Pakistan and the ISI."

## Historical function

Referencing the earlier Badlands piece "Operation Cyclone: The Seeds of Terror," the article notes that in the 1980s, when the Reagan government was arming and funding the [[al-qaeda]] precursors fighting the Soviets, one function of the CIA-controlled ISI was to serve as a major clearinghouse for U.S. weapons, equipment and funds sent out to various CIA and [[department-of-defense|DoD]] conflict zones. That function, the article asserts, continues today.
