# Michael Rapaport

Michael Rapaport is an American actor and commentator known for years of vocal criticism of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## 2023 video on voting for Trump

In late 2023, Rapaport released a video suggesting that, as much as he despised Trump, he might have to vote for him in the 2024 election, on the grounds that the Biden administration was performing so poorly.[1]

Badlands Media reads the statement not as a genuine change of heart but as narrative construction: by publicly conceding that one may vote for a candidate one hates if that candidate would run the country better, Rapaport — and, the author predicts, other celebrity Trump critics — supplies permission for reluctant voters to back Trump guilt-free.[1] The article speculates that Rapaport either cut a deal or had been playing a role all along, and frames the episode as evidence that "propaganda works both ways" and that the [[white-hats|White Hats]] are counter-punching.[1]
