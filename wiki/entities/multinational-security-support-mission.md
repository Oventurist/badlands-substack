# Multinational Security Support Mission

The Multinational Security Support (MSS) mission is an internationally assembled security deployment intended to support the Haitian National Police and restore order in [[haiti|Haiti]].[1]

## Funding and deployment in March 2024

During the week of March 13, 2024, as armed gangs consolidated control over much of Port-au-Prince during the [[2024-haiti-gang-crisis|2024 Haiti gang crisis]], the Department of Defense announced that it had doubled its funding for the MSS mission.[1] In a statement issued through [[us-southern-command|US Southern Command]], the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] said it was "working with Haitian, Kenyan, and other partners to expedite its deployment to support the Haitian National Police and to restore security in Haiti."[1] [[kenya|Kenya]] was identified as the principal partner nation contributing to the mission.[1]

The funding increase came in the same week that the [[state-department|State Department]] requested the deployment of the Marine Fleet Anti-terrorism Security Team to reinforce the US embassy in Port-au-Prince, and shortly after the resignation of Haitian Prime Minister [[ariel-henry|Ariel Henry]].[1]
