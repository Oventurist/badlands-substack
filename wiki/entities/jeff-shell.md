# Jeff Shell

Jeff Shell is an American media executive who served as chief executive officer of [[nbcuniversal|NBCUniversal]] until his abrupt departure in April 2023.[1]

## Departure from NBCUniversal

On Sunday, April 23, 2023, NBCUniversal announced that Shell had left his role as chief executive effective immediately because of an "inappropriate relationship with a woman in the company."[1] The announcement was one of three senior media exits compressed into roughly forty-eight hours: it was followed on Monday, April 24 by [[fox-news|Fox News]]'s announcement that prime-time host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] was leaving the network immediately, and, an hour after that, by [[cnn|CNN]]'s firing of anchor [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] over sexist on-air comments and reported mistreatment of female colleagues.[1]

CNBC's coverage of the Lemon firing grouped Shell's exit with the other two as part of a single extraordinary news cycle in corporate media.[1] Writers at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the clustering as evidence against coincidence and as an illustration of coordinated churn within what they call the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]].[1]
