# Brown University

Brown University is an Ivy League institution in Providence, Rhode Island. It drew commentary in the July 12, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] following the publication of survey data from its student newspaper, the Brown Daily Herald, on student sexual identification.[1]

## 2023 student survey on sexual identity

The Herald's Spring 2023 poll found that 38% of Brown students do not identify as straight — over five times the national rate reported by Gallup.[1] Between 2010 and 2023, the share of students at Brown identifying as LGBTQ+ nearly tripled, a trend the Washington Examiner said "provides further evidence that the increase in LGBT identification is driven by social pressures."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the Brown figures as a warning signal about American culture rather than a demographic curiosity.[1] He argued that sexuality is "one part nature and ten parts nurture," and that the internal structures of mind and identity are built from the womb through roughly age five or six.[1] In his framing, the ability of humans to choose an identity at odds with their nature demonstrates the reality of free will, and also demonstrates that a sick or toxic culture produces distortions.[1]

Deschamps cited the claim that nearly all LGBTQ+-identifying people have at least one Adverse Childhood Experience, asking why a purely natural feature of humanity would require a disturbed childhood to manifest.[1] He held that entertaining mutually exclusive propositions exacts a cost he described as the loss of the soul — the coherent, stable, growing part of a person — and attributed the spike in LGBTQ+ identification at universities to a cultural environment of social media, pornography, and gender-bending trends.[1] At the same time he urged that those affected not be discarded, arguing they should be treated as Jesus treated people, with the door opened to all willing to walk through it.[1] He concluded that Americans have ceded control of their culture to "psychopaths and criminals" and must learn to retake it.[1]
