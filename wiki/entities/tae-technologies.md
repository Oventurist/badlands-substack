# TAE Technologies

TAE Technologies is a US fusion energy company developing beam-driven fusion technology, led by chief executive Dr. Michl Binderbauer.[1]

## Merger with Trump Media & Technology Group

TAE agreed to a $6 billion all-stock merger with [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] (TMTG), the parent company of Truth Social, with completion expected in mid-2026 pending regulatory approvals.[1] Upon closing, TMTG chief executive [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] and Binderbauer are to serve as co-CEOs of the combined entity.[1]

## Commercial fusion power plant

TMTG and TAE launched a site selection process for a pioneering 50 MWe fusion power plant, with construction planned to begin in 2026 following the closure of the merger.[1] The project aims to deploy TAE's advanced beam-driven fusion technology, which the companies promise will deliver safe, carbon-free electricity and industrial heat without risks of meltdown, long-lived radioactive waste or weapons proliferation, positioning the United States to meet surging power demand, lower energy costs and lead in artificial-intelligence development.[1]

Candidate sites must span at least 20 acres and offer grid access, proximity to metropolitan areas and talent pools, supportive local governments and robust security.[1] Future expansions envision larger reactors in the 350–500 MWe range.[1] Nunes described the effort as steps toward an "American energy renaissance," while Binderbauer highlighted fusion's timely momentum amid growing electricity needs.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the venture within his "Weapons of the Future" series, arguing that nuclear fusion is the key to a promised Age of Abundance and that the original Manhattan Project was designed to poison the word "nuclear" in the global mindscape — making [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s repeated invocation of a "New Manhattan Project" an inversion of the original.[1]

## Merger terms and December 2025 announcement

The merger was formally announced on Thursday, December 18, 2025 as an all-stock transaction valued at more than $6 billion, with shareholders of each company expected to own roughly 50 percent of the combined entity on a fully diluted basis.[2] Trump Media agreed to provide up to $200 million in cash at signing, with an additional $100 million available upon the initial filing of merger documents with the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]].[2] The combined entity will include Trump Media's platforms, such as [[truth-social|Truth Social]], alongside TAE's businesses, including TAE Power Solutions and TAE Life Sciences.[2] Closing is expected in mid-2026, pending customary shareholder and regulatory approvals.[2] Further detail is collected at [[trump-media-tae-technologies-fusion-merger|Trump Media–TAE Technologies fusion merger]].[2]

Under the announced buildout plan, the companies intend during 2026 to select a site and begin construction on the world's first utility-scale fusion power plant with an initial output of 50 megawatts, subject to regulatory approvals, and to develop larger facilities of 350–500 megawatts in subsequent phases.[2]
