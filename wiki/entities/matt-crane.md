# Matt Crane

Matt Crane is a Colorado election-administration figure described in Badlands coverage as the "handler" of the state's county clerks.[1] After Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported in real time that election consultant [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] was present at a Colorado Clerks meeting, Crane delivered a reprimand to the clerks, framed around the claim that "we're not safe if people know where we are."[1]

Ashe countered that she had disclosed only Macias's attendance and never the meeting's location, arguing that the reprimand was driven by embarrassment rather than any actual security concern.[1] Sources familiar with the episode told her that, as a consequence, Colorado clerks are no longer allowed to post to social media during their meetings on safety grounds — a restriction she characterized as an inversion of democratic transparency.[1]

## Testimony in Coomer v. Lindell (June 2025)

Crane testified in the Denver defamation trial [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]] in June 2025, taking the stand immediately after plaintiff [[eric-coomer|Eric Coomer]] was cross-examined.[2] Evidence shown to the jury included a photograph from a barbecue at Coomer's house in which Crane appeared alongside state and local election officials, among them Hillary Rudy, who works for Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]], as well as a promotional poster for the election vendor Global Mobile featuring Crane's face.[2]

Sources in the courtroom told [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] that Crane, a good friend of Coomer, admitted he was invested in the outcome of the case, struggled with questions about his wife's relationship with Coomer as colleagues at [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]], and alleged that the risk-limiting audit was a security measure ensuring voters could trust the 2020 elections — while not knowing that only four states used risk-limiting audits in 2020, a fact he conceded on the stand.[2] "He looked like a fool in front of the jury," one witness to the cross-examination said.[2]
