# William Tiller

**William Tiller** is cited in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for his work in psychoenergetics.[1]

## Role in the article

The essay states that Tiller's research shows focused intention does not merely shift a person's mood but alters the physical properties of the surrounding environment, from the structure of water to electromagnetic fields.[1] This claim anchors the article's section on [[embodiment|embodiment]], which argues that one of the greatest mistakes in awakening is remaining in the intellectual realm — understanding concepts without embodying them — and that true transformation occurs only when mind and body align.[1]

Building on Tiller, the article compares the human energy field to a tuning fork: coherent, it resonates with clarity; scattered, it picks up static from the surrounding world.[1] The body is thus framed as "the interface between consciousness and physical reality," so that reclaiming one's energy is presented not only as protection from external manipulation but as an active restructuring of the environment.[1]
