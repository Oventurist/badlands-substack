# Jonathan Pollard

Jonathan Pollard is a convicted Israeli spy, described in Badlands commentary as an American traitor, who has spoken publicly about episodes in the history of the [[israeli-nuclear-program|Israeli nuclear program]].[1]

## Account of the 1973 nuclear threat

According to Pollard, during the [[yom-kippur-war|Yom Kippur War]] of 1973 President [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]] cut off shipments of weapons and ammunition to [[israel|Israel]] in an attempt to de-escalate and free the United States of liability or involvement.[1] Pollard says Israel responded by loading a nuclear warhead onto a plane, parking that plane on a runway, and telling the Nixon administration to look at the runway with its satellites.[1] He claims Israel then threatened to use the weapon against the Arabs if conventional shipments were not immediately resumed, and that the shipments resumed the very next day.[1]

## Espionage conviction and commutation

Pollard worked in U.S. Naval Intelligence and was arrested in 1985 after being caught passing stolen classified material to the government of [[israel|Israel]], including a ten-volume manual detailing how the National Security Agency gathers signals intelligence.[2] He pled guilty and confessed to his treason, and Israel formally acknowledged its role in the scheme and apologized to the United States.[2] Pollard spent nearly 30 years in prison until President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] commuted his sentence at the end of his first term in 2020, after which Pollard boarded [[sheldon-adelson|Sheldon Adelson]]'s private jet and flew to Israel.[2] On returning to Israel he endorsed [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], later [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s National Security Minister, who has advocated forcibly relocating the entire population of Gaza to Ireland.[2]

## 2025 embassy meeting with Mike Huckabee

In July 2025, Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]], Trump's envoy to Israel, met Pollard at the United States Embassy in Jerusalem, a meeting Pollard himself confirmed to *The New York Times*.[2] The highly unusual meeting caught some U.S. officials by surprise and appeared to be a sharp break with years of precedent for American diplomats; the [[new-york-times|Times]] learned of it from three U.S. officials speaking on condition of anonymity.[2] Pollard said it was the first time a U.S. official had hosted him at an American government office since his release a decade earlier, calling it "a friendly meeting."[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Huckabee had traveled to Israel in 2011 to quarterback the lobbying effort for Pollard's release, and asked why Huckabee advocated so strongly for a confessed traitor and whether Huckabee is himself compromised by the [[israel-lobby|Israel lobby]].[2]

## Assessment of Trump (2025 commentary)

The May 2025 Badlands essay "On Infiltration and Israel" cited Pollard, described as an Israeli spy, as a witness against the claim that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is an Israeli puppet.[3] According to the author, Pollard has said Trump is anything but a friend to Israel, because everything Trump does that appears to benefit Israel comes at a cost the Israeli state sorely wishes not to pay.[3]

## Lekem handling and the 1986 fallout

Pollard was a United States Navy intelligence employee at the Naval Antiterrorist Alert Center in Washington, D.C., and was paid by Israel for the delivery of large amounts of classified documents to [[lekem|Lekem]], the Israeli Bureau of Scientific Relations, then run by [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]].[4] Lekem was disbanded in 1986 following his arrest, and he has been called "the most damaging spy" to have worked on behalf of Israel.[4] [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], who went so far as attempting to extort Bill Clinton in the 1990s for Pollard's release, finally succeeded at the close of the Obama era, and Pollard returned to Israel after 35 years in 2020.[4]

The affair took Washington and Tel Aviv by surprise: [[wolf-blitzer|Wolf Blitzer]], then Washington correspondent for the *Jerusalem Post*, had written in a book published just weeks before the arrest that there was no real fear in the U.S. government that American Jews were leaking information to [[mossad|Mossad]].[4] Pollard's espionage on U.S. nuclear secrets ran concurrent in 1984 with Eitan's tasking of [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] to place the bugged [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] in the Los Alamos national laboratories.[4]
