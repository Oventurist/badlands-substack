# Beyahad

Beyahad — Hebrew for "Together" — is the Israeli political party announced in April 2026 by former prime ministers [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]] and [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]], who said they would run together in Israel's upcoming national election in a bid to unseat Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] Bennett said the new party would be formed under his leadership.[1]

## Formation

Lapid described Bennett as "a right-wing politician, but an honest one," and said the two had built trust between them.[1] Earlier the same Sunday, Lapid said the alliance would unify what he called the "Repair Bloc."[1] Bennett said that if elected he would establish a national commission of inquiry into the failures preceding the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack — an inquiry the current government has rejected.[1]

Bennett and Lapid previously led a coalition government formed in June 2021, which collapsed in 2022, after which Netanyahu returned to power.[1] Opinion polls cited in the reporting suggest Bennett is the opposition figure best positioned to defeat Netanyahu in the election, which must be held no later than the end of October.[1]

## Badlands analysis

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry wrote that the merger was exactly what he had anticipated, recalling that Lapid and Bennett had ousted Netanyahu in 2021 through a co-premiership arrangement in which Bennett served the first twelve months, and that during Lapid's stint in 2022 Netanyahu orchestrated the collapse of their support in the [[knesset|Knesset]] while negotiating an alliance with the most radical zealots in Israeli politics — figures who now sit in Netanyahu's Security Cabinet.[1]

What is different this time, he argued, is that Bennett and Lapid have actually merged their parties rather than merely coalescing, effectively running on a single ticket and likely to implement some form of co-premiership if they win in October.[1] He predicted the resulting electoral pressure would push Netanyahu deeper into war, since the prime minister's only route to retaining a public mandate would be either expanding [[israel|Israel]]'s borders or achieving a strategic defeat of [[iran|Iran]] — all while President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] prepares to negotiate peace with Iran and exit the conflict.[1]
