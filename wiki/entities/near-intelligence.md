# Near Intelligence

Near Intelligence is an international location-data broker with ties to the defense industry that has been described as "troubled" and roiled by allegations of mismanagement and fraud.[1] The company aggregates precise mobile-device location signals and resells derived analytics, a business model that permits reconstruction of where individual devices travel and where they habitually rest overnight.[1]

## Epstein island disclosure

In a story published in April 2024, [[wired|Wired]] reported that it had discovered maps generated from Near Intelligence data documenting nearly 200 mobile devices that visited [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s island, [[little-saint-james|Little Saint James]], in the years prior to his death.[1] The data revealed with high precision the residences of many guests of the United States Virgin Islands property, effectively de-anonymising visitors by pairing on-island pings with home and office locations.[1]

The disclosure became a case study in the wider privacy theme running through the same [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] brief — that commercially traded behavioural data functions as a de facto surveillance system, and that the individuals surveilled retain no ownership of the record.[1]
