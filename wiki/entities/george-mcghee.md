# George McGhee

George McGhee was an American diplomat who served as U.S. ambassador to West Germany and was a participant in the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites McGhee for one of the most concrete admissions of Bilderberg influence on European integration: McGhee acknowledged that "the Treaty of Rome, which brought the European Common Market into being, was nurtured at Bilderberg meetings."[1] DeLarme presents the statement in the context of his claim that the medieval [[knights-templar|Knights Templar]] aimed at a unified Europe under centralized control, an aim he says appears well on its way to realisation through groups like the Bilderbergers and the eventual creation of the [[european-union|European Union]].[1]
