# Abelardo de la Espriella

Abelardo de la Espriella is a Colombian politician reported to have won the country's June 21, 2026 presidential election, according to the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief.

Incumbent President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] challenged the outcome, reportedly claiming the real winner was [[ivan-cepeda|Iván Cepeda]] and alleging the result was manipulated using technology from California. The [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] coalition — the United States and twelve regional partners — issued a July 10, 2026 joint statement urging Colombian officials to respect the certified results and ensure a peaceful, orderly, transparent transition, and rejected any effort to delegitimize the mandate or obstruct Colombia's institutional transition. See [[2026-colombian-presidential-election|2026 Colombian presidential election]].
