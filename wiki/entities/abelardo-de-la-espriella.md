# Abelardo de la Espriella

Abelardo de la Espriella is the winner of [[colombia|Colombia]]'s June 21, 2026 presidential election.[1]

His victory was certified by Colombian electoral authorities, but was challenged by outgoing President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], who reportedly claimed the real winner was Iván Cepeda and alleged the result had been manipulated using technology from California.[1] On July 10, 2026 the United States and twelve regional partners issued a joint statement through the [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] coalition rejecting efforts to delegitimize the certified mandate and urging a peaceful, orderly and transparent institutional transition to the certified winner.[1] Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] shared the statement publicly.[1]

## Petro's refusal to recognize the result

By early July 2026 Petro had escalated the dispute, refusing to recognize de la Espriella's legitimacy outright and alleging that Colombia's election systems were manipulated through servers located in California with the involvement of Israel's intelligence agency, [[mossad|Mossad]].[2] Petro demanded a comprehensive forensic audit, a recount, and an independent review of the election infrastructure before the results were treated as final, though he released no supporting evidence; Colombian electoral authorities defended the vote's integrity and Israeli officials did not publicly respond.[2] The runoff had been closely contested, with Petro-backed candidate [[ivan-cepeda|Iván Cepeda]] narrowly losing.[2]

Petro alleged that de la Espriella has old ties to drug trafficking, questioned publicly why he holds US citizenship, and named an associate under the alias "Boliche," urging President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to consult his intelligence agencies about him.[2] He further accused Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and bad actors in the US government of aiding the [[clan-del-golfo|Clan del Golfo]] cartel's attempt to return to power through de la Espriella's election.[2] Petro also claimed the president-elect intends to restore the tax and monetary policies of former president [[ivan-duque|Iván Duque]]; Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that if "La Tigre" does so, it would mark him as most likely a Deep State operator.[2]

## Election result and Cepeda's conditions

De la Espriella, a 47-year-old conservative lawyer, won the June 21 runoff with 49.6% of the vote to [[ivan-cepeda|Iván Cepeda]]'s 48.7%, and was scheduled to be inaugurated on August 7.[3] He gained U.S. citizenship as an adult while running a Florida law firm and received an endorsement from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the campaign.[3] On June 30, 2026 Cepeda announced he would not recognize de la Espriella as president unless he renounced that U.S. citizenship, clarified whether he is acting as a U.S. "agent," and halted any effort to extradite Petro, threatening "peaceful civil disobedience" otherwise.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that de la Espriella had served as lawyer for [[alex-saab|Alex Saab]] beginning in 2014, making him an ally of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], with speculation that he facilitated Saab's deal with the [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] and FBI.[3] While de la Espriella claims he will aggressively combat the cartels and round up street gangs, Petro had already waged war on the cartels in collaboration with the US military since at least 2023 and destroyed tens of thousands of acres of coca by incentivizing farmers to replace it with cocoa and coffee.[3] See [[2026-colombian-presidential-election|the election]] for fuller coverage.[3]
