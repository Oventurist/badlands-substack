# Public Interest Legal Foundation

The Public Interest Legal Foundation (PILF) is an election integrity watchdog organization that litigates over voter list maintenance and public access to election records.[1] Its president is [[j-christian-adams|J. Christian Adams]].[1]

## Colorado ERIC litigation

In September 2024, PILF won an order from the U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado requiring Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] to release [[electronic-registration-information-center|Electronic Registration Information Center]] (ERIC) reports suspected of containing dead registrants on the state's voter rolls, including individuals who may have died within the previous three years.[1] Griswold signed the stipulation after the court denied her motion to dismiss, and Judge [[philip-brimmer|Philip Brimmer]] ordered the secretary of state's office to disclose the requested 2021 ERIC reports by November 1, permitting only minimal redactions to the ERIC Report Key.[1] With the settlement reached, the judge dismissed PILF's claim that Griswold had violated the National Voter Registration Act of 1993.[1]

Adams framed the outcome as a transparency victory, saying that "PILF has knocked down ERIC's wall of secrecy in the voter list maintenance process" and that "states cannot use third parties to hide election records that the public has a right to see."[1]
