# Jeffrey Yass

Jeffrey Yass is an American financier and Republican megadonor who holds a partial ownership stake in ByteDance, the Chinese parent company of the short-video platform TikTok.[1]

## Role in the 2024 TikTok divestment debate

Yass entered the center of the March 2024 fight over the House-passed TikTok divestment bill because of his dual position as a major conservative political donor and a part-owner of [[bytedance|ByteDance]].[1] Badlands Media commentary noted that former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had previously sought to ban TikTok by executive action, softened his stance after meeting with Yass, and speculated that Trump's own media conglomerate might be "shark circling" around the platform as a potential acquirer.[1] The same commentary paired the Yass meeting with Trump's contemporaneous meeting with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], reading the two encounters as evidence that ownership of major social platforms — rather than a simple national-security ban — was the real object of the maneuvering.[1]

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] framed the episode as a "very thick fog of war," arguing that both a "black hat" reading (the Deep State building a pretext for new systems of information control) and a "white hat" reading (a repositioning of platform ownership) remained live possibilities.[1]

## Alleged donations to Thomas Massie

In his March 2025 essay "Massie Maximalists," Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] asserted that Yass — whom he described as a "Zionist billionaire" — appears to be the largest financial donor to Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]], offering the claim as a rejoinder to Massie supporters who treat the congressman's refusal of [[aipac|AIPAC]] money as proof that no outside interest could influence him.[2] Carlson did not document the donation figures, instead inviting readers to "look it up."[2]
