# Central Military Commission

The Central Military Commission (CMC) is the highest military body of the People's Republic of China and the institution through which the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] exercises command over the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]]. Its chairman is [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].

## January 2026 purges

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Chinese authorities announced investigations into CMC Vice Chairman General [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]], 75, and General [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] for "serious violations of discipline and law," the standard formulation indicating corruption. The probes were revealed over the weekend of January 25–26, 2026, with the *People's Liberation Army Daily* accusing the pair of "seriously betraying" the Party's trust, "trampling on and undermining" the Central Military Commission, and threatening the authority of its chairman.

The removals reduced the CMC to Xi and a single other member, leaving senior PLA ranks in disarray and creating a leadership void after years of anti-corruption campaigns that had ousted dozens of high-ranking officers since 2023. Unverified reporting cited by the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] alleged that Zhang had leaked core nuclear weapons data to the United States and accepted bribes; other explanations offered included factional infighting and Zhang's accumulation of excessive personal power.

Analysts quoted in the source described the moves as strengthening Xi's absolute control over the military while raising concerns about operational readiness, morale, and a climate of suspicion that could deter promotions and encourage overly cautious decision-making. No impact was reported on strategic ambitions such as Taiwan.

## Interpretation in the corpus

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the CMC purge not as instability at the apex of Chinese power but as its consolidation — the "methodical expulsion of Globalist Deep State tendrils" from China's institutional framework, and a further stage of Xi's [[tigers-and-flies|Tigers and Flies]] campaign, framed within the corpus's [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] model.
