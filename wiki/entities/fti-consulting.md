# FTI Consulting

FTI Consulting is an American business advisory firm. It figures in the Badlands Media essay [[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] as the employer [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] left in July 2009 before taking his unpaid post as [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s economic envoy to Northern Ireland.

According to *Politico*, Kelly had been working as a top executive for FTI Consulting; upon stepping down he signed a two-year noncompete agreement barring him from doing consulting work for anyone else, while retaining the option to continue working for FTI part-time as a consultant to [[dow-chemical|Dow Chemical]] and Coca-Cola. He chose to keep those relationships, and between August 2009 and January 2011 Dow paid him $2.4 million through FTI.

The article identifies this noncompete as the reason [[teneo|Teneo]] — planned from 2009 by Kelly, [[doug-band|Doug Band]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]] — was not formally launched until June 2011, and argues that Kelly spent the intervening two years using his [[state-department|State Department]] position to make corporate connections that would later benefit both Teneo and the Clintons.
