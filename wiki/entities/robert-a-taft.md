# Robert A. Taft

Robert A. Taft was a United States Senator from 1938 to 1950 and a member of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, listed in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" among its most notable public-facing members.[1] He belonged to the same Taft line as the Order's founding member [[alphonso-taft|Alphonso Taft]] and President [[william-howard-taft|William Howard Taft]], a lineage the article offers as an illustration of how family ties have always been an important factor in Bones selection.[1]
