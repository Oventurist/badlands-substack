# Pandemic Response Accountability Committee

The Pandemic Response Accountability Committee (PRAC) is the federal watchdog body established to oversee spending under the United States' COVID-19 stimulus programs.[1]

## $5.4 billion suspect Social Security number alert

In an alert issued on Monday, January 30, 2023 and shared in advance with the Washington Post, PRAC estimated that the U.S. government may have awarded roughly $5.4 billion in coronavirus aid to small businesses whose applications carried potentially ineligible Social Security numbers.[1] The finding was presented as further evidence that the speed of the federal response early in the pandemic opened the door to widespread waste, fraud and abuse.[1] The alert landed as House Republicans prepared their first hearing to examine the roughly $5 trillion in total federal stimulus aid approved since spring 2020.[1]

Reporting noted that the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]] had already granted full or partial forgiveness to more than 93 percent of Paycheck Protection Program recipients, complicating any effort to recover improperly disbursed funds.[1]
