# Daniel Lacalle

Daniel Lacalle is a fund manager, economist and author who has argued that ordinary Americans bear the cost of the gradual deterioration of the monetary system.[1]

## Argument on monetary deterioration

Speaking to [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] in March 2023, Lacalle said ordinary Americans could expect their wealth to be "repeatedly chipped away" as the monetary system degrades and requires progressively more intervention by authorities to perpetuate itself, though he cautioned it may take "a very long time" for the system to actually break.[1]

He argued that the failures of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] and [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]] were symptoms of a broader problem that the central bank cannot fix.[1] Framing the issue as "the concept of 'what can be done?'", he contended that [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] market interventions intended to smooth over perturbations merely redistribute risk and losses while making the system more fragile over the long run.[1] "Every time they try to solve a bubble with more liquidity injections, they create another bubble," he said; "what you have to do first is not implement crazy monetary policies."[1] The remark referred to the policy of extremely low interest rates the Fed maintained for most of the preceding decade.[1]
