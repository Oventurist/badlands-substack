# Lauren Chen

Lauren Chen is the founder of [[tenet-media|Tenet Media]], the Tennessee-based media company at the center of a federal indictment unsealed on September 4, 2024 concerning nearly $10 million allegedly funneled from employees of [[russia-today|Russia Today]].[1]

According to the indictment as reported, Chen allegedly knew the true source of the funds and told the commentators working with the company that the money came from a wealthy private investor named "Eduard Grigoriann."[1] The influencers involved — including [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]], [[benny-johnson|Benny Johnson]], [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] and [[lauren-southern|Lauren Southern]] — were described by prosecutors as having been deceived and unaware that they were taking Russian money.[1]
