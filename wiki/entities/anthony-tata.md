# Anthony Tata

Anthony Tata is a U.S. defense official who, as of September 2025, served as Undersecretary of Defense for Personnel and Readiness.[1]

## Charlie Kirk recruiting campaign

In September 2025, [[pentagon|Pentagon]] leaders were reported to be considering a new military recruiting campaign that would encourage young people to honor the legacy of assassinated conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] by enlisting.[1] According to two officials familiar with the planning, the campaign was to be framed as a national call to service, with discussed slogans including "Charlie has awakened a generation of warriors."[1]

Tata was identified as the official leading the effort.[1] Planning also reportedly contemplated using chapters of Kirk's political organization, [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]], at schools across the United States as military recruitment centers.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright presented the proposed campaign as evidence that Kirk's death was being weaponized by actors who did not have the public's interests in mind, invoking what he called the "Con Inc. Doom Brigade."[1]
