# Supreme Court of the United Kingdom

The Supreme Court of the United Kingdom is Britain's highest court of appeal. In April 2025 it issued a widely reported judgment on the meaning of "woman" in UK equality law.[1]

## 2025 ruling on the definition of a woman

On 16 April 2025 the court ruled that trans women do not fall within the legal definition of women under the [[equality-act-2010|Equality Act 2010]], holding that the legal definition of a woman rests on biological sex.[1] The judgment was characterised as a landmark and as a setback for transgender rights campaigners, arriving amid intense public debate over the intersection of transgender rights and women's rights.[1]

The five judges hearing the case stressed the limits of their holding, saying it was not the court's role to adjudicate on the meaning of gender or sex and that the ruling addressed only the interpretation of statutory language.[1] Commentators noted possible consequences for single-sex services such as domestic violence shelters and for equal pay litigation.[1]
