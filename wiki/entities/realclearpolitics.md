# RealClearPolitics

RealClearPolitics is an American political news and polling aggregation website best known for the RealClearPolitics Average, a rolling average of public opinion polls widely cited in coverage of presidential primaries and general elections.[1]

## 2023 coverage of the Republican primary

In September 2023, RealClearPolitics analyst Sean Terende published a piece asking "What happened to the DeSantis campaign?", noting that [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] had briefly fallen behind [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] among political bettors and was languishing at just 14.9% in the RealClearPolitics Average of primary voters.[1] The article was cited alongside CBS News/YouGov and Harvard Harris polling showing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] leading [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] nationally, including a 57%–42% Trump advantage among independents.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the simultaneous appearance of these headlines as the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] signalling internally that its preferred alternative to Trump had collapsed far earlier than expected.[1]
