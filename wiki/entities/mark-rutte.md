# Mark Rutte

Mark Rutte is the Secretary-General of [[nato|NATO]] and the alliance's chief interlocutor with Washington during the 2026 crisis over the U.S.-Israeli war against [[iran|Iran]]. He appears in the April 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-282|Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks"]], which reports that Rutte was expected to travel to Washington to meet [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in an effort to ease tensions within the alliance.

The dispute Rutte was tasked with defusing arose after the February 28, 2026 U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran. European member states questioned the legality of the war and declined to contribute militarily, including refusing to deploy naval forces to the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]]. Trump publicly criticised the allies' reluctance and questioned whether the United States should remain committed to NATO — a withdrawal that, as the source notes, would require the approval of [[congress|Congress]]. Officials and analysts quoted in the Brief describe the resulting divisions as a significant crisis for the alliance's cohesion and future role, and Rutte's Washington visit is presented as the principal diplomatic response to it.

## Tariff pause, January 2026

The January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-577|Badlands Brief]] reports that the transatlantic controversy over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s threatened tariffs and his pressure campaign over [[greenland|Greenland]] eased mid-week when Trump announced what he described as a "framework of a future deal" with Rutte as [[nato|NATO]] Secretary General, temporarily pausing tariff actions that had rattled global markets and alarmed European diplomats.
