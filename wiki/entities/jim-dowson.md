# Jim Dowson

Jim Dowson is described by Badlands Media as the leader of a network of "New International Templars" associated with the British nationalist milieu around [[tommy-robinson|Tommy Robinson]].[1]

The article raises Dowson's circle rhetorically: it asks whether Robinson, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] or their New International Templar friends led by Dowson ever make reference to the financier oligarchy and the occult orders as the actual oppressors of the people — and answers that of course they do not.[1] In this reading the movement channels genuine grievance over decades of woke and [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]] infiltration of education and political institutions into tribal conflict with dark-skinned immigrants and their supposed "Chi-Com managers," while leaving the London–Wall Street financial interests untouched.[1]

Badlands situates this pattern within the older formula of Bolshevik versus National Socialist chaos in the 1920s, in which the hand of the bankers funding both extremes was systematically ignored.[1]
