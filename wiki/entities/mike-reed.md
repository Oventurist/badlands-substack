# Mike Reed

**Mike Reed** is a Republican political operative who served as chief of staff of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] from January 2022 until his announced departure in February 2024.[1]

## Departure from the RNC

Reed informed RNC staff by email on Tuesday, February 6, 2024, that he would step down later that month, writing that his "growing family needs and deserves my attention."[1] Two people familiar with the move told Politico that it had been in the works for roughly a year: Reed accepted a new job with Cornerstone, a government relations and public affairs firm, the previous fall, but chose to remain at the committee through the RNC's winter meeting in Las Vegas.[1]

The timing nonetheless coincided with mounting speculation that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], the strong frontrunner for the Republican nomination, would push for a leadership shakeup at the committee then chaired by [[ronna-mcdaniel|Ronna McDaniel]].[1] Reed acknowledged that context in his note, writing: "I know the timing of this news comes as many rumors in the press swirl and we prepare to merge with the presumptive nominee. I assure you, the RNC is in an incredibly strong position."[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the deeper story was that shakeups in RNC leadership were expected after the South Carolina primary and were being directed by Trump himself — "It's Trump's party. That's the takeaway." — and that [[politico|Politico]]'s tempered language showed institutions bracing for the return of the 45th president.[1]
