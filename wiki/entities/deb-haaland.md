# Deb Haaland

Deb Haaland served as United States Secretary of the Interior in the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], heading the [[department-of-the-interior|Department of the Interior]].[1]

## Cancellation of ANWR oil and gas leases

In September 2023, Haaland announced the cancellation of seven oil and gas leases in Alaska's Arctic National Wildlife Refuge that had been legally awarded in a 2021 lease sale.[1] "On day one of this administration, President Biden directed us to look at the oil and gas leases sold in the refuge by the previous administration," Haaland said in a call with reporters, adding, "What we have found in our analysis is that the lease sale itself was seriously flawed and based on a number of fundamental legal deficiencies."[1] The department simultaneously proposed a rule that would protect 13 million acres in a different part of Alaska.[1]

The action was criticized as "illegal" and "reckless" by opponents, and was folded by outlets such as [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] into a broader pattern of Biden energy decisions — including cancellation of the [[keystone-pipeline|Keystone XL pipeline]] and drawdown of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve — argued to weaken United States energy and national security.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] offered a competing reading, suggesting these events could equally be interpreted as a controlled demolition of the Deep State in the public mind rather than simple capture of the administration.[1]
