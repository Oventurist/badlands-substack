# United States Senate

The United States Senate is the upper chamber of [[congress|Congress]], exercising confirmation, appropriations and oversight powers over the executive branch.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] records the Senate acting in its oversight capacity through a Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing held on February 10, 2026, at which Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] testified and faced bipartisan questioning over ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] revealed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s document release.

Democratic senators [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], [[chris-coons|Chris Coons]] and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]] pressed Lutnick on discrepancies with his earlier account of when he severed contact with Epstein and accused him of a lack of transparency, with some issuing calls for his resignation. Republican leaders in the chamber emphasised the need for those named in the files to provide answers but largely avoided direct confrontation on the issue.
