# United States Senate

The United States Senate is the upper chamber of [[congress|Congress]], exercising confirmation, appropriations and oversight powers over the executive branch.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief records the Senate acting in its oversight capacity through a Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing held on February 10, 2026, at which Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] testified and faced bipartisan questioning over ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] revealed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s document release.

Democratic senators [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], [[chris-coons|Chris Coons]] and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]] pressed Lutnick on discrepancies with his earlier account of when he severed contact with Epstein and accused him of a lack of transparency, with some issuing calls for his resignation. Republican leaders in the chamber emphasised the need for those named in the files to provide answers but largely avoided direct confrontation on the issue.


## April 2026: the Brennan records standoff

The Brief of April 20, 2026, citing [[just-the-news|Just the News]], reports that the Senate had not transmitted transcripts and records requested by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in connection with former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], missing a February 23 deadline and delaying part of an investigation based in Fort Pierce, Florida. Aides to Senate Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] told the Justice Department he was seeking unanimous consent to release the records while discussing the matter with Democrats to avoid a floor vote, and prosecutors were recently allowed to review one [[senate-intelligence-committee|Senate Intelligence Committee]] report in a secure room.

## The Fauci subpoena (July 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that on July 7, 2026, Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] announced a subpoena for [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] to testify before the Senate, the culmination of the wave of legal and administrative actions against scientists affiliated with the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]] over COVID-19 research, with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].
