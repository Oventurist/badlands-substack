# United States Senate

The United States Senate is the upper chamber of Congress, treated in the March 23, 2026 Badlands Brief as the structural chokepoint for election-integrity legislation.[1]

## SAVE America Act weekend debate

The Senate worked through the weekend of March 21–22, 2026 debating the proposed [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]], a Republican-backed bill that would impose stricter voter registration and identification requirements nationwide, requiring proof of U.S. citizenship such as a passport or birth certificate to register for federal elections and expanding federal oversight of voter registration systems.[1]

The measure faced significant obstacles in the closely divided chamber, where most legislation requires 60 votes to advance; Democrats have largely opposed the bill, making passage unlikely in its current form.[1] [[polymarket|Polymarket]] priced the Act's odds of becoming law in 2026 at 11% over that weekend.[1]

## Badlands critique

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the episode to indict the institution directly, writing that if the Senate cannot pass proof-of-citizenship legislation she says is supported by more than 80% of the electorate, "Americans should ask themselves why we have the body at all" — since senators are "the main beneficiaries of diluting (and undermining) the will of the People through fake voters."[1] She urged readers to call or email their senators and ask why the beneficiaries of corruption are the only ones empowered to end it.[1]
