# Harvard Kennedy School

The Harvard Kennedy School of Government is the public policy and government faculty of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]].[1]

## Role in Badlands Media's account

Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* identifies the Kennedy School, alongside the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|School of Public Health]] and the [[berkman-klein-center-for-internet-and-society|Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society]], as one of the Harvard units from which the modeling and behavioral infrastructure deployed during the COVID-19 response originated, including lockdown modeling, behavioral nudging, and contact tracing protocols.[1]

The article situates the school within a broader argument that Harvard trains and then places personnel into government ministries, United Nations working groups, and the administrative machinery of global digital compliance, forming what it calls a recruitment pipeline rather than a neutral academic program.[1]
