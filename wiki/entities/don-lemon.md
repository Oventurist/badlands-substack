# Don Lemon

Don Lemon is an American broadcast journalist and former prime-time host at [[cnn|CNN]]. In the Badlands corpus he appears principally in connection with the January 2026 protest at [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota.

## Cities Church allegations

According to the January 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, Lemon is alleged to have joined a crowd of dozens of left-wing activists who stormed Cities Church during a Sunday service, apparently targeting a pastor believed to be linked to the local field office operations of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]]. The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] subsequently placed him "on notice" for potential federal charges, with Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] identifying the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] and the FACE Act as potentially applicable statutes. Lemon publicly dismissed the allegations as "fake news."

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the episode as a reversal of the usual direction of KKK Act litigation, writing that the statute "hasn't been aimed at the likes of Don Lemon before" and that "empathetic mockingbirds rarely get clap backs like 'Klan.'" She adds that people like Lemon "need to get better acquainted with it, to see what it's become in 2026."
