# Ghost of BPH

**Ghost of BPH** is a pseudonymous commentator associated with the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] network.[1]

## GART appearances

Ghost of BPH was listed among the expected speakers for [[gart-4-dallas|GART 4: Dallas]], the fourth stop of Badlands Media's Great American Restoration Tour, held April 18–21, 2024 at the [[hilton-dallas-southlake|Hilton Dallas Southlake]] in Texas.[1] The billing placed Ghost of BPH alongside Badlands hosts and contributors including [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], [[kate-buckley|Kate Buckley]], [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[canncon|CannCon]], [[gmoney|GMoney]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute1776]].[1] Speakers at the event appeared in live in-person versions of Badlands shows, on panels with audience question-and-answer sessions, and at meet-and-greets with attendees.[1]
