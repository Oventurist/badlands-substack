# Jesuits

The Jesuits are presented in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the controlling intelligence behind the French territories of the Americas and as a persistent source of covert operations against the republican cause.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Colonial operations

Up to the time of the [[battle-of-quebec|Battle of Quebec]], the Jesuits who controlled the French territories of the Americas had done the most work in organising alliances with different native bands, and had done immense damage in manipulating these groups against each other and sometimes against colonists.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) The danger for the English republicans was that if the Mohawks or Iroquois allied with anyone in the war it would in all probability have been the Jesuit-run French; the efforts of [[william-johnson|William Johnson]] were decisive in preventing that outcome.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

The article describes Jesuitical intelligence operations as conducting slanders and promoting gossip to induce hostility between white men and natives, and as manipulating certain native groups from Canada and Quebec in a racist manner in order to deploy them in massacres using what it calls very high-level MK Ultra brainwashing operations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) These are said to be outlined by Cynthia Chung in her book *The Shaping of a World Religion: From Jesuits, Freemasons and Anthropologists to the Ghost Dance Religion*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Article 4 of the [[penn-treaty-of-1682|Penn Treaty of 1682]], which required both parties to inquire of each other before believing rumours, is read as a direct countermeasure to this pattern of manipulation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Later documentation

Friedrich Schiller wrote about these methods in his 1792 study *The Jesuit Government in Paraguay*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) [[samuel-morse|Samuel Morse]] later exposed the operation in the Americas in his 1841 *Foreign Conspiracy Against the Liberties of the United States*, describing Prince Metternich's Holy Alliance deploying Jesuits throughout the Americas to undo the American Revolution.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
