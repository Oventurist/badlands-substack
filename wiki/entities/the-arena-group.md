# The Arena Group

The Arena Group is the media company that operates [[sports-illustrated|Sports Illustrated]] and other publishing brands, including The Street.[1] In late November 2023 the company came under scrutiny after reporting by Futurism alleged that Sports Illustrated had published articles credited to fake, AI-generated writers whose author biographies and headshots were fabricated.[1]

## AI-generated author controversy

One of the bylines at issue, "Drew Ortiz," carried a biography describing an outdoorsman who spent weekends camping, hiking, and visiting his parents' farm.[1] Outside of Sports Illustrated, however, Ortiz had no social media presence and no publishing history, and his profile photograph was offered for sale on a website marketing AI-generated headshots, where the image was described as a "neutral white young-adult male with short brown hair and blue eyes."[1] A person involved in creating the content, who spoke anonymously to avoid professional repercussions, said Ortiz was not the only AI-generated author published by the magazine.[1]

Badlands commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the episode as one of the most "comically-dystopian" stories of the week and accused The Arena Group of shifting its position — initially blaming third-party vendors while denying that AI content was involved.[1] That defense, the commentary argued, inadvertently revealed to readers that Sports Illustrated and The Street content had been licensed from outside vendors rather than produced in house, and further backpedaling followed once the AI-generated headshots were traced to a commercial database.[1] The broader lesson drawn was that the American public was being educated in how much Establishment output is fabricated.[1]
