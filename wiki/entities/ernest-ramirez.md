# Ernest Ramirez

Ernest Ramirez is an American father who became a public critic of COVID-19 vaccination policy after the death of his 16-year-old son, Ernesto, and who alleges that officials of the [[fema|Federal Emergency Management Agency]] offered him financial assistance in exchange for changing his son's cause of death.[1]

## Death of Ernesto Ramirez

Ramirez said he wanted to do the right thing for his son and therefore had Ernesto take the COVID-19 vaccine, having been told the shot was "100 percent safe."[1] Five days later, on April 24, 2021, his son died from complications related to an enlarged heart.[1] Ramirez recounted the sequence of events to LifeSiteNews and to The Western Journal, which reported the story in January 2023.[1]

## Alleged FEMA offer

Soon after his son's death, Ramirez says he received a call from FEMA in which agency officials asked him to change the recorded cause of death to "COVID," offering to help him financially if he did so.[1] He refused and went public with the account.[1]
