# Cindy McCain

Cindy McCain is the widow of Arizona senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and a figure in the governance of the [[mccain-institute|McCain Institute]].[1]

## Role at the McCain Institute

According to the *Washington Post* fact check cited in the Badlands article, Cindy McCain sits on the McCain Institute's Human Trafficking Advisory Council, working with the nonprofit to raise awareness of her main policy issue.[1] Her position is presented as one of several facts undercutting her husband's 2016 claim that he had "nothing to do with" the institute beyond lending it his name: John McCain regularly attended institute events and fundraisers, many of his longtime political allies sat on its board of directors, and a longtime McCain fundraiser, Carly Eudy, maintained the McCain Institute Foundation's financial records.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

The Badlands account uses the family and staff overlap to argue that McCain's disavowal of the institute was a panicked response to reporting that the Saudi government had donated $1 million to the institute's fundraising arm, and that the arrangement placed allies in control of the financial records the article suggests could reveal ties to Prince [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]].[1]
