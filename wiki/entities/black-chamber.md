# The Black Chamber

The Black Chamber was a shadowy American military intelligence organization established in 1918 by [[ralph-van-deman|Ralph Van Deman]], the protégé of British intelligence officer [[claude-dansey|Claude Dansey]].[1]

Led by Marlborough Churchill, an American cousin of Sir [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], the Black Chamber excelled at signals intelligence and was, in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account, given a new name in 1930 under the banner of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]].[1] Its creation is presented as one of at least two American secret intelligence agencies traceable to Dansey's work out of the [[sleepy-hollow-club|Sleepy Hollow Club]], forming part of a parallel security apparatus established outside the control of the United States government.[1]
