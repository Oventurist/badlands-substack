# Ingersoll Lockwood

**Ingersoll Lockwood** was an American author who published a series of books in the 1890s that, in the reading advanced by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], appear to anticipate present-day events surrounding the Trump family.[1] Among the works cited are *The Last President* and *Baron Trump's Marvelous Adventure*.[1]

## The claimed parallels

Lockwood's books center on a character named Baron Trump, who lives in Trump Castle and keeps a pet dog named Bulger.[1] Proponents of the parallel note that there is today a real [[barron-trump|Barron Trump]], who is presumed to spend considerable time at [[trump-tower|Trump Tower]], and that the Trump family has a dog named Patton — [[george-s-patton|George S. Patton]] being famous for winning the Battle of the Bulge, echoing the name Bulger.[1]

In *The Last President*, a riot takes place on Fifth Avenue at the precise location where the New York Trump Tower stands today.[1] In the same books, Baron receives mission-critical instructions from a figure known as the "master of all masters," whose name is Don.[1]

## Interpretation

American Hypnotist presents these resemblances as "uncanny" and as circumstantial support for the broader theory that the Trump family possesses some knowledge of time travel, a theory also tied to [[john-g-trump|John Trump]]'s receipt of [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]]'s papers.[1] The author acknowledges that further alleged coincidences exist beyond those listed, and directs readers to video material rather than exhausting the subject in the essay.[1] Within the essay's framework, such coincidences are treated as evidence for timeline manipulation rather than as chance.[1]
