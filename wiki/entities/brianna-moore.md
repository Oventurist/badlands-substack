# Brianna Moore

Brianna Moore is a Florida college student accused of killing her newborn daughter in a University of Tampa dormitory bathroom.[1]

## Allegations

According to Hillsborough County prosecutors, Moore, 19, allegedly suffocated her baby girl soon after giving birth in April of the prior year and then discarded the body in the trash.[1] Newly released court records obtained by the *Tampa Bay Times* included text messages sent roughly eight months before the birth in which Moore discussed abortion and pills and suggested that killing a baby was her preferred method of birth control.[1] "Hey man, sometimes you need a plan C," Moore texted an unidentified man, who allegedly replied: "Plan A was condoms. Plan B was the pill. Plan C was to kill (the) kid." Moore responded, "Plan C is my favorite."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called the case "a horror show" and apparently premeditated murder "against the most innocent among us," while declining to treat it as an isolated act of individual evil.[1] She asked whether "the feminists, the eugenicists, and the propagandists" who target women with the message that their child is not human bear any blame, and whether "the comprehensive propaganda machine" deserves scrutiny for convincing generations of women "to ignore their biology and treat their offspring as disposable trash."[1] Explicitly declining to defend Moore — arguing she should at minimum be removed from free society — she framed the case as evidence of programming likely more widespread than most readers would find comfortable "in the age of shouting your abortion."[1]
