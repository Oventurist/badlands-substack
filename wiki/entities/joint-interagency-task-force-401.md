# Joint Interagency Task Force 401

Joint Interagency Task Force 401 (JIATF 401) is a United States Department of Defense task force created in 2025 to counter drone threats and protect U.S. airspace.[1]

Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] announced the initiative, saying the department wanted to address a growing threat and improve its systems to protect people and equipment both at home and overseas.[1] "We're moving fast – cutting through bureaucracy, consolidating resources, and empowering this task force with the utmost authority to outpace our adversaries," Hegseth said. "We will innovate, we will lead and we will win."[1]

Hegseth directed Secretary of the Army [[dan-driscoll|Dan Driscoll]] to establish the task force, which according to a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] memo is to gather top talent from across agencies.[1] The Pentagon characterized drones — which it refers to as unmanned aircraft systems, or UAS — as a particularly dangerous threat.[1] Badlands host [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] described the announcement as "signal."[1]
