# Isabel Maxwell

Isabel Maxwell is a daughter of the publishing magnate [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] and the twin sister of [[christine-maxwell|Christine Maxwell]].[1] Badlands Media notes that the twins have received minimal to no public attention despite holding senior positions at the Israeli intelligence front company [[inslaw|Inslaw]].[1]

## Inslaw and PROMIS

Through Inslaw, Isabel and Christine are said to have facilitated their father's most consequential act of espionage on behalf of Israel: the sale of the compromised [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to the U.S. national laboratories at the core of the country's nuclear weapons system.[1]

## Successor ventures and the Internet

Following the multimillion-dollar sale of the venture to a competitor, both sisters became associated with successors to the PROMIS scandal, courting leading figures of the U.S. technology industry — a period overlapping with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s investments in Israeli intelligence-linked firms such as [[carbyne|Carbyne]] and his claimed blackmail holdings on tech CEOs.[1] The article argues that while [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] drew the media scrutiny, Isabel and Christine were the true inheritors of their father's work, capitalising on the emergence of the Internet.[1]
