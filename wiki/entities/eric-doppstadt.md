# Eric W. Doppstadt

Eric W. Doppstadt is the investment chief who manages the [[ford-foundation|Ford Foundation]]'s endowment, described in "The Harvard Control Grid" as a $16 billion portfolio, and is identified in the article as a member of Harvard's endowment board.[1]

Badlands Media argues that the role "might sound innocuous, until you understand Ford's historical role as a CIA-adjacent soft power organ."[1] During the Cold War, the article states, the Ford Foundation coordinated with intelligence officials to fund "pro-democracy" groups abroad, front-facing academic initiatives and propaganda outlets.[1] Through Doppstadt, the article concludes, Harvard "retains an indirect line into legacy intelligence foundations with a proven history of cultural and psychological operations."[1]

Doppstadt is also named alongside [[rick-slocum|Rick Slocum]] as a [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] insider who currently helps manage the assets of legacy foundations.[1]
