# The AlphaWarrior Show

The AlphaWarrior Show is a program within the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] network whose host, known as Alpha, appears in live event and breaking-news coverage alongside other Badlands contributors.[1]

## 2024 Democratic National Convention coverage

The AlphaWarrior Show co-hosted Badlands Media's live coverage of Day 1 of the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], streaming on Rumble.[1] Following the program, the two broke down their favorite moments from the evening, concluding that the night's defining quality was desperation on the part of the assembled Democrats.[1]
