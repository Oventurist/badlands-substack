# Preston Pysh

**Preston Pysh** is a Bitcoin-focused investor and commentator who appears in Badlands Media coverage as a participant in an April 2023 Twitter exchange used to illustrate the capabilities of generative [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]].[1]

## Role in the ChatGPT–Kevin Hart demonstration

In "Don't Bring Shiny Rocks to Digital Gunfights," [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] described how United States Space Force Major [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]] posted a [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 4.0 summary of his book [[softwar|Softwar]] on Twitter.[1] Roughly an hour later, Pysh replied with a different ChatGPT output that rewrote Lowery's summary in the style of comedian Kevin Hart.[1]

Pysh followed that tweet with a link to a recording platform hosting a video clip in which a separate AI voice tool delivered the same summary in Kevin Hart's voice and speaking style.[1] The Badlands author described the clip as a "must listen" and reported being "truly mind blown" — not primarily by the quality of the synthesised voice, impressive though he found it, but by the realisation that no human had written the summary and no human had spoken the words.[1] He stressed that the real Kevin Hart had not said them and that the feelings, emotions and thoughts conveyed were not Hart's, yet were mimicked extremely well.[1]

## Significance in the article's argument

The Pysh contribution completed a chain of AI tools — text generation, stylistic rewriting and voice synthesis — that the essay treated as evidence that AI-generated renditions of real people are already convincing enough to fool anyone who does not identify and consider the source.[1] That demonstration underpinned the author's central hazard claim about humanity's diminishing ability to distinguish truth from AI-generated fantasy, and his proposal to impose "severe physical costs" on machine-produced data through [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] micro-transactions.[1]
