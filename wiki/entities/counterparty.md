# Counterparty

**Counterparty** is a second-layer protocol built on top of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] that allows users to issue and trade tokens whose records are written into the Bitcoin blockchain.[1] It is the platform on which the Rare Pepe card series was issued, and in Badlands Media commentary it is treated as the technical substrate that ties the [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe]] meme to Bitcoin.[1]

## Tokens discussed in Badlands commentary

The Badlands Brief article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile" cites several Counterparty assets. The first token ever minted on the protocol was named TEST, issued on 1/13/18; the author connects this to Q [[qanon|drop]] #519, which states "SEC TEST" and is dated 1/13/14, describing the interval as "a great 4-year delta."[1] The [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token was minted by an anonymous party called "Mike" on 9/9/16, the same day as [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[basket-of-deplorables|"basket of deplorables"]] remark.[1] A further token, PEPETRAIN, is highlighted for its resemblance to the [[trump-train|Trump Train]] motif, with its series and supply numbers said to contain the [[covfefe|Covfefe]] signal and its card number, #47, read as pointing to 11/5/24.[1]

## Tokenization as proof of publication

The article describes a token called FIRSTMARKER, minted on Counterparty by [[gmoney|GMONEY]] to store research on the Bitcoin time chain in an unalterable record and to obtain a timestamped proof-of-publication receipt — a capability the author says [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] considered extremely important, and speculates may be the kind of "[proof]" Q said would begin on 11.3.[1] Readers are directed to Episode 79 of [[rugpull-radio|Rugpull Radio]], titled "Bitcoin Tokenization is the Future," for further background.[1]

## Role in the COVFEFE Dig

"The COVFEFE Dig" (March 2023) describes Counterparty as a second-layered protocol on [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] that works through the OP_RETURN code on the Bitcoin network.[2] [[gmoney|GMONEY]] uses it as the substrate for the article's blockchain evidence: the first Pepe token, [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]], was minted on Counterparty on 9/9/2016 and is verifiable on block 428,919, while the GAMESOFTRUMP card — bearing a heart of 17, a shield of 17 and an initial supply of 1717 — is verifiable on block 482,855 and was issued 58 days before the first [[qanon|Q]] drop.[2] The wider [[rare-pepe-directory|Rare Pepe Directory]] of 1,774 cards, including PEPERARE and PUTINPEPE, is likewise issued on the protocol.[2]
