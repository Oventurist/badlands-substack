# Volant Media

Volant Media is a privately owned, United Kingdom–based media company that owns the Persian-language broadcaster [[iran-international|Iran International]].[1] The ownership relationship is disclosed in the footer of Iran International's own website.[1]

## Ownership and funding controversy

Volant Media is owned by a British-Saudi film executive named [[adel-abdulkarim-alabdulkarim|Adel Abdulkarim Alabdulkarim]].[1] A 2018 report by the Financial Times of London accused Alabdulkarim of receiving funding from [[israel|Israel]], an allegation he denied.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that a nominally "Middle East" news organization headquartered in the UK, owned by a Saudi royal with British citizenship, and beaming its signal directly into televisions inside Iran, should be presumed to be an instrument of [[british-intelligence|British intelligence]] rather than an independent outlet.[1] He cited Iran International's May 2026 false report that Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] had resigned as evidence that the company functions as a propaganda vehicle.[1]
