# International Security Assistance Force

The International Security Assistance Force (ISAF) was the [[nato]]-led military mission in Afghanistan.[1] It appears in the [[badlands-news-brief]] in connection with [[rahamim-shy]], the former Obama administration policy adviser charged in Britain with child sex offences, who is described as having served as a high-level consultant to ISAF as part of the administration's strategy toward the [[taliban]] and [[al-qaeda]].[1]

Badlands commentary paired that ISAF consultancy with Shy's reported senior role at the [[us-department-of-the-treasury]] aimed at ending the financing of terrorism, arguing that during his 2008–2014 tenure radical Islamic terrorism grew to unprecedented levels and [[isis]] emerged across Syria and Iraq.[1] The writer used those affiliations to place Shy within a network including [[barack-obama]], [[hillary-clinton]], [[john-podesta]], NATO's military structures, Citi Bank Group, and radical Islamist organizations.[1]
