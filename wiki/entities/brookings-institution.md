# Brookings Institution

The Brookings Institution is a Washington, D.C.-based public policy think tank whose published analyses are frequently cited in coverage of American and European politics.[1]

In June 2024 Brookings published an analysis titled "The European Parliament elections have upended French politics," examining the [[2024-european-parliament-elections|2024 European Parliament elections]] held June 6–9.[1] The piece reported that final projections pointed to more continuity than expected in the parliament's overall composition while the French results amounted to a political earthquake, with the [[national-rally|National Rally]] of [[jordan-bardella|Jordan Bardella]] and [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]] taking about 31.4% against 14.6% for [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s Renaissance party.[1] Brookings warned of a high risk that the RN would win the ensuing snap elections, which would give France a far-right prime minister for the first time since the end of the Second World War.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Brookings analysis approvingly on one narrow point: its observation that the RN "has completely appropriated much of the vocabulary traditionally reserved to the left," a tactic Brookings compared to the MAGA wing of the US Republican Party, and which the think tank said is upending and fundamentally restructuring French politics.[1] She read that admission as corroboration that MAGA is a global movement and that deconstructing establishment narratives is working.[1]
