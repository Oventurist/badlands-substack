# Brookings Institution

The Brookings Institution, referred to in the source as the Brookings Institute, is a Washington-based public policy think tank listed in the Badlands Media essay [[and-iran-iran-so-far-away|"And Iran, Iran So Far Away"]] among the institutions comprising the Western "old guard."

The article places Brookings alongside the [[atlantic-council]] and the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] as one of "the various think tanks behind the [[european-union|EU]], the [[united-nations|UN]] and [[nato]]" — part of the interagency bureaucratic amalgam that the author says has long controlled and spread Western hegemony, and that fought to keep the United States inside the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear agreement]].
