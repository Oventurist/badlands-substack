# Brookings Institution

The Brookings Institution is a Washington, D.C.-based public policy think tank whose published analyses are frequently cited in coverage of American and European politics.[1]

In June 2024 Brookings published an analysis titled "The European Parliament elections have upended French politics," examining the [[2024-european-parliament-elections|2024 European Parliament elections]] held June 6–9.[1] The piece reported that final projections pointed to more continuity than expected in the parliament's overall composition while the French results amounted to a political earthquake, with the [[national-rally|National Rally]] of [[jordan-bardella|Jordan Bardella]] and [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]] taking about 31.4% against 14.6% for [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s Renaissance party.[1] Brookings warned of a high risk that the RN would win the ensuing snap elections, which would give France a far-right prime minister for the first time since the end of the Second World War.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Brookings analysis approvingly on one narrow point: its observation that the RN "has completely appropriated much of the vocabulary traditionally reserved to the left," a tactic Brookings compared to the MAGA wing of the US Republican Party, and which the think tank said is upending and fundamentally restructuring French politics.[1] She read that admission as corroboration that MAGA is a global movement and that deconstructing establishment narratives is working.[1]

## Harvard-linked membership

"The Harvard Control Grid" lists Brookings among the institutions where Harvard-linked elites — including [[glenn-hutchins|Glenn Hutchins]] and [[mohamed-el-erian|Mohamed El-Erian]] — hold seats or fellowships, alongside the [[group-of-thirty|Group of Thirty]] and the [[peterson-institute-for-international-economics|Peterson Institute for International Economics]].[2] These bodies, the article argues, "shape narratives around central banking, international development, digital currency rollouts, and the global debt system."[2] Brookings boards are also named among the seats occupied by the same people who run Harvard's endowment, forming what the piece calls "a closed loop of thought, power, and self-preservation."[2]

## Incubator of trilateralism

Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain – Part 2" identifies the Brookings Institution as the setting in which [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] researched the need for closer cooperation between Europe, North America and Asia — the work that became the intellectual basis for the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]].[5] Brookings's director of foreign policy studies, Henry Owen, attended the Commission's private organizing meeting at [[kykuit|Kykuit]] in July 1972 as one of the founders hand-selected by Brzezinski and [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]], and later served as a White House economic aide in [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]]'s administration.[5]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] listed Brookings among the "college of institutions" — NGOs, GONGOs and think tanks — that in his account carry out the bulk of deep political activity.[3] He noted that its membership has included [[avril-haines|Avril Haines]], described as a spook, Biden's Director of National Intelligence and instrumental to Russiagate and COVID narrative control; Lawrence Korb, characterized as a military-industrial complex functionary; and [[tom-donilon|Tom Donilon]], described as a Bilderberg attendee and Democrat operative.[3]

The article states that Brookings has also housed [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], who chairs what DeLarme called its color revolution planning arm.[3] The institution published the 300-page book Overcoming Trumpery with Eisen as editor, and publishes his annually updated domestic [[color-revolution|color revolution]] planning guide, The Democracy Playbook, most recently in a January 2025 edition.[3]

## Danchenko's employment and the 2009 solicitation

The Brookings Institution figured in the third day of the [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] trial as Danchenko's former employer and as the setting for conduct that first brought him to FBI attention.[6] On re-redirect examination, Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] drew from Agent [[kevin-helson|Kevin Helson]] that in 2009 Danchenko had solicited people at Brookings who were moving into the Obama administration for classified information he could sell, and that this matter was never looked into again after the earlier counterintelligence file on him was closed.[6] Helson was asked whether Danchenko was employed at Brookings in the March–April 2016 period, at the time he was also working for [[orbis-business-intelligence|Orbis Business Intelligence]] and meeting [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles Dolan]].[6]
