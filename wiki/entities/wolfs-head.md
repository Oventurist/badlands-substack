# Wolf's Head

Wolf's Head is one of the three senior societies at [[yale-university|Yale University]] singled out in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" as having stood out among all others for ages, alongside [[scroll-and-key|Scroll and Key]] and the [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society.[1]

The article treats these societies collectively as a recruiting ground for young men destined for careers in government, law, finance and other influential sectors of American life, noting that they are not part of any nationwide public association and are cloaked in secrecy.[1] Within that hierarchy Skull and Bones is described as the elite of the elite, with only Scroll and Key able to claim a near-equal influence on American affairs over the preceding 160 years.[1]
