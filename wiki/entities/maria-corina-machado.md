# María Corina Machado

María Corina Machado is a Venezuelan opposition leader. She appears in the January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]] as the figure passed over by Washington in favour of acting President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] following the removal of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].

The Brief relays an unverified political commentary claiming that Machado brought into the [[white-house|White House]] an advisor who had previously insulted [[donald-trump|President Trump]] — specifically, according to a report cited by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], an advisor who once publicly called Trump "an idiot" for accusing former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] of spying on him. Ghost characterises the episode as "Brilliant."

Asked why he was supporting Rodríguez over Machado, Trump answered with an Iraq analogy about the wholesale dismissal of police and generals producing ISIS. Ghost reads this as an assertion that installing Machado as Venezuelan president "would effectively yield the same result."
