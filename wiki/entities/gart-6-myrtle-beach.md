# GART 6: Myrtle Beach

GART 6 was the sixth stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live, ticketed conference series run by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1] Announced on July 26, 2024, the event was scheduled to run from November 14 to 17, 2024 in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.[1]

## Announcement and framing

The announcement opened with the slogan "We are the news, now," which Badlands described as a saying that had come to define a growing movement within the Truth and America First communities and which the network said it had been doing its best to make a reality.[1] Badlands credited reader support with its growth into what it called one of the most-watched independent networks online, carrying dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities across its shows, and presented the continuing tour as taking the network "to the next level."[1]

The promotion explicitly positioned the November dates around the 2024 United States presidential election, telling readers that "whatever happens on November 5, you're going to want to be among like-minded patriots in the aftermath."[1] At the time of publication the speaker list had not been finalised, the announcement carrying only a placeholder heading reading "Speakers to be Announced."[1]

## Programme and ticketing

The event was billed as offering live, in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, live Q&A sessions with Badlands panels, and meet-and-greets with show hosts throughout the weekend.[1] Badlands emphasised the social dimension of the gathering, describing GART as an environment for attendees to form lifelong connections with fellow "Badlanders," which the network called the best community in the movement.[1]

In-person tickets included a Welcome Reception with Badlands Media on the Friday night, breakfast and lunch across the weekend, and open time with other attendees and hosts as the community sought to "level up in the Information War together."[1] A separate VIP package added a Thursday night dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands hosts and personalities to open the festivities.[1] In-person sales were handled through the network's TicketSpice page.[1]

## Virtual attendance

For supporters unable to travel, virtual tickets were sold through a separate TicketSpice page, providing a live stream of all three days of panels together with months of post-event access to panel replays.[1] Virtual purchasers also received a direct invitation to an exclusive "GART: Myrtle" [[telegram|Telegram]] chat in which they could interact with other virtual and in-person attendees and with speakers both before and after the event.[1] Badlands said the Telegram chat would go live closer to the event dates and that invitations, along with stream links, would be sent automatically to the email address used at purchase.[1]
