# Joan Baez

Joan Baez is an American folk singer and longtime political activist. In Badlands Media commentary she is cited as an example of celebrity opposition to the second Trump administration.[1]

## Netflix talk show appearance

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described watching a Netflix talk show featuring Trump-critical guests including actor [[michael-keaton|Michael Keaton]] and Baez, on which Baez stated that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were "attacking democracy," a remark that drew applause from the panel and studio audience.[1] Carlson presented the moment as evidence of what he characterized as [[trump-derangement-syndrome|Trump Derangement Syndrome]] among entertainment figures.[1]

## Spotify catalogue removal

Carlson also recalled that Baez had insisted Spotify remove her catalogue from the platform because podcaster [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] had taken ivermectin, framing the episode as part of a broader pattern of celebrity protest against dissent from official pandemic messaging.[1]

## Assessment in Badlands commentary

Carlson used Baez, alongside other performers making symbolic protest gestures, to argue that such demonstrations carry no personal risk because, in his view, Trump supporters are not violent — in contrast to the vandalism directed at [[tesla|Tesla]] vehicles and dealerships during the same period.[1]
