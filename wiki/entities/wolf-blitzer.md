# Wolf Blitzer

Wolf Blitzer is an American television news anchor described by Badlands Media as a "talking head" who was formerly the Washington correspondent for the *Jerusalem Post*.[1]

## Writing before the Pollard arrest

In a book published just several weeks before the arrest of [[jonathan-pollard|Jonathan Pollard]], Blitzer wrote that there was no real fear in the U.S. government that American Jews were leaking information to [[mossad|Mossad]].[1] Badlands cites the timing to underline how thoroughly the Pollard affair took Washington and Tel Aviv by surprise.[1]
