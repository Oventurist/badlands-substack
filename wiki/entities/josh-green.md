# Josh Green

Josh Green is the Governor of Hawaii. In August 2023 he became a central figure in the public response to the catastrophic wildfires that destroyed much of the town of Lahaina on Maui.[1]

## Response to post-fire land speculation

As reports spread that investors and realtors were cold-calling families who had just lost their homes with below-market offers for their land, Green publicly spoke out against the practice, warning that Lahaina fire victims were being pressured to sell.[1] His intervention followed viral testimony from a local resident who said she "personally" knew "multiple families" who had been "offered money from investors and realtors" in the immediate aftermath of the fire, and who urged victims to record the business names of callers.[1]

The controversy unfolded against a backdrop of rapidly appreciating Maui property values: the average Lahaina home was worth roughly $600,000 at the start of 2020 and approximately $1 million by 2023.[1] Badlands commentary by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] situated Green's warnings within a wider critique of what it termed disaster opportunism.[1]

## Statements on the Lahaina death toll

On Friday afternoon, August 11, 2023, Green told CNN's Wolf Blitzer that it could take weeks to sift through Lahaina's "rubble and ashes" to arrive at a final death toll.[2] "Over the course of the next week, we'll get as close to a complete assessment as possible," he said, adding that "starting tomorrow, we will see a lot more certainty about how many individuals perished."[2] He described the scene as resembling a war zone, noting that "sometimes in natural disasters, or essentially wartime disasters ... you will sometimes discover later that someone has perished, because it was burned to the ground."[2] At the time of those remarks the confirmed toll stood at 80 with roughly 1,700 buildings destroyed.[2] See [[lahaina-wildfires|Lahaina wildfires]].[2]
