# New York State Board of Elections

The New York State Board of Elections is the state agency responsible for administering and enforcing New York's election law, including through its Division of Election Law Enforcement.[1]

## RFK Jr. voter fraud complaint

In January 2025 the Board became the venue for a complaint filed by the left-leaning watchdog group [[accountable-us|Accountable.US]] alleging that [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], then President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominee to lead the Department of Health and Human Services, had committed felony election fraud by voting in the 2024 election.[1] The complaint asked the Board's Division of Election Law Enforcement to investigate Kennedy for "registering for and voting" from "a New York residence at which he does not legally reside."[1]
