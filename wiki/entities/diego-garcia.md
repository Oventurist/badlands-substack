# Diego Garcia

Diego Garcia is the largest island of the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]] in the Indian Ocean and the site of a British airbase operated jointly with the United States. [[rt|RT]] described it as one of Washington's most significant overseas military installations, and [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes in [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]] that it is often characterized as "an unsinkable aircraft carrier."

Under the arrangement approved by London in May 2025, the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] would transfer sovereignty of the archipelago to [[mauritius|Mauritius]] while retaining access to Diego Garcia through a 99-year lease. The Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] now seeks to bypass the British and deal directly with Mauritius to purchase the base, which GhostofBasedPatrickHenry writes "would leave the British sidelined and at the mercy of the United States." While arguing for closing the overwhelming majority of the 800-plus American installations abroad, he identifies Diego Garcia as one of the few whose strategic value justifies retention.
