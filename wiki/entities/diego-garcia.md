# Diego Garcia

Diego Garcia is the largest and most notorious island of the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]] in the Indian Ocean, and the site of a British airbase operated jointly with the United States.[1] RT described it as one of Washington's most significant overseas military installations, and commentators frequently characterize it as "an unsinkable aircraft carrier."[1] A 2002 map of the island reproduced in the June 9, 2026 *Badlands Brief* denotes its military installations.[1]

Under the arrangement approved by London in May 2025, the United Kingdom agreed to transfer sovereignty over the Chagos Islands to [[mauritius|Mauritius]] while retaining access to Diego Garcia through a 99-year lease.[1] U.S. officials have since discussed a proposal, reported by *The Telegraph* and cited by RT, that could lead to the United States purchasing the islands from Mauritius outright after the transfer.[1] [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] had criticized the UK–Mauritius deal as "an act of great stupidity" with national security implications.[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], while favoring the closure of most of the 800-plus American installations abroad, singled out Diego Garcia as an example of a base whose strategic value justifies retention.[1] He argued that a direct US–Mauritius purchase would sideline the British and leave them "at the mercy of the United States."[1]
