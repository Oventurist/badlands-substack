# National Alliance

The National Alliance is a neo-Nazi organization based in West Virginia that features centrally in the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s superseding indictment of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]].[1]

## Role in the SPLC case

Prosecutors allege that a confidential informant identified only as "F-9," embedded in the National Alliance, received more than $1.2 million in SPLC donor funds over roughly two decades at the direction of former SPLC Intelligence Project director [[heidi-beirich|Heidi Beirich]].[1] While receiving those payments, F-9 allegedly continued fundraising for and supporting the National Alliance's activities, a pattern prosecutors characterize as the SPLC financially sustaining the very extremist organizations it publicly denounced.[1]

The indictment additionally claims the SPLC paid another informant, Randolph Dilloway, $6,000 to falsely take responsibility for a 2014 burglary at the National Alliance's West Virginia headquarters.[1]
