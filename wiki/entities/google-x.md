# Google X

Google X is the research-and-development division of Google, described in the July 2023 Badlands Media article "Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason" as the company's "clandestine research-and-development arm." The organization is referenced in the corpus principally through [[mo-gawdat|Mo Gawdat]], its former Chief Business Officer, whose statements on the AI [[technological-singularity|singularity]] supply much of the article's argument.

The article situates Google X within a broader claim about the visibility gap in AI development: the capabilities the general public is permitted to see — "the Chat GPTs of the world" — are described as "toys in comparison to those capabilities being developed by governments, tech R&D firms, and defense contractors." Corporate research arms of this kind, the source argues, will not halt their work under any voluntary moratorium, because doing so "would be both competitive and national security suicide."
