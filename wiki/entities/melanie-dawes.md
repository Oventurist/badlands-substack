# Melanie Dawes

**Dame Melanie Dawes** is the Chief Executive of [[ofcom|Ofcom]], the United Kingdom's communications regulator, appointed shortly before the passage of the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]].[1]

## Statements on the Online Safety Bill

Following the bill's passage through the [[uk-parliament|UK Parliament]] in September 2023, Dawes declared: "Today is a major milestone in the mission to create a safer life online for children and adults in the UK. Everyone at Ofcom feels privileged to be entrusted with this important role, and we're ready to start implementing these new laws."[1] Badlands Media described her as "genuinely gleeful" in delivering these remarks, reading the statement as enthusiasm for a newly expanded censorship mandate rather than for child safety.[1]
