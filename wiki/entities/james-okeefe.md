# James O'Keefe

James O'Keefe is an American undercover video journalist and the founder of the [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]]. In the Badlands Media corpus he functions as a recurring evidentiary source: his hidden-camera recordings are cited as the raw material that later reappears in federal indictments and investigations, particularly on election-integrity questions.

## March 2026 Los Angeles registration drive

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief credits O'Keefe's outfit with undercover documentation, published in March 2026, of a voter registration drive targeting homeless people in [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]]. According to the Brief, the footage showed money changing hands and fake names being entered on registration forms. Commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the O'Keefe material as the precursor to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] indictment announced on May 18, 2026 against [[brenda-lee-brown-armstrong|Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong]], a longtime ballot-initiative signature collector charged with paying individuals — including residents of [[skid-row|Skid Row]] — to register to vote.

## Re-amplification after the June primary

The Brief records that on June 8, 2026 — a day after [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] was reported to have lost the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|Los Angeles jungle primary]] and the second runoff slot was called for [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] — O'Keefe reposted his March video to remind audiences that Los Angeles voter registration had been affected by the documented fraud. Ashe in America builds the chain from that post to the [[fbi|FBI]]'s Skid Row operation of June 18, summarizing the logic as: "Fake names = fake voters = fake ballots = fake counts = fake outcomes = fake elections."
