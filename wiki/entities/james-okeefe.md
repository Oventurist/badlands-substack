# James O'Keefe

James O'Keefe is an American undercover journalist and the founder of [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]], an outlet that specializes in hidden-camera investigations of political organizations, government agencies, and nonprofit groups.[1]

## Skid Row voter registration investigation

In March 2026, O'Keefe Media Group published undercover footage documenting a Los Angeles voter registration drive aimed at homeless people, in which money was reportedly exchanged and fake names were entered onto registration forms.[1] The recordings became a reference point for later official activity: the Department of Justice announced on May 18, 2026 the indictment of a California signature collector accused of paying individuals — including homeless residents of Skid Row — to register to vote, an action that Badlands writers characterized as following O'Keefe's documentation.[1]

On June 8, 2026, one day after the announcement that [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] had lost a jungle primary spot in the Los Angeles race, O'Keefe re-shared his March video, reminding audiences that Los Angeles voter registration had been affected by the documented fraud.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the implication of the footage with the formulation "Fake names = fake voters = fake ballots = fake counts = fake outcomes = fake elections."[1]

## Significance in the Badlands narrative

Within the Badlands framing, O'Keefe's undercover work functions as an early-warning mechanism whose findings are subsequently validated by federal enforcement — in this instance the [[fbi|FBI]] operation on Skid Row involving roughly twenty agents investigating alleged fraud in the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|2026 Los Angeles mayoral election]].[1]

## Phoenix airport migrant bussing video (2023)

In December 2023, amid a surge of citizen-journalist reports alleging that migrants from the southern border were being flown around the country through U.S. airports, O'Keefe posted a video filmed at the Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport drop-off terminal.[2] In it he interviewed a bus driver working for a limo service contracted by an unknown entity to haul migrants from the border to the airport; asked "Are these migrants from the border?", the driver replied, "Correct."[2] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] described the footage as possibly the best evidence yet supporting claims made by X users about the [[phoenix-airport-migrant-bussing-story|Phoenix airport migrant bussing]] allegations.[2]

## Resignation from Project Veritas (February 2023)

In February 2023 O'Keefe delivered a resignation speech to staff at [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]], the undercover-journalism organization he founded; video of the address circulated on Rumble and was featured in the Badlands News Brief of February 22, 2023.[3] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that the organization's board "lied to the staff, O'Keefe, and the public" and engaged in malfeasance suggesting the group had been infiltrated and its weaknesses leveraged to take it out.[3]

Deschamps credited O'Keefe's willingness to speak out with limiting the damage, arguing that otherwise the board "would have smeared a good man" successfully.[3] He also faulted the organization's governance, suspecting its bylaws failed to define its values, procedures and required oaths — protections that, in his view, would have given O'Keefe as founder more power to stop the board from ruining what he had built, in line with the maxim that "rights not invoked have no power."[3]

## Removal from Project Veritas (February 2023)

On Monday, February 20, 2023, O'Keefe announced to his staff that he had been ousted as chief executive of [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]] and removed from its board of directors, delivering the news in an emotional fifteen-minute video.[4] He had earlier been placed on paid leave while the board considered whether to remove him, following reports that a group of his staff had moved to oust him.[4] Employees had expressed dissatisfaction with his management, alleging that he wasted money and was "outright cruel" to staff, according to an internal memo reported by The Daily Beast.[4]

In the video O'Keefe quoted a former board member who had told him years earlier that "Project Veritas will never be stopped from the outside, it will only be because we stopped ourselves," calling the remark prophetic given his situation.[4] He stated that he had "been stripped of my authority as CEO and removed from the board of directors," and argued that nothing about his conduct over the preceding thirteen years had fundamentally changed: "The only thing that has changed is that we broke the biggest story in our organization's history," a reference to the organisation's [[pfizer|Pfizer]] exposé.[4]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[just-human|Just Human]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], summarised the episode tersely: "Well, that's the end of Project Veritas. But certainly not the end of James O'Keefe."[4]

## "Stay tuned" claim about the Bragg case (2023)

In late March 2023 O'Keefe suggested publicly that he had insiders within Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]]'s case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[5] After a political operative speculated that Bragg had likely hidden exculpatory evidence from the Trump grand jury, given that its meetings had been postponed, and asked whether O'Keefe had "got to someone on the inside," O'Keefe replied: "The real question is how many do we have on the inside? Stay tuned."[5]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote in the March 28, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that O'Keefe had not missed a beat since being "railroaded out of his own company," and that being hated by powerful people across the country is what a good American journalist should look like.[5] DeLarme found it unusual for O'Keefe to appear to show his hand and potentially expose sources in advance, offering two readings: a bluff designed to sow mistrust within Bragg's camp, or — in his view more likely — an indication that the operation was complete and had reached the preparation-of-information stage of the [[project-veritas|Veritas]] process.[5]

## Pfizer confrontation (January 2023)

O'Keefe personally conducted the on-camera confrontation that followed [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]]'s undercover recording of [[jordan-trishton-walker|Jordan Trishton Walker]], the man identified as a [[pfizer|Pfizer]] executive describing a "[[directed-evolution|directed evolution]]" plan for COVID-19.[6] Confronted, Walker recanted, described himself as a "liar … trying to impress a person on a date," invoked race in calling for police involvement, and physically attacked O'Keefe and his film crew.[6] The resulting footage drew congressional attention while being subjected to intermittent platform throttling and a partial press blackout, with the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]] removing its article on the sting.[6]
