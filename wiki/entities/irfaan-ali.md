# Irfaan Ali

Irfaan Ali is the president of [[guyana|Guyana]], a small country on the northern coast of South America.[1]

## Rubio visit, March 2025

Ali appeared alongside US Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] at a news conference in Georgetown, the Guyanese capital, in late March 2025, at which Rubio warned there would be "consequences" if [[venezuela|Venezuela]] used forceful actions to assert its claims to resource-rich territory under Guyanese control.[1] Rubio said the US government would steadfastly oppose "the regional threats based on illegitimate territorial claims by a narco-trafficking regime," adding that "there will be consequences for adventurism" and that the US-Guyana partnership would be important in that regard.[1]

The dispute concerns the [[essequibo-dispute|Essequibo region]], which makes up roughly two-thirds of Guyana and includes oil-rich offshore waters where [[exxonmobil|ExxonMobil]] leads production.[1]
