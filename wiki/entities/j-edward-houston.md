# J. Edward Houston

J. Edward Houston was the third partner in the 1985 Miami office building deal involving [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] and [[armando-codina|Armando Codina]], and the man who actually took out the loan that sank [[broward-federal-savings-and-loan|Broward Federal Savings and Loan]].[1]

Houston owned his own thrift, South Florida Savings.[1] He acquired the $4.6 million loan from Broward Federal used to help purchase the 1390 Brickell building alongside Bush and Codina, then defaulted on it when his own savings and loan went bankrupt and needed a bailout.[1] Broward Federal became insolvent in 1988 and the federal government paid more than $4 million to make good the loan; the failure cost taxpayers around $235 million.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents the transaction as a perfect example of the corruption that ran through the whole [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan scandal]].[1]
