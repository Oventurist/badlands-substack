# Harry Dunn

Harry Dunn is a former officer of the [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] who became a prominent public figure through his testimony about the events of January 6, 2021, and who subsequently entered electoral politics.[1]

## January 6 testimony

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, Dunn is described as the Capitol Police officer who lied under oath during the January 6 Committee hearings about the death of officer Brian Sicknick.[1] That characterization reflects the Truth Community's broader contention that the committee's public record was shaped by unchallenged and unverified officer testimony rather than by adversarial fact-finding.[1]

## Role in the Oath Keepers trial testimony dispute

Dunn featured indirectly in a January 2024 controversy over testimony in the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] seditious-conspiracy trial.[2] Capitol Police Special Agent [[david-lazarus|David Lazarus]] testified that he had witnessed Dunn interacting with Oath Keepers members inside the Capitol on January 6, 2021, testimony that helped secure convictions.[2] Journalist [[steve-baker|Steve Baker]] reported that time-stamped closed-circuit footage placed Lazarus elsewhere in the Capitol complex at that moment, indicating the account of the Dunn encounter could not have been accurate.[2]

## Congressional campaign

In January 2024, Dunn was quoted in The Guardian saying, "Today, I'm running for Congress, to stop Trump's Maga extremists and ensure it never happens again."[1] Commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited that quotation while reporting on the [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] impeachment hearing in [[colorado|Colorado]], drawing a line between January 6 narrative production and the ongoing effort to disqualify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from ballots.[1]
