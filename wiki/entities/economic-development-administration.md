# Economic Development Administration

The Economic Development Administration (EDA) is the US Department of Commerce agency that provides grants and assistance for regional economic recovery and development.[1]

## East Palestine recovery funding (2026)

In February 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that his administration would provide $10 million through the Economic Development Administration to help East Palestine, Ohio continue its economic recovery from the 2023 [[east-palestine-train-derailment|train derailment]].[1] The award was described as coming in addition to the administration's long-term health study for the benefit of residents.[1] Trump framed the funding as a corrective to what he called the town's abandonment under former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], writing, "It was horrible what was allowed to happen there, and we are with you for the LONG HAUL."[1]
