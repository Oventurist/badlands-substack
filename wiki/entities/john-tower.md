# John Tower

John Tower was a Republican United States Senator from Texas and a member of the Senate Armed Services Committee who served as vice-chairman of the 1975 [[church-committee|Church Committee]], formally the Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities.[1]

## Role on the Church Committee

Tower was selected to join chairman [[frank-church|Frank Church]] at the head of the committee as part of Majority Leader [[mike-mansfield|Mike Mansfield]] and Republican Leader [[hugh-scott|Hugh Scott]]'s effort to balance experienced and junior members across a variety of political viewpoints.[1] Together with Church he met President [[gerald-ford|Gerald R. Ford]] and his top national security advisors to secure a presidential pledge that the [[white-house|White House]] would cooperate with Senate investigators — access that, while sometimes slow, proved seemingly unprecedented.[1]

## Appended separate view

In a separate view appended to the committee's April 29, 1976 final report, Tower acknowledged the "intelligence excesses" the investigation had documented and the "need for expanded legislative, executive and judicial involvement in intelligence policy and practices."[1] He cautioned, however, that Congress should not "unnecessarily" restrain the president from exercising discretion in the realm of national security — a position that distinguished his conclusions from the committee's more sweeping reform recommendations.[1]
