# Anne Wojcicki

Anne Wojcicki is an American businesswoman, the founder and chief executive officer of the consumer genomics company [[23andme|23andMe]].[1] She is a sister of the former [[youtube|YouTube]] chief executive [[susan-wojcicki|Susan Wojcicki]] and the daughter of the Stanford physicist Stanley Wojcicki; a third sister, Janet, is a pediatrician and anthropologist.[1]

Anne Wojcicki married [[google|Google]] co-founder [[sergey-brin|Sergey Brin]], a connection Badlands Media highlighted in its August 12, 2024 news brief when tracing the overlap between the founding circle of Google and the consumer-genetics industry — describing her as "the sister with everyone's genetic material."[1] The brief presented this network of family, search-engine dominance and genomic data collection as part of what it called the foundation of "dystopia's shadow government."[1]
