# TechCrunch

TechCrunch is an American technology news publication whose reporting on data exposures, cybersecurity lapses and startup activity is periodically cited in independent media roundups.[1]

## Reporting on the 2023 Defense Department email exposure

TechCrunch was the outlet that reported, on February 21, 2023, that the U.S. Department of Defense had secured an exposed server which had been spilling internal U.S. military emails onto the open internet for the previous two weeks.[1] According to the TechCrunch account quoted by Badlands Media, the exposed server was hosted on Microsoft's Azure government cloud for Department of Defense customers — an environment that uses servers physically separated from other commercial customers so that sensitive but unclassified government data can be shared on it.[1] The server formed part of an internal mailbox system storing roughly three terabytes of internal military email, much of it pertaining to U.S. Special Operations Command.[1] A misconfiguration had left the server without a password, so that anyone on the internet could reach the mailbox data through a web browser if they knew the server's IP address.[1]

## Role in the Badlands News Brief

TechCrunch's story was one of several mainstream and independent items excerpted in the Badlands News Brief of February 22, 2023, alongside items from Politico, The Epoch Times, Breitbart News and the Financial Times.[1] Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] used the TechCrunch report as the basis for a commentary noting that the exposure "could be the result of human error or ... not," raising the possibilities that it represented an attempt to compromise U.S. Special Operations Command's civilian network or a means of transferring information that had been temporarily resident there.[1] See [[2023-defense-department-email-server-exposure]] for the incident itself.
