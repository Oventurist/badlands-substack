# Oxfam

Oxfam is an international charity and anti-poverty confederation cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] for its documentation of corporate profiteering during the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]

## "Power, Profits and the Pandemic"

DeLarme describes Oxfam's report, entitled "Power, Profits and the Pandemic," as a remarkable document that laid bare the discrepancy between how much the world's largest companies profited as a direct result of the pandemic and how the rest of the world fared.[1] According to the report, 32 of the world's largest companies saw profits jump by $109 billion (£84.1 billion) in 2020 alone, while globally more than half a billion people dropped below the poverty line.[1]

## Role in the argument

The Oxfam findings are used to support DeLarme's broader claim that COVID-19 produced the largest upward transfer of wealth in human history, alongside the observation that the ten richest men in the world each doubled their wealth during the pandemic and that Amazon chief executive Jeff Bezos increased his wealth by $24 billion in April 2020 alone.[1] He pairs these figures with the closure of myriad small businesses and small-town grocery stores while [[walmart|Walmart]], [[target-corporation|Target]] and virtual meeting platforms such as [[zoom-video-communications|Zoom]], Google Hangouts and Microsoft absorbed the displaced demand — all companies in which [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] are among the largest shareholders.[1]
