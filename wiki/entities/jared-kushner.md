# Jared Kushner

Jared Kushner is an American businessman who served as senior adviser to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and is Trump's son-in-law. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears in [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s June 2023 article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", which names him as the driving force behind the push for a [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]] by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].

Lange introduces Kushner as "Trump's most trusted advisor," describing him as the man tasked with the biggest behind-the-scenes developments in the administration and one who never leaks. The evidentiary basis is a CNBC report from January 2022, which drew on a 250-page release of Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]]'s crypto-related email correspondence covering his four years at the [[treasury-department|Treasury]], obtained by CoinDesk through a Freedom of Information Act request. According to CNBC, the documents show that "one of former President Donald Trump's most trusted and senior advisors was secretly pushing for the White House to lean into crypto, well before skyrocketing crypto prices reignited mainstream interest in the space" — specifically that in 2019, in the middle of a multi-year crypto bear market, Kushner was quietly advocating for a U.S. digital dollar issued by the Fed.

The article quotes the emails directly. "Steven – Would you be open to me bringing a small group of people to have a brainstorm about this topic?" Kushner wrote, adding: "My sense is it could make sense, and also be something that could ultimately change the way we pay out entitlements as well saving us a ton in waste fraud and also in transaction costs…"

CNBC judged Kushner's position prescient, noting that few countries were seriously exploring national digital currencies in mid-2019 whereas at least 87 countries representing more than 90 percent of global GDP were doing so by 2022, according to [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]] research. Lange treats Kushner's advocacy as evidence that the CBDC program originated inside Trump's circle rather than with the [[cabal]], and therefore as a plan to dismantle rather than extend elite control of the financial system.

## Board of Peace and the Gaza master plan (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Kushner was a named participant in the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] at its charter ratification ceremony in Davos, and that he presented a multi-phase reconstruction and economic plan for [[gaza|Gaza]] during Thursday's ceremony, aiming to raise the Strip's GDP and increase average annual household incomes over the next decade. Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the presentation as showing off the "Master Plan for Gaza" first revealed around September 2025, and noted Kushner's closing callout, in which he said Israelis needed to stop persecuting pro-Palestine activists online and "give Trump 30 days."

Kushner also met, together with [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] during the Davos week; Kremlin aide [[yuri-ushakov|Yuri Ushakov]] described the talks as "substantive, constructive and very frank."
