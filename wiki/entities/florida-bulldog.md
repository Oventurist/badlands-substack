# Florida Bulldog

Florida Bulldog is an investigative news outlet whose reporting on the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] has focused on alleged Saudi Arabian links to the hijackers and on the withholding of intelligence by United States agencies.[1]

## 2023 reporting on the 9/11 coverup declaration

In April 2023 Florida Bulldog published an account of a sworn declaration by [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]], an investigator with the Department of Defense's [[military-commissions-defense-organization|Military Commissions Defense Organization]], in which ex-[[fbi|FBI]] agents accused top [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and FBI officials of a 9/11 coverup and alleged that the CIA used Saudi intermediaries for illegal domestic spying operations.[1] The outlet obtained a brief interview with Canestraro, who confirmed he was part of the defense team for Guantanamo detainee [[ammar-al-baluchi|Ammar al-Baluchi]].[1]

The report described the declaration's central allegation: that CIA officials, including Director George Tenet, intentionally kept from the FBI intelligence that known al Qaeda operatives and future hijackers Nawaf al Hazmi and Khalid al Mihdhar had entered the United States at Los Angeles shortly after an al Qaeda summit in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia in early January 2000.[1] Florida Bulldog noted that the accounts support the theory, never fully explored by either the 9/11 Commission or Congress, that the CIA stayed silent because it was working with its Saudi counterpart to recruit the two men as informants.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] republished and commented on the story in its April 17, 2023 news brief.[1]
