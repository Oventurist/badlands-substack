# James Biden

James Biden is a brother of United States President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and a figure in congressional investigations into the Biden family's business dealings.[1]

## House Oversight investigation

As reported in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] covering March 14, 2023, United States banks had flagged more than 150 suspicious activity reports (SARs) generated by [[hunter-biden|Hunter]] and James Biden, involving "large" amounts of money tagged for further review by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]].[1] The Treasury agreed to release those reports to the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], which was investigating the Biden family business for nine alleged violations including wire fraud and money laundering.[1]

The records were expected to provide detail on the foreign business transactions of Hunter, James, and Frank Biden, and on the broader question of whether Joe Biden remained compromised by foreign governments — particularly communist China — through his family's business activity.[1]
