# David Faber

David Faber is a CNBC journalist and co-host of the network's program "Squawk on the Street," broadcast from the floor of the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]].[1]

## Public Square opening bell (2023)

During the July 20, 2023 opening-bell ceremony marking the public listing of the conservative marketplace [[public-square|Public Square]], Faber was reported to have covered his ears as the exchange floor chanted "USA! USA!", with the broadcast turning instead to a discussion of home prices.[1] [[jim-cramer|Jim Cramer]] was described as reacting with similar disapproval.[1]
