# Federal Railroad Administration

The Federal Railroad Administration (FRA) is the agency within the US Department of Transportation responsible for regulating railroad safety.[1]

## East Palestine derailment

The FRA joined the [[national-transportation-safety-board|National Transportation Safety Board]] in examining the February 3, 2023 [[east-palestine-train-derailment|East Palestine train derailment]] of a [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern]] freight train near the Ohio–Pennsylvania border.[1] The two agencies determined that a failed and overheated wheel bearing on a railcar caused the derailment, which spilled and burned hazardous materials including vinyl chloride and forced the evacuation of residents within roughly one mile.[1]

## Missing ammonium nitrate shipment (2023)

The FRA was one of the bodies investigating the [[2023-missing-ammonium-nitrate-shipment|disappearance of approximately 61,000 pounds of ammonium nitrate]] from a railcar that traveled from Cheyenne, Wyoming, to a rail stop in the Mojave Desert in California in April 2023.[2] The car, loaded and sealed by [[dyno-nobel|Dyno Nobel]], arrived still sealed but completely empty, and the FRA's inquiry ran alongside those of the [[california-public-utilities-commission|California Public Utilities Commission]], [[union-pacific|Union Pacific]] and the shipper itself, with the railcar returned to Wyoming for inspection.[2]
