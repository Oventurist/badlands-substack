# Federal Railroad Administration

The Federal Railroad Administration (FRA) is the agency within the United States Department of Transportation responsible for railroad safety regulation and enforcement.

## Determination on the East Palestine derailment

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the FRA and the [[national-transportation-safety-board|National Transportation Safety Board]] (NTSB) jointly determined that the February 3, 2023 [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern]] derailment in [[east-palestine|East Palestine]], Ohio was caused by a failed/overheated wheel bearing on a railcar. The 38-car derailment, 20 of whose cars carried hazardous materials including vinyl chloride, prompted an evacuation of the surrounding area and became the subject of subsequent wrongful-death litigation.
