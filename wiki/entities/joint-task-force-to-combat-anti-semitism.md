# Joint Task Force to Combat Anti-Semitism

The Joint Task Force to Combat Anti-Semitism is a Trump administration interagency body whose work includes reviewing federal funding relationships with American universities.[1]

## Harvard and Columbia reviews

On March 31, 2025, the administration announced it would initiate a review of federal contracts with [[harvard-university|Harvard University]], describing it as an inquiry similar to the one conducted at [[columbia-university|Columbia University]], which had $400 million in funding paused and had yet to see it restored despite agreeing to all of the administration's stated demands.[1] The Departments of Education and Health and Human Services and the US General Services Administration were tasked with assessing $255.6 million in contracts between the federal government and Harvard and its affiliates, along with more than $8.7 billion in multiyear grant commitments.[1] The review was to determine whether any funding should be paused, and was described as part of the efforts conducted by the task force.[1]
