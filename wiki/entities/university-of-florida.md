# University of Florida

The **University of Florida** (UF) is the flagship public university of the State University System of Florida, and in 2024 became a national test case for state-level restrictions on diversity, equity and inclusion programming in higher education.[1]

## Elimination of DEI positions, March 2024

In a memo sent on Friday, March 1, 2024, the university announced it was firing all employees in [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|Diversity, Equity and Inclusion]] positions and administrative appointments.[1] The memo stated: "To comply with the Florida Board of Governor's regulation 9.016 on prohibited expenditures, the University of Florida has closed the Office of the Chief Diversity Officer, eliminated DEI positions and administrative appointments, and halted DEI-focused contracts with outside vendors."[1]

Under the direction of UF Human Resources, employees whose positions were eliminated were to receive the university's standard twelve weeks of pay, and were "allowed and encouraged to apply, between now and Friday, April 19, for expedited consideration for different positions currently posted with the university."[1] UF HR committed to fast-tracking the interview process and providing an answer on all applications within the twelve-week window.[1]

The memo further stated that the Office of the Chief Financial Officer would reallocate the approximately $5 million in funds previously reported to Tallahassee for DEI expenses into a faculty recruitment fund.[1]

## Political context

Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], who had made halting DEI a cornerstone of his effort to overhaul education in the state, responded on X: "Florida is where DEI goes to die."[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that the Florida state legislature had been working to eliminate what he termed Marxist and communist programs for the better part of three years, and described the terminations as "well-timed Actuals injected into the news cycle."[1]
