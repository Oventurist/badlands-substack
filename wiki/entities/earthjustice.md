# Earthjustice

Earthjustice is a United States non-profit public interest environmental law organisation.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief names Earthjustice, alongside the [[environmental-defense-fund|Environmental Defense Fund]], among the environmental groups that strongly condemned the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's rescission of the [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]]'s 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]]. These groups characterised the action as a major assault on federal climate policy, warned of increased pollution, thousands of premature deaths, higher health and fuel costs and accelerated climate impacts, and pledged immediate court challenges based on settled science and the Supreme Court precedent of [[massachusetts-v-epa|Massachusetts v. EPA]].
