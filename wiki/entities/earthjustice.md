# Earthjustice

Earthjustice is an environmental legal advocacy organization that opposed the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s 2026 rescission of the [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]]'s [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]].[1]

## Opposition to the 2026 rescission

Along with the [[environmental-defense-fund|Environmental Defense Fund]], Earthjustice strongly condemned the action as a major assault on federal climate policy, warning of increased pollution, thousands of premature deaths, higher health and fuel costs, and accelerated climate impacts, and pledged immediate court challenges based on settled science and Supreme Court precedent upholding the finding.[1]
