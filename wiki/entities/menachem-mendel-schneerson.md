# Menachem Mendel Schneerson

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, commonly referred to as "the Rebbe," was a leading Jewish religious figure whose teachings on the coming of the messiah have been cited as an influence on Israeli political leaders.[1]

## Influence on Benjamin Netanyahu

Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] has publicly described Schneerson as "the most influential man of our time." The two men had a documented interaction in 1990, when Netanyahu served as Deputy Foreign Minister under Prime Minister Yitzhak Shamir; Netanyahu shared a photograph of that meeting publicly in July of the preceding year relative to the article's publication.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the recorded exchange between Schneerson and Netanyahu concerned the arrival of the Jewish messiah, and that Schneerson — along with many others, including members of Netanyahu's security cabinet — held that the construction of the [[third-temple|Third Temple]] would summon the messiah into the physical realm.[1]
