# Hillary Rudy

Hillary Rudy is the Deputy Elections Director for the Colorado Department of Elections, where she has been employed since 2006, and a witness in the 2023 trial over whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] may appear on Colorado's 2024 presidential ballot.[1]

## Testimony in the Colorado ballot trial

Rudy took the stand on the third day of the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification trial]], testifying to the processes and the teams responsible for granting or denying ballot access, as well as other procedures within the Department of Elections.[1] Her appearance produced an unusual courtroom dynamic: she was cross-examined by Scott Gessler, lead attorney for Trump and a former Colorado Secretary of State under whom Rudy had previously worked. Observers watching the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] livestream described their exchanges as cold.[1]

Following Rudy's testimony, Gessler moved for a directed verdict; the court recessed for lunch, and Judge Sarah B. Wallace denied the motion upon returning.[1] The defense then called [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[1]
