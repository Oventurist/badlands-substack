# Curtis Sliwa

Curtis Sliwa is the founder of the Guardian Angels, a volunteer street-patrol organization, and a long-running figure in New York City politics.[1] He headlined the anti-migrant demonstration held outside Gracie Mansion, the New York City mayor's residence, on Sunday, August 27, 2023, addressing the crowd with a microphone and PA system.[1]

## Gracie Mansion protest

Speaking against Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]]' handling of the city's migrant influx, Sliwa said of the arrivals: "These migrants have jumped the queue," adding that if he were a migrant offered the chance to skip the line, stay in a hotel and receive three meals a day — more than homeless Americans or down-on-their-luck veterans receive — "you're damn right they're gonna keep coming!"[1] The rally, reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and the *New York Post*, was disrupted by black-clad [[antifa|Antifa]] counterprotesters, producing scuffles that police were called to contain.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated Sliwa's reappearance as a signal rather than a coincidence, writing that it was "hard to believe this is organic, especially since the non-Antifa side featured Curtis Sliwa, who I haven't seen in the news since the last Summer of Love."[1] In that reading, the confrontation was staged groundwork for immigration-themed street unrest in the following election year.[1]

## 2025 mayoral race and Trump's spoiler argument

Sliwa remained the Republican candidate in the [[new-york-city-mayoral-election-2025|2025 New York City mayoral election]] through election eve, when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] endorsed independent [[andrew-cuomo|Andrew Cuomo]] and declared that "a vote for Curtis Sliwa is a vote for [[zohran-mamdani|Mamdani]]."[2] Trump's post urged New Yorkers to consolidate behind Cuomo to block the Democratic nominee, whom he labeled a Communist.[2]
