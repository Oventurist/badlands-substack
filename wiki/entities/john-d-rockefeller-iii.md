# John D. Rockefeller III

**John D. Rockefeller III** was the eldest of the five sons of [[john-d-rockefeller-jr|John D. Rockefeller Jr.]] and a leading figure in the family's international philanthropy.[1]

## Philanthropic career

He became chairman of the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and guided millions of dollars to international agencies such as the India International Centre and the International House of Japan.[1] His personal money went to an extensive Oriental art collection and to the creation of the Population Council, a center concerned with overpopulation and family planning — an interest Badlands Media links to the family's longstanding involvement with [[eugenics]] and population control.[1]

## Death and succession

He died in 1978.[1] His son, John "Jay" Davison Rockefeller, carried on the family's political interests by serving as governor of West Virginia.[1]
