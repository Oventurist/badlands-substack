# Recep Tayyip Erdogan

## Signaling role in US-Israel relations

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued in July 2026 that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] repeatedly uses Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan to signal his intentions toward [[israel|Israel]].[1] He noted that in September 2025, when Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] sought to "crash the White House" during the UN General Assembly, Trump reacted by inviting Erdogan to the White House the day before Netanyahu was scheduled to address the assembly of nations; with Netanyahu again inviting himself in 2026, Trump was again elevating Erdogan and [[turkey|Turkey]], praising the country as a "tremendous ally."[1]
