# Julian Amery

Julian Amery, son of [[leo-amery]], was a British political figure who, according to the article, worked closely with unreconstructed Nazis after World War Two as part of [[operation-gladio]].[1]

## Nazi transplants to the Middle East

The article states that it was under Julian Amery that Nazis such as [[otto-skorzeny]], Walter Rauff and Alois Brunner were transplanted to the Middle East, where some are said to have worked for [[mossad]] after the CIA played a direct role in establishing that organization in 1951.[1]

## Suez and the Muslim Brotherhood

In a footnote, the article recounts that after the short-lived 1954 Egypt–UK agreement over the Suez Canal and British basing rights, Britain, France and Israel by 1956 concocted a plot against Egypt aimed at toppling Nasser and seizing the canal, a conspiracy that enlisted the [[muslim-brotherhood]].[1] Citing Stephen Dorril's *MI6: Fifty Years of Special Operations*, it says two British intelligence agents, Col. Neil McLean and Julian Amery, helped MI6 organize a clandestine anti-Nasser opposition and made contact in Geneva with members of the Muslim Brotherhood, informing only MI6 of the demarche and keeping it secret from the rest of the Suez Group.[1] Amery is described as directly linked to the Gladio networks.[1]
