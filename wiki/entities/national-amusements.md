# National Amusements

National Amusements Inc. is the privately held holding company that owned the controlling stake in [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] during the period when the media conglomerate explored a merger with [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]].[1] Because National Amusements held voting control rather than merely an economic interest, any change of control at Paramount Global required the assent of the family holding company and its principal, Shari Redstone.[1]

## Role in the December 2023 merger talks

When Warner Bros. Discovery chief executive [[david-zaslav|David Zaslav]] pursued a possible combination of the two companies in December 2023, he met not only with Paramount Global chief executive [[bob-bakish|Bob Bakish]] but also spoke separately with Shari Redstone, in her capacity as the controlling owner through National Amusements, about a potential combination.[1] The talks, first reported by [[axios|Axios]] and confirmed by *[[variety|Variety]]*, were described as very preliminary, with bankers engaged on both sides and terms undetermined.[1] Representatives for Paramount Global and Warner Bros. Discovery declined to comment.[1]

The contemplated transaction would have pooled television, film, sports, and streaming assets — in particular combining Paramount+ and Max to compete with [[netflix|Netflix]], [[disney|Disney]]'s Disney+, and Hulu — placing National Amusements at the center of one of the largest proposed consolidations in the legacy media industry.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the episode as a data point in a wider macroeconomic pattern, arguing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that "consolidation precedes collapse" and that a smaller firm entering merger talks with a larger one typically does so out of necessity rather than strategic choice.[1]
