# Mar-a-Lago

**Mar-a-Lago** is [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Palm Beach, Florida estate and the venue from which he delivered the November 2022 address announcing his candidacy for the 2024 presidential election.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## The November 2022 announcement

Badlands commentary treated the Mar-a-Lago speech as a set piece whose function had been anticipated in advance. The author had argued, before the speech was delivered, that its purpose would be twofold: to separate the MAGA and America First wheat from the [[con-inc|Con Inc.]] controlled-opposition chaff, and to undercut any political move being entertained by other influential figures gathering directed political energy from the conservative base in an attempt to sweep that base out from under Trump's leadership.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

Expectations that Trump would use the stage to audit the 2022 midterms or to announce the arrest of the sitting president were dismissed as implausible, given that the audience was highly partisan and the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] had already branded it extremist.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) The speech contained no major revelations but did include what the author called tantalizing tidbits, notably the statement that "the voting will be much, much different in 2024" and a warning that things would get "much worse" before people "see clearly."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## February 2026 perimeter breach

On Sunday, February 22, 2026, an armed man breached the secure perimeter of Mar-a-Lago at approximately 1:38 AM ET near the property's north gate.[2] The suspect, identified as 21-year-old [[austin-tucker-martin|Austin Tucker Martin]] of North Carolina, was shot and killed by [[us-secret-service|US Secret Service]] agents and a Palm Beach County sheriff's deputy after he raised a shotgun into a shooting position; he had also been carrying a fuel canister, which he dropped when ordered to do so.[2] Martin had been reported missing by his family days earlier and had reportedly fixated on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] stories according to text messages obtained by news outlets, with the FBI and local authorities investigating his motive.[2]

[[donald-trump|President Trump]] and First Lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] were at the White House in Washington, DC at the time and were not at the estate.[2] Addressing the incident publicly the following Monday, Trump remarked, "I don't know how long I'll be around, got a lot of people gunning for me, don't I, hon?"[2]

## The August 2022 FBI raid and internal DOJ doubts

The [[fbi|FBI]] raided Mar-a-Lago on August 8, 2022, in a search personally approved by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]].[3] Two days later, senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] emailed National Security Division attorney [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] questioning whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had declassified the seized documents, noting that while standard declassification procedures exist, "the President as Commander in Chief" is not necessarily bound by them — an analogy she drew to the pardon power.[3]

The email, obtained by Just the News and discovered during the Department's own review of federal law enforcement actions, was reported in May 2026 as evidence of early internal doubts about the raid's legal footing.[3] The search followed prior assertions from Trump's legal team, including a May 2022 letter from attorney [[evan-corcoran|Evan Corcoran]], that Trump possessed broad declassification authority and had issued a standing order treating documents removed to his residence as declassified.[3] Badlands treated the disclosure as another marker of the ongoing [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization]] investigation.[3]

## Role in the SCIF argument

Badlands commentary on the August 2023 SCIF dispute stressed that special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s case rests on the accusation that classified documents in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s possession at Mar-a-Lago were kept in a facility certified for such use only during his presidency — which [[just-human|Just Human]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued made the fight over granting Trump a [[sensitive-compartmented-information-facility|SCIF]] for his defense particularly absurd.[4]

## Venue of the 2017 Xi summit (Lange, 2023)

Badlands Media's "The Master Builder" (February 2, 2023) reports that [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] was credited with arranging an April 2017 summit between [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Mar-a-Lago.[5] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] emphasizes that the property is outfitted with a [[sensitive-compartmented-information-facility|Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility]], meaning the two leaders would have had no difficulty communicating in private — a point central to the article's claim that Beijing sought a channel to Trump beyond the reach of the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[5]

## The August 2022 FBI search

On August 8, 2022, the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] descended on the Mar-a-Lago estate to execute a search warrant authorised by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] and approved by Magistrate Judge [[bruce-reinhart|Bruce Reinhart]], following a referral from the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]].[6] The redacted affidavit later described boxes shuffling in and out of the property's halls while the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] observed, and concluded that classified documents had been deliberately concealed and removed there.[6] The estate subsequently became the factual centre of the [[2023-trump-classified-documents-indictment|37-count indictment]] of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and, through the July 27, 2023 superseding indictment, of estate staff [[walt-nauta|Walt Nauta]] and [[carlos-de-oliveira|Carlos De Oliveira]].[6] [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]]'s one-year retrospective on the [[mar-a-lago-raid|raid]] treated it as a defining episode in what he called the erosion of America.[6]
