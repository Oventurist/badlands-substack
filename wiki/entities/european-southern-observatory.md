# European Southern Observatory

The European Southern Observatory (ESO) is an intergovernmental astronomy organization that operates the Very Large Telescope in Chile, one of the world's biggest telescopes.[1]

## 2025 Sculptor galaxy image

In June 2025, astronomers used the ESO's Chile-based Very Large Telescope to compile 50 hours of observations into an ultra-detailed image of the [[sculptor-galaxy|Sculptor galaxy]] (NGC 253), rendered in roughly 4,000 different colours corresponding to specific wavelengths in the optical spectrum.[1] Because galactic components emit light differently across the spectrum, the resulting dataset offers unprecedented detail on the inner workings of an entire galaxy, from star formation to the large-scale motion of interstellar gas.[1]
