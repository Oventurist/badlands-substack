# Scouting America

Scouting America, formerly known as the Boy Scouts of America, is the principal American scouting organization. Its relationship with the U.S. military dates back more than a century, and the armed forces have formally supported the Scouts since 1937.[1]

## 2025 dispute with the Department of War

In November 2025 the organization responded publicly to a reported memo from Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] seeking to cut military ties with the group, saying it was "surprised and disappointed" by the potential policy change.[1] In its statement, Scouting America emphasized that "the Scouting movement has had a strong relationship with our nation's military going back more than a century," citing the support of the West Virginia National Guard at National Jamborees and Scout troops that "provide stability for the children of military families deployed around the globe."[1] It also noted that "an enormous percentage of those in our military academies are Scouts and Eagle Scouts."[1]

Hegseth was reported to be upset with the organization over its promotion of diversity, equity and inclusion. His memo, first reported by NPR, accused Scouting America of attacking boy-friendly spaces and of being "genderless."[1]
