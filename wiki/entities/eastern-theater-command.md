# Eastern Theater Command

The Eastern Theater Command is the People's Liberation Army command responsible for the military zone facing Taiwan and the East China Sea, and is the formation that conducts and publicly comments on Chinese operations in the [[taiwan-strait|Taiwan Strait]].[1]

## April 2023 response to the Milius transit

After the [[uss-milius|USS Milius]] transited the Taiwan Strait on April 16, 2023, China protested the following day.[1] A statement attributed to Eastern Theater Command spokesperson Shi Yilu called the U.S. transit "public hype" and said the command was ready at any time to "resolutely safeguard the country's sovereignty, safety, and regional peace and stability."[1]

The protest came shortly after the command had concluded large-scale air and sea drills simulating an encirclement of Taiwan, exercises Beijing described as a "serious warning" following [[tsai-ing-wen|Tsai Ing-wen]]'s April 5 meeting with [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1]
