# Bureau of Economic Analysis

The Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) is the United States federal agency, part of the Department of Commerce, responsible for national economic accounts including gross domestic product and, jointly with the [[us-census-bureau|Census Bureau]], the monthly international trade report.

## November 2025 trade deficit

According to the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]], the BEA and Census Bureau released the *U.S. International Trade in Goods and Services* report on January 29, 2026, showing that the trade deficit increased 94.6 percent from October to November 2025 during ongoing trade and tariff negotiations. Reporting noted that the October 2025 figure had been the lowest deficit since 2009. The data landed alongside a 2 to 3 percent rise in oil prices driven by reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was weighing military strikes on [[iran|Iran]], and a sharp selloff in software equities led by [[servicenow|ServiceNow]].
