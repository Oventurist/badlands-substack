# National Students for Justice in Palestine

National Students for Justice in Palestine (NSJP) is a pro-Palestinian student organization with chapters at American universities. Following the October 7, 2023 attacks, Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] forced the deactivation of the group's University of South Florida chapter within days.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Reuters reported that Florida's university system, working with DeSantis, ordered colleges to shut down the organization, "marking the first U.S. state to outlaw the group whose national leadership backed Hamas' attack on Israel."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] notes that the Florida chapter in question did not itself make the contested claims; the national chapter stated that it supported Hamas's actions and considered them justified, citing decades of brutality from the Israeli government and military.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) DeLarme describes that stance as reprehensible and one-sided but insists it remains protected speech under the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]], summarizing: "Repellent speech is still free speech."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The deactivation is presented as the opening move in a wave of Republican-sponsored speech restrictions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## Alleged Soros funding of the 2024 campus encampments

In April 2024 the New York Post reported that [[george-soros|George Soros]] and allied left-wing funders were paying agitators fuelling an explosion of radical anti-Israel protests at colleges across the United States.[2] The protests began when students took over [[columbia-university|Columbia University]]'s Morningside Heights campus lawn, and copycat tent cities were subsequently established at institutions including [[harvard-university|Harvard]], [[yale-university|Yale]], Berkeley, the Ohio State University and Emory in Georgia — all of them, according to the Post, organized by branches of the Soros-funded Students for Justice in Palestine, with students clashing with police at some campuses.[2] The Post stated that the SJP parent organization has been funded by a network of nonprofits ultimately financed by, among others, Soros.[2]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry treated the funding revelation as an example of paid operatives deployed to generate angst and animosity, arguing that such campaigns are designed to control both sides of a conflict and to draw in "useful idiots" — an application of the [[controlled-opposition|controlled opposition]] framework.[2]
