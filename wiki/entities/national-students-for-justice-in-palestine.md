# National Students for Justice in Palestine

Students for Justice in Palestine (SJP) is a pro-Palestinian student movement with chapters on many American college campuses. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," the deactivation of its University of South Florida chapter is cited as a leading example of conservative-led censorship after October 7.

## Deactivation of the USF chapter

The article reports that Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], within days of the October 7, 2023 attack, "forced the deactivation of the National Students for Justice in Palestine's University of South Florida chapter." It quotes Reuters: "Florida's university system, working with Governor Ron DeSantis, ordered colleges on Tuesday to shut down a pro-Palestinian student organization, marking the first U.S. state to outlaw the group whose national leadership backed Hamas' attack on Israel."

The article notes the local chapter had not made the statements at issue — rather, "their national chapter did say that they supported the actions taken by HAMAS, and that those actions were justified, citing decades of brutality from the Israeli government and military." The author calls this stance "reprehensible and one-sided" but insists "it is still protected speech, whether or not you or I like it," adding: "Repellent speech is still free speech."
