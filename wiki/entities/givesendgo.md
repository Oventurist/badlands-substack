# GiveSendGo

GiveSendGo is a crowdfunding platform that bills itself as "The #1 Free Christian Fundraising Site" and which, by 2023, had become the principal venue for legal-defense fundraising by January 6 defendants.[1]

## Role in January 6 fundraising

Associated Press reporting relayed by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noted that most January 6 fundraising efforts appear on GiveSendGo, which "has become a haven for Jan. 6 defendants barred from using mainstream crowdfunding sites, including GoFundMe, to raise money."[1] Dozens of defendants set up online appeals for help with legal fees; prosecutors conceded that soliciting help for attorney expenses is legitimate, but in some cases questioned where funds were going because many defendants had government-funded counsel.[1] The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] pursued forfeiture of donations in cases such as that of [[daniel-goodwyn|Daniel Goodwyn]], and asked judges to enhance fines to offset supporter donations.[1]

## Contrast drawn in commentary

ZeroHedge contrasted this posture with the treatment of the 2020 George Floyd riots, when protesters benefited from a bail fund endorsed by [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]
