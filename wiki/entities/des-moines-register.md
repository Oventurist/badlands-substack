# Des Moines Register

The Des Moines Register is an Iowa newspaper best known nationally for the Iowa Poll, conducted by pollster [[ann-selzer|J. Ann Selzer]].[1]

## 2024 election poll and litigation

The Register published Selzer's final pre-election survey showing Kamala Harris ahead of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by three points in Iowa, a state Trump subsequently carried by 14 points.[1] On December 16, 2024, Trump sued the newspaper and Selzer in Polk County, Iowa, alleging violations of Iowa's consumer fraud statutes through deceptive polling.[1] The suit sought damages, an injunction against further "deceptive polls," and disclosure of the underlying methodology and data.[1]

Badlands commentary observed that the outlet was standing by Selzer, the poll and its reporting, and predicted that position would not hold.[1] The case was described as part of Trump's escalating legal campaign against media organizations, which also included ABC, CBS, journalist Bob Woodward and the Pulitzer Prize board.[1]

## 2024 Iowa Poll miss

The Register's final pre-election Iowa Poll in November 2024 showed [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] leading [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] 47–44 percent among likely voters, a result widely amplified by other outlets; Trump went on to win the state 56–43 percent, a [[2024-des-moines-register-iowa-poll-miss|16-point miss]].[2] Pollster [[ann-selzer|J. Ann Selzer]] subsequently ended her election polling operation and acknowledged the "big miss."[2] Kristin Roberts, chief content officer of Gannett Media, which owns the paper, said the Iowa Poll would "evolve" and conceded that the Register "did not deliver on that promise" of trusted content.[2]
