# Joe Francescon

**Joe Francescon** is a U.S. national security official whose nomination as deputy director of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] was withdrawn by the [[white-house|White House]] in December 2025.[1]

## Career

Francescon began working for the NSA in 2008 and subsequently served in roles on the White House National Security Council and at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] In August 2025, Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] designated him deputy director of the NSA, a designation approved by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Withdrawn nomination

Nebraska Republican Representative Don Bacon said on Friday, December 12, 2025, that the White House was withdrawing the nomination, just as the agency's acting deputy director was set to retire at the end of the month.[1] The White House offered no comment on the withdrawal. The move followed a disclosure by Trump ally [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] that Francescon had donated to the 2023 campaign of Colorado Democratic Representative [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]].[1]
