# True Detective

***True Detective*** is an [[hbo|HBO]] anthology crime series that Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] discussed as a case study in entertainment-industry symbolism.[1]

## Season one

The 2014 first season follows two detectives investigating the ritualistic rape and murder of a young, troubled woman in a case that appears Satanic in nature.[1] The victim's corpse is displayed with antlers fixed to her head and a spiral pattern painted on her back.[1] Carlson wrote that the antler imagery is difficult to separate from the well-known photograph of a Rothschild party at which a woman wears a deer's head complete with antlers, and that the spiral closely resembles the emblem the [[fbi|FBI]] identifies as meaning "little boy lover."[1]

Carlson noted that when he first watched the season he was, in his words, fully asleep — unaware of symbolism, the Deep State Cabal, or international Satanic pedophile rings.[1]

## Season four and the DARPA link

Carlson found the fourth season, a murder mystery set in northern Alaska during the period of round-the-clock winter darkness, a disappointment, which prompted him to rewatch the first season.[1] He went on to connect the series to artificial intelligence, asking what the odds were that [[darpa|DARPA]] would name an AI program after the place where children were ritualistically murdered in *True Detective*.[1]
