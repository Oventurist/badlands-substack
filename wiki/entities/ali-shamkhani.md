# Ali Shamkhani

Ali Shamkhani is a senior adviser to Iran's supreme leader and a long-serving figure in the Islamic Republic's national security establishment.[1]

## Statement on missile capabilities (February 2026)

On Wednesday, February 11, 2026, during an event marking the 47th anniversary of the Islamic revolution, Shamkhani declared that "The Islamic Republic's missile capabilities are non-negotiable."[1] The statement, reported by Al Jazeera and consistent with Reuters reporting the same week, restated Tehran's position as US and Iranian officials continued indirect nuclear talks held in Oman, with a further round expected.[1]

The remarks came the day after Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] met President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Washington for closed-door discussions that included Iran's nuclear program.[1] [[iran|Iran]] holds that it retains the right to enrich uranium under international agreements, while US officials maintain that preventing Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon remains a policy objective.[1] Israeli officials have separately raised concerns about Iran's ballistic missile program and its support for regional armed groups.[1]
