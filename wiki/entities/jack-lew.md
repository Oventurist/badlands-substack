# Jack Lew

Jack Lew is an American official who served as United States Ambassador to Israel during the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]

## Role in the June 2024 arms-delivery dispute

In June 2024 Lew figured in the dispute between Washington and Jerusalem over paused American weapons shipments to Israel.[1] Israeli government spokesman David Mencer acknowledged during a Wednesday briefing that a planned high-level meeting between American and Israeli security officials in Washington had been cancelled, but said that Lew had reassured Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that the weapons in question were being delivered.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] subsequently issued a rebuttal to reporting from [[axios|Axios]] and Hebrew-language outlets, clarifying that the meeting had only been rescheduled because of a timing issue rather than cancelled.[1]

The episode followed a video posted online by Netanyahu on the Tuesday, in which he claimed the Biden administration had been withholding certain weapons and urged the White House to resume shipments; press secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] responded hours later that the administration did not know what Netanyahu was referring to.[1] Unconfirmed reports earlier that week had suggested Washington assured the Israeli government that deliveries would soon resume, after the administration paused some arms shipments in early May.[1]
