# Joshua Weil

Joshua Weil is a Democratic politician and public school educator in Osceola County, Florida, who won his party's primary to compete in the April 2025 special election for Florida's 6th Congressional District.[1]

## 2025 special election

Weil faced Republican [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]], a member of the Florida Senate representing the 19th District based in Brevard County, in the contest to replace [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]], who resigned his House seat upon becoming national security adviser to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Also on the ballot were Andrew Parrott as a Libertarian, Randall Terry as a non-party-affiliated candidate, and Chuck Sheridan as a write-in candidate.[1]

The district covers all of Putnam County, all of Flagler County, and parts of St. Johns County, including Butler Beach, Crescent Beach, Flagler Estates, Hastings, St. Augustine Shores and St. Augustine South.[1] See [[florida-6th-congressional-district-special-election]].
