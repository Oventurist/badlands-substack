# Frederick Schwarz

Frederick Schwarz served as Chief Counsel to the 1975 [[church-committee|Church Committee]], the Senate select committee investigating abuses by U.S. intelligence agencies.[1]

## The NSA general counsel remark

Schwarz is best known in Badlands Media's account of the committee for recounting a statement made to him at the outset of the investigations by the general counsel of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]]: "...The constitution does not apply to the National Security Agency."[1] The remark is presented as emblematic of the legal posture the committee encountered inside the intelligence community, and as context for the committee's eventual finding that intelligence agencies had undermined the constitutional rights of citizens because the framers' checks and balances "have not been applied."[1]
