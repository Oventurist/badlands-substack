# Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus

**Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus** is the Director-General of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 Tedros is quoted on the [[ebola|Ebola]] outbreak in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]] and [[uganda|Uganda]], saying that the delay in detection means authorities are "playing catch-up with a very fast-moving epidemic" and that the outbreak is currently outpacing scaled-up operations. His agency was coordinating surveillance, isolation and contact monitoring with the Congolese and Ugandan governments and partners, backed by roughly $500 million pledged for response and prevention. Badlands commentary treats such statements skeptically, as part of what it characterizes as fearmongering over successive public health crises.
