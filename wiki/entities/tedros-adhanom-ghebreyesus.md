# Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus

Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus is the Director-General of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]

During the [[2026-ebola-outbreak|2026 Ebola outbreak]] of the Bundibugyo strain in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] and Uganda, Tedros said the delay in detection meant authorities were "playing catch-up with a very fast-moving epidemic" and that the outbreak was outpacing scaled-up operations.[1] The WHO coordinated response efforts with the DRC and Ugandan governments and partners, with roughly $500 million pledged for response and prevention.[1]

Badlands treated such statements skeptically, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] questioning the recurrence of "potential pandemics" forecast in advance by global think tanks.[1]

On August 14, 2024, Tedros declared the mpox outbreak in Africa a public health emergency of international concern after the WHO's emergency committee advised him that the situation warranted it, saying: "The emergency committee met and advised me that the situation constitutes a public health emergency of international concern. I have accepted that advice."[2] He called the detection and rapid spread of a new clade of mpox in eastern DRC and its appearance in neighboring countries "very worrying," described two clades of concern — one highly transmissible and productive of severe disease — and said "it's clear that a coordinated international response is essential to stop these outbreaks and save lives."[2] The WHO cited more than 17,000 mpox cases in Africa since the start of that year.[2] Badlands commentary was hostile: [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called him "the guy that was wrong about everything (but highly effective at spreading tyranny)" during COVID and asked what was being planned "to the people in Africa (again)."[2] See [[2024-mpox-global-health-emergency|2024 mpox global health emergency]].[2]

## Warnings about future pandemics (2023)
In connection with the June 2023 [[world-health-organization|WHO]]–[[european-union|EU]] plans for a global [[digital-vaccine-passport|digital vaccine passport]], Tedros declared that "the threat of another variant emerging that causes new surges of disease and death remains," and that "the threat of another pathogen emerging with even deadlier potential remains."[3] He added: "When the next pandemic comes knocking — and it will — we must be ready to answer decisively, collectively, and equitably."[3] Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the remarks as evidence that global health bodies were confident another pandemic was imminent.[3]
