# Jeremy Bentham

Jeremy Bentham was an English philosopher whom Badlands Media identifies as one of the definers of "The British System" of social order that dominated the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.[1] His Hedonistic Calculus is described as the backbone of today's behaviorist economists.[1]

Bentham is paired in the article with [[thomas-malthus|Thomas Malthus]], whose "law of overpopulation" formed the basis of the dismal science and the utilitarian cult of [[transhumanism|transhumanism]].[1] Together with the British liberalism of [[adam-smith|Adam Smith]], [[david-ricardo|David Ricardo]] and [[john-stuart-mill|John Stuart Mill]] — which promoted the notion that value was rooted in hedonistic impulses to "buy low, sell dear" while avoiding pain to maximize pleasure — this school taught that invisible hands shaped the marketplace and that national governments should abrogate their rights to use national banking, protectionism or regulation of private capital in the general welfare.[1] The real-world application of that system, the article argues, produced the systemic destruction of nations and a worldwide web of dependency under the control of the [[city-of-london|City of London]] and the [[british-east-india-company|British East India Company]].[1]
