# Ecuadorian Embassy in London

The **Ecuadorian Embassy in London** is the diplomatic mission of Ecuador in the United Kingdom, best known in the Badlands corpus as the building in which [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] publisher [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] took refuge for roughly seven years.[1]

## Assange's asylum

Assange sought asylum at the embassy in 2012, two years after publishing US Army intelligence analyst [[chelsea-manning|Chelsea Manning]]'s footage of a 2007 US airstrike in Baghdad.[1] He remained there until his 2019 arrest and transfer to a British prison.[1]

## Publishing from inside the embassy

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats the embassy period as the central anomaly in Assange's story.[1] From inside the building — with his exact location known to every intelligence service on earth, including the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] — Assange and WikiLeaks assisted [[edward-snowden|Edward Snowden]] in 2013, published the [[dnc-email-leak|DNC emails]] in July 2016, released the [[podesta-emails|John Podesta emails]] months later, and in March 2017 published [[vault-7|Vault 7]], the largest leak of CIA documents in history.[1] Carlson argues it is "far fetched" that an agency Assange himself described as capable of hacking essentially any internet-connected device was helpless to stop a man whose address it knew.[1]

He also highlights the narrowness of the only visible retaliation: the Ecuadorian government cut Assange's internet access for just six weeks, and only after the Podesta releases, with the customary justification being the ongoing US election.[1]

## Alleged kidnap and assassination planning

According to Reuters reporting of February 20, 2024 quoted in the same essay, defence barrister Mark Summers told the High Court that there was evidence of a "truly breathtaking plan" to kidnap or murder Assange while he was in the embassy, and that then-President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had asked for "detailed options" to kill him.[1] Yahoo News had reported in 2021 that CIA officials drew up such options for the Trump administration while Assange was in the London embassy, with sketches reportedly prepared.[1] These allegations are part of the wider [[assange-extradition-case|Assange extradition case]], and [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] has repeatedly attributed the planning to then-CIA Director [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]].[1]
