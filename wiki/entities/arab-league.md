# Arab League

The Arab League is a regional organization of nations formed in 1945.[1]

The article uses its current membership, together with the definition of the region advanced by the leaders of the 1916 Arab Revolt — King Hussein and his son Prince Faisal — to delineate the area it calls Arabia on the author's own reductive map of the Middle East.[1] The author stresses the relative size of Arabia so defined and how it forms the natural geographic axis of the entire region.[1]

He also cautions that such mapping is inherently interpretive, noting that a 2014 Vanity Fair article offers a more nuanced if incomplete socio-political breakdown of the modern Middle East, and that forty more maps would yield forty different regional designations — "because all history is interpretation."[1]

## Condemnation of the Huckabee remarks (February 2026)

In February 2026 the League of Arab States joined the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]] and individual governments including [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], Egypt and Jordan in publicly condemning US Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] over his statement that [[israel|Israel]] holds a biblical right to territory "from the Nile to the Euphrates."[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that the resulting joint statement drew co-signatories from NATO, BRICS, the European Union, the Arab League, the OIC and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, an alignment he read as unprecedented unification of the Muslim world and much of the wider international community against Israel.[2]

## Delisting of Hezbollah (2024)

Badlands reporting in July 2024 noted that in the preceding week all 22 nations of the Arab League, led by [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], had removed [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] from their list of designated terrorist organizations.[3] The commentary contrasted this with [[russia|Russia]], which it said had never designated Hezbollah as a terrorist group and had always considered it a legitimate socio-political organization.[3] The delisting was presented as part of a broader realignment in which Saudi diplomacy — including the announced reopening of its embassy in Taliban-controlled Afghanistan and intensive bilateral contacts with Turkey, China, Russia and Iran — was positioning the kingdom as a central mediator in the Ukraine and Israel conflicts.[3]

## Syria's 2023 readmission in Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the readmission of Syria to the Arab League on May 19, 2023 — which he credited to Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — as evidence that "the Middle East is healing," coming eleven years after Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] allegedly used her clout to have [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]]'s government expelled in 2012.[4]

## Emergency Cairo summit on Palestine (March 2025)

An emergency summit of the League of Arab States was scheduled to take place in Cairo on Tuesday, March 4, 2025.[5] Participants were to discuss the Palestinian issue in general and the situation in the Gaza Strip and the enclave's future in particular, with the main topic being a plan to restore Gaza prepared by Egyptian authorities; if approved, Egypt's plan would be presented to the international community.[5] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the central question for the Arab states was what to do about [[benjamin-netanyahu|Bibi Netanyahu]], contending that Netanyahu had shown no interest in cooperating with his neighbors for long-term peace while the Arab states had demonstrated patience and resolve, and predicting that they would nonetheless continue to pursue peace.[5]

## 33rd Arab Summit (2024)

The [[arab-summit-2024-bahrain|33rd Arab Summit]] convened in Bahrain in May 2024, hosted with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and attended by UN Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|António Guterres]] as special guest of honor.[6] The group issued a joint statement demanding that Israel permanently withdraw the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] from Palestine — not only Gaza — and calling on the entire international community to bear down on the government in Tel Aviv.[6]
