# Yechiel Leiter

Yechiel Leiter is an Israeli diplomat serving as Israel's Ambassador to the United States.

## Statements on the June 8, 2026 strikes on Iran

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Ambassador Leiter said the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Air Force]] operation against military targets in central and western [[iran|Iran]] focused on missile launch sites and related infrastructure and did not target energy facilities. Explosions were reported in multiple Iranian cities after the strikes, which came in response to Iran's launch of 11 ballistic missiles toward [[israel|Israel]] — the first direct Iranian attack since the April 8 ceasefire — and which Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] ordered in defiance of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s urging not to retaliate.

Leiter's assurances about the operation's scope, and the separate Israeli Embassy statement dismissing the reported [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] assessment of Israeli espionage as politically motivated, bracket the diplomatic tension between the Trump administration and the Israeli government over Iran and developments in [[lebanon|Lebanon]].
