# Yechiel Leiter

Yechiel Leiter is an Israeli ambassador who served as a public spokesman for the Israeli government's account of its June 2026 retaliatory strikes on [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## June 2026 strikes

After [[israel|Israel]] absorbed an Iranian ballistic missile barrage on June 8, the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] carried out air strikes against military targets in central and western Iran.[1] Leiter said the operation focused on missile launch sites and related infrastructure and did not target energy facilities.[1] Explosions were reported in multiple Iranian cities.[1]

His framing — limited, counter-force, energy infrastructure deliberately spared — was offered as Israel's answer to concerns that the strikes would trigger a wider regional war at the moment President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was pressing for a negotiated settlement with Tehran.[1]

## Statement on the Capital Jewish Museum shooting (May 2025)

Following the fatal shooting of two Israeli embassy staff members outside the [[capital-jewish-museum|Capital Jewish Museum]] in Washington, D.C. on May 21, 2025, Leiter, Israel's Ambassador to the United States, said the two victims were dating and on the verge of engagement, and that the man had just purchased a ring "with the intention of proposing to his girlfriend next week in Jerusalem."[2] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] singled out that detail as "quite the narrative needle to thread" and part of what made the incident feel to him like a possible false flag.[2]
