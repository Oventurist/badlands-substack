# Bruce Greyson

**Dr. Bruce Greyson** is cited in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for research on near-death experiences.[1]

The essay reports Greyson's finding that people who undergo near-death experiences "often return with clear purpose and a loss of fear."[1] The author immediately qualifies the point for the reader: "you don't need to almost die to live this way," because the reader is already "awake… aware… tuned."[1]

The citation appears in the section "Resonance, Leadership & the Living Grid," between [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]] on the "quantum signature" and [[candace-pert|Candace Pert]] on the body as subconscious mind, and supports the article's claim that the transformation it describes is available without crisis.[1]
