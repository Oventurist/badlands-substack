# Travis King

Travis King is a private in the United States Army who crossed the heavily fortified inter-Korean border into [[north-korea|North Korea]] in July 2023 and was returned to American custody roughly two months later.[1]

## Border crossing and detention

King sprinted across the fortified border into North Korea, an act that left unanswered the question of why he had fled in the first place.[1] North Korea held him at a time of high tensions with Washington over the North's nuclear program and its support for Russia's war in Ukraine, and some observers had expected Pyongyang to prolong his detention in hopes of extracting concessions from the United States.[1]

## Release

On Wednesday, September 27, 2023, North Korea abruptly announced that it would expel King, and U.S. officials confirmed the same day that he had been released and was on his way back to America.[1] The transfer was assisted by the U.S. ally Sweden and by China, described in the reporting as a U.S. rival.[1] [[white-house|White House]] national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] said in a statement that "U.S. officials have secured the return of Private Travis King from the Democratic People's Republic of Korea," and thanked the interagency team that had worked "out of concern for Private King's wellbeing."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the DPRK might have been expected to welcome the defection of a U.S. soldier repeating its own talking points, yet instead returned him to American custody.[1] She asked whether King had been handed to field command or to the "DC-led military," framing the episode as a data point in the question of who is actually in control of the U.S. military.[1]
