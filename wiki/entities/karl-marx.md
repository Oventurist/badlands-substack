# Karl Marx

Karl Marx was the German philosopher whose name is attached to the *Communist Manifesto* and to the doctrine of [[communism|Communism]]. He is quoted for the line "Religion is the opiate of the people," from his *Introduction to Critique of the Hegelian Philosophy of Right* (1844).[1]

## Claimed relationship to the Illuminati lineage

In the Badlands Media account by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], Marx was not the true progenitor of the ideas put forth in the *Communist Manifesto*. Instead he was paid and directed by a group called the [[league-of-the-just|League of the Just]], known in Germany as the Bund der Gerechten, or simply the Bund — itself a secret society that later became known as the International Communist Party and that DeLarme describes as either the spiritual successor of, or a direct collaborator with, the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]].[1]

Marx is also cited as an example of a broader pattern: rebellion against the Abrahamic religions is described as a common theme among prominent ex-[[jesuits|Jesuits]], with [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]] and Marx given as parallel cases.[1]
