# Farm Aid

Farm Aid is a concert charity co-founded in 1985 by [[willie-nelson|Willie Nelson]] — and, as Badlands Media noted, also by [[neil-young|Neil Young]] — to raise awareness and funding for American family farming.[1]

## Mission and self-description

The organization states on its website: "We know that to keep family farmers on the land we have to increase the number of people buying their good food. From our annual concert event that features family farm food and unites farmers, artists, and concerned citizens, to our inspiring and informative tv, radio, mail and web campaigns…we are building a powerful movement for good food from family farms."[1]

## Badlands critique

Badlands Media argued that Farm Aid "continues to thrive as family farms continue to go out of business," and that most or all of the stars headlining the 2024 event support [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], the Democrats, and "the status quo of the global regime."[1] The outlet described the charity as "living its best life, year over year, expanding in parallel with the destruction of America's agricultural systems and food supply chain, in parallel with the loss of generational family farms and the expansion of foreign capture."[1] It generalized the point to nongovernmental organizations broadly: "Many such cases with NGOs. They're literally the worst at accomplishing stated objectives."[1]

The critique was offered in the context of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s pledge to curb Chinese Communist Party purchases of American farmland, with the writer concluding that "reclaiming American farmland is a big part of how we make America healthy again," requiring "no over indulgent concert."[1]
