# Nick Kerigan

Nick Kerigan is the head of innovation at [[swift|SWIFT]], the global bank messaging network. In March 2024 he described SWIFT's latest central bank digital currency trial, which ran for six months and involved a 38-member group of central banks, commercial banks and settlement platforms — in his account one of the largest global collaborations on CBDCs and "tokenised" assets to date.[1]

Kerigan said the trial focused on ensuring that the CBDCs of different countries could be used together even when built on different underlying technologies or protocols, with the aim of reducing payment system fragmentation risks as SWIFT prepared to launch a CBDC connection platform within one to two years.[1]
