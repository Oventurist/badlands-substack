# Illinois State Board of Elections

The Illinois State Board of Elections is the state agency charged with administering elections and maintaining statewide voter registration records in Illinois.[1] In 2025 it became the defendant in litigation brought by the conservative watchdog group [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] over its obligations to maintain accurate and publicly accessible voter rolls.[1]

## Judicial Watch litigation and DOJ statement of interest

In the case [[judicial-watch-v-illinois-state-board-of-elections|Judicial Watch v. Illinois State Board of Elections]], the plaintiffs argued the board had failed to conduct adequate list maintenance under the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]].[1] On July 8, 2025, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed a statement of interest reinforcing its position that states must take "reasonable efforts" under the NVRA to remove ineligible voters and keep registration lists transparent.[1] The filing argued that the NVRA's list maintenance provision demands effective programs rather than symbolic ones.[1]

Assistant Attorney General [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet K. Dhillon]], head of the Civil Rights Division, said it was "critical to remove ineligible voters from the registration rolls so that elections are conducted fairly, accurately, and without fraud," and that the department would vigorously enforce the statute's requirements.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] welcomed the movement on election integrity but cautioned that any court should retain jurisdiction to enforce resulting agreements, citing the earlier Judicial Watch v. Griswold settlement in Colorado as a cautionary precedent.[1] She argued that genuinely clean voter rolls would require decentralized voter roll management and maintenance, describing that as a non-negotiable systemic change.[1]
