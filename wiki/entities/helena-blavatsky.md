# Helena Blavatsky

Helena Blavatsky, the theosophical author of *The Secret Doctrine*, is cited in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] for her commentary on the [[divine-pymander|Divine Pymander]].[1]

In *The Secret Doctrine* Blavatsky refers to the Pymander as the "Thought Divine," the Inspired Teacher of that Race, and speaks of it as "the oldest and most spiritual of the Logoi of the Western continent."[1] She also notes the possibility of a Hebrew derivation of the name from PI, meaning a mouth, command or instruction — an alternative to the Greek derivation from *poimen* (shepherd) and *aner*, which yields "Shepherd of men."[1]



## Influence on Nazi occultism

Blavatsky's Theosophical cosmology and her concept of the [[black-sun|Black Sun]] — introduced in *The Secret Doctrine* as the invisible centre of the universe and the Gnostic "creative light" — were adopted directly by Nazi occultists, with [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] commissioning a marble Black Sun for [[wewelsburg-castle|Wewelsburg Castle]].[2] In her writings the notion of "black light" is attributed exclusively to a secret Aryan teaching brought from the far North and associated with a legendary people beyond the Arctic Circle.[2] [[lanz-von-liebenfels|Lanz von Liebenfels]] popularized her cosmology and a racist spiritual eugenics for a German audience through the journal Ostara, substituting a Nordic Wotan ethos for Theosophy's Indian emphasis.[2] Her later followers, Ehret argues, spawned the occult substrate of twentieth-century fascism, including reverence for [[edward-bulwer-lytton|Bulwer-Lytton's]] hollow-earth master race.[2]

## Theosophy and the Nazi Reichs Bible

Matthew Ehret's third installment of "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler" traces the 1939 [[reichs-bible|Reichs Bible]] to a sub-branch of Theosophical followers of Blavatsky whose movement had spearheaded the Rosicrucian revival in England and the United States in the nineteenth century.[3] Founded in 1875, her [[theosophy|Theosophists]] attempted to create a syncretic new world religion drawn from the world's mystery religions, a project Aldous Huxley later celebrated as "The Perennial Philosophy."[3] Through her system the West was introduced to the swastika, Hermeticism, Kabbalah, esoteric Buddhism, and Kali-Isis earth mother goddess worship.[3]

Blavatsky also introduced a spiritualized materialism premised on spirit beings moving humanity through evolutionary phases of "root races," culminating in a Teutonic race expressed by pure-blooded Germanic tribes; in *The Secret Doctrine* this race is the last phase of the Aryan fourth root race, destined to usher in a fifth root race of trans-humans, or Übermenschen.[3] Her cosmology posited a Great White Lodge of Ascended Masters in Shamballah communicating telepathically with special mediums such as herself, in a cosmic battle against a Great Black Lodge of evil priests.[3] She nevertheless made clear that her new world religion would be devoted to Lucifer and would require the destruction of Christianity, writing that "that which the clergy of every dogmatic religion – pre-eminently the Christian – points out as Satan, the enemy of God, is in reality, the highest divine Spirit – (occult Wisdom on Earth)."[3]

Ehret argues that [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s embrace of Darwinism, Nietzscheanism, and eugenics was not as materialistic as modern scholars believe, but the material application of the Luciferian concepts Blavatsky first promoted, later reworked by [[guido-von-list|Guido von List]] into [[ariosophy|Ariosophy]] and by Franz Hartmann into a Germanic Theosophy.[3]
