# W. Cleon Skousen

**W. Cleon Skousen** was a former FBI agent who served as police chief of Salt Lake City in the late 1950s and wrote about international conspiracies in several books, including *The Naked Communist*.[1]

## On race and the global power structure

Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" invokes Skousen in support of its argument against reducing the "High Cabal" to a single ethnic or religious group.[1] Skousen is quoted as concluding that racial identification was "an oversimplified explanation for the rise of the global power structure which has snared mankind … in studying the global conspiracy, it is important to keep in mind that it was not any one race or religion but the passion for money and power which has drawn the tycoons of world finance into a tightly knit, mutual-aid society."[1]

DeLarme uses the passage alongside his own distinction between Hebrews, Jews and [[zionism|Zionists]] to argue that blaming "the Jews" for the actions of a small financial elite plays into the hands of the international criminals he says span many cultures, from Venice to [[khazaria|Khazaria]] to Geneva.[1]
