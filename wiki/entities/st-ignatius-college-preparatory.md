# St. Ignatius College Preparatory

St. Ignatius College Prep is a [[jesuits|Jesuit]]-run secondary school in San Francisco described in Badlands reporting as an institution sitting at the very heart of the California power structure.[1] Its significance in the Kamala Harris coverage stems from the clergy abuse documents obtained by District Attorney [[terence-hallinan|Terence Hallinan]] from the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|Archdiocese of San Francisco]], which reportedly touched on well-connected institutions including St. Ignatius.[1]

The school's alumni network included powerful Californian figures such as [[jerry-brown|Jerry Brown]], who was governor at the time of the article's writing, as well as members of the highly influential [[getty-family|Getty family]].[1] The article presents these connections as part of the explanation for why the archdiocese records were politically explosive and why the effort to release them was abandoned once [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] succeeded Hallinan as District Attorney.[1]
