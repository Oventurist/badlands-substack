# Brad Schimel

Brad Schimel was the Republican-backed candidate in the 2025 Wisconsin Supreme Court race.[1]

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] played a major role in that contest, with Musk and affiliated groups spending at least $20 million in support of Schimel's candidacy.[1] Musk's [[america-pac|America PAC]] also offered $100 to voters who signed a petition opposing "activist judges" or who referred others to sign it.[1] Musk's separate offer of $1 million to voters in the same race later led the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]] to refer complaints for criminal review.[1]
