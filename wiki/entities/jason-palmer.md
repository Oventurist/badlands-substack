# Jason Palmer

**Jason Palmer** is an American businessman who ran for the 2024 Democratic presidential nomination and became the only candidate to defeat President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in a nominating contest on [[2024-super-tuesday|Super Tuesday 2024]].[1]

Described in coverage as a little-known Democratic businessman, Palmer won the caucus in American Samoa, a small U.S. territory in the Pacific Ocean, on March 5, 2024, in what was otherwise an overwhelming Biden sweep of every state called by the Associated Press.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the American Samoa result — Biden losing "to some guy named Jason Palmer" — as one of the two biggest surprises of the night, alongside [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]]'s projected win in Vermont.[1]
