# Orion Critical Mineral Consortium

The Orion Critical Mineral Consortium (Orion CMC) is a US-backed investment group seeking a major ownership stake in critical mineral assets in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

## Glencore transaction

Orion CMC and [[glencore|Glencore]] entered a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding covering the potential acquisition of a 40% stake in Glencore's DRC assets, [[mutanda-mining|Mutanda Mining]] and [[kamoto-copper-company|Kamoto Copper Company]], implying a combined enterprise value of around $9 billion.[1] Orion CMC would gain rights to appoint non-executive directors and to direct the sale of its share of production to nominated buyers under the U.S.–DRC Strategic Partnership Agreement.[1]

## Strategic rationale

The consortium aims to acquire significant interests in cobalt, copper and other strategic mineral projects, a move framed as part of broader Western efforts to secure supply chains for energy-transition technologies and reduce reliance on China-dominated processing and sourcing.[1]
