# Laura Loomer

Laura Loomer is an American right-wing activist and online commentator known for her proximity to figures in the Trump movement and for her strongly pro-Israel positions.

In [[badlands-brief-029|the June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]], Loomer is described as citing reports from the office of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that Israel was not a party to the reported United States–[[iran|Iran]] agreement, and thereby "openly subverting President Trump." The Brief groups her with Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] and the broadcaster [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] as figures whose reaction to [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s cancellation of planned strikes revealed, in the writers' view, a prior loyalty to Israeli policy preferences. The commentary describes her as "sharpening her knives" in the hours after the announcement.
