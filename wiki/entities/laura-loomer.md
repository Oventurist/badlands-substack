# Laura Loomer

Laura Loomer is an American right-wing activist described in Badlands coverage as one of the most aggressive campaigners for federal action against [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] in March 2026.[1]

## Campaign against Tucker Carlson

After Carlson said the [[cia|CIA]] was preparing a criminal referral over his contacts in [[iran|Iran]], Loomer posted that she would take credit if Carlson were charged, stating that she had been "relentless" in contacting GOP representatives, law enforcement and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] about him.[1] Her posts were noted in a ZeroHedge report on the affair alongside the administration's denial that any CIA investigation of Carlson existed.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that segments of the truth community — people who had spent years discussing [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]], psyops and federal infiltration — were cheering the prospect that Carlson would go to prison because Loomer told the DOJ he was a foreign agent for Iran.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] grouped Loomer with [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] as figures being baited by [[donald-trump|President Trump]] into overreach on speech criminalization, escalating the confrontation over the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]

## Ukrainian ancestry claim (July 2026)

In July 2026 Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited an appearance by Loomer on Ukrainian television in which she said she had learned she is of Ukrainian descent, describing her as a "Mossad Media shill" and treating the disclosure as further evidence of a link between [[israel|Israel]] and [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[2] He connected the claim to an assertion that Israel was founded by Bolsheviks and their relatives, mostly atheists, who came from Ukraine — an editorial framing rather than a documented finding.[2]

## Rhetoric around the Khamenei funeral (July 2026)

On July 2, 2026, Loomer posted that the [[funeral-of-ali-khamenei|funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]] was a "target rich environment," adding a bomb emoji.[3] The July 6 Badlands Brief reported that she spent the Fourth of July rage-posting about [[iran|Iran]] and calling for terrorist attacks against civilians and world leaders attending the funeral, with [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] described as chiming in.[3] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented her posts as the defining example of [[mossad-media|Mossad Media]] behavior, contrasting the treatment of Palestinian children who throw rocks at IDF soldiers with Israelis openly fantasizing about bombing a funeral, and reproducing the dictionary definition of terrorism in response to anticipated accusations of sympathizing with terrorists.[3]

The same commentary offered back-to-back posts in which Loomer promoted [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] and disparaged [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as "additional proof" that she is a [[deep-state|Deep State]] propagandist.[3]

## Qatar dispute (February 2026)

At the first [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] meeting held in Washington, DC in February 2026, [[donald-trump|President Trump]] indirectly called out Loomer and [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] for their incessant attacks on Emir Tamim of [[qatar|Qatar]].[4] Rather than comply, Loomer posted the video of Trump's remarks and doubled down, declaring that she "hates Qatar."[4] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented the exchange as part of a broader pattern of pro-Israel media figures openly defying Trump's peace efforts in the region.[4]

## Reaction to the June 2026 Iran peace deal

Loomer was cited among the pro-Israel media voices reacting against the announced U.S.–[[iran|Iran]] peace framework in June 2026.[5] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] grouped her posts with those of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]], and Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] under the heading of "Mossad Media" responses, arguing that the [[israel-lobby|Israel Lobby]] would use its influence over [[congress|Congress]] to prevent the deal's finalization.[5]

## Reaction to the June 2026 Netanyahu call leak
Loomer publicly denounced disloyalty within [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s camp after [[axios|Axios]] reported his expletive-filled June 1, 2026 phone call with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[6] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited her response, along with [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]]'s call for an FBI leak investigation, as evidence that the call likely occurred and that its participants feared an audio recording existed.[6]

## Ukraine advocacy (July 2026)

In July 2026 Loomer conducted a high-profile visit to Kyiv, interviewing [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] and afterward declaring full support for him — a shift that, together with Ukrainian battlefield gains, created limited openings within MAGA and America First circles long wary of open-ended support for Ukraine.[7] Positive comments from Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] followed, though many hard-line Republicans remained skeptical.[7]

Loomer also stated that an FPV drone presented to the late Sen. [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] as a birthday gift by the Ukrainian manufacturer [[skyfall|SkyFall]] during his final visit to Kyiv would be deployed on the front lines "to kill Russians."[7] SkyFall subsequently released an advertisement showing Loomer holding one of its drones in the company's factory, and she predicted that SkyFall and similar Ukrainian firms were about to become major players in the U.S. [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]].[7] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the trip as a "propaganda tour."[7]

## Ukraine reversal and "Putin droned Jesus Christ" (July 2026)

Following her visit to [[ukraine|Ukraine]], Loomer publicly reversed her previous position on the Russia-Ukraine war and apologized for earlier comments downplaying the conflict, saying she had underestimated the impact of Russia's attacks on civilians.[8] In subsequent posts she strongly criticized Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], writing "Putin droned Jesus Christ" after reports that a Russian strike damaged Kyiv's historic Saint Sophia Cathedral.[8] Her shift drew attention amid ongoing debate within conservative circles over US policy toward Ukraine.[8]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded that Loomer had taken the Lord's name in vain and was weaponizing Christ despite, by her own account, not believing in Him, and argued that she shills constantly for what he called a foreign theocracy.[8] He connected her output to the concept of [[mossad-media|"Mossad Media"]], which he tied to the Hasbara program within [[israel|Israel]]'s national budget, and suggested that the Israeli diaspora has other means of compensating figures who operate on the Israeli government's behalf.[8] In his reading, her Ukraine press tour signals a formal merger of the Ukrainian and Israeli war narratives, prompting speculation about whether Ukraine might next be turned against Iran, Turkey, Cyprus or Greece on Israel's behalf.[8]

## Ukraine deployment (July 2026)

In July 2026 Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that Loomer had been deployed to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] for what he described as a massive public relations campaign to keep the war with [[russia|Russia]] going.[9] He characterized her as a "Mossad Media asset" and placed the trip alongside Ukraine's drone strikes on Turkish vessels and the new Ukrainian commander-in-chief's declaration that Russia has no right to exist, arguing that the Uniparty regime was attempting to open a new front and turn the Black Sea and Eastern Mediterranean into active war zones.[9]

The commentary emphasized the timing: the campaign coincided with Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s meeting with Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] in Manila, at which Washington expressed its desire to help end the war.[9]

## Call to deport Xi Jinping's daughter (May 2025)

On Wednesday, May 28, 2025, Loomer called for the deportation of Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s daughter, Xi Mingze, in the wake of the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s move to revoke the visas of some Chinese students, particularly those linked to the Chinese Communist Party.[10] Citing the Financial Express, Loomer said Xi Mingze had previously attended [[harvard-university|Harvard University]].[10] "DEPORT XI JINPING'S DAUGHTER! She lives in Massachusetts and went to Harvard! Sources tell me PLA guards from the CCP provide her with private security on US soil in Massachusetts!" she wrote on X.[10] The post came hours after Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] announced the administration would "aggressively" revoke visas for students from China.[10]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] pushed back, arguing that Loomer did not appear to know Xi's origin story — that his parents were persecuted by the communist party, that his father sought to open China's markets, and that since taking power Xi has hunted down and imprisoned what Badlands describes as the worst communist leaders in China.[10] The same commentary asserted that President [[donald-trump|Trump]] and Xi are genuine friends who discuss their children and grandchildren when they meet.[10]
