# Robert Garcia

Robert Garcia is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives and the top Democrat on the House Oversight and Government Reform Committee.[1]

## Epstein files oversight

In March 2026 Garcia backed the committee's subpoena of Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], which required her to appear for a deposition on April 14 regarding the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s handling of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigation and its compliance with the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]].[1] Garcia accused Bondi of leading a "White House cover-up" of the full Epstein files.[1] The subpoena passed with full support from every Democrat on the panel plus several Republicans.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the episode as performative, noting that the Justice Department had already offered to brief the committee and to let lawmakers view unredacted files in person.[1]

## Ruemmler questioning (July 2026)
As ranking member of the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], Garcia took part in the July 2026 closed-door interview of former Obama White House counsel [[kathryn-ruemmler|Kathryn Ruemmler]], questioning the credibility of her testimony about her relationship with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and saying she failed to explain why she maintained it.[2] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited his remarks in arguing that the proceedings amounted to guilt by association.[2]
