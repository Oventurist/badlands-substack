# Robert Garcia

Robert Garcia is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from California and the ranking Democrat on the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Oversight and Government Reform Committee]].

In the March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, Garcia appears in connection with the committee's subpoena of Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], issued by Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] and requiring Bondi to appear for a deposition on April 14 about the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s handling of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigation and its compliance with the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]]. As the panel's top Democrat, Garcia accused Bondi of leading a "White House cover-up" of the full Epstein files.

The subpoena passed with the support of every Democrat on the panel plus several Republicans. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the entire episode as a performative exercise, noting that the Justice Department had already offered to brief the committee and had invited lawmakers to view unredacted files in person.
