# Daniel Levitt

**Daniel Levitt** was a [[x|Twitter]] user, posting under the handle @daniellevitt22, whose September 16, 2020 tweet summarising eight claims about COVID-19 was cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] as capturing a widely shared dissenting view of the pandemic response.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## The tweet

Levitt's post presented a recap of what he asserted was then known about COVID: that lockdowns increase deaths; that Sweden was right; that Neil Ferguson was wrong; that masks are ineffective; that 95% of infections are asymptomatic; that up to 80% of people may be immune; that transmission stops at roughly 20% antibody prevalence; and that more cases do not equal more deaths.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) When White saved the tweet to his personal files on September 17, 2020, it had accumulated 136 replies, 2,053 retweets and 4,085 likes, which he took as evidence that many people agreed with its eight points.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Disappearance from the platform

By the time White wrote his December 2022 column, both the tweet and Levitt's account had been erased from the platform.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) White wrote that Twitter had either harassed Levitt into ghosting himself or had "disappeared him themselves," and described the outcome as the normal result for many on the conservative side of the aisle — an illustration of the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship]] dynamic that forms the backbone of his argument.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)
