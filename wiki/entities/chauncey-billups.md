# Chauncey Billups

Chauncey Billups is the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers of the [[national-basketball-association|NBA]], and was identified in October 2025 as one of the most notable individuals arrested in a sweeping federal indictment targeting illegal sports gambling and rigged poker games.[1]

## Arrest in the 2025 federal gambling case

Billups was arrested alongside Miami Heat player [[terry-rozier|Terry Rozier]] as part of what was described as a massive federal indictment covering illegal sports betting and the rigging of poker games.[1] Reporting on the case noted that several organized crime groups were implicated in the same enforcement action, placing the arrests of prominent basketball figures within a much larger criminal conspiracy.[1]

[[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] characterized the conduct uncovered by investigators as "mind-boggling" fraud, and said the arrests were the product of a yearslong illegal gambling investigation that "envelops both the NBA and La Casa Nostra."[1] Patel further stated that the operation had also executed "a system of justice against [[la-cosa-nostra|La Cosa Nostra]]," specifically naming the Bonanno, Gambino, Genovese and Luchese crime families.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the convergence implied by the sting should not be underestimated, suggesting there was a reason the Trump administration's FBI was amplifying the case while tying it back to some of the most organized crime syndicates in the United States.[1] The commentary framed the episode as evidence of the [[system-of-systems|System of Systems]] operating throughout American institutions, and concluded bluntly that "sports are fake."[1]
