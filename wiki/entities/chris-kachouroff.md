# Chris Kachouroff

Chris Kachouroff is an American attorney who served as defence counsel for [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] in the Colorado defamation trial [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]].[1]

## Opening statement in the Coomer trial

According to a partial trial transcript provided to reporters by Denver station KUSA, Kachouroff told the jury in June 2025 that it was not necessary to prove Lindell's voting-machine claims were true.[1] "This trial is about whether Mike Lindell believed his statements were true at the time he made them," he reportedly said. "It doesn't have to be true, but he has to believe at the time he made them that they were substantially true."[1] KUSA's Kyle Clark summarised the position as: "This morning, Lindell's attorneys told the jury in their defamation case, it doesn't matter if what Lindell said was true or not because he believed it to be true."[1]

## Badlands assessment

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], covering the trial in person for [[badlands-media|Badlands]], argued that reporting casting Kachouroff's opening as a retreat was misinformation, since Lindell's affirmative defences had always been framed that way — as documented in the April pretrial order — and the difference between the pretrial filing and the opening statement was merely one of clinical brief versus narrative.[1] She wrote that Lindell's team would show at trial why he believes the machines are rigged, what limited knowledge he had of [[eric-coomer|Coomer]] at the time of the allegedly defamatory statements, and why he used the word "traitor" from a place of personal belief — a posture she said could not meet Coomer's burden of willful negligence and actual malice.[1]
