# Charlie Kirk

Charlie Kirk is an American conservative activist and commentator cited alongside [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] as a figure whose campus appearances drew demands for cancellation during the [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]] controversies of the late 2010s.[1] When Kirk and Shapiro appeared at universities they were met with howls of execration and calls for administrators to cancel their events so that campuses could serve as safe spaces — not for an ethnic or sexual minority, but for anyone who disagreed with the speaker's opinions.[1] Kirk had several such engagements cancelled during the same period in which Shapiro lost at least five.[1]
