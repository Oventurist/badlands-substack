# Charlie Kirk

Charlie Kirk is a conservative American activist and founder of Turning Point USA, a youth-oriented political organization, and is described in the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard" as a prominent conservative campus figure of the late 2010s.

According to the article, Kirk — alongside [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] — was among the conservative personalities who, when they appeared on college campuses, were greeted with hostility and calls for universities to cancel their events in the name of creating "safe spaces." The source states that Kirk had several speaking engagements canceled by universities in this period, framing him as one of the figures on the receiving end of the campus deplatforming dynamic that the article criticizes. The article uses this history to argue that the right's free speech advocacy was later abandoned when the targets of censorship shifted after October 7, 2023.

## Assassination conspiracy claims and the Robinson hearing (July 2026)

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] argued that accused killer [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] did not act alone, implicating [[israel|Israel]], Egypt, the US military, [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] staff, and Kirk's widow [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]], and that her claims drew condemnation from conservative figures after Robinson's preliminary hearing. Politico reported TPUSA staff received death threats and increased security after Owens's broadcasts named them. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues the online drama around the case — including a supposedly secret "4K video" that [[canncon|CannCon]] found matched the [[court-tv|Court TV]] stream — is intended to influence the jury pool.
