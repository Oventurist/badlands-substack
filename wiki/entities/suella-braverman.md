# Suella Braverman

Suella Braverman served as Home Secretary of the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]], the cabinet office responsible for policing and domestic security.[1]

## Guidance to police after the October 7 attacks

In the wake of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks on Israel]], the UK government urged police chiefs to consider whether waving a Palestinian flag could constitute a criminal offence, following calls by senior figures for people deemed to have expressed support for [[hamas|Hamas]] to be prosecuted.[1] In a letter issued on Tuesday, October 10, 2023, Braverman called on police forces to be alert for displays of Hamas flags or logos, or other demonstrations of support for the group, which the UK proscribed as a terrorist organisation in 2021.[1]

Braverman also asked police to weigh the context in which Palestinian flags were waved, in order to assess whether public order offences of harassment or incitement had been committed. "Behaviours that are legitimate in some circumstances, for example, the waving of a Palestinian flag, may not be legitimate such as when intended to glorify acts of terrorism," she wrote.[1] The guidance was reported by [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]] and drew attention as an example of speech-adjacent enforcement discretion being pushed onto police forces during the conflict.[1]
