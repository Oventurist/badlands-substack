# Michael Ragusa

Michael Ragusa is the head of security for former New York City mayor [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]].[1]

Ragusa served as the public spokesman for Giuliani following a serious motor vehicle accident in New Hampshire on the evening of August 30, 2025, disclosing details of the incident in a statement posted to X on the following Sunday.[1] In the statement he said that prior to the crash Giuliani "was flagged down by a woman who was the victim of a domestic violence incident," that Giuliani "immediately rendered assistance and contacted 911," and that he "remained on scene until responding officers arrived to ensure her safety."[1]

Ragusa further stated that, "while traveling on the highway, Mayor Giuliani's vehicle was struck from behind at high speed," and that Giuliani was transported to a nearby trauma center where he was diagnosed with a fractured thoracic vertebrae, multiple lacerations and contusions, and injuries to his left arm and lower leg.[1] Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] commented on the framing of the incident, remarking that "you don't say things like, 'this was not a targeted attack' unless it sounds exactly like a targeted attack."[1]
