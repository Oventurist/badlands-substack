# Russian Direct Investment Fund

The Russian Direct Investment Fund (RDIF) is Russia's sovereign wealth fund, headed by [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]], who also serves as a Kremlin special envoy under President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1] The fund's leadership has functioned as a channel for economic diplomacy between Moscow and Washington, with Dmitriev — a Harvard- and Stanford-educated financier born in Soviet-era Ukraine — long advocating expanded U.S.-Russian economic cooperation.[1]

## Role in the October 2025 U.S. Talks

In late October 2025 Dmitriev traveled to the United States in his dual capacity as RDIF head and Kremlin envoy for official talks with Trump administration officials, meeting Special Envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] in Miami.[1] He said the visit had been arranged "a while ago based on an invitation from the U.S. side" and that continued dialogue was "vital for the world."[1] The trip came two days after Washington sanctioned Russia's largest oil companies, [[rosneft|Rosneft]] and [[lukoil|Lukoil]], while pressing Moscow for an immediate ceasefire in Ukraine.[1]

The delegation's discussions ran into a third day, during which Dmitriev — identified in coverage as the RDIF's head — briefed U.S. counterparts on the Russian economy and the frontline situation, arguing that "any attempts to pressure Russia are simply pointless" and that the Ukraine conflict could only be resolved by "eradicating its root causes."[1] He asserted the Russian economy was "in good shape" and that the ruble had been the "most successful currency this year," strengthening roughly 40% against the U.S. dollar.[1] Dmitriev has also used his platform to promote a proposed "Trump-Putin" tunnel linking Alaska to the Russian Far East, a project framed in Badlands commentary as narrative kindling for a multipolar settlement rather than a serious infrastructure proposal.[1]
