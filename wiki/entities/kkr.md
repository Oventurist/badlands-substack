# KKR

KKR is a global private equity firm identified in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* as one of the managers of illiquid holdings offloaded by the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] during its 2025 endowment liquidation.[1]

## Harvard exposure

Public filings and insider leaks cited by the article indicate that HMC sold stakes in vehicles managed by [[blackstone|Blackstone]], KKR, and [[bain-capital|Bain Capital]] as part of a disposal of more than $1 billion in private equity positions.[1] Badlands Media characterized the sale not as portfolio rebalancing but as distress liquidation undertaken to keep the university afloat.[1]

Former and current HMC board members are further described as holding ties to KKR alongside [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]], Morgan Stanley, the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]], and the Ford and [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller]] Foundations.[1]
