# National Declassification Center

The **National Declassification Center** (NDC) is a body created within the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] during the administration of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1] Obama appointed [[david-ferriero|David Ferriero]] to head the new center simultaneously with his service as [[archivist-of-the-united-states|Archivist of the United States]].[1]

The center was given four years to work through 400 million pages of federal documents that remained top secret, some of which dated to World War I.[1] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that the practical effect of this arrangement was to place a single official in possession of hundreds of millions of the government's most sensitive historical records — a concentration of access that, in his framing, later made that official a potential witness to decades of concealed wrongdoing rather than a guardian of it.[1]
