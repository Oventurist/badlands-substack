# Hanoch Milwidsky

Hanoch Milwidsky is a member of the [[knesset|Knesset]] from the [[likud|Likud]] party, cited in the March 13, 2026 Badlands Brief for remarks made during a publicly broadcast Knesset debate over whether it should be lawful for Israeli personnel to sodomize male prisoners with objects.

The debate followed the arrest of [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers over the alleged assault of a Palestinian detainee at [[sde-teiman|Sde Teiman]]. Milwidsky is quoted as saying, "Shut up! If he is a Nukbha, then everything is legitimate to do!" — a reference to the Nukhba forces of Hamas. The Badlands commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presents the remark as emblematic of a political debate concerned not with whether the abuse occurred but with whether it should be permitted.
