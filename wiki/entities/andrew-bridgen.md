# Andrew Bridgen

**Andrew Bridgen** is a former British Member of Parliament who became a prominent dissident voice within UK politics. According to the Badlands News Brief, Bridgen was excommunicated from his political party after suggesting that elections are fake and after being labeled a "conspiracy theorist" over his statements on COVID-19.[1]

## Ukraine allegations

Bridgen subsequently turned whistleblower on Ukraine, stating that he had informed British Intelligence of child-trafficking and organ-trafficking rings operating there, as well as secret and illegal bioweapons laboratories.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Bridgen has since lived in fear for his life, and used his account to argue that any peace settlement in Ukraine must be accompanied by dismantling the criminal networks that Western governments helped create — comparing silence on the issue to those who knew about Jeffrey Epstein and had the power to act but did not.[1]
