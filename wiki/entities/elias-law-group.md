# Elias Law Group

The Elias Law Group is the election-law firm founded by Democratic attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], a former partner at Perkins Coie.[1] The firm represents all of the major Democratic entities in Washington and a wide range of senators and members of Congress.[1]

## DNC separation, April 2023

In April 2023 the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] parted ways with Elias, a decision attributed by sources to a number of strategic disagreements.[1] Elias had represented the DNC since 2009.[1] Despite the split, the firm continued to work for the DCCC, DSCC, DAGA and DLCC.[1]

Federal Election Commission filings cited in the reporting showed the firm had already collected roughly $100,000 from the DNC in 2023, following payments of just under $2 million during the 2022 cycle.[1]

## Litigation footprint

The firm is the vehicle for Elias's [[democracy-docket|Democracy Docket]] project, an array of voting-rights challenges that as of April 2023 covered 45 cases across 18 states.[1] Commentary by [[just-human|Just Human]] in the Badlands News Brief argued that the DNC's decision was surprising given the firm's litigation record, and floated the possibility that the separation reflected legal exposure arising from [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s inquiry, before which Elias had been interviewed and placed in front of a grand jury.[1]
