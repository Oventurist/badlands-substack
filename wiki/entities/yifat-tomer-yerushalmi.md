# Yifat Tomer-Yerushalmi

Maj. Gen. Yifat Tomer-Yerushalmi is an Israeli military lawyer who served as the Israel Defense Forces' military advocate general — the top legal officer in the IDF — until her resignation in late October 2025 over the leak of the [[sde-teiman|Sde Teiman]] abuse video.[1]

## Resignation and disappearance

Tomer-Yerushalmi resigned on Friday, October 31, 2025, over her involvement in the leak of video footage from the Sde Teiman detention facility, a matter under police investigation.[1] Days later she went missing for several hours after reportedly leaving a concerning note for her family, prompting fears for her wellbeing and a large-scale search effort before she was found alive and well.[1] Commentary circulating online speculated that she had been "suicided" by Israeli authorities; [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted she was ultimately located on a beach.[1]

## Arrest

Police subsequently arrested Tomer-Yerushalmi along with the military's former chief prosecutor, [[matan-solomosh|Col. Matan Solomosh]].[1] Both were suspected of obstruction of justice in connection with the leak and an alleged subsequent cover-up involving false reports given to officials about an internal investigation into the leak.[1] Police described the pair as suspected of "leaking and other serious criminal offenses" and said they would be brought before the Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court for an extension of their remand.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry framed the case as an indictment of Israeli political culture, arguing that the leaked video showed IDF soldiers sexually assaulting Palestinian prisoners and that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] responded by complaining about damage to Israel's international reputation rather than the underlying conduct.[1]
