# NBCUniversal

NBCUniversal (NBCU) is a major American media and entertainment conglomerate whose advertising division became newsworthy in May 2023 when its head of advertising, [[linda-yaccarino|Linda Yaccarino]], was reported to be in talks to become chief executive of [[twitter|Twitter]].[1]

## Advertising operations

Yaccarino served as chairman of global advertising and partnerships at NBCUniversal for more than a decade, during which the company pushed for better methods of measuring advertising effectiveness.[1] As head of NBCU's advertising sales she played a key role in the launch of the company's ad-supported Peacock streaming service.[1]

## Significance in the Twitter transition

The prospective move of NBCU's top advertising executive to Twitter was read by observers as an attempt to repair the social network's advertising business, which had been losing revenue after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s $44 billion acquisition.[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted Yaccarino's simultaneous ties to the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] as a point of concern for free-speech advocates.[1]
