# Kimberly Cheatle

Kimberly Cheatle was the director of the [[united-states-secret-service|United States Secret Service]] at the time of the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Butler, Pennsylvania. She resigned shortly afterward following congressional testimony about the security failure.

## Testimony and resignation

The Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]] places Cheatle's appearance before the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|Committee on Oversight and Accountability]] within a tight chronology: the assassination attempt on July 13; the death of Representative [[sheila-jackson-lee|Sheila Jackson Lee]] six days later; [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s exit from the presidential race two days after that; and Cheatle's testimony the following day. The article notes that during the hearing Cheatle "made mention of Jackson Lee's passing" and observed that Jackson Lee "was engaged in oversight of the Secret Service."

According to the source, Cheatle stepped down from her post the day after that testimony. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presents the compressed sequence without asserting a causal chain, instead using it to frame open questions for readers about what connected the shooting, Jackson Lee's death, Biden's withdrawal, and Cheatle's departure.
