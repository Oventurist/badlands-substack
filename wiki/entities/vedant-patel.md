# Vedant Patel

Vedant Patel is a spokesman for the United States [[state-department|State Department]], serving as a principal deputy spokesperson during the Biden administration and regularly conducting the department's press briefings.[1]

## Response to the 2024 Russian cabinet reshuffle

At a press briefing in May 2024, Patel characterized [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s replacement of defense minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] with economist [[andrey-belousov|Andrey Belousov]] as a sign of Kremlin weakness. "Our point of view is that this is further indication of Putin's desperation to sustain his war of aggression against Ukraine, despite it being a major drain on the Russian economy and the heavy losses of Russian troops, with some estimates as high as 315,000 casualties," he said.[1] Patel added that "the Kremlin's mobilization of its war of aggression against Ukraine has caused so many families to suffer" and that "Russia started this unprovoked war against Ukraine. Putin could end it at any time by withdrawing his forces from Ukraine."[1]

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited Patel's remarks as an example of State Department messaging that, in his reading, projected Western difficulties onto Moscow rather than describing Russian decision-making accurately.[1]
