# Signature Bank

Signature Bank (ticker SBNY) was a United States commercial bank whose failure in March 2023 followed within days of the collapse of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]], and which was treated in contemporaneous Badlands Media coverage as the second visible failure in an unfolding banking crisis.[1]

## March 2023 failure

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that the fallout from "the SIVB and now SBNY failures is just beginning," noting that despite the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] stepping in to effectively bail out the banks, markets continued to tumble in response.[1] In his reading, the decisive variable was not capital but trust: banks, he argued, are in the business of confidence, with the word itself derived from *confidere*, "to have full trust," making a bank a trust company in the general sense.[1]

## Commentary

Deschamps framed the failures as symptomatic rather than exceptional, arguing that the rigging of the system in favor of bankers is "baked in," that breaches of trust, backroom deals and violations of the spirit of fiduciary duty are standard business practice, and that "corruption isn't an outlier, it's the business model."[1] He compared trusting the international banking cartel to trusting "a doctor who's a serial killer or a firefighter who's an arsonist," and concluded that only a public recovery of literacy in law, trust and fiduciary finance would allow citizens to see modern banking clearly.[1]

The failures of Signature Bank and Silicon Valley Bank together drove sharp declines in regional bank shares, multiple trading halts, and rising credit-default-swap spreads at global majors including [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1]

## Relationship with the Trump family

Signature Bank, based in New York, once maintained a close business relationship with the family of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], with [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka Trump]] serving on the bank's board of directors from 2011 to 2013.[2] The bank severed ties with Trump and closed his personal accounts in protest of the January 6, 2021 riot at the Capitol, and publicly called for his resignation.[2] Two years later, in March 2023, Signature Bank collapsed and was taken over by regulators — a coincidence framed in conservative coverage as the "Trump curse."[2]

Badlands contributor [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] highlighted the account closures in commentary on the failure, arguing that as the fiat system cracked "under the weight of its own corruption" it was becoming apparent that the [[deep-state|deep state]] was losing its grip on a monetary system used as a means of control since its inception, and predicting that Signature and [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] would not be the last large banks to go under.[2]
