# Sisters of Perpetual Indulgence

The Sisters of Perpetual Indulgence are an activist organization whose members appear in drag as Catholic nuns. In the May 2023 coverage aggregated by Badlands Media, the group was described as an anti-Catholic organization that does "a bit of charity work" when it is not sponsoring what the cited Red State report called sacrilegious drag shows.[1]

## Dodgers Pride Night dispute

The group was slated to appear as honored guests at the [[los-angeles-dodgers|Los Angeles Dodgers]]' 2023 Pride Night and to receive the club's Community Hero Award.[1] After objections — including from Sen. [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], who commented that sanity had for once prevailed in California — the Dodgers disinvited the group, before ultimately reconciling with it and restoring the invitation.[1]

Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] described the reversal as the franchise capitulating to an anti-Christian and anti-Catholic organization, and argued the affair alienated players and the Dodgers' heavily Latino and Catholic fan base.[1] The Red State account cited by Badlands held that the initial disinvitation reflected fan anger rather than political pressure from Rubio.[1] The episode was placed in the same 2023 sequence as the [[bud-light|Bud Light]] boycott as an example of a brand misjudging its own customers.[1]
