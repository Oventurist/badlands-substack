# Hjalmar Schacht

Hjalmar Schacht was president of the Reichsbank from 1923 to 1930 and Germany's Minister of Economy from 1934 to 1937.[1] In Matthew Ehret's account he functioned less as an independent German policymaker than as an instrument controlled by [[montagu-norman|Montagu Norman]], the Governor of the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]].[1]

## Role at the BIS

The [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] was established under the [[young-plan|Young Plan]] and was nominally steered by Schacht as a mechanism for the repayment of First World War debts.[1] In practice, Ehret argues, the Swiss-based "Central Bank of Central Banks" was the key mechanism through which international financiers funded the Nazi machine, and it remained under the total control of Norman rather than Schacht.[1]
