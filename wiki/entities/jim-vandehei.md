# Jim VandeHei

Jim VandeHei is an American media executive and co-founder of both [[axios|Axios]] and Politico.[1]

## Defense of the Trump–Morning Joe meeting

After the November 2024 meeting between President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[morning-joe|Morning Joe]] hosts [[joe-scarborough|Joe Scarborough]] and [[mika-brzezinski|Mika Brzezinski]] drew a furious public reaction, VandeHei defended the hosts on social media, writing: "It is insane for critics to NOT think all of us in the media need to know more so we can share/report more."[1]

Associated Press coverage characterized such defenses as sounding "quaint," on the grounds that Morning Joe is not traditional journalism and that the episode illustrated the broader displacement of impartial fact-finding by opinionated news.[1]
