# BHR Partners

BHR is a Chinese private equity fund run by [[jonathan-li|Jonathan Li]] and cited by the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] in its examination of the [[hunter-biden|Biden family]]'s foreign business dealings.[1]

## Role in the Hunter Biden inquiry

Hunter Biden was listed as a member of BHR's board of directors.[1] On October 13, 2019, his lawyer George Mesires stated that Hunter Biden served with BHR "only as a member of its board of directors," which was purportedly an "unpaid position."[1]

The Oversight Committee tied BHR's principal to a $250,000 wire transfer sent on August 2, 2019 that listed [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Delaware home as the recipient address — the same house where classified documents were later found in the garage and where Hunter Biden was living while receiving millions from CCP-linked business dealings.[1] Business associate [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] described Joe Biden's familiar relationship with Li, including a Beijing coffee meeting, a phone call, and college recommendation letters for Li's children.[1]
