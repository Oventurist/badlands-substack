# Chester Arthur

Chester Arthur became Vice President of the United States as a compromise choice on the 1880 Republican ticket and succeeded to the presidency after the assassination of [[james-garfield|James Garfield]].[1]

Arthur was a protégé of Senator [[roscoe-conkling|Roscoe Conkling]], who had appointed him to run the [[new-york-custom-house|Custom House of New York]], where he was the highest-paid federal employee before entering national office.[1] Joe Lange presents this sequence — Garfield attacking the customs house patronage machine, then being assassinated and replaced by the machine's own former customs collector — as more than coincidence.[1]
