# Scott Gessler

Scott Gessler is a Colorado attorney and former Colorado Secretary of State who served as lead counsel for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the 2023 Denver trial over whether Trump could be barred from the state's 2024 presidential primary ballot under the disqualification clause of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

## Role in the Colorado ballot trial

During the third day of the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification trial]], Gessler cross-examined Colorado Deputy Elections Director Hillary Rudy, who testified about the processes and teams responsible for granting or denying ballot access within the state Department of Elections.[1] The exchange carried an unusual dynamic because Rudy had previously worked under Gessler during his tenure as Secretary of State, and observers described their cross-examination exchanges as noticeably cold.[1]

Following Rudy's testimony, Gessler moved for a directed verdict in Trump's favor. The court broke for lunch, and after the recess Judge Sarah B. Wallace denied the motion, allowing the case to proceed.[1] Gessler's defense team then called [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] as a witness, followed by Katrina Pierson.[1] Trump's campaign position throughout the proceeding was that his conduct surrounding the January 6, 2021 riot consisted of constitutionally protected political speech and therefore could not trigger the Fourteenth Amendment's insurrection bar.[1]
