# Juan Guaidó

Juan Guaidó is a Venezuelan opposition politician and former head of the country's National Assembly.[1]

## Essequibo arrest warrant

In December 2023, as [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] moved to annex the oil-rich Essequibo region of [[guyana|Guyana]] following a Sunday referendum, Venezuela's Prosecutor's Office issued arrest warrants for a dozen opposition members, including Guaidó.[1] Also targeted were three staff members of opposition presidential candidate [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]], charged with conspiring against the referendum.[1]

The warrants accompanied Maduro's placement of the disputed territory under military jurisdiction, a step that jeopardized [[exxonmobil|Exxon]] and [[chevron|Chevron]] operations there, while Guyana conducted joint military drills with the United States.[1]
