# Patrice Talon

Patrice Talon is the President of [[benin|Benin]], the West African state wedged between [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] and [[nigeria|Nigeria]].[1]

In December 2025, mutinous soldiers attempted to seize power and capture Talon, briefly taking over state television to announce his ousting and the suspension of the constitution while citing an alleged deterioration of security in northern Benin.[1] Beninese Foreign Minister Olushegun Adjadi Bakari said forces loyal to Talon were working to restore order and that "a large part of the army is still loyalist."[1] Mutineers clashed with the Republican Guard around 5am local time as they tried to storm Talon's residence, with casualties on both sides before loyal troops forced them to retreat.[1] The government said Nigerian airstrikes immobilized armored vehicles used by the mutineers and were decisive in defeating the attempt.[1]

Talon has for several years publicly denied accusations — voiced most prominently by Burkinabè President Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] — that Benin had allowed French military bases to be established near its border, a dispute that fed allegations the coup had backing from the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]].[1]
