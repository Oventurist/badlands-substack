# Laura Dogu

Laura Dogu is an American career diplomat who previously served as US ambassador to Honduras and to Nicaragua.[1]

## Chargé d'affaires in Caracas

Dogu has led United States efforts on the ground in Caracas as chargé d'affaires, overseeing restoration work at the chancery building ahead of a broader return of personnel and the eventual resumption of consular services at the [[us-embassy-in-caracas|US Embassy in Caracas]], which formally resumed operations in late March 2026 after being closed since 2019.[1] [[state-department|State Department]] officials noted that Dogu met with US investors in the week before the reopening, emphasizing that the private sector would play a central role in rebuilding [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s economy.[1]
