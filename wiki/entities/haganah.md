# Haganah

The **Haganah** was one of four Zionist paramilitary organizations that Badlands Media identifies as the perpetrators of the [[nakba|Nakba]], the 1947–48 terror campaign against Palestinian civilians.[1]

## Role

Badlands describes the Haganah, alongside the [[palmach|Palmach]], as providing tactical support to the [[irgun|Irgun]] and [[lehi|Lehi]] during the [[deir-yassin-massacre|Deir Yassin massacre]] of April 1948, in which more than 100 women and children were rounded up and killed.[1] One month after that massacre, the Haganah merged with the Irgun, Lehi and Palmach to formally create the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Defense Force]].[1]

Badlands presents this lineage as central to understanding Palestinian hostility to the IDF and to the recurring Israeli practice of "temporary" civilian evacuations later seen in the [[naksa|Naksa]] and in modern Gaza and [[west-bank|West Bank]] operations.[1]
