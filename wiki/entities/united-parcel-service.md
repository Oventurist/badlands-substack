# United Parcel Service

United Parcel Service (UPS) is an American package delivery and logistics company whose air cargo operations are centered on one of the largest air cargo hubs in the United States, located in Louisville, Kentucky.[1]

## Louisville cargo plane crash

On Tuesday, November 4, 2025, a UPS cargo plane crashed after taking off from Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport in Louisville, Kentucky, killing at least seven people and sending a cloud of thick smoke over the hub.[1] Governor [[andy-beshear|Andy Beshear]] of Kentucky said the plane, which was carrying three crew members, hit two businesses near the airport and that the death toll could rise.[1] Mayor [[craig-greenberg|Craig Greenberg]] of Louisville said four of those killed were on the ground at the time of the crash and that eleven others were injured.[1]

The aircraft exploded into a fireball and left a fiery trail through an industrial area near the airport, prompting officials to issue a shelter-in-place order for nearby residents.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that dash cam and overhead footage of the crash was "apocalyptic," expressed hope that the cause would eventually be established, and offered prayers for the injured and the families of the deceased.[1]
