# McKinsey & Company

McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm invoked in Badlands commentary for its role in popularizing the concept of a "polycrisis."[1]

## Polycrisis framing

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that McKinsey "told us a Polycrisis was coming" and that the sustained crisis cascade which followed overwhelmed the nation — suggesting the firm was revealing a plan rather than making a prediction.[1] She attributed the pattern not to Democrats but to "unipartisan globalists," adding that the system was interconnected and was now being dismantled and restructured.[1]

## AI decision-making critique (July 2025)

In July 2025 Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited McKinsey — described as "the world's strategy firm, driving world-changing strategies through every industry sector and geography in the world" — while criticizing the growing delegation of consequential decisions to artificial intelligence, drawing a parallel to the [[doge-ai-deregulation-decision-tool|DOGE AI Deregulation Decision Tool]].[2] Invoking the IBM maxim that "a computer can never be held accountable, therefore a computer must never make a management decision," she concluded that "reality collapse is accelerating."[2]
