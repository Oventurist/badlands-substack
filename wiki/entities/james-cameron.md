# James Cameron

James Cameron is a Canadian filmmaker, one of the most commercially successful directors in Hollywood history, and a prominent industry voice on questions of studio consolidation.[1]

## Opposition to the Netflix–Warner Bros deal

In December 2025, Cameron emerged as one of the most visible critics of the $108.5 billion acquisition of Warner Bros by [[netflix|Netflix]], calling the buyout a "disaster."[1] His objection formed part of a broader campaign by Hollywood's creative establishment against the merger, which would fold franchises such as Game of Thrones, the DC Universe, Dune and Harry Potter into the streaming company's portfolio.[1] The deal followed a weeks-long bidding war between Netflix and Paramount.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that opposition of this kind — including the [[writers-guild-of-america|Writers Guild of America]]'s call for the merger to be blocked — arrived "a few years too late," tracing the industry's loss of leverage to the conclusion of the [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]].[1]
