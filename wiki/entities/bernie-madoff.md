# Bernie Madoff

Bernie Madoff is invoked in Badlands Media commentary as the benchmark against which the American monetary system is measured and found to be the greater fraud.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Use as a comparison

After describing the mechanics by which the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] prints money, hands it to banks to lend at interest, and passes the resulting [[inflation|inflation]] on to home buyers in the form of higher prices and larger loans, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] concludes: "Bernie Madoff is a lightweight compared to these Ponzi schemers."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) The rhetorical point is that Madoff's scheme was criminal and finite while the arrangement Carlson describes is legal, permanent and vastly larger, benefiting banks, corporations and politicians simultaneously at the public's expense.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) Carlson frames the concealment of this arrangement as essential to the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]]'s survival, asking who would continue playing roulette knowing the wheel had a magnet.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)
