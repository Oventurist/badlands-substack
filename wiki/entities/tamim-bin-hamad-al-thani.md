# Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani

Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani is the Emir of [[qatar|Qatar]]. In the February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief he is discussed in connection with the first Washington meeting of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]].

## The Loomer and Levin dispute

According to [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Trump used the Board of Peace meeting to indirectly call out [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] and [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] over what the author describes as their incessant attacks against Emir Tamim. Loomer responded by posting video of Trump's remarks and doubling down, stating that she "hates Qatar." The Brief presents the exchange as one item in a longer list of behaviors by pro-Israel media figures and Israeli officials that it characterizes as undermining and disrespecting the President's regional peace efforts, in which Qatar and its emir are cast as partners rather than adversaries.
