# Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani

Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani is the current Emir of Qatar and the son of former Emir [[hamad-bin-khalifa-al-thani|Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani]].[1] He received the throne on June 25, 2013, when his father announced his abdication in a brief televised address rather than reigning until death.[1]

Tamim's accession was reported in the July 13, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] in the context of his father's death at age 74, one of a cluster of prominent deaths announced in the same news cycle as that of Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1] Badlands commentary situated the 2013 handover within a wider Gulf pattern, noting that Saudi King Salman similarly abdicated in favor of his son Mohammed four years later.[1]

## Badlands assessment of the House of Thani

Commentary published in the March 6, 2025 Badlands News Brief described Tamim as "an old friend of Donald Trump's" who was most likely introduced to the president by their mutual friend [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]], and assessed that the emir appeared to be working with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[2] The piece framed the March 2025 clash between [[qatar|Qatar]] and Israel's [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] over funding for [[hamas|Hamas]] as a symptom of the fracturing of the [[old-guard|Old Guard]] in the Middle East, noting that Qatar had long been under the influence of the [[cia|CIA]], MI6 and the [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]].[2] It further held it reasonable to assume that the House of Thani — which traces its lineage back to Abraham through his son Ishmael, first settled in Qatar in 1720, and has ruled the maritime nation since the 1800s — would like to regain its sovereignty, and that the price of that sovereignty is the death of the Old Guard.[2]

## Mar-a-Lago meeting with Trump, September 2024

On September 23, 2024, Tamim met former U.S. president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at his Mar-a-Lago resort in Florida, together with Qatari Prime Minister [[mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani|Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani]].[3] Revealing the meeting in a Truth Social post, Trump hailed the leadership of [[qatar|Qatar]] — then under attack from Republican lawmakers over its hosting of [[hamas|Hamas]] and its bankrolling of [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] — writing that "the emir has proven to be a great and powerful leader of his country, advancing on all levels at record speed" and that "he is someone also who strongly wants peace in the Middle East and all over the world."[3] Trump added: "We had a great relationship during my time in the White House, and it will be even stronger this time around!"[3]

The 2024 encounter echoed a September 2017 White House visit, when Trump hosted Tamim during the Gulf embargo and noted that the two had known each other a long time — "which may surprise some people" — while promising to resolve the dispute between Qatar and its neighbors.[3] Trump ultimately negotiated the reconciliation between Tamim and Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] on January 4, 2021, two weeks before leaving office; Badlands Media speculated that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] also played a facilitating role.[3]

## Saudi–Qatari high-speed rail agreement (December 2025)

On December 8, 2025, following a meeting in Riyadh with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] under the Saudi–Qatari Coordination Council, the Emir's government signed an agreement with [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] to build a high-speed rail link connecting Hamad International Airport in Doha with King Salman International Airport in Riyadh.[4] The project was expected to take six years, create 30,000 jobs and carry more than 10 million passengers a year, reducing travel time between the capitals to roughly two hours.[4]

Badlands commentary presented the agreement as the culmination of a reconciliation that began during the 2017 blockade of [[qatar|Qatar]], when Sheikh Tamim visited President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the White House in September of that year and Trump pledged to help resolve the dispute with MBS; a peace deal was signed the week Trump left office in 2021.[4]
