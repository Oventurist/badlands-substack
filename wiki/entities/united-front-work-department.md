# United Front Work Department

The **United Front Work Department** is the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] organ responsible for cultivating influence among groups and individuals outside the party, including foreign officials.[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* describes United Front networks as the mechanism by which the CCP penetrates U.S. governance at the state level, below the threshold of federal scrutiny.[1] The essay cites the 2024 indictment of former New York deputy chief of staff [[linda-sun|Linda Sun]] — accused of acting as an unregistered agent of the CCP while serving under Governors [[andrew-cuomo|Andrew Cuomo]] and [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]], suppressing pro-Taiwan interactions and embedding CCP-aligned messaging into state policy — as a case that "pulled back the veil" on this strategy.[1]

According to the essay, at least a dozen current and former U.S. governors have appeared on CCP "friendship" lists, participating in cultural exchanges and diplomatic forums designed by the United Front Work Department to cultivate influence, shape perception, and embed ideological leverage.[1] The essay characterizes these engagements as "tactical alignments - cloaked as diplomacy, executed as occupation," and groups them with [[confucius-institutes|Confucius Institutes]] and CCP student-monitoring operations as expressions of a unified strategy to compromise decision-making and steer narratives within American governance.[1]
