# East Palestine

East Palestine is a village in Columbiana County, eastern Ohio, near the Ohio–Pennsylvania border, and the site of the February 3, 2023 [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern]] freight train derailment that released hazardous materials. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a case study in the perceived abandonment of working-class America by the Biden administration and its rehabilitation under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## The 2023 derailment

On February 3, 2023, a Norfolk Southern freight train derailed in East Palestine — 38 cars, with 20 of them carrying hazardous materials including vinyl chloride, ethylene glycol, ethylhexyl acrylate, butyl acrylate, and isobutylene. According to Congressional records cited in the February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, emergency responders intentionally vented and burned vinyl chloride from some tank cars to reduce the risk of a catastrophic explosion. Residents within about a one-mile radius of the derailment were ordered to evacuate during the incident. No fatalities were reported at the time.

In February 2025, new lawsuits were filed alleging that at least seven people — including a one-week-old infant — died as a result of exposure to toxic chemicals from the derailment and its aftermath. These deaths are allegations in civil suits and have not, as of those reports, impacted official casualty figures.

The [[national-transportation-safety-board|National Transportation Safety Board]] (NTSB) and [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]] (FRA) determined that the derailment was caused by a failed/overheated wheel bearing on a railcar.

## The $10 million recovery pledge (February 2026)

On Thursday, February 19, 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posted on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that East Palestine would receive $10 million for economic recovery, provided through the [[economic-development-administration|Economic Development Administration]], in addition to the administration's long-term health study for residents. Trump's post framed the town as "disgracefully betrayed and forgotten about by Crooked Joe Biden" — a contrast drawn throughout the corpus between the Biden-era response and Trump's recovery promises.
