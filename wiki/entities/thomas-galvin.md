# Thomas Galvin

Thomas Galvin is a member of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] in Arizona. He is named in his official capacity among the defendants in [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s December 9, 2022 election contest arising from the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], alongside [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], [[clint-hickman|Clint Hickman]], [[jack-sellers|Jack Sellers]], and [[steve-gallardo|Steve Gallardo]].

As described in "Arizona Earthquake!", the Board is accused of misrepresenting the timeline of Election Day tabulator failures across [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s 223 vote centers and of concealing the number of centers with waits over 90 minutes.
