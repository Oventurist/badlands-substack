# Cathars

The Cathars were a medieval religious movement of southern France whose fortress at Montségur was the object of an earlier search by the Nazi Grail researcher [[otto-rahn|Otto Rahn]].[1]

In his report to [[heinrich-himmler|Himmler]] and the [[new-templar-order|New Templars]], titled *Lucifer's Court*, Rahn identified the Cathars as a "pure" Christian sect which practiced Luciferianism before being wiped out by the Catholic Church during the Albigensian Crusade of 1209.[1] This reading, in which Lucifer is recast as a light-bearer opposed to the darkened churches of Rome, formed part of the Gnostic-Luciferian framework Rahn supplied to the SS.[1]
