# Richard Barron

Richard Barron is the former Fulton County, Georgia elections director whose testimony during the 2020 certification week became a central exhibit in later election-integrity investigations.[1]

## Certification-week testimony

Barron testified on the Friday of Georgia's 2020 certification week that county officials were aware their tallies fell short, stating "we knew we were short" and that "we called the state."[1] Investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]], presenting to the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] in January 2026, assumed the reference to "the state" meant the office of Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]].[1]

Rossi paired Barron's statement with a report generated at 12:06 p.m. on December 3, 2020 and emailed minutes later to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], arguing the sequence showed intentional human intervention in the count.[1] Badlands writers summarized the resulting narrative as: officials knew on November 19, 2020 that the counts did not match, and on December 3, 2020 they appear to have made them match.[1]
