# Global Times

Global Times is a Chinese state-affiliated newspaper used as an outlet for official and semi-official messaging directed at foreign audiences.[1]

## Publication of the CIA color revolution report

On May 4, 2023, Global Times released a report compiled by China's National Computer Virus Emergency Response Center and the cybersecurity company 360, alleging that the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] had spent decades fomenting [[color-revolution|color revolutions]] around the world using technical means.[1] The document claimed that Washington's technological advantage allowed it to hold sway over institutions and individuals worldwide that rely on US-made digital equipment or software.[1]

According to the report as published, the CIA had attempted to overthrow governments in at least 50 states, with the 2014 Maidan coup in Ukraine, the 2014 "Sunflower Revolution" in Taiwan and the 2009 "Green Revolution" in Iran among the most notable examples.[1] It further alleged that the agency relied on methods including "48 advanced cyber weapons," and that the toolkit involved encrypted network communication services such as US-developed TOR technology to help protesters communicate while avoiding government surveillance.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary held that the report indicated that long-standing claims about CIA-driven regime change no longer amounted to conspiracy theory.[1]

## Coverage of the 2024 US election

Shortly before the [[2024-iowa-caucus|2024 Iowa Caucus]] began, the *Global Times* urged the world to "prepare for the possibility of Donald Trump being re-elected as president," publishing assessments from its stable of American-politics experts who lamented that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would likely emerge as the Republican nominee and perform strongly in Iowa.[2] The paper predicted that a Trump return would accompany a more divided United States, and assessed that Trump was so popular that the biggest obstacle to his return to power was Democratic attempts to remove him from presidential ballots through the courts.[2]

The outlet routinely disparages American presidential elections, describing the United States as a "low-quality democracy" compared with rule in China under [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[2] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that it did not help China, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] or globalism for Chinese state media to prepare the collective mind for Trump's return, reading the item instead as "a wink from the sovereign alliance" that cut against three years of narrative that Trump was finished, unelectable and heavily indicted.[2]
