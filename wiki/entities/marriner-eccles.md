# Marriner Eccles

**Marriner Eccles** was a former governor of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1] He is widely quoted for a remark that Badlands Media used to summarize the debt basis of the modern money supply: "If there were no debts in our money system, there wouldn't be any money."[1]

The quotation is cited in the outlet's explanation of [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]], which argues that because money enters circulation only through lending, every dollar in existence is owed by someone to someone else.[1]
