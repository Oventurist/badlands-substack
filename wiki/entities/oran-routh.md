# Oran Alexander Routh

Oran Alexander Routh is the son of [[ryan-wesley-routh|Ryan Routh]], the suspect held in connection with the second apparent assassination attempt against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2024.[1] He was arrested on federal child pornography charges in September 2024.[1]

## Arrest and charges

Oran Routh was taken into custody on Tuesday, September 24, 2024, according to court documents.[1] Investigators said they found "hundreds" of child pornography files while searching his residence in Guilford County, North Carolina, on the preceding Saturday, in connection with an investigation described as "unrelated" to child exploitation.[1] He faced two charges: receipt of child pornography and possession of child pornography.[1]

His father remained in custody at the time on two federal gun charges — possessing a firearm as a convicted felon and possessing a firearm with an obliterated serial number — while being investigated over the apparent assassination attempt.[1]
