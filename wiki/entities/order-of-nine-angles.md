# Order of Nine Angles

The Order of Nine Angles is an occult network described in reporting as an affiliate of the neo-Nazi organization [[the-base|The Base]].[1]

In an August 2024 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited reports that The Base and affiliates including the Order of Nine Angles engaged in satanic rituals involving animal sacrifice, and treated this as incongruous with the apparently devout Roman Catholic background of The Base's founder [[rinaldo-nazzaro|Rinaldo Nazzaro]].[1] The inconsistency was offered as one of several indications that the public profile of the group may be a constructed narrative rather than an organic movement.[1]
