# Chris Kise

Chris Kise is an American attorney who served as principal counsel to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the New York civil fraud case brought by Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]].[1]

## Role in the New York civil fraud case

After Judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]] issued a $355 million judgment against Trump in February 2024, Kise announced that the defense would challenge Engoron's underlying definition of fraud.[1] He told Newsweek that "the case raises serious legal and constitutional questions regarding 'fraud' claims/findings without any actual fraud."[1] Asked about timing, Kise said the appeal "will depend on many factors so it's hard to say at the moment, but in any event, it will fall within the 30-day clock" allowed by the court.[1]

Kise also accused James and Engoron of attempting to drive Trump out of New York, arguing that the outcome was bad for the city.[1] The dispute forms part of the broader [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud trial]].[1]
