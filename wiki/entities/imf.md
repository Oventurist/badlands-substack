# International Monetary Fund

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) is the Bretton Woods institution charged with monitoring the international monetary system and lending to states in balance-of-payments difficulty. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], the IMF appears as a vehicle of the liberalization applied to the former Soviet bloc.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]] as "the IMF's" representative in the Western side of Perestroika, alongside Vice President [[al-gore|Al Gore]], Rhodes Scholar [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbott]] and the Goldman Sachs banker [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]. In the essay's argument, the resulting "privatization and liberalization reforms" were "applied viciously to Ukraine and other Warsaw pact countries," compressing into a decade a process that had taken thirty years in the West and producing the oligarchic class of the 1990s.

The IMF thus belongs, in the article's schema, to the same institutional family as the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]], the [[european-union|European Union]] and [[nafta|NAFTA]] — instruments through which nations are said to have lost control of their own powers of credit generation.
