# Kim Iversen

**Kim Iversen** is an American independent commentator who appeared on programming produced by [[the-hill|The Hill]] alongside [[robby-soave|Robby Soave]].[1]

## Coverage of the Greensburg rally

Badlands contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] cites a segment in which Iversen and Soave discussed how "out of touch" [[donald-trump|Trump]] was with his base after he introduced [[woody-johnson|Woody]] and Suzanne Johnson of [[johnson-and-johnson|Johnson & Johnson]] at the Save America rally in Greensburg, Pennsylvania on May 6, 2022, and the crowd booed.[1] Gillespie presents the clip approvingly as evidence of mainstream commentary missing what she reads as a deliberate signal from Trump rather than a political misstep.[1]
