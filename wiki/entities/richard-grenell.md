# Richard Grenell

Richard "Ric" Grenell is an American diplomat and envoy associated with both [[donald-trump|Trump]] administrations. In the January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presents Grenell as the connective thread in a long-running American channel to the Venezuelan government.

Ghost notes that Grenell met [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]] — brother of acting President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] — in Mexico City in late 2020. PJ Media reported the meeting at the time as an attempt by a Trump ambassador to secretly negotiate the exit of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], although Ghost writes that he is "unconvinced that was the real intent." Grenell reunited with Jorge Rodríguez in January 2025, shortly after Trump's second inauguration, travelling to Caracas to meet Maduro and bring home six imprisoned Americans.

Ghost cites this sequence as evidence of "a clear continuity of the Maduro plotline between the first and second Trump administrations," and as support for his broader claim that the post-Maduro diplomacy reflects a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] arrangement.
