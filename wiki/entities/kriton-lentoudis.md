# Kriton Lentoudis

Kriton Lentoudis is a Greek shipping magnate identified in Badlands Brief reporting as the controlling figure behind [[evalend-shipping|Evalend Shipping]], the owner of the tanker Kiku.[1]

The Kiku was reportedly struck by [[iran|Iran]] in late June 2026 after attempting to bypass the traffic lanes agreed for the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] under a memorandum of understanding between Washington and Tehran.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Lentoudis's Evalend had acted as a commercial counterparty to [[vs-tankers-fze|VS Tankers FZE]], formerly AISSOT, leasing that firm many of its ships — a company later named by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] within the sanctioned oil-smuggling network of [[salim-ahmed-said|Salim Ahmed Said]].[1] On that basis the author argued that Lentoudis's vessels were entangled in the laundering of Iranian oil and money through [[iraq|Iraq]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]].[1]
