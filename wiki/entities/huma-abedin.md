# Huma Abedin

Huma Abedin is a longtime aide within the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] political orbit. Badlands Media commentary references her 2024 engagement to [[alex-soros|Alex Soros]] as an event with dynastic significance.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) She has arguably been closer to Hillary Clinton than any other single person over two decades, and her rise — from girlhood in [[jeddah|Jeddah]], Saudi Arabia, to a lowly White House intern during the Monica Lewinsky scandal, to campaign vice chairman and prospective White House chief of staff — is described as one of the least understood aspects of Clinton's organization.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Early life and family

Abedin was born in Kalamazoo, Michigan, in 1976 to Pakistani and Indian Muslim academics.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) At the age of two she moved with her parents to Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, returning to the United States at 18 to study at [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Her parents spent their careers at Saudi Arabian universities: her mother Saleha is a sociologist and vice dean at a women's college in Jeddah, while her late father Syed helped start the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]], an international journal devoted to the Muslim diaspora in non-Muslim lands.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Badlands account stresses her Pakistani background as an important data point and notes that Jeddah is also the base of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] and of the [[osama-bin-laden|Bin Laden]] family — "so many roads lead to Jeddah."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Career with Hillary Clinton

Abedin began working for Hillary Clinton in 1996, when she was a 19-year-old intern fresh from George Washington University "assigned" to the First Lady's office — the same year [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] began his affair with [[monica-lewinsky|Monica Lewinsky]] and the same year Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] died.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) When the sex scandal detonated in 1998, Abedin was the gofer in the girls' club forming a leak-proof shield around the humiliated first lady.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Over the years she served as Clinton's "body woman," traveling chief of staff, senior adviser, and deputy chief of staff at the [[state-department|State Department]], and finally as vice-chair of the 2016 presidential campaign; whatever the title, the role was consistently that of confessor, confidante and constant companion.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) A former adviser to Bill Clinton called her "a mini Hillary," and she has been described as a "second daughter" to the Clintons.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) She accompanied Clinton to Chicago in November 2008 to meet President-Elect [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] about the Secretary of State post, and was present through Clinton's nearly eleven-hour congressional testimony on [[benghazi|Benghazi]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Abedin controlled access to Clinton: whoever wished to curry favor had to go through her, and as one former adviser put it, "Everybody fights to be at the center, and Huma controls a lot of that dynamic."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) One longtime Clinton observer called her, along with Chelsea, "the third rail" of the Clinton political world, while campaign chair John Podesta praised her as "an integral part of the team."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) As deputy chief of staff she arranged meetings with world leaders, scanned Clinton's talking points, and was copied on sensitive email traffic between her boss, diplomats, the White House and foreign heads of state — giving her access to the most sensitive information at the top of the Obama administration.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) She had also been Clinton's closest aide during her Senate service, when Clinton sat on committees overseeing the Armed Forces, Education and the Budget.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Muslim Brotherhood allegations

Abedin worked part time at the *Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs* between 1998 and 2009, serving as assistant editor for a dozen years — from the start of her Clinton White House internship in 1996 until just before she joined the State Department — and overlapping for seven of those years with [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], then on the journal's advisory board.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Naseef, described as a wealthy Saudi financier of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]], had established the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]] and recruited Abedin's parents to run it.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

While an intern and assistant editor in the mid-to-late 1990s, Abedin also sat on the executive board of the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at George Washington University, heading its Social Committee; that chapter was spiritually guided a few years after she left by the Al-Qaeda-affiliated cleric [[anwar-al-awlaki|Anwar al-Awlaki]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Former Representative Michele Bachmann's 2012 accusation that Abedin might be a [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] sleeper was dismissed in Clinton circles as risible, and Vanity Fair's coverage painted her critics as right-wing conspiracy theorists.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Badlands account rejects that framing, arguing she was a Muslim Brotherhood plant from the beginning and questioning how she ever obtained a security clearance given her family's ties.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Teneo, the Clinton Foundation and the State Department

The second installment of the America for Sale series takes as its organising instruction the Q admonition "FOLLOW HUMA," drawn from Q drop 71 of November 4, 2017 — posted the day before [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] was arrested in Saudi Arabia — which asked who connects HRC and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] to Saudi Arabia and who has ties to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]].3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) In that reading Abedin is the connection between [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], the Clinton Foundation and Saudi Arabia, and her birth in Michigan made her an American citizen and therefore the ideal choice to serve as Alwaleed's liaison, given that her family had moved to Jeddah, Alwaleed's home town.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The article adds a connection it says Q did not mention: Teneo.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Citing The Washington Times, it reports that while still working at the [[state-department|State Department]] Abedin was paid by the private consulting firm [[teneo-holdings|Teneo Holdings]] to help stage a star-studded reception on September 20, 2012 at the Essex House in New York City — days after the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] tragedy — at which [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and former British Prime Minister Tony Blair spoke, entertaining potential Teneo clients with access to three former world leaders on a single stage.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Over a seven-month period she earned a $15,000-a-month consulting fee from Teneo while simultaneously drawing pay as a "[[special-government-employee|special government employee]]" advising Clinton at the State Department.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author treats this as an obvious conflict of interest, noting that Abedin was not an ordinary employee needing a second job but a person privy to secret information at the highest levels.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The same special government employee status allowed Abedin to consult simultaneously for the Clinton Foundation, where she assessed the charity's ongoing programs to pave the way for Clinton's return there after she left the State Department in early 2013.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) She was therefore, in the article's account, liaison both between Hillary Clinton and Teneo and between the Clinton Foundation and the Secretary of State — placing her at the centre of what it calls a massive money laundering operation.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Breitbart is cited reporting that a few months after Clinton took office in 2009, Abedin began communicating with Clinton Foundation donors who were asking favours from the State Department, an arrangement the article labels a [[pay-for-play|pay-for-play]] spider web.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Throughout this period she remained assistant editor of the Muslim Brotherhood journal her parents helped start in Jeddah.3(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The Weiner laptop emails

When FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] announced he was closing the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] email investigation for a second time days before the 2016 election, he certified to Congress that the bureau had "reviewed all of the communications" discovered on a personal laptop used by Abedin and her husband, [[anthony-weiner|Anthony Weiner]]; Real Clear Investigations reported that virtually none of that account was true.[6] One career FBI special agent involved in the case complained to New York colleagues that officials in Washington had tried to "bury" the new trove, which he believed contained the full archive of Clinton's emails, including long-sought missing messages from her first months at the State Department.[6]

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues the date range is the significant detail: emails from Clinton's earliest months as Secretary of State would encompass the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One]] deal and therefore expose [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s role in covering it up as FBI director, giving Comey a personal motive to protect his longtime friend as well as Clinton.[6] The laptop was among the matters overseen by the [[southern-district-of-new-york|SDNY]], and [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]]'s New York law-enforcement relationships are said to have given him access to far more of its contents than was ever disclosed publicly, forming part of his "insurance file."[6]

Q drop 3 (October 29, 2017) ended with the instruction "Follow Huma," which Lange flags as central and connects to a promised future argument linking Abedin, the Podesta brothers and the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[6] Q drop 15 had listed "11.6 - Huma indicted."[6] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## Bachmann's security clearance challenge

Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and others roundly criticized Bachmann for raising the issue, a defense Lange characterizes as protecting the enemy from within.[7] Q drop #58 of November 2, 2017 recounted the episode and asked "Who took an undisclosed trip to SA? What was the purpose of a f2f v phone call?", while Q drop #1360 of May 13, 2018 tied Abedin's Brotherhood connections to the Syria weapons pipeline, in which the Brotherhood helped the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] smuggle shoulder-fired missiles into Syria with Turkish assistance.[7] Lange places Abedin at [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s right hand throughout the [[arab-spring|Arab Spring]] period and the associated push to advance [[sharia-law|Sharia law]] both abroad and within the United States.[7]
