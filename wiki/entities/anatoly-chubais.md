# Anatoly Chubais

Anatoly Chubais is a Russian politician and economist who directed the privatization of state assets in Russia during the 1990s. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names him as one of the monetarist "reformers" who compressed into a single decade a liberalization process that had taken thirty years in the West.

## Role in the source

The article states that the undoing of the sovereign powers of the former Soviet bloc "was done under the direction of such monetarist 'reformers' such as Anatoly Chubais and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] under [[boris-yeltsin|Yeltsin]]." Ehret links to his own March 2022 piece, "As New Purge of Fifth Columnists Approaches: Anatoly Chubais Jumps Ship," originally published by the Strategic Culture Foundation, marking Chubais's departure from Russia.

The essay pairs these domestic reformers with Western participants including the IMF's [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]], [[al-gore|Al Gore]], [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbott]] and [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], and holds the combined process responsible for producing the oligarchic class that included [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]], [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]] and [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]].
