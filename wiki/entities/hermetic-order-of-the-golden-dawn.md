# Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn

The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn is an occult order named in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as one of the two groups whose practice of the Hermetic teachings has given the tradition "a bad rap."[1] The other is [[freemasonry|the Freemasons]].[1]

DeLarme argues that despite these associations the teachings themselves are relatively neutral, and that it is the way they are applied that determines whether they are for good or ill — the framing with which he introduces the reader to the [[corpus-hermeticum|Corpus Hermeticum]], the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]] and the other Hermetic works.[1]
