# PDD Holdings

**PDD Holdings** is the Chinese e-commerce group that owns the discount retail app [[temu|Temu]].[1]

## Tariff pass-through to US consumers

In late April 2025, PDD-owned Temu appeared to be passing on nearly all of U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s new import taxes to American consumers, more than doubling the cost of some products in a move seen as adding to concern about the inflationary impact of tariffs.[1] Parcels priced up to US$800 from China, previously exempted from levies under the so-called [[de-minimis-exemption|de minimis rule]], became subject to an ad-valorem tax of 120 per cent of a product's value, or a per-postal-item fee of at least US$100 starting 2 May.[1] Temu required customers to pay those levies on top of the original cost of the goods.[1]

A review of 14 shipped-from-China items on Temu's bestsellers list showed taxes exceeding the value of the product; a US$19.49 power strip attracted US$27.56 in import charges, or 1.41 times the price of the product.[1] There were no import surcharges on items already held in U.S. warehouses, keeping the prices of such goods generally stable.[1] Rival Chinese retailer [[shein|Shein]] also raised prices over the same period.[1]
