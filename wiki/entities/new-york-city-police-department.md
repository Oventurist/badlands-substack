# New York City Police Department

The New York City Police Department (NYPD) is the municipal police force of New York City. In September 2024 the department was drawn into a federal investigation of senior city officials that culminated in the resignation of its commissioner, [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]].[1]

## 2024 federal investigation and leadership change

Caban announced his resignation on September 12, 2024, effective the following day, about a week after federal agents raided his home along with the homes of other members of the administration of Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[1] Caban told the department that coverage of the raids had "created a distraction" and that the NYPD "deserves someone who can solely focus on protecting and serving New York City."[1]

Adams defended the department's record under Caban's leadership, citing the seizure of more than 18,000 illegal guns and significant reductions in shootings and robberies.[1] [[tom-donilon|Tom Donilon]] was named as Caban's replacement.[1]

## Alleged influence-peddling

Federal scrutiny extended to alleged influence-peddling touching the department. James Caban, brother of the commissioner, was reported to be under federal scrutiny for allegedly acting as a fixer for Manhattan restaurants and nightclubs experiencing trouble with the NYPD.[1] Both [[philip-banks-iii|Philip Banks III]], the deputy mayor for public safety, and Edward and James Caban were reported to be former NYPD officers, a detail Badlands cited as evidence of an insider network spanning policing, city government, lobbying, and NGOs.[1]

## Raid on the interim commissioner

[[thomas-donlon|Thomas Donlon]] became interim commissioner following [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]]'s abrupt resignation, and roughly ten days later, on September 20, 2024, federal authorities raided Donlon's home.[2] Donlon acknowledged the search in a brief department statement issued late the following Saturday night, saying investigators "took materials that came into my possession 20 years ago and are unrelated to my work with the New York City Police Department," and adding that the department would not comment further because it was not an NYPD matter.[2] Press speculation held that the raid was a message from federal authorities to Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]], while Badlands commentary treated the "unrelated" framing as a likely cover story.[2]

## 2025 mayoral race stakes

The [[new-york-post|New York Post]] editorial board's August 2025 appeal for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to endorse Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] rested substantially on policing, citing the lowest number of shootings in the first half of 2025 since the city began keeping records and warning that "defund the police" activist [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]] would hobble the department.[3]
