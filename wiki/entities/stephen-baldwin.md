# Stephen Baldwin

Stephen Baldwin is an American actor and the father-in-law of singer [[justin-bieber|Justin Bieber]]. He appears in Badlands Media commentary as a public figure signaling awareness of an impending reckoning against the entertainment industry.[1]

## "Calm before the storm" videos

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reported that Baldwin "has been making cryptic videos stating we are in the calm before the storm," and observed that most readers would understand this as a [[qanon|Q]] reference — an allusion to [[the-storm|the Storm]], the anticipated mass exposure and arrest of corrupt elites.[1] The essay linked those posts to its account of alleged abuse suffered by Bieber as a child performer in Hollywood.[1]

## Context

Baldwin's remarks are used in the essay to argue that insiders in the entertainment world understand that the prosecution of [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] marks the beginning of a broader purge of the [[cabal|Cabal]].[1] Badlands Media notes that its articles represent the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
