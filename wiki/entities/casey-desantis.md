# Casey DeSantis

Casey DeSantis is the wife of Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] and, in her own right, a significant political figure in Florida Republican politics.[1] By April 2025 she was laying the groundwork for a potential campaign to succeed her husband as governor — an effort complicated both by resistance within the state party and by a funding scandal attached to her signature initiative.[1]

## Political ambitions

Long regarded as a political force independent of her husband, Casey DeSantis encountered "quiet but firm resistance" as she prepared a bid for the governorship.[1] A run would set her against Representative [[byron-donalds|Byron Donalds]], the candidate handpicked by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to lead the state, making the race a proxy contest between the DeSantis and Trump wings of the Florida GOP.[1]

## Hope Florida scandal

Casey DeSantis is the public face of [[hope-florida|Hope Florida]], a state assistance program whose associated charity became the subject of a legislative investigation in spring 2025.[1] Lawmakers examined why $10 million from a state Medicaid settlement was routed to a charity connected to Hope Florida, which then transferred the same amount to two groups that financially backed a DeSantis-led campaign against legalizing recreational marijuana.[1] Key lawmakers publicly suggested the flow of money appeared illegal.[1]

The couple denied wrongdoing and stood by the program; the governor dismissed the criticism as "all political," while Casey DeSantis, appearing alongside him, described Hope Florida as "a philosophy" that "shows we can help people in need."[1] Notably, the investigation was driven by Republicans rather than Democrats — a marker of the governor's eroding influence in a capital he had once controlled with unchallenged authority.[1]
