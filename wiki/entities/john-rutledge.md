# John Rutledge

John Rutledge was the governor of South Carolina during the [[siege-of-charleston|British invasion of 1780]], who retreated from the state intending to raise additional militia for the American cause.[1]

## Escape from Charleston

As the British closed on Charleston, Rutledge was escorted north by a party of [[continental-army|Continental]] dragoons.[1] They fell in with a body of Virginia militia that had marched down from North Carolina to reinforce the city, and the combined force turned north toward Charlotte.[1] The dragoons and militiamen made camp at Waxhaw, South Carolina, instructing the governor to ride on to safer ground — a decision that spared him the [[waxhaws-massacre|Waxhaws massacre]] inflicted on the camp by [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]].[1]
