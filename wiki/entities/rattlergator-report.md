# RattlerGator Report

RattlerGator Report is a daily program on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] network, introduced as one of several new additions to the lineup announced in the January 16–22, 2023 show schedule.[1] It airs Monday through Friday at 8am ET and occupies the earliest slot on the network's daily programming grid.[1]

## Format

The program is described as a one-hour discussion intended to "start your day off right," covering current affairs, religion, and public life.[1] Its placement ahead of [[badlands-daily|Badlands Daily]] positions it as the network's morning opener.[1]

## Distribution

As with all Badlands Media shows named in the January 2023 schedule, live streams and replays of RattlerGator Report were consolidated onto the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
