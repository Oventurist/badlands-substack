# Alan Turing

**Alan M. Turing** was a British mathematician and logician who made major contributions to mathematics, cryptanalysis, logic, philosophy, and mathematical biology, as well as to the fields later named computer science, cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and artificial life.[1]

## Codebreaking

Turing led Ultra, the secret British codebreaking group set up in 1939 after the Poles — who had first broken the [[enigma-machine|Enigma]] code in the early 1930s — handed their work to Britain in the face of likely German invasion.[1]

## The universal Turing machine

In the course of his work on the Entscheidungsproblem, Turing invented the universal Turing machine, an abstract computing device that encapsulates the fundamental logical principles of the digital computer.[1] The Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" cites this as a foundational building block of the transition from the Industrial Age to the Network Age.[1]
