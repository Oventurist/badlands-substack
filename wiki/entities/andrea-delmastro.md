# Andrea Delmastro

Andrea Delmastro is an Italian lawmaker of the [[brothers-of-italy|Brothers of Italy]] party and one of the initiators of a 2025 legislative proposal to ban Islamic face coverings in public spaces across [[italy|Italy]].[1]

## Face covering bill

In a Facebook post on Wednesday, October 8, 2025, Delmastro said, "Religious freedom is sacred, but it must be exercised in the open, in full respect of our constitution and the principles of the Italian state."[1] The bill he helped initiate would prohibit garments covering the face — including the burqa and the niqab — in all public spaces such as shops, schools and offices, with violations punishable by fines of €300 to €3,000.[1]

The proposal was presented as a measure against "Islamic separatism" and forms part of a broader bill addressing what the right-wing party of Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] has described as "cultural separatism" associated with Islam.[1] The same package would also restrict mosque funding.[1]
