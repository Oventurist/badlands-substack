# World Liberty Financial

World Liberty Financial is a cryptocurrency venture co-founded by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his sons, and the issuer of the $WLFI token.

## Defamation suit against Justin Sun

As reported in the [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]], World Liberty Financial filed a defamation lawsuit on Monday in Miami-Dade County, Florida, against crypto billionaire [[justin-sun|Justin Sun]], accusing him of launching a coordinated effort to damage the company's reputation. The complaint seeks unspecified damages and a public retraction of statements Sun made on social media.

The company alleges that Sun bet against the $WLFI token and used third parties to conceal his purchases, and that World Liberty moved to freeze assets tied to one of his firms. According to the filing, Sun responded with a "scorched earth" campaign — including threats of litigation and public attacks — after the company refused his demands. The suit further accuses him of spreading false claims about the company's operations and amplifying them through influencers and bot accounts, generating millions of views and causing significant reputational harm. Sun, the founder of [[tron|Tron]], had not publicly responded to the allegations at the time of publication.
