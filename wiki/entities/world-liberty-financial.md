# World Liberty Financial

World Liberty Financial is a cryptocurrency venture co-founded by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his sons.[1]

## Lawsuit against Justin Sun

In May 2026 the company filed a defamation lawsuit against crypto billionaire [[justin-sun|Justin Sun]], accusing him of launching a coordinated effort to damage its reputation.[1] The complaint, filed in Miami-Dade County, Florida, seeks unspecified damages and a public retraction over statements Sun made on social media.[1]

World Liberty alleges Sun bet against its $WLFI token and used third parties to conceal purchases, while the company moved to freeze assets tied to one of his firms.[1] The filing claims Sun responded with a "scorched earth" campaign, including threats of litigation and public attacks after the company refused his demands.[1] The suit further accuses Sun of spreading false claims about the company's operations, amplifying them through influencers and bot accounts, and generating millions of views that caused significant reputational harm.[1]
