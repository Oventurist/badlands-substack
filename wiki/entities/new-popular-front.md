# New Popular Front

The New Popular Front is a left-wing French electoral coalition that emerged as the leading bloc in the second round of the 2024 French parliamentary elections.[1]

## 2024 parliamentary elections

The coalition was assembled hastily after President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] called a snap parliamentary election, a decision announced after his centrist Together alliance was heavily beaten by [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]]'s anti-immigration [[national-rally|National Rally]] in the European elections. The snap vote was widely read as a gamble intended to prevent the far right from taking power in France, with voters urged to back either the left-wing coalition or the centrist group.[1]

In the second round, held on a Sunday in early July 2024, the New Popular Front took a surprising first place with 182 seats — short of an outright majority. Macron's centrist Ensemble alliance finished second with 168 seats, and the National Rally, which had won the first round and was widely expected to win overall, finished third with 143 seats.[1]

## Reaction from American commentators

The outcome prompted a backlash among supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], numerous of whom asserted that the far right had been "cheated" out of victory and drew comparisons between the French result and the 2020 and forthcoming 2024 United States elections.[1]
