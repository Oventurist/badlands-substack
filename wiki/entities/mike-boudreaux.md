# Mike Boudreaux

Mike Boudreaux is the Sheriff of Tulare County, California, and was a Republican candidate in the May 2024 special election for California's 20th Congressional District.[1]

## 2024 special election

Boudreaux was defeated by fellow Republican [[vince-fong|Vince Fong]], a California State Assembly member backed by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and endorsed by deposed former House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], in the contest to fill the remainder of McCarthy's term through January.[1] The all-Republican runoff took place in the Central Valley farm belt district and was treated as a test of Trump's continuing influence over Republican primary electorates ahead of the November 2024 general election.[1]
