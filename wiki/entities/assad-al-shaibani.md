# Assad al-Shaibani

Assad al-Shaibani is the Foreign Minister of [[syria|Syria]] under the government of President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]].[1]

Al-Shaibani participated in senior-level talks in Paris hosted by the United States that produced an agreement between Israel and Syria to establish a joint "fusion mechanism" for intelligence sharing, military de-escalation, diplomatic engagement and exploration of commercial opportunities.[1] He attended alongside Syrian intelligence chief Hussein Salameh, Israeli officials including Ambassador [[yechiel-leiter|Yechiel Leiter]], and US envoys [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]].[1]
