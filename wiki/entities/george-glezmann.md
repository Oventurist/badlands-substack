# George Glezmann

George Glezmann is an American citizen and Delta Airlines mechanic who was detained in Afghanistan by the [[taliban|Taliban]] and released in March 2025 after more than two years in captivity.[1]

## Detention

Glezmann, 66, was on holiday in Afghanistan when he was seized by the Taliban's intelligence services in December 2022.[1] He was subsequently designated by the U.S. government as wrongfully detained.[1]

## Release

Glezmann was freed after weeks of negotiations led by Qatari and U.S. mediators.[1] Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] confirmed the release in a statement, saying that "after two and a half years of captivity in Afghanistan, Delta Airlines mechanic George Glezmann is on his way to be reunited with his wife, Aleksandra."[1] He arrived in [[qatar|Qatar]] on Thursday, March 20, 2025, after a deal was struck between the Taliban and the Trump administration that Qatari negotiators helped broker, according to the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]

Badlands commentary argued that mainstream coverage buried the more significant detail: photographs of the handover showed [[adam-boehler|Adam Boehler]], the Trump-appointed hostage envoy, present at the release.[1] Boehler had days earlier drawn attacks from [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] allies for meeting face-to-face with [[hamas|Hamas]], and Israeli journalist [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]] confirmed his involvement in the Glezmann negotiation.[1]
