# Hanno Pevkur

Hanno Pevkur is the Defense Minister of Estonia.[1]

## NATO Summit remarks, July 2024

On the opening day of the [[2024-nato-washington-summit|NATO Summit in Washington]] in July 2024, Pevkur appeared alongside the defense ministers of Latvia and Lithuania at a panel hosted by Politico and the German newspaper Welt, where the Baltic officials made a case for the [[nato|NATO]] alliance that was conspicuously tailored to former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The ministers laced their remarks with tough love for allies that were not spending heavily on defense while muting direct criticism of Trump's quasi-isolationist instincts.[1]

Pevkur employed vocabulary aimed at the golf-loving former president and real estate developer: "NATO is a club. When you have club rules, then you respect the rules and you expect that everyone will also respect the rules. When you pay your fee in the golf club, you can play. It doesn't matter how big is your wallet."[1] The episode was reported under the framing "Mar-a-NATO," describing how European leaders were tailoring their message in anticipation of a possible Trump return to the presidency.[1]
