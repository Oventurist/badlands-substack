# Ed Siskel

Ed Siskel is the [[white-house|White House]] counsel who authored the May 2024 letter formally asserting executive privilege over audio recordings of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s interviews with special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]].[1]

## Executive privilege letter

In a letter obtained by [[the-hill|The Hill]], Siskel wrote that "because of the President's longstanding commitment to protecting the integrity, effectiveness, and independence of the Department of Justice and its law enforcement investigations, he has decided to assert executive privilege over the recordings."[1] Siskel also called into question the motives of the Republicans seeking the recordings.[1]

The assertion came just hours before House Republicans were set to meet to consider resolutions holding Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] in contempt of Congress for failing to turn over files subpoenaed by the House Judiciary and Oversight committees.[1]
