# Michigan Republican Party

The **Michigan Republican Party** is the state affiliate of the national Republican Party in Michigan.[1]

## Absentee ballot litigation

In December 2025 the party was a plaintiff, alongside the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] and Chesterfield Township Clerk [[cindy-berry|Cindy Berry]], in a successful lawsuit over Michigan's treatment of absentee ballots with mismatched identification numbers.[1] The plaintiffs argued that ballots whose stub number did not match the return envelope number must be rejected, though voters should be given a chance to fix the problem, and the court agreed — holding that election inspectors may count absentee ballots only when the numbers match.[1] The decision rejected the position of Secretary of State [[jocelyn-benson|Jocelyn Benson]] and Director of Elections [[jonathan-brater|Jonathan Brater]] that such ballots could be tabulated as "challenged ballots" under decades-old guidance.[1] The case is documented at [[michigan-absentee-ballot-id-match-ruling|the Michigan absentee ballot ID match ruling]].[1]
