# Larry Fink

Larry Fink is the chairman and chief executive officer of [[blackrock|BlackRock]], the world's largest asset manager. He co-founded the firm in 1988 and built it into a dominant institutional investor whose holdings span the largest American corporations and media conglomerates.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Fink was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] to push for new economic agreements with Beijing. The delegation — which also included [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], [[tim-cook|Tim Cook]] of [[apple-inc|Apple]], and [[david-solomon|David Solomon]] of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] — was described as a mix of American industrial and financial leadership prepared to lock in investment flows ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
