# Scott Jennings

Scott Jennings is a Republican commentator and CNN contributor known for defending President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[maga-coalition|MAGA]] movement on panel programming dominated by critics.[1]

## Rebuttal of the "MAGA is dead" narrative

In late November 2025, amid a wave of mainstream commentary declaring the MAGA movement finished, Jennings pushed back on CNN's *NewsNight*, arguing from polling numbers and the fundamental dynamics on the ground that Trump's grip on the Republican Party remained strong.[1] Where critics and some insiders claimed cracks in that grip, Jennings maintained that the facts told a very different story — one of strength rather than decline.[1]

The exchange occurred on a program where host Abby Phillip had referenced a senior House Republican complaining that the White House team "has treated all members like garbage," and that "the arrogance of this White House team is off-putting to members who are run roughshod and threatened."[1] Syracuse University's Nayyera Haq, on the same panel, cast Representative [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]]'s resignation as a symbol of the movement's troubles.[1] Jennings's response was cited in coverage arguing that the media narrative leaned on anecdotes and disgruntled-insider quotes rather than measurable support.[1]

## Reception in independent media

Badlands commentary situated the episode within a broader information-warfare frame, arguing that one goal of irregular warfare is to attack the legitimacy of its target, and questioning why self-identified MAGA influencers were portraying Trump as incompetent — and why, if he were incompetent, he would remain such a threat to his opponents.[1]

## Criticism of Harris over Hurricane Milton (October 2024)

As CNN senior political commentator, Jennings rebuked Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] in October 2024 for attacking Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] over unreturned phone calls as [[hurricane-milton|Hurricane Milton]] approached the state.[2] "She's attacking the governor of a state that's about to get lashed with one of the worst storms," Jennings said, adding, "Is she crazy?"[2]
