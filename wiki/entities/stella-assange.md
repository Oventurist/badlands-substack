# Stella Assange

**Stella Assange** is a lawyer and the wife of imprisoned WikiLeaks publisher [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], and a leading public advocate against his extradition to the United States.[1]

In December 2023 she publicly confirmed that her husband's appeal hearing would take place on February 20–21, 2024, before two High Court judges at the Royal Courts of Justice in London — likely his final opportunity to resist extradition on [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] charges as part of the [[assange-extradition-case|Assange extradition case]].[1]
