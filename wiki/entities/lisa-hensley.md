# Lisa Hensley

Lisa Hensley is an American virologist and researcher at the [[national-bio-and-agro-defense-facility|National Bio and Agro-Defense Facility]] (NBAF) in Kansas.[1]

## Career

Hensley's career has taken her "from laboratory to laboratory and country to country" studying high-consequence pathogens including SARS, monkeypox, MERS and Marburg.[1] She has described the field's subject matter as an "alphabet soup" of ghastly diseases.[1]

## Liberia and the Ebola outbreak

Hensley has said that an Ebola outbreak in Liberia brought home what it meant to work in a country whose health-care system was overwhelmed by a high-mortality disease with no readily available treatment or vaccination options, and where public attitudes about the threat were distorted.[1] She recalled that reactions "were across the board," that many people did not believe Ebola existed and thought it was "something the government was doing," and that when she went to set up a laboratory people would not take anything from her hands out of fear.[1] The single treatment facility in Liberia's capital could not keep pace with the sick and dying; those transporting the ill or burying the dead themselves became infection vectors, and in other parts of the country treatment centers were attacked by skeptics who did not believe the virus was naturally occurring.[1]
