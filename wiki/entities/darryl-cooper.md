# Darryl Cooper

Darryl Cooper is a self-proclaimed historian who appeared on [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s podcast in early September 2024, generating a major media controversy.[1] Cooper had been accused of being a "Nazi apologist" over comments characterized as sympathetic toward Adolf Hitler.[1]

## The Carlson interview

The two-hour conversation was condemned by many on social media, including [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]], while [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] promoted the video to his 196.5 million followers on X, calling it "very interesting" and "worth watching" before apparently deleting the post.[1] Carlson, who hailed Cooper as "the most important historian in the United States," asked him how he would assess [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]].[1] Cooper, saying he was being "a little hyperbolic," replied: "I thought Churchill was the villain of the Second World War," adding, "He didn't kill the most people, he didn't commit the most atrocities… I think when you get into it and tell the story right, you see he was primarily responsible for that war becoming what it did."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that, having listened to the interview in its entirety, he considered the accusations against Cooper "complete and utter bullshit," noting that Cooper never sympathized with Hitler during the conversation and explicitly stated that viewing Churchill as the villain did not make Churchill's opponents the protagonists.[1] He and [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] devoted an entire episode of [[breaking-history|Breaking History]] to the interview, arguing that the convergence of figures such as [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]] and Babylon Bee owner Seth Dillon with corporate media showed that the Hitler/WW2/Holocaust subject remained a "third rail" that could not be discussed.[1]
