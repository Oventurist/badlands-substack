# Jane Addams

Jane Addams was the American social reformer who ran the temperance movement that produced [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1]

Addams studied the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]]'s London settlement house experiment at [[toynbee-hall|Toynbee Hall]], an attempt to replace private charity with government programs that served as a blueprint for the modern welfare system.[1] In the reading offered by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the Toynbee model failed in the same way the modern welfare system fails — it does not lift people out of poverty, though it succeeds if the goal is to keep the poor perpetually poor.[1] Her leadership of the temperance movement placed her at the head of an organization financed by the Astors, the Vanderbilts, the Warburgs, and the Rockefellers.[1]
