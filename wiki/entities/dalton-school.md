# Dalton School

The Dalton School is a prestigious independent, progressive K–12 private school in New York City.[1]

## Epstein's employment

[[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] taught at Dalton in the mid-1970s despite holding no degree or conventional credentials, and the connections he made there led to his 1976 hiring at [[bear-stearns|Bear Stearns]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that mainstream Epstein coverage routinely fails to ask how an applicant with no degree was hired at a school that typically requires a bachelor's degree in a relevant field plus three or more years of teaching experience.[1]

## Donald Barr

At the time of Epstein's employment, the school's headmaster was [[donald-barr|Donald Barr]], a former [[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services]] officer and the father of Attorney General [[william-barr|Bill Barr]].[1] DeLarme presented this without speculation as one of many instances of Epstein moving in the orbit of intelligence.[1]
