# Eric Swalwell

Eric Swalwell is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives, best known in Badlands commentary for his association with Christine Fang ("Fang Fang"), a suspected Chinese intelligence operative who cultivated relationships with California politicians and helped place an intern in his office.[1]

The Swalwell–Fang Fang case is invoked as the canonical example of why congressional staff vetting matters. In coverage of [[kat-cammack|Kat Cammack]]'s H.Res. 500, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] listed "Swalwell and Fang Fang," Dianne Feinstein's longtime driver, and other staffer scandals as proof that [[congress|Congress]] had left an obvious counterintelligence gap unaddressed for years.[1]

## FBI file release push (2026)

In 2026, FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] pressed to release a decade-old investigative file involving Swalwell, despite no public evidence of wrongdoing tied to the lawmaker.[2] Patel directed agents in the [[fbi|FBI]]'s San Francisco field office to accelerate redaction of documents in preparation for possible public disclosure; the file concerns Swalwell's past contacts with [[christine-fang|Christine Fang]], a woman the bureau investigated as a suspected Chinese intelligence operative.[2] Current and former officials said the effort raised internal concerns about departing from standard FBI practice and politicizing investigative records, noting the bureau had previously examined the matter and brought no charges.[2]

Swalwell responded that the country was "literally at war" and faced threats against the homeland, saying Patel "should be spending every moment trying to keep us safe, not scoring political points" and that while many had "bent the knee to this administration," he and the people of California would not.[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Swalwell was not removed from the House Intelligence Committee until the two-year investigation concluded, questioned the official finding of innocence as a product of the Biden/Garland DOJ, and framed the renewed push as part of a broader tying-up of shelved national-security loose ends.[2]

## 2026 California gubernatorial campaign and withdrawal

Swalwell suspended his campaign for governor of California on April 12, 2026, amid multiple allegations of sexual misconduct, saying he would fight the claims while acknowledging past "mistakes in judgment."[3] The allegations included claims by several women, among them a former staffer who accused him of sexual assault, as reported by [[cnn|CNN]] and the San Francisco Chronicle; Swalwell denied the accusations and called them false.[3] The controversy prompted calls from some Democratic lawmakers and allies for him to withdraw.[3] Separately, the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] opened an investigation into Swalwell's employment of a Brazilian nanny.[3]

[[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] told NBC that the young woman making the allegations "must be respected and heard" and that the matter was best investigated "outside of a gubernatorial campaign"; Swalwell withdrew shortly afterward.[3] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Swalwell had faced no consequences over the earlier [[christine-fang|Christine Fang]] scandal and had still been able to mount a run for chief executive, compared his removal to Andrew Cuomo's ouster over staff conduct rather than nursing-home deaths, and credited the research group [[marco-polo|Marco Polo]] with identifying the woman in a viral video involving Swalwell.[3]

## Resignation from Congress and leak allegations (April 2026)

Swalwell resigned from [[congress|Congress]] alongside Representative [[tony-gonzales|Tony Gonzales]] in April 2026, with rumors that additional resignations were expected the same week.[4] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] and Adam Kinzinger were also rumored to be under investigation.[4] Journalist Catherine Herridge reported that [[tulsi-gabbard|DNI Gabbard]]'s document dump had uncovered allegations against Swalwell of leaking state secrets — a claim Ashe connected back to his relationship with the suspected Chinese intelligence operative.[4]

## 2026 sexual misconduct scandal and resignation

In the [[eric-swalwell-sexual-misconduct-scandal|April 2026 scandal]] Swalwell resigned from Congress effective immediately, one day after suspending his campaign for California governor, amid a surge of sexual misconduct allegations.[5] His office cited the mounting claims plus an ongoing House Ethics Committee investigation as the reason for stepping down.[5] Beginning April 10, the San Francisco Chronicle and CNN reported allegations from a former staffer and three additional women; the Manhattan District Attorney's office opened a criminal investigation into a 2024 New York assault allegation, and on April 14 former model Lonna Drewes alleged he had drugged, raped, and choked her unconscious in a 2018 hotel room.[5] Total public accusations reached at least five, with some reporting saying six.[5] Additional scrutiny involved a hotel-room video verified as authentic by forensic analysis and held by Representative Anna Paulina Luna, showing Swalwell kissing an alleged sex worker.[5] Swalwell denied all accusations, acknowledging only past "mistakes in judgment," and the allegations remain unproven in court.[5]

## Removal from the House Intelligence Committee (2023)

On January 24, 2023, House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] formally blocked Swalwell and fellow California Democrat [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] from serving on the [[house-intelligence-committee|House Intelligence Committee]], fulfilling a pledge he had made before taking the gavel.[6] Special rules for the panel allow the Speaker to halt the seating of lawmakers without a full floor vote, and McCarthy informed House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] by letter that he was rejecting the appointments "in order to maintain a standard worthy of this committee's responsibilities."[6] Jeffries had recommended Swalwell and Schiff anyway despite McCarthy's stated intention to block them along with [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]].[6] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted that McCarthy had so far kept his word, citing [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]]'s remark after the speakership vote that "trust is earned," while cautioning that the 118th Congress was only a month old.[6]

## Appearance alongside Hunter Biden, December 2023

Representative Eric Swalwell stood beside Hunter Biden at the Capitol press conference of December 13, 2023 at which Biden refused his subpoenaed closed-door deposition and instead demanded to testify publicly.[7] Badlands commentary mocked the pairing, quipping that if one intended to hold a press conference consisting of nothing but hot air, Swalwell was the man to have at one's side.[7]
