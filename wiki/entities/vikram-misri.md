# Vikram Misri

Vikram Misri is an Indian diplomat who served as Foreign Secretary of India.[1]

## 2024 border announcement

In October 2024 Misri announced from New Delhi that India and China had concluded an agreement on patrolling arrangements along the Line of Actual Control, the countries' contested frontier.[1] He said that "over the last several weeks, Indian and Chinese diplomatic and military negotiators have been in close contact with each other in a variety of forums," and that the resulting agreement had produced "disengagement and a resolution of the issues that had arisen in these areas in 2020."[1]

The announcement, part of the wider [[india-china-border-dispute|India-China border dispute]] settlement, came immediately before Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] travelled to Russia for the [[2024-brics-kazan-summit|BRICS summit in Kazan]], where he was expected to meet Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]
