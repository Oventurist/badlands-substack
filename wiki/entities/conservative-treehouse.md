# Conservative Treehouse

The Conservative Treehouse (theconservativetreehouse.com) is an American conservative blog whose analysis is frequently excerpted in the Badlands News Brief.[1]

## Coverage of the Biden withdrawal

In a July 28, 2024 post headlined "It All Tracks – Hersch Reports Barack Obama Threatened Biden with 25th Amendment Removal," the site amplified investigative journalist [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]]'s Substack reporting that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] had driven [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] out of the 2024 presidential race.[1] The post opened by glossing the youth idiom "that tracks" as a shorthand for new information making sense of what is already known, then argued that Hersh's account of Obama phoning Biden on July 20 to say "we have Kamala's approval to invoke the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]]" aligned with every existing datapoint about the period.[1]

The site framed the episode within its longstanding thesis that "Team Obama have always been in control" of the Biden administration, observing that the Obamas never left Washington, D.C. after leaving office.[1] It highlighted the quoted line that Obama "had an agenda and he wanted to seek it through to the end, and he wanted to have control over who would be elected," describing that as the point at which observers forget the enduring risk to Team Obama.[1]
