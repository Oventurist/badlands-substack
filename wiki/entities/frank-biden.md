# Frank Biden

Frank Biden is a brother of former U.S. President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] who drew attention in July 2024 for public remarks about the president's health following Biden's withdrawal from the 2024 presidential race.[1]

## July 2024 CBS remarks

While Joe Biden offered no reason beyond "the greater good" for ending his candidacy as the Democratic nominee, Frank Biden told CBS News that ailing health "absolutely" played a "considerable role" in the decision.[1] He added, "I'm incredibly proud of my brother. Selfishly I will have him back to enjoy whatever time we have left," phrasing that was widely read as suggesting the president did not have long left to live.[1]

The CBS anchor expressed surprise at the statement, since the White House was at the time bluntly denying that age or health had anything to do with the withdrawal.[1] The contradiction between the family's account and the official line fed speculation about the president's condition, with some commentators questioning whether Biden was even still alive given the absence of public appearances.[1] Fox News host Dana Perino noted that the White House "didn't even release a White House still photograph," and said observers could be forgiven for "wondering if we are really living Weekend at Bernie's right now," calling for proof of life.[1]

The episode was covered in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief as a bonus item illustrating the gap between the Biden family's candor and official White House messaging during the succession crisis that elevated [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]
