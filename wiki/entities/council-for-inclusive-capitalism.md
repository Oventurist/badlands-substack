# Council for Inclusive Capitalism

The Council for Inclusive Capitalism is a corporate and institutional partnership headed by [[lynn-forester-de-rothschild|Lynn Forester de Rothschild]] that promotes "inclusive capitalism," a stakeholder-oriented alternative to shareholder-primacy economics.[1] Alt-Market analyst [[brandon-smith|Brandon Smith]] first covered the group in July 2022 as a "barely publicized project," describing it as the culmination of decades of globalist agendas and as open, admitted evidence of a global-governance program that critics had long been told was a paranoid fantasy.[1]

## Role in the post-ESG pivot

Badlands coverage places the Council at the center of what it describes as a rebranding effort following the collapse of [[esg|ESG]] as a usable public label.[1] After Forester de Rothschild conceded ESG's defeat at the B20 Summit in India, alternative-media commentary framed "inclusive capitalism" as the successor vocabulary through which the same corporate-governance objectives would be pursued under terminology the public is less familiar with.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the need to rebrand at all constituted a defeat in what he calls the Mind War, and evidence that the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] was broader than commonly assumed.[1]
