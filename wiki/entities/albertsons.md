# Albertsons

Albertsons is an American grocery company operated in common with [[safeway|Safeway]]. In June 2025 the two chains faced coordinated labor action in Colorado over alleged unfair labor practices.[1]

## June 2025 Colorado strikes

Workers struck against Safeway and Albertsons beginning in mid-June 2025, with the walkout entering its third day on Tuesday, June 17, 2025, and picketing in Grand Junction prompting a temporary store closure.[1] Safeway and Albertsons workers in Fort Collins, Greeley, Longmont, Loveland and Colorado Springs voted by margins over 95% to authorize a strike on the night of Monday, June 16 and the morning of Tuesday, June 17, with negotiations set to resume.[1] Roughly 30 workers were seen picketing at one Grand Junction store in a video posted by [[united-food-and-commercial-workers|UFCW]] Local 7.[1]

## Merger fallout

Badlands commentary attributed much of the labor tension to the failed Safeway–Albertsons merger: preparations for the deal drove aggressive overhead reductions, especially cuts to hours and staffing in unionized meat departments, and conditions deteriorated further after the merger collapsed and the company struggled to define its future.[1]
