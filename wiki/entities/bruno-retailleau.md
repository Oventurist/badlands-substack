# Bruno Retailleau

Bruno Retailleau is a French politician serving as interior minister and a leading figure in the conservative party [[les-republicains|Les Républicains]].[1]

In May 2025 Retailleau and his party moved to end the decades-long monopoly held by left-leaning non-governmental organizations over the provision of legal advice inside France's migrant detention centres.[1] A Senate bill tabled by Les Républicains sought to strip organizations such as [[cimade|Cimade]] and [[france-terre-dasile|France terre d'asile]] of their exclusive role advising undocumented migrants awaiting expulsion, replacing them with what the government described as a more neutral and accountable system free of publicly funded activists obstructing deportation policy.[1]

Retailleau's proposal would transfer responsibility for legal assistance to the [[french-office-for-immigration-and-integration|French Office for Immigration and Integration]], a public agency under his own ministry, or alternatively to a neutral judicial figure such as a magistrate.[1] He argued that such a shift would ensure greater impartiality, criticizing the existing arrangement as lacking neutrality on the grounds that the NGOs act as "judge and party" in the process.[1]
