# Augusto Pinochet

Augusto Pinochet (1915–2006) was a Chilean military officer and general who came to power in the September 11, 1973 coup that overthrew President [[salvador-allende|Salvador Allende]], ruling Chile as a dictator for seventeen years until 1990. In the Badlands Media article A More Perfect (Labor) Union, Pinochet's crackdown is described as having been aided by AFL-CIO-funded labor operations.

## Role in the 1973 coup

The article quotes Tim Shorrock's *The Nation* piece, which states that U.S.-government-funded labor aid to right-wing unions "helped finance the revolt by Chile's professional class and fanned the flames of social unrest that provided the pretext for Gen. Augusto Pinochet's violent crackdown and the justification for his seventeen-year dictatorship." The article presents Pinochet's regime as the beneficiary of the covert labor operations conducted by the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]'s [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]].
