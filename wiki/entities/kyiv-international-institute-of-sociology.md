# Kyiv International Institute of Sociology

The Kyiv International Institute of Sociology (KIIS) is a Ukrainian polling organization whose surveys of public opinion became a point of contention in the February 2025 dispute between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]].[1]

## February 2025 polling

After Trump claimed on February 18, 2025 that Zelenskyy's approval rating had fallen to 4% and called for elections in Ukraine, KIIS reported the following day that trust in Zelenskyy stood at 57%.[1] Zelenskyy responded on February 19 by calling Trump's characterization disinformation.[1]

A separate survey conducted February 3–9, 2025 using face-to-face tablet interviews (CAPI) with 2,000 respondents over the age of 18 in government-controlled areas of Ukraine found that 41.3% believed Zelenskyy should decide for himself whether to stay in politics, 39.2% thought he should leave politics after the war, and 15.1% said he should remain.[1]

### February 2025 Zelensky trust poll
On February 19, 2025 the institute released a telephone poll of 1,000 respondents finding that 57 percent trusted Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, 37 percent did not, and the rest were undecided.[2] Described by wire coverage as a respected pollster, the institute found that trust in Zelensky had risen five percent since its previous survey in December, despite Ukraine suffering military setbacks.[2] The result was published hours after President Donald Trump claimed Zelensky's approval rating stood at just four percent.[2]
