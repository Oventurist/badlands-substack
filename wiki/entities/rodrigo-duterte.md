# Rodrigo Duterte

Rodrigo Duterte is a Filipino politician who served as President of the Philippines from 2016 to 2022. He is best known internationally for the "war on drugs" he launched during his presidency, an anti-narcotics crackdown in which thousands of purported drug dealers and users were killed.[1]

## ICC custody (March 2025)

On Wednesday, March 12, 2025, Duterte was taken into the custody of the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] following his arrest in Manila on murder charges linked to the drug war.[1] The ICC stated that Duterte had been "surrendered to the custody of the International Criminal Court," having been "arrested by the authorities of the Republic of the Philippines...for charges of murder as a crime against humanity."[1]

Then 79 years old, Duterte arrived at Rotterdam airport aboard a chartered plane earlier the same day and was transferred to a detention unit on the Dutch coast, with an initial appearance before an ICC judge in The Hague scheduled for the following days.[1] Reuters reported that he could become the first Asian former head of state to stand trial at the court.[1] The ICC arrest warrant alleges that, as president, Duterte created, funded and armed the death squads that carried out murders of purported drug users and dealers.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry framed the arrest sardonically, suggesting that intelligence-community interests were the aggrieved party in the drug-war killings, and questioned the legitimacy of an institution that also pursues Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu yet cannot take him into custody.[1] The commentary also gestured at what Donald Trump might think of Duterte, whose populist style and hardline drug policy drew comparisons to Trump's own.[1]
