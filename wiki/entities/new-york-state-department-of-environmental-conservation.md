# New York State Department of Environmental Conservation

The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) is the state agency responsible for wildlife regulation and environmental enforcement in New York. In late October 2024 the agency became the focus of national controversy over its handling of a rescued squirrel kept as a pet.[1]

## The P'nuts Freedom Farm raid

Following an anonymous complaint against P'nuts Freedom Farm, DEC personnel conducted a five-hour raid on the home of Mark Luongo, seizing [[peanut-the-squirrel|Peanut the squirrel]] and a raccoon named Fred.[1] Both animals were euthanized, with authorities stating the purpose was to test them for rabies; no rabies was found.[1] Reporting indicated the underlying complaint came from a woman reportedly located in Texas, far from the New York property.[1]

## Reaction

The raid drew widespread public anger in the days before the 2024 election, and was cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators as an illustration of disproportionate state power against ordinary citizens — the argument that a government capable of granting every wish is equally capable of raiding a home and killing its pets.[1] The incident also became one of the most virally circulated news items of the pre-election weekend, breaking through to audiences that ordinarily avoid political news.[1]
