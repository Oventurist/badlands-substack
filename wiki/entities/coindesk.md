# Coindesk

Coindesk is a digital-assets news outlet whose reporting on United States crypto policy is cited in Badlands Media coverage.[1]

## Coverage of the March 2025 stablecoin hearing

Coindesk reported on the [[house-financial-services-committee|House Financial Services Committee]] hearing of March 11, 2025, noting that the pro-digital-assets witnesses included a senior executive from BNY and Davis Polk & Wardwell lawyer [[randy-guynn|Randy Guynn]].[1] The outlet framed the panel's composition as a marker of shifting alignments in Washington: after years in which crypto insiders desperately sought the attention of [[us-congress|Congress]], representatives of the traditional financial system were putting a foot on the scales in favor of [[stablecoin|stablecoin]] regulation as congressional momentum toward crypto legislation rose.[1] Coindesk reported Guynn's argument that the [[stable-act|STABLE Act]]'s safeguards should place issuers under banking-like protections.[1]
