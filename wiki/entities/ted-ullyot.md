# Ted Ullyot

Ted Ullyot is an American attorney and former general counsel of Facebook who co-founded the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]] with former Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]].[1]

The Virginia-based law and consulting firm expanded in January 2024 with the addition of several [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration lawyers and the opening of a Washington office.[1]
