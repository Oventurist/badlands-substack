# Don Beck

**Don Beck** is a co-author, with [[chris-cowan|Chris Cowan]], of *[[spiral-dynamics|Spiral Dynamics]]*, cited in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

The essay credits Beck and Cowan with the argument that ascending to higher levels of consciousness transforms societal structures by fostering collaboration, inclusivity, and innovation, and uses their theory to support its account of how [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] dismantles survival-based conditioning.[1] Their framework is also invoked in the essay's discussion of reprogramming collective narratives toward more resilient systems of thought and governance.[1]
