# Steven Mnuchin

**Steven Mnuchin** served as United States Secretary of the Treasury throughout the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration. In the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", Mnuchin functions as the documentary hinge of the argument: his correspondence is the evidence that senior Trump figures were privately advocating a [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## The Mnuchin files

CoinDesk obtained roughly 250 pages of Mnuchin's crypto-related email correspondence from his four years at Treasury through a Freedom of Information Act request, and CNBC reported on the contents.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The release showed that in 2019 [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] was quietly pressing for a U.S. digital dollar issued by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], writing to ask whether Mnuchin would be open to a brainstorming session and suggesting a digital dollar could change how entitlements are paid out while saving on waste, fraud, and transaction costs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

The same trove showed that [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]], then chairman of the [[commodity-futures-trading-commission|CFTC]], was among the crypto-friendly voices lobbying the Treasury Secretary, with a July 2018 exchange documenting his insistence on an in-person meeting.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Giancarlo said he routinely briefed Mnuchin during 2018 on the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] futures market under CFTC oversight.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## TikTok acquisition bid (2024)

In March 2024, Mnuchin — by then head of the private investment firm [[liberty-strategic-capital|Liberty Strategic Capital]] — told CNBC's "Squawk Box" that he was assembling an investor group to buy [[tiktok]] from Chinese parent [[bytedance|ByteDance]].[2] "I think the legislation should pass and I think it should be sold," he said. "It's a great business and I'm going to put together a group to buy TikTok."[2] He added: "This should be owned by U.S. businesses. There's no way that the Chinese would ever let a U.S. company own something like this in China."[2]

The announcement came a day after the House of Representatives passed a bipartisan divest-or-ban bill that, if enacted, would force ByteDance to sell the app or face an effective U.S. ban; the measure moved to an uncertain future in the Senate, though President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said he would sign it if it reached his desk.[2] Reporting noted a pre-existing link between the parties: Masayoshi Son's [[softbank|SoftBank]] Vision Fund invested in ByteDance in 2018 and is also a limited partner in Liberty Strategic.[2]

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], citing research by independent journalist [[ian-carroll|Ian Carroll]], argued that Mnuchin's bid represented Israeli rather than purely American interests and questioned how much of the "TikTok is a CCP weapon" narrative served as cover for other influence operations on the platform.[2]
