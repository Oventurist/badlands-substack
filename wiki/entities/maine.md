# Maine

Maine is a state in the northeastern United States (New England). In the Badlands Media corpus it enters as the venue of the 2026 U.S. Senate race between Republican incumbent [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]] and Democratic challenger [[graham-platner|Graham Platner]].

## The 2026 Senate race

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Platner's Democratic campaign for the Senate seat was thrown into turmoil by public sexual assault allegations from former romantic partner [[jenny-racicot|Jenny Racicot]]. Platner denied the allegations as "categorically false," framing them as a coordinated political hit "timed just before Maine's candidate filing deadline," and announced he was stepping back temporarily while weighing the best path forward for the goal of defeating Collins. The Brief also notes that Badlands commentary treats the episode in the context of its wider claim that American elections are "fake," with the author (Ashe in America) quipping that "Platner's pullouts" would otherwise be concerning.
