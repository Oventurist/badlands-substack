# Maine

Maine is a U.S. state in New England that became a focal point of the 2024 election-law fight when its Secretary of State, [[shenna-bellows|Shenna Bellows]], removed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's 2024 presidential primary ballot at the end of 2023 under the disqualification clause of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

## Ballot disqualification and appeal

Unlike [[colorado|Colorado]], where disqualification arose from litigation and a state supreme court ruling, Maine's removal came from a unilateral administrative determination by the Secretary of State.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] described the situation in Maine as "despotism," emphasizing that a single official had decided which candidates the state's voters would be allowed to consider.[1]

Trump's campaign said it would immediately appeal to the Maine Superior Court, the state's top trial court. Maine statute requires the court to move quickly, with a ruling expected by January 17, 2024 either affirming or overturning Bellows' decision.[1]
