# John Cena

**John Cena** is an American actor and former professional wrestler cited by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as a recent example of what he describes as ritual public shaming in [[hollywood|Hollywood]].[1]

## Alleged symbolism

Carlson pointed to Cena's naked appearance at the Oscars, calling it "the epitome of public shaming — a ritual necessary to be in the club."[1] He also noted that on the poster for Cena's [[amazon|Amazon]] Prime comedy *Ricky Stanicky*, the actor poses in a Catholic schoolgirl's uniform with a blonde pigtailed wig, an outfit Carlson read as a direct imitation of [[britney-spears|Britney Spears]] in the video for "Baby One More Time."[1]

Carlson placed Cena in a longer line of leading men — including Brad Pitt, Tom Hanks, Robin Williams, Dustin Hoffman and [[tyler-perry|Tyler Perry]] — who he said were required to shame themselves publicly by dressing as women before their careers advanced.[1]
