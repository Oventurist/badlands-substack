# Andriy Biletsky

Andriy Biletsky is a Ukrainian politician described in Bloomberg's reporting as the right-wing figure who founded the [[azov-battalion|Azov Brigade]] in 2014.[1] The regiment he created played what that coverage called a crucial role during the Russian siege of Mariupol in 2022, and later gave rise to the 3rd Separate Assault Brigade.[1]

Badlands Media disputed the completeness of the standard account of Azov's origins, arguing that coverage crediting Biletsky alone obscures the financing role of Israeli-Ukrainian billionaire Igor Kolomoisky — a point the commentary raised while attacking Bloomberg correspondent Cameron Manley's recruitment-friendly framing of the unit.[1]

## Ideological profile and the 2023 Zelensky meeting

Reporting by [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]] in August 2023 characterised Biletsky as one of the most notorious neo-Nazis in modern Ukrainian history, and quoted a pledge attributed to him to "lead the white races of the world in a final crusade…against Semite-led Untermenschen."[2] Beyond his military role, Biletsky has long served as a figurehead for the closely aligned National Corps political movement.[2]

On August 14, 2023 — just over an hour after Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] announced a further $200 million in military aid to Kyiv — Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] published a video to his Telegram channel showing him holding court with Biletsky, in what Zelensky called an "open conversation" with the 3rd Separate Assault Brigade on the outskirts of Bakhmut.[2] The brigade, which announced in a January YouTube video that "the SSO AZOV is expanding to a brigade" and was now "the 3rd separate assault brigade of the Ground Forces of the Armed Forces of Ukraine," remains under Biletsky's leadership.[2] Days later Zelensky praised the unit on Twitter and alluded to a separate meeting with the [[aidar-battalion|Aidar Battalion]], another formation accused of war crimes by Amnesty International.[2]

Western media had frequently dismissed evidence of neo-Nazi influence in Ukraine by citing Zelensky's Jewish heritage; the footage of the meeting with Biletsky was presented by The Grayzone as a direct rebuttal to that framing.[2] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the emergence of such figures among forces backed by the establishment and [[nato|NATO]] represented one of the most significant revelations of the ongoing [[information-war|information war]].[2]
