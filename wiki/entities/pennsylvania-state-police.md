# Pennsylvania State Police

The Pennsylvania State Police is the statewide law enforcement agency of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, led during 2024 by Commissioner Colonel [[christopher-l-paris|Christopher L. Paris]].[1]

## Role in the July 13, 2024 rally security failure

The agency was among the law enforcement bodies involved in security at the July 13, 2024 rally in Pennsylvania at which former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was shot at, one attendee was killed and two others were critically injured.[1] With the FBI and the Department of Homeland Security declining to appear before the [[house-homeland-security-committee|House Homeland Security Committee]] on July 23, 2024, Paris became one of the two principal witnesses, alongside Patrick Yoes of the [[fraternal-order-of-police|Fraternal Order of Police]].[1]

Badlands coverage described the resulting testimony as obstructive, with witnesses repeatedly declining to answer on the grounds of the ongoing investigation and the unfinished after-action review, leaving questions about timelines and resource assignments unresolved.[1] Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] grouped this with [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]]'s resignation and [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]]'s deflection as evidence of a broader accountability failure.[1]
