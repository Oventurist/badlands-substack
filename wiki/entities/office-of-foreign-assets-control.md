# Office of Foreign Assets Control

The **Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC)** is the arm of the [[department-of-the-treasury|United States Department of the Treasury]] that administers and enforces economic and trade sanctions programs.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 OFAC is identified as the office conducting scrutiny of more than 40 Americans over potential violations of U.S. sanctions law arising from the March 2026 [[nuestra-america-convoy|Nuestra America Convoy]] to [[cuba|Cuba]]. Subpoenas issued in the probe — including to streamer [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and activist [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]] — sought financial, logistical and communications records related to the trip. The investigation is described as unfolding amid a tightened Cuba blockade, Cuba's designation as a state sponsor of terrorism, and a Justice Department indictment of [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]].
