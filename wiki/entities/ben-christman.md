# Ben Christman

Ben Christman was an American college football offensive lineman who died on February 11, 2025 at the age of 21.[1]

## Death

Christman, a senior offensive lineman at the [[university-of-nevada-las-vegas|University of Nevada, Las Vegas]], was found dead in an off-campus apartment on Tuesday morning, February 11, 2025; the university announced the death the following day.[1] UNLV said it had no other details at the time and that the cause of death would be released by the Clark County Coroner's Office in Nevada.[1] The university informed Christman's family and the football team and made counseling available to those in mourning.[1]

Christman had transferred to UNLV that offseason after spending the previous two years at Kentucky, where he appeared in 12 games on special teams in his final season there.[1]

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] carried the New York Times Athletic report among its bonus items, in keeping with the outlet's ongoing tracking of sudden deaths among young athletes.[1]
