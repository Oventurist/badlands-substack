# Raz Zimmt

Raz Zimmt is an Israeli analyst and director of the Iran and Shiite Axis program at Tel Aviv University's [[institute-for-national-security-studies|Institute for National Security Studies]] (INSS).[1]

## Assessment of Trump's Iran pause

In May 2026, Zimmt argued that [[iran|Iran]] had gained greater incentive to resist U.S. demands and avoid major concessions — even if hostilities resumed — following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s decision to pause planned renewed airstrikes at the request of Gulf allies hoping to revive talks.[1]

Zimmt said Tehran drew two conclusions. First, the pause demonstrated that the United States and its partners fear the economic and political fallout of renewed war more than Iran does.[1] Second, Trump's statements are unreliable, so Iran should not capitulate on core issues such as its nuclear enrichment infrastructure, and should instead demand upfront guarantees: a full end to the conflict, sanctions relief or unfrozen assets, and arrangements recognizing Iranian control of the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1] The analysis came amid stalled U.S.–Iran negotiations over Iran's nuclear program, with both sides holding to initial positions.[1]
