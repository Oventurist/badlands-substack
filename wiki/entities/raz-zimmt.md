# Raz Zimmt

Raz Zimmt is an Israeli analyst who directs the Iran and Shiite Axis program at Tel Aviv University's [[institute-for-national-security-studies|Institute for National Security Studies]] (INSS).

## Assessment of Iranian strategy in 2026

In analysis summarized by the May 20, 2026 [[badlands-brief-311|Badlands Brief]], Zimmt argued that [[iran|Iran]] now has greater incentive to resist U.S. demands and avoid major concessions, even if hostilities resume, following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s decision to pause planned renewed airstrikes at the request of Gulf allies hoping to revive negotiations.

Zimmt said Tehran drew two conclusions. First, the pause proves that the United States and its partners fear the economic and political fallout of renewed war more than Iran does. Second, Trump's statements are unreliable, so Iran should not capitulate on core issues such as its nuclear enrichment infrastructure, and should instead demand upfront guarantees: a full end to the conflict, sanctions relief or unfrozen assets, and arrangements recognizing Iranian control of the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]]. His analysis appeared amid stalled U.S.–Iran negotiations in which both sides held to their initial positions.
