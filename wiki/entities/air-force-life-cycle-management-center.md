# Air Force Life Cycle Management Center

The **Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)** is the awarding office of the [[united-states-air-force|United States Air Force]] identified as the contracting authority for the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order awarded to [[icr-inc|ICR, INC.]] in July 2021.[1]

In the award record examined by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the awarding agency is the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]], the sub-agency is the Department of the Air Force, and AFLCMC appears as the awarding office — described in the article as functioning as the Air Force's vendor management office.[1] Both the Red Dawn III project and its parent program, [[cognition-cognos-support|Cognition Cognos Support]], are delivered to the Air Force through this channel.[1]

The Red Dawn III award was made [[sole-source-contracting|sole source]], with the justification asserting that no other vendor could perform the work, and its period of performance was later extended from October 2023 to April 2024.[1]
