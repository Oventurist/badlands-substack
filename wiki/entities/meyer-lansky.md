# Meyer Lansky

Meyer Lansky is described in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as the figure sitting at the top of the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]].[1]

## Organization

Under Lansky, according to Carlson, [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] ran Hollywood and [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]] ran Las Vegas.[1] That arrangement ended when Lansky, Siegel's good friend, learned that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money and had Siegel killed.[1]

## Alleged CIA relationship

Carlson writes that the [[cia|CIA]] could not allow the American public to know that "their guy," Lansky, was in charge of running the agency's drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War.[1] He connects this to the timing of [[the-godfather|The Godfather]], published in 1969 and released as a film in 1972 in the heat of that war, arguing the franchise redirected public attention onto the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] and allowed the Jewish Mafia to disappear into the shadows.[1]
