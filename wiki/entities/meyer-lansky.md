# Meyer Lansky

Meyer Lansky is identified by Erik Carlson as the figure sitting at the top of the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Role in the article

Under Lansky, [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] ran Hollywood and [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]] ran Las Vegas — an arrangement that ended when Lansky, Siegel's good friend, discovered that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money and had him killed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

Carlson further asserts that Lansky was the [[cia|CIA]]'s man, running the agency's drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War, and that the CIA could not allow the American public to know this.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He connects that motive to the timing of *[[the-godfather|The Godfather]]*, published in 1969 and released as a film in 1972 in the heat of the Vietnam War, which redirected public attention onto the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Entry into the international narcotics trade

Agents of Lansky and fellow crime boss [[arnold-rothstein|Arnold Rothstein]] made their first trips to the Far East during the 1920s to buy heroin from the British, the arrangement described in [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]] under which British opium refineries in Shanghai and [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]] supplied the American market created by [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[2] Lansky ran organized crime worldwide until his death in 1983, including the [[cia|CIA]]'s drug running out of the [[golden-triangle|Golden Triangle]] during the Vietnam War.[2] His best friend [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]], whom he later had killed, opened the first casino in Las Vegas.[2]

When liquor was legalized again in the early 1930s, the crime syndicates built during Prohibition turned to casino gambling, and Lansky moved to the Caribbean to prepare the way for British offshore unregulated banking.[2]

## Role in Operation Gladio financing

Meyer Lansky (1902–1983) is listed among the Jewish mobsters of the early twentieth century who, alongside Italian mafia families, ran liquor, gambling and prostitution rackets and were also used by large business enterprises needing dockworkers, newspapers and bankers handled.[3] He formed the National Crime Syndicate focused on Cuba and Las Vegas.[3] With [[lucky-luciano]] he set up [[the-commission-mafia|the Commission]] in 1931, the twelve-boss committee that managed all mafia dealings and which, in the Badlands Media account, became the apparatus used to build the American leg of the [[operation-gladio]] drug network.[3] Lansky attended the Cuba meeting after Luciano's deportation, alongside [[frank-costello]], [[vito-genovese]], [[albert-anastasia]] and [[santo-trafficante]], that framed the entire Gladio drug-funding structure.[3]
