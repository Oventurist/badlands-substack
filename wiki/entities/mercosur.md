# Mercosur

Mercosur is the South American trade bloc whose member economies have pursued a long-negotiated free trade agreement with the [[european-union|European Union]]. It appears in [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] as one example of the alternative trade arrangements that U.S. partners are accelerating in response to tariffs and threats from the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.

The Brief describes an EU–Mercosur bloc covering more than 700 million people, alongside an [[india|India]]–EU free trade deal and other diversification efforts. It links these developments to reported declines in the dollar's value and shifts toward gold holdings by central banks, and to additional U.S. actions including threatened 100% tariffs on [[canada|Canada]] over electric vehicle policy alignment with [[china|China]] and increased tariffs on South Korea. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] interprets such pacts as instances of "shielding" within his [[tripolar-alliance|Tripolar Alliance]] framework.
