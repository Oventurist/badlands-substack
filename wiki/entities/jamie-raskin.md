# Jamie Raskin

Jamie Raskin is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Maryland. He appears in [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] as one of the congressional critics of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s release of more than three million pages of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigative files.

The release was framed by Deputy Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] as the final phase of compliance with the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]], which had mandated full disclosure by December 19, 2025. Raskin and other Democrats described the process as a potential cover-up, pointing to more than 10,000 redactions and to the fact that the released material amounted to roughly half of the more than six million pages initially identified, with the remainder deemed duplicative, non-responsive or withheld.
