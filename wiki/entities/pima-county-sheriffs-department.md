# Pima County Sheriff's Department

The Pima County Sheriff's Department is the principal county law enforcement agency in Pima County, Arizona, a jurisdiction that includes a long stretch of the US–Mexico border and the community of Arivaca.[1]

## Role in the 2026 Arivaca use-of-force investigation

Following the January 27, 2026 shooting of [[patrick-gary-schlegel|Patrick Gary Schlegel]] by a [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] agent near Arivaca, the Pima County Sheriff's Department took the lead on the use-of-force investigation at the request of the [[fbi|FBI]].[1] The arrangement follows the common practice of assigning an outside agency to review shootings involving federal officers so that the reviewing body is independent of the agency whose personnel discharged their weapons.[1]

Authorities said Schlegel had fired at agents and at a [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] helicopter before agents returned fire; he survived and was hospitalized in serious but stable condition.[1] US Attorney [[timothy-courchaine|Timothy Courchaine]] praised the coordination among "all levels of Arizona law enforcement" in responding to the incident.[1]
