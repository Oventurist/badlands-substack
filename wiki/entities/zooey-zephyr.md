# Zooey Zephyr

Zooey Zephyr is a Democratic member of the Montana House of Representatives who, in April 2023, was voted off the floor of the chamber for the remainder of the year's legislative session following a series of confrontations over legislation restricting medical gender transition for minors.[1] Zephyr, described in coverage aggregated by Badlands Media as a biological male identifying as a trans bisexual woman, represents District 100, centered on Missoula.[1]

## Election and background

District 100 is one of the few districts in Montana with a dominant college culture and a majority-Democrat electorate, a profile that reporting cited by the brief credited with making Zephyr's 2022 victory comparatively easy.[1] Zephyr publicly identified as transgender only a couple of years before the election and had little identifiable public background prior to entering politics, aside from some notoriety as a competitive video game player in the Super Smash Bros. competitive scene.[1]

## Censure and removal from the House floor

After what ZeroHedge characterized as a series of activist disruptions and rants in the chamber, Zephyr was first censured and then banned from the floor of the Montana State Legislature for the rest of 2023.[1] The confrontation centered on Montana's consideration of a slate of bills aimed at restricting drag performances, curbing what supporters called trans indoctrination in schools, and banning sex-change surgeries and hormone therapies for minors — among them SB 99.[1] Zephyr told colleagues that if such measures became law, "I hope the next time there is an invocation when you bow your heads in prayer, you see the blood on your hands," remarks that critics read as insinuating that blood might spill.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for Badlands Media, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] endorsed the expulsion as the correct action, framing transgender ideology as an "anti-truth" phenomenon that he argued degrades both individuals and the societies that adopt it, and arguing that public silence about it had cost children dearly.[1] The item ran in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief alongside coverage of the [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] departure from [[fox-news|Fox News]] and congressional testimony on migrant child trafficking.[1]
