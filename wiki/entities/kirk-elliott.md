# Kirk Elliott

Dr. Kirk Elliott is a financial analyst and a sponsor of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], where he contributes commentary on monetary policy and precious metals.[1]

Commenting on the April 2023 introduction of the [[gold-standard-restoration-act|Gold Standard Restoration Act]], Elliott noted that the federal proposal followed several state-level efforts to restore gold and silver "to their rightful place as official government money," pointing to a Texas proposal for a state-issued gold-backed digital currency.[1] He described a renewed interest in constitutional money and argued that although such proposals might not pass in the near term, they provide ready-made frameworks for a future currency crisis: "when a currency crisis occurs, it's good to have a plan ready."[1]

## Commentary on central bank gold buying (April 2023)

In April 2023, responding to data showing eleven consecutive months of [[central-bank-gold-buying|central bank gold buying]], Elliott argued that although "disingenuous Fed Chairs" claim central banks buy gold because of "tradition," the "Wizards of Oz" know better than anyone that gold represents the true value on their balance sheets.[2] He noted that China is notorious for withholding key data about its gold holdings, so if the [[peoples-bank-of-china|People's Bank of China]] is publicly buying record amounts, its real holdings can be assumed to be even higher.[2] Elliott presented the episode as a classic example of how retail investors benefit from following the actions of global players rather than their rhetoric.[2]

## Commentary on Indonesia's exit from the dollar system (2023)

As a Badlands sponsor and financial analyst, Elliott contributed commentary to the March 2023 news brief covering [[indonesia|Indonesia's]] decision to move away from [[visa|Visa]] and [[mastercard|Mastercard]].[3] He argued that the American financial system had spent generations consolidating its network effect, and that the "brazen move by the US to push [[russia|Russia]] out of the system has set a series of dominos to fall."[3] The domino that fell that day, he said, was that a country of 278 million people would no longer use the American system.[3]

Elliott concluded that savings held in US dollar–denominated assets, as most Americans hold them, were "clearly becoming riskier," and directed readers toward the asset class central banks around the world were buying: precious metals.[3]

## Commentary on the 2023 real estate loan firesale

In the April 11, 2023 Badlands News Brief, Elliott responded as a Badlands Media sponsor and financial analyst to reporting on a sudden firesale of real estate loans at small banks and [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]]'s projection of 40% downside in commercial property.[4] He wrote that "we've seen structural problems in our global financial system before, and now those same symptoms are beginning to present in our current crisis."[4] While mainstream economists and talking heads argued over whether the United States was in a recession, he said, "the wise are preparing for a cascade of economic disasters from banking failures to world wars."[4]
