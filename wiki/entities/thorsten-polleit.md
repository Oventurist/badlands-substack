# Thorsten Polleit

Thorsten Polleit is a German economist who serves as chief economist at the precious-metals dealer Degussa.[1]

## Commentary on a gold-backed BRICS currency

In July 2023, as reports circulated that the [[brics|BRICS]] bloc would introduce a gold-backed reserve currency, Polleit told Kitco that "at first glance, a new transaction unit, backed by gold, sounds like good money – and it could be, first and foremost, a major challenge to the US dollar's hegemony."[1]

He qualified that assessment sharply, arguing that "for making the new currency as good as gold, a truly sound currency, it must be convertible into gold on demand," and said he was unsure whether Brazil, [[russia|Russia]], India, [[china|China]] and [[south-africa|South Africa]] had that in mind.[1] Using gold as money so that the unit of account was itself gold would, in his words, be "a true game changer," but could also "lead to a sharp devaluation of many fiat currencies vis-à-vis the yellow metal" — including the BRICS members' own fiat currencies — and could "catapult up goods prices in terms of fiat currencies."[1] He concluded that such a move "could be a shock to the global fiat money system," and doubted that this was what the BRICS states wished to achieve.[1]
