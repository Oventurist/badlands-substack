# Hells Angels

The Hells Angels are an outlaw motorcycle club that appears in Badlands Media commentary as a recurring symbolic thread linking professional wrestling theatrics to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s political persona.[1]

## Wrestling and political appearances

Badlands notes that when [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]] turned "heel" at the 1996 summer pay-per-view and formed the "nWo" stable, the group staged antics including entering the arena with the Hells Angels.[1] The same biker club volunteered to work security for Trump rallies in 2016.[1] On May 20, 2024, Trump entered a New York courtroom with an entourage so large it exhausted the available seating; among those present was Chuck Zito, former president of the New York chapter of the Hells Angels.[1] The Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the parallel between Hogan's nWo entrances and Trump's courtroom entourage as part of the [[trumpamania|TrumpaMania]] narrative frame.[1]
