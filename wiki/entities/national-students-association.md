# National Students Association

The National Students Association was an American student organisation launched in 1951 and founded and presided over by [[allard-lowenstein|Allard Lowenstein]].[1] According to the research of [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]] and [[elizabeth-gould|Elizabeth Gould]] in [[valediction-three-nights-of-desmond|Valediction: Three Nights of Desmond]], the association operated as a [[cia|CIA]] front group designed to recruit both talented young Americans and foreign students alike, who would later be propped up in various foreign governments during the Cold War.[1]

The discovery of the association's intelligence function was a turning point in the authors' investigation, since Lowenstein — by then a Kennedy political operative who had turned against the machine he helped build — was murdered shortly after telling Fitzgerald of plans to expose CIA involvement in the deaths of the Kennedy brothers.[1]
