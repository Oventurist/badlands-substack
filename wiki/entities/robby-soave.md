# Robby Soave

Robby Soave is an American journalist and commentator who co-hosted [[the-hill|The Hill]]'s web series [[rising|Rising]] alongside [[briahna-joy-gray|Briahna Joy Gray]].[1]

## Israel–Palestine exchange

In October 2023 Soave clashed on air with Gray over her criticism of the Israeli government during the Israel–Palestine conflict.[1] Badlands Media described Soave as "taking a page from [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]]," delivering a dramatic performance that it judged "every bit as impassioned as it is off-putting."[1] The publication argued that neither Soave nor Shapiro, whatever their reputations for intelligence, were in those moments displaying the type of mindset one should have when making choices with extreme consequences.[1]

The exchange was cited as part of a broader pattern of alternative-media fracturing over the war, in which emotionally charged responses — of the kind Badlands Media argues proved disastrous after 9/11 — dominated the debate.[1]
