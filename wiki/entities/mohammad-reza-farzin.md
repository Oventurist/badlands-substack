# Mohammad-Reza Farzin

Mohammad-Reza Farzin is the Governor of the [[central-bank-of-iran|Central Bank of Iran]] (CBI).[1]

## Announcement of Russia–Iran de-dollarization

Speaking at the 11th Conference on Modern Banking and Payment Systems in Tehran in November 2024, Farzin stated that [[russia|Russia]] and [[iran|Iran]] had abandoned the US dollar in bilateral trade and shifted entirely to their national currencies, describing the move as part of a plan to counter "unjust sanctions."[1]

"We have entered into a currency agreement with Russia and abandoned the dollar. Now we only trade in rubles and rials," Farzin was quoted as saying by Fars News.[1] He also disclosed that the two countries' financial authorities had agreed on the exchange rate to be used for foreign trade transactions.[1] Iran and Russia, both under US sanctions, had first announced plans to substitute their national currencies for the dollar in mutual trade in July 2022.[1]

The announcement is treated in the Badlands corpus as a data point in the broader [[de-dollarization]] trend, discussed alongside [[nato|NATO]] Admiral [[rob-bauer|Rob Bauer]]'s warning that businesses prepare for a "wartime scenario" and the abrupt exit of Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]].[1]
