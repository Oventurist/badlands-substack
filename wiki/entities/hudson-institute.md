# Hudson Institute

The Hudson Institute is a Washington-based foreign policy think tank. Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme described it as "notorious" and characterized it as one of "a college of think tanks comprising the international deep political milieu" — a network of nominally independent policy institutes that, in his reading, shape state behavior on behalf of entrenched transatlantic interests.[1]

## Platform for European hawks

The Institute served as the venue for remarks by [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]], the former Estonian prime minister and the European Union's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, in which she argued that Europe must continue to confront Russia through Ukraine even if the United States chose to withdraw its support.[1] Kallas rejected American commentary suggesting Russia could not be beaten, and tied credibility on Russia to credibility on China, asking how Washington could claim it would defend Asian partners against a larger Chinese military if it conceded that the West collectively could not pressure Russia effectively.[1]

DeLarme presented the appearance as evidence that the transatlantic think-tank circuit functions as a conveyor belt for war advocacy, arguing that "the international elite thrive on endless war" and that the [[european-union|EU]], [[nato|NATO]], and the American CIA–Pentagon–State Department nexus had long been paving the way toward conflict with both Russia and China.[1]
