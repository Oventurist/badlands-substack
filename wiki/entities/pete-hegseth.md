# Pete Hegseth

Pete Hegseth is the United States Secretary of Defense in the second Trump administration. In the Badlands corpus he appears in connection with efforts to reorient the American defense industrial base toward wartime production.

## Wartime footing for the industrial base

The May 20, 2026 [[badlands-brief-311|Badlands Brief]] reports that Hegseth has described the shift toward converting idle civilian manufacturing capacity into defense output as placing the industrial base on a "wartime footing," a response to stockpiles depleted by conflicts across Eurasia. The remark is presented in the context of Trump administration talks with automakers including [[ford-motor-company|Ford]], [[general-motors|GM]], and [[oshkosh-corporation|Oshkosh]]. Ford's announcement that it was exploring supplying North American and European militaries with mobility, transport, and field-power solutions — invoking its World War II production record — is treated as the leading example of this policy taking hold.

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the same developments as the revival of [[detroit|Detroit]]'s "Arsenal of Democracy" role, but for "sovereign power projection" rather than what he called abstract globalist adventures.
