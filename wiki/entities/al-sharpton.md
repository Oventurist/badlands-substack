# Al Sharpton

Al Sharpton is an American civil rights activist and broadcaster who appears in Badlands Media commentary chiefly as a rhetorical archetype rather than as a subject of reporting.[1]

## Use as the archetypal "grandstander"

In his March 2025 essay "Massie Maximalists," Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] called Sharpton "the ultimate grandstander," defining a political grandstander as someone who takes credit for a movement they did not contribute to and who climbs aboard only once the momentum has already swung and the risk has passed.[1] Carlson argued that Sharpton shows up to every protest of police after a black person is killed by an officer, but does not protest the black-on-black violence occurring in Chicago every weekend and does not otherwise serve the black community — appearing, in Carlson's framing, only where there is something to gain.[1]

The comparison functioned as the benchmark against which Carlson measured Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]], whom President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had described as a grandstander during the March 2025 [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]] fight.[1] Carlson used the Sharpton example to distinguish position-taking that carries no personal risk from deal-making that requires compromise and "skin in the game."[1]
