# Joint Committee on Taxation

The Joint Committee on Taxation is the nonpartisan committee of the United States Congress whose professional staff analyzes and scores federal tax legislation for members of both chambers.[1] Its chief of staff, [[thomas-barthold|Thomas Barthold]], has served on the committee's staff since 1987.[1]

## Role in the 2025 reconciliation debate

In May 2025, as the House of Representatives considered [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s "big, beautiful bill," committee staff were called on to explain the reconciliation package's actual provisions to lawmakers.[1] Representative [[tom-suozzi|Tom Suozzi]], a member of the Ways and Means Subcommittee on Tax, questioned Barthold about the gap between Trump's campaign promises on tips, overtime and Social Security taxes and the bill's text.[1] Barthold confirmed that the bill continued the payroll tax on tips, undercutting the promise of [[tax-free-tips|fully tax-free tips]].[1] The bill passed the House by a single vote and moved to the [[us-senate|Senate]].[1]
