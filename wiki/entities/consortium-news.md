# Consortium News

Consortium News is an independent investigative outlet named by Badlands Media, alongside [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]], as representative of the [[decentralized-media|decentralized media]] sector.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

The article uses it to illustrate the coverage gap between the two media ecosystems: subjects thoroughly discussed at Consortium News and The Grayzone are absent from [[cnn|CNN]], just as the difficult topics examined on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] are avoided by [[fox-news|Fox News]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm) Badlands argues that the apparent ideological opposition between mainstream outlets conceals a shared refusal to examine the establishment itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

## Lawsuit against NewsGuard and the US government (2023)

In October 2023, Consortium News sued the media-ratings firm [[newsguard|NewsGuard]] together with the Biden administration, naming the Pentagon's [[us-cyber-command|Cyber Command]] for contracting with NewsGuard to identify, report and abridge the speech of American media organizations that dissent from official U.S. foreign policy positions.[2] The complaint alleged that NewsGuard was acting jointly or in concert with the United States to coerce news organizations into altering viewpoints regarding Ukraine, Russia and Syria, and that this constituted censorship and repression of views diverging from U.S. and allied policy.[2]

Consortium News further noted that NewsGuard had branded all of its more than 20,000 published articles as unreliable on the basis of only six disputed examples — the same approach the outlet says was applied to [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and other independent publishers.[2] The suit drew public support from [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], who denounced NewsGuard as a "scam" in connection with a strike issued against commentator [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]].[2] Badlands commentary presented the litigation as the first clear-cut, documentable instance of the government-adjacent demonetization apparatus that alternative media had previously only been able to describe anecdotally.[2]
