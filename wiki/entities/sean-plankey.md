# Sean Plankey

**Sean Plankey** is the nominee to lead the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] (CISA). As of March 2026 his nomination remained stalled, leaving the agency without a Senate-confirmed director.[1]

The vacancy contributed to prolonged instability at CISA. [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]] served as acting director following [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]], while chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] announced his departure after 18 years at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], and other senior staff received transfer orders.[1]
