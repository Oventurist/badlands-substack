# Jewish News Syndicate

The Jewish News Syndicate (JNS) is a news outlet covering Jewish and Israel-related affairs.[1]

## Coverage of the 2026 insider trading case

JNS reported on the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] insider trading indictments under a framing that identified the indicted group as Jewish and noted the allegation that participants used "going to Israel" as code for illegal sales.[1] The outlet also interpreted the DOJ's designation of defendant [[god-izraelov|God Izraelov]] as located in [[israel|Israel]] to mean he had fled the United States to avoid extradition.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the JNS coverage as precedent for his own commentary on the defendants' shared identity and alleged Israeli ties.[1]
