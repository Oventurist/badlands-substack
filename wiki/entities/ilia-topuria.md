# Ilia Topuria

Ilia Topuria is a mixed martial artist who held the UFC lightweight world championship going into [[ufc-freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]] in June 2026.[1]

## Loss at Freedom 250

Topuria lost the lightweight title to American challenger [[justin-gaethje|Justin Gaethje]] at the White House event on June 14, 2026, in a bout Badlands commentary described as an amazing match that would "live in infamy."[1] The card was held on the South Lawn of the [[white-house|White House]] to mark the United States' 250th anniversary and [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s 80th birthday.[1]
