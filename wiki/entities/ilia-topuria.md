# Ilia Topuria

**Ilia Topuria** is a Georgian-Spanish mixed martial artist who held the [[ultimate-fighting-championship|UFC]] lightweight championship going into the promotion's 2026 White House card.

## Loss at UFC Freedom 250

Topuria entered [[freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]] on June 14, 2026 — staged on the South Lawn of the [[white-house|White House]] for the United States' 250th anniversary and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 80th birthday — as the reigning world champion in the lightweight division. He was defeated by American challenger [[justin-gaethje|Justin Gaethje]], who took the title in what the Badlands Brief called an amazing match that would "live in infamy."

The result was read within the Badlands corpus less as a sporting outcome than as symbolism: a American underdog beating the world champion on the lawn of the executive mansion, before the President and First Lady, during the nation's semiquincentennial.
