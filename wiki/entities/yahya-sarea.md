# Yahya Sarea

Yahya Sarea is the military spokesman for the [[houthis|Houthi]] movement in [[yemen|Yemen]].[1]

## Statements on the Red Sea clash

Speaking on the Yemeni television channel Al-Masirah after the December 31, 2023 [[maersk-container-ship-attack|Maersk container ship attack]] and the U.S. helicopter response, Sarea confirmed that U.S. forces had killed ten Houthi fighters.[1] He stated: "US enemy forces attacked three boats belonging to the Yemeni Naval Forces, which led to the martyrdom and the loss of ten people from the Naval Forces."[1]

Sarea framed the group's maritime campaign as legitimate, saying its fighters were "performing their humanitarian and moral duty" in deterring Israel-related commercial vessels from transiting the Red Sea "in solidarity and support for the Palestinian people."[1] A Houthi spokesperson also warned of "consequences and repercussions" for the U.S. strike.[1]
