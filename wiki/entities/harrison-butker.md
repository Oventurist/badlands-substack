# Harrison Butker

Harrison Butker is the Kansas City Chiefs kicker whose May 2024 commencement address at [[benedictine-college|Benedictine College]] triggered a national controversy over his remarks on homemaking and vocation.[1]

## The speech

Butker told graduates: "How many of you are sitting here now, about to cross this stage, and are thinking about all the promotions and titles you are going to get in your career? Some of you may go on to lead successful careers in the world. But I would venture to guess that the majority of you are most excited about your marriage and the children you will bring into this world."[1] He characterized homemaking as one of the "most important" jobs a woman can have.[1]

## Reaction

The speech produced what ZeroHedge described as a collective gasp from the left, which the outlet said has spent decades programming young women to believe that becoming a housewife is tantamount to failure.[1] Coming to Butker's defense was "Real Time" host [[bill-maher|Bill Maher]], who said during a Friday night panel discussion that he did not see "what the big crime is" and mocked Butker's critics for trying to make him out to be "history's greatest monster": "I can't express how much this guy is not like me. He's religious. He loves marriage. He loves kids… And he's now history's greatest monster."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the only reason there is outrage against Butker is that what he said is true, recounting her own experience as a working mother and arguing that "it's not that women are less capable" but that "women are built for something better, more important… something generationally transcendent."[1] She cited [[gk-chesterton|G.K. Chesterton]]'s *What's Wrong with the World* and its chapter "Feminism, Or the Mistake about Woman": "How can it be a large career to tell other people's children about the Rule of Three, and a small career to tell one's own children about the universe?"[1]
