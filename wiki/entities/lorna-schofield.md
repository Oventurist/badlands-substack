# Lorna Schofield

Lorna Schofield is a United States District Judge and an Obama appointee. In January 2026 she disqualified [[john-sarcone|John Sarcone III]] as US Attorney for the Northern District of New York and invalidated subpoenas his office had issued to New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]].[1]

## The opinion

In a 24-page opinion, Schofield held that Sarcone had been serving unlawfully.[1] She wrote: "When the Executive branch of government skirts restraints put in place by Congress and then uses that power to subject political adversaries to criminal investigations, it acts without lawful authority."[1]

The decision was part of a broader run of rulings rejecting the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s practice of installing US attorneys without Senate confirmation.[1]
