# Park An-Su

**Park An-Su** is the South Korean officer who served as martial law commander during President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]]'s brief declaration of martial law in December 2024.[1]

## Treason complaints

Following the retraction of the martial law order — which Yoon rescinded less than six hours after announcing it, once MPs voted against the measure and thousands of protesters took to the streets — the opposition filed legal complaints accusing Yoon, former defense minister [[kim-yong-hyun|Kim Yong-hyun]], and Park of treason.[1] Kim was arrested after voluntarily submitting to questioning by prosecutors, and the [[corruption-investigation-office-for-high-ranking-officials|Corruption Investigation Office for High-ranking Officials]] said it would investigate "the leader and key offenders related to treason."[1]

Badlands commentary characterised the arrests of the military leaders who went along with what it described as a coup attempt in [[south-korea|South Korea]] as a positive development, praising the Korean people and opposition party for shutting the effort down.[1]
