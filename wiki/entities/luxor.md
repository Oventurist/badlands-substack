# Luxor

**Luxor** is a city in Upper Egypt whose West Bank contains some of the densest concentrations of ancient Egyptian funerary architecture, including the Dra' Abu el-Naga necropolis.[1]

In May 2025, an archaeological discovery at Dra' Abu el-Naga on Luxor's West Bank revealed three previously unknown tombs belonging to prominent statesmen of Egypt's New Kingdom era (1550 B.C.E. to 1070 B.C.E.).[1] Inscriptions inside the tombs allowed the all-Egyptian research team to identify the names and titles of their owners, and the first tomb was dated to the [[ramesside-period|Ramesside Period]], belonging to Amum-em-Ipet of the temple or estate of Amun.[1] Mohamed Ismail Khaled, secretary-general of the [[supreme-council-of-antiquities|Supreme Council of Antiquities]], said further research was required to learn more about the owners, but that the findings enriched understanding of the period.[1]
