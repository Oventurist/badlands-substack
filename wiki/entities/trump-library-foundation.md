# Trump Library Foundation

The Trump Library Foundation is the entity organized to develop and hold assets for the [[donald-j-trump-presidential-library|Donald J. Trump Presidential Library]] in Miami, Florida.[1]

## Role

Prime waterfront land in Miami was transferred to the foundation by [[miami-dade-college|Miami-Dade College]] as the site for the proposed 50-story library announced on March 31, 2026 by [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]].[1] The transfer became the focus of criticism from historian [[marvin-dunn|Marvin Dunn]], who called it a "land giveaway," while critics of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] more broadly mocked the building's gold cladding and giant statue.[1]
