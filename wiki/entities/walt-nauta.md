# Walt Nauta

Walt Nauta is a personal aide to former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and a co-defendant in the [[justice-department|Justice Department]]'s classified records prosecution of Trump.[1]

## Superseding indictment

In a superseding indictment filed on Thursday, July 27, 2023, the Justice Department accused Trump of acting with Nauta and with Mar-a-Lago property manager [[carlos-de-oliveira|Carlos de Oliveira]] in an attempt to delete surveillance footage at Trump's Mar-a-Lago property.[1] The filing brought the total number of counts facing Trump to 40 and added a further [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] charge tied to military documents Trump had reportedly boasted of possessing in a recorded meeting, in which he warned that he could not share them because he had not declassified them.[1]

## Commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 28, 2023 covered the superseding indictment under the heading "The Walls Are Closing in ... Or So We're Told," treating the new charges as part of a recurring media narrative cycle rather than a decisive legal turn.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] greeted each new indictment with anticipation rather than alarm, interpreting the prosecutions through the lens of [[devolution|devolution]] and an ongoing disclosure operation.[1]
