# The Daily Mail

The Daily Mail is a British newspaper whose online edition maintains an extensive United States politics operation and is frequently cited in Truth Community aggregation for reporting that American outlets decline to run.[1]

## Reporting on the Hunter Biden plea hearing (July 2023)

In July 2023 the Daily Mail reported that a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official said the department was discussing delaying [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s plea hearing, following a firestorm of "sweetheart deal" allegations from whistleblowers and claims that Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] had lied to [[congress|Congress]] about the Biden criminal probe.[1] The report noted that on 26 July a Delaware federal judge, [[maryellen-noreika|Maryellen Noreika]], was set to decide whether to accept the First Son's plea deal over two tax misdemeanours, and that Republican lawmakers were calling on her to toss out the "slap on the wrist" entirely.[1] It cited [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers who had investigated Hunter Biden for five years and said he could instead have been charged with a raft of more serious tax and corruption crimes, and pointed to a legal filing revealing that the Justice Department was considering delaying finalisation of the deal amid the opprobrium.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the details of the story came from an anonymous Justice Department official and cautioned readers to weigh that before drawing conclusions, while crediting the reporting for attributing the developments to Republican outcry.[1]
