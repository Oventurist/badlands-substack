# Transparency International

Transparency International is an international anti-corruption watchdog organization whose survey work is widely cited as a benchmark for measuring graft in national governments and public services.[1]

## Ukraine corruption findings

A 2016 report by Transparency International found that between 38 percent and 42 percent of Ukrainian households said they had paid bribes simply to access basic public services.[1] The same body of research placed Ukraine among the most corrupt countries in Europe, with only Russia rated as more corrupt on the continent.[1]

These findings were invoked in April 2023 commentary on U.S. aid to Ukraine, where they were used to argue that reported embezzlement of American assistance was consistent with a long-standing pattern rather than an isolated scandal.[1] The citation appeared alongside reporting by [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] that Ukrainian officials had embezzled at least $400 million in U.S. funds, and alongside a separate [[security-service-of-ukraine|SBU]] disclosure of a military food-procurement embezzlement scheme.[1]

## Significance in the funding debate

Because Transparency International is a mainstream, non-partisan institution, its data gave critics of open-ended Ukraine funding a source that could not easily be dismissed as pro-Russian advocacy — a dynamic that [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described as pushing the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] back toward objective analysis of wartime spending.[1]
