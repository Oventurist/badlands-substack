# James Stavridis

James Stavridis is a retired United States admiral who served as NATO Supreme Allied Commander Europe and who remains a frequent commentator on international security matters.[1]

## Comments on the Israel–Hezbollah ceasefire

In a Sunday interview with radio host John Catsimatidis on "The Cats Roundtable" on WABC 770 AM, broadcast in early December 2024, Stavridis said he believed the Israel–[[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] ceasefire was successful in part because of the impending return to power of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] "Iran [is] very vulnerable," he said. "What that means is that in the fight that Israel continues to have with Hezbollah … you're seeing Hezbollah willing to go to a ceasefire," adding, "I think that's a direct result of the presence of a new Trump administration coming on."[1]

The truce, which began the preceding Wednesday, was viewed as a first step toward resolving regional conflict following the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023]] Hamas attack on Israel, though both Hezbollah and Israel accused each other of violating it.[1]

## Assessment of Trump's leverage

Stavridis said foreign leaders were waiting for Trump to take office before making permanent decisions: "In many capitals around the world, the leaders are holding their breath. [They're] just unsure of what's coming."[1] He drew a distinction between adversaries and allies, noting that unpredictability "used as a negotiating ploy, as a way to keep an opponent off balance" works well, but that "when it's used with our allies and partners and friends, like the [[nato|NATO]] alliance, it can have consequences that are less good."[1]
