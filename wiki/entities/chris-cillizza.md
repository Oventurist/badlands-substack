# Chris Cillizza

Chris Cillizza is an American political commentator who served as [[cnn|CNN]]'s editor at large prior to his departure from the network in 2022.[1]

## Lab leak reversal

On Monday, January 27, 2025, Cillizza posted a thread on X conceding that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had likely been right about the COVID-19 lab leak theory, opening with the admission "I screwed up."[1] The concession followed the [[cia|CIA]]'s announcement that Saturday that it had changed its assessment of the origins of COVID-19, stating it believed "with low confidence that a research-related origin of the COVID-19 pandemic is more likely than a natural origin based on the available body of reporting."[1] See also the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]].

Badlands commentary dismissed the mea culpa as an attempt to treat a five-year-old error as newly revealed, noting that no genuinely new information had emerged, and argued that Cillizza misunderstood the reasoning failures that produced his original position and was seeking to preserve his reputation rather than reckon with his record.[1]
