# Rothschild Family

The Rothschild family is a European banking dynasty that appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the archetypal owners of the international debt system. In [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" the name is used interchangeably with the [[cabal]] to denote the ultimate beneficiaries of global fiat finance.

The article makes two direct references. Explaining why so many countries are simultaneously developing [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] while buying record quantities of gold, Lange writes that "they all know the Rothschilds global debt system is ending, and they are preparing to move quickly to a gold-backed currency." Later, summarizing his thesis, he calls "this entire plan to free the world from a Rothschilds-controlled debt system" brilliant.

The claim is presented as interpretation rather than documented fact; the article offers no sourcing for the attribution and uses it as shorthand for the concentrated private control of money that the author says the [[gold-standard|gold standard]], [[brics]], and blockchain-based settlement will together dissolve.
