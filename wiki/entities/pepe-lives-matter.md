# Pepe Lives Matter

Pepe Lives Matter is the pen name of a citizen journalist and commentator who contributes to the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] and publishes on Substack.[1]

## Commentary

Responding to [[joe-biden|President Joe Biden]]'s March 2023 remarks about his cranial aneurysm — in which Biden joked that doctors "had to take the top of my head off a couple times, see if I had a brain" — Pepe Lives Matter dubbed him "the red pill and chief," writing that Biden did "every single thing that might wake up the sleeping masses" and did so "in such an outrageous and blubbering way."[1] The contributor compared the spectacle to a Naked Gun scene in which a figure insists there is "nothing to see here" while practically begging onlookers to look.[1]

Pepe Lives Matter argued that anyone paying even slight attention to politics should recognise that presidents have historically been controlled puppets, a fact he said Biden displayed "in immaculate fashion," and concluded that whether purposeful or not, the effect was "a net positive for the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]]."[1]
