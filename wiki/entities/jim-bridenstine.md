# Jim Bridenstine

Jim Bridenstine served as administrator of [[nasa|NASA]] during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and was the public architect of the original [[artemis-accords|Artemis Accords]].[1]

In Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s essay "Time to Revive the Spirit of JFK," Bridenstine is credited, alongside Trump, with an initial design for the Artemis Accords that Ehret argues was oriented toward genuine international partnership — including with Russia and China — before being abandoned under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] Ehret contends that had this original design not been discarded, the United States could already be working with Russia and China to revive the spirit of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] "to go beyond the limits of knowledge."[1]

When the accords were first announced in 2019, Bridenstine stated: "It's a new dawn for space exploration! Today I'm honored to announce the Artemis Accords Agreements- establishing a shared vision and set of principles for all international partners that join in humanity's return to the moon. We go, together!"[1]

Bridenstine's tenure coincided with direct outreach to Russia's space agency, and he was photographed with former [[roscosmos|Roscosmos]] chief [[dmitry-rogozin|Dmitry Rogozin]] during the period in which lunar cooperation was under discussion.[1]
