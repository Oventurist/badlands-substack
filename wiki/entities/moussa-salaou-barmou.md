# Moussa Salaou Barmou

General Moussa Salaou Barmou is the self-declared defense chief installed by the military junta that seized power in [[niger|Niger]] following the ouster of President [[mohamed-bazoum|Mohamed Bazoum]] on July 26, 2023.[1]

## Meeting with Victoria Nuland

Barmou was among the junta officials who met Acting Deputy Secretary of State [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] during her surprise visit to Niamey in August 2023.[1] Nuland confirmed that Barmou had previously received military training from elite American operatives — a detail characterized in commentary as "yet another irony and failure of US foreign policy."[1] Nuland described the talks with the military leadership as "extremely frank and at times quite difficult," and said the leaders, Barmou among them, were "quite firm in their view on how they want to proceed," in a manner that "does not comport with the constitution of Niger."[1]
