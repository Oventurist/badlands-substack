# Jews Against Soros

Jews Against Soros is an advocacy organization launched in June 2023 by two Jewish conservative activists to contest the claim that criticism of the left-wing billionaire Democrat donor [[george-soros|George Soros]] is inherently antisemitic.[1] The group's founders were [[josh-hammer|Josh Hammer]], a writer for [[newsweek|Newsweek]], and [[will-scharf|Will Scharf]], then a candidate for state attorney general in Missouri.[1]

## Launch and purpose

The organization was introduced through the website JewsAgainstSoros.com, which the founders used to argue that there is nothing antisemitic in opposing Soros's political agenda and to push back on the framing of such criticism as bigotry.[1] The launch was reported by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] on June 1, 2023.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the group's formation as an illustration of a broader point about collective attribution, writing that history "has repeatedly shown that entire groups of people pretty much never walk in lockstep" and that any ethnicity or culture contains philosophical, political and cultural points of bifurcation that generate sub-groups.[1] He drew analogies to the many sects of Christianity and to internal Jewish divisions, and concluded that the story suggested Jewish people were not "some maniacal hive-mind hell bent on world domination" but "a people like any other who harbor a percentage of individuals who seek to serve themselves over the whole," a pattern he said held true of all religions and races.[1]
