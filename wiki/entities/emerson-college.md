# Emerson College

Emerson College is an American institution whose polling operation conducts widely cited surveys of United States electoral contests.[1]

## 2023 Iowa Republican primary poll

In results released on the morning of Thursday, May 25, 2023, an Emerson College poll of Iowa found that 62 percent of Republican voters would choose former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as the party's nominee, giving him a roughly three-to-one margin over Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], who registered 20 percent.[1] [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] and [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] were tied for third at 5 percent.[1]

The survey placed DeSantis roughly on par with his standing in the RealClearPolitics national average, indicating that his heavily covered visits to Iowa earlier that month and his formal campaign launch the previous day in a Twitter livestream with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had not yet produced a polling boost.[1]

Emerson's crosstabs suggested a demographic fault line within the Republican base: Trump was preferred across the board but especially among voters under 35 (75 percent) and those without a college degree (70 percent), while DeSantis ran much closer among Republicans holding postgraduate degrees.[1] The poll also tested general-election matchups in Iowa, finding Trump leading President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] 49 percent to 38 percent with 10 percent choosing a third party and 6 percent undecided, and DeSantis leading Biden 45 percent to 38 percent.[1]
