# The Bunker

The Bunker is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program listed among the new shows added to the network's lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 schedule, airing Saturdays at 7:30pm ET.[1]

## Format and hosts

The show is hosted by [[trureporting|TruReporting]] and [[wearerise|WeAreRise]], who take on the geopolitical issues of the day.[1] TruReporting also co-hosts the network's Sunday-night [[ncswic-podcast|NCSWIC Podcast]], giving him two weekend slots on the schedule.[1]

## Distribution

Live streams and replays are carried on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
