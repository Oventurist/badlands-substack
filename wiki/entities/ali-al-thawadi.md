# Ali Al-Thawadi

**Ali Al-Thawadi** is a Qatari mediator involved in the United States–[[iran|Iran]] negotiations of June 2026.[1]

Axios reported that negotiators reached a tentative agreement on a Wednesday night following talks involving Al-Thawadi and Iranian Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]], citing diplomats and US officials familiar with the discussions.[1] The resulting framework, reportedly to be called the [[islamabad-agreement|Islamabad agreement]], was mediated jointly by [[qatar|Qatar]] and [[pakistan|Pakistan]].[1]
