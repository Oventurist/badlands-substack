# Mountain Pass Mine

The Mountain Pass Mine is a rare-earth mine in the San Bernardino County community of Mountain Pass, California, operated by [[mp-materials|MP Materials]] and among the largest rare-earth mines in the world.[1]

## Geology and operations

The open-air pit is nearly 500 feet deep and lies just north of the 15 Freeway near Bailey Road, at almost 5,000 feet elevation on the highway between Southern California and Las Vegas.[1] More than 200,000 drivers traverse that stretch of freeway each day, and many are unaware of the mine's existence.[1] MP Materials CFO [[ryan-corbett|Ryan Corbett]] attributed the deposit's quality to events "many billions of years ago," calling it "almost a freak of nature": the way local fault lines formed allowed rare-earth-bearing rock formations to surface at a concentration, and in a chemically tractable form, "that you really don't see anywhere else in the world."[1] Many of the seventeen rare earth elements on the periodic table occur at the site.[1]

## Trade-war importance

As tariff conflict and supply-chain competition intensified, domestic industry increasingly relied on Mountain Pass for rare-earth feedstock, making the mine a focal point of United States critical-minerals policy.[1]
