# Chris Deluzio

Chris Deluzio is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives and a veteran of the armed services or intelligence community.[1] He was one of the six lawmakers who appeared in the 90-second video posted from Senator [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]]'s X account in November 2025, addressing U.S. service members and urging them to uphold the Constitution and refuse "illegal orders."[1]

## Sedition controversy

The video prompted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to accuse the participants — Slotkin, [[mark-kelly|Mark Kelly]], [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]], Deluzio, [[maggie-goodlander|Maggie Goodlander]] and [[chrissy-houlahan|Chrissy Houlahan]] — of sedition "punishable by DEATH" on November 20, 2025.[1] The group issued a joint statement condemning what they called the President's "calls for our murder and political violence."[1] The episode became known in Badlands coverage as the [[seditious-six|Seditious Six]] affair.[1]
