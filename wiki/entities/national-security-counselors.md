# National Security Counselors

National Security Counselors is a public interest law firm specializing in government transparency and national security law.[1]

The firm filed a 30-page complaint against the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] within minutes of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration on January 20, 2025, alleging that the nongovernmental DOGE panel led by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] violates a 1972 federal law requiring advisory committees to the executive branch to follow rules on disclosure, hiring and other practices. The complaint, obtained by The Washington Post ahead of filing, argues that DOGE meets the statutory definition of a "federal advisory committee" — a class of legal entity regulated to ensure that the government receives transparent and balanced advice.[1]

The suit opened what observers described as a legal battle over a central element of the incoming administration's agenda, filed the same evening as the [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]]'s challenge to Trump's [[birthright-citizenship|birthright citizenship]] order.[1]
