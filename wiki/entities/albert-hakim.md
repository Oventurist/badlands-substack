# Albert Hakim

Albert Hakim was an Iranian-American businessman and the business partner of retired Air Force Major General [[richard-secord|Richard Secord]] during the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra affair]].[1] Secord had known Hakim since the 1970s, when Secord was selling weapons to the Shah of Iran and both men profited from the trade.[1]

According to the New York Times, an American businessman said he turned down direct requests from King Fahd and other Saudi officials to serve as a conduit for $15 million intended for the Nicaraguan rebels in 1984 and 1985, and that the funds were to have been sent through Secord and Hakim.[1] Congressional investigators concluded that Secord and Hakim were involved in the covert program to supply the Contras during the period when [[congress|Congress]] had placed strict controls on American assistance.[1]

Joe Lange argues that Secord and Hakim were the facilitators of the secret condition attached to the 1981 [[awacs-sale-to-saudi-arabia|AWACS sale]] — a Saudi commitment to fund anti-Communist resistance groups worldwide — and that this arrangement is the clearest exposure of the [[shadow-government]]'s effort to build funding sources beyond congressional reach.[1]
