# David McGee

David McGee is a former federal prosecutor who was indicted and later convicted for attempting to extort Don Gaetz, the father of Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]].[1]

## The extortion scheme

In March 2021, Matt Gaetz appeared on [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] Tonight and claimed that his father had been the target of an extortion attempt by McGee seeking $25 million, and that his father had worn a wire during meetings with McGee as part of a sting operation.[1] The story struck contemporaneous listeners as implausible — even Carlson did not appear to believe it — yet in hindsight it appears Gaetz was telling the truth.[1]

McGee had been recruited by Air Force intelligence officer Bob Kent, who was attempting to organize the rescue of retired FBI agent Bob Levinson.[1] McGee was brought into the effort because he knew both Levinson and Russian oligarch [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]], having worked in the Organized Crimes Task Force at the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], while Levinson had specialized in Russian money laundering at the FBI.[1] The FBI point man on the arrangement was [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]].[1]

According to the account, McGee told Don Gaetz — who had no knowledge or reason to believe his son was under federal investigation — that if he provided $25 million to finance Levinson's rescue, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would pardon Matt Gaetz of any potential crimes arising from the investigations then open against him.[1]

## Indictment and conviction

A federal grand jury indicted McGee in September 2021 for attempting to extort Don Gaetz.[1] He was later convicted and sentenced to five years in prison.[1] The indictment prompted [[scott-adams|Scott Adams]] to come forward publicly with text messages bearing on the Gaetz allegations.[1]
