# Dmitri Peskov

Dmitri Peskov is the Kremlin spokesman for the Russian presidency. In the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] edition of July 20, 2026, Peskov is cited via Russian state media as publicly stating that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had no current plans to speak by telephone with Ayatollah [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]], the new Iranian supreme leader.

Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] paired this statement with Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]]'s admission that he had never met Mojtaba Khamenei in person, noting that no world leader had yet spoken with him. From the conjunction the author raised the possibility that the new supreme leader may not be a real or conventional figure at all, and speculated that the arrangement could be a narrative device used to unwind the theocratic system associated with [[iran]].
