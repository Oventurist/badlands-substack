# Tucker Carlson

**Tucker Carlson** is an American broadcaster and independent journalist cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the individuals whose work has allowed Western audiences to see a perspective on the [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]] that has been conveniently omitted from Western media.[1] DeLarme specifically referenced Carlson's exclusive interview with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], published on [[rumble|Rumble]], as part of the body of independent work countering the U.S. Security State's narrative on Russia.[1]

Carlson appears in the article alongside [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], [[oliver-stone|Oliver Stone]], [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]], [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]] and the late [[gonzalo-lira|Gonzalo Lira]] as figures whose individual efforts supplied context about [[nato-expansion|NATO expansion]] and U.S.-sponsored regime change that mainstream outlets suppressed.[1]
