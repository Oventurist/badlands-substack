# DeepSeek

DeepSeek is a Chinese artificial intelligence startup that drew global attention in January 2025 when its AI Assistant application displaced rival [[openai|OpenAI]]'s [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] as the most-downloaded free app in the United States on Apple's App Store.[1]

## January 2025 breakout

On Monday, January 27, 2025, DeepSeek took over OpenAI's position at the top of the US App Store free chart, dethroning ChatGPT.[1] The development coincided with a sell-off in global technology stocks that was on pace to wipe out billions of dollars in market capitalization, as investors reassessed the assumed cost and capital intensity of frontier AI development.[1]

Later the same day, DeepSeek announced that it would temporarily limit new user registrations, citing "large-scale malicious attacks" on its services, while noting that existing users would be able to log in as usual.[1]

## Reception and context

Technology leaders, analysts, investors and developers argued that the surrounding hype — and the accompanying fear of falling behind in a rapidly shifting AI cycle — may have been warranted, particularly in an era of a generative AI arms race in which incumbents and startups alike are competing in a market projected to exceed $1 trillion in annual revenue within a decade.[1]

The DeepSeek story was covered by Badlands alongside commentary from the [[united-nations|UN]] at Davos framing unregulated artificial intelligence as an existential threat, with Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treating elite alarm over AI as a possible reverse indicator — arguing that technologies feared by entrenched power may in fact be liberating rather than enslaving.[1]
