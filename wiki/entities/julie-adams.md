# Julie Adams

Julie Adams is a Republican member of the [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] Board of Elections and Registrations in Georgia.[1] She brought the lawsuit that produced an October 14, 2024 ruling by Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[robert-mcburney|Robert McBurney]] on the certification obligations of county election officials.[1]

Adams filed suit after the county's appointed election director allegedly denied her repeated requests for access to election results and processes.[1] McBurney ruled against the broader theory that certification could be withheld, writing that no election superintendent or member of a board of elections and registration "may refuse to certify or abstain from certifying election results under any circumstance," and that while requested information should be provided promptly where not protected by law, any delay in receiving it is not a basis for refusing to certify.[1]
