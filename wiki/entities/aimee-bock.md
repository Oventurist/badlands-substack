# Aimee Bock

Aimee Bock was the central figure in the [[feeding-our-future|Feeding Our Future]] fraud scheme, described by federal prosecutors as its mastermind.[1]

## Conviction

Bock and co-defendant [[salim-said|Salim Said]] were convicted in March 2025 in connection with the $250 million fraud, part of what remains the largest COVID-era fraud scheme prosecuted to date.[1] At least 78 defendants have been indicted in the broader scandal, with Somali-specific indictments continuing as recently as November 2025.[1] Federal prosecutors allege stolen US government funds were used to finance luxury beachfront accommodations, high-end vehicles including a Rolls-Royce and a Lamborghini, travel, and Minnesota real estate, with the money nominally distributed to serve an alleged "food desert."[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], responding to commentary framing the scandal in ethnic terms, urged investigators to "follow the money and prosecute the Americans," singling out Bock — "that Aimee Brock person who was convicted last March" — and calling to "roll em' all up."[1]
