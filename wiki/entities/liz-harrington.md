# Liz Harrington

Liz Harrington is an American conservative communicator and spokesperson, best known as a spokeswoman for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and as a prolific commentator on election-integrity litigation. In the Badlands Media corpus she serves as a principal conduit for the contents of [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s December 2022 Arizona election contest.

## Twitter thread on the Lake complaint

On the night of Friday, December 9, 2022, hours after Lake filed her 70-page suit in [[maricopa-county-superior-court|Maricopa County Superior Court]], Harrington published a Twitter thread summarizing the complaint. The Badlands article [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]], which describes her as "the incredible Liz Harrington" and says she "took to Twitter to tear the lawsuit apart," reproduces that thread at length as its central evidentiary section.

Her summary covers the claim that illegal votes far exceeded the 17,117-vote margin; the cyber expert's assertion that machine failures "could not have occurred absent intentional misconduct"; the estimate that between 15,603 and 29,257 Republican voters were disenfranchised; signature-verification statistics from the 2020 review of 230,339 envelopes and their 2022 echoes; whistleblower testimony about level-2 managers reversing rejected signatures and off-site curing by the Star Center contractor; the allegation that 298,942 early ballots handled by [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]] lacked chain of custody; the "Door 3" ballot dispute; and the charge that [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] and [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]] colluded with the federal government to censor Americans. The thread concludes with the complaint's assertion that maladministration and illegal votes caused Arizona to wrongly name Hobbs the winner — which Harrington renders as "In sum: KARI WON."
