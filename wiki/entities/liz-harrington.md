# Liz Harrington

Liz Harrington is a conservative communications figure and spokeswoman associated with Donald Trump, active on Twitter under the handle @realLizUSA.[1]

On the night of Friday, December 9, 2022, hours after [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] filed her 70-page election contest, Harrington posted an extended Twitter thread breaking the complaint down claim by claim. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] reproduced that thread at length as the spine of her article on the filing, describing Harrington as "incredible."[1]

The excerpts Gillespie carried covered the suit's core assertions: that the number of illegal votes cast far exceeded the 17,117-vote margin; that hundreds of thousands of illegal ballots had "infected" the election in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]; that between 15,603 and 29,257 Republican voters were disenfranchised by machine failures; that more than 300,000 mail-in ballots had no chain of custody; and that signature verification in the county was, in the thread's phrasing, "a joke."[1] Harrington's thread also relayed the suit's allegation that [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] and [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]] had colluded with the federal government to censor Americans in violation of the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]], and closed on the complaint's summary conclusion that maladministration in Maricopa County caused Arizona wrongfully to name Hobbs the winner.[1]

## Election-integrity advocacy (2025)

Harrington remained a prominent voice on election-integrity questions into the second Trump administration. In April 2025, as Democratic officials in 19 states sued to block President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[2025-trump-elections-executive-order|elections executive order]], Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] cited Harrington's response to the litigation with the observation that "Liz Harrington is not messing around."[2] Paul contrasted her posture with what he described as attention-farming commentators downstream of [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]], whom he accused of persuading the "Uniparty Right" that elections are legitimate and that the remedy is greater funding and power for TPUSA.[2]
