# Johann Wadephul

Johann Wadephul is the Foreign Minister of Germany.[1]

## Visit to India

In early September 2025 Wadephul travelled to India on a two-day official visit, meeting External Affairs Minister [[s-jaishankar|S. Jaishankar]].[1] Without criticizing the United States, he echoed Jaishankar's emphasis on partnership stability, saying that "reliability," "friendship" and "predictability" in times of uncertainty are "invaluable."[1] Indian media read the joint messaging as a signal directed at Washington amid the perceived unpredictability of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's dealings with New Delhi.[1] Jaishankar used the occasion to argue for far closer cooperation between India and both Germany and the [[european-union|European Union]] in the face of global economic volatility.[1]
