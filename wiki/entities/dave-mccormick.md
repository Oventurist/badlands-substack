# Dave McCormick

Dave McCormick is an American Republican politician who won election to the United States Senate from Pennsylvania in the November 2024 general election, defeating the incumbent Democrat [[bob-casey|Bob Casey]].[1] Following the election he was referred to as Senator-elect while the outcome remained subject to a mandatory statewide recount.[1]

## 2024 Senate race and recount

Unofficial returns reported by the Pennsylvania Department of State showed McCormick receiving 3,380,310 votes, or 48.93 percent of the vote, against 3,350,972 votes, or 48.50 percent, for Casey — a margin narrow enough to trigger Pennsylvania's automatic recount provision.[1] Al Schmidt, the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, announced on Wednesday, November 13, 2024 that the results had "triggered a legally required statewide recount."[1] The recount was scheduled to begin no later than November 20, 2024, with counties required to complete it by noon on November 26, 2024 and to report results to the Secretary of the Commonwealth by noon the following day.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the race "should have been called on election night," characterizing the Democratic refusal to concede as a form of election denial, and framed the recount as a source of additional data useful for "deconstructing the centralized, complex web of corruption" in American elections.[1] The same commentary placed the McCormick–Casey contest against the backdrop of a Republican sweep of the presidency, the Senate and the House of Representatives in 2024.[1]
