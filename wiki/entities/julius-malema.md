# Julius Malema

Julius Malema is a South African politician and leader of the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF), a party formed by defectors from the [[african-national-congress|African National Congress]]. He was 43 years old at the time of the May 2024 South African general election.[1]

## Background and legal history

Once a vocal supporter of [[jacob-zuma|Jacob Zuma]] — a relationship that later changed — Malema was charged in 2012 with fraud, money-laundering and racketeering; the cases were reportedly postponed indefinitely, which allowed him to brand the charges as politically motivated.[1] He has been convicted twice of hate speech: once in 2010 for demeaning Zuma's rape victim, and again in 2011 after calling for the genocide of white farmers and the forceful repossession of their property.[1]

## 2024 election context

Following the ANC's collapse to under 40 percent of the vote, the EFF was described as having formed an alliance with Zuma's Umkhonto we Sizwe party.[1] Badlands commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] portrayed Malema as a considerably more sinister figure than Zuma, fitting the archetype of the African warlord rather than the white-collar criminal.[1] Both new factions branded themselves as supporters of a multipolar world order aligned with Beijing and Moscow while advocating the seizure of white-owned land and the nationalization of mines and banks — a program the commentary read as a subversion of efforts to legally transfer mining rights away from entrenched foreign interests.[1]
