# Walter Bedell Smith

Walter Bedell Smith was a United States general and Director of Central Intelligence who appears in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account of the founding of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

DeLarme writes that when [[jozef-retinger|Józef Retinger]] was first brought to America by Averell Harriman, then U.S. ambassador to England and a member of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], Retinger visited prominent citizens including [[david-rockefeller|David]] and [[nelson-rockefeller|Nelson Rockefeller]], [[john-foster-dulles|John Foster Dulles]] and Smith, who was then [[cia|CIA]] Director.[1] Smith is also listed among the well-known American participants in Bilderberg meetings, alongside figures such as [[dean-rusk|Dean Rusk]], [[mcgeorge-bundy|McGeorge Bundy]], [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] and [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]].[1]

His presence forms part of DeLarme's argument that, given the density of U.S. intelligence connections around the group's creation, it can legitimately be argued the Bilderberg conferences were at least partially organised and sponsored by the CIA.[1]
