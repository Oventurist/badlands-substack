# Lina Mendoni

Lina Mendoni is the Greek Minister of Culture, who in February 2024 publicly objected to the portrayal of [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]] as homosexual in the [[netflix|Netflix]] docudrama "The Making of a God."[1]

## Objection to the Netflix series

Mendoni said the series was full of historical inaccuracies, which "shows the negligence of the director and the lack of quality of the script."[1] Addressing the depiction of Alexander's relationship with his closest companion, she stated that "there is no mention in the sources that this friendship goes beyond the boundaries of friendship as defined by Aristotle."[1]

Asked by [[dimitris-natsios|Dimitris Natsios]], president of the right-wing [[niki-party|Niki]] party, whether the Greek government would take action against Netflix, Mendoni replied that "such a move would be unconstitutional," noting that Greece's constitution has protected artistic freedom since the early 19th century.[1]

## Coverage and commentary

The dispute was reported by [[remix-news|ReMix News]] and carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024, where [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] framed the series as an operation in a broader [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] campaign to "queer history."[1]
