# National Fraud Enforcement Division

The National Fraud Enforcement Division is a new division of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] created by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2026 to investigate and prosecute fraud against taxpayers and federal programs.

## Creation and leadership

Trump announced the division and the nomination of [[colin-mcdonald|Colin McDonald]] as its first Assistant Attorney General late on January 28, 2026, describing it as "a new Division at the Department of Justice, which I created to catch and stop FRAUDSTERS that have been STEALING from the American People." The January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief notes that the role is described as targeting large-scale misuse of federal funds and related financial crimes, and that primary reporting did not limit it to election fraud, although election-related financial crimes may fall within its scope.

## Stated impetus

Trump pointed to fraud schemes uncovered in Minnesota and California in which, he said, "these thieves have stolen Hundreds of Billions of Taxpayer Dollars" — a reference that in the corpus connects to the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota fraud exposures]] and the [[feeding-our-future|Feeding Our Future]] case. Contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treats the division, together with the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County raid]], as the moment when long-running "Narrative" claims about systemic fraud crossed into "Actual" institutional action.
