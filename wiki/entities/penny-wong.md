# Penny Wong

Penny Wong is the Foreign Minister of Australia.[1]

In August 2024 she met in Washington with US Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], alongside Australian Defense Minister [[richard-marles|Richard Marles]], during a period of acute Middle East tension following the killings of Hamas leader [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran and Hezbollah commander [[fuad-shukr|Fuad Shukr]] in Beirut.[1] At the close of that meeting Blinken said Washington had communicated directly to both [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]] that the conflict must not escalate, urging all parties in the region to "take stock of the situation, understand the risk of miscalculation, and make decisions that will calm tensions."[1] The remarks were reported by the [[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]] and excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Assange advocacy

At a July 2023 meeting in Brisbane, Australian Foreign Minister Wong pressed U.S. Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] to end the prosecution of [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], and was publicly rebuffed when Blinken said Assange faced "very serious criminal conduct" charges.[2] The exchange formed the backdrop to Ambassador [[caroline-kennedy|Caroline Kennedy]]'s later suggestion that a plea deal might resolve the case.[2]
