# Alliance for the Union of Romanians

The Alliance for the Union of Romanians (AUR) is a Romanian nationalist opposition party described in reporting as far-right.[1]

## May 2026 no-confidence vote

AUR joined with the [[social-democratic-party-romania|Social Democrats (PSD)]], the largest party in [[romania|Romania]], to pass a no-confidence motion against Prime Minister [[ilie-bolojan|Ilie Bolojan]]'s pro-EU minority government.[1] The motion carried with 281 votes, well above the 233 required, collapsing a coalition that had been assembled roughly ten months earlier for the express purpose of blocking far-right gains.[1]
