# Justin Fairfax

**Justin Fairfax** was the Democratic Lieutenant Governor of Virginia, serving under Governor [[ralph-northam]]. His tenure was marked by sexual assault allegations that surfaced during the 2019 crisis that engulfed the entire top tier of Virginia's executive branch.

In mid-April 2026, roughly three weeks before the May 6 [[fbi]] searches of State Senator [[louise-lucas]]'s office, Fairfax shot and killed his wife and then himself. Fox News reported the murder-suicide; no information emerged tying the deaths to any public corruption scandal.

The Badlands Brief noted the event as "a totally unrelated note," while observing that the principals in Virginia's cascading legal and political troubles — Fairfax, Northam, Lucas, [[steve-descano]] and Governor [[abigail-spanberger]] — all knew one another through the state Democratic Party, and that the timing was therefore "worth mentioning."
