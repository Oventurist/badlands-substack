# Justin Fairfax

Justin Fairfax is a former Lieutenant Governor of Virginia who served under Governor [[ralph-northam|Ralph Northam]].[1]

## Death

Approximately three weeks before May 2026, Fairfax shot and killed his wife and then himself, according to reporting cited by Badlands.[1] Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the killings as a terrible tragedy and noted that there did not appear to be much information, or any indication, connecting the deaths to a public corruption scandal.[1] He nonetheless flagged the episode as worth mentioning given contemporaneous federal actions against Virginia Democrats — including the FBI searches involving [[louise-lucas|L. Louise Lucas]] — and the fact that the individuals involved knew one another and belonged to the same party.[1]
