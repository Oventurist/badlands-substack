# Zeng Guang

Zeng Guang is a chief epidemiologist at China's Center for Disease Control who, in a February 9, 2021 interview with Chinese media, publicly argued that a laboratory origin for SARS-CoV-2 should not be discounted — while rejecting the claim that the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] was the source.[1]

Zeng pointed instead to the vast, globally extended network of United States bioweapons laboratories, and cited what he characterized as America's proven track record of deploying bioweapons as part of its asymmetrical war arsenal since the Second World War.[1] In his interview he asked why so many laboratories operate under United States control around the world and what their purpose is, observing that Washington "requires others to be open and transparent, only to find that it is the United States itself that is often the most opaque," and urging that it "should take responsibility for proving itself to the world, rather than being caught up in hegemonic thinking."[1]

Zeng's remarks were echoed by Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson [[hua-chunying|Hua Chunying]], who called for the opening of [[fort-detrick|Fort Detrick]] and greater transparency regarding more than 200 overseas United States bio-labs.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats the head of the Chinese CDC's line of questioning as one of the geopolitical reference points supporting his conclusion that China's Wuhan lab is being set up as a scapegoat.[1]
