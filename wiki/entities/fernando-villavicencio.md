# Fernando Villavicencio

**Fernando Villavicencio** was an Ecuadorian legislator, former investigative journalist and presidential candidate who was assassinated in broad daylight at a campaign rally in Quito on August 9, 2023.[1]

## Background

Villavicencio served as a legislator in Ecuador's National Assembly and had built his public profile as an investigative journalist, having years earlier exposed government corruption and bribery deals connected to the country's oil industry.[1] On the campaign trail he was described as tireless in speaking out against organized crime, drug trafficking and government corruption, and he repeatedly argued that [[ecuador|Ecuador]] had become a "narco state" run by a "political mafia."[1]

## Assassination

Villavicencio was gunned down as he entered his vehicle on a crowded street in the capital, Quito, immediately after speaking at a high school before an audience of young supporters.[1] Video of the killing circulated widely, showing an assassin firing multiple shots at close range.[1] A member of his campaign team said that "when he stepped outside the door, he was met with gunfire," adding that "there was nothing to be done, because they were shots to the head."[1]

The suspected gunman was wounded in a shootout with security personnel at the scene and later died in police custody; nine other people and two police officers were injured in the attack.[1] Because Villavicencio had been by far the most vocal candidate against drug traffickers and corrupt dealings with the state, his killing was widely blamed on those same criminal elements.[1] The morning after the shooting, Ecuador declared a countrywide state of emergency for 60 days.[1]
