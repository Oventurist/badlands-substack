# Alphonso Taft

Alphonso Taft was an American lawyer and cabinet officer identified in the Badlands Media series "The Men Behind the Curtain" as a founding member of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, established on the [[yale-university|Yale]] campus in 1832.[1] He served as Secretary of War under President Rutherford B. Hayes from 1876 to 1880.[1] His son [[william-howard-taft|William Howard Taft]] followed him into the Order and became the only person to serve as both President and Chief Justice of the United States, an example the article uses to illustrate the hereditary character of Bones membership, in which family ties to the Order have "always been an important factor" in selection.[1]
