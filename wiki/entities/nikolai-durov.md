# Nikolai Durov

**Nikolai Durov** is a Russian mathematician and the brother of [[pavel-durov|Pavel Durov]], the founder and public face of [[telegram|Telegram]].[1]

Badlands Media describes Nikolai as the "genius savant" who actually created the encryption software protecting Telegram's users, holding two PhDs in mathematics and living a low-key life in Moscow, where he teaches at the university.[1] Commenting on the French arrest of Pavel Durov in August 2024, Badlands remarked that French authorities had "nabbed the wrong brother," since it is Nikolai rather than Pavel who built the platform's cryptography.[1]
