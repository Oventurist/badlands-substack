# John Brennan

**John Brennan** served as President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s top counterterrorism adviser — the White House "anti-terror czar" — during the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]], and became Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] only months afterward.[1]

## Path to the CIA

Obama had originally nominated Brennan to head the CIA upon taking office, but Brennan withdrew after drawing fire from [[congress|Congress]] over his involvement in the agency's rendition and torture programs.[1] His alternative post as anti-terror czar nevertheless gave him substantial power inside the administration, and the article maintains he was fully in the loop on events in Benghazi.[1]

## Drone doctrine and the chain of command

Investor's Business Daily, quoted at length, described Brennan as Obama's point man for the "surgical" war on al-Qaida, relying on drone strikes and promising in 2010 to use a "scalpel" instead of a "hammer."[1] The article stresses that Brennan ran the drone program out of the [[white-house|White House]] and had to approve any battlefield action, placing him at the top of the chain of command that hampered the military and, in the author's view, allowed [[isis|ISIS]] to expand.[1] The same publication argued the Drone Doctrine failed to eradicate al-Qaida, which retrenched in Libya and the Sinai and — using heavy arms seized from the Libyan army — struck the Benghazi consulate through al-Qaida in the Islamic Maghreb (AQIM), killing the ambassador and three aides.[1] Former CIA officers spent months warning the White House that thousands of shoulder-fired missiles were reaching [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]], and their warnings "fell on deaf ears."[1]

## Role in the cover-up

Brennan was conspicuously silent in the media after Benghazi, despite having been "all over TV gloating" about the bin Laden raid.[1] As CIA director he told a congressional hearing, under questioning from Rep. Devin Nunes of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, that security operators involved in the Benghazi rescue were required to re-sign non-disclosure agreements because the documents were being updated.[1] The article characterizes this as intimidation, noting that two officials were presented the NDAs at a May memorial service at Langley honoring [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]] and [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]], and that Brennan's promotion was a reward for his part in the cover-up, consistent with the CIA mantra "admit nothing and deny everything."[1] It also notes that Brennan, Obama and [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] share a Muslim background, a point flagged for a later article.[1]
