# Todd Vasos

Todd Vasos is the chief executive officer of [[dollar-general|Dollar General]], the American discount retail chain.[1]

## Warnings on consumer distress (2025)

Speaking on Dollar General's fourth-quarter 2024 earnings call, Vasos said the discount chain had seen fewer shoppers because of their "worsened" financial situations and that those circumstances would not improve anytime soon.[1] "Our customers continue to report that their financial situation has worsened over the last year, as they have been negatively impacted by ongoing inflation," he said, adding that many customers reported having only enough money for basic essentials and that some had to sacrifice even on necessities.[1]

Looking ahead, Vasos stated: "As we enter 2025, we are not anticipating improvement in the macroenvironment, particularly for our core customer. In turn, we know our customers expect value and convenience more than ever."[1] He characterized those core customers as "always strained," meaning they could not handle any additional financial burdens.[1] His remarks accompanied results showing a one percent year-over-year decline in customer traffic and an approximately 49 percent collapse in operating profits.[1]
