# John P. Carlin

John P. Carlin is a former [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official named in a May 2026 referral filed by the [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]].[1] The complaint cites Carlin among several former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who later took positions with [[microsoft|Microsoft]] or with firms connected to the company, offering that pattern as evidence of a revolving-door relationship between federal cyber enforcement and the software vendor.[1]

The referral's central target was former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]], but it asked inspectors general to examine whether Carlin and other officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions.[1]
