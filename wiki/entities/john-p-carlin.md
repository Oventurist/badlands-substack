# John P. Carlin

John P. Carlin is a former senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official who later took a position with [[microsoft|Microsoft]]. In the Badlands corpus he appears in the May 2026 referral against [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] as an example of the revolving door between the Biden administration's cybersecurity leadership and Microsoft.

## Role in the Monaco referral

The [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]] cited Carlin among several former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who later took jobs with Microsoft or firms connected to the company, including former FBI cyber official [[bryan-vorndran|Bryan A. Vorndran]]. The foundation's referral asked DOJ inspectors general to investigate whether these officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions in connection with the alleged failure to bring enforcement action against Microsoft despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 tied to Russian and Chinese hackers.
