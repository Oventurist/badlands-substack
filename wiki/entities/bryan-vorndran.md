# Bryan Vorndran

Bryan A. Vorndran is a former [[fbi|FBI]] cyber official who later took a position with [[microsoft|Microsoft]]. In the Badlands corpus he appears in the May 2026 referral against [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] as an example of the revolving door between the Biden administration's cybersecurity establishment and Microsoft.

## Role in the Monaco referral

The [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]] cited Vorndran among several former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who later joined Microsoft or firms connected to the company, alongside former DOJ official [[john-p-carlin|John P. Carlin]]. The foundation asked DOJ inspectors general to investigate whether these officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions, alleging that the Biden DOJ shielded Microsoft from enforcement despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 tied to Russian and Chinese hackers that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.
