# Bryan A. Vorndran

Bryan A. Vorndran is a former [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] cyber official named in the May 2026 [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]] referral to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].[1] He is cited as one of several former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who subsequently took jobs with [[microsoft|Microsoft]] or firms connected to the company.[1]

The foundation asked departmental inspectors general to determine whether Vorndran and similarly situated officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions in connection with the government's handling of Microsoft cybersecurity matters.[1]
