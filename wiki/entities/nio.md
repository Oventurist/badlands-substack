# Nio

**Nio** is an electric vehicle manufacturer listed in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as one of the five EV companies he checked when evaluating whether the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's climate agenda had benefited electric-car makers.[1]

Carlson reported Nio down 85 percent between the January 20, 2021 inauguration and May 2023.[1] Together with [[tesla|Tesla]] (down 42 percent), [[nikola|Nikola]] (down 96 percent), [[rivian|Rivian]] (down 90 percent), and [[polestar|Polestar]] (down 65 percent), the result formed the empirical core of his contention that the stated purpose of energy policy — accelerating electrification — does not match its observable market effects.[1]
