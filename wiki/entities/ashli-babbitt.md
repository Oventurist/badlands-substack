# Ashli Babbitt

Ashli Babbitt was a protester who was shot and killed inside the United States Capitol during the events of January 6, 2021.[1] Badlands Media commentary has repeatedly cited her death as one of the central grievances of the "Truth Community" regarding that day, characterizing her as an unarmed woman who was murdered rather than lawfully stopped.[1]

## In Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of June 5, 2023, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] listed Babbitt's killing alongside other contested elements of the January 6 record — including the [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] escorting [[jacob-chansley|Jacob Chansley]] through the complex and the collapse of the official narrative around the death of [[brian-sicknick|Brian Sicknick]] — as evidence that the "insurrection" framing was a fabrication.[1] The same commentary argued that the police line was breached only because Capitol Police accidentally gassed themselves and peaceful protesters, and that federal law enforcement had deployed numerous agent provocateurs in MAGA attire that day.[1]

Babbitt's death is treated in that commentary as part of a broader indictment of officials who, in the author's words, treat the oath of office as "meaningless theatre," while January 6 defendants remain imprisoned and senior officials face no accountability.[1]
