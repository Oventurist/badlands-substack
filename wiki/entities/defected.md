# Defected

Defected is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Sundays at 9pm ET in the January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[just-human|Just Human]].[1] The two take a macro view of current events through the prism of narrative warfare, game theory and [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation strategy]], with the stated aim of helping viewers escape what they describe as a system of control and learn to program their own minds.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Defected on Sundays at 9pm ET, with Burning Bright and Just Human taking a macro view of current events through narrative warfare, game theory and Fifth-Generation strategy.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Defected in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
