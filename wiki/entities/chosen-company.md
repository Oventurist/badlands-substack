# Chosen Company

Chosen Company is one of the best-known units of international volunteer troops fighting on behalf of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] against Russian forces.[1] Led by an American, the unit became the subject of a July 2024 investigation by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] into the killing of surrendering and wounded Russian soldiers.[1]

## Allegations of killing surrendering Russians

According to Caspar Grosse, a German medic serving in the unit, in August of the preceding year a wounded and unarmed Russian soldier crawled through a nearly destroyed trench in eastern Ukraine seeking help from his captors.[1] Grosse said the soldier pleaded for medical attention in a mixture of broken English and Russian at dusk while a team member searched for bandages; a fellow soldier then hobbled over and fired into the Russian's torso, after which another soldier "just shot him in the head."[1]

Grosse said he confronted his commander over the episode and later spoke publicly to the Times after what he regarded as unwarranted killings continued — an unusual step for a serving soldier discussing battlefield conduct involving men he still considered friends.[1] While his recollection was the only available evidence of the trench killing itself, his accounts of other incidents were corroborated by contemporaneous notes, video footage and unit text messages reviewed by the newspaper.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media placed the allegations within a wider argument that the Ukrainian war effort had come to rely on criminality: the Ukrainian legislature's passage of a bill permitting conscription of prisoners, the recruitment of foreign mercenaries, and media glorification of units such as the [[third-separate-assault-brigade|3rd Separate Assault Brigade]].[1] The commentary predicted that "the Ukraine narrative is going to get much darker and more twisted as the lies unravel."[1]
