# Magura V5

The Magura V5 is a series of Ukrainian-made kamikaze naval drones, one of which was recovered off the Turkish Black Sea coast carrying 300 kilograms of explosives at the end of September 2025.[1]

## Trabzon recovery

A Ukrainian-made suicide drone laden with 300 kilograms of explosives was found drifting off the Black Sea coast near [[turkey|Türkiye's]] port city of Trabzon on Monday night, according to the Haberturk outlet.[1] Local fishermen caught the "object believed to be of foreign origin" in their nets and hauled it to port, the Trabzon Governor's Office said in a statement the following day.[1] Security measures were taken and elite Turkish Underwater Defense (SAS) diving teams were called in alongside other security services, while the office warned citizens not to interact with such objects.[1] Authorities determined the weapon to be a Magura V5 series kamikaze naval drone.[1]
