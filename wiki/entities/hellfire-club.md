# Hellfire Club

The Hellfire Club is referenced in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the emblem of an occultism embedded throughout the British imperial elite.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

Discussing [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]]'s painting of Saul and the Witch of Endor — in which King Saul, having expelled the oracles from his kingdom, secretly consults a witch and by believing her forecast of defeat and death ensures his own destruction — Ehret suggests one message West may be communicating concerns this well-known occultism within the British elite, which West and [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] understood viscerally.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) He observes that a republican painter treating such a theme would have been making a great many enemies among the intelligentsia of the Hellfire Club's devotees and their heirs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
