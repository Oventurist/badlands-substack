# OpenVAERS

OpenVAERS is a U.S. transparency project, founded by [[liz-willner|Liz Willner]], that republishes data from the federal Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System in a form the public can browse and search directly.[1]

## Role in the vaccine safety surveillance debate

OpenVAERS became a reference point in the controversy that followed the February 15, 2024 hearing of the House [[select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]], where [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] official [[peter-marks|Peter Marks]] and [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] official [[daniel-jernigan|Daniel Jernigan]] were asked by Rep. [[nicole-malliotakis|Nicole Malliotakis]] whether active long-term surveillance of early [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccine]] recipients was being conducted.[1] Willner subsequently told [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] that no long-term vaccine safety surveillance system exists in the United States.[1]

Badlands Media commentary credited OpenVAERS with making it possible to demonstrate that officials who dismissed vaccine-skeptical parents were not tracking the long-term effects of the products they mandated, and tied the episode to demands that legislators be publicly pressed for accountability if sworn testimony proved misleading.[1]
