# Kastle Systems

Kastle Systems is a building-security firm whose office card-swipe data is treated as the gold-standard measure of office occupancy trends in the United States.[1]

Its figures became a widely cited barometer of the post-Covid office slump during the 2023 [[commercial-real-estate-crisis|commercial real estate crisis]]. As of April 2023, Kastle data showed average office occupancy across Washington, D.C. at only 43%, still well short of pre-pandemic levels — a backdrop to [[brookfield|Brookfield]]'s default on a $161.4 million mortgage covering twelve D.C. office buildings.[1]
