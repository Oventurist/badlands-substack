# Edison Research

Edison Research is an American survey and election-data firm best known for conducting exit polling and supplying election-night vote reporting to major United States news organizations.[1] Its work also extends beyond the United States, and the firm conducted exit polling for the July 28, 2024 Venezuelan presidential election contested by [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and opposition candidate [[edmundo-gonzalez|Edmundo González]].[1]

## Venezuela 2024 exit poll

Edison's Venezuelan exit poll projected that González would take roughly 65% of the vote against approximately 31% for Maduro — a result sharply at odds with the official tally announced by the Venezuelan electoral authority, which awarded Maduro a third term with 51% against 44% for González.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated Edison's role in Venezuela as one of several "reruns" of American election controversies rather than as an isolated foreign story.[1] She observed that Edison was being "heralded as an authoritative source of reliable election night reporting" in the Venezuelan coverage, and pointed out that Edison's systems did not go down on Venezuelan election night as, in her account, they had during the 2020 United States election — a contrast she flagged with the hashtag #EdisonZero.[1] The broader argument advanced in the brief was that Venezuela's disputed vote reinforced a "50/50 nation" framing used as a predicate for narrow victory margins, and that the parallels between Venezuelan and American election administration — including the Venezuelan origins of [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] — were "impossible to ignore."[1]
