# Joaquin Arambula

Joaquin Arambula is a Democratic member of the California State Assembly representing a Fresno-area district, best known nationally as the author of Assembly Bill 1840, a 2024 measure to extend state first-time homebuyer assistance to illegal immigrants.[1]

## Assembly Bill 1840

Arambula introduced [[assembly-bill-1840|Assembly Bill 1840]] in mid-January 2024.[1] The bill would amend existing law so that undocumented applicants are eligible for the [[california-dream-for-all|California Dream for All Fund]], a program that provides interest-free down-payment loans to first-time homebuyers.[1] After several amendments, the measure advanced in the state legislature and was referred to the Committee on Housing and Community Development, where it awaited further action as of early March 2024.[1]

Arambula told the Fresno-based outlet GV Wire in January 2024 that he "wanted to ensure that qualified first-time homebuyers include undocumented applicants."[1] Speaking to the Los Angeles Times as the bill advanced, he argued that homeownership has historically been the principal route by which people accumulate generational wealth in the United States, and that "the social and economic benefits of homeownership should be available to everyone" — contending that it was wrong to exclude people from the California Dream for All Fund because of their immigration status.[1] Some lawmakers voiced opposition as the bill moved closer to passage.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] characterised the framing of the bill around families, compassion and generational wealth as "an incredible abuse of the American dream," arguing that the perversion of national ideals was itself the objective of the actors he identified with the Deep State.[1] He drew a pointed contrast between the bill's rhetoric and the lyrics of "California Dreamin'," in which the narrator enters a church and pretends to pray.[1]
