# ETH Zurich Center for Security Studies

The Center for Security Studies at ETH Zurich is a Swiss academic research institute focused on international and security policy.[1]

## 2024 report on Chinese hacking competitions

In June 2024, the Center published a report on China's "hack for hire" program which its authors said was "unlike anything we have ever seen."[1] The report described how Chinese spy agencies use talent competitions to recruit young hackers for cyberattacks on the United States, effectively weaponizing the country's young technophiles.[1]

According to the report, Beijing has banned homegrown talent from competing in international hacking contests — events in which participants compete to exploit vulnerabilities in widely used software for cash prizes — and created its own government-sanctioned domestic equivalents.[1] By forcing Chinese hackers to compete only in state-run events, the authorities give their intelligence services a pipeline of knowledge and manpower that can be exploited against the U.S. and its allies.[1] Cyber experts responding to the findings called on government and big business to do more.[1]

## Badlands framing

The report was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] alongside [[matthew-bunn|Matthew Bunn]]'s nuclear war warning, with commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] arguing that the pairing represented a coordinated narrative deployment intended to condition the public to expect Chinese attacks on U.S. energy and voting infrastructure ahead of the November 2024 election.[1]
