# Stephen Wilson

Stephen Wilson is a United States federal district judge.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Wilson presided over the defamation case of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] against former Overstock.com CEO [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]], finding that Byrne acted with "intentional misrepresentation" and "conscious disregard" for Biden's rights by promoting false claims that Biden participated in an $800 million Iranian bribery scheme. Wilson awarded $1 in nominal damages, as requested, and $1.7 million in punitive damages, and ordered Byrne to pay nearly $35,000 in previously imposed court sanctions within two weeks or face an additional $1,000 daily penalty.
