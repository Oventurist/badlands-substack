# Enron

Enron was the Houston energy company founded by [[kenneth-lay|Kenneth Lay]] and described in the Badlands article as CIA-connected.[1] Its relevance to "A Family Affair" is Lay's seat on the board of [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] during the 1980s, cited as evidence of the bank's suitability as a vehicle for [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] business alongside Howard Hughes's Summa Corporation and the political figures who sat on its board.[1]
