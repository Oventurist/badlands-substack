# Jordan Sather

**Jordan Sather** is an independent researcher and commentator active on X and Substack, known within the Badlands orbit for tracking government disclosure material on unidentified anomalous phenomena.[1]

## UAP file coverage (2026)

Following the May 8, 2026 launch of the [[pursue|PURSUE]] portal by the [[department-of-war|Department of War]], Sather opened a running thread on X collecting "interesting data points" found within the Trump administration's UFO file releases.[1] He noted that at the time only war.gov/ufo was live while aliens.gov had not yet come up, and said he would update the thread through the weekend and as further files were uploaded.[1] The post, dated May 8, 2026, drew roughly 7,530 views, 240 likes and 62 reposts.[1] The Badlands Brief pointed readers to the thread as its recommended follow-up on the release.[1]

## Planned rescheduling executive order (December 2025)

In December 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was reported to be preparing to push the federal government to dramatically loosen restrictions on marijuana, reducing oversight of the plant and its derivatives to the same level as some common prescription painkillers and other drugs, according to six people familiar with the discussions.[7] Trump discussed the plan with House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] during a Wednesday phone call from the Oval Office, and was expected to ease access through an executive order directing federal agencies to pursue reclassification.[7]

The move would not legalize or decriminalize marijuana, but it would ease barriers to research and improve the finances of legal cannabis businesses.[7] Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] characterized the plan as a shift from Schedule I to Schedule III and predicted it would "blow so many left wing AND right wing minds."[7]
