# Jonathan Gilliam

Jonathan Gilliam is a former [[fbi|FBI]] agent and Navy SEAL who appears as a commentator on law enforcement and national security matters.[1]

## Comments on Russiagate prosecutions

Speaking on the "Just the News, No Noise" television show in August 2025, Gilliam said prosecutors may be able to build a criminal subversion or racketeering case against the government officials who carried out the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] scandal.[1] "I think also what we have to start looking at is things like subversion," he said. "You know, subversion, like treason and insurrection...these are all criminal actions that there's law for."[1]

His remarks followed reporting by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] that newly declassified memos showed federal prosecutors had gathered evidence from [[james-comey|James Comey]]'s top lieutenants — ex-general counsel [[james-baker|James Baker]] and ex-chief of staff James Rybicki — indicating that the former FBI director authorized the leak of classified information to reporters just before the 2016 election, though prosecutors declined to bring charges.[1] Those passages had been redacted by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in copies sent to [[congress|Congress]] until Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] intervened to remove the redactions and dispatch unredacted versions to the House and Senate Judiciary committees; the documents themselves had been discovered by FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[1]
