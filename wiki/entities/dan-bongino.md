# Dan Bongino

Dan Bongino is an American commentator and former law enforcement officer who served as Deputy Director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] during the second Trump administration. He appears in the Badlands Media corpus primarily in connection with transparency disputes at the Bureau.

## Role in the FBI records litigation

The March 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, Alternative Annexations & New(ish) Regime Change, reports that a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the FBI seeks, among other things, internal Bureau communications involving Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] and former Deputy Director Bongino regarding the discovery of as many as 1.9 million pages of records held in a [[sensitive-compartmented-information-facility|Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility]] at FBI headquarters, along with directives on how those materials were handled. The reference to Bongino as *former* Deputy Director places his departure from the post before the March 2026 filing. The litigation, filed in August 2025 after a June FOIA request went unfulfilled, is presented in the source as a test of whether senior Bureau leadership acted to disclose or to delay disclosure of the cache.
