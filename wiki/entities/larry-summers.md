# Larry Summers

Larry Summers is a former United States Treasury Secretary whose name was raised by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in July 2025 in connection with the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] sex-trafficking case.[1]

## Named in Trump's Epstein deflection (July 2025)

Speaking to reporters on Friday, July 25, 2025, as he departed for a trip to Scotland, Trump said that those asking about the late financier's case should redirect their attention elsewhere.[1] "You ought to be speaking about Larry Summers. You ought to be speaking about some of his friends that are hedge fund guys," Trump said. "They're all over the place. You ought to be speaking about [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]."[1]

Trump went on to accuse Clinton of having visited Epstein's private island, which the financier used as a home base for his trafficking operations, and said that he himself had never visited the island; Clinton has likewise denied ever visiting.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the exchange as part of an "accelerated Ouroboros pattern" in which the original scandals that launched the information war return with a vengeance, arguing that the enemy clutches at scandals like [[epstein-files|Epstein]] to smear Trump while sacrificing a few of its own — and that Trump was accelerating rather than fearing the episode.[1]

## Withdrawal from public commitments (November 2025)

On November 17, 2025, Summers announced plans to step back from public commitments amid fallout from the release of emails between him and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[2] The former Clinton and Obama administration official had exchanged emails and text messages with the financier well after Epstein's 2008 guilty plea in Florida for sex crimes, continuing up until Epstein's July 2019 arrest on federal child sex trafficking charges; the correspondence appeared in a trove of documents released by Republicans on the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[2]

"I am deeply ashamed of my actions and recognize the pain they have caused," Summers said in a statement, adding, "I take full responsibility for my misguided decision to continue communicating with Mr. Epstein."[2] He described stepping away as "one part of my broader effort to rebuild trust and repair relationships with the people closest to me," while saying that at age 70 he would continue to fulfill his teaching obligations at [[harvard-university|Harvard]].[2]
