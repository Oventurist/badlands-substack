# Techno Fog

Techno Fog is the pen name of an attorney and political commentator who publishes legal analysis at the Substack newsletter The Reactionary, and whose work has been cited in coverage of the [[durham-report|Durham Report]] and the wider [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] story.[1]

## Analysis of the Durham Report

In May 2023, Techno Fog published "a deeper dive into the Durham report" at The Reactionary, portions of which were reproduced in outlets including [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1] He observed that [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s scope of review included the [[fbi|FBI]] investigations "directed" at the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] campaign, and inferred that the purpose of that portion of the review was to assess and compare the favorable treatment Clinton received against the targeting of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

His account described two of the three concurrent [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]]-related investigations in detail. In the first, an FBI confidential human source reported that a foreign government was sending a person "to contribute to Clinton's anticipated presidential campaign, as a way to gain influence with Clinton should she win the presidency"; an FBI field office sought a [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|FISA]] warrant against the foreign contributor, but headquarters ignored the request for four months out of caution because Clinton was "involved," with one agent quoted saying colleagues were "pretty 'tippy-toeing' around HRC because there was a chance she would be the next president."[1] The FISA was approved only on the condition that the bureau provide Clinton with defensive briefings.[1]

In the second, the same source reported in November 2015 that another foreign government sought to contribute to the Clinton campaign "in exchange for the protection of [that country's] interests should Clinton become President," and subsequently made a $2,700 donation on behalf of a foreign insider in violation of the federal ban on contributions by foreign nationals.[1] The source told their handling agent that the campaign "were fully aware from the start" of the arrangement.[1]
