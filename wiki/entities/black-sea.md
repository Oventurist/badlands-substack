# Black Sea

The **Black Sea** appears in the Badlands Media series "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]]" as a location of alleged occult significance.[1] In "Creation Myth Clues," the author recalls that an earlier installment of the series proposed that a literal portal to hell may be located near — or even within — the Black Sea, in the same region where the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult is said to have originated.[1]

The article notes that the presence of demons is traditionally associated with the smell of sulphur, and that the Black Sea contains an unusually high concentration of sulphur, which makes it a deadly body of water for most living creatures.[1] Combining this with the [[moho-discontinuity|Moho Discontinuity]] — the claim that vast caverns exist tens of kilometers beneath the surface everywhere on Earth — the author suggests that something subterranean may have "surfaced" in the Black Sea region, which he offers as an explanation for why that part of the world produced the wolf cult.[1]

The author further observes that the original homeland of the Koryos, in the territory of modern [[ukraine|Ukraine]], is also described by many commentators as a "hotbed" of cabal activity in the present day, and invites readers to draw their own conclusions.[1]
