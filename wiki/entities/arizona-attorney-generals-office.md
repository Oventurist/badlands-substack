# Arizona Attorney General's Office

The Arizona Attorney General's Office is the state's chief legal and law-enforcement agency, headed during the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] by [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]], who had served as Attorney General since 2015.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Election Integrity Unit

The office maintains an election-integrity function, referred to in correspondence simply as "the Unit." In August 2019 the office hired [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] as an assistant attorney general focused on election integrity; a *Talking Points Memo* article of August 22, 2019, headlined "GOP Voter Fraud Hawk Hired For Arizona AG's New 'Elections Integrity' Unit," described her as a Tea Party-linked voter-fraud activist and former GOP mayoral candidate who had worked for the group Verify the Vote.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

On November 19, 2022, the Unit, through Wright, sent a four-page letter to [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]] of the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]] stating that hundreds of citizen complaints, including first-hand witness accounts, had raised concerns regarding Maricopa County's lawful compliance with Arizona election law.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) The letter demanded a comprehensive accounting of [[ballot-on-demand-printers|ballot-on-demand printer]] configuration failures, Election Day "check-out" procedures, and the handling of [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3" ballots]], and required a response on or before the county submitted its official canvass to the Secretary of State, which under A.R.S. § 16-642(A) had to occur by November 28, 2022.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) The full letter was published on the office's website, azag.gov.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Criticism

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], contrasted the seriousness of the Unit's letter with Brnovich's own public conduct, arguing that the Attorney General had done nothing to fight election fraud after 2020 despite bravado on social media, and adding that she would not "hold working for Brnovich against Ms. Wright."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She also noted that under Article V, Section 6 of the Constitution of Arizona, the line of succession to the governorship runs to the Secretary of State first and only then to the Attorney General, questioning why Brnovich rather than [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] was described as acting governor when Governor [[doug-ducey|Doug Ducey]] left the state.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
