# Cori Bush

Cori Bush is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Missouri who lost her 2024 primary following record outside spending by pro-Israel political groups.[1]

## 2024 primary defeat

Pro-Israel super PACs, principally the super PAC of the [[american-israel-public-affairs-committee|American Israel Public Affairs Committee]], spent a combined $25 million against Bush and Representative [[jamaal-bowman|Jamaal Bowman]], making their races the two most expensive House primaries ever conducted.[1] The spending was driven by the two members' criticism of Israel during the war with [[hamas]].[1] Bush's defeat allowed Wesley Bell to advance in a safe blue Missouri district in her place.[1]

Bush's loss, coming after Bowman's, convinced progressive Democrats that the strategy was repeatable and that they had no organized financial counterweight ahead of the 2026 cycle.[1] Senator [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] said of AIPAC, "I think they smell success," adding that the group's intimidating presence extended over every member of [[congress]] and that it "bothers me that there hasn't been more outrage."[1]
