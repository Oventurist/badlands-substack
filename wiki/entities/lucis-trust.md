# Lucis Trust

**Lucis Trust** is an esoteric publishing and educational organization, originally incorporated as the Lucifer Publishing Company, that holds consultative status with United Nations bodies.[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* describes Lucis Trust as a UN "emissary" of the wider control architecture it maps, feeding spiritual doctrine into global compacts.[1] The essay characterizes the organization's teaching as an inversion that "presents the light as ascension" while reversing its meaning, summarizing the resulting doctrine as "external savior, internal surrender, bureaucratic enlightenment."[1]

Lucis Trust appears in the essay alongside claims about [[luciferian-rites|Luciferian rites]], black-cube and Saturnian symbolism, and the [[sabbateanism|Sabbatean-Frankist]] theology of redemption through inversion — all treated as a single current of inverted spirituality surfacing in United Nations sustainability language and [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] "stewardship" doctrine.[1] The essay presents this as a disguised spiritual war fronted by bureaucrats rather than an overt religious movement.[1]

These characterizations reflect the essay's interpretive frame; Lucis Trust describes itself as a nonprofit promoting the writings of Alice Bailey and the practice of world service.[1]
