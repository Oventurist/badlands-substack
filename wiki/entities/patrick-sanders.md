# Patrick Sanders

General Sir Patrick Sanders is the head of the British Army, and a prominent voice in the United Kingdom's debate over whether its population should be prepared for the possibility of major war.[1]

## "Prewar generation" remarks

In January 2024, Sanders spoke about the need for the UK's "prewar generation" to prepare for the possibility of future conflict, adding that this would require a "whole of nation undertaking."[1] His comments were part of pressure from leading figures in the UK military for young people to be readied for war.[1]

## Relation to the national service debate

That pressure formed the backdrop to [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]]'s May 2024 pledge — his first major policy announcement of the general election campaign — to reintroduce mandatory national service for 18-year-olds, requiring either a 12-month placement with the armed forces or community work.[1] National service had been abolished in Britain in 1960.[1]
