# Raland J. Brunson

Raland J. Brunson is a Utah activist who filed an unusual lawsuit against sitting justices of the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] over their refusal to hear a challenge to the results of the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## The lawsuit

Brunson, representing himself, sued over "breach of contract, intentional infliction of emotional distress, fraud and civil conspiracy," all of which he framed as arising under the justices' oath of office.[1] His filing argued that the justices "have taken the oath of office, and after having done so have violated their oath of office by giving aid and comfort to enemies of the Constitution, which is an act of treason, fraud and a breach of contract."[1] He also argued that the justices were not immune from the litigation, contrary to a lower court's ruling.[1] In his petition he wrote that "the issues presented in this case are very sensitive, serious and wide spread and affects all of America."[1]

## Recusals and disposition

In an unsigned order in late May 2024, the Court's three Democratic appointees — Justices [[sonia-sotomayor|Sonia Sotomayor]], [[elena-kagan|Elena Kagan]] and [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] — recused themselves, citing recusal requirements applicable because they were named parties in the case.[1] The high court ultimately rejected the long-shot suit.[1] The episode was reported in Badlands Media's news brief as part of a broader pattern of litigation surrounding [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
