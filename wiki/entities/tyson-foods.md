# Tyson Foods

Tyson Foods is an American multinational food company and one of the four largest beef packers in the United States, also holding a leading position in poultry and pork processing.

## Antitrust investigation

According to the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, Tyson Foods is named alongside [[jbs|JBS]], [[cargill|Cargill]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]] in an intensified antitrust investigation announced by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[united-states-department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]], citing price manipulation, foreign ownership risks, and supply chain issues. Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] disclosed a review of more than three million documents and hundreds of rancher interviews, and said the department would announce a "historic settlement" directly affecting the prices of proteins such as chicken, pork, and turkey. The enforcement push follows an executive order issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in November 2025 directing action against anti-competitive behavior in the food supply market.
