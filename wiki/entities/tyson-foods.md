# Tyson Foods

Tyson Foods is an American food processing company and one of the "Big Four" US beef packers.[1]

## 2026 antitrust investigation

Tyson was named with [[jbs|JBS]], [[cargill|Cargill]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]] in the [[beef-packer-antitrust-investigation-2026|antitrust investigation]] announced in May 2026 by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and [[department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]] over price manipulation, foreign ownership risks, and supply chain issues.[1] The probe followed President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s November 2025 directive and preceded an announced settlement affecting prices of proteins such as chicken, pork, and turkey.[1]

## Entry into the insect protein market (2023)

In October 2023, Tyson Foods announced its entry into the insect protein market through a partnership with the Netherlands-based insect food manufacturer [[protix|Protix]], consisting of a cash investment in Protix plus an agreement to build a large insect-protein facility.[2] Tyson stated that the to-be-built U.S. facility would house an enclosed system supporting all aspects of insect protein production, including the breeding, incubating and hatching of insect larvae, with processed larvae used as ingredients for aquaculture and pet food and potentially within livestock and plant feed.[2]

Badlands coverage emphasized that Protix had been selected in 2015 as one of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s Technology Pioneers and produces roughly 14,000 metric tons of insect food per year under the stated rationale of fighting climate change.[2] Contributor Ashe in America noted that Tyson holds about a 14% share of the global protein market, arguing that a corporation of that scale partnering on bug protein manufacturing in the United States refutes mainstream characterizations — including a 2023 [[npr|NPR]] podcast episode — of the "eat the bugs" critique as a racist conspiracy theory, particularly given NPR's own favorable coverage of insect eating in 2016, 2017 and 2018.[2]
