# Federal Housing Finance Agency

The Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) is the United States government agency that regulates the housing finance system, appearing in the March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief as the institution through which Director [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] referred New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for potential criminal prosecution.

## Referral of Letitia James (March 2026)

The Brief reports that Pulte again referred James to the DOJ, citing allegations that she may have provided false information on a homeowner's insurance application. Pulte sent letters Wednesday to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois requesting they "authenticate and investigate" the claims.

According to the letters, one application submitted to a Florida-based insurer indicated James' Norfolk, Virginia property would remain unoccupied for five months each year. Testimony referenced in the referral states her niece, Nakia Thompson, lived in the home for several years, contradicting the claim that the residence was vacant for extended periods. A separate filing cited in the Illinois referral shows James allegedly indicated the home would be occupied by a single adult with no children, while public reporting referenced by Pulte states the property was occupied by her niece and three children.

The referral cites analysis from attorney [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]], who pointed to court filings he says show discrepancies in James' insurance representations.
