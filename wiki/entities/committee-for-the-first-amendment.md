# Committee for the First Amendment

The Committee for the First Amendment is one of the advocacy groups that organized the April 13, 2026 open letter opposing Paramount's $111 billion acquisition of [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]].[1] Working alongside the Future Film Coalition, it gathered signatures from more than 1,000 writers, actors, and directors — including Bryan Cranston, Joaquin Phoenix, Tiffany Haddish, Lily Gladstone, Denis Villeneuve, and J.J. Abrams.[1] The letter warned that the deal would accelerate industry consolidation, producing fewer films, narrower story diversity, reduced job opportunities for creators, higher production costs, and less choice for global audiences.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the campaign as revealing more than it concealed, arguing that [[hollywood|Hollywood]] does not fear consolidation in the abstract but fears this particular consolidation and the Gulf sovereign capital behind it.[1] See [[paramount-warner-bros-discovery-merger|the merger]] for the transaction itself.[1]
