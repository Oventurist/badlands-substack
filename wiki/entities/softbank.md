# SoftBank

SoftBank is the Japanese investment conglomerate identified in "A New Hollywood" as one of the three corporate partners — with [[oracle|Oracle]] and [[openai|OpenAI]] — in [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]], the $500 billion American artificial intelligence infrastructure plan announced by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites the partnership mainly to establish the scale of Trump's AI push and the centrality of [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] within it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## $100 billion U.S. investment pledge (2024)

On Monday, December 16, 2024, SoftBank CEO [[masayoshi-son|Masayoshi Son]] announced a $100 billion investment in the United States over four years during a visit to President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Mar-a-Lago residence in Palm Beach, Florida.[2] The joint announcement promised the creation of at least 100,000 jobs focused on artificial intelligence and related infrastructure, with the money deployed before the end of Trump's term.[2] Son said his "confidence level to the economy of the United States has tremendously increased with his victory," adding, "President Trump is a double down president. I'm going to have to double down."[2] Trump called the pledge "a monumental demonstration confidence in America's future" that would help ensure artificial intelligence and emerging technologies are grown in the USA.[2]

The commitment echoed a similar 2016 announcement in which SoftBank agreed to invest $50 billion in the U.S. with the aim of creating 50,000 jobs.[2] Funding could come from various SoftBank-controlled sources including the Vision Fund, capital projects, or chipmaker Arm Holdings, of which the firm is majority owner; some of the money was not newly raised and could include previously announced commitments such as SoftBank's $1.5 billion investment in [[openai|OpenAI]].[2] Badlands noted that Son created the $100 billion SoftBank Vision Fund in 2017 in tandem with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], using a $45 billion stake from the Saudi [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]].[2]
