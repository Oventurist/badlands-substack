# House Committee on Oversight and Accountability

The Committee on Oversight and Accountability is the principal investigative committee of the United States House of Representatives, with broad jurisdiction to examine the conduct of federal agencies.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

The committee appears in the Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]] as the forum in which [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] director [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]] testified about the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. According to the article, the hearing took place the day after [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] withdrew from the presidential race, and Cheatle resigned the day after appearing.

The essay highlights that Cheatle used the hearing to note the recent death of Representative [[sheila-jackson-lee|Sheila Jackson Lee]] and her involvement "in oversight of the Secret Service" — a remark the author, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], treats as significant enough to build his closing set of questions around. The committee thus functions in the article as the public venue where the threads of the Butler failure, congressional oversight, and the July 2024 political upheaval briefly intersected on the record.
