# Rockefeller Institute

The **Rockefeller Institute** was the medical research body endowed by [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] and a recurring institution in Badlands Media's account of how American medicine was reorganized in the twentieth century.[1]

[[simon-flexner|Simon Flexner]] sat on its board of directors and from that position proposed his brother [[abraham-flexner|Abraham Flexner]] for the medical-school survey that became the [[flexner-report|Flexner Report]].[1]

The institute also appears in the article's eugenics narrative. Dr. [[alexis-carrel|Alexis Carrel]] of the Rockefeller Institute, a Nobel Prize winner, publicly applauded [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] for advocating the mass murder of mental patients and prisoners and himself advocated the use of poison gas to dispose of "useless eaters."[1] In a 1969 talk recounted by Dr. [[lawrence-dunegan|Lawrence Dunegan]], Dr. [[richard-day|Richard Day]] claimed that cures for nearly all cancers had been developed but were being withheld at the Rockefeller Institute so that populations would not increase.[1]
