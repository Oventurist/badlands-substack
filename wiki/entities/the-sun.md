# The Sun

The Sun is a British tabloid newspaper whose American edition, The U.S. Sun, publishes celebrity, crime and political coverage. Badlands Media cited the outlet in September 2023 for an exclusive interview with [[errol-musk|Errol Musk]].[1]

## Errol Musk interview

The U.S. Sun reported that Errol Musk, a retired electromechanical engineer then aged 77, feared that his son [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] — described as the world's richest man — could be assassinated for going up against the US government.[1] The paper noted that Musk, then 52, was weathering legal attacks from the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and negative press, and that [[joe-biden|President Joe Biden]] had suggested in July that "there's a lot of ways" of investigating the tech tycoon.[1] Errol Musk told the outlet he had previously warned his son to increase his security and that Elon shared the same fear; the paper also recorded Elon Musk's earlier joke that he could "die in mysterious circumstances."[1]
