# Alan Garber

Alan Garber is the president of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]].[1]

## 2025 confrontation with the Trump administration

In April 2025, Garber led Harvard's formal rejection of demands from the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration that the university alter its teaching, admissions, and hiring practices.[1] Harvard argued that the administration's directive violated its constitutional rights and academic freedom, with Garber asserting that no government can dictate what private universities teach or whom they admit and hire.[1]

Hours after the rejection, the administration announced it was freezing $2.2 billion in grants to Harvard over concerns about antisemitism on campus, placing nearly $9 billion in total federal funding at risk.[1] The administration's proposed conditions included eliminating DEI initiatives, screening international students for support of terrorism and antisemitism, reporting foreign students for misconduct, and monitoring ideological diversity.[1]

Badlands commentary disputed Garber's framing, arguing that the administration was not dictating curriculum or hiring but rather attaching conditions to federal funding, and characterized his response as an expression of institutional entitlement.[1] The [[general-services-administration|General Services Administration]] and the Department of Education said Harvard's statement "reinforces the troubling entitlement mindset that is endemic in our nation's most prestigious universities and colleges — that federal investment does not come with the responsibility to uphold civil rights laws."[1]
