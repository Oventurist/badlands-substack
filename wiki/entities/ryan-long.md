# Ryan Long

**Ryan Long** is a comedian whose satirical video sketches are circulated as media criticism on the platform X.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Cited sketch

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] cites a Long skit as an apt demonstration of the essay's thesis that audiences have lost track of where they are supposed to direct their outrage over the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]], which the author reads as evidence of a breakdown in [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] influence.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The essay pairs the sketch with a comic strip by the cartoonist Stonetoss making a similar point.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)
