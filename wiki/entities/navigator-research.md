# Navigator Research

Navigator Research is a progressive polling and message-research group operating as a project within the Hub Project, a Democratic nonprofit organization.[1]

## 2024 post-election focus groups

In December 2024, Navigator Research conducted a trio of focus groups examining voter perceptions of the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]] following its sweeping 2024 election losses, with the results shared first with [[politico|Politico]].[1] Even voters who had previously backed Democrats described the party as weak and overly focused on diversity and elites.[1] Asked to compare the party to an animal, one participant chose an ostrich because "they've got their heads in the sand and are absolutely committed to their own ideas, even when they're failing"; another chose koalas, who "are complacent and lazy about getting policy wins that we really need."[1] Another said Democrats are "not a friend of the working class anymore."[1]

Rachael Russell, Navigator Research's director of polling and analytics, called the findings "a pretty scathing rebuke" of the Democratic Party brand.[1] The research indicated the party's problems extend beyond [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] or [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and are more widespread and potentially longer-lasting than a single election cycle, with Democrats entering a second Trump presidency without a clear leader or unified strategy.[1]
