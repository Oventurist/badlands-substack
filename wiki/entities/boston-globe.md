# Boston Globe

The Boston Globe is a major American daily newspaper based in Boston, Massachusetts.[1] It is best known internationally for its Spotlight team's Pulitzer Prize–winning investigation into the cover-up of child molestation within the Catholic Church, an inquiry led by then-editor [[marty-baron|Marty Baron]] before he moved to [[washington-post|The Washington Post]].[1]

## Coverage of the 2024 non-endorsement controversy

On October 25, 2024, the Globe reported Baron's public denunciation of his former paper's decision to withhold a presidential endorsement, quoting his description of the move as "cowardice, with democracy as its casualty" and "disturbing spinelessness at an institution famed for courage."[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the Globe's report as part of his survey of establishment media reaction to the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
