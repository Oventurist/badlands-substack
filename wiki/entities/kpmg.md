# KPMG

**KPMG** is a multinational accounting and professional-services firm whose New York offices are located at 345 Park Avenue in Midtown Manhattan.[1]

## 345 Park Avenue shooting

KPMG was named among the principal tenants of the tower — alongside [[blackstone|Blackstone]] and the [[national-football-league|NFL]] — in reporting on the [[345-park-avenue-shooting|345 Park Avenue shooting]] of July 28, 2025, which was carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1] The gunman, [[shane-tamura|Shane Tamura]], was found dead on the 33rd floor of the building, a floor housing offices of the building's owner, Rudin.[1]
