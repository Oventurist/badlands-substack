# Jena Griswold

Jena Griswold is the Secretary of State of Colorado and the state's chief election official. She is a recurring antagonist in election-integrity commentary within the Badlands Media corpus.

## The 2026 overseas voting lawsuit

According to [[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]], Griswold was named as defendant in a Denver District Court suit brought by the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] along with Douglas County Clerk [[sheri-davis|Sheri Davis]] and Representative Jeff Crank. The action targets guidance issued by her office interpreting the [[uniformed-and-overseas-citizens-absentee-voting-act|Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act]], under which certain U.S. citizens living abroad may register and vote in Colorado despite never having lived in the state, so long as a parent, guardian, spouse or domestic partner previously resided there. The plaintiffs argue the guidance conflicts with Colorado's constitutional residency requirements and seek both to void the policy and to strike affected voters from the rolls.

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], a Colorado resident, writes that she is "always supportive of suing Jena Griswold" but doubts this particular suit because of the plaintiffs bringing it and its timing on the eve of the Colorado primary election day.
