# Autodefensas Unidas de Colombia

The **Autodefensas Unidas de Colombia** (AUC), or United Self-Defense Forces of Colombia, was a Colombian paramilitary group designated a terrorist organization by the United States and known for its violence in the South American country.[1] It was disbanded in 2006.[1]

## Chiquita funding case

The AUC was at the center of a civil lawsuit brought in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida against [[chiquita-brands-international|Chiquita Brands International]], which alleged that the banana company should be held responsible for the murders of eight individuals by the group.[1] On June 10, 2024, a jury found Chiquita liable and ordered it to pay the victims' families $38.3 million.[1]

The verdict followed Chiquita's 2007 guilty plea to making more than 100 payments to the AUC totaling more than $1.7 million, though a source told the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] that the payments had been made under threat of violence.[1] The jury concluded that Chiquita "knowingly provided substantial assistance to the AUC to a degree sufficient to create a foreseeable risk of harm to others."[1]
