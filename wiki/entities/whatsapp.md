# WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a widely used encrypted messaging service owned by [[meta-platforms|Meta]].[1]

## Lawsuit against NSO Group

Since late 2019 WhatsApp has pursued a lawsuit in the United States against the Israeli firm [[nso-group|NSO Group]], alleging that the company exploited a vulnerability in the messaging service to target more than 1,400 of its users in 20 countries over a two-week period.[1] NSO has denied the allegations.[1]

The litigation became a high-stakes matter for the Israeli state because it threatened to expose closely guarded secrets about [[pegasus|Pegasus]], NSO's flagship hacking tool.[1] Leaked files reported in July 2024 suggested that Israeli officials seized Pegasus-related documents from NSO in an unusual legal manoeuvre designed to prevent the company from complying with WhatsApp's disclosure demands in the U.S. court, on the grounds that disclosure would cause "serious diplomatic and security damage" to [[israel|Israel]].[1] One of NSO's lawyers in the case was former U.S. Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]], who reportedly asked whether the Israeli government would "come to the rescue" in the fight with WhatsApp.[1]
