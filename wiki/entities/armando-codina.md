# Armando Codina

Armando Codina is a Coral Gables Cuban-American real estate investor, famous and highly successful in the Miami area, and one of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]]'s earliest and largest financial supporters in his run for president.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

So tight was he with the president that he gave [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] more than his first job in Florida: he put Bush's name on the company and gave him 40 percent of its profits, despite Jeb having no real estate experience.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article contrasts this with Hunter Biden's reported ten percent cut of deals for his father, calling Biden an amateur by comparison — Jeb contributed nothing and received 40 percent plus the naming.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Sweetheart deals followed. In 1984 Bush put just $1,000 into an office building called Museum Tower and by 1990 sold out for about $346,000, with similar deals after; Codina made them possible.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The Codina Bush firm was later hired by IBM Corp. to sell its Boca Raton office park, sold in early 1997 to a group including [[mark-guzzetta|Mark Guzzetta]]; Codina associate Hank Klein, who worked the deal, called it "a trophy property . . . one of the largest we've ever done," while the firm declined to disclose its commission.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
