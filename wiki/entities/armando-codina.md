# Armando Codina

Armando Codina is a Coral Gables Cuban-American real-estate investor and one of the most successful developers in the Miami area. He was among [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]]'s earliest and largest financial supporters in his presidential run.[1]

So close was he to Bush that he gave [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] more than his first job in Florida: he put the Bush name on the company and granted him 40 percent of its profits, despite Jeb having no real-estate experience and contributing no capital.[1] The article contrasts this with the scrutiny of Hunter Biden's reputed ten percent cut.[1]

Codina made a series of sweetheart deals possible. In 1984 Bush put just $1,000 into an office building called Museum Tower and by 1990 sold out for about $346,000, with similar deals following.[1] The Codina Bush firm was later hired by IBM Corp. to sell its Boca Raton office park, a transaction that ended with [[mark-guzzetta|Mark Guzzetta]]'s group acquiring the property far below assessed value.[1]
