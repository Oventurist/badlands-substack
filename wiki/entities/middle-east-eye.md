# Middle East Eye

Middle East Eye (MEE) is a news outlet covering the politics, conflicts and diplomacy of the Middle East.[1]

## Reporting on Iran–US backchannel diplomacy

In January 2024, Middle East Eye reported, on the basis of three sources in Iran, that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] had assumed a new role as intermediary between [[iran|Iran]] and the United States nine months after Riyadh and Tehran restored diplomatic ties. According to MEE, high-ranking Saudi officials were instrumental in relaying messages between the two governments and in reducing tensions arising from [[israel|Israel]]'s war on Gaza.[1]

The outlet traced the start of the process to November 2023, when Iranian Foreign Minister [[hossein-amirabdollahian|Hossein Amirabdollahian]] attended an emergency summit in Riyadh and carried a message for Washington that Saudi officials subsequently delivered.[1]

Badlands Media cited the MEE report as supporting evidence for its reading of a realigning, multipolar diplomatic landscape.[1]
