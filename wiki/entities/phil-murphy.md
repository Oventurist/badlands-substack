# Phil Murphy

Phil Murphy is the Democratic Governor of New Jersey, whose executive orders imposing COVID-19 restrictions made him a central antagonist for opponents of pandemic-era lockdown policy.[1]

## Atilis Gym prosecution

Murphy's administration pursued more than 80 charges against [[ian-smith|Ian Smith]] and Frank Trumbetti, co-owners of Atilis Gym in Bellmawr, who kept their gym open in defiance of the state's COVID-19 restrictions. The charges included violations of a governor's order, public nuisance, disturbing the peace, and operating without a license.[1]

In May 2024 all of the charges were dropped with prejudice, and Smith characterized the outcome as a victory that would allow further pushback against what he called "the treasonous actions of Phil Murphy and his lackies [sic]."[1] The dismissal was reported as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of May 23, 2024.[1]

## Repeal of teacher basic skills testing

Murphy enacted Act 1669 in June 2024 as part of New Jersey's 2025 budget, a measure billed as a response to a teacher shortage that took effect on January 1, 2025.[2] Under the [[new-jersey-basic-skills-test-repeal|repeal]], candidates for an instructional certificate no longer need to pass the Praxis Core Test in reading, writing, and math administered by the state's Commissioner of Education.[2] Democratic Senator Jim Beach, the bill's sponsor, defended it when the chamber cleared the measure 34-2 in May 2024, saying "we need more teachers" and that "this is the best way to get them."[2]
