# Cooper Jo Frederick

Cooper Jo Frederick is a Fort Collins, Colorado resident indicted by a federal grand jury in connection with a fire at a [[tesla|Tesla]] Service Center in Loveland, Colorado.[1]

## Charges

The United States Attorney's Office for the District of Colorado announced that Frederick was indicted on one count of Malicious Destruction and Attempted Destruction of Property by Fire and one count of Possession of an Unregistered Destructive Device; investigators determined the Loveland fire had been caused by an incendiary device.[1] Frederick was arrested on Friday, March 27, 2025, in Frisco, Texas.[1] The charges in the indictment are allegations, and the defendant is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty beyond a reasonable doubt.[1]

## Investigation

The investigation was handled by the Denver Field Office of the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives and the Loveland Police Department, with assistance from the ATF's Dallas Field Office, the [[fbi|FBI]] Dallas Field Office, and the Frisco, Texas Police Department.[1] The prosecution was handled by the Violent Crimes and Immigration Enforcement Section of the U.S. Attorney's Office, and the case was brought as part of [[operation-take-back-america|Operation Take Back America]].[1]
