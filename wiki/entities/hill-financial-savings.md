# Hill Financial Savings

Hill Financial Savings of Red Hill, Pennsylvania was a thrift that put up $80 million to seal the Florida land deal financed alongside [[robert-corson|Robert Corson]]'s [[vision-banc-savings|Vision Banc Savings]], and lost $40 million on it.[1]

Journalist Pete Brewton reported that the thrift, which has since gone bankrupt, had ties to the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and organised crime, as did Miami lawyer [[lawrence-freeman|Lawrence Freeman]], the convicted drug-money launderer who helped assemble the deal.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] describes Hill Financial as a CIA thrift whose partnership with Corson's institution ended with both going belly up — another instance of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]] functioning as a covert funding and laundering channel.[1]
