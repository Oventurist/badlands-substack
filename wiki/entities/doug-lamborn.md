# Doug Lamborn

Doug Lamborn is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Colorado's fifth congressional district, which covers [[el-paso-county|El Paso County]] — the most populous county in the state and home to five military commands.[1]

## 2022 ballot access

Ahead of the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|2022 Colorado Republican State Assembly and Convention]], Lamborn declined to participate in the assembly process altogether.[1] Colorado law allows candidates to petition onto the primary ballot as an alternative to seeking the nomination from party delegates, and Lamborn chose that route.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] attributed the decision to his deep unpopularity among Republicans in his own district, arguing that petitioning allowed him to avoid facing the party's own voters.[1]

Lamborn subsequently won his primary and the general election, an outcome Ashe in America grouped with those of [[lauren-boebert|Lauren Boebert]] and [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]] as preserving the status quo despite grassroots [[america-first-movement|America First]] energy at the assembly.[1]
