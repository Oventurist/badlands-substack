# Donna Brazile

Donna Brazile is an American political strategist and former interim chair of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]], described in Badlands Media commentary as about as establishment a Democrat as one can find.[1]

## Account of the 2016 primary

Brazile is cited as a key insider witness to the claim that the DNC rigged the 2016 Democratic presidential primaries in favor of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and against [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]].[1] A 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] pointed readers skeptical of the rigging claim to Brazile's own account, noting that she detailed the arrangement in one of her books, as reported by [[vanity-fair|Vanity Fair]] in November 2017.[1] The same Vanity Fair article carried Sanders' reply to Chuck Todd when asked whether his fellow Democrats had treated him fairly: "No... Look, we're taking on the establishment. That's pretty clear."[1]
