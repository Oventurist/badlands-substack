# Donna Brazile

Donna Brazile is an American political strategist and former interim chair of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]], described in Badlands Media commentary as about as establishment a Democrat as one can find.[1]

## Account of the 2016 primary

Brazile is cited as a key insider witness to the claim that the DNC rigged the 2016 Democratic presidential primaries in favor of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and against [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]].[1] A 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] pointed readers skeptical of the rigging claim to Brazile's own account, noting that she detailed the arrangement in one of her books, as reported by [[vanity-fair|Vanity Fair]] in November 2017.[1] The same Vanity Fair article carried Sanders' reply to Chuck Todd when asked whether his fellow Democrats had treated him fairly: "No... Look, we're taking on the establishment. That's pretty clear."[1]

## Q drop #45 and the DNC book

Badlands' "[[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]]" Chapter 6 reads [[qanon|Q]] drop #45 of November 2, 2017 — which asked "why is Donna running?" — as a reference to Brazile, described there as former Chair of the [[democratic-national-committee|DNC]].[2] The chapter recounts that she released a book accusing the DNC of rigging the primaries for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] because the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] was the driving financial force behind the DNC.[2] The author offers two readings of her motive: that she was getting out in front of the story to save herself, or, as he believes Q implies, that she had made a deal with "patriots" and was instructed to release the information.[2] Either way, the chapter concludes, Brazile was at that point throwing both the DNC and Clinton under the bus.[2]

The chapter also notes that Q asked whether the target of interest was Brazile herself or those who told her to break the law, answering that it was the latter — Brazile being "a small fish that led to larger fish."[2] Q's further question as to why the material was leaked rather than prosecuted publicly is answered in the chapter as part of the broader [[red-pilling|red-pill]] process meant to reveal the depth of the corruption.[2] The topic was, per the chapter, echoed by [[donald-trump|President Trump]] in a tweet the following day.[2]
