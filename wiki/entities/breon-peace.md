# Breon Peace

Breon Peace is the United States Attorney whose office brought the federal fraud prosecution of Representative [[george-santos|George Santos]] in the Eastern District of New York.[1]

## Role in the Santos prosecution

Announcing the 13-count indictment against Santos — which included wire fraud, money laundering, theft of public funds, and false statements on federal disclosure forms — Peace said the case "seeks to hold Santos accountable for various alleged fraudulent schemes and brazen misrepresentations."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that while "fraud is fraud" and Santos deserved his day in court, Peace's statement was "particularly poignant" given that the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] held a press conference the same day alleging millions of dollars in foreign payments to the Biden family, and asked whether the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] could apply the same rigor to what the commentary called presidential-level corruption.[1]
