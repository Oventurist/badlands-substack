# Greta Van Susteren

Greta Van Susteren is an American television journalist and interviewer working at Newsmax.[1]

## Netanyahu interview on the Kirk assassination

In September 2025, days after the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]], Van Susteren interviewed Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and, citing "online conspiracy theorists," asked him directly whether Israel had anything to do with Kirk's assassination.[1] Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the exchange as one of the more bizarre moments in recent memory, suggesting that Van Susteren appeared to have been fed the question and characterizing Netanyahu's response as "not very convincing, at all."[1] He said he would discuss the exchange further on his program [[geopolitics-with-ghost|Geopolitics with Ghost]] on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Rumble channel.[1]
