# Dave Abrams

Dave Abrams served as communications adviser and media director for Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]]' 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Departure from the DeSantis campaign

[[politico|Politico]] reported in July 2023 that Abrams, along with external affairs lead [[tucker-obenshain|Tucker Obenshain]], was leaving the campaign to help a pro-DeSantis outside political nonprofit group, a move that came as DeSantis struggled to gain traction against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The campaign confirmed the departures in a statement and framed them as part of a "DeSantis is everywhere strategy" aimed at early nominating states including Iowa, New Hampshire and South Carolina.[1]
