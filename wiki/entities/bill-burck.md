# Bill Burck

Bill Burck is a Washington defense attorney known for representing senior officials in congressional and executive-branch investigations.[1]

## Representation of Robert Hur

In February 2024, special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]] retained Burck as his personal attorney as House Republicans sought his testimony before the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] about his report on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of classified documents.[1] CNN reported that while no date had been set, the parties were looking toward the end of February for an appearance.[1]
