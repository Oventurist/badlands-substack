# George O'Toole

George O'Toole is a federal judge on the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts who in February 2025 issued the ruling that allowed the Trump administration's federal-workforce "deferred resignation" program to proceed.[1]

## Deferred resignation ruling

O'Toole allowed the administration's bid to offer "deferred resignations" to federal workers who voluntarily leave government service to continue, and lifted a previous court order that had paused the program's deadline.[1] Ruling on Wednesday, February 12, 2025, he held that the plaintiffs — unions representing federal employees — lacked standing to bring the case in the first place, and further that he did not have jurisdiction to stop the program from proceeding.[1]

[[office-of-personnel-management|Office of Personnel Management]] spokesperson McLaurine Pinover said the office was "pleased" with the court's decision and that the deferred resignation program was closed as of 7 p.m. Wednesday.[1] Democracy Forward's Skye Perrymann said on behalf of the unions that they would "continue to pursue all legal options to defend the civil service and protect the American people from extremism."[1]
