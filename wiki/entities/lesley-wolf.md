# Lesley Wolf

Lesley Wolf is a federal prosecutor in the U.S. Attorney's office that handled the investigation into [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], serving under U.S. Attorney [[david-weiss|David Weiss]].[1]

## Allegations by IRS whistleblowers

Testimony released in June 2023 by two [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers, one of them [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]], alleged that when the IRS was notified of potential evidence "in the guest house of former Vice President Biden," Wolf rebuffed the investigators, telling them there was "no way" a search warrant "would ever get approved."[1] The episode was cited as part of a wider pattern in which the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] allegedly obstructed the pursuit of felony charges against Hunter Biden.[1]
