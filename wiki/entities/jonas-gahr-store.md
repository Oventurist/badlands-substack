# Jonas Gahr Støre

Jonas Gahr Støre is the Prime Minister of [[norway|Norway]].[1]

## Trump's Greenland letter

In January 2026 Støre received a provocative letter from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — confirmed by Norway and shared by Trump with other [[nato|NATO]] leaders — linking Norway's refusal to award him the Nobel Peace Prize to a shift in U.S. policy.[1] Trump wrote: "Since your country decided not to give me the Nobel Peace Prize... I no longer feel an obligation to think purely of peace... but can now think about what is good and proper for the United States," referencing his claim to have stopped eight wars.[1]

Støre responded firmly, affirming Norway's support for [[denmark|Denmark]]'s sovereignty over [[greenland|Greenland]].[1] Norway was one of eight countries — with Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Sweden and the United Kingdom — targeted by Trump's threatened additional 10–15% tariffs effective February 1, after they deployed a small military contingent to Greenland to bolster Arctic security and demonstrate solidarity.[1] The eight nations issued a joint statement condemning the tariff threats as undermining transatlantic relations and risking a "dangerous downward spiral."[1]

## Response to the 2026 U.S. troop withdrawal

Støre downplayed the numbers involved in the U.S. decision to withdraw thousands of troops from Germany, but stressed that such changes be handled harmoniously within the NATO framework.[2]
