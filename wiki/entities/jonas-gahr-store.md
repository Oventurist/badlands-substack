# Jonas Gahr Støre

Jonas Gahr Støre is the Prime Minister of [[norway|Norway]] and leader of the Norwegian Labour Party.

According to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, Støre received a provocative letter from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] amid the escalating dispute over [[greenland|Greenland]]. The letter, confirmed by Norway and circulated by Trump to other [[nato|NATO]] leaders, linked Norway's failure to award Trump the Nobel Peace Prize — despite his claim to have "stopped 8 wars" — to a change in American policy: "Since your country decided not to give me the Nobel Peace Prize... I no longer feel an obligation to think purely of peace... but can now think about what is good and proper for the United States."

Støre responded firmly, affirming Norway's support for [[denmark|Denmark]]'s sovereignty over Greenland. Norway was among the eight European states threatened with additional 10–15% tariffs from February 1 for deploying a military contingent to Greenland, and joined their joint statement warning of a "dangerous downward spiral" in transatlantic relations.
