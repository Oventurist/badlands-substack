# Nestlé

Nestlé is the Swiss multinational food and beverage corporation referenced in Badlands Media coverage in connection with the elevation of its former chief executive, [[peter-brabeck-letmathe|Peter Brabeck-Letmathe]], to the chairmanship of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] after [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] stepped down as executive chairman in April 2025.[1]

## Water controversies

The article states that Brabeck-Letmathe oversaw Nestlé's participation in the "global water grab," including the extraction of groundwater in vulnerable regions.[1] It cites his position that water should not be recognized as a human right but treated as a "foodstuff" commodity carrying a market price, with targeted measures reserved for populations unable to obtain access.[1]

## Symbolic role in the article

Nestlé's water business functions in the Badlands narrative as an illustration of what the author calls a "typical globalist play": create a problem, then present yourself as the hero by offering a meager solution to that problem, always in exchange for something.[1] The company's former CEO is offered as evidence of the character of the transnational corporate and think-tank class the article contrasts with populist political movements.[1]
