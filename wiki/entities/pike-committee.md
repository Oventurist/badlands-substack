# Pike Committee

The Pike Committee was a congressional investigative body of the 1970s that, together with the [[church-committee|Church Committee]], exposed the abuses of the American intelligence community to the public for the first time.[1]

## Findings and consequences

Badlands Media lists the revelations produced by the two committees as including assassination plots, human experimentation, domestic surveillance, mail interception, propaganda dissemination and media manipulation, and inhumane covert operations.[1] The disclosures left the Western intelligence apparatus exposed and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] regarded as radioactive — corrupting anything it touched and causing foreign governments to recoil from any CIA-adjacent organization operating in their territory.[1]

Because many of the exposed functions were considered essential to maintaining Western hegemony, the article argues that the response was not abolition but restructuring: the construction of the [[plausible-deniability-network|plausible deniability network]] of NGOs and quasi-governmental institutes that could perform the same work at a safe remove from the CIA, the [[state-department|State Department]], and the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]].[1]
