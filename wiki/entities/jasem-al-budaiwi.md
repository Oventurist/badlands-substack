# Jasem al-Budaiwi

Jasem al-Budaiwi is the Secretary General of the [[gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]] (GCC).[1]

## China–GCC free trade talks (September 2024)

On Wednesday, September 11, 2024, al-Budaiwi met in Riyadh with Chinese Premier [[li-qiang|Li Qiang]], who urged Beijing and the GCC — which includes [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the United Arab Emirates, as well as Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain — to accelerate free trade negotiations, according to Chinese state news agency Xinhua.[1] The Saudi Press Agency reported that al-Budaiwi stressed the importance of moving forward and finalising the trade talks in the "near future."[1]

The negotiations, begun nearly 20 years earlier, had stalled over Saudi concerns that cheap Chinese imports would undercut the products it hopes to manufacture domestically and damage its industrial agenda; sources told Reuters in May 2024 that the talks were at an impasse.[1] Badlands commentary placed the meeting within a wider pattern of [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s diplomacy, coming two days after Russian Foreign Minister [[sergei-lavrov|Sergei Lavrov]] was hosted in Riyadh, and read it as evidence of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] positioning itself against what the author called the unipolar globalist hegemony.[1]
