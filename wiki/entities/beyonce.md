# Beyoncé

**Beyoncé** (Beyoncé Knowles-Carter) is an American pop singer whose brief appearance at a [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] campaign rally in Houston in October 2024 became a flashpoint in Badlands Media's coverage of the 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## 2024 Harris campaign involvement

Ahead of the [[2024-harris-houston-rally|Harris rally in Houston]] on October 25, 2024, an announcement led the public and press to believe Beyoncé would perform.[1] Roughly 30,000 attendees turned out and around a million people were reported to have sought tickets on that expectation.[1] Instead, the singer appeared only briefly, delivering a short and largely inaudible speech before leaving the stage without singing a single lyric.[1] The rally then descended into disorder, with attendees leaving or protesting and Harris being booed and heckled.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the appearance as "four minutes of emotional blackmail about abortion from the pop icon," and argued that apolitical Beyoncé fans felt tricked by the arrangement.[1] Citing widespread rumors that the campaign had paid the performer $10 million for those four minutes, she suggested the campaign simply could not afford an actual performance, and concluded that if money was the reason it was "a fatal blow to the counterfeit joy movement," since a genuine believer "would have sung for free."[1]
