# Chuck Robbins

Chuck Robbins is the chairman and chief executive officer of Cisco, the American networking and communications technology company.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Robbins was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. His inclusion placed Cisco in a delegation of American technology leadership — alongside [[tim-cook|Tim Cook]] of [[apple-inc|Apple]], [[dina-powell-mccormick|Dina Powell McCormick]] of [[meta-platforms|Meta]] and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] of Tesla — traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
