# Awad al-Shammari

Awad al-Shammari was a [[bedouin|Bedouin]] shepherd credited in Badlands commentary with discovering two Israeli military bases secretly built in remote parts of [[iraq|Iraq]].[1]

## Reported death

According to that account, after reporting his discovery to the relevant authorities, al-Shammari was gunned down in a helicopter strike.[1] The episode is cited alongside a Wall Street Journal report that [[israel|Israel]] had built and defended a secret base in Iraq during the [[iran-war|Iran war]] to support operations against Tehran, and used to argue that the drone attack on the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] — which came from the west — was not Iranian in origin.[1]
