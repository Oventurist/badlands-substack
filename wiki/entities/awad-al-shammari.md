# Awad al-Shammari

Awad al-Shammari was a Bedouin shepherd in [[iraq|Iraq]] who, according to reporting cited in Badlands commentary, discovered two Israeli military bases secretly built in remote parts of the country during the Iran war.

## Discovery and reported killing

The May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]] recounts, via contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], a recent report of two Israeli military bases that were secretly built in remote parts of Iraq. The bases were discovered by al-Shammari, who — after reporting his discovery to the relevant authorities — was reportedly gunned down by a helicopter strike. The commentary presents the episode as context for the May 17, 2026 drone attack on the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] in the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], arguing that the strike's western origin points toward Israel and Iraq rather than [[iran|Iran]]. The account is presented by the source as a report without independent confirmation in the Brief.
