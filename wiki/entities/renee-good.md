# Renee Good

Renee Good was a woman fatally shot by an agent of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] in the Minneapolis–St. Paul area in January 2026. According to the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, she was killed after allegedly attempting to run over a federal officer with her vehicle during federal deportation operations targeting criminal illegal aliens in the sanctuary-city region.

The Brief describes her death as a catalyst for ongoing anti-ICE riots and protests in the Twin Cities, compounding an already volatile situation involving left-wing activism, organized pressure campaigns by nonprofits, and escalating federal law-enforcement responses. In the aftermath, the federal government deployed 3,000 ICE agents to the area with 1,500 troops on standby, and activists stormed [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul during a Sunday service, prompting a [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] civil-rights investigation.

## Invocation at the March 2026 Senate hearing

Good's death on January 7, 2026 was raised at the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026, reported in the [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]]. Together with the killing of [[alex-pretti|Alex Pretti]] on January 24, it framed Democratic accusations that federal officers used excessive force during [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]] and that Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] wrongly labeled the victims "domestic terrorists."
