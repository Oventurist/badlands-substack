# Priscilla Almodovar

Priscilla Almodovar is the chief executive officer of [[fannie-mae|Fannie Mae]], the government-sponsored enterprise that underpins much of the United States residential mortgage market.[1]

## Role in the 2025 Fannie Mae terminations

In April 2025, Fannie Mae announced that it had fired more than 100 employees for unethical conduct, including the facilitation of fraud against the company.[1] The purge was driven by [[william-pulte|William J. Pulte]], chair of Fannie Mae's board of directors, who was sworn in on March 14, 2025 after being appointed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Almodovar publicly endorsed the action rather than resisting it, stating: "I would like to thank Director Pulte for his empowering of Fannie Mae to root out unethical conduct, including anyone facilitating fraud."[1] Her statement positioned the CEO's office as aligned with the new board leadership's anti-fraud posture, in which Pulte declared that under the Trump administration there would be no room for fraud, mortgage fraud, or "any other deceitful act that can jeopardize the safety and soundness of the housing industry."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] questioned why it required a second Trump term to address fraud and abuse inside Fannie Mae, arguing the reckoning should have come during the 2008 financial crisis but was avoided under "too big to fail" logic, and that public trust requires accountability from the entity.[1]
