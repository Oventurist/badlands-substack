# Lori Chavez-DeRemer

Lori Chavez-DeRemer is the U.S. Secretary of Labor in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, heading the [[us-department-of-labor|Department of Labor]].[1]

## Workforce strategy

In connection with the release of [[americas-talent-strategy|America's Talent Strategy: Equipping American Workers for the Golden Age]], Chavez-DeRemer thanked Secretaries [[howard-lutnick|Lutnick]] and [[linda-mcmahon|McMahon]] for working with the Labor Department on what she called a critical, forward-thinking strategy laying the foundation for the future of the nation.[1] She said that whether through expanding proven workforce models such as Registered Apprenticeships or improving artificial-intelligence readiness, the departments had developed at the president's direction a concrete plan to put the American Worker First.[1] She added that the effort would ensure the workforce is prepared to fill hundreds of thousands of good-paying, in-demand jobs created by Trump's efforts to restore the United States as the world's leading economic superpower.[1]
