# James Lindsay

James Lindsay is an American author and commentator who has worked as a contributor associated with [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] and who runs the website New Discourses.[1] He is best known within right-of-center media for coining the term "Woke Right," a label the Badlands News Brief characterizes as being applied to anyone who identifies as a supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] while remaining critical of the Israeli government.[1] According to the brief, Lindsay spent roughly two years attempting to popularize the term.[1]

## Turning Point USA controversy

Lindsay appeared on stage with [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] at a Turning Point USA event during the summer of 2025.[1] Kirk told the audience that the organization had received numerous petitions demanding that it cancel Lindsay's appearances and sever ties with him, and Kirk scoffed at the suggestion.[1]

## Prior writing and reputation

The brief notes that Lindsay previously published a satirical academic paper titled "Dildos: Going In Through The Back Door: Challenging Straight Male Homohysteria And Transphobia Through Receptive Penetrative Sex Toy Use," produced as part of an effort to embarrass academic publishing, and that the paper and similar works remain published on his New Discourses website.[1] The brief also states that Lindsay was once a vocal Never-Trump figure who publicly mocked Christians as gullible.[1]

## Decline in standing

Lindsay said on a podcast that he is no longer invited to speaking engagements, has lost roughly half of his subscribers and nearly all of his income, and claims to have been blacklisted by the Trump administration.[1] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry presented this decline as one element in a broader realignment of conservative media over coverage of Israel.[1]
