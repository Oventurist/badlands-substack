# Charles Lindbergh

Charles Lindbergh was an American aviator who became one of the best-known members of the [[america-first-committee|America First Committee]], the organization formed in 1940 to keep the United States out of the Second World War.[1]

## Role in the non-interventionist movement

Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson cites Lindbergh's involvement as evidence of the mainstream character of the anti-war coalition, noting that the Committee's roughly 800,000 members were nonetheless "considered Nazi supporters" by their opponents.[1] Carlson uses that treatment as a historical parallel for the modern labeling of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s America First supporters — including the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] — as racists and Nazis.[1]
