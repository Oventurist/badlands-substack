# Hilde Vautmans

Hilde Vautmans is a Belgian member of the [[european-parliament|European Parliament]] who served as foreign affairs coordinator for the [[renew-europe|Renew Europe]] group.[1]

## Defense of the Migration Pact

After the Parliament approved the [[eu-asylum-and-migration-pact|EU Asylum and Migration Pact]] in April 2024, Vautmans was among the lawmakers who conceded the package's imperfections while defending the vote, stating that "The new legislation is not perfect but we can only make migration manageable and humane with one European solution."[1] Her comment reflected a broader pattern in which MEPs on both the left and the center-right acknowledged voting for the compromise text despite its flaws.[1]
