# John Barrasso

John Barrasso is a Republican United States Senator from Wyoming who, in September 2025, served as Senate Majority Whip.[1]

## Role in the September 2025 confirmations

On Thursday, 18 September 2025, Senate Republicans used newly adopted confirmation rules to confirm 48 of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominees along party lines, many of them undersecretary or assistant secretary positions across federal agencies as well as ambassadorships.[1] Speaking on the Senate floor during the effort, Barrasso framed the change as a necessary response to Democratic tactics: "Why has not a single nominee been confirmed by voice vote or by unanimous consent? We know why... It's Democrat obstruction."[1]

The rules change had been enacted the previous week when Senate Republicans went "nuclear" after a last-minute deal with Democrats collapsed, permitting confirmation of unlimited nominees in en bloc batches by simple majority — though still requiring procedural steps and 30 hours of debate.[1]
