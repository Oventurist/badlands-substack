# Jörg Urban

Jörg Urban is the state and parliamentary group leader of the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] (AfD) in Saxony.[1]

## Response to the 2024 Saxony election error

After the [[2024-saxony-state-election|2024 Saxony state election]], in which the AfD finished first but lost a parliamentary seat to an alleged vote-counting software error, Urban announced that the party would investigate.[1] "We want to know exactly what went wrong," he said in a statement, demanding an exact error analysis and adding, "If there are any irregularities, we will take legal action."[1] The disputed seat cost the AfD its blocking majority in the state parliament, which would have allowed it to block the appointment of certain judges in Saxony.[1] The party simultaneously called for an end to [[mail-in-voting|mail-in ballots]], citing security concerns and shady practices.[1]
