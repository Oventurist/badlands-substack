# Richard Day

**Dr. Richard Day** was national medical director of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], an organization funded by the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]].[1]

In 1969 Day addressed a meeting of pediatric physicians in Pittsburgh attended by Dr. [[lawrence-dunegan|Lawrence Dunegan]], M.D., who later recounted the talk. According to Dunegan's account, Day told the audience that in the future hard-to-cure diseases would be created, and that cures for nearly all cancers had already been developed but were being hidden at the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] so that populations would not increase.[1]

Badlands Media presents the Day lecture as a candid statement of the population-control aims it attributes to the twentieth-century [[medical-industrial-complex|Medical Industrial Complex]].[1]
