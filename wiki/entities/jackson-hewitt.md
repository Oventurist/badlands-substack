# Jackson Hewitt

**Jackson Hewitt** is an American retail tax preparation company.[1]

In March 2025 journalist Savannah Hernandez posted footage on X in which, she said, she "caught a Jackson Hewitt employee coaching illegals on how they could get up to $14,000 in tax refunds whether they're 'working or not.'"[1] Hernandez suggested that identifying who is receiving tax refunds should be the next area of scrutiny for [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1]
