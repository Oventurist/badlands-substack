# Jewish Defense League

The Jewish Defense League (JDL) was an activist group founded in New York City by the far-right rabbi [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]].[1] The organization was accused of planning numerous violent terrorist attacks, including some that were carried out, and was classified as a terrorist organization by law enforcement agencies throughout the United States.[1] The group ceased operating in 2015.[1]

## Legacy

The JDL is treated in commentary as the American institutional root of Kahanism, the ideological current that later found expression in Israeli politics through [[meir-kahane|Kahane]]'s Knesset career, the Kahane Chai movement, and successors such as [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]].[1] Discussion of the League resurfaced in November 2025 after [[likud|Likud]] MK [[nissim-vaturi|Nissim Vaturi]] told the [[knesset|Knesset]] that Kahane "was right" and was not a terrorist.[1]
