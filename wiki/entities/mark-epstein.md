# Mark Epstein

Mark Epstein is the brother of financier and convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], and has publicly pressed New York authorities for records related to his brother's 2019 death in federal custody.[1]

## Search for the pre-hospital care report

In a video shared on social media by former Fox News host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] in late December 2023 — an apparent preview of a forthcoming interview — Mark Epstein was recorded attempting to obtain the pre-hospital care report (PCR) written by the EMS team that moved his brother's body out of the cell.[1] In the exchange he asks an employee, "But as of right now, you're telling me you can't find the PCR report?" and the employee replies, "Not for the 10th of August 2019... It's not in the fire department database. I don't know why."[1]

Carlson framed the episode as a reason for skepticism about the official ruling that Jeffrey Epstein died by suicide, asking why so few records were available from that night if the government's account were true.[1] The exchange was reported by Caden Pearson of [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and amplified by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]
