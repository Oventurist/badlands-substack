# Steve Wynn

Steve Wynn is an American casino magnate and Republican donor who became the defendant in a Justice Department civil suit alleging violations of the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] (FARA). The case appears in the Badlands corpus as a comparison point in arguments about asymmetric enforcement of foreign-influence law.[1]

## FARA case

In May 2022, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed suit against Wynn, alleging that he had leveraged his relationship with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to advocate for the Chinese Communist Party's desired return of US asylum seeker [[miles-guo|Miles Guo]]. Announcing the filing, Assistant Attorney General Matthew G. Olsen said: "The filing of this suit … demonstrates the department's commitment to ensuring transparency in our democratic system. Where a foreign government uses an American as its agent to influence policy decisions in the United States, FARA gives the American people a right to know."[1]

The case was ultimately dismissed by US District Court Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]].[1]

## Use as a comparison in Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Wynn prosecution as an illustration of what she termed an asymmetrical justice system: while the Justice Department publicly invoked FARA's transparency rationale in pursuing Wynn, no comparable statements had been made by the attorney general's office or DOJ regarding the [[biden-crime-family|Biden family]]'s dealings, despite documented evidence of strategy, execution, and payment for services rendered by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to foreign powers, most notably the Chinese Communist Party and Ukraine.[1]
