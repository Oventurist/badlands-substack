# ZeroHedge

ZeroHedge is an alternative financial and political news website widely read in dissident and market-focused circles.[1]

On March 16, 2026, ZeroHedge published the report that carried [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s claim that the [[cia|CIA]] was preparing a criminal referral over his contacts with people in [[iran|Iran]], quoting Carlson's line that the supposed offense was "talking to people in Iran before the war" and that "They read my texts."[1]

The outlet later updated the article to report that "top admin officials" had told Axios reporter Marc Caputo there was "no CIA investigation of Carlson," and included activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]]'s statement that she would take credit if Carlson were charged and had been "relentless" in lobbying GOP representatives, law enforcement and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] about him.[1]

## April 2026 Kash Patel commentary

On a Sunday in April 2026, ZeroHedge published an opinion article — credited to Matt Margolis via PJMedia and issued under the site's "Tyler Durden" pseudonym — criticizing a recent [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]] report on [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] and arguing the magazine could face legal consequences.[2] The post cited statements from attorney [[jesse-binnall|Jesse Binnall]] disputing The Atlantic's claims and included social-media responses attributed to Patel and other officials.[2]

## Coverage of the Youngkin pardon

In September 2023 ZeroHedge reported that Virginia Governor [[glenn-youngkin|Glenn Youngkin]] had pardoned Scott Smith, the Loudoun County father convicted of obstruction of justice and disorderly conduct after confronting school officials over his daughter's rape in a school bathroom and the district's attempted cover-up.[3] The report was reproduced as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]; see [[loudoun-county-school-bathroom-assault-case]].[3]

The first reported [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s warning to [[gaza|Gaza]] residents to clear out and his pledge that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] would use "all its strength" to "destroy" [[hamas|Hamas]] following the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]].[4] The second covered [[bill-maher|Bill Maher]]'s monologue accusing Democrats of looking like "sanctuary city hypocrites" over the busing of migrants to northeastern [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary cities]].[4]

## Coverage of the 2023 banking turmoil and Epstein calendar

In April 2023, ZeroHedge relayed Wall Street Journal reporting that [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]] and [[pnc-financial-services-group|PNC]] were set to acquire [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]] following a government seizure, arguing that the structure left taxpayers holding the toxic assets while the largest U.S. bank grew larger.[5] The outlet also coined the framing that the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] was pursuing "monetary tightening through bank collapse" after failing to contain inflation by conventional means.[5]

The same month, ZeroHedge summarized the Wall Street Journal's report on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s private calendar, which listed [[william-burns|William Burns]], a top [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] lawyer, and [[noam-chomsky|Noam Chomsky]].[5]

## Debt ceiling and J6 donation coverage (2023)

ZeroHedge claimed to have been "the first to correctly summarize" the bottom line of the [[debt-ceiling-agreement-2023|2023 debt ceiling deal]]: a 0.2% of GDP cut in nominal spending, which it called a "Total Farce" given that real spending would rise the following year.[6] The outlet cited [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]]'s post-mortem finding that the deal would reduce spending by 0.1–0.2% of GDP year over year in 2024 and 2025 against an inflation-indexed baseline, but that the nearly 10% FY23 funding boost meant discretionary spending would still be slightly higher in real terms despite the new caps.[6] It noted that even the [[congressional-budget-office|CBO]] shows the long-term debt trajectory "in its full, hyperinflationary glory," and mocked Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] for promoting the cut on Twitter.[6]

In a separate item, ZeroHedge reported that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] was trying to claw back donations made to January 6 defendants, contrasting that with the Kamala Harris-endorsed bail fund available to protesters during the George Floyd riots.[6] It relayed AP findings that prosecutors sought more than $25,000 raised by [[daniel-goodwyn|Daniel Goodwyn]] and had asked judges to enhance fines to offset supporter donations, with most fundraising occurring on [[givesendgo|GiveSendGo]].[6]

## December 2023 reporting

ZeroHedge produced two items excerpted in the December 22, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[7] The first argued that it was "too late for US naval deterrence in the Red Sea after Biden misled world on Houthi attacks," documenting a full-blown [[houthis|Houthi]] war on commercial shipping, Pentagon frustration at White House restraint, and the *Wall Street Journal*'s judgment that American deterrence was "going, going, maybe even gone."[7] The second covered [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]]'s statements that Gazans should be resettled in "pro-Hamas countries" — naming [[qatar|Qatar]], [[iran|Iran]] and [[turkey|Turkey]] — and her campaign-rally claim of a connection between Vladimir Putin and [[hamas|Hamas]] on the grounds that October 7 fell on Putin's birthday.[7]

The outlet also reported [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]]'s confrontation of a bus driver at Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport who confirmed he was hauling migrants from the border to the airport, in a story on [[phoenix-airport-migrant-bussing-story|the bussing of migrants]] that noted a Community Note disputing an earlier viral claim.[7]

## Draft anxiety and DeSantis coverage (October 2023)

ZeroHedge reporting excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] mocked young Americans newly alarmed by the prospect of a [[military-draft|military draft]], arguing that the generation most inclined to support [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and to back war against [[russia|Russia]] in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] had lost its "blood thirst" once personally exposed to conscription.[9] The outlet noted that Google searches for "Will I get drafted into war..." had hit their highest level since the 2007 Iraq surge, that recruitment had been dismal, and that a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] study found up to 77% of [[generation-z|Gen Z]] ineligible for service.[9] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] explicitly disagreed with the piece, writing that its words "drip with disdain for the youngest of American adults."[9]

In a separate item, ZeroHedge covered Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]]'s facilitation of shipments of weapons, ammunition, drones, body armor and helmets to [[israel|Israel]], describing his contest with [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] over loyalty to a foreign country as "an ongoing spectacle that would repel Washington and Jefferson."[9]

## March 2023 banking coverage

ZeroHedge's report "Big Trouble In Little Banks - Bailout Sparks Buying Panic In Bonds, Bitcoin, & Bullion" was excerpted in the Badlands News Brief for March 13, 2023.[10] The piece observed that the [[federal-reserve|Fed]], Treasury and [[federal-deposit-insurance-corporation|FDIC]] had "saved the world" again the previous night, but that "nobody told regional banks," whose shares fell dramatically the following day amid multiple trading halts.[10] It identified [[first-republic-bank|FRC]], WAL and MYFW as the highest default-risk banks in the Russell 3000 Banks Subsector according to Bloomberg, and noted that all of the global majors were seeing credit-default-swap spreads rise, with [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] CDS never having closed higher and implying more than double the risk seen at the peak of the 2008 financial crisis.[10] The report closed by asking why systemic signals were worsening if the Fed's backstop was so good and the banking system so resilient.[10]

## October 2023 reporting

ZeroHedge reported in late October 2023 that Israel had again delayed its planned ground invasion of Gaza at Washington's request while the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] rushed nearly a dozen air-defense systems into the region, and that [[centcom|US Central Command]] had disclosed more than a dozen drone attacks on US troops and installations in Iraq and Syria.[11] In the same period the outlet highlighted a [[washington-post|Washington Post]] report that the [[cia|CIA]] was running covert operations inside Russia — including the killing of [[darya-dugina|Darya Dugina]] — framing it as another "conspiracy theory" belatedly admitted as fact while world attention was focused on Gaza.[11]

## Coverage of the global "day of rage" (October 2023)

ZeroHedge reporting cited in the October 18, 2023 Badlands News Brief described Wednesday as "shaping up as quite a global day of rage," documenting protests in major cities worldwide after the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza.[12] The outlet reported riot police and tear gas deployed against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]-flag-waving crowds outside the United States Embassy in [[beirut|Beirut]], and broke the news that the [[white-house|White House]] had canceled the entire Jordan leg of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s trip as Air Force One departed Washington.[12]

## Coverage of the September 2024 Fed cut

ZeroHedge covered the September 18, 2024 [[federal-open-market-committee|FOMC]] decision under the headline "'Apolitical' Fed Slashes Rates By 50bps With Stocks & Home-Prices At Record Highs," noting that only 9 of 113 economists surveyed had expected a 50 basis point cut and that the committee slashed its dot-plot rate expectations without adjusting growth forecasts.[13] The outlet emphasized the timing—"all this two months before the election"—and characterized the move sarcastically as a "crisis-level rate cut" delivered into an economy officials described as strong.[13] See [[september-2024-fed-rate-cut|September 2024 Federal Reserve Rate Cut]].

## Coverage cited in the NewsGuard litigation (2023)

ZeroHedge reporting supplied several items in Badlands' October 2023 news roundup, including coverage of Russia's accusation that the US was stoking escalation through its Middle East force build-up, the [[consortium-news|Consortium News]] lawsuit against [[newsguard|NewsGuard]], and the [[customs-and-border-protection|CBP]] disclosure of record illegal-crossing figures.[14] In the NewsGuard case, Consortium News noted that the ratings firm had branded the entirety of its 20,000-plus articles as unreliable on the basis of six disputed examples — "the exact same thing they do with ZeroHedge and other independent media."[14]

## Coverage of the Maine ballot removal

In January 2024 the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] excerpted ZeroHedge's report on Maine Secretary of State [[shenna-bellows|Shenna Bellows]], who had removed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's primary ballot and then argued that "better leaders" must hold power to prevent "election sabotage."[15] The ZeroHedge commentary warned that the precedent set by states such as [[colorado|Colorado]] and [[maine|Maine]] "could change the political landscape of America forever if allowed to go unchallenged," and that removing candidates on unilateral opinion rather than adjudication was "the most slippery of slopes."[15] It further argued the tactic was "not only about Donald Trump" and could be turned against any conservative candidate labeled a "threat to Democracy."[15]

ZeroHedge also supplied the Brief's reporting on the Red Sea, including the Iran-backed Houthi [[maersk-container-ship-attack|attack on a Maersk container ship]], the U.S. helicopter response that killed ten rebels, and Iran's dispatch of a warship to the Red Sea.[15]

## UAP whistleblower coverage (2023)

In June 2023 ZeroHedge aggregated and amplified [[the-debrief|The Debrief]]'s report on [[david-grusch|David Grusch]], writing that a new report from two veteran mainstream journalists citing a decorated whistleblower "provides stunning insight into the US Government's history with UFOs."[16] It framed the story as decisive for both believers and for those who regard recent government UAP disclosures as a psyop, and summarized Grusch's career at the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|NGA]] and [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] as well as the reorganization of the [[unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force|UAP Task Force]] into the [[all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office|All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office]].[16]

## BlueAnon Watch coverage, July 2024

In July 2024 ZeroHedge published a "BlueAnon Watch" item reporting that a [[morning-consult|Morning Consult]] poll had found one-third of Democrats — 34 percent — believed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] faked the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on his life]], a higher share than the roughly 23 percent of Republicans who endorsed the core QAnon claim at its 2021 peak.[17] Citing the Washington Free Beacon, the outlet traced the staged-shooting theory to Democratic operative [[dmitri-mehlhorn|Dmitri Mehlhorn]], adviser to [[reid-hoffman|Reid Hoffman]], who circulated a memo to reporters hours after the shooting framing it as a false flag from [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s playbook.[17] ZeroHedge noted that the memo ignored photographs of a bullet passing Trump's head, the wound to his right ear, and the death of rally attendee Corey Comperatore.[17]

## Coverage of the June 2024 debate aftermath

ZeroHedge wrote that the [[2024-presidential-debate|June 2024 presidential debate]] had "mortally wounded" President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s political career and that the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] editorial board had hammered a significant nail in the coffin by declaring that "the greatest public service Mr. Biden can now perform is to announce that he will not continue to run for re-election."[18] The outlet argued that Biden's departure from November ballots was taking on an air of inevitability, while suggesting that "Team Trump is reckoning with what may have been a strategic error — enabling a premature kill shot that could leave Trump facing a worse matchup."[18] It also noted the significance of a cornerstone liberal media institution declaring an incumbent Democratic president mentally incapable of running for re-election, predicting a snowball effect among other leftist entities and elected officials.[18]

Badlands contributor Burning Bright treated the piece as a "New Con Inc." deployment, singling out the "premature kill shot" framing as emblematic of commentators who believe they "know better" than [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — dismissing the argument that Trump's debate strategy backfired, and insisting that fake polling and election interference would shape 2024 regardless of the Democratic nominee.[18]

## Coverage of the 2024 Paris Olympics opening ceremony

ZeroHedge's report on the [[2024-paris-summer-olympics|2024 Paris Olympics]] opening ceremony argued that sporting spectacles such as the Olympics, the Super Bowl and the Commonwealth Games had grown increasingly political and that their symbology had become steadily more bizarre.[19] The outlet recalled its May 2024 reporting that drag queens and transgender activists would carry the Olympic Torch, noting that torchbearers are conventionally chosen for significant contributions to their communities.[19] It read the Games' stated tenets — Community, Diversity and The Collective — as meaning "the theme of this year's Olympic Games is woke," criticized artistic director Thomas Jolly's stated wish that "everyone feel represented," and described a ceremony featuring a horde of drag queens, sexualized dancing and an LGBT recreation of The Last Supper.[19] ZeroHedge also cited Gallup polling projecting record-low U.S. viewership for the Paris Games, below even the 2022 Beijing Winter Olympics.[19]

## Reporting on the Northeast gasoline reserve (2024)

## Coverage of media collapse and the Peanut seizure (2024)

In the final days before the November 2024 election, ZeroHedge reported [[cnn|CNN]] media analyst [[brian-stelter|Brian Stelter]]'s circulation of an anonymous television executive's warning that "a Trump victory means mainstream media is dead in its current form," and endorsed the assessment.[24] The outlet argued that trust in corporate media had been sliding for decades and had imploded to record lows, and that history might record the 2020s as the decade the curtain was lifted on a "censorship blob" — a combination of legacy media, government, big tech, nonprofit fact-checkers and globalist billionaires it accused of sowing division, drawing the country into foreign wars, and engineering a news environment favorable to far-left policy.[24]

ZeroHedge also drove coverage of the New York seizure and euthanasia of [[peanut-the-squirrel|Peanut the squirrel]], reporting that "the internet is ablaze with rage" after the state took the animal from Mark Luongo's home and killed it along with a raccoon, ostensibly to test for rabies.[24] The outlet separately reported [[dmitry-medvedev|Dmitry Medvedev]]'s Telegram post arguing that the U.S. election outcome was irrelevant to Russia and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "may get JFK'd" if he attempted to halt the Ukraine war.[24]

## SVB insider loans report (March 2023)
