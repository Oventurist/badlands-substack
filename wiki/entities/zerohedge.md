# ZeroHedge

ZeroHedge is an American financial and political news website known for aggregating market commentary and contrarian reporting, and for its prominence in the alternative-media ecosystem from which the Badlands corpus draws.

The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief cites a ZeroHedge article of the same date reporting that [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] said he had learned the [[cia|CIA]] was preparing a criminal referral over his contacts with people in [[iran|Iran]]. ZeroHedge subsequently updated the article to report that "top admin officials" had told [[axios|Axios]] reporter [[marc-caputo|Marc Caputo]] there was "no CIA investigation of Carlson," and to note that activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] had said she would take credit if Carlson were charged, describing herself as "relentless" in contacting GOP representatives, law enforcement and the Justice Department about him. The Brief's use of both the original report and its update illustrates the corpus's practice of tracking a claim and its official denial together.


## Reporting on the allied coalition refusal (March 2026)

The March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief cites a ZeroHedge article, "Not Our War: Allies Shun Trump Coalition, Iran Security Chief Alleged Killed, Oil," for the reporting that [[russia|Russia]] is pushing [[iran|Iran]] to stay in the fight — treating the continuation of the conflict as either a negotiating chip or a proxy war against [[israel|Israel]] — and that [[china|China]] is likewise working to keep Iran engaged.
