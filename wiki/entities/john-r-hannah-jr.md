# John R. Hannah Jr.

John R. Hannah Jr. is an Arizona Superior Court judge who presided over [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s lawsuit seeking access to mail-in ballot affidavit signatures from the November 2022 midterm election in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].[1]

On Aug. 24, 2023, Hannah ordered that a two-day trial would begin Sept. 21 at 9 a.m., with trial exhibits due by Sept. 14.[1] Earlier in 2023 the court had ruled that Lake's request to access affidavit envelopes should not be dismissed; Hannah wrote in June that county recorders generally include ballot affidavit envelopes in voter registration records, and that the court is "not required to defer to the elections officials in how they have historically interpreted" the law.[1] He rejected arguments by Maricopa County lawyers that ballot affidavit signatures form part of the voter registration record and are confidential under state law.[1]
