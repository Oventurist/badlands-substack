# Catholic Charities

Catholic Charities is a network of Catholic-affiliated nonprofit social service organizations operating through regional chapters across the United States, several of which have historically administered federally funded refugee resettlement programs.[1]

## Closure of the mid-Missouri resettlement program (2025)

In March 2025, the Central and Northern Missouri chapter of Catholic Charities announced that it would end its refugee resettlement program on March 31, after 14 years of operation.[1] In an email to supporters, executive director Litz Main said the nonprofit had to end the program because of the lack of federal funding, adding: "We deeply appreciate your partnership and shared commitment to serving those in need. As we navigate this transition, we welcome opportunities to collaborate in new ways to continue supporting refugee families in our region."[1]

The cuts originated at the federal level after [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] suspended the [[us-refugee-admissions-program|U.S. Refugee Admissions Program]] and halted all funding for agencies that support refugees.[1] Although a federal judge blocked the executive order in February 2025, local resettlement programs still faced an absence of federal funds, and the case was expected to be appealed by the administration.[1]
