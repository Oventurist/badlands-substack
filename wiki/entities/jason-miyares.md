# Jason Miyares

Jason Miyares is a Republican politician who served as Attorney General of Virginia and was defeated for re-election in November 2025.[1]

## 2025 defeat

On November 4, 2025, former Virginia Del. [[jay-jones|Jay Jones]], a Democrat, was projected to oust Miyares from the state's top law enforcement office.[1] The race had been considered one of the more competitive statewide contests in Virginia that cycle, and was disrupted in its final month by the resurfacing of violent 2022 texts Jones had written about then-state House Speaker [[todd-gilbert|Todd Gilbert]] — a controversy that nevertheless failed to save Miyares' seat.[1] The loss formed part of a wider Democratic sweep of the first major elections of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term.[1]

## Purdue Pharma opioid settlement

In late January 2025, Miyares announced a $7.4 billion multistate settlement in principle with members of the [[sackler-family|Sackler family]] and [[purdue-pharma|Purdue Pharma, Inc.]] over their leading role in creating and fueling the national opioid crisis, with Virginia projected to receive up to approximately $107.4 million over fifteen years.[2] "The fight to secure this monumental settlement has been long and hard fought, but my office never gave up," Miyares said, describing the agreement as historic accountability for the Sacklers and Purdue that would also ensure communities across Virginia and the nation receive support to heal from opioid addiction.[2] The settlement terminates the Sacklers' control of Purdue and their ability to sell opioids in the United States, directing funds to treatment, prevention and recovery programs over fifteen years.[2]
