# Billboard Chris

Billboard Chris is the public name of a well-known activist who campaigns against the medical transition of children. In Badlands Media coverage by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], he is offered as evidence that opposition to paediatric medical transition is not confined to the political right: Esler noted that Billboard Chris has openly received support from droves of left-wing citizens.[1]

Esler placed him alongside [[gays-against-groomers|Gays Against Groomers]], which he described as operating outside the false framing of the issue as a gay-versus-straight conflict, and alongside [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s departure from the Democratic National Committee and her interview with detransitioner [[chloe-cole|Chloe Cole]], as apolitical events in the same saga.[1] These examples supported his central argument that the adversary behind [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] needs the issue to appear political in order to maintain situational control, and that uncovering its apolitical elements enables networks across the political divide that undermine operations relying on political division.[1]
