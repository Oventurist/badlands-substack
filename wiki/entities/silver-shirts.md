# Silver Shirts

The Silver Shirts were an American Nazi movement led by [[william-dudley-pelley|William Dudley Pelley]], with which [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] openly associated within the United States between the wars.[1]

The organization sat at the intersection of American fascism and Theosophical occultism, with Pelley's ties extending to the early UFO contactees George Adamski and George Hunt Williamson.[1] Pelley reproduced the Protocols of Zion leaflets that were distributed through Congressional offices under Viereck's direction during World War II.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] groups the Silver Shirts with the other fascist movements of the era — the Canadian [[adrien-arcand|Adrien Arcand]]'s Quebec blue shirts and Sir Oswald Mosley's [[british-union-of-fascists|British Union of Fascists]] — as coming in different flavors but united by a cold utilitarian philosophy of government, a devout love for [[eugenics|eugenics]] and an addiction to City of London and Wall Street money.[1]
