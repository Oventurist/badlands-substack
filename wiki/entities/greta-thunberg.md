# Greta Thunberg

**Greta Thunberg** is a Swedish activist, originally known for climate campaigning, who by 2025 had become a prominent participant in maritime aid convoys attempting to reach the Gaza Strip.[1]

## Gaza flotilla participation and detention

Thunberg was among roughly 170 activists deported by Israel after boats belonging to the [[global-sumud-flotilla|Global Sumud Flotilla]] were stopped and their passengers detained in early October 2025.[1] During their detention, activists alleged mistreatment, with particular allegations concerning Thunberg, who was said to have been "paraded like a trophy wrapped in an Israeli flag."[1] Fellow activists further alleged that she was dragged by her hair and forced to kiss an Israeli flag while in custody.[1]

The deportations preceded a further Israeli interception days later of vessels associated with the [[freedom-flotilla-coalition|Freedom Flotilla Coalition]], including the boats *Thousand Madleens to Gaza* and *Conscience*.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the repetition of the pattern, remarking that she had "lost count of how many times Greta has been bombed or detained on her way to Gaza. At least three. Maybe more," and asking sardonically what "the carbon footprint of deported flotilla activists" might be before adding that carbon footprints were no longer a subject of concern.[1]

## Madleen interception (June 2025)

In June 2025 Thunberg sailed aboard the Gaza-bound civilian aid boat Madleen, operated by the [[freedom-flotilla-coalition|Freedom Flotilla Coalition]], alongside French MEP [[rima-hassan|Rima Hassan]] and Irish actor [[liam-cunningham|Liam Cunningham]].[2] Israeli naval forces intercepted the vessel in international waters early on a Monday and towed it to the port of Ashdod, with Israel's Foreign Ministry — which had dubbed the boat a "selfie yacht" — confirming the detentions and saying the passengers were "safe and unharmed" and had been "provided with sandwiches and water."[2] Thunberg reported before the interception that the boat had been sprayed with a mysterious white substance by an IDF aircraft, prompting speculation about a bioweapon or a night-visibility marking agent.[2] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described her as a climate-change poster child "created by the Party of Davos" and speculated that she might become an Islamic martyr for the Third World were she harmed in IDF custody — "the inversion of woke."[2] See [[madleen-flotilla-interception|Madleen flotilla interception]].[2]

## Detention and forced screening of October 7 footage

After Thunberg and eleven other activists aboard a boat attempting to challenge Israel's blockade on Gaza were detained, Israeli Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] ordered the military to screen them a 43-minute video of the October 7, 2023 attacks produced by the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] spokesperson's office.[3] The uncensored footage, much of it taken from terrorists' bodycams, showed people being massacred and bodies mutilated.[3] Katz said in a statement that it was "appropriate that Greta the antisemite and her Hamas-supporting friends should see exactly who is the terror group [[hamas|Hamas]] that they support and act on behalf of," describing Thunberg as the most famous of the twelve activists aboard the vessel.[3] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded with a "countdown till Greta goes full Zionist."[3]
