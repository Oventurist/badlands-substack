# The Libertarian Institute

The Libertarian Institute is an American libertarian news and commentary outlet focused heavily on U.S. foreign policy and antiwar reporting.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In October 2024 the outlet reported that roughly 51,000 Ukrainians had deserted the armed forces during the year, aggregating figures from *The Times of London*, *El País* and Al Jazeera and noting Kyiv's lowering of the conscription age from 27 to 25 amid pressure from American politicians to drop it to 18.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] excerpted this report in its news brief alongside coverage of Western weapons attrition in Ukraine, using it to argue that the [[ukrainian-desertion-crisis|Ukrainian desertion crisis]] undermined official optimism about the war.[1]

## Coverage of the 2023 Ukrainian defense purge

The Libertarian Institute reported in September 2023 that President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] had dismissed six high-ranking deputy defense ministers ahead of a trip to Washington to lobby [[congress|Congress]] for a multi-billion-dollar funding package.[2] The report noted that the firings followed the sacking of Defense Minister [[oleksii-reznikov|Oleksii Reznikov]] over corruption two weeks earlier, that neither Cabinet representative Taras Melnychuk nor new minister [[rustem-umerov|Rustem Umerov]] gave a reason, and that Ukraine had been infamous as the most corrupt country in Europe before the Russian invasion — since which Washington has sent Kiev over $100 billion in weapons and direct financial assistance.[2] See [[2023-ukrainian-defense-ministry-purge|the 2023 Ukrainian defense ministry purge]].[2]
