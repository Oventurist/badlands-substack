# Stockton Rush

Stockton Rush was the chief executive officer of [[oceangate|OceanGate]], the firm behind the Titan submersible.[1]

## Death aboard the Titan

Rush was one of the five people killed when the Titan imploded in June 2023 during a dive to the wreck of the Titanic.[1] He had been the boss of [[david-lochridge|David Lochridge]], the OceanGate employee sacked in 2018 after raising warnings about deficient equipment and safety practices at the company.[1]

A [[us-coast-guard|US Coast Guard]] report published in August 2025 concluded that OceanGate's failures over safety, testing and maintenance were the main cause of the disaster.[1] Lochridge, reflecting on the company Rush led, said that "people were sold a lie."[1]
