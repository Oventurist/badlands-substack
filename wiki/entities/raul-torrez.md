# Raul Torrez

Raul Torrez is the Attorney General of New Mexico.[1] In October 2023 he authored a letter, made public in Manhattan federal court, objecting on behalf of multiple states to the terms of [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]]'s $290 million class-action settlement with accusers of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Objection to the JPMorgan settlement

Torrez wrote that "Jeffrey Epstein's surviving victims should be fully compensated for the profound harm they have suffered," but that "as it now stands, the settlement agreement improperly seeks to release (the states') claims for victim-specific relief."[1] The objection targeted settlement language barring "any sovereign or government" from seeking damages arising from sex trafficking by Epstein and his associates — language the attorneys general said would deter states from pursuing damages for trafficking victims generally under the federal Trafficking Victims Protection Act, not only for Epstein's victims.[1]

The letter was signed by the attorneys general of 16 states and Washington, D.C., including Arizona, California, Connecticut, Delaware, the District of Columbia, Hawaii, Illinois, Maryland, Minnesota, Mississippi, New York, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Utah and Vermont.[1] The officials noted that [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]]'s comparable $75 million settlement with Epstein accusers, approved days earlier, did not contain the offending release language.[1]
