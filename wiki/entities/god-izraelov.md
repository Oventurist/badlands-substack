# God Izraelov

God Izraelov is one of the defendants named in the 2026 [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] insider trading indictments.[1]

## Fugitive status

The DOJ's indictment asserts that Izraelov is currently located in [[israel|Israel]] and is considered a fugitive.[1] The [[jewish-news-syndicate|Jewish News Syndicate]] interpreted that designation to mean Izraelov had fled the United States for Israel, presumably to avoid extradition.[1] Two defendants in the overall case, located in [[russia|Russia]] and Israel, are treated as fugitives.[1]
