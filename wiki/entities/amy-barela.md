# Amy Barela

**Amy Barela** is the Chairwoman of the Republican Party of New Mexico.[1]

In late March 2025, after the party's Albuquerque headquarters was damaged in an alleged arson attack, Barela shared images of the destroyed entryway on X and stated that the incident was "a deliberate act of arson which also included spray-painting the words 'ICE=KKK.'"[1] Her party characterized the attack as a "direct assault on our values."[1] See [[albuquerque-gop-headquarters-arson|Albuquerque GOP headquarters arson]].[1]
