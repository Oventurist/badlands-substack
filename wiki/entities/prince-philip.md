# Prince Philip

**Prince Philip Mountbatten**, consort of Queen Elizabeth II, is presented by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the central royal patrons of the modern environmental movement.[1]

Philip co-founded the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] alongside UNESCO founder Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] and [[prince-bernhard-of-the-netherlands|Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands]].[1] All three were present at Bernhard's founding meeting of the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] group in 1954, an event Ehret characterizes as advancing a "grand conversion of society into a willful self-extermination."[1] In 1970 Philip and Bernhard co-founded the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Nature Trust]] alongside 999 other wealthy participants to fund the growing environmental movement.[1] [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] served as a vice-president of the World Wildlife Fund under Philip.[1]

Ehret disputes any sincere conservationist motive, writing that Philip and Bernhard "have been recorded to have killed more endangered species on safari than most people have killed mosquitos," and concluding: "They just don't like people."[1]

## WWF and the 1001 Club

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names Prince Philip Mountbatten as one of the close associates — with [[prince-bernhard-of-the-netherlands|Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands]] and Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] — who created the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] shortly before Bernhard founded the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Trust]] in 1970.[2] Canada's [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] later served as Vice President of the WWF under Philip's presidency, replacing the 1001 Club member [[louis-mortimer-bloomfield|Louis Mortimer Bloomfield]].[2]

Ehret also refers to Lord Mountbatten — under whom Canadian [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] member [[omond-solandt|Omond Solandt]] served as science advisor during the Second World War — as Prince Philip's "pedophiliac mentor."[2]
