# Hugo Carvajal

**Hugo Carvajal Barrios**, known by the nickname "El Pollo," is the former Venezuelan intelligence chief who served as Director of Military Intelligence under both Hugo Chávez and [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1] A three-star general, he broke publicly with the Maduro government in 2017, fled Venezuela, and was later extradited to the United States.[1]

## Letter from U.S. federal prison

In December 2025 Carvajal issued a ten-page letter from a U.S. federal prison, addressed to "President Trump and the People of the United States," alleging that Maduro's government systematically used drug trafficking, criminal gangs, espionage networks, and even electoral technology as tools to undermine the United States.[1] The statement asserts that Venezuela's ruling elite operates as a "narco-terrorist organization" with global reach and explicit anti-American intent.[1]

Carvajal writes that he is "sitting in an American prison because I voluntarily plead guilty to the crimes charged against me: a narco-terrorism conspiracy," and frames the letter not as a political intervention but as an act of accountability — a decision to reveal "the full truth so that the United States can protect itself from the dangers witnessed for so many years."[1] He insists that even knowing he faced prosecution he acted "with the strongest conviction to dismantle Maduro's criminal regime and bring freedom to my country," and argues that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s policies toward Venezuela are "not only correct, but absolutely necessary to the United States' national security."[1]
