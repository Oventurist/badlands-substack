# Salim Said

Salim Said is a co-defendant convicted alongside [[aimee-bock|Aimee Bock]] in March 2025 in the $250 million [[feeding-our-future|Feeding Our Future]] fraud scheme.[1]

## The case

The Feeding Our Future prosecution remains the largest COVID-era fraud scheme prosecuted to date, with at least 78 defendants indicted and Somali-specific indictments filed as recently as November 2025.[1] Prosecutors allege that funds stolen from federal child-nutrition programs — nominally meant to serve an alleged "food desert" — were spent on luxury beachfront accommodations, high-end vehicles including a Rolls-Royce and a Lamborghini, travel, and Minnesota real estate.[1] Prosecutions in the scandal remain ongoing, and Badlands noted that this COVID-era public-benefit fraud predates the separate Somali-linked childcare scandal that later went viral on social media.[1]
