# Sanseito

Sanseito is a Japanese right-wing populist party that campaigns under a "Japanese First" slogan and rose from fringe status to become one of the biggest winners of Japan's upper house election held on July 20, 2025.[1] The party built its support around warnings of a "silent invasion" of immigrants, combined with pledges of tax cuts and expanded welfare spending.[1]

## Origins

Sanseito was born on YouTube during the COVID-19 pandemic, where it circulated claims about vaccinations and about a cabal of global elites, before breaking into mainstream electoral politics with its "Japanese First" campaign.[1]

## 2025 upper house election

In the July 2025 contest the party won 14 seats, adding to the single lawmaker it had secured in the 248-seat upper chamber three years earlier.[1] It held only three seats in Japan's more powerful lower house at the time of the election.[1] Party leader [[sohei-kamiya|Sohei Kamiya]], then 47, told the broadcaster Nippon Television after the vote that "the phrase Japanese First was meant to express rebuilding Japanese people's livelihoods by resisting globalism," adding that he was not calling for a complete ban on foreigners or for every foreigner to leave Japan.[1]

The same election saw Prime Minister [[shigeru-ishiba|Shigeru Ishiba]]'s Liberal Democratic Party and its coalition partner Komeito lose their upper house majority, leaving the government further dependent on opposition support after a lower house defeat the previous October.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary framed Sanseito's rise as a continuation of the nationalist current associated with the late [[shinzo-abe|Shinzo Abe]], and argued that a genuine populist-nationalist movement in Japan could turn the country into a real ally of the United States rather than a postwar vassal state.[1]
