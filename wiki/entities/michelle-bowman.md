# Michelle Bowman

Michelle Bowman is a governor of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]

## Position on FedNow and CBDCs

In a 2022 speech, Bowman said that [[fednow|FedNow]] could offer some of the same benefits as a central bank digital currency, thereby weakening the case for adopting a [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDC]] in the United States.[1] Badlands Media cites her remarks as part of the debate over whether FedNow is a genuine alternative to a digital dollar or an early prototype of one, a debate in which Fed chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] maintained that a CBDC remained years away from evaluation.[1]
