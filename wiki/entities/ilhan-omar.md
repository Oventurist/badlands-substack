# Ilhan Omar

Ilhan Omar is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Minnesota, a Somali-born naturalized citizen and a frequent critic of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Trump's January 2026 Truth Social post

Trump intensified the controversy over the Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal in a January 19, 2026 Truth Social post that directly targeted Omar, suggesting she be jailed or "sent back to Somalia" over the "$19 Billion Dollars in Minnesota Somalia Fraud."[1] He accused her of knowing "everything" about the scheme and described her as a "constant complainer who hates the USA."[1]

The post came as Republicans in [[congress|Congress]] alleged a deliberate Democratic strategy to build voting blocs through fraudulent entitlements, and as the administration pushed the [[scam-act|SCAM Act]] to expand citizenship revocation powers.[1]

## Absence from the June 2026 denaturalization list

When the Trump administration announced on June 8, 2026 that it was seeking to revoke the citizenship of 17 naturalized Americans in what it called the largest [[2026-denaturalization-campaign|denaturalization campaign]] ever undertaken, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Badlands had most often discussed the subject in the context of Omar, "the Minnesota congresswoman that allegedly married her brother to help him obtain lawful status."[2] Omar was not among the 17 named, which Ashe took as evidence that "obviously there's more to do."[2]

## 2026 State of the Union guest arrest

In February 2026, Omar made headlines after a guest she had invited to President Trump's 2026 State of the Union address was arrested by the [[united-states-capitol-police|US Capitol Police]] following the event.[3] Omar publicly stated that the detention was tied to immigration enforcement concerns and said the guest had been manhandled.[3]

Trump responded by criticizing Omar and Rep. [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] in a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post referencing disruptions and protest-related conduct surrounding the address, writing that the two had "screamed uncontrollably" at the event and should be "sent back from where they came."[3]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Omar sat "smack dab in the middle of the Somali scandal in her state" while raising the treatment of her State of the Union guest, and joked about a "Clinton-Willis '28" ticket, adding that "the foreigner is ineligible."[3]

## Participation in the January 2026 anti-ICE protests

Representative Ilhan Omar joined thousands of demonstrators at a Minneapolis rally during the January 2026 nationwide anti-ICE protest wave, which followed the fatal shooting of [[renee-nicole-good|Renee Nicole Good]] by an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agent in the city.[4] The protests were organized under the banner "ICE Out For Good" by [[indivisible|Indivisible]] and allied groups, whose organizers claimed more than 1,000 events nationwide with the largest gatherings in Washington, D.C. and Philadelphia.[4]

## Removal from the House Foreign Affairs Committee (2023)

On Thursday, February 2, 2023, the Republican-led House passed a resolution removing Omar from the [[house-foreign-affairs-committee|House Foreign Affairs Committee]] by an unofficial vote of 218-211, along party lines.[5] House Republicans argued that Omar, a Minnesota lawmaker, should not sit on the committee given past statements related to Israel that had sparked controversy and that Republicans and some fellow Democrats had criticized as antisemitic.[5]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] said [[congress|Congress]] had "for far too long" failed to deal with character defects and bad faith among representatives and senators, calling Omar "one of the most corrupt, at least what we know in the public domain," and expressing hope that the GOP would use its increased control to "clear out all the corrupt influences within the government."[5]

## Trump's Thanksgiving 2025 attack

In a Thanksgiving 2025 Truth Social statement on immigration, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] described Omar as the worst member of [[congress|Congress]], writing that she was "always wrapped in her swaddling hijab" and repeating the allegation that she likely entered the United States illegally on the theory that "you are not allowed to marry your brother."[6] He charged that she does nothing but complain about the United States and its Constitution and about how badly she is treated.[6] The statement paired the attack on Omar with criticism of Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] over Somali resettlement and with Trump's announced permanent pause on migration from Third World countries.[6]
