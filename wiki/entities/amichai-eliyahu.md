# Amichai Eliyahu

**Amichai Eliyahu** is an Israeli politician who served as Minister of Heritage in the government of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and who became known internationally for hardline public statements on the conduct of Israel's wars in Gaza and Lebanon.[1]

## Statements on the 2024 Lebanon ceasefire

In November 2024, as US envoy [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]] pressed Israel to accept an American ceasefire proposal with [[lebanon|Lebanon]], Eliyahu said publicly that any ceasefire deal would amount to no more than a "tactical delay" until [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] re-entered office.[1]

The remark was cited by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as evidence that Netanyahu's apparent openness to a ceasefire was "lip service," and that several cabinet members had come forward "to voice such hubris."[1] Eliyahu's comment was placed alongside a recorded statement by Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] expressing the hope that the incoming Trump administration would help pressure Palestinians into leaving their land, as part of an argument that the zealots surrounding Netanyahu were inadvertently exposing a plan intended to unfold over a much longer time scale.[1]
