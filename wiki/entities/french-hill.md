# French Hill

French Hill is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives who serves as Chair of the House Financial Services Committee, the panel with primary jurisdiction over banking, securities, and oversight of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]

## Defense of Jerome Powell

Hill drew attention in January 2026 when he publicly defended [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] after the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] opened a criminal investigation into Powell's congressional testimony about renovations to the Federal Reserve's headquarters.[1] Hill said that he had known Powell since the two "worked together at Treasury during the George H.W. Bush Administration," and described him as "a man of integrity with a strong commitment to public service," adding that despite policy disagreements over the years he had found Powell "forthright, candid, and a person of the highest integrity."[1]

Badlands commentary treated the statement as self-defeating. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that invoking a shared tenure in the Treasury Department of the George H. W. Bush administration was a "hilariously tone deaf failed flex," reading it as an inadvertent reminder of the institutional continuity that Badlands writers argue links legacy Washington figures to the entrenched financial establishment.[1] In that framing, Hill's character reference for Powell reinforced rather than rebutted the case for dismantling what Ashe called the "institutions and fake power centers" of the administrative state.[1]

## Significance

Hill's position as Financial Services Chair makes his stance materially relevant to any congressional response to the Powell probe, since his committee would handle Fed oversight hearings arising from the matter.[1] His defense placed him alongside Senator [[thom-tillis|Thom Tillis]] among Republican lawmakers who reacted critically or protectively toward the Fed chair rather than supporting the investigation.[1]
