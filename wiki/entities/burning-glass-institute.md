# Burning Glass Institute

The Burning Glass Institute is a labor-analytics research firm cited in coverage of the United States labor market.[1]

Its director of economic research, Guy Berger, was quoted by [[business-insider|Business Insider]] in August 2024 assessing the deterioration in US employment data following the July jobs report, saying that "there was a lot of data that was sort of in the greenish area, and now there's a lot more data that's flashing yellow lights."[1] The remark appeared in an article excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] arguing that America's post-pandemic hiring boom had ended, a subject covered at [[july-2024-us-jobs-report|the July 2024 US jobs report]].[1]
