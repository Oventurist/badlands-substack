# Bundesnachrichtendienst

The Bundesnachrichtendienst (BND) is Germany's foreign intelligence agency. It became the subject of controversy in March 2025 over reporting that its assessment of the origins of the COVID-19 pandemic had been suppressed by successive German governments.[1]

## Covid origins assessment

A joint investigation by the German newspapers Süddeutsche Zeitung and Die Zeit alleged that Germany covered up an explosive BND intelligence report finding a strong probability that the pandemic was caused by a laboratory accident in China.[1] The BND concluded in 2020 that an accident in a laboratory in Wuhan led to the outbreak of the coronavirus pandemic.[1]

The assessment drew on public records, data from the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]], and a German intelligence operation known as "Saaremaa," and found an "80 to 95 per cent" probability that COVID-19 originated in Wuhan.[1] The BND presented the findings to then-Chancellor [[angela-merkel|Angela Merkel]], who according to Süddeutsche Zeitung chose to keep the information "under lock and key."[1] Her successor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]] was likewise said to have turned a blind eye to the report.[1]
