# Hassan Sheikh Mohamud

Hassan Sheikh Mohamud is the President of [[somalia|Somalia]].[1]

## Response to Trump remarks

In early January 2026, Mohamud issued a public statement condemning remarks attributed to US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In December 2025 Trump had reportedly said, "We can go one way or the other, and we're going to go the wrong way, if we keep taking in garbage into our country," adding that Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] "is garbage. She's garbage. Her friends are garbage."[1] Mohamud said millions of Somalis contribute positively to societies around the world, but when asked about immigration enforcement he reportedly said, "We respect the sovereignty of other nations when it comes to immigration enforcement."[1]

## Mogadishu elections

Somalia reportedly held its first genuine elections since 1969 on December 25, 2025.[1] Dr Hussein Sheikh Mohamud, Senior Advisor to the President on Constitutional Affairs and Deputy Secretary General of the Justice and Solidarity Party, wrote in an opinion piece that after "56 years of appointed councils, clan negotiations, and backroom deals, over 233,000 Mogadishu residents actually cast votes for their local representatives," describing "Real voters. Real ballots. Real counting."[1] Several opposition factions boycotted the vote, citing a lack of inclusivity, questions about the legal framework and a rushed timeline; the presidential advisor countered that "they boycotted because they knew they would lose."[1]
