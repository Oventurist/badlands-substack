# USS Milius

The USS Milius is a United States Navy warship assigned to the forward-deployed forces of the [[us-7th-fleet|U.S. 7th Fleet]] in the western Pacific.[1]

## April 2023 Taiwan Strait transit

On Sunday, April 16, 2023, the USS Milius transited the [[taiwan-strait|Taiwan Strait]] in what was the first known U.S. warship passage through the waterway since China conducted a large-scale encirclement exercise around Taiwan.[1] The U.S. 7th Fleet characterized the transit as routine, stating that the ship "transited through a corridor in the Strait that is beyond the territorial sea of any coastal State."[1]

Beijing protested the transit the following Monday, calling it "public hype," with the [[eastern-theater-command|Eastern Theater Command]] spokesperson Shi Yilu saying Chinese forces stood ready at any time to "resolutely safeguard the country's sovereignty, safety, and regional peace and stability."[1]

## Context

The transit followed Chinese air and sea drills conducted in retaliation for Taiwanese President [[tsai-ing-wen|Tsai Ing-wen]] meeting House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] in California on April 5, 2023.[1] Commentary from [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the Milius transit within a broader pattern of U.S. warship posturing that could feed a Gulf of Tonkin-style [[scare-event-theory|Scare Event]] scenario in the strait.[1]
