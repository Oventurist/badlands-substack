# Alpha Warrior

Alpha Warrior is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host who co-presented [[sitrep|SITREP]] with [[canncon|CannCon]] on Thursdays at 9pm ET as of the January 2023 schedule, a program devoted to the stories of American veterans and the sacrifices made on behalf of the nation.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## Dismissal of charges (January 2025)

On Monday, January 13, 2025, the government dropped all charges against Alpha Warrior, a development [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] highlighted with congratulations at the top of that day's news brief.[3]

## Written work

Alpha Warrior is a U.S. military veteran and co-host of several Badlands Media shows, including SITREP and Roll Call, who also publishes his own work on Substack.[4] On August 11, 2023 he published "Unveiling the Mar-a-Lago Raid – 1 Year Delta," a retrospective marking one year since the [[mar-a-lago-raid|FBI search of Mar-a-Lago]] and subtitled "Indictments & the Erosion of America."[4] The essay recounted the August 8, 2022 search, the warrant authorised by [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] and approved by Magistrate Judge [[bruce-reinhart|Bruce Reinhart]], and the subsequent [[2023-trump-classified-documents-indictment|37-count indictment]] of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], before broadening into an extended argument about [[politically-motivated-imprisonment|politically motivated imprisonment]] and the reforms needed to reclaim the republic.[4] Its artwork was generated using MidJourney AI, and it closed with a quotation from Martin Luther King Jr.[4]
