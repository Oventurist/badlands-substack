# Cyrus Scofield

**Cyrus Scofield** was the American minister who produced the Scofield Reference Bible, the annotated edition that popularized dispensationalist theology among English-speaking evangelicals.[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* describes Scofield as "a convicted felon turned theological puppet" who assisted a circle of Zionist organizers in rewriting Christian doctrine.[1] In the essay's account, the Scofield Reference Bible was the instrument by which Zionism was inserted into evangelical eschatology, producing what the essay calls "a biblical forgery. A psyop in a pew."[1]

The essay places Scofield's work downstream of ideological groundwork laid by Samuel Untermeyer, Rabbi Stephen Wise, and [[chaim-weizmann|Chaim Weizmann]], all of whom it says regarded [[zionism|Zionism]] as a political weapon rather than a return to faith, and alongside the 1917 [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] addressed to Walter Rothschild.[1] The resulting doctrinal current is linked in the essay to [[princeton-university|Princeton]]-trained elites who helped encode [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionism]] into U.S. foreign policy through think tanks, NGOs, and the Christian-Zionist political machine.[1]
