# Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud

**Abdulaziz** is identified in Chapter 9 of the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] as the former King of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] who died in 2015.[1] The chapter introduces him as the starting point of a succession chain that it treats as essential background to the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]].[1]

## Succession and its consequences

The chapter states that after Abdulaziz's death, [[salman-bin-abdulaziz|Prince Salman]] acceded to the throne, and that the most important consequence of that accession was the elevation of Salman's son, [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], to Crown Prince in 2017.[1] It is following this appointment, the author writes, that the plan to purge the Saudi government and royal family was set in motion.[1]

## Note on identification

The chapter's use of the name is compressed. The Saudi monarch who died in 2015 and was succeeded by Salman was King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz; the founder of the modern Saudi state, Abdulaziz ibn Saud, died in 1953. The article's phrasing conflates the two, and this page reproduces the article's account while flagging the discrepancy.[1]
