# Anti-Saloon League

The Anti-Saloon League was an offshoot of the [[womens-christian-temperance-union|Women's Christian Temperance Union]] and one of the organizations that campaigned for American [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1]

Together with the WCTU, the League is described in [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]] as part of a small, well-financed and highly organized circle backed by the Astors, the Vanderbilts, the Warburgs, and the Rockefellers.[1] In the account given by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the League's agitation was instrumental in producing the legal ban on alcohol that in turn created large-scale organized crime and opened the United States to British-supplied narcotics traffic.[1]
