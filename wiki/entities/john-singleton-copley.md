# John Singleton Copley

John Singleton Copley was an American painter born the same year as [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]], who studied with West at the [[royal-academy-of-fine-arts|Royal Academy of Fine Arts]] in England and appears in West's 1795 group painting *The Royal Academicians*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## The death of Chatham

In 1781 Copley produced his own rendition of the death of [[william-pitt-the-elder|Lord Chatham]] in Parliament, three years after the event and after West's version of the same moment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Copley's treatment juxtaposes the scene with the 1588 defeat of the Spanish Armada depicted on the wall behind — the moment two centuries earlier at which England became the seat of a world maritime empire, replacing the Spanish Habsburg Empire.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] reads this as expressive of the past grandeur of the British Empire's romantic memories dying with Chatham at a moment of historical change, since the success of the American Revolution raised the possibility that England's maritime primacy would no longer hold.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

Copley is listed among the American painters trained by West in England alongside Charles Willson Peale, Rembrandt Peale, Gilbert Stuart and John Trumbull.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
