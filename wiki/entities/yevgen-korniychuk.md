# Yevgen Korniychuk

Yevgen Korniychuk is the Ukrainian Ambassador to [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Statements on the "axis of evil"

In an interview with the Israeli outlet Ynet, Korniychuk stated that [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and Israel are "fighting from a different angle against the same axis of evil."[1] He highlighted shared threats from Iranian drones and their proxies, noting that [[russia|Russia]] has used Iranian drone variants against Ukraine while Israel has faced thousands of drone attacks from [[iran|Iran]] and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]

## Drone cooperation

Korniychuk said Ukraine had already sent about 200 drone experts to the Middle East to assist the United States and Arab states in countering Iranian drones.[1] He expressed regret that Israel had shown little interest in cooperating on anti-drone technology despite Ukraine's proven expertise, and noted frustration among many Israelis that their country was missing opportunities to better protect its soldiers.[1] He also lamented that President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelenskyy]]'s recent regional visits for security cooperation had not led to deeper engagement with Israel.[1]

Both nations are among the top recipients of U.S. military aid, yet bilateral relations remain strained; Korniychuk emphasized his role in seeking common ground for mutual benefit on security issues.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the ambassador's remark as a signal, arguing that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was deliberately distancing Israel from the Zelensky government, and pointing to Zelensky's public accusation that Israel purchased "stolen" Ukrainian grain from Russia.[1]
