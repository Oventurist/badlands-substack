# Brian Brooks

**Brian Brooks** is an American banking regulator described as a Bitcoin maximalist who was appointed to head the [[office-of-the-comptroller-of-the-currency|Office of the Comptroller of the Currency]] on May 21, 2020 by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steve Mnuchin]].[1]

Within two months of the appointment, by July 22, 2020, Mnuchin and Brooks had made it legal for banks to custody bitcoin.[1] The article "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" treats this action as evidence that Trump quietly took steps to give Americans access to buy and sell [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], notwithstanding his public scepticism toward the asset — asking why he would have allowed it if he genuinely disliked Bitcoin.[1]

Brooks's appointment is placed alongside the blocking of [[facebook|Facebook]]'s [[diem|Diem]] stablecoin as a paired regulatory move: a corporate digital currency stopped while the decentralised alternative was given a legal on-ramp through the banking system.[1]
