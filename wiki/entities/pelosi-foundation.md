# Pelosi Foundation

The **Pelosi Foundation** is named in Chapter 9 of the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] as one of several American foundations that Q pointed to as recipients of what the chapter calls "dirty Saudi money."[1]

## In the Chapter 9 reading of drop #70

Working through Q drop #70 of November 4, 2017, the chapter lists the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], the [[mccain-institute|McCain Institute]], the Pelosi Foundation and Senator [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] together as beneficiaries of Saudi funds, and reads the grouping as Q's demonstration that compromised individuals exist on both sides of the political aisle.[1] The chapter connects the entity to [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] only by name and provides no further detail on its structure, funding or activities.[1]

Later in the same chapter, the author generalises the point, writing that following the money shows how corrupt most of these "foundations" are and that most function primarily as slush funds and laundromats.[1]

## Assessment

This page records the article's claim; the source offers no documentation, and no widely recognised charitable entity operates under the name "Pelosi Foundation." The entry should be treated as low confidence and as a reflection of the Badlands series' interpretive framework rather than an established organisational profile.[1]
