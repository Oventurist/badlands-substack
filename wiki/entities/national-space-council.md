# National Space Council

The National Space Council is the White House's top space policy-coordinating body, a panel of cabinet members traditionally chaired by the vice president.[1]

## Revival and restructuring

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] revived the council during his first term, and reporting in 2025 indicated he had considered abolishing it.[1] His December 18, 2025 executive order [[ensuring-american-space-superiority|"Ensuring American Space Superiority"]] appeared to cancel the body outright, reorganizing national space policy coordination under chief science adviser [[michael-kratsios|Michael Kratsios]].[1]

An administration official subsequently said the council would not be cancelled, and suggested it would live on under the White House's Office of Technology Policy in a different structure in which the president, rather than the vice president, would be chairman.[1] Badlands commentary interpreted the change as Trump displacing Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] from the chairmanship.[1]
