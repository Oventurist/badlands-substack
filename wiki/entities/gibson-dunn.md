# Gibson Dunn

Gibson Dunn is a large American law firm. In October 2025, Gibson Dunn partner [[orin-snyder|Orin Snyder]] led the launch of a network of more than 40 large law firms formed to provide free legal assistance to Jewish people facing antisemitism in the wake of the October 7, 2023, attack on Israel and the subsequent war in Gaza.[1]

Snyder described the effort as "building an army of lawyers who will be a virtual law firm dealing with these problems."[1] Participating organizations included [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], [[cooley|Cooley]], [[covington-and-burling|Covington & Burling]], and the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]].[1] The initiative emerged as law firms' pro bono work came under scrutiny from the Trump administration, with many firms retreating from more controversial representation of parties challenging the government; combating antisemitism proved relatively safe ground, and nine firms that struck deals in spring 2025 to avoid punitive executive orders had agreed to perform pro bono work on issues the president supports, including fighting antisemitism.[1]
