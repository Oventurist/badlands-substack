# Haj Amin al-Husseini

Haj Amin, the Grand Mufti of British Mandate Palestine (rendered "Grand Mullah Haj Amin" in the source), is presented in the article as a British asset and provocateur.[1]

## Collaboration with British intelligence

The article states that Haj Amin frequently collaborated with British intelligence from Britain's Cairo office, including with the [[muslim-brotherhood]], in order to assassinate moderate Arabs seeking economic cooperation with the Jews, and to kill Jews so as to stoke revenge sentiments — a program the article compares to the earlier practice of keeping Protestant and Catholic wars ablaze in Europe.[1]

His story as a British asset is said to be told in full by [[cynthia-chung]] in her book *The Empire on Which the Black Sun Never Set*, specifically Chapter 11, "Nazis, the British, and the Middle East."[1]

## Context

The article situates Haj Amin within a broader pattern in which British intelligence simultaneously supported Islamist movements — from Jamal al-Din al-Afghani, founder of Salafiyyism, to the Muslim Brotherhood — and the most fascist and violent Zionist ideologues, arguing this is not contradictory but united by the principle of maintaining global dominance for the "Church of the British Empire."[1]
