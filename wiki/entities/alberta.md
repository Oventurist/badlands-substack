# Alberta

Alberta is the western Canadian province that supplies the great majority of [[canada|Canada]]'s hydrocarbon output and the focus of a growing independence movement organized through the [[alberta-prosperity-project|Alberta Prosperity Project]].

## Energy weight

As summarized in the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]], Alberta provides 80 percent of Canada's oil and 60 percent of its natural gas, and accounts for most of the country's energy exports. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that this makes the province "the powerhouse province" whose departure would leave "greater Canada" as the only loser in an independence settlement.

## Independence campaign

The Alberta Prosperity Project has met three times with [[state-department|State Department]] officials in Washington since April 2025 and is seeking a $500 billion United States line of credit to support the province in the event of a successful independence referendum. No referendum has been called. Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] has said he expects Washington to respect Canadian sovereignty and that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has never raised Alberta separatism with him. Badlands commentary frames possible Albertan independence as separation "from Britain" and speculates that other productive provinces might follow.
