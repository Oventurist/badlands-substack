# Kitty Little

Dr. Kitty Little is the source of a testimony recounted in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 4," which DeLarme says he encountered while searching for firsthand accounts of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]]'s exclusive gatherings and which, if true, sheds insight into the long-term planning capabilities of the secretive group.[1]

Little claimed to have worked for Britain's Ministry of Aircraft Production during the Second World War and later for the Atomic Energy Research Establishment.[1] She recounted attending a Labour Party "study group" at Oxford University in 1940 where the speaker, a young man, claimed membership in a deliberately nameless group plotting a "Marxist takeover" of Britain, Europe and parts of Africa, whose members would pose as moderates so that critics could be dismissed as right-wingers.[1] The speaker said he had been chosen to head the group's political section and expected to become prime minister of the United Kingdom; Little identified him as [[harold-wilson|Harold Wilson]].[1]

DeLarme treats her account as evidence that the nameless group Wilson described is what is now colloquially called the Bilderbergers.[1]
