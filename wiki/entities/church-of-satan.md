# Church of Satan

The Church of Satan is the satanic organization founded by [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey]] into which [[mark-passio|Mark Passio]] says he was recruited as a teenager circa 1995, on the strength of his ability to write ideologically accurate satanic song lyrics.[1]

## Grotto structure

The organization is described in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" as operating through local chapters known as Grottos.[1] LaVey is said to have appointed Passio to the position of high priest and granted him authority over a Grotto in the Philadelphia area, where he attended and oversaw midnight meetings of the membership.[1]

## Membership claims

Passio's account emphasizes the social composition of those meetings: rather than the disaffected youth he expected, he reported encountering politicians, lawyers, teachers, policemen, priests and other influential figures.[1] Attendees spoke of an ancient tradition and of pride in the ideology of Satanism as a bond uniting a diverse group of society's movers and shakers, and claimed the group had orchestrated almost every major event of history reaching back into ancient pre-history.[1] Members further asserted that their mind control over the general population was so near-absolute that a public declaration of their identity and agenda would simply not be believed.[1]

The article distinguishes this milieu from the Hollywood image of occultism — ouija boards, black masses and men dressed like devils — arguing that such theatrics are minor aspects of a broader phenomenon better understood through the word's root meaning of that which is hidden from sight.[1]



## Aquino and the Temple of Set split

[[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] joined the Church of Satan in 1966 after initiation into [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey's]] Californian coven, and following a claimed mystical experience in 1975 broke away to found the [[temple-of-set|Temple of Set]], taking 28 members with him as founding priests.[2] The two bodies nonetheless continued to support each other and both promoted [[aleister-crowley|Crowley's]] Thelemic doctrine, "Do What Thou Wilt Shall be the Whole of the Law."[2] Ehret also notes overlaps between the church and the Manson murders: Susan Atkins, who stabbed [[sharon-tate|Sharon Tate]], had performed as a topless vampire in LaVey's Witches' Sabbath ceremony, and former follower Jay Sebring was among the victims.[2]
