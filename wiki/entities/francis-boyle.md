# Francis Boyle

Francis Boyle, J.D., Ph.D., is a professor of international law at the University of Illinois and a bioweapons expert who drafted the [[biological-weapons-anti-terrorism-act-of-1989|Biological Weapons Anti-Terrorism Act of 1989]].[1]

## Position on the WHO instruments

Boyle has been among the most prominent legal critics of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]'s proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and the amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]], describing them as "an attempt to take over the United States of America by using the WHO as a front for that purpose."[1]

He argued that the effort was "being backed by the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]], the Chinese communist dictatorship, Big Pharma and the biowarfare industry."[1] His assessment aligned with that of attorney [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]], who warned that passage of the instruments "would basically be the end of our national sovereignty."[1]

## Detailed warnings in "Behold, a Trojan Horse" (May 2023)

In a May 2023 Badlands Media article, "Behold, a Trojan Horse," Boyle was quoted at length opposing the WHO instruments.[2] He argued the pandemic treaty "will automatically come into effect immediately upon its signature," a feature he said was without precedent among the treaties he had studied going back to the [[first-hague-peace-conference-of-1899|First Hague Peace Conference of 1899]].[2] Boyle warned that under the proposed instruments "a medical global police state" would issue orders reaching down to U.S. state and local health authorities, governors, attorneys general, surgeons general, and other democratically elected officials, contravening them.[2] The article listed his credentials as a professor of international law at the University of Illinois and a bioweapons expert who drafted the Biological Weapons Anti-Terrorism Act of 1989.[2]
