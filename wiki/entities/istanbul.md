# Istanbul

Istanbul is the largest city of [[turkey|Turkey]], straddling the Bosphorus strait, and in June 2026 it became the venue for a surprise concert by rapper and designer [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] that Badlands treated as a marker of shifting global cultural authority.[1]

## June 2026 Kanye West concert

Thousands of people gathered near the Bosphorus for the late-night event, which Turkish media described as one of the city's largest recent live music gatherings tied to an international artist.[1] The performance followed a string of canceled appearances in Europe, after multiple European venues and festival organizers distanced themselves from West over his public remarks and online activity.[1]

Badlands framed the show as evidence that Istanbul — and Turkey more broadly — has taken on a growing role as an alternative destination for artists, influencers, and public figures facing restrictions or reputational pressure in parts of Europe and North America.[1] See [[kanye-west-istanbul-concert-2026|Kanye West Istanbul concert (2026)]].[1]
