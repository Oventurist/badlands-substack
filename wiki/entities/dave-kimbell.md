# Dave Kimbell

Dave Kimbell is the chief executive officer of [[ulta-beauty|Ulta Beauty]], the American beauty retail chain. In April 2024 his remarks to investors at a JPMorgan Chase & Co. conference triggered a sharp sell-off in the company's shares.[1]

## April 2024 demand warning

Kimbell told investors that first-quarter demand for beauty products had cooled, saying, "We have seen a slowdown in the total category."[1] He added that the company had entered the year expecting the category to moderate after several years of strong growth, but "did not anticipate it would continue at the rate that it's been growing."[1] He forecast first-quarter comparable sales at the lower end of the low-single-digit growth range previously guided for the first half of the fiscal year, and acknowledged that Ulta had lost market share in the prestige makeup and hair categories.[1]

Kimbell described the slowdown as "a bit earlier and a bit bigger" than anticipated, pointing to high credit card debt and student loan payments as financial pressures weighing on consumers.[1] Oppenheimer analyst Rupesh Parikh wrote that his team was "surprised by the moderation" and unclear whether it represented a shorter-term blip.[1] Ulta shares closed down 15 percent, the largest daily decline since March 2020.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the warning as evidence of collapsing discretionary spending under [[bidenomics]], arguing that households were retrenching from luxury beauty products and that each purchasing decision had become an activist choice.[1]
