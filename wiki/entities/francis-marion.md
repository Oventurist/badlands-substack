# Francis Marion

Francis Marion was an American Revolutionary War partisan commander, popularly remembered as the "Swamp Fox," and the subject of Part 1 of [[patriot-chronicles|The Patriot Chronicles]] by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]].[1]

## Treatment in The Patriot Chronicles

The introduction to the series closed with a teaser headed "Beware of the Swamp…" that previewed the Marion installment without naming him directly.[1] It described how, long before the emergence of the novel [[deep-state|Deep State]], the swamp belonged to "another apex predator" — a phantom leading a host of hardened war veterans who came to personify the teachings of Sun Tzu and forever changed the Art of War.[1] Armed with nothing more than muskets and their wits, these patriots were said to have stopped the most powerful global empire of all time in its tracks at the most critical moment in the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]], and in doing so the phantom became a legend.[1] The author invited readers who assumed they already knew the story to reconsider, before directing them to Part 1.[1]

The introduction was illustrated with William Washington's painting "Marion and His Men in the Swamp," reinforcing the guerrilla-in-the-wetlands imagery around which the installment was built.[1]
