# Troy Carter

Troy Carter is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Louisiana and a member of the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]]'s Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Protection Subcommittee.[1]

At the subcommittee's February 6, 2024 hearing on securing [[operational-technology|operational technology]] in the water sector, Carter raised saltwater intrusion affecting Louisiana water systems and the resulting need for alternative water supplies.[1] [[kevin-morley|Kevin Morley]] of the [[american-water-works-association|American Water Works Association]] replied that planning for alternative water supply is "certainly a critical need," that the situation in that portion of Louisiana had been challenging, and that addressing it requires a collaborative approach among the [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]], the Army Corps of Engineers, and [[cisa|CISA]], adding that the [[water-sector-coordinating-council|Water Sector Coordinating Council]] had made emergency water supply a critical priority.[1]

The Badlands account contrasted this answer with the panel's dismissal of non-digital redundancy as a defense against cyber attack, arguing the two positions were at odds.[1]
