# Pecos Wellness

Pecos Wellness is a supplement company introduced in September 2024 as a new sponsor of the Badlands Substack.[1]

The company offered the Badlands audience 10% off its Pecos Gold Resin supplement using the promo code "Badlands," promoted through a banner linking to badlandsmedia.tv.[1] The sponsorship appeared at the top of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] edition covering the news of Thursday, September 12, 2024, part of the reader-supported and sponsor-supported model by which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] keeps its content free.[1]
