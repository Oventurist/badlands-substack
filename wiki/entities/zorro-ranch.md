# Zorro Ranch

Zorro Ranch is the New Mexico property owned by [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] In Badlands commentary on the deaths of [[gene-hackman|Gene Hackman]] and [[betsy-arakawa|Betsy Arakawa]], the ranch was noted as lying 39 miles from Hackman's Santa Fe home.[1] The same commentary described Zorro Ranch as the place where Epstein pursued interests in eugenics and transhumanism.[1] The contributor was explicit that no direct connection between Hackman and Epstein could be shown, presenting the geographic proximity only as a data point worth examining.[1]
