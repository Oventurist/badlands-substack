# Scroll and Key

**Scroll & Key** is a senior secret society at [[yale-university|Yale University]] described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as performing functions similar to [[skull-and-bones|Skull & Bones]], grooming candidates for strategic intelligence work.[1]

The article places Scroll & Key within Yale's role as the "covert recruitment center" of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]], a campus whose societies feed the CIA, the [[state-department|State Department]] and transnational deal-broker networks.[1] Like the other Ivy societies catalogued in the piece, it is characterized as an induction point where "future agents of empire are inducted, compromised, and sworn to ideological fidelity."[1]

## Standing among Yale's senior societies

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" names Scroll and Key as one of the three [[yale-university|Yale]] senior societies that have stood out among all others for ages, alongside [[wolfs-head|Wolf's Head]] and [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]].[2] While Bones is described as the elite of the elite, the article holds that only Scroll and Key can claim a near-equal influence on American affairs over the past 160 years.[2] Like its counterparts it is not part of any nationwide public association, is cloaked in secrecy, and serves as a recruiting ground for young men destined for careers in government, law, finance and other influential sectors of American life.[2]
