# Ebrahim Zolfaghari

Ebrahim Zolfaghari is a spokesman for the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] of [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## March 2026 press briefing video

Over the weekend of March 21–22, 2026, the IRGC released a video showing Zolfaghari giving a press briefing in which he mocked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] using the president's two signature phrases, "You're fired" and "Thank you for your attention to this matter."[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who surfaced the clip, treated it as evidence of the meme-literate character of the information war surrounding the conflict, summarizing it as "TrumpaMania, brother."[1]

The video circulated in the same news cycle as the [[cardboard-ayatollah|Cardboard Ayatollah]] meme campaign and Trump's 48-hour ultimatum over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]
