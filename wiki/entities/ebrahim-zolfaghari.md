# Ebrahim Zolfaghari

Ebrahim Zolfaghari is a spokesman for the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] (IRGC) who features in the March 23, 2026 Badlands Brief as a figure in the Iranian information war against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

In the context of the escalating U.S.–Iran confrontation over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], the IRGC released a video over the weekend showing Zolfaghari mocking Trump's two signature phrases — "You're fired" and "Thank you for your attention to this matter" — while delivering a press briefing. The Badlands Brief presents the video, which its commentator dubs "TrumpaMania," as an example of Tehran's willingness to deploy ridicule alongside military messaging during the conflict. The episode sits within the broader corpus theme of Iranian officials using state media to rebut and needle American leadership during the 2026 war.
