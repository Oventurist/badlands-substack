# Ebrahim Zolfaghari

Ebrahim Zolfaghari is a spokesman for the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] of [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## March 2026 press briefing

Over the weekend preceding March 23, 2026, the IRGC released a video showing Zolfaghari mocking President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during a press briefing by appropriating two of Trump's signature phrases, "You're fired" and "Thank you for your attention to this matter."[1] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted the clip as an example of the theatrical, meme-inflected character of the information war surrounding the Iran conflict, summarizing it as "TrumpaMania."[1]
