# Bob Goodlatte

Bob Goodlatte (R-VA) served as Chairman of the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]]. Badlands Media presents him as a central figure in the covert criminal investigations of Obama-administration conduct.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The DOJ slush fund investigation

In October 2017 Goodlatte told fellow lawmakers that internal Department of Justice documents confirmed the existence of a departmental "slush fund" under the Obama administration, and that DOJ officials "went out of their way" to exclude conservative groups.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He made the claim just ahead of a House vote on a bill barring government officials from entering into or enforcing settlement agreements that provide payments to entities other than the United States.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Goodlatte said the legislation was needed after an extended committee investigation found the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] had systematically subverted Congress's budget authority by using settlements from financial institutions to funnel money to "left-wing activist groups."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Declaring that "it is not every day in congressional investigations that we find a smoking gun," he released emails he said proved the practice; his committee found roughly $1 billion directed to outside organizations over two years, and in January had asked the DOJ to preserve all related documents.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Role in the wider investigations

The article says Goodlatte and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] played major roles in criminal investigations conducted in the background before both resigned from Congress, and that they remained active afterward because much of the evidence is classified.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) A [[qanon|Q]] drop asked who briefed Goodlatte and Gowdy on classified intelligence regarding the DOJ and FBI; the author identifies Ezra Cohen Watnick, General Flynn's deputy, whom Trump later installed at the DOJ for that purpose.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] launched the [[john-huber|Huber]] investigation in response to a request from Goodlatte, Gowdy and Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) After completing their House investigations, Goodlatte and Gowdy handed their evidence to Grassley in the Senate.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Trump publicly thanked both men, telling them their work "will never be forgotten."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The House-to-Senate handoff

Badlands Media's "Do You Believe in Coincidences?" names Goodlatte, with [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]], as a recipient of declassified material purposely provided by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for the House investigations into the Russian collusion hoax.[2] A Q drop reproduced in the article twice repeats that "Goodlatte, Gowdy and Sessions all resigned," which the author reads as the deliberate conversion of the three into expert witnesses guiding the [[united-states-senate|Senate]]'s successor investigations into [[spygate|Spygate]] and [[obamagate|Obamagate]].[2]
