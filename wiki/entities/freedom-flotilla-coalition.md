# Freedom Flotilla Coalition

The **Freedom Flotilla Coalition** is an activist network that organizes maritime convoys carrying humanitarian aid toward the Gaza Strip in defiance of the Israeli naval blockade.[1]

## October 2025 interception

In early October 2025 the Israeli military intercepted and boarded boats belonging to the coalition, including vessels named *Thousand Madleens to Gaza* and *Conscience*, as they approached Gaza as part of the wider aid flotilla movement.[1] The interception was reported through an official statement from the [[global-sumud-flotilla|Global Sumud Flotilla]], which said that "at roughly 120nms away from Gaza, Israel has attacked our flotilla," that at least two boats had been boarded, that most livestreams had been disconnected, and that the military was attempting to divert the boats' route.[1]

The episode followed by days Israel's deportation of around 170 activists — among them [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]] — who had been detained when Global Sumud Flotilla boats were stopped.[1] Reporting of the interception carried the activist framing that "pirates are here again."[1]
