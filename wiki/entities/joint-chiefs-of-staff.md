# Joint Chiefs of Staff

The Joint Chiefs of Staff is the body of senior United States military leaders whose chairman serves as the principal military adviser to the president; in April 2026 its chairman, [[dan-caine|Dan "Razin'" Caine]], was assigned a direct diplomatic role in the [[israel-lebanon-ceasefire|Israel–Lebanon ceasefire]] effort.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he had directed Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], together with the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, to work with [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]] to achieve a lasting peace following the announcement of a ten-day ceasefire.[1] The assignment placed the chairman alongside civilian diplomatic leadership rather than in a purely advisory military capacity, reflecting the mixed political-military character of the negotiation.[1]

The Joint Chiefs sit atop the uniformed side of the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]], the same institution whose 2026 outreach to automakers and civilian manufacturers to scale weapons production was read as a shift toward a sustained wartime footing and an emerging [[war-economy|war economy]].[1]
