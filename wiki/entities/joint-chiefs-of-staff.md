# Joint Chiefs of Staff

The Joint Chiefs of Staff is the body of senior uniformed leaders of the United States armed forces that advises the president, the secretary of defense, and the National Security Council. Its chairman is the highest-ranking military officer in the country. In the Badlands Media corpus the institution is discussed principally through the role of its chairman within the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] and the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].

In the April 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-07c|Badlands Brief]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he had directed Chairman [[dan-caine|Dan "Razin" Caine]], together with Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], to work with [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]] toward a lasting peace agreement following the announcement of a ten-day ceasefire. The assignment situates the chairmanship in an explicitly diplomatic function rather than a purely advisory military one.
