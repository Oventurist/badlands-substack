# Free Democratic Party (FDP)

The Free Democratic Party (Freie Demokratische Partei, FDP) is a German classical-liberal party and the smallest member of the "traffic light" coalition that governed Germany alongside the [[social-democratic-party-of-germany|Social Democratic Party]] and the Greens as of October 2023.[1]

## Survival threshold crisis

Infratest dimap polling published in October 2023 put the FDP at 5 percent — exactly the threshold required to enter the German parliament — leaving the party under threat of being removed entirely and placing the coalition on increasingly shaky ground.[1] Commentary at the time raised the prospect of an early FDP exit from the coalition and the government's collapse.[1] In the same survey the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] polled at 23 percent, more than the SPD and FDP combined.[1]

## Ejection from the coalition (November 2024)

On the evening of Wednesday, November 6, 2024, Chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]] announced he would fire Finance Minister [[christian-lindner|Christian Lindner]], the FDP leader, over persistent rifts on spending and economic reforms, a move that ejected the fiscally conservative FDP from the coalition and collapsed the government.[2] Lindner and the FDP had insisted the government stick to strict spending rules and cut taxes, while left-wing coalition partners wanted to maintain social spending and boost German industry through stimulus.[2] The rupture forced Scholz to schedule a confidence vote for January 15, with a snap election expected by the end of March.[2] See [[2024-german-government-collapse|2024 German government collapse]].[2]
