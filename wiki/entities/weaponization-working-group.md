# Weaponization Working Group

The **Weaponization Working Group** is a body established within the United States Department of Justice by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] as part of her first-day directives in February 2025.[1]

## Mandate

The group was created to review the activities of all federal law enforcement agencies over the preceding four years in order to identify instances of "politicized justice."[1] Its creation accompanied a broader set of day-one orders instructing the department to combat the weaponization of the legal system, to direct prosecutors to seek the death penalty where appropriate, and to work with the Department of Homeland Security to "completely eliminate" cartels and transnational criminal organizations.[1]

A related Bondi memorandum on "zealous advocacy" stated that Justice Department attorneys are responsible both for "aggressively enforcing criminal laws passed by Congress" and for "vigorously defending presidential policies and actions on behalf of the United States against legal challenges," adding that prosecutorial discretion "does not include latitude to substitute their personal political views or judgments for those that prevailed in the election."[1]
