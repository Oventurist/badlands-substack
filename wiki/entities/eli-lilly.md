# Eli Lilly

Eli Lilly is a major American pharmaceutical company.[1]

## Acquisition of Fauci-linked mRNA startup

In 2026 reporting by [[natalie-winters|Natalie Winters]] described Eli Lilly as buying, for up to $1.55 billion, a stealth mRNA vaccine startup that [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] had quietly joined in 2023. According to that account, the Biden administration had previously awarded the startup more than $28 million to develop vaccines for viruses with pandemic potential.[1]

The reporting circulated in the aftermath of Fauci's Senate hearing on Covid origins, where he invoked the Fifth Amendment, and was cited by Badlands commentary as part of a broader pattern of pandemic-era financial entanglement.[1]
