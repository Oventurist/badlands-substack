# Eli Lilly

Eli Lilly and Company is a major American pharmaceutical manufacturer headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. It appears in the July 30, 2026 Badlands Brief in connection with allegations concerning Anthony Fauci's post-government business interests.

The Brief cited reporting by [[natalie-winters|Natalie Winters]] stating that Fauci quietly joined a stealth mRNA vaccine startup in 2023, that the Biden administration subsequently awarded that company more than $28 million to develop vaccines for viruses with pandemic potential, and that Eli Lilly is now acquiring the firm for up to $1.55 billion. Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] presented the transaction as evidence of what she described as the depth of Fauci's "portfolio of crime," framing the acquisition as the monetisation of federally funded pandemic-preparedness work by an official who had directed federal pandemic policy. The Brief did not present a response from Eli Lilly, and the characterisation of the deal is the contributor's interpretation rather than an established finding.
