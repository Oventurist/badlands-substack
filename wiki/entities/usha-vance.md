# Usha Vance

Usha Vance is the Second Lady of the United States, wife of Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]].[1] In March 2025 she drew international attention when her planned visit to [[greenland|Greenland]] alongside national security adviser [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]] provoked anger from Greenlandic officials.[1]

## Greenland visit controversy

Greenland's prime minister, [[mute-egede|Múte B Egede]], called for the international community to step in after the announcement that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s national security adviser and the U.S. second lady would visit the Arctic island, accusing Washington of "foreign interference."[1]

Waltz and Vance were scheduled to arrive in Greenland during the week of March 24, 2025 as part of a delegation that also included U.S. energy secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]].[1] A spokesperson for the Danish police confirmed on Monday that extra personnel and sniffer dogs had been sent to Greenland as security measures were stepped up ahead of the visit.[1] Local media also reported the arrival of four bulletproof cars in Nuuk city centre, delivered by plane from the United States.[1]

Trump told reporters after a cabinet meeting the same Monday: "I think Greenland is going to be something that maybe is in our future."[1]
