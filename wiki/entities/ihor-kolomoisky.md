# Ihor Kolomoisky

Ihor Kolomoisky is a Ukrainian oligarch who was once the richest man in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], having built his wealth during the 1990s after the collapse of the Soviet Union.[1]

## Business empire

Working with a few partners, Kolomoisky created PrivatBank and the umbrella company Privat Group, which on paper owned thousands of companies mostly in energy and raw materials.[1] Most of these companies ended up being fake, part of a money laundering scheme.[1]

## Maidan and paramilitaries

Kolomoisky was a vocal public leader of the Maidan Coup in 2014 and was appointed Governor of the Dnipropetrovsk Oblast.[1] He used his wealth to create the Donbass Battalion and pumped money and other resources into the Azov Battalion, which had already formed, as well as other volunteer militias.[1] He was ultimately dismissed from his position as governor.[1]

## Zelensky and downfall

A few years after his dismissal, Kolomoisky recruited a comedy actor from the television production company he owned to run for president of Ukraine: [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1] Zelensky ended up imprisoning Kolomoisky for corruption and revoking his citizenship; he is still awaiting trial.[1]

## Context

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited Kolomoisky while discussing warnings from the [[european-anti-fraud-office|European Anti-Fraud Office]] that surging EU defense budgets are a "magnet" for criminals, asking how many other Ukrainian oligarchs would exploit weapons deals for personal gain.[1]

## Mindich connection
According to Kolomoisky, Ukrainian businessman [[timur-mindich|Timur Mindich]] — whose alleged ties to Ukraine's 2026 government reshuffle drew scrutiny — is the former fiancé of Kolomoisky's daughter.[2] Badlands coverage refers to Kolomoisky as a disgraced oligarch.[2]

## Role in Zelensky's rise, per Badlands analysis

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued in July 2024 that Kolomoyskyi propped up [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] — an actor by profession — to serve as "the Ukrainian version of Barack Obama," a proxy through whom war with Russia could continue to be waged.[3] In this reading Zelensky is a career puppet whose strings can be cut or transferred, a framing Burning Bright used to explain the Ukrainian president's apparent willingness in 2024 to move toward the peace terms [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] offered in March 2022.[3]

## NABU completes the PrivatBank probe (July 2025)

On July 23, 2025, the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine]] announced it had completed its investigation into Kolomoiskyi over the misappropriation of UAH 9.2 billion from [[privatbank|PrivatBank]].[4] The bureau said the case was the fourth episode in the wider PrivatBank embezzlement investigation but the first to directly implicate Kolomoiskyi.[4] The probe concluded one day after Prosecutor General [[ruslan-kravchenko|Ruslan Kravchenko]] publicly criticized NABU for slow progress on Kolomoisky-related cases.[4] Badlands commentary observed that completion of the investigation meant the trials of the detained billionaire could now begin, and placed the development against the backdrop of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s move to subordinate Ukraine's anti-corruption bodies to the executive.[4]
