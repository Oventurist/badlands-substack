# Wesley Clark

General Wesley K. Clark is a retired United States Army general who ran for president as a Democrat in 2008. He is cited throughout the Badlands corpus for a single disclosure about American war planning.

## The seven countries memo

assad day in damascus describes Clark as having made "a stunning but highly relevant admission in 2007 regarding a memo he saw in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] post 9/11 that outlined a plan to 'take out seven countries in five years, starting with Iraq and then Syria, Lebanon, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, and finishing off with Iran.'"

The article treats this account as corroboration of the agenda articulated by the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]], establishing that "the more nefarious players in the neocon faction of the Western hegemon have had it in their hearts to go after [[syria]] and other countries since at least the 90s." Clark's list is used as a framework for reading the 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] as the delayed execution of a decades-old plan rather than an organic uprising.
