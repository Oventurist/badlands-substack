# Wesley Clark

General Wesley K. Clark is a retired U.S. Army general who ran for president in 2008 as a Democrat.[1]

Badlands Media cites Clark for what it calls a stunning but highly relevant admission made in 2007: that after 9/11 he saw a memo in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] outlining a plan to "take out seven countries in five years, starting with Iraq and then Syria, Lebanon, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, and finishing off with Iran."[1] The article treats Clark's account as corroboration that the neoconservative faction associated with the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] had targeted Syria since at least the 1990s, framing the December 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] as the delayed execution of that plan.[1]
