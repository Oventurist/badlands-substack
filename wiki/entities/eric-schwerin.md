# Eric Schwerin

Eric Schwerin is a business associate of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] connected to [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]], the investment vehicle through which much of Hunter Biden's consulting work was routed.[1]

Schwerin appears in the November 2015 email thread over the [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]] proposal for [[burisma|Burisma]]. After [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]] objected that the proposal named no "top US officials in Ukraine," Schwerin wrote to [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] and Hunter Biden confirming that the omission was "definitely done deliberately to be on the safe and cautious side and that Sally and company understand the scope and deliverables" — a reference to [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]] and her firm.[1] Archer then asked Hunter Biden to convey that assurance to Pozharskyi, after which Pozharskyi agreed to sign the contract.[1]
