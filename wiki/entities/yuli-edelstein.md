# Yuli Edelstein

Yuli Edelstein is an Israeli legislator who chairs the [[knesset|Knesset]] Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee.[1] In June 2025 he became a central figure in the coalition crisis over military conscription of ultra-Orthodox yeshiva students.[1]

## Role in the draft-exemption crisis

Edelstein refused to back down from his pledge that any conscription law emerging from his committee would impose sanctions on draft dodgers, a position that became the principal obstacle to negotiations over the exemption bill sought by the ultra-Orthodox parties.[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] met with Edelstein directly amid threats to the coalition's survival, as the Sephardic ultra-Orthodox party [[shas|Shas]] signaled it would join [[united-torah-judaism|United Torah Judaism]] in supporting a bill to dissolve the Knesset over the failure to pass an exemption law.[1]

Badlands commentary framed Edelstein's stance as one prong of a broader bind for Netanyahu: the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s depleted ranks require replenishment for ongoing operations in Gaza and Lebanon, while the [[haredi-judaism|Haredi]] community is prepared to bring down the government rather than accept conscription.[1]
