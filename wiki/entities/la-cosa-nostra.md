# La Cosa Nostra

La Cosa Nostra is the collective name for the American Mafia's network of organized crime families, and was a central target of the October 2025 federal indictment that also swept up prominent [[national-basketball-association|NBA]] figures in an illegal sports gambling and poker-rigging case.[1]

## Role in the 2025 gambling indictment

While the arrests of Portland Trail Blazers coach [[chauncey-billups|Chauncey Billups]] and Miami Heat player [[terry-rozier|Terry Rozier]] drew the most attention, several mafia groups were implicated in the same federal action.[1] [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] said the bureau had "entered and executed a system of justice against La Cosa Nostra to include the Bonanno, Gambino, Genovese and Luchese crime families."[1]

The four families named by Patel are among the traditional pillars of the American Mafia. Patel described the fraud involved as "mind-boggling" and said the case emerged from a yearslong illegal gambling investigation that "envelops both the NBA and La Casa Nostra."[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the convergence between professional sports and organized crime revealed by the sting was significant, and that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's [[fbi|FBI]] had reasons for signal-boosting a case that tied betting corruption back to the most organized crime syndicates in the United States.[1] The episode was presented as an instance of the [[system-of-systems|System of Systems]] — overlapping networks of institutional control operating in plain sight.[1]
