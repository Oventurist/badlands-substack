# Angela Rayner

Angela Rayner is a British politician who served as Housing Secretary, heading the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government.[1]

## Chinese "super embassy" decision

In August 2025, plans for [[china|China]]'s so-called "super embassy" in London were delayed after UK officials demanded clarification over "greyed-out sections" of its design and Beijing refused to disclose further details, according to the Financial Times.[1] The Financial Times reported that Beijing had refused to "identify… the rationale and justification" for the greyed-out Cultural Exchange Building and Embassy House in the compound layout, despite a request from Rayner as Housing Secretary.[1]

The project, dubbed a "super embassy" in British media, would be the largest such compound in Europe and had faced multiple bureaucratic setbacks since Beijing first applied for a permit in 2022.[1] A spokesperson for the Chinese embassy in the UK voiced "serious concern" over the ministry's postponement, saying Beijing had followed all "customary diplomatic practices" and relevant procedures and had answered British officials' questions comprehensively, and asserting that "it is an international obligation of the host country to provide support and facilitation for the construction of diplomatic premises" — noting that the UK is also planning a new embassy building in Beijing.[1]
