# Plasma Science and Fusion Center

The Plasma Science and Fusion Center (PSFC) is a research laboratory at the [[massachusetts-institute-of-technology|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] devoted to plasma physics and magnetic-confinement fusion energy research. It is one of the largest laboratories at MIT, employing more than 250 researchers across seven buildings and focusing on advancing clean energy technology.[1]

## Leadership and the death of Nuno Loureiro

The center was led by [[nuno-loureiro|Nuno F. G. Loureiro]], a Portuguese-born professor of physics and nuclear science whom MIT hired in 2016 and who took over as director of the PSFC in 2024.[1] Loureiro, 47, was shot multiple times at his home in Brookline, Massachusetts, on the night of Monday, December 15, 2025, and died of his injuries early the following morning; as of Wednesday, December 17, authorities had disclosed no motive and had no suspects in custody.[1]

Israeli officials were reported to be examining intelligence suggesting an Iranian connection to the killing, an assessment that had not been verified and was not supported by official findings from US investigators.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] was skeptical of the Iran framing, noting that hedge fund manager [[bill-ackman|Bill Ackman]] deleted a post asking whether Iranians had killed "one of our nuclear scientists," and argued that the more notable angle was the nature of Loureiro's research itself.[1]

## Research significance

Loureiro's work at the center concerned plasma turbulence, instabilities, magnetic reconnection, and confinement in fusion devices — research described as essential to developing clean, effectively limitless energy that produces no carbon emissions, avoids meltdown risk, and generates minimal long-lived radioactive waste.[1] Against a backdrop of billions of dollars in annual global fusion investment, commentators observed that such a technology "sounds like a massive threat to a lot of interests."[1]
