# David Steiner

David Steiner is the chief executive officer of the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]]. He is identified in the [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]] as one of the officials participating in [[white-house|White House]] interagency talks on expanding federal involvement in voter verification and mail-in ballot rules, alongside [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] [[civil-rights-division|Civil Rights Division]] head [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] and [[department-of-homeland-security|DHS]] election-integrity official [[heather-honey|Heather Honey]].

Steiner's involvement follows from [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s March 2026 executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]], which calls on the Postal Service to draft rules requiring states to provide voter registration lists as a condition of processing mail-in ballots. According to the source, USPS stated that it is reviewing the executive order and working on draft regulations.
