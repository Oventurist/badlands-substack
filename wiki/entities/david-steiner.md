# David Steiner

David Steiner is the chief executive officer of the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]].[1]

## Role in federal election integrity talks

In May 2026 Steiner was reported to be among the senior officials taking part in [[white-house|White House]] interagency discussions aimed at expanding federal involvement in voter verification and mail-in ballot rules.[1] The talks also involved [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] Civil Rights Division head [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] and her deputies, and [[department-of-homeland-security|DHS]] election integrity official [[heather-honey|Heather Honey]].[1]

Under President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s March executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]], the Postal Service is called upon to draft rules requiring states to provide voter registration lists in order for mail-in ballots to be processed.[1] USPS stated that it was reviewing the executive order and working on draft regulations.[1]
