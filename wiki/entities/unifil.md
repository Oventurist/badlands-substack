# UNIFIL

UNIFIL is the [[united-nations|United Nations]] peacekeeping mission deployed in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], to which [[italy|Italy]] contributes troops.[1]

In April 2026 the mission became a flashpoint in Italian–Israeli relations when Israeli forces fired warning shots at Italian troops serving with UNIFIL in southern Lebanon, damaging a vehicle.[1] Italy summoned Israel's ambassador in response, and the incident was cited among the reasons Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] suspended the automatic renewal of Italy's long-standing defence cooperation memorandum with [[israel|Israel]] on April 13, 2026.[1] The episode occurred against the backdrop of Israeli military operations in Lebanon that had caused hundreds of deaths and thousands of injuries.[1]

## October 2024 base incursion

On Sunday, October 13, 2024, the United Nations said Israeli tanks had burst through the gates of a UNIFIL base in southern Lebanon, the latest in a series of accusations of Israeli violations denounced even by Israel's allies.[2] UNIFIL stated that two Israeli Merkava tanks destroyed the main gate of the base and forcibly entered before dawn, and that after the tanks left, shells exploded roughly 100 metres away, releasing smoke that blew across the base and sickened U.N. personnel.[2]

The Israeli military gave a different account, saying [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] militants had fired anti-tank missiles at Israeli troops, wounding 25, very close to a UNIFIL post, and that a tank helping evacuate casualties under fire had backed into the post.[2] Military international spokesperson Nadav Shoshani told reporters, "It is not storming a base. It is not trying to enter a base. It was a tank under heavy fire, mass casualty event, backing up to get out of harm's way," and the military said a smoke screen was used to cover the evacuation of wounded soldiers without endangering peacekeepers.[2]

Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] addressed a statement to U.N. Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|Antonio Guterres]] saying, "The time has come for you to withdraw UNIFIL from Hezbollah strongholds and from the combat zones," and asserting that repeated [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] requests had met refusal, with the effect of providing Hezbollah terrorists with human shields.[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the report of the IDF using chemical weapons was corroborated by UNIFIL's own X account, and compared the episode to the 2013 [[ghouta-chemical-attack|Ghouta chemical attack]] in Syria, asking whether Netanyahu and the IDF would face the same treatment as [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] or whether Israel would keep enjoying impunity granted to no other country.[2]
