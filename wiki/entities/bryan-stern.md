# Bryan Stern

**Bryan Stern** is a U.S. Special Forces veteran and founder of the [[grey-bull-rescue-foundation|Grey Bull Rescue Foundation]], described as the central figure in the operation that extracted Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] from Venezuela so she could accept the Nobel Peace Prize in Oslo in December 2025.[1]

## Grey Bull Rescue and the Machado extraction

Stern and his team of U.S. military veterans have carried out hundreds of extractions around the world, but he characterized the Machado mission as different in kind.[1] "She's the second most popular person in the Western Hemisphere after Maduro," he said, adding that her profile — "that signature" — is what made the operation very hard.[1]

The team had only a week to plan the escape, a mission they codenamed [[operation-golden-dynamite|Operation Golden Dynamite]], a nod to Alfred Nobel, the Peace Prize founder who also invented dynamite.[1] Machado had spent more than a year in hiding after her opposition movement defeated President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in the previous year's election by a wide margin according to voting records validated by international observers; Maduro refused to leave office and ordered a crackdown on the opposition.[1]
