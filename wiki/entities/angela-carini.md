# Angela Carini

Angela Carini is an Italian boxer and police officer who, at age 25, competed in the women's boxing tournament at the 2024 Paris Olympics.[1] Her bout against Algerian boxer [[imane-khelif|Imane Khelif]] on August 1, 2024 became one of the most widely discussed incidents of the Games.[1]

## The abandoned bout

After 46 seconds and two heavy shots to the head, Carini threw her helmet onto the mat and abandoned the fight, shouting "This is unjust!"[1] She refused to shake Khelif's hand, yanked her arm away from the referee who had raised Khelif's hand in victory, and stormed off before dropping to her knees in tears.[1] "I'm used to suffering. I've never taken a punch like that, it's impossible to continue. I'm nobody to say it's illegal," she said afterwards.[1]

## Aftermath

Khelif had been banned from the 2023 world championships by the [[international-boxing-association|International Boxing Association]] after failing gender-qualification tests, but was cleared to box in Paris by the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]].[1] Carini appeared to seek a ruling from the IOC on the fairness of the matchup, though Badlands commentary predicted no justice for her and no change in IOC policy.[1] The incident produced widespread outrage on social media, with one user writing that the outcome had "legitimised men beating the sh*t out of women."[1]
