# Quatuor Coronati Lodge

The Quatuor Coronati lodge is a British Masonic research lodge whose leading Grand Master was Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that Warren's role at the lodge "set the stage for reviving [[solomons-temple|Solomon's Temple]]," linking the lodge's scholarly programme to his excavations beneath the [[temple-mount|Temple Mount]] and to his conviction that the temple had been a vast complex of mass human sacrifice built on Kabbalistic proportions.[1] The lodge thus appears in Ehret's account as part of the institutional network — alongside the [[palestine-exploration-fund|Palestine Exploration Fund]] — through which occult and Masonic interests shaped the modern [[zionism|Zionist]] project.[1]
