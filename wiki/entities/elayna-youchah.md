# Elayna J. Youchah

**Elayna J. Youchah** is a magistrate judge in [[nevada|Nevada]] identified in the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 as the judge who released [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]] from custody pending trial in February 2026 in the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab case]].

According to the article, Youchah cited "no concern" that Solomon would return for trial, a decision the source characterizes as difficult to reconcile with his Israeli citizenship, his nonimmigrant visa status, and the flight to China of [[zhaoyan-wang|Zhaoyan Wang]], the business partner of Solomon's employer [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]].

The commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] speculates about the judge's religious background and, together with parallel remarks about prosecutor [[sigal-chattah|Sigal Chattah]], advances the contested claim that the case's handling reflects foreign influence over the U.S. justice system. That framing is the author's opinion rather than an established finding.
