# Elayna J. Youchah

Elayna J. Youchah is the magistrate judge who, in February 2026, ordered the pretrial release of [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]], the property manager arrested in connection with the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas illegal biolab]].[1]

Youchah cited "no concern" that Solomon would fail to return for trial.[1] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] criticized the decision as inexplicable given Solomon's Israeli citizenship and the fact that [[zhaoyan-wang|Zhaoyan Wang]] — the business partner of Solomon's employer [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]] — had already fled the country to evade indictment, and remarked on the judge's apparent Jewish identity alongside that of prosecutor [[sigal-chattah|Sigal Chattah]].[1]
