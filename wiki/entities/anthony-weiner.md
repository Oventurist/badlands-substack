# Anthony Weiner

Anthony Weiner is a former Democratic congressman from New York whose name became attached to the controversy over [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s handling of classified material through his connection to Clinton aide Huma Abedin and the discovery of Clinton-related emails on his laptop.[1]

## Reference in the Trump Bedminster recording

Weiner was invoked in the July 2021 Bedminster, New Jersey audio recording of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] discussing classified documents, which [[cnn|CNN]] obtained and aired in June 2023.[1] After Trump described a document as "secret information," a staffer joked that "Hillary would print that out all the time, you know. Her private emails," to which Trump responded, "No, she'd send it to Anthony Weiner," prompting laughter in the room.[1]

The exchange formed part of a two-minute recording that prosecutors treated as evidence in special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s indictment of Trump over the handling of classified information.[1]
