# Hansjörg Wyss

Hansjörg Wyss is a Swiss billionaire philanthropist and Democratic Party mega donor. In May 2025, at the age of 89, he became the subject of a sexual harassment lawsuit filed by a 30-year-old employee at one of his California wineries.[1]

## Sexual harassment lawsuit

The plaintiff, Madison Busby, an employee at Halter Ranch Winery, alleged in her legal filing that Wyss subjected her to repeated sexual propositions, groped her, and exposed himself to her.[1] Busby further claimed that after she rejected Wyss's advances, the Swiss billionaire retaliated against her.[1]

Her attorney, John Ly, said after the filing that "Madison has suffered severe emotional distress from the harassment which took place over the course of many years, and also from lost wages and then future damages. She's been harmed immensely."[1]

## Political funding controversy

Wyss's contributions to far-left advocacy groups and Democratic Party-aligned nonprofits have long been controversial because he holds neither U.S. citizenship nor permanent residency.[1] Though less publicly recognized than donors such as [[george-soros|George Soros]] or Reid Hoffman, Wyss has quietly become one of the party's most influential financiers over the past decade.[1]
