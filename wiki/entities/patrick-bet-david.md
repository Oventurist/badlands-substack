# Patrick Bet-David

Patrick Bet-David is an American entrepreneur and podcaster who hosts the PBD Podcast, a current-events program.[1]

## Cuomo ivermectin interview

In May 2024, the PBD Podcast was the venue for [[chris-cuomo|Chris Cuomo]]'s public reversal on [[ivermectin]].[1] Appearing on Bet-David's show, the NewsNation personality said he was taking a regular dose of the antiviral to address an ongoing inflammatory response and "brain fog" associated with long COVID.[1] Cuomo told the program that "Ivermectin was a boogeyman during COVID. That was wrong. We were given bad information about Ivermectin. The real question is, why?"[1] The remarks were widely covered because Cuomo had, as a [[cnn|CNN]] anchor, said that anyone promoting the drug should be "shamed."[1]
