# Folarin Balogun

Folarin Balogun is a forward for the United States men's national soccer team. He became the subject of a brief political controversy during the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] when he was shown a red card during the United States' Round of 16 match against Belgium.[1]

## Red card controversy

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly criticized [[fifa|FIFA]] over the decision, calling it unfair and directing administration officials to raise concerns with FIFA leadership, according to Axios.[1] The effort drew criticism from Democratic lawmakers and ethics experts, who argued the [[white-house|White House]] should not attempt to influence decisions made by an international sporting organization; administration officials responded that the president was simply advocating for fair treatment of the US team.[1]

FIFA gave no indication that it would revisit the officiating decision. The United States was eliminated from the tournament with a 4–1 loss to Belgium, which advanced to the quarterfinals.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the episode, writing that hockey is "the only true sport" and that the loss was of limited consequence.[1]
