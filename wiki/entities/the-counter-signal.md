# The Counter Signal

**The Counter Signal** is an alternative Canadian media outlet cited by Badlands Media for its reporting on the 2022 [[canadian-residential-schools|residential school]] mass grave narrative.[1]

## Role in the mass grave debunking

In the summer of 2022, when the Canadian government and media began what Badlands Media describes as a massive propaganda operation claiming to have uncovered mass graves of indigenous children, alternative Canadian media such as *The Counter Signal* quickly debunked the entire narrative, according to the article, proving that there were no mass graves.[1] The article stresses that the underlying residential school abuses were real — First Nations people were tortured and murdered — which is what makes the government's handling of the story read as a [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]].[1]
