# Al-Sahel Hospital

Al-Sahel Hospital is one of Lebanon's largest hospitals, located in Dahiyeh, the southern Beirut suburb where [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] is largely based.[1] In October 2024 it became the focus of an [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] information campaign when IDF spokesman Rear Adm. [[daniel-hagari|Daniel Hagari]] alleged that roughly "half a billion dollars in dollar bills and gold" was held in a bunker beneath the facility, part of what Israel described as Hezbollah's financial infrastructure.[1]

Hagari said the Israeli Air Force was monitoring the compound but would not strike the hospital itself.[1] The hospital's director, Fadi Alameh, denied the allegations outright and invited the Lebanese army to visit and inspect the site; the hospital evacuated its patients to a safer location regardless.[1] The claim preceded more than a dozen Israeli airstrikes on the surrounding suburbs during the [[2024-israeli-strikes-on-dahiyeh|Dahiyeh strikes]].[1]
