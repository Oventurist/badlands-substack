# Ben Bergquam

Ben Bergquam is a correspondent for [[real-americas-voice|Real America's Voice]] known for on-the-ground reporting from the United States–Mexico border.[1]

## Yuma border reporting

On Monday, May 15, 2023, Bergquam reported from the border at Yuma, Arizona during an appearance on The War Room, describing hundreds of migrants streaming into Arizona overnight and identifying the people he encountered as coming from Moldova, Egypt and Sudan — a mix he characterized as evidence of a "global invasion."[1] In the same segment he reported that [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] agents were collecting tagged bags belonging to illegal border crossers and delivering them onward to the migrants' destinations, a practice [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] characterized on air as an "illegal alien concierge service."[1]

Bergquam's report was amplified by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which framed the luggage handling as part of a broader pattern of accommodation by the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] under the Biden administration.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] tied the account to earlier reports that migrants had received cell phones at taxpayer expense along with immigration court dates set in the 2030s, arguing that Border Patrol had been reduced to "badged luggage porters" instead of being used for border security.[1]
