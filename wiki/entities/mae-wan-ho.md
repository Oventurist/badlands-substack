# Mae-Wan Ho

**Dr. Mae-Wan Ho** is cited as a biophysicist in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay credits Ho with describing quantum coherence in living systems — the state in which "each part of the body communicates with the whole in real time."[1] The author characterises this condition as "not just equilibrium" but "elegance," and "the body in symphony."[1]

Ho's concept appears in the section "The Intelligence of Embodiment," between the essay's treatment of [[stephen-porges|Stephen Porges]]'s [[polyvagal-theory|Polyvagal Theory]] and its quotation of [[shamini-jain|Shamini Jain]], and serves as the scientific gloss on the article's claim that [[coherence|coherence]] operates at the cellular level.[1]
