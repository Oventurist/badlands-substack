# Peru

Peru is a South American republic whose government drew international attention in May 2024 when its health ministry formally categorized transgender and intersex people as "mentally ill."[1]

## 2024 mental illness decree

The Peruvian health ministry defended the decree by arguing that the classification was the only mechanism by which Peru's public health services could "guarantee full coverage of medical attention for mental health."[1] Critics rejected that rationale, and the move prompted a fierce backlash in a society already marked by high levels of homophobic, transphobic and gender-based violence, and where LGBT and gender rights had been under sustained assault for several years.[1]

The decree followed other legislative moves in the same direction, including a ban by lawmakers on references to gender equality in school textbooks — a change that reportedly had a devastating effect on classroom programs intended to prevent domestic assault and femicide.[1]

## Political context

Health minister [[cesar-vasquez|César Vásquez]] declined to comment on the controversy, focusing instead on defending President [[dina-boluarte|Dina Boluarte]], who was under investigation over her unexplained acquisition of expensive jewellery, including a diamond-encrusted Cartier bracelet valued at £40,000.[1] The overlap of the two stories reinforced perceptions of a government simultaneously advancing socially contentious health policy while managing a corruption scandal at the presidential level.[1]
