# Derek Mitchell

Derek J. Mitchell is an American diplomat and Democratic Party insider who served as president of the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] (NDI) from 2018 to 2023.[1] Before leading the institute he was [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s ambassador to Myanmar, and Badlands Media argues that this combination of diplomatic and party ties underscores NDI's blend of policy work and domestic political operation.[1]

The article describes Mitchell as a Democratic insider long before he took the NDI presidency, and places him in the orbit of Obama's rise in 2008 together with NDI board member [[donna-brazile|Donna Brazile]].[1] It contends that NDI's years of perfecting "voter education" and community organising techniques abroad — notably during Ukraine's [[orange-revolution|Orange Revolution]] — suspiciously mirrored the tech-savvy, door-knocking grassroots machine that powered Obama's "hope and change" campaign.[1]

Mitchell was succeeded at NDI by [[tamara-cofman-wittes|Tamara Cofman Wittes]] in 2024.[1] In the article's broader thesis, figures such as Mitchell illustrate how the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]]'s post-[[church-committee|Church Committee]] cut-outs recycled operatives between overseas democracy programmes and the domestic party establishments.[1]
