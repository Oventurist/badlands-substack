# Trevor McFadden

Trevor McFadden is a United States District Judge for the District of Columbia, presiding over federal criminal cases in Washington, D.C.

## The WHCA dinner attack case (May 2026)

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that McFadden presided over the initial federal court appearance of the man accused of attempting to attack President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the [[white-house-correspondents-association|White House Correspondents' Association]] dinner, who pleaded not guilty to federal charges including attempted assassination and assaulting a federal officer.

When defense attorneys sought to remove Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] and US Attorney [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] from the prosecution over an alleged conflict of interest — arguing both attended the event and could be victims or witnesses — McFadden did not immediately rule and asked the defense to further explain the scope of the request.
