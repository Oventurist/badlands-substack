# Neo Space Group

Neo Space Group (NSG) is a Saudi space and satellite company launched in May 2024 by the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, as the fund's first investment focused on the space industry.[1]

## Mandate

NSG "aims to develop and enhance commercial space operations in Saudi Arabia, providing innovative satellite and space solutions locally and globally," according to the PIF statement announcing its creation.[1] The group is also intended to invest in localization, technology, start-ups and knowledge within the Saudi space and satellite sector, in line with the Kingdom's broader economic diversification agenda.[1] Omar al-Madhi, PIF's co-head of MENA Direct Investments, described the establishment of NSG as "an important milestone in the development of the growing satellite and space sector in Saudi Arabia."[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands commentary treated the NSG launch as an entry point into a wider question about Saudi capital and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], noting that Saudi investment in space-faring technology naturally invites speculation about connections to Musk — regarded in the Truth Community as a potential player in [[devolution|Devolution]] — and that the same line of inquiry led to Saudi participation in Musk's [[xai|xAI]] funding round.[1]
