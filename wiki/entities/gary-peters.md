# Gary Peters

Gary Peters is a Democratic United States senator from Michigan. He has served in a leading role on the Senate Homeland Security & Governmental Affairs Committee, the panel with jurisdiction over federal ethics and government operations legislation.[1]

## Congressional stock trading ban

In July 2024, Peters was one of four senators — with Republican [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]] of Missouri and Democrats [[jon-ossoff|Jon Ossoff]] of Georgia and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]] of Oregon — who negotiated and announced a bipartisan deal to bar members of [[congress|Congress]] from trading stock.[1]

The amendment would immediately forbid members of Congress, the president and the vice president from purchasing stocks and other covered investments, and give sitting lawmakers 90 days to divest existing holdings. It was the first proposal in the yearslong effort to restrict congressional trading to receive formal committee consideration, set for the Homeland Security & Governmental Affairs Committee on July 24, 2024.[1]

Hawley framed the effort at a Capitol Hill press conference by saying, "Congress should not be here to make a buck. There is no reason why members of Congress ought to be profiting off of the information that only they get." Ethics experts quoted in the coverage said lawmakers' access to nonpublic information gives them a potential unfair advantage over the investing public.[1]
