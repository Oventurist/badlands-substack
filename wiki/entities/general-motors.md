# General Motors

General Motors (GM) is the largest American automobile manufacturer, headquartered in Detroit, Michigan, and one of the principal employers covered by the [[united-auto-workers|United Auto Workers]] under the national contract reached in 2023.

## Factory Zero and the 2026 robotics dispute

The July 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2b2|Badlands Brief]] summarizes a July 4 Futurism report, itself citing Crain's Detroit Business, describing a labor dispute at GM's Factory Zero electric-vehicle plant in Detroit following the installation of 50 AI-integrated manufacturing robots. The UAW contends that more than 1,000 employees at the plant were idled after the robots began operating. UAW Local 22 President [[james-cotton|James Cotton]] told Crain's that the union was concerned about the introduction of robots in the wake of those workforce reductions.

The machines are described as collaborative robots, or "cobots," intended to work alongside human workers; Futurism, citing Autoblog, reported that they are used primarily to attach body panels to electric vehicles at the Detroit facility. Several key details on staffing levels and robot deployment were attributed to the prior Crain's and Autoblog reporting, and GM's own response was not included in the material reviewed.

## Interpretation in the Badlands corpus

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the Factory Zero episode as confirmation of the thesis of her October 2023 essay "[[a-time-to-strike|A Time to Strike]]": that global corporations have invested in [[automation|automation]], robotics and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] for more than a decade and that the "future of work" will contain fewer jobs than people. Although the 2023 UAW contract was meant to run through 2028, she argues the transformation is running ahead of schedule and that organized labor lacks the power to arrest it.
