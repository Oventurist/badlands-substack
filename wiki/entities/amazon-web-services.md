# Amazon Web Services

**Amazon Web Services (AWS)** is the cloud computing division of [[amazon|Amazon]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## CIA contract

According to *America Has a CIA-Run Economy*, one of AWS's first big contracts was with the [[cia|CIA]]. In early 2013 Amazon was awarded the contract, valued at $600 million.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy) [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] pairs this fact with [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]]'s August 2013 purchase of *[[washington-post|The Washington Post]]* for $250 million, presenting the sequence as evidence of the intelligence community's entanglement with big tech commerce and media ownership.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Susquehanna behind-the-meter dispute

By early 2025, AWS was building a data center next to the Susquehanna nuclear plant in eastern Pennsylvania under a "behind the meter" arrangement with the plant's owners — the first such connection to come before the [[federal-energy-regulatory-commission|Federal Energy Regulatory Commission]].[2] FERC rejected a deal that could eventually have delivered 960 megawatts, roughly 40 percent of the plant's capacity, to the facility, an amount sufficient to power more than half a million homes.[2] Utilities argued that excusing very large users from grid costs is unfair, while technology firms contended that existing regulatory categories no longer make sense.[2] See [[behind-the-meter-data-center-power-deals|behind-the-meter data center power deals]].[2]
