# Amazon Web Services

**Amazon Web Services (AWS)** is the cloud computing division of [[amazon|Amazon]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## CIA contract

According to *America Has a CIA-Run Economy*, one of AWS's first big contracts was with the [[cia|CIA]]. In early 2013 Amazon was awarded the contract, valued at $600 million.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy) [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] pairs this fact with [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]]'s August 2013 purchase of *[[washington-post|The Washington Post]]* for $250 million, presenting the sequence as evidence of the intelligence community's entanglement with big tech commerce and media ownership.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)
