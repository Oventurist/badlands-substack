# Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party

The **Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party** is a committee of the United States House of Representatives created to examine strategic competition between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the committee is cited as the source of a report on the unlicensed laboratory operated in Reedley, California, in connection with [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]]'s [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech]]/Prestige Biotech operation. According to that report, vials recovered from the 2022 lab were labeled as pathogens including HIV, tuberculosis, malaria, hepatitis and Ebola. The finding is invoked in the article to establish the seriousness of the biological-materials issue underlying the later [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab case]].
