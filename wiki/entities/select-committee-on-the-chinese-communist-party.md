# Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party

The Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party is a U.S. congressional committee tasked with investigating the activities of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] as they affect the United States.[1]

A report from the committee documented the contents of the 2022 Reedley, California warehouse laboratory operated by [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]] through [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech Inc.]], finding vials labeled as pathogens including "HIV," "tuberculosis," "malaria," "hepatitis," and "Ebola."[1] That finding is cited in Badlands coverage as context for the later discovery of the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas illegal biolab]] on a property Zhu owned.[1]
