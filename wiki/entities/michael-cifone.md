# Michael Cifone

Michael Cifone is a co-founder of the [[society-for-uap-studies|Society for UAP Studies]], an organization working to establish the academic study of unidentified anomalous phenomena.[1]

Cifone has emphasized a rigorous academic approach to studying [[unidentified-anomalous-phenomena|UAPs]] alongside a variety of other unexplained phenomena, and helped convene an international conference at which researchers discussed methodologies and frameworks for the proposed discipline.[1] He and the society have declined to take a position on whether UAPs indicate extraterrestrial life, stating that "we're not necessarily taking a position," and framing the objective as securing serious scholarly attention to the subject.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the effort as an example of institutions inventing their own authorities, credentials and awards in order to govern a field of inquiry.[1]
