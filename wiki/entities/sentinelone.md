# SentinelOne

SentinelOne is a cybersecurity company associated with former [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|CISA]] director [[chris-krebs|Chris Krebs]] after his departure from government.[1]

## April 2025 presidential memorandum

In the April 2025 presidential memorandum titled "Addressing Risks from Chris Krebs and Government Censorship," President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] directed the heads of executive departments and agencies to revoke any active security clearance held by Krebs and to suspend any active security clearances held by individuals at entities associated with Krebs, naming SentinelOne specifically.[1] The same directive ordered a review of Krebs' activities as a government employee, including his leadership of CISA, and recommendations for remedial or preventative action.[1]

## Krebs resignation (April 2025)

On Wednesday, April 16, 2025, [[chris-krebs|Chris Krebs]] announced his resignation from SentinelOne, a week after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed an executive order targeting him and calling on the government to suspend the security clearances of any entities with which he was associated — an order that specifically named the company.[2] SentinelOne, described as a cybersecurity company with a $5.6 billion market capitalization, had employed Krebs as chief intelligence and public policy officer since late 2023, when it acquired his consulting firm.[2]

Krebs said the decision was his alone, telling staffers in an email the company posted on its website that the fight ahead would require his complete focus and energy outside of SentinelOne.[2] CNBC framed the swift departure as an example of the effect Trump was having on the private sector when pressuring people and institutions he personally dislikes.[2] Badlands commentator Ashe in America observed that the firm did not want the heat but that it remained to be seen whether SentinelOne was actually off the hook, since the presidential memorandum directed the suspension of "any active security clearances held by individuals at entities associated with Krebs, including SentinelOne."[2]
