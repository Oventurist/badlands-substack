# Likud Party

Likud is the Israeli right-wing political party led by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]. In the lineage traced by Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Likud descends directly from the [[irgun|Irgun]]: after the Irgun's formal disbandment in 1949, its membership morphed into the [[herut-party|Herut Party]], which became Likud in 1977.[1]

That 1977 transition coincided with Netanyahu's exit from the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] and the beginning of his collaboration with his father to plot his political career.[1] Netanyahu would publish many books beginning in 1977 that framed the term "terrorism" for Western audiences, equating it with Islam.[1]

Through this genealogy, Likud is presented as the institutional continuation of [[zeev-jabotinsky|Ze'ev Jabotinsky]]'s [[revisionist-zionism|Revisionist Movement]] and its [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]] territorial ambitions.[1]

## Annexation demands (July 2025)

Badlands commentary in July 2025 cited what the Likud party was "now openly demanding" — the annexation of Palestine — as the reason to expect no real progress from [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s repeated White House meetings with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the Gaza ceasefire.[2] The same commentary noted that when Trump was asked about a two-state solution and redirected the question to Netanyahu, the prime minister effectively stated that a Palestinian state would present an existential threat to Israel.[2]
