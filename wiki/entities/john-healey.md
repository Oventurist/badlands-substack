# John Healey

John Healey is a British politician serving as Secretary of State for Defence in the United Kingdom, heading the [[uk-ministry-of-defence|Ministry of Defence]].[1]

In February 2026 Healey ordered officials to search more than two decades of Ministry of Defence records for evidence that convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] had used [[royal-air-force|Royal Air Force]] facilities — including RAF Northolt and RAF Marham — to traffic women into the United Kingdom.[1] The directive followed revelations that Epstein's private jet may have landed at RAF bases, and coincided with police examination of related flight logs and airport movements.[1]


## Eastern Sentry (September 2025)

As UK Defence Secretary, Healey commented on the [[nato|NATO]] operation [[eastern-sentry|Eastern Sentry]] in September 2025, under which [[royal-air-force|RAF]] Typhoons based in Lincolnshire joined Danish, French and German forces in air defense missions over [[poland|Poland]] following Russian drone incursions.[2] Healey stated that NATO had responded with unity to the challenge posed by Moscow, while Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Sir Keir Starmer]] called Russia's behaviour "a direct threat to European security."[2]
