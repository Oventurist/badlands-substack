# Robert Sepehr

**Robert Sepehr** is described by Badlands Media as a researcher and anthropologist, and is cited as the author of the book *1666 Redemption Through Sin*.[1]

## Cited work

Badlands Media invokes Sepehr's book in support of the claim that "satanic groups" infiltrated religious and social institutions, specifically the [[sabbateanism|Sabbateans]] and Frankists of the 17th and 18th centuries — identified in the article as the same people who went on to form the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] in 1776 — and that these groups used infiltration to corrupt society and "literally weaponized sin."[1]
