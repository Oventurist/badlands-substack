# Federal Office for the Protection of the Constitution (BfV)

The Federal Office for the Protection of the Constitution, known in German as the Verfassungsschutz or BfV, is Germany's domestic intelligence agency.[1]

## Designation of the AfD as extremist

In a statement released in early May 2025, the BfV labeled [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative für Deutschland]] (AfD) — described in coverage as the country's popular right-wing party — a right-wing extremist organization.[1] The designation was reported as opening the door for the German government to expand and intensify its surveillance of the party's activities.[1] Badlands coverage framed the move as the agency finding a way to surveil right-wing political actors even more aggressively.[1]
