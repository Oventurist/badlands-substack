# Mazen Faraya

Mazen Faraya is the Interior Minister of [[jordan|Jordan]] who announced the Kingdom's formal dissolution and outlawing of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] in April 2025.[1]

## Announcement of the Brotherhood ban

Speaking at a press conference on Wednesday, April 23, 2025, Faraya outlined what Arab News described as decisive steps aimed at safeguarding national security and public stability.[1] He confirmed that membership in or affiliation with the Muslim Brotherhood was now prohibited by law and that all of the group's offices across the Kingdom had been permanently closed, with a judicial order issued to facilitate the closure of headquarters and branches and security forces deployed to enforce the decision and confiscate the organization's assets.[1]

Faraya described the group's continued activities as a threat to citizens, a barrier to national development, and a destabilizing force, and said members of the dissolved organization had planned to target sensitive sites, stored weapons and explosives in residential neighborhoods, and operated covertly to undermine public security.[1] He stated that "the presence of hidden agendas and divisive rhetoric from within the group is incompatible with Jordanian unity," adding, "we cannot allow division among members of a single society."[1]
