# Social Democratic Party of Germany (SPD)

The Social Democratic Party of Germany (Sozialdemokratische Partei Deutschlands, SPD) is a center-left German political party and, as of October 2023, the largest partner in the country's three-party "traffic light" governing coalition alongside the Greens and the [[free-democratic-party|Free Democratic Party]].[1]

## October 2023 polling collapse

Polling reported in October 2023 showed the ruling coalition at a new low, with all three governing parties commanding a combined 33 percent of the vote.[1] The SPD itself drew only 15 percent, placing it eight points behind the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]], which reached a record 23 percent — an unprecedented lead for the AfD over the ruling party.[1] The AfD polled higher than the SPD and the FDP combined.[1]
