# United Food and Commercial Workers

The United Food and Commercial Workers (UFCW) is the labor union representing grocery workers at [[safeway|Safeway]] and [[albertsons|Albertsons]] stores in Colorado, where its Local 7 led strike activity in June 2025.[1]

## June 2025 Colorado strikes

UFCW Local 7 posted video to its Facebook page showing roughly 30 workers picketing the Safeway store at 2901 F Rd. in Grand Junction, action that prompted a temporary closure of the store.[1] Union members in Fort Collins, Greeley, Longmont, Loveland and Colorado Springs voted by over 95% to authorize strikes, with negotiations set to resume and walkout timing undetermined.[1] Meat cutters — among the most skilled and hardest-hit classifications — were prominently represented at every striking location.[1]

Badlands commentary was critical of the union's earlier performance, arguing that during the pre-merger period of staffing reductions in unionized meat departments "the union couldn't get their shit together" because members expected a post-merger improvement that never materialized.[1]
