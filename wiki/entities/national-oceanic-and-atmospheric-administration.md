# National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration

The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) is the US federal agency responsible for atmospheric, oceanic, and climate science.[1]

## Connection to the 2026 White House climate office

[[david-legates|David Legates]], the climate policy critic named in July 2026 to head a new [[white-house|White House]] climate office, is a former NOAA official who has questioned aspects of mainstream climate modeling.[1] The office was tasked by administration officials with reviewing federal climate science and policy to ensure analyses are objective and free from political bias, a step critics said could weaken existing climate initiatives and alter how climate research informs policymaking.[1]

## California sea level rise mapping

A NOAA projection map showed that a ten-foot rise in sea levels would considerably impact California's coastline, prompting December 2025 coverage of which cities could go underwater.[2] Researchers cited alongside the [[noaa-california-sea-level-rise-projection|projection]] said such a rise would render significant portions of one-third of US coastal cities uninhabitable, and that nuisance flooding was already forcing coastal cities to fund resiliency plans.[2]
