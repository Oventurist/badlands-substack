# Hussein Musawi

Hussein Musawi was identified in United States intelligence reporting as the head of an Islamic terrorist group called [[amal-movement|Amal]] in Lebanon.[1]

## The September 1983 NSA intercept

In late September 1983 the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] intercepted a message from the Iranian Ministry of Information and Security to the Iranian ambassador in Syria. The ministry ordered the ambassador to get in touch with Musawi, who was to turn his sights on the multinational forces in Lebanon and mount a "spectacular action against the United States Marines."[1]

The Beirut airport was the only plausible place to launch such an attack, making the intercept the clearest indication that the Marines were targeted; but the message was not delivered to senior military officials until two days after the [[1983-beirut-barracks-bombings|October 23, 1983 barracks bombing]] that killed 241 Americans.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites the intercept as definitive proof that Iran was behind the attack, and asks why the United States would then negotiate arms sales to Iran a year later.[1]
