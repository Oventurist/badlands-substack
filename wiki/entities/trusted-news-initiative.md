# Trusted News Initiative

The **Trusted News Initiative** (TNI) is described as "a global coalition of news organizations and tech firms with the stated goal of combating fake news," set up in 2019 by the BBC to protect audiences and users from disinformation, "particularly around moments of jeopardy, such as elections."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Role in the censorship regime

Badlands Media presents the TNI as an information gatekeeper activated around election cycles, and links its activity directly to the campaign against [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]] and to the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] passed in the same period.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The article's expectation was that TNI activity would intensify over the following year as part of the broader [[fifth-generation-warfare|5GW]] offensive ahead of 2024.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## RFK Jr. litigation

The TNI has already attempted to interfere with Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s campaign, prompting a lawsuit against the BBC and other large media companies for hobbling online rivals through the initiative.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The article treats this as evidence that the coalition functions as a coordination mechanism among incumbent outlets rather than as a neutral fact-checking body.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)
