# Trusted News Initiative

The **Trusted News Initiative (TNI)** is described in the Badlands Media corpus as a global coalition of news organizations and technology firms founded in **2019** by the **BBC** with the stated goal of combating fake news. According to the source, the TNI specifically focuses on disinformation around "moments of jeopardy, such as **elections**," positioning itself as a gatekeeper of information during electoral cycles. The initiative is cited in *5GW Counter-Offensive – The Censorship Regime and the Road to 2024* (September 27, 2023) as an institutional actor in the coordinated censorship offensive that the Badlands corpus frames as a recurring feature of Western election cycles.

## Organizational Profile

The TNI brings together legacy news organizations and major technology platforms. The BBC's role as founder is explicitly noted in the source, which quotes the TNI's self-description as *"a global coalition of news organizations and tech firms with the stated goal of combating fake news."* The initiative is portrayed in the Badlands corpus as extending the reach of legacy media gatekeeping into the digital sphere, coordinating the suppression of narratives and voices that fall outside establishment-approved parameters.

## Electoral Interference Allegations

The Badlands article asserts that the TNI has already attempted to interfere with the **2024** presidential campaign of **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.**, prompting a lawsuit. The source cites a February 2023 *Daily Sceptic* article reporting that RFK Jr. sued the BBC and major media companies for allegedly hobbling online rivals through the TNI framework. The article argues that this represents the TNI fulfilling its role as a gatekeeper during election cycles, and predicts that the initiative will become more active in the lead-up to November 2024.

The TNI is linked in the source to the British government's request to big-tech platforms to censor **Russell Brand's** online content following the September 2023 sexual abuse allegations. DeLarme writes that the TNI, *"which fancies itself a global coalition of news organizations and tech firms with the stated goal of combating fake news,"* is effectively being activated to remove effective critics of the state during moments of electoral jeopardy.

## Relationship to Broader Censorship Framework

Within the Badlands corpus, the TNI is positioned as a coordinating mechanism that bridges state regulatory power (exemplified by the **Online Safety Bill** and **Ofcom**) and private platform enforcement. The article connects the TNI to the **Anti-Defamation League's** campaign against **Elon Musk**, framing both as components of a quadrennial censorship offensive. The source argues that the establishment needs both **Rumble** and **X (Twitter)** eliminated or brought back under control: Rumble as the most vulnerable and intensely challenging alternative platform, and X as the former mainstream platform that must be reverted to its previous censored state.
