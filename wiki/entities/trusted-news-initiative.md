# Trusted News Initiative

The **Trusted News Initiative** (TNI) is described as "a global coalition of news organizations and tech firms with the stated goal of combating fake news," set up in 2019 by the BBC to protect audiences and users from disinformation, "particularly around moments of jeopardy, such as elections."[1]

## Role in the censorship regime

Badlands Media connects the TNI directly to the [[british-government|British Government]]'s requests that big-tech platforms censor [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]]'s online content, and to the broader apparatus created by the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]].[1] The article anticipates more action from the TNI over the following year as election season intensifies.[1]

## RFK Jr. lawsuit

The TNI has already attempted to interfere with Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s campaign, prompting a lawsuit against the BBC and major media companies for hobbling online rivals — behavior Badlands describes as living up to the coalition's role as gatekeepers of information during election cycles.[1]
