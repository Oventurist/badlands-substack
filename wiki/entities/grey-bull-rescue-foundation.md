# Grey Bull Rescue Foundation

The **Grey Bull Rescue Foundation** is a nonprofit extraction organization founded by U.S. Special Forces veteran [[bryan-stern|Bryan Stern]], staffed by U.S. military veterans who have conducted hundreds of rescue operations around the world.[1]

## Operation Golden Dynamite

In December 2025 the foundation was credited with orchestrating the escape of Venezuelan opposition leader and Nobel Peace Prize laureate [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] from Venezuela to the award ceremony in Oslo, an operation the team called [[operation-golden-dynamite|Operation Golden Dynamite]] and planned in only a week.[1] Stern described the mission as unusually difficult because of Machado's public profile, calling her "the second most popular person in the Western Hemisphere after [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]]."[1]
