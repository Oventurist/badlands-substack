# William Barber II

Bishop William Barber II is an American clergyman and social activist. On April 17, 2023 he led a protest against gun violence at the Tennessee State Capitol, during which state Representative [[justin-jones|Justin Jones]] carried a child-size prop coffin through the building and attempted to bring it into the legislature.[1]

Video circulating online showed Capitol security stopping Barber and Jones. In the footage Barber asks, "Is that a constitutional rule? Or state rule?" after the sergeant-at-arms refused to allow the casket onto the House floor, citing "House rules. No props."[1]
