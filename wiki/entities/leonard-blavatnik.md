# Leonard Blavatnik

**Leonard Blavatnik** is a billionaire investor and philanthropist.[1]

He was named as one of the individual donors to the $500 million global fundraising drive launched in May 2024 by the [[academy-of-motion-picture-arts-and-sciences|Academy of Motion Picture Arts & Sciences]], a campaign intended to diversify the Academy's revenue away from its shrinking Oscars television broadcast.[1] Academy chief executive [[bill-kramer|Bill Kramer]] said roughly $100 million of the target had already been raised at the time the campaign was disclosed.[1]
