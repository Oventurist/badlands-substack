# Carl Channell

Carl Channell was one of the few private citizens convicted in the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] scandal.[1]

Through [[palmer-national-bank|Palmer National Bank]] — where the [[national-endowment-for-the-preservation-of-liberty|National Endowment for the Preservation of Liberty]], the front organisation for [[oliver-north|Oliver North]], maintained an account — Channell obtained a $650,000 "loan" to purchase weapons for the Nicaraguan Contras.[1] The same bank was used to channel money to North's Swiss bank account, and NEPL raised over $10 million to assist the Contras.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites the Channell loan as evidence that friends of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] built a bank that helped fund the [[shadow-government|shadow government]]'s covert operations beyond congressional oversight.[1]
