# Cory Mills

Cory Mills is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Florida (R-FL). He appears in the Badlands Media corpus among the congressional Republicans quoted in a February 2026 Politico report on the party's reluctance to embrace President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s call to abolish mail-in voting.[1]

## Position on election reform

Mills offered a qualified caution rather than an endorsement of the President's position, saying, "We have to be very cautious about mail-in-ballot voting," and locating the remedy upstream in the registration process: "But I think that if we get the registration process correct, then that'll fix a lot of this."[1] His framing — that voter-roll integrity rather than ballot delivery is the operative problem — is one of the recurring Republican positions catalogued by the Brief.[1]

## Significance in the corpus

The Brief presented Mills's comments alongside those of [[mike-lawler|Mike Lawler]], [[byron-donalds|Byron Donalds]] and [[derrick-van-orden|Derrick Van Orden]] as a composite portrait of a House Republican conference unwilling to move as far as the White House on election administration.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued the pattern pointed toward "another fake election" in the absence of meaningful reform before the midterms.[1]
