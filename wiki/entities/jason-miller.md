# Jason Miller

Jason Miller is a Republican political communications strategist and senior adviser to Donald Trump's 2024 presidential campaign, where he served as one of the campaign's principal public spokesmen on debate negotiations and media strategy.[1]

## Role in the 2024 debate negotiations

In late August 2024, Miller became the Trump campaign's chief voice in the dispute over the format of the September 10 presidential debate hosted by ABC. Speaking to Politico Playbook, he rejected requests attributed to the Kamala Harris campaign that the candidates be seated, be permitted written notes, and deliver opening statements, saying: "Enough with the games. We accepted the ABC debate under the exact same terms as the CNN debate. The Harris camp, after having already agreed to the CNN rules, asked for a seated debate, with notes, and opening statements. We said no changes to the agreed upon rules."[1]

Miller framed the request as part of a broader pattern of shielding the vice president from unscripted settings, noting that the Harris campaign had not permitted a press conference or a long-form interview. "If Kamala Harris isn't smart enough to repeat the messaging points her handlers want her to memorize, that's their problem," he said, adding that in his view the campaign was "looking for a way to get out of any debate with President Trump."[1]

Badlands Media commentary treated Miller's statements as evidence that accusations of Trump avoiding the ABC event were a projection by the political press, and noted separately that Tulsi Gabbard was reportedly assisting Trump with debate preparation.[1]
