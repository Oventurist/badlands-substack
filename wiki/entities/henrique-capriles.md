# Henrique Capriles

**Henrique Capriles** is the Venezuelan opposition politician who contested the April 2013 presidential election against [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

Maduro won that narrow, contested election by 1.5 percentage points.[1] According to Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]] immediately refused to recognize the results, keeping with what he characterizes as its standard modus operandi and setting the tone for the following decade of confrontation with [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]
