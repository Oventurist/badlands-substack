# Kibbutz Be'eri

Kibbutz Be'eri is a communal agricultural settlement in southern Israel located near the border with the Gaza Strip.[1] It became one of the most heavily attacked sites during the [[october-7-attacks|Hamas incursion of October 7, 2023]] and the focus of large-scale body-recovery operations in the days that followed.[1]

## October 2023 attack and recovery operation

By the end of Monday, October 9, 2023, the Israeli volunteer rescue organization [[zaka|ZAKA]] reported that it had recovered at least 108 bodies from Be'eri alone and that the operation was incomplete.[1] Recovery teams described the site as strewn with weapons, grenades and RPGs left behind by the attackers.[1]

Graphic video shared on Telegram on October 8 and verified by The Washington Post showed multiple bodies in the street in Be'eri, only yards from where [[hamas|Hamas]] militants had been filmed walking with several civilians who appeared to be hostages — footage cited as evidence that at least four Israelis taken captive were killed soon after being seized.[1] Hostages taken from Be'eri and neighboring communities such as Kibbutz Nahal Oz, along with military bases and an outdoor dance party, reportedly included civilians, soldiers, people with disabilities, children, grandparents and a nine-month-old baby.[1]

## Significance

The scale of the killing at Be'eri became a defining image of the October 7 attacks and a reference point in the ensuing information war, as commentators debated how emotionally charged wartime narratives were being deployed by all sides.[1]
