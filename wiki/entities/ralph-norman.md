# Ralph Norman

Ralph Norman is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from South Carolina and a member of the House Rules Committee.[1]

## Ukraine aid and must-pass spending bills

In an exclusive interview with Breitbart News published in February 2023, Norman said House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] had set the "gears in place" for conservatives on the House Rules Committee to strip Ukraine aid from must-pass spending bills.[1] The remarks came as House conservatives objected to the inclusion of Ukraine funding in vehicles such as the $1.7 trillion omnibus spending bill and stop-gap measures known as continuing resolutions.[1]

Asked whether conservatives on the Rules Committee would move to strip Ukraine aid from must-pass legislation, Norman replied, "We're going to make sure it happens."[1] He elaborated that "if it's spending American dollars, then each representative is going to have to take a vote on it," adding, "We're simply not going to let them bundle it up on something completely off the radar with having anything to do with where the money's going."[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that if McCarthy followed through and stripped the funding, it would be significant, and that "the Regime likely believed McCarthy wouldn't have the political will to strip money from Ukraine" — but that the war was extremely unpopular and public opinion would back the effort.[1]
