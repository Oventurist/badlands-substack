# Corie Barry

Corie Barry is the Chief Executive Officer of [[best-buy|Best Buy]], the American consumer electronics retailer.[1]

## November 2023 guidance cut

Ahead of Black Friday 2023, Barry announced third-quarter results that beat on profitability but missed on revenue, saying the outcome demonstrated "ongoing, strong operational execution as we navigate through the near-term sales pressure our industry has been experiencing for the past several quarters."[1] She simultaneously warned that "in the more recent macro environment, consumer demand has been even more uneven and difficult to predict," and that based on third-quarter and early-November sales trends the company believed it "prudent to lower our annual revenue outlook."[1] Barry noted that the midpoint of the company's annual non-GAAP diluted EPS guidance nonetheless sat slightly above the original midpoint set at the start of the year.[1]

Describing the holiday shopping outlook, Barry said Best Buy was "prepared for a customer who is very deal-focused."[1] Her remarks were reported alongside quarterly sales of $9.76 billion, down nearly 8% year over year, and a 6.9% drop in comparable sales.[1]
