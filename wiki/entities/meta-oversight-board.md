# Meta Oversight Board

The **Meta Oversight Board** is the advisory and appellate body established by [[meta-platforms|Meta Platforms]] to review and issue binding decisions on content moderation questions affecting [[facebook|Facebook]] and [[instagram|Instagram]].[1]

## Female-presenting nipples decision (January 2023)

On January 17, 2023 the board published a decision (case BUN-IH313ZHJ) directing changes to Meta's adult nudity rules, the effect of which was to allow transgender and non-binary users to post photographs and videos of their bare breasts on Facebook and Instagram.[1] The board underscored that cisgender women would remain prohibited from posting the same imagery, writing that "the same image of female-presenting nipples would be prohibited if posted by a cisgender woman but permitted if posted by an individual self-identifying as non-binary."[1]

Alternative media coverage highlighted the asymmetry as evidence that "tech oligarchs" were applying identity-based rather than neutral standards to speech and imagery on their platforms.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the ruling as part of a broader campaign of cultural warfare waged through platform policy.[1]
