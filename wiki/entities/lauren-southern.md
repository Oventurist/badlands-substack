# Lauren Southern

Lauren Southern is a Canadian right-wing political activist and online commentator.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Southern was among the prominent conservative figures who condemned [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] after evidence from [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]]'s preliminary hearing undercut her claims of a conspiracy in the murder of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].
