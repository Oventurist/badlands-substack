# Lauren Southern

Lauren Southern is a conservative media commentator named among the influencers associated with [[tenet-media|Tenet Media]] in the federal indictment unsealed on September 4, 2024.[1]

The indictment specifically noted that Southern, along with [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]], [[benny-johnson|Benny Johnson]] and [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]], had no idea they were taking money originating with employees of [[russia-today|Russia Today]] and were deceived about its source, having been told by Tenet founder [[lauren-chen|Lauren Chen]] that the funding came from a private investor named "Eduard Grigoriann."[1]
