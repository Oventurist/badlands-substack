# Jacob Schiff

Jacob Schiff was a Wall Street financier described in Badlands Media coverage as a central private-sector actor in the financial destabilization of Imperial Russia during the first two decades of the twentieth century.[1]

## Financing of Japan in the Russo-Japanese War

In 1905, at the moment the [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]] was completed and American engineers were drawing up plans for the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait]] rail project, Schiff extended $200 million to the Japanese government to assist its victory over Russia in the [[russo-japanese-war|1904–05 Russo-Japanese War]].[1] The Russian navy was destroyed in the conflict, and the Russian state and military were left crippled.[1] For this assistance Schiff was awarded the Medal of the Rising Sun at the Meiji Palace in 1907.[1]

## Financing of revolution inside Russia

After the war, Schiff turned his attention to financing revolutionary activity inside Russia itself.[1] The scale of that spending remained obscure until 1949, when Schiff's grandson John Schiff told the *New York Journal* that his grandfather had given $20 million "for the triumph of communism in Russia."[1]

Schiff worked in tandem with the journalist and propagandist [[george-kennan|George Kennan]], who publicly credited "a New York banker you all know and love" with financing the distribution of revolutionary literature to Russian prisoners of war held in Japan.[1] On March 18, 1917, Schiff published a statement in the *New York Times* expressing "my joy that the Russian nation, a great and good people, have at last effected their deliverance from centuries of autocratic oppression and through an almost bloodless revolution have now come into their own."[1]

Schiff is grouped with [[alfred-milner|Lord Alfred Milner]] and [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]] as backers who intended [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] to lead the movement that would take power over the bodies of the Romanovs.[1]
