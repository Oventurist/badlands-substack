# Sullivan & Cromwell

Sullivan & Cromwell is a prominent New York law firm that Badlands analysis treats as a central node of the American [[deep-state|Deep State]], on the grounds that it functioned as the [[cia|CIA]] before the CIA existed — both Dulles brothers maintained employment there throughout most of their lives.[1]

## Venezuela debt restructuring

The Venezuelan opposition, operating out of the United States and led by Juan Guaido among others, retained Sullivan & Cromwell in 2019 to prepare to restructure the roughly $60 billion in debt that [[venezuela|Venezuela]] carried.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited this retainer as evidence of an organized creditor interest positioned to seize Venezuelan assets in the event of regime change.[1]

## Target of the 2026 executive order

Badlands assessed that when [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] signed his [[safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue-executive-order|executive order safeguarding Venezuelan oil revenue]] — intended to protect Venezuela's oil profits from creditors and other "vultures" — Sullivan & Cromwell was at least one of the intended targets of the order.[1] The firm's role sits alongside the broader Badlands account of the campaign against Venezuela, which traces the Deep State's crusade to former ExxonMobil CEO and Secretary of State Rex Tillerson beginning in 2007, and to the litigation filed by [[david-boies|David Boies]] against dozens of entities in a RICO-styled corruption case.[1]
