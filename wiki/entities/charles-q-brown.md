# Charles Q. Brown Jr.

General Charles Q. Brown Jr. is a United States Air Force officer who served as Chairman of the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs of Staff]].[1] In December 2023 he drew attention for public warnings about the possibility of a Chinese invasion of [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1]

## Taiwan warning

Asked during an interview at the Reagan National Defense Forum on December 2, 2023 about the possibility of an invasion of Taiwan by communist [[china|China]], Brown said that Americans "should be worried."[1] He pointed to Hong Kong as a cautionary example: "Just think about what happened in Hong Kong. ... We all should be worried whether it's going to happen or not. And part of the reason why deterrence is so important is so that conflict does not occur."[1]

Since Beijing enacted the national security law in Hong Kong in 2020, the city has seen significant erosion of the freedoms promised when the former British territory was handed over to the mainland in 1997, with authorities suppressing protests, imprisoning pro-democracy activists, and banning gatherings including the annual Tiananmen Square massacre vigil.[1]

Brown also said Beijing is putting pressure on Taiwan and other countries in the Indo-Pacific region for "their own gain."[1] His remarks aligned with findings of the Reagan National Defense Survey, presented during the interview by host Shannon Bream, in which 73 percent of respondents said they were somewhat concerned about a possible Chinese invasion of Taiwan.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the statement as part of a cluster of simultaneous narratives from the Biden administration that week: evaporating funding for the Ukraine proxy war, a pivot to de-escalation in the Israel–Hamas war, and a Joint Chiefs warning about a "pending" Taiwan invasion characterized as an attempt to seed the next proxy-war funding narrative.[1] In that reading, "the Deep State isn't getting the Wars they wanted or needed, but we are getting the Stories we need."[1]
