# International Republican Institute

The International Republican Institute (IRI) is a U.S. non-profit organization founded in 1983 that promotes democracy abroad, closely aligned with the Republican Party and heavily funded by the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]]. In the Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]], IRI is listed among the "CIA-adjacent cut-outs" funded by NED.

## Role in the NED network

The article groups IRI with the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute (NDI)]] and the [[center-for-international-private-enterprise|Center for International Private Enterprise (CIPE)]] as CIA-adjacent organizations through which NED distributes funding. The article describes NED as "the CIA's chief grantmaking body" and portrays these institutes as instruments of the "democracy promotion" agenda it characterizes as regime destabilization. It notes that the NED-affiliated [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]] is the channel for NED/CIA influence over labor unions.
