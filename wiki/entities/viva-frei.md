# Viva Frei

**Viva Frei** (rendered "Viva Frie" in the source text) is a Canadian lawyer and commentator cited as a source on the financial measures taken against participants in the 2022 Ottawa protests.[1]

According to [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] in the first installment of the [[money-wars|Money Wars]] series, it was on Viva Frei's account that banks in Canada and elsewhere unlawfully froze the bank accounts of [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]] protestors and organizers.[1] Deschamps notes that the parties ultimately behind the unprecedented move cannot be confirmed, while arguing that it undoubtedly benefited globalist interests.[1]
