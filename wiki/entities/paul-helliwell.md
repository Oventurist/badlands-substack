# Paul Helliwell

Paul Lional Edward Helliwell (1915 – 24 December 1976) was an American lawyer, banker, OSS official and CIA officer.[1] While serving in that capacity he became director of Sea Supply, Inc. and president of [[castle-bank-and-trust]]. He was instrumental in setting up Civil Air Transport and Castle Bank & Trust, both CIA proprietary companies, and according to The Wall Street Journal was "deeply involved" in financing covert actions against Cuba from 1964 to 1975.[1]

Joe Lange describes Helliwell as a member of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] from its inception and the agency's point man for global money laundering operations — "a high level untouchable" whose name almost nobody knows.[1] Civil Air Transport, which he helped establish, was another CIA airline used to traffic drugs and weapons; Castle Bank & Trust, based in the Bahamas, became the model for CIA money laundering operations.[1]

The Washington Post reported in 1980 that Castle Bank was "set up and principally controlled by the late Paul Lionel Edward Helliwell, a Miami lawyer," who had longstanding ties to the US intelligence community and was instrumental in directing a network of CIA undercover operations and proprietaries — a proprietary being a concern secretly set up and controlled by the CIA, ostensibly as a legitimate business.[1] Officials at the CIA, the Justice Department and the Helliwell law firm refused repeated requests for comment.[1]

Lange further reports that Helliwell helped set up the [[nugan-hand-bank]] — a photograph shows him with [[michael-jon-hand]] and [[frank-nugan]] as the new bank was being organized — and speculates that [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]] received his laundering training through him.[1] Castle Bank, in this account, became the template for later operations such as BCCI.[1]
