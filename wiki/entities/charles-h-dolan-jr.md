# Charles H. Dolan Jr.

**Charles H. "Chuck" Dolan** is a longtime Democratic Party operative and public-relations executive identified during Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s prosecution of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] as one of the sources behind material in the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]].[1]

## Background and political career

Dolan is described in Badlands Media's account as a longtime Democratic operative and power broker whose involvement in high-level national politics dated back to the presidency of Jimmy Carter.[1] His decades-long standing inside Democratic political circles is what made his appearance in the dossier's sourcing chain significant: it indicated that material presented to the [[fbi|FBI]] as Russian-origin intelligence was in part sourced from a domestic partisan operative.[1]

## Role in the dossier and the Danchenko trial

The FBI learned during the course of its work that one of the dossier's sources was Dolan.[1] Durham surfaced this fact at the Danchenko trial as part of a broader body of evidence that had been concealed from the public for more than five years, alongside disclosures about the Bureau's failure to verify any dossier allegation and its awareness that Danchenko had himself been the subject of a counterintelligence investigation.[1] In the Badlands Media framing, the Dolan revelation undercut the premise of the [[russiagate-hoax|RussiaGate]] narrative by showing that the dossier's supposed Russian sourcing partly traced back to the American political operation that commissioned it.[1]
