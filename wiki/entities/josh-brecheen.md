# Josh Brecheen

Josh Brecheen is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Oklahoma and one of the twenty-one conservatives who initially refused to back [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] for Speaker in January 2023.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

A freshman at the time, Brecheen was part of the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]]-aligned bloc whose resistance produced the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]].[1] He won seats on the [[house-homeland-security-committee|Homeland Security]] and Budget committees in the resulting assignments.[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson highlights that many of these holdouts were freshmen who nonetheless received influential postings.[1]
