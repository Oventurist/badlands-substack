# Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Psychiatry

The **Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Psychiatry** was one of two German research institutes funded by the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] out of foundation funds, the other being the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-anthropology-eugenics-and-human-heredity|Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity]].[1]

The Rockefellers' chief executive over both institutions was the Swiss psychiatrist [[ernst-rudin|Ernst Rudin]], assisted by [[otmar-verschuer|Otmar Verschuer]] and [[franz-kallmann|Franz J. Kallmann]].[1] Badlands Media presents the institute as an operational arm of the Rockefeller-created specialty of "psychiatric genetics," and as the setting in which Germany's 1933 sterilization law — modelled on American statutes — was prepared.[1]
