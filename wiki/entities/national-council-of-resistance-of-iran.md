# National Council of Resistance of Iran

The **National Council of Resistance of Iran** (NCRI) is a France-based group opposing the Iranian government.[1]

## Ties to the incoming Trump administration

In January 2025, [[keith-kellogg|Keith Kellogg]], the incoming special envoy on the Ukraine conflict for President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], spoke at an NCRI event in Paris, where he called for reviving a [[maximum-pressure-campaign|"maximum pressure" campaign]] against Iran.[1] Kellogg, a retired US Army lieutenant general, had attended more than one NCRI event, which analysts said suggested ties between the group and the incoming US administration.[1]

The Iranian Foreign Ministry issued a statement condemning France for hosting the NCRI gathering, as Tehran considers the organization a terrorist group.[1]
