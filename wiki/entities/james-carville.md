# James Carville

James Carville is a veteran Democratic Party strategist who became one of the most prominent internal critics of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 2024 re-election bid.[1] Earlier in 2023 he publicly warned fellow Democrats that Biden could lose to former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2024, breaking with the party's customary election-year discipline.[1]

His criticism drew a blunt rebuke from Senator [[john-fetterman|John Fetterman]] (D-Pa.), who told Politico in a late-December 2023 interview that he would use the occasion as "another opportunity to tell James Carville to shut the f---up," adding that Carville "hasn't been relevant since grunge was a thing" and defending Biden as "an incredibly strong and decent and excellent president."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the exchange as a source of "cognitive dissonance" for conservatives: satisfying to hear anyone publicly tell Carville off, yet uncomfortable because the rebuke came in defense of Biden, while Carville's own position — questioning Biden's fitness for office — was one the commentariat on the right welcomed.[1] Both men, in that reading, were disrupting the usual norm of Democratic election-year unity, a development the commentary characterized as a welcome sign of life within the party's internal debate.[1]
