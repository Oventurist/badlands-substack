# Rick Staly

Rick Staly is the sheriff of Flagler County, Florida, heading the [[flagler-county-sheriffs-office|Flagler County Sheriff's Office]].[1]

## 2025 nurse impersonation case

Staly's office announced the August 2025 arrest of [[autumn-bardisa|Autumn Bardisa]], who was accused of treating 4,486 patients at [[adventhealth|AdventHealth]] Palm Coast Parkway since July 2023 while falsely claiming to be a registered nurse.[1] In a statement, Staly described the matter as "one of the most disturbing cases of medical fraud we've ever investigated" and said that Bardisa "potentially put thousands of lives at risk by pretending to be someone she was not."[1]
