# US International Command

US International Command is a newly created combatant command entity proposed under a sweeping [[pentagon|Pentagon]] overhaul of American military command structures reported in December 2025.[1] Under the draft plan, the restructuring would diminish the standing of [[us-central-command|US Central Command]], [[united-states-european-command|US European Command]], and [[us-africa-command|US Africa Command]] by bringing those theatres under the new International Command.[1]

## Context

The plan is being driven in large part by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]]'s pledge to "break the status quo" and reduce the number of four-star generals across the armed forces, according to sources quoted by the Washington Post.[1] The overhaul is reported to go well beyond staffing and personnel changes, reshaping the entire US global command and headquarters structure and the balance of influence among senior generals.[1] A parallel entity, [[us-americas-command|US Americas Command]] — informally "Americom" — would absorb [[us-southern-command|US Southern Command]] and [[united-states-northern-command|US Northern Command]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the streamlining of the combatant commands suggests the earlier historic meeting of all military leaders convened by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] may in fact have been a purge, asking whether generals were questioned, polygraphed, or offered early retirement if legally clean but an impediment to the military's core mission.[1] He read the shift to a two-command structure — one international and one domestic — as reinforcing the idea that the US military will increasingly be used at home to defend the homeland and defeat cartels.[1]
