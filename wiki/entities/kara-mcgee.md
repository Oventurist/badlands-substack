# Kara McGee

Kara McGee is a prospective juror who was excused from the Manhattan criminal trial of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in April 2024 and who publicly described the selection process afterward.[1]

## Dismissal and Public Comments

McGee spoke to media outlets outside the courthouse on Tuesday, April 16, 2024, saying she had been dismissed because of her job in the cybersecurity sector, while maintaining that she believed she could have served impartially.[1] She nonetheless allowed that it would be "very difficult for anyone really in this country to not come to this without prior opinions," adding, "We all have prior opinions on the defendant, unless you've been living in a cardbox."[1]

Asked about her personal feelings toward Trump, McGee said, "I'm not a fan," citing above all her view of his handling of the COVID-19 pandemic response.[1]

## Account of the Questioning

McGee offered insight into the questions posed to potential jurors in the [[trump-hush-money-case|hush-money case]].[1] One asked whether jurors had opinions about the ability of a former sitting president to be tried in a court of law, which she believed revealed how respondents felt about the case.[1] Another asked whether they held opinions about legal limits on campaign finance donation amounts, which she likewise read as a device to gauge sentiment about the specific charges.[1]

## Significance

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited McGee's remarks as confirmation of a long-held view within the Truth Community that Trump was unlikely, if not unable, to receive a fair trial in New York, given a jury pool she described as tainted by fake news and government-generated hoaxes.[1]
