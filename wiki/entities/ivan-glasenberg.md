# Ivan Glasenberg

Ivan Glasenberg is the former chief executive of the commodities trader and miner [[glencore|Glencore]], who left the company in July 2021.[1] His departure is grouped in Badlands commentary with those of fellow Glencore executives Telis Mistakidis, who retired in December 2018, and [[alex-beard|Alex Beard]], who departed in 2019, as part of a pattern of exits associated with the company's long-running international corruption probes.[1]

## Alleged role in Congo operations

Citing a 2018 Bloomberg feature on Glencore's misadventure in the Congo, Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Glasenberg, Mistakidis, and Israeli billionaire [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] all worked closely together to exploit the Congolese people in their mining operations.[1]

The same commentary noted that although Glasenberg is a South African citizen, his father was a Jew born in Lithuania, and argued — on the basis of the writer's study of the Israeli Mafia and what he terms the international cartel — that this was not a coincidence given Glasenberg's ties to Israeli oligarchs such as Gertler.[1] The writer framed the wider sequence of settlements and prosecutions as evidence that Africa is being liberated from globalist oligarchs.[1]
