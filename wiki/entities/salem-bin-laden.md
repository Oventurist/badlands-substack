# Salem bin Laden

**Salem bin Laden** was a Saudi billionaire and a relative of [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]].[1] According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in Badlands Media commentary, Salem bin Laden was one of two rich Saudis — the other being banker and [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|BCCI]] insider [[khalid-bin-mahfouz|Khalid bin Mahfouz]] — represented in North America by Texas businessman [[james-bath|James Bath]].[1]

Bath's $50,000 investment in [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]'s 1979 [[arbusto-energy|Arbusto]] oil partnership was probably made using Bin Laden–Bin Mahfouz funds, making Salem bin Laden an indirect early financier of the future president's business career.[1] The article identifies him as one of three names central to the story and promises a fuller treatment in a subsequent installment.[1]

Bin Laden family members were also among the twelve rich Saudi families who invested in the [[carlyle-group|Carlyle Group]] after [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] joined the firm in 1993.[1]

## Assessment

These connections are reported from quoted journalism; the conspiratorial framing is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
