# Jamieson Greer

Ambassador Jamieson Greer is the United States Trade Representative in the second Trump administration, heading the [[office-of-the-united-states-trade-representative|Office of the United States Trade Representative]].[1]

In July 2026 Greer announced that, at President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s direction, the administration was imposing a 25% tariff on certain imports from Brazil under [[section-301-of-the-trade-act-of-1974|Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974]], following a yearlong investigation into unfair trade practices.[1] Officials determined that several Brazilian policies were harming American businesses, workers and farmers.[1] Greer framed the tariffs as part of the administration's "America First" trade policies, arguing that Brazil's practices had limited American access to a market of more than 210 million consumers.[1]
