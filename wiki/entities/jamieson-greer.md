# Jamieson Greer

Jamieson Greer is the United States Trade Representative in the second [[donald-trump]] administration, heading the [[office-of-the-united-states-trade-representative]] with the rank of ambassador. A trade lawyer by background, he served as chief of staff to the USTR during Trump's first term and is a principal architect of the administration's tariff-centered [[america-first]] trade strategy.

## Brazil tariff action

In July 2026, as reported in the Badlands Brief of July 17, Greer announced that the administration would impose a 25% tariff on certain imports from [[brazil]] following a yearlong investigation into unfair trade practices. He said the action was taken at President Trump's direction under [[section-301]] of the [[trade-act-of-1974]], after officials determined that several Brazilian policies were harming American businesses, workers, and farmers.

Greer framed the tariffs as part of the administration's America First trade policies, arguing that Brazilian practices had limited American access to a market of more than 210 million consumers. The underlying investigation covered digital trade, electronic payment services, preferential tariffs, anti-corruption enforcement, intellectual property, ethanol market access, and illegal deforestation, and concluded after review of more than 360 public comments and a July hearing with 77 witnesses.
