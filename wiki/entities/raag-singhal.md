# Raag Singhal

Raag Singhal is a U.S. District Judge for the Southern District of Florida, appointed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Dismissal of Trump's suit against CNN

On Friday, July 28, 2023, Singhal dismissed Trump's $475 million [[trump-v-cnn-defamation-lawsuit|defamation lawsuit]] against [[cnn|CNN]], ruling that the former president could not sue the network for defamation over statements that his attorneys said likened him to Adolf Hitler.[1] "Being 'Hitler-like' is not a verifiable statement of fact that would support a defamation claim," Singhal wrote in the dismissal, adding that "CNN's statements, while repugnant, were not, as a matter of law, defamatory."[1] He further held that bad rhetoric does not equate to defamation.[1]

The ruling drew criticism from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who argued that Singhal — a Trump appointee — had effectively raised the bar for defamation claims against major media outlets, dismissing as merely "repugnant" statements she regarded as verifiably false and damaging to Trump's reputation.[1]
