# Saleha Abedin

Saleha Abedin is a sociologist and the mother of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]. She spent her career at Saudi Arabian universities and served as vice dean of a women's college in [[jeddah|Jeddah]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Move to Jeddah and the Journal

Saleha and her husband [[syed-abedin|Syed Abedin]], Pakistani and Indian Muslim academics, moved from Kalamazoo, Michigan to Jeddah when Huma was two years old.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The couple were recruited by the Saudi financier [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] to run the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]], whose principal purpose since its founding in the late 1970s has been to publish the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

After Syed Abedin's death, Saleha took over operations at the journal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Badlands account treats the family's role as foundational to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization/stealth jihad]] strategy, in which academic publishing supplied universities worldwide with material promoting sharia acceptance and Islamic supremacist perspectives in non-Muslim countries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Significance in the Abedin narrative

Because Naseef was, in the article's characterization, a known wealthy Saudi financier of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and a former secretary-general of the [[muslim-world-league|Muslim World League]], the family's long association with him is presented as the basis for questions about how Huma Abedin obtained a United States security clearance while working simultaneously for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and for the journal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
