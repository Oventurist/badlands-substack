# Tony Gonzales

Tony Gonzales is a United States Representative from Texas whose district encompasses a long stretch of the US–Mexico border. He appears in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief as a congressional voice responding to reports that cartel-linked drones had entered American airspace near El Paso and been disabled by the [[federal-aviation-administration|FAA]] and the Department of War.

Quoted by *Newsweek*, Gonzales downplayed the novelty of the incident rather than the incursion itself, saying: "For those of us who live and work along the border, daily drone incursions by criminal organizations are part of everyday life. It's a Wednesday for us." The remark functioned in the Brief's account as corroboration from a sitting member of [[congress|Congress]] that unmanned aircraft operated by criminal organizations routinely cross the border, cutting against Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]]'s denial that any such activity had occurred.
