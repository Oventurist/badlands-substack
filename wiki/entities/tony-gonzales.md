# Tony Gonzales

Tony Gonzales is a United States Representative from Texas whose district encompasses a long stretch of the US-Mexico border, giving him a frequent role in border security debates.[1]

## Response to the El Paso drone incursion (February 2026)

After the [[federal-aviation-administration|FAA]] shut down airspace over El Paso and the administration confirmed a cartel drone incursion, Gonzales downplayed the novelty of the event.[1] Quoted by *Newsweek*, he said: "For those of us who live and work along the border, daily drone incursions by criminal organizations are part of everyday life. It's a Wednesday for us."[1]

His remark stood in contrast both to the alarm generated by the sudden airspace closure and to Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]]'s denial that any drone activity had occurred at the border.[1]

## Resignation (April 2026)

Gonzales, representing Texas's 23rd district, resigned on the same Tuesday in April 2026 that the Eric Swalwell scandal reached its peak, a coincidence noted by Badlands commentator Ashe in America in her account of the week's congressional departures.[2]


## Resignation from Congress (April 2026)

Gonzales resigned from [[congress|Congress]] in April 2026 alongside Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]], with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reporting rumors that additional resignations were expected the same week.[3] The departures coincided with the release of documents by DNI [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] concerning the [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|first impeachment of Donald Trump]] and with rumors that [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] and [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] were under investigation.[3]

## Eagle Pass border tour (September 2023)

In September 2023, Gonzales hosted a group of House Republican colleagues in Eagle Pass, Texas, to draw attention to thousands of daily illegal crossings along the Texas–Mexico border.[4] "I hosted a group of my #HouseGOP colleagues here in Eagle Pass today — it's clear our border is broken," he wrote on X, adding that "the first thing [[congress|Congress]] needs to do is pass funding that secures our border and puts an end to this madness."[4] He also visited a migrant processing center holding more than 4,000 immigrants.[4]

Gonzales separately spoke with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], confirming to him that the southern border was "overwhelmed" by unprecedented and still-growing numbers of crossings; Musk cited the conversation in announcing his own visit to Eagle Pass later that week.[4]
