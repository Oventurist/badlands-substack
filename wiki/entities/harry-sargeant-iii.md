# Harry Sargeant III

Harry Sargeant III is an American businessman with interests in the oil sector who became the subject of a Wall Street Journal story in February 2026 concerning US dealings with [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## Trump disavowal

In a Truth Social post on the state of US–Venezuela relations, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] singled out the report, stating that Sargeant "has no authority, in any way, shape, or form, to act on behalf of the United States of America, nor does anyone else that is not approved by the State Department."[1] Trump added that without [[state-department|State Department]] approval, no one is authorized to represent the country, and framed the disavowal as a step to prevent "confusion or misrepresentation" about who speaks for the United States in the Venezuela negotiations.[1]
