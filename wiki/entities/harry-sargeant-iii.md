# Harry Sargeant III

**Harry Sargeant III** is an American oil businessman who surfaced in February 2026 reporting by the *Wall Street Journal* in connection with United States dealings in [[venezuela]]. The story prompted an unusually direct public disavowal from President [[donald-trump]].

## Trump's disavowal

In a Truth Social post celebrating warming US–Venezuela relations, Trump wrote: "There is a story about a man named Harry Sargeant III in The Wall Street Journal. He has no authority, in any way, shape, or form, to act on behalf of the United States of America, nor does anyone else that is not approved by the [[state-department|State Department]]. Without this approval, no one is authorized to represent our Country."

The statement served two functions: it repudiated any suggestion that Sargeant carried an official mandate, and it asserted that Venezuela policy runs exclusively through channels approved by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio]] and the State Department, rather than through private intermediaries or unofficial back-channels.

## Context

The disavowal arrived as Energy Secretary [[chris-wright]] toured Venezuelan oil infrastructure and as Trump described relations with President [[delcy-rodriguez]] as "extraordinary," with oil beginning to flow. Trump's insistence that "we speak only for ourselves, and don't want there to be any confusion or misrepresentation" reflects the sensitivity of sanctions-adjacent oil dealmaking, where unauthorized brokers can create both legal exposure and diplomatic confusion.
