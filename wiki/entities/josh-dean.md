# Joshua Dean

Joshua "Josh" Dean (c. 1979 – April 30, 2024) was an American quality auditor at [[spirit-aerosystems|Spirit AeroSystems]], a major supplier to [[boeing|Boeing]], and one of the first whistleblowers to allege that Spirit leadership had ignored manufacturing defects on the 737 MAX airliner.[1]

## Career and disclosures

Dean lived in Wichita, Kansas, where Spirit AeroSystems is based.[1] He gave a deposition in a Spirit shareholder lawsuit and filed a complaint with the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]] alleging "serious and gross misconduct by senior quality management of the 737 production line" at Spirit.[1] Spirit fired Dean in April 2023, after which he filed a complaint with the [[department-of-labor|Department of Labor]] alleging that his termination was retaliation for raising aviation safety concerns.[1]

## Death

Dean died on the morning of Tuesday, April 30, 2024, after a struggle with a sudden, fast-spreading infection, having spent two weeks in critical condition.[1] He was 45 years old, had been in good health, and was noted by those who knew him for maintaining a healthy lifestyle; his aunt Carol Parsons confirmed the circumstances of his death.[1] Spirit spokesperson Joe Buccino said, "Our thoughts are with Josh Dean's family. This sudden loss is stunning news here and for his loved ones."[1]

The death drew wide attention in independent media because it came shortly after that of another Boeing-related whistleblower, and Badlands Media flagged the item in its news brief coverage.[1]
