# Joe Morelle

**Joe Morelle** is a Democratic member of the US House of Representatives from New York and the ranking Democrat on the House Administration Committee.[1]

## Republican fundraising inquiry

In May 2025, Morelle joined Representatives [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]] of Maryland and [[gerald-connolly|Gerald Connolly]] of Virginia — the top Democrats on the House Judiciary, Oversight, and Administration Committees — in asking the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] to turn over any evidence of suspicious transactions connected to Republican and Trump-aligned fundraising platforms, including [[winred|WinRed]] and the Elon Musk-affiliated [[america-pac|America PAC]].[1]

The demand for "suspicious activity reports" was a response to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s and the GOP's parallel effort to pressure [[actblue|ActBlue]], the Democratic small-dollar fundraising platform that Republicans had targeted over allegations of illegal foreign donations.[1] The exchange formed part of a broader legal and political fight in which each side sought to undermine the other's engine of small-dollar political participation.[1]
