# Tibet

Tibet is an autonomous region of [[china|China]] cited in the Badlands Media corpus, alongside [[xinjiang|Xinjiang]], as evidence for the claim that Chinese development policy preserves rather than erases minority cultures.

## Claims in the corpus

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s [[avoiding-wwiii|Avoiding WWIII]] reports that high-speed and conventional rail have connected Tibetan communities that had long lived in poverty to broader global markets, with durable technical skills and training growing among the younger population. He states that Buddhist temples are thriving with the full support of the government, and that cultural heritage centres, rising literacy and state patronage of traditional languages, songs, stories and dances are found across China's minority ethnic zones.

The article contrasts this account with what it calls the tradition of exploitation and cultural genocide deployed by the western oligarchy, and attributes contrary reporting to "[[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]]-controlled propaganda outlets" and Five Eyes-aligned media. These are the source's contested assertions rather than settled fact. Ehret extends the same argument to Africa, where he says local cultural traditions thrive in tandem with [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road]] investment.
