# Michelle Childs

Michelle Childs is a judge on the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-dc-circuit|U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit]] appointed by President Joe Biden.[1]

In April 2026 Childs dissented from the sharply divided panel decision ordering U.S. District Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]] to immediately end his criminal contempt probe against [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] officials over the disputed 2025 deportation flights.[1] The majority, composed of Trump appointees [[neomi-rao|Neomi Rao]] and [[justin-walker|Justin Walker]], held that the probe improperly targeted high-level executive branch deliberations on national security and diplomacy.[1]
