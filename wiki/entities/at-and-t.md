# AT&T

AT&T is a major American telecommunications carrier.[1]

## Role in the Hunter Biden secret phone story

In June 2023, investigative journalist and *Clinton Cash* author [[peter-schweizer|Peter Schweizer]] revealed that [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] had been paying roughly $300 per month for a secret global phone line from AT&T. Speaking to [[fox-news|Fox News]]' [[maria-bartiromo|Maria Bartiromo]], Schweizer said the [[hunter-biden-laptop|laptop]] showed that Hunter Biden's business paid for a private phone line used by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] while he was vice president, describing it as an AT&T global phone that could reach someone anywhere in the world.[1] Journalist [[john-solomon|John Solomon]], who obtained the number from leaked documents, called the line and Joe Biden answered.[1]
