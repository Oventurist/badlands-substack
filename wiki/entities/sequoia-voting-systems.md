# Sequoia Voting Systems

Sequoia Voting Systems was a United States voting equipment manufacturer that passed through several owners in the 2000s and serves, in the Badlands corpus, as the connective tissue between [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] and [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]].

According to the account given by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, Sequoia was purchased in 2005 by Smartmatic from the British firm [[de-la-rue|De La Rue PLC]] for $16 million — a $19 million loss for De La Rue against the $35 million it had invested four years earlier. Smartmatic announced two years later that it would divest Sequoia, but an April 2008 Delaware court filing revealed that Smartmatic retained a $2 million unsecured promissory note from the divestiture and, more significantly, that Sequoia was still using Smartmatic's intellectual property.

Dominion Voting Systems acquired Sequoia a few months after acquiring [[premier-election-solutions|Premier Election Solutions]] in 2010. Dominion's press release stated that it had "acquired the assets of Sequoia Voting Systems, a major U.S. provider of voting solutions serving nearly 300 jurisdictions in 16 states," including "Sequoia's inventory and all intellectual property, including software, firmware and hardware, for Sequoia's precinct and central count optical scan and DRE voting solutions."
