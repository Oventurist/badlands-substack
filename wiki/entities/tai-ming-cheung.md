# Tai Ming Cheung

Tai Ming Cheung is a scholar of the Chinese military affiliated with the University of California San Diego, quoted in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" on the extent of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s control of the armed forces.[1]

## Assessment of Xi's military control

In an October 2017 observation cited by the article, Cheung stated: "No other Chinese Communist Party leader, not even Mao Zedong, has controlled the military to the same extent as Xi does today. Mao had to share power with powerful revolutionary-era marshals."[1] The article uses the quotation to support its account of Xi's direct hold on the central command structure of the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]], including the creation, for the first time, of a commander-in-chief position in the PLA Joint Battle Command — a designation typically split among generals — and his simultaneous personal authority over the [[peoples-armed-police|People's Armed Police]].[1] The comparison with [[mao-zedong|Mao Zedong]] anchors the piece's claim that Xi's degree of military centralisation is historically unprecedented within the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[1]
