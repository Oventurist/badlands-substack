# Sergey Naryshkin

Sergey Naryshkin is the head of Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR), the country's principal civilian foreign-intelligence agency.[1] In Badlands Media coverage he appears primarily as the official voice attributing alleged Western-directed sabotage plots inside Russia to British and American intelligence services.[1]

## Kursk nuclear plant allegations

Speaking at a meeting of intelligence and security principals of post-Soviet states held in Astana, Naryshkin alleged that British and American spies had helped Ukraine develop plans for blowing up the [[kursk-nuclear-power-plant|Kursk Nuclear Power Plant]], a scheme he said would have plunged Europe into a second radiation catastrophe.[1] His remarks were made public on a Monday in early October 2024, roughly two months after Ukrainian troops crossed into Russia's Kursk Region in August but were stopped short of the plant at Kurchatov.[1]

According to Naryshkin, "the planned terrorist attack included taking and mining the Kursk NPP," and had it been carried out "Europe would have faced an environmental and humanitarian disaster comparable to Chernobyl."[1] He further claimed that Western intelligence agencies, "primarily the British [[mi6|MI6]]," had systematically prepared Ukrainian sabotage and reconnaissance groups to stage provocations at a number of Russian nuclear power plants, and that British and Ukrainian operatives were developing an operation to blow up the power lines connecting nuclear plants to the Russian national energy grid.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands writers treated Naryshkin's claims as corroboration of a longstanding thesis in the community: that terrorism functions as an industry substantially sustained by UK and US civilian intelligence agencies, which would otherwise lose their institutional rationale, since threats from foreign state militaries fall to military intelligence.[1]
