# Anderson Cooper

Anderson Cooper is an American television journalist and anchor for [[cnn|CNN]].[1]

## October 2024 Harris town hall

In October 2024 Cooper moderated a CNN town hall with Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], during which he challenged her over the Biden administration's record on illegal immigration and her shifting position on a border wall.[1] Cooper pressed Harris to explain her support for a bipartisan immigration deal that included $650 million for border wall spending, given that she had previously criticized the wall more than fifty times and called it "stupid," "useless" and "a medieval vanity project."[1]

Harris responded by pivoting to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], noting that Trump had said Mexico would pay for the wall, asserting that only about 2 percent of it was built, and claiming that Trump staged a photo op at a section of wall constructed under President Obama.[1] Commentators judged that Harris "focused a lot more on Donald Trump" than on her own policies.[1] During the same appearance Harris called Trump a fascist, echoing remarks by [[john-kelly|John Kelly]]; Trump replied on Truth Social that Harris was "a Threat to Democracy" and "not fit to be President of the United States."[1]

The encounter was described by *Newsweek* as a bruising session for Harris, and it came as polling by Redfield & Wilton Strategies found 53 percent of Democratic-leaning voters supporting a stronger approach to immigration.[1]

## Anderson Cooper segment, July 2023

In July 2023, [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]] ran a segment taking Cooper to task over his televised "takedown" of the philosopher and presidential candidate [[cornel-west|Cornel West]], who opposes the NATO alliance and holds that the West provoked war with Russia.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], relaying the exchange, characterized Cooper as a "CNN/CIA operative" and treated the clash as an illustration of American politics shifting from a Left-Right-Center axis to an Establishment-versus-anti-Establishment one.[2]

## Reaction to the Cohen cross-examination

After [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]]'s May 16, 2024 cross-examination of [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]], [[cnn|CNN]] host Anderson Cooper told viewers: "It was incredible ... lawyers want to build a box around the witness & slam it shut — that's what Todd Blanche did to Cohen ... it was an extraordinary cross ... Cohen was cornered in ... a lie."[3] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterised the moment as Cooper spending three and a half minutes explaining to a shocked audience that the prosecution's star witness had lied.[3]

## Mockingbird allegations in Q for Dummies

Chapter 14 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series presents Cooper as a paradigm case of [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]], the CIA programme the series describes as controlling the mainstream media.[4] The author argues it is near certain that many journalists and on-air personalities are undercover intelligence officers used to disperse the [[deep-state|Deep State]] narrative, and names Cooper as "just such a clown."[4]

Two facts are offered in support: that Cooper never attended school for journalism, and that he interned at the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] — the latter described as the icing on the cake establishing him as a Mockingbird media asset.[4] The chapter also emphasises his descent from the [[vanderbilt-family|Vanderbilt family]] through his mother [[gloria-vanderbilt|Gloria Vanderbilt]], and repeats claims that [[cornelius-vanderbilt|Cornelius Vanderbilt]] funded Hitler and was implicated in the Kennedy assassination.[4]

The section extends into speculation about the family's [[biltmore-estate|Biltmore Estate]], where the author compares the drained pool to artwork attributed to the Podestas and suggests one image resembles a young Cooper.[4] It also raises the suicide of Cooper's brother at age fourteen, proposing abuse or sacrifice as the explanation.[4] These claims are the author's opinion and are unsupported by evidence in the source.[4]
