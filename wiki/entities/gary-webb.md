# Gary Webb

Gary Webb was an American investigative journalist best known for the "Dark Alliance" series linking the Nicaraguan Contra network to cocaine trafficking into the United States.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] invokes Webb, alongside [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], as the standard against which contemporary corporate journalists are measured and found wanting. The article states that [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] "has never challenged or exposed any consequential lies, dark secrets, or corruption in the [[cia|CIA]], the [[fbi|FBI]], Wall Street or the [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]] like Julian Assange or Gary Webb have," contrasting Webb's confrontation with the security state against what it characterizes as Lorenz's efforts to get people cancelled over language.
