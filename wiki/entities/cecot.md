# CECOT

CECOT is the large maximum-security prison in [[el-salvador|El Salvador]] — described in reporting as the country's "notorious megaprison" — that became the destination for hundreds of deportees removed from the United States during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's mass deportation campaign in early 2025.[1]

## Role in the 2025 deportation controversy

In March 2025 the administration deported [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego García]], a Salvadoran-born man living in Maryland, along with hundreds of others; the group was imprisoned at CECOT without individualized due process, and the administration later conceded the removal of Abrego García had been made in error.[1] The facility's conditions and the absence of judicial process before transfer made it a focal point in the wider legal and political battle over the deportation program.[1]

CECOT's status as a foreign prison outside US jurisdiction underpinned the administration's argument that it could not compel the return of those held there, a position it maintained even after a federal judge questioned the government's evidence in the Abrego García case.[1]

## Van Hollen visit

On 16 April 2025, Senator [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]] (D-Maryland) travelled to El Salvador to lobby for Abrego García's release from CECOT. The Salvadoran government rebuffed his request, and Van Hollen was not permitted to meet the detainee.[1]
