# Burning Bright Publishing

Burning Bright Publishing is an independent American publishing house associated with the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] orbit and with the host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[1] Its stated premise is that "politics is culture, and culture starts with stories," positioning fiction publishing as a front in what Badlands describes as the "War of Stories."[1]

## Saving Seoul: Sword Punk 1

The imprint's debut novel is *Saving Seoul*, a martial arts epic following Akio Prince, a fighter battling a crime syndicate in a near-future Far East.[1] It is the first book in the *Sword Punk Trilogy*.[1] The launch was funded through a Kickstarter campaign running through 31 August 2025, with backers offered a signed, leatherbound collector's edition.[1]

## Saving Seoul Kickstarter (2025)

Burning Bright Publishing's debut novel, Saving Seoul, was promoted in August 2025 as a martial arts epic about Akio Prince, a fighter battling a crime syndicate in a near-future Far East, and the first book in the Sword Punk Trilogy.[2] The publisher ran a Kickstarter campaign through August 31 offering a signed, leatherbound collector's edition, pitched under the slogan "Join the War of Stories" and the premise that politics is culture and culture starts with stories.[2]
