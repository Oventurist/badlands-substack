# Janno Lieber

Janno Lieber is the head of the New York [[metropolitan-transportation-authority|Metropolitan Transportation Authority]] (NYMTA).[1]

## Federal funding ultimatum (March 2025)

On March 19, 2025, Transportation Secretary [[sean-duffy|Sean Duffy]] sent Lieber a letter threatening to cut off federal funding to the agency if it did not address the administration's demands regarding subway crime.[1] Duffy demanded that the NYMTA "ensure a safe and clean environment, reduce crime and fare evasion, and maintain a safe operating system" to protect New Yorkers.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the letter as a negotiating posture by [[donald-trump|Trump]], arguing that public transportation agencies are rife with waste, fraud and abuse — citing Colorado's RTD, with a one-billion-dollar budget, as a comparison — and that a serious effort would require an audit before any federal funds were released.[1]
