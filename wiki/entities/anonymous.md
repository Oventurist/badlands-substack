# Anonymous

**Anonymous** is a decentralized hacker collective known for hacktivist operations against governments and corporations.[1]

## GlobalX hack (2025)

In May 2025 Anonymous targeted the Trump administration once again by defacing the website of [[globalx|GlobalX]], the U.S. airline hired by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] to deport alleged migrant gang members to El Salvador.[1] Referring to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a "fascist," the group left a lengthy message on the landing page of a GlobalX subdomain protesting the removal of "alleged members of a Venezuelan gang."[1]

"You lose again Donnie," the hacktivists wrote, referencing a May 1 ruling by a U.S. federal judge declaring the reasoning behind the deportations illegal.[1]
