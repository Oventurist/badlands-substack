# Dominic Cummings

**Dominic Cummings** is a British political strategist and former senior adviser in the UK government.[1]

## China hacking claim

In October 2025, Cummings claimed that [[china|China]] had hacked into Britain's most secret systems.[1] The UK government rushed to deny the claim, a reaction commentators attributed to the political heat surrounding the collapsed Chinese spy trial.[1] Writing in The Spectator, one analysis argued that even if Cummings' story proved false, the underlying truth remained: China has been systematically targeting Western networks for years and extracting vast quantities of sensitive information.[1] What was striking, the piece argued, was not the allegation but the reaction of a government so anxious not to call China a threat that it pretended not to see one.[1]
