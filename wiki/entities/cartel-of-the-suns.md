# Cartel of the Suns

The **Cartel of the Suns** (*Cartel de los Soles*) is the name given to the network of senior Venezuelan military and government figures around [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] accused of profiting from illicit trafficking and resource extraction.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situates the cartel within the parallel economy that sustained the Maduro government under U.S. sanctions. Beginning in 2018, Russian mercenaries — often ex-Spetsnaz and associated with the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] — secured illegal gold mines in the [[orinoco-mining-arc|Orinoco Mining Arc]].[1] These operations, run by Maduro's inner circle including the "infamous" Cartel of the Suns, generated off-the-books revenue used to pay soldiers, buy loyalty, and fund repression.[1]

DeLarme is explicit that he does not present Maduro as a good actor, listing human rights abuses, opposition bans, financial corruption, and the facilitation of drug trafficking among the government's offences — while arguing that the United States routinely overlooks identical conduct by leaders who are pro-U.S. and pro-NATO.[1]
