# Center for International Private Enterprise

The Center for International Private Enterprise (CIPE) is one of the four core surrogate institutions established beneath the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1] Badlands Media describes its remit as CIA/NED influence over business and chamber-of-commerce networks.[1]

## Position in the network

CIPE operates alongside the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] (Republican politics), the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] (Democratic politics), and the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]] (labor unions, affiliated with the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]), each with a specialized sector of society to cultivate.[1] NED functions as the grantmaking head of this structure, funding cut-outs that specialize in regime-change operations.[1]

## Context

The article situates CIPE within the [[plausible-deniability-network|plausible deniability network]] built in the 1980s to relocate formerly [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]-run influence work into nominally private organizations, insulating the [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]] from attribution.[1]
