# Joseph A. Ladapo

Dr. Joseph A. Ladapo is the Surgeon General of Florida and one of the most prominent state-level public health officials to publicly criticize vaccine policy.[1]

## Move to end vaccine mandates

On September 3, 2025, Ladapo announced alongside Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] that Florida planned to become the first state to end all vaccine mandates, including those applying to schoolchildren — rejecting a practice that public health experts have credited for decades with limiting the spread of infectious diseases.[1] Speaking at an event in Valrico, Florida, near Tampa, Ladapo told an applauding audience: "Who am I to tell you what your child should put in their body? Your body is a gift from God."[1] He added that the administration would be "working to end" all vaccine mandates.[1]

The New York Times characterized Ladapo as a vocal denigrator of vaccines and noted that DeSantis, who rose to national prominence during the coronavirus pandemic, had over time espoused increasingly anti-vaccine views.[1]
