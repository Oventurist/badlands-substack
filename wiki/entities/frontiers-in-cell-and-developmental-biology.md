# Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology

Frontiers in Cell and Development Biology is a peer-reviewed scientific journal that in February 2024 was publicly humiliated after publishing a fabricated paper containing AI-generated illustrations that bore no relationship to biology.[1]

## The AI-generated rat paper

The paper, attributed to Chinese researchers, purported to describe the signalling pathway of sperm stem cells.[1] Its lead figure instead depicted a rat sitting upright with a penis larger than its own body and four oversized testicles.[1] The illustration was reportedly produced with [[midjourney|Midjourney]], the AI imaging tool, which labelled the diagram with terms that do not exist, including "dissilced," "testtomcels" and "senctolic."[1] A second image showed "sterrn cells" being spooned out of a Petri dish.[1]

Scientists who read the published article immediately recognised that the images and descriptions were not grounded in "any known biology."[1] The journal retracted the paper, issued an apology, and said it was working to "correct the record."[1] The episode was reported by The Telegraph and [[modernity-news|Modernity News]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] wrote that "the geniuses that populate the peer review system were not able to catch a giant rat dick labelled with literal gibberish, or the fake paper that surrounded it," noting that the same institutions are cited as authorities when dissenters are dismissed as anti-scientific.[1] He drew a parallel to the [[grievance-studies-affair|Grievance Studies affair]] conducted by [[helen-pluckrose|Helen Pluckrose]], [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]] and [[peter-boghossian|Peter Boghossian]], and argued that while the incident might be read as a Chinese infiltration of North American academia, the alleged agents were performing the same exposing function.[1]
