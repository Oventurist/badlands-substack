# Norman Ornstein

**Norman Ornstein** is an American political scientist and emeritus scholar at the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]].[1]

## Commentary on Trump's 2025 plans

Ornstein was quoted in an [[axios|Axios]] report on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s plans for a second term, saying that what Trump was proposing for 2025 amounted to "the trappings of a democracy" but was in substance "a Potemkin village."[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] singled out the quotation as ironic, noting that the term "Potemkin village" entered common usage because Soviet authorities staged fabricated towns for visiting foreign leaders in order to present communism as prosperous — an inversion, in his framing, of the accusation being levelled at Trump.[1] Deschamps treated the hostility of the coverage as evidence that the agenda described was genuinely antithetical to [[globalism|globalist]] plans rather than merely authoritarian.[1]
