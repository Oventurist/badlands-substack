# Media Research Center

The Media Research Center (MRC) is an American conservative media watchdog organization that monitors and quantifies the coverage output of national news outlets. Its principal method is airtime accounting: measuring how many minutes broadcast and cable networks devote to particular stories, then publicizing disparities as evidence of ideological filtering.[1]

## Role in the June 2023 coverage-disparity report

In June 2023 the MRC published a tally that became one of the most widely circulated data points in the conservative media ecosystem that week. The group found that the broadcast networks [[abc-news|ABC]], [[cbs-news|CBS]] and [[nbc-news|NBC]] had devoted zero seconds of coverage to allegations that then-President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had accepted a $5 million bribe from a Ukrainian oligarch, while spending 291 minutes on the federal indictment of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The MRC promoted the finding on Twitter with the framing, "Have you heard about the Biden Burisma bribery scandal? Probably not, since it's received ZERO seconds of coverage from @ABC, @CBSNews and @NBCNews, compared to the 291 minutes they've spent on the Trump indictment."[1]

The tally was significant because the two stories broke on the same day: the FBI provided Congress with an informant document describing the alleged bribe on the same Thursday that the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] announced the Trump indictment.[1] The MRC's numbers were subsequently amplified by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] and, through it, by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the MRC finding as confirmation rather than revelation, writing that the numbers filed under "things that surprise no one" and that ABC, CBS, NBC and Fox had again proven themselves "social conditioning outlets, not news organizations."[1] In that reading, the airtime asymmetry was not editorial accident but a deliberate mechanism for keeping the [[biden-burisma-bribery-allegation|Biden bribery allegations]] out of the awareness of left-of-center audiences who would not seek the story from conservative or independent outlets.[1]

## FOIA work on DHS counterterrorism grants

Beyond airtime accounting, the MRC also pursues document-based investigations. In May 2023 the group obtained records through Freedom of Information Act requests showing that a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] program intended to fight terrorism was funding a university program whose work had explicitly targeted the American political right, grouping the Republican Party together with Christian and conservative organizations in the same category as Nazis.[2] The documents were shared exclusively with Fox News Digital, and the MRC outlined its findings in a report arguing that what it had found warranted criminal prosecution.[2] Founder and president [[brent-bozell|Brent Bozell]] said the task force was working to "demonize and eliminate Christian, conservative, and Republican organizations using federal taxpayer dollars."[2] See [[dhs-targeted-violence-and-terrorism-prevention-grants|DHS Targeted Violence and Terrorism Prevention grants]].[2]
