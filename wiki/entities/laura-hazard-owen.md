# Laura Hazard Owen

Laura Hazard Owen is a journalist with [[niemanlab|NiemanLab]], the Harvard-affiliated outlet covering the news industry, who in February 2026 publicly exposed that a widely shared [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]] essay about an unvaccinated child dying of measles was fiction.[1]

## The Bruenig disclosure

Owen wrote that when she first read [[elizabeth-bruenig|Elizabeth Bruenig]]'s story she was "stunned" at the apparent revelation that an Atlantic staff writer's unvaccinated child had died of measles in the 2020s.[1] On reaching the editor's note describing the piece as "based on extensive reporting and interviews with physicians," she concluded the piece was fiction and sent a gift link to her "mom group" asking, "What do we think about this choice? I am very conflicted!!!"[1] Her stated concern was that although the piece was heavily researched, it was not a true story, and that the very readers whose minds it aimed to change — parents who do not vaccinate — would dismiss it as fake.[1]

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] amplified Owen's finding, reporting that The Atlantic had been "busted fabricating" the dead-kid measles story.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] credited the episode as an instance of pro-vaccine journalists "policing their own for once," while mocking the assumption that Atlantic or Harvard-adjacent commentary could persuade vaccine-skeptical parents on anything substantive.[1]
