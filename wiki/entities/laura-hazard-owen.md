# Laura Hazard Owen

Laura Hazard Owen is a journalist and editor at the [[niemanlab|Nieman Journalism Lab]], the Harvard-affiliated publication covering the future of journalism. In the Badlands corpus she is the reporter who exposed *[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]]*'s fictional measles story as fiction.

## Busting the Atlantic measles story (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Owen busted [[elizabeth-bruenig|Elizabeth Bruenig]]'s measles essay, which had been published as what read like a first-hand account of a mother whose unvaccinated child died of measles. Owen wrote: "When I initially read Bruenig's story, I was stunned: An Atlantic staff writer's unvaccinated child had died of measles in the 2020s, and now she was writing about it?" She described sending a gift link to her mom group with the message "as far as I can tell this piece is fiction," and expressed her conflict about whether the heavily-researched but untrue story would convince or repel the vaccine-hesitant readers it was aimed at.

According to the Brief, multiple journalists questioned the truthfulness of the piece, which led to the editor's note disclaimer. Owen's flagging of the story is cited approvingly by Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as "the vax-maxis policing their own for once."
