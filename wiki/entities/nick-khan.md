# Nick Khan

Nick Khan is an American media executive who served as chief executive officer of [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] (WWE).[1]

## WWE–UFC merger

In April 2023, WWE agreed to merge with the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] to form a new publicly traded combat sports and entertainment company controlled by [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]].[1] Under the terms announced, Endeavor would own 51 percent of the new company and WWE shareholders the remaining 49 percent, with the deal valuing WWE at $9.3 billion and UFC at $12.1 billion.[1]

Khan was slated to remain as president of the wrestling business within the combined company.[1] [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] was to serve as chief executive of both Endeavor and the new company, [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]] as executive chairman, [[mark-shapiro|Mark Shapiro]] as president and chief operating officer, and [[dana-white|Dana White]] as continuing president of UFC.[1] The transaction was expected to close in the second half of 2023; on the day of the announcement WWE shares fell more than 2 percent and Endeavor shares declined over 5 percent.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the merger as unfortunate news for the alternative sports marketplace, arguing that UFC and WWE audiences skew heavily toward MAGA and counterculture viewers and that Endeavor's earlier acquisition of UFC had produced stagnation and homogenization of the product.[1]
