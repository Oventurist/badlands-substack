# Bo Xilai

Bo Xilai is a former senior figure of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] named in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" as one of six party leaders removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule over alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

Bo appears in the article alongside security chief and [[politburo-standing-committee|Politburo Standing Committee]] member [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]], [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] aide [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]], and generals [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]], [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]].[1] The piece treats the cluster of removals as evidence that Xi moved early and decisively against rival power centres inside the party, consistent with its broader thesis that Xi has spent a decade rooting out entrenched networks from the halls of power in China.[1]
