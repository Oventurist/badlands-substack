# David Grusch

David Charles Grusch is a former United States intelligence official who, in June 2023, went public as a whistleblower alleging that the U.S. government operates deeply covert programs in possession of retrieved intact and partially intact craft of "non-human origin."[1] At the time of his disclosure he was 36 years old and was described as a decorated former combat officer who had served in Afghanistan.[1]

## Career

Grusch worked at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency ([[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|NGA]]) and the [[national-reconnaissance-office|National Reconnaissance Office]] (NRO).[1] He served as the NRO's representative to the [[unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force|Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force]] from 2019 to 2021, and from late 2021 to July 2022 he was the NGA's co-lead for UAP analysis as well as that agency's representative to the same task force.[1]

## Disclosure

Grusch provided extensive classified information to [[congress|Congress]] and to the Intelligence Community Inspector General regarding the alleged retrieval programs.[1] He is represented by an attorney who previously served as the original Intelligence Community Inspector General.[1] His account was published by journalists [[leslie-kean|Leslie Kean]] and [[ralph-blumenthal|Ralph Blumenthal]] in [[the-debrief|The Debrief]], and reporting indicated that other intelligence officials, both active and retired, had independently provided corroborating information on and off the record.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that while the claim of recovered and reverse-engineered extraterrestrial craft is "outlandish," it is "not beyond reproach," noting that the military-industrial complex has repeatedly fielded technology denied to exist until later acknowledged.[1] Deschamps also raised the possibility that Grusch's testimony forms part of a staged sequence of disclosures leading toward a "cosmic false flag," citing [[operation-paperclip|Operation Paperclip]] scientist [[wernher-von-braun|Wernher von Braun]]'s reported claim that such an operation had been contemplated since the 1960s.[1] He further observed that the story broke on the same day as an FBI-related Biden bribery story, raising the question of whether it functioned as distraction.[1]
