# Gregory Bateson

Gregory Bateson was an anthropologist and cybernetic theorist described by Badlands Media as an [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] contractor who, alongside [[margaret-mead|Margaret Mead]], collaborated closely with [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]] and drove a "human reprogramming" project centered on LSD and psilocybin.[1]

## Cybernetics and the Macy Conferences

In *Gregory Bateson, Cybernetics and the Social/Behavioral Sciences*, Dr. Lawrence S. Bale discusses Bateson's leading role in the Macy Conferences on Cybernetics after the Second World War and his part in infusing this new system of control into anthropology.[1] Bale writes that "Gregory Bateson embraced the concepts and vocabulary of cybernetics because this interdisciplinary field offered a more rigorous formulation of theoretical concerns with which his work had already been dealing," noting that a systems approach to biology and the behavioral sciences was for Bateson never a foreign concept.[1]

Bateson, like [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]], was a devotee of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] — the science of control developed by acolytes of Bertrand Russell and led by Russell's disciple [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]].[1]

## The double bind

Bateson formulated the [[double-bind-theory|double bind]] as the basis for inducing schizophrenia in a confused target population.[1] He observed that a victim will often break down into a schizophrenic state when confronted with contradictory messages, an effect that could be intensified with hallucinogenic cocktails.[1] Badlands Media applies this framework to the ecological debate: told that goodness requires saving nature from humanity, some subjects act from above to shut down industrial civilization while others, like [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]], declare war on it from below — with both paths converging on the same techno-feudal outcome.[1]
