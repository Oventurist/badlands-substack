# Brianne Dressen

Brianne Dressen is an American preschool teacher from Utah who suffered an injury after participating in [[astrazeneca|AstraZeneca]]'s COVID-19 vaccine clinical trial and who sued the company on May 13, 2024.[1]

## Clinical trial and injury

Dressen chose to enroll in AstraZeneca's clinical trial in 2020 because she wanted to help the company develop its COVID-19 vaccine.[1] The consent form she signed stated that AstraZeneca would "cover the costs of research injuries" and "pay the costs of medical treatment."[1] As her complaint puts it, "With these reassurances should something go wrong, Bri signed the form, rolled up her sleeve, and let the drug company inject the experimental product into her arm."[1]

She soon began experiencing problems including blurred vision, tinnitus, and vomiting, later becoming extremely sensitive to light and suffering spikes in her heart rate.[1] She consulted numerous doctors while attempting to identify her condition and obtain treatment, and has paid tens of thousands of dollars for drugs to treat a nervous system disorder and other issues.[1]

## Lawsuit

Dressen's suit, filed in federal court in her home state, alleges that AstraZeneca breached its contract by failing to pay for the medical care her injury requires.[1] "They left us no choice," she told The Epoch Times in an email.[1]
