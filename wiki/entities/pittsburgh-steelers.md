# Pittsburgh Steelers

The Pittsburgh Steelers are a National Football League franchise based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

The team features in Badlands Media's October 2024 coverage as the franchise where wide receiver [[antonio-brown|Antonio Brown]] spent the prime of his career, from 2014 through 2018, breaking a wide range of receiving records before his departure.[1] Brown returned to the Steelers' orbit politically in October 2024, leading a voter registration drive at a tailgate party ahead of the team's Sunday evening game against the New York Jets and distributing Trump–Vance rally towels in Steelers yellow.[1]
