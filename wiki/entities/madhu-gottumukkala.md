# Madhu Gottumukkala

Madhu Gottumukkala is a US cybersecurity official who served as acting director of the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]].[1] By March 2026 he had been replaced in that role on an acting basis by [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]].[1]

During his tenure, conflicting reports circulated over whether Gottumukkala had tried to push CISA chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] out of his position.[1] House Homeland Security Chairman [[andrew-garbarino|Andrew Garbarino]] said in February 2026 that it was "good" that an earlier reported attempt to move Costello out of the CIO role had failed.[1] Costello announced his own departure from the agency the following month.[1]
