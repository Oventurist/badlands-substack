# Madhu Gottumukkala

**Madhu Gottumukkala** is a former acting director of the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] (CISA).[1]

## Role in CISA leadership turmoil

Gottumukkala featured in conflicting reports over whether he had attempted to push CISA chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] out of his post.[1] House Homeland Security Chairman [[andrew-garbarino|Andrew Garbarino]] said in February 2026 that it was "good" that an earlier reported attempt to move Costello out of the CIO role had failed.[1]

By March 2026 Gottumukkala had been replaced in the acting director role by [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]], part of a broader wave of leadership changes at the agency that also included transfer orders for Costello and for acting chief human resources officer Kevin Diana, while the nomination of [[sean-plankey|Sean Plankey]] to lead CISA remained stalled.[1]
