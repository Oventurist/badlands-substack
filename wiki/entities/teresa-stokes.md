# Teresa Stokes

Teresa Stokes is a magistrate judge for the Charlotte District in Mecklenburg County Court, North Carolina, whose bail decisions came under national scrutiny in September 2025.[1]

## The DeCarlos Brown Jr. case

Stokes presided over a prior case involving [[decarlos-brown-jr|DeCarlos Brown Jr.]] months before he was accused of murdering [[iryna-zarutska|Iryna Zarutska]], a 23-year-old Ukrainian refugee stabbed in Charlotte on August 22, 2025.[1] In January 2025, Brown had been arrested for alleged "misuse of the 911 system" and was released after signing a written promise to appear at his next hearing, on court documents signed by Stokes.[1] As a magistrate, Stokes is responsible for making bail decisions.[1] Social media users also linked her to a charity providing mental health and addiction support, though Newsweek was unable to verify those claims.[1]

## Calls for removal

Ten members of [[us-congress|Congress]] called for Stokes's removal in a letter to Chief District Court Judge Roy Wiggins, alleging she had been "reckless" in releasing Brown despite his criminal history and characterizing the decision as a "willful and persistent failure to perform the duties of the office."[1] Brown had fourteen prior cases in Mecklenburg County and had previously served six years in prison for robbery with a dangerous weapon, breaking and entering, and larceny.[1] Badlands host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Stokes "released a deranged criminal into the interior of our nation" and should be federally indicted as an accessory to murder, adding that [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] had alleged — unverified — that Stokes and her wife were receiving kickbacks from mental health clinics.[1]
