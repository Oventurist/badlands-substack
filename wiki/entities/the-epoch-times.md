# The Epoch Times

The Epoch Times is an independent news outlet frequently cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a source for domestic U.S. political and legal reporting.[1]

In the October 9, 2023 Brief, The Epoch Times supplied two items. The first reported that the [[fbi|FBI]] had denied a Newsweek story alleging that the Bureau was targeting supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ahead of the 2024 election under its [[anti-government-anti-authority-violent-extremism|anti-government or anti-authority violent extremism]] category; the outlet noted it had contacted the bureau for further comment.[1] The second reported that an appeals court had granted a stay on a judge's order dissolving Trump's businesses amid the [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud trial]], a partial victory since the trial itself was permitted to continue.[1]

In the December 22, 2023 Brief, The Epoch Times supplied a bonus item reporting economist [[harry-dent|Harry Dent]]'s warning to Fox Business that 2024 would bring "the biggest single crash year we'll see in our lifetimes."[2] Dent, who runs HS Dent Investment Management, attributed the condition to "100 percent artificial" money printing and deficits totaling $27 trillion over fifteen years since 2009, argued that US markets had been in a bubble since late 2021, and predicted a decline closer to the 1929–32 collapse than to a correction, becoming obvious by May.[2]

## Coverage of the 2023 Speaker fight

The Epoch Times supplied the reporting behind the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] item on [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]]'s reaction to the election of [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] as Speaker of the House, in which Gaetz declared that the "swamp is on the run" and "MAGA is ascendant."[3] Its account documented that after a three-week leadership void the GOP conference chose Johnson as its nominee and that he won 220 votes on the first floor ballot, that Gaetz had led the motion to oust former Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], and that Johnson had been runner-up to [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]], the third nominee, after [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] and [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] failed to secure sufficient support.[3]
