# Premier League

The Premier League is the top division of English professional soccer and one of the most-watched sporting competitions in the world. In coverage from Badlands Media, the league has featured primarily in connection with on-field cardiac incidents involving its players and with the vaccine policies it maintained during the COVID-19 period.[1]

## December 2023 Lockyer collapse

On December 16, 2023, [[tom-lockyer|Tom Lockyer]], the 29-year-old club captain of [[luton-town|Luton Town]], collapsed in the 59th minute of a Premier League fixture against Bournemouth, prompting the abandonment of the match.[1] The club later confirmed Lockyer had suffered a cardiac arrest, his second cardiac-related on-field episode in seven months.[1] Reporting described the cause of the collapse as a "mystery," with the player remaining hospitalized for testing.[1]

## Vaccine mandate and commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] noted that the English Premier League had a vaccine mandate in effect for its players during the COVID-19 period, and argued that the recurrence of young, elite athletes collapsing during play should not be normalized.[1] Fleuret emphasized that soccer players are routinely among the most well-conditioned athletes in the world, making such episodes especially anomalous, and questioned why comparable clusters of collapses had not been observed in United States professional leagues.[1] Online commentary following the Lockyer incident frequently referenced Pfizer's acknowledgment of elevated myocarditis and pericarditis risks associated with mRNA [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]].[1]
