# Prince William

Prince William is the heir to the British throne and, according to reporting summarized in the Badlands News Brief of October 29, 2025, the driving force behind efforts to remove his uncle [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] from Royal Lodge on the Windsor Estate.[1]

## Royal Lodge ultimatum

The Sun reported that William threatened to strip Andrew's daughters, Princesses Beatrice and Eugenie, of their titles if Andrew and his ex-wife Sarah "Fergie" Ferguson refused to leave Royal Lodge.[1] Andrew, 65, and Ferguson, 66, were understood to be in "advanced talks" over vacating the 30-bedroom mansion in Windsor, while still seeking a house each on the Windsor Estate — Andrew reportedly wanting Frogmore Cottage, the former home of Harry and Meghan, and Ferguson expressing interest in Adelaide Cottage, William and Kate's former property.[1] Emily Maitlis, who conducted Andrew's infamous 2019 Newsnight interview, claimed Andrew was being pressured to move out by his two daughters following William's ultimatum.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America wrote that "things are windy in the House of Windsor," suggesting King Charles III was running cover but resembled [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] as an aged-out figurehead with no real power projection, and asking who was actually calling the shots.[1] She speculated about what kind of king William would become once his father stepped down, treated the purging of alleged abusers from the family as an encouraging start, and noted the coordination between the two monarchs walking from Marine One as well as William's newly grown beard.[1] She concluded that while the royals have "zero impact on Americans," the current season was mildly entertaining.[1]
