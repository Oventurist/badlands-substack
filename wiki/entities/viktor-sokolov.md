# Viktor Sokolov

Viktor Sokolov is a Russian naval officer who served as commander of Russia's Black Sea Fleet, headquartered in the Crimean port of Sevastopol.[1] He became internationally prominent in September 2023 when Ukrainian special forces publicly claimed to have killed him in a missile strike on the fleet's headquarters, a claim that was contradicted within a day by Russian video evidence.[1]

## The September 2023 death claim

On Monday, 25 September 2023, Ukraine's special forces announced that Sokolov had been killed in a missile attack on the headquarters of the [[black-sea-fleet|Black Sea Fleet]] in Sevastopol, on the Russian-annexed Crimean peninsula.[1] The Ukrainian statement said Sokolov had died alongside 33 other officers and that a further 105 people had been wounded in the strikes.[1]

The following day, the Russian defence ministry released video and photographs — subsequently broadcast on Russian state television — showing Sokolov taking part in a video conference with Defence Minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]] and other senior admirals and army chiefs.[1] The imagery was reported by [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] and undercut the Ukrainian claim that the fleet commander had been killed.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the episode as a case study in wartime information operations. Writing in the Badlands News Brief for 26 September 2023, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the Sokolov reversal belonged to a pattern of Ukrainian war reporting that had repeatedly proven false, listing the "Ghost of Kiev," the Nord Stream 2 pipeline sabotage narrative, the Wagner coup, and the Kerch Bridge explosion as comparable examples.[1] She contended that such reporting sustained a narrative used by U.S. neoconservatives to justify continued taxpayer funding of Ukraine, and said she would support no further funding regardless of conditions.[1]
