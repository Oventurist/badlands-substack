# Sun Zhengcai

Sun Zhengcai is listed in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" among the generals and senior figures removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule over alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

The article groups Sun with [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]] and [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]], and with [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]], [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]] and [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]].[1] These purges are presented as the sharp end of a decade-long effort by Xi to bring the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] and its military organs under a single unified command, an effort the author suggests may amount to rooting legacy globalist corruption out of the Chinese state.[1]
