# Kat Cammack

Kat Cammack is a member of the U.S. House of Representatives and the sponsor of H.Res. 500, a resolution that would amend House rules to require criminal background checks for all House employees.[1] In July 2026 she called for an immediate floor vote on the measure, which she had introduced earlier.[1]

## H.Res. 500

The resolution would also mandate public disclosure — filed with the Clerk of the House and made publicly available — of any payments, contracts, or agreements a prospective or current staffer had with foreign governments in the three years prior to employment, as well as any foreign citizenship or nationality.[1] The requirements would apply both to current staff and to new hires.[1] Cammack has argued that congressional staff routinely handle sensitive personal information yet face no mandatory background checks of the kind required for executive-branch employees, and that efforts to advance the measure have repeatedly stalled.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

[[anna-paulina-luna|Anna Paulina Luna]] amplified Cammack's bill over the weekend, which [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized as an attempt "to appear heroic" that "backfired spectacularly," because Americans reacted with shock that background checks were not already required.[1] Ashe cited the [[eric-swalwell|Swalwell]]–Fang Fang episode and the case of Dianne Feinstein's driver as prior examples of staffers acting as foreign assets, arguing that [[congress|Congress]] "continues to prove that they are the People's real problem — and problems don't get to design their own solutions."[1]
