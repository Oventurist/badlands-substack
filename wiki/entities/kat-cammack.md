# Kat Cammack

Kat Cammack is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Florida. In the Badlands corpus she appears as the sponsor of H.Res. 500, a measure to impose security vetting requirements on congressional staff.

## H.Res. 500

As described in the July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief, Cammack called for an immediate House vote on the resolution, which she had introduced earlier. H.Res. 500 would amend House rules to require criminal background checks for all House employees. It would additionally mandate public disclosure — filed with the Clerk of the House and made available to the public — of any payments, contracts, or agreements with foreign governments in the three years prior to employment, as well as any foreign citizenship or nationality held by the employee. The requirements would apply both to current staff and to new hires.

Cammack's stated rationale is that congressional staff routinely handle sensitive personal information yet face no mandatory background checks of the kind required for executive-branch employees, and that repeated efforts to advance the measure have stalled.

## Reception in the corpus

The Brief reports that [[anna-paulina-luna|Anna Paulina Luna]] amplified Cammack's bill over the weekend. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the effort "backfired spectacularly, as Americans everywhere reacted in shock that this wasn't already happening," citing the [[eric-swalwell|Swalwell]]–[[fang-fang|Fang Fang]] affair and [[dianne-feinstein|Feinstein]]'s longtime driver as examples of counterintelligence failures that should long ago have prompted such a rule. Her broader argument is that [[congress|Congress]] "continues to prove that they are the People's real problem — and problems don't get to design their own solutions."
