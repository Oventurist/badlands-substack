# Sloan Rachmuth

Sloan Rachmuth is an investigative journalist associated with Education First Alliance of North Carolina whose reporting alleged that leading North Carolina medical institutions — [[duke-university|Duke]], UNC, and ECU — were offering "transgender" treatments to children as young as two, three, and four years old.[1]

## Reporting

Rachmuth's investigation documented the 2015 launch of the Duke Medicine Gender Clinic under director [[deanna-adkins|Dr. Deanna Adkins]], which offered a comprehensive range of treatments for gender dysphoria in children as young as two.[1] The findings were amplified nationally by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and circulated through the Truth Community via the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

Commentary accompanying the coverage disputed the framing that early-childhood care is benign, noting the standard claim that "before puberty, treatment does not include any drug or surgical intervention" but instead encourages social transition.[1]
