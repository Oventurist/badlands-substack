# Castro Family

The Castro family ruled [[cuba]] from the 1959 revolution until the transfer of the presidency to [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel]], with [[fidel-castro|Fidel Castro]] and later his brother Raúl heading both the state and the Communist Party. The family name remains shorthand in Cuban politics for the revolutionary dynasty and its surviving network of officials.

In the [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]], the family resurfaces in speculation prompted by reports that [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] met with Díaz-Canel and senior regime officials while the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration tightened sanctions and pursued regional isolation of Havana. Commenting on the combination of economic pressure and backchannel diplomacy, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] suggested Trump was "queuing up Cuba to be the next domino to fall" and asked whether "the Castro Family [will] return to power as a partner of President Trump" — an interpretive claim advanced without documentary support in the source.
