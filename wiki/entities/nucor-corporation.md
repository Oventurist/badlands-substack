# Nucor Corporation

Nucor Corp. is the largest steel producer in the United States.[1] In February 2025 the company publicly backed the tariffs imposed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on China, [[canada|Canada]] and [[mexico|Mexico]].[1]

Nucor chairman, president and chief executive Leon J. Topalian said, "Nucor applauds the first steps taken by President Trump in his America First Trade Agenda," adding that the company looked forward to working with the president to enforce U.S. trade laws and strengthen American manufacturing.[1]

## Context

Trump's order, signed on February 1, 2025, imposed the tariffs under the [[international-emergency-economic-powers-act|International Emergency Economic Powers Act]], effective the following Tuesday, applying a 25 percent tariff on imports from Canada and Mexico and a 10 percent tariff on imports from China, with Canadian energy imports facing a reduced 10 percent rate.[1] A one-month delay was agreed at the eleventh hour for the Mexican tariffs while Mexico negotiated with the administration.[1]
