# Grand Duke Constantine

Grand Duke Constantine was the brother and advisor of Czar [[alexander-ii|Alexander II]] and one of the statesmen who gained influence in the reformed Russian government that followed the [[crimean-war|Crimean War]].[1]

Before the American [[american-civil-war|Civil War]] began, Constantine wrote to Ambassador [[eduard-de-stoeckl|Eduard de Stoeckl]] that "the United States of North America should in the course of events be eager to conquer all of North America and will therefore meet us sooner or later and there is not the slightest doubt that it will seize our colonies without great effort, and we shall be in no possession to retain them."[1] The observation anticipated the logic of the eventual [[alaska-purchase|sale of Alaska]].[1]

On 26 December 1864, Secretary of State [[william-seward|William Seward]] wrote encouraging Constantine to visit America, saying such a visit "would be beneficial to us, and by no means unprofitable to Russia," and leaving his reasons unstated on the assumption the Grand Duke would grasp them.[1]
