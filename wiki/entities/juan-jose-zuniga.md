# Juan José Zúñiga

Juan José Zúñiga is the disgruntled former Bolivian army chief who led the short-lived assault on the presidential palace in La Paz on Wednesday, June 26, 2024.[1]

## The 2024 palace assault

Armoured vehicles circled the Plaza Murillo, the normally tranquil central square of historic downtown La Paz, before a phalanx of troops marched on the presidential palace.[1] By 2:30pm a small tank was repeatedly ramming the gates of the neoclassical Palacio Quemado until troops forced their way in, and Zúñiga confronted President [[luis-arce|Luis Arce]] face to face.[1] Flanked by cabinet ministers and clasping a ceremonial baton symbolizing his rank as head of state, the 60-year-old Arce ordered Zúñiga to stand down, telling him: "I am your captain … withdraw all of your troops right now, general."[1] The filmed exchange lasted several minutes and ended with Zúñiga leaving through the same broken door and departing in a bulletproof army vehicle.[1]

## Allegations of a self-coup

Arce was subsequently accused of plotting the coup against himself to boost his popularity.[1] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the episode as a contemporary example of a ruling regime staging a military coup against itself to build public sympathy, drawing a parallel to intelligence-driven optics management.[1]
