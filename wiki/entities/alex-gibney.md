# Alex Gibney

Alex Gibney is an American documentary filmmaker and producer. He produced [[the-bibi-files|*The Bibi Files*]], the documentary built around leaked police interrogation videos of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], which premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2024 despite Netanyahu's attempts to block its screening.[1]

## The Bibi Files

Gibney received the leaked interrogation footage from a source; director [[alexis-bloom|Alexis Bloom]] had begun work on the project before October 7, 2023, and the leaked videos arrived by way of Gibney.[1] Of Netanyahu, Gibney said: "I've never seen the depth of moral corruption as I've seen in this man."[1]

## Earlier work

Badlands Media noted Gibney's credibility on corporate criminality, pointing to his 2005 documentary *Enron: The Smartest Guys in the Room*, which exposed the depths of criminality surrounding that scandal.[1]

## Badlands framing

Writing in the September 11, 2024 *Badlands News Brief*, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that the film's core argument — that Netanyahu prolonged the war in Gaza and allied with the violent far right and attempted a supreme court overhaul to save himself from prosecution — "all sounds familiar," because Badlands had been covering the same pattern on the Brief for months.[1]
