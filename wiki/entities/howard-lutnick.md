# Howard Lutnick

## Open-source AI (2026)

White House Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick — described by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as "the core voice behind Trump's Tariff Trade Agenda" — reportedly said in late July 2026 that the administration aims to "protect open source AI."[1] The statement was cited as one of three weekend indicators, alongside remarks by former AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] and China's timed open-weight model releases, that the [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source AI]] shadow war was breaking in favor of acceleration rather than the regulatory approach urged by [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[anthropic|Anthropic]].[1]
