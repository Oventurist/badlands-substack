# Laura A. Jacobs

Laura A. Jacobs is a New York–based psychotherapist and trans-identified male who, according to reporting by [[reduxx|Reduxx]] summarized in the Badlands News Brief, influenced the development of transgender health guidelines concerning "gender dysphoria" in adolescents.[1]

## Role in WPATH

Jacobs was presented publicly as a certified member of the [[wpath|World Professional Association for Transgender Health]], but Reduxx reported that his influence within the organization ran deeper: he served as an influential member of the committee that established the most recent [[wpath-standards-of-care|Standards of Care]].[1]

## TEDx remarks

Video of a TEDx talk Jacobs delivered three years prior to the March 2024 report began circulating on social media, shared by the accounts Gays Against Groomers and Helen Joyce, and drew criticism for his speculation about experimental surgeries.[1] In the clip Jacobs said: "Medical interventions allow some of us to change our primary and secondary sex characteristics… but it's clear that cisgender bodies are still the reference point. Will technology give us options that are artistic and creative? Do we have to stick to penis and vagina norms? Can we have genitalia that look like flowers or abstract sculpture? Can we have multiple? Can they be interchangeable?"[1]

## Writings and practice

Jacobs previously contributed a chapter to an anthology in which he promoted BDSM practices, including "age play" and "genital torture."[1] He counsels troubled pre-teens, has described himself in professional directory listings as a "kinky, BDSM sexworker," and states that he works with clients ranging from "adolescents to the elderly."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] read the Jacobs case as evidence of a convergence between queer theory, [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]] and [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] ambitions, arguing that [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] oppressor/oppressed framing serves as a "plug & play" mechanism that could eventually be applied to normalize pedophilia.[1]
