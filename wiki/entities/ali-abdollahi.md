# Ali Abdollahi

Ali Abdollahi is an Iranian military officer identified as commander of Iran's Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters, the joint command responsible for coordinating the country's armed forces in operational matters.

In the July 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, Abdollahi appears through [[rt|RT]] reporting on the funeral of Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]]. He is described as warning the United States and Israel against carrying out any attacks during the multi-city funeral ceremonies, which ran from Tehran through Qom, Najaf and Karbala to burial in Mashhad. His warning is reported in the Brief alongside a statement attributed to Israeli Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] that [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] was "marked for death," and alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s claim that hostilities and U.S.–Iran talks would both pause for roughly a week during the mourning period.
