# Jennifer Lopez

Jennifer Lopez, commonly referred to as "J Lo," is an American entertainer who became a subject of political controversy during the closing days of the 2024 United States presidential campaign. She publicly warned voters against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a stance that drew sharp criticism from [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

## Musk criticism over Sean Combs

Speaking on [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s podcast, Musk argued that Lopez's willingness to campaign against Trump sat uneasily beside her long public silence regarding her former boyfriend [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]]. Musk said: "J Lo was like his ex-girlfriend and it's like now deciding she's like warning people against Trump. How many people did she warn against Diddy, right? Oh zero, ok. Maybe we shouldn't trust her."[1]

Lopez and Combs dated from 1999 to 2001, a period that overlaps with the time frame of several later allegations against Combs, including an alleged assault on a 13-year-old girl.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media commentary framed the exchange as significant less for what it said about Combs — described as a figure already "presented to us as a super villain" — than for its focus on the people who allegedly gave him cover. Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Musk's remarks were relevant because an incoming Trump administration would have the opportunity to act on promises to pursue predators, and asked whether "the next phase" of accountability for the enablers of Combs's circle had already begun.[1]
