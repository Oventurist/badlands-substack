# Philip Holloway

Philip Holloway is an Atlanta-based criminal defense attorney and legal analyst who has commented publicly on the Fulton County prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Comment on the McAfee campaign donation

In February 2024, Holloway told the Daily Caller News Foundation that the $150 campaign contribution made by Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] to District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] was "nominal."[1] He nevertheless argued that the donation should still have been disclosed to the defendants so that they could determine "whether they believed that amounted to a conflict of interest on the part of the judge."[1] The remark was quoted in the [[daily-caller|Daily Caller]] report excerpted by the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024.[1]
