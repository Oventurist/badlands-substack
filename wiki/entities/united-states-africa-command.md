# United States Africa Command

United States Africa Command (AFRICOM) is one of the geographic combatant commands of the U.S. military, responsible for American military operations and security cooperation across the African continent.[1]

## Proposed merger with European Command

In March 2025, CNN reported that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] was considering merging Africa Command with [[united-states-european-command|U.S. European Command]] into a single combatant command, and separately merging U.S. Northern Command with U.S. Southern Command.[1] The reports came amid parallel reporting that the administration might relinquish the [[supreme-allied-commander-europe|Supreme Allied Commander Europe]] post held by an American four-star general.[1]

Senate and House Armed Services Committee chairmen [[roger-wicker|Roger Wicker]] and [[mike-rogers|Mike Rogers]] objected publicly, describing combatant commands as "the tip of the American warfighting spear" and warning against unilateral restructuring or troop reductions undertaken without coordination with the [[white-house|White House]] and [[congress|Congress]].[1] Badlands commentary framed the consolidation as a rational streamlining measure consistent with a posture in which the United States stops conducting wars around the world.[1]
