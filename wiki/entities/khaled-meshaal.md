# Khaled Meshaal

Khaled Meshaal is a former chief of [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## "Day of Jihad" call

On 13 October 2023, Meshaal designated a "Day of Jihad," a call that drew international attention amid the escalating Israel-Hamas conflict.[1] His declaration came one day before [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] warned law enforcement of a rising number of terror threats against Americans and of the risk that lone actors inspired by recent events might attempt Hamas copy-cat attacks on US soil.[1]
