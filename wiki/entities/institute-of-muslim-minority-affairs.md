# Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs

The Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs (IMMA) is the entity established in the late 1970s by Saudi financier [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], who recruited the parents of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] to run it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Since its founding, the IMMA's main purpose has been to publish the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The Badlands account presents the Institute as the organizational vehicle by which the Abedin family was brought to [[jeddah|Jeddah]] and placed at the beginning of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s long-term strategy of "civilization/stealth jihad" — the incremental infiltration of sharia principles into the law, institutions and public policy of non-Muslim countries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Naseef's association with the enterprise persisted for decades until he was named a defendant in the civil suit brought by 9/11 victims over his alleged fundraising for [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
