# Ziad El Chaar

**Ziad El Chaar** is the chief executive officer of [[dar-global|Dar Global]], the internationally listed luxury real estate arm of the Saudi developer [[dar-al-arkan|Dar Al Arkan]].[1]

## Statements and activity in 2024

Announcing his company's second collaboration with [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]] on July 1, 2024 — the planned [[trump-tower-jeddah|Trump Tower Jeddah]] — El Chaar said Dar Global was "delighted to strengthen our ongoing relationship with The Trump Organization and expand our portfolio by delivering premium properties to redefine Saudi Arabia's high-growth real estate market," adding that the agreement would attract more international investors and vacationers and reflected a commitment to elevating standards of luxury living in the region.[1]

Days earlier, El Chaar had disclosed that Dar Global intended to enter the United States market for the first time with roughly $300 million in residential investment across New York, Miami, and Los Angeles, saying the firm hoped to announce a development before year-end and had met with top U.S. developers while declining to name them.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read this reticence as an indication that the unnamed partner was likely the Trump Organization.[1]
