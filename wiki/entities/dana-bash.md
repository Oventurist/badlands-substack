# Dana Bash

Dana Bash is an American television journalist and anchor for CNN, where she conducts political interviews with senior U.S. officials.[1]

## March 2026 Trump interview

During the war involving [[iran|Iran]], [[israel|Israel]] and the United States, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] discussed broader foreign policy questions in a phone interview with Bash.[1] In that conversation Trump said [[cuba|Cuba]] "is gonna fall pretty soon" and indicated he planned to send Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] there for potential talks.[1] Asked about Iran's future government, Trump affirmed that he would be willing to accept a "religious leader" rather than a democratically elected one, provided the leader was friendly to the United States.[1]

The interview became a focal point of commentary because it coincided with the selection of [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] as Iran's new supreme leader and with signs of a rift between the [[white-house|White House]] and the Israeli government.[1]

## Harris-Walz interview (August 2024)

Bash conducted the first joint television interview of the 2024 Democratic ticket on August 29, 2024, questioning [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] on CNN.[2] Badlands Media characterized her questioning as consisting mostly of softballs that allowed Harris to rehabilitate her record of policy reversals, though Bash did raise [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s comments about Harris's racial identity, which Harris deflected as the "same old tired playbook."[2]
