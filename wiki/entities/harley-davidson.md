# Harley-Davidson

Harley-Davidson Inc. is the American motorcycle manufacturer that in 2024 became one of the earliest and most prominent corporations to abandon diversity, equity and inclusion programs under pressure from the anti-woke campaign led by commentator and filmmaker [[robby-starbuck|Robby Starbuck]].[1]

## 2024 DEI reversal

Reporting by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] identified Harley-Davidson, alongside Tractor Supply Co. and Deere & Co., as companies that reversed or entirely eliminated [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] programs following Starbuck's exposés, a sequence that continued when he named [[jack-daniels|Jack Daniel's]] and its parent [[brown-forman|Brown-Forman Corp.]] as his next target.[1]

Badlands commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the company approvingly: "Harley Davidson gets it. At the end of the day, despite the immense pressure and payoffs from the Davos crowd, the customer is, actually, always right."[1] The broader argument held that consumer voice and spending had made a once-inevitable societal transformation reversible, and urged readers to reject globalism in favor of family, community and nation.[1]
