# Jessica Reed Kraus

Jessica Reed Kraus is an independent writer who covers American politics and the 2024 presidential campaign on Substack and through her House Inhabit social media accounts.[1]

In September 2024 Kraus was announced as the moderator of a conversation with [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and former Democratic Representative [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] at Arizona Christian University in Glendale, Arizona, held as part of campaigning for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The event followed Kennedy's decision to end his independent presidential campaign and endorse Trump at an earlier Arizona rally.[1]

Her selection as moderator reflected the growing role of independent, subscription-based journalists in campaign events that in earlier cycles would have been mediated by legacy outlets.[1]
