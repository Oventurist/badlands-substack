# National Legal and Policy Center

The National Legal and Policy Center (NLPC) is a watchdog organization that investigates and reports on ethics and financial disclosure issues involving public figures and nonprofit organizations.[1]

## Reporting on the Women's Wellness Spa(ce)

In May 2025, the NLPC was cited as the source of findings that the Women's Wellness Spa(ce), a tax-exempt nonprofit founded in 2023 by [[ashley-biden|Ashley Biden]], failed to report a total of $500,000 in donations in its first year to the Internal Revenue Service.[1] The organization states that it serves women who have experienced trauma.[1] According to the watchdog's findings, one of the unreported donations came from a nonprofit affiliated with Prince Harry and Meghan Markle.[1] The reporting was framed as part of continuing financial questions surrounding the family of former president [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]
