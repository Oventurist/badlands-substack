# Jim Biden

James "Jim" Biden is the younger brother of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and a recurring figure in reporting and commentary about the Biden family's foreign business dealings.[1]

## 2017 Ireland trip

According to analysis published by Badlands Media contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], Jim Biden traveled to Ireland in September 2017 together with his nephew [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] at the same time that Joe Biden — then a recently departed vice president — was in the country.[1] Flight itineraries cited by Herold place Joe Biden in Ireland from 11 September 2017 to 17 September 2017, with Jim and Hunter Biden traveling on the same dates and their arrangements coordinated by longtime Biden aide [[kathy-chung|Kathy Chung]].[1]

While in Ireland, Jim and Hunter Biden are described as having met "off site" with members of the Chinese energy conglomerate [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC China Energy]] and associated "trade group" figures, a location choice attributed in the underlying documents to the "sensitivity of the discussion and participants."[1] The meeting is dated to 13 September 2017.[1]

## Rosneft follow-up

Two days after the meeting, Herold reports, Jim Biden received a follow-up email from Sam Morison of the "Trade Group" outlining two options, one of which was described as "the Rosneft transaction" — a reference to the Russian state oil company [[rosneft|Rosneft]].[1] The Biden party returned to the United States on 17 September 2017, and four days later Hunter Biden sent the widely circulated "office mates" email describing shared office arrangements involving CEFC and Joe Biden.[1]

Herold frames this sequence as raising an unresolved question: whether Joe Biden himself attended the sensitive Ireland meeting.[1] The commentary also links the itineraries to congressional oversight activity by Representative [[james-comer|James Comer]], who has sought testimony from Chung.[1]

## Qatari business ties

In April 2024, new details about Jim Biden's foreign fundraising efforts emerged in a Kentucky bankruptcy court, where testimony indicated that the president's brother had partnered with Qatari government officials in his quest to find money for U.S. health care ventures.[2] Sworn testimony by fund manager Michael Lewitt, a former business partner of Jim Biden's, attested that two companies which facilitated the efforts were part-owned by "members of the Qatari government."[2] One company named in the testimony partnered directly with Jim Biden in the multi-year fundraising effort, while the second provided financial backing for a series of loans that a hospital chain paid Jim Biden to arrange, according to documents and testimony Lewitt submitted in the federal bankruptcy proceedings.[2]

Reporting by [[politico|Politico]] noted that if substantiated, the alleged arrangements would constitute some of the closest known financial links between a relative of President Joe Biden and a foreign government.[2] The arrangements are said to stem from an effort by Jim Biden to raise money from Qatari sources for U.S. ventures beginning in the months after his older brother left the vice presidency — a period when [[qatar|Qatar]], a small gas-rich nation, faced a financial blockade from its neighbors and was spending lavishly to shore up its political standing in the West.[2]
