# Food Brings Hope

Food Brings Hope is a Florida-based charitable organization that "provides assistance for homeless and economically disadvantaged students."[1] Its founder and chair is [[forough-hosseini|Forough Hosseini]], a Volusia County philanthropist and political donor married to homebuilding executive [[morteza-hosseini|Morteza "Mori" Hosseini]].[1]

## Political context

The organization drew attention in July 2023 when [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] examined the Hosseinis' donation history in connection with Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]].[1] Ashe noted that the same figure who chairs a charity for homeless children had donated to the [[public-affairs-alliance-of-iranian-americans|Iranian American Political Action Committee]], whose recipients included Democratic groups such as "Friends of Hillary Clinton" and "Friends of Anthony Weiner," and that she was simultaneously "making policy decisions about homeless children in Florida" through her gubernatorial appointment to the [[florida-children-and-youth-cabinet|Florida Children and Youth Cabinet]].[1]
