# Vance Boelter

Vance Luther Boelter is the man identified by authorities as the suspect in the June 14, 2025 targeted shootings of two Minnesota lawmakers, which killed Minnesota House Speaker [[melissa-hortman|Melissa Hortman]] and her husband Mark at their Brooklyn Park home and wounded State Senator [[john-hoffman|John Hoffman]] and his wife Yvette at their home in Champlin.[1] He was 57 years old at the time of the attacks.[1]

## Manhunt and capture

Investigators said the shooter had been dressed as a police officer during the attacks.[1] Boelter's car was located on Sunday, June 15, according to CBS Minnesota, and the Minnesota State Patrol reported that a stack of "No Kings" flyers was found inside the vehicle — flyers referencing the anti-Trump [[no-kings-protests|"No Kings" protests]] held in cities nationwide that Saturday.[1] Statewide "No Kings" events planned for Minnesota were canceled following the shootings.[1] A Minnesota police source confirmed to Fox News that Boelter was arrested in Sibley County on the evening of Sunday, June 15, 2025.[1]

## Background and investigation

Records cited in reporting showed Boelter had served on a state board with Hoffman for more than six years, and authorities said they were looking into whether he knew either lawmaker personally.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] highlighted the unusual details of Boelter's biography that circulated online, quoting a widely shared post asking whether he was an intelligence asset "or just a normal 7/11 manager turned preacher who owns a security company and lives in a halfway house away from his wife and 5 kids."[1] The commentary described the case as "a real head scratcher" and objected to attempts by figures in both parties to assign the assassin to a partisan camp.[1]
