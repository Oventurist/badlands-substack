# World Economic Forum

The World Economic Forum (WEF) is the Davos-based international organization that convenes political leaders, corporate executives, and technocrats. Across the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as a principal institutional vehicle of globalist governance rather than as a neutral convening body.

## Treatment in the Badlands corpus

In the November 7, 2025 roundup [[a-look-at-recent-features-84d|"A Look At Recent Features"]], the Forum appears in the excerpt for [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s feature "On Ted Kaczynski and False Prophets," which speaks of "the leading oligarchists running the World Economic Forum and [[great-reset|Great Reset]] today." The phrasing identifies the WEF with an identifiable elite stratum — "oligarchists" — and links it directly to the Great Reset program.

The same excerpt asserts that these figures share "common axioms" with [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]], the Unabomber: that is, a shared hostility to industrial civilization and to the premise of open-ended human material progress. In this reading, the WEF's decarbonization and degrowth agendas are not a break with anti-technological extremism but a boardroom expression of it. This connects to the companion Ehret features listed in the same brief, "Combating the Transhumanist Religion" and "The Rising Scourge of Eco-Terrorism," which describe coordinated "above ground" and "below ground" movements working toward what the author calls "the willful self-destruction of industrial civilization."
