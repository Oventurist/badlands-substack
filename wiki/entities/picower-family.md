# Picower Family

The Picower family are the single-largest beneficiaries of [[bernie-madoff|Bernie Madoff]]'s Ponzi scheme and, according to reporting published in January 2025, are financing climate change litigation and judicial influence campaigns in the United States.[1]

## Climate lawfare funding

Writing for the American Energy Institute, an analyst argued that the Picowers are "forcing shoddy electric appliances into every American home to assuage their guilty consciences," and that as the largest beneficiaries of the Madoff scheme they "have plenty to feel awkward about."[1] One way they are said to be excising that guilt is by financing climate change lawsuits and left-wing judicial influence schemes that would strip Americans of conveniences such as hot showers or efficient dishwashers.[1]

The American Energy Institute report described a broader pattern in which left-wing city governments and blue states sue energy providers over climate change with the goal of forcing unpopular lifestyle products into every home.[1] Plaintiffs could achieve that goal either through government spending financed by legal awards or through settlement terms requiring energy companies to change their product offerings, an effort the report says is being midwifed by a left-wing lobbying campaign disguised as judicial education.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer Ashe in America tied the story to the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]], arguing that globalists weaponize law for global power and control and that "climate change has always been the umbrella op for the expansion of global communism."[1] The commentary distinguished between the uncontroversial claim that climate changes and the disputed claim that man-made warming justifies radical lifestyle mandates, noting that elites pursue "net zero" offsets for themselves while demanding behavioral change from ordinary people.[1] It recommended citing the article to describe climate policy as, literally, the Bernie Madoff of scams.[1]
