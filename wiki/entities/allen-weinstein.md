# Allen Weinstein

Allen Weinstein (1937–2015) was an American historian and the founding figure behind the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] (NED), the congressionally funded nonprofit established in 1983 as a bipartisan vehicle for "democracy promotion" abroad.[1]

## The 1991 admission

Weinstein is best known in critical literature for a remark given to the Washington Post in 1991: "A lot of what we do today was done covertly 25 years ago by the CIA."[1] Badlands Media's "The Information Imperium" treats this statement as the single most revealing description of the NED's function, arguing that it confirms the endowment operates as "a CIA grant-making cut-out that provides the funding and cover for various intelligence operations."[1]

## Context

The article situates Weinstein's creation of the NED in the aftermath of the 1970s exposure of [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] media operations — including the revelations produced by the [[church-committee|Church Committee]] and the history of [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]] and [[frank-wisner|Frank Wisner]]'s "Mighty Wurlitzer."[1] With covert propaganda discredited, the NED provided an overt, congressionally appropriated channel to continue similar work under a democratic-assistance banner, complementing [[usaid|USAID]]'s resources and legitimacy.[1]

By 2019 the endowment Weinstein founded was distributing roughly $300 million annually, funnelled principally through four core grantees — the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]], the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]], the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]] and the [[center-for-international-private-enterprise|Center for International Private Enterprise]] — plus a wide web of NGO grants.[1]
