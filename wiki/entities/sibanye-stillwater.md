# Sibanye-Stillwater

Sibanye-Stillwater is a South African precious metals mining company that operates the Kloof gold mine, located roughly 37 miles west of Johannesburg and among the company's deepest operations.[1]

In May 2025, 260 miners became trapped underground at Kloof after an accident on Thursday damaged the hoist system used to access a shaft; all were brought safely to the surface on Friday afternoon with no injuries reported.[1] The company said it was "engaging with the Chief Inspector of Mines, the Department of Mineral Resources and Energy, and trade unions," and that operations would resume only once safety and risk assessments were concluded and the shaft deemed safe.[1] Chief executive [[neal-froneman|Neal Froneman]] said safety remained the company's core priority and thanked management, mine rescue, and medical teams for returning the workers to their families.[1]
