# Maru Campos

Maru Campos is the governor of the northern Mexican state of Chihuahua.[1]

## Pressure after the 2026 crash

Campos came under political pressure in May 2026 following the fatal crash that killed two [[cia|CIA]] officers and two Mexican officials returning from a counter-narcotics raid in the El Pinal area of Chihuahua.[1] She skipped a congressional hearing during the week of the incident, saying the decision was intended to "ensure the proper development of the ongoing proceedings, avoiding at all times the compromising of information of a confidential or classified nature."[1]

The episode also cost state attorney general [[cesar-jauregui-moreno|César Jáuregui Moreno]] his post after he admitted "omissions" about the American agents' presence, and unfolded alongside fallout from the [[indictment-of-mexican-officials-in-the-sinaloa-cartel-case|U.S. indictments of Mexican officials]] over alleged [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] ties.[1]
