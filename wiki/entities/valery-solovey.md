# Valery Solovey

Dr Valery Solovey is a Russian political analyst and former professor at Moscow's Institute of International Relations (MGIMO), an institution described in reporting as a training school for Russian spies and diplomats.[1] He claims to have sources inside the Kremlin.[1]

## Prigozhin claim

In August 2023 Solovey claimed that Wagner leader [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] had survived the plane crash attributed to him and was "alive, well, and free" in an unnamed country, with a body double killed in his place.[1] He alleged that the assassination attempt had been sanctioned by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and prepared by the Russian security council, and that Russian authorities were lying about Prigozhin's DNA being identified at the Tver region crash site.[1]

## Track record and criticism

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] catalogued a series of prior unsupported Solovey claims about Putin, including that Putin "seriously intends to demonstrate the capabilities of nuclear weapons — tactical nuclear weapons," that he was "in very poor physiological health," that he was "not able to adequately assess the situation and make proper decisions," and that his health was "dramatically deteriorating."[1] Solovey also claimed Putin harbored "a very strong desire to take the world with him" because he knew he would "pass away in a limited time."[1] These prior claims were offered as reason to treat the Prigozhin assertion with caution.[1]
