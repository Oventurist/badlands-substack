# Tudor Dixon

Tudor Dixon is an American conservative commentator and former Republican gubernatorial candidate in Michigan who hosts a political podcast.[1] In September 2023 she conducted a widely circulated conversation with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in which she pressed him on the transparency of COVID-19 vaccine safety data.[1]

## Interview with Donald Trump on vaccine data

During the podcast conversation, Dixon told Trump that "numerous pharmaceutical companies have refused to release their data on vaccine side effects," while noting that cases of myocarditis, blood clots, and heart attacks were increasing and that the underlying research had never been released.[1] She asked Trump directly whether he would demand that pharmaceutical companies release their vaccine data to the public so that Americans could see what the manufacturers themselves were observing about side effects.[1]

Trump responded that the companies "should do that," adding that "we're all in this together, and they should be doing that," and said that in the context of the Biden administration's push to fund a new COVID-19 vaccine, "anything new has got to be looked at very carefully."[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands Media commentary framed the exchange as significant because Trump's relationship to the COVID-19 vaccines remained "one of the most contentious issues still rearing its head within the Truth Community."[1] Writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump had been consistent in framing masking and vaccination as an individual choice free of coercion, and speculated that Dixon's line of questioning might mark the beginning of a clearer Trump position on pandemic policy ahead of the 2024 election cycle.[1]
