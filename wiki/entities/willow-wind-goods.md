# Willow Wind Goods

**Willow Wind Goods** is a small artisanal textile business that has appeared as a sponsor of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], the Substack newsletter published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Products

The company sells handmade alpaca and Merino wool goods, described in its sponsored placement as "artisanal alpaca and Merino wool treasures," with every item "uniquely knit and woven to perfection from the finest fiber to keep you warm and comfortable for all occasions."[1] Its product line includes beanies, mittens, socks, and knit and woven throws, marketed as gifts for loved ones.[1]

## Relationship with Badlands Media

Willow Wind Goods is promoted to Badlands readers through a dedicated landing page at badlandsmedia.tv/annie, associated with Willow Wind Farm, and offers a discount to readers who use the promo code "Badlands."[1] The placement appeared in the December 25, 2025 Christmas edition of the Badlands News Brief, an edition that suspended the newsletter's usual news roundup in favour of a seasonal message to readers.[1] Such sponsored segments are part of the revenue model that allows Badlands Media to distribute its content free of charge while soliciting voluntary paid subscriptions.[1]

## December 2025 sponsorship

Willow Wind Goods was featured as a sponsor in the December 24, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], promoted with the promo code "Badlands."[2] The advertisement described artisanal alpaca and Merino wool products - beanies, mittens, socks and knit and woven throws - each uniquely knit and woven from fine fiber, and directed readers to Willow Wind Farm.[2]
