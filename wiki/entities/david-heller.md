# David Heller

**David Heller** is a former [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] executive named in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as one of two Goldman Sachs figures appointed by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] to his finance committee after the 2008 election.[1] The other was [[bruce-heyman|Bruce Heyman]].[1]

Carlson introduces the appointments as part of a chain connecting Goldman Sachs's investment in [[first-solar|First Solar]] and its campaign contributions to Obama with the federal money — 2009 stimulus funds, [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] loan guarantees, and green energy subsidies — that subsequently flowed to the company.[1] Heller himself is not accused of any specific act in the article; his relevance is as evidence of Goldman Sachs's institutional proximity to the administration's finance operation.[1]
