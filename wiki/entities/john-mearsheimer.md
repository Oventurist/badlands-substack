# John Mearsheimer

**John Mearsheimer** is an American international-relations scholar cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the leading intellectuals of the anti-war, anti-establishment faction within the broader [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]

## Position in the coalition

In DeLarme's taxonomy of factions in [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], the anti-war, anti-establishment group is described as approaching issues with greater nuance and recognizing that both political parties are influenced by corporations, intelligence agencies and the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1] Mearsheimer is named alongside [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]] as an intellectual thought leader of the group, with anti-war commentators [[scott-horton|Scott Horton]] and [[glenn-greenwald|Glenn Greenwald]] listed as its other key voices.[1]

The faction spans left and right, including journalists [[max-blumenthal|Max Blumenthal]] and [[aaron-mate|Aaron Maté]] on the left and [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] and [[megyn-kelly|Megyn Kelly]] on the right.[1] Opposing [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]], the group naturally rejects U.S. funding of [[israel|Israel]]'s actions in Gaza and the West Bank — an opposition DeLarme identifies as central to the fracture in [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s coalition.[1]
