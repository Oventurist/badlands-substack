# Zabihullah Mujahid

Zabihullah Mujahid is the spokesman for the [[taliban|Taliban]]. He has served as the movement's principal public voice since its return to power in Afghanistan in August 2021.[1]

## Second anniversary statement

On Tuesday, August 15, 2023, marking the second anniversary of the Taliban's capture of Kabul, Mujahid issued a statement congratulating "the mujahid [holy warrior] nation of Afghanistan" and asking Afghans "to thank Almighty Allah for this great victory."[1]

He asserted that "overall security is ensured in the country, the entire territory of the country is managed under a single leadership, an Islamic system is in place and everything is explained from the angle of Sharia [Islamic law]."[1] The anniversary was observed as a public holiday; security was tight in Kabul, and convoys of Taliban members gathered at Massoud Square near the abandoned US embassy building, some carrying weapons and others taking selfies as anthems played and boys sold the movement's white flag inscribed with the Islamic declaration of faith.[1]

## Rejection of Trump's Bagram demand (September 2025)

As chief [[taliban|Taliban]] spokesman, Mujahid rejected President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s assertions about retaking [[bagram-air-base|Bagram Air Base]] on a Sunday in September 2025, urging the United States to adopt a policy of "realism and rationality."[2] He recalled that under the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] the United States pledged that "it will not use or threaten force against the territorial integrity or political independence of Afghanistan, nor interfere in its internal affairs," and said Washington needed to remain faithful to its commitments.[2]
