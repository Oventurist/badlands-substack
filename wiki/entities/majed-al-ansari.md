# Majed Al-Ansari

Majed Al-Ansari is the spokesperson for the foreign ministry of [[qatar|Qatar]], and in that role served as the public voice of Qatari mediation between Israel and [[hamas|Hamas]] during the November 2023 Gaza truce negotiations.[1]

On November 27, 2023, Al-Ansari confirmed in a statement posted on X that the truce — which had produced the return of 50 Israeli women and children from captivity in Gaza along with dozens of other nationals — had been extended by two additional days beyond the initial four-day pause.[1] Leaders in Israel and the United States quickly welcomed the announcement, and Hamas said it had agreed with Qatar and Egypt to extend the truce "under the same conditions reached before."[1]

By the time of the extension, 69 hostages had been freed by Hamas, 150 Palestinian women and children had been released from Israeli prisons, and several dozen truckloads of aid had reached the besieged enclave in what was described as the first respite for Palestinians in nearly two months of war.[1]
