# Edward Bernays

Edward Bernays was an American pioneer of public relations and propaganda theory, frequently cited in Badlands commentary as an articulator of elite management of mass opinion.[1]

Bernays famously wrote that "the engineering of consent is the very essence of the democratic process, the freedom to persuade and suggest."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the line while discussing [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s departure from day-to-day management of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], presenting Bernays as an early expositor of the belief system she attributed to the global elite — a worldview she compared to the *Hunger Games* prequel's President Snow and his claim that "the whole world is an arena."[1]
