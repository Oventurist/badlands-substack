# United States Northern Command

United States Northern Command (USNORTHCOM) is the U.S. combatant command responsible for homeland defense and defense support of civil authorities in North America.[1]

## Role in the June 2025 Los Angeles deployment

In June 2025, USNORTHCOM issued a statement regarding the protection of federal property and personnel in Los Angeles amid protests against immigration enforcement raids.[1] Officials said that about 2,000 [[california-army-national-guard|California Army National Guard]] soldiers had been placed under federal command to help protect federal personnel and property in the greater Los Angeles area, with roughly 300 Guard members from the 78th Infantry Brigade Combat Team deployed at locations in Los Angeles, Paramount and Compton.[1]

In addition, about 500 [[united-states-marine-corps|Marines]] from Twentynine Palms were placed in a "prepared to deploy status" in case they were needed to support the Department of Defense's protection of federal property and personnel.[1] California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] said President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was escalating the situation by threatening to deploy Marines into the city.[1]

## Response to the 2023 Russian-Chinese patrol off Alaska

In early August 2023, USNORTHCOM and the [[norad|North American Aerospace Defense Command]] deployed aircraft and ships to monitor a joint Russian and Chinese naval patrol that operated off the coast of Alaska.[2] A USNORTHCOM spokesperson told [[cnn|CNN]] that the patrol amounted to a show of force but did not pose a threat to the United States or Canada, and that the foreign vessels remained in international waters throughout.[2] Alaska's senators said eleven Russian and Chinese vessels had operated near the [[aleutian-islands|Aleutian Islands]] and were met by four [[us-navy|US Navy]] destroyers.[2]

## February 2024 command change

On February 5, 2024, Northern Command underwent a change of command presided over by Deputy Secretary of Defense [[kathleen-hicks|Kathleen Hicks]], one of several senior military leadership changes she oversaw in the days immediately before Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]]'s emergency hospitalization.[3] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted the timing alongside command changes at [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence]] and [[us-cyber-command|Cyber Command]] on February 2, 2024, noting Northern Command's stated mission to "plan, organize and execute homeland defense and civil support missions" and its relevance amid a wave of warnings about vulnerabilities in U.S. water and communications infrastructure.[3]

## Acquisition of Greenland in its area of responsibility (2025)

On June 17, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ordered [[greenland|Greenland]] moved from [[united-states-european-command|U.S. European Command]] into Northern Command's area of responsibility, on the rationale that the command is charged with defending the U.S. homeland.[4] Chief [[pentagon|Pentagon]] spokesman [[sean-parnell|Sean Parnell]] said the shift would strengthen the Joint Force's ability to defend the homeland, contribute to a more robust defense of the western hemisphere, and deepen relationships with Arctic allies and partners.[4] See [[2025-transfer-of-greenland-to-us-northern-command|2025 transfer of Greenland to U.S. Northern Command]].[4]
