# Eileen Higgins

Eileen Higgins is a Democratic politician who in December 2025 flipped the mayor's office of Miami, becoming the first Democrat to hold the post since 1997, according to Decision Desk HQ.[1] She defeated Republican Emilio Gonzalez, a former Miami city manager who had served on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Homeland Security Department transition team, succeeding incumbent Mayor Francis Suarez (R) in a technically nonpartisan race.[1]

The victory was described as the latest sign of Democratic momentum heading into the following year's midterms, following better-than-expected November elections and a strong showing in a special House election in Tennessee.[1]
