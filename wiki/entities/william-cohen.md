# William Cohen

William Cohen is a former Republican United States Senator from Maine who served as Secretary of Defense under President Bill Clinton.[1]

## 2024 endorsement of Kamala Harris

In September 2024, Cohen was among more than 100 Republican former national security figures who signed an open letter endorsing Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] for president and declaring [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "unfit to serve again as President, or indeed in any office of public trust."[1] See [[2024-republican-national-security-endorsement-of-harris|2024 Republican National Security Endorsement of Kamala Harris]].
