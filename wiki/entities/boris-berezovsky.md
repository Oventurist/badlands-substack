# Boris Berezovsky

Boris Berezovsky was a Russian oligarch of the 1990s, cited in Badlands commentary as an archetype of the tycoon class produced by the privatization wave of the post-Soviet era.[1]

## The 1990s oligarchy

An October 2025 Badlands News Brief analysis by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed Berezovsky alongside [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]] among the tycoons who, during [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]]'s "shock therapy" privatizations, snapped up state assets at fire-sale prices and amassed fortunes in energy, media and metals while ordinary Russians plunged into poverty.[1]

## Subordination under Putin

The same commentary described how [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], after ascending in 2000, waged a calculated war on this new elite — purging oligarchs who meddled in politics while allowing compliant ones to thrive under the Kremlin's umbrella.[1] The mechanism was the taking of state stakes in private companies, especially those pertinent to strategic defense and production, a de-privatization framed in Russia not as a communist restoration but as the reclamation of national destiny from Western-backed predators.[1]
