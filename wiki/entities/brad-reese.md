# Brad Reese

Brad Reese is the grandson of the inventor of Reese's Peanut Butter Cups.[1]

## Open letter to Hershey

In February 2026, Reese published an open letter to [[hershey-company|The Hershey Company]] accusing it of "quietly replacing" the ingredients in Reese's products and declaring that the candy's "identity is being rewritten."[1] He argued that his grandfather had built the brand on "a simple, enduring architecture: Milk Chocolate + Peanut Butter," and objected to formulation decisions substituting compound coatings for milk chocolate and "peanut-butter-style crèmes" for peanut butter across multiple Reese's products.[1]
