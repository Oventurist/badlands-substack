# Minouche Shafik

Minouche Shafik is the president of [[columbia-university]] during the pro-Palestinian campus demonstrations of April 2024.[1] After dozens of demonstrators set up an encampment of tents on campus, Shafik authorized the [[nypd|New York Police Department]] to help remove the encampment, an intervention in which more than 100 protesters were taken into custody according to NBC News.[1]

Her decision came amid national scrutiny of the [[2024-columbia-university-protests]], including a [[white-house]] statement condemning "blatantly Antisemitic" calls for violence and physical intimidation targeting Jewish students, and reports that a rabbi associated with the university had urged 300 Jewish students to leave campus ahead of Passover.[1] The campus Hillel publicly disagreed with that advice while arguing that the university and the city needed to do more to ensure student safety.[1]
