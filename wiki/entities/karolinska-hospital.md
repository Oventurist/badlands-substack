# Karolinska Hospital

Karolinska Hospital is a major Swedish teaching hospital associated with the Karolinska Institute in Stockholm. In Badlands Media coverage it is identified as the institution whose letter announced Sweden's withdrawal from the practice of prescribing [[puberty-blockers|puberty blockers]] and cross-sex hormones to children under 16 experiencing [[gender-dysphoria|gender dysphoria]].[1]

## The 2021 letter

In May 2021, Sweden became the first country to stop the practice, with the news breaking through a letter from Karolinska Hospital.[1] The letter cited a December 2019 overview of the knowledge base published by the SBU, the Swedish Agency for Health Technology Assessment and Assessment of Social Services, which showed "a lack of evidence for both the long-term consequences of the treatments, and the reasons for the large influx of patients in recent years."[1] It further warned that the treatments are "potentially fraught with extensive and irreversible adverse consequences such as cardiovascular disease, osteoporosis, infertility, increased cancer risk, and thrombosis," making risk-benefit assessment difficult for clinicians and still more difficult for minors and their guardians attempting to give informed consent.[1]

The letter went on to cite the contemporaneous protocol changes at the [[nhs|NHS]] in England, along with a highly publicised court ruling holding that "it is highly unlikely, if at all possible, for an individual under the age of 16 to give informed consent to this treatment," and advising that court approval be sought for 16- to 18-year-olds because the treatment should be considered experimental.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] presented the Swedish reversal as the second of three international developments that, in his account, should have ended the [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative model]] in North America, asking how another entire nation could abandon the model while it remained practised and defended in Canada and America — and answering: "Information warfare."[1]
