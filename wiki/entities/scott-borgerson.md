# Scott Borgerson

Scott Borgerson is a technology executive best known publicly as the former husband of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], the convicted sex trafficker and longtime associate of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Divorce settlement and Maxwell's appeal

Reporting in February 2023, sourced to an unnamed person close to the family and published exclusively by the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]], stated that Maxwell had reached a divorce settlement with Borgerson, who had previously refused to release money from Maxwell's $20 million trust.[1] Maxwell had sought access to those funds in order to pay an outstanding legal fee of $878,302 and to retain the lawyers who had represented [[harvey-weinstein|Harvey Weinstein]] to work on her own appeal.[1]

Following the settlement, the source described the then 60-year-old Maxwell as "ready to fight back," adding that "her lawyers are confident that she has a strong case for appeal."[1] The funds were reported to underwrite a $10 million appeal against her twenty-year sentence.[1]
