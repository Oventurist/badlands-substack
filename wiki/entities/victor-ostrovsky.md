# Victor Ostrovsky

Victor Ostrovsky is a former [[mossad|Mossad]] officer who published a whistleblower book exposing what he described as the corruption of the organization, titled *By Way of Deception*.[1]

## The Mossad motto

Ostrovsky is cited in Badlands commentary for the claim that Mossad's original official motto was "By way of deception, thou shalt do war."[1] The reference arose in response to a CBS News *60 Minutes* segment in which retired Mossad officers described the agency's motto, drawn from Proverbs 24:6, as meaning in effect that one should wage war through deception and trickery — likened in the segment to the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s "smoke and mirrors."[1]
