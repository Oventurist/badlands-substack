# Victor Ostrovsky

Victor Ostrovsky is a former officer of [[mossad|Mossad]], the Israeli foreign intelligence service, who became an author and public critic of the agency. He is best known for *By Way of Deception*, an insider account of Mossad's methods.

In the June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief, Ostrovsky is cited as a "Mossad whistleblower" for the claim that [[israel|Israel]] possessed advance knowledge of the 1983 attack that killed 241 United States Marines in Beirut and deliberately withheld the warning, on the reasoning that American deaths would draw the United States into fighting in the Middle East. The Brief invokes this claim while disputing the broadcaster [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]]'s attribution of the bombing to [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], stating that the attack was instead carried out by a group called Islamic Jihad. The assertion is presented by the newsletter as established history but remains contested outside its editorial frame.
