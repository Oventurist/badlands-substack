# Erel Segal

Erel Segal is an Israeli journalist and broadcaster identified as a close ally of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] He is cited in Badlands Media coverage as a representative voice of the segment of the Israeli right that reacted with anger to the January 2025 Gaza ceasefire agreement.[1]

## Opposition to the January 2025 Gaza ceasefire

Following the announcement of the [[gaza-ceasefire|Gaza ceasefire]] brokered in the weeks before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second inauguration, Segal publicly attacked the deal and placed the blame for it directly on Trump rather than on Netanyahu.[1] Badlands Media presented his reaction alongside that of Israeli national security minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] as evidence that "a significant portion of the Israeli right is furious with Trump," complicating the common assumption that Trump's Middle East policy was uniformly aligned with the Israeli political right.[1]

The article framed this backlash as part of a broader pattern: hardline figures in and around the Netanyahu government objected that the agreement forced Israel to halt its bombardment of Gaza without having achieved its stated aims, including the destruction of [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]
