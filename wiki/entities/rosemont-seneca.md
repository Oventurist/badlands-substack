# Rosemont Seneca

Rosemont Seneca is an investment firm associated with Hunter Biden that features prominently in Russian military allegations regarding biological laboratories in Ukraine.[1]

## Kirillov testimony

In testimony delivered at a formal hearing, Russian Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]] asserted that Rosemont Seneca was responsible for developing the COVID-19 pathogen in various bioweapons laboratories it allegedly operated illegally in Ukraine.[1] During the same testimony Kirillov connected the US government, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], the George Soros Foundation, and the Los Alamos laboratory — where the Manhattan Project was developed during the Second World War — to the alleged network.[1] Badlands commentary described this testimony as Kirillov's biggest claim to fame and a key reason for his significance in the modern information war.[1]

## Russian bioweapons allegations (2022)

The commander of the Russian Nuclear, Biological and Chemical Protection Forces named Rosemont Seneca in alleging a "scheme of interaction between US government agencies and Ukrainian biological objects," pointing to "the financing of such activities by structures close to the current US leadership, in particular the investment fund Rosemont Seneca, which is headed by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]."[2] Intelligence experts described the claim as a propaganda ploy meant to justify [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s invasion of Ukraine and sow discord in the United States, though emails from Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop did show he helped secure millions of dollars in funding for [[metabiota|Metabiota]], a [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] contractor researching pandemic-causing pathogens.[2]
