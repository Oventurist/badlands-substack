# Ontario

Ontario is the Canadian province whose government, led by Premier [[doug-ford|Doug Ford]], paid for a televised advertisement that triggered a rupture in US–Canada trade talks in October 2025.[1]

## Reagan tariff advertisement

The advertisement used audio from an address by former President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] criticizing tariffs.[1] [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called the ad "fraudulent," suspended trade talks with Canada, and said he would impose an additional 10 percent tariff on Canadian goods as punishment.[1] Ford announced on Friday, after speaking with Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], that the ad would stop airing on Monday.[1]

The episode illustrated Canada's broader approach to managing Trump — described as removing all irritants — which had already included walking back retaliatory tariffs and suspending, in June, a tax on American technology companies after Trump threatened to end negotiations until it was lifted.[1]

## Reagan tariff advertisement and the 2025 trade rupture

Ontario's provincial government aired an [[ontario-reagan-tariff-advertisement|advertisement]] in the United States featuring former President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] speaking negatively about tariffs, with Premier [[doug-ford|Doug Ford]] saying the province would spend $75 million on the campaign.[2] On Thursday night, October 23, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he was terminating all trade negotiations with [[canada|Canada]] over the ad, accusing Canada of trying to influence a pending U.S. Supreme Court case that could doom many of his tariffs.[2] The Ronald Reagan Presidential Foundation and Institute said the spot misrepresented an April 1987 radio address and that Reagan's remarks had been edited without permission.[2]
