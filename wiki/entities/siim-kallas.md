# Siim Kallas

Siim Kallas is an Estonian politician and the father of [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]], the European Union's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy.[1] Like his daughter, he served as Prime Minister of Estonia, and he also held office as a European Commissioner.[1]

Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme highlighted the sequence of his career — a period in Communist Russia followed by a stint as a central banker before his rise to European office — as illustrative of how thoroughly the Kallas family is embedded in the European governing class.[1] DeLarme offered the sardonic gloss that "government officials with intelligence ties make great bankers," using the family lineage to argue that his daughter's Russia policy reflects inherited elite position rather than democratic mandate.[1]
