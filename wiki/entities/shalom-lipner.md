# Shalom Lipner

Shalom Lipner is an Israeli writer and analyst affiliated with the Brookings Institution and the Atlantic Council.[1]

## "Israel's Trump Delusion"

In November 2024 Lipner published an article in Foreign Affairs titled "Israel's Trump Delusion: Why Netanyahu's Ambition to Remake the Middle East Is Unlikely to Succeed."[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the piece as evidence that not all establishment analysts expected [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s regional ambitions to be underwritten by the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, contrasting Lipner's skepticism with the maximalist rhetoric of Israeli cabinet ministers such as [[amihai-eliyahu|Amichai Eliyahu]] and [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]].[1]
