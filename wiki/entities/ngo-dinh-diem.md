# Ngo Dinh Diem

Ngo Dinh Diem was the president of South Vietnam who was murdered on November 2, 1963, only three weeks before the assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1]

As early as 1962, Kennedy had been coordinating an American extraction from Southeast Asia with Diem, a policy formalized in NSAM 263, introduced in October 1963 to begin a full withdrawal; Kennedy was assassinated one month later.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] wrote that JFK was furious when Diem was murdered by the same [[cia|CIA]] that would soon oversee his own assassination — the same agency that promoters of the Marilyn Monroe secret-diary narrative ask readers to believe was controlled by the Kennedys to kill their enemies.[1]

Diem's killing is listed by Ehret among the assassinations — alongside those of Trujillo, Lumumba, Mattei, JFK, [[robert-f-kennedy|RFK]] and Martin Luther King Jr. — managed through the NATO stay-behind apparatus inherited by General [[lyman-lemnitzer|Lyman Lemnitzer]].[1]
