# Laurance Rockefeller

**Laurance Spelman Rockefeller** was the third-eldest surviving son of [[john-d-rockefeller-jr|John D. Rockefeller Jr.]] and, in Badlands Media's account, the most business-savvy of the five brothers, making his mark as a venture capitalist.[1]

## Aviation and venture investments

An early interest in aviation paid off handsomely.[1] He invested in Eastern Airlines in 1938 alongside the aviator Captain Eddie Rickenbacker and helped turn the carrier into one of the world's largest.[1] He also invested heavily in the ambitions of a young Scotsman named James McDonnell Jr., who went on to launch what became the McDonnell-Douglas Aircraft Corporation.[1]

## Environmentalism

Laurance entered the field of environmentalism, serving as chairman of the Citizens Advisory Committee on Environmental Quality, president of the American Conservation Association, and chairman of the New York Zoological Society.[1]

## Podesta allegation

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] notes the recurring claim that Clinton aide and Biden climate adviser [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] was an illegitimate child of Laurance Rockefeller, adding that he has been unable either to find proof of the claim or to disprove it entirely.[1] A photo caption in the same article remarks that Laurance's features are "eerily similar" to Podesta's.[1]
