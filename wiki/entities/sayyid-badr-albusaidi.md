# Sayyid Badr Albusaidi

**Sayyid Badr Albusaidi** is the Foreign Minister of [[oman|Oman]], a state that has repeatedly acted as an intermediary between Washington and Tehran.[1]

## Role in the 2025 Iran nuclear proposal

In late May 2025, Albusaidi presented a US proposal for a nuclear agreement to [[iran|Iran]].[1] According to an Axios report cited by The Times of Israel, the proposal would allow Iran to retain low levels of uranium enrichment for civilian uses such as nuclear medicine and commercial power generation if it agreed to shut down its heavily protected underground enrichment sites for a period of time.[1] The report, sourced to two people with direct knowledge of the proposal and confirmed by two US officials speaking anonymously to the Associated Press, described terms that appeared to mirror the 2015 accord that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] withdrew from during his first term.[1]

Trump publicly insisted that any agreement with Iran would ban all enrichment, and lead US negotiator [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] said he agreed with [[israel|Israel]]'s insistence that any deal require Iran to cease all enrichment activity — a demand Tehran has steadfastly rejected.[1]
