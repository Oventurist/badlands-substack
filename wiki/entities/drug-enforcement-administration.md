# Drug Enforcement Administration

The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) is the federal agency responsible for administering the controlled substances schedules under United States drug policy.[1]

## Role in the 2026 marijuana rescheduling

When the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration reclassified state-licensed medical marijuana from Schedule I to Schedule III, the change set up a system under which state-licensed producers would register with the DEA.[1] The rescheduling did not legalize marijuana at the federal level but eased restrictions on a substance that had been in the most restrictive DEA category since the 1937 Marijuana Tax Act.[1]

Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], announcing the move through the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], said the reclassification would permit research into the safety and efficacy of medical marijuana and announced plans for a further expedited hearing, with set deadlines, aimed at fully rescheduling the drug.[1]


## Memphis Safe Task Force (September 2025)

The Drug Enforcement Administration was designated as a participating agency in the [[memphis-state-task-force|Memphis Safe Task Force]], created by executive order of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on 15 September 2025 to combat violent crime in [[memphis|Memphis]], Tennessee.[2] The task force also included the [[fbi|FBI]], [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and the [[us-marshals-service|U.S. Marshals Service]], and accompanied a deployment of [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops; the order set no timeline for deployment.[2]
