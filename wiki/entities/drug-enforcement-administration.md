# Drug Enforcement Administration

The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) is the United States federal agency responsible for administering the controlled substances schedules and enforcing federal drug law. It appears in the [[badlands-brief-0fb|April 24, 2026 Badlands Brief]] as the agency through which the reclassification of medical marijuana is implemented.

## Role in the 2026 marijuana rescheduling

According to the source, the Trump administration reclassified state-licensed medical marijuana from Schedule I to Schedule III. The move does not legalize marijuana at the federal level but eases restrictions on a substance that had occupied the most restrictive category since the 1937 Marijuana Tax Act. Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], whose [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] oversees the DEA, announced the change and referenced a December 2025 executive order directing an expedited rescheduling process along with the removal of federal hurdles to medical marijuana and CBD research.

Practically, the reclassification sets up a system under which state-licensed producers register with the DEA, legitimizes medical marijuana programs operating in 40 states, and allows tax deductions for state-licensed companies. Blanche indicated that a further expedited hearing with set deadlines would be convened to complete the rescheduling process.
