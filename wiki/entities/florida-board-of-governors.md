# Florida Board of Governors

The **Florida Board of Governors** is the body that oversees the State University System of Florida and sets binding regulations for its institutions.[1]

## Regulation 9.016

The board's regulation 9.016, governing prohibited expenditures, became the legal instrument through which [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|diversity, equity and inclusion]] programming was dismantled across Florida's public universities.[1] Citing compliance with regulation 9.016, the [[university-of-florida|University of Florida]] announced in a memo on Friday, March 1, 2024 that it had closed its Office of the Chief Diversity Officer, eliminated DEI positions and administrative appointments, and halted DEI-focused contracts with outside vendors.[1]

The university reported that roughly $5 million previously identified to Tallahassee as DEI expenditure would be reallocated by its Office of the Chief Financial Officer into a faculty recruitment fund.[1] Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], for whom halting DEI had become a cornerstone of his overhaul of state education, responded to the news by writing on X: "Florida is where DEI goes to die."[1]
