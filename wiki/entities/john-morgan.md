# John Morgan

John Morgan is a Florida attorney and Democratic mega-donor who has known [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for decades.[1]

In December 2024 Morgan wondered aloud whether Biden had deliberately forced [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] onto the ticket — throwing his endorsement behind her within minutes of withdrawing from the race — in order to spite [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], both of whom had worked behind the scenes to push Biden out and both of whom held serious reservations about Harris's capabilities.[1]

"[Biden] basically had the palace coup from all directions, from George Clooney to Pelosi. I think he got pissed off [and] said, 'F*** you', and gave us Harris," said Morgan, who had attended a "thank you" dinner for donors at the [[white-house|White House]] the previous month. He added: "Pelosi had told her delegation that there would be a convention and a nominating process. And Barack Obama did not endorse [Harris] for five days."[1]

## Refusal to fundraise for Harris (July 2024)

On Sunday, July 21, 2024 — as the country digested Biden's social media resignation from the 2024 race — Morgan announced that he would not fundraise for the Democrats if [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] was the nominee.[2] He suggested that Biden's decision to endorse her thirty minutes after announcing his withdrawal was a "fuck you" to those who had pushed him out.[2] "If Trump World could pick anybody to run against, I think they pick her," Morgan told ABC News, adding in a follow-up post on X: "You have to be enthusiastic or hoping for a political appointment to be asking friends for money. I am neither."[2]

Morgan suggested that "any combination" of [[joe-manchin|Joe Manchin]], [[andy-beshear|Andy Beshear]], [[gretchen-whitmer|Gretchen Whitmer]] and [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]] would win, while "@MichelleObama wins in a landslide with any of these picks."[2] Badlands commentary read his remarks as confirmation that the party's donor class had a prearranged replacement plan that [[joe-biden|Biden]] declined to follow, and as evidence that the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]]'s public show of unity behind Harris was hollow even as delegates rapidly cleared her path to the nomination.[2]
