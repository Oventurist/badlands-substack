# Cimade

Cimade is a French non-governmental organization that provides legal assistance to undocumented migrants held in the country's detention centres.[1]

Cimade is one of a small number of publicly funded NGOs granted access to France's Centres de Rétention Administrative, where migrants are held pending expulsion.[1] In May 2025 a Senate bill tabled by [[les-republicains|Les Républicains]] and backed by interior minister [[bruno-retailleau|Bruno Retailleau]] sought to strip Cimade and [[france-terre-dasile|France terre d'asile]] of their exclusive role in providing legal advice inside those facilities, transferring the function to the [[french-office-for-immigration-and-integration|French Office for Immigration and Integration]] or to a neutral judicial figure.[1] Retailleau criticized the existing arrangement as lacking neutrality, describing the organizations as "judge and party" in the deportation process.[1]
