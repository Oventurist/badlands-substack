# 9/11 Commission

The 9/11 Commission was the body Congress established to investigate government failures surrounding the September 11, 2001 attacks.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article introduces the commission as the motive behind [[sandy-berger|Sandy Berger]]'s removal of classified documents from the National Archives in October 2003: Congress had set up a commission to look into the government's failures on 9/11, and President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] "was really worried about what they would find."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The documents Berger took detailed how the Clinton administration had responded to the threat of terrorist attacks at the end of 1999.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

What the article argues Clinton feared being revealed was the August 1998 Memorandum of Notification that barred the [[cia|CIA]] from killing [[osama-bin-laden|Osama Bin Laden]], a restriction that former agents said tied their hands when they had him cornered.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Exposure of that memorandum, in the article's telling, would have laid blame for the attacks that killed thousands of Americans at Clinton's door.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
