# Hostages and Missing Families Forum

The Hostages and Missing Families Forum is the Israeli advocacy organization representing the relatives of people taken captive or missing following the attacks of October 7, 2023.[1]

## Statement on Omer Neutra

After the Israeli military announced in December 2024 that [[omer-neutra|Omer Maxim Neutra]] had been killed on October 7 rather than taken alive, the Forum issued a statement describing him as a young man who "loved sports, playing soccer, basketball, and volleyball" and who served as captain of his school's sports teams.[1] It added that family and friends described him as "a warm, optimistic, and caring individual who 'lights up the room the moment he enters.'"[1]

The Forum's role in publicizing hostage cases sits at the center of the Badlands commentary on the announcement, which questioned the official account that [[hamas|Hamas]] had retained Neutra's body in Gaza through more than a year of bombardment and raised the [[hannibal-directive|Hannibal Directive]] as an alternative explanation for October 7 deaths.[1]
