# Robert Kardashian

Robert Kardashian was an American attorney and a member of the defense team that represented [[oj-simpson|O.J. Simpson]] during his criminal trial for the 1994 killings of [[nicole-brown-simpson|Nicole Brown Simpson]] and [[ronald-goldman|Ronald Goldman]].[1] Simpson was acquitted of the criminal charges.[1]

Kardashian was the former husband of [[kris-jenner|Kris Jenner]], who was married to [[caitlyn-jenner|Caitlyn Jenner]] at the time of the killings and was reportedly close to Nicole Brown Simpson.[1] Badlands Media commentary published after Simpson's April 2024 death cited this overlap as evidence that the Kardashian and Jenner families sat on both sides of the case, and connected the Simpson trial to the family's later media prominence.[1]
