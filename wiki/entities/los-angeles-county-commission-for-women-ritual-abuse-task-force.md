# Los Angeles County Commission for Women Ritual Abuse Task Force

The Ritual Abuse Task Force of the Los Angeles County Commission for Women was a county body whose 1991 report is cited as one of the earliest official documents to treat [[ritual-abuse|ritual abuse]] and its mind-control component as a real phenomenon requiring institutional attention.[1] Badlands Media presents the report as reflecting the "early enlightenment" of the period regarding how mind control is created within ritual abuse and for what purpose.[1]

## The 1991 report

The task force concluded that "mind control is the cornerstone of ritual abuse, the key element in the subjugation and silencing of its victims," and that victims are subjected to a rigorously applied system designed to rob them of free will and impose upon them the will of the cult and its leaders.[1] It described that control as achieved through "an elaborate system of brainwashing, programming, indoctrination, hypnosis, and the use of various mind-altering drugs."[1] The stated purpose of the [[mind-control-programming|programming]], according to the report, was to compel victims to keep the secret of their abuse, to conform to the beliefs and behaviors of the cult, and to become functioning members who carry out the directives of its leaders without being detected within society at large.[1]

## Context

The report arrived at the end of a decade in which therapists had "rediscovered dissociation," and at the point when enough clinicians internationally had encountered similar allegations for them to be taken seriously.[1] Claims about the reality and pervasiveness of ritual abuse and mind control came from alleged victims, former perpetrators and witnesses alike.[1]
