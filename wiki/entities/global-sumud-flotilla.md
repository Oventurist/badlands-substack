# Global Sumud Flotilla

The Global Sumud Flotilla was a convoy reported to have carried about 430 international volunteers trying to reach Gaza with humanitarian aid, intercepted by [[israel|Israel]] in May 2026. Its interception became an international incident after Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] posted video of detained activists kneeling and handcuffed.

## The interception

Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] defended the interception but distanced himself from Ben-Gvir's treatment of the detainees and ordered their deportations expedited. Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] accused Ben-Gvir of acting "like a true Nazi" after the video showed detained flotilla activists, including Colombians, kneeling and handcuffed.

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] commented that the hubris on display in the video, posted by Ben-Gvir himself, was "an increasingly common pattern seen among Israeli leaders," and linked it to the [[yitzhak-kroizer|Yitzhak Kroizer]] remarks at the [[knesset|Knesset]].
