# Public Investment Fund

The Public Investment Fund (PIF) is the sovereign wealth fund of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the principal financial vehicle of the kingdom's Vision 2030 economic diversification programme. It has become one of the world's largest state investors, deploying capital across domestic giga-projects, foreign equities, sports, and technology.

## 2026–2030 strategy

The February 10, 2026 edition of the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], "[[badlands-brief-4ec|Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes]]," reported, citing sources who spoke to [[reuters|Reuters]], that the fund was expected to unveil a new strategy covering the years 2026 to 2030 that week. According to those sources, the plan would concentrate on industry, minerals, [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]], and tourism, while scaling back some of the kingdom's most expensive mega-projects — including the linear city development known as "The Line" — as part of a broader adjustment of Saudi investment priorities.

The Brief carried the item in its international round-up section alongside Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]'s visit to [[armenia|Armenia]] and Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]]'s planned trip to [[venezuela|Venezuela]], grouping the three as indicators of shifting global economic and diplomatic alignments.
