# Public Investment Fund

The Public Investment Fund (PIF) is Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund and the principal financial vehicle behind the Kingdom's [[saudi-vision-2030|Vision 2030]] economic diversification agenda.[1]

At the [[saudi-us-investment-forum|Saudi-US Investment Forum]] in Riyadh on May 13, 2025, Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] said that the United States is "a major destination for the Public Investment Fund, accounting for approximately 40 percent of the fund's global investments."[1] He tied the fund's American exposure to a broader package of deals worth more than $300 billion signed during the visit, and to a pipeline of investment opportunities he valued at $600 billion with ambitions of reaching $1 trillion.[1]

The crown prince presented joint investment through the fund as one of the most important pillars of the Saudi–American economic relationship, while framing cooperation with Washington as extending beyond commerce to peacemaking in the region and the wider world.[1]
