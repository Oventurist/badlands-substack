# Peter Thiel

Peter Thiel is a German-born American entrepreneur, venture capitalist, and political donor. He co-founded PayPal, was the first major outside investor in Facebook, and co-founded the data analytics and defense contracting firm Palantir Technologies. Through his investment vehicles and personal fellowships he has cultivated an extensive network of technologists and political operatives, several of whom have taken senior roles in Republican politics and in the federal government.

## The "Thiel Doomer" thesis

In the March 26, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] discusses what she calls "Thiel Doomers" — commentators who argue that Thiel "is shadow running our government through his vast network of insider placements." Writing in response to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|PCAST]] appointments, she counts roughly half the appointees — [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]], [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]], [[fred-ehrsam|Fred Ehrsam]], [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]], [[david-friedberg|David Friedberg]], and [[jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] — as belonging to Thiel's orbit.

Ashe distances herself from the strongest version of the claim, noting that having read *1984*, *Brave New World*, and other technology-driven dystopian works she recognizes "all the classic earmarks of doomer's delight." At the same time she treats the concern as serious enough to warrant vigilance, framing the appointments as evidence that "the race to technocracy" is underway and arguing that the People must retain the capacity to say no. Whether the resulting arrangement serves [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s plan, Thiel's, or something else, she leaves open.
