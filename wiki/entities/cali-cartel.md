# Cali Cartel

The Cali Cartel was a Colombian cocaine-trafficking organization and the counterparty in the deal that reshaped Mexican trafficking economics. By renegotiating terms with the Cali Cartel, [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]] secured for the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] 50 percent of every shipment out of Colombia as payment for delivery, replacing the US $1,500 per kilo previously paid — in exchange for guaranteeing every shipment's arrival.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The Badlands Media account also names Cali alongside the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]] as one of the Colombian organizations for which [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] is said to have established banking and money-laundering links to the [[cia|CIA]] from his post in Caracas.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
