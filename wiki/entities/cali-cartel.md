# Cali Cartel

The Cali Cartel was a Colombian cocaine organization whose supply arrangements with Mexico were restructured by [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]]: instead of the roughly US$1,500 per kilo previously paid for transport, the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] came to receive 50 percent of every shipment leaving Colombia, in return for guaranteeing delivery.[1]

The article also names the Cali Cartel, alongside the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín cartel]], as one of the Colombian organizations to which [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] established banking and money-laundering links on behalf of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] while stationed in Caracas.[1]
