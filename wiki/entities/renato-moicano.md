# Renato Moicano

Renato Moicano is a Brazilian mixed martial artist competing in the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]].[1]

## Post-fight advocacy of Austrian economics

In April 2024 Moicano used his post-fight microphone time on a Saturday night UFC card to promote Austrian economics.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the moment as evidence that UFC athletes are "virtually impossible for the establishment to control by virtue of their very makeup," and that the sport's rise in cultural importance has mirrored and even front-run the rise of the America First movement.[1] He argued the episode showed that "sovereignty and self-actualization is becoming a popular sentiment," and that the UFC's athletes and fans are threatening to the establishment not only domestically but globally.[1]
