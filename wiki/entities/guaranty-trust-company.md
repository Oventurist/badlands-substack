# Guaranty Trust Company

Guaranty Trust was a New York investment banking firm which, according to "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8," was one of two Wall Street houses dominated since the turn of the twentieth century by members of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, the other being [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]].[1]

The article alleges that both firms were heavily involved in the financing of Communism and of [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s regime.[1] It claims that funding and political maneuvering by Bonesmen and their allies helped the Bolsheviks prevail in Russia, and that in defiance of federal laws the network financed industries, established banks and developed oil and mineral deposits in the fledgling USSR.[1]
