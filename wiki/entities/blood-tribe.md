# Blood Tribe

Blood Tribe is an American neo-Nazi street-demonstration group associated with uniformed, masked marches in Florida.[1] The organization drew national attention in early September 2023 when a large contingent of its members rallied in Florida wearing matching new red shirts and face masks, waving swastika flags and shouting antisemitic slurs.[1]

Only a handful of participants appeared unmasked; one of them was identified by [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] as [[kent-mclellan|Kent "Boneface" McLellan]], a man she said had been arrested by the [[fbi|FBI]] for domestic terrorism in 2012 and had later fought with [[right-sector|Right Sector]] in Ukraine.[1] Loomer also reported that the marchers professed support for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] during the demonstration.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that Blood Tribe is "yet another glaringly, or should I say glowingly, obvious front for whatever three-letter agency is currently running COINTELPRO ops," comparing its aesthetic — ski masks and wraparound sunglasses on middle-aged men — to that of [[patriot-front|Patriot Front]].[1] DeLarme contended that progressive commentators had eagerly embraced the spectacle because it validated a cartoonish narrative linking such groups to [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], while the same critics raised no objection to unaudited American funding flowing to nationalist formations in Ukraine.[1]
