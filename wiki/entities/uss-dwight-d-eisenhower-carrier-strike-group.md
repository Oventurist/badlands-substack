# USS Dwight D. Eisenhower Carrier Strike Group

The USS Dwight D. Eisenhower carrier strike group is a United States Navy formation whose deployment to the Middle East in October 2023 became a focal point of the debate over American escalation following the October 7 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on Israel.[1]

## October 2023 deployment

The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] dispatched additional warships to the Middle East region in the weeks after October 7, a build-up that Russia characterized as a source of escalation risk in the Gaza conflict.[1] Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergei Lavrov]], speaking in Tehran on Monday, October 23, said that "the more a state takes this kind of proactive measures, the greater the risk and the danger of an escalation of the conflict," and identified Washington as already among the countries intervening the most.[1]

Badlands contributor Ashe in America reported that over the preceding weekend the United States had re-routed the Eisenhower carrier group into the Persian Gulf as a show of force toward [[iran|Iran]], describing the move as objectively an escalation.[1] The warships had initially been reported as headed for the Mediterranean in support of Israel; the redirection left American carrier groups flanking Saudi Arabia.[1]

## Context

The deployment coincided with Pentagon assessments that Iran-backed groups were planning to intensify attacks on U.S. forces amid sporadic drone and missile strikes against American bases in Syria and Iraq, and with National Security Council spokesman [[john-kirby|John Kirby]]'s statement of a "very direct connection" between those groups and the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

## Initial order to the eastern Mediterranean

Earlier in October 2023, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] had ordered the Eisenhower strike group to the eastern Mediterranean near Israel as a second carrier presence intended to deter [[iran|Iran]] or [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] from joining the Israel-Hamas conflict, according to U.S. officials cited by [[abc-news|ABC News]].[2] The Eisenhower group was directed to join the [[uss-gerald-r-ford|USS Gerald R. Ford]] carrier strike group, which had arrived in international waters off Israel earlier that week.[2] Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] confirmed the deployment in a statement, saying, "I have directed the USS Dwight D. Eisenhower Carrier Strike Group (CSG) to begin moving to the Eastern Mediterranean," as part of an effort "to deter hostile actions against Israel or any efforts toward widening this war following Hamas's attack on Israel."[2] Senior U.S. officials said publicly that the carrier presence, together with additional Air Force fighter jets sent to the region, was intended to demonstrate U.S. commitment to Israel and deter Iranian and Hezbollah involvement.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] criticized the build-up, noting that within a week of the conflict's outbreak two carrier groups were in the Mediterranean amid neoconservative calls to formally declare war, and arguing that both sides of the information war had been caught spreading false headlines, years-old videos, and AI-generated images.[2]
