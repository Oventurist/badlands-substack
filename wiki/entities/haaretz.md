# Haaretz

Haaretz is described in the March 13, 2026 Badlands Brief as the oldest newspaper in Israel and an editorial opponent of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].

In that edition, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites Haaretz reporting that nearly a dozen Iranian "smart missiles" — each said to contain dozens of smaller bombs with independent guidance systems — penetrated the [[iron-dome|Iron Dome]] and struck central Israel overnight, in what was described as one of many salvos during the night. The commentary uses the reporting to argue that Israel's air defenses had failed and that the country was taking heavier losses than the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s strike announcements suggested.


## April 2026: settler condemnation and the reservist crisis

The Brief of April 20, 2026 cites two Haaretz items. In an interview earlier in the month, [[pinchas-wallerstein|Pinchas Wallerstein]], a founder of the modern Israeli Settler Movement, said he was horrified by the rise of Jewish terrorism in the region, labelling it "treason" against the State of Israel. Separately, Haaretz published a piece by Tom Levinson documenting an explosion in suicide, depression, and PTSD among [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] reservists forced into military service after [[october-7|October 7]]; many are psychologically broken by what they witnessed in [[gaza|Gaza]] and the [[west-bank|West Bank]], with some diagnosed with PTSD pressured to serve six or seven tours and those who refused arrested.
