# Stephanie Pope

Stephanie Pope is the [[boeing|Boeing]] executive who, as the company's chief operating officer, was named in March 2024 to lead the division that builds aircraft for airlines and other commercial customers.[1] She replaced [[stan-deal|Stan Deal]], who retired immediately as part of the leadership reshuffle Boeing announced on Monday, March 25, 2024.[1]

Pope's elevation was one element of a broader overhaul that also included the announced end-of-year departure of chief executive [[dave-calhoun|Dave Calhoun]], undertaken while Boeing was under mounting pressure from regulators, airlines and passengers over its most significant safety crisis in years.[1] The immediate trigger was the January 2024 incident in which a panel blew off a 737 Max 9 midair during an Alaska Airlines flight, which renewed concerns about the manufacturer's commitment to safety and quality five years after two 737 Max 8 crashes killed nearly 350 people.[1]
