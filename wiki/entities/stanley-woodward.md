# Stanley Woodward

Stanley Woodward is an American defense attorney who represented Walt Nauta, a co-defendant of former President Donald Trump in the federal classified documents prosecution brought by Special Counsel Jack Smith.[1]

## Conflict-of-interest question

In the summer of 2023, Smith's prosecutors sought a so-called "Garcia" hearing — a proceeding used in federal court to examine potential conflicts of interest in a defendant's choice of counsel — on the ground that Woodward simultaneously represented Nauta and other individuals who could be called to testify in the classified documents case.[1] The motion for that hearing became the vehicle for a broader dispute over sealing and grand jury practice.[1]

On Monday, August 7, 2023, District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]] responded to the Garcia motion by striking down two of the special counsel's filings, ordering them unsealed, and directing Smith to justify the use of a Washington, DC grand jury in a case indicted in the Southern District of Florida.[1] Coverage of the ruling, reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and the Daily Caller, treated the Woodward conflict question as the procedural origin of a filing that ultimately drew a judicial rebuke of the prosecution.[1]

Woodward's dual representation situated him at the intersection of the defense strategy in the [[trump-classified-documents-case|classified documents case]] and the government's efforts to secure testimony from witnesses within [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s orbit.[1]
