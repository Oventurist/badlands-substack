# All Voting Is Local

**All Voting Is Local** is a voting-rights advocacy organization active in U.S. election administration debates, including in Georgia, where Kristin Nabers serves as state director.[1]

Nabers observed the post-election counting following the November 7, 2023 Georgia municipal elections, including the advance-voting discrepancies reported in [[spalding-county|Spalding County]].[1] Speaking to the [[atlanta-journal-constitution|Atlanta Journal-Constitution]], she said she saw "hand counts as part of a push by bad-faith actors to spread lies and conspiracy theories," and that "discrepancies are to be expected because of human error, but any discrepancy, no matter how small, could be used to cast doubt on the counting process."[1]

Critics writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] characterized this position as an argument that discrepancies should be ignored to prevent voters from questioning elections, and contrasted it with the view of [[citizens-defending-freedom|Citizens Defending Freedom]] that the 2023 results demonstrated Georgia should stop using voting machines.[1]
