# William Stuart

William Stuart is a Masonic researcher and author cited in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2" for his framework of "[[red-masonry|Red Masonry]]."[1]

Stuart uses the term "Red Masonry," also rendered *Fratres Lucis*, for what he describes as the fusion of the old Pagan Celtic religion with the [[eleusinian-mysteries|Eleusinian Mystery School]] of ancient Greece.[1] He has compiled a list of the secret societies formed under that Rosicrucian umbrella together with their founding years — hundreds of orders, of which the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] was only one.[1]

Stuart further claims that there have been multiple incarnations of the same agenda after the Order's suppression, citing the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg Group]] and the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] as more recent examples.[1] [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] draws on Stuart's list to argue that the interwoven societies "cannot be simply reduced to the singular term 'Illuminati'."[1]
