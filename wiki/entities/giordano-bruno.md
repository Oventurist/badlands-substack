# Giordano Bruno

Giordano Bruno is listed in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] among the Christian writers who regarded [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]] as a wise pagan prophet who foresaw the coming of Christianity.[1] The others named are Lactantius, Augustine, [[marsilio-ficino|Marsilio Ficino]], Campanella and Giovanni Pico della Mirandola.[1]

Bruno and his contemporaries subscribed to [[prisca-theologia|prisca theologia]], the doctrine of a single true theology threading through all religions and passed down from antiquity through prophets including Zoroaster and Plato; to demonstrate its verity they appropriated the Hermetic teachings for their own purposes.[1]
