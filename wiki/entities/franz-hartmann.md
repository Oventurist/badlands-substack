# Franz Hartmann

**Franz Hartmann** was an influential German occultist and Theosophical Aryan supremacist who, in Matthew Ehret's account, did much to oversee the growth of a Germanic version of [[theosophy|Theosophy]].[1]

Hartmann and [[lanz-von-liebenfels|Lanz von Liebenfels]] promoted a German branch of the New Templars, and both associated closely with the occult secret society known as the [[thule-society|Thule Society]], which sought — like the covens of earlier times — to use long-haired priestesses in sacred orgies, séances, and soothsaying.[1] This German Theosophical current fed directly into the [[ariosophy|Ariosophist]] milieu around [[guido-von-list|Guido von List]] that shaped the beliefs of the Nazi high command.[1]
