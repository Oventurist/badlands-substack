# The Clay Travis & Buck Sexton Show

The Clay Travis & Buck Sexton Show is a nationally syndicated American conservative talk radio program hosted by Clay Travis and Buck Sexton.[1]

## Dispute with Seth Keshel over the 2025 Wisconsin race

In April 2025, the program engaged with a viral post by election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] on X that had drawn more than 1.4 million views concerning the [[2025-wisconsin-supreme-court-election|Wisconsin Supreme Court election]].[1] According to Keshel, the show concluded that [[susan-crawford|Susan Crawford]] would have won regardless, reasoning primarily that there were no smoking-gun examples of what Milwaukee County typically does in close races to draw upon, and that the count had not proceeded with sudden reversals into the early hours of Wednesday morning.[1] Keshel recounted the exchange in his essay "Inside the Modern Elections Casino," in which he maintained that structural advantages such as [[automatic-voter-registration|Automatic Voter Registration]], universal [[mail-in-voting|mail-in voting]] and [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]] weight outcomes in favor of "the house" even when individual races remain genuinely winnable.[1]
