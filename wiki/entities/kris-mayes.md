# Kris Mayes

Kris Mayes is an Arizona Democrat who won the state's 2022 election for attorney general against Republican [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]] by one of the narrowest margins in American statewide electoral history.

## The 2022 attorney general race

As of the December 5, 2022 certification of the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], Mayes led Hamadeh by 511 votes out of approximately 2.5 million cast — a margin of less than 0.1 percentage points. Because Arizona law requires an automatic recount in any race decided by less than 0.5 points, the contest went to a recount that began on Wednesday, December 7, alongside recounts in the state superintendent race and a state House race. Hamadeh separately filed a lawsuit challenging the result after certification, having had an earlier suit dismissed as premature.

The Badlands Media article [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]], citing *The Hill*, treats Mayes's lead as provisional and predicts that Hamadeh will prevail once the recount is complete.
