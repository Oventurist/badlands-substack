# Tiff Macklem

Tiff Macklem is the Governor of the [[bank-of-canada|Bank of Canada]].[1] In prepared remarks delivered in Ottawa on 8 May 2025, Macklem warned that the Canadian economy and financial system faced "a new threat" from United States tariffs, saying that "[t]ariffs and uncertainty have sharply reduced prospects for global economic growth" and that "financial markets have been rocked by chaotic policy announcements and reversals."[1]

His remarks accompanied the central bank's Financial Stability Report, which cautioned that market volatility driven by U.S. tariffs could produce a sharp repricing of assets and, in extreme circumstances, outright "market dysfunction."[1] The report identified President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s protectionist trade policies as a continuing source of uncertainty for the Canadian economy after they roiled global markets in April 2025.[1]
