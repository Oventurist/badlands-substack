# Fort Detrick

Fort Detrick is the United States Army's principal biological laboratory installation and the facility most frequently named by Chinese officials in counter-claims about the origins of COVID-19.[1]

Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson [[hua-chunying|Hua Chunying]] publicly demanded that Washington "open the biological lab at Fort Detrick," extend transparency to its 200-plus overseas bio-labs, and invite [[world-health-organization|WHO]] experts to conduct origin-tracing inside the United States.[1] The demand was part of a broader Chinese argument, also advanced by CDC epidemiologist [[zeng-guang|Zeng Guang]], that the concentration of American biological laboratories worldwide — and Washington's own opacity about them — undercut the credibility of the [[lab-leak-theory|Wuhan lab leak hypothesis]].[1]

Fort Detrick sits at the centre of the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] biodefense complex documented by investigative journalist [[dilyana-gaytandzhieva|Dilyana Gaytandzhieva]], whose 2018 reporting traced a multibillion-dollar budget sustaining bioweapons laboratories in 25 nations and 11 within the United States itself.[1] Funding for that complex is described as having grown from roughly $5 billion when the Bioshield Act was passed in 2004 — in the wake of the December 2001 weaponized anthrax attack that killed five Americans — to over $50 billion.[1]
