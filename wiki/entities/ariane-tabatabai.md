# Ariane Tabatabai

Ariane Tabatabai is a United States defense official who became a recurring subject of speculation in independent media over alleged Iranian influence within American policymaking circles and, later, over the origin of leaked assessments of Israel's plans to strike Iran.[1]

## 2024 leak speculation

In the autumn of 2024, classified documents attributed to the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]] and the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] describing Israeli preparations for a strike on Iran surfaced on a Telegram channel, and commentary in the alternative press linked Tabatabai to their disclosure.[1] Tabatabai stated that no agency had reached out to her regarding any investigation and that she did not know what the reports were about.[1]

The charging of [[asif-william-rahman|Asif William Rahman]] in November 2024 for willful transmission of national defense information appeared to identify a different source for the leak.[1] Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] acknowledged the apparent vindication while cautioning that Rahman's indictment neither proved Rahman guilty nor established Tabatabai's innocence, and treated the ambiguity as characteristic of the ongoing [[information-war|information war]].[1]
