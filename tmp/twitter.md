## The "Flippening" (2023)

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" refers to "the advent of the Twitter 'Flippening'" — the change in the platform's ownership and moderation posture — as a turning point in the [[information-war|Information War]].[n] The author writes that as a result "the normies are starting to hear once again … that there is more than one narrative," and treats the shift as evidence that the movement presenting a choice of "Freedom vs. Tyranny" is no longer confined to a small group of change-makers.[n]
