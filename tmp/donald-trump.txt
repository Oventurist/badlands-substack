## June 2026: Freedom 250, the Iran deal, and the Netanyahu rebuke

The 15 June 2026 *Badlands Brief* placed Trump at the center of three simultaneous storylines. On 14 June — his 80th birthday, and a date the Brief noted also marks the 1775 founding of the Army and the 1777 adoption of the national flag — the [[white-house|White House]] hosted [[freedom-250|"UFC Freedom 250"]] on the South Lawn to mark the nation's 250th anniversary. Trump attended alongside Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and thousands of guests; a federal judge declined to block the event after two Virginia residents sued over the government's decision to stage mixed martial arts bouts at the executive mansion. [[justin-gaethje|Justin Gaethje]] took the lightweight title from [[ilia-topuria|Ilia Topuria]] and paid respects to Trump and the First Lady after the bout.

The same weekend Trump announced that U.S. and Iranian negotiators had agreed on a framework to end the war, lift the U.S. blockade of [[iran|Iran]] and reopen the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], posting that the deal was "now complete." The [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-2026|memorandum of understanding]] was scheduled for signature in Switzerland that Friday.

Hours before the planned signing, [[israel|Israel]] struck [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] targets in Beirut's Dahiyeh district. Trump told Axios reporter [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]] by phone that the attack had infuriated him: "Why did Bibi have to do a fucking attack? I was so pissed off... He has no fucking judgement." Trump said the deal remained on track despite a delay of several hours. Badlands commentators read the episode as evidence that Trump had decoupled American policy from that of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and from the [[israel-lobby|Israel Lobby]], and predicted the [[congress|Congress]]ional GOP would turn on him over it.

Trump also announced on 12 June that a U.S. military strike had killed [[hector-guerrero-flores|Héctor "Niño" Guerrero Flores]], leader of [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]], in an operation conducted with the cooperation of the government of [[venezuela|Venezuela]].
