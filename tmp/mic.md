## In the 1960s awakening narrative

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" describes the 1960s as the decade in which "the American people were waking up to the sham that was the Military Industrial Complex and more."[n] The article treats criticism of the complex as one of the most dangerous positions a public figure can adopt: [[john-lennon|John Lennon]], it notes, "heavily questioned the Military Industrial Complex," and while such criticism from a private citizen is inconsequential, from "a major culture creator with the type of staying power that lasts generations" it invites suppression by "whatever mafia sits at the top of their control pyramid."[n]

The essay links the complex to two adjacent pillars — "the central banking system and the intelligence apparatus" — which it says [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] threatened, thereby writing "his own death warrant."[n]
