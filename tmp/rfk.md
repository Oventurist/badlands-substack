## Heroes of the Great Awakening

The February 2023 Badlands Media essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" argues that Robert F. Kennedy "would have very likely been President in the future, had history charted a different course."[n] It presents him and his brother [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] as members of "a political family that was willing to stand up to the Establishment," standing for "true justice and 'people power'."[n]

The article states that many in the Badlands audience believe both brothers "were killed by the very power structure that is battling Trump, his family and his allies now," a structure variously called the Establishment, the Cabal or the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[n] Their deaths are grouped with those of [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]], [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] and [[bob-marley|Bob Marley]] as part of "a seeming attempt to stop the spread of awareness in America."[n]
