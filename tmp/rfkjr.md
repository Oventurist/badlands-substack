## Heroes of the Great Awakening

In its February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening," Badlands Media describes Robert F. Kennedy Jr. as "the world's preeminent thought-leader for medical freedom," noting the recent publication of his best-selling book.[n] The reference appears in a passage arguing that the present period rhymes with the 1960s: alongside a revived [[george-magazine|*GEORGE*]] magazine and a new [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]], Kennedy Jr.'s work is offered as evidence that the family's tradition of challenging entrenched power has resurfaced in a new form.[n]
