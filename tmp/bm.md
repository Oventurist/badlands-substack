## Editorial mission as stated in "Heroes of the Great Awakening"

In the February 2023 feature "Heroes of the Great Awakening" by [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]], Badlands Media restates its operating model: the outlet "will always put out our content for free," while readers may support it by becoming paid subscribers to its newsletter, helping "our collective of citizen journalists take back the narrative from the MSM."[n] The article closes with the outlet's standing disclaimer that its articles and features "represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself."[n]

The essay also describes the network's two-platform distribution strategy in explicit terms — "This is why we post features on [[substack|Substack]]. This is why we post videos on [[rumble|Rumble]]" — presenting both as free-speech venues vital "to keeping liberty alive" in an era when censorship is the principal weapon used against dissidents.[n]
