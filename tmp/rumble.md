## Role in the Great Awakening narrative

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" names Rumble, alongside [[substack|Substack]], as one of the "free speech platforms" that are "vital to keeping liberty alive."[n] The article argues that because ordinary citizens can now produce news reports and music with a smartphone, censorship has become "the weapon being used against dissidents in the current era," making uncensored distribution infrastructure a strategic necessity.[n]

The essay identifies Rumble specifically as the video half of Badlands Media's output — "This is why we post videos on Rumble" — and cites Rumble-hosted footage of Donald Trump discussing his letter from [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] on *Larry King Live*.[n]
