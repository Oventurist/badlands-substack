## June 2026: Beirut strike and Trump's rebuke

Netanyahu's government ordered a strike on [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] positions in the Dahiyeh area of southern Beirut on Sunday 14 June 2026, describing it as a "precise" hit on a Hezbollah command center in response to three projectiles fired toward northern [[israel|Israel]]. Lebanese security sources told [[reuters|Reuters]] that two missiles appeared to have been used; [[lebanon|Lebanon]]'s state news agency reported two killed and four wounded.

The timing — Trump's 80th birthday and the day the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-2026|US–Iran memorandum of understanding]] was expected to be signed — provoked an unusually blunt reaction from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. According to Axios reporter [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]], Trump said in a phone call: "Why did Bibi have to do a fucking attack? I was so pissed off. I let him know. He has no fucking judgement." Trump said the deal was still proceeding, delayed only by several hours.

Badlands commentary framed the episode as the moment Netanyahu lost effective veto power over U.S. Middle East policy, predicting he would nonetheless be pressured domestically to escalate further — by [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]] and [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] on his right and by opposition figures [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]] and [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]], who called the deal a national failure.
