## Heroes of the Great Awakening

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" refers to Clinton in polemical terms as "the queen of the coven herself," in the context of the 1999 New York Senate race she is said to have coveted.[n] The article contends that [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] was on course for a "projected and even probable win" of that seat before his plane went down over Martha's Vineyard, and that his death "in purely coincidental fashion—seemed to mirror that of so many political enemies of the Clinton clan."[n]
