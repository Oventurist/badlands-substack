## Heroes of the Great Awakening

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" treats King as a case study in the power of nonviolent resistance, writing that "it befuddles and even infuriates the enemy when we fight this war through nonviolent means."[n] The author argues that King "was a threat to the establishment because—much like the current sociopolitical climate—they want us divided against each other, and this man was a unifier."[n]

King's death is listed in the article alongside those of the Kennedys, [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] and [[bob-marley|Bob Marley]] as part of what the author characterises as "a seeming attempt to stop the spread of awareness in America."[n]
