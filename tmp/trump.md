## Heroes of the Great Awakening

The February 2023 Badlands Media essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" casts Trump as the contemporary successor to the Kennedys, arguing that the same power structure that killed [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] is "battling Trump, his family and his allies now."[n] The author notes that this structure goes by several names — "the 'Establishment', the 'Cabal' or the '[[deep-state|Deep State]]'" — and that whatever it is called, it does "their best—and worst—work in the shadows."[n]

The essay also emphasises Trump's personal connection to [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]], stating that according to Trump himself the two men were friends, and citing a 1996 remark in which Kennedy Jr. appeared to predict a Trump presidency two decades in advance.[n] Trump is thus positioned as an ally in what the article calls the effort "to counter this 'repugnant' and secretive cabal."[n] At the same time the author cautions against personality-driven expectations, writing that "there are charismatic figures that come and go" but that "it was not one man who beat fascism in the 1940's, and it will not be one man in the current epoch either."[n]
