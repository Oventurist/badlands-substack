## Terminology and method in "Heroes of the Great Awakening"

Badlands Media's February 2023 essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" treats "Deep State" as one of several interchangeable labels for the same adversary, writing that the power structure believed to have killed [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]], and to be "battling Trump, his family and his allies now," may be called "the 'Establishment', the 'Cabal' or the 'Deep State'."[n] Whatever the name, the author writes, "they do their best—and worst—work in the shadows."[n]

The essay identifies two functions it regards as so central to this structure's control that any threat to them is treated as fatal: "when JFK threatened the central banking system and the intelligence apparatus, he wrote his own death warrant."[n] It further argues that the Deep State's apparent invincibility is itself a projection — "it may seem that the establishment is a monolith that is impossible to upend, but that is only what they want you to think" — and that its vulnerability lies in mass, rather than individual, opposition.[n] The author quotes Kennedy's condemnation of "secret societies, to secret oaths and secret proceedings" as the movement's founding text on the subject.[n]
