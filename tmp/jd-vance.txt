## June 2026: Freedom 250 and the Iran ceasefire

Vance attended the [[freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]] card on the South Lawn of the [[white-house|White House]] on 14 June 2026 alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and other administration officials.

In the aftermath of Israel's strike on Beirut the same day, Vance told [[fox-news|Fox News]] that [[iran|Iran]] had been preparing to retaliate but stood down at the request of the United States — a claim Badlands writers cited as the clearest public confirmation that Tehran was cooperating directly with Washington rather than with [[israel|Israel]], and that the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-2026|US–Iran memorandum of understanding]] excluded Israel as a party.
