## "Heroes of the Great Awakening" (2023)

Sean Morgan is the author of the February 27, 2023 Badlands Media feature "Heroes of the Great Awakening," subtitled "We Are Not Alone."[n] The essay argues that the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] underway in the United States rhymes with an earlier one in the 1960s, when the Kennedys, [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]], [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] and [[bob-marley|Bob Marley]] challenged the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] and, in Morgan's telling, were killed for it.[n]

Morgan's central claim is that the movement's strength is collective rather than personal: "when lone figures go up against the machine, they are doomed to fall, but when we rise up as a nation, there is nothing that can stop us."[n] He borrows [[charles-eisenstein|Charles Eisenstein]]'s account of myth to argue that the belief that [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] survived is a myth carrying a non-literal truth, and concludes that Kennedy's "lineage is more than just a bloodline."[n] He frames the "plandemic" and related operations as having awakened a new generation of heroes — "the 1960's anti-war movement with smart phones" — whose "bullets" are "articles, songs and videos" distributed on [[rumble|Rumble]] and [[substack|Substack]].[n] The piece directs readers to more of his work at seanmorganreport.com.[n]
