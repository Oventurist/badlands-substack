## The multinational offensive against online speech (October 2024)

In an October 29, 2024 feature, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the most pressing concern facing the Western world is the multifaceted attack on free expression in the digital realm, and that free speech is the ultimate indicator of how healthy a society and its government are — a metric by which, in his view, the entire Western world appears terminally ill.[N] He listed ten reasons the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] is the most important, among them that free speech is the foundation of democracy, preserves individual autonomy and dignity, checks government power, promotes social progress and innovation, enables cultural exchange, protects other rights, allows error correction in the marketplace of ideas, aids personal development, is philosophically intrinsic to human nature as a negative right, and is validated by historical lessons in which the suppression of speech led to authoritarianism.[N]

DeLarme wrote that the offensive was being facilitated on multiple fronts by governments and NGOs working in concert, referencing his earlier article "The War on Dissent," which catalogued Canada's C-11 [[online-streaming-act|Online Streaming Act]], the UK's [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]], and the European Union's [[digital-services-act|Digital Services Act]].[N] Where the 2018 and 2020 "ban hammer" waves targeted small YouTube channels, streamers and online publications, the new phase targeted the most consequential platforms themselves — [[twitter|Twitter (X)]], [[rumble|Rumble]] and [[telegram|Telegram]].[N]
