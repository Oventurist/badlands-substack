## The Trinity thesis ("Mirror, Mirror," December 2022)

The December 14, 2022 Badlands Media essay "Mirror, Mirror," the fifth entry in the network's China series, placed [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as one leg of a "Trinity" of sovereign leaders whose apparent opposition, the author argued, masks a shared direction of travel.[N] Its central device was a "Mirrored Quadrant" of four attributes said to be common to all three men: a populist rise, military backing, a war on their own national oligarchy, and a war on the globalists.[N]

On the first count the essay held that Xi rode a populist wave to an almost unheard-of swell of nationalist populism in the Far East, campaigning on a promise to go to war with the "[[tigers-and-flies|tigers and flies]]" of an entrenched enemy that would otherwise control the country from on high.[N] On the second, it repeated the claim from the preceding series entry "Empire's Enigma" that Xi broke with long-standing Chinese political tradition by forming his own security and protective service rather than relying on one appointed and funded by the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], restructured the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]] chain of command so as to make himself effectively commander-in-chief in mirror to his Western counterpart, and placed renewed emphasis on kinetic power in service of a "multi-polar" world vision set against globalist collectivism.[N]

On the third, the article argued that Xi had gone further than either Trump or Putin against his domestic oligarchy — the legacy families and bloodlines of the Chinese elite — rooting them out, arresting and imprisoning them, sometimes with shocking military deployments and in mass style, with millions officially punished for corruption and thousands of government and administrative insiders accused of the same.[N] The author conceded it is difficult or impossible to know whether Xi's arrests were justified, but claimed the net effect was the purging of legacy families from a CCP that had served its own people up to the American oligarchy represented by [[apple|Apple]], [[google|Google]] and [[amazon|Amazon]], farming out a slave class to the collectivist West.[N]

On the fourth, the essay read Xi's antagonistic posture toward [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] and the European bureaucracy in Brussels, together with his open and strengthening economic and military ties with the [[brics|BRICS]] nations and with the "SATE" grouping of Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Egypt, as indicative of the sovereign cooperative he seeks to help forge — notwithstanding that Xi is decried by sovereign-leaning Westerners as readily as by the leftists who once praised him.[N] The engineered COVID pandemic and ensuing lockdowns were said to have produced a staggering and rapid decoupling of the eastern and western economic systems.[N]
