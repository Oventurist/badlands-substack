## June 2024 testimony before the Select Subcommittee

On Monday, June 3, 2024, Fauci was grilled by Republicans during testimony before the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]], where he repeatedly denied allegations that he had sought to cover up the COVID-19 lab-leak theory, calling the accusation "absolutely false and simply preposterous."[13] In his opening statement he denied trying to silence officials and scientists who thought the virus was created in, or had escaped from, the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] in China, and rejected the claim that he had influenced scientists by bribing them with millions of dollars in grant money, saying he "had no input into the content of the published paper."[13]

Following the opening statement, Fauci was questioned over the government's pandemic response, defending the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] over mask mandates, lockdowns and other public policy, while Democrats on the panel repeatedly defended him.[13] Representative [[michael-cloud|Michael Cloud]] (R-TX) got into a heated exchange with Fauci, at one point forcing the former [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|NIAID]] director to admit that the COVID-19 vaccine does not stop transmission.[13]

The hearing ran more than three hours; attendees waited in line for hours to secure seats in a packed chamber, the crowd had to be gaveled down multiple times, and two people were removed for shouting about Fauci's alleged crimes.[13] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the "Fauci didn't know" defense ruled the day, noting that Fauci had been the highest paid federal government employee before retiring and "was paid to know," and argued for ending qualified immunity so officials could be held accountable.[13] Legislators on both sides thanked him for his service, with one Democrat thanking him for his "science."[13] [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] (R-GA) was the most combative questioner, at one point refusing to refer to Fauci as "doctor," which halted the proceeding for several minutes.[13] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] carried special coverage of the hearing on Rumble.[13]
