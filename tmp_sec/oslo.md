## Calls for Cancellation (2026)

In February 2026, Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] announced that the next Israeli government must cancel the Oslo Accords outright, extend Israeli sovereignty across the West Bank and encourage Palestinians to emigrate from both the West Bank and the Gaza Strip.[N] Speaking at a gathering of his [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionism]] party, Smotrich described the steps as essential to foreclose any possibility of a Palestinian state, insisting there was no other lasting solution to the conflict.[N]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] summarized the 1993 agreement as a framework for the two-state solution in which the Palestinians would immediately recognize the State of Israel while the Israel Defense Forces implemented a five-year plan of incremental withdrawal from Gaza and the West Bank, culminating in recognition of Palestinian statehood.[N] He noted that Prime Minister [[yitzhak-rabin|Yitzhak Rabin]], a retired IDF general, was assassinated by a Jewish extremist after signing the accords, opening the way for the rise of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], who has since boasted that upon taking power in 1996 he deliberately subverted the accords and ensured the troop drawdown never occurred.[N]
