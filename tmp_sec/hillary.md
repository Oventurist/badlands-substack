## Epstein Files Disclosure Dispute (February 2026)

In February 2026 Clinton publicly accused the Trump administration of slow-walking the release of the Jeffrey Epstein files and of attempting a cover-up.[N] Speaking in a BBC interview from Berlin, she demanded complete public disclosure of the records along with open congressional testimony, stressing that she and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] had nothing to hide despite their names appearing in the roughly three million pages released under the disclosure legislation.[N] Her intervention came as Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] pressed Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] over redactions during a [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] hearing, and as Badlands commentators argued the resulting pressure had been redirected away from the networks the files documented and toward the Trump administration itself.[N]
