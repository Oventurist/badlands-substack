## Fiscal Year 2027 Preliminary Budget

On February 17, 2026, Mamdani released a preliminary $127 billion budget for fiscal year 2027 that included a proposed 9.5 percent increase in property tax rates — the first such increase in more than two decades.[N] He characterized the hike as a reluctant "last resort" to close a $5.4 billion deficit inherited from the previous administration, which he attributed to years of fiscal mismanagement.[N]

Mamdani laid out two paths for closing the gap: pressing Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]] and state lawmakers in Albany to raise taxes on high earners and profitable corporations, or relying on the limited tools available to the city — a broad-based property tax increase on millions of residential and commercial properties, combined with withdrawals from reserve funds.[N] The property tax increase would generate roughly $3.7 billion.[N] City Comptroller [[mark-levine|Mark Levine]] called the property tax route "a pretty extreme option."[N]
