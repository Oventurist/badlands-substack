## Discovery of the Comey leak memos (2025)

Memos detailing evidence that former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] authorized leaks of classified information to reporters before the 2016 election were discovered by Patel earlier in 2025, according to Just the News.[{n}] The documents had been redacted by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in versions initially transmitted to [[congress|Congress]], until Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] removed the redactions and sent new copies to the House and Senate Judiciary committees.[{n}]
