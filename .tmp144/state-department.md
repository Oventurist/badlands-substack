## Continuous vetting of 55 million visa holders (August 2025)

On Thursday, August 21, 2025, the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] said it was reviewing more than 55 million people holding valid U.S. visas for any violations that could lead to deportation, part of a growing crackdown on foreigners permitted to be in the United States.[{n}] In a written answer to a question from The Associated Press, the State Department said all U.S. visa holders — including tourists from many countries — are subject to "continuous vetting," with an eye toward any indication they could be ineligible for permission to enter or stay.[{n}] Should such information be found, the visa would be revoked, and a holder inside the United States would be subject to deportation.[{n}] The department's language suggested the vetting process, which officials acknowledge is time-consuming, is far more widespread than previously understood and could mean even those approved to be in the U.S. abruptly lose permission.[{n}] Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted that the Migration Policy Institute estimates temporary visa holders represent only 5% of total foreign-born U.S. residents, quipping that either the estimate is far off "or we have 1.1 billion illegal aliens here."[{n}]

## Bounty on Nicolás Maduro (August 2025)

Roughly a week before August 21, 2025, the State Department placed a $50 million bounty on Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s head.[{n}] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the bounty as convincing evidence of the "Trump–Maduro kayfabe," arguing it gave Maduro the narrative shielding he needed to avoid being targeted by the cartels while covertly aiding U.S. efforts against the drug trade.[{n}]
