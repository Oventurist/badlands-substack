## Caribbean deployment and the drug war (August 2025)

In August 2025 the United States sent air and naval forces to the southern Caribbean Sea in what the [[white-house|White House]] described as part of a broader campaign against drug trafficking groups operating in Latin America, particularly those linked to Venezuela.[{n}] Bolivian President [[luis-arce|Luis Arce]] criticized the deployment as an attempt to control the region for its natural resources rather than a genuine effort against narcotics trafficking.[{n}] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued instead that the tension between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] — including a $50 million [[state-department|State Department]] bounty on Maduro — was staged conflict giving Maduro cover while both men pursued the cartels and corrupt commodity-trading executives.[{n}]
