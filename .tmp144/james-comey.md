## Declassified memos on authorized 2016 leaks

Federal prosecutors gathered evidence from Comey's top lieutenants that he authorized the leak of classified information to reporters just before the 2016 election, but declined to bring criminal charges, according to recently declassified memos reported by Just the News in August 2025 — documents that call into question the former [[fbi|FBI]] director's testimony to [[congress|Congress]].[{n}] The revelations involved former FBI general counsel [[james-baker|James Baker]] and former Comey chief of staff James Rybicki, and were memorialized in documents that FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] discovered earlier that year; the relevant passages had originally been redacted by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in versions sent to Congress.[{n}] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] intervened to eliminate the redactions and dispatched new versions of the memos to the House and Senate Judiciary committees.[{n}] The memos detail evidence and interviews gathered by U.S. Postal Inspection Service agents concerning classified information leaked to *The New York Times* in October 2016, ahead of the election in which [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] defeated Hillary Clinton.[{n}]
