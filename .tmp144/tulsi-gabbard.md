## NOFORN directive on Ukraine negotiations (2025)

According to CBS News reporting relayed by the Kyiv Post in August 2025, Gabbard signed a classified memo on July 20 directing U.S. intelligence agencies to designate all analysis and reporting on the Russia–Ukraine peace negotiations as "NOFORN," meaning no foreign dissemination.[{n}] The order effectively barred the [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] alliance — the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and New Zealand — from intelligence they had long relied on to coordinate with Washington, and restricted distribution even within the U.S. intelligence community to the agencies that produced the material, with the sole exception of reports already made public.[{n}] The directive did not cover diplomatic reporting or military intelligence unrelated to the negotiations, but experts told CBS News it risked undermining decades of allied trust.[{n}]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] welcomed the move as "a delightful development," connecting it to the previous day's coverage of stripped security clearances and arguing that if the horrors of recent decades were directed by a globalist intelligence apparatus, the cutoff was cause for excitement.[{n}] Invoking George Washington's farewell address warning against habitual national attachments and antipathies, she concluded that "Tulsi, it appears, is finally taking him seriously."[{n}]
