## Ukraine peace demands after the Alaska summit (August 2025)

In August 2025, less than a week after meeting President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Alaska, Putin made clear what it would take for him to cease military operations in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], and his demands were formally delivered to Washington on the following Friday, according to sources familiar with the Kremlin's negotiations cited by Reuters.[{n}] Moscow's terms were no [[nato|NATO]] membership for Ukraine, no Western troops on Ukrainian soil, and the surrender of the Donbas region.[{n}] Reporting also indicated Putin would agree to freeze the front lines where they stand in Kharkiv and Zaporizhzhia and relinquish some territory Russia had captured in the Kharkiv, Sumy and Dnipropetrovsk regions.[{n}]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed Putin as the one world leader still in power who publicly opposed Western intervention in Iraq in 2003, who claimed Barack Obama was responsible for funding and forming [[isis|ISIS]] before Trump did, and who described the United States as run by a cabal of Satanists before Q did.[{n}] From this he posed a series of questions about when "the good guys" began fighting back, who started what he calls the Sovereign Alliance, and why the Deep State made the alleged Trump–Putin "secret relationship" its most powerful anti-MAGA narrative — asking what if Russia collusion was real "and what if it was a good thing."[{n}] He concluded that Trump and Putin are not engaged in negotiations so much as "taking the collective mind along a narrative arc whose endgame is peace," at the expense of the globalist hegemon.[{n}]
