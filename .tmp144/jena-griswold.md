## Kline appointment and the Peters case (2025)

At the beginning of 2025 Griswold's deputy secretary resigned and was replaced by "former" [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] attorney [[andrew-kline|Andrew Kline]], who according to Badlands reporting was secretly appointed in March 2025 to run Griswold's office with no announcement — his name simply began appearing on Colorado Department of State mastheads.[{n}] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] raised the appointment while covering President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s August 2025 demand that Colorado release [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]], describing Griswold's office as the one that tried to remove Trump from the ballot "for democracy," that persecuted Trump supporters as a matter of policy, and that was the allegedly injured party in the prosecution of Peters.[{n}]
