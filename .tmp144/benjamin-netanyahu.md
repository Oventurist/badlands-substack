## Gaza City offensive and the "conquer all of Gaza" statement (August 2025)

Speaking to Sky News Australia on Thursday, August 21, 2025, Netanyahu said Israel would conquer all of Gaza even if [[hamas|Hamas]] agreed to a hostage deal, remarks made after the Israel Defense Forces announced the first stage of an operation to seize Gaza City, which it described as a Hamas stronghold.[{n}] "We're gonna do that anyway. There was never a question that we're not going to leave Hamas there," he said.[{n}] He cited the support of U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had said earlier that month that Hamas "can't stay" in Gaza, and compared leaving the group in place to "leaving the SS in Germany ... you clear out most of Germany, but you leave out Berlin with the SS and the Nazi core there."[{n}] Netanyahu argued the war "could end today" if Hamas released the remaining hostages, disarmed and demilitarized Gaza, and dismissed international criticism as a "tsunami of anti-Semitism."[{n}]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called it "a strange negotiating tactic, telling your enemies that you plan to kill them even if they surrender and release all of the hostages," and questioned whether there were any living hostages left — a question he noted Trump had raised on the campaign trail.[{n}] He concluded that Netanyahu had likely made the statement to subvert any chance of a peace deal taking root, since Hamas now had little reason to negotiate, adding sardonically that "you gotta hand it to Bibi. He is truly a skilled politician."[{n}]
