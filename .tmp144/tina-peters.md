## Trump's demands for her release (August 2025)

On Thursday, August 21, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] renewed his call for Colorado to free Peters, a state election official convicted of multiple felonies after breaching voting equipment in the 2020 election, warning he would take "harsh measures" if she were not released.[{n}] On Truth Social he called her "a brave and innocent Patriot who has been tortured by Crooked Colorado politicians, including the big Mail-In Ballot supporting the governor of the State," said she "did nothing wrong, except catching the Democrats cheat in the Election," described her as an old and very sick woman, and demanded her immediate release.[{n}] He did not specify the measures, though he had previously threatened to withhold funding from states that do not align with his policies.[{n}]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that federal funding is the primary lever available to Trump, judging novel theories such as declaring Peters a federal witness a long shot.[{n}] She noted that funding cuts were already existential for Colorado — by some reports as much as 40% of the state's budget comes from federal subsidies — and that Trump's post landed just as the Colorado Legislature opened a special session to address a $1.2 billion deficit caused by federal cuts, calling the timing "perfect" and "the art of the deal in action."[{n}] She argued that the truth about what Peters found, and what was done to her because of it, "will bring down the whole corrupt temple in Colorado and the nation," and connected the case to the secret March 2025 installation of former [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] attorney [[andrew-kline|Andrew Kline]] as deputy running Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]]'s office — the office that tried to remove Trump from the ballot and that was the allegedly injured party in Peters's prosecution.[{n}]
