## Syria raid and Sahel allegations (August 2025)

On August 19, 2025, U.S. forces killed a senior ISIS official during a raid in northern Syria, according to a [[centcom|CENTCOM]] announcement two days later; the unnamed official was described as a key financier who helped orchestrate attacks in Syria and Iraq.[{n}] CENTCOM Commander Admiral Brad Cooper said the command would continue to pursue ISIS terrorists "with unwavering determination, throughout the region."[{n}]

Separately, Russian deputy UN representative [[dmitry-polyansky|Dmitry Polyansky]] criticized the UN Secretary-General's latest report on the threat posed by Islamic State in Africa while alleging that [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s special services were arming and training militants in the Sahel.[{n}] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] connected the allegations to the 2011 [[nato|NATO]] intervention against Gaddafi and to the creation of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and ISIS, contending that ISIS affiliates in the Sahel were being animated by "Ukraine" — which he glossed as the [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] — while the M23 group in the Democratic Republic of the Congo was being animated by [[israel|Israel]].[{n}]
