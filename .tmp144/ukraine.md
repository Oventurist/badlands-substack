## Alleged subversion in Africa's Sahel (2025)

In August 2025 Russia's deputy representative to the United Nations, [[dmitry-polyansky|Dmitry Polyansky]], told a [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] briefing that Ukraine was providing weapons, drones and training for militants to stage coordinated attacks on governments in Africa's Sahel region.[{n}] He said "concrete facts" demonstrated that Ukrainian special services, including the Defense Intelligence of Ukraine, had been involved in subversive activities in Sahel countries and other parts of Africa, and called for an investigation into what "the Kiev regime is doing on the African continent."[{n}] Badlands commentary treated the allegations as consistent with its long-running thesis linking the 2011 NATO intervention in Libya, the creation of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and [[isis|ISIS]], and present-day Western intelligence operations conducted through Ukrainian proxies.[{n}]

## Putin's terms for ending the war (August 2025)

Moscow's formal demands for ending the war, delivered to Washington days after the Alaska meeting between [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], were no [[nato|NATO]] membership for Ukraine, no Western troops on Ukrainian soil, and surrender of the Donbas region.[{n}] Reporting indicated Putin would agree to freeze the front lines in Kharkiv and Zaporizhzhia and relinquish some captured territory in the Kharkiv, Sumy and Dnipropetrovsk regions.[{n}] Intelligence and analysis on the negotiations was simultaneously withheld from the [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] partners under a NOFORN directive signed by [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]].[{n}]
