## Pressure and the 2025 reset thesis

In August 2025, as Chairman [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] prepared his final Jackson Hole speech, the Federal Reserve faced mounting pressure from the [[white-house|White House]] to lower interest rates while inflation remained above the 2% target and the labor market weakened, with July's jobs report showing fewer additions than expected and downward revisions erasing prior gains.[{n}] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the central banking system as "the invisible enemy propping up the fiat regime" and argued that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had cornered it on both Actual and Narrative fronts, forcing every decision to accelerate the system's exposure and eventual reset.[{n}]

He described the episode as part of a controlled demolition of the Fed and the fiat apparatus, exposing hypocrisy at the top and cultivating a public mandate to question Fed independence while revealing how insiders had gamed the system for personal gain.[{n}] Trump, in this account, amplified the narrative push without intervening in the actual squeeze, letting market pressure build long enough to trigger capitulations among fiat-dependent players.[{n}] Signals of a coming reset included the fast-tracking of [[stephen-miran|Stephen Miran]] to a board vacancy and Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]]'s open anticipation of a "global economic reordering," with the [[lisa-cook|Lisa Cook]] controversy spotlighted as a key fracture point.[{n}]
