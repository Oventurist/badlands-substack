## Unredaction of the Comey leak memos (2025)

In August 2025, Attorney General Bondi intervened to eliminate redactions in declassified memos concerning former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]], dispatching unredacted versions to the House and Senate Judiciary committees.[{n}] The memos, discovered earlier that year by FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], recorded evidence gathered by U.S. Postal Inspection Service agents from former FBI general counsel [[james-baker|James Baker]] and former Comey chief of staff James Rybicki indicating that Comey had authorized leaks of classified information to *The New York Times* in October 2016, before the election won by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[{n}] The passages had originally been redacted by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in the versions first sent to [[congress|Congress]].[{n}]
