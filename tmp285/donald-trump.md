## February 2026: State of the Union, Jalisco, and Mar-a-Lago

Trump was scheduled to deliver the 2026 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address to a joint session of [[congress|Congress]] at 9:00 PM ET on February 24, 2026, the first formal State of the Union of what Badlands writers call the Golden Age; the media had insisted the previous year's joint address was technically not a State of the Union.[{n}] The speech came amid tariff policy disputes and international tensions, and dozens of House and Senate Democrats — reportedly including Ed Markey, [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], Ruben Gallego, Tina Smith, and Jeff Merkley — planned to skip it, while more than a dozen Democrat congresswomen planned to wear white in protest.[{n}] Trump invited both US Olympic hockey teams following their Milan gold medals; the women's team declined citing academic and professional commitments after the Games, while the men's team appeared in a viral video to accept, prompting Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] to say leadership would "work and do what we can to accommodate" despite chamber space limits.[{n}]

On the Mexican front, Trump responded to the killing of [[el-mencho|Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes]] in Jalisco by posting, "Mexico must step up their effort on Cartels and Drugs!"[{n}] The *New York Post* reported that his administration had quietly secured the transfer of roughly 100 cartel leaders — including El Mencho's brother — from Mexico into US custody before the raid, and Trump confirmed he had offered to send US troops against the cartels but was turned down by Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]].[{n}]

In the early hours of February 22, 2026, an armed man breached the perimeter of [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] and was shot dead by Secret Service agents and a sheriff's deputy; Trump and the First Lady were at the White House at the time.[{n}] Addressing the incident on Monday, Trump said, "I don't know how long I'll be around, got a lot of people gunning for me, don't I, hon?" — a remark directed at [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]].[{n}] Badlands commentary framed the episode as another apparent assassination attempt downplayed by corporate media, and urged prayer for the President's protection.[{n}]

Badlands also cast Trump as the figure restraining a war with Iran, arguing he had given his word to Arab partners to bring regional peace and that standing up to the Israel Lobby and the [[neoconservatives|neoconservatives]] would be the greatest possible demonstration of strength.[{n}]
